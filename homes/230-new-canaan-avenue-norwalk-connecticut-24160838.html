About this home

LIVES LIKE A HOUSE, WITH THE EASE OF A CONDO! Tucked along the scenic Norwalk/New Canaan border, this free-standing townhome offers breathtaking treetop views in the sought-after Orchard Lakes complex, anchored by Hams Pond. Enjoy exceptional privacy -- and a glimpse of the water -- from the expansive wrap-around deck; an ideal setting for birdwatching, morning coffee or unwinding at sunset. Designed to live like a single-family home, the residence blends generous space with the convenience of a well-maintained community. The sun-drenched two-story living room features soaring ceiling-to-floor windows and an open plan layout, creating an inviting and airy feel throughout. A serene primary suite with a large, spa-like primary bathroom completes the main level. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom offer flexibility for guests, office space or everyday living. The over-sized, finished lower level, with an additional full bathroom, walks out onto a private patio and offers endless potential for a future media room, family room, in-law suite, gym or large office. An adjacent, detached 2-car garage completes this offering. Surrounded by beautifully landscaped grounds and tranquil waterside walking trails with charming gazebo, this home is part of a secure, thoughtfully managed condominium community. Just a short drive to New Canaan for shopping and restaurants, yet you'll also enjoy private beach rights as a Silvermine resident! Don't miss this rare opportunity!
