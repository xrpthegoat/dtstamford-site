About this home

Amazing Location | Turnkey Updates | Sun-Drenched Living! Welcome to this beautifully updated, light-filled ranch set on over half an acre in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury section, bordering Westport. Freshly painted inside and out, this move-in-ready home blends classic charm with thoughtful modern upgrades. The spacious, flexible layout features 3 bedrooms plus a versatile additional room-perfect for a home office, gym, nursery, or guest space-and 2 full baths, including a newly renovated primary bath. The brand-new kitchen shines with modern finishes (Samsung Bespoke appliances & hand-made porcelain tiles from Spain as backsplash) and functionality, while newly refinished hardwood floors add warmth and continuity throughout. At the heart of the home, a bright living room with exposed beams and a wood-burning fireplace creates an inviting atmosphere. Additional living spaces include a dining room, family room, and an eat-in kitchen with a walk-in pantry-ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The primary suite offers a private retreat with an en suite bath with walk in shower and an adjacent room with its own entrance-perfect for today's flexible lifestyle needs. Two additional bedrooms and a full bath are thoughtfully situated for added privacy. Brand new washer/dryer.
