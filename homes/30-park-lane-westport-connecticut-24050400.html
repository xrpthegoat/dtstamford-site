About this home

30 Park Lane is more than just a home; it's a uniquely charming retreat where you can enjoy the best of both worlds - a peaceful, nature-filled setting with easy access to all the amenities and conveniences of town living as well as being only 5 minutes from Compo Beach. As you step inside, you'll be greeted by a warm and welcoming atmosphere, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Large windows bathe rooms in natural light and provide picturesque views of lush surroundings. A spacious, newly renovated deck serves as an ideal spot for alfresco dining or relaxing with a good book. The primary bedroom is a peaceful retreat with ample closet space and a serene ambiance. Three additional bedrooms provide plenty of space for guests, a home office, or a creative studio. Please see Agent Only Remarks.
