About this home

Welcome to 7 Gray Squirrel Drive, a meticulously updated 4-bedroom, 4-bath colonial on a private cul-de-sac in desirable West Norwalk set on a flat 1.11 acres. Step into the stone-floored foyer and discover a living room with vaulted ceilings, brand new hardwood floors, and a wood-burning fireplace. This connects seamlessly to the spacious formal dining room that overlooks the pool. The eat-in kitchen features wall oven, cooktop, 2025 dishwasher, 2024 range hood, 2026 refrigerator, new LVP flooring, and fresh backsplash. You step through a butler's pantry into the family room/den that offers a second fireplace, ceiling fan, and 'glass wall' opening to a screened porch with pool views. There is an office and full bath on this level. Upstairs, there are 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. The primary suite boasts a 2026 remodeled bath with rain shower and frameless glass door, while the hall bath features a custom double vanity. A 4th bedroom over the 2-car garage includes ensuite bath, walk-in closet, and attic access-perfect for guests or in-laws. The bright basement has ample space for storage or to be finished in the future (with laundry). Outside, enjoy a 20x40 in-ground pool with 2025 liner, coping, filter, and pump, with a flat, fenced yard, and mature gardens with fruit trees and flowering plants. This amazing home sits on a quiet dead-end street near I-95, Merritt Parkway, Waveny Park, downtown Darien and New Canaan, beaches, and one-hour train to Manhattan.
