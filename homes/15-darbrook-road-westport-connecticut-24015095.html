About this home

Nestled discreetly on a premier cul-de-sac near Town, this masterfully built home by Cugno Architecture seamlessly connects the interior living spaces with the outdoors to create a resort-like ambiance. The open living spaces flow effortlessly into one another with walls of windows. The main floor includes a spectacular great room with 30' ceilings and a Primary suite, all with breathtaking views, flooded with light. The property is beautifully landscaped by a master gardener and features a stone gunite pool and waterfall, blue stone patios and luscious plantings. This exquisite setting provides multiple entertaining areas that accommodate large parties as well as intimate gatherings. Additional features include cost-saving, energy efficient solar panels, a three-car garage, an enormous soundproof lower level with endless options for work and play, plus amazing storage areas. This is a rare opportunity to a live in a meticulously maintained modern home, sited on 1.45 majestic manicured acres in the heart of Westport.
