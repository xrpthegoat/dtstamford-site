About this home

LOCATION! LOCATION! LOCATION! Who doesn't love Roosevelt Rd? I mean...if you are going to live anywhere...the location and lifestyle at Compo Beach is in a league of its own and envied by all. Steps to the water, beach and all that Compo has to offer! Start your day watching peaceful sunrises over the water's edge and then after a long busy day hit the reset button as you gaze at breathtaking sunsets. This traditional 2 Bedroom 1.5 Bath colonial, located on sought-after quiet street, is filled with charming character and sits on a beautiful oversized lot perfect for outdoor enjoyment w/ mature landscaping, flat yard, pet friendly fencing, and a modern personal electric car charger for your convenience. Perfect place to hang your hat while building your dream home, or just wanting to check out what it's like to live in this amazing award-winning town. Walk to beach, water, marina, and only minutes to Longshore Park offering golf, tennis, pool, boating, paddle tennis, winter skating and more. While here enjoy Westport's nationally acclaimed library, amazing five-star music rocking Levitt Pavilion, Westport Country Playhouse, Museum of Contemporary Art, wonderful seasonal Farmers Market, and of course the vibrant downtown areas with premier shopping, and top rated restaurants. Award winning schools and only minutes to Metro North train for easy commute. Available yearly or short term. Immediate occupancy.
