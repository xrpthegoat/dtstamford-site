About this home

Welcome to Heights Crossing-Darien's premier luxury apartment community offering elevated living with unmatched convenience. Ideally located directly across from the Metro North train station, Heights Crossing offers effortless access to New York City while nestled in the heart of a vibrant coastal town. This newly constructed 1-bedroom, 1.5-bath apartment blends refined design with modern functionality. The thoughtfully designed layout includes a spacious living area with charming bay windows, a stylish powder room, and wood-style luxury vinyl tile flooring throughout. The open-concept eat-in kitchen is appointed with Quartz countertops, a bar-height seating area, custom cabinetry, GE appliances, and a designer tile backsplash. A welcoming foyer and in-unit washer and dryer add to everyday convenience. The full bathroom-featuring a walk-in shower-is located just outside the bedroom for added flexibility. The bedroom itself includes a generous walk-in closet, offering ample storage without compromising on space. Crafted with comfort in mind, the building features enhanced soundproofing between floors and high-performance exterior insulation. Residents will enjoy a beautiful fountain courtyard and retail shopping on the ground level, all just steps from Noroton Heights' shopping district, grocery stores, and pharmacy. Darien's beaches and scenic Great Island are just a short drive away.
