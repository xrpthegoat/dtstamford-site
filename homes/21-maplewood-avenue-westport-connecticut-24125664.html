About this home

Step into a world of moon dances and magic at 21 Maplewood Avenue, Westport, CT. This beautifully renovated home sings with sophistication and modern charm and character, offering a boutique-like living experience that resonates with timeless melodies. Unparalleled workmanship, led by award winning female builder and team who envisioned, created and completed a massive renovation thoughtfully touching every major component of the home. New siding, roof, gutters, windows (where needed), energy efficient electric heat pump HVAC system all add to the integrity of the existing structure. The redesigned layout resulted in a sun filled foyer with direct line of sight to the oversized, over-the-sink kitchen window highlighting the private back deck and garden. With a now seamless interior flow, including new first floor bedroom suite, wide plank white oak flooring, elevated lighting fixtures throughout, this team created a like-new construction experience. Flexible spaces offer a plethora of possibilities to suit every lifestyle need. The repurposed and customized gourmet kitchen with high end appliances sets a culinary stage. Outside, find your own oasis, offering tranquility and a perfect spot for intimate moments or lively get-togethers. This home is where the days like this, come to life, creating a harmonious blend of modern luxury and lyrical inspiration. Imagine a bright side of the road existence while taking a short walk to all cultural experiences that Westport offers.
