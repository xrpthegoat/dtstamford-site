About this home

Rolling Ridge is one of the most sought out condo complexes in Norwalk. This one bedroom with den is spacious with lots of storage. Large living room with plenty of light, an L shaped room that has a dining space with sliders to a balcony. The perfect place to relax and watch the sunset. Kitchen offers space to cook a great meal. Off the living room is a den that has built ins and a closet. The room could be used as an office or nursery. A half bath is located in the room. The primary bedroom is spacious and gets a beautiful morning light when the sun rises. Walk in closet with ample storage and a full bathroom with shower/tub as well as, a linen closet. In unit laundry is located in the hall, the unit has two newer A/C units for summer cooling. This property does not disappoint, every season offers beautiful foliage that changes, a pool for summertime enjoyment as well as a dog park. Convenient location for highway interchanges, shopping, restaurants and so much more. HOA includes Heat/Hot Water. A must see it will not last!
