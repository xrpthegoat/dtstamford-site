About this home

Well-maintained 1BR/1BA residence at The Biltmore, a full-service luxury tower in downtown Stamford. This 557 sq. ft. condo features an open layout, engineered hardwood floors, an updated kitchen with granite countertops and breakfast bar, and a bright living/dining area with a full wall of windows. The bedroom offers a spacious walk-in closet and access to an updated double-entry bathroom. Additional features include central air and the convenience of an in-unit washer/dryer. Building amenities include 24-hour concierge, fitness center, community room/lounge, landscaped 4th-floor courtyard, dry-cleaning services, shuttle to Metro-North, and available fiber-optic internet. Monthly and guest parking options are available through Propark at the adjoining parking facility. Pet friendly. Conveniently located near Bedford and Summer Streets, with their restaurants and shops, Metro-North, Pickleball America, and the Stamford Transportation Center. Easy access to I-95 adds to the appeal of this centrally located residence, offering comfortable living and convenient access to downtown Stamford amenities.
