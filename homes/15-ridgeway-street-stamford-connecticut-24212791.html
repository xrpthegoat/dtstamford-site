About this home

Classic New England charm, completely reimagined! Welcome to 15 Ridgeway Street, a beautifully renovated 3-bedroom Cape in the heart of Springdale, just a walk to Springdale Village, where modern comfort meets energy-conscious living. An inviting, nearly 200 sq. ft. covered front porch sets the tone, while a massive brand-new rear deck overlooks a gorgeous landscaped backyard with lush lawn, mature trees, gardens, and room to entertain. Inside, discover bright, open living spaces featuring new flooring, windows, insulation, and smart LED lighting. The stunning remodeled kitchen boasts two-tone cabinetry, a spacious center island, ENERGY STAR appliances, a convection/air-fry wall oven, and a multifunction convection microwave. Beautifully renovated bathrooms showcase contemporary finishes, including a luxurious main-level full bath with radiant heated floors extending right into the shower! Beyond the aesthetics, thoughtful efficiency upgrades include an active solar system, a high-efficiency heat-pump water heater installed in 2026, new insulation and windows, and a roof approximately two years old. Enjoy the perfect blend of timeless character, modern convenience, and exceptional indoor-outdoor living, all within walking distance of the Springdale Train Station and the restaurants, cafes, and shops of Springdale Village. Move right in and enjoy!
