About this home

Welcome to the village of Rowayton and 14 Crooked Trail! This charming ranch sits on a level half acre of land and is in move in condition. Spacious rooms filled with natural light, finished space in lower level, full two car garage and plenty of useable outdoor space. Livingroom with wood burning fireplace and book cases opens to dining room and very large deck. The kitchen opens to oversized family room with built-ins taking in the backyard view. 3 bedrooms all with large closets, two full baths and a home office round out over 2,200 square feet of living space on one floor. The lower level has a great workout/play-room and tons of storage space. Recent improvements include freshly painted, refinished hardwood floors, skylights, deck, roof, basement windows, water main, generator hardwiring. Walk to all Rowayton amenities: shops, restaurants, train station to NYC/New Haven, beach, library, schools, Five Mile River marinas, dog park and nature preserve.
