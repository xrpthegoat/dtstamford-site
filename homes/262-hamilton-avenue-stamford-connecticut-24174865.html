About this home

Endless potential awaits in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood! Situated on a corner lot, this property offers an exciting opportunity for investors, contractors, renovators, or buyers ready to bring their vision to life and create instant equity. Flexible layout with potential for an accessory dwelling Unit (ADU) setup with private entrance, the possibilities here are tremendous. First floor Living room with Wood Burning Fireplace opens to kitchen, full bathroom and 2 bedrooms. Upstairs Has 2 bedrooms, office and efficiency kitchen. The upstairs bathroom has been dismantled with plumbing still remaining, providing a head start for renovation plans. Major improvements already completed include a high efficiency boiler (apx 8 yrs), new on demand hot water system (3/24) and a newer roof on the garage (apx 4-5 yrs). Hardwood floors are under carpets on 2nd floor. Nice side yard with mature plantings, ample parking and large detached two car garage with second floor. Garage is currently used for commercial business supply storage. Conveniently located close to Glenbrook train station, shopping, restaurants, downtown Stamford, and major commuting routes. Property is being sold AS IS including contents. Bring your imagination, roll up your sleeves, and transform this property into something truly special.
