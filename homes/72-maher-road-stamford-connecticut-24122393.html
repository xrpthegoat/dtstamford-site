About this home

Welcome to this meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom Colonial offering 1,926 square feet of living space. Set on a quiet side street in the desirable R-75 single-family zone, this classic home has had only two owners in its 96-year history and exudes timeless character throughout. The main level features a spacious living room, a comfortable dining room, and two generously sized bedrooms that share a full bath. The updated kitchen boasts granite countertops and modern amenities while preserving the home's vintage charm. Hardwood floors can be found under most of the wall-to-wall carpeting throughout the house. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms that share a second full bath, along with a charming older kitchenette (sink only), a walk-in storage closet, and a cozy dining nook. Don't miss the walk-up attic-perfect for storage or potential future expansion. The unfinished basement leads directly to a rare two-car tandem garage, offering plenty of storage and workspace options. Enjoy relaxing evenings on the rocking chair front porch with elevated western exposure and distant views of the city. This home is heated by oil, with natural gas fueling the hot water heater and stove. Conveniently located near I-95, Exit 9, for an easy commute. Sold As-Is.
