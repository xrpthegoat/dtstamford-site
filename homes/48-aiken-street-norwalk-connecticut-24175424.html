About this home

Withdrawn until July 1, upon vacancy of the current tenant. Welcome to this charming halfduplex townhouse in a prime Norwalk location. Lovingly maintained, this home offers 3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, a finished basement, and numerous updates including a new roof (2026) and a newer deck. The first floor features a bright living room with hardwood floors, a dining room with parquet hardwood, a convenient half bath, and a kitchen that opens to a spacious deck-perfect for entertaining. All three bedrooms are located on the second floor, each with beautiful hardwood flooring. The finished basement includes a large closet, laundry area, and walkout access to a quiet patio area. Located close to shopping, dining, schools, and major highways, this home offers both comfort and convenience. The property is part of an HOA with no monthly fee; bylaws are in place, and exterior expenses along with the master insurance policy are shared. A wonderful opportunity-don't miss it.
