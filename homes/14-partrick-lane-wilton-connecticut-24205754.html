About this home

Charming 1927 converted barn set on two private acres in Wilton's desirable Nod Hill neighborhood. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac, 14 Partrick Lane offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and 2,610 square feet of heated living space, combining the character of a classic New England home with flexible spaces for today's lifestyle. At the heart of the home is a sun-filled living room anchored by an impressive stone fireplace. The spacious kitchen features white cabinetry and a quartz-topped island and opens to the family room with a large bay window. A charming sunroom provides the perfect spot to read, relax, or work from home. A main-level bedroom and full bath offer added flexibility. Upstairs, the generously sized primary bedroom features its own fireplace, along with an additional bedroom, full bath, and second-floor laundry. The walk-out lower level adds approximately 450 square feet of heated living space with a recreation room and full bath, creating even more options for guests, work or everyday living. Outside, two acres of mature grounds create a wonderful sense of privacy, while a detached two-car garage and additional finished space offer even more possibilities. Located in the sought-after Nod Hill area of Wilton, close to town, schools, shopping, and dining, this is a wonderful opportunity to own a home with history, character, land, and room to make it your own. Offered at $899,000 - come discover the charm and possibilities of 14 Partrick Lane.
