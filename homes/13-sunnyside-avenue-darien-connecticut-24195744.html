About this home

A beautifully maintained home with thoughtful updates throughout, this property blends comfort, style, and everyday convenience-especially with the elementary school just a short walk away. Outdoor living shines with a welcoming covered front porch and a bluestone patio set within a private fencedin yard. It's a truly moveinready home in a prime location.
