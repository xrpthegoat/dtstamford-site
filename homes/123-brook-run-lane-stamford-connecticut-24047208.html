About this home

Welcome to 123 Brook Run Lane! This Spectacular Turn-Key 3-bedroom 2-bath Ranch home located in the desirable Mid-Ridges section of Stamford. Sunny & spacious through out this residence offers ample space for the most discerning buyer. The main level features a bright and airy living room, dining room leading out to an expansive deck w/ sliders overlooking a picturesque backyard. A well-appointed newly renovated kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances, and three generous bedrooms with custom ship-lap trim, & hardwood floors through-out. The two full baths are both updated along with the full lower level which is expansive and newly renovated as well w/ Sliders to your private paver patio & access to the enclosed private yard. The garden level showcases a finished rec room & family room, ideal for entertaining, potential home office, or 4th bedroom. This home is situated in a convenient neighborhood, close to parks, Merritt Pkwy, & downtown Stamford and all local amenities, making it a perfect choice. .
