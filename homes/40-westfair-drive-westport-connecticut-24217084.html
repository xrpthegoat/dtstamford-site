About this home

Welcome to 40 Westfair Drive! Tucked away on a quiet residential street, this charming Cape offers the perfect combination of comfort, privacy, and convenience. Freshly painted throughout, the home features a beautifully renovated kitchen and updated bath, hardwood floors in the main living areas, and an inviting living room. The primary bedroom and two upstairs bedrooms feature new carpeting, while an additional main level bedroom with hardwood floors offers wonderful flexibility as a home office, guest room, or den. Thoughtfully refreshed and move-in ready, this delightful home provides a peaceful retreat away from the bustle of main roads while remaining just minutes from Westport's shopping, dining, transportation, and everyday amenities. An exceptional rental opportunity for those seeking the charm of a neighborhood setting with the convenience of all that Westport has to offer.
