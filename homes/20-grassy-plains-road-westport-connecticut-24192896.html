About this home

Beautifully designed for effortless living, this light-filled and airy home at 20 Grassy Plains blends luxury, comfort, and convenience in one exceptional offering. Featuring a highly sought-after first-floor primary suite, this residence is ideal for both everyday living and long-term flexibility. An elevator connects multiple levels, providing ease and accessibility throughout the home, along with a finished basement offering approximately 1,500 square feet of additional living space. The open-concept floor plan is enhanced by abundant natural light, soaring ceilings, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The chef's kitchen is equipped with top-of-the-line appliances, custom cabinetry, and premium finishes, opening beautifully to the main living and entertaining spaces. Step outside to a spacious deck perfect for outdoor dining, relaxing, and entertaining guests. Thoughtfully designed with both elegance and practicality in mind, this home offers modern luxury, low-maintenance living, and exceptional comfort in a desirable location. Prefer a lease of 2 years or longer, with rent including garbage collection, lawn care, and snow removal. Perfect for those seeking a sophisticated lifestyle with a first-floor primary bedroom, elevator access, luxury finishes, open floor plan, and bright contemporary living.
