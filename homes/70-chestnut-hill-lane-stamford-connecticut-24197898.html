About this home

Welcome to 70 Chestnut Hill Lane, a beautifully situated 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath colonial home tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in the highly desirable, southern part of North Stamford. Set on a phenomenally landscaped property, everything here is beautifully manicured and meticulously maintained, from the sprawling front lawn to the expansive backyard. Designed for everyday living and entertaining, the kitchen opens to the family room, creating a natural gathering place at the heart of the home. Cozy up by the fireplace while enjoying views of the gorgeous property. The crown jewel is the sunporch, where windows on three sides bring the outdoors in and create a peaceful setting for dining, relaxing and enjoying the changing seasons. Formal living and dining rooms offer additional space for entertaining. The primary suite features a huge walk-in closet, spa-like bath and a connected fourth bedroom that offers incredible versatility and can easily function as a dressing room, home office or bedroom. A finished basement provides even more space for recreation or entertaining, while generous closets and built-in storage are found throughout. Outside, enjoy the beautifully maintained grounds and the added bonus of Chestnut Hill Park, a neighborhood favorite, just down the street. Close to shopping, dining, schools and major commuting routes, this special home offers the best of Stamford living with everyday convenience.
