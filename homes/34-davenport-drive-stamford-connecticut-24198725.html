About this home

Renovated 3-Bedroom home in the sought-after Davenport Point, a private waterfront association. This home offers a blend of comfort, style, and convenience. The sun-filled interior features a custom kitchen with granite counter-tops, newer appliances, and a center island, opening to a living/dining area with fireplace. The primary bedroom includes an en-suite bath and multiple closets, while two additional bedrooms and a full bath are also located on the second level. Flexible living space includes a home office area and large playroom, with a unfinished basement. Multiple doorways open to a generous blue-stone patio and level backyard, creating an ideal setting for outdoor dining and relaxation. A Tesla Solar Roof adds low energy costs. Close to parks, train, and restaurants.
