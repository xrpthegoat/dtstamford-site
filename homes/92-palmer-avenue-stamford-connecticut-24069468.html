About this home

Discover the Cove through this spacious, updated, multi-family home that sits on a quiet, dead-end street in walking distance to Chelsea Piers and Stamford's beautiful Cove Park, as well as minutes away from downtown Stamford and the train station. Recent upgrades include: newer vinyl siding, roof, and front porch. The main level features three generous sized bedrooms, a full bathroom, kitchen and a washer/dryer unit. The apartment above presents a large, bright dining room/living room, kitchen, two bedrooms, a full bath and sunroom. The lower level has two additional bedrooms, a large basement area, bathroom and a laundry room with sink. The back of the home allows a separate entry to each level. Outside, you will find three detached car garages, which can be used for additional storage or potential workspace. This property has endless income opportunities, or multi-generational living. Come tour this home today and discover the Cove and all its possibilities.
