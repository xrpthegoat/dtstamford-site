About this home

This wonderful bright turn-key modern colonial has 4600+ square feet with 9 feet ceilings and open floor plan. Spacious kitchen offers 5/4" thick maple custom cabinets, wine bar and sink and high-end quartz countertops. All hardwood floors through out from main floor to attic. There are 2 large rooms with beautiful tile in finished basement and a full bath perfect for office, gym, recreation room or a nanny suite. Abundant storage space make the house clean and neat. Upstairs you will find a luxurious master, a walk-in closet, and a bath complete with a shower and soaking tub. 3 additional bedrooms and a laundry room all conveniently in second floor make the life easy. With beautiful baths adjacent to or en-suite in every room, this floor plan is easily tailor made. Third floor boasts 2 large rooms and full bath.Outside is a beautiful fully fenced back yard and large patio, 1 car garage with epoxy floors and tech wall storage system. Located in the Hindley "Blue Ribbon" elementary school district. One house over to a beautiful town maintained "Baker Park" which provides baseball and soccer field, playground and abundant free parking. A short walk to the train. Don't miss this dream home!!!
