About this home

Multiple offers - Best and Final by Monday 8pm, May 5th. Located in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, this stunning Colonial was completely rebuilt in 2017. This meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 3-full bathroom home also features a home office that can be used as a bonus room or 5th bedroom. Notable features include a luxurious kitchen with Carrara marble counters, stainless steel appliances, and an oversized island perfect for entertaining. The sun-filled family room impresses with vaulted ceilings, a gas fireplace, and sliders that open to a flat, fully fenced yard with a blue stone patio-ideal for seamless indoor/outdoor living. The spacious mudroom offers charming shiplap details, built-in cubbies, a custom bench, food pantry, and coat closet-conveniently located off the kitchen with direct garage access. Custom built-ins are complemented by Restoration Hardware lighting and finishes throughout the home, adding refined character and functionality. The primary suite features a large walk-in closet with built-in storage and a luxurious bath with marble countertop, double sinks, and a walk-in glass shower. Upstairs also includes a second-floor laundry room and a hallway bathroom with double sinks. Additional highlights include a pull-down attic and two exterior sheds with electricity and plumbing. Enjoy easy access to downtown, Whole Foods, Ridgeway Shopping Center, schools, parks, and transportation.
