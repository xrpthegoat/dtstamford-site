About this home

Now accepting backup offers. Introducing a rare opportunity to own a meticulously maintained Colonial Cape that exudes both charm and sophistication. This North Stamford home is on 1.24 acres in a private setting, and is less than 5 minutes to the Merritt Parkwy and the High Ridge shopping area. This immaculate maintained home has been designed for both comfort and style, and has 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, over 3,100 sf of living space, plus an inground gunite heated pool. The bright, sunny rooms create a welcoming atmosphere throughout the home. The main level is open, and has an eat in kitchen w/granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. The kitchen opens to the large formal living room with a wood burning fireplace. The family room opens directly to the backyard entertainment area. The dining room, two bedrooms, a full and 1/2 bath, and a beautiful entrance foyer completes the main level. The primary bedroom with a new full bath and a second bedroom are on the upper level. The full unfinished low level incl the washer/dryer, cedar closet and has a high ceiling. The backyard is spectacular with the stone patio, pool, shed, stone & wood fence which cover most of the backyard, lots of yard space surrounded by tall trees for privacy. Recent improv incl a new roof, new pool heater, new siding, new shed, pool was painted and a new well tank & pump. Addit features incl hardwood flrs, attic stor, attach 2 car garage, irrigation sys & granite stone on the exterior of the home.
