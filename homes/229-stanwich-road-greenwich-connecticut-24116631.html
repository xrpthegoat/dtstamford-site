About this home

Timeless Grandeur Meets Modern Luxury in Greenwich. Welcome to an extraordinary estate that redefines elegant living. Perfectly sited on over five acres in prestigious Greenwich, this elegant Center Hall Colonial offers the ultimate in privacy and sophistication. With six spacious bedrooms, twelve beautifully appointed bathrooms, this expansive residence seamlessly blends classic architectural details with refined modern amenities. The sun-drenched interiors are graced with gleaming hardwood floors, oversized windows that frame serene natural views, and exquisite millwork throughout. The main level showcases a layout ideal for both everyday living and formal entertaining. A private au pair suite and guest area provides flexibility for extended guests or live-in staff. Car enthusiasts will appreciate the multiple garages with capacity for up to nine vehicles, a rare and coveted feature. Fitness and recreation are at the forefront with a huge exercise room complete with fireplace, offering a perfect space to unwind. Step outside to a quiet oasis where a resort-style heated pool takes center stage, accompanied by your own private basketball court, all surrounded by rolling lawns and mature trees. Whether hosting a late summer gathering or enjoying a quiet morning, the abundant natural light and peaceful landscaping create a sense of tranquility rarely found. This exceptional estate is more than a home, it is a way of life.
