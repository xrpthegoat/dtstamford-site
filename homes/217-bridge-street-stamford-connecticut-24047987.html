About this home

Welcome to Mill Bridge, a highly sought after complex nestled in desirable mid-city, yet surrounded by brick walkways, park-like grounds and views of the picturesque river. Designer renovated kitchen boasts butcher block counters and shaker-style cabinets. Enjoy hosting holidays in the spacious dining room with bay window that flows directly into the open concept living room with fireplace and balcony overlooking scenic surroundings with picnic table and Adirondack chairs and Rippowam River. Upstairs boasts a bedroom with vaulted ceilings and ample closet space. Primary bedroom has 2 generous sized closets and private full bath. Full size washer and dryer in unit adds to the perfection. Exterior of complex freshly painted and newer roof installed. 1 carport directly under unit and visitor spots close to unit. Walk to Scalzi Park, restaurants, shopping, entertainment, Metro North, and all that downtown Stamford has to offer. Enjoy the city lifestlye with a country living feel. It's all here for the savvy buyer. Just unpack and move right in. Being sold "as-is".
