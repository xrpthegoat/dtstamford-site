About this home

This classic colonial offers spacious, light-filled living and dining areas anchored by a recently renovated eat-in kitchen. A comprehensive renovation and expansion in 2007 brought modern systems, updated finishes, and a more functional layout, giving the home the fresh feel of a much newer build. With four bedrooms-three upstairs plus a generous first-floor guest room-and an optional fifth bedroom ideal for a nursery or home office, the home provides remarkable flexibility. A full unfinished basement offers abundant storage and future potential. Outside, enjoy perennial plantings, a private level backyard with a vegetable garden, and a roomy two-car garage. Just minutes from the Noroton Heights train station, Darien Commons, and Hindley Elementary, and close enough to bike to Weed Beach on warm weekends.
