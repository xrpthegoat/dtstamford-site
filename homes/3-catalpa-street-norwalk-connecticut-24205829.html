About this home

Welcome to this inviting 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath single-family home, offering comfort, space, and convenience in a sought-after location. Beautifully maintained inside and out, this home features generous living spaces and an abundance of natural light throughout. The first floor offers a spacious living room, a comfortable bedroom with a full bathroom, and convenient laundry access. The second floor features an eat-in kitchen, two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a separate dining area-providing a functional layout for everyday living and entertaining. The home has been well cared for both inside and outside, with several important updates, including a new chimney, a recently renovated and freshly painted porch, and an AC compressor replaced in 2021. A wonderful opportunity to own a well-maintained home in a convenient and desirable location
