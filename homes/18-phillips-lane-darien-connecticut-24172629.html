About this home

18 Phillips Lane offers a rare combination of neighborhood energy and privacy, set on one of Darien's most sought-after cul-de-sacs. Phillips Lane is known for it's close-knit community; enjoy spontaneous yard-to-yard socializing, biking on the cul de sac with friends, trick-or-treating, and walks to school. Inside, natural light pours through more than 4,200 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space (plus additional 356 sqft. lower-level space). The heart of the home is a stunning great room with vaulted ceilings, a gas fireplace, and a fully outfitted wet bar, all seamlessly connected to the outdoor living area. The kitchen is magazine-worthy, featuring quartz countertops, white oak cabinetry details, and high-end appliances. A bright sunroom and built-in banquette create the perfect everyday dining space, complemented by a formal dining area and living room with a wood-burning fireplace. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite offers a luxurious en suite bath with a double shower and a spacious walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms and two beautifully updated full baths complete the second floor. Outside, enjoy a large +1330 sqft. deck, app-controlled hot tub, outdoor kitchen, and gas fire pit. Natural stone steps lead to a flat, private yard-perfect for both entertaining and quiet moments. 18 Phillips Lane is an incredible opportunity, book your showing today.
