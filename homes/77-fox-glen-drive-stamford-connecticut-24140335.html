About this home

Beautiful Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac on one of the most desirable streets in North Stamford. This home features an effortless open flow with crown molding, limestone and hardwood floors, and two marble fireplaces that add warmth and character throughout. There are formal living and dining areas, perfect for hosting guests, and the gourmet eat-in kitchen opens to a vaulted family room with a fireplace and a deck overlooking the private yard, ideal for everyday living or entertaining. A special first-floor office with custom built-ins provides an ideal work-from-home setup. Upstairs, the private master suite features a tray ceiling, his and her closets, and a luxurious bathroom with a vaulted ceiling and Jacuzzi tub. There is a large bedroom with an en suite bathroom, two additional bedrooms with a Jack-and-Jill bath, and a huge bonus room perfect for a playroom or media room. The walk-up attic and full basement are unfinished, offering potential to expand living space. Additional highlights include central air, security system, sprinklers, and a three-car garage on 2.3 private acres, combining style, function, and room to grow.
