About this home

Remarkable remodeled 3 bedroom, lower level has an office space that can also be converted into a bedroom, 2.5 bath home available. Gorgeous, updated kitchen, spacious living/dining space with newly finished hardwood floors, recessed lighting, updated half bath, one bedroom/office and washer/dryer complete the main floor. Heat is forced air fueled by Natural Gas and Central Air is throughout. 2 parking spaces in the private lot. Beautiful, turnkey rental that is centrally located near Norwalk amenities, shops & restaurants. This is a multi-family and the lower level 1 bedroom unit is occupied. Rentspree application with credit score, application and references required.
