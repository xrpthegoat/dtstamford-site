About this home

Welcome to 16 Perna Lane, an elegant sanctuary in Stamford, CT, where sophistication meets comfort. This remarkable 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home unfolds across 2,068 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space. Upon entry, you are greeted by an open floor plan seamlessly connecting the living room, dining room, and kitchen, creating a spacious and inviting atmosphere. The windowed kitchen is expertly appointed with stainless steel appliances, including a range, refrigerator, dishwasher, and range hood, offering a perfect setting for culinary creativity. The home's beautiful hardwood floors create a cozy and welcoming ambiance. The expansive full basement offers versatile possibilities, from a playroom to a home office. Outside, enjoy the meticulously landscaped private outdoor space, complete with fruit trees, an organic garden, a patio, and a fenced yard, creating a serene retreat. This home offers captivating water views, promising a lifestyle of tranquility and refinement. Discover your ideal haven today. In 2024, this home underwent significant enhancements, including the addition of two custom California closets, a convenient pantry, and a built-in desk in the office. The backyard now features a new shed, providing ample storage space for all exterior needs. For added security and convenience, motion sensor lights have been installed over the garage and around the exterior of the house, along with a state-of-the-art burglar alarm system.
