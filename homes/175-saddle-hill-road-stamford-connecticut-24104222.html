About this home

Welcome to your private retreat on 2 peaceful acres in one of North Stamford's most coveted locations. This gracious Colonial offers lake access & breathtaking views from nearly every room-w/ no homes in sight across the water, just trees & tranquility. A circular driveway leads to the home's elegant entry, where a curved staircase anchors the foyer w/ views of the lake straight through the dining rm. The front-to-back living rm features a fireplace, large windows, & access to a screened-in porch. The dining room opens to a spacious deck w/ tiered steps down to multiple walking paths & your private dock-perfect for boating, fishing, swimming, or simply enjoying the view. The sunny kitchen incl s/s appliances, pantry, dining area, nearby laundry & full bath. The den w/ built-ins, fireplace, & second entrance to the full bath complete the main floor. Upstairs, you won't want to leave the primary suite featuring full lake views, a walk-in closet & renovated luxe bath w/ radiant heat, Jacuzzi tub, & walk-in shower. Two add'l bedrms & large bath finish off the main second floor, w/ two add'l bedrms up the back staircase. A few steps down from the kitchen is a flexible family rm/gym w/ wet bar, half bath, basement access & breezeway to the oversized garage w/ workshop. Other highlights: hdwd flrs, 22kW whole house generator, newer roof/windows/AC, new furnace, & freshly painted exterior. See feature sheet. A rare lakefront gem shared by just 7 homes-Lake Miranda is waiting for you!
