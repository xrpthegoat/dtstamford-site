About this home

Welcome to 2 Wildgoose Lane, a refined residence tucked away at the end of a private road in Norwalk's sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Set on nearly an acre of beautifully landscaped, park-like grounds, surrounded by mature trees & lush greenery on a level lot that offers a sense of privacy & tranquility. The front porch & manicured yard create an inviting first impression, leading into an interior filled w/ natural light. The main level welcomes you with an expansive living room w/ soaring ceilings, picture window, & a seamless flow into the dining area. The open layout is ideal for modern living, anchored by a chef's style kitchen appointed w/ Bosch appliances, double ovens, & generous workspace for culinary creativity. Sliding glass doors open to a Trex-style deck overlooking the tranquil backyard, an ideal setting for entertaining or unwinding in privacy. 4 spacious bedrooms on the main floor & 2 full baths, including a serene primary suite w/ its own private bath, provide a thoughtfully designed living experience. The finished walkout lower level expands the living space w/ a large family room featuring a wood-burning fireplace, a sophisticated laundry room w/ custom built-ins, & a versatile office or flex room. Highlights include an oversized 2-car garage, expansive storage shed, & a prime location minutes from Cranbury Shopping Center, 227 acre Cranbury Park, Stew Leonard's, restaurants, SoNo, Calf pasture beach, both I-95 & Merritt Parkway. Rare opportunity awaits
