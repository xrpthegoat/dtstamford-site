About this home

8 Harriett Lane East is a sunny, welcoming home on a quiet Darien cul-de-sac. Located in the Tokeneke School district, there is nearby sidewalk access to both Darien and Rowayton amenities. The open floor plan allows for a great deal of flexibility including 5 bedrooms and 3 full baths on two floors with a walkout lower level. The spacious family room with a built-in dry bar area and fireplace is perfect for lounging or entertaining. The kitchen features a central island, high-end appliances, double ovens and adjacent cozy sitting area with a fireplace and access to a private deck. The primary bedroom features an ensuite bath with whirlpool tub, shower, double sinks, walk-in closet and access to an additional balcony. Sited on a flat open .53 acre lot with lovely curb appeal!
