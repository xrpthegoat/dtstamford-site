About this home

Welcome to 43 Benedict Court in Norwalk! Tastefully updated Half Duplex/Townhouse style rental in the Spring Hill/West Norwalk area in Norwalk. Conveniently located on cul de sac with low traffic yet just minutes from all major highways (I95, Rt 7, Merritt PWKY), train Stations (SONO/East Norwalk) & CT Ave/SONO shops/restaurants. Upon entering you'll notice a good sized living/family room with hardwood floors, recessed lighting & brand new windows. Plenty of natural light. Newer insulation, windows & Natural Gas, Central air keep utilities LOW! On the main level you'll also find a completely renovated EIK with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, modern style cabinets & MORE! Ample closets/cabinet space in this unit! Door from kitchen leads to rear composite deck overlooking trees & greenery. Truly peaceful. Great for grilling/BBQ's in the spring/summer months. An updated full bath with stand up shower also located on main level. Upstairs you will find 2 comodious bedrooms with high ceilings & recessed lighting + an updated full bath with tiled floors, new vanity & tub. In the lower level you have a 600sq ft+ basement with full sized washer/dryer + plenty of space for storage. Private driveway fits 3+ cars. Tenants MUST have good credit, proof of income & references. * Unit 43 Right side - please do NOT disturb next door *
