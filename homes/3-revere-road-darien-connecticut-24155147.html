About this home

Serene cul-de-sac retreat bathed in sunlight, only steps from schools, train, restaurants and The Commons best describe this beautifully renovated 3,395 sq ft home with 5 spacious bedrooms, 3 full updated baths, and a 3-car garage. The open living room flows to the dining room which is adjacent to a gourmet eat-in kitchen with vaulted ceilings, Wolf range, stainless appliances, and granite counters. A deck overlooks a flat, fenced backyard with a flagstone patio perfect for indoor-outdoor entertaining. The luxurious master suite features a vaulted ceiling, dual walk-in closets, full bath with double vanities, an oversized shower, and sitting area. Four generous additional bedrooms share two baths. Lower level Family Room with fireplace provides extra living space or cozy retreat. Prime walkable location meets modern comfort and privacy. A true must see!
