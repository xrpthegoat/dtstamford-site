About this home

Wonderful yearly rental opportunity on a centrally located, quiet Long Lots cut-de-sac. Offering 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths with an open floor plan this colonial style home is comfortable and clean with an office on the second floor and play/media space in the lower level. This home offers greatly reduced utility charges as it has solar panels, a heat pump, two electric car charges with a Powerwall, and a generator all of which are an added bonus. Can be rented unfurnished or furnished. Available October 2024 until early August 2025. Owner may consider a shorter term rental.
