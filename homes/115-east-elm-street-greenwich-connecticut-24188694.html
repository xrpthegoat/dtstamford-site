About this home

Completely renovated in 2026, this exceptional 4-bedroom Colonial is ideally located in the heart of downtown Greenwich, just moments from Greenwich Avenue's premier shopping, dining, and amenities. Thoughtfully redesigned for today's lifestyle, the open-concept floor plan offers seamless flow and beautifully updated living spaces throughout. The main level features a stylish living room, gourmet kitchen, dining area, and spacious family room with direct access to a generous patio, creating the perfect setting for indoor-outdoor entertaining. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite boasts a large walk-in closet and a beautifully appointed bath, while three additional bedrooms complete the second floor. The finished lower level offers a comfortable family room, mudroom, powder room, and abundant storage. Surrounded by lush gardens and featuring off-street parking, this turnkey home combines modern comfort, privacy, and the convenience of downtown Greenwich living.
