About this home

Beautifully maintained and updated 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath condo in the heart of Wilton. Walk to town! The kitchen, completely remodeled in 2023, opens to the Living room/Dining room. It boasts quartz countertops, and high-end appliances; the microwave has convection bake and air fryer features. The cozy den on the main floor can serve as an office or TV room. The loft on the second floor is great, doubling as a guest bedroom as well as office. The loft has an open wall overlooking the stairwell and a large picture window view to the outdoors. Not all condos have wonderful outdoor space, but this one does. The flagstone patio, accessible from the main living area, is spacious and private. There's plenty of storage in the crawl space adjoining the garage. The complex is convenient to Wilton's award-winning public schools, and you can walk to nearby stores, restaurants, doctors' offices, the library, etc. It is just 10 minutes to I-95 and the Route 7 connector/Merritt Parkway. It is also close to train lines!
