About this home

Located in Stamford's vibrant Mid City neighborhood, 13 Dolsen Pl offers easy access to shopping, dining, parks, transportation, houses of worship, and more - putting convenience at your doorstep. Set on a quiet residential street, the two-story layout provides great flow for both daily living and entertaining. Practical amenities include gas heat, central air, an in-unit washer/dryer, and private parking. A smart option for those seeking comfort and accessibility in the heart of Stamford.
