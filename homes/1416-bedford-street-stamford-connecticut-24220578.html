About this home

Endless opportunity awaits savvy investors at this unique and desirable property in the heart of Stamford. This building stands proudly at the corner of Bedford Street & 2nd Street in the RMF Zone, ready to provide a new owner with a variety of uses or development options. Most recently used as the private medical office of one of Stamford Hospital's most beloved surgeons with a two-level apartment above, this 2,700+/- sqft building offers tremendous flexibility. The main level consists of a large entrance and various exam/reception/storage rooms plus a pantry/kitchenette/laundry and bathroom. Two staircases lead to what was used as a residence on the 2nd & 3rd floors. This sunny, cheerful home has an open kitchen/dining area, sun-filled living room, several flexible bedrooms/other rooms, two full baths and space for an additional laundry. The full, unfinished basement provides extra space. This prime property is located just steps from the Ridgeway Center and near endless shopping, dining & conveniences, houses of worship, parks, major employers and more. Attractive surrounding properties have been developed into housing, while others are utilized as professional buildings and some as mixed-use facilities. Private off-street parking and a two car garage/storage building add to the offerings of the property. 1416 Bedford Street has been cherished by the longtime owners and is ready for a new chapter, sold as-is. Please see attached Zoning information for details on RMF Zone.
