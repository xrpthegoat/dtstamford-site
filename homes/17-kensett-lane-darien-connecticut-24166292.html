About this home

This highly desirable corner Norwood unit, with a newly redone private patio and secluded backyard is located in a great spot in the Kensett community. The 4 bedroom unit has high-end finishes throughout including professional appliances, extra thick granite countertops and beautiful plantation shutters. The light filled open floor plan with high ceilings invites casual entertaining or cozy time in front of the beautiful gas fireplace. The main floor sun room with two walls of windows and additional fireplace affords private space for a home office. Upstairs the luxurious master suite has a huge walk-in closet with built-ins, and a beautiful master bath with a soaking tub, large shower and double marble topped vanity. Two additional large bedrooms, both with private baths and walk-in closets complete the second floor. The lower level has large finished area for entertainment or recreation. There is another study area with a full bath and walk- in closet which could be an additional office or 4th bedroom. There is additional storage space and an owner's locked closet. Come enjoy the easy, highly energy efficient life style at Kensett including a heated pool, clubhouse, fitness center and firepit. The complex abuts a 4 acre land trust complete with walking trails.
