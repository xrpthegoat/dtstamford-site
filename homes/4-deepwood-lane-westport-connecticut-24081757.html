About this home

RIVER WATERFRONT OASIS - Sited on 2 lush acres on the Aspetuck River, this elegant custom 6 bedroom, 5.1 bath home offers a rare blend of natural beauty & refined living, an idyllic sanctuary with abundant natural light. Designed for both everyday comfort and grand entertaining, the property features exceptional indoor & outdoor spaces all thoughtfully crafted to capture the breathtaking views of the river, waterfall, & perennial gardens. An open floor plan creates a seamless flow between the sunlit living areas. The well-appointed gourmet kitchen with a spacious dining area opens to the inviting family room & nearby den/playroom-making it the true heart of the home. Walls of glass frame the formal living & dining spaces with access to a large deck that spans the length of the house. Exquisite primary suite is a private haven with stunning views from the balcony plus a sitting area, dressing room & spa-like bath. Each additional bedroom offers ensuite comfort. Expansive walk-out lower level includes an accessory apartment with flexible options for guests or added recreational space. Notable features include: scullery, pantry, mudroom, wood burning stove, 2 fireplaces, generator, solar panels, sound system, hardwood floors, paved courtyard, ample parking, 5 garage spaces, EV charger, porch, 2 irrigation systems. Ideally located with easy access to top rated schools, the Merritt Parkway, & town, this unique property has the perfect balance of luxury, privacy & convenience
