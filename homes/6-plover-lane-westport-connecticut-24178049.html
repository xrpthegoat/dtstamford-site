About this home

Brand new furnished luxury coastal retreat available for rent in the highly sought-after Saugatuck Shores neighborhood. Privately situated on a quiet cul-de-sac, this exceptional 3,540 SF home offers 4 bedrooms, 4 full baths, and 2 half baths with breathtaking direct views of the Long Island Sound and surrounding marshlands. The lower level features a spacious 2-car garage, mudroom with large storage closet with a premium dry-cleaning steam closet system, recreation room w/ glass doors to rear yard, & exterior storage rm. The main level showcases an expansive open-concept design with a dramatic living room centered around a gas fireplace and floor-to-ceiling windows capturing panoramic water views. The eat-in chef's kitchen is beautifully appointed with custom cabinetry, high-end stainless steel appliances, pantry closet, drawer microwave, beverage cooler in the island w/ seating. Step outside to the rear deck overlooking the peaceful marshlands. A powder room and first-floor bedroom suite, thoughfully furnished as a private home office, complete this level. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite features a French balcony, 2 walk-in closets, linen closet, and spa-inspired bath w/ heated floor, soak tub, walk-in shower, and private water closet w/ smart bidet toilet. Bedrooms 3 & 4 are both en-suite with walk-in closets. Laundry room w/ storage closet. 3rd floor den w/ half bath and a spectacular rooftop deck offering stunning sunsets over Long Island Sound. Min. 1 year rent
