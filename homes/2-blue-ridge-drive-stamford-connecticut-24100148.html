About this home

Discover the perfect blend of elegance & contemporary comfort in this stunning Tudor-style colonial. Boasting a unique mix of stucco, wood, and vinyl siding, this residence captures the essence of classic architecture while offering the durability and ease of modern living. The home welcomes you with a grand stone fireplace that anchors the living space, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere for family gatherings also features 4 well-appointed bedrooms, including a spacious Primary suite that promises a private & luxurious retreat with ample room for relaxation and rejuvenation. The 2 full bathrooms and a convenient half bath ensure comfort & functionality. A mix of Marble and hardwood floors flow throughout the home, adding a touch of sophistication and ease of maintenance. The newly installed central air conditioning system and oil tank provide peace of mind, ensuring year-round climate control and energy efficiency. Entertain with ease in the outdoor living area with a deck & sizeable patio offer the perfect backdrop for social gatherings. The .46-acre level private backyard is a blank canvas for your landscaping dreams simply enjoying the tranquility of your surroundings. There is an additional 200 square foot partially finished lower level, offering additional space for a recreation, City sewer is being brought up to the area.
