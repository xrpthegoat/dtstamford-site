About this home

Located on the Greenwich-Stamford border, this income-producing retail property offers a stable 6 percent cap and is leased to a successful long-term tenant under a NNN (Triple Net) lease, ensuring minimal management responsibilities for investors. Positioned on Havemeyer Lane, this property benefits from high visibility, strong consumer traffic, and easy accessibility, making it an attractive location for tenants and business operators. The combined GB (business) and R7 (residential) zoning allows for future redevelopment opportunities, offering flexibility for both commercial and residential uses. Surrounded by essential retail, dining, and service providers, this property taps into the high-income demographics of Greenwich and Stamford, with a population of nearly 200,000 residents. With passive income potential, strong tenant demand, and a high-traffic location, this is an exceptional opportunity for investors seeking a low-risk commercial asset in one of Connecticut's most affluent markets.
