About this home

Welcome to Clocktower Close a beautiful example of modern sophistication & amenity entwined in celebration of industrial provenance & historical charm. This unit features 1 bedroom, 1.5 baths, & is 825sqft adorned with warm natural wood ceilings, magnificent walls of exposed brick, robust beams, 20ft tall ceilings & 2 story windows. Allow the buildings architectural excellence bring you in as you step inside this top floor picturesque Manhattan style loft. Just past the coat closet & guest powder room you will find a sizable and well appointed kitchen with ample counter space perfect for entertaining. Take a few steps into the large sunken living room as ceilings soar above & the space floods with natural light via the large windows overlooking a manicured courtyard. Head upstairs to your loft space, ever closer to the wood accents & find the main bedroom space completely open to the sunny living room below enriched with walk-in closet & modern updated on-suite. Experience the communities leading edge onsite amenities, including permitted off street parking, on level laundry, renovated gym, locker rooms w/sauna, dog walk, outdoor picnic areas, community kitchen, business flex spaces, game room, onsite storage center & a available secured parking garage. In close proximity to I-95, Merritt Pkwy, Rt.7, Metro, and all you know & love about Norwalk, including resident beaches, abundant dining & shops. Just minutes to downtown SoNo. Come see why this home may be perfect for you.
