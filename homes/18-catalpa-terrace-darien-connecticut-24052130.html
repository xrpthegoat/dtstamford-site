About this home

Welcome home to 18 Catalpa Terrace. This Cape style charmer has enjoyed many new updates including new furnace, siding, windows, driveway, updated kitchen and bath. Situated on a quiet/private road that leads to the water. Enjoy the cozy fireplace, eat-in kitchen, seasonal water views from the primary bedroom and access to Holly Pond for kayaking or paddle-boarding. Conveniently located to Weed Beach, Pear Tree Beach, Noroton Heights train station, and the Darien YMCA. Darien Commons, only a short distance away, features many shops and restaurants. Fun fact...the last remaining Catalpa tree is in the front yard of #18. Highest & best Tuesday October 15th at 3pm to: Attorney Jeremy Kaye *List Agent for details
