About this home

Nestled on a picturesque residential street surrounded by magnificent homes, this residence offers comfort and charm, with easy access to everything that makes Westport so desirable. A perfect neighborhood for walking, biking, and enjoying your surroundings. This level, 1ac property, w/mature plantings and a park-like setting, will make you never want to leave home. Sip your morning coffee in your sun-drenched three-season room or on the wrap-around deck overlooking the garden & stone patios. Inside, you'll be equally impressed by this beautifully maintained, light-filled home. The main level offers a welcoming living room w/fireplace and large windows. The kitchen features a skylight, beautiful cabinetry, granite counters with a center island. On the upper level, the primary suite includes a full bath w/double vanity. Skylights bring in wonderful natural light. Two additional bedrooms flank another room that can easily be used as a sitting room, office, bedroom, etc. On the lower level, a spacious family or playroom provides additional living space along w/a laundry room, half-bath, and a bright extra room perfect for a second office, art studio, or hobby space. Direct entry from the two-car garage adds everyday convenience. This home is walking-distance to Coleytown Elementary School & minutes to Westport's highly ranked school system. It's just a short drive to Westport's vibrant downtown shopping & dining, beaches, parks, and Metro-North train to NYC
