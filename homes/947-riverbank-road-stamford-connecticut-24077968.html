About this home

Located on a majestic 2-acre property in a sought-after North Stamford neighborhood near the Greenwich border, this elegant custom colonial, built by the current owner, is approached via a Belgian Block-trimmed driveway. Professionally landscaped with up-lighting that highlights the trees and shrubs, the home offers a breathtaking nighttime approach. This 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home features gracious formal spaces and an upstairs family room with versatile uses. Generously sized rooms and a seamless flow create an inviting yet sophisticated atmosphere. Cherished by its original owners, the home is ready for its next chapter of love and memories. The spacious kitchen, with a central island, serves as the heart of the home, opening to a private backyard with a patio, in-ground pool, and spa-an ideal setting for summer gatherings. This prime location provides easy access to Larocca's Country Gourmet Market & Wine Shop, the Mead School, the Long Ridge School and summer camp, and nearby riding trails. Sunrises and sunsets paint spectacular views from this exceptional property. The owner gives the agent permission to list the property on the Greenwich MLS as well.
