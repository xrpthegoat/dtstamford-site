About this home

Set in one of Stamford's desirable residential neighborhoods, this beautifully appointed Colonial blends timeless character with an effortless sense of style. An inviting front porch welcomes you into a home filled with warmth, architectural detail, and inviting living spaces. The heart of the home is the thoughtfully designed kitchen, where reclaimed wood beams add character and warmth to the space. The living room offers an elegant yet comfortable setting centered around a striking wood-burning fireplace, creating a natural gathering place for family and friends. With four bedrooms and two and a half baths, the home offers a versatile layout with plenty of space for both everyday living and entertaining. The finished third floor provides an additional private retreat, complete with a full bathroom and distinctive wood-paneled character that gives the space a cozy, cabin-inspired feel. Outside, the property truly shines. The expansive backyard and private pool create a wonderful setting for summer entertaining, relaxing, and enjoying time with family and friends. From its classic Colonial architecture and welcoming front porch to its distinctive interior details, finished third-floor retreat, and exceptional outdoor space, 25 Tremont Avenue offers a rare combination of character, comfort, and timeless appeal in a wonderful Stamford neighborhood.
