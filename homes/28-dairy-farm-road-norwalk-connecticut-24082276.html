About this home

An amazing location, central to all things Norwalk has to offer. Walk to Schools, Walk to playgrounds and fields. Short drive to all things shopping, train station, waterfront. On a quiet subset of streets. Great curb appeal and flat back yard to enjoy. Inside the home only enhances the value. Not only 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, you also have an extra room which can be an office, two living rooms, a dining room and eat in kitchen. Nice hardwood floors on 2 of the 3 floors of living space, both bathrooms are updated. An attached oversized 1 car garage. Flat, wide driveway to accommodate many cars if entertaining. Additionally, a basement that has extra room for storage.
