About this home

This expansive private estate, set on 1.5 acres of park-like grounds in a tranquil North Stamford cul-de-sac, is nestled within a beautiful enclave of homes. The charming split-level residence welcomes you with a gracious entryway leading to the upper level, where a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace flows seamlessly into the dining room. The adjacent kitchen opens to a bright sunroom, complete with a custom bar, laundry area, and stunning views of the backyard. This outdoor retreat features a pool, cabana, deck, and lush garden-perfect for relaxation and entertainment. Upstairs, you'll find four generously sized bedrooms, thoughtfully separated from the main living areas, along with a full bath. The primary suite boasts ample closet space and a newly renovated en-suite bath. The lower level has a walkout family room with sliders which includes a kitchen and indoor grill, a spa room with sliders that open onto the expansive deck, an office and half bath. Additionally there is a basement area with new carpeting which provides even more flexible space to suit your needs. Don't miss this incredible opportunity to make this stunning home your own!
