About this home

Picture yourself taking walks by the water in Cove Island Park. Don't miss the opportunity to live in this beautiful, freshly painted and totally renovated 3 Bedroom 2 Full Bath Ranch set on level property one house away from the water. Short walk to Cove Island Park with ice skating rink, tennis courts, baseball fields and of course the beautiful beach. Must see inside to appreciate! Please note that the Dining Room off the Kitchen and the Bedroom next to the laundry are not reflected in the total square footage. Please see quote for Flood Insurance in the documents section.
