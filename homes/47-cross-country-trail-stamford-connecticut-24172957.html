About this home

Welcome home to this elegant North Stamford Colonial set on a quiet cul-de-sac, offering the perfect blend of space, light and privacy. A dramatic two-story entry with oversized windows welcomes you into an open floor plan designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Sun-filled rooms, gleaming hardwood floors, and seamless flow define the main level, including a dedicated home office perfect for today's lifestyle. The spacious kitchen features a walk-in pantry and opens to inviting living and dining areas. Upstairs, the serene primary suite boasts a cozy fireplace, accompanied by four additional generously sized bedrooms. The finished lower level includes a full bath, and space ideal for a recreation area, home gym or guest suite. Enjoy outdoor living on a large deck overlooking a private backyard. A circular driveway and three-car garage complete this exceptional offering. **Best offers by tomorrow 6/2 at 5pm**
