About this home

A Rare Opportunity to lease a designer owned, 1 bedroom Executive Loft, masterfully renovated and offered for immediate rent for the first time. Rich in character and impeccably modern, this exceptional residence features soaring cathedral ceilings, spectacular harbor views, and an open-concept design with uninterrupted sightlines from the great room through the kitchen. The stunning modern kitchen showcases quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, a custom built-in coffee bar, and a spacious walk-in pantry, creating a space that is both elegant and functional. A combined washer and dryer, designer finishes throughout, and abundant natural light complete this sophisticated retreat. Ideally located 15 minutes walk to Metro-North and SONO Collection mall and ten minutes walk to SoNo, acclaimed dining, parks and pickleball, this one-of-a-kind residence offers luxury coastal living with exceptional style and convenience.
