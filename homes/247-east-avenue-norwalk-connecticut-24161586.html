About this home

Enjoy coastal living in this sun-drenched second floor apartment overlooking Mill Pond in sought-after East Norwalk. This spacious one bedroom, one and a half bath home features a bright living room with dining area and gleaming hardwood floors, a beautiful sunroom ideal for a private home office, studio, or wellness space. The kitchen includes a cozy enclosed porch overlooking the pond. Additional conveniences include in-unit laundry and access to a walk up attic with exceptional storage space. Enjoy a beautiful private front entrance with foyer plus a private driveway accommodating two cars for off street parking. Landlord is responsible for seasonal lawn care and snow removal for effortless living. Great neighborhood close to the train, SoNo, Norwalk Harbor, and countless amenities.
