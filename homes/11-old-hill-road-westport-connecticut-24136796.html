About this home

Nestled in the heart of Westport's coveted Old Hill neighborhood, this home offers the charm of an English cottage and a thoughtful design that blends classic character with the modern desire for open, connected living. Encompassing nearly 4,000 square feet, it features four ensuite bedrooms, walk-in closets, and a curated layout that combines timeless architectural details with contemporary comfort. At its center, a sunlit great room anchors the home, seamlessly connecting the custom kitchen, dining area, and living room-ideal for casual family living. While the open-concept design fosters connection, the home also offers refined spaces for formal entertaining. A versatile third-floor retreat provides a spacious bedroom or office that opens to a generous outdoor deck-perfect for morning coffee, remote work, or unwinding under the stars. Meticulous millwork, bespoke cabinetry, and finely crafted finishes are paired with a clean, modern aesthetic, creating a home both rooted in tradition and refreshingly current. Outside, an expansive bluestone patio invites alfresco dining and relaxed gatherings. Mature evergreen trees and fragrant plantings surround the property offering exceptional privacy. Just a short stroll to downtown Westport's restaurants, shopping, library, and the Levitt Pavilion, this home offers historic charm, modern ease, and in-town living.
