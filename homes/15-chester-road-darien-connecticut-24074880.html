About this home

Beautiful, fully-renovated colonial with a modern farmhouse style located on a quiet dead-end street, backing up to a 64 acre Woodland Park Nature Preserve within walking distance to schools. Enjoy the beautiful finishes with period charm, hardwood floors with walnut inlays, pocket doors and original brass/glass hardware. Chefs kitchen with high-end Bosch appliances, 10K Fisher Paykel gas stove, large 10' island to accommodate seating for four and gourmet food preparation with pantries galore. Step down from dual entry on either side of the kitchen into a wonderful lower family room with coffered ceilings & a wall of glass windows with fantastic views of the rear garden & the tranquil preserve. This room offers access to a large mudroom & fully renovated garage. Large front family room features enhanced ceiling details & access to the dining room and flex space for an office/playroom or kitchen extension having built-in buffet space & wine fridge. The second floor includes two bedrooms, a full bath plus a primary bedroom with a stunning ensuite renovated bath with Grohe shower kit, pocket doors, large custom walk-in closets & custom storage shelving. The third floor has a second primary bedroom with ensuite bath and ample storage. Updates include new roof (2024), landscaping (20), new interior & exterior paint, kitchen and baths (21), A/C (22), and high-end efficiency boiler (23).This meticulously maintained home won't last!
