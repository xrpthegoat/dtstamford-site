About this home

3800 SF of total living space to enjoy! Expansive, four bedroom, four full bath home offers freshly painted rooms, polished hardwood floors and amazing storage, all ideally sited on popular Pipers Hill. The truly flexible floor plan encompasses 3.5 levels including a full, walk-out lower level and bonus den/family room above the two car garage. The formal living and dining rooms both boast bay windows, hardwood floors and wood burning fireplaces for gracious entertaining. The sun-filled, all white kitchen includes stainless appliances and a generous island for casual dining. Relax on the inviting screen porch or retreat to the oversized family room that could also easily be used for guests or as a home theatre. Upstairs the front-to-back primary suite includes a walk-in closet plus renovated bath with double vanities. There are 3 additional very generous family bedrooms on the upper level, all with hardwood floors, and a full hall bath. The walk-out lower level with laundry room, mudroom and an additional full bath is a sunny retreat for fun and games with sliders to the large back yard and has tremendous potential for added living space. Newer roof, added insulation and updated systems ensures peace of mind and no surprises. When you're ready to leave home, enjoy the nearly adjacent 140 acre Wilton Forest, or a hike at Weir Farm National Historic Park. This wonderful colonial is a happy home to call your own.
