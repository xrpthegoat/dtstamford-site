About this home

You have found paradise with this spectacular waterfront 5 bedroom home with magnificent views from most rooms of LI Sound, Calf Islands, Island Beach and Great Captains Island. Not only will you have a private deep water dock, this special home also boasts a large rooftop deck and multiple terraces to enjoy the captivating water views. An open floorplan, great flow and soaring ceilings define the spaces with plenty of rooms for formal and casual entertaining inside and out to enjoy vacation all year long! A large sophisticated renovated kitchen with waterfall countertops, top of the line appliances is open to the family room. Formal Living room with fireplace and formal Dining room. A private office with custom bookshelves offers great space for work. Wake up to shimmering water views in the gracious primary suite boasting a walk in closet with recently renovated chic bath and balcony. 5 BRs all have en suite baths. 7 baths.
