About this home

Welcome to 22 Camore Street, a charming move-in ready Cape set on a beautiful leveled lot in Stamford. This well-maintained three bedroom, one bath home offers a bright and inviting interior with hardwood floors throughout, a freshly updated kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances and quartz countertops, and the added efficiency of a tankless water heater. The home also features durable wood siding, a garage, and a spacious deck overlooking the large backyard, creating the perfect setting for outdoor entertaining, everyday enjoyment, or peaceful relaxation. Situated on a quiet street in the desirable neighborhood of Newfield, this property is within walking distance to local restaurants and offers easy access to major highways and the train, making commuting to New York City simple and convenient. This home is ideal for first-time buyers, downsizers, or anyone looking for comfort, convenience, and easy living in a sought after Stamford location.
