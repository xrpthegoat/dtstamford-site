About this home

With sparkling water views and a private beach only steps away, this Shippan Point home is coastal living at its best! The living room with fireplace, sunroom, and open kitchen, dining, and family room all take in the views, with sliders opening to a new (2023) Ipe wraparound deck overlooking the Sound. Upstairs, there are two comfortable bedrooms and a primary suite with additional water views, a walk-in closet, an updated bathroom and a private deck offering panoramic vistas. A first-floor bedroom with an adjoining bath and a third-floor suite add versatility. Outside, a level yard framed by a beautifully crafted Pennsylvania fieldstone retaining wall, a hornbeam hedge, and a cedar outdoor shower-ideal for rinsing off after a day at the beach-completes this gem. Additional highlights: hardwood floors throughout, deeded beach rights, irrigation system, and raised garden bed. Located just minutes from downtown, train, and highways-NO flood insurance required!
