About this home

Experience the perfect blend of classic charm and modern elegance! This Colonial-style, beautifully updated home features 7 bedrooms, 5 full bathrooms plus 2 half baths. The kitchen, equipped with high-end stainless steel appliances & Sub-Zero refrigerator, seamlessly flows into an inviting entertaining space with an abundance of light pouring in through large windows with views overlooking the treetops and secluded backyard. The elegant living room includes gleaming hardwood floors, crown moldings, thoughtful built-ins & over-sized yet cozy fireplace. The spacious primary suite includes two walk-in closets & bathroom with jetted tub & glass-enclosed shower. Laundry room conveniently located on 2nd floor. 2,284 sqft finished, walk-out, lower level features a bedroom and a full bathroom, ideal for in-law / au-pair needs. Step outside to a brand new composite deck (2024) which is accessible from both the main and lower levels; perfect for enjoying picturesque sunrises & memorable gatherings. Enjoy the saltwater pool that was fully renovated (liner, stairs, mechanicals, safety fence, full patio surround) in 2022. Located in a highly-desirable neighborhood & tucked back from the road, this private oasis, is in close proximity to local treasures and amenities. Move-in-ready home includes a 3rd fl. playroom, 3-car garage, main floor rec room, invisible fence, smart/zoned thermostats & security system. Cedar roof resealed 2022. Dry Basement.
