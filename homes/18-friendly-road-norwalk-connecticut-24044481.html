About this home

Ideally located in the sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood of Norwalk, this stunning home offers over 3,600 square feet of living space designed for comfort and style. With four spacious bedrooms and three remodeled bathrooms, this property provides ample room and a great value. An expansive back deck overlooks a fully usable and level backyard. The recent updates to this home are truly impressive, featuring a new roof, hardwood flooring, upgraded molding and trim, and a brand-new gourmet kitchen equipped with custom cabinetry, a center island, a stainless steel farm-style sink, and beautiful light stone countertops. The updated bathrooms, central air, mini-splits, and generator hook-up ensure modern convenience and peace of mind. The location is unbeatable, with close proximity to Cranbury Park, Wolfpit Elementary School, and a leisurely walk to local favorites like Stew Leonard's, Wave Hill Bread Bakery, and coffee shops. Enjoy the nearby farmers market on the green and newly paved streets, perfect for walking and bike riding. This residential neighborhood offers both tranquility and convenience, the best of both worlds! In addition to its charming neighborhood, the property is conveniently located with multiple train station options and just 50 miles from NYC, making it a perfect home base for commuters. This updated, turnkey home is ready to welcome its new owners. Don't miss the opportunity to live in a spacious, beautifully renovated property in a prime location.
