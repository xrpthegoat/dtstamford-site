About this home

This spacious rental provides 3 floors of living space with an attached 2 car garage for added convenience. The main level boasts a lovely family room with a wood burning fireplace, a separate dining area, an updated kitchen with quartz countertops and ss appliances, a half bath, and two sun rooms to relax in. Upper level provides 2 spacious bedrooms with a full bath. Lower level space can be used as a playroom, office, or spare bedroom, and also provides a full bath, stackable washer dryer, and access to the garage. Enjoy the convenient location to shopping, downtown Greenwich, I-95, the merritt pkwy, parks and schools!
