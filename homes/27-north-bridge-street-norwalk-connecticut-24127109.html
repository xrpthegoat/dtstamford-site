About this home

Welcome to this inviting Cape Cod-style home, nestled in a wonderful neighborhood perfect for walking and enjoying the outdoors. This 2-bedroom gem exudes a breezy, beachy feel with tasteful white and blue accents throughout. Step inside to an open floor plan that makes everyday living and entertaining a breeze. The layout is bright, airy, and thoughtfully designed for comfort and flow. Outside, you will enjoy entertaining on your deck and having a great flat yard which includes a garden! Enjoy peace of mind with several recent updates, including a newer washer and dryer, hot water heater, furnace and a Honeywell smart thermostat-making this home as efficient as it is beautiful. Located in a lovely coastal community near the Long Island Sound, this inviting home offers easy access to scenic beaches, walking trails, and charming local shops and restaurants. Enjoy the convenience of nearby parks, dining and shopping, plus quick access to the East Norwalk train station. Just minutes from the vibrant Saugatuck section of Westport, known for its great restaurants and local flavor. 27 N. Bridge Street is a perfect blend of comfort, location and lifestyle!
