About this home

Discover living at Glen River East in the heart of Wilton town center near boutique shops, Schenck's Island trails & seasonal community events. Tucked behind the building, this updated 1 BR home offers wooded views & easy one-level living. Foyer w/ coat closet, dedicated laundry rm & utility closet provides excellent storage. Open concept main living drenched in sunlight. LR enveloped in windows & hardwood flrs create an airy, comfortable feel. The DR is complimented by a statement lighting fixture & connects naturally to the LR w/sliders to deck for indoor/outdoor enjoyment. Timeless galley style kitchen offers warm wood cabinetry, dark counters & decorative tile backsplash. Updated stainless appliances, stylish new overhead lighting, a pass-thru to DR & pantry create a space that is refined & functional. The generous BR retreat w/soft light & calm atmosphere provides 2 full-length closets that form a hall to the full bath, maximizing storage while keeping the sleeping area uncluttered. Sumptuous refreshed full bath w/glass-enclosed shower & contemporary finishes is sure to delight. True laundry rm w/stackable w/d offers space for shelving/folding. Utility rm w/addtl storage & provides a pegboard organizer to maximize vertical space. A long elevated deck takes in tranquil views of nature, w/private exterior storage closet & stairs to the grounds, ideal for quick steps out with a pet. Residents enjoy an outdoor pool w/pool house perfect for gathering, unwinding, & summer fun.
