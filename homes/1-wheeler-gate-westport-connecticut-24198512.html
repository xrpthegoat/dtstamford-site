About this home

Compo Commons classic! Impeccably maintained, and recently renovated, this wonderful home seamlessly pairs Connecticut charm with a contemporary edge, and a nod to what is current. The dramatic foyer welcomes you to a gracious and tailored living room with a fireplace or the intimate dining room with a built-in dry bar and beautiful bay window. The gorgeous gourmet kitchen offers enviable counter space, a huge center island, high-end appliances and custom cabinetry; it opens to a soaring great room with cathedral ceiling, Shaker-inspired paneling, a striking wood burning fireplace, and 2-story windows. The abundant natural light and spaciousness is captivating. Upstairs, the luxurious primary bedroom suite includes separate lounge/seating areas, a stunning bathroom, dressing area and walk in closet. There are three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms completing the second floor. A portion of the full basement has been finished to offer maximum flexibility for end use. The professionally landscaped half acre property, boasts a stone patio with retractable striped awning for those lingering, late afternoon golden hour conversations. After 29 years and a full roster of meaningful Westport memories, the current owners are looking for the next stewards to take the reins. This is an amazing opportunity to be close to all that makes this coastal town tick and become a part of a coveted neighborhood near restaurants, shopping, train and beach.
