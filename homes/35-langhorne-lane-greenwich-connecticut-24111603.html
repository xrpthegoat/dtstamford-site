About this home

Located on 4.2 private acres on a quiet mid-country cul-de-sac, this contemporary retreat blends sleek design with resort-style living. A dramatic double-height foyer welcomes you into sunlit spaces with panoramic property views. The open layout features a chef's kitchen with waterfall island and 8-burner range, flowing seamlessly into living, dining, and family rooms-all with access to the outdoor oasis. Highlights include a Connecticut stone gas fireplace, walk-in wine cellar, and elegant bar. The home offers a luxe primary suite, office/gym, two additional bedrooms, and a flexible bonus room. Outside, enjoy a pool, spa, pergola, fire-pit, outdoor kitchen, and built-in speaker system. Exceptional amenities include a golf simulator, car lift, EV charging station, and possible expansion potential (FAR). Stylish, private, and amenity-rich this is elevated living at its finest.
