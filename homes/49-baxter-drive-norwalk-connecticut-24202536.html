About this home

Welcome to Harbor Shores! Away from the bustle of downtown Sono, on a tree lined street, this delightful Cape is ready for you! Pride of ownership with many updates including newer kitchen and baths, central air, fenced yard, and great outdoor living space. Enjoy living room with fireplace, dining room opening to spacious kitchen with stainless appliances and gas range. Home office with French doors and sliders to inviting back deck with plenty of room for grilling and entertaining. Three bedrooms offer main floor and upper level options. 720 square feet of bonus space in the finished lower level include full bath, play room, sitting area, huge storage room, new washer/dryer and new hot water heater. The back yard has a great patio area and is fenced for pets and kids. Stroll to private Baxter Beach along the shores of the Norwalk River with views of the harbor and Long Island Sound. Easy access to Metro North, shopping, restaurants, transportation and tons fun activities.
