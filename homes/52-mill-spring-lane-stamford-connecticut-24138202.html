About this home

*HIGHEST & BEST OFFERS BY MONDAY 11/10 at 12:00pm* Welcome to an exquisite retreat in North Stamford, where sophistication & nature harmoniously converge. This magnificent 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath contemporary residence is gracefully positioned on an expansive corner lot. As you enter, you are greeted by soaring ceilings and exquisite wood beams, complemented by a wall of windows that offer breathtaking views of the verdant landscape. The main level is artfully designed, featuring a luxurious 1st floor primary suite w/deck, a spa-like bathroom & indulgent radiant floor heating-an ideal sanctuary for those crisp winter mornings. The open-plan living & dining areas exude an air of refined elegance, seamlessly extending to deck, perfect for hosting gatherings or intimate alfresco dining. The chef's kitchen is a masterpiece, equipped w/ quartz countertops, SS appliances, gas cooktop, dual wall ovens, and expansive pantry, ensuring every culinary adventure is a delight. Beyond the main living area, a serene library awaits, adorned with custom built-in bookcases, offering a perfect nook for literary escapes. Adjacent to this is the guest suite, full bath and ample closet space, providing comfort & privacy for visitors. The lower level is a haven for relaxation and entertainment, two additional bedrooms, updated full bath, and a spacious family room for memorable movie nights or engaging game days. Step outside to the expansive lush garden.
