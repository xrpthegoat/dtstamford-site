About this home

Welcome to Fountain Terrace, where comfort and convenience meet in the heart of Stamford. Situated on the 5th floor, this bright and inviting two-bedroom residence features a private balcony overlooking the community's picturesque fountain, an ideal spot to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind at the end of the day. The home offers updated features including modern crown molding, a stylish kitchen with stainless steel appliances. Residents enjoy access to an in-ground pool, fitness center, playground, guest parking, and 24-hour security. Heat, hot water, cooking gas, water, and sewer are all included in the common charges. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, shopping, dining, and major commuter routes, this move-in-ready home offers exceptional value and low-maintenance living.
