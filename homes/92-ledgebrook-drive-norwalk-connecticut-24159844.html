About this home

This beautifully renovated 2 bedroom 1 bath ranch home is ideally sited in the desirable Ledgebrook community. As you step inside its lovely entry way, you are immediately welcomed with wide plank floors and decorative wainscoting. The adjacent living room provides the perfect setting for relaxation and tranquility with its grand beehive fireplace and large picture window that offers captivating outside views of the private, wooded backdrop and natural rock garden. The tastefully remodeled kitchen with stainless steel appliances and granite countertops blends contemporary design with the conveniences of today's lifestyle. The spacious primary bedroom has newly installed plush carpeting, a generous closet and scenic wooded outdoor views, while the second bedroom offers fresh carpeting and a sizable walk-in closet. The updated full bath has heated floors and is conveniently located near the in-unit full size washer and dryer. The screened-in porch allows shaded enjoyment of the outdoors in the warmer months and provides an easy, additional access to the exterior of the property. The Ledgebrook community is highly regarded for its professionally landscaped grounds with seasonal perennials, pond, and walking paths, along with the amenities of an in-ground pool and clubhouse with fitness room and library. The prime West Norwalk location is convenient to shopping, dining, train and major highways. $7,000 special assessment paid in full.
