About this home

*SELLERS ARE AKSING FOR HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY 4PM MONDAY JUNE 1ST*Set on a beautifully landscaped, bucolic acre at the top of a quiet cul-de-sac in Westport's coveted Old Hill neighborhood, this charming cottage-style ranch home offers the perfect blend of modern convenience, and luxurious Westport lifestyle. Move-in ready and beautifully maintained it's thoughtful layout is ideal for both everyday living and gracious entertaining. Light pours in through picture windows, French doors, and skylights giving a bright airy feel throughout. At the heart of the home is a stunning chef's kitchen equipped with high-end stainless steel appliances. The open dining area is perfect for feasts with family. Sliders leading to multiple decks make indoor-outdoor living seamless. Relax in the inviting living room with a cozy fireplace, or entertain outdoors on the expansive deck overlooking lush lawns. Retreat to the primary suite that boasts a newly renovated spa-like bath. Two additional bedrooms & full bath complete this level. The lower level offers a family room, perfect for movie nights, as well as office built-ins to WFH. A playroom/rec space, plus a fourth bedroom and full bath, make an ideal setup for guests, in-laws, or au pair. Step outside to your resort-like oasis featuring a stunning, sun-drenched saltwater pool and cabana with bar & Sonos - summer entertaining perfection! Energy efficient with Tesla Solar Roof, newer mechanicals and a generator give peace of mind.
