About this home

Experience refined living in this beautifully updated condominium where modern upgrades meet a truly exceptional setting. Thoughtfully enhanced with a new HVAC system, water heater, range, dishwasher, garage door, insulation, and sliding glass door, this home offers both style and peace of mind. The full list of renovations and enhancements is available upon request. Enter from your cheerful front porch overlooking the lush grounds to a cheerful foyer. You will find your customized hall closet, as well as a fully renovated powder room for you and your guests. The beautifully appointed kitchen has a large eating area, granite counters and loads of counter and storage space. The elegant living room features a spacious dining area, a fireplace and opens seamlessly via a new sliding glass door to a spacious private deck overlooking a dramatic stone wall and natural waterfall-an extraordinary backdrop that creates a tranquil, resort-like atmosphere rarely found at home. Both bedrooms are designed as private suites with their own baths, including a luxurious primary bath illuminated by a skylight. Each bedroom boasts walk-in closets with custom storage, blending sophistication with everyday functionality. The finished lower level provides exceptional versatility, ideal for a family room, guest space, home office, or gym, and is complemented by a large storage and laundry area for added convenience. One car garage plus one carport with ample parking for guests
