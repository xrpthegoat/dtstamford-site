About this home

Welcome to this inviting and well-crafted 4-bedroom home set on a quiet street in desirable Norwalk. The main level features a sun-filled living room with a large picture window and a cozy fireplace, leading to an updated kitchen that opens seamlessly to the dining room-ideal for everyday living and entertaining. Beautiful hardwood floors run throughout much of the home, and the first-floor full bath has been tastefully updated for modern comfort. The primary bedroom with its own half bath and 2nd bedroom are located on the main level for convenience. Upstairs are two additional bedrooms and a shared bath. The lower level offers a versatile family room with garage access, giving you flexible space for work or relaxation. A professionally waterproofed basement adds peace of mind, and a cedar closet provides great seasonal storage. Enjoy the private, level backyard perfect for gardening, summer barbecues, or quiet relaxation. With thoughtful updates and a warm, functional layout, this home is ready to welcome its next owner.
