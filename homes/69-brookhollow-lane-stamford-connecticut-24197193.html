About this home

THE GIVING HOUSE. For 39 years, 69 Brookhollow has given this family something that can't be measured in square feet: a home that adapted beautifully as life changed around them. Set high above the street on a cul-de-sac in sought-after Westover, this ranch offers 2,714 finished square feet on one level with a smart, functional layout. The giving begins at the heart of the home-the oversized kitchen offers vaulted ceilings, skylights, an island, granite counters, a brick wall with fireplace/BBQ, and even a built-in office/study area. It flows into a warm family room with high beamed ceilings and access to the deck and level backyard. The living room with welcoming fireplace opens to a generous dining room, the setting for holidays and gatherings. The primary suite offers a true dressing room and spacious bath, while two additional bedrooms share a full bath. This home adapted to every chapter: a private fourth bedroom with full bath served as guest, nanny and caregiver quarters. Hardwood floors, central air and a generator add to the appeal. The two-car garage is essentially at main-level height-a feature that became especially valuable as this family grew older. Another 2,430 square feet of unfinished lower level offers potential. After nearly four decades, this is more than a house that worked for a family. It gave them room to raise children, welcome grandchildren, gather, find comfort and grow older with ease. Now, The Giving House is ready to give to another family.
