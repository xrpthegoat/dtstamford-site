About this home

Rarely does an opportunity like this come up. Walk to town from this romantic, fairy tale stone home. Situated on a beautiful large lot with a 16X40 foot inground pool framed by beautiful gardens and an expansive blue stone patio. The interior of this home is simply enchanting from the beautiful Rose Adams' kitchen with its brick floor to the two gathering rooms both with its own gas fireplace. There is a two room suite on the first floor with its own full bath and separate entrance. This has been used at different times as an at home office, an apartment and as a bedroom suite. Upstairs all three bedrooms are ensuite. The primary bath is wonderful! If charm is what you are yearning, look no further!
