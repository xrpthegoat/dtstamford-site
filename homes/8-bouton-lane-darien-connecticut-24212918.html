About this home

Experience the pinnacle of modern luxury at 8 Bouton Lane, a masterful new construction that sets a new benchmark for craftsmanship in Darien. Every inch of this residence reflects an uncompromising commitment to quality, from the soaring nine-foot ceilings on the main level to the intricate millwork and exquisite waist-high paneling that graces the formal dining and living rooms. At the heart of the home, a chef's dream kitchen awaits, equipped with top of the line appliances, centered around an expansive island w/ample seating. The open flow leads seamlessly into inviting spaces anchored by a fireplace in both the living and family rooms, perfect for sophisticated entertaining. The second floor is a sanctuary of comfort, headlined by a sumptuous primary suite. This retreat features a dramatic tray ceiling in primary bath, a private sitting area w/its own gas starter fireplace, dual walk-in closets and floor-to-ceiling wainscoting. The 4 additional spacious bedrooms on this level share two thoughtfully designed Jack&Jill baths. The finished 3rd level, not to be missed, has two rooms with vaulted ceilings, skylights, custom shiplap walls, and a full bath. This flex space can be the 6th bedroom, home office, or any customized use. Not to be outdone, the finished lower level offers nearly 1,000 sq ft featuring a elegant tray ceiling, side paneling, a walk-in cedar closet, and convenient walk-out access.
