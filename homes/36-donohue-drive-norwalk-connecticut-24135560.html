About this home

Charming Norwalk home on Rare oversized lot. Welcome to 36 Donohue Drive, an inviting, warm retreat, nestled back from the street. This thoughtfully maintained and move in ready 3-bedroom 1-1/2 bath raised ranch blends comfort and privacy, offering a rare combination of space, charm, and convenience in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood. Step inside to find a cozy family room with a wood burning stove, wood floors and sliding doors that deliver plenty of natural light and convenient access to a large deck and the outdoors. On the opposite side is the laundry room with up-to-date washer and dryer and spacious 2-car garage with room for shelves, storage, and direct access into the house. Upstairs, three comfortable bedrooms offer plenty of natural light and closet space. The main bedroom features its own "en-suite" half-bathroom. A well-appointed kitchen with granite counter tops and stainless steel appliances leads to the dining area which flows into the living room with wood floors, and a fireplace, great for entertaining. A second deck just off the dining room enhances the home's natural flow between indoor comfort and outdoor relaxation. The home layout provides both functionality and flexibility, ideal for growing families, first-time buyers or anyone who appreciates a peaceful setting.
