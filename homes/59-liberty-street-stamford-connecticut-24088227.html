About this home

This beautiful move-in ready townhome features custom paneling, crown moldings, and hardwood floors throughout. The entry level offers an attached 2-car garage with bonus room for storage. Upstairs, the main level includes a sun-filled living and dining area with sliders to a private balcony, plus a kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops and ample cabinetry. The top floor offers two ample sized bedrooms, a full bath and in-unit laundry as well as access to a full attic for extra storage. Conveniently located just minutes from Stamford Hospital, Mill River Park, train, I-95, UConn campus, and all that downtown Stamford has to offer. Pet-friendly, several guest parking spots and low HOA fees. It's great place to call home!
