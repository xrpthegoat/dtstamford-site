About this home

Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a completely updated two-bedroom townhouse with stunning soaring ceilings in the highly desirable Ledgebrook community. Lovingly cared for, this sun-filled end unit offers beautiful hardwood floors throughout, updated utilities and an open, airy layout designed for today's lifestyle. The newly remodeled designer kitchen features Custom Inset Cabinetry, Bosch range and Dishwasher, LG fridge, porcelain backsplash, and granite countertops, seamlessly blending style and function. The primary bedroom has an abundance of closet storage with en-suite powder room. A dramatic open-hearth brick corner fireplace anchors the living space, adding architectural character and warmth, while new Marvin sliding doors open to a private corner deck, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. New full size LG washer and dryer included. Dedicated Garage parking spot and storage unit are just steps from the front door. Ledgebrook provides a true sense of community with beautifully landscaped grounds, mature trees, scenic walking paths, and a tranquil pond with fountain, along with resort-style amenities including an in-ground pool, clubhouse, and fitness center. This turnkey home delivers comfort, design, and an exceptional lifestyle just minutes from shopping, dining, highways, and Metro-North in the heart of Norwalk. No assessments.
