About this home

Beautifully designed and thoughtfully finished, this exceptional New home blends sophisticated style with modern comfort and effortless everyday living. From the moment you enter, wide-plank French oak floors, 9-foot ceilings, Andersen windows, and curated designer details create a warm yet elevated atmosphere. The dining room and office make a statement with rich color-drenched finishes, while custom lighting, polished chrome hardware, and refined millwork carry a cohesive aesthetic throughout. At the heart of the home, the kitchen is both striking and functional, featuring quartz countertops and backsplash, soft-close cabinetry, under-cabinet lighting, and high-end Thermador appliances with separate refrigerator and freezer towers. Antique brass accents add warmth to the space, which flows naturally into the inviting family room centered around a large fireplace, perfect for both relaxed evenings and entertaining. Upstairs, the serene primary suite offers two spacious custom-fitted closets and a beautifully designed bath with designer stone tile, Kohler fixtures, and a Kohler smart toilet. Custom closet systems throughout the home, blackout shades in every bedroom, and privacy shades on the remaining windows and sliders add thoughtful finishing touches. A generous mudroom with custom built-in cabinetry makes everyday arrivals and departures beautifully organized. The living space continues well beyond the main floors.
