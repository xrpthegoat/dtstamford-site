About this home

HUGE RARE SONO MULTI LEVEL TOWNHOUSE! 1,400Sq Ft 4-Level Townhome with garage in the heart of SoNo next to the legendary O'Neils Pub! This 2 bedroom plus office unit offers loads of space with hardwood floors and high-end finishes throughout. The ground floor has a private one car garage and spacious foyer/mud room. The first floor has an open stainless steel and granite kitchen, breakfast bar, spacious living room, dining area, and half bath. The second floor has a large bedroom, full bath, office and full-size washer-dryer in the closet. Third floor has a fantastic primary bedroom suite with a deluxe bathroom with spa tub and shower. Just steps to all SoNo has to offer...Restaurants, shops, theater, harbor, parks and the SoNo Collection Mall. Walk to SoNo Train Station, easy access to all major highways. Pets case by case.
