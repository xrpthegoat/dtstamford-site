About this home

Absolutely gorgeous turnkey 3 BR 2 Full Bath in the highly sought after Glenbrook neighborhood! This beautifully expanded ranch style home offers an open floor plan & tons of recent upgrades. Nothing to do but move in and start living! From the moment you walk into the inviting sun-filled living room, you'll notice the stunning redone stone fireplace & charming dining room - perfect for hosting holidays! Newly renovated, modern white kitchen boasts SS appliances, quartz countertops, center island, dishwasher, & tons of cabinet space. Also on the main level are 2 spacious bedrooms, updated full bath w/ shower/tub & newer LG washer/dryer. As you make your way upstairs you'll find the expansive Primary BR Suite which comes fully equipped w/ skylights, gleaming hardwood floors, versatile sitting/home office area, ensuite w/ Carrara marble vanity & tile shower, & massive walk-in closet w/ access to the partially finished attic space which provides ample storage. Enjoy the summer months unwinding or entertaining in the backyard, which is like your very own private Oasis complete w/ heated, gunite in-ground swimming pool & hot tub, Trex deck, gazebo & plenty of space for yard games! This unbeatable home also features central A/C, Navien tankless HW heater, Nest smart thermostats, recessed lighting, attached 1 car garage, new gutters, & new flooring throughout! Close proximity to Downtown Stamford, Metro-North Station, I-95, parks, beach, shopping & top notch restaurants. A MUST SEE!
