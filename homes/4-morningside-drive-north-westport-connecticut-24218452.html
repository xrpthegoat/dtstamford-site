About this home

Residence 102 at Coastal Point offers 820 sq. ft. of beautifully designed single-level living in a boutique collection of just 12 residences, built in 2017 in central Westport. Select residences are being offered for sale for the very first time, presenting a rare opportunity to own in this sought-after community. Built by Westport's preeminent builder, Coastal Luxury Homes, to the standards of a luxury single-family home, this spacious one-bedroom, one-bathroom residence combines thoughtful design, generous proportions and high-end finishes. The open, easy-flowing floor plan is complemented by clean architectural lines, abundant natural light, hardwood floors, solid wood doors and custom millwork throughout. The well-appointed kitchen, in-unit laundry, air conditioning and private indoor parking offer everyday convenience, while beautifully landscaped grounds and attractive exterior finishes enhance the setting. Shops, restaurants and everyday services are within walking distance, with downtown Westport, the train and Long Island Sound beaches just a short drive away. An exceptional opportunity to enjoy the space and quality of luxury homeownership with the ease and convenience of low-maintenance condominium living.
