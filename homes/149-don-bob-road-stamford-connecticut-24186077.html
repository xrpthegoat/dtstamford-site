About this home

Discover this stunning newly constructed 4 BR, 3.5-bath home in desirable North Stamford, offering modern elegance set on a beautifully private lot. The heart of the home is the gourmet kitchen featuring Thermador appliances, including a professional-grade gas range, along with a Bosch dishwasher. It boasts white shaker cabinetry, a striking marble tile backsplash, luxurious quartz countertops. The kitchen opens seamlessly into the family room with a cozy gas fireplace with white mantel and stone surround. Formal living room and convenient built-in cubbies in the mudroom. Sliding glass doors off the kitchen lead to a spacious deck overlooking the private backyard - an ideal setting for outdoor entertaining amid natural privacy. Upstairs, 4 generous bedrooms include a primary suite with walk-in closet and bath. One additional hall full bath. Second floor laundry plus an office. A standout feature is the large finished third-floor family room, perfect for a home theater, playroom or gym. Additional highlights include a 2 car garage and a spacious unfinished high ceiling basement. Throughout the home you'll enjoy light hardwood flooring, abundant natural light from oversized windows and the quality of true new construction. This move-in-ready residence is contemporary luxury.
