About this home

The Best of Both Worlds - Single-Family Comfort Without the Upkeep! Perfectly situated on the scenic Norwalk/New Canaan line, this exquisite townhouse seamlessly blends style, convenience, and natural beauty. Expansive windows bathe the living, dining, and eat-in kitchen areas in natural light, while multiple decks invite you to savor breathtaking lake views and peaceful wildlife. The main level features a spacious family room and a full bath, offering versatile living space. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite boasts an ensuite bath, accompanied by two additional bedrooms. The fully finished lower level impresses with a generous recreation room, a full bath, and an oversized walk-in cedar closet. Additional highlights include a detached two-car garage with pull-down attic storage, beautifully maintained green spaces, and scenic walking trails-all within a secure, well-kept community. This exceptional home offers the perfect blend of tranquility and convenience. The lower level is above grade and is finished with a full bathroom. The total above sq. ft. is 3,168. You could use the lower level as a 4th bedroom. This is an absolute must-see!
