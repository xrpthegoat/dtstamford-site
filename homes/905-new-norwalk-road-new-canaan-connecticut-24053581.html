About this home

This attractive ranch provides a wonderful opportunity to own a 3-bedroom home in beautiful New Canaan! Situated on .42 fenced acres, the home features comfortable and bright living spaces including beautiful wood floors, a living room with a fireplace, sunroom surrounded by large windows to let in the natural light, and a newly renovated bathroom. Located only a 5-minute drive to downtown New Canaan and with easy access to The Merritt Parkway, this home is perfectly situated for trips to town or commuting. The home has been in the family since it was built in 1954 and has been loved and cared for over the years. Whether you're looking for your forever home or a rewarding investment opportunity, this property has the potential to shine with your unique vision.
