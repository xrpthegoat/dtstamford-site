About this home

Discover this beautifully expanded Cape Cod home just a short stroll from Norwalk Harbor & the picturesque Long Island Sound. Embrace the coastal lifestyle with nearby Calf Pasture Beach. Perfect for leisurely walks, biking, and playful afternoons. Nestled in the sought-after East Norwalk community, this property combines convenience with tranquility. Featuring a private pool and easy access to the area's best amenities. Step inside to an inviting foyer that leads to a sun-drenched living room, complete with an open hearth fireplace-ideal for cozy gatherings. The modern kitchen boasts a spacious island and a sunroom that overlooks your expansive, fenced yard & serene in-ground pool. The flexible first-floor layout includes a versatile space that can serve as a guest room, in-law suite, or home office. You'll also find a full bathroom and a 2-car garage on this level. Ascend to the 2nd floor to find a large primary bedroom, full bathroom, and two additional bedrooms. Making this home ideal for family living. The finished lower level offers ample storage and is ready for your finishing touches. Take advantage of the nearby Metro-North station, kayaking opportunities, playgrounds, dog parks, restaurants, and marinas. This home is more than a residence; it's a gateway to the coastal lifestyle.
