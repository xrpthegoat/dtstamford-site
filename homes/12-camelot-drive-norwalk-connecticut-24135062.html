About this home

Welcome to 12 Camelot Drive, Unit B1 in desirable Norwalk. This lovely townhouse in a well maintained complex is tucked away in a peaceful residential neighborhood, and offers the space, layout and features for what a savy buyer is looking for today. Perched on a hill, the end unit features a renovated eat-in kitchen with shaker style cabinets and newer stainless steel appliances that lead to a large deck to enjoy all of your outdoor entertaining needs and lovely backyard. Living room with gleaming hardwood floors and a half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs boasts two bedrooms and a renovated full bath. Need to work from home? An additional room, accessed thru the attached garage can accommodate your remote needs or use it as a media or playroom. Washer/dryer add to the perfection. Enjoy the community pool during the summer. This home combines comfort, convenience, and a serene setting. Make your home ownership dreams a reality and move right in!
