About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul de sac in the desirable Hunt Club area of Long Lots in Westport is a new construction lux home from CCO Habitats. 3 levels of finished living space with 10 foot ceilings on the 1st floor give an amazing sense of openness, as well as better functionality than 4 level homes. Think about it. Who really wants to walk up to the third floor every day? That's right; no one. And you don't want your guests walking past your bedroom to get to a third floor guest room either. The family bedroom floor should be private. Wider homes are much more desirable & functional than taller homes. The home has a generous first floor guest bedroom & full bath. Much better. It's not just for grandma you know, although she will love it. Who knows when you're going to tear your meniscus or ACL?? Pickleball hurts! :) Or - it's a great, convenient play room for little ones. Or a gamers' paradise for the older set. #Choices. We also have two designated offices; one on the first floor & a second in a private wing with its own staircase on the second floor. Three wet bars and a dry bar. Custom cabinetry & built ins throughout. There's a 20x40 salt water pool. Oversized tilting European windows & sliders drench the home in natural light. Organic modern; warm & functional. A true walk out lower level that easily doubles as a pool house with a full bath, second laundry and entertaining spaces. A full house generator and even an EV outlet. It's all here. Come visit. Come home.
