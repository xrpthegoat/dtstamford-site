About this home

Set on a private, wooded property in a prime Stamford location, this residence blends architectural character with exceptional indoor-outdoor living. A dramatic living room with vaulted beamed ceilings and a stone fireplace opens through French doors to a terrace overlooking the front gardens. The grounds are designed for entertaining, featuring an inground pool, outdoor kitchen, and private tennis court. The home offers a distinctive artist's kitchen, breakfast nook with a built-in bench, and a dining room with custom woodwork and French doors. A first-floor office and a well-appointed laundry room with storage add everyday functionality. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a sitting room with fireplace and a conservatory overlooking the gardens and pond. Three additional bedrooms and two full baths complete the space. A detached garage with guest house above provides added flexibility. A peaceful setting with convenient access to Stamford's amenities, dining, and commuting options.
