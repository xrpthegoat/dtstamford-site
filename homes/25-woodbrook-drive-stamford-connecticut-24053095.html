About this home

Welcome to 25 Woodbrook Drive! This stunning center Colonial home sits on over an acre of land in one of the most sought after neighborhoods in upper Springdale. The first floor boasts open foyer entry, family room, powder room, laundry room, formal living room with fireplace, gourmet kitchen equipped with SS appliances, pot filler and granite counter tops, a beautiful sunroom porch, perfect for 3 season entertaining. The 2nd floor has a large Primary bedroom with en-suite bathroom & a huge walk-in closet, 3 additional bedrooms & full bathroom. The lower level features a fully finished playroom w/powder room and plenty of storage. Additional features include; 2 car garage, numerous updates, minutes to train, Merritt Parkway, schools, and more. Property is private and level. A must see!
