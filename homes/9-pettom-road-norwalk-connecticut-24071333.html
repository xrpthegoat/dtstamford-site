About this home

Welcome to this charming, fully renovated single-family raised ranch that combines modern living with comfort and convenience while being pet friendly! With 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a spacious layout, this property is perfect for anyone looking for a cozy and functional place to call home. On the lower level you have a family room with fireplace, an additional bedroom, and a full bath and laundry area. Outside take advantage of the deck and plenty of private outdoor space. In order to move in you will need a credit check, as well as 3 months rent. No smoking allowed in home.
