About this home

Welcome to 35 Drum Hill Lane, located in the Westover neighborhood of Stamford, conn. Nestled at the end of a convenient cul-de-sac, is a sweeping driveway that leads up to this well maintained four bedroom two and a half bath colonial. Originally built for the builder, this prime level lot sits high above the prized neighborhood. An inviting entry foyer with stone floor is the first introduction to this well maintained home. The first floor features; a front to back sunken living room with 9ft ceilings and handsome hardwood floors, generous sized dining room and family room with cozy fireplace off the spacious eat in kitchen. The center of the home is the kitchen, renovated with abundant cabinetry, stone countertops and top grade stainless steel appliances and a couple of steps to the two car garage. Sliding glass doors flood light into the home and lead to an oversized deck overlooking the level backyard with built-in BBQ and woods. Upstairs, there are four generous sized bedrooms all with large closets plus a remodeled bath. The primary suite features two walk in closets with windows and a private bath. Convenient for commuters, minutes from Downtown Stamford or Greenwich, and under a hour to Midtown Manhattan, this stunning warm home, resonates with a quiet splendor.
