About this home

Come home to the elegance of 11 June Avenue. A stunning 4-bedroom West Norwalk home between Silvermine & New Canaan. This modern 2004 center-hall colonial on a corner lot has been meticulously maintained and thoughtfully updated by its original family. A true smart home; equipped with advanced appliances, security, and conveniences for ease of living. Step inside the 18-foot foyer and gallery wall to an inviting layout filled with abundant windows and sunlit spaces designed for comfort and functionality. With 10 rooms across 2,600 square feet, this home provides a perfect balance of space and style. The upper level features a spacious primary suite with two walk-in closets and a luxurious en-suite bathroom, complete with a marbled shower and whirlpool tub. Three additional bedrooms with large walk-in closets provide comfort and privacy. The kitchen is a chef's dream, with solid wood cabinetry and granite countertops. It flows seamlessly to the breakfast room, family room with a marble-faced fireplace, and a versatile office or den with a picture window. Step through sliding doors to the Laredo stone patio and enjoy a private, fenced yard enhanced by lush greenery and flowers that bloom from spring through fall. The additional 1,000-square-foot basement with high ceilings presents endless possibilities for a home gym, recreation area, or more. Move-in ready, this exceptional home with a 2-car garage has it all.
