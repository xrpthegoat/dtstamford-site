About this home

11 Charcoal Rd offers a compelling combination of space (2,032 sf plus 672 sf finished lower level), light, and thoughtful investment in a convenient Norwalk setting. A welcoming family room with exposed beams and a large picture window creates a bright, comfortable focal point. The home's layout flows easily through dining, living, and eat-in kitchen areas. Upstairs are four well-proportioned bedrooms with good natural light and a hall bath, an increasingly valuable offering at this price point. A wood-paneled first-floor office/den with a full closet offers flexible use for work-from-home, guest space, or alternate needs. The finished lower level adds additional room for recreation, hobbies, or storage. A dedicated laundry room functions as a practical mudroom entry, complemented by a two-car garage. Outdoor living includes a screened-in porch, patio, and level .58-acre yard enhanced by a substantial retaining wall and landscaping investment. Set on a quiet cul-de-sac with half-acre properties, where seasonal foliage enhances privacy. Convenient to Norwalk amenities, transportation, nearby schools along Highland Avenue, and the Rowayton village area. Propane boiler and on-demand domestic hot water.
