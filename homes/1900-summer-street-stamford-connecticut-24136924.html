About this home

Welcome to the ultimate in carefree, city-chic living! This sunny, immaculate 2-bed, 2.5-bath townhouse blends cosmopolitan charm with a serene, move-in-ready retreat in the sought-after Greenbriar/Mid City community. Tucked quietly at the back of the well-managed complex-away from the street-this light-filled home boasts an open floor plan with gleaming hardwood floors, a cozy wood-burning fireplace, and a versatile sunroom/office overlooking a peaceful courtyard-ideal for remote work. The spacious living & dining areas flow seamlessly into a gourmet eat-in kitchen featuring granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, breakfast bar, ample storage, and sliders to a private deck. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms each offer en-suite baths and abundant closets; the primary suite shines with a newly renovated spa-like bath, while the second includes a large walk-in. Enjoy one covered carport, an additional reserved parking spot, and plenty of guest parking. Steps from vibrant Downtown Stamford/Mid City shopping, dining, parks, public transit, and nightlife-this turnkey townhouse delivers the perfect balance of style, convenience, and tranquility.
