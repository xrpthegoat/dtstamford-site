About this home

Welcome to Eastside Commons, where contemporary comfort, thoughtful amenities, and a convenient location come together for easy urban living. This freshly painted 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo features 9-foot ceilings and updated LVP flooring, creating a bright, open feel throughout. The kitchen features an island breakfast bar and has been updated with freshened cabinetry, a new microwave, and newer refrigerator-ideal for everyday living and entertaining. Additional features include in-unit laundry, a private balcony, garage parking, and a new gas-fired hot water heater. Residents enjoy amenities that extend your living space beyond the condo-Concierge service Monday through Saturday, an exercise room, an outdoor courtyard with seating and grills, and a community lounge residents can reserve for private gatherings. The location couldn't be more conveniet. The Stamford train station is approximately one mile away, while shops, restaurants, and everyday conveniences are close at hand, including Pickleball America at Stamford Town Center! Easy access to I-95 makes getting on the road convenient, while Stamford's beaches and parks offer even more opportunities to enjoy time outdoors. Urban convenience, community amenities, and a move-in-ready home-all at Eastside Commons in Stamford.
