About this home

Historic Charm Meets Modern Luxury - Once the carriage house for the famed 1760s estate "The Owl's Nest," this exquisitely renovated home blends timeless charm with today's comforts. Original doors and vintage details have been preserved, while high-end updates elevate every space. Inside, enjoy 9-ft ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, and two stunning fireplaces (one stone, one brick). The spacious living room opens to an expansive two-tiered stone patio featuring a hot tub-perfect for entertaining. The gourmet kitchen offers granite counters, stainless appliances, and flows into a welcoming family room with a brick fireplace. A warm formal dining room and elegant half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs, you'll find four large bedrooms and two updated baths. The sunlit primary suite with a bath featuring radiant heated floors, custom cabinetry, and refined tilework. Two bedrooms are double-sized with walk-in closets, and the fourth is perfectly sized for guests, a nursery, or a stylish home office-bright and versatile. Hall bath has a radiant heated floor. All bedrooms have hardwood floors, and the upper hallway offers exceptional storage. Surrounded by professionally landscaped and lush plantings, this home is a private oasis. A detached studio with electricity offers endless possibilities: gym, office, artist studio, or just a quiet retreat. Located in sought after and convenient Westover, this home is a rare find-a must-see.
