About this home

Wonderful sun-drenched Split Level home with many desirable features including large remodeled open kitchen with Island and breakfast nook; Primary en suite bedroom with vaulted ceiling, spacious full bath with walk-in shower and large walk-in closet; Living room with marble surround fireplace, family room; plus an additional recreation room/office or workshop on the lower level. Enjoy the summers with a "stay-cation" pool surrounded by a large deck, private cabana dressing room and shower. Beautifully landscaped with many flowering gardens and plants/shrubs. Plus, there is a flagstone patio-- a perfect size for enjoying a quiet evening with friends around a firepit. This home also features gleaming hardwood floors throughout; central air conditioning and oversized generator (22 kilowatts). Ideally located on the Westport/Norwalk line with walking distance to all 3 schools and only moments to highways, train station and shopping/restaurants.
