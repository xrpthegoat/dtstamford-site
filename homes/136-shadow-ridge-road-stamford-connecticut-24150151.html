About this home

Prepare to be wowed by this stunning expanded 3-bedroom ranch, thoughtfully updated offering true move-in-ready living w/ a seamless blend of modern luxury & everyday comfort. Step inside to discover open concept living at its best. Bright & airy living room overlooks the spectacular renovated kitchen. The kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring a gorgeous waterfall quartz island, newer stainless steel appliances, recessed lighting, overlooking the private, fully fenced backyard. Sliding doors lead directly to the deck-perfect for morning coffee, outdoor dining, and effortless entertaining. The level yard offers exceptional privacy as it borders a nature preserve and includes a large storage shed, while also providing ample space for recreation. Dont miss the dining room with custom built ins. The home's layout offers both functionality and privacy, with 2 spacious bedrooms on the left wing of the house, a beautifully updated full bath, and conveniently located main-level laundry. Privately situated, the luxurious primary suite is a true retreat. Featuring vaulted ceilings, natural light, a generous walk-in closet, and a stunning fully renovated en-suite bath with dual vanities, a soaking tub, oversized walk-in shower, and spa-like finishes, this space offers both comfort and sophistication. Additional highlights include an attached garage, walk in attic, central air, natural gas, city water/sewer. Near parkway, High Ridge shopping, train & downtown.
