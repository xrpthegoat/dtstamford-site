About this home

We've saved the best for last! Of the 4 homes built at the end of this quiet and centrally located Westport cul-de-sac, this is the most expansive, and it's loaded with top-tier amenities. Entertain or hang out in style in gracious rooms curated by Susan Tracy Designs featuring a warm color palate, and not often found finishes like Venetian plaster and built-in hallway millwork. The open kitchen/family room is a wonderful place to cook, eat and live. It's bathed in natural light and features top-of-the-line appliances including two dishwashers, Thermador wine column, Elkay water dispenser and ultra convenient 2-drawer refrigerator in the scullery. The back of the house opens to a large wrap-around patio accessed from the kitchen eating area as well as the private office, all overlooking a beautiful yard and Signature pool. Once upstairs the Primary bedroom suite steals the show! Bright and airy with a cozy fireplace, huge closet and luxurious bath. Lots more to talk about like the spacious laundry room outfitted with two sets of stack washer/dryers or the lower-level gym with glass doors, or the pool cabana bath with changing area. The list goes on! Come see for yourself.
