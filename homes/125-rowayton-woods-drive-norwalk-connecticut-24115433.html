About this home

Rowayton Woods is a highly sought-after complex set on 22 acres of park-like land, featuring a pool and tennis courts. This ranch-style unit is situated on the lower level near the pond, offering water views from all main rooms. The spacious two-bedroom, one-bath unit has been a home for over 20 years and is being sold "as is," allowing the next owner to customize it for future use. Inside, you'll find a large living room and dining room with a gas fireplace, along with sliding doors that open to a deck and lawn, which is ideal for pet owners. The primary bedroom provides direct views of the pond, while the second bedroom is conveniently located near the entryway. Both bedrooms share a full bathroom featuring a shower/tub combination. Additional features include a laundry room and a spacious entry closet. The unit comes with an assigned parking space (#181) that is conveniently located nearby, as well as visitor parking spots. There is also a separate, secure storage area located above the garage. This property is within walking distance to the Metro North train station and close to Rowayton Village, which offers a variety of restaurants, shops, and events like Shakespeare in the Park. The seller prefers quick close.
