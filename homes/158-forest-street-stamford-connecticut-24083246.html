About this home

Completely Renovated 3 Bed / 2.5 Bath Townhouse in Downtown Stamford This stunning, fully renovated townhouse offers modern living in a prime downtown location-just steps from restaurants, shopping, theaters, and the train. The open floor plan features brand-new wide plank hardwood flooring and oversized windows, creating a bright, airy feel. The sleek, modern kitchen boasts top-of-the-line appliances and a charming bow window. The spacious living and dining area flows seamlessly to a private balcony, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite has its own balcony with serene courtyard views and a large walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms provide ample space. The ground level adds 800 sqft of versatile living space with a full walk-out-ideal for a home office, media room, or guest retreat. A one-car attached garage, pull-down attic, and oversized storage room complete the home. This is the only complex in town where each unit includes a fully fenced private courtyard plus two large balconies-offering rare indoor-outdoor living. A must-see!
