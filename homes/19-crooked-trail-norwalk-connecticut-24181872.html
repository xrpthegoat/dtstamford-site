About this home

Welcome to 19 Crooked Trail, a beautifully maintained ranch nestled on a coveted private .50-acre property in the sought-after Rowayton neighborhood of Norwalk. Offering exceptional one-floor living, this move-in ready home blends comfort, style, and convenience in a truly special setting. The residence features 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, complemented by sun-filled living spaces designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining including a spacious living room/dining room with fireplace, eat in kitchen and family room that opens up to a spacious deck and private yard. The finished lower level adds valuable versatility, ideal for a recreation room, home office, fitness area, or additional living space. Set on a serene and private half-acre, the property offers a rare sense of retreat while remaining close to all that Rowayton has to offer. Two oversized garage bays provide ample space for vehicles, storage, or workshop needs. Brand new central AC system. An exceptional opportunity to enjoy refined single-level living in a premier location-this is not to be missed.
