About this home

Welcome to 62 Weed Hill Avenue, a charming 1920 colonial offering 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths. The spacious colonial brimming with charm and character offers 2228 sq. ft of living space. Front porch creates amazing curb appeal and inviting atmosphere. Enter through the front door notice the high ceilings and spacious living room with fireplace, spacious formal dining room, roomy eat-in-kitchen drench in natural light. 1st floor is complete with office/den or 1st floor bedroom, your choice, and full bath. Head upstairs where a large primary bedroom with walk-in closet drench in natural light will make you say WOW. Additional two 2nd floor bedrooms and full bath offer desirable layout. Don't miss the walk-up attic that can be used as a bedroom or additional living space (unheated) great bonus space! Lovely deck to enjoy the private level backyard with large separate garage, ample parking, ample storage. Easy living with Gas for heat/cooking, City Water, City Sewer. Main Roof is 1 yr., lower roofs approx. 10 yrs, Tankless Boiler 3 yrs. Washer/Dryer located in basement. Close proximity to everything the desirable village of Springdale has to offer: Weed Memorial Library, Springdale School, restaurants, shops and Springdale Train Station less than one mile away make this a commuter's dream. Come fall in love. Home sold "as is".
