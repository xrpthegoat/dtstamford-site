About this home

Village Creek mid-century modern. One of the original homes built in Village Creek in 1950, it is filled with the warmth of natural light directly on the Long Island Sound tidal marsh. Open floor plan with views from every window. Indoor outdoor living at it's best. Two primary bedrooms with full baths and a 3rd bedroom and half bath. The house is remarkably versatile as it can be lived-in as a single family home or the lower level can be used as a separate in-law suite with a kitchenette. Separate studio perfect for an in-home office or art studio. Village Creek association has a beautiful private beach, tennis court and playing field. And if you are a boater the Village Creek marina has slips available for an additional charge.
