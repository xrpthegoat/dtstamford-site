About this home

This home offers privacy and natural beauty in a peaceful setting. Nestled on a scenic 1.4-acre wooded lot, this charming 1,980 sq ft residence combines modern comforts with picturesque surroundings, making it a perfect retreat for those who appreciate tranquility without sacrificing convenience. As you approach the property, you'll be greeted by a private driveway that winds through lush trees, leading to the front of the house where a serene pond provides a beautiful focal point. This stunning natural feature enhances the home's curb appeal and creates a calming atmosphere that extends throughout the property. The house's unique setting is further enriched by the Norwalk River, which runs behind the home, providing peaceful views and the soothing sound of flowing water. Each of the three bedrooms is generously sized and thoughtfully designed to provide comfort and privacy. Outdoors, the property truly shines. With 1.4 acres of wooded land, there's ample space to enjoy nature and unwind in a secluded setting. Whether you're sipping coffee on the porch in the morning, enjoying the gentle sound of the river behind the house, or taking in the views of the pond at dusk, this property offers an ideal escape from the hustle and bustle of daily life. For those who enjoy outdoor activities, the expansive yard and wooded surroundings provide plenty of options, from gardening and picnicking to exploring the nearby trails and riverbanks.
