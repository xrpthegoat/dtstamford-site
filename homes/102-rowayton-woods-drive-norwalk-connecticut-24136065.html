About this home

This lovingly cared-for home welcomes you the moment you step inside. Offering 900 square feet of living space - this sunny garden-level condo in the highly desirable Rowayton Woods complex combines comfort, convenience, and style. Recent renovations include new flooring, new kitchen appliances, fresh kitchen paint, new hot water heater, recessed lighting, nest systems, and keyless lock, making this move-in ready. Enjoy a bright east-facing kitchen that fills with morning light, a spacious living and dining area with sliders to a private patio, and a generously sized bedroom with excellent closet space. The laundry room provides additional ample walk-in storage. Rowayton Woods is known for its beautifully maintained grounds and wonderful amenities, including a heated in-ground pool, pool house, tennis court, and community garden area - creating one of the most serene and sought-after condo settings in town. Located just moments from the Rowayton Train Station, local shops, schools, and downtown Rowayton, this home offers the perfect blend of tranquility and convenience.
