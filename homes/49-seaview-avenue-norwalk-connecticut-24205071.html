About this home

Enjoy breathtaking sunsets and panoramic water views from this fully renovated and remodeled premium East Norwalk waterfront 2nd Floor apartment.Surrounded by windows overlooking Norwalk Harbor and Long Island Sound, this exceptional unit is filled with natural light and showcases spectacular water views from nearly every room.At the heart of the home is a fully updated kitchen featuring new cabinetry, quartz countertops, a tiled backsplash, stainless steel appliances and center island.The open layout flows seamlessly into the spacious living room, where sliders lead to a private balcony overlooking the harbor and waterfront neighborhood. The spacious bedroom offers an adjoining dressing/lounging area with great closet space, while the updated full bath completes this comfortable and inviting retreat. Additional features include private keyless entry, gleaming hardwood floors, new windows, doors, and lighting fixtures throughout.Residents also have access to lower-level laundry room, a one-car garage, and convenient on-street parking. Ideally located in the vibrant East Norwalk community, this waterfront gem offers easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and the Norwalk River Valley Trail. Just a short walk or ride away are Veterans Memorial Park, historic SoNo, the South Norwalk and East Norwalk train stations, Norwalk Harbor, the Maritime Aquarium, beaches, parks, specialty shops, cafes, ice cream destinations, and an array of fine dining options.
