About this home

Welcome to this bright and spacious 3/4-bedroom, 2-bath single-family home nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Mid-Ridges neighborhood. The main level features hardwood floors throughout, an inviting living room, an eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a dining room with sliding glass doors leading to a private deck overlooking the level, fully fenced backyard. Three generously sized bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. The finished lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a large family room, fourth bedroom or home office, second full bath, laundry area, and direct access to the attached one-car garage. Additional highlights include a whole-house generator, ample storage, and a pet-friendly policy. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, Merritt Parkway, and downtown Stamford, this home offers both comfort and convenience.
