About this home

This spacious 5-bedroom, 4 full-bath residence sits on nearly half an acre in one of Darien's most sought after neighborhoods. Nine-foot ceilings, generously proportioned rooms, and abundant natural light create an airy, inviting atmosphere throughout. The kitchen flows into a spacious family room with a wood-burning fireplace and opens to a large deck. The finished walk-out lower level features a full bath, private office, and flexible space perfect for a playroom, gym, or guest suite. A two-car attached garage and ample storage complete the offering. A rare combination of space and convenience, this home offers easy access to Holmes Elementary School via the Phillips Lane footpath and a seamless NYC commute from the Glenbrook and Noroton Heights train stations. Shopping and restaurants are minutes away in both downtown Darien and New Canaan. Space, convenience and community - it's all at 43 Phillips Lane.
