About this home

A home that is anything but ordinary, 25 Wildwood Road is for the discerning buyer who seeks character and craftsmanship. Rooted in the romance of a 1931 Tudor, this is a residence where the splendor of a bygone era meets every luxury of today - set on 2.16 private acres in North Stamford. Original hand-crafted moldings and casement windows speak to the home's enduring soul, carefully preserved through an elegant renovation. Light pours through new windows, illuminating 9 ft ceilings and newly refinished hardwood floors. Generously proportioned living and dining rooms set an effortless stage for entertaining. The kitchen is a true showpiece with custom cabinetry, granite countertops and premium Thermador appliances. The adjoining butler's pantry dazzles with brass fixtures, refrigerated drawers, and exceptional storage. A wood-paneled study offers the perfect work-from-home retreat. Upstairs, the primary suite is grand - multiple walk-in closets and a spa-inspired bath. Three/four additional bedrooms and a bonus room complete the upper level. Additional finished space on the main floor offers flexibility, including as an in-law suite. The grounds are as extraordinary as the home. The family room and screened-in porch open to a new bluestone patio with built-in grill, firepit, an eight-person Caldera spa, and pergola. This is where summers are made. 25 Wildwood Road is not simply a home. It is a statement of how beautifully the past and present can coexist.
