About this home

Now complete! Set on over 2 acres in one of Westports's most desired neighborhoods, this stunning new construction by HOBI award winner, Rick Benson, blends modern luxury with timeless design. Surrounded by mature trees, this home offers privacy & serenity minutes from trains, town, shopping & beaches. Discover luxurious living spaces, 6 ensuite bedrooms, 7 full/1 half baths, seamless flow between open-concept living & refined formal spaces. The gourmet kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring 60" Wolf range, Sub-Zero appliances, galley sink, fridge drawers, hidden shelves next to range, & a working pantry. Elevator-ready for 5 stops, the home is built for long term comfort. Entertain effortlessly in the family room containing a full wet bar with wine column & beverage center; the room opens to the covered porch & outdoor kitchen & dining area. The resort-style backyard is anchored by a 20'x40' foot pool with internal spa & sunning platform, looking over the extensive graceful lawn. The sumptuous primary suite is marked by vaulted ceiling, spacious deck with glass railings & wellness room. Additional highlights include 10" ft ceilings on the 1st floor, 9" on other floors, Lutron lighting, 2 instant hot water heaters, 4 gas fireplaces, fabulous walk-out lower level with full bar, gym, large gathering space & wine tasting area. Space exists for a 1000 sq ft ADU or 300 sq ft pool house, huge unfinished 3rd floor awaiting your ideas. Schedule your private tour today.
