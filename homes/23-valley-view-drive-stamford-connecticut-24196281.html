About this home

Experience refined living in this exceptional contemporary residence, privately set on 1.5 beautifully landscaped level acres in one of North Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering 4,700 square feet, 5 spacious bedrooms, and 4.5 bathrooms, this home combines elegance, comfort, and privacy just minutes from downtown Stamford. Step inside to an impressive, sun-filled living room featuring dramatic soaring cathedral ceilings, skylights, and a stunning fireplace, creating an inviting space for both everyday living and unforgettable entertaining. The expansive eat-in kitchen flows effortlessly into the oversized family room, formal dining room, and large outdoor deck-perfect for hosting gatherings or enjoying peaceful mornings surrounded by nature. The thoughtfully designed main level includes a private guest suite with its own full bath, a convenient laundry room, and a powder room. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite serves as a tranquil retreat with a spacious ensuite bath, while three additional generously sized bedrooms-including another ensuite-provide comfort and flexibility for family, guests, or home offices. Surrounded by mature trees and complete privacy, this remarkable property offers the serenity of country living while remaining just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, shopping, restaurants, parks, and major commuting routes.
