About this home

474 Brookside Road is a rare, turnkey Connecticut estate-an elegant 1938 home set on 2.75 private acres, transformed through thoughtful, high-end updates into a luxury retreat. Classic architectural character meets modern scale, light, and lifestyle. Inside, the home is defined by warmth and sophistication with a sense of history and comfort. Sunlight moves from room to room across perfectly proportioned spaces, designer finishes, and timeless millwork. The updated kitchen opens to an inviting family room-an everyday gathering space. The living room transitions from the family room with French doors that spill out to the terraces-one with a corner firepit. The serene primary suite offers a newly finished spa bath, radiant light, and tree-top views. The entertainment "barn" provides an architectural art form of custom cut stone floor with radiant heat, peaked ceilings, barn doors, and wide-plank floors.
