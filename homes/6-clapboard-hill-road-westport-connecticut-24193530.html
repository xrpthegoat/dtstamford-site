About this home

At the top of Clapboard Hill, through the gate and over the brick drive, an iconic Westport Colonial welcomes with an immediate feeling of privacy and classic beauty. The main entrance reveals a double height foyer, sweeping staircase and historic charm. The formal living room spans front to back with multiple seating areas flanking the original fireplace. A smart, chic library lands just off the center hall, followed by the gorgeous dining room ready for family dinners and holiday entertaining. The white and bright kitchen is anchored by an island and includes a full butler's pantry, a prohibition era wine closet, and the sunniest breakfast room overlooking the patio. The family room is so spacious with room for lounging by the fire and piling in for movie nights. Upstairs holds a total of six bedrooms. The primary suite includes a beautiful bath, multiple walk-in closets and sweeping views. A sweet set of bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bath. There are three additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a laundry room. The third level is fully finished as a recration room. The two acre property is fully irrigated, professionally landscaped, and sectioned for pool, play, patio spaces and privacy. The stunning pool includes a lap lane and the pool house is cabana perfection with a main room, poolside necessities (including a secondary bbq!) and full bath. There is a three car garage and automatic gate to split driveway. So close to beaches, GF train stations, schools and town!
