About this home

The Levi Coley House, circa 1848, is widely regarded as Wilton's most significant Greek Revival residence. A grand portico with four Doric columns sets a striking tone, while inside, historic character blends seamlessly with modern updates. Original wide-plank floors, soaring ceilings, oversized windows, and five of six original fireplaces (four functional, 2 gas and 2 wood burning) highlight the home's authenticity. The updated kitchen features a Wolf range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, island, and fireplace. Five renovated baths include two ensuites and a third-floor guest suite. A four-season glass solarium adds a distinctive finishing touch.
