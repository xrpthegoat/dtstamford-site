About this home

Welcome to 160 Rock Rimmon Road, a Stamford retreat designed for unforgettable gatherings and effortless everyday living. Set on a quiet, scenic stretch, this home offers the perfect balance of privacy, space, and flow-ideal for those who love to host. At the center of it all is a chef's kitchen designed by Andrea Immer-Robinson, Chef, Master Sommelier and Television personality. The kitchen was designed to impress-designed for serious cooking and seamless entertaining alike. With generous prep space and a layout that keeps the host connected to guests, it's equally suited for intimate dinners or large-scale celebrations. This is where meals become events and entertainment feels effortless. This home also offers a well-balanced first floor layout with generously sized rooms. The light filled living spaces flow easily from room to room creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The four bedrooms are comfortably proportioned, offering flexibility for primary sleeping quarters, guest rooms or home office use. The primary suite is a peaceful retreat with generous closet space and a well-appointed bath. Outdoor spaces further elevates the experience, providing a natural backdrop for summer barbecues, al fresco dining, or evenings under the stars. The inground pool with custom lighting is the pinnacle of entertaining delight. The setting feels tucked away and yet remains conveniently close to Stamford's dining, shopping and commuter routes.
