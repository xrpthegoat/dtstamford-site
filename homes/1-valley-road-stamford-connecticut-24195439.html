About this home

Welcome to this bright and spacious 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse in a convenient downtown Stamford location. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. The dining room opens to the living room with a fireplace and sliding glass doors leading to a private balcony, perfect for relaxing. A half bath is located on the main level. The upper level offers a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet, a second bedroom, and an in-unit washer and dryer. Hardwood floors throughout and an Attached Garage. Conveniently located near Downtown Stamford, shopping, restaurants, Metro-North, and I-95. Available August 16, 2026. Tenants must cover at least 50% of the first-floor area with padded carpet or area rugs. Carpet padding is required under all wall-to-wall carpeting.
