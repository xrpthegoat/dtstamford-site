About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated Colonial tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in sought-after West Norwalk. Located in a small, well-maintained HOA community, this turn-key home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Step inside to find brand new hardwood floors and a grand staircase, with soaring cathedral ceilings and natural light pouring through every room. Cozy up by the fireplace in winter or enjoy zoned central air year-round. The spacious dining room flows into a brand-new gourmet kitchen featuring quartz countertops, new appliances, and a large island that opens into a second living area with a fireplace and sliders to an expansive deck that spans the home, complete with a built-in hot tub. A 2-car garage with additional storage, centrally placed half bath, and laundry room complete the main level. Downstairs, enjoy a newly finished basement with wet bar, full bath, flexible, ample-sized living space, and a glass-enclosed bonus room-perfect for a gym or playroom. The whole basement is a possible in-law suite. Upstairs, you'll find four generous bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with cathedral ceilings, two walk-in closets and a spa-like bath with double vanity, jacuzzi tub, and skylight. A second full bath serves the three large remaining bedrooms. All of this located off the beaten path, yet close to shopping, dining, I-95, other transportation and Connecticut Ave. Come see this turn-key gem before it is gone!
