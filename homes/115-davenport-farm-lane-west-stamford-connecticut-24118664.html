About this home

Timeless Colonial Charm in Exclusive Davenport Farm. Perfect blend of classic elegance and modern updates in this charming Colonial, ideally situated at the end of a serene cul-de-sac in the coveted Davenport Farm private community nestled in North Stamford on the New Canaan border. Character-rich residence showcases five stunning masonry fireplaces, three with wood burning stove inserts. The home features spacious rooms and much natural light. The first floor features a beautifully renovated kitchen opening to a family room with stone hearth and stove insert, a large home office/library with built in oak cabinetry and desk, spacious living room and dining room, both with hearth fireplaces, and a sun filled room with floor to ceiling windows, skylights and beautiful stone hearth and stove. On the second floor, the primary suite retreat has a brick hearth fireplace and a sun-filled atrium that infuses the room with natural light. Large renovated master bath, three more bedrooms and two baths complete the second floor. Huge ensuite bedroom could easily be divided into two separate rooms to suit your lifestyle needs. Partially finished basement includes large space for gym or play area, and unfinished space for storage. Full house generator. Set on elevated grounds providing magnificent sunset views and outstanding curb appeal, this distinguished home offers the tranquility of a private community just minutes from premier shopping, dining, and commuting options.
