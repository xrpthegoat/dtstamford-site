About this home

*Highest and Best offers due 7/31 at 12pm* **HUGE INVESTOR OPPORTUNITY** Welcome to 34 Deepwood Lane, Norwalk, an exceptional opportunity to transform a well-built home into something truly special. Nestled on one of the area's most desirable cul-de-sac streets, this property is surrounded by beautifully maintained homes and manicured lawns, offering an outstanding setting for your investment or future residence. With solid construction and "good bones," this four-bedroom home is ready for its next chapter. Whether you're an investor seeking your next renovation project, a buyer looking to create instant equity, or someone eager to design a home tailored to your own style, the possibilities are endless. Existing features provide an excellent foundation, including hardwood floors, a spacious sunroom overlooking the backyard, a deck for outdoor entertaining, a fireplace, a full basement, an attached garage, and a quiet, private backyard that offers a peaceful retreat. Ideally located just minutes from the Rowayton train station, shopping, dining, parks, and major commuting routes, this home combines an outstanding location with unlimited potential. Use your vision and creativity, and seize the opportunity to restore this property to its full potential in a neighborhood where your investment will truly shine.
