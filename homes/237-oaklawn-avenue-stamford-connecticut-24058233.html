About this home

Single family unfurnished rental located in central Stamford conveniently located close to everything. The four bedroom cape cod style home features two bedrooms on the main level and two on the upper level. The living room has newly refinished hardwood floors and the entire house was recently painted. The spacious eat-in kitchen leads out onto the large, relatively new wood deck and level, fully fenced back yard. The lower level has a large open room that could be used for recreational purposes, and also has a workspace with work table and plenty of storage shelving. The one car garage is extra deep with plenty of storage for bicycles, automotive and sports gear. The house has plenty of closets and storage space for the active lifestyle. Tenant will be responsible for grounds maintenance, including grass cutting and snow removal, as well as all utilities. Prospective tenants must have excellent credit and be able to verify all income. A credit check and complete rental application will be required. Pets will be considered.
