About this home

Location, Location! Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac, this move in ready raised ranch offers comfort, convenience, and space to grow. Thoughtfully maintained, the home features 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and hardwood floors throughout the main level. The Living Room, complete with a wood burning fireplace, flows into a sun-filled Dining Room and an updated Kitchen, ideal for everyday living and entertaining. A spacious Family Room at the back of the house is wrapped in windows, offering natural light from every angle-a cozy spot for movie nights, morning coffee, or curling up with a good book. From here, step out onto the deck and enjoy the level backyard with a separate sitting area, perfect for BBQs, gatherings, or just relaxing outdoors. The lower level adds flexibility with its own full bath and great natural light, ideal as a guest space, home office, or potential in-law setup. Additional highlights include storage under the house, an oversized 2-car garage, you'll appreciate the newer driveway and attractive stone wall-adding both curb appeal and function and recently updated natural gas heat and central air. A rare blend of peaceful living and unbeatable convenience, close to beaches, shopping, schools, parks, and major highways.
