About this home

Step into a truly special offering- a stately New England home, thoughtfully RENOVATED and completely MOVE-IN READY. Conveniently located close to the train, elementary school, downtown shopping/dining, and parks. Situated on over half an acre this home also enjoys access to a private Cul de sac ideal for bike rides and walks, creating a true neighborhood feel. As you enter the home, you are greeted by a two-story foyer and 6,195 sqft of character, elegance, and modern convenience. The gourmet kitchen is thoughtfully appointed with a large center island, six-burner gas range, Sub-Zero refrigeration, warming drawer, wet bar, and a convenient back staircase to the second level. Two potential primary suites provide exceptional flexibility, each offering spa-like baths and peaceful retreats. SOME RECENT UPDATES include all new central air(2023 & 2024), new boiler, water heater, natural gas conversion & new flat roof. Integrated sound runs throughout the main living areas, primary suite, and veranda. A stunning formal living room with a soaring 12-ft ceiling is among the home's most impressive features, highlighted by a beautiful bow window and opening to the veranda and sun-filled garden room with heated slate floors. This extraordinary space creates an unforgettable first impression and is even more impressive in person than photos can capture. Rich hardwood flooring, natural wood beams, intricate moldings, and three fireplaces showcase timeless craftsmanship rarely found today.
