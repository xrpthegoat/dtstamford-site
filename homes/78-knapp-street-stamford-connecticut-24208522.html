About this home

Walk to everything from this lovely 4 bedroom/2.5 bath 1929 Colonial home in Springdale section of Stamford! Shopping, dinning and even the train Station are within walking distance. Very versatile floor plan with options for Primary bedroom suite on the main level or upstairs and there is even in-law suite potential. Great solid and well maintained period home with lots of recent updates including roof, natural gas boiler, hot water heater and new washer & dryer. Central air conditioning has been added throughout the home (wall units pictured are from before central air was installed). Estate Sale.
