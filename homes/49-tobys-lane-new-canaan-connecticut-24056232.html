About this home

Discover this stunning new build by Woodcrest Homes on a prime cul-de-sac, in the West school district, set on 2.2 private acres with ample space for a pool and pool house. This transitional-style home features 10-foot ceilings, custom millwork, and six ensuite bedrooms, offering both luxury and comfort. Enjoy the elegance of four fireplaces that create a wonderful flow throughout the living spaces. A bright chef's kitchen with an adjacent breakfast room, and a family room with a coffered ceiling. A private paneled library and formal dining room with butler's pantry. The primary suite boasts a breathtaking cathedral ceiling bath with radiant heat floors, providing a spa-like retreat. The finished walkout lower level and 6th bedroom with a full bath offers even more living options. A large mudroom with radiant heat floors and enclosed cubbies adds practicality to this exquisite home. Design services are provided by Haley Design Company, ensuring every detail is thoughtfully crafted. Experience luxury living at its finest - January 2025 occupancy.
