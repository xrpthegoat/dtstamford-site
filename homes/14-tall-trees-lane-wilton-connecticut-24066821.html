About this home

Highest and best deadline by Monday, 1/13 at 5pm. Located on a quiet lane in South Wilton this 4 bedroom 2.5 bath Colonial home awaits its new owner. Situated on 2.24 acres of parklike property, this home is private yet convenient to schools, train, major highways as well as Wilton and Westport town center. As you enter the home you will find a spacious living room with a fireplace and dining room, ideal for entertaining family and friends. The updated kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances plus an island with a breakfast bar. A few steps away you will enter the family room that can serve as additional living space or a home office. As you venture up to the second floor, you will find a primary bedroom with full bath and room for expansion over the garage. 3 additional bedrooms and a full hall bath complete the second level. Other features of the home include a first floor half bath, hardwood floors, laundry on the main level, attic, plus a full basement ready for the next owner to finish for a playroom or additional living space and a 2-car garage with ample storage space. Outside you will appreciate the large private backyard, patio, and shed. This home has a flexible floor plan and it's conveniently located on a quiet street with endless possibilities!
