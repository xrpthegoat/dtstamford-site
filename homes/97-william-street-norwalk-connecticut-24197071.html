About this home

Charming vintage 1934 split level with beautiful landscaping/gardens offers many lovely features including living room with fireplace, primary bedroom with private full bath, large stone backyard patio off of an enclosed porch. 4 floors of living space (all above grade) enable flexibility with usage. Lower level bedroom has it's own private full bath--ideal for guest bedroom or at home office. Many recent improvements including a brand new boiler--just a few months old, 2 fully remodeled baths, newer A/C system and freshly painted interior. Huge garage with extra storage room. This special one-of-a-kind home is located on the Norwalk/Westport line and is walking distance to all three schools.
