About this home

*HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY MONDAY MARCH, 24th at 5:00pm* Welcome to this exquisite bright and light 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom modern farmhouse-inspired home located in the coveted Sasqua Hills neighborhood, offering direct, unobstructed views of the Shorehaven Golf Course. The property's curb appeal is enhanced by an adorable garden with beautiful spring flowers and a charming porch, ideal for enjoying your morning coffee or a sunset cocktail. Upon entering, you'll find a welcoming foyer that leads to the main level, featuring a cozy living room, dining area, and an eat-in kitchen. The kitchen is equipped with a farmhouse sink, breakfast bar, quartz Caesarstone countertops, and ample storage space. The second level boasts a primary ensuite with vaulted ceilings and a luxurious bathroom. Additionally, there are two spacious bedrooms with stunning views and a full bath, complete with a soaking tub and walk-in shower. On the third level, you'll find a second primary ensuite, which can also serve as an office/den/playroom, offering magnificent views of the golf course and Long Island Sound. This home is conveniently located within walking distance to beaches, parks, train station, and Westport, making it truly one-of-a-kind. If you're looking to experience a vacation-like atmosphere every day, then this home is perfect for you!
