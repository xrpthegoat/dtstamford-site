About this home

Rare Norwalk opportunity! This first and second floor duplex at 26 Center Ave sits on a tree lined street with convenient sidewalks - offering the perfect house-hack setup: live in one unit and rent the other, or hold both as a turnkey investment. One unit features 1 bedroom (currently being used as 2) and 1 baths across 742 sq ft of well-designed living space, the second unit is upstairs and has 2 bedrooms on one floor and another on the third floor with over 900 sq ft of living area. Solidly built in 1915, this move-in ready property offers solid quality that's hard to find at this price point. Whether you're an owner-occupant looking to offset your mortgage or an investor seeking cash flow in one of Fairfield County's most desirable towns, 26 Center Ave delivers flexibility, privacy, and long-term value on a peaceful street just minutes from shopping, the train station, and all that downtown Norwalk has to offer. Two homes, endless possibilities - don't miss this one.
