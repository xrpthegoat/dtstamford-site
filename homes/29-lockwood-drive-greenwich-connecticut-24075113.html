About this home

Stylish five-bedroom Contemporary provides a modern oasis with stunning pool, Ipe wood decks and secluded backyard on a quiet drive south of the village near shops, trains, school and beach. Striking architectural details ranging from maple wood floors, glass stair railings on three levels, high ceilings, expansive windows, sliding doors and skylights enhance 4,286 sq. ft. interior. Boasts sleek kitchen with Gaggenau appliances, Geoluxe counters and butler's pantry. Family and dining rooms with shared linear fireplace and games room, all with deck access for outdoor entertaining. First floor guest suite or office. Second floor has serene primary suite with spacious closet and luxurious bath plus three more ensuite bedrooms. Lower level with media room, mudroom and garage for up to eight cars.
