About this home

Welcome to Riverside and to this beautifully updated and expanded 4 bedroom Cape Cod. The main level has a new large Chef's kitchen with custom cabinetry, Thermador appliances and leathered finished Granite countertops. The kitchen opens up to a fieldstone patio with a stone grill, great for entertaining. There is a large living/dining room with fireplace, family room, new mudroom and a full bathroom. The second floor has a large new master suite with a modern bathroom, 'his & her' walk in closets and space for seating and/or work. Three additional bedrooms and another updated bathroom. Finished lower level. Stroll or bike to the beach, Binney park, Old Greenwich shops and restaurants. Close to elementary and middle schools as well as two train stations. A must see!!
