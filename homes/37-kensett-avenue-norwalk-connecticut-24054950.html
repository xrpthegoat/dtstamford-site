About this home

Stunning Contemporary Colonial featuring striking architectural elements, open-concept & located in the desirable Cranbury neighborhood on the Wilton line. Enter this beautiful home & be captivated by the expansive Living Rm adorned w/walls of windows, a soaring two-story vaulted ceiling, skylights, & rich hw floors. The impressive floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace serves as a focal point. The flow is seamless to the Dining area, complete w/bright atrium window, wet bar & sliders opening to one of the 3 inviting decks. Perfect for hosting gatherings & enjoying the outdoors. The eat-in Kitchen is equipped w/ss appliances, pantry, cozy breakfast nook all open to the family rm. Around the corner is the laundry rm w/half bath and access to the 2-car garage. The main level also boasts a bedroom/in-law suite featuring a fireplace, spacious den/office w/vaulted ceiling & full bath w/double sinks, relaxing jacuzzi tub & stall shower. Head upstairs to discover the impressive Primary Suite, highlighted by vaulted ceiling w/beams, an amazing dressing rm & beautifully renovated bath w/double sinks, a sizable stall shower, & skylights. The second floor also includes 2 additional generous bedrooms, renovated hall bath & versatile loft area. The lower level includes a rec/playroom, 2 more bedrooms, full bath, option for a home gym & tons of storage. Step outside to view the stunning gunite pool & spa, a Tiki Hut lounge area complete w/TV and expansive level property. This home is a Must See!
