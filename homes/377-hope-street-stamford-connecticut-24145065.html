About this home

Brand new from the studs up, remodeled 2BR, 1.5 bath townhouse. Every detail has been reviewed. Plenty of natural light, spacious living room with recessed LED lighting, brand-new vinyl flooring that flows seamlessly into a formal separate dining area. The beautifully designed eat-in kitchen features white quartz countertops, New soft close premier tall cabinets, subway tile backsplash, large white quartz breakfast island, and all new appliances, including gas cooking. The owner/designer has meticulously renovated every detail for elegance. Enjoy gas heat, central air, new LED efficient light fixtures, freshly painted throughout, and a luxurious walk-in shower with custom glass doors and modern tile work. Both bedrooms are generously sized, and each offers rare oversized custom walk-in closets. Additional conveniences include a remodeled half bath on the main level, custom designed panel doors for the laundry room, and two included parking spaces in front of the unit. Located in the heart of Glenbrook walk to the train, restaurants, cafes, shops, and nightlife. Property manager is always present. Located minutes to downtown Stamford and directly on the Darien border. A must-see rental opportunity.
