About this home

Steps from Norwalk Hospital - Ideal Two-Family with Basement Unit! Located just a short walk from Norwalk Hospital, this two-family home offers a fantastic opportunity for investors or owner-occupants looking for space, flexibility, and upside. The first unit features 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath with its own laundry hook-up. Below, a fully finished basement includes additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a kitchen-great for extended living or rental potential. The second unit includes 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath, also with laundry hook-up. Hardwood floors are present throughout most of the home. Out back, you'll find a detached shed that was previously rented, offering even more income options. The property sits on a spacious lot with plenty of parking-a valuable perk for tenants or guests. While the home could use some cosmetic updates, it has solid bones and a layout ready for your vision. Just minutes from I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and Route 7, and close to beaches, parks, shopping, dining, and more , this location is perfect for commuters and locals alike. Previous rents were $2600/month for the two bedrooms, $1400/month for the one bedroom basement unit, and $800/month for shed/storage Don't miss your chance to own 72 Maple St-an income-generating property with great potential in the heart of Norwalk!
