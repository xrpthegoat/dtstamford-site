About this home

Welcome to this little piece of heaven! You will feel the warmth as you enter into this condo rental. It has all you need with new appliances (just a few months old), newer windows and sliders (1 year old) and a wood burning fireplace. You can cozy up in front of the fireplace or simply breath in tranquility as you sit on 1 of 2 balconies. Would you like your morning cup of something from your living room balcony or your bedroom balcony? It's your choice to eat in the kitchen or enjoy your meal at the dining table. You have your own private bathroom en-suite while your guests have their own hall powder room to use. This unit comes with a reserved covered parking space and it's own private storage unit. There is an outdoor community area under the trees where you can read a good book or simply enjoy nature. If you love shopping or you need a last minute gift, you can walk to the Stamford Town Center. Foodie? All good, Downtown is a walk away. Need to commute to the City? Got you covered. Metro North and Amtrak are less than 1 mile away. Welcome Home!
