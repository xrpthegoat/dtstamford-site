About this home

Welcome to 9 Lambert Common-an immaculate, beautifully upgraded townhouse in a premier South Wilton location. Offering 1,615 SF above grade, this 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath home features a private no-step entry, hardwood floors throughout & spacious floor plan. The remodeled kitchen offers white cabinetry, stone counters, stainless appliances, tile backsplash & generous workspace, opening to an oversized living/dining area with wood-burning fireplace & sliders to a private stone patio. Mature landscaping & green space beyond create a peaceful outdoor setting. Upstairs, two exceptionally spacious bedrooms include a primary suite w/ remodeled private bath & dual closets in both w/ custom systems. An updated hall bath, linen closet, & upper-level laundry complete the 2nd floor. Additional highlights include a remodeled powder room, Andersen wood-frame windows, Trane HVAC, AO Smith hot water heater & full unfinished basement offering excellent storage. The HOA recently installed a new roof. An assigned parking space is directly outside the front door, with a detached garage nearby. Lambert Common offers a peaceful, established setting w/ easy access to Wilton Center, Westport, New Canaan, Metro-North, shopping, dining & major routes. Beautifully remodeled, meticulously maintained & thoughtfully upgraded, 9 Lambert Common offers an impressive combination of space, privacy & convenience. A truly special opportunity to enjoy turnkey living in one of South Wilton's premier settings!
