About this home

This delightful colonial home is brimming with original character and modern updates. The inviting front porch, framed by elegant stone pillars, welcomes you into a warm and spacious retreat. Inside, the expansive living/dining room features a stunning stone mantle, perfect for cozy nights and daily gatherings. The heart of the home, a spacious kitchen, boasts sleek stainless steel appliances, Carrara marble countertops, and a fresh, classic subway tile backsplash, making meal prep a pleasure. The back hall allows for easy flow, and is where you'll find a convenient full bath and additional access to the bright and charming study/guest room. Upstairs, two sun-filled bedrooms await, along with a bright and spacious bathroom featuring a double vanity. A sun room off the large primary bedroom is the ideal spot for a home office, reading nook, or simply a place to bask in the sunlight. Outdoors, enjoy the privacy of your level back garden, complete with a new brick patio and granite steps-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. .5 miles of sidewalks to Metro North Springdale station. Move right in and enjoy all the conveniences of Stamford, CT.
