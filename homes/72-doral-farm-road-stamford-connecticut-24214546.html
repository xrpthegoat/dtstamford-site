About this home

Contemporary colonial perfectly sited on the tranquil pond at desirable Doral Farm. Elegantly and lovingly maintained and updated by its original owners, this largest of the units in the secluded gated community boasts an open floor plan with sweeping architectural interior and soaring ceilings, a neutral palette, extensive use of granite, high end materials, hardwood and luxurious carpeting. The open flow and unusual amount of light in the unit welcome you with feelings of luxury and ease. The new oversized maintenance free deck overlooks the magnificent waterfront that is adorned by willowy plantings and the many sounds of nature. The 64 acres upon which the 58 free standing homes are built is often described as a bucolic nature preserve, providing beautiful and inviting walking opportunities. The complex offers a heated swimming pool, clubhouse and both tennis AND pickleball courts.
