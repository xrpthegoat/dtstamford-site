About this home

Move right into this timeless classic Colonial, beautifully sited on 1.49 manicured acres with a fully fenced backyard in the heart of desirable Westover. Brimming with character and thoughtfully updated throughout, the home welcomes you with a stunning foyer that opens to an expansive living room with a wood burning fireplace and a bright, elegant formal dining room. The chef's kitchen offers generous space for cooking and entertaining and flows into an expansive pantry and mudroom for exceptional everyday functionality. The grounds are perfect for entertaining, a large front patio, and back deck for BBQ's. Plenty of room for a pool and site plans available for a 3 car garage. Freshly painted throughout, the home also features beautifully refinished hardwood floors across the first and second levels. Upstairs, the completely renovated primary suite features a spacious walk-in closet and luxurious marble bath with Waterworks fixtures. The third floor, converted in 2024 adds an additional bedroom, full bath, and bonus office space, ideal for guests, working from home, or flexible living. Extensive improvements include a new 5 bedroom septic system (2021); Full HVAC and Electrical upgrades (2022); EV charger (2023); and third floor renovation (2024). With classic architecture, beautifully refreshed interiors, and extensive modern upgrades, this exceptional Westover home presents a rare opportunity in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods.
