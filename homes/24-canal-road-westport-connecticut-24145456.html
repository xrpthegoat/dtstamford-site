About this home

VIEWS, VIEWS, VIEWS....Nestled in the coveted coastal community of Saugatuck Shores, this exceptional contemporary beach house beckons. A true waterfront gem - enveloped by water views and with its own private dock granting access to the Long Island Sound, it is the boater's paradise imagined. The turn-key home has an open interior living plan with walls of windows for a bright, spacious and airy flow to the space. The real highlight however, is its rooftop deck with 360-degree views of the water, stunning sunsets and star-gazing. The expansive deck with lounge, dining and bar areas is an entertainers dream. Stroll to nearby beaches, enjoy paddle-boarding, kayaking, fishing or just lounging in this unique waterfront enclave. Minutes to train, shops, restaurants and all that the cosmopolitan town of Westport has to offer. A very special opportunity to own this fabulous home where one can experience luxury and coastal charm. Ask about proposed renovation plans.
