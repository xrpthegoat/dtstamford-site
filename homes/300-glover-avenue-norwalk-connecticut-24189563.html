About this home

Located in the heart of Norwalk, CT, The Curb combines community, nature, and forward momentum in an urban environment. Our pet-friendly luxury apartments boast impeccable modern layouts with new stainless-steel appliances, including a walk-in shower, in-unit full-size washer and dryer, wood-finish plank flooring, and oversized windows, and high ceilings. The Curb offers numerous amenities that act as an extension of your home and help you add variety and quality to your day-to-day life. Take in a breath of fresh air with any of the numerous courtyards and outdoor spaces available, which include fire pits, grilling stations, a pool, and resort-style outdoor lounges. Enjoy games and entertainment in The Club Room, which includes a kitchen, perfect for entertaining. Recharge at the state-of-the-art Fitness Center or give man's best friend the attention they deserve at our onsite dog park. In addition to offering loads of amenities, there are year-round events perfect for socializing and getting to know your neighbors. The Curb has easy access to major transit, fine dining, entertainment, and retail.
