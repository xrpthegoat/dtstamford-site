About this home

Your Perfect Summer Retreat in Westport - Available July & August 2025! Escape to this stunning contemporary home, located on a quiet cul-de-sac, just minutes from downtown, beaches, and top-rated restaurants. This beautifully designed residence provides the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. The spacious home features 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and 4,711 square feet of living space, including a finished basement. The open floor plan boasts soaring ceilings, oversized windows filled with natural light, and a double-sided brick fireplace. Key Features: Heated gunite pool surrounded by a landscaped backyard, ideal for outdoor fun. Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, gas range, and heated wood floors. Formal dining room for 10, two living areas, and a cozy foyer with a piano. Lower-level entertainment room with ping pong table, toys, books, and fitness equipment. Luxurious master suite with soaking tub, heated floors, and walk-in closet. Central air conditioning, attached 2-car garage, and public utilities. Porch, deck, gas grill, and poolside seating for outdoor enjoyment. Enjoy Westport's coastline, golf course, tennis courts, and Sherwood Island State Park, all just minutes away. Available July & August 2025, with limited availability in June. Reach out to the listing agent for more information.
