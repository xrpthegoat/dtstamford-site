About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 1970s gem, offering a perfect blend of classic charm and modern convenience. This home boasts a fresh coat of paint, new windows, a new oil tank, and new gutters, ensuring you can move in with peace of mind. Step inside to discover a bright and airy interior highlighted by hardwood floors throughout the main level. The updated kitchen and bathrooms feature contemporary finishes, making everyday living a pleasure. The spacious lower level is a standout feature, providing a versatile living area with sliders that lead to a welcoming patio, perfect for outdoor relaxation. This level also includes a large bedroom, a full bath, and a laundry area, making it an ideal space for guests or extended family members. Located conveniently close to shopping and I-95, this home offers easy access to all your needs while maintaining a serene, comfortable atmosphere. With nothing left to do but unpack and enjoy, this property is a true move-in-ready opportunity. Don't miss out on making this charming house your new home!
