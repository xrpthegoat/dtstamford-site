About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse in the heart of downtown Wilton, offering the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Filled with natural light, the main level features hardwood floors throughout and a welcoming foyer with French doors leading to a spacious living room with custom built-ins, a fireplace, and sliding doors to the newly refinished private patio. The updated kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a built-in wine chiller, flowing seamlessly into the dining area and remaining open to the living room for effortless everyday living and entertaining. A convenient half bath completes the main level. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features vaulted ceilings, a renovated bath, and a walk-in closet. A second bedroom, full bath, upper-level laundry, and a versatile flex room ideal for a home office, den, guest space, or playroom complete the second floor. An additional bonus area off the staircase offers the perfect spot for a home gym, reading nook, or extra storage. Newer windows, sliders, and an updated HVAC system provide efficiency and peace of mind for years to come. Enjoy the convenience of a dedicated carport, ample guest parking, and a desirable community with a pool, all just a short stroll to Wilton Center's restaurants, shops, library, train station, and everything the village has to offer.
