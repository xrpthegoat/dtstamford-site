About this home

Nestled in complete privacy at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac, this stunning 4-bedroom, 4-bath ranch features the perfect blend of tranquility and convenience, just two minutes from town. Bathed in sunlight, the expansive family room features a breathtaking wall of windows, seamlessly connecting indoor and outdoor living. The cozy living room features built-in bookcases and a charming fireplace, perfect for relaxed evenings at home. The luxurious master suite is a true retreat, boasting a vaulted ceiling, sitting room, and sliding doors that lead to a private deck. The spacious master bath includes a walk-in closet and a roll-in shower for added convenience. Three additional bedrooms and baths provide plenty of room for family or guests. Enjoy the outdoors year-round with expansive decks, mature trees, lush gardens, and meandering paths. The centerpiece of the backyard is the in-ground heated gunite pool, complete with a diving board and surrounded by a huge pool deck - perfect for entertaining or simply relaxing in your own private oasis. This home is as practical as it is beautiful with Sub Zero and Wolf appliances, it also features new energy-efficient windows, a whole-house generator, upgraded mechanicals, and more. With too many features to list, this extraordinary home is truly a must-see! Don't miss your chance to own this secluded paradise just minutes from all the amenities of town. More photos coming soon.
