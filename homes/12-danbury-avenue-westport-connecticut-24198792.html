About this home

FABULOUS BEACH HOUSE! Winter/Academic/Summer rental start AVAILABLE end of July through August 2026 (30K MONTH) Walk to BEACH. Exquisite architectural 3 story Nantucket Style beach home steps from the sands of Compo Beach. "The Seahorse" house was masterfully crafted by award winning Westport Architect Peter Cadoux and is featured on his website. It graced the cover of East Coast Magazine March/April 2012 as well. Stroll to the beach a few steps away then toast with friends on your rooftop deck facing the water! This impeccable home has outdoor porches, decks with water views , media room, fireplaces indoor and out allowing you to embrace the "Compo Beach Lifestyle" so highly coveted! Every detail is custom built. Gourmet kitchen , large leathered limestone counters, Wolf Range, hand built cabinets. Beautiful soft colors grace the walls of this beach house. Unique furnishings create a calm, interesting atmosphere, subtle seahorse images imbedded in staircases and starfish add a little whimsy. All bedrooms have historic paint, beautiful wood finishes and ensuite glass tile and marble bathrooms. Luxurious wood wraps walls and ceilings of 3rd level~ owners favorite . Finished like the cabin of a luxury yacht. Here you'll find desk area for working remotely ,wet bar, 4th bedroom, half bath and rooftop deck. Beach chairs available and outdoor shower. Owner would like their cleaning serv. to be used throughout lease at tenants expense. Listing rented for 45 days; totaling $43,00
