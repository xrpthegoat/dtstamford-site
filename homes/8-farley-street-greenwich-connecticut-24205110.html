About this home

Newly renovated 3-bed, 3.5-bath home on a quiet dead-end street near downtown Greenwich. This freshly updated two-story home blends classic New England proportions with a clean, modern interior. Gray siding, crisp white trim, and a covered front porch with black railings set a polished curb appeal. Two front doors sit under the porch roof, and the landscaping and walkway are newly finished. Inside, an open main level features light hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and a striking linear fireplace set into a dark stone feature wall. The kitchen is the centerpiece: mixed light-wood and white cabinetry, full-height marble backsplash, waterfall-edge island, a Wolf range , and a Sub-Zero refrigerator, all in stainless steel. The layout flows easily from living area to kitchen to the rear of the house. There are three bedrooms and 3.5 baths, with two configured as en suites. Bathrooms match the rest of the house's high-end finish-glass-enclosed showers with rain heads and tiled niches, floating wood vanities, double sinks in the primary bath, and backlit mirrors. A full finished basement with walk-out access adds usable living space, with wood-look flooring, good ceiling height, and multiple rooms. Out back, a large newly landscaped patio with fresh flagstone and a rear porch/deck area provide private outdoor living, thanks to the dead-end location.
