About this home

Discover this charming and well-maintained waterfront home in the heart of Rowayton. With serene water views and your own private dock, this property offers the ultimate in coastal living. Sliding double glass doors lead from both the kitchen and living rooms to a spacious deck, perfect for entertaining and enjoying the water views. The second floor includes 3 bedrooms, two with sliders opening onto private balconies. Beautiful hardwood floors, well-proportioned rooms, a den with a fireplace, and an open living / dining room add to the home's appeal. Just steps from Rowayton's chic village, vibrant community, and Bayley Beach. Your flat private backyard opens up to the tranquil Farm Creek Nature Preserve, where you can relish the tranquility and birdsong. Paddleboard, kayak, or motor boat right from your private dock; venture out to Long Island Sound during high tide. Experience the ease and beauty of living in this sweet Rowayton home, where nature and community converge seamlessly.
