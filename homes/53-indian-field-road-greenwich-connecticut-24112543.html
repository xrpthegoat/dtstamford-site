About this home

Classic Colonial Living in Central Greenwich - 6 Beds, 5 Baths, Walk to Town! Discover timeless elegance and unbeatable convenience in this sun-drenched 6-bedroom, 5-bath Colonial located in the heart of Central Greenwich. Just moments from downtown shops, schools, and easy access to major highways, and Metro-North Train, this beautiful home offers both comfort and location. Step inside to find hardwood floors, central air, and a thoughtfully designed layout perfect for modern living. The spacious kitchen features updated appliances, a pantry, and flows seamlessly into a large dining room with pocket doors, ideal for entertaining. A true highlight is the private study with full bath, accessible from the back porch-perfect for a home office, guest suite, or creative studio. The sunroom with a gas fireplace offers a cozy retreat, while the living room and butler's pantry bring classic charm and function. Outside, enjoy a level yard, circular drive with abundant parking, a two-car garage, The front porch features skylights, while the expansive back porch provides the perfect setting for relaxing or hosting guests.The lower level offers ample storage space and a laundry area, completing this versatile and elegant home.
