About this home

This is waterfront living! Start your morning on the dock and end the day watching the sunset from your rooftop deck. This beautifully updated Harborview home offers the coastal lifestyle buyers dream of. Located in one of East Norwalk's most sought-after waterfront communities, the property includes a private dock, boat mooring, and access to a clubhouse, beach, tennis courts, ballfields, playground, and year-round neighborhood events. Designed to maximize water views and natural light, the home features expansive windows, skylights, French doors, and a dramatic multi-slide glass wall that seamlessly blends indoor and outdoor living. The renovated kitchen includes new countertops, island, appliances, pantry, and custom banquette seating. Refinished floors, a glass-railed staircase, updated fireplace surround, and new interior and exterior doors enhance the home's modern coastal appeal. The primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, loft space, and a private balcony overlooking the water. Outdoor living shines with a year-round plunge pool/spa, new dock and walkway, multiple decks, firepit, seawall, professional landscaping and a rooftop deck with panoramic views of Long Island Sound and the Norwalk Islands.
