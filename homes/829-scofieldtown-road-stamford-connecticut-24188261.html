About this home

Spacious Colonial set on a serene and private 1.5-acre property with breathtaking sunset views. Offering incredible flexibility and endless potential, this expansive home features 5 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and a separate second kitchen ideal for extended family or possible in-law accommodations. A massive walk-out basement provides abundant storage and the opportunity to create additional finished living space. The main level showcases a generously sized eat-in kitchen with center island and stainless steel appliances, a sun-filled living room with hardwood floors and a wood-burning fireplace, plus a charming screened porch perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Also on the first floor are a spacious bedroom with adjoining full bath that can serve as a primary suite, two additional bedrooms with hardwood flooring, and a second full bath. The second level offers its own oversized kitchen, a large primary bedroom, fifth bedroom, and a full bath - creating excellent potential for multi-generational living. Additional highlights include a detached 2-car garage, newer roof, and a flat, usable backyard with plenty of room for a pool. This home is being sold as-is and presents a fantastic opportunity to customize and make it your own.
