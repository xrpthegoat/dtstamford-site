About this home

Experience luxurious waterfront living in this stunning home in the coveted Shippan Point neighborhood, offering breathtaking water views, seamless indoor-outdoor entertaining spaces, & private access to the ALC association's private park, beach, & playground with an annual membership. The open-concept first floor features a gourmet kitchen with a gas Wolf Range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, breakfast bar, & a large island with double-sided seating, all overlooking expansive water vistas. Enjoy unparalleled front-row seats to Stamford's Fourth of July Fireworks, & year-round sunrises from your living room & patio. The primary suite provides a luxurious bath and an expansive deck overlooking Long Island Sound. Additionally, a first-floor suite with separate access is complemented by a full wet bar & en suite bathroom. Upstairs, enjoy a water-facing bedroom with bay window views, a full hallway bathroom, & a third-floor retreat with another water-facing bedroom, full bath, sitting room, and cedar closet. The finished basement offers an exercise area, lounge, laundry, and ample storage. Located within a short distance to West Beach, Brennan's Pub, Stamford Yacht Club, Woodway Beach Club & just minutes from Cummings Park, Cafe Silvium, shops, restaurants, concerts, and movies in downtown Stamford, with easy access to the train to NYC-just five minutes away-this serene waterfront oasis combines luxury, convenience, and community for an unparalleled living experience.
