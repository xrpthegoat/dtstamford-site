About this home

Discover refined living in this elegant center hall colonial, ideally set on a private cul-de-sac just minutes from downtown Greenwich. Meticulous attention to detail is evident throughout, from sophisticated coffered ceilings to exceptional craftsmanship. The open-concept gourmet kitchen is the heart of the home, with generous counter space and a seamless flow into the spacious family room. Five ensuite bedrooms offer comfort and privacy, highlighted by a luxurious primary suite with its own sitting room for a serene retreat. Versatile living areas provide endless options for a home office, study, playroom, or home theatre. Additional features include a three-car garage, finished basement, and fenced yard.
