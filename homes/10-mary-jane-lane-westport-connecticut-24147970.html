About this home

New construction by renowned Red Coat Homes, ideally set on a quiet cul-de-sac in a premier Westport neighborhood. Designed by architect Judith Larson, this exceptional home offers over 7,000 sq ft of refined living space on more than one acre, complete with a resort-style pool. Timeless architecture and sophisticated finishes create spaces ideal for entertaining, work, and everyday living, with generous room sizes, flexible living areas, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Additional highlights include a three-car garage and thoughtful design throughout. Conveniently located close to Westport schools, train, beaches, and town amenities, this is a rare opportunity to own a custom home in a coveted setting.
