About this home

Coveted Glenville location offers a gracious four-bedroom Cape with new covered deck and level backyard near schools, shops, Byram Park with town pool, beach and Greenwich as well as Port Chester trains. Ideally sited on a .2 acre in a wonderful, established neighborhood. Light-filled, 1800 sq. ft. interior boasts rich hardwood floors in an expansive living room with wood-burning fireplace and adjacent dining room with built-in corner cabinetry; efficient kitchen, inviting screened-in sunroom with access to deck/yard, bedroom, full bath and office/den which leads into wood-paneled bedroom. Two bedrooms, one with built-in desk, cupboards and full bath comprise the second floor. Unfinished lower level features laundry and storage. With an attached one-car garage.
