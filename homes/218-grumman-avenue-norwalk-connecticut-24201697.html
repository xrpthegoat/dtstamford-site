About this home

Charming 3-bedroom single-family home in a convenient Norwalk location. The main level features a spacious family room with exposed beams and a wood-burning fireplace, a bright eat-in kitchen with plenty of natural light, center island, built-in dining area, and generous cabinet and counter space, plus a dining room and full bath. Upstairs you'll find three well-sized bedrooms, a full bath, and a convenient laundry closet with full-size washer and dryer. Enjoy multiple outdoor spaces including a welcoming front porch, screened porch, deck, and a spacious level yard. Two-car attached garage with additional storage. Grounds maintenance, snow removal, and trash service are included. Pets considered on a case-by-case basis. Credit check and references required.
