About this home

Welcome to this well-loved home for over 18 years in Parkview Green in the sought-after Springdale area. This is an exceptionally spacious townhouse that offers comfort and convenience in a prime location with easy access to the train for a quick commute to NYC, Springdale Elementary School, parks, library, shopping, and restaurants and a quick jaunt to Darien and New Canaan. This specially designed home spans 4 finished levels, offering both functionality and style. Cook in your kitchen and entertain in your open dining and living room with a cozy fireplace and large sliding doors to the deck that invites natural light and provides a perfect retreat to relax and unwind. The spacious primary bedroom features a custom designed walk-in closet, an ensuite updated bathroom and a deck off of the primary. An additional bedroom and bath are also spacious. The basement is perfect for a home office or playroom, with its adjacent laundry room, lots of storage. The expansive upstairs loft area with a bathroom and separate heating and cooling makes this a home with room to fit all of your needs and wants. The heating and cooling systems are only 3 years old. You have 2 assigned parking spots right in front of your home, and the exterior vinyl siding, insulation, gutters, downspouts and decks are all updated. This rarely available beauty in Parkview Green will not last.
