About this home

Welcome to 7 Dubois Street, a stunning fully renovated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence thoughtfully reimagined with refined finishes and modern design throughout. The expansive primary suite offers a custom walk-in closet and a spa-inspired bath adorned with elegant white tile. At the heart of the home, a show-stopping kitchen features all-new appliances, quartz countertops, and a marble backsplash, seamlessly flowing into the dining and living areas highlighted by a custom white quartz fireplace and beautifully refinished hardwood floors. The finished lower level adds exceptional versatility with two additional bedrooms, a home office, and a spacious family room with sliders opening to a private backyard oasis-complete with patio seating and a fire pit, perfect for effortless indoor-outdoor entertaining. Additional upgrades include all-new high-efficiency propane heating and central air, updated electrical with LED lighting, front and rear irrigation systems, professionally designed low-maintenance landscaping, outdoor and driveway lighting, and an outdoor grill. Ideally located in Stamford's highly sought-after High Ridge neighborhood, just minutes from shopping, dining, downtown Stamford, and multiple train stations for an easy NYC commute.
