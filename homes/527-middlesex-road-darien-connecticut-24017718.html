About this home

Immerse yourself in the exceptional setting and architectural elegance of 527 Middlesex Road. Every aspect of this home was thoughtfully designed to complement its surroundings. The generous open layout effortlessly guides you through the home, offering views of the tree-lined driveway, and picturesque Highland Farm. The grand terrace with ample seating and fire pit overlooks the views of the sprawling landscaped yard and the serene woodlands that adjoin the Wee Burn Country Club. The stunning Gunite pool is the perfect finishing touch to this special property. The home showcases expert craftsmanship with a classic layout designed to incorporate formal areas and private spaces, while maximizing natural light and cardinal point orientation. The gourmet kitchen and family room provide a warm and inviting space for gatherings. The dining, library, and living rooms are elegantly arranged around a grand main hall, creating a seamless connection between the main and upper levels. This spectacular home and property are not to be missed.
