About this home

Beautifully expanded ranch set on 1.18 acres in desirable North Stamford. This spacious home combines classic charm with modern updates, featuring a newer roof and driveway for true move-in readiness. The expansive primary suite includes a walk-in closet, private deck, and a luxurious bath with radiant heat, vaulted ceiling, whirlpool tub, and oversized shower. The large living room offers beamed vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, and wide-plank wood floors. The updated eat-in kitchen features custom cabinetry, granite countertops, and access to a generous deck and fully fenced yard. Set back from the road on a private, park-like property with serene pond views, yet just minutes to downtown Stamford, the Merritt Parkway, and Greenwich.
