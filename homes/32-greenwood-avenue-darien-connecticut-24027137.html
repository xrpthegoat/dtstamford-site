About this home

Stunning, immediate move-in condition with all modern amenities! Fully renovated, featuring white oak wood flooring, white kitchen cabinetry, quartz countertops, new stainless steel Bosch appliances, gas furnace and stove, and tankless gas water heater. New windows and bright, dimmable lighting throughout. Meticulously maintained, the first floor's open layout overlooking the backyard seamlessly connects the living, dining, and kitchen areas-ideal for family time and entertaining. It also includes an additional living area with a barn door, perfect for a cozy family room, office, or guest room. Completing the first floor is a new laundry area and a full bathroom. The second floor boasts three bedrooms, all with customizable storage system closets and light-filtering/room-darkening/blackout window treatments. A second full bathroom is on this level. The third-floor bonus space provides flexibility for any additional living space you may need. A full unfinished basement offers ample storage, while the oversized detached one-car garage provides space for your vehicle and more. The private, level, fully fenced backyard ensures a secure space for outdoor activities, complete with a playscape and new "hardwood" stamped concrete for relaxation and play. With its blend of contemporary design, modern amenities, ample storage, and convenient location, it's the perfect fit.
