About this home

Discover spacious elegance in this beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home with 4,487 sq. ft. of living space on 1.3 treed acres, just north of the Merritt Parkway. Designed for entertaining, it features formal and casual areas, multiple dining options, a chef-ready kitchen with Viking appliances, and a sun-filled enclosed sunroom. The finished basement includes a flexible room with its own bath - perfect for guests, home office or recreation. Investing over $500,000, updates include a new roof, skylights, windows, A/C units, and a generator for peace of mind, plus so much more. Complete with a 2-car garage, with EV charger outlet and easy access to downtown Stamford and the train to NYC, this home is ready for your next chapter of memories.
