About this home

Welcome to coastal living at its finest in the highly sought-after Marina Bay community! This beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath condo offers approximately 1,450 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, just moments from the water, beaches, and vibrant downtown Stamford. Step inside to find a sun-filled, open-concept main level featuring a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Hardwood flooring runs throughout the home, adding warmth and continuity to every space. The dining area flows seamlessly to private outdoor space, ideal for enjoying the coastal breeze. The kitchen has been tastefully updated with new countertops, offering both style and functionality for everyday living. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, including a primary suite with ample closet space and a private bath. A standout feature is the recently renovated second-floor bathroom, showcasing modern finishes and elevated design. Convenient second-floor laundry adds to the home's practicality. Additional recent improvements include a new rooftop A/C unit and an 80-gallon hot water heater, providing efficiency, comfort, and peace of mind. Residents of Marina Bay enjoy resort-style amenities including a waterfront pool, clubhouse, and easy access to nearby beaches, marinas, parks, and transportation. This is a rare opportunity to own a move-in ready home in one of Stamford's most desirable waterfront communities.
