About this home

In the most sought after private area in Stamford, yet convenient to shopping and train, you find on a tree lined cul de sac, this secluded, charming farmhouse. Rarely available and one of the original homes in the area, the four bedroom 2819 sq. ft. home sits on .58 acres of flowering bushes, beautiful trees, and a charming gazebo. From the airy covered porch you enter the large family room with original beams and hardwood floors, showing all the character and unique features only found in the craftsmanship of yesteryear. You will move seamlessly into the high ceilinged welcoming living room and then the large dining room with its' own separate porch and entrance, great for entertaining. Down a short hall is the powder room and the bright, easy flow kitchen with updated appliances and large wooden movable island. There is a separate but attached well sized breakfast room area that opens onto a brick porch for outdoor dining. There is a nicely sized laundry/mud room with radiant and electric floors. A beautiful wooden staircase leads to the main en suite bedroom with new bath. Across the hall is the second of the four charming bedrooms. In the hall is the other full bath. There is lots of storage space and an attic. The basement has a second laundry and a charming playroom, plus more storage. But it is the location, so private but central. Add your special touches and create the home of your dreams as you make this diamond sparkle. Close to train to NYC.
