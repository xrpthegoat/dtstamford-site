About this home

Step into the revitalized legacy of the Joel Smith House, where history harmonizes with cutting-edge convenience. This captivating 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home showcases its expertly preserved historical charm, yet beneath this timeless elegance lies a commitment to modern living. A vibrant front door welcomes you into a bright and immaculate interior, where gleaming hardwood floors and discreetly integrated modern wiring and plumbing ensure seamless contemporary functionality. The spacious living room flows effortlessly into a generous dining room. A sun-drenched family room or library/office offers a tranquil retreat, wired for your modern communication needs. The heart of the home is the sizable country kitchen with updated appliances, and a cozy fireplace with a gas-fired potbelly stove. A renovated laundry room with updated systems, a fully updated bathroom featuring modern fixtures, and a sunroom bathed in natural light complete the first floor. The upper level features two charming bedrooms and a primary suite with an en-suite changing room. Peace of mind comes with a new roof and a newer gas furnace, ensuring efficient modern comfort. Discover two generously sized attic spaces for storage and a well-maintained, completely fenced in backyard . With its prime location near schools, beach and vibrant dining and shopping options, the Joel Smith House offers a unique opportunity to embrace historical charm without sacrificing any of today's essential comforts. Sold as is.
