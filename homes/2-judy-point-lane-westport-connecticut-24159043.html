About this home

Set on over 3 private acres with sweeping water views, 2 Judy Point Lane is a remarkable Westport waterfront estate offering approximately 14,000 square feet of living space and a rare compound-style layout. The property consists of two substantial residences connected by a breezeway, creating exceptional flexibility for multi-generational living, extended guests, or a private guest house. Dramatic interiors feature soaring ceilings, expansive windows, and multiple stone fireplaces that frame the natural beauty of the surrounding landscape. Generous living and entertaining spaces flow effortlessly to outdoor terraces and landscaped grounds designed for both relaxation and gathering. The property includes a waterside pool overlooking the water, a private dock for boating, and a gated driveway set among mature plantings that enhance the estate's sense of privacy. Additional amenities include the potential for an indoor pool and spa, offering an opportunity to further elevate the home's resort-like experience. A rare offering combining scale, versatility, and waterfront living in one of Westport's most sought-after coastal settings.
