About this home

This charming two-bedroom, one-bathroom house is situated on a corner lot, offering ample natural light and privacy. The property boasts a total of 1127 square feet of living space, with a lot size of approximately 0.13 acres, providing enough space for outdoor activities and gardening, highlighted by the spacious yard and patio that is a fantastic area for entertaining. This property is in a C zone and could be expanded to be a multifamily home. The house features a functional layout, with a spacious living room that flows seamlessly into the dining area, creating an open and inviting space. The bedrooms are well-proportioned and offer ample closet space. The corner lot provides additional privacy and outdoor space, perfect for relaxing or entertaining guests. The property is conveniently located near local amenities, including shops, restaurants, and parks, making it an ideal location for those seeking a convenient and comfortable lifestyle. Overall, this property offers great opportunity for those looking for a cozy and comfortable home in a convenient location that is close to the East Norwalk train station, great restaurants, and access to the Saugatuck section of Westport with another collection of great restaurants and more. There is a lot of potential in this property not only in the house but the yard. The upgrades possible for this property at this price in this location create some real investment potential into the rapidly growing and appreciating city of Norwalk.
