About this home

Looking for space, privacy, and a home that feels like a retreat? This classic 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath colonial sits on over 8 acres of peaceful land and is available for annual or extended rental. The home has tons of character with modern updates throughout - updated kitchen with newer appliances, refreshed hardwood floors, renovated bathrooms, and a light-filled family room with a beautiful solarium. There's also a partially finished lower level and laundry room with newer washer and dryer. The home has two two-zone central air/heating system and a full-house generator for peace of mind. The best part? The landlord takes care of the lawn maintenance, so you get all the beauty of the outdoors without the hassle. Whether you're relaxing at home or entertaining guests, the property offers tons of space to spread out and enjoy. It's a unique and welcoming home you won't want to miss!
