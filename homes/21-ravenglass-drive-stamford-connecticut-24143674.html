About this home

BEST & FINAL DUE BY SUN. 3-1-26 AT 7PM. 21 Ravenglass offers the rare balance of peaceful waterfront living & the amenities of the gated Windermere on the Lake community. Tucked at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, the home sits on a park-like property w/mature landscaping, blooming gardens, an enclosed vegetable garden, & a sunny yard opening to the lake & protected nature preserve-ensuring enduring privacy & tranquility. Scenic pathways lead to the community pool, tennis/pickle ball courts, clubhouse, & lakeside trails just steps away. The exterior of this exceptional Colonial is enhanced by a new Belgium block-edged driveway w/ inset lighting, creating a stunning evening approach. Inside, the sun-filled home is pristine & designed to capture beautiful lake views through oversized windows. Highlights include soaring ceilings, rich hardwood floors, & an open, elegant floor plan. The kitchen features an oversized marble island, breakfast room w/patio access, high-end appliances, & a built-in Miele coffee maker, along with a butler's pantry and custom mudroom. A light-filled office enjoys lake views, while both the great room and primary suite offer gas fireplaces. The primary suite includes a custom dressing room & spa-like bath. All bedrooms are ensuite or Jack/Jill. A generous bonus room adds flexibility, & an expansive unfinished basement & walk-up attic provide exceptional future potential. This is more than a home-it's a place to slow down, enjoy nature, & savor every sea
