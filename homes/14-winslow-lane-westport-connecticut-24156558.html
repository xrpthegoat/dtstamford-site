About this home

Custom built colonial available for one- or two-year lease starting July. Fully rebuilt in 2011, this beautiful home (with stylish, modern furnishings) rests on a manicured 0.7-acre lot in a fantastic location at the end of a small private road walking distance to vibrant downtown Westport shopping, dining, gyms, Library, and Levitt Pavilion. It boasts an open layout: 4 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 updated baths, and upstairs laundry. The main level includes an open kitchen w/ built-ins and large dining area flowing to family room with fireplace that opens to a large patio with fireplace; as well as a cozy living room with fireplace; dining room, foyer, half bath, and large butler's pantry. Upstairs rooms include: a primary suite with a vaulted ceiling, large custom walk-in closet & a luxurious spa bath. Three additional bedrooms (one more ensuite), two bedrooms, a large hall bath and laundry room complete the 2nd floor. The walk-out lower level provides a small private office, mudroom, plus gym/guest room with half bath. Owners emphasized superior construction and eco-friendly features: Energy Star rated, spray foam insulation for reduced electric costs, 24/7 heat recovery ventilator for fresh air, and whole-house water filtration on municipal supply for purer water. Offered as an annual or multi-year rental only - NOT for sale.
