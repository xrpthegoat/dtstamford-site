About this home

Set on Five Mile River with no flood insurance required! One level living with sunlight and nature views from the large windows in every room. One acre on one of West Norwalk's most sought after streets. Brick fireplace, cathedral ceiling and sliders to deck in the open concept living and dining rooms. Kitchen opens to family room with natural light coming in on three sides. Primary bedroom offers a private bath, walk-in closet and sliders to deck. There are two additonal bedrooms and a second bath. Hardwood floors throughout, wet bar and an oversized two car garage complete this home. The unfinished basement offers storage as well as options for additional living space to fill your needs - playroom, media room, gym, office or guest suite. Take advantage of this beautiful setting and make this house your own.
