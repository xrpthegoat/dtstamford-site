About this home

Welcome to one of Ledgebrook's most desirable homes, offering the rare combination of detached single-family living with the convenience of a low-maintenance condominium lifestyle. Tucked away in a private setting, this beautifully maintained home provides the space, comfort, and privacy you've been looking for. The inviting floor plan is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring sun-filled rooms, generous living spaces, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Whether you're relaxing at home or hosting family and friends, this home offers the perfect balance of warmth and functionality. Enjoy the convenience of a covered parking garage, along with abundant guest parking conveniently located throughout the community. Ledgebrook is one of Norwalk's premier condominium communities, renowned for its beautifully landscaped grounds, scenic walking trails, tranquil aerated lake with its charming footbridge, swimming pool, and well-equipped fitness center. Residents enjoy a true neighborhood atmosphere where neighbors gather, stroll the picturesque grounds, and appreciate the peaceful surroundings. Ideally located, Ledgebrook offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience, just minutes from shopping, restaurants, public transportation, major highways, Norwalk Hospital, and Oak Hills Park Golf Course.This is a rare opportunity to enjoy the privacy and feel of a detached single-family home while embracing the carefree, low-maintenance lifestyle of condo living.
