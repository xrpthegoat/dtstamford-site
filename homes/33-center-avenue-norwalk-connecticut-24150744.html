About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated and meticulously maintained half-duplex rental offering 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths. Freshly painted throughout, this home features refinished hardwood floors, a newer gas furnace and tankless water heater system (2024), and a spacious eat-in kitchen appointed with granite countertops, new gas range, tile backsplash, and a convenient half bath. The sun-filled living room showcases large windows and an inviting layout. The upper level offers a generous primary bedroom, two well-proportioned bedrooms, and a full bath. Enjoy a fully fenced property with ample parking, providing both privacy and convenience. An exceptional opportunity for tenants seeking comfort, quality, and thoughtful upgrades. Convenient location to local restaurants, stores, and easy access to Route 7, Merritt Parkway and I-95. Please schedule your private viewing before it's gone!
