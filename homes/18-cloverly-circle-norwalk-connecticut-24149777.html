About this home

Nestled at the end of a tranquil cul-de-sac in the sought-after East Norwalk area near Shorehaven, this meticulously maintained 4-bedroom home captivates with its unique blend of privacy, charm, and accessibility. The interiors display a warm and sophisticated color palette, seamlessly marrying timeless character with modern comforts. Embrace the essence of coastal living with convenient access to pristine beaches just 1.3 miles away, the East Norwalk train station at 0.6 miles, as well as the vibrant dining scene of East Norwalk, featuring beloved spots like Harbor Lights, Knot Norms, and Frosty's, alongside the bustling shops and nightlife of SoNo, a mere 1.3 miles distance. As new walking destinations and ongoing developments enrich the neighborhood, the allure of this location continues to rise. The main level unfolds a thoughtfully designed layout, featuring an open concept kitchen with an elegant dining room highlighting a gas fireplace, a warm family room, and a spacious mudroom complete with laundry facilities, a half bath, and direct access to the 2-car garage. 2 offices for the perfect work at home environment. A large deck, accessible from both the kitchen and family room, provides an ideal space for entertaining and enjoying outdoor gatherings. On the second floor, you'll discover a generous primary suite boasting a full bath, amazing walk-in closet, and nursery. You will find an additional three bedrooms and another full bath.
