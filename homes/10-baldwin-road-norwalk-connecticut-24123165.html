About this home

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac and set on a beautifully landscaped .61-acre corner oversized lot, this sun-filled 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch combines comfort, character, & convenience. Just minutes from schools, shopping, dining, and the best of both Norwalk & Westport, the location truly can't be beat. Step inside to discover a bright, open layout that offers both comfort & ease of living. The spacious family room flows effortlessly into the eat-in kitchen and dining area, ideal for both casual gatherings & entertaining. The updated kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, & ceiling-height cabinetry, while refinished hardwood floors, fresh paint, & new lighting create a crisp, inviting vibe throughout. The private primary offers a peaceful retreat with its own en-suite bath, while 2 additional bedrooms share a full hall bath. A wide hallway, newer furnace, updated electric panel, & Nest system provide comfort & efficiency. The expansive lower level adds the opportunity for ~1,700 sq ft of add'l finished space, perfect for a playroom, gym, media area, or home office. Outside, enjoy morning coffee on the covered porch or host friends in the level backyard framed by mature plantings. Whether you're entertaining, gardening, or simply relaxing, this outdoor space makes everyday feel special. A rare find in a prime location.
