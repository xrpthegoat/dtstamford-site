About this home

Expected for completion in mid-spring 2025, this new construction by the esteemed Johnny Schwartz of Sam Development is situated on a highly sought-after cul-de-sac in Long Lots. This 5/6 bedroom, 6.5-bath residence will combine thoughtful design, exceptional craftsmanship, & modern luxury across four beautifully finished floors. This open-concept home is designed to impress, with generously proportioned living spaces perfect for today's lifestyle. Expansive windows will bathe the interiors in natural light, & premium finishes have been carefully selected to create an environment of refined elegance. The chef's kitchen will feature Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry, & a large center island, flowing seamlessly into the living and dining areas. The walk-out lower level will offer flexible spaces for recreation, fitness, or entertaining, while the upper floors will provide private retreats for rest and relaxation. The primary suite will be a haven of luxury, complete with a spa-like bath & custom walk-in closet. Step outside to your private oasis, where a 15 x 30 pool will serve as the centerpiece of a backyard designed for relaxation & play. Whether enjoying quiet mornings on the patio or hosting summer gatherings, this outdoor space will elevate your lifestyle. Located in the desirable Long Lots neighborhood, 6 Morningside Lane offers proximity to Westport's top-rated schools, vibrant downtown area, stunning beaches, and all the amenities that make this town special.
