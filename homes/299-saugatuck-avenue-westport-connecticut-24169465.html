About this home

*THANK YOU FOR YOUR INTEREST, SELLER REQUESTS HIGHEST/BEST OFFERS BY 3PM MONDAY 6/1/26*Set on over an acre of park-like, usable property, this stunning Modern home with an oversized gunite pool is a showcase of exhilarating design. In an area known for major watefront properties as well as immediate convenience to the railroad station and all commuting routes, this home blends sophisticated elegance with an easy lifestyle. A gracious, landscaped approach brings you to the grand double-door entrance lined with windows and into a vaulted ceiling foyer. The interior captures gorgeous light all day through walls of glass & numerous skylights. The floor plan is an entertainer's dream, accommodating groups large or small in refined surroundings. The living and family rooms each have floor-to-ceiling stone fireplaces and glass doors to the rear deck. The DR has a picture-perfect view across the property. Gourmet E-I-K w/Wolf gas cooktop, Sub Zero ref/frz, Viking double ovens, pantry, center island w/seating & a breakfast area enhanced by scenic views. An adjacent sunroom transitions the space out to the deck. Two half baths complete this level. The airy atmosphere continues up front & rear stairs to the 2nd level, beginning w/a grand primary suite w/priv sitting rm, king-size BR w/pool view balcony, vast dressing rm & two lux baths. Three addl' BRs have double clsts & two full baths. Fin LL w/rec rm, office, storage. Enjoy a porch that leads to the pool/spa. 3 car garage, generator.
