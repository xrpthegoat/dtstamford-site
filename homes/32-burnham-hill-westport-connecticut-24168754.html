About this home

Experience refined coastal living in this exceptional hilltop residence, just a short walk to Compo Beach. Built by celebrated Bluewater Home Builders, this stunning Nantucket-style home set on a rare & secluded .92-acre property on a quiet cul-de-sac is ideally positioned on one of the area's most prestigious & sought-after streets, offering an unparalleled blend of privacy & lifestyle. This home is surrounded by beautiful landscaped grounds and features a gunite pool, spa & water views of Mill Pond. Spanning 4 finished levels, the home offers 6 bedrooms, 7 baths, including 2 home offices-perfect for today's lifestyle. The grand foyer leads to the Dining Room with Butler's Pantry and the Living Room with fireplace. At the heart of the home is the Gourmet Kitchen with upscale appliances and an open floor plan to the Family Room. There is a screened in 3 season porch off the kitchen. On the second floor the luxurious Primary Suite features its own private office and a 2nd floor balcony with water views. 3 additional bedrooms are ensuite. A private third floor offers a bedroom, full bath and balcony ideal for guests, family or a secluded work space. The finished above ground walk-out lower level to the pool/patio is designed for both relaxation and fun, complete with a Playroom, Exercise Room, Bedroom, Full Bath, Wine Cellar, second laundry area and extensive storage. There is a full house generator. NO flood insurance is needed. Minutes to train station. Vacation year round.
