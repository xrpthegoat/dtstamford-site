About this home

This is the one you've been waiting for! Warm, inviting and lovingly maintained classic Colonial on Keelers Ridge, a quiet, walkable neighborhood. Custom built by Kellogg Brothers with exceptional craftsmanship, this home features crown moldings, paneling, built-ins, and soaring ceilings. Graciously sized main rooms are ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The bright sunroom offers cathedral ceilings, skylights, and views of the large deck, stone fire-pit and level backyard. The spacious eat-in kitchen complete with a large center island, gas cooktop, wall ovens, abundant cabinetry serves as the heart of the home. It opens to a lovely family room with cathedral ceilings, beams, floor-to-ceiling fireplace, built-ins and a charming window seat. There's a cozy study/library for those work from home days. An expansive great room with loft provides flexible space for recreation, media, or play. Upstairs, the primary suite features high ceilings, fireplace, walk-in closet, and an updated bath. Three additional bedrooms with hardwood floors share an updated hall bath with double sinks. Enjoy 2 private acres abutting the 60 acre Quarry Head Park with scenic hiking trails. A beautifully crafted home offering timeless elegance, privacy and exceptional space in a premier neighborhood.
