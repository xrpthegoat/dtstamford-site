About this home

Boater's Paradise-Live the Ultimate Saugatuck Shores Waterfront Lifestyle. Experience unparalleled, direct waterfront access from this striking contemporary perched directly on the shores of Long Island Sound. Featuring its own rare private dock, this property is a true boater's/water lover's paradise. Enjoy all that Saugatuck Shores has to offer-boating, jet-skiing, paddle boarding, or kayaking from your own backyard. Dramatic walls of glass bathe the interior with natural light throughout effortlessly blending modern architectural lines with unparalleled, panoramic views of Bermuda Lagoon, Peck Ledge Lighthouse and boating channels which can be seen from almost every room. The open-concept main level transitions seamlessly to a massive sundeck, setting the ultimate stage for indoor-outdoor entertaining. Spend summers relaxing by your private waterfront gunite pool and spa, rinse off in your outdoor shower or unwind in the custom indoor sauna. Positioned within the coveted, private Covlee Beach Association, residents enjoy access to a private sandy beach with additional dock slips, kayak and paddleboard storage, a playground, picnic area, and fire pit just steps away, blending complete privacy with an active coastal community. From the Great Room with its dramatic fireplace and vaulted ceiling, to the custom Kitchen with fireplace, to the cozy Family Room and Study, there are so many spaces to delight. All bedrooms, including primary suite, have decks overlooking the Sound.
