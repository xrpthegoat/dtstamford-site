About this home

Tucked along one of Rowayton's sweetest and most peaceful streets, 9 Flicker Lane is a brand-new home designed for real life. Step inside through the front door or slip in through the mudroom off the two-car garage, where daily comings and goings feel effortless. The main level balances refinement with comfort. A proper dining room and living room for when the occasion calls, alongside an easygoing family room that spills into the breakfast area and kitchen, complete with an oversized island and generous pantry. A private study and two powder rooms round out the floor. Upstairs, the primary suite feels like its own retreat, with a sitting area, dual walk-in closets, and a spacious bath designed to unwind in. Three additional bedrooms each enjoy their own en suite. The third floor offers a tucked-away bedroom/bath and play space, while the lower level is thoughtfully laid out to accommodate two separate rooms, ideal for a gym and media room, or whatever suits your needs. Outside, a newly built pool, wide patio with outdoor fireplace, and ample yard create a setting made for summer afternoons and evening gatherings. A rare find in Rowayton - a beautifully built home with real space to live, a pool to enjoy, and outdoor space to match.
