About this home

Just 383 steps from the sands of Compo Beach, this storied 1920s residence claims one of Westport's most sought-after addresses. Approximately 3,255 square feet hold five bedrooms and four baths. An open, updated kitchen with top-tier appliances flows into the living and gathering areas. The primary suite is a generous retreat with vaulted ceilings, a spa bath, and a walk-in closet. Four additional bedrooms are ample in scale - one en-suite, with a Jack-and-Jill bath connecting two others - while a full bath and a dedicated office complete the first level. Outdoors, an expansive terrace invites al fresco dining and entertaining above a manicured, secluded yard set on a coveted corner lot. Beyond the door: sunrise strolls to the water, sunsets over the boardwalk, Longshore Club, downtown shops, dining, and the library, plus a straightforward Manhattan commute via Saugatuck Metro-North. Homes in this enclave seldom surface.
