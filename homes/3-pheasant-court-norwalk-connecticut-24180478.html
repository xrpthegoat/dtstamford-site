Located at the end of a coveted cul-de-sac in one of Norwalk's most beloved neighborhoods, where the warmth of a close knit neighborhood meets the serenity of a private retreat. Beautifully updated 3 bedroom, 2 full bath & 2 1/2 bath ranch offers 2,790 sq ft of single level living on .60 acres. Step inside to a bright foyer that flows into a living room anchored by a wood burning fireplace and elegant dining room beyond. The updated kitchen features granite countertops and new appliances, opening seamlessly to the family room where floor-to-ceiling windows frame sweeping river views and natural light pours in. Step out to the freshly painted oversized deck, an entertainer's paradise with multiple lounging areas and a beautifully integrated heated pool. Enjoy summer days poolside in your own private backyard oasis, with an outdoor half bath and pool storage just steps away. The primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, a double walk-in closet, private en suite bath, and deck access, perfect for morning coffee outdoors. The grounds overlook the river, with the Darien Land Trust preserve bordering the rear, ensuring the natural beauty and privacy of this property is preserved forever. Brand new roof.
