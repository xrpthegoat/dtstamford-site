About this home

Beautifully renovated, spacious, and sun-filled condo located in the heart of downtown. Rich dark engineered wood floors run throughout, complementing a stylish kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, white cabinetry, black granite countertops, and a convenient breakfast bar. The expansive living area offers endless possibilities for entertaining, along with a perfect nook for a home office setup. Sliding doors lead to a private balcony-an ideal spot to unwind and enjoy city views. The generously sized primary bedroom includes two large closets, while the updated full bathroom offers dual access from both the bedroom and the foyer. A foyer closet conveniently houses the in-unit washer and dryer. Notable upgrades include engineered wood flooring (2016), a new heating and air conditioning system (2017), a kitchen renovation along with new bathroom cabinetry (2020), new washer and dryer (2024). Residents enjoy exceptional amenities, including a penthouse-level fitness center, lounge, and a glass-enclosed heated pool for year-round enjoyment. A third-floor outdoor patio provides space for grilling, relaxing, or spending time with your pet. With a full-time concierge, assigned parking, and all your favorite shops, dining, and entertainment just steps away, this condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and city living.
