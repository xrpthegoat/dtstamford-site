About this home

This charming 2-family 1920's Colonial home is located in the East Norwalk beach community. The first floor, owner occupied, unit features a large one bed, one bath unit with finished basement and a large deck looking out your spacious backyard. Upstairs unit has two bed one bath and large kitchen. Outside, enjoy a private backyard oasis surrounded by beautiful landscaping, ideal for morning coffee or weekend gatherings. Conveniently located within a short walk to the harbor, beach, dog park or the restaurants and shops of South Norwalk. Experience the coastal lifestyle of East Norwalk!
