About this home

Now under construction with a summer move-in, this exceptional 6-BDRM SIGNATURE Home is set on a quiet cul-de-sac in Westport's sought-after Long Lots school district. Built by SIR Development-renowned for over 30 years and 300+ homes of exceptional craftsmanship-this residence seamlessly blends timeless design with modern luxury. A welcoming interior showcases 10-foot ceilings on the main level, highlighted by custom oak beam detailing and expansive windows that fill the home with natural light. The thoughtfully designed open floor plan is ideal for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The chef's kitchen features custom SIR cabinetry, premium Thermador appliances, and a fully appointed larder with wet bar, wine cooler, and floor-to-ceiling storage-perfectly tailored for both function and style. The living room opens through sliding glass doors to a granite patio and spacious, level backyard, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor connection. Add'l main-level highlights include a private office with custom built-ins, formal LR, and a well-designed mudroom for everyday convenience. Upstairs, 5 generously sized BDRMs include a luxurious primary suite w/ a vaulted, beamed ceiling, fireplace, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath designed for relaxation. The finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional versatility, complete w/ 6th BDRM, full bath, media room, gym, and custom built-ins w/ref drawers-ideal for guests,recreation, or extended living.
