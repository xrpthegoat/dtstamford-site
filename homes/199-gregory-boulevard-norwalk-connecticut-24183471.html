About this home

Welcome to the waterfront community at Dorlon Terrace. This spacious, light-filled, and updated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse-style condo w/Garage is situated in the desirable Dorlon Terrace complex, in the East Norwalk beach area on the picturesque Gregory Point peninsula. As you enter this unit, you'll immediately be greeted by stunning water views and a welcoming foyer. The unit features a beautiful eat-in kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances. The living and dining rooms boast double sliders that lead to a private patio overlooking Charles Creek. Completing the first floor is an updated half bath, hallway laundry, and interior and water-side garage access. On the second floor, you'll find a spacious primary bedroom suite with an ensuite full bath, a walk-in closet, and sliders opening to a private double balcony offering views of Long Island Sound. The second bedroom also includes a walk-in closet and sliders with views of the Sound, a third bedroom with a walk-in closet. An updated full bath completes the upper level, along with a full attic for additional storage accessed via pull-down stairs. Residents can enjoy a private marina and pool house, available for rental for gatherings, or take a dip in the in-ground heated pool on the waterfront. Boat slip rental may be available at additional cost.
