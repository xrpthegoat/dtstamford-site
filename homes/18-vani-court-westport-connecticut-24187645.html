About this home

*Please submit all offers by Monday, 7/6 at 5pm. Compo Beach Living, Reimagined. At the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, where neighbors gather, ride bikes until dusk, and a hidden footpath connects you to the very best of Westport, you'll find a home that captures everything people love about Compo Beach living. Built in 2021 and thoughtfully designed for modern life, this custom home offers the rare combination of newer construction, timeless style, and a location that invites you to leave the car behind. Walk to the train in minutes, stroll to Saugatuck's celebrated restaurants for dinner, spend afternoons at Longshore or Compo Beach, and end the day back on one of Westport's friendliest streets. Inside, four finished levels provide the flexibility today's lifestyle demands. Bright, open living spaces flow effortlessly for everyday living and entertaining, while the finished lower level creates the perfect setting for movie nights, workouts, or play. Outside, the level, fully fenced backyard is surprisingly private, framed by mature greenery and designed for effortless enjoyment. More than anything, this is a home defined by its setting. A neighborhood where people know one another, where impromptu gatherings replace scheduled meets, and where convenience becomes part of everyday life. Beautifully built. Exceptionally located. Ready for its next chapter.
