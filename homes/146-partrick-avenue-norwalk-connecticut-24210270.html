About this home

*** Due to the positive response we have received, Sellers have asked that all interested Buyers please submit their highest and best offers in writing by Monday, October 5th, 5pm. No Escalation Clauses.*** Step into extraordinary living at 146 Partrick, where timeless tradition meets luxury resort-inspired living. Located in Norwalk's coveted Cranbury neighborhood near the Westport line, this impressive 5 bedroom, 4 full bath home finished on 3 levels spans over 4500SqFt blending generous scale w/ everyday comfort. Dramatic two-story foyer welcomes you home, setting the stage w/ soaring ceilings and walls of windows that flood the interior w/ natural light. The open chef's kitchen shines w/ new state-of-the-art appliances and flows seamlessly into the dining and gathering rooms, perfect for modern-day living. Popular floor plan offers 2 home offices and main-level bedroom suite perfect for multi-generational living or guests. Upstairs, 4 additional ensuite bedrooms await, including the primary suite designed for comfort and privacy. Step outside to a secluded, level backyard with heated in-ground pool, stone terraces, and a deck perfect for barbecues and alfresco dining under the stars. Charming covered poolside lounge offers shaded tranquility by day and romance by night. Effortless access to Westport and Norwalk's downtowns, premier shopping, dining, and vibrant beaches, plus both Metro-North stations for easy NYC commute.
