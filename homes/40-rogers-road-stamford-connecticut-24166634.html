About this home

Tucked within Stamford's coveted Shippan Point, 40 Rogers Road presents a refined coastal offering just moments from the water. Thoughtfully reimagined, the residence blends timeless character with a comprehensive suite of recent upgrades, delivering a turnkey living experience. Sun-filled interiors offer well-proportioned spaces with seamless flow for everyday living and entertaining. The second floor is anchored by a private primary suite, creating a peaceful retreat. The updated kitchen features new countertops, backsplash, sink, hardware, and faucet, along with upgraded appliances and bar amenities. Recent improvements include freshly painted interiors, refinished hardwood floors, new lighting and electrical finishes, and a gas fireplace insert. Major system upgrades include new furnaces, a tankless hot water system, enhanced HVAC, WiFi thermostats, updated drain lines, chimney repointing with a custom copper cap, blown-in insulation, and new gutters. Outdoors, a private, resort-like setting features a two-bedroom, two-bath pool house with sauna, a newly replastered pool with LED lighting, and refreshed landscaping. A new main roof (2025) underscores the home's exceptional upkeep. Moments from beaches, marinas, and downtown Stamford, this is coastal living at its finest. Across the street from your private deeded beach.
