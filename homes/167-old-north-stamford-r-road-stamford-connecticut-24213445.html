About this home

Welcome to this charming 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home, boasting a stunning new kitchen, sun-filled rooms, and an expansive deck perfect for gatherings. On the main floor, a cozy living room with a wood-burning fireplace and built-ins invites relaxation, while the rear of the house offers a versatile space ideal for play or morning sunshine. There are two bedrooms upstairs and two on the main floor, including the serene primary suite. The lower level easily adapts to your lifestyle needs as a play room/ art studio/ home gym/ etc. Enjoy the unbeatable location, just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro North, and major highways, making daily errands a breeze by foot or bike. Nestled in a coveted cul-de-sac setting, this home also features a large 2-car garage, ample storage, and a pet-friendly policy (insurable breeds only). Excellent credit and proof of income is a must!
