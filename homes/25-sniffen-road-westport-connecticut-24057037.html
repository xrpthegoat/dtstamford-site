About this home

Presenting an exceptional new construction by Cypress Development, this luxurious home on a private corner lot epitomizes sophisticated contemporary living. With 6 bedrooms and 7 bathrooms across over 7,000 sq ft, this residence offers a perfect blend of elegance and comfort. Enter through the impressive 2-story foyer to discover sunlit interiors with 10ft ceilings, hardwood floors, and an open floor plan. The gourmet kitchen, equipped with high-end Sub Zero appliances and 2 lrg islands w waterfall edges, seamlessly flows into the great room, dining room, and living room, creating an ideal space for entertaining. Multiple fireplaces add warmth and charm throughout. A wall of glass opens to the expansive backyard with room for a pool and spa, offering a serene retreat. The second floor features a luxurious primary suite with vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, his and hers walk-in closets, and a spa-like bathroom. Four additional bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and a convenient laundry room complete this level. The third floor provides a spacious entertainment room, an additional bedroom with a full bath, and a home office. The lower level is designed for ultimate recreation and relaxation, featuring a game room, home gym, wine cellar, sauna or additional room, and full bath. Perfectly situated just 5 minutes from Westport's vibrant town center, train station, beaches, and top-rated schools, this home represents a rare opportunity to enjoy modern luxury living in a prime Westport location.
