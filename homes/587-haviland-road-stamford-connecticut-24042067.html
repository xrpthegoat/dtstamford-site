About this home

Character and charm fill this impeccably updated antique farmhouse. The beautifully landscaped, 1.51 level acres boasts a sparkling inground pool and expansive rear yard allowing for the ideal entertaining space, multiple play areas and idyllic unwinding space. The upper level of the home consists of an en-suite primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms with a shared hall bath. The main level flows perfectly and is appointed with a cozy living room/ library, light-filled family room/ dining area, updated kitchen, office equipped with pocket doors for privacy, and a flexible main level, en-suite bedroom with a separate entrance. A true North Stamford oasis yet still convenient to amenities and the Merritt Parkway. This is the home and setting which will help you create a lifetime of wonderful memories.
