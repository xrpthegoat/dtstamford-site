About this home

Tucked behind a gated entry in Old Hill, this stunning 5 bedroom, 6.2 bath home offers a rare blend of elegance, privacy and natural beauty. The property has multiple serene water features-a heated saltwater gunite pool with hot tub, a tranquil private pond with fountain and picturesque Stony Brook. The stately stone and wood exterior blends seamlessly with the lush surroundings, while inside, an expansive, light-filled layout is ideal both for grand entertaining and quiet retreat. A dramatic wall of windows captures sweeping views of the brook, creating a seamless connection between indoor comfort and the surrounding landscape. The ground floor consists of a gourmet kitchen, large family room with fireplace, dining room with wet bar, living room with fireplace + lounge area with water views, a large entry foyer with stone floors and two powder rooms. Upstairs, a primary bedroom suite with two balconies overlooking the pond and brook, two walk in closets & beautifully appointed bathroom, 4 additional bedrooms each with its own bathroom, a large home office/6th bedroom with fireplace and brook & pond views, a large playroom with full bathroom, windowed home gym, and another family room on the 3rd floor, offering multiple options for relaxation and entertainment. Fully fenced for privacy with automated driveway gates, it has a heated 3 car garage, Crestron lighting system, full house generator, mature landscaping, and a gas grill seamlessly built into the stonework by the pool.
