About this home

A rare opportunity to own an exceptional woodland estate, where space, serenity, and natural beauty converge. Set beyond a long, gracefully winding driveway, this expansive residence is surrounded by mature trees, creating a remarkable sense of arrival and seclusion. Thoughtfully suited for multigenerational living, the home features a separate wing offering flexibility for extended family, an in-law suite, or long-term guests, allowing for both privacy and connection under one roof. Generous living spaces provide an ideal setting for sophisticated entertaining and relaxed everyday living, while tranquil woodland views create a peaceful backdrop throughout the seasons. Outdoors, a stunning natural gunite pool offers a private, resort-like oasis, beautifully integrated into the wooded landscape and perfect for summer entertaining and relaxation. Combining versatile living arrangements, exceptional space, and a truly serene setting, this distinctive property offers a rare opportunity to experience the best of private estate living. Pre-inspection items have been completed and repaired, providing added peace of mind for the next owner.
