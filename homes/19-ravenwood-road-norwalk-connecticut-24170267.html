About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST by 12 noon Tuesday! Perfectly situated on the Darien and New Canaan borders, this home is nestled within a highly sought-after neighborhood known for its mature trees, established landscaping, and strong property values-offering both charm and exceptional value. Set on a picturesque, level half-acre on a quiet dead end street, the property is ideal for outdoor living, entertaining, or simply enjoying the peaceful setting. Inside, the home features a light-filled interior with hardwood floors throughout, complemented by updated kitchen and baths -including one in the primary suite - and a clean, modern aesthetic. A sun-drenched sunroom overlooks the private, beautifully landscaped backyard-creating a perfect space to relax and enjoy year-round. Designed with flexibility in mind, the home offers a spacious family room and a full lower level (including the sunroom, it is an additional approx. 1000 SQ ft of usable space), providing ample room for living, working, or recreation. Major system updates add peace of mind, including a whole-house Generac generator, Weil-McLain furnace, Andersen windows, and Connecticut Basement waterproofing system. Conveniently located just minutes to train stations, major highways, shopping, dining, and recreation-this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and timeless appeal.
