About this home

Welcome to 7 Mortar Rock, a thoughtfully designed living space. This stunning 6 bedroom transitional modern farmhouse showcases the exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail that have made the Thomas Brothers Builders highly sought after. Perfectly blending beauty and functionality, this home is tailored to today's lifestyle while delivering timeless appeal. At the heart of the home is a spectacular chefs kitchen featuring quartz countertops and a large island with seating for four and an expansive dining area ideal for everyday living and entertaining. Sun filled spaces with nearly 10 foot ceilings on the main level creates and airy welcoming atmosphere throughout the home. Patio doors off the kitchen open seamlessly to a beautifully landscaped backyard with stone patio and built in gas fire pit. Ideally situated on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Westport's most desirable locations this property offers the rare convenience of being just minutes from downtown shopping, dining, commuting options and Compo Beach.
