About this home

Welcome to this charming two-family home in the desirable Wolf Pit section of Norwalk! This property features a 2-bedroom unit and a 3-bedroom unit, each with one bath. The spacious upstairs layout provides ample room. Upon entering you will find your own laundry/mudroom which leads in to the large kitchen plenty of room for an eat in, but if not your spacious living and dining area are just perfect. You have one bed off the living room and two more upstairs. making it perfect for tenants or multi-generational living. Downstairs cozy 2 bed also recently updated with access to basement. Both units have laundry and ample parking. Located in a quiet, friendly neighborhood with easy access to shopping, schools, and parks, this home offers great potential for investors or those looking for a comfortable living arrangement. Don't miss the opportunity to make this house your own!
