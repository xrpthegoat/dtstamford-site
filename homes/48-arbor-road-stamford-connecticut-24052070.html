About this home

There's a reason why this house has only had two previous owners. It's an absolute gem! And, it's as neat as a pin! Meticulously maintained by the current owners for the past 52 years, the pride of ownership is palpable as soon as you arrive to the front door. With approx. 75 feet of living space from one end of the house to the other, this house is the one you have been waiting for. A terrific ranch style layout with wide hallways, rich hardwood floors, and generous sized rooms flooded in natural light will surely please the next home owner who wants to put their stamp on this home. At one end of the house there is an eat-in kitchen, a open plan dining and family room with fireplace and wall to wall windows, a living room with wet bar, a half bath and mud room, while 4 large bedrooms and 2 full baths are at the other end. Situated on an acre plus property with a private back yard, this house features a 2 car attached garage (brings you straight in to the house. No lugging groceries from the basement), a generator, and an irrigation system. And, all of this in a desirable lower North Stamford neighborhood, convenient to shopping, dining and Merritt P/Way. Don't miss this one! Being sold AS-IS.
