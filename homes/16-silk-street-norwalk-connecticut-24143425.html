About this home

Welcome to 16 Silk Street - a beautifully updated and move-in-ready rental available immediately. This home features a fully renovated interior (2018) with a bright, open main level offering a spacious living area, modern kitchen, two comfortable bedrooms, and a stylish full bath. The primary suite occupies the entire second floor and feels like a private retreat, complete with a generous bedroom, walk-in closet, additional storage space, and a brand-new shower just installed in the full ensuite bathroom. A finished lower level adds fantastic bonus space-perfect for a playroom, home office, gym, or media room-and includes a convenient half bath. Additional features include hardwood floors throughout, 2-zone central air, recessed lighting, and replacement windows. Outside, enjoy vinyl siding, a new deck, a partially fenced level backyard with a storage shed, and a one-car garage with an oversized driveway. The location offers excellent convenience with quick access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, plus Metro-North less than 10 minutes away. Available immediately, don't miss this fantastic rental in a prime Norwalk location.
