About this home

Set along the picturesque Five Mile River with its own private beach and dock, this exceptional waterfront home captures sweeping views of the Long Island Sound and unforgettable sunsets from multiple outdoor living spaces, including a terrace with a gas fireplace and two expansive decks. Located in the heart of the sought-after Rowayton Beach Association, the residence offers a bright, open floor plan with water views from nearly every room. Featuring four bedrooms and two-and-a-half baths, including a convenient first-floor primary suite, the home is in wonderful condition. Ideal for those looking to downsize without compromise, with the flexibility to expand for a larger lifestyle-all just a short walk to downtown Rowayton and its charming amenities.
