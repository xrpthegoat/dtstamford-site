About this home

This architectural gem will captivate with its luxurious, modern design and impress with its meticulous, energy-efficient construction. The award-winning CMG BUILDER of New Canaan CT will craft this home to the German Passive House Institute (PHI) standard, resulting in a visually stunning home that's 80 percent more energy efficient than a typical, code-built residence. Thoughtfully designed to fuse form and function, the expansive living spaces, clean lines, and exquisite modern finishes deliver next level sophistication. South-facing walls of glass will bathe the living space in natural light, while framing breathtaking views that shift with the seasons. Designed for superior air quality and wellness, other key features include strategic shading, airtight construction,and an efficient whole-house energy recovery ventilation system. The absence of fossil fuels, along with highly efficient European windows and doors, enhances energy savings and creates a remarkably quiet and serene living environment. Nestled on a private 2-acre lot on a North Stamford cul-de-sac, this idyllic setting offers a peaceful escape that's within reach of urban conveniences. Since this home is "to be built" you have the opportunity to collaborate with the builder to select finishes, ensuring a home that's as extraordinary as you are. Your dream home awaits.
