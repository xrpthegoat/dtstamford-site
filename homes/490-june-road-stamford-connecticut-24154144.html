About this home

Welcome to this North Stamford retreat, gracefully set on 2.34 flat acres along the Greenwich border. Here, privacy and comfort meet refined updates in a home that feels expansive without being oversized. Thoughtfully designed with four bedrooms - including a desirable first-floor primary suite with coffered ceiling - the layout offers both flexibility and ease. The updated chef's kitchen opens to sunlit dining and living spaces highlighted by vaulted ceilings and exceptional natural light. Upstairs, a second potential primary suite and two additional bedrooms. A finished lower level adds an additional 1800 square feet and includes a gym, playroom, game room, wine cellar and extensive storage. Recent upgrades include a beautifully reimagined primary bath, new windows, and whole-property high-speed WiFi - seamlessly extending indoors and out. This home is an entertainer's dream! A striking pebble-finished gunite pool and spa, outdoor shower, professional landscaping and three distinct bluestone terraces create a resort-like feel. A home like this doesn't come along often!
