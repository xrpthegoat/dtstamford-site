About this home

Charming one bedroom, one bath cottage yearly furnished rental in the Old Hill neighborhood of Westport. This stand alone cottage has lovely natural light with hardwood floors, high ceilings & a full kitchen with a breakfast bar. The primary bedroom has a walk in closet & hardwood floors. There is a stackable washer/dryer laundry in the full bath. The deck & additional patio offer lovely outdoor space. The tenant has access to one bay of the garage. The landscaping, snow removal & trash collection are included in the rent. Move right in & enjoy all that Westport has to offer.
