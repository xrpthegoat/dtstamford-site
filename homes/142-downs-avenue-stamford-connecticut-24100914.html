About this home

Nestled in the highly sought after waterfront community of Shippan Point,142 Downs will make you truly feel like you are living the coastal lifestyle. This beautifully maintained home features 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, an open floor plan filled with natural light and stunning water views from every front window. The living and family rooms are connected by an elegant double-sided gas fireplace, creating a cozy and inviting atmosphere. A large 4th Bedroom Suite (currently a gym) with the option for a private entrance is a space with limitless potential to suit your needs. The generous deck overlooking a fenced backyard is an ideal setting for entertaining or relaxing amid lush rose bushes, hydrangeas, and gently swaying seagrass. A spacious and recently upgraded primary suite with a luxurious marble bathroom is your private retreat offering breathtaking water views and even Manhattan skyline in the distance. Private deeded beach access is steps from your door (no HOA). The perfect spot to launch your paddleboard or kayak, relax, and enjoy stunning sunsets. Shippan Point offers miles of quiet roads ideal for enjoying spectacular vistas of Long Island Sound. Just minutes from vibrant downtown Stamford, residents enjoy a wealth of shopping, dining, entertainment, city parks, marinas and beaches. A 5 minute drive to Stamford train station makes New York City reachable in under an hour. This exquisite home seamlessly combines beachside tranquility with elegant coastal living.
