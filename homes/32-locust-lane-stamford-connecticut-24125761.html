About this home

Tucked Away Yet Close to It All! Set on a quiet mid-city cul-de-sac, East-facing, this sun-filled and impeccably maintained home offers the perfect balance of comfort, space, and convenience. A bright living room with a cozy fireplace opens to the dining area, ideal for everyday living or entertaining. The inviting eat-in kitchen overlooks nearly a half-acre of beautifully landscaped, level, and fully fenced grounds surrounded by mature trees and flowering plantings offering a comfortable and functional space for daily living. Featuring four bedrooms and three renovated full baths, this home offers flexibility for every lifestyle. The fully finished walkout lower level includes a full bath, partial kitchen, and private entrance, perfect for guests, an in-law suite, or a home office. Features include hardwood floors throughout, central air, and thoughtful updates over the years, including a new roof and water heater. The septic system is sized for five bedrooms, providing peace of mind and room for future flexibility. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, and commuter routes including I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, a rare find that feels like home the moment you arrive.
