About this home

Welcome to 6 Cook Street, an exquisite residence designed by Beinfield Architecture and newly constructed in 2022. Just steps away from Rowayton's community dock, this home perfectly blends modern aesthetics with luxurious amenities, making it an ideal place to call home. Upon entering, you're greeted by an abundance of natural light and soaring 9-foot ceilings. To the left, a versatile space serves as a cozy office or sitting room, followed by a stylish half bath. The open-layout kitchen and living room are a highlight, featuring white oak floors, beams, and ceilings with unique steel detailing. Anchoring the kitchen is a Wolf oven, flanked by a built-in fridge and freezer. The adjacent butler's pantry offers ample cabinetry and an oversized steel sink. The first floor is completed by a convenient laundry room with an additional sink. Ascend the steel and white oak floating stairs to the second floor, where you'll find an ensuite secondary bedroom, two additional bedrooms sharing a Jack and Jill bath, and a generous primary suite. The primary suite boasts a large walk-in closet and a lush bathroom with a freestanding tub and shower. The third floor offers a fifth bedroom, a full bath, and a spacious bonus room perfect for various uses. Outside, a bluestone back patio and a one-car garage round out this impressive property. With the beach and Rowayton Seafood just moments away, 6 Cook Street offers an unparalleled living experience.
