About this home

Yearly Unfurnished Waterfront Rental in Westport, CT. Commanding a rare, full-frontage position on the shimmering setting of Nash's Pond, 27 Kings Highway North offers an extraordinary opportunity to lease a Mediterranean-style home in the heart of Westport, Connecticut. Set on 1.2 private acres, this striking residence delivers serene water views, refined architectural details, and a coveted in-town location. Spanning over 5,300 square feet, the home features four spacious bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bath, offering exceptional comfort and privacy. A dramatic double-height circular foyer sets the tone, opening to graciously scaled living spaces including a formal dining room, expansive family room, eat-in kitchen, and two additional flexible living rooms ideal for a home office, library, or lounge. Classic Mediterranean details abound: arched doorways, hardwood floors, French doors, and seamless indoor-outdoor living with multiple terraces and patio access overlooking the water. Perfect for entertaining or quiet moments by the pond. Additional highlights include a three-car garage, security system, and ample storage throughout. Located just minutes to downtown Westport, with easy access to the Westport train station and I-95, this home is a commuter's dream while still offering a peaceful waterfront setting rarely found so close to town.
