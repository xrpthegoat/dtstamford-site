About this home

European Storybook home in Shorefront Park w/lavish gardens & harbor views. Functional & beautiful spaces inside & out.Completely remodeled & expanded. New plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation & top-of-the-line finish materials in 2016. 9' ceilings & hardwood floors throughout. Marble entry foyer leads to lg DR with sight lines through Chef's kitchen to breakfast area with water views. Kitchen entry framed by antique wrought-iron doors. Lacanche range, pot filler, custom built-ins, Carrera and Black absolute granite floors, open pantry & granite island complete kitchen. Adjoining FR and FB offer potential 1st-floor bedroom/in-law suite with private entrance. Venetian plaster in LR and lg wood-burning FP. Grand terrace off LR. PR, utility & laundry rooms on Main floor. 2nd floor features lg primary bedrm w/ sitting room opening to oversized Ipe deck overlooking harbor, marina and SoNo. Lg WIC. Marble and tile master bath with soaking tub & zero-entry shower. 3 more bdrms & bath with custom tile and tub complete the 2nd fl. Cal closets throughout.Porcelain & stone patio, terrace and fire pit provide outdoor entertaining spaces. Evergreens, trellised roses, hydrangeas and perennials provide visual interest, shade and privacy. Detached 2 car garage w/new tinted glass garage doors & endless possibilities for special use. Private beach access, Kayak launch & community events abound in this premier waterfront community. Walk to Metro & SONO!
