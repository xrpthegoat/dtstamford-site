About this home

*AVAILABLE 04/01/26* Welcome to Bowline Living's newest luxury property in Stamford, a stunning property with six 3-bedroom units one block from the Stamford train station. This building has been reimagined with convenience and style top of mind, and no detail left behind! Each unit is spacious and comfortable with nearly 1,000 Sq ft of living space with soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, and artfully designed fixtures throughout. The beautiful kitchen features a soft and subtle backsplash, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and an oversized breakfast bar that has convenient charging stations on each end. There are 2 bathrooms, uniquely designed with porcelain tile floors, chic vanity, storage/shelving, and tub/shower with sliding glass doors. 3 spacious bedrooms with ample closet space conclude the unit, including the primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and work-from-home nook, the perfect spot for a desk! Added features included each bedroom controlling it's own heat/AC, in-unit laundry and keyless entry. Unbeatable location, just a stone's throw from the train where you can hop on the Harbor Point Trolley that takes you all over downtown Stamford, UConn, and all that Harbor Point has to offer; restaurants, boardwalk, bars, brewery, parks, and more! Pet friendly; your furry friends will enjoy the space in front of the building or playing at the Harbor Point dog park, as well as a dog shower in the shared basement for those muddy days!
