About this home

Located in the heart of the Silvermine Historic District, this enchanting home offers classic New England charm and a prime location just a short stroll to the acclaimed GrayBarns Inn & Tavern and the Silvermine Arts Center. This location blends heritage, culture, and natural beauty in one of Norwalk's most coveted enclaves. A light and bright spacious foyer greets you as you enter the home. With its classic New England stone chimney anchoring fireplaces in both the living room and upstairs bedroom, the home is rich in character and architectural detail. A separate home office provides the space to work from home. While full of character, this home does require significant updating-offering a rare opportunity to renovate and customize within one of Norwalk's most desirable and picturesque neighborhoods. Bring your vision and restore this Silvermine gem to its full potential!
