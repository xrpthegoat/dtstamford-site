About this home

HIGHEST & BEST Monday, 11/17, 2PM. Outstanding, move-in ready Ranch-style living in Glen River, freshly painted throughout with numerous updates and renovations, just steps from downtown Wilton, Schenk's Island and close to the Norwalk River Valley Trail. Imagine one story living in this spacious end unit boasting an open floor plan, wood burning fireplace, beautiful hardwood floors and sun-filled Dining Room with cathedral ceiling & slider to the private deck & garden area. Pride in ownership is evident with many updates, including the Kitchen's new white subway tile backsplash, quartz counters, new flooring & ceiling lights.The Nest Smart thermostat is easy to control remotely as is the front porch lighting, adding to the home's convenience. Newer kitchen appliances, new hot water heater, washer/dryer, Lutron dimmers & extra insulation in the attic & basement add to comfortable living. Retire to the BR Suite with large walk-in closet & convenient washer/dryer. New Hunter Douglas shades that slide from diffused light to blackout are another quality feature. A true peaceful haven right outside with plentiful perennial plantings, tall holly bushes for privacy & a pretty gravel path from the deck to a bluestone walkway, great for entertaining, grilling and an easy walk to the pool in summer months! Wilton is known for its community, top-rated schools, great dining, farmer's market, outstanding library & year-round town events! Want to be in your new home for the holidays?
