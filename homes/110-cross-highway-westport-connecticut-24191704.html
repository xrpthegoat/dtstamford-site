About this home

Experience modern luxury in this beautifully reimagined home, set on 1.57 scenic, park-like acres. Thoughtfully renovated, this 2,864 sq ft residence offers open-concept living spaces filled with natural light and sophisticated finishes throughout. The designer kitchen features a stunning island and premium cabinetry, flowing seamlessly into the dining and living areas with a dramatic linear fireplace. The spacious family room opens to a sun-soaked bluestone patio overlooking an expansive backyard extending to the pond-an idyllic setting for entertaining and everyday enjoyment. The fabulous primary suite is a private retreat with a cozy fireplace, incredible walk-in closet, and beautifully appointed modern bath. Two additional bedrooms complete the second level, while a private 4th-bedroom ensuite is ideal for guests or flexible living space. A main-level office/5th bedroom, powder room, and mudroom with custom built-ins leading to the 2-car garage add versatility and convenience. Ideally located within walking distance of Staples High School and Bedford Middle School, and minutes from Westport's beaches, vibrant downtown, shopping, dining, and train station.
