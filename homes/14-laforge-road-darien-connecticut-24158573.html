About this home

A cottage in Darien! Well-maintained 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home on a quiet street, conveniently located near the beach, shopping, and approximately a 10-minute walk to the train. The kitchen opens to a spacious family room with double sliders leading to the backyard and bluestone patio-ideal for indoor-outdoor living. A built-in work/study area and a mudroom off the garage add everyday functionality, and the living room features a wood-burning fireplace. Upstairs includes four bedrooms and two full baths, plus easy access to walk-up third-floor storage. Laundry is located in the mechanical area off the mudroom. Situated within the Hindley School district.
