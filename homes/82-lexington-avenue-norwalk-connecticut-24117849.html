About this home

Spacious and versatile multi-family home located in the vibrant South Norwalk neighborhood. This well-maintained property features two distinct living units, offering an ideal opportunity for investors or multi-generational living. The first-floor unit includes 2 generously sized bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, a bright living room, and a functional kitchen with plenty of storage. The second unit, spread across the second floor and finished attic, boasts a total of 5 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms-perfect for large families or shared living arrangements. The home also includes a finished basement, providing additional space for recreation, storage, or flexible use. Each unit has its own private entrance, offering both convenience and privacy. Separate utilities with recently installed on demand gas heating hot systems. Outside, enjoy a private backyard with potential for outdoor entertaining, along with driveway/street parking. Home is located just minutes from local shops, restaurants, Metro-North train, I-95, and schools.
