About this home

Experience the joys of Shippan Point! This classic colonial is ideally located on prestigious Ocean Drive West, just a stone's throw from the Stamford Yacht Club and only a five-minute drive from the Stamford train station, where express trains to Manhattan run multiple times an hour and reach the city in just 45 minutes. Inspired by breathtaking sunsets and glimpses of Long Island Sound, the home offers exceptional scale and comfort for both entertaining and everyday living. The welcoming foyer and living room with fireplace open through French doors to a sunny office or den. The elegant dining room features custom millwork, hardwood floors, built-in cabinets, and six-over-one windows showcasing water views. A bright, spacious kitchen boasts two islands, stone countertops, Wolf range, SubZero refrigerator, and a cozy breakfast nook. The adjoining family room offers a cathedral ceiling, built-ins, fireplace, and French doors leading to a stone terrace and beautifully landscaped backyard. Upstairs, the large primary suite includes a marble bath, walk-in closets, and a private balcony to enjoy glimpses of the Sound. Four additional bedrooms, three and a half baths, and a finished basement-perfect for a playroom, home gym, or media room-complete the home. The best of Shippan awaits just in time for summer! Come see what Connecticut coastal living is all about.
