About this home

Discover the ultimate in living at 172 Proprietors Crossing and experience all that the wonderful town of New Canaan has to offer. Nestled on a tranquil west side cul-de-sac, this stunning 5-bed colonial offers an unparalleled opportunity to join the New Canaan community renowned for its exceptional schools, amenities & proximity to NYC. Set on 4 private acres of lush lawns & majestic trees, this residence is a true gem. The heart of the home is a recently renovated gourmet kitchen that opens into a spectacular family room w/ a soaring cathedral ceiling. This vibrant space flows seamlessly into a sunroom, perfect for soaking in natural light year-round. A cozy den w/ a loft & fpl located next to a 1st floor bedroom & full bath is flex space for guests, au pair, in-laws. The first level also features a classic library, ideal for a home office, living room with fireplace and a formal dining room, making it perfect for entertaining. Upstairs offers four generous bedrooms, highlighted by the primary suite. This sanctuary boasts a walk-in closet, custom cabinets and a sun-filled spa bath and shower. Step outside to your own private oasis, featuring a spectacular in-ground pool with a heater and automatic cover. This perfect, level backyard retreat is ideal for hosting unforgettable summer parties and providing a serene escape for relaxation. Additional amenities include a 20KW generator, irrigation system, a spacious deck and landscape lighting.
