About this home

Attention all buyers!! Come see this pristine home with great character and details. Beautifully appointed eat-in-kitchen with granite counters, glass cabinets, stainless steel appliances that have been recently replaced, breakfast bar and accent brick detail. Spacious bright dining/sitting area in kitchen adds to the wonderful family atmosphere. Huge front porch and rear deck with fenced yard combine to make this home a welcoming location for family gatherings. Lower level offers convenient laundry that again has been recently replaced and storage as well as an additional area to enjoy either private office time or play/exercise. This home also has a converted garage space with heat, electricity and wifi, perfect for a professional office or studio with separate entrance. Located in the friendly Glenbrook neighborhood on a beautiful lot walking distance to train and restaurants.
