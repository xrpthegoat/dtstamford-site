About this home

Located on a desirable quiet cul-de-sac with a short 8 min walk to train, this charming and unusually spacious home has so much to see! Freshly painted throughout, this home boasts 5 bedrooms, 2 full and 1 half baths. Enjoy the spacious Living Room with formal Dining area, fireplace, and built-in cabinetry. Proceed further into the large Family Room with more built-in cabinets, coffered ceiling and a pass-through counter into the Kitchen. The all-white kitchen is fully updated with stainless appliances and recessed lighting. Upstairs you are greeted by 4 bedrooms, including the primary BR. There is also a 3rd level 5th bedroom or possible rec-room. And the recently finished lower-level playroom further complements this unusually spacious 5 BR, 2.5 Bath layout - extraordinary at this price point. Included with home are curtain rods and shades, dehumidifier, auto garage door opener, and patio/deck furniture. It all adds up to an exceptional value.
