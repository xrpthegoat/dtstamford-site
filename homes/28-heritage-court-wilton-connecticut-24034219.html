About this home

Serenely sited on a tree-covered knoll at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac near the Weston border, this meticulously designed home exudes charm and attention to detail at every turn. An elegant living room and an oversized formal dining room provide a delightful setting for hosting family and friends. The expansive kitchen features an attractive dining area that is perfect for casual meals. The highlight of the home is a spacious family room with a vaulted ceiling creating a bright and inviting atmosphere for gatherings! The sun-drenched all-season room serves as a peaceful retreat within the home, while an oversized deck with multiple levels is perfect for hosting large summer parties. Best of all, it is located on a street with a strong sense of community where friendly neighbors help each other and enjoy socializing together. Title also includes a shared interest in conservation land.
