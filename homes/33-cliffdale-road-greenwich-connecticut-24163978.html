About this home

Golden opportunity to own four private acres in Greenwich's coveted Back Country. Tucked behind a gated stone entrance, this stunning Colonial sits on rolling lawns with sunset views to scenic Laub Pond. Renovated by renowned Lighthouse Architects, it blends classic New England charm with modern luxury and includes plans for future expansion. The primary suite is a true retreat, featuring mahogany vaulted ceilings and a fireplace, evoking a boutique hotel feel. Four additional bedrooms offer ample space, while expansive living areas are perfect for both quiet nights and entertaining. A sparkling pool, sweeping lawns, and rare privacy complete the setting. Just minutes from top schools, country clubs, and town.
