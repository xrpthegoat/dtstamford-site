About this home

This is the land and location Westport buyers are chasing hardest right now, and finding least. It won't last more than a moment. Bring your architect or builder or we can share ours. The acre is flat, easily buildable, screened on every side. The house could be reimagined however the value is in the land. Its a private blank canvas on a cul-de-sac. Long Lots, on the cusp of Hunt Club - the neighborhood and the country club. Close to town but not too close. A quick drive to Greens Farms station, Burying Hill Beach, and the many Post Road amenities - dangerously close to Fatto a Mano, though proximity to Equinox balances that right out. Zone Specifics: AA Zone, 30 ft from the street and 25 ft from the side setbacks, max 3 stories height of 40 ft, 25% lot coverage. No wetlands. Public water, septic. Access to both Gregg Lane and Grist Mill. Existing solar will be removed and settled by the trust. Buyers are responsible for their own due diligence; neither the trust nor the seller's agent can make any representations. The address is 4 Grist Mill Lane, the driveway is actually at the end of Gregg Lane. Not subject to probate. AS-IS. Ideal for a 6,000 sq foot plus new home in the $5-6+M value range. The deadline for submitting offers is 9/18 by 5pm.
