About this home

Newly completed by Compo Luxury Homes, this exceptional modern estate offers approximately 9,200 square feet on 2.1 private acres just minutes from downtown Westport, Compo Beach, and Metro-North. Thoughtfully designed with European craftsmanship, premium finishes, and sophisticated smart-home technology, the home blends timeless design with exceptional functionality. A dramatic floating staircase anchors four finished levels featuring soaring ceilings, custom millwork, and engineered white oak flooring. The chef's kitchen showcases dual quartzite waterfall islands, custom rift white oak cabinetry, a walk-in pantry, and a professional appliance suite. The primary suite offers cathedral ceilings, a fireplace, private balcony, dual dressing rooms, and a spa-inspired bath with heated floors and a soaking tub. Designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living, the nearly 950-square-foot covered terrace features granite flooring, a wood-burning fireplace, outdoor kitchen, heated 20' 40' saltwater pool, year-round spa, and room for a future tennis or pickleball court. The walk-out lower level includes a family room, wet bar, fitness studio, guest suite, and flexible space for a theater, golf simulator, or media lounge. Additional highlights include a DaVinci slate roof, premium European windows and lift-and-slide doors, Lutron lighting, whole-home networking, seven-zone HVAC, security system, and a 32kW Generac generator.
