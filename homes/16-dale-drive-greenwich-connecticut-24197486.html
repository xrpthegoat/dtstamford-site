About this home

Stylish four-bedroom Colonial with private Trex deck, quiet backyard and seasonal second floor River views is ideally located on a cul-de-sac convenient to schools, park, shops and trains. Recent updates, including refinished main level wood floors and new second level wood floors, add to the appeal of this 2014 tastefully renovated home. Chef's kitchen boasts Quartz-clad center island with seating open to dining room, office/playroom or possible bedroom, living room with fieldstone fireplace flanked by built-in bookcases and powder room. Primary suite with walk-in closet, sumptuous bath, two bedrooms with Jack and Jill bath enhanced by a new Porcelanosa floor and laundry comprise second floor. Lower level offers a built-in homework station, family room, gym and/or office.
