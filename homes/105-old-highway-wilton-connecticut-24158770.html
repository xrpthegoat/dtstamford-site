About this home

Nestled on 2.02 private acres in Wilton, this spacious 4-bedroom, 4-bath home offers a thoughtful layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The great room with vaulted ceiling and fireplace creates an impressive central gathering space filled with natural light, while the formal dining room with French doors and built-ins adds classic detail. The large open kitchen features a walk-in pantry, breakfast bar, and pastry counter, and opens directly to a family room with fireplace, creating a warm and inviting space for casual living. From the family room, step out to a large deck overlooking peaceful woodlands, ideal for outdoor dining and relaxing in a private natural setting. The first-floor primary suite provides convenience and privacy. The second level includes a den with extra-height ceilings and built-ins, along with three additional bedrooms and a hall bath. Additional highlights include hardwood flooring, a walk-out finished lower level offering flexible living space for recreation, gym, or media room, an office, and an attached three-car garage. Set on over two acres, this property offers space, privacy, and a desirable Wilton lifestyle.
