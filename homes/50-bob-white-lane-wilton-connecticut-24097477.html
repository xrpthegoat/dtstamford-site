About this home

Set on a rare, level 1.6-acre lot in desirable So. Wilton, this move-in ready and impeccably cared for 3-BR ranch offers easy one-level living close to Wilton Center, shopping, dining, major commuting routes and only 10 mins to SONO train. Surrounded by mature trees, the property provides exceptional privacy and a peaceful natural setting. The flexible layout functions as a traditional 3-bed home or offers potential for a private guest, in-law, or au pair suite. The kitchen opens to a combined living and dining area with vaulted ceilings and oversized sliders leading to a sun-filled patio-ideal for indoor-outdoor flow and relaxed everyday living. The professionally landscaped grounds feature golf-course-style turf, a 15-zone irrigation system, teak decking, and a stone fire pit area tucked into the landscape. A 40-ft. impact net offers space to practice golf, lacrosse, or soccer, making the backyard as fun as it is functional. A Weber grill connected to the LP gas line makes BBQ's effortless. The home is heated with clean-burning propane, a more efficient and lower-maintenance alternative to oil, and also fuels the water heater, stove, grill. Additional features include a high-efficiency LP gas furnace, WiFi-enabled thermostats, and generator-ready wiring. Comfortable, adaptable - privately set on one of Wilton's rare level parcels, 50 Bob White Lane offers a special lifestyle opportunity.
