About this home

Large one bedroom condo apartment located on the top floor with spectacular west-north-east views of Stamford. This apartment is in excellent condition and features hardwood floors, lots of closets and storage space, an updated full bathroom, plus a half bath in the living area. You'll truly enjoy sitting outside on the private and spacious screened-in balcony porch. The galley-style kitchen features a gas range, plenty of counter-top and cabinet space, plus a large pantry. Gas, heat, water, hot water and sewer are all included in the rent. The convenient laundry room is located on the same floor as the unit. You'll be amazed at how much living space this condo has to offer. As a top-floor unit this one is sure to go quickly.
