About this home

This newly reimagined Colonial, fully renovated in 2024, is set on 2.38 impeccably manicured acres and offers complete privacy. The property features a large stone patio, a sparkling pool, a charming pool house, and a soccer field. Located in the desirable Silvermine Area, the home is nestled in a private cul-de-sac, perfect for dog walking and ensuring safety for children. Inside, you'll find designer finishes by Haley Design Company, soaring ceilings, and an open-concept floor plan. The renovations were masterfully completed by Farrell Remodeling Inc. The residence includes 6 bedrooms, 8 full and 3 half bathrooms, a private home office, 3 fireplaces, gym, home theater and a 700 bottle wine cellar with a tasting room. With all-new high-efficiency mechanicals, a 48 kW generator and a 3-car garage with epoxy floors, this home is truly irresistible.
