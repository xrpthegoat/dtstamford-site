About this home

This Ultra-stylish 5 bedroom home in Noroton Manor welcomes you with a beautiful entry hall to generously scaled rooms. The sun-washed interior balances comfort with luxury showcasing custom millwork with plenty of light from all sides. The gourmet kitchen/family room combo is spectacular with Subzero, Wolf and Bosch appliances, along with an oversized kitchen island with waterfall marble countertop and Custom Shiplap ceilings. Double walls of French doors lead to a spectacular parklike property with bluestone terrace, built in firepit & playset surrounded by a beautiful tree lined property. First floor guest bedroom or au pair suite. 2 car garage with custom Lift, bright and airy mudroom with built-ins and full house generator. Primary suite with fireplace, custom closets and luxurious bathroom. 4 additional double sized bedrooms all ensuite with sizable closet space in each. Fantastic finished lower level offers tons of options for playroom, Gym or media room with full bath. Located within the desirable private Noroton Manor Association situated on a coveted tree-lined street which residents enjoy kayaking, tennis courts, paddle boarding, fishing, private water access & small boat moorings. An active neighborhood with fun social events throughout the year! Close to Weed Beach, award winning blue ribbon Hindley school and local shops & restaurants.
