About this home

Fabulous Westover location, wonderfully proportioned rooms and lovingly cared for & maintained by the current owners for 31 years, this deceivingly spacious home offers so many opportunities, plus a bit of history - owned at one time by Giants football player Junior Coffey! Living Room features a big picture window and opens to Dining Room with sliders to Deck. Eat-In Kitchen with Granite Counters opens to breakfast room with vaulted ceiling & skylights making this a true "country kitchen". The Bedroom wing offers a true Primary Bedroom Suite incorporating large bedroom, sitting room, full bath and walk-in closet. Two additional Bedrooms and Full Hall Bath complete the Main Level. The Lower Level is awesome & designed for hours of enjoyment, with room for everyone & everything - Family Room with brick fireplace, built-ins and convenient bar sink for entertaining and then there's the spacious "Pool Table" room with bay window and door to yard. The Full Bath/Laundry and entry to the oversized 2 Car Garage complete the Lower Level. Hardwood Floors, refreshed bathrooms, tons of storage including a shed, updated mechanicals, lots of natural light and the list goes on... Convenient location to all commuting, shopping, schools and Downtown & Harbor Point Entertainment Districts.
