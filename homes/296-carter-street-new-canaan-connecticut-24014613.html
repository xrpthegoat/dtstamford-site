About this home

Nestled on a parklike property with beautiful flowering gardens, this special home offers a sanctuary of comfort and style. Step inside & be enchanted by the timeless elegance that defines this unique New Canaan landmark. The Matthew Finch House exudes historical charm & has been meticulously restored & thoughtfully enhanced & expanded for modern living. Entertain with ease in the great expansive entertaining room with central stone FPL The family room w/fireplace & wet bar features French doors opening to a patio area & screened-in porch overlooking the stunning pool & spa area seamlessly blending indoor & outdoor living. The gracious library w/FPL, bay window & custom built-ins provides a perfect in-home office. The primary suite w/FPL & sitting area provides a luxurious & spacious retreat with two sets of French doors opening to a west facing balcony overlooking the picturesque property & breathtaking sunsets. The primary bedroom also features high ceilings, generous custom dressing rooms & spa bath. The expansive light filled 2nd floor family room/playroom w/vaulted exposed beamed ceilings & floor to ceiling windows is a fabulous highlight and leads to a spacious guest suite. Three additional bedrooms, one with en-suite bath complete the 2nd floor. Additional highlights include a finished lower level, wine cellar, 3 car garage & sports court. Experience the allure of the Matthew Finch House and make this exceptional New Canaan gem your own
