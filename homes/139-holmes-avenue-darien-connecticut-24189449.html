About this home

Picture yourself in a welcoming, walkable community where neighbors become friends, evening strolls are part of everyday life, and parks, shops, and local amenities are just a short distance away. This meticulously maintained Colonial blends timeless charm with modern updates, offering a home that's as inviting as the neighborhood surrounding it. Set on a beautifully landscaped property with a sweeping lawn, this home has been beautifully renovated with stylish finishes throughout. The sun-filled main level features an eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, cozy living room with a fireplace, an office and a comfortable family room with French doors leading to a sunny deck. Upstairs along with a spa-inspired bath are 3 bedrooms including a primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings and a generous walk-in closet. The finished lower level offers a full bathroom/laundry room and versatile space for a home gym, playroom, or recreation room. If you're looking for entertaining space, the spacious deck provides room for bbq's, dining, and lounging with friends while overlooking the spacious, level and professionally landscaped and fenced lawn. Complete with a home generator and a two-car garage, this exceptional home delivers the perfect balance of historic character, modern comfort, and everyday convenience in a friendly, vibrant neighborhood you'll love coming home to.
