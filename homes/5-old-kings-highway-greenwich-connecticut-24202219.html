About this home

Set on a quiet stretch of Old Kings Highway, this welcoming four-bedroom home is designed around the way a family lives. The light-filled living and dining rooms feature 12-foot ceilings, a fireplace, custom built-ins, and generous windows. The kitchen is thoughtfully built with high-end appliances, quartz countertops, a large center island, and opens naturally to a comfortable family room/playroom. Upstairs, four bedrooms include a spacious primary suite and two additional full baths. Outside, the fenced yard provides room to play, gather, and unwind, with a back deck, mature landscaping, and stone fire pit creating an easy extension of the living space. A finished garage adds practical storage and convenience. Located close to Old Greenwich, the beach, and schools, with Dundee, Eastern Middle, and Greenwich High School serving the property. A well-rounded home for everyday family life, with the best of Old Greenwich close at hand.
