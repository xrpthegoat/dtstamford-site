About this home

This just might be the most convenient location in all of Wilton! On the southernmost edge of town, this property is 2 homes away from the locally beloved Cranbury Park with amazing nature walking trails for both humans and canines! Only 2 mins from Route 7, 3 mins to Merritt Parkway & 5 mins to I95! This adorable low maintenance Cape Cod cottage has mature landscaping, beautiful outdoor open play areas and a 600sqft 2-tier deck. Inside this dollhouse you'll find a sleek kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances including a wine chiller. The sunny Living Room has a romantic stone hearth and large bay window. The great room has 14' cathedral ceiling with fun flexible use options. The primary bedroom and full bath are located on the first level. The second floor has a full length 2nd bedroom which could potentially be divided adding a 3rd bedroom. Attached garage. Home was upgraded to expand with 4 zones of heat and an oversized septic system. Home has a new roof, new well pump & purification system, new water pressure bladder, new filtration and a new garage door was also recently installed. This is an ideal home for so many home hunters due to its location and expansion possibilities. Sold As Is, grab it fast! Agent related to owner.
