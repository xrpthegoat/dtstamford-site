About this home

Welcome to Shorefront Park! Enjoy breathtaking sunrises in this premier waterfront community, offering unique amenities including two private beach areas, kayak/ paddleboard launch, plus community events. Be prepared to be wowed by this updated 4 bed 2.5 bath seaside colonial. This meticulous home has countless upgrades with tremendous attention to detail, including a show stopping NEW eat in kitchen with island, open to the family room, both brimming with natural light. The Sub Zero fridge, ample cabinets, quartz counters, and Wolf cooktop are sure to please any culinary enthusiast. Just off the kitchen is a large DR open to the living room with fireplace. The primary suite has 2 newly outfitted walk in closets. Upstairs there are three additional bedrooms and a hall bath. Recessed lighting throughout the home, gleaming hardwood floors, and new window treatments are not to be missed. Enjoy beautiful perennials in the professionally landscaped fenced in backyard, two car attached garage, new furnace and tankless hot water heater. Walk to the vibrant SONO section where you will find dining, shopping, and entertainment. Easy access to the train station and just 60 minutes to NYC. This home and neighborhood offer lifestyle living at its best and is truly a must see!
