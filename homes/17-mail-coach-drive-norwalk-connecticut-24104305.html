About this home

Welcome to your own slice of paradise in the sought-after Silvermine neighborhood. This charming four/five-bedroom home sits on over an acre of level, landscaped property featuring classic stone walls and mature plantings. Perfectly positioned on the Wilton border, the home offers a Wilton address with access to the Norwalk school system. Step inside to discover numerous updates, including a stylish kitchen with gorgeous quartz countertops, hardwood floors throughout, and a newly renovated primary bedroom. The spacious master bedroom includes an impressive dressing room with multiple closets- ideal for a home office, nursery, or private retreat. The inviting family room with fireplace creates a cozy atmosphere, while the full walkout basement offers endless possibilities for additional living space, recreation, and storage. While the home's exterior-deck, roof, siding, and windows could use some attention, it's being sold as is, offering an amazing opportunity to bring your own vision to life. Enjoy the convenience of walking to Gray Barns, The Mercantile, and Silvermine market. This is a rare chance to own a highly desirable location with tremendous potential-don't miss it!
