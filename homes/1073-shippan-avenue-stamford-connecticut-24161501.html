About this home

Walk to the beach by summer! Welcome to this beautifully renovated Shippan Colonial that perfectly blends modern updates w/classic charm. With 5 BRs, 2.5 baths, & multiple flex spaces, this home offers comfort & versatility. An inviting front porch sets the tone, while inside, the open flow allows light to pour in from all windows. The spacious LR flows seamlessly into the DR & showstopping kitchen w/quartz waterfall countertops, s/s appls, pantry & abundant cabinetry-perfect for everyday living & entertaining. Beyond the kitchen are 2 BRs & a full bath creating the perfect opportunity for a 1st flr primary suite, guest space, or home office. Upstairs, offers 3 add'l BRs & an updated bath + finished walkup attic ideal as an office, hangout space or gym plus add'l storage. The fully finished LL w/ separate entry & garage access, expands your options w/half bath, kitchenette, laundry & oversized media/family room-great for entertaining or multi-gen living. The 2 car heated & insulated garage is large enough for add'l storage. Outside, enjoy a large yard w/ plenty of room to play, garden, or relax. A covered patio provides a cozy retreat for outdoor dining & lounging or can be used as a carport. While perfect the way it is, there is also plenty of expansion potential. Minutes from I-95, Metro North & Downtown, the highlight is a short walk to Brennan's, Cummings Park & West Beach! Don't miss your chance to own in one of Stamford's most sought after communities!
