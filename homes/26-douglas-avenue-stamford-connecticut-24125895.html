About this home

Pristine 3BR/3.5BA end-unit over 3,000 sqft townhome on a quiet dead-end street with a rare fenced-in private backyard, finished lower level, and just a short walk to the Glenbrook train for easy NYC commuting. Freshly painted throughout with gleaming hardwood floors, crown molding, and recessed lighting, this home is in immaculate move-in condition. The main level offers a bright, airy open floorplan with an eat-in kitchen featuring gas range, custom cabinetry, and center island, flowing seamlessly into the living room with gas fireplace and sliders leading to a large private deck overlooking the beautifully landscaped, fenced-in yard-an ideal setting for entertaining, outdoor dining, or quiet relaxation. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite boasts a walk-in closet and spa-like bath with separate jetted tub and stand-up shower, while two additional bedrooms, a hall bath, and a laundry room provide comfort and convenience. An office nook with a striking palladium window adds charm and natural light. The finished lower level with full bath is ideal for a family room, office, playroom, or guest suite. A sizable garage, extra off-street parking, and zoned gas heat/central air add to the appeal-but what truly sets this property apart is that it comes with *no common charges*, a rare find for townhome living. Minutes to downtown Stamford's restaurants, shopping & nightlife, with easy access to I-95 & CT-15-this home delivers the space, style, and lifestyle you've been waiting for
