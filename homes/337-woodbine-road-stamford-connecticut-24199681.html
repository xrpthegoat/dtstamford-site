About this home

Welcome to this thoughtfully renovated 6-bedroom home, set on 1.12 lush acres on a peaceful corner lot in desirable North Stamford. Offering 3,421 sq. ft. of living space, the home features a versatile split-level layout with hardwood floors throughout. The main level offers an open living room, dining room, and updated kitchen with new appliances. On the next floor, the primary suite is joined by two additional bedrooms, a hallway bath, and linen closet. The top level features a private in-law suite with two bedrooms, a full bath, full kitchen with new appliances, new washer and dryer, and both interior and separate exterior access. The lower level offers seamless indoor-outdoor living with a living area opening to the patio, plus an additional bedroom ideal for an office, guest room, or flex space, and a separate laundry room. An additional 900 sq. ft. basement provides exceptional storage. A flexible, beautifully updated home in a peaceful North Stamford setting.
