About this home

A rare chance to get into a prime Norwalk location at the right price. This is the kind of opportunity that doesn't come around often-an entry point into a highly desirable Norwalk neighborhood at a price that allows you to create value over time. Whether you're a buyer looking to personalize your home or an investor seeking long-term upside, this property offers a compelling combination of location, potential, and equity-building opportunity. The home's interior was just professionally painted & features vinyl siding, vinyl replacement windows, and an expansive wrap-around deck with multiple entry points-offering great outdoor living potential. Though updates are needed inside and out, the home offers strong bones and a layout that's ready to be re-imagined to suit your style and needs. With the right vision and improvements, you can maximize the home's value and make the most of its strong footprint. You're also just a short drive to Calf Pasture Beach, Taylor Farm, Cranbury Park. Whether you're looking for your next project or simply want to break into a great area at an attainable price, this is your chance to capitalize on a unique opportunity. Property to be sold 'as-is'.
