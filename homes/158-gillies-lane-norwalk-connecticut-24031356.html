About this home

A private entrance leads to this spacious 3 bedroom, 2 bath ranch style unit with vaulted ceilings and windowed walls that bring the scenic outdoors in. Open and oversized living room/dining room combination with two sided fireplace and sliders to the deck overlooking the tranquil brook and natures bounty. Master bedroom with full bath and multiple double closets, comfortably fits a king sized bed. Two walk in closets in another bedroom provide ample storage. Plenty of convenient parking for unit owners and visitors. Complex has pool, gym, club house, aerated pond, walking trails and is pet friendly. Conveniently located to Route 1 (Connecticut Avenue) and I95 at Exit 14 and a short ride to downtown New Canaan.
