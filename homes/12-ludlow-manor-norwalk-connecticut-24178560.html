About this home

Have you ever had a daydream that lasted a lifetime? If not, now's your chance. The words out, East Norwalk is Fairfield County's best kept secret. And this show stopper is like no other. You arrive, pull into the drive, and slip through the garage into the laundry room/mudroom w/ multicolor slate floor-an everyday luxury that sets the tone. Step through the main entrance and the story begins: a formal living room with hardwood floors & fireplace, that leads to an open concept eat-in-kitchen, dining area & family room w/ stone fireplace, warm enough to slow the world down. A wet bar for evenings that linger, French doors that grant you passage and a remodeled wraparound deck for mornings that don't rush. Need a quiet place to work? The sunken office w/ updated powder room adds to its excellence. On the second floor you will find four bedrooms, two full baths, & finishes chosen w/ intention-from the flooring to the fixtures. The primary suite becomes its own chapter, complete w/ vaulted ceilings, wool carpeting, Juliet balcony, a marble bath with double sinks, shower heads, spa tub & ample closet space. 3 more bedrooms & a full bath complete the second floor layout. If that wasn't enough there are 2 basements. One for utility & the other is finished for fun. Out back is an oasis surrounded by plenty of mature plantings. Centrally located to everything including beaches, restaurants, public transport, this house will be its very own love letter to yourself.
