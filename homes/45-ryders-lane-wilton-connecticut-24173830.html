About this home

Highest and best by 2:00 pm on Monday, 5/18. Set on a coveted lower Nod Hill cul-de-sac, 45 Ryders Lane is the kind of home that quietly wins people over the moment they arrive. Classic in style yet thoughtfully updated for modern living, this 4-bedroom Colonial blends timeless architecture with warm, inviting spaces designed for real life. Inside, light pours through generously sized rooms, creating an easy elegance throughout the home. The renovated kitchen is both beautiful and functional, featuring a Wolf range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, and seamless flow into the home's casual gathering spaces. Nearby, the mudroom and updated laundry area make everyday living refreshingly effortless. Upstairs, the primary suite feels like a private retreat, complete with a beautifully renovated bath featuring radiant heated floors and a heated towel bar. Three additional spacious bedrooms, an updated hall bath, and a walk-up attic provide flexibility for today's lifestyle. Outside, summer practically writes its own plans. A charming screened porch overlooks the property, while the stone patio and expansive yard offer room for entertaining, gardening, or simply slowing down for the evening. Thoughtful improvements, including a new cedar roof, solar panels, upgraded electrical service, an invisible fence, and a new water heater, add comfort and peace of mind. A special home in one of Wilton's most sought-after neighborhoods.
