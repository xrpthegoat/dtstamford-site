About this home

Introducing 9 Carleton Street, an exceptional new-construction residence by a 2026 HOBI Award-winning builder, where timeless New England architecture meets modern luxury. Thoughtfully designed with 10-foot main-level ceilings, 9-foot upper-level ceilings, and wide-plank white oak floors, the home blends elegant finishes with effortless livability. The living room is distinguished by a spectacular custom fireplace wall clad in polished Calacatta Fabbricotti marble, creating a striking architectural focal point. The chef's kitchen features custom oak cabinetry and stunning honed Taj Mahal stone, while the luxurious primary suite offers a spa-inspired bath, spacious walk-in closet, and private glass-railed deck overlooking the pool. Every full bathroom showcases elegant, thoughtfully curated finishes and the added comfort of radiant heated floors. A private elevator, beautifully landscaped grounds, swimming pool, and four-car garage complete this remarkable offering. Ideally located moments from Greenwich Avenue's premier shopping, dining, and convenient train service to NYC.
