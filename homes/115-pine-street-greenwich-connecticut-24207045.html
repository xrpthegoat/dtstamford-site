About this home

Exceptional income producing two-family property in the heart of Byram. Featuring two identical units, each with 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, this property offers a strong combination of rental income, tenant appeal, and long term appreciation. Conveniently located near shopping, transportation, parks, and restaurants. This is a rare opportunity to acquire a multifamily of this type and condition.
