About this home

Nestled atop Drum Rd in the heart of Rowayton, this iconic property offers a perfect blend of classic charm and modern sophistication. Built in 1887, the home exudes timeless elegance with its original moldings and exquisite architectural details. Spanning 4,576 sf, the 1st floor is designed for both comfort and entertaining, featuring a spacious kitchen outfitted with high-end appliances, a cozy family/breakfast room, a formal dining area, sun-drenched living room, music room, and a full bath. Take the front or rear staircases to the 2nd floor, where you'll find a distinguished library, a luxurious primary ensuite bedroom, three additional bedrooms, and another well-appointed bath. The 3rd floor offers versatility with a flexible space, a full bath, two additional bedrooms, and an office. The wrap-around porch seamlessly blends with the lush outdoor surroundings. Situated on a generous .36 AC lot, the property boasts a beautifully landscaped yard complete with a charming storybook playhouse. The finished garage adds further flexibility, ideal for a home office, gym, creative studio or more. Just a stone's throw from the town center, enjoy easy access to local favorites such as Rowayton Seafood, Brendan's 101, Rowayton Pizza, and Bayley Beach. This tasteful home captures the essence of historic charm while catering to contemporary lifestyles, offering a unique and elevated sanctuary for the discerning homeowner.
