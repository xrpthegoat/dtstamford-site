About this home

UNIQUE OPPORTUNITY! This oversize level property is one-third of an acre (.33) in an R-12 zone, in an excellent neighborhood in the heart of Glenville close to schools, shops and restaurants. The house is solid but in need of TLC and with some work could be a wonderful family home. Nice layout; the house has good bones but needs everything. House is being sold AS-IS. A new or expanded house could be up to 4,582 square feet. The house is currently 1,713 sq feet, has hardwood floors, 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, an eat-in kitchen, a large living/ dining room and a spacious family room. Primary bedroom is en-suite. Natural Gas is available at this property if wish to convert from Oil. This property offers exclusive access to Greenwich's four beaches, marinas, a town-owned golf course, and an indoor ice rink.
