About this home

Welcome to 120 Webb Avenue, Stamford, CT Nestled in the highly desirable Cove neighborhood, just a short stroll from Cove Island Park and Beach, this charming Cape offers the perfect balance of comfort, convenience, and coastal lifestyle. Located on a quiet street, it's an ideal home for first-time buyers, young families, or anyone looking to enjoy all that Stamford's shoreline has to offer. Step inside and you're greeted by a sun-filled entry with gleaming hardwood floors. From the front door, you can see straight through to the dining area and into the bright, eat-in kitchen. The first floor features two well-proportioned bedrooms and a full bath, providing flexible living options for bedrooms, guest space, or even a home office. Upstairs, the spacious unfinished attic offers great potential-transform it into a home office, playroom, or bonus room to suit your needs. The back of the home opens to a spacious deck overlooking a large, level backyard. Perfect for gatherings, sports activities, or simply relaxing outdoors, this space makes the home as enjoyable outside as it is inside. Location Highlights: * Just minutes from Cove Island Park & Beach-enjoy beaches, walking trails, and water views. * Close to Chelsea Piers, I-95, and Stamford Train Station for easy commuting. * Near local shops, dining, and schools while maintaining a quiet neighborhood feel.
