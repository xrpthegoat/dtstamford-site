About this home

NEW IMPROVED PRICE!! If you are looking for luxury, privacy, and location, look no further! This magnificent colonial, with its manicured property at the end of a cul-de-sac in the sought-after Cranbury area, has it all! Beautifully maintained to the highest standards, the home features 9' ceilings on the main level, hardwood floors throughout, artisan millwork, oversized windows, and French doors. The gracious foyer flows into the living room and formal dining room, leading to the stunning family room with a coffered ceiling, dramatic fireplace, and wet bar. The state-of-the-art eat-in kitchen, with a large granite center island and stainless steel appliances, is the heart of the home and leads to a sun-drenched dining area with windows, opening to a private deck-perfect for true indoor/outdoor living. The upper level features an elegant primary bedroom suite with a luxurious bath, fireplace, and walk-in closets. This floor also boasts three additional gracious en-suite bedrooms, including one with a private balcony for relaxing any time of day. The finished third level includes a recreational area, an additional bedroom, and a full bath, and spacious storage area. A two-car garage completes this unparalleled home in the heart of Norwalk, conveniently located near all amenities.
