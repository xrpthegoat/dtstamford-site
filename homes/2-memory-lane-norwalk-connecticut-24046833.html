About this home

This remarkable Rowayton -Nantucket style colonial is waiting for you! This bright, charming and spacious home has so much - including a full kitchen renovation in 2023 with all new cabinets, countertops and appliances. There's even a sunny eat-in area with bay window seating and access to a lovely patio for grilling and entertaining. There's a formal dining area that leads to a generous sized living room with fireplace. Beyond the dining room is a home office/5th bedroom and full bath. Through the living room you'll be wowed by the sunny and spacious family room which leads to the back patio and fenced in yard. Upstairs the primary bedroom with vaulted ceiling includes a dressing area, walk in closets and full bath. Three additional bedrooms which share a large full bath complete the upper level. The perfect blend of sunshine and privacy creates an ideal backyard space for entertaining and relaxing. Ride your bike to Bayley Beach for Sunday Summer concerts, walk to downtown village shops and restaurants and train. 2 Memory Lane is the Rowayton home you've been waiting for.
