About this home

Large recently remodeled colonial with 3 bedrooms; possibly a 4th bedroom and 2 full baths, located in the Wolfpit neighborhood and on a dead-end street. This home offers over 2,400sqft of living space with hardwood floors throughout. A lovely eat in kitchen with granite counter tops, leading into a spacious dining room for family dinners. Entertain guest in your spacious den. Laundry room has a new washer and dryer which is located on the main level. On the second floor you have 3 spacious bedrooms, one bedroom has a loft space. Lovely front deck with a ramp. One car garage available can be used for extra storage and driveway has ample amount of parking. House is centrally located near shopping centers, beaches, transportation, schools, and parks. Tenants responsible all utilities and are responsible for lawn care and snow removal.
