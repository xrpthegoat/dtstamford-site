About this home

Welcome to 98 Holmes Avenue - a charming and inviting home offering warmth, flexibility, and a convenient lifestyle. With approximately 1,770 square feet of living space, the main level flows beautifully and features a spacious living room with fireplace, a sunroom that can either serve as a playroom or office, a dining room, and a kitchen that opens to a large screened-in porch - perfect for relaxed indoor-outdoor living. Upstairs are three comfortable bedrooms, plus a flexible fourth bedroom on the third floor ideal for guests, a home office, or a creative studio. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced front, lovely new landscaping, and a generous hardscaped patio in back designed for entertaining. Set on a friendly, tree-lined street within walking distance to the train and Woodland Park, this home effortlessly combines everyday comfort with true neighborhood charm.
