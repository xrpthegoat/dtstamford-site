About this home

Coastal Charm with Bay Views Experience the best of coastal living in this inviting single-level ranch, where sweeping views of Long Island Sound Bay shine from nearly every room. The vaulted living and dining area feels bright and airy, centered around a rustic stone fireplace perfect for cozy gatherings. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and natural gas cooking-ideal for everyday living or entertaining. With three bedrooms and two full baths, the layout is flexible and comfortable. The third bedroom has a private entrance, perfect for guests, a home office, or a quiet retreat. Tile floors throughout add coastal practicality. Step outside to a spacious deck for barbecues or sunset views, or enjoy morning coffee on the covered porch overlooking the Sound. Go kayaking, paddleboarding, or relax at nearby Cove Island Park, which offers beaches, a marina, tennis courts, and picnic areas. A fenced garden adds privacy and charm, and a one-car garage completes the picture. This home perfectly blends everyday comfort with easy coastal living.
