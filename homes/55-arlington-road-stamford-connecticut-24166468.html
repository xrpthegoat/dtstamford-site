About this home

Sun-filled colonial offering 3,797 square feet of bright, comfortable living just minutes from downtown Stamford restaurants, nightlife, and Metro North and Amtrak trains. A two-story foyer opens to formal living and dining rooms, followed by the spacious family room with gas fireplace and sliders to the backyard, next to the eat-in chef's kitchen with Thermador and Bosch appliances, quartz countertops, and wet bar. The second floor features four bedrooms including a generous primary suite with spa bath and walk-in closet. The finished third floor adds flexible living space with a full bath. The backyard is a private oasis with bluestone patio, firepit, shed, and outdoor sound system. Numerous upgrades include built-ins, automatic shades, electric car charger, expanded driveway, and mature landscaping
