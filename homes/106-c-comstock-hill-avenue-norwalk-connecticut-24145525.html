About this home

Stunning Colonial with Legal Accessory Apartment on Private Street. Locate in one of Norwalk, Connecticut's most desirable areas, this expansive Colonial offers 5,851 sq ft of beautifully updated living space on a 1.17-acre lot. Built in 1986, the home delivers the scale and feel of a true single-family residence w/ the added benefit of a legal accessory apartment already in place. The main home features a bright, open layout w/hardwood floors, oversized windows, and west-facing exposure filling the living and dining spaces w/ strong afternoon light & beautiful sunset views. Recent upgrades include a brand new roof, brand new driveway, and fully renovated kitchens and bathrooms, making this a truly move-in-ready home. 2 fireplaces add warmth and character. With 7 bedrooms, 6 full baths and a 1/2 bath, the layout accommodates a variety of lifestyles, from growing families to multi-generational living. The legal accessory apartment, offers outstanding flexibility. It can be used as an income-producing rental, an in-law suite, or private guest space. Comparable apartments in the area rent for approximately $2,500+/mo, providing meaningful upside while maintaining the single-family feel. Additional highlights: an oversized 3-car garage, full basement, expansive wood deck patio & open porch offering generous indoor/outdoor living space. Thoughtfully updated, well located, this home presents a rare opportunity to own a substantial residence in a premier Norwalk neighborhood.
