About this home

Sited perfectly on a small knoll, this Beautiful and Pristine Colonial, located in one of Wilton's most coveted neighborhoods is a rare find. Upon entry, it is obvious that this 5 bedroom, 3 1/2 bath home is truly special. The spacious custom-designed TURNKEY kitchen with quartz counters & top of the line appliances is a show stopper and chef's dream. Adjacent to the kitchen, the gracious dining room is ideal for entertaining & family gatherings. Elegant living room with lovely stone fireplace & french doors as well as cozy & charming family room with built-ins & additional fireplace provide many spaces to gather & relax. Completing the first floor is an updated powder room and light-filled office. Upstairs you will find the primary bedroom suite with spa-like bathroom and walk-in-closet, along with three additional generously sized bedrooms & another full bathroom. An added bonus off the kitchen is an office/playroom, bedroom & full bathroom which can be used as an au pair/in-law suite. Walk out lower level (1056 sq ft) is ideal for a recreation room & storage. Enjoy stunning sunsets from the AWESOME front porch & host friends on the large Trex deck. Ideally located between Wilton & Ridgefield Town Centers, Linden Tree is well known for its ice skating on Kent Pond & scenic walking/running trails where one experiences privacy & beauty. Pride of ownership is evident throughout this exceptional home & exquisite property. Tremendous opportunity! Do not miss out on this one!!
