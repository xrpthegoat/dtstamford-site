About this home

Exceptional custom-built 4 to 5 bedroom Colonial in desirable North Stamford! This impressive 4,644-square-foot residence welcomes you with a dramatic two-story entry hall and offers sophisticated living on a beautifully landscaped 1.1-acre lot. Four generous bedrooms and four full baths plus one half bath provide ample space for family and guests. A versatile bonus in-law suite over the three-car garage features convenient back stair access, perfect for multi-generational living or au pair accommodations. Plus 2200 square foot lower level with high ceilings is just waiting to be finished. Outdoor living is a dream with a level yard, expansive wood deck and patio with a heated in-ground pool-ideal for entertaining throughout the seasons. The prime North Stamford location offers tranquility and privacy while remaining just minutes from the Merritt Parkway for easy commuting. Experience the perfect balance of luxury, functionality, and location in this distinguished home.
