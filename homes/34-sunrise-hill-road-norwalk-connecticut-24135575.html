About this home

Three-level Norwalk townhouse * 3 beds * 11/2 baths * open-concept living/dining with sliders to private deck * attached oversized one-car garage * updated kitchen * pet-friendly community with pool, paddle court & playground. Welcome to this rare three-level residence within the desirable Sunrise Hill complex in Norwalk. Step into an airy open-concept living and dining area where sliding glass doors lead out to your private deck-ideal for morning coffee or evening relaxing. The beautifully updated kitchen offers modern finishes and generous workspace for any cooking enthusiast. On the upper level you'll find three comfortable bedrooms and one full bath, while the lower level presents a versatile bonus room perfect for a home office, gym or playroom-plus convenient laundry and interior access to your oversized one-car attached garage. Nestled in a well-maintained, pet-friendly community, you'll enjoy access to a pool, playground and paddle tennis court, with HOA covering landscaping, snow removal and trash. Conveniently located near amenities, you're just a short stroll from Cafe Aroma (~0.3 mi) for your daily caffeine fix, only ~0.8 mi to Stop & Shop for effortless errands, and about ~1.2 mi to East Norwalk station for Metro-North commutes. With easy access to major highways and shopping centers, this home delivers comfort, style and convenience in Fairfield County living.
