About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST ON TUESDAY, JULY 21 AT 12:00. Discover coastal luxury on this exceptional 0.75-acre property in one of Shippan Point's premier locations. This exceptional 6-bedroom home offers 5,600 square feet of refined living with water views. The home was completely renovated in 2018 with exceptional quality and designer finishes. The backyard is a true resort-style oasis, featuring a stunning 50' x 20' pool with ipe decking, year-round spa, expansive bluestone patio and outdoor kitchen all added in 2024, perfect for entertaining or unwinding in style. Professionally landscaped grounds provide lush, manicured beauty from every angle. Beyond the property, this home includes ownership in a private beach, just steps away, and is located within walking distance to the Stamford Yacht Club. Set in one of Shippan Point's most coveted enclaves, this residence combines premium living with thoughtfully upgraded outdoor spaces - a true standout in today's market. Don't miss your chance to own a slice of coastal paradise.
