About this home

OFFERS by Monday 7/13 at 5pm. Opportunity knocks in the heart of Mid City, one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. This 3,200 sq ft Colonial is a rare chance to create your dream home or your next standout project, in a location that simply can't be beat. The historic charm is unmistakable, from the elegant crown molding to the oak hardwood floors that run throughout - original details just waiting to be preserved and brought back to life. 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms across a generous, traditional floor plan. Potential runs throughout from the walk-up attic-ideal for additional living space-to the walk-out basement offering even more room to reimagine. Outside you'll find an in-ground pool and a detached 2-car garage. Additional perks include solar panels, natural gas heat, and city water and sewer. Best of all is the address. Mid city puts you within walking distance of downtown Stamford's dining, shopping, parks and entertainment, with the Metro-North and I-95 just minutes away. Bring your contractor and build equity in a neighborhood where demand stays strong. Bring your vision and make it yours. This is the one you've been waiting for.
