About this home

This inviting first-floor end unit in the Glenbrook area of Stamford is a perfect combination of style and convenience. In 2018, the unit underwent renovations, including updates to the kitchen and bedrooms. The kitchen now boasts newer appliances-refrigerator, range, and microwave-along with modernized lighting. The bathroom was refreshed with a new vanity, medicine cabinet, and a reglazed bathtub. Hardwood floors were also refinished, enhancing the unit's warmth and character. This home is ideal for commuters, located within walking distance of the train station and offering easy access to I-95. The bright living room, filled with natural light, creates a welcoming atmosphere, and the hardwood floors extend throughout both bedrooms, maintaining a cohesive and cozy environment. Heat and hot water are included in the common charges, and the unit is currently rented at $1,850 per month, making it an attractive investment opportunity. Close to Stamford's best shopping and dining, this property provides both convenience and charm in one package.
