About this home

Wake up to a spectacular sunrise every morning! Don't miss this rare opportunity to build your waterfront dream home on the Norwalk Harbor with views of the East Norwalk coastline and Long Island Sound. This buildable lot is located in coveted Shorefront Park, a quiet community of 80 homes where seasonal gatherings are held at the two private waterfront parks owned by the neighborhood association. Enjoy kayaking or paddle boarding from your own backyard or build a dock for your motor boat. When you're not enjoying the water, there is so much to explore in South Norwalk! Stroll into SoNo for brunch or dinner or to the South Norwalk Metro North station for a trip into NYC. Walk to the soon-to-be-developed Manresa Island Park which will offer walking trails and 1.75 miles of public waterfront access. Call your architect - a year-round vacation lifestyle can be yours!
