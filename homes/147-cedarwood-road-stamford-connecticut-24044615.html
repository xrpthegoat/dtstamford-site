About this home

Nestled on a peaceful, no-outlet private road in one of North Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, this stunning three-four bedroom, three-bath home offers privacy and tranquility. Set on a level corner lot over an acre, the property combines seclusion with convenience. Step inside to a spacious living room featuring a cozy fireplace, hardwood floors and built-in bookshelves. French doors lead to a large, sun-filled stone patio-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. A bright breezeway with vaulted ceilings opens to a family room anchored by an impressive stone fireplace, creating a warm space for gatherings. The formal dining room connects to a modern white kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances and everything today's buyer desires. The home offers a main-level bedroom, with two additional bedrooms upstairs allowing flexibility in choosing your primary suite. A bonus room with a full bath above the garage serves as a perfect office, playroom, guest suite or in-law setup. Recent upgrades include a new air conditioning system and hot water heater (2021), a new roof (2021) and a new driveway (2024), making this home move-in ready. Outside, the expansive backyard with mature trees, adorned with a charming swing and a stone patio creates a private oasis, perfect for enjoying the changing seasons. Conveniently located near shopping and dining in both downtown Stamford and New Canaan, this home blends peaceful living with easy access to amenities.
