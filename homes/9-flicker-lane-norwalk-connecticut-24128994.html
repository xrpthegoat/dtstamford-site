About this home

On nearly a half-acre in one of Rowayton's most sought-after and quiet neighborhoods, this property is available for the first time in over 40 years - an exceptional opportunity to create your dream home through renovation, expansion, or new construction. Surrounded by high-end homes, the setting offers both potential and long-term value. The existing layout features three bedrooms, a full bath, living room, kitchen, and den on the main level. The partially finished lower level offers a playroom/family room with walk-out access to the patio, creating an easy flow to the outdoors, along with an additional bedroom and full bath. Storage, utilities, and laundry are also located on this level. An attached one-car garage and ample driveway parking add everyday convenience. Outdoors, the generous backyard is ideal for entertaining, gardening, or adding a pool. A generator is included for added peace of mind. Situated just blocks from the waterfront, beaches, schools, and village amenities, the home also offers easy access to neighboring towns. The property is being sold "as is." Showings are by appointment only through the listing agent; please do not walk the property without appointment. Please submit all offers by Tuesday, 10/7/25 at 5 pm.
