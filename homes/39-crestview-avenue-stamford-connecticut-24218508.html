About this home

Location, Location, Location! Welcome home to this bright and inviting center hall Colonial, ideally situated in the highly desirable Springdale neighborhood. Enjoy the convenience of being just a short walk to the Metro-North train station, local restaurants, and shopping. This special home offers a wonderful blend of charm, space, and functionality. The sun-filled, front-to-back living room features beautiful hardwood floors, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliding glass doors leading to an expansive deck-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The eat-in kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, and an open flow to the dining room, currently being used as a home office. A convenient hall powder room completes the main level. The second level features four generously sized bedrooms, all with hardwood floors, including a spacious primary bedroom with a large walk-in closet. Plus a hall full bath. The expansive basement provides plenty of storage and excellent potential to create additional finished living space to suit your needs. Step outside to the large backyard, complete with a storage shed and plenty of room for gardening, outdoor entertaining, and recreation. A wonderful opportunity to own a home in one of Springdale's most convenient and sought-after locations. This home truly has so much to offer and is a must-see to fully appreciate all it has to offer!
