About this home

Your private oasis awaits! This beautifully renovated home offers an ideal location with easy access to town, shopping, and the beach. Designed with comfort and style in mind, the home features inviting living spaces including a two-story family room with an open staircase. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is open to a sitting area with a corner fireplace. Two family bedrooms with en-suite baths complete the main level. Upstairs, an additional living area is perfect for family movie nights. A large primary suite and additional bedroom suite surround the second level. Step outside to enjoy the ultimate outdoor lifestyle-lounge by the sparkling swimming pool, relax under the pergola, or retreat to the pool house before gathering for al fresco dinners on the terrace, complete with a built-in grill. The large, level backyard is perfect for outdoor fun and catching fireflies on warm summer evenings. A rare opportunity to own a slice of paradise.
