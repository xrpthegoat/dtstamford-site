About this home

Convenience and location is key! Bring your creativity and design ideas to this 1960's 3 Bed 2 Bath home on a private cul de sac in a centrally located Westport neighborhood. Main Floor features sun filled rooms with hardwood floors in bedrooms, dining room and living room with fireplace. Finished lower level adds approx 528 additional sqft and features an alcove with walk out access to back yard (possible in-law), updated full bath with washer and dryer, and a stone fireplace with built-ins, perfect for a family room, office or exercise room. Enjoy the peaceful views of the pond and brook from the expansive front deck with benches and planters. Property is an estate and being sold "As Is". Buyers to do own due diligence
