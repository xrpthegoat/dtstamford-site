About this home

Beautifully situated on a flat, fenced, landscaped 1/2+ corner lot, this elegant 4BR, 2.5 bath colonial might be the home of your dreams. The desirable Westover location, close to schools, parks, transportation and shops, is a commuter's delight. The large foyer welcomes you and the exquisite hardwood floors are immediately noticed. The original living room with wood burning fireplace now serves as this family's formal dining room. Beyond it is the addition of the dramatic, high ceilinged, beamed family room/now/living room. The first floor office (former dining room) is perfect for the new working style of today's "work from home" schedules. The 4 bedrooms upstairs have hardwood floors and bright exposures. Two full baths complete this level. So many improvements have been made to this home, no detail has been omitted. This very well might be the home for which you have waited!!!
