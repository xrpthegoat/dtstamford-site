About this home

So much house..So well located..At such a good price! If you think you can't have it all in ready move in condition then think again. Welcome to 44 Sniffen Road nestled on a tranquil street in a coveted walk to town neighborhood. Step inside & discover natural light pouring into the open concept floor plan encompassing the living & dining rms & kitchen all with hardwood floors creating the perfect atmosphere for your comfort & the ease of entertaining. With quality & style the updated kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, granite counters & breakfast bar open to the dining room with french doors leading to the deck, the perfect location for your barbecues & relaxation. The family room features media cabinet, full bath & access to the outdoor patio overlooking the amazing level fully fenced backyard perfect for play, chilling & entertaining. On the upper level are three well sized bdrms with hardwood floors & a full bath. An additional lower level with laundry provides flexible space for your needs be it office, playroom, gym or storage. And there is more..For the garden enthusiast, a deer proofed completely enclosed garden w/ raised beds. At the rear of the backyard there is a storage shed for garden or sports equipment. Professionally landscaped with mature specimen trees & perennials the property also features a stone wall, Belgian block lined driveway and paths and a stone patio. This is an opportunity not to be missed - minutes from Westport's exceptional amenities.
