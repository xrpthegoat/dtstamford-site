*Best & Final Monday 8/11 at NOON*,Discover the perfect blend of space, comfort, and convenience in this well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhome nestled within the sought-after Glen Arthur Estates community. Step inside to an inviting open-concept living and dining area with warm hardwood floors and a cozy gas fireplace-perfect for relaxing evenings or entertaining friends and family. The bright eat-in kitchen opens to a private bluestone patio, ideal for morning coffee or intimate outdoor gatherings. Enjoy exceptional commuter access-just a 5-minute walk to Glenbrook train station, 4 minutes to I-95, 10 minutes to the Merritt Parkway, and 10 minutes to downtown Stamford's vibrant shopping, dining, and entertainment.
