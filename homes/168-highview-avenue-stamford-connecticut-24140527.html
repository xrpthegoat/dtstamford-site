About this home

2 story town house with an easy-to-live-in layout. Main level has a spacious and bright living room that gets excellent sun leading into a kitchen with granite counters and dining area with direct access to the deck. A half bath completes the first floor. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has 2 closets, and the second bedroom has a walk-in closet. The hallway features an updated full bathroom along with 2 additional closets, so storage is never a struggle. Additional storage space and coin-operated laundry are located on the lower level. The unit includes 2 assigned parking spaces, and just behind the parking area is a peaceful green space, perfect for relaxing, getting some fresh air, or enjoying a quiet moment outdoors.
