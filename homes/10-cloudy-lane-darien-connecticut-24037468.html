About this home

This meticulously maintained center hall colonial on a desirable private street boasts 2.25 acres of gorgeous land and features an inviting pool, making it an ideal oasis for relaxation and entertainment. Great flow between rooms, with sunny and well sized spaces throughout, the home features living room and family room with fireplaces, white kitchen with high end appliances, spacious dining room, screened porch for enjoying the serene surroundings, and an office with built ins. Six bedrooms and four full baths upstairs offers wonderful room for all. With its spacious and well-appointed interior, along with the allure of the outdoor pool and expansive grounds, this property is sure to captivate those seeking a comfortable lifestyle in a serene setting.
