About this home

Welcome to 200 Woodridge Dr S, a charming 1964 Colonial offering 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and almost 2000 square feet of comfortable living space on a 0.24-acre lot in Stamford. This lovingly maintained home flows gracefully from the spacious living room and dining area, into the kitchen and den. The bright kitchen showcases updated countertops, a stylish backsplash, a new sink, and modern appliances--a chef's dream. Every feature is perfect for entertaining including access to the yard and patio. It features a long list of valuable updates, including central air (2018), a Generac whole-house generator (2017), a newer roof and Eversource energy audit (2019), a fresh driveway (2025), and updated water heaters (most recently in 2025). Upstairs, there are four spacious bedrooms including the primary suite with reimagined walk-in closet. Step outside to enjoy a welcoming front porch, deck, patio, and secondary backyard patio (built in 2023). Perfectly positioned to offer the ideal balance of suburban tranquility and city accessibility, this home is wonderful for all types of living situations. You will love being close to Mianus Park, the Stamford Arboretum, local beaches, great restaurants, and the Merritt Parkway for an effortless commute--not to mention all that High Ridge and Long Ridge Road has to offer. Find the perfect balance here, before someone else does!
