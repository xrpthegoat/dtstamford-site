About this home

15 Timber Lane, Westport, CT | School Year Furnished Rental Experience luxury living in the coveted Roseville neighborhood of Westport. Situated on 1.45 private acres at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this beautifully furnished 4-bedroom, 5-bath home offers the perfect school year retreat. Designed for comfortable living and entertaining, the home features two spacious primary suites with vaulted ceilings, a cathedral ceiling family room with fireplace, formal living room, private home office, screened porch, and multiple gathering spaces. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is appointed with limestone countertops and premium Sub-Zero, Viking, and Bosch appliances. Step outside to your own private oasis featuring a heated gunite pool, hot tub, expansive patio, pergola-covered deck, and beautifully landscaped grounds. Ideally located just minutes from Westport's award-winning schools, downtown shops and restaurants, Compo Beach, Longshore Club Park, Metro-North train station, and major commuter routes.
