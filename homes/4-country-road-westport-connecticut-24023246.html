About this home

Discover the epitome of Westport living at 4 Country Road, nestled on one of the town's most coveted cul de sacs. This freshly painted colonial is a must see. A circular driveway welcomes you to nearly an acre of park-like property with specimen trees, mature landscaping and a heated pool and spa. Just a short walk from everything downtown has to offer, this home is full of natural light with southern exposure in the kitchen, dining room and living room areas. A well thought out layout offers generous and functional spaces throughout. The house offers four bedrooms on the second floor and a potential in law or guest suite on the lower level with a walkout, already plumbed for a bathroom. A patio overlooks the pool and hot tub creating an amazing outdoor entertaining space or private place to relax. A truly special home, property and location.
