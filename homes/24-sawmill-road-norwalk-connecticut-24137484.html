About this home

You'll fall in love with this stately 1930 Colonial with all the charm and details of the era. Beautifully crafted woodwork throughout. Gracious Entry Hall leading to formal DR and LR/with fireplace and built-ins; Study; Sunny FR with walls of glass overlooking rose garden and backyard; bright, cheery Kitchen with vintage Pantry; even a bygone telephone room and back staircase from Kitchen leading to BR/Bath which could be used for 5th BR, Office, or Playroom. Main 2nd Floor has 4 BRs and 2 Full Baths. Two BRs could be combined with adjoining Bath for MBR Suite. There is walk-up Attic that is sheet rocked with heat and A/C, large unfinished Basement, and attached heated 2-car Garage. Lots of storage throughout! Sited on .35 acre with a beautiful landmark maple tree gracing the front yard and fenced-in backyard with very spacious slate patio...perfect for entertaining. There is extra parking area for visitors. Included is a vintage playhouse. A very special, one-of-a-kind offering ready to be lovingly updated. This estate property is being sold as-is. No disclosures. Great opportunity to own this well-built vintage home!
