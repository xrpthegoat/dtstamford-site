About this home

Modern Townhouse near the Heart of Stamford's Harbor Point Experience the perfect blend of luxury and convenience with this stunning 2-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhouse, located in the Waterside neighborhood. The main floor offers a seamless open layout, featuring a sunlit living room, an elegant dining space, and a gourmet kitchen with high-end appliances and finishes. Hardwood floors flow throughout, and a private deck provides the perfect outdoor retreat for summer relaxation. On the second floor, the master suite is a true sanctuary with vaulted ceilings, skylights, a spacious walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired en-suite bathroom complete with heated floors. The generously sized second bedroom boasts its own en-suite bathroom and ample closet space, while the conveniently located laundry room enhances everyday ease. The third level offers a versatile bonus room, ideal for a home office, guest bedroom, or personal gym. This property includes a one-car garage, an assigned parking space, and additional storage in the basement. The complex features a professionally maintained courtyard, a pool, and a jacuzzi, perfect for relaxation. Located within walking distance from high-end waterfront dining and vibrant nightlife, and just a short water-taxi ride from Harbor Point, this townhouse offers unmatched convenience and lifestyle opportunities. Don't miss this rare opportunity to own in one of Stamford's most sought-after locations!
