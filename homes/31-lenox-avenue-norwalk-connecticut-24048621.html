About this home

Located on a quiet street in the charming Brookside neighborhood, this two-bedroom home is sun-filled and spacious and is the entire top floor of the two-unit building. The large living room features built-in wall units and windows to the south and east. The kitchen offers generous cabinet space and includes a utility closet with a stacked washer dryer unit, for convenient in-unit laundry. The home has been updated with energy efficient LED recessed lighting. Central A/C. Ample storage in the attic. Shared patio and outdoor space in the rear of the property. Reserved off-street parking space available in driveway, as well as plentiful street parking. The white kitchen appliances shown in the photos will be replaced by stainless steel appliances prior to tenant moving in. New carpeting to also be installed. Location convenient to SONO, Metro-North train stations, and I-95.
