About this home

Lovely private 3 bedroom home in West Norwalk offers one-floor living. Large eat-in kitchen w/stainless appliances and granite counters is a cook's dream. Adjoining great room with vaulted ceiling and wet bar provides the perfect place to relax. Living room with fireplace, 3 spacious bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and an attached garage space round out this perfect home. Rent price includes all utilities, grounds maintenance, garbage and snow plowing. Tenant pays cable /internet and phone. The home is set back through a gated entrance and is close to Norwalk Community College (NCC), and shopping. Landlord lives on the premises. Tenants have no use of the pool.
