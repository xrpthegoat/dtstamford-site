About this home

Welcome to an exceptional country estate where timeless elegance meets modern luxury. Set on 1.37 beautifully landscaped and private acres, this impeccably renovated 6-bedroom, 3.2 half bath Colonial offers a sophisticated turnkey lifestyle in one of the area's most desirable settings. Thoughtfully redesigned with both comfort and entertaining in mind, the heart of the home is a stunning chef's kitchen featuring granite countertops, premium stainless-steel appliances, gas cooking, a walk-in pantry, expansive center island, and sun-filled dining area. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the inviting family room, anchored by a fireplace and opening to a screened porch overlooking breathtaking natural vistas. Refined living spaces include a dedicated home office, mudroom/laundry suite, and six generously sized bedrooms. The luxurious primary retreat offers its own dedicated heating and cooling zone, a private sitting area, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath complete with heated floors, double vanity, oversized glass-enclosed shower, and private water closet. The expansive walk-out lower level is bathed in natural light and designed for exceptional versatility, featuring a spacious family room with fireplace, wet bar, powder room, and a private in-law or guest suite complete with a bedroom, walk-in closet, and ensuite bath.
