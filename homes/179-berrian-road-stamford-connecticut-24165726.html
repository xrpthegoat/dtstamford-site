About this home

Welcome to 179 Berrian Road, a well-maintained ranch-style home set on a quiet dead-end street in Stamford's desirable Pepper Ridge neighborhood. Offering the ease of one-level living on a flat half-acre lot, this 4-bedroom, 2-bath residence combines space, functionality, and a private outdoor setting that's increasingly hard to find. With approximately 2,270 square feet, the home features a practical layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The single-level design provides seamless flow between living spaces. The four bedrooms offer flexibility for a variety of needs, whether for guests, a home office, or additional living space. Two full bathrooms add convenience, and the overall footprint makes the home feel comfortable and well-balanced. Recent upgrades contribute to both efficiency and peace of mind, including a newer HVAC system (2023), generator (2021), and fencing (2021). These improvements enhance the home's functionality while reducing the need for immediate updates. The outdoor space is a standout feature. Set on a flat, half-acre lot, the fully fenced backyard offers privacy and versatility-ideal for relaxing, entertaining, or simply enjoying the space. A patio provides a great setting for outdoor dining or gatherings, while additional features such as a shed and chicken coop add unique utility and character to the property.
