About this home

Stunning Townhouse in Downtown Stamford! Just a short stroll from the area's large selection of cafe's, shops, restaurants, and more! Beautiful crown moulding and oak hardwood floors lead you through this bright and spacious end-unit. The main level includes a spacious living room with a fireplace, powder room, dining room with sliders to the balcony, and a chic kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a beautiful backsplash. The second floor features two spacious, sunny bedrooms with plenty of closet space, full bathroom, and the added convenience of 2nd floor laundry! The 3rd floor master suite boasts soaring ceilings, a private balcony, walk in closet, and a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a stall shower and deep soaking tub for ultimate relaxation! This unit comes with 2 assigned parking spots adjacent to the unit. Quick drive to Metro North, I-95, and The Merritt Parkway for an easy commute to NYC!
