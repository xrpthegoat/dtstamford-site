About this home

Welcome to 11 Cedar Gate Road, a coastal-style charmer located at the edge of Delafield Island in Darien's private Cedar Gate Association. Completely renovated and expanded in 2014, this Nantucket-style Colonial offers the best of the suburban lifestyle. With 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and 2,447 square feet of beautifully designed living space, the home is filled with thoughtful architectural details, abundant natural light, and an inviting flow. The main level includes both formal and casual living areas, anchored by a well-equipped kitchen that serves as the central hub, along with a guest bedroom and full bath. Upstairs, the primary suite provides a quiet retreat with a spacious walk-in closet, plus two additional bedrooms (currently combined into one) that share a full bathroom. Step outside to enjoy peaceful, private outdoor living with professionally landscaped gardens, bluestone patios, and multiple gathering areas that evoke the feel of a Cape Cod escape. Heated 2-car garages provide ample space for vehicles, hobbies, and storage. Don't miss the opportunity to own this move-in-ready gem.
