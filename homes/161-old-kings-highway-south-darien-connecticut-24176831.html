About this home

Set in a peaceful and private Darien setting, 161 Old Kings Highway South offers a wonderful blend of comfort, space, and charm. This 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home features a large, inviting great room with soaring ceilings and a Palladium window, a gracious living room, a private dining room, and a warm family room with wood beams and a fireplace that flows into an eat-in kitchen designed for everyday living and easy entertaining. The primary bedroom suite provides a comfortable retreat with a recently remodeled bath, while two additional bedrooms, hardwood floors throughout, and a screened-in porch enhance the home's appeal. With its spacious layout and secluded setting, this is a terrific opportunity in a highly desirable location.
