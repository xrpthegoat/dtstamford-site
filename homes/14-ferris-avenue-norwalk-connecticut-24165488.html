About this home

This freshly updated 2-bedroom, two-story condo offers a thoughtful layout spread across two levels, providing both privacy and style. The main level welcomes you with a bright living room featuring just-refinished cherry hardwood floors that lead into a functional eat-in kitchen updated with a brand-new refrigerator and dishwasher. With a full bathroom located on each floor, the unit ensures maximum convenience for the two generous bedrooms which are flooded with natural light. The home also includes in-unit laundry and a rare private 2-car garage with ample extra room for storage. Perfectly situated in the heart of Norwalk just off Connecticut Avenue, this location is incredibly convenient to all major big-box stores and offers an effortless commute with quick access to the South Norwalk Train Station and all major highways.
