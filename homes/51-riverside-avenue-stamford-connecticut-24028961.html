About this home

Welcome to this spacious and well-appointed 3-level townhouse on a quiet street located in mid-town Stamford. This home features three bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, offering ample space and comfort for modern living. Upon entering, experience the main level with the living room and sliders to a private deck, and dining area, both with gleaming hardwood floors, an eat-in kitchen with maximum counter space, and a powder room for guests. The upper level, your very own sanctuary, offers a primary bedroom suite with full bathroom and sliders leading to a private deck. Rounding out the upper level are two additional bedrooms and a full bath in the hallway. The lower level offers a bonus room, laundry room which includes a washing machine and dryer, additional storage area and sliders that lead out to a patio, providing additional outdoor space to enjoy the fresh air and scenery. Residents of this complex have exclusive access to a private pool, ideal for summer enjoyment and relaxation. For added convenience, the unit includes a dedicated 1-car garage. Situated just a short walk from Scalzi Park, residents have easy access to recreational activities and green space. Stroll to nearby dining and shopping, including Bull's Head Shopping Center with a new Shake Shack, Pepe's Pizza and Starbucks all nearby. Commuter options are equally convenient, with easy access to both I-95, the Merritt Parkway, buses, and trains, making travel straightforward and efficient. Don't miss the opportunity!
