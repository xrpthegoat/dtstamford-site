About this home

Welcome to this wonderful, move-in ready, Westover ranch. This thoughtfully, well maintained home awaits it's new owner and offers endless possibilities. The inviting, large foyer opens to a sunken living room with gas fireplace which steps up to formal dining. Hardwood floors grace this area and also the well appointed, spacious eat-in-kitchen and family room/den. The den has a gas fireplace and French doors opening to the large deck with pergola, which overlooks the private backyard. On this main level are 3 of the 4 bedrooms. The primary bedroom has a walk-in closet with built-ins. The primary bath has a walk-in shower and wrap around vanity. The hallway bath, with shower/whirlpool tub, accommodates the other 2 bedrooms. This home does not disappoint! With 2 separate stairways to the lower level your extended living area awaits. One stairway leads down to a living area with a separate exterior entrance. It has a large living room with kitchenette and walk-in pantry, bedroom and full bath with stall shower. A second stairway leads to your large, carpeted bonus room with a wall of closets and built-ins. The storage/mechanicals room is located on this level. A walk up attic adds to the potential of this gracious home. The attached, spacious two car garage is equipped with a ramp leading to the kitchen area entry. This is city living at it's best: close to the vibrance of downtown Stamford, ease of commuting, and the solitude of country.
