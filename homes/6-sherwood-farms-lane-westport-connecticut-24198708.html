About this home

"Sherwood Farms"- a private enclave of upscale homes in Greens Farms designed by architect, Roger Ferris. Conveniently located near beaches, town owned golf club, tennis, sailing, train stations and all Westport has to offer in way of shopping & dining and the most desirable Greens Farm schools. Gracious interior features spacious, bright and open rooms with 9 ft. ceilings. Gourmet kitchen with granite countertops opens to a Gathering Room. Living room and gathering room feature fireplaces. Four oversized ensuite bedrooms. Fourth bedroom is a large private studio suitable as a home office or guest suite w/full bath. Outdoor amenities include open verandas and patio/terrace plus association pool and Clubhouse.
