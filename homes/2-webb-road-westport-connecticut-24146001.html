About this home

Winner of the 2025 HOBI Award for "Best In Town Spec Home" in CT! Exceptional new construction, on a quiet cul-de-sac, in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods. Walk to Post Road shops, restaurants, Winslow Dog Park, Main Street, Library & Levitt Pavilion. Only 3 miles to train! This stunning home offers 6 bedrooms, 5 full and 2 half baths across 4 finished floors. Features include a Gunite pool with automatic safety cover, a large, flat, fenced in, private yard with walk-out lower level, full house generator, EV charging outlet, and city water, sewer, and natural gas. Traditional layout with modern, high-end finishes and an abundance of natural light. Built by multi-award-winning Westport Family Homes.
