About this home

FOR SALE - 34 Baxter Avenue, Stamford, CT 06902 NRES is pleased to present 34 Baxter Avenue, a rare opportunity to acquire a standalone light industrial building in one of Stamford&apos;s most accessible and in-demand commercial corridors. The property consists of approximately 3,200 square feet above grade, along with an additional 1,000 square foot basement, offering flexible space ideal for a wide range of industrial, contractor, or flex users. Currently operated as a custom cabinet shop, the layout is well-suited for manufacturing, storage, workshop use, or owner-occupants seeking a functional and efficient footprint. The building features a drive-in bay for convenient loading, 3-phase power to support heavy-duty operations, and 12-foot ceilings providing excellent usability and functionality. In addition, the property includes a finished rear section that combines office space with a half bathroom and a one-bedroom, one-bathroom apartment setup with central air. This integrated space offers flexibility for owner use, employee housing, or supplemental office operations, with the rear portion currently built out as office space with a non-conforming apartment component. There is also potential for additional parking across the street, further enhancing the functionality of the property.
