About this home

Beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath condo offering stylish finishes and comfortable living in a convenient Norwalk location. This move-in ready home features a bright, open-concept living and dining area with gleaming hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and sliding doors that bring in natural light. The renovated kitchen showcases granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, subway tile backsplash, and modern cabinetry. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms with generous closet space and an updated full bath, while the main level includes a convenient half bath for guests. Ideally located just minutes from shopping, dining, parks, and major commuter routes including I-95, Route 7, and the Metro-North Railroad, this home offers easy access to The SoNo Collection, Calf Pasture Beach, and vibrant South Norwalk. A perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience.
