About this home

Beautifully renovated 5-bedroom, 2-bathrooms home offering modern comfort and turnkey living. Step inside to be greeted by an open layout, Gleaming Hardwood Floors and a stunning Custom-New Kitchen. Sun-drenched living room with stylish fireplace. The upper level, which offers Four Bedrooms and Two Full Bathrooms, is highlighted by a Private Ensuite in the primary bedroom with a full bath and large walk-in closet.
