About this home

Carefree living in an amazing location - just minutes to train, town, beach and Saugatuck - the place to dine! Tri level cond0. First level brings you in from the 2 car attached garage w/ a little mud room area , second level has a kitchen open to the bright and spacious living room w/ fireplace, dining area w/ sliders to small deck. Lots of oversized windows bringing in lots of natural light. The third level is the Primary bedroom with vaulted ceiling and a balcony + bath and dressing area. Additionally, there is second bedroom, full hall bath and laundry on this level. Huge Attic for storage. It's the perfect package - great living space and the perfect location! Walk across the street to the river and watch the paddler boarders or rowers go by or take a stroll into Saugatuck to dine at one of the many delicious restaurants. Just a short trek to downtown and only minutes to Westport's Jewel - Compo Beach. Commuter? Even better - its only 5 minutes to the train!
