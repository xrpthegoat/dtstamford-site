About this home

: Final Offers Due 7/1 at 5PM : One of the most coveted enclaves in Fairfield County, WINDOVER, is a HOBI award-winning 8-home, 12-acre, private community just over the Wilton/Westport line. Highly sophisticated, designer-curated & professionally landscaped, this home is sited on one of the most private lots overlooking meadows, pond & near Cranbury park. A one-of-a-kind, primary or second residence AND HOA managed for a maintenance-free, Main-level living lifestyle. The stunning kitchen/great room area is swathed in natural light from the wall of sliders opening to the elevated bluestone patio w/ custom rail system. NEW private fenced courtyard off DR and rear fenced yard with owner-curated gardens. Every room is adorned w/ luxe, branded fixtures, appliances, & designer finishes. Subzero fridge + drawers, Wolf stovetop/oven & quartz-topped island overlooking the FR fireplace, wide-plank oak 7" floors, & sleek lines of custom trim work. The expansive butler's pantry incl: barn-style doors, custom cabinetry, wetbar, 2nd dishwasher, wine fridge & quartz tops. The primary suite is sumptuous w/ its vaulted ceilings, steam shower, radiant heated floor, soaking tub & WIC. Fully equipped laundry, mudroom & WIC complete the 1st floor. Upstairs, discover 2 well-proportioned BR suites w/ en-suite baths, plus light-filled office. 2 car garage w/ epoxy flooring & garage system from Garage Living. Just completed finished lower level w/ gym, office cedar closet & workshop.
