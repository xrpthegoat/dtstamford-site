About this home

Welcome to the highly sought after East Norwalk area. This beautifully maintained and recently updated colonial offers both charm and convenience. Situated within walking distance to the East Norwalk train station, a short drive to I-95, and close to the Westport border, this home is perfect for anyone seeking a versatile and accessible living space. The flexible floor plan includes a primary bedroom suite that can also function as a private area with its own entrance, second staircase, laundry hookups and sitting room, or it can be integrated with the additional bedroom for added space. The updated eat-in kitchen provides direct access to the backyard, while the living room features a fireplace, hard wood floors, and the sunroom/family room is filled with natural light, built-ins, and access to the outdoors. The flat, fully fenced yard offers a great outdoor space, while the tandem two-car garage and partially finished basement with a fireplace add valuable storage and additional living areas. Recent updates include a full interior paint job, newly refinished hardwood floors, exterior power wash, new high-efficiency tankless hot water heater, blown insulation in the attic and an HRV radon mitigation system, enhancing the home's modern appeal. Move-in ready and conveniently located near Norwalk's great beaches, shopping, dining, and transportation options. This property combines practicality & ample space with charm in a highly desirable location. Just move in and enjoy!
