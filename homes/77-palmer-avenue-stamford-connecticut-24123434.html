About this home

Welcome to this charming cape-style home, perfectly situated on a quiet cul-de-sac just minutes from the sand and shoreline of Cove Beach. With undeniable curb appeal, the home features a welcoming front porch and well-maintained landscaping. Inside, the sun-filled living room offers a cozy wood-burning fireplace and warm hardwood floors. The eat-in kitchen is designed for both function and style, featuring granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, tile flooring, and custom cabinetry. A screened-in porch extends the living space outdoors-ideal for summer nights and entertaining guests. The main level hosts two comfortable bedrooms with a newly remodeled full bathroom, highlighted by elegant tile surrounds. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, a luxurious bathroom with a stall shower, glass door, built-in bench, and custom vanity, creating a private retreat. The backyard is fully fenced, providing both privacy and space for recreation, and includes a detached garage for added convenience. Located just 5 minutes from downtown Stamford's shopping, dining, and premier attractions, and with quick access to major highways, you're only about 55 minutes from NYC-making this home the perfect blend of suburban tranquility and city convenience.
