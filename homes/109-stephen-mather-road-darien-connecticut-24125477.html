About this home

This classic Colonial has character, charm and generous space. The formal Living Room has a fireplace and adjoins the Music Room. The spacious Dining Room has a fireplace bordered by Dutch tiles and features a custom "River" mural. The kitchen has a bright copper exhaust hood, gas stove and opens to the family room with cathedral ceiling, skylights, fireplace and French Doors to the terrace. The private library has ample space for a full selection of books and accesses a 2nd terrace. The spacious Primary bedroom has a fireplace, cedar closets and a custom marble bath with skylights. There are 4 additional bedrooms, a walk up attic and a waterproofed basement with a cushioned floor. The estate like, level property has mature plantings and complete privacy. Make this gracious home and idyllic setting your own!
