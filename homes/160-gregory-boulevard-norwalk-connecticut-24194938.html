About this home

A rare opportunity in Marvin Beach with direct views of Charles Creek, this well-maintained expanded Cape is set in one of East Norwalk's most beloved waterfront communities. Offering three bedrooms and two full baths, the home is just a quiet stroll from Norwalk Harbor. This comfortable home features a spacious primary suite with a marble bath and steam shower, plus two additional bedrooms, one of which can be used as a home office. A wood-burning fireplace and water views anchor the living space. Bright eat-in kitchen. Newer mechanicals and roof, central A/C, and irrigation system. Set on a level lot, the property also includes a detached garage and long driveway for off-street parking. Enjoy waterfront living with easy access to East Norwalk's beaches, restaurants, train station, and amenities. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy life by the water in a neighborhood people love to call home. Membership in the Marvin Beach Association is voluntary and currently $150 annually.
