About this home

Move-in ready in Prime East Norwalk Location! Welcome to 8 Platt Street, a beautifully renovated and energy-efficient home offering 1,408 sq ft of flexible living space. This 2-3 bedroom, 2/1 bath residence features city water, city sewer, and natural gas with zoned high-efficiency heating and cooling. All major mechanicals are new, along with the roof, siding, appliances, bathrooms, and more-everything has been updated within the last three years. Step inside to a welcoming entry with mudroom features including built-in shelves, hooks, and a statement mirror-perfect for keeping things organized. The main level offers an updated kitchen, spacious living/dining area, and a well-designed half bath with laundry, complete with custom pull-out hampers and hanging space. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms and a full bath provide comfort and convenience. The finished lower level offers exceptional versatility-with a full bath, laundry and kitchen hookups already in place, it's ideal as a bonus room, third bedroom, or easily converted into an income-producing efficiency apartment. Smart thermostats, modern systems, and thoughtful updates make this home move-in ready. Enjoy a perfect, easy-to-maintain backyard-ideal for relaxing, entertaining, or pets. All of this, just steps to the East Norwalk train station, Norwalk River Esplanade, restaurants, Mr. Frosty's, and Veterans Park. Experience the best of East Norwalk living-walkable, coastal, and completely turnkey.
