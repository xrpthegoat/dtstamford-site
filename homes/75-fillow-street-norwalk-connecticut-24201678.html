About this home

Brand-new 2026 luxury construction offering approximately 3,800 sq. ft. of finished living space with 4 bedrooms and 3.5 baths. This modern farmhouse features approximately 2,500 sq. ft. above grade plus a 1,300 sq. ft. finished lower level, providing exceptional flexibility for today's lifestyle. The light-filled interior showcases white oak hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, oversized windows, designer finishes, and a striking Roman clay fireplace. The chef-inspired kitchen features J&K Cabinetry, ZLINE appliances, quartz and marble finishes, waterfall-style island, premium fixtures, and generous storage. The primary suite offers a walk-in closet and spa-inspired bath with heated floors and dual vanities. The finished lower level includes a bedroom and flexible space ideal for guests, recreation, fitness, media, or home office use. Additional highlights include two central-air units, natural-gas heating, enhanced insulation, attached 2-car EV-ready garage, wine chiller, covered front porch, level 0.22-acre lot, and home warranty. Completed and never occupied, 75 Fillow Street delivers modern luxury, quality craftsmanship, and versatile living in Norwalk.
