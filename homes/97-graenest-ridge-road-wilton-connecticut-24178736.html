About this home

HIGHEST & BEST OFFERS REQUESTED BY MONDAY, 6/22 AT 10:00 AM. Exquisite Dream Home in South Wilton: A Sanctuary of Elegance and Nature. Nestled at the end of a South Wilton cul-de-sac on an elevated site, this extraordinary home blends luxury with natural beauty. Surrounded by 83 acres of Bradley Park conservation land with hiking trails and a short walk to Wilton Town Center, it offers privacy and convenience. Spanning 5,560 sq ft, this light-filled residence features four spacious bedrooms, ideal for elegant living and entertaining. With 9+ ft ceilings, hardwood floors, two fireplaces, French doors, and deep moldings, the home exudes sophistication. The open layout includes a recreational room, an 800-bottle wine cellar, and a butler's pantry. A fifth or sixth bedroom can be added in the den/office or rec room. Over $400,000 in upgrades elevate this property, including a heated Signature Gunite saltwater pool, a solar panel system with Tesla Powerwall, a Fiero Forni pizza oven, vegetable gardens, a chicken coop, and a slate patio with built-in seating for outdoor dining. Nature enthusiasts will enjoy proximity to Bradley Park, Norwalk River Valley Trail, and vast conservation land. Just minutes from NYC, this home offers luxurious living, privacy, and convenience-a true sanctuary and rare find.
