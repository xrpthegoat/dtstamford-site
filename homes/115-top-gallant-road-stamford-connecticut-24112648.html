About this home

Welcome to 115 Top Gallant Road, Unit 6 - a beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated townhome nestled adjacent to Stamford's Dolphin Cove and Southfield Point, with easy access to downtown and nearby Old Greenwich. This light-filled 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers the perfect blend of charm, comfort, and convenience. Step inside to find soaring ceilings, a cozy gas fireplace, and an open layout ideal for both relaxing and entertaining. The versatile second floor bonus space is perfect as a home office, reading nook, or additional sitting area. The kitchen and dining room flow seamlessly out to your private backyard oasis, featuring a large expanded patio-perfect for outdoor dining or simply unwinding. This home has been meticulously cared for, with numerous updates including a new roof, HVAC system, furnace, hot water heater, washer/dryer, windows, and trim. There is also an attached one-car garage! Don't miss this exceptional opportunity to own a turnkey home in one of Fairfield County's most convenient locations!
