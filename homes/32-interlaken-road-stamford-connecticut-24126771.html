About this home

Stunning historic English Countryside house built by Henri Bendel of New York as his summer retreat. This unique 4/5 bedroom, 3.5 bath, stucco and slate roof home is situated among parkland trees and an expansive lawn on a 1.1 acre lot. The gourmet kitchen has beautiful custom cabinets, granite countertops and premium appliances including a Viking Professional stove. Bathrooms are all updated and oak hardwood floors flow throughout the house. The large living area has tall walls and a vaulted ceiling with an impressive stone fireplace. Off the living area is a cozy reading/piano room for relaxing. The large master bedroom has an attached reading/exercise room. Throughout, you will find many original sconces and light fixtures chosen by Henri Bendel. Outside there are individual porches on all four sides, a fenced back lawn, and sprinkler system to keep everything looking nice in summer. You will not see another house with the history and unique features embodied in this home.
