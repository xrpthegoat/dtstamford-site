About this home

A rare and distinguished offering in mid-country Greenwich, an estate of architectural significance, ready to be reimagined for modern luxury living. The Helen C. Douglas house was designed by the renowned Twachtman Brothers, this timeless brick English country home blends classic elegance with warmth, sophistication and scale. Set on 3.8 acres of beautifully landscaped, private grounds, the property features a pool, pool house, clay tennis court, and a striking allee of chestnut trees. The estate was also featured in the 2014 film ''And So It Goes'' starring Michael Douglas and Diane Keaton. A gracious front-to-back foyer invites you into the home's classic interiors. Formal living and dining rooms are complemented by a wood-paneled library, chef's kitchen, spacious family room and additional entertaining space.
