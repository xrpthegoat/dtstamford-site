About this home

Extremely spacious end unit with four floors of usable and move-in ready living space! Freshly painted, this unit is available for immediate occupancy. Special and custom features include a private side entrance with ramp for easy and/or handicap access to the unit and an elevator from the Entry Level to the Main Living Level where a light/bright and "dine-in" kitchen with stunning black granite countertops and stainless appliances, as well as a center island, is located. Also on this floor is the generous sized living/dining area with deck access. The Second Floor bedroom level has a full-size laundry adjacent to the primary bedroom which has a vaulted ceiling, bay window and en suite bath with heated floor, jetted tub and separate shower. The second/guest bedroom is also en suite. The Third Floor features a loft style 3rd bedroom with wall-to-wall carpeting, high ceilings and a Juliet balcony accessed through lovely French doors. The oversized and attached garage provides additional storage and an alternate entry to the unit. This entry level floor provides excellent space for a den/office arrangement or perhaps a home gym... Other plus features include central vac and hook-ups for a second laundry, plus full size bath. 442 is well-located with easy access to the RT 7 Connector, Merritt Pkwy and I-95, the recently updated and expanded MetroNorth RR station, as well as shopping and dining.
