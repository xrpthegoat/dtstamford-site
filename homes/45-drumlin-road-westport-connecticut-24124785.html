About this home

Yearly unfurnished rental in a "walk to Compo Beach" neighborhood. This fully renovated home lives big with 3 bedrooms plus 2 full baths, a family room with fireplace, and an office/den w French doors to a private patio. The dining room, with built -in glass hutch, is large for entertaining or every day dining -right off the kitchen. The kitchen has granite surfaces and handsome stainless steel appliances with a gas range, plus a laundry room with LG washer/dryers located off the kitchen. The private backyard is fenced on three sides, has mature trees and shrubs and a large usable shed- a lovely place to soak in the sun and BBQ. Hardwood Floors through-out. A well-cared for home - The location cannot be beat-minutes to the Westport Train Station. Move right in and enjoy all that Westport has to offer. Available immediately!
