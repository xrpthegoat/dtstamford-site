About this home

Coastal Living at Its Finest - Compo Beach, Westport, CT Some homes are simply houses - others are a lifestyle, and this exceptional Compo Beach Colonial is unmistakably the latter. Built in 2012 and maintained in pristine condition, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath residence offers the rare combination of newer construction quality and effortless coastal elegance, nestled just minutes from the sparkling shores of one of Westport's most coveted neighborhoods. Step inside to an open, airy floor plan bathed in natural light, where a spectacular great room and chef's kitchen flow seamlessly together - crisp whites, soothing blues, and natural textures creating a relaxed yet decidedly high-end atmosphere that simply lives beautifully. When the weather calls, the oversized porch becomes the true soul of the home - the perfect setting for morning coffee, evening gatherings, and the unhurried moments that define summer on the Connecticut shore. Upstairs, the primary suite redefines comfort with a private sitting room, home office, and a spa-inspired bath featuring heated floors, a heated towel rack, and a deep soaking tub - thoughtful luxuries that make every day feel like a getaway. With generous secondary bedrooms, meticulous finishes throughout, and a location that puts Compo Beach, Westport's beloved amenities, and Metro-North all within easy reach, this is coastal Connecticut living at its absolute finest.
