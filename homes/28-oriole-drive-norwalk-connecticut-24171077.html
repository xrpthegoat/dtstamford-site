About this home

Tucked away on a quiet street, this beautifully maintained and sun-filled 4-bedroom ranch offers a seamless blend of comfort, style, and everyday ease. Set on a picturesque .31-acre lot, the home showcases hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and bright, inviting living spaces designed for both relaxed living and effortless entertaining. The kitchen is finished with Caesarstone countertops and flows seamlessly into the main living areas, creating an easy layout for everyday living and entertaining. Step outside to a standout backyard setting complete with a spacious deck, bluestone patio, and professionally landscaped grounds enhanced by irrigation and landscape lighting, perfect for both hosting and unwinding. With central air, an attached garage, and a flexible lower level, this home offers comfort, functionality, and a move-in ready opportunity in a prime Norwalk location close to everything.
