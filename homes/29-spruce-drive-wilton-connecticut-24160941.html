About this home

Light-filled contemporary retreat with a California vibe and sweeping sunset views, set on an lovely cul-de-sac. Designed for effortless indoor/outdoor living, this home offers over 3,500 square feet with open floor plan and natural light, vaulted ceilings, and walls of windows. The kitchen, dining room, and living room are thoughtfully arranged around a striking two-sided fireplace, creating a central focal point and seamless flow for everyday living and entertaining. Rich architectural character and warm wood detailing are carried throughout the home, including wood floors, coffered ceilings, and custom craftsmanship that add depth and texture. Multiple flexible living spaces provide room to gather and unwind, complemented by a dedicated oversized office, as well as convenient laundry and mud room areas. A recently rebuilt sunroom with new windows and wainscoting, along with a huge screened-in porch, extend the living space and offer bright, welcoming and comforting spaces. The lower-level family room features a dramatic fieldstone fireplace, while a private balcony off the primary suite adds another peaceful escape. Outdoor living includes a spacious bluestone patio and oversized fire pit, ideal for entertaining or quiet evenings under the stars. Recently updated with a new septic tank, new well head, refrigerator, freshly painted interior and exterior (2026), it's a rare opportunity to enjoy a home rooted in nature, and a delight to experience, just ten minutes from town
