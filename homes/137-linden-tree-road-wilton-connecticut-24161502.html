About this home

Welcome to 137 Linden Tree, a masterpiece of modern luxury and thoughtful upgrades in the heart of Wilton. This meticulously maintained home blends elegance, functionality, and high-end amenities for an extraordinary living experience. The property's curb appeal is striking, with a circular driveway, manicured lawns, curated plantings, and a full-property irrigation system. In the backyard, a custom Gunite pool with a Jacuzzi, crafted by Glen Gate, anchors a resort-style oasis. Surrounded by lush landscaping, stone patios, and premium outdoor furnishings, the space is perfect for entertaining or unwinding. A poolside bar with an 8-burner grill and refrigeration elevates summer gatherings, while safety features like a child-resistant pool cover and code-compliant fencing ensure peace of mind. Nearby, an enclosed garden with raised beds, drip irrigation, and deer-proof fencing awaits gardening enthusiasts. The inside has been freshly painted and carpeted. The family room offers a cozy WB fireplace with a gas starter, a 76" Samsung TV with surround sound and deck access. A fully equipped lower level adds another entertainment hub. Additional space is available in the walk-up attic. Smart home features abound, from a security system, automated exterior lighting, pool, and a whole-house generator. Nestled in the serene community of Wilton, this property features resort-quality amenities, elegant interiors, and smart conveniences designed for comfortable living.
