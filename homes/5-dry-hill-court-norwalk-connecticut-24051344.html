About this home

This gifted Designer's personal home has been painstakingly & artfully reimagined so that it truly deserves the designation "turnkey." 5 Dry Hill Court is positioned perfectly on a quiet cul de sac in the coveted Wolfpit neighborhood, convenient to Stew Leonards, Whole Foods & I-95. This home beckons you with its new white siding, modern masonry, & thoughtful landscaping. The flat back yard & large patio are enveloped by immaculate, white privacy fencing highlighted by the warm touch of arborvitaes. As you enter, the black front door is a nod to the mid century modern influences that lie within. This light-filled home boasts an open layout, vaulted ceilings, new contemporary lighting, inviting luxury wood look vinyl flooring, a welcoming wood burning fireplace surrounded by an organic modern accent wall, & sliders/windows that integrate the interior & exterior. The 1st floor includes: a large bedrm; full bathrm with tub/shower & a chic graphic tile; a large, expansive, white kitchen with quartz countertops, peninsula with seating & stainless steel appliances which opens into a dining rm with vaulted ceilings & sliders to the patio as well as the great rm also with vaulted ceilings & plenty of room for tv, entertaining or enjoying the fireplace. A mudrm/laundry rm completes this level. Upstairs is a large primary bedrm with a walk-in closet & upscale full bath with oversized shower & double sinks, as well as an adjacent 3rd bedrm. Truly a turnkey home, ready for you to enjoy!
