About this home

A Timeless Charmer! Picture yourself in this wonderful Antique Center-Hall Colonial Farmhouse. Bring your vision and infuse your style to these inviting spaces with its grand living room featuring a cozy fireplace, large dining room, and an oversized family room. The kitchen boasts classic cabinets and many rooms have their original hardwood floors. Relax in the quaint sunlit enclosed porch. Transform the lush 1.40 acre property with its mature trees and flowering plants into a serene retreat. A terrific standout feature, the legal one-bedroom apartment above the three-car garage. Ideal rental income or flexible options for an in-law suite, au-pair quarters or a private home office. With some fresh paint, modern updates, and a touch of landscaping, this home's vintage character can be beautifully remodeled into your own sanctuary. It's a place where the past and present blend seamlessly, offering a truly one-of-a-kind opportunity you won't want to miss!
