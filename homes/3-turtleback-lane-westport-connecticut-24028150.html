About this home

Welcome to 3 Turtleback Lane. Embrace this rare opportunity to own this 1919 historical society plaqued, turnkey, in-town home. Every inch exhibits choice finishes and sophisticated style, blending timeless charm with modern updates. The current owners have made notable improvements throughout, including interior/exterior painting, totally renovated the pool, extensive landscaping, whole house generator, new primary, new deck and guest bathroom, all new a/c, 3-week-old driveway with Belgian block, new windows and much more. Enjoy the spacious sun filled living room with high ceilings and fireplace, lovely dining room, chef worthy kitchen with granite counters and all new high-end appliances and inviting family room with fireplace and built-ins on the first level. The primary bedroom is a retreat with fireplace, large walk-in closet and redesigned bathroom offering comfort and style. Three additional bedrooms and 2 full hall baths complete the level. The lower level features a bright multi-purpose walk out room, exercise room, storage area and full bath. The exterior is stunning with pool and patio area, lush level lawn for play and lovely colorful gardens. 3 Turtleback Lane offers the lifestyle that everyone looking to live in Westport seeks: in-town walking convenience (Trader Joe's is a 5-minute walk), amenities such as Longshore, Compo and Sherwood Island beaches, shopping, restaurants, library, Country Playhouse, Levitt Pavilion, Farmers Market and the YMCA and trains.
