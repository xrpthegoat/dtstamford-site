About this home

Welcome home to this immaculate and newly renovated two-bedroom in the sought-after Ledgebrook community. Step inside to a spacious living room with a large hearth stone fireplace, perfect for cozy evenings, and an open dining area ideal for everyday meals or entertaining. The easy ranch layout means just a few steps to enter, with a private wooded back deck- fully covered-for year-round enjoyment and lake view. Updates give peace of mind, including all new windows and slider, new flooring, remodeled bathroom with medicine cabinet, new appliances (refrigerator, microwave, and stove), built in butcher block, fresh window treatments, and updated electrical with fire alarm/carbon monoxide system. The home has been freshly painted with updated baseboards, plus a security system and private locked storage unit. Residents of Ledgebrook enjoy resort-style living with a sparkling pool, relaxing spa, fitness center, and library. Stroll the beautifully landscaped grounds along a scenic walking path that winds around an aerated lake with a picturesque bridge and pond with fountain, creating the perfect setting for both relaxation and recreation. Conveniently close to 1-95 Metro North, and fabulous shopping, this home blends comfort, style, and convenience in one inviting package.
