About this home

An exceptional opportunity to rent the main private residence of a spectacular waterfront home in Westport. Set along the Saugatuck River and including a pool, this tastefully renovated home in a prime location invites you to experience the very best of Westport living. Offering 4 bedrooms and 3.5 baths, the home provides generous space for comfortable everyday living. Thoughtfully designed work-from-home options allow for privacy and productivity, while a loft flex space and fully finished lower-level offer options for a playroom, gym, additional office and more. The home is beautifully appointed with a gourmet kitchen, luxurious baths, welcoming balconies, hardwood floors, and designer fixtures and finishes throughout. Available fully furnished, every space feels curated, comfortable, and effortlessly elegant. Outside, the waterfront living unfolds in every season allowing you to enjoy panoramic views of the river flowing through downtown and out to Long Island Sound. Quiet and private, yet moments to beaches, golf, exceptional restaurants, live entertainment, and more. Convenient access to NYC, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy refined waterfront living for the academic year. Please inquire with the listing broker regarding lease and conditions.
