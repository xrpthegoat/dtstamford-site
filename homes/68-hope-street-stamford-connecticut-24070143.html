About this home

This bright and spacious tri-level townhouse offers style, comfort, and an unbeatable location just moments from downtown Stamford. The open-concept kitchen boasts granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, and an island overlooking the dining and living areas. Gleaming hardwood floors flow seamlessly into the dining and living room, which boasts sliders leading out to your private balcony - an ideal spot to relax and unwind. A convenient half bath completes the main level. Upstairs, a skylight fills the space with natural light, and two well-sized bedrooms each feature their own half bath, connected by a shared tub/shower. The primary bedroom also includes a generous walk-in closet. The lower level provides additional living space, perfect for a family room, office, or guest area, with sliders opening to a private patio. A dedicated laundry/utility room adds extra convenience. Additionally, this well-maintained complex offers low HOA fees and the convenience of two reserved parking spots right at your doorstep. Don't miss this opportunity!
