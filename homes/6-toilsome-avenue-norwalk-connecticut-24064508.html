About this home

Renovated Home In The Cranbury Neighborhood of Norwalk. Welcome to this stunning and meticulous 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home, offering the perfect combination of modern luxury, privacy and natural beauty. This home boasts exceptional craftsmanship, chic interior design, and stunning views. Abundant natural light streaming through the large picture windows, coupled with the open floor plan, creates an airy atmosphere . The exquisite kitchen is designed for both form and function. Kitchen features include an AGA range, handsome white custom cabinetry complimented by quartz surfaces and glass tile backsplash. The expansive peninsula, plus a large dining area with picture windows, makes it ideal for casual dining and for entertaining. The spacious living room is a true retreat, with a wood stove, and more picture windows that frame views of the backyard and Fourteen Acre Pond, where you can enjoy the ever-changing seasons. The master suite is a private sanctuary with its own ensuite bath. Two additional bedrooms share a beautifully appointed hall bath. Additionally, the lower level has a large family room, with wet bar, plus French doors to the sunroom, backyard and pool. For those who seek relaxation or wellness at home, this property features an indoor lap pool. The pool room provides the perfect setting for a morning swim or yoga practice.
