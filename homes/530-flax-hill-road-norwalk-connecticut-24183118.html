About this home

Rent your own private escape on nearly three acres of lush, rolling property. This beautifully renovated apartment offers the perfect balance of secluded tranquility and incredible convenience, situated just moments from Darien and Rowayton, local shopping, and just 1 mile from the Rowayton train station. Experience the effortless flow of one-level living in a space bathed in natural light, with top-tier craftsmanship and custom cabinetry. The spacious, open living area and eat-in kitchen with Corian counters look out over an expansive level backyard and oversized deck, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The primary suite is a true sanctuary, featuring a generous en-suite bath and a custom-designed walk-in California Closet. Thoughtful extras include a dedicated laundry area, an additional half-bath and three private entrances. Attached garage has extra storage space in the convenient walk-up attic. Whether you're commuting from the nearby Rowayton station or just looking for a polished, turn-key space, this property delivers high-end comfort in an unbeatable location. Please note: An additional $300 monthly charge includes parking, heat, hot water, electric, and cable TV.
