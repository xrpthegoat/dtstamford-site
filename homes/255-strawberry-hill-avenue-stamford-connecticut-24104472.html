About this home

Welcome to this 2 Bedroom 2 Full Bath unit at The Manor Association. The Manor is one of the most sought after complexes in Stamford for it's close proximity to downtown, restaurants/shops, parks, playgrounds, public transportation, I-95, and houses of worship. The open layout of this unit makes it a great space for entertaining. You and your guests could not only relax on the spacious deck and enjoy the serenity of the courtyard, but also go for a swim in the associations in ground pool, which is directly in front of you! One of the best aspects of this unit is that it's located toward the rear of the complex and you could enjoy peace and quiet throughout the day and night. This unit comes with 1 reserved parking spot, has a full washer and dryer located within the unit and there are many visitor parking spaces. Credit check, references, proof of income all required.
