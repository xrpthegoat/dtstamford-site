About this home

Rare opportunity at Owenoke Park, a private cul-de-sac situated between Long Island Sound and Grey's creek. Originally built in 1930 this classic waterfront home is something you would expect to find on Nantucket. Beautifully updated with pool, spa, dock and gardens, all just a quick 50 miles from N.Y.C. The dramatic chef's kitchen will be an instant gathering spot for family and friends. High end appliances, vaulted ceiling, a walk-in pantry and sliders to the deck and pool, with views of Longshore beyond. The main level also features a formal living room with wood burning fireplace, dining room with built-ins, an office with access to the pool and a very large family room with a gas fireplace which also opens to the pool, property and private dock. Located on the upper level the primary bedroom is a sanctuary with floor to ceiling windows providing amazing water views, plus a recently refreshed private bath and two walk in closets. There are four additional bedrooms and two baths on this level. A short walk to Westport's famous Compo Beach and Longshore Park add to the magic of this location. Minutes to downtown's shops and restaurants as well as the train and highways serve to increase the value of this property.
