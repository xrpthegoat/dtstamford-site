About this home

Beautifully renovated, exceptionally maintained, and designed to impress from the moment you arrive, 22 Heather Lane delivers the rare combination of style, space, and true move-in-ready living. Set on a gorgeous landscaped property with new siding, beautiful walkways, patios, and a lush green backyard, this four-bedroom, two-bath home has been thoughtfully improved inside and out. The main level features a bright, wide-open layout with stunning hardwood floors, effortless flow, and a beautiful deck overlooking the backyard. Both bathrooms are showstoppers - fully renovated with double sinks and high-end style. The lower-level bath takes it even further with a stunning walk-in double shower and glass doors. Downstairs offers incredible bonus space, including an oversized bedroom, family/recreation room, beautiful laundry room with sink and cabinetry, and a pristine garage with an epoxy floor. Polished, spacious, stylish, and meticulously cared for - 22 Heather Lane is not just move-in ready. It is the kind of home buyers wait for. Highest & Best offers due by 5PM Monday, June 29th
