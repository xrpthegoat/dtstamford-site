About this home

Welcome to 51 Ledgewood Drive, a beautifully updated rental home offering the perfect blend of comfort, space, and convenience. Thoughtful upgrades throughout create a warm and inviting environment, ideal for everyday living. The upper level features four well-proportioned bedrooms and a full bathroom, providing a functional and private layout. The main level offers a spacious living room, dining area, kitchen, and a half bathroom, along with an additional approximately 300 square feet family room (not reflected in the tax records), enhancing the home's open and versatile layout. The family room and fully finished, heated basement significantly expand the living space, offering exceptional flexibility. These areas are perfect for a home office, playroom, entertainment space, fitness room, or guest accommodations. The home will be freshly painted prior to occupancy, and the current dining room light fixture will be replaced, ensuring a refreshed and move-in-ready experience for the next tenants. Ideally located near shopping centers, parks, restaurants, and medical facilities, and just minutes from all that Norwalk and neighboring New Canaan have to offer, this home delivers both convenience and generous living space in a highly desirable location. Microwave not included.
