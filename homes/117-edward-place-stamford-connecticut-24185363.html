About this home

***HIGHEST & BEST: DUE TUES, JUNE 30TH AT 10AM*** Looking for that perfect suburban neighborhood feel without sacrificing convenience? This New England Colonial sits on the corner of a cul-de-sac and dead-end street in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, with underground utilities creating a clean streetscape that's hard to find; easy access to Glenbrook and Springdale Metro North stations, downtown Stamford, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, walk to the JCC, Newfield Shopping Center and houses of worship. Inside, the layout simply works; the foyer opens to a dramatic living room with double-height cathedral ceilings, formal dining room and a renovated kitchen with a rare double-island design that serves as the heart of the home; open to the family room, dining room and living room, it's built for both everyday living and entertaining. A mudroom and laundry room are perfectly positioned off the oversized 2-car garage. Upstairs are 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths, including a primary suite with cathedral ceilings, finished walk-in closet and renovated bath. The recently finished lower level adds a full bath and flexible space for a playroom, TV room, office, gym or guest suite. Set back from the street, the property offers privacy while still delivering the neighborhood lifestyle buyers love; the cul-de-sac is perfect for bike riding, basketball, hockey and gathering with neighbors. A rare combination of privacy, neighborhood feel and everyday convenience.
