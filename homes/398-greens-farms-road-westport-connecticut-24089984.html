About this home

Welcome to an exquisite rental located at 398 Greens Farms Road in the prestigious town of Westport, CT. This elegant residence offers a seamless blend of sophistication and comfort, promising an unparalleled living experience. Spanning over 7000 square feet, this home features 6 spacious bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite complete with two walk-in closets and dressing room. The residence boasts five full, two half bathrooms and a gourmet kitchen equipped with state of the art appliances, an envious pantry, a butler's pantry and a built-in coffee machine. The property is situated on an expansive 1.25 acres with waterfront views of a serene lake. Enjoy the gunite heated in-ground pool with safety cover, outdoor fireplace or drive a short distance to the beach, while participating in the vibrant amenities of Westport. This residence offers ample storage and flexibility with a 3-car garage, third-floor flex space suitable for a playroom, office, or additional storage.
