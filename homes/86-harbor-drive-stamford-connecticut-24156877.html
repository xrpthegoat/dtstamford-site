About this home

Welcome to this spacious top floor 3-bedroom, 2-bath rental in the highly sought-after waterfront community of Shippan Point! Pets considered! Nestled in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and coastal charm. This unit is filled with natural light, especially in the inviting living room with hardwood floors. The open & very large eat-in kitchen offers ample cabinetry, plus a dishwasher, oven, and Subzero refrigerator for easy meal prep and entertaining. The primary bedroom suite has hardwood flooring & includes a private full bath with a shower stall and two generous closets. Two additional bedrooms also with hardwood floors share a full hallway bathroom with a shower/tub combo. You'll also enjoy the convenience of a dedicated laundry room with full-size washer/dryer units and extra storage. Outdoor deck/balcony space located off the kitchen with a shared fully fenced level backyard. Heat and hot water are included! Off-street parking in the shared driveway. Landscaping and snow removal are taken care of, so you can relax and enjoy everything this wonderful location has to offer. Just minutes from Stamford's beaches, parks, downtown shops, restaurants, and Metro-North. Shippan Point is the ideal place to call home!
