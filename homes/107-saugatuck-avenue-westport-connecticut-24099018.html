About this home

Live in style in the Heart of Saugatuck - A Gem of a Westport neighborhood. This may be the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Meticulously maintained unit, nestled mere minutes from Westport train, shops and restaurants. The freshly painted and deep-cleaned interior space is bathed in natural light, and boasts ample room to entertain or just chill. Also well-positioned to access Westport's vibrant Downtown, famous beaches/tranquil Long Island Sound, Y, public golf course, 95 and the Merritt-this residence is a commuter's dream. The Kitchen is well-appointed; LR, BR and Bath are all spacious. And complemented by energy-efficient HVAC systems, including a programmable thermostat, quality dishwasher and conveniently located washer and dryer. All poised to ensure your comfort year-round. Easy to clean and healthy elegance of hardwood and tile flooring creates a timeless appeal. As a resident, you'll benefit from included trash removal and snowplowing, as well as one space in the off-street parking lot, steps from the building front door. Embrace the wonderful cultural amenities the Fairfield County lifestyle has to offer!! Don't miss this exceptional opportunity to make this coastal gem in Mid Fairfield County your new home. The previous tenant stayed for 9 years! Please Note: There is ONE off street parking space with this rental. And the landlord seeks a 2 year lease. Landlord requires applications using RentSpree.
