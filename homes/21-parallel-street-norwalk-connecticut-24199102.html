About this home

Charming bungalow-style home in Norwalk's desirable Broad River area, offering comfortable living with a versatile layout. The main level features hardwood floors, a bright living room, dining area, two bedrooms, and a full bath. The updated kitchen offers granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and access to a deck overlooking the patio and private yard. The partially finished lower level provides flexible space for a family room, office, or guest area, plus a second full bath. A detached one-car garage adds convenience. Ideally located near schools, shopping, dining, and transportation.
