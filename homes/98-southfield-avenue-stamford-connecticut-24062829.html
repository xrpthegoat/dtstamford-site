About this home

A rare opportunity to enjoy the ultimate waterfront lifestyle in this renovated 2 Bedroom 2.5 Bath 2-level Penthouse in the heart of Stamford Harbor. The Upper Level is bathed in natural light thanks to 15-ft ceiling with skylights, and sliding glass doors. The open-concept layout seamlessly blends the modern kitchen, dining, and living areas. The kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring custom cabinetry, sleek quartz countertops, SS appliances, and a functional peninsula. Beautiful wide-plank HW floors throughout the space add a touch of warmth and luxury. Stunning views of the city, the harbor, and breathtaking sunrises over LI Sound can be enjoyed from the Primary Bedroom, and Private Balcony where you, no doubt, will spend most of the warm months. Or cozy up by the Living Room fireplace in the colder months while still enjoying unobstracted views. 2 spacious Bedrooms, 2 luxurious Full Bathrooms and in-unit Laundry are located on the Lower Level. Smart Home systems, ample storage with California closets on both levels. Elevator building with modern conveniences such as secured garage and garbage chute. Enjoy the community inground pool, hot tub, waterfront park & boardwalk to marina and 2 restaurants, or take a complimentary water taxi to Harbor Point for even more local amenities. Just minutes to I-95, Metro-North (under an hour train ride to NYC), and the vibrant life of downtown Stamford. Available 2/1/2025. Excellent credit and proof of income required. No smokers.
