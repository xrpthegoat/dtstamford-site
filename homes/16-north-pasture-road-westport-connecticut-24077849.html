About this home

This bright and spacious contemporary home is nestled at the end of a sought-after cul-de-sac, offering both privacy and convenience. Featuring 5 bedrooms, 3 full and 1 half baths, and a flexible three-level floor plan, this home is designed for modern living. Ideally located within walking distance to top-rated Bedford Middle School and Staples High School renowned for academic excellence and exceptional extracurricular programs. Beyond its prime location, the neighborhood offers a wealth of amenities, including Wakeman Town Farm, a vibrant community space with educational workshops, gardening and programs for kids and adults, and opportunities to connect with farm animals. The expansive 1.18-acre flat, dry lot provides ample outdoor space, while a multi-tiered deck and newly upgraded patio create the perfect setting for entertaining. Residents can also enjoy easy access to Compo Beach, Longshore Golf, swimming, and tennis, experiencing the best of Westport's renowned coastal lifestyle.
