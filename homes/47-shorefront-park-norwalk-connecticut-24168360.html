About this home

Own Your Slice of Paradise. Discover the charm of coastal living in this beautifully updated and expanded Cape nestled in one of the area's most desirable waterfront communities. Surrounded by landscaped perennial gardens, this home welcomes you with inviting curb appeal and a tranquil patio perfect for outdoor living and entertaining. Inside, the open floor plan blends classic character with modern comfort. The living room features a wood-burning fireplace, graceful arches, and a sunlit bay window. It flows effortlessly into the dining room, bright and airy family room, and a modern kitchen highlighted by a distinctive backsplash, high-end European appliances, and thoughtful design details. The first floor offers two comfortable bedrooms and a full bath, while upstairs the private primary suite creates a serene retreat with a full bath featuring walk-in shower and double vanity and large walk-in closet. Located in Shorefront Park and just a short stroll to the private Beach and Kayak Parks, residents enjoy water access, picnic areas, and spots for launching small craft. The community, rich with over a century of coastal heritage, offers a vibrant seaside lifestyle. And with SONO's restaurants, museums, shops, and marinas all within walking distance, convenience meets coastal charm. This property isn't just a home - it's an invitation to live the waterfront dream. HIGHEST & BEST BY NOON ON WEDNESDAY, 5/6
