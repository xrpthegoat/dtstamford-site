About this home

Renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom condominium with a private balcony in an elevator building on East Putnam Avenue in Old Greenwich. The immaculate, light-filled interior features wood flooring and fresh paint. Two outdoor parking spaces are included, and laundry is located in the building. This well-maintained Old Greenwich building offers low-maintenance condo living, close to the Old Greenwich Metro-North train station, Tod's Point (Greenwich Point) beach and park, and Old Greenwich restaurants. A move-in-ready option for first-time buyers, downsizers, commuters, or investors seeking a renovated condominium with easy access to everything Greenwich has to offer.
