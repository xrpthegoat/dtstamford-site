About this home

This stunning 4-bedroom, 4.5-bath home is ideally located just minutes from the beach and downtown. The lower level serves as versatile family room with a full bath, wet bar, and its own entrance, perfect for guests or entertaining. The main floor features an open-concept layout with hardwood floors throughout the living, dining, and kitchen areas, with French doors leading to a private patio. The second floor includes two guest bedrooms that share a full bath, along with a convenient laundry room. One of two master suites is also located on this level, featuring a luxurious master bath with a soaking tub, and stall shower.. On the third floor, you'll find another spacious master bedroom with an en-suite bathroom. This beautiful and efficient property also has a 4 zone HVAC system, one on each floor.
