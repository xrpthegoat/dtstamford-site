About this home

Move-in ready and thoughtfully updated, this beautifully maintained residence delivers the turnkey condition today's buyers are seeking. Renovations completed within the past 20 months include a refreshed kitchen with quartz countertops, stylish backsplash, and freshly painted cabinetry, along with remodeled hall and half baths. Refinished hardwood floors span the main living areas and bedrooms, complemented by new luxury vinyl flooring in the sunroom, foyer, and lower level. Set on 1.97 acres, the outdoor spaces are ideal for entertaining and everyday enjoyment, featuring an expansive deck, bluestone patio, and a new stock tank pool. Vaulted ceilings, oversized windows, and two white stone fireplaces create light-filled, inviting living spaces. A walk-out lower level offers an above-grade office or bedroom-perfect for guests or remote work. With a freshly paved driveway and meticulous upkeep throughout, this home presents a rare opportunity for buyers seeking condition, comfort, and privacy without compromise. Walkable neighborhood, convenient to Wilton Town Center and Westport Town Center. Several options for NYC commute.
