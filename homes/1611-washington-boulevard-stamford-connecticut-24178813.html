About this home

Dive right in just in time for summer! Welcome to Washington Mews where the in-ground pool, Scalzi Park (28 acres across the street) & Mill River Park (ove 30 acres 1/2mi away) serve as an extension of your backyard, yet everything else in mid-city & downtown Stamford is at your fingertips! Step inside to a very spacious & pristinely presented 2BR, 2.5BTH townhome w/separate den & attached garage. The open & airy layout starts w/the expansive living room featuring extra-large windows - a perfect place for gatherings large & small. Eat-in kitchen shines w/stainless steel appliances & dining area bathed in natural light. Impressive Primary Bedroom has double closets & FBTH w/newer tiling. A well-sized second bedroom & FBTH, too. Den (or office & gym!) w/sliders out to private patio area is a rare find - enjoy the blue skies & summer breeze! Entry hall/mud-room area w/coat closet leads to garage & laundry area. Newer flooring, modern paint colors, newer central air & additional parking. Location, location! Head across the street for tennis, pickleball, basketball, volleyball, baseball/softball, bocce, picnic pavilions, playground & running/biking/hiking trails; or 4 blocks to Mill River Park, downtown entertainment district, shopping, farmers market, grocery, restaurants, fitness, coffee, conveniences & UCONN; 1mi to Metro North TRAIN & I-95; & just over 1mi to golf. Seize this opportunity to be in the center of it all yet have just the space you need to be comfortable at home!
