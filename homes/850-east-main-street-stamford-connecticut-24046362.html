About this home

This stunning 2 bedroom, 2 full bathroom + loft, with in-unit storage is located at the highly sought-after Eastside Commons. Year-round sunset views stream through the living room windows illuminating the first floor and loft with breathtaking natural light! This exceptional corner unit boasts impressive 9-foot+ ceilings and an array of desirable features. Upon entering, you'll be greeted by a spacious layout. The primary bedroom features an en suite bathroom and a generous walk-in closet for added convenience. The well-appointed kitchen has stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and an expansive island that provides ample storage and doubles as a convenient breakfast bar. The loft with a storage room has a bird's-eye view of the large living room and balcony, creating an open and airy ambiance throughout. Residents enjoy access to a range of outstanding amenities within the building, including a fitness center, a spacious courtyard complete with gas grills and comfortable seating, and garage parking with two elevator access points for added convenience. Conveniently located just a short distance from I-95, downtown Stamford, and the Stamford Metro North / Amtrak train station, this property has exceptional accessibility to transportation and urban conveniences.
