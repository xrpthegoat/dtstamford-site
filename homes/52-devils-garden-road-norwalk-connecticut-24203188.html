About this home

Welcome to 52 Devils Garden Road, a beautifully maintained retreat set on over half an acre in a coveted Norwalk location just moments from Rowayton. Tucked away among mature greenery, this special property offers a rare combination of privacy, tranquility, and exceptional convenience. Designed for effortless one-level living, the main floor features three bedrooms, one-and-a-half baths, and bright, inviting living spaces with hardwood floors throughout. The spacious living room, anchored by a cozy fireplace, provides a warm and welcoming setting for gathering, while the eat-in kitchen opens directly to an expansive deck overlooking the lush backyard. Outdoor living takes center stage, with a beautiful patio and outdoor kitchen creating an exceptional setting for al fresco dining, entertaining, summer gatherings, or simply unwinding in a peaceful natural setting. The finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional flexibility and feels like a private extension of the home, complete with an additional bedroom and full bath, recreation room, home office/gym, and playroom. Whether used for guests, extended family, work, or play, this versatile space adapts effortlessly to a variety of lifestyles. Embrace the quintessential coastal Connecticut lifestyle with the Rowayton train station just a 10-minute walk away, offering an approximately one-hour commute to NYC. Enjoy easy access to local beaches and marinas, as well as Rowayton's waterfront, charming shops, and restaurant
