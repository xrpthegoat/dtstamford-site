About this home

This is a stunning 3 bedroom stand-alone home designed by Judith Larson, and built by The Gardiner Group. The architectural detail is extensive and beautiful featuring generous trim, coffered ceiling, built-ins, paneling, custom closets, plantation shutters, marble baths, and generator! The state of the art kitchen has granite countertops, sub-zero refrigerator/freezer, Miele dishwasher, Wolf gas range and large island. It is complemented by a generous, eating area adjacent to an outdoor deck, wonderful for grilling and dining. The Davenport home is beautifully sited within Jelliff Mill Falls, which borders the Noroton River providing wonderful views, open green space, a carefree lifestyle, and convenience. This freestanding turn-key home is truly a MUST SEE! Per previous owner, design includes potential for an elevator. Please ask for association, bylaws and restrictions.
