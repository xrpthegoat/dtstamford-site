About this home

Resort living at its finest in this highly desirable gated association offering vacation-like amenities! This sun-filled contemporary end-unit townhouse invites you from the ante room, foyer into a large living room showcasing a 2-story soaring ceiling, designer bamboo wall & double-sided gas fireplace for gatherings. The family room flows off the living room & has a large wall of glass sliders leading to an expansive stone patio of flowering roses, rhododendron & sprawling lawn. You'll love meal time in your chef's kitchen with upscale appliances: 5-burner Viking gas stove, Miele dishwasher & double-oven wall unit making cooking a delight! An abundance of white cabinets, multiple prep areas, stainless steel frig, additional built-in wine cooler makes entertaining easy. There's also a breakfast bar for quick meals or enjoy dining fireside for warmth & ambiance. For al fresco coffee, enjoy the separate private patio accessed off the kitchen slider. Retire to either of 2 bedrooms on the main floor w/updated baths & custom closets. The primary bedroom features a vaulted ceiling w/an overhead fan, walk-in closet, slider to outdoor patio, hall laundry & beautiful updated bathroom w/heated floors, double sinks, stone counter tops & tub shower.
