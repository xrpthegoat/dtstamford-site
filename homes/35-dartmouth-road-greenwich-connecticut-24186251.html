About this home

Nestled on a peaceful cul-de-sac, this chic home boasts an open-concept layout with soaring vaulted ceilings in the family room that create an inviting and spacious atmosphere. The light wood floors enhance the natural light streaming through expansive windows, adding warmth and elegance throughout. The gourmet kitchen is connected to the family, living, and dining rooms, offering a seamless flow for entertaining. Sliding doors open to an expansive terrace, providing picturesque views of the private backyard, which features a stunning pool and spa with an adjacent patio offering a serene retreat. The second floor includes the primary suite with two walk-in closets and two additional bedrooms that share a hall bathroom. The lower level has an additional en-suite bedroom, an office, mudroom, laundry room, and a cozy sitting room. Located just minutes from shops, train stations, and top schools, this home is both light-filled and beautifully appointed.
