About this home

Come check out this fully remodeled 1 bedroom / 1 bathroom apartment in the Bulls Head area of Stamford! The unit features lots of natural light, brand new hardwood flooring freshly painted throughout. A fully remodeled bathroom, kitchen, sun room/office, (can be used as an additional bedroom, dining room all with with exquisite finishes, designated off-street parking and natural gas heat complete the unit. Location is a commuter's dream with less than 10 minute drive to I-95, Merritt Parkway and Stamford Train Station. The property is just moments away from the bustling downtown area and Ridgeway Shopping Center, offering a wide array of shopping, dining and entertainment options.
