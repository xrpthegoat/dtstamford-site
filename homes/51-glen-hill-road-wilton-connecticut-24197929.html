About this home

A rare opportunity in desirable South Wilton! Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac and set on a gentle knoll, this 2-acre property offers privacy, potential, and an exceptional location close to everything. Surrounded by significantly more expensive homes, the lightly wooded setting provides a wonderful backdrop for reimagining, renovating, or expanding the existing split-level home to create something truly special. Enjoy the convenience of being just minutes from Wilton Center, shopping, restaurants, the train, major commuting routes, and exceptional Wilton Schools while still enjoying the peaceful setting of a cul-de-sac neighborhood. Opportunities at this price point are rarely available in this sought-after location, making this an exciting option for buyers, renovators, or builders looking to unlock the property's full potential. The home needs updating and is being sold in "as-is" condition, but the location, acreage, and possibilities make it well worth the effort. Bring your vision and take advantage of this tremendous value in South Wilton.
