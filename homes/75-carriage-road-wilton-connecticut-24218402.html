About this home

Welcome to 75 Carriage, a classic oversized 4/5 bedroom Colonial on a sought-after street, just three miles from town and a short walk or bike ride to famous Kent Pond. Enjoy summer days fishing, winters skating, and the swans' annual return-a cherished neighborhood tradition. Set atop one of Wilton's highest peaks, this home offers exceptional privacy on two mature, professionally landscaped acres. Beautiful stonework, a custom oversized outdoor fireplace, and an outdoor kitchen create an ideal setting for al fresco dining. A gourmet open-concept kitchen features stone countertops, a Wolf range stove, and a Sub-Zero refrigerator/freezer. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill a light, airy sunroom, while a fireplace keeps the family room just off the kitchen cozy. Your toes will stay warm on the radiant-heated mudroom. Above the heated three-car garage sits an expansive private great room. This bonus room features a cathedral ceiling with exposed wooden beams, a fourth fireplace with beautiful stonework, and a wet bar. Upstairs, five bedrooms include three with ensuite baths for added privacy; two have oversized soaking tubs for comfort. The primary suite also boasts a radiant-heated bathroom floor and a steam shower. The finished basement includes a built-in bar, full bathroom, and 8-person sauna. Start your day with a run on the nearby open roads, or a bike ride to the pond. Room to host. Room to play. A quiet, low-traffic neighborhood to call your own.
