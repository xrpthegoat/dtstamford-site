About this home

Spacious Colonial with Private Backyard Oasis! Discover an exceptional home designed for modern living in a highly sought-after, quiet cul-de-sac location. This impressive, four/five bedroom colonial sits on a generous .61-acre lot and delivers space on every level. The main floor boasts an ideal layout: a bright living room, elegant dining room, private office and an additional room that can function as a first-floor fifth bedroom. The large, inviting family room features charming wood-paneled ceilings and provides easy access via sliders to your private backyard oasis, complete with a large deck and an inviting above-ground pool-perfect for summer entertaining! The second floor is highlighted by a huge Primary Suite, your private retreat with vaulted ceilings, a massive walk-in closet, and an ensuite bathroom with jacuzzi tub and separate shower. With three additional bedrooms upstairs and an expansive finished lower level, you'll never run out of room. Newer furnace and hot water heater. This house checks all the boxes for space, privacy, and fun!
