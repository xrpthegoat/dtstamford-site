About this home

Welcome to Sylvan Knoll Community - Owner occupied complex and pet friendly - Location, Location, Location. 429 Sylvan Knoll is located in one of the best locations within the complex with amazing park-like rear property. This inviting 2-bedroom townhome is filled with warmth the moment you enter. It is a quasi-corner unit with additional windows that provides wonderful natural light to the living room and to the primary bedroom. It boasts a remodeled Eat-in Kitchen enhanced by new quartz counters, new sink, SS appliances and new flooring. Bright living room with new flooring. Bedrooms with hardwood floors under carpeting. Newer finished lower level is a perfect home/office or a great playroom/gym space. It includes large closet area and separate laundry/utility room. Bathroom with new pedestal sink and toilet. Lovely patio to sit and enjoy any time of the day. It is freshly painted. Parking lot close to the unit makes this a complete package. Short distance to Stamford beautiful beaches, Chelsea Piers, Downtown, Highway and train station. Sold as-is condition.
