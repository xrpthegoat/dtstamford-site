About this home

Welcome to Heights Crossing, Darien's premier luxury apartment community, where quiet luxury meets effortless convenience. Located directly across from the Metro North train station, this newly constructed development provides effortless access to NYC while nestled in a vibrant coastal town. This 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence is thoughtfully designed for modern living, featuring an expansive living and dining area with an open, inviting flow. The gourmet kitchen includes Quartz countertops, a large center island with bar seating, GE Monogram appliances, a designer backsplash, and a pantry. The primary bedroom suite features dual closets and opens to a private balcony, creating a perfect retreat. The secondary bedroom also offers two closets, providing exceptional in-unit storage. Each bedroom includes its own private en-suite bath, ensuring both comfort and privacy. A spacious laundry room with side-by-side washer/dryer and a powder room complete the home. Residents enjoy secure building access, garage parking, elevators, and a full suite of amenities including a fitness and wellness center, coworking spaces, golf simulator, and fireside lounge. Trash and recycling chutes located in hallways for convenience. Heights Crossing also features a beautifully landscaped courtyard with a fountain and curated retail below, bringing the best of Darien living to your doorstep. Darien's beautiful beaches and Great Island are just minutes away.
