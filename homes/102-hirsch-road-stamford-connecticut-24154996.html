About this home

Welcome to 102 Hirsch Rd in Stamford, a fully renovated, move-in ready 3-bedroom, 2 full bath home in the desirable Pepper Ridge neighborhood. The open-concept main floor features a spacious living room with fireplace, refinished wood floors, and a stunning new eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances and easy access to the rear deck just off the kitchen and fenced backyard. The upper level offers vaulted ceilings, recessed lighting, a new full bath, and two generous bedrooms, while the third bedroom is located on the first floor. The lower level includes newly finished office space and abundant storage. Exterior highlights include new windows, doors, siding, Timberline roof, renovated front porch, double decks, new fencing, sprinkler system, detached garage with new doors, long driveway, mini-split A/C, and new combi boiler.
