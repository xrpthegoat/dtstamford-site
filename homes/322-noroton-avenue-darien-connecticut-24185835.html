About this home

Discover the perfect blend of modern luxury and suburban convenience at 322 Noroton Ave. This beautifully renovated residence offers a bright, sparkling interior where every room feels generous and inviting. From the moment you step inside, the crisp aesthetic and thoughtful updates create an atmosphere of effortless sophistication. The heart of the home is the stunningly renovated kitchen, featuring high-end finishes that flow seamlessly into the spacious living areas. On chilly evenings, the living room serves as a cozy retreat, centered around a classic fireplace. For those seeking versatility, the floor plan includes an optional fourth bedroom that can easily transition into a secondary lounge or home office to suit your lifestyle. The primary suite is a true standout, boasting a gorgeous, spa-like bathroom and impressive closet space. Downstairs, the finished basement adds significant value, providing a large recreation room perfect for a home gym or playroom. Step outside to find a large, fenced backyard-a private oasis complete with a deck designed for outdoor dining and relaxation.
