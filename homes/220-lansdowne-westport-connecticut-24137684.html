About this home

Incredible opportunity to own at Lansdowne, one of Westport's most desirable gated communities. This elegant townhouse offers spacious, sun-filled living with beautiful architectural details and serene outdoor views. The large living room features a cathedral ceiling, hardwood floors, and a fireplace, leading to a bright sunroom with sliding doors to an oversized patio overlooking a picturesque yard with a pond and summertime fountain-the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining. A formal dining room with vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, triple windows, and a wet bar, provides an inviting space for gatherings. The eat-in kitchen is appointed with granite counters, white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a nearby powder room. A desirable first-floor primary suite includes a walk-in closet, full bath, and convenient laundry. A second primary suite is located on the upper level, featuring a loft area, full bath, walk-in closet, and ample storage space-ideal for guests or a private retreat. Enjoy the ease of townhouse living with Lansdowne's sought-after amenities, including a pool, tennis courts, clubhouse, and lush landscaped grounds-all within minutes of downtown Westport, Compo Beach, and Metro-North. Experience comfort, style, and convenience in this beautifully maintained home in one of Westport's premier communities.
