About this home

Highest and best offers due by Thursday, 11/14, at 5:00. This Wolfpit split level includes 3 bedrooms in the main home and a legal 1 bedroom apartment. The main home includes a spacious living room with gas propane fireplace, dedicated dining area, kitchen with plenty of space for preparing meals and a wonderful, light filled all season porch with vaulted ceiling, skylights and access to the deck. The 3 bedrooms include the primary bedroom with full bath and double length closet with closet organizers. The 2 additional bedrooms are good sized with nice closet space. The lower level family room is spacious and includes a wall of built-ins along with a powder room. On this level is the apartment with separate entrance. The apartment includes a fully equipped kitchen, living room with sliders to a patio, one bedroom and separate laundry. The apartment is great for an extended family and the space could also be converted to be part of the lower level living area if an apartment is not needed. The 2-car garage has interior access and is large enough to also store any landscaping equipment. The home sits back from the road on a level property and includes a shed. The back deck is private and perfect for outdoor entertaining. Easy access to the "Cranbury market" and downtown Westport yet tucked away in beautiful Wolfpit. This home is ready for new owners to just move in and make it their own.
