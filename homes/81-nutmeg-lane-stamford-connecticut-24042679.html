About this home

Welcome to this desirable Newfield neighborhood offering that's sure not to disappoint. 81 Nutmeg Lane has been completely updated and is in move-in ready condition. This four bedroom home features freshly painted interiors, hardwood floors, charming fireplace and updated kitchen and baths. The first floor has an open floor plan, a renovated eat-in kitchen, living/dining room plus triple exposure family room with glass doors that's ideal for today's living. On the first floor, there is direct access to a large private stone patio, the perfect place for alfresco dining & entertaining. Upstairs, there are 3 guest bedrooms all w/hardwood floors, renovated guest bath plus a spacious primary suite. The primary has a spa-like modern renovation bath with soaking tub, oversized shower, double vanity and large custom walk-in closet. Not to be overlooked is the walk-out family room with new carpeting, 1/2 bath, laundry & flex room for office or storage space. Recently redone roof, siding, windows, spray foam insulation and natural gas utilities allow for energy efficient living. The exterior has mature trees & shrubs ample private blue stone patio space surrounded by trees in a convenient sought after neighborhood. Block Party next Saturday 9/7!
