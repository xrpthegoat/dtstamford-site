About this home

Prime Retail Opportunity in the Heart of Downtown Stamford An exceptional opportunity to position your business in one of Stamford's most vibrant and high-traffic locations, directly across from the renowned Palace Theatre. As one of the area's premier entertainment venues, the theatre consistently draws large audiences for concerts, comedy shows, and live performances, with patrons gathering in the courtyard before and after events-creating a steady, built-in stream of foot traffic right outside your door. Perfectly situated along the crosswalk connecting Atlantic Street to Summer Street, this space benefits from constant pedestrian visibility and seamless access between two of downtown's most active corridors. Surrounded by popular restaurants, local businesses, and the nearby shopping mall, the location offers an energetic atmosphere ideal for a wide range of retail or service concepts. The approximately 550 square feet of renovated retail space is currently operating as a bubble tea shop, featuring a clean, modern layout that can be adapted to suit your vision. In addition, the property includes approximately 400 square feet of basement storage, providing valuable support space for inventory and operations.
