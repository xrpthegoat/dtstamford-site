About this home

Bring your ideas to this charming 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom home in Norwalk, located in a quiet, friendly-oriented neighborhood with convenient access to Route 7, between the Merritt Parkway and I-95. Step into the enclosed front porch and into the inviting living room. The first floor also features a separate dining room, a full bath, and one bedroom. Upstairs, you'll find the second bedroom, which offers ample storage space. The basement is ready to be finished to add additional living space to suit your needs. The backyard is a level lot, perfect for outdoor activities. The property includes a one-car garage and a long driveway with space for several cars. Recent updates include a new roof, electrical system, HVAC, plumbing, and central air. Bring your vision and make this house your new home!
