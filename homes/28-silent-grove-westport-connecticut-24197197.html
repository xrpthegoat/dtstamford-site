About this home

Light-Filled Westport Rental on a Private One-Acre Cul-de-Sac. Experience the best of Westport living in this beautifully updated, warm and welcoming 3 to 5 bedroom rental home, ideally situated on a picturesque one-acre property at the end of a highly desirable cul-de-sac, just moments from Wakeman Town Farm and all that Westport has to offer. Filled with natural light, the open-concept floor plan is designed for both comfortable daily living and effortless entertaining. The gourmet fully updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and smart home technology, all while opening to inviting living spaces that seamlessly connect to the expansive outdoor deck, perfect for al fresco dining and gatherings. The main level offers three bedrooms plus a flexible office that can easily serve as a fourth bedroom. The finished lower level provides exceptional additional living space, including an oversized family room, updated full bath, and an additional guest room or office. Completing the home are a laundry room with ample storage and an attached two-car garage. A rare opportunity to enjoy space, comfort, and convenience in one of Westport's most sought-after locations.
