About this home

Rare opportunity to rent a bright, spacious apartment with 1 car garage in a beautifully restored Victorian home in the coastal community of East Norwalk. 5 minute walk to Calf Pasture beach, Cove Marina/boating, harbor waterfront, restaurants, entertainment, Metro North train station, SoNo, and pickle ball/parks. This charming apartment combines classic architectural details with modern updates and convenient location. Additional features include, dining room with built-in cabinet, large living room that can allow for guest area, large bedroom, victorian bath hardwood floors, private front and back entrance, shared backyard, and front enclosed porch. Landlord requires great credit.
