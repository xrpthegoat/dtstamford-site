About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated Center Hall Colonial, ideally situated on a private cul-de-sac in desirable North Stamford. Designed for both comfortable living and entertaining, this move-in-ready home offers exceptional indoor and outdoor spaces. A bright eat-in kitchen boasts a breakfast bar, gas cooktop, stainless steel appliances, and updated finishes. The formal living room features a wood-burning fireplace and bay window, while the adjoining dining room is accented by classic wainscoting. The spacious family room offers a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, custom built-ins, wet bar, and French doors leading to a sun filled room ideal for a home office or playroom. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a walk-in closet and spa-inspired bath with a whirlpool tub and separate shower. Three additional bedrooms share an updated hall bath with a double vanity and marble countertop. Additional features include hardwood floors throughout, a partially finished lower level with extra entertaining space and custom cubbies, central air, a two-car garage, city water, and a private back lot with professionally landscaped grounds. The backyard is a private oasis featuring a new in-ground pool, expansive patio, fire pit, spacious deck, three-season porch with skylights, and custom playground, creating an exceptional setting for family gatherings and everyday enjoyment. An outstanding entertaining home offering privacy, style and an opportunity not to be missed!
