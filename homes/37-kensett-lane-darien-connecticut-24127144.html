About this home

This exceptional Norwood townhome at Kensett is close to conservation land, offering privacy and natural beauty in a highly desirable location. 10-foot ceilings and hardwood floors throughout, with large windows bringing in excellent natural light. The well-designed kitchen features premium appliances, a center island with quartzite counter and Butler's pantry with walk-in storage. French doors lead to a private bluestone terrace. The open layout connects the kitchen to the spacious living room with gas fireplace and dining just beyond. A half bath and private den with its own fireplace complete the main level. The second floor includes a primary suite with oversized dressing room with custom built-ins and marble bath featuring dual vanities, soaking tub, and glass shower. Two additional bedrooms feature walk-in closets and share a full bathroom. Second-floor laundry (and bonus home office space) adds convenience. The finished basement with full bath and walk-in closet provides 775 square feet of flexible space with potential for a fourth bedroom and recreation area suitable for a gym, office, or family room. The attached two-car garage offers direct access. This beautifully appointed unit boasts many upgrades including two hot water heaters, custom lighting, plantation shutters and other elements sure to wow and delight. Kensett amenities include a clubhouse, pool, fitness center, and walking trails. The community offers luxury living with minimal maintenance.
