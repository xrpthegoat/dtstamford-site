About this home

New listing in Greenwich, CT. This beautiful and bright 3 bedroom, 2 1/2 bath townhome with central air conditioning is in an attractive private setting. The kitchen has granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The baths are redone. Spacious living and dining rooms are off the central foyer. Garden level has a family room/office or 3rd bedroom, full bathroom and laundry area. Covered front porch and private back deck with space for lounging, dining, gardening and grilling. Detached garage and attic have additional storage spaces. Features hardwood floors, moldings and lots of closets. Close to the beach with pool, town parks, golf, schools, downtown Greenwich, shops and restaurants. Glenville school district. Additional off-street parking.
