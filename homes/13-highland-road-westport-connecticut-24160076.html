About this home

Easy, one level living in this wonderful refreshed Ranch located on a beautifully landscaped dry, level acre on a great cul-de-sac. Located in the Coleytown section of Westport, this lovingly cared for home has many recent improvements including a fabulous sunlit Christopher Peacock chef's kitchen with Breakfast Bar and a dedicated Office with built-ins and Laundry. The flexible floor plan offers a Living Room with fireplace that connects to a spacious FamilyRoom/Sunroom, which in turn opens to the Private Back Yard with Patio, Deck and Garden Area. The Primary Bedroom has a walk-in closet and private Bath. The 2 other well-proportioned Bedrooms share another Bath. The separate 4th Bedroom has a murphy bed and an adjacent full Bath, offering a private space for guests/inlaw. Enjoy 3 remodeled Baths, newer Mud Room, engineered walnut flooring, newer windows,, newer septic (2012) exterior/interior paint and the fireplace convertion to gas. The 2nd bay of the 2 Car Detached Garage is being used for storage and can be converted back to 2 bays.
