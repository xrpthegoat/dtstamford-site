About this home

Welcome to this charming expanded Cape, beautifully situated behind a classic New England stone wall on nearly half an acre in Darien's sought-after Tokeneke area. Surrounded by mature trees, lush gardens and hydrangeas, this inviting home offers timeless curb appeal and a picturesque setting. Featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths and three levels of finished living space, the home blends classic charm with comfortable everyday living. The main level offers a welcoming living room with fireplace, well-appointed kitchen, dining room and spacious, sun-filled family room. A delightful sunroom with wood-burning stove overlooks the beautifully landscaped grounds, providing a cozy retreat throughout the seasons. Step outside to a level, private backyard featuring an in-ground pool, bluestone terrace and lush gardens, perfect for summer entertaining and alfresco dining. An in-ground sprinkler system and two-car garage with covered breezeway complete this special property. Ideally located minutes from downtown Darien, the charming coastal village of Rowayton and Long Island Sound, with convenient access to shopping, dining and the Darien train station for an easy NYC commute. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy timeless New England charm and the best of Fairfield County's coastal lifestyle.
