About this home

Welcome to this beautifully renovated cottage, ideally located on sought-after Newfield Avenue with all utilities included. This charming home blends modern amenities with cozy comfort, making it a perfect rental for those seeking convenience and tranquility. Equipped with a brand-new refrigerator, stove, dishwasher, and full washer/dryer for your ease and convenience. Stylish new vinyl floors throughout the spacious living room, which leads to a set of sliders that open up to a private patio, perfect spot for outdoor dining, relaxation, or enjoying a quiet morning coffee. Upstairs, you'll find an oversized bedroom with a dedicated closet space, offering plenty of storage. The versatile lower level can function as a second bedroom, a recreational area, or an office to suit your lifestyle. The driveway easily accommodates two cars, with additional space for guest parking. Situated in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, this home offers easy access to the Italian Center for recreation, a nearby public golf course, and quick access to the Merritt Parkway, making commuting or running errands a breeze.
