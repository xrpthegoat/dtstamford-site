About this home

This delightful, 3 bedroom, 1.5 bathroom ranch-style home is ideally located on a spacious corner lot in the sought-after West Norwalk area. The interior and exterior of this well-maintained and charming home have been freshly painted, featuring beautiful, newly refinished hardwood floors throughout. Spanning 1,496 sq. ft., this comfortable single-level living space is filled with natural light. Additionally, there's over 1,000 sq. ft. in the walk-out basement, perfect for finishing off for extra living space or utilizing as ample storage. Outside, savor the expansive yard with plenty of potential for gardening, outdoor entertainment, and recreation on a 20'x20' sports court, ideal for gatherings or weekend relaxation. With proximity to local parks, schools, shopping and train stations, this home is the perfect blend of suburban living with urban access.
