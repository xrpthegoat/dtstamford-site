About this home

Welcome to low maintenance living in Greenwich! Ideally located just minutes from Greenwich Ave and the train, this freshly painted, move-in-ready townhouse has been lovingly maintained by its original owner. Natural light fills the spacious foyer, complete with a coat closet and powder room, setting the tone for the inviting interior. The eat-in kitchen features granite countertops and a natural gas range, while the bright and open living space is enhanced by built-in speakers and oversized windows framing a slider to the private deck. Upstairs, boasts a primary bedroom with walk-in closet and two more generously sized bedrooms, each with ample closet space. Large full bath with wall storage and deep soaking tub/shower. Additional storage is available in the pull down attic. Recessed lighting flows throughout the home. The fully finished lower level provides versatile bonus space, complete with sliders to a walk-out patio and a large laundry room with utility sink. Enjoy the ease of an attached garage, two reserved parking spaces, and guest parking. With newer central air, gas heat, epoxy garage floor, roof, siding, windows, sliders, and deck, this well maintained home is truly move-in ready. Don't miss this opportunity!
