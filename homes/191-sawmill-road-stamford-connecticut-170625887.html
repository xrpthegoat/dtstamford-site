About this home

Classic Mid-Century Ranch on 1.4 coveted private Saw Mill Association acres. Gracious living on one floor. In ground swimming pool with terraced flagstone patio. In addition to 3,333 square feet on main floor, lower level has 1,600 square feet of finished space. Main Floor boasts a large family room, sun room off of the pool, extra bed room/art studio, large downstairs entertainment area with wet bar and en suite for in-laws or Au Pair which opens to its own walk-out flagstone patio. New roof, New Central AC, New Hot Water Heater. All new appliances. All hard wood floors redone, new carpeting in sun room. The exterior has been freshly painted as well as the majority of the interior. Agent Related to Seller.
