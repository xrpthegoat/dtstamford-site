About this home

Beautiful newly Renovated 3800 square foot home in lovely Westport, CT at coveted Stony Point location. Walk to Compo beach, train, shops, restaurants, longshore, pool, tennis, and golf. Well maintained home inside and outside. Open floor plan with gorgeous hardwood floors. The kitchen has all new Viking appliances, wine chiller, and Subzero refrigerator.First floor laundry room. Six bedrooms and four baths. One master bedroom on each level. The second floor also has an open office and playroom. Lovely fenced-in yard, with manicured gardens, and an eye-catching backyard bordered by stonewall and large green giants along with stunning wood patio and Neapolitan outdoor pizza oven. Green Giants also line the front yard with a long winding driveway with a large metal gate. Property surrounded in front by great 14 foot brick wall. New large storage building. Don't lose out on the opportunity to have this Westport home and Westport's top rated schools.
