About this home

Newly renovated four-acre estate majestically perched overlooking the Laurel Reservoir, complete with seasonal water views. Ideal for outdoor recreation, the property features a large, level soccer-field-sized yard and a basketball court in the driveway. At the heart of the home is an open-concept living space seamlessly connecting the kitchen, dining, and family rooms. Property Highlights- - Kitchen & Dining: The brand-new Bilotta kitchen features a center island with seating for six, premium appliances (including a Wolf range with eight gas burners, two ovens, and an additional wall oven), two dishwashers, two sinks, a microwave, ample counter space, and a large walk-in pantry with high storage capacity and enough room for a 2nd refrigerator. -Dining Room opens directly to the kitchen and comfortably seats 20. -Main Level: Adjacent to the kitchen and dining area is a large family room with skylights, a fireplace, and direct access to the outdoor deck. The main floor also includes two home offices, a powder room, and a formal living room with a fireplace. -Upper Level: Features five bedrooms and three full baths, including a primary suite with a brand-new luxury bath equipped with a soaking tub, double sinks, and a glass-enclosed shower. -Lower Level: The fully finished lower level provides excellent indoor recreation space, including a playroom and home gym. This move-in-ready home is a fantastic opportunity.
