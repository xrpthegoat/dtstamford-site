About this home

After nearly seven decades in the same family, this West Norwalk home is ready for its next chapter. Located in the desirable Flower Estates neighborhood, this home features 5 Bedrooms (one serving as an office), 2-1/2 Baths, and has 2,416 square feet of living space. Inside, you'll find a good-size Living Room, and a cozy Den with a wood-burning Fireplace. Original Wood Paneling and Built-Ins add character. A picturesque Bow Window in the Dining Room lends charm to family gatherings. The sunny Kitchen features large windows and solid Maple cabinets. The original footprint of the home was expanded by adding a Main Suite with Vaulted Ceiling, and a Home Office. Hardwood Floors run throughout the Foyer, Dining Room, Staircase, and Bedrooms. Ample Closet Space makes storage a breeze. Looking outdoors, large Picture Windows frame views of Daffodils and Redbuds in spring. The very rare native Franklin Tree offers sweet flowers in late summer, while the majestic green-to-gold Sugar Maple lends protective shade. Location is everything! The private one-acre property is covered with mature Hardwoods, and it borders the bucolic Holy Ghost Fathers "Ferndale" property, with its scenic walking trails and placid ponds. The whole scene gives visions of tree forts and play dates with school mates! Fox Run Elementary School is a short 1/4 mile walk away. The home's location is also a Commuter's Dream, with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and three Metro North rail stations
