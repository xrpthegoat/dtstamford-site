About this home

Welcome to this beautiful completely renovated modern farmhouse with hardwood floors throughout. The foyer entrance leads into a stunning living room with double height ceilings and floor to ceiling stone fireplace. The stunning chef's kitchen includes custom cabinetry, Viking appliances, quartz counter tops, and eat in kitchen. The bright and spacious dining room and family room flow perfectly for entertaining large gatherings. The upper level continues to impress. The primary bedroom features a generous walk-in closet, private spa like bathroom with a hot tub and oversized shower. The two additional bedrooms have large closets. A third bonus room and an office space included on the upper level. The sunroom leads to the deck and backyard which provides a tranquil spacious escape for outdoor entertaining. Across the street is Birchwood Country Club which includes pickleball, tennis, pool, and golf course. This spectacular home has it all!
