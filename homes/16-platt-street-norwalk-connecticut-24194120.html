About this home

Welcome to East Norwalk at Oyster Bend waterfront condominiums. Home to the Connecticut Boat Club. This recently renovated townhouse of 2200 sq.ft. sits high above the Norwalk River. This rental's main level offers hardwood flooring, eat-in kitchen, powder room, spacious living room with fireplace and balcony overlooking the river. Upstairs offers a large bedroom, hall bath and a wonderful primary suite with whirlpool bath and private balcony. Oversized walk up loft makes a great office, gym or extra entertainment space. Oversized large laundry room off 1 car garage and additional attic storage. Close to train, dining, recreation, and transportation. Rent includes water, snow plowing, garbage.
