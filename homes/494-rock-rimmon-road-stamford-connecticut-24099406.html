About this home

*multiple offers received; highest and best due Tuesday, 6/24 at 1:00PM*Tucked away in a private, park-like setting, this architectural gem in North Stamford offers over 5,000 sqft of light-filled living with panoramic views of a paisley-shaped pond, lighted fountain, and the iconic Rock Rimmon boulder. A striking example of Shed-style design with mid-century modern influences, the home features soaring ceilings, walls of glass, & eight sliders opening to three levels of decking-perfect for entertaining or simply soaking in nature. The main level centers around a chef's kitchen with Sub-Zero, Wolf, Thermador, and Bosch appliances, plus a butler's pantry with a second dishwasher & laundry. It flows into a dramatic great room with floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace and opens to formal living and dining rooms. Upstairs, the serene primary suite offers a private deck, spa-like marble bath with steam shower, jetted tub, and a cedar walk-in closet. Two bedrooms, a sunlit loft, and full bath complete the level. The walkout lower level features a 4th bedroom, full bath, wet bar, and access to the lower deck-ideal for guests or multigenerational living. New septic, new electric panel, ample storage, and a quiet setting perfect for star-gazing complete the package. Don't miss out on this one!
