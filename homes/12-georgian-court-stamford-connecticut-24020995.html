About this home

Welcome to 12 Georgian Court, nestled on a private cul de sac in the coveted neighborhood of Stamford. This exquisite Georgian Colonial home seamlessly blends the timeless art of craftsmanship with modernized improvements, meeting the highest standards of contemporary living. Upon arrival, the stately exterior captivates with its arched openings, grand front columns and architectural windows, all showcased by bright white trim against dark gray siding. The symmetrical design, along with the use of arched openings and large columns in front entrance , imparts a sense of grandeur and inviting charm to the home's entrance. Inside, the two-story foyer welcomes you with an open view down the entry hall crowned with moldings. To the right; the living room with double-hung windows flanked by full-length curtains and detail paneled millwork. A dual-sided fireplace connects to the home office with custom built-ins and glazed walls. Walk past the wet bar and through the French glass double doors to the impressive formal dining room. In the heart of the home, the custom designed chef's kitchen boasts matching marble counters and backsplash, built-in SS appliances and a large island Italian marble. The octagonal sunroom is enclosed by casement windows and a double barrel-vault ceiling with wood paneling. French doors lead to a serene outdoor oasis with a waterfall-fed saltwater pool, built-in outdoor grill & stone fireplace with beautiful flagstone surrounds.
