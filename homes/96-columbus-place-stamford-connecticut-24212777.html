About this home

Welcome to this spacious 2 bedroom, 1.5bth home is ideally located surrounded by charming towns-New Canaan, Springdale, and Darien. Minutes from the Merritt. EIK kitchen with open floor plan. Hardwood floor throughout the unit. Private laundry in basement. The complex is positioned nicely back from the road, slightly elevated offering a bird's eye view of the neighborhood. Upon arrival, notice the overhead balcony offering access & views where one can enjoy fresh air, evening breezes & distant views. The front of the kitchen opens to a generous-sized light-filled living room ideal for lounging. Sliders open to the outdoor balcony. Upper level-Master bedroom/guest bedroom & full bathroom with tub/shower. Enjoy grilling & serenity on the patio. The ground level offers storage laundry room. Attached 1 car garage with direct interior access. Water, snow removal, and one additional off-street parking space are included in the rent. Must have GOOD credit score.
