About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac on a private lane, 46 Wakeman Road is a quintessential Country Colonial set on 4 beautifully landscaped acres. Meticulously maintained, the home blends timeless elegance with modern comfort, offering bright, expansive living spaces in a serene setting. The main level features soaring ceilings, refined details, and a gracious flow. The large, elegant living room and formal dining room open to an expansive screened porch creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. A stunning kitchen with spacious breakfast area, transitions effortlessly to the inviting family room with walls of windows and glass doors creating a dramatic, sun-drenched space that accesses the wraparound deck framing the picturesque grounds. The 1st-floor primary suite is a serene haven with glass doors to its own deck, dual walk-in closets and sitting area. A handsome library/office with fireplace and built-ins offers a warm workspace. Upstairs are 3 bedrooms, including a spacious secondary suite. A large bonus room with vaulted ceiling and built-in workspaces is ideal for play, media or studio use. The walkout lower level recreation room/gym has potential for a 5th bedroom or guest suite. Outdoors, rolling lawns and mature trees create a peaceful backdrop. Additional highlights include 4 fireplaces, 3-car garage, generator, 2 laundry rooms, and a windowed butler's pantry. A rare blend of privacy, space & convenience just minutes from town, top-rated schools and commuter access.
