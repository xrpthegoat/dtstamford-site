About this home

Welcome to this renovated 2-bedroom second floor unit. Private entrance, spacious kitchen, freshly painted, with a brand-new bathroom. Private laundry room off of the kitchen, and a pull down attic for storage space. 2 off street parking spots, and shared green space. This unit is also close to Byram Beach, the pool/ park, Shubert Library, New Lebanon Elementary School, and Dorthy Hamill Skating Rink. Commuting is simple with easy access to the train station. $200/monthly utility fee for heat, water, trash, snow, and grounds care. Electric/internet is billed separately.
