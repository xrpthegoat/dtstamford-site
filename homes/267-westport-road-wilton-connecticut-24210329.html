About this home

Tucked away on a secluded two-acre property, this antique farmhouse offers exceptional privacy, fully fenced grounds, and a picturesque setting framed by mature trees. A newly added saltwater pool, thoughtful landscaping, and custom natural stone walls create a serene, resort-like retreat. Inside, historic character is complemented by grandly scaled rooms and soaring ceilings. The main level unfolds through gracious living spaces, including a formal living room with fireplace and built-ins, sun-filled eat-in kitchen, generous dining room, expansive family room, and two four-season rooms- one overlooking the pool and another with a fireplace and French doors to the deck. Nestled within the mature tree canopy, the deck offers an enchanting setting for dining, entertaining, or quiet afternoons. A private office, intimate parlor or study, and full bath complete the main level. Upstairs are three bedrooms and two full baths, including a spacious primary suite with fireplace, sitting or dressing area, and en-suite bath, plus a charming lofted playroom. The walk-out lower level offers a private fourth bedroom with en-suite bath, laundry room, and flexible space ideal for a gym, guests, au pair, or possible in-law use. Recent improvements include new Unico high-velocity air conditioning systems serving all three levels, a new 330-gallon Granby Ecogard oil tank, and a brand-new drinking water well.
