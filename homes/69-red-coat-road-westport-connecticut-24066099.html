About this home

Nestled on 2.73 acres in the prestigious Red Coat cul-de-sac, this stunning English manor seamlessly blends classic elegance with modern luxury. Designed by R. Cardello and built in 2008 by Schmiedeck Construction, this exceptional 6-bedroom, 7.5-bath estate is a true masterpiece. A private wrought iron gate opens to a stone-paved circular driveway, leading to a grand entrance framed by exquisite stonework. Inside, the welcoming foyer showcases impeccable craftsmanship. The main level features a formal living room and elegant dining room, a spacious family room with a striking stone fireplace, and a refined office/den. The chef's kitchen boasts top-tier appliances, a sunlit breakfast room, a butler's pantry, and a marble island beneath antique beams.Upstairs, four generously sized bedrooms with en-suite baths complement the lavish primary suite with a spa-like bath and two dressing rooms. The third level consists of a great room with beamed ceilings, a wet bar, and a full bath. The walk-out lower level is designed for ultimate relaxation, featuring a home theater, gym, sauna, wine room, craft room, and an additional great room with a bar. French doors lead to beautifully landscaped grounds with multiple terraces, covered decks, a gunite pool, and a built-in grill, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living. Special features include an elevator, a three-car garage, and six fireplaces, ensuring a perfect blend of timeless sophistication and modern comfort.Floor plans available.
