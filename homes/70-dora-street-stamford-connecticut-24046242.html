About this home

This meticulously maintained, bright, detached townhome offers the feel of a large single family home. The formal entry has a double closet and leads to the oversized two car garage (plus two parking spots outside of garage), as well as a separate one bed one full bath suite on the ground floor. It is absolutely ideal for an au pair, guests, in-laws, a gym, office, hobby area, and more. There are high ceilings, mouldings throughout, with an open concept kitchen, very large living space, plus a separate dining room, and lovely powder room. There is a primary suite with full bath, two bedrooms and additional full bathroom and laundry on the upper level. The huge unfinished basement and lots of closet space are great for storage. This luxurious home is in very close proximity to gorgeous Cove beach, Terry Connors skating rink, Chelsea Piers Fitness Center, Hospital for Special Surgery, and the Metro North Station. It is perfect for enjoying nature and the many more wonderful activities that the Cove offers including tennis, basketball, picnic areas, the marina, walking trails, bicycling, skateboarding, playgrounds, and rollerblading. It is a short distance to Darien, and only one exit away from Downtown Stamford! Lease duration: 6 months to 2 years.
