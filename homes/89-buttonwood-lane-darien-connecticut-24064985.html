About this home

Storybook four-bedroom home exudes charm and character on a manicured .72 level acre with inviting stone courtyard offering exquisite original details. This enchanting home features a grand living room with vaulted, beamed ceiling, a wall of windows and a wood burning fireplace. The versatile floor plan provides the choice of a master suite either on the first level or upstairs which includes two additional bedrooms. Additional amenities include 3 zone ductless split air conditioning and a fireplace in the cozy office. Nestled on level landscaped grounds, this unique residence is ideally located close to schools, town, train and shopping. Move right in or Build your dream home ! Updated Survey work has been completed and new architectural drafts are available for building. Property Sold as is.
