About this home

Never before rented, this designer owned 4 bedroom first floor residence offers a rare opportunity to enjoy elevated coastal living in one of Norwalk's most coveted waterfront neighborhoods. Built from the ground up, this newly constructed home showcases soaring 9 foot ceilings, natural hardwood floors, custom feature walls, and curated designer finishes throughout. The stunning gourmet eat in kitchen flows seamlessly into the sun filled family room, creating the perfect space for everyday living and entertaining. Two luxurious spa inspired baths add a touch of everyday indulgence. Enjoy personalized comfort with four separate mini split zones, a full size washer and dryer, private front porch, and separate rear entrance. Harbor views, immediate occupancy, and an unbeatable walkable location near the water, marina, Metro North, restaurants, shopping, and all that vibrant South Norwalk has to offer make this a truly exceptional rental rarely available on today's market.
