About this home

Contemporary townhouse-style condo in an unbeatable location close to everything. This desirable two-bedroom, two-bath end unit features hardwood floors throughout, abundant closet space, and a bright open floor plan. The renovated kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, stylish backsplash, and adjacent dining area. The expansive family room impresses with a vaulted two-story ceiling and wrought-iron railing overlooking the upper level. The first floor includes a bedroom and full bath, ideal for guests or flexible living. Upstairs, the primary suite features large closets and an updated full bath. Additional highlights include two parking spaces and dedicated basement storage. Minutes to transportation, shops, restaurants, and I-95 for easy city access. A fantastic rental opportunity not to be missed.
