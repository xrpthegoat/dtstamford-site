About this home

Silvermine Cottage: Large, enchanting Cottage with private patio located in the heart of the vibrant Silvermine Community. This unique, charming cottage offers versatile space whether you are downsizing or work from home or just want a home surrounded by nature. Natural light pours in through oversized windows on both levels. The cozy living room offers oversized windows, room for a dining table and built-in shelving. The second floor loft space would make a great work studio or additional sleeping space. Step out to the large private patio and side yard to relax, grill and entertain. A wonderful retreat close to Gray Barns, The Mercantile, Silvermine Art Center and Silvermine Golf Club.
