About this home

Start with Real Estate Rule #1: Location, Location, Location. Add #2: Great curb appeal, and you've found the perfect introduction to this classic, timeless Center Hall Colonial. Designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining, the home offers spacious interiors paired with a lovely, level property. And so much more: The gracious front-to-back living room with fireplace provides a warm and inviting gathering space, while the formal dining room comfortably accommodates 12-14 guests for memorable celebrations. A true cook's kitchen is the heart of the home, featuring two sinks, two dishwashers, three ovens including a 6-burner range, plus a full-size refrigerator and full-size freezer-ideal for hosting and daily living alike. The flexible floor plan includes a first-floor bedroom with a private, newly renovated full bath-perfect for guests or multi-generational living-along with a dedicated home office and a bright family room with French doors and skylights that bring the outdoors in. Upstairs, three spacious bedrooms offer generous closet space, while the finished lower level is warm and welcoming with a brand-new full bath, creating the perfect space for recreation, fitness, or additional living. Bathed in beautiful southern exposure, natural light fills the home throughout the day. A rare blend of charm, functionality, and exceptional location.
