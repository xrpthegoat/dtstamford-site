About this home

Spectacular quintessential New England home in Darien has it all! Step into a world of refined elegance with this stately 8,000+SF Colonial masterpiece. Set back on nearly two acres of manicured grounds, boasting a custom 2021 Gunite pool, outdoor kitchen, new patio, huge backyard and firepit, this treasured residence is the ultimate venue for indoor & outdoor living and entertaining. The main level features a soaring 2-story foyer that transitions into a formal living room w/fireplace & stunning formal dining room w/ butlers pantry, wet bar, wine fridge & food pantry. Step into the chef's kitchen w/ 16ft marble island, Miele range, dual Sub-zeros & sun-drenched breakfast area. The kitchen flows into a massive family room w/ stone fireplace & adjoining sunroom w/ 25ft stone fireplace w/French doors to backyard & pool. There's a large, office w/ custom paneling & coffered ceiling, a mudroom w/built-ins, plus a full bath, a powder room and back staircase to upper level rooms. The 2nd floor primary suite is a private sanctuary, defined by 11-foot barrel vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, 2 walk-in closets and a spa-inspired marble bath w/double sink, soaking tub & steam shower. There are 4 additional en-suite bedrooms, a laundry room & open space for study/gaming. The 3rd floor has over 2,400sqft of storage/ potential living space!
