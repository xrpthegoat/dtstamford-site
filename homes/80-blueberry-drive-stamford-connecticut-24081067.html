About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST 5PM TUESDAY 4/1. Nestled in the highly coveted Westover neighborhood of Stamford, 80 Blueberry Drive is like new construction, offering the perfect blend of modern luxury and timeless elegance. This beautiful Colonial features high-end finishes with marble countertops, Thermador gas range and oversized refrigerator, farmhouse sink, custom cabinetry, and a spacious island. The open-concept layout with 9-foot ceilings is ideal for both entertaining and everyday living. Additional highlights include a separate mudroom, butler's pantry, a dedicated office, and a formal living and dining room. The impressive primary suite upstairs offers a spa-like bath with heated marble floors, a soaking tub, and custom his & her walk-in closets. Each bedroom boasts it's own ensuite luxury marble bathroom and walk-in closet providing exceptional privacy. A centrally located laundry room completes the second level. A walk-up attic provides additional storage or the opportunity for future expansion. The unfinished basement boasts 9-foot ceilings and is pre-plumbed for a future bathroom, offering endless potential for customization. Situated on a beautifully landscaped, level lot, this home features a private backyard with a bluestone patio, perfect for outdoor relaxation. Full home generator as well as an irrigation system along with public water and sewer add to the convenience of this stunning home.
