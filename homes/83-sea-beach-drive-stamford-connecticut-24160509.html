About this home

Welcome to this stately colonial, one of only 19 homes with deeded rights to the coveted Sea Beach Association on Shippan Point. Enjoy updated living in this spacious home, with water views from most of the rooms. Professionally decorated with a comfortable/homey feel, the versatile floor plan offers plenty of options for formal and informal entertaining. The heart of the home is the kitchen which opens to a family room with wood-burning fireplace, and sliders onto the patio and gardens. The original part of the house features 6 bedrooms and 5 1/2 bathrooms, including 2 primary suites with full bathrooms, walk-in closets, and balconies. The carriage house apartment over the 2 1/2 car garage was recently updated and includes a full bath and kitchenette, perfect for multi-generational living or to use as a rec room for all ages. The fully fenced outdoor living space includes two patios, one covered, and plenty of yard to throw a ball or bounce on a trampoline. With mature plantings including perennials, hydrangeas, a vegetable garden and a rose garden, it is awash with color from spring through fall. Enjoy the active outdoor lifestyle on Shippan Point, steps away from your private beach and Woodway Beach Club, and only minutes to downtown Stamford and Metro North train.
