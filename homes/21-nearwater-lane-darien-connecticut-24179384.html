About this home

Discover elegant coastal living in this updated 4-5 bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home offering 3,553 sf of thoughtfully designed living space on a flat, private .65-acre property. Ideally located a short distance from Weed Beach and only steps from Darien's award-winning Blue Ribbon elementary school, this home combines convenience, comfort, and versatility. Located within the desirable Noroton Manor Association, homeowners enjoy private tennis courts, waterfront access, and an active neighborhood community. Inside, a beautifully renovated gourmet kitchen showcases high-end stainless steel appliances, while hardwood floors and custom millwork add warmth and sophistication throughout. The versatile floor plan offers multiple options for today's lifestyle, including a private office with its own bath that can easily function as an additional bedroom. Not to be missed are the abundant storage options and radiant-heated floors in the primary and upstairs bathrooms. Above the attached two-car garage, a spacious, versatile finished room with a half bath provides endless possibilities as a playroom, guest suite, home office, or additional bedroom. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home features an expansive 700+ sf deck overlooking the private backyard. The property is enhanced by an irrigation system and offers ample space for outdoor activities and entertaining.
