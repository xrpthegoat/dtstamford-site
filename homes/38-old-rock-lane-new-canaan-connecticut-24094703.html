About this home

Mid-Century Masterpiece expertly restored to reflect the original vision of renowned architect John Black Lee, this sun-drenched mid-century modern home seamlessly blends with its natural surroundings. Set on two park-like acres with mature specimen trees and thoughtfully designed landscaping, the property offers exceptional indoor-outdoor living. Expansive glass walls connect generous living spaces to the outdoors, including a newly renovated kitchen with an adjoining deck, perfect for entertaining or relaxing with nature views. The home features three fireplaces, a spacious office loft flooded with natural light, and a well-integrated open-concept layout. The luxurious primary suite includes a spa-like bath and a large walk-in closet. Secondary bedrooms offer built-in storage, while the flexible lower level provides potential for an au pair or in-law suite, complete with private access via the oversized garage. Located just minutes from the village of New Canaan, top-rated schools, parks, and transit, this impeccably designed home offers a lifestyle of comfort, convenience, and timeless architectural elegance.
