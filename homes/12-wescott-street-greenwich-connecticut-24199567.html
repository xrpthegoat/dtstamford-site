About this home

Fully Renovated 3BR/3.5BA in Prime Riverside. Welcome to this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath home, ideally situated on a corner lot in sought-after Riverside. Completely transformed inside and out, this turnkey home offers modern comfort, flexible living space, and exceptional outdoor space. The bright, open floor plan features hardwood floors throughout, a spacious living room with wood-burning fireplace, separate dining area, and brand-new windows. The stunning new open kitchen features stainless steel appliances, under-cabinet lighting, generous cabinetry, and abundant counter space. Three bedrooms include two with ensuite baths, plus a versatile additional room ideal as a den, playroom, office, or potential 4th bedroom. Central heat/AC, washer/dryer, and whole-house generator add to the convenience. Outside, enjoy a new roof, siding, driveway with parking for 3 cars, new landscaping, stone entry and front patio, plus a beautiful stone patio in the private, fully fenced backyard and newly built outdoor. Convenient to shopping, dining, transportation, and all that Greenwich has to offer, this is a rare opportunity to rent a completely renovated home in one of Riverside's most desirable locations. Nothing to do but move in and enjoy!
