About this home

Welcome to 78 Saint Charles Avenue, a well-maintained home offering comfort, convenience, and exceptional value in one of Stamford's most accessible neighborhoods. Whether you're a first-time homebuyer, downsizer, or investor, this property presents an outstanding opportunity to own in a location close to everything Stamford has to offer. Inside, you'll find a practical and functional floor plan designed to maximize the home's living space. The main level provides comfortable everyday living with well-proportioned rooms and a layout that works for today's lifestyle. Thoughtful updates and pride of ownership are evident throughout, creating a home that is ready for its next chapter. A standout feature of the home is the finished walkout lower level, offering valuable additional living space complete with a full bar, half bath, and direct access to the backyard. Whether you're entertaining guests, hosting game nights, creating a media room, or simply looking for extra flexibility, this space provides endless possibilities. Outside, enjoy a manageable backyard with room to relax, entertain, garden, or play. The property offers the perfect balance of outdoor enjoyment and low-maintenance living. Conveniently located just minutes from Springdale train station, shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, and both I-95 & Merritt Parkway, 78 Saint Charles Avenue combines neighborhood charm with everyday convenience.
