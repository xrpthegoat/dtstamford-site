About this home

Set on a quiet, convenient street in Westport's coveted Old Hill, this charming home captures the essence of Fairfield County living. A picturesque acre with level lawns, a sparkling pool, and expansive patios creates an exceptional outdoor retreat ideal for relaxing or entertaining. A welcoming front porch leads into a thoughtfully renovated interior with beautifully proportioned spaces and distinctive architectural details. The dramatic living room features a soaring beamed cathedral ceiling, fireplace, and oversized windows, while a central butler's pantry connects seamlessly to the dining room with its own fireplace and adjacent wine area. The sunlit gourmet kitchen offers customized cabinetry, a professional gas range, and a banquette dining area. The comfortable family room has a third fireplace. The flexible 4-bedroom layout includes a serene primary suite with a luxurious bath, soaking tub, separate shower, and walk-in closet. Additional bedrooms are spacious, including one with a private sitting area. Outdoor living is enhanced by multiple patios, including a covered space perfect for dining beside the pool. Enjoy close proximity to Earthplace, town, schools, train, and shopping.
