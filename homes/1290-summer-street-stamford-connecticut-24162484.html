About this home

Available for lease are multiple office suites in the Stamford Wellness/Medical Center on Summer Street in the heart of Downtown Stamford, CT. 5-story, 41,153 sf building with a wide range of existing successful medical services. The ideal central Summer Street location offers a wide ranging tenant mix including medical, therapeutic, wellness, anti-aging, and regenerative practices within walking distance of 12,000 downtown residents. Suites available range from 500 square feet to 8000 square feet. The layout is uniquely designed to optimize privacy for patients and staff with a U-shaped design, double interior staircases and multiple entrances and exits on various sides of the building. Tenants will be included in a dedicated property website, extending each practice's visibility to the thousands of residents in the surrounding Stamford communities. Located just a mile from both the Stamford Hospital and Stamford Health Tully Center with easy access from I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Abundant on site covered and open parking is available.
