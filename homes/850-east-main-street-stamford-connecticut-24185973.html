About this home

Fully remodeled loft-style condominium in the heart of downtown Stamford. Located on the 5th floor, this spacious residence offers soaring vaulted ceilings, abundant natural light, and an open-concept layout designed for modern living. The updated kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a large island with seating that flows seamlessly into the living and dining areas. Both bathrooms have been tastefully renovated, complemented by new flooring throughout the main living areas and new carpeting in the bedrooms and loft. The dramatic second-floor loft provides exceptional flexibility with two separate rooms, currently configured as a home office and an additional sleeping area, offering the functionality today's buyers are looking for. Enjoy your private balcony overlooking the professionally landscaped courtyard, plus the convenience of garage parking. This full-service building features concierge service, a fitness center, and an unbeatable downtown location just minutes from Metro-North, restaurants, shopping, and entertainment. Ideal as a primary residence or investment property with strong rental appeal. Fully remodeled loft units with this combination of size, finish, and flexible living space are rarely available in this building.
