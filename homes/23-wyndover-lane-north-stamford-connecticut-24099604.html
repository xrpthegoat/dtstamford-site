About this home

Welcome to this fully rebuilt five-bedroom modern masterpiece on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Westover neighborhood. Completed in 2020, this home features soaring vaulted ceilings, oversized skylights, and sleek German-engineered windows that flood the house with natural light. The chef's kitchen features Calacatta Gold marble countertops, premium appliances, a breakfast area, and a balcony with tranquil pond views. The open-concept living and dining areas are anchored by a fireplace and open to a terrace, firepit, and lush, level backyard. The walk-out lower level includes two bedrooms, a family room, a glass enclosed wine cellar, and private entrance. Smart home tech and luxury finishes complete this turnkey gem
