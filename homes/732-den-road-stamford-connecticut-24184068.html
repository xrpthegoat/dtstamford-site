About this home

Welcome to 732 Den Road, your own secluded retreat tucked away in the heart of North Stamford. Set on just over an acre of beautiful lush green-space (Landscaping Included!!), this sprawling 4-bedroom, 3.5 bath contemporary offers the perfect blend of space, natural light and everyday comfort. The main level is designed for easy living and effortless entertaining, featuring a sun-filled living room w/soaring ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace that flows seamlessly into the formal dining room. The open-concept eat-in-kitchen and family room w/a second fireplace opens through sliding glass doors to a spacious deck, perfect for summer BBQs. An office w/cathedral ceilings, full bath, powder room and separate laundry room complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite feels like a true escape w/a walk-in-closet and an ensuite bath w/a large soaking tub and separate shower. Three additional bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or additional work-from-home spaces. The lower level has a playroom, an abundance of storage and access to the 3-car garage. Additional highlights include hardwood floors throughout, central air, generous living spaces and a peaceful setting w/room to relax and enjoy nature. This North Stamford home offers a rare combination of privacy and convenience - just minutes to the Merritt Parkway, shopping, restaurants, schools, and easy access to the Stamford train station. A true slice of tranquil paradise, right where you want to be
