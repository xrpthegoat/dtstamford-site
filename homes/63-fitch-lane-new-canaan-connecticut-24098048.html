About this home

Located in the sought-after Hoyt Farm neighborhood, this exceptional 5-bedroom residence sits at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, offering privacy and serenity in a prime setting. A rare first-floor primary suite serves as a private retreat, complete with a dedicated office, walk-in closet and spa-like bath. The heart of the home is a large custom kitchen, perfectly designed for both daily living and entertaining, and complemented by an adjacent breakfast room featuring a striking shiplap-paneled vaulted ceiling. The formal living room exudes elegance with a gas fireplace and detailed coffered ceiling, while the expansive family room offers built-in cabinetry and sliders that open to a spacious outdoor deck-ideal for seamless indoor-outdoor living. Enjoy quiet mornings or summer evenings in the charming screened porch, surrounded by nature. The enormous finished lower level provides endless versatility with multiple living spaces, perfect for a media room or play area, half bath and a dedicated exercise room This meticulously maintained home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and style in one of the area's most desirable neighborhoods.
