About this home

What a terrific, convenient location near beautiful Holly Pond and an opportunity to make this house your own! Sited on a nice, level .20/acre lot on a great street in the Cove, this home awaits your loving care. There is a living room, large kichen, dining room and two bedrooms. The home has a gas furnace (2021), gas hot water heater as well as a gas stove. Close to Cove Island Park, which offers beaches, a marina and skating rink, as well as the terrific Chelsea Piers sports complex which is just up the road! Shopping, restaurants and public transportation are near by too! Please note, this property is an estate sale and will be sold in as-is condition.
