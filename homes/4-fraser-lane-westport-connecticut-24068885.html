About this home

4 Fraser Lane is a one-of-a-kind beauty nestled in at the end of a private cul-de-sac. Beyond the gated entrance, you will see how truly magnificent this home is. Sited on 2+ meticulously manicured acres the property features a stunning heated Gunite pool and spa. With over 10,000 sq. ft. of luxury living space, this home is a perfect vacation destination. A gracious foyer invites you to the formal living room with a beautiful stone fireplace and French doors that open to a covered stone porch and gardens. The formal dining room with wet bar leads to the eat-in gourmet cook's kitchen. Step down into the double height family room with floor to ceiling windows, expansive ceiling beams and views of the gardens and pool. Professional outdoor kitchen is perfect for summer BBQ's and gatherings with friends. On the second floor, the primary bedroom suite includes a generous walk-in closet and changing area. There are 6 additional bedrooms all with ensuite baths, an impressive laundry room, and mudroom. The lower level features a family/playroom, half bath, and a full exercise room. An additional guest room above the garage with a private entrance is perfect for summer guests. 4 Fraser Lane is the premier location for an exciting and memorable summer!
