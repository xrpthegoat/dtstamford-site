About this home

Stunning retreat in Fairfield County's favorite beach town! Nestled perfectly between the serene beach and a quick walk to town, this exquisite home in offers the best of all worlds. High and dry, but with quick access to everything that makes Rowayton special, but also completely private, 7 highland has it all. Meticulously taken down to the studs and remodeled by the current owners, this residence boasts impressive 10+ ft ceilings in the kitchen and dining, and 12 + ft ceilings in the living room. The open floor plan allows for seamless indoor/ outdoor flow. French doors in the dining room and oversized living room lead to the beautifully manicured back patio. The home features garage, offering ample space to park, or the potential for a creative bonus space. The master suite is its own private level, with a cozy bedroom leading to a dressing hall and a luxurious bathroom. Just a few steps down from the main floor, you'll find two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom, each with private access to the front patio. There is a large dining space, seating area, and a built in grill. The grounds are truly impressive. This house is an exceptional blend of modern luxury and beach town charm, offering an unparalleled living experience. Don't miss your chance to call this private oasis your home.
