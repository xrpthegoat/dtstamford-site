About this home

Nestled in a serene woodland setting, surrounded by charming old stone walls, this inviting home offers modern comfort with a newer roof, air conditioning, and a Nest system. Step through the sunlit two-story foyer into spacious living and dining areas, freshly painted interior, featuring hardwood floors and abundant natural light from multiple windows. The updated kitchen boasts quartz countertops, a breakfast bar, a farmhouse sink, and stainless steel appliances, flowing seamlessly onto a generous deck w/ string lighting overlooking the terraced yard complete with a fire pit and outdoor hot tub. The main level includes a primary suite with a newly refreshed en-suite bath, plus two additional bedrooms and a second full bath also just refreshed. On the lower level, you'll find a large family room with a cozy wood stove, a bar with a beverage refrigerator, a fourth bedroom, a full bath, and a laundry room. This level also provides access to the sizable two-car garage. Conveniently located just minutes from I-95, the Merritt Parkway, schools, and local amenities, this property offers the perfect balance of tranquility and accessibility for an easy commute.
