About this home

Beautiful single family home in the sought-after Westover area. As you enter the home you're welcomed by the entry foyer which leads to a spacious living room featuring hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and sliders opening to a family room. The generously-sized dining room flows seamlessly into the large eat-in kitchen, which boasts built-in seating and Silestone countertops. The kitchen opens to a den with a gas stove and a bay window, providing easy access to the driveway. A second powder room, along with a laundry area and entry to the two-car garage, is conveniently located off the kitchen. As we head into the bedrooms the primary bedroom has a walk-in closet, two additional closets, and a full remodeled en-suite bathroom. Three more bedrooms and another newer full bath complete this home. The spectacular yard is perfect for outdoor fun, and entertaining family and friends surrounded by lush plantings and bordered by Water Company property, ensuring privacy. This home is just minutes from the Merritt Parkway and a short drive to schools, and nearby amenities.
