About this home

Perched atop Nylked Terrace in Rowayton's private Pine Point Beach Association, this fully renovated (2018), turnkey 3-bedroom, 3-bath Cape offers direct, elevated views of Long Island Sound and the newly restored Greens Ledge Light. Best of all, enjoy coastal living with private access to Crescent Beach via a historic stone trolley path, and the peace of mind of a non-flood zone location. The main level boasts an open-concept layout centered around beautiful water views from the living room with a gas fireplace, a bright dining area, and a gourmet kitchen equipped with quartz countertops, top-tier appliances, and custom shiplap details. A spacious Ipe deck spans the rear of the home, framing spectacular water views for effortless indoor-outdoor entertaining. A large family room, a quiet office, and a full bath complete the first floor. Upstairs, the primary suite captures peaceful water views with a private balcony for quiet mornings. The spa-like primary bath features a glass-enclosed wet room with a deep soaking tub and double shower, complemented by both traditional and walk-in closets. Two secondary bedrooms share a hall bath, located alongside a convenient second-floor laundry room. The finished walk-out lower level offers flexible bonus space, garage access, and an outdoor shower. Stunning water views, timeless high-end finishes, and private beach association perks on a most desirable coastal peninsula. Welcome home!
