About this home

Overlooking a picturesque golf course, this beautifully updated 3-level townhouse offers a rare blend of space, light, & privacy in a prime Stamford location. The home is filled with natural light & features a clean, modern aesthetic with new flooring, fresh paint, & thoughtfully curated updates throughout. The main level is designed for both everyday living & entertaining, with seamless flow between the living & dining areas & a recently renovated kitchen with stainless steel appliances. A private back deck provides a quiet outdoor retreat. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms offer a comfortable & serene escape, complemented by a well-appointed full bath. The fully finished lower level adds exceptional versatility, ideal as a recreation room/home office/guest space, & includes a second full bath. Additional features include in-unit laundry, 2 assigned parking spaces, & ample guest parking, all within a well-maintained & peaceful community setting. Ideally located near the Greenwich border, moments from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, Old Greenwich & Scalzi Park, this home offers both convenience & a sense of retreat.
