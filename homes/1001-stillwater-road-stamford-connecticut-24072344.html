About this home

Welcome to your serene riverside retreat in Stamford, CT! This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 3-bath single-family home offers a perfect blend of comfort, space, and natural beauty. Each bedroom offers ample closet space, and the primary suite includes its own private bath. Step outside to enjoy morning coffee or evening gatherings by the water - your private slice of nature awaits. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, shopping, dining, and more. A must-see for those seeking both convenience and calm.
