About this home

Nestled in one of West Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods, this spacious split-level home offers the perfect blend of comfort, flexibility, and privacy. Featuring 5 bedrooms and 4 full baths, including a separate 2-bedroom in-law apartment with its own private entrance, this home is ideal for extended family or multi-generational living. Filled with natural sunlight, the home offers hardwood floors throughout, crown molding, and a welcoming living room with fireplace. The updated kitchen features quartz countertops, center island, and stainless steel appliances. Enjoy the enclosed porch, covered deck, and an oasis-like backyard with plenty of space for entertaining and relaxing in privacy. The main home includes central air, while the in-law apartment features its own split-system heating/cooling. A rare opportunity in sought-after West Norwalk with space, versatility, and charm.
