About this home

The one you've been waiting for! Rarely does a home offer the character of an established neighborhood with the quality and convenience of essentially brand-new construction. Completely renovated from top to bottom, this turnkey 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home is perfect as a starter home, condo alternative, or right-sizing opportunity.
