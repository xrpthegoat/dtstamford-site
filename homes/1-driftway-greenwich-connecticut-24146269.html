About this home

Coveted, close to the water location boasts a recently updated four-bedroom Colonial on a peaceful cul-de-sac in close proximity to the park with beach, new pool, tennis and dock. This move-in ready home features newly refinished wood floors, freshly painted interior/exterior as well as updated kitchen and baths. Sundrenched entry, enhanced by multiple skylights, leads into the living room with fireplace, dining room with bay windows, chef's granite-clad, eat-in kitchen with island and deck access, den/playroom, main level bedroom and full bath. Second floor provides primary suite with vaulted beamed ceiling, walk-in closets, spa bath, adjacent sitting room/office with bookcases as well as two bedrooms, full bath, half bath and laundry. With FAR available up to add on or build new.
