About this home

Looking for a bright home in Westport, CT, close to the train, beaches, and commuting routes to New York City? Welcome to 104 Newtown Turnpike, a light-filled 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offering privacy, comfort, and exceptional convenience in one of Westport's most accessible locations. Set high above the road, the home enjoys beautiful natural light and a peaceful setting, creating a bright and airy atmosphere throughout the living spaces. The heart of the home is the beautifully updated kitchen, which opens seamlessly into the sun-filled family room with a fireplace, creating a warm and inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. The home features two fireplaces, adding charm and comfort throughout. Large windows bring in abundant sunlight, giving the home a light, welcoming feeling year-round. Step outside to a lovely private patio, perfect for outdoor dining, relaxing with friends, or enjoying quiet mornings surrounded by nature. The location offers the best of Westport living with easy access for NYC commuters. The home is conveniently located near: * The Merritt Parkway for an easy commute to New York City * The Westport Metro-North train station * Compo Beach and Longshore Club Park * Downtown Westport's vibrant restaurants, shopping, and cultural attractions. Ideally located near beaches, downtown, and NYC commuter routes, 104 Newtown Turnpike offers comfort, convenience, and the best of Westport living. A bright, light-filled home ready for its next owner.
