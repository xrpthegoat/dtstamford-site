About this home

This neo-colonial home brings together timeless architectural style suitable for modern living. Situated on nearly one-acre, 62 West Norwalk Road offers both privacy and convenience, located minutes from town, transportation, and every day amenities. The open concept first floor offers nine-foot ceilings adorned with wood slats and exposed ceiling joists, polished concrete floors equipped with radiant heat, steel beams, and Anderson windows throughout. Kitchen is furnished with Carrara marble counters and flows into the dining area, living room with gas fireplace, and separate office. A unique three-story stair floats within a timber frame structure, wrapped in floor to ceiling windows on all three sides. The primary suite is a true retreat with cathedral ceilings, private deck, large walk-in closet, and a light filled bathroom outfitted with Kohler fixtures, double sinks, soaking tub, and glass shower. Need a playroom, gym or just more space? This walk-out basement is an original featuring corrugated steel ceiling and full height storage areas with a work bench. A glass door leads you to a large back patio and level backyard, which is buffered by serene woodland. Newer mechanicals with targeted zoning allow for more control and higher energy efficiency. Underground power, town water and sewer provide reliability. A designed and distinctive property.
