About this home

This beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom multi-level condo boasts a seamless flow with an integrated 2-car garage, and is ideally located near downtown Stamford, Ridgeway Shopping Center, and Scalzi Park. The spacious living room opens into the dining area, creating an inviting, open-concept space. The modern kitchen is equipped with a brand-new refrigerator, dishwasher, oven hood, and microwave. New hardwood floors grace the 2nd and 3rd floors, adding warmth throughout. The master bedroom features a private en-suite with a whirlpool tub, separate shower, and a walk-in closet for ultimate convenience. The 3rd floor offers versatile space for an additional bedroom, office, playroom, or a combination of all three. A brand-new washer and dryer are conveniently located just outside the main bedrooms. The garage is equipped with extra storage and a newly installed garage door opener. Freshly painted and move-in ready, this condo is waiting for you to enjoy.
