About this home

A wonderful and versatile home with professionally landscaped yard, private fence, large deck, and cozy 3 season porch for outdoor fun. Not only designed for indoor/outdoor entertaining but there is an additional space for extended family that includes a family room and bedroom. Many special features include large country kitchen, remodeled full bath, warm hardwood floors, generous closets and storage areas, and attached garage. The bright open floor plan allows easy access to the dining area, living room, kitchen, and sunporch/deck. The 3 large bedrooms are located on the main floor and each have hardwood floors. The finished lower level, laundry room, and attached garage add convenience and space to this warm and charming home. Ideally located on the Norwalk/Westport line and only a 5 minute drive to shopping, movie theatre, schools, restaurants, highways and train stations, beach, and SoNo.
