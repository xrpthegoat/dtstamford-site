About this home

Discover comfort and expansive living in this well-maintained 1BR/1BA residence at the sought-after Hayes House Condominiums. This spacious layout features classic parquet floors throughout and oversized windows that fill the home with natural light while showcasing open city views. The generous living room offers exceptional scale rarely found in one-bedroom units and flows seamlessly to a dedicated dining area and private balcony-perfect for morning coffee or relaxing outdoors. The bright galley kitchen features crisp white cabinetry, tile backsplash, and ample counter space, opening into a sun-filled dining area. The oversided bedroom easily accommodates a full furniture set and offers excellent closet space. A clean, well-maintained full bath features a tub/shower combination and functional storage. Additional highlights include a welcoming foyer with multiple closets, storage locker, and designated garage parking. Hayes House residents enjoy full-service amenities including concierge, 24-hour security, on-site superintendent, fitness center, community room, shared courtyard with grills, bike storage, and laundry facilities. HOA dues include heat, cooling, and hot water. Ideally located just moments from Downtown Stamford, Metro-North, I-95, restaurants, shopping, parks, beaches, and medical centers, this inviting residence offers exceptional value and convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable full-service buildings.
