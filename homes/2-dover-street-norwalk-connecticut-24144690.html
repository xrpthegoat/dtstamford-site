About this home

Highest and best offers requested by Tuesday 1/20 at 5PM for seller's review. Enjoy the best of vibrant Norwalk living in this beautiful light-filled, conveniently located townhouse. This thoughtfully updated two-bedroom, one-and-a-half-bath home is located in a well-maintained, intimate six-unit complex and offers comfortable, easy living across two well-designed levels. The main floor features a spacious living room and an eat-in kitchen with space for a full dining table, plus sliding glass doors leading to a back deck-creating an easy indoor-outdoor connection and a convenient area for grilling. A tasteful half bath completes the first level. Upstairs are two generously sized, sun-filled bedrooms, a full bathroom, and convenient in-unit laundry. Pull-down attic stairs plus a basement crawl space via hatch provide valuable extra storage. The home includes two assigned parking spaces perfectly positioned in front of the home. Centrally located near the heart of Norwalk you will be just minutes away from Metro-North, major highways, schools, shopping, trendy restaurants, waterfront dining, and The SoNo Collection Mall, this home offers exceptional convenience with easy access to New York City and the surrounding Fairfield County towns. Owner occupancy required; rentals are not permitted. A fantastic opportunity to own a move-in-ready, beautifully maintained townhouse. Available immediately.
