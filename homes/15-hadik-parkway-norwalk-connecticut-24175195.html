About this home

This Cape Cod-style home is in the Brookside neighborhood of Norwalk. It offers a blend of classic charm and functional living space, positioned at the end of a quiet street. You'll see the amazing community garden (Fodor Farm) close by too! Enter to a welcoming foyer with closet and you are drawn in to the living room with hardwood floors and a stunning fireplace (new lining!). The large kitchen has attached dining area with large pantry & sliders to the deck (Newer floor boards on deck!)and WOW the gorgeous back yard for entertaining or every day enjoyment! One bedroom on the first floor has adjacent full bath and two additional bedrooms on the upper level also have a full bath. The lower level includes a finished room great for an exercise space, playroom or office (was fully gutted and redone with new baseboard heater, pex water pipes, and new insulation!). There is also significant storage space (in addition to the under eave storage off the bedrooms upstairs!). Detached garage too! New hot water heater (6/2026!).
