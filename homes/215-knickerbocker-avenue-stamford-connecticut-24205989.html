About this home

Welcome to 215 Knickerbocker Avenue, an expansive 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath Colonial offering exceptional space, flexibility, and comfort in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood. The main level is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring a kitchen with quartz countertops, a large center island, pantry, and stainless-steel appliances, opening to an expansive family room. Formal living and dining rooms with hardwood floors provide additional gathering space, while a main-level bedroom and full bath offer excellent flexibility for guests, a home office, or multigenerational living. Upstairs, the impressive primary suite creates a true private retreat with cathedral ceilings, two walk-in closets, a dressing area, and a luxurious en-suite bath featuring heated floors, integrated lighting, and sound. Two additional bedrooms and another full bath complete the upper level. The full walk-out lower level expands the home's versatility with a generous recreation/family room, wet bar, bathroom, abundant storage, and access to the patio-creating excellent potential for entertaining, extended living, recreation, or a private guest space. With 3,653 square feet of above-grade living space, 11 rooms, central air, natural gas, public water and sewer, and a flexible floor plan designed to accommodate today's lifestyles, this home delivers exceptional space in a convenient Stamford location.
