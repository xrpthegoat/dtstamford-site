About this home

A private sanctuary awaits with this renovated gem. This sunny and move-in ready four bedroom colonial overlooks over two acres of spectacular property complete with heated pool, pool house, gardens and the Silvermine river flowing along the bottom of the property. A multi-level, completely rebuilt deck provides an elevated, breezy space with a view for entertaining, dining al fresco, or relaxing with coffee, surrounded by the subtle sounds of the river, birds and wildlife. En suite primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms and a shared bath upstairs, and another bedroom and full bathroom on the first floor. A formal dining room, formal living room, family room and sunroom turned gym (heated and cooled) complete the first floor. A walk out finished basement provides easy access to the back yard and garage.
