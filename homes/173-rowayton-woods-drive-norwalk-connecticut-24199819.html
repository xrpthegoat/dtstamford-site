About this home

A peaceful, garden-view approach sets the tone for this spacious and inviting 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo in sought-after Rowayton Woods. The condo is updated & ready to move right in, featuring an oversized eat-in kitchen, cozy fireplace, a surprising abundance of closet space, and a private covered deck facing west-the perfect spot to unwind and take in beautiful sunsets! Enjoy the association's resort-like pool and tennis court, plus an unbeatable location just a quick walk to the Rowayton train station for easy NYC access. Plenty of visitor parking & designated owner parking spot closest to entry walkway. Walk or bike into Rowayton village for coffee, dinner, shops and the waterfront. Spacious, peaceful and wonderfully convenient-easy living at its best!
