About this home

Welcome to 218 Knickerbocker Avenue - a beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath Colonial nestled in the highly sought-after Springdale section of Stamford. This move-in ready home has been freshly painted and is ready for its new owner. The first level offers a gracious flow with formal living and dining rooms, perfect for entertaining, plus sliders leading out to a large deck overlooking the beautifully landscaped, fenced backyard. Enjoy long afternoons outdoors surrounded by well-manicured lawn and stunning perennials. A true oasis for relaxing or hosting guests. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms with gleaming hardwood floors, along with a large full bath featuring double sinks. Hardwood floors extend throughout the home, complemented by a kitchen appointed with stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. Additional highlights include a one-car garage and a prime location offering easy access to Metro-North for a convenient commute into New York City, as well as nearby shopping and dining. Don't miss this rare opportunity in one of Springdale's most desirable pockets. Schedule your showing today!
