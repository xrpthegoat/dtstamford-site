About this home

Exceptional value with this location. This sun filled, southern exposure unit is ideally located. It faces a large, beautifully landscaped courtyard in front as well as a peaceful grassy area behind. This provides serene views, privacy and a quiet location. Other top features include an updated bathroom and a family room on the lower level which extends the full length of the basement. Units typically have the area split between finished space and laundry/storage space. The laundry is in separate room to the side. A convenient, nearby parking lot also adds to this unit's appeal. There are a few updates needed in the kitchen, and ideally the hardwood floors should be refinished. Owner will offer a $2500 credit toward those items. Sylvan Knoll is conveniently located off of exit 9 on I-95. It is walking distance to Cove Island Park, the beach and Chelsea Piers and just a short drive to town, shopping and the train station. Located on 13 park-like acres with sidewalks throughout, Sylvan Knoll is a pet friendly community. The real estate taxes are included in the common charges. Units are all owner occupied and cannot be rented. Real estate taxes are included in the common charges.
