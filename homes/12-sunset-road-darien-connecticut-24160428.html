About this home

This gracious Colonial blends timeless 1930s charm with thoughtful modern updates, ideally located on one of Darien's most sought-after in-town streets, known for its traditions and strong sense of community. Set on a level, one-acre property in a coveted half-acre zone, the home offers rare expansion potential and exceptional lifestyle appeal.Extensively renovated and expanded, the home features a reimagined layout designed for today's living. The updated kitchen with custom cabinetry, granite countertops, and high-end appliances opens seamlessly to the family room with fireplace. A butler's pantry, walk-in pantry, mudroom, and dedicated home office add both functionality and convenience. A versatile room above the garage offers flexible living space, ideal as a fifth bedroom, guest retreat, or potential in-law suite. Recent improvements include renovated bathrooms and a luxurious primary suite with spa-like bath featuring heated floors, soaking tub, marble walk-in shower, and custom built-ins. Mechanical upgrades include multiple air handlers, updated HVAC systems, full-house generator, new boiler and hot water heater, and replaced roof & windows. Classic details such as original hardwood floors and elegant millwork preserve the home's character throughout. The private, level property is framed by mature plantings and features a thoughtfully designed patio-ideal for entertaining, recreation, or future expansion. A rare combination of location, land, community & charm
