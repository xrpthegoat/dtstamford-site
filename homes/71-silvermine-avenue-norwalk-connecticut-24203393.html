About this home

Welcome to this classic Cape ideally located at the entrance to sought-after Silvermine, with convenient access to shopping, restaurants and everyday amenities. Move-in ready and filled with charm, this inviting home offers a flexible floor plan featuring a first-floor bedroom plus two generously sized bedrooms on the second level. The brand-new kitchen is complemented by a formal dining room and a comfortable living room with a fireplace. Hardwood floors throughout add warmth and character. Outside, enjoy a level lot with a patio perfect for relaxing or entertaining, along with a storage shed for added convenience. An attached one-car garage completes this wonderful home. A great opportunity to enjoy the charm of Silvermine and make this classic Cape your own!
