About this home

Well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath Ranch home on a quiet residential street in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood. The remodeled kitchen features granite countertops, newer stainless-steel appliances, custom cabinetry, tile flooring, and direct access to the wood deck. The main-level family room offers a wood-burning fireplace, convenient half bath, access to the oversized 2-car garage, and sliders leading to the private backyard. The spacious primary bedroom suite includes a full bath and walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms and another full bath complete the main level. The lower level provides a finished room ideal for a home gym, studio, office or hobby space, along with a laundry area and abundant unfinished space for storage or future expansion. Enjoy outdoor entertaining on the wood deck and patio overlooking the spacious, private yard, with plenty of room for a home garden to grow your own vegetables and flowers. A maintenance-free exterior with vinyl siding and replacement thermopane windows adds to the home's appeal. Peace of mind comes with a heating system, hot water heater, and well-water equipment all three years old or less. Central air provides added comfort. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, beaches, SoNo, Stew Leonard's, and more. Easy commuting with Metro-North, I-95, Route 7, and the Merritt Parkway all just minutes away. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable one-level living in a desirable Norwalk neighborhood
