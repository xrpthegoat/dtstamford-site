About this home

Welcome to Ledgebrook, one of Norwalk's most coveted condominium communities, where resort-styled living meets everyday convenience. Surrounded by lush landscaping, mature trees, & picturesque pond, this beautifully maintained community feels like a private retreat while being just minutes from shopping, dining, beaches, SoNo, Metro-North, & I-95. Enjoy exceptional amenities including a sparkling Olympic sized pool, clubhouse with gym, sauna, & inviting atmosphere. Beautifully updated & move in ready, this light-filled 2 bedroom, 2 bath condo is the perfect place to call home. The spacious living room, anchored by a cozy fireplace, opens to a private deck, ideal for morning coffee, evening cocktails, or simply unwinding after a long day. The bright kitchen features granite countertops, abundant cabinetry & plenty of workspace. Two generously sized bedrooms, beautifully updated bathrooms, & two versatile bonus nooks offer endless possibilities for a home office, reading lounge, or creative space. A welcoming foyer leads up to a short flight of stairs to the main living level, while a detached one-car garage provides added convenience and also private storage. This is more than a home! It's a lifestyle. Combining a premier location outstanding community amenities, & stylish updates throughout, this is a rare opportunity you won't want to miss.
