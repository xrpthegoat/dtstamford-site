About this home

RARE OPPORTUNITY, SPACIOUS &VERSATILE TWO-FAMILY HOME IN WESTPORT . Discover this meticulously maintained 3300 square feet property offering two oversized units evenly spilt, and an impressive 3 sides covered porch, that welcomes you to private entrances for each unit, setting the tone for warmth and character. The first-floor unit features 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a large living room and an oversized eat-in kitchen, while the second-floor unit provides 2 bedrooms, one bathroom, a large and bright open-concept kitchen/dining area, that leads to a separate living room with elegant French doors. This property features central air on each floor, a full house 24K generator powering the entire property, recess lighting throughout, sprinkler system,... This property is, walking distance to Compo Beach and 2 miles from vibrant downtown Westport, 1.5 miles from the train station, welcome to endless possibilities, live on one floor and rent the other, or use it as a full investment income property. Also included is a barn currently used for storage and garage spaces with potential for an accessory apartment, plus a basement and a walk up attic that could be partially finished, featuring impressive original beams and field stone walls (interested parties to conduct their own diligence on feasibility for all potential conversions, seller makes no representation regarding permits, applications and approvals). With its charm, flexibility and location, this home is a rare find!
