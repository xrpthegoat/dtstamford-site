About this home

Who doesn't love living by the beach? I mean... if you are going to live anywhere in Westport, Compo Beach is the place. Start your day with a soothing beachside walk, invigorating bike ride, or jog along the water's edge. After a long busy day hit the reset button as everything grows quiet and you gaze at breathtaking sunsets. The life style at Compo Beach is in a league of its own and envied by all. This traditional 2 Bedroom 1.5 Bath colonial, located on sough-after quiet street, is filled with character and sits on a beautiful oversized lot with large yard, mature landscaping, pet friendly fencing, and a modern personal electric car charger for your convenience. Perfect place to hang your hat while building your dream home, or just wanting to check out what it's like to live in this amazing award-winning town. Only steps to beach, water and marina, and only minutes to Longshore Park offering golf, tennis, pool, boating, paddle tennis, winter skating and more. Be entertained by Westport's nationally acclaimed library, amazing five-star music rocking Levitt Pavilion, as well as fine Arts and Cultural events found at the Westport Country Playhouse and Museum of Contemporary Art. Enjoy vibrant downtown areas with premier shopping, restaurants and a wonderful seasonal Farmers Market. Award winning schools and only minutes to Metro North train to the City.
