About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated townhouse offering 1,662 sq. ft. of finished space in the heart of Glenbrook. Bright and inviting, the main level features an upgraded kitchen, dining area, spacious living room, and a full bath. Upstairs, you'll find three comfortable bedrooms and another full bath. The finished lower level provides incredible versatility, whether you need a playroom, home office, gym, or cozy family room. A dedicated laundry room is also conveniently located on this level. Small outdoor space ideal for grilling. Two assigned off-street parking spaces. A prime location just minutes from the Glenbrook Train Station, public transportation, retail shops, supermarkets, restaurants, and entertainment, this townhouse offers both comfort and convenience.
