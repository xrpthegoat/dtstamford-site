About this home

What a pristine beautiful home! Ready to move in! Freshly painted from top to bottom. Loads of fabulous living space and yard for all to enjoy! HUGE open lower level with a half bath and full toolroom. In addition to the attached 2 car garage, there is a large barn for cars or just storage!
