About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 3,700 sq. ft. property, lovingly cared for and meticulously upgraded by its owners who've enjoyed their house for 26 years. Located in a highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood, this residence offers a perfect blend of indoor and outdoor living. In addition to a formal living room and formal dining room, there's a striking spacious sunlit dining and sitting area off the kitchen overlooking a resort-like private backyard with a lush, detailed landscaping, pristine swimming pool, two gorgeous cabanas (one of them has a summer kitchen), and an impressive fountain to uplift your mood and create a cooler environment on those hot summer days. The primary bedroom suite serves as a true sanctuary, featuring an exceptionally spacious, custom ensuite bathroom. Located minutes from serene Cranbury Park, top-rated schools, and scenic nature trails, this home offers an idyllic lifestyle in a premier location.
