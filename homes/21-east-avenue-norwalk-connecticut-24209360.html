About this home

Charm-filled Circa 1888 Colonial offering 3 bedrooms and timeless character in the heart of Norwalk, remarkably convenient to everything! Rich in vintage detail, this inviting home features stained-glass windows, intricate moldings, hardwood floors throughout, and a welcoming front porch. Spacious rooms and a wonderful floor plan provide an appealing foundation for a personalized renovation. A large private patio and yard offer additional outdoor living space, while the unfinished attic and basement present exciting opportunities for future expansion. Additional highlights include natural gas service, public sewer and city water. Ideally situated near downtown proper, the Norwalk Green, farmers markets, shops, dining and entertainment, with easy access to the train, the Merritt Parkway and I-95. The home is being sold "as-is". Amazing opportunity for a savvy buyer, designer, investor or builder who understands the beauty of this vintage home and its great potential in this most sought-after neighborhood.
