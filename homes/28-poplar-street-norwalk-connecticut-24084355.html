About this home

Beautifully updated East Norwalk home offers wonderful views of Shore Haven Golf Course and seasonal views of Long Island Sound and salt marsh. Sunny and bright with huge windows, these amazing views can be seen from many rooms of the home, however the panoramic views from the rooftop deck are stunning! Ideal for entertaining, this home features an open dining room and great room, both with patio doors to large deck and patio. Attention empty nesters and those looking for bedrooms on the first floor--this home provides 2 bedrooms and a full bath on the first floor along with a separate laundry room. Need office space or recreation space? Look no further! Finished heated basement or main floor alcove is perfect for those needs. Many updates and recent improvements including brand new efficient gas heating system, new roof and more. Perfect East Norwalk Location on the Westport line, this special home is walking distance to all the great things that make East Norwalk special--including beach, restaurants, marinas, country clubs and dog parks. Moments to I-95 and train station.
