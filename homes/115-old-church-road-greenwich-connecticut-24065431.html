About this home

This exceptional 1900s Colonial on Old Church Road in Central Greenwich effortlessly blends timeless elegance with modern luxury. The living room, featuring intricate moldings, period woodwork, and a cozy fireplace, flows into a spacious dining room perfect for entertaining. Distinctive rounded walls and a wide staircase add architectural grace, while the first floor boasts a sun-filled, updated eat-in kitchen, family room, office, two powder rooms and expansive great room with 14' vaulted ceilings with custom beams and beautiful wood-burning fireplace. Upstairs, seven bedrooms and four baths provide ample space and comfort. A heated salt-water pool, outdoor patios and thoughtful updates throughout make this home a rare fusion of classic sophistication and contemporary convenience.
