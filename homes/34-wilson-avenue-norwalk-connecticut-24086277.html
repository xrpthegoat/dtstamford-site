About this home

Don't miss out on this exceptional opportunity to own a coastal Cape-style home in the highly sought-after coastal village of Rowayton. Situated on an oversized lot of 0.61 acres, this charming residence offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience, just a short walk to the town center, schools, and stunning beaches, with the space to add a pool, garage, or potentially a second lot with a subdivision. This home boasts three comfortable bedrooms, including a spacious first-floor bedroom ideal for those seeking a primary bedroom on the main level. The beautifully renovated kitchen features a high-end Viking range and hood and large eat-in island, providing the perfect space for cooking and entertaining. Enjoy ample space to unwind with two inviting sitting areas. One of them features French doors that open to the large level backyard, complete with a patio area, perfect for outdoor dining, and a cozy fire pit for those cool coastal evenings. The lower level is fully finished with a large media room, ideal for movies, gaming, or family gatherings. This home offers a fantastic blend of coastal charm, modern updates, and abundant living space. Move in just in time for summer in Rowayton!
