About this home

This 4BR/3.5BA home has it all! Nestled on a picturesque 3+ acres in the sought after town of Wilton, 104 Scarlet Oak features gleaming hardwood floors throughout with natural light streaming in each room. The main level includes a fabulous family room with a soaring ceiling, exposed beams and a wood-burning fireplace (with stove insert) and a large eat-in kitchen, with skylights, gas range and convection oven, as well as dining and living rooms with beautiful crown moldings, and an office. A wonderful screened porch, that leads to the large deck, is the perfect spot to unwind in the warmer months. Four bedrooms are located on the upper level, including the spacious primary suite that features a vaulted ceiling and two walk-in closets. Convenient second floor laundry room! An additional 1,000sf of finished space can be found in the lower level - home gym or playroom, perhaps! With a walk-up attic and tons of closet space, there is no shortage of storage space in this house! Recent upgrades include a new furnace, oil tank and A/C condenser, new bluestone walkway, and a 5-year old roof! Here's your chance to own a fabulous home in a great neighborhood that is convenient to schools, shopping and more!
