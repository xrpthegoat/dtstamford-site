About this home

Welcome to this 5 bedroom 4.5 bath arts & crafts home on over 2 private acres. Spacious layout and elegant finishes, this residence offers the perfect blend of modern luxury and classic charm. Grand Living Room with soaring ceilings and wall of windows to enjoy the park like vistas. Dining Room with built in hutch. Private, light filled Library/home office with built-ins and access to deck. Sunny Gourmet Chef's kitchen fully renovated with top-of-the-line Wolf and SubZero appliances incl dedicated Coffee Bar. Family Room with built-ins perfect for movie night. The serene Primary Suite is a perfect retreat that has been beautifully updated with walk in closet and new bathroom for your personal sanctuary. The outdoor oasis features a large swimming pool with integrated spa complemented by a spacious outdoor deck perfect for entertaining. Easy access to all of Westport's amenities.
