About this home

**An Exceptional Opportunity in Desirable North Stamford!** Welcome to 45 Smoke Hill Drive, beautifully situated on a peaceful cul-de-sac surrounded by mature trees and the natural beauty of North Stamford. Offering an exceptional combination of location, value, and interior condition, this home is a remarkable opportunity for buyers seeking a property they can make their own without taking on an extensive interior renovation. Step inside and discover a wonderfully maintained interior in excellent condition, featuring lovely upgrades, inviting living spaces, and the opportunity to add your own personal style and finishing touches.
