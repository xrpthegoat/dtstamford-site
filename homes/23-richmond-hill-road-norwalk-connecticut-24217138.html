About this home

Welcome to 23 Richmond Hill Road, where classic Colonial charm meets modern comfort! Beautifully updated and thoughtfully maintained, this move-in-ready 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath Colonial offers over 1,500 square feet of living space. Freshly painted interiors with newly refinished hardwood floors, and charming architectural details. The spacious, sun-filled living room offers a cozy fireplace, custom built-in bookshelves, and an oversized picture window overlooking the backyard and pool. A separate dining room flows into the updated kitchen, with granite countertops, white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, including a Thermador refrigerator, dishwasher and recessed lighting. A screened-in porch and newly updated powder room complete the main level.Upstairs offers three generously sized bedrooms, an updated full bath, and a walk-up attic for additional storage. The partially finished lower level, featuring recent renovations and tile flooring, provides a flexible space for recreation or work. Enjoy your own private backyard with an in-ground pool, stone patio, and grassy area, perfect for relaxing. Upgrades include a roof (2019), replacement windows (2017), gutters with leaf guards, whole-house water filtration system (2024), newly installed garage door, and updated driveway and walkway.Conveniently located near I-95, Metro-North, shopping, dining and The SoNo Collection. A wonderful blend of charm and modern updates. Don't miss the opportunity to make this home yours!
