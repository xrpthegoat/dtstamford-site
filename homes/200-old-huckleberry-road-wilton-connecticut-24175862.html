About this home

Nestled in the desirable Silvermine area just minutes from downtown Wilton, award-winning schools, and major highways, this charming home sits on a flat property near both New Canaan and Wilton town amenities. Surrounded by high-end homes, the property offers an open floor plan with endless opportunities to customize and make it your own. Inside, the bright and inviting interior, features hardwood floors on the main level, a sun-filled sunroom and a spacious living room with fireplace that opens seamlessly to the dining area - perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen is equipped with an island and stainless steel appliances, while the primary bedroom offers the convenience of a walk-in closet. The partially finished lower level adds valuable flexibility with a family room, laundry room, and mudroom, providing excellent additional living and storage space. Additional highlights of the home include a 1-car garage, 2-full baths and attic. Don't miss this opportunity!
