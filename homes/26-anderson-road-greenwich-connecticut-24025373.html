About this home

Gracious Milbrook Association Center Hall Colonial with classic white pillars welcomes you to this grand home infused with Southern elegance. The foyer opens to a step-down living room featuring original coffered extra-height ceilings, wood burning fireplace and French doors leading to a stone terrace. This lovely home boasts a large formal dining room, 6 bedrooms and 3.1 baths and on the 2nd floor a Primary ensuite bedroom with balcony. Additional features include attached 2 car garage, walk-up attic and cedar closet. Situated on a corner lot of .52 acres, the property is conveniently located near the Milbrook Club, schools, Greenwich Ave. and train station.
