About this home

30 Hitchcock is truly a summer vacation retreat. The property features a recently built 40 foot saltwater, gunite heated pool and spa and guest cottage. The expansive outdoor patio and deck is perfect for summer gatherings. An upper deck off the main level with BBQ grill, dining and seating, covered lower deck with outdoor TV and seating, expansive bluestone patio, pergola, and poolside dining and seating complete with a firepit table and heat lamp. The lower level features a family/recreation room and gym with a separate refrigerator and full bathroom that opens up to the patio/pool. The front porch is perfect to view the sunset over cocktails. The expansive square footage is well utilized with 9+ high ceilings and a great room living space, separate den, 2 home offices, a downstairs guest bedroom suite/full bath, and 5 additional bedrooms upstairs. Beach pass available. NYC an hour to 1 hour 15 min away by train. Availability: flexible dates Jun 14th- Labor Day.
