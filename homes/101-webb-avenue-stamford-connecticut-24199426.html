About this home

Discover this inviting, solidly built, and legally certified three-family home in 'The Cove', one of Stamford's most sought-after coastal neighborhoods! Cherished by the same family for more than six decades, this multi-generational property offers exceptional flexibility-perfect for an owner occupant, an investor, or anyone seeking versatile living arrangements. The main level features a very spacious 3BR/2BTH + den, while the 2nd floor offers both a 2BR/1BTH unit and a 1BR/1BTH unit. Each residence includes generously sized rooms, beautiful wood floors and separate gas & electric meters (the 1BR unit is all electric). The basement provides additional space for an office, workshop, or storage. A rare bonus, the detached oversized three-car garage is perfect for business equipment,winter boat storage, hobby gear, or extra vehicles. Behind the garage, a peaceful grassy area offers the opportunity to create your own garden sanctuary. Recent updates include newer roofs on both the home(2022)and the garage(2021). And the location is unbeatable-a short walk to the beach at Cove Island Park, marina/harbor, and skating rink, with downtown Stamford, the train station, and I-95 just minutes away! Homes like this don't come around often!
