About this home

Storybook charm meets effortless coastal living in this enchanting Cape Cod, beautifully set behind a classic white picket fence on one third of an acre just moments from Rowayton. Rich in character and thoughtfully updated, the home welcomes you with hardwood floors, timeless architectural details, and a gracious living room centered around a fireplace. An adjoining sunroom with built-in bookcases creates an inspiring home office or intimate retreat. The formal dining room flows into the heart of the home, the sun-filled eat-in kitchen features quartz countertops and a charming breakfast nook overlooking the lush backyard. Step outside to a private patio framed by mature landscaping and vibrant hydrangeas - an idyllic backdrop for al fresco dinners, summer entertaining, or quiet afternoons beneath the sail shade. Back inside, the first floor offers a versatile den/possible fourth bedroom with a full bath complete with a tub/shower combo. Upstairs, the serene primary suite is a true retreat, featuring vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, connected to a newly renovated spa-inspired bath with dual vanity, oversized walk-in shower, vaulted ceiling, and private water closet. Two additional bedrooms complete the second floor. An attached garage, abundant lower level storage, and a covered patio overlooking the backyard add everyday convenience and versatility. Additional amenities include central air, irrigation system, main level laundry & charming front porch.
