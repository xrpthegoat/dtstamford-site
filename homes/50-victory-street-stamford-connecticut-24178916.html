About this home

Opportunity knocks in the heart of Stamford's desirable West Side! This well-maintained two-family home offers the perfect opportunity for investors or owner-occupants seeking income potential and flexible living space. Featuring a flat backyard, private outdoor areas for both units, and off-street parking, this property checks all the boxes. The first-floor unit offers 1 bedroom and 1 full bath with an easy flow between the living room and kitchen, a spacious bedroom with updated flooring, ample closet space, and in-unit laundry hookup for added convenience. Outdoor covered patio off the kitchen allows for extra private outdoor space. The second-floor unit features 2 bedrooms and 1.5 baths with a spacious eat-in kitchen that opens seamlessly into a sun-filled living room. Two generously sized bedrooms with great closet space, a full hall bath, mudroom entry, laundry room with half bath, an attic storage area provides excellent functionality. A large wraparound porch extends the living space outdoors and creates the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining. Conveniently located close to downtown Stamford, Metro-North, I-95, parks, shopping, and restaurants, this is an exceptional opportunity to own a versatile multi-family property in a prime location.
