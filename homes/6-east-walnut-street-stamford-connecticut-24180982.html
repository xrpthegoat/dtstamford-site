About this home

Welcome to the South End of Stamford, where convenience, walkability, and city living come together. This updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment (in a multi-family) is ideally situated just across from Harbor Point, placing you in one of Stamford's most connected and sought-after locations. Inside, you'll find updated flooring, refreshed interiors, and a practical layout designed for comfortable everyday living. The apartment features two versatile bedrooms, a bathroom with stand-up shower, and a bright eat-in kitchen with new gas cooking range, refrigerator, updated cabinetry, and in-unit washer for added convenience. Natural light fills the space, creating a clean and inviting atmosphere throughout. One of the standout features is the covered front porch, offering a welcoming outdoor space to enjoy morning coffee, unwind after work, or simply take in the neighborhood atmosphere. Location is everything, and this address delivers. Nestled in the heart of Stamford's South End and directly across the street from Harbor Point, residents enjoy easy access to transportation, parks, waterfront areas, shopping, restaurants, cafes, library, corner stores, grocery options, and everyday amenities. Stamford Train Station, downtown nightlife, and major commuter routes are all within close reach, making this an ideal location for those seeking both accessibility and lifestyle. This is a rare opportunity to enjoy South End living in a location that truly places you in the middle of it all.
