About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY TUESDAY 5/6 AT 8PM. Charming Ranch on Quiet Cul-de-Sac - Move-In Ready! Welcome to this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch, perfectly situated in a highly desirable, family-friendly neighborhood. This home features a bright, open-concept layout where the sun-filled living room with cozy fireplace and custom built-ins flows seamlessly into the dining area and updated kitchen. The kitchen is a true highlight with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar, and a stylish subway tile backsplash. Both full bathrooms have been tastefully renovated, and the finished basement adds tremendous versatility with a spacious family room, playroom, bedroom/home office, mudroom with laundry, and abundant storage space. Step outside to enjoy a professionally landscaped yard with lush green lawn, thanks to the new irrigation system and complete with a gorgeous bluestone patio, -ideal for dining al fresco, entertaining or relaxing. Tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac, yet just minutes from shopping, Whole Foods, Starbucks, and more, this home offers the perfect blend of modern comfort, charm, and convenience.
