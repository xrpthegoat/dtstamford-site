About this home

Welcome to this exquisite 4-Bed,3.5 Bath Cornell Colonial, perfectly situated on a peaceful cul-de-sac in the charming town of Wilton. This home is the epitome of comfort and warmth, with two inviting fireplaces, including one in the gorgeous kitchen, truly making it the heart of this home. The kitchen is designed for gathering and culinary creativity, featuring 5 burner gas range and SubZero, plus custom Pantry and window seat. Dining area and separate sitting area are donned with coffered ceiling and recessed lighting, ideal for hosting intimate dinners or enjoying casual meals with family. The large family room on the 3/4 level, complete with closet and full bath with a separate garage entry, offers flexibility and could serve as an in-law suite or au pair quarters, adapting to your unique lifestyle needs. Step into the large screen porch and embrace the seamless integration of indoor and outdoor living, where you can savor the beauty of the surrounding nature. The meticulously landscaped grounds are a sight to behold, complete with a blue stone patio, professional gardens and fire pit, creating an idyllic setting for outdoor entertainment or tranquil relaxation. This home is not just a residence; but a retreat that offers both the charm of country living and the convenience of modern amenities. Don't miss the opportunity to make this Wilton gem your own while enjoying the reputation of Blue Ribbon Schools and convenience to train, shopping, schools, YMCA. Just unpack!!
