About this home

Classical Georgian Mini Estate, C.1930 by renowned architect Nelson Breed stands proudly as one of Nod Hill's most distinguished properties. Beautifully sited on an expansive 5.5-acre private parcel w/stunning pool setting, ever changing gardens, specimen trees + wonderful open meadow w/flower, vegetable & fruit beds. Renovated to a very high standard, this home showcases exquisite period details combined w/modern conveniences. Impressive foyer introduces a grand staircase, detailed moldings, 10' ceilings, & sunlight floods every room. Layout seamlessly flows into a formal Living Rm, a handsomely paneled Library, & Dining Rm capable of hosting 18 guests. Chef's Kitchen is a culinary masterpiece w/professional-grade appliances, marble countertops, Butler's Pantry, & Sitting area w/FPL & triple French Doors opening to beautiful garden views, remarkable stonework, terrace & covered Porch. The Family Rm, highlighted w/FPL, built-ins, & coffered ceiling is ideal for entertaining. A MudRm, Laundry Rm, & access to the Garage complete the main level + direct access to spacious In-law/AuPair Suite. Upstairs, the Primary Suite is a retreat w/FPL, luxurious marble Bath, walk-in closets, & sweeping views of the property. Three addl. ensuite Bdrms complete the upper level. 3rd floor, w/built-ins, provides ample space for play, Gym, Home Office. Discover why Nod Hill Rd. in Wilton is considered in the top 10 of the most coveted addresses in the US. Approx. 1 HR to NYC
