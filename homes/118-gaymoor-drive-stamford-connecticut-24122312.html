About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated Cape Cod-style home, where timeless architecture blends seamlessly with a thoughtfully modernized floor plan. Located in the highly desirable Springdale neighborhood, this home offers the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and community. Step into a bright and open living space where the living room, kitchen, and dining area flow effortlessly together, creating a welcoming environment perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Just off the dining room, sliding glass doors lead to a spacious Trex deck, ideal for outdoor gatherings, summer barbecues, or simply relaxing with a morning coffee. The home boasts 3 bedrooms plus a dedicated office space or 4th bedroom! In addition don't miss the partially finished basement for even more living space! This home is nestled in a vibrant neighborhood known for its strong sense of community and walkable access to local amenities. Whether you're commuting to NYC or enjoying a weekend stroll to the local cafe or deli, this location offers the best of suburban living with urban convenience!
