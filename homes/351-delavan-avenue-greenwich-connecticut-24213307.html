About this home

Fully renovated and modernized, this farmhouse seamlessly blends historic charm with contemporary style across four finished levels. The 4-bedroom, 2-bath home features exposed brick, red fir floors, cherry cabinetry, elegant Brazilian granite and a pantry. The primary suite boasts vaulted ceilings, custom birch closets and a slate double shower. A walk-out lower level offers additional finished space, built-in homework stations and a media room. Additional highlights include whole-home water filtration, kitchen reverse osmosis, an expansive deck, generous green space, a long driveway and potential for an ADU. Near Byram Park's sandy beach, marina, tennis courts, playground, pool and splash pad, with easy access to shops, dining, Metro-North and schools.
