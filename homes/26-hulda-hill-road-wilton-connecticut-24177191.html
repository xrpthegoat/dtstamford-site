About this home

Set on over 2 private acres, this 2,800+ square foot home captures the clean lines and connection-to-nature spirit of mid-century design. A thoughtfully updated retreat (Roof, deck, garage doors, windows in primary bedroom, driveway all recently updated) just under a mile from the Cannondale train station, this is effortless commuting paired with total seclusion at home. Step inside to a dramatic living room anchored by a floor-to-ceiling stone, wood-burning fireplace (there are 4 fireplaces!) and wide plank hardwood floors that set the tone for the whole home. The open, light-filled layout flows naturally from room to room, with a renovated primary bath, updated powder room, and some fresh paint giving every space a crisp, move-in-ready feel. A private office on the main level could also serve as an occasional guestroom and the primary bedroom features a fireplace as well as spectacular views from the large, newer windows. Outdoor living takes center stage here. A wraparound deck - rebuilt for both style and durability - overlooks the wooded grounds and features its own outdoor fireplace, made for slow evenings and weekend entertaining alike. A stone patio off the kitchen adds another spot to gather, while the partially fenced yard offers room to roam. Practical upgrades round out the package: central air conditioning, a second-floor laundry, a radon mitigation system, a UV water filtration system, and a generator
