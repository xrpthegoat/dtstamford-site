About this home

Discover an extraordinary opportunity to build or renovate your dream home on this magnificent one-acre property located in the heart of Greens Farms in one of Westport's most coveted neighborhoods. Positioned at the end of a distinguished cul-de-sac, this 2,176 sqft 4 bedroom, 2 full and 1 half bath mid-century ranch style home offers unparalleled privacy and tranquility on beautifully landscaped park-like grounds with mature specimen plantings and enchanting natural beauty. Seemingly a world away yet minutes to town, Compo Beach, the train and the library, this is for the discerning buyer seeking both tranquility and convenience.
