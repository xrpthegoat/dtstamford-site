About this home

This bright and sunny, pristinely maintained corner end-unit townhouse sits on a beautifully landscaped cul-de-sac in the Georgetowne North Association. The living and dining area features custom bult-ins and opens to a private terrace overlooking a quiet pond. A cozy breakfast nook accents the kitchen, while the second floor hosts the primary bedroom with its en-suite bath and private sauna and a convenient laundry area. Two additional bedrooms, a second full bath, one-car garage, and ample guest parking complete the home. Residents enjoy an association pool and close proximity to shopping, dining, medical facilities, parks, and beaches. One hour from NYC. Minimum one-year lease is required, June 2028 end date preferred.
