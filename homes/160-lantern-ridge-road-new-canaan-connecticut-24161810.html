About this home

A truly exceptional property situated high above Milne Lake. This residence offers breathtaking 180-degree water views, soaring ceilings, and abundant natural light throughout. Set on 4.13 acres, the estate is surrounded by nature and features a variety of premium amenities, including private walking paths, Zen gardens, bridges over streams, a dedicated pond and waterfall, and a tennis court. For relaxation and entertaining, the property boasts a spacious deck and patio, 2-story greenhouse atrium, as well as an impressive indoor heated pool. Additionally, the grounds include a separate guest house, caretaker cottage or in-law house. This unique and peaceful setting offers an unparalleled living experience.
