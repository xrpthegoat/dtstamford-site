About this home

Newly renovated and centrally located Greenwich Gem! This intelligently updated 3-bedroom, 2.5 bath multi-level apartment offers built-in USB interfaces (Type A & Type C) for smart devices, all stainless steel smart appliances, new central heat/AC, high efficiency complete home air filtration for allergy free indoor living, an expansive remodeled "Chef's Kitchen" with multi-function, extra-large capacity appliances and double wall oven (Convection, Air-fry, Bake, Broil, Roast). Well-appointed bathrooms and convenient in-unit laundry makes tasks a breeze. Large sun-drenched living space opens up to a private back deck for additional extended outdoor living and off-street parking for 3 SUV sized vehicles all yours. Convenient and flexible use basement storage space of over 300 sq ft., and two separate entrances. This great location offers a quick 6-minute access to both I95 and The Merritt Parkway. Easy access to NYC via Metro North station only 5 minutes away. Historic Byram Park Beach and Marina is a 6-minute drive and beautiful Pemberwick Park w/ tennis, basketball and Baseball field is a 5-minute walk. Wholefoods, Citarella, Stop n Shop, Costco, AMC Theatres, Hydro Gym, Greenwich YMCA, Equinox, Carvel, CVS, and the Greenwich Avenue corridor all within either walking distance or a quick 2-6 minute drive. Schedule your showing today, to experience all that Greenwich has to offer!
