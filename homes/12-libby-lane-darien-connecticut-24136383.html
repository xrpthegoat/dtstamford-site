About this home

Modern ranch perfection in one of Darien's most sought after neighborhoods. This exceptional home was stripped down to the studs and completely rebuilt, offering the rare combination of one-level living with the style and confidence of a near-new construction renovation. Thoughtfully re-imagined with clean and modern finishes and an easy, open flow, this home is designed for today's lifestyle- comfortable, clean-lined and turn-key. Outside the property has undergone a dramatic transformation. Dozens of trees were removed and extensive work on the drainage was conducted. New landscaping was installed to create an epic, expansive yard- ideal for entertaining, play, pets and future outdoor living enhances. A detached garage offers additional bonus space ready to be transformed to an office, gym, studio, playroom. This location is unbeatable- right near Darien High School, trains and shopping!
