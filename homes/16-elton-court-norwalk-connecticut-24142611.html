About this home

Charming 2 BR Colonial with Fireplace - Perfect for the Buyer with Vision** Welcome to 16 Elton Court, a sweet 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath colonial full of character and possibility. Nestled on a quiet street, this home offers warmth, charm, and solid bones - perfect for the buyer looking to personalize a home with their own style. Step inside to an inviting living room featuring original wood paneling, hardwood floors, and a classic wood-burning fireplace that anchors the space with warmth and vintage appeal. Natural light fills the room, highlighting the craftsmanship and creating a cozy atmosphere. The kitchen and dining area provide a functional layout with access to the backyard, along with a convenient half bath on the main level. Upstairs are two comfortable bedrooms and a full bath. This home offers a wonderful opportunity to update at your pace and build equity while restoring its charm. Ideal as a first home, an investment property, or a condo alternative. Additional features include a full basement with storage potential, mature trees, level yard space, and a street close to shopping, dining, SONO, beaches, and commuter routes. A rare chance to own a charming home on a peaceful street - and make it your own. We have multiple offers, sellers are asking for Highest and Best by Noon on 12/9.
