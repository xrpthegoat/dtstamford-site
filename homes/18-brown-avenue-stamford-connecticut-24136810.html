About this home

This updated three-bedroom, two-bath townhouse-style condo offers modern living in one of Stamford's most convenient locations. Just minutes from the Stamford Train Station, Downtown, and I-95, this home combines style, comfort, and accessibility. The spacious open layout features hardwood floors and recessed lighting throughout the main and upper levels. The renovated kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, a large center island, and plenty of cabinet space, flowing seamlessly into the dining and living areas. Upstairs, the primary bedroom offers two closets, while the second bedroom and a full hallway bath complete the level. The lower floor adds flexibility with an additional bedroom that opens to the backyard, plus an office/laundry area, a modern full bath, and extra storage with a 2022 boiler and water heater. The fenced backyard provides a private retreat with a level lawn, bluestone patio, and elevated deck-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Street parking is conveniently located right out front. Low monthly HOA.
