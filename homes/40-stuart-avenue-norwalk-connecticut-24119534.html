About this home

Welcome to your bright and sunny 4-level townhouse, perfectly situated in a prime commuter location. This spacious and inviting home boasts 3 generously sized bedrooms, 2.5 well-appointed baths, and an open-concept living and dining area that is perfect for both entertaining and everyday living. The living and dining areas feature beautiful hardwood floors that add warmth and elegance to the space. Step out onto the cozy balcony off the dining room, where you can enjoy your morning coffee or unwind in the evening with a good book. The kitchen is a chef's delight, equipped with Corian countertops that provide ample workspace and new stainless steel appliances that make meal preparation a breeze. On the second floor, you'll find the luxurious Primary Suite, which offers a serene retreat with its full bath, walk-in closet, and newer carpeting that adds a touch of comfort. This floor also includes another large bedroom, perfect for family or guests, a full bath, and a convenient laundry area that makes household chores more manageable. The third-floor loft bedroom is a versatile space with newer carpeting, whether you need a home office, an exercise room, or a playroom, this loft can easily adapt to your needs and lifestyle. The ground-level entrance provides easy access to a 2-car garage with interior entry. Convenient to shopping, restaurants, public transportation, and I-95. Schedule your viewing before it's gone!
