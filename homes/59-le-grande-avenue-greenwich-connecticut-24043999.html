About this home

Recently renovated, this sophisticated, airy and bright condo in a convenient downtown gated association is a must see. Cathedral ceilings greet you in the large living/dining room. The amazing chefs kitchen boasts a huge island for seating and entertaining with black absolute counters, top of the line stainless steel appliances and a huge walk in pantry and overlooks the family room with gas burning fireplace, built-ins and sliders beckoning you to the adjacent deck. The generous primary suite features a gas fireplace and recently renovated chic spa bath, including large walk in shower with double shower heads, double sinks and sauna and a large walk in closet. Second bedroom and full bath and laundry room on the second floor. The walk out lower level offers a third bedroom, full bath and rec/room with fireplace. Separate workroom and loads of storage. Attached garage and plenty of parking.
