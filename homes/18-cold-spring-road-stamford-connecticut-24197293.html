About this home

Come check out this fully remodeled 1 bedroom / 1 bathroom apartment in the Bulls Head area of Stamford. The unit features lots of natural light, brand new hardwood flooring and is freshly painted throughout. A fully remodeled bathroom and kitchen with exquisite finishes, backyard balcony, designated off-street parking and natural gas heat complete the unit. Select appliances are brand new, and include an in-unit washer and dryer. The property features new siding, and the owner intends to install a new asphalt driveway and front porch and landscape exterior grounds during spring. Location is a commuter's dream with a 10 minute drive to I-95, Merritt Parkway and Stamford Train Station. The property is just moments away from the bustling downtown area and Ridgeway Shopping Center, offering a wide array of shopping, dining and entertainment options.
