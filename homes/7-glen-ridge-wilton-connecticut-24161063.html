About this home

This beautifully updated, light-filled townhouse is located in one of Wilton's most sought-after communities. Recently refreshed with a newly painted interior and an updated kitchen, this home offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and convenience. The kitchen was thoughtfully upgraded with modern finishes, complemented by stainless steel appliances, creating a clean and functional space for everyday living and entertaining. The open-concept living and dining areas are filled with natural light, hardwood floors, custom built-ins, and a cozy wood-burning fireplace. A wide sliding door leads to a private patio, perfect for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or unwinding at the end of the day. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite offers a walk-in closet with a custom California Closet system and an updated full bath. A versatile second-floor den provides flexibility for a home office, guest space, or third bedroom. A full-size washer and dryer on the second level adds everyday convenience. Residents enjoy access to a private pool and the benefit of a prime Wilton location, just minutes to town, train, shopping, dining, and scenic walking trails. Move right in and enjoy low-maintenance living in a location that's hard to beat.
