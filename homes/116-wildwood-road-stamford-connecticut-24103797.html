About this home

Light and welcoming, completely renovated yet still traditional, New England colonial on lovely North Stamford's Wildwood Road. Expansive backyard for play and/or entertainment on the newer deck or the stone patio. Beautifully done GE Monogram kitchen with mahogany highlights over the stovetop and bar area to match the fireplace mantle in the open eating and family room area. Look out the back through French doors with a view to the shimmering water on the neighbor's pond in the wintertime. Formal dining and living room, crown moldings throughout. First floor bonus room with historic and rustic feel makes a great entertainment room or play area. Four bedrooms upstairs and renovated bathrooms, including a tastefully done, Carrara marble master en suite. Entire exterior to be painted. Partial finished basement used as a home gym and workshop but has potential for another play area, guest bedroom, or remote office. Two car garage large enough for additional storage and workbench; a new shed also on the yard for your equipment and outdoor furniture. ... Easy access to the Merritt Parkway and 50 min train ride from Stamford to NYC. Neighboring families have loved Northeast elementary and have gone to top colleges from Westhill High School. LaRocca's Country Market provides all your conveniences nearby without having to go into town. A short drive to the Stamford Nature Center & Museum.
