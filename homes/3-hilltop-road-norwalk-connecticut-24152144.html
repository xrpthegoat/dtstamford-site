About this home

Set on a quiet 1.99-acre knoll just steps from the water, 3 Hilltop offers a rare opportunity to build in one of the area's most desirable private communities. The elevated setting provides privacy and strong potential for a custom home, with the natural landscape and topography already doing the work. Wilson Point is known for its close-knit community and coastal lifestyle, with access to a private association beach, clubhouse, tennis courts, and the Norwalk Yacht Club-all easily reached by golf cart. Beyond the association, you're minutes from the Village of Rowayton and historic SONO, with marinas, restaurants, shops, and parks nearby. Whether you're planning a quiet retreat or a home designed for hosting, this 1.99-acre property offers flexibility and location that are increasingly hard to find. The existing structure is being sold as-is. Wilson Point is a private association; all showings must be scheduled with the listing agent.
