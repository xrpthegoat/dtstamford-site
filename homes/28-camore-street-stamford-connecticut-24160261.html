About this home

Move right into this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-full bath home located in the desirable Newfield neighborhood of Stamford. The freshly painted interior features a newer kitchen with island, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, ceramic tile flooring, recessed lighting, and abundant natural light throughout the home. The main level offers an open floor plan featuring a spacious living room with hardwood flooring and fireplace. Two bedrooms and a nicely updated full bath completes the main level. The upper level features a private primary suite and plenty of closet space. Enjoy relaxing on the screened-in covered porch overlooking a level, fully fenced 1/4-acre yard-perfect for outdoor entertaining. Additional highlights include an attached garage along with new vinyl siding, gutters, and trim for low-maintenance living. Situated on a quiet street with a neighborhood feel, this home is within walking distance to local restaurants and offers easy access to major highways and the train, making commuting to New York City simple and convenient. A perfect opportunity for first-time buyers or those looking to downsize.
