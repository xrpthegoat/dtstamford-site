About this home

Welcome to Valeur Lofts, where historic character meets brand-new construction in the heart of downtown Stamford. Thoughtfully restored and brought to modern standards, this distinctive historic building preserves many of its original architectural details while offering the comfort, finishes and conveniences of a newly constructed residence. Inside, enormous windows fill the apartments with exceptional natural sunlight and offer expansive city views, creating bright, open living spaces with a dramatic sense of scale. Sleek kitchens feature waterfall quartz countertops, adding a clean, modern finish that complements the building's historic character. The downtown location is equally compelling, with a 9 out of 10 walkability score and restaurants, entertainment, nightlife, shopping and the seasonal farmers market all close by. Stamford Train Station is less than 10 minutes away, with Metro-North service to Grand Central Station in approximately 50 minutes. Valeur Lofts offers the opportunity to live in a piece of Stamford history, completely reimagined for modern downtown living.
