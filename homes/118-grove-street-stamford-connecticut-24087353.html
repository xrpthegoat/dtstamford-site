About this home

Welcome to Cambridge Court unit 2, located in desirable downtown Stamford. This beautifully updated, sun-drenched townhouse is filled with character and light, and offers the space and layout for how we live today. Savvy buyers will love this open concept 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath condo. Tucked away from Grove Street down a tree lined courtyard, you will find the private entrance to this updated yet secluded home. Well equipped designer renovated kitchen will delight the serious home chef with beautiful granite counters, sleek cabinets and stainless steel appliances. The sunny living room/dining area leads to a balcony deck and boasts a lovely fireplace. Need to work from home? A cozy nook with bay window affords you this opportunity. And updated half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs, enjoy the oversized primary suite with 2 closets and large private bath with tub, shower and vanity. An additional bedroom with its own private bath adds to the perfection. Enjoy all that downtown Stamford has to offer-shopping, restauarants and walking distance to the Stamford train station. Lots of closet space, pull down attic, reserved garage parking #26 and storage. Newer front loading washer and dryer. Wall to Wall carpeting in 2nd bedroom, hallway and stairway will be replaced prior to closing. Make your home ownership dreams a reality. Move right in!
