About this home

Chic, sophisticated and contemporary luxury best describes this spectacular backcountry retreat. Fully renovated 2022-2024 classic shingle style residence w/pool, pool house, gardens, level lawns and tennis court has it all. Iron gates, long driveway and large courtyard present the perfect approach to 5 bed/6.3 bath masterpiece wi/high ceilings, beautiful floors, exquisite wall coverings, dramatic staircase, amazing kitchen, perfect bathrooms, private office/ library, and full gym. Soaring stone fireplace in Great Room, stunning glass wine wall in adjacent game room & solarium w/ flr to ceiling windows set the tone for both elegant entertaining and family gatherings. 2nd Level: primary suite w/sitting room & luxury bath w/infrared sauna, 4 en-suite bedrooms w/custom walk-in closets and a separate wing with separate entrance and half bath.. 3rd Level: Gym with half bath. Amenities include pool, pool house, tennis court /pickleball court, Koi pond, proper fenced in vegetable garden, 5 car garage and private walking trail. This estate is the epitome of modern luxury, offering an unmatched lifestyle amid Greenwich's country estates.
