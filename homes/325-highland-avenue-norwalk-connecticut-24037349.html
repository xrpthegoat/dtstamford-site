About this home

Well-loved and maintained 3-4 BR Colonial in move-in condition in sought-after convenient Brookside location! Many recent updates including kitchen,baths, mechanicals and central air! EIK with quartz countertops, new shaker cabinets, and stainless steel appliances walks out to 3-season covered, screened porch oasis overlooking beautiful tree-lined, level, landscaped backyard. Freshly painted with refinished hardwood floors, this home offers a very flexible floor plan: 3-4 Bedrooms OR use one of both of the first floor BRs as a Formal DR and/or Den/Office. Formal Living Room with Fireplace. Large upstairs bedrooms with generously-sized closet space on either side of the full bath. Another full bath on 1st floor. 1-car attached garage walks into spacious, unfinished basement with newer mechanicals, washer, dryer, and unfinished playroom/gym/work/storage space. First time on market in decades - don't miss this rare opportunity to own this charming home and move right in!
