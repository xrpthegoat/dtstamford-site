About this home

Perched atop a beautifully landscaped property in the desirable Cos Cob neighborhood, this elegant three-bedroom, updated Tudor offers refined living with expansive entertaining terraces and decks. A sunlit interior boasts a chic living room with a stacked-stone fireplace, a sunroom/den, and a sophisticated dining room. The chef's kitchen dazzles with Quartz countertops, a waterfall island, radiant-heated floors, and a breakfast nook with picturesque backyard views. The second floor features a luxurious primary suite with a walk-in closet and spa-like bathroom, plus two bedrooms and a stunning full bath with a freestanding tub. A walkout laundry room and attached two-car garage complete the lower level. Moments from schools, shops, and parks, this exquisite home is a rare gem!
