About this home

This historic 1800s Colonial at 88 Drum Hill has been thoughtfully expanded and renovated, blending timeless charm with modern luxury. Original features - wide plank floors, multiple fireplaces, high ceilings, and classic moldings - honor the home's heritage, while updates create a fully livable, elegant residence. With 6 bedrooms, 4 full baths, and 1 half bath, this updated historic home offers flexible spaces for entertaining, work-from-home setups, and family gatherings. Set on a beautifully landscaped 2-acre property, the home features a stunning in-ground pool, outdoor entertaining areas, and private spaces perfect for relaxation. Unique historic touches, including a sleeping porch, library/study, and charming updated kitchen accents, blend seamlessly with modern comforts, including a chef's kitchen and light-filled living and dining areas. Ideally located minutes from Wilton Center and top schools, this luxury Wilton CT home combines historic integrity, modern updates, and a lifestyle designed for year-round enjoyment - a rare opportunity in Wilton real estate.
