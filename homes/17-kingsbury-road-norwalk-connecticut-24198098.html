About this home

Quiet Cul-de-Sac Living! Move right into this beautifully updated and bright home. The upper level features an open-concept layout with hardwood floors, crown molding, and recessed lighting throughout. The kitchen is a chef's dream with stainless steel appliances (including a smart-touch refrigerator), quartz countertops, and a large island - seamlessly connected to the spacious living and dining areas. From the dining room, step outside to a private deck perfect for outdoor dining and relaxation. The main level offers two generously sized bedrooms and a beautifully renovated full bath. The primary suite boasts a custom barn door leading to a dream walk-in closet. The lower level includes a versatile third bedroom, a second updated full bath, laundry area, and a cozy family room with a fireplace insert set in a bluestone and granite surround. Sliding doors open to the patio and backyard with a firepit - ideal for summer nights. Additional highlights include rented solar panels for energy efficiency, an attached 1-car garage. All in a prime location just minutes to Saugatuck, houses of worship, shops, restaurants, and I-95.
