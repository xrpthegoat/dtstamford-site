About this home

Bright and Spacious 2-Bedroom Condo with Great Layout. Welcome to this sunny and inviting two-bedroom, one-bath condo located on the second floor of a charming two-story building. The thoughtfully designed layout features an eat-in kitchen, a separate formal dining room with direct access to the backyard, a generously sized living room, and two well-proportioned bedrooms.
