About this home

Welcome to Milbrook Manor! No need to live in mid or back country to enjoy a sprawling estate. This prestigious, private sanctuary spanning 4 light filled floors with 7 fireplaces is situated on 1.66 acres in the picturesque Milbrook neighborhood of Greenwich with its own gated security and country club. Built with the finest interior and exterior designer finishes- cedar roof, copper gutters, cedar siding and front stone siding and chimneys comprised of stone from the surrounding grounds- There is no other new construction home currently on the market of this size and scale in town close to elite restaurants, school and shops. The walk out lower level with tall 10' ceilings and top floor feature multiple bars, fireplaces and flex spaces for sleeping quarters, office and recreational use for family or staff with all floors accessible by elevator. This energy efficient smart home compound sports a sophisticated AV prewired system in the theater room, music/dance studio, gym and separate golf simulator room with extra height ceilings in the garden level. Pet grooming station, second laundry room, sauna, beauty parlor and temperature controlled wine room finish the magnificent lower level wellness retreat. An inviting double height foyer, double staircase and soaring ceilings greet all who enter this breathtaking modern New England colonial home with open floor plan.
