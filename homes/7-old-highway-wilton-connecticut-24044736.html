About this home

Introducing the charming home you've been waiting for! This vintage farmhouse retreat in South Wilton is now available for rent. With 5 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, it offers ample space, comfort, and serenity for the entire family. Relax on the welcoming front porch or entertain in the beautiful living room, featuring a cozy stone fireplace and built-in shelving that highlight the home's 1930s character. Adjacent to the living room is a versatile family room, ideal for everyday living. The kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, a generous island, and a sunlit formal dining room that connects seamlessly-perfect for family gatherings. A powder room and mudroom complete the main floor. Upstairs, discover an elegant primary bedroom suite with his-and-hers closets and a full ensuite bath, along with four additional bedrooms, each with walk-in closets, and a hallway bath. The basement provides excellent storage space and includes a washer and dryer. Outside, enjoy the spacious, leveled backyard-perfect for outdoor fun and relaxation. Conveniently located near top-rated schools, stores, restaurants, and the scenic Norwalk River Trail, this home combines comfort with easy access to the best local amenities. Secure the opportunity to make it your own today!
