About this home

The perfect blend of charm, location, and space, 267 Brookside offers the very best of Darien living. Set on 1.55 level acres with views of the Country Club of Darien golf course, this beautifully renovated and expanded home feels both private and inviting. Inside, graciously scaled, light-filled spaces include an updated open kitchen and family room with breakfast nook and fireplace, complemented by formal living and dining rooms with high ceilings and custom built-ins. With four bedrooms and three full and one half baths, the home offers both comfort and flexibility for today's lifestyle. Ideally located near Ox Ridge Elementary School, Cherry Lawn Park, and just minutes to both downtown Darien and downtown New Canaan, this is effortless living in a truly exceptional setting.
