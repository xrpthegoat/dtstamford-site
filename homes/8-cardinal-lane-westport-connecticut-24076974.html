About this home

Colonial home set on 2 acres, privately situated on a quiet cul-de-sac. The home has been extended with high quality upgrades over the years and features: 5 bedrooms, 3 full baths and one-half bath. The main floor 5th bedroom is adjacent to the Living Room with a full bathroom and separate doorway onto front porch. The spacious gourmet kitchen with a large center island has updated appliances, plentiful seating at the island and separate dining area. Huge Family room off the kitchen with vaulted ceilings opens out onto a large deck overlooking the spacious and private backyard. Brick base and chimney flue are already in place should new owner want to install a woodburning stove. The Primary Bedroom has 2 closets and a spacious, beautifully updated bathroom with a soaking tub, separate shower and double sink vanity. The large, lower-level recreation room is bright and versatile, ideal for a home gym, game or media room. There is a separate laundry room on the lower level. City water, Septic, 200 amp electric. Boiler for heating & hot water and the Air conditioning system have both been updated since 2015. 2 car garage. Nice size shed opposite the garage, ideal for bikes, landscaping equipment, and outdoor toys. The home and property are a haven from the bustle of town, but still convenient to shops, restaurants, train stations and all that Westport offers.
