About this home

Discover this serene setting in a sought after Old Hill neighborhood. Two thoughtful and beautifully executed additions have made this sprawling 12 room home flow with the versatility to fit today's varied lifestyles. Comfortable open living spaces have been designed with the flexibility to offer multi-generational spaces, a home office or simply to allow for room to grow in the way that pleases you best. The welcoming living room with its broad bay window and formal fireplace provides ample room for your guests and the Baby Grand piano! The custom country kitchen is both practical and beautiful with wide plank wood floors, cherry and glass cabinetry, granite counters and a walk-in pantry. The adjoining family room with it's cathedral ceiling is filled with natural light and includes a fireplace, wet bar and French doors that open to a wraparound deck. The most recent addition is an impressive library with long, tall windows set amid tailored built-ins. An additional exterior entry makes it ideal for home office use while the adjacent full bathroom and closet offers the possibility of a first floor bedroom suite. The informal rear foyer includes access to a laundry area, powder room and extra closets. Just beyond is a 2nd private office or den. Upstairs you'll discover the primary suite with full bathroom and walk-in closet, as well as 4 very spacious additional bedrooms. Unseen value found in the new roof, updated mechanicals and whole house generator!
