About this home

Timeless elegance meets modern luxury in this meticulously restored vintage Colonial, Ideally situated in the highly sought after Revonah neighborhood just a short walk to everything downtown Stamford has to offer. Rich in architectural character, this exceptional home showcases coffered 9' ceilings, classic wainscoting, custom millwork, built-ins, handsome hardwood floors, and gracious sun-filled living spaces that beautifully preserve its historic charm while embracing today's lifestyle. Thoughtfully renovated throughout, the heart of the home is a gourmet chef's kitchen featuring premium appliances, custom cabinetry, & stone countertops, flowing seamlessly into inviting gathering spaces & the outdoor entertaining area. Luxuriously renovated marble baths offer timeless sophistication, while the versatile third-floor suite provides two additional rooms and a full bath ideal for guests, a home office, playroom, or au pair. Extensive improvements include Andersen windows & doors, blending classic beauty with modern efficiency. Outdoors, enjoy a private backyard hideaway featuring a spacious stone patio, professionally landscaped grounds+outdoor cooking station perfect for entertaining or quiet evenings at home. Rarely does a home so successfully preserve its original character while offering the quality, comfort, & amenities of modern living. A truly turnkey opportunity in one of Stamford's premier walk-to-everything neighborhoods.
