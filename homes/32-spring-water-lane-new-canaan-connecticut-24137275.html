About this home

Charming 1930's cottage has been totally updated. Loads of charm and loads of space. The kitchen alone is worth a look. Huge island, breakfast nook, top of the line appliances and it opens to a cozy den. The formal living room/dining room boasts historic details--amazing fireplace, leaded glass windows, wood beams, and cathedral ceiling. A large primary suite features 2 large walk in closets, updated spa like bath and office/loft, perfect for work from home space. There are 3 additional bedrooms and 2 more updated full baths. A large family room has a fireplace, built ins and sliding door to lovely bluestone terrace. The expansive yard is private and flat. The detached 2 car garage also has room for storage above. All of this conveniently located close to schools, parks, train and the Merritt Parkway. Must see.
