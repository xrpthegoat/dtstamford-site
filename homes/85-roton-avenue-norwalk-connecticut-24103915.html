About this home

This is a rare opportunity to live in one of Rowayton's most stunning waterfront locations. Situated on the edge of scenic Farm Creek, this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers expansive, unobstructed water views that create a peaceful and inspiring backdrop year-round. From morning sunrises to the changing colors of the seasons, the natural beauty surrounding this home is truly exceptional. Inside, the chef's kitchen features top-of-the-line appliances, including a Viking Professional 6-burner range with hood, Thermador refrigerator, Miele dishwasher, wine refrigerator, and a large walk-in pantry. The kitchen opens to an eat-in area with sliding glass doors leading to a stone patio-perfect for outdoor dining and enjoying the tranquil setting. The spacious family room includes a wood-burning fireplace, a wall-mounted TV, and additional sliding doors to the patio. The primary bedroom suite features radiant heat in the ensuite bathroom, a generous walk-in closet, and a private balcony with breathtaking creek views. Other amenities include a detached two-car garage with a 220V outlet for EV charging, new high-efficiency propane boiler, upgraded internet and alarm systems, Ring doorbell, and a smart front door lock. Just a short walk to Bailey Beach and the heart of Rowayton village. Unfurnished 1-2 year lease. Tenant responsible for all utilities. No smoking.
