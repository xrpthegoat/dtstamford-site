About this home

Beautifully appointed contemporary Colonial on a quiet private road in Westport, just minutes from the beach, town shops, restaurants, and everyday conveniences. Offered fully furnished for a seasonal rental, this spacious home offers comfortable, sophisticated living. The home features high-end European furnishings from distinguished designers, an impressive collection of artwork, and carefully selected finishes. The main level includes a light-filled Great Room and chef's kitchen with a substantial stone island, gas range, geometric backsplash, and breakfast bar. The living areas open to a private stone patio and beautifully landscaped grounds. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with a distinctive barn-door entry and spa-inspired bath featuring a deep freestanding soaking tub and tree-lined views. An additional bedroom is comfortably furnished for guests. The lower level and other flexible spaces offer room for a home office, additional living area, fitness space, guest area, or private retreat. With generous space throughout, the layout can adapt easily to different needs. Fully furnished and move-in ready, this Westport home combines high-end European design, curated artwork, generous living space, and a private setting close to the beach, shops, and restaurants.
