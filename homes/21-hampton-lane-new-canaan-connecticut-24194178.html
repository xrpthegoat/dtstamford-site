About this home

Charm Of A Cape With A Unique Layout That Lives Like A Split-Level. The inviting floor plan features a step-down living room and dining room, creating distinct spaces for entertaining and everyday living. Offering three bedrooms and three full baths, the spacious primary suite includes two walk-in closets and a private full bath. Additional highlights include a first-floor family room, some hardwood flooring, two fireplaces, central air conditioning, and a propane generator for added peace of mind. The finished lower level provides flexible space for a recreation room, home office, gym, or guest area. Situated on a beautiful, approximately half-acre lot on a private road, this property offers a peaceful setting while remaining conveniently close to town, shopping, restaurants, and the train station. Completing the package is an attached two-car garage and plenty of space both inside and out to enjoy. A wonderful opportunity to own a charming home in a desirable location with comfort, convenience, and versatile living spaces throughout.
