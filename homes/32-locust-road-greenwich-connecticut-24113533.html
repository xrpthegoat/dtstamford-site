About this home

Enter through the gated driveway to this ranch-style residence, quietly set on nearly four acres in Greenwich. The property offers a tranquil atmosphere with mature landscaping, a slate terrace, and an in-ground pool. While the home has good bones, it presents a prime opportunity for renovation and customization. Inside, the front foyer opens to a combination of hardwood and carpeted flooring. The primary bedroom features wood floors and a private bath, while two additional bedrooms-also with hardwood floors share a full hallway bathroom. The living room includes wall paneling, a large picture window, carpeting, and a classic stone fireplace. Just off the living room, the dining area transitions between carpet and wood floors. The kitchen with access to the rear patio and pool, includes vinyl flooring, laminate counters, and dated appliances such as an electric stove, microwave, and dishwasher, a newer refrigerator. Adjacent to the kitchen is a laundry area with a utility sink. A bright sunroom with sliding glass doors opens to the backyard and also provides entry to the attached garage. Off the dining room are stairs to a private office w/hardwood floors and a full bath offers potential as a home office, studio, or guest suite/fourth bedroom. A walk-out basement adds valuable storage or finishing possibilities. With a functional layout and substantial lot, this home is well-suited for a buyer eager to restore and personalize a property in Greenwich. "Sold As Is"
