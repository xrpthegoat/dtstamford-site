About this home

Wow! The hard work has been done, and all you need to do is unpack your belongings~ Nestled on a quiet corner lot this spacious home 4 bed, 2 full bath home offers a decorative fireplace plus a new 4 season sunroom/office allowing for comfortable living! Enjoy new energy efficient windows, a new water heater, dual-zone central air/heating as well as fresh paint throughout. The lower level combines a new side-by-side washer & dryer with a convenient mudroom off the attached 1 car garage. Additional parking for 2 cars in the driveway. There's an abundance of storage in the bright, clean & basement and a separate utility room! Step out from the living/dining area through French doors onto a new wrap around deck leading to a stone patio & a fully fenced-in backyard. The lawn has been seeded and you'll be able to enjoy an effortless irrigation system! Tech enthusiasts will appreciate the fiber internet connection, pre-wired network connection on all 3 levels, wifi thermostats as well as an 240V outlet in the garage for EV charging. The location, the peaceful & friendly neighborhood as well as the care & attention to detail brought to this house contributes to creating a very desirable place to call home! Public records sq. footage does not take into account newly heat & cooled sun room/ office & unheated lower level laundry/mud room. Take note of Hindley Elementary, Blue Ribbon School.
