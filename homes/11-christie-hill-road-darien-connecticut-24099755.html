About this home

Situated high on a fully fenced private half acre surrounded by mature plantings sits this meticulously maintained 1910 Colonial Farmhouse. Covered porches welcome visitors into this sunny and gracious home featuring four bedrooms, three full baths (primary bathroom and hall bath with radiant heat), living room with fireplace and coffered ceiling, dining room, eat-in kitchen, and family room with built-in shelves. Spacious primary suite offers a sitting room, walk-in closet, and full bath. Three additional bedrooms and full bath on second floor and a third floor playroom. Professionally landscaped property with irrigation system and full house generator. Conveniently located to elementary school, train, town, and shopping.
