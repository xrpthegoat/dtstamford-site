About this home

Set on an elevated, private acre in Greenwich, 23 Daffodil Lane is a thoughtfully updated Colonial with mature, expansive grounds anchored by a stone patio for outdoor entertaining and a potential pool site for future enhancement. That same sense of flexibility carries inside, where a first-floor guest suite with ensuite bedroom and separate living room offers privacy and versatility, an oversized second-floor family room doubles as a fifth bedroom, and the walk-out lower level includes a home gym. Generously scaled principal rooms are equally suited to entertaining and everyday life, highlighted by a dramatic double-height entrance and a comfortable family room with a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace. This home has been maintained and upgraded consistently over the years, with quarter-sawn oak flooring, custom cabinetry, and millwork reinforcing its elevated finish throughout. Just a short drive to Tod's Point and downtown Greenwich.
