About this home

Two-Family House with 3rd level In-law Apartment. This home has been lovingly cared for by the same owner for 69 years!! The first floor unit features a spacious Living room with fireplace and hardwood floors under the carpet. The Den is a flex space which was once the Dining Room and a Bedroom. The large Eat-in Kitchen has stainless appliances with access to the back hall and partially finished full basement with washer/dryer. This unit has 2 bedrooms, one with a 1/2 bath and a hall full bath completes this level. The 2nd floor apartment has hardwood floors, Kitchen, Living room, 3 bedrooms and full bath. The 3rd level has a huge In-law apartment with the 3rd full bath. This home is owner occupied with family living on each level, therefore no rental history. This well maintained home also has replacement windows and a 2 car garage, located in a very convenient neighborhood.
