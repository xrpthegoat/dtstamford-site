Please ensure all offers contain signed disclosures and pre-approval or proof of funds Welcome to 1087 Stillwater Road - Classic Charm Meets Modern Comfort Discover this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home nestled in one of Stamford's most convenient locations. Set on nearly half an acre of level land, this sun-filled home offers a perfect blend of comfort, function, and timeless style. Step into the open-concept main floor where a spacious Living Room welcomes you with a large bay window and a cozy fireplace-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The Dining area features sliders that lead to a private deck overlooking the expansive backyard, while the updated granite and stainless-steel Kitchen includes a breakfast bar, perfect for casual meals or morning coffee. Upstairs, you'll find a generous Primary Bedroom, two additional bedrooms, and a full bath with classic finishes. The Lower Level offers a comfortable Family Room with access to the one-car garage, a half bath, laundry area, and walk-out to the yard. A lower-level bonus room that is ideal for a home gym, office, playroom, or extra storage. Hardwood floors throughout, custom moldings, and a flexible layout make this home move-in ready. All this in a fantastic location just minutes from downtown Stamford, top-rated schools, shopping, restaurants, golf, tennis, and multiple commuting option
