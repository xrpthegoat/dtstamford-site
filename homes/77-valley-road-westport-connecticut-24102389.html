About this home

Charming Greens Farms Gem - Newly Renovated From Top to Bottom! Welcome to this stylishly furnished rental in the sought-after neighborhood. This 3-bedroom/3-bath residence has been thoughtfully updated, blending timeless charm with modern comforts. Situated in a prime Westport location, this home offers easy access to everything that makes life here so special. You're just minutes from beautiful beaches, local shops, and award-winning restaurants, as well as Longshore Club Park, where you can enjoy golf, tennis, swimming, sailing, and so much more. Only 5 minutes from the train station and 95. The convenience and lifestyle can't be beat. Upon entering, you're greeted by a light-filled living room, a cozy den, and a full formal dining room-ideal for gatherings and everyday living. The eat-in kitchen offers a welcoming space to cook and dine, featuring stylish finishes and plenty of room to enjoy morning coffee or casual meals. Upstairs, you'll find 2 generously sized BRs w/ample closet space & natural light. The fully finished lower level adds wonderful flexibility with a 3rd bedroom, full bath, & spacious rec room that opens directly onto the brand-new deck. Step outside to enjoy a fully fenced backyard, perfect for outdoor entertaining, summer BBQs, or simply relaxing in your private oasis.
