About this home

Custom-built in 2023 & set at the end of a desirable Long Lots cul-de-sac in Westport, this Modern Colonial filled w/high end features not found in a spec home offers over 6,000 sqft across 4 finished levels. Designed for today's lifestyle, the open floor plan features a custom kitchen w/high end appliances, 2 islands, & walk-in pantry which flows seamlessly into the large dining room & spacious family room. The dining room, which can accommodate both intimate & large gatherings, offers a full wine fridge & wet bar area w/custom cabinetry. The family room w/custom built-in cabinetry, gas fireplace, & full access to the pool & outdoor entertaining spaces for harmonious indoor/outdoor living. The primary suite offers a vaulted ceiling, gas fireplace, dual walk-in closets, & a spa-like bath w/heated floors creating an oasis for rest & relaxation. Three additional bedrooms, custom built-in workstation & laundry complete the second floor. The third floor designed as a possible 6th bedroom option w/walk-in closet & full bath would also make an excellent bonus room or even second office. The finished walk-out lower level features a sun filled bedroom, full bath, spacious family room space, & gym w/dry bar. The private, fully fenced yard is meticulously landscaped creating an ideal place to relax by the saltwater pool & spa, cook in the outdoor kitchen, or relax on the patio with automatic screens. Minutes from schools, commuting, & shopping make this a home not to be missed.
