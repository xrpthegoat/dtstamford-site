About this home

This colonial home in the sought-after Silvermine neighborhood is tucked at the end of a quiet lane off Old Kings Highway, and is private and peaceful, yet incredibly accessible. You're just minutes from the downtowns of both Wilton and New Canaan, multiple train stations, and the Merritt Parkway. The main level flows seamlessly. A spacious eat-in kitchen complete with deck (where you'll catch breathtaking sunset views!), the dining room with picture windows and an oversized living room complete with a fireplace and walls of windows that frame the private front and side yards, all make for effortless living and entertaining. The vaulted-ceiling family room offers a perfect retreat complete with a home office tucked around the corner with extra storage space. The fully finished walk-out lower level features a half bath and possible 5th bedroom/flex space. The primary suite features a marble bathroom, spacious shower, a walk-in closet and picture windows looking over the treetops. The home enjoys the convenience of city water, easy-care grounds and a whole-house automatic generator. And as added perk, there's a walking path down to Forge Road (and just a 10-minute stroll to Greybarns)!
