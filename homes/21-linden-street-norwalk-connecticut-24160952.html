About this home

Welcome to Linden Ridge, one of Norwalk's most desirable condo communities. This spacious townhouse offers 2 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, featuring a large living room with a wood-burning fireplace and a dining area with hardwood floors-perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The walk-through kitchen provides great functionality, while the upper level includes a primary ensuite with two large custom closets, a second bedroom with wall-to-wall carpeting, a hallway full bath and a convenient stackable washer/dryer. An unfinished basement offers excellent potential for additional living space along with ample storage. Completing the home is a generous one-car attached garage. Ideally located just seconds from Main Avenue shopping, restaurants, the Merritt Parkway, and Route 7 Connector for easy access to I-95 and the South Norwalk train station. A perfect place to call home!
