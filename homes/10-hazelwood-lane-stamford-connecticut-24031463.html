About this home

Welcome to this beautifully rebuilt Cape Cod/Ranch, meticulously redesigned from the studs in 2008 to offer a seamless blend of contemporary elegance and functional accessibility. This single-floor residence is located in a peaceful residential neighborhood, this three-bedroom, 2.5-bath gem sits on a 0.24-acre corner lot and boasts an open floor plan perfect for modern living. As you approach the home, a Belgian block-lined driveway leads to an oversized two-car garage. Step inside to discover the open layout that harmoniously connects the living room, kitchen, and dining room. The primary and secondary bedroom are situated off of the dining room. The family room and third bedroom are located away from the main living areas, and could be used as a separate suite. Dramatic 13' ceilings enhance the spaciousness of the home. Stunning hardwood floors with radiant heat flooring throughout provide warmth and comfort. The home is uniquely designed to be fully handicap accessible, featuring wide hallways and doors, a roll-in shower, and a completely step-free layout. A full house generator ensures uninterrupted power supply.Surround sound in living room and central vac throughout. Garage and partial basement provide ample space for storage. The quiet and serene neighborhood location offers a retreat from the hustle and bustle, yet is just minutes away from the shops and restaurants on High Ridge and Downtown Stamford.
