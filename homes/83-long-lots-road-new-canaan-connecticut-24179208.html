About this home

Perched on a sunny knoll overlooking two acres of beautifully landscaped grounds, this thoughtfully expanded center hall colonial in coveted Hoyt Farms is a rare find. Mature specimen trees of remarkable scale anchor the property, lending an established presence that simply cannot be replicated. Inside, a seamless expansion honors the home's colonial origins while adding the generous proportions that today's living demands - five bedrooms, six baths, and a layout configured for both practical family life and gracious entertaining. The pool area is the undisputed centerpiece; a stunning pool and separate spa set the stage for effortless gatherings, while a fully equipped outdoor kitchen and wood-fired pizza oven make it a natural destination from spring through fall. At this scale, on these grounds, in Hoyt Farms - 83 Long Lots is a genuinely special offering.
