About this home

Discover luxurious living in this stylish 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo, nestled in an upscale elevator building in Norwalk. Built in 2005, this residence seamlessly blends modern elegance with comfort and convenience. Beautifully appointed kitchen featuring granite countertops, premium stainless steel appliances, and a custom tile backsplash. Spacious layout includes rich hardwood floors and impressive 9+ foot ceilings. The primary suite offers a serene escape with a jacuzzi tub, stall shower, and ample walk-in closet space. Cozy up by the gas fireplace in the bright and inviting living room, or step out to your private porch to savor the outdoors. Perfectly located within walking distance to the train and convenient access to Route 7, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, facilitating easy commuting.
