About this home

Fantastic waterfront location on Holly Pond in desirable Darien, CT. This three bedroom, two bath ranch has been extensively renovated to include all new stainless appliances, stove and dishwasher. Washer/dryer in basement, Spacious living room with wood burning fireplace. Newly refinished hardwood floors. New energy efficient HVAC system including central air. Large basement with storage area. One car attached garage. New covered deck looking out on the Pond. Flat almost one acre with fenced in backyard. Walk to YMCA. In the Blue Ribbon Hindley School district. Lawn care and plowing included. Credit check, references, and two months security required. Ask about pets. One year or more lease. House is set back 180 feet from the road.
