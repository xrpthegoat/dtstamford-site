About this home

Prestigious Hoyt Farm Tudor: An Elegant Two-Acre Oasis Near the Village. Nestled on two beautifully landscaped, ultra-private acres in the highly coveted Hoyt Farm neighborhood, this exceptional Tudor offers over 7,400 square feet of sophisticated luxury and warmth. A grand, two-story foyer welcomes you into a seamless, light-filled layout featuring a formal living room with dramatic vaulted ceilings, a handsome wood-paneled library, and an open chef's kitchen that flows effortlessly into a spacious family room with a fireplace. The primary suite is a true sanctuary, boasting its own cozy fireplace, a private balcony overlooking the grounds, a generous dressing room, and a sun-drenched, spa-like bath. Four additional bedrooms and four-and-a-half baths ensure plenty of space and comfort for everyone. Outside, your personal resort awaits: host gatherings on the tranquil brick terrace by the outdoor fireplace, swim in the stunning heated pool, or simply enjoy the park-like property framed by mature specimen trees. Complete with a three-car garage, this magnificent estate offers unparalleled privacy and convenience, located just minutes from the train, vibrant village life, and top-rated New Canaan schools.
