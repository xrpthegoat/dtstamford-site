About this home

Looking for your dream rental home? Look no further, you've found your destination! Experience stylish, urban living right in the heart of Downtown Stamford! This meticulously maintained townhouse built in 2012, features three bedrooms, two-and-a-half bathrooms, and 1,999sqft of living space -each detail of the dwelling exudes sophistication and superior quality. The main level enjoys an open-concept living and dining area, accentuated by French doors leading to a charming patio. A modern kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a contemporary backsplash, and a handy powder room. Additionally, a full-sized washer and dryer form the laundry space, located near the kitchen. The second level unveils a lavish primary bedroom featuring a vaulted ceiling and walk-in closet, along with two additional bedrooms and a full bathroom. Nestled in the heart of Stamford, this dwelling provides effortless access to a range of amenities, from top-notch dining options and cafes, to shopping and leisure facilities. It's the perfect setting for those desiring an upscale living experience. Moreover, Stamford's Amtrak/Metro Train Station is merely a stone's throw away, ensuring rapid transit to NYC in just 55 minutes.
