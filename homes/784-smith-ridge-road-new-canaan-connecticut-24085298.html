About this home

New Canaan Country Retreat. This beautifully renovated home blends modern elegance with a lifestyle of comfort and convenience. Designed with today's lifestyle in mind, the home's heart is an open floor plan where the kitchen, dining, and family rooms merge into a seamless space for living and entertaining. The chef's kitchen features an oversized quartz island and high-end appliances, while the family room boasts a striking stone fireplace, cathedral ceilings, and skylights. Entertain in the gracious light filled living room with a fireplace and French doors, or work in peace in the warm paneled library with yet another fireplace. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a marble bath and walk-in closet, alongside three additional bedrooms and a laundry room. The third floor provides flexible space for a playroom or office. Outside, enjoy two decks, a saltwater heated pool, expansive play area that promises endless summer days for relaxation. For the car enthusiasts there is a four-car tandem garage. This retreat is a stone's throw from New Canaan's award-winning schools and charming village, offering the best in sophisticated country living.
