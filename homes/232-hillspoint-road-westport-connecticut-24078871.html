About this home

Light-filled Contemporary/Ranch offering breathtaking water views of the LI Sound. Embrace the coveted Compo Beach lifestyle while enjoying the spectacular sunrises and sunsets from the comfort of the sunroom or the sun-drenched deck. The open-concept layout is thoughtfully designed and bathed in natural light, with walls of windows framing the sweeping water vistas. The sleek Poggenpohl kitchen with stainless steel appliances opens seamlessly to a private rear terrace - perfect for outdoor dining and grilling - set within a secluded garden setting. The serene primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a sitting area, spa-inspired bath and its own private deck. Three additional generously sized bedrooms with hardwood floors, custom built-ins ideal for work from home needs, and another full bath complete the bedroom wing. Set on a beautifully landscaped property with an outdoor shower, this home delivers both tranquility and convenience. Ideally located just moments away from Compo Beach and Westport's premier Longshore Park offering dining, golf, pool, boating, tennis, and marina access. Minutes to the train station, and vibrant town center with all of Westport's renowned amenities. An exceptional opportunity to live, work, and unwind by the water - where refined coastal living meets timeless memories. The monthly rent of $20,000 is for a 12 month lease. Shorter term rents are Seasonally adjusted. Summer rent for June, July and August is $33,000 per month
