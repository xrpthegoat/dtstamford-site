About this home

DIRECT WATERFRONT -- Experience the best of harbor-side living in this stunningly renovated direct waterfront rental. With docks right out your back door, this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom home perfectly balances modern style with tranquility. The open-concept main level features a cozy fireplace, beautiful white oak floors, renovated kitchen and private balcony with fresh air and river views. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with its own private balcony overlooking the water. Practicality is built-in with a pull-down attic, a dedicated storage room, one garage space, and an additional unassigned parking spot. Enjoy a low-maintenance lifestyle, as the rent conveniently includes heat, water, trash, & snow removal. Located just moments from the train, local shops, and dining, this home is a rare find!
