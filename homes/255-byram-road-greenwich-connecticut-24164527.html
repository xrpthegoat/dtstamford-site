About this home

Welcome to 255 Byram Road - a charming and move-in ready Colonial that offers a thoughtful layout and updates throughout. The main level is anchored by a welcoming foyer, a living room with fireplace, and a dining room with newly refinished hardwood floors. The kitchen and powder room complete the first floor. Upstairs are three bedrooms, a full bath, and an additional room perfect for a home office or playroom. A screened porch overlooks the flat yard, and a one-car garage completes the property. The home is ideally located near downtown Greenwich, restaurants, shops, parks, and the waterfront. Proximity to the train and I-95 offers easy commuting.
