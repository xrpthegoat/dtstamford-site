About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet West Norwalk cul-de-sac, this distinctive Mid-Century Modern home offers a rare combination of architectural character and natural beauty. Set on nearly an acre along the scenic Five Mile River, the property provides a peaceful waterfront setting with wooded views and exceptional privacy. The open-concept main level features abundant natural light, hardwood floors, a two-sided fireplace, and seamless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas. Offering 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and approximately 1,925 square feet of living space, the home blends comfort and functionality with a flexible lower level ideal for family room, guest space, or home office. Enjoy morning coffee or evening gatherings on the expansive deck overlooking the tranquil surroundings. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, Metro-North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, this unique property is a rare opportunity to own a waterfront retreat in sought-after West Norwalk.
