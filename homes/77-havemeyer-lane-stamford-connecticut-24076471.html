About this home

Palmer Hill! Stunning, 3 bedroom/3 1/2 bath completely updated townhouse located in the very desirable, gated, Palmer Hill community, on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. This beautiful home has been totally repainted. The main floor has an open plan living/dining room, gourmet kitchen with new SS appliances, gas fireplace, custom lighting, wood floors, powder room, mud room and 2 car attached garage. There is a wonderful, private patio off the main level. The upper floor has a skylight lit loft, primary bedroom suite with French doors, tray ceiling, custom lighting, luxury bath and custom walk in closet. There are 2 additional bedrooms both with walk in custom closets, new carpet, and custom lighting. A full bathroom and laundry room complete this floor. The lower level is completely finished with new carpet, full bathroom, and additional storage. Amenities include a clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym with yoga room above, and business center. Minutes to I95 and Metro North. Dog Friendly! Move Right In!
