About this home

Welcome to this charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath ranch-style home, the perfect "downsizer" on one level, or 1st time buyer condo alternative. 134 Courtland is perfectly situated in a highly convenient location, near exit 9 of I-95. Lovingly maintained by the same owner for 63 years, this property is ready for new owners to add their personal touch and make it very special. The home features a cozy fireplace, ideal for warming up on colder nights, and a spacious private backyard, perfect for outdoor gatherings or quiet relaxation. The beautiful curb appeal is enhanced by a meticulously manicured lawn, making a great first impression. Located just minutes from shopping, dining, entertainment, Chelsea Piers, the Glenbrook or main train stations, and downtown Stamford and Darien, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Additional features include full attic for storage, updated windows, newer furnace and oil tank. Plus, you're just a quick drive away from Cove Island Park, where you can enjoy time at the beach and unwind by the water.
