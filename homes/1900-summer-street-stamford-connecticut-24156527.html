About this home

Located in the desirable Greenbriar complex on Summer Street in Stamford, this 2nd and 3rd floor townhouse style condominium offers spacious living with convenient access to downtown, shopping, dining, Metro-North, and Scalzi Park. This 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath unit features a clean and functional layout with generously sized rooms, abundant storage, and reserved garage parking. The main level includes a full kitchen with dining area open to the living room, fireplace, half bath, and a bright sunroom offering additional living space and natural light. The upper level features two spacious bedroom suites, each with its own full bathroom, providing flexibility and privacy for a variety of living arrangements.
