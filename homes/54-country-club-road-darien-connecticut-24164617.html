About this home

Some homes are ready for a new life the moment the door opens. This is one of them.Tucked into one of Darien's most beloved cul-de-sac neighborhoods, this beautifully updated four-bedroom, three-and-a-half-bath colonial strikes the balance every renter is looking for: the warmth of classic New England architecture with every modern comfort already in place. No projects. No waiting. Just move in and settle in. A showpiece eat-in kitchen anchors the main level alongside a formal dining room and bright living room. The family room, centered around a fireplace, is the kind of space that keeps people in the room. A dedicated home office, private workout room, and large finished playroom give everyone their own corner of the house. Outside, a welcoming patio opens to a beautifully framed backyard with stone walls, mature plantings, and built-in irrigation. A two-car garage, central air, and whole-house generator provide comfort and peace of mind in every season. Talmadge Hill Metro-North is just moments away, with easy access to New Canaan and Stamford. And beyond the commute, Darien delivers: private beaches, boating, tennis, boutique shopping, and a community calendar that fills itself. Available May 1, 2026. Credit check, rental application, and two-month security deposit required.
