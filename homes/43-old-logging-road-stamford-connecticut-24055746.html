About this home

Welcome home to this sun drenched sprawling ranch on idyllic property. The kitchen has a country flair with fireplace, a generous eat-in area and opens to screened in porch. The oversized living room is flooded with natural light. An additional room is flexible as formal dining room, den or playroom. The primary bedroom offers a large bathroom with whirlpool tub and walk in shower. 3 additional bedrooms have ensuite baths, and a 5th bedroom is flexible as a home office. Laundry room and powder room are also conveniently located off the foyer. Generator added in 2018. Gunite pool was never used by current owner and is offered "as is".
