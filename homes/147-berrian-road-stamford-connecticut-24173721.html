About this home

**HIGHEST & BEST: MONDAY, 5/18 @5PM** Pepper Ridge is one of those Stamford neighborhoods buyers wait for: quiet streets, great commuting options, and a true neighborhood feel. Set on a dead-end street minutes to the Merritt Pkwy, MetroNorth train to NYC, Trader Joe's, High Ridge shopping, and Newfield Swim Club. Sellers have taken meticulous care and it shows! Sitting on nearly flat .6 acres with mature evergreens, the setting is just as special as the home itself. Inside, oversized windows and natural light create a warm, inviting feel throughout. The layout offers great flow between the living and dining spaces, ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The living room centers around a cozy wood-burning fireplace, while the updated kitchen features double ovens, gas cooktop, peninsula seating and excellent storage. The breakfast area with skylights and windows overlooking the backyard connects you to the outdoors, the kind of space that makes morning coffee feel better. Right off the 2-car garage is a custom mudroom and office space with built-ins, cubbies, hooks, and storage buyers dream about. Upstairs offers a spacious primary suite plus additional bedrooms filled with natural light. Outside, the backyard offers flagstone patios/walkways and expansive flat lawn space perfect for sports, swing sets, gardening, or even a future pool. Additional features include public utilities, updated mechanicals, and CT Basement French drains. Pepper Ridge living at its best.
