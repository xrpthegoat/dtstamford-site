About this home

Once in a lifetime, a home of this caliber becomes available. Sited on a cul-de-sac in a coveted neighborhood close to town, this exceptional residence pairs custom design with luxurious indoor-outdoor living areas enhanced with smart technology and supported by upgraded mechanicals. A gracious front-to-back foyer showcases elegant living and dining rooms. Everyday living and elevated entertaining are effortless with a new custom kitchen designed by Deane appointed with Simon Pearce pendants, premium appliances, wine frig, and a Zip tap for still and sparkling water. A sun filled eat-in area flows seamlessly into a coffered ceiling family room with fireplace and a covered porch-with flat screen TV, stone fireplace and Bromic heating. A richly paneled office, mudroom and second staircase complete the main level. Upstairs, five bedrooms include a serene primary suite with fireplace and sitting room, a private guest suite and an additional office. The outdoor experience is nothing short of a 5-star resort-with a saltwater Glengate pool, sun shelf, spa and a new outdoor kitchen fully equipped with a built-in grill, pizza oven, Traeger smoker, and refrigeration-all surrounded by stone terraces, lush landscaping, audio system and curated lighting. The lower level boasts a chalet-inspired retreat with recreation, fitness rooms, an infrared sauna, and bedroom suite with a flexible private entry. An exceptional offering for those seeking outstanding craftsmanship and luxury!
