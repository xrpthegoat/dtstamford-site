About this home

Quiet neighborhood, private paved road , large flat open landscaped property and a classic 5 bedroom 2.5 bath home colonial in South Wilton in move in condition is ready for you to make memories. Enjoy the Luxury Kitchen with hardwood floors which is open to family room with fireplace as well as private den and formal dining room. The home has updated bathrooms of tile and granite. The master suite has a personal sauna as well as an oversized walkin closet that currently is an adjoining bedroom. A full home Generac generator with in ground 500 gallon propane storage supply provides 24/7 emergency coverage. The 1.5 acres is professionally landscaped with formal stone walls with a beautiful backyard pond and oversized composite deck and storage shed. The 1500 gallon septic system was relocated and renewed in 2014 .The home has central air conditioning on the upper level and a portable unit for main level.
