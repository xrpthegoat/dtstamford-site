About this home

This desirable condo offers a spacious primary ensuite with a large bathroom, plus a flexible den that can serve as a second bedroom with its own full bath. The living room features a charming fireplace and opens to a private balcony. Additional highlights include in-unit laundry, an indoor parking space, and a private storage unit. Residents of the sought-after Gables complex enjoy an array of amenities: swimming pool, fitness center, squash court, and a party room for entertaining. Perfectly located near the heart of Old Greenwich, this home is close to the train station, Tod's Point Beach, Binney Park, and convenient access to I-95.
