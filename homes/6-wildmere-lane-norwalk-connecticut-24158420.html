About this home

Welcome to 6 Wildmere Lane, a thoughtfully renovated 3-bedroom, 3-bath Cape in a quiet Norwalk neighborhood close to everything. The light-filled first floor offers an open kitchen with breakfast bar, stainless steel double oven, deep sink, dishwasher, and recessed lighting flowing into the living room with an open staircase. The first-floor primary suite features a huge walk-in closet with bathroom access, creating a private retreat. A renovated full bath with new tile, vanity, and stand-up shower completes the main level. Upstairs, 2 spacious bedrooms with new flooring can each accommodate king beds, plus a second renovated full bath and 2 eave storage areas accessible from the bedrooms. The full-height heated basement is a standout: dedicated laundry room with sink and cabinets, third full bath, and flexible storage/office/playroom with 2 closets, and a 1 car garage. Recent updates include a Trex front deck, replaced back deck, fresh paint inside and out, new front walkway, and back retaining wall. The private backyard with deck and patio offers a peaceful retreat. The location gives you everything you want from Westport Ave, including Stew Leonards, Wave Hill Breads, and more, but is tucked in a neighborhood that you can enjoy without through traffic. Close to the beach, aquarium, Cranbury Park, SoNo and more, this home offers an ideal blend of comfort, storage, and location.
