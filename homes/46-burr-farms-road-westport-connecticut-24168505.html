About this home

Experience turnkey Westport living in this beautifully furnished home, ideally located in the heart of town. Filled with natural light and designed for both comfort and style, the home features soaring ceilings and an open flow throughout. The gourmet eat-in kitchen with large island, pantry, and high-end appliances opens to the spacious family room and bluestone patio erfect for everyday living and entertaining. A formal dining room comfortably seats eight. The main level offers a private office and a first-floor bedroom with full bath, ideal for guests or flexible living. An additional space can serve as a gym or optional bedroom. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a sitting area, two walk-in closets, and a spa-like bath. Three additional en suite bedrooms and a playroom with bar and half bath provide ample space. Set on beautifully landscaped grounds, enjoy a gunite saltwater pool and spa, plus a pool house with bar, TV, bathroom and outdoor shower. Offered fully furnished for effortless living.
