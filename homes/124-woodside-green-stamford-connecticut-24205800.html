About this home

A wonderful opportunity to own a spacious and beautifully updated alcove studio, perfect for both personal enjoyment or investment! Located in an excellent location, this inviting home has been freshly painted and thoughtfully renovated, featuring brand new soft-close kitchen cabinets, quartz countertops, new bathroom tile flooring, vanity and light fixtures, plus refinished wood floors throughout. Hot water and cooking gas are included in the common charges. The convenient on-site laundry room features brand new machines, and plenty of parking adds to the ease of living here. Enjoy all that Woodside Green has to offer, including a beautiful center green, scenic walkways, grilling areas, gazebo, dog area, and secure bike room. Ideally located across the street from grocery shopping, a gym, coffee shops, restaurants, and more. Woodside Green is a well-run, pet-friendly, and welcoming community. Don't miss this wonderful opportunity to own a beautifully updated home in a convenient and vibrant setting!
