About this home

Experience the epitome of modern luxury in this extraordinary 6-bedroom, 6/2 bath 7700+ sf new construction waterfront home with a pool and a dock. Built by Bear Paw Construction and designed by Don Fairbanks, this stunning light-filled home is finished on 4 levels. With meticulous attention to detail and a sleek, contemporary design, this home sets a new standard for sophistication. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame the breathtaking views, allowing natural light to flood the interiors and showcasing the serene setting creating a harmonious flow between the indoor and outdoor spaces. Step inside and a clean and streamlined aesthetic merge effortlessly. The main floor features a dining room, stylish study, living room/lounge area, and magnificent kitchen that transitions into the family room, complete with a fireplace. This seamless integration of luxury and beauty creates a space perfect for both casual entertainment and cosmopolitan refinement. Indulge in the sumptuous primary suite, offering unparalleled views and a luxurious bath. Four additional bedrooms and bathrooms complete the 2nd Floor. The walkout lower level offers flexible recreation space, additional bedroom and bathroom. On a peaceful cul-de-sac in Old Hill, embrace the picturesque surroundings that evoke a profound sense of peace, while enjoying close proximity to excellent shopping and amenities.
