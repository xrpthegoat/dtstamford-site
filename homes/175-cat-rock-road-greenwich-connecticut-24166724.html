About this home

Available furnished or unfurnished. This picturesque farmhouse sits on 2.6 acres of landscaping with an expansive lawn & antique stone walls. The original home was totally renovated with the highest quality by a renowned architect. The spacious light filled home has generous sized rooms w/ a total of 5 fireplaces & radiant heated wide oak floors on the main level. Every room on the main floor also offers access to the beautiful outside terraces & covered porches. The main house has 5 bedrooms on the 2nd floor & either a bedroom suite or multi purpose room on 1st floor. Custom built in's, millwork & wood beams are in the office, library and family room. Heated 2 car garage & a separate guest house (480 sq ft). Come fall in love with this beautiful home, the land, proximity to school & town.... All the best that Greenwich offers.
