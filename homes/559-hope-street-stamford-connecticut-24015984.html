About this home

Welcome to this stunning, 4-bedroom attached Colonial-style townhouse nestled between the desirable neighborhoods of Glenbrook and Springdale in the heart of Stamford. With over 2700 sq ft, including a spacious, open lower level with full bath, this home boasts the best value for living space in Stamford. Step into the living room, where a cozy gas fireplace creates a warm ambiance for relaxing evenings. The open-plan design leads to a sleek kitchen with stainless steel appliances, offering both functionality and elegance. Hardwood throughout the main floors. The family room with sliding doors opens on to a patio, creating an ideal space for indoor-outdoor living and entertaining. Upstairs you will find 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms and an office located that could double as 5th guest bedroom if desired. Cost effective and environmentally beneficial with majority of electricity provided via solar energy. This house is set on a low-maintenance 0.10-acre lot, with 3 other homes built as a PUD, perfect for those seeking a blend of convenience and style. With easy access to local amenities, transportation, and schools, this house offers a lifestyle of comfort and convenience. Don't miss out on the opportunity to make this your new home. Please see addendum for information on PUD and solar energy details.
