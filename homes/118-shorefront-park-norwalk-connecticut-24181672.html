About this home

Steps from vibrant South Norwalk, 118 Shorefront Park blends an exciting city lifestyle with exclusive coastal perks. Just a 10-min walk to the Metro-North train and moments from SoNo's top-tier restaurants, bars, and nightlife, this location is a commuter's dream. Enjoy deeded rights to the association's private beach/boat lots and social events without sacrificing city convenience. Set on a hill with year-round peekaboo water views, this fully updated turn-key home requires zero flood insurance! The dynamic 1,824 sq ft layout is optimized for modern living. A stunning chef's kitchen boasts custom cabinets, stainless appliances, quartz counters, and a walk-in pantry, opening directly to the main living space. Unwind in the light filled family room featuring sliders to the deck, a cozy wood stove, and movie projector setup, or relax by the living room's 2nd fireplace. The main floor also hosts a full bath and a bonus room with vaulted ceilings and a built-in desk, serving as the ultimate remote work headquarters or a 3rd bedroom. Upstairs, find a renovated full bath and 2 bright bedrooms, including the spacious primary retreat. Outside, the private deck and yard are perfect for hosting friends before heading downtown. Live where beach vibe meets city hustle, just a stone's throw from the train and SoNo!
