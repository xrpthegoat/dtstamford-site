About this home

This cozy 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom ranch, nestled in the historic & sought-after Silvermine area of Norwalk, awaits its discerning new owner. The residence features a comfortable-sized living room with a wood-burning fireplace and an eat-in kitchen. Both bedrooms boast substantial closet space for easy storage & organization. The main level is complemented by a tastefully styled bathroom & an enclosed den, enhancing single-floor living. The lower level houses the home's mechanical systems, with good storage space, laundry facilities, a washer/dryer, & a 1-car garage. A nice-sized yard for leisure and outdoor activities. This home provides access to major highways, a variety of shopping destinations, parks, & much more. Set on a private road, the property spans .41 acres. Home/Property being sold "as is."
