About this home

Welcome to 65 Willard Terrace! This spacious home with easy access to Merritt Parkway, local shopping and dining features a serene setting with town sewer being added in the spring/summer of 2025! The top level includes fresh paint throughout with new kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops and soft close cabinetry with access to upper deck for morning breakfast or evening entertainment, a primary bedroom with renovated ensuite bathroom plus two additional bedrooms with new full bath featuring soaking tub. The lower floor, also freshly painted, features a fourth bedroom perfect for an office along with family room with renovated wood burning fire place, half bath and sliders leading to the expansive back yard. Upgrades include recessed lighting, new HVAC system (8 years warranty remaining), refinished floors and new insulation. Best and Final by 6:00pm Monday January 13th, 2025, Please.
