About this home

Located just minutes from Downtown Westport and all it has to offer, this home is an amazing value to get into Westport. The home has an ideal flow for entertaining. Walking in the front door, you are welcomed in to a Living Room with fireplace open to spacious dining room. The large kitchen has a breakfast area and walk-in pantry (ideal for a half bath). Rounding out the main level, is an enclosed porch. The second level offers three bedroom and a full bath. With some renovations, this will be a dream home is a dream location. The home is sold "as-is" and subject to probate approval. Photos are virtually staged. SCG on-line indicates there is natural gas service available to the home if you wanted to convert from oil.
