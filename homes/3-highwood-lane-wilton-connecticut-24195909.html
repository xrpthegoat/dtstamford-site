About this home

2.09 AC buildable/conforming lot on idyllic/scenic side street. One of the best lots on the street - the lot is priced at a discount because of the power lines that run through a portion of the property. Plenty of room to build a large center hall colonial/dream home - the owner built 9 Highwood Lane and has house plans/survey/etc available if interested. The lot is triangle shaped with 368' of depth one on side and 451' on the other - would provide complete privacy along with scenic views overlooking the rolling hills. Right on the Westport line off of Newtown Tpke just minutes to RT.15, this is the once in a lifetime find priced to sell quick.
