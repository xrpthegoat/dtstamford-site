About this home

Welcome to 6 Bon Air Avenue. This classic 1925 Tudor style home has been meticulously kept while preserving the character and style of the era. Every room has unique features such as built ins, arched doorways, curved entrance doors, exposed beams, a dining nook, cedar closets, and rounded plaster walls to name a few. An abundance of natural light is evident throughout the home. The flow on the main floor has an open feeling, yet each room feels unique and cozy. This great combination of interior flow with preserved features is very hard to find. The home is situated on an oversized corner lot within walking distance to the Springdale train station and an has a private park like yard. The current owners have completely redone the yard with multiple outdoor entertainment areas, tons of new plantings for increased privacy, and updated irrigation. The interior has updated bath rooms, refinished hardwood floors (Oak/Douglas fir), quartz counters, water softener system, newer hot water heater, and refinished basement rec room. Great find close to train and in a quiet neighborhood.
