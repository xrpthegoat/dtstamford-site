About this home

This stunning custom Colonial at 125 Nod Hill Road was crafted by renowned architect Rick Moisan. Nestled on 2.48 acres with peaceful water views, the 6,046 sq ft residence blends timeless architecture with modern comfort in an idyllic setting. A mahogany front porch welcomes you inside, where high ceilings, custom millwork, and sun-filled rooms create an atmosphere of refined elegance. The thoughtful layout ensures every space has purpose, offering expansive living and dining areas perfect for entertaining. French doors open to a stone patio and a secluded backyard oasis surrounded by nature. The gourmet kitchen provides abundant workspace and is complemented by a well-appointed catering pantry, ideal for seamless hosting. Five bedrooms and six baths provide flexibility for family and guests. The luxurious primary suite offers water views, a wood-burning fireplace, and a versatile flex space perfect for an office or gym. Two spacious walk-in closets and a spa-like ensuite with whirlpool tub and private sauna create a haven of relaxation. The lower level offers excellent space for a gym, hobbies, or storage and includes a 600 bottle wine cellar. A spacious three-car garage adds convenience and ample room for vehicles or extra storage. Thoughtful updates, including a whole-house generator, ensure comfort and peace of mind, while the expansive lawn and vibrant gardens invite enjoyment in every season-all just minutes from Wilton Center and an hour commute to NYC.
