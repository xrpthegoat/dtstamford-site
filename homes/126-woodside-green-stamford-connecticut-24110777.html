About this home

With a prime location in midtown Stamford, this spacious and sun-drenched studio space, one-bath on the second-floor apartment puts you mere footsteps from one of the city's most thriving public parks, and nearly on the same block as some of the area's best restaurants, shops, and bars, as well as a six-screen movie theater next door. The amenities on the inside are just as strong a selling point, with classic hardwood floors running throughout the unit. The convenience, thankfully, doesn't require any concessions in square footage. The kitchen includes an abundance of cabinet and countertop space, and elsewhere in the apartment you'll find a large closet, a murphy-bed and plenty of overflow storage in the building's basement. Additional amenities include a community laundry and mail room, as well as an expansive green space surrounding the property with a charming gazebo. When you're craving more of an escape, you're just minutes from downtown, and walking distance to the train station. Residents are also provided with two parking spots, as well as guest passes for an additional spot. Water, hot water, and cooking gas are all included.
