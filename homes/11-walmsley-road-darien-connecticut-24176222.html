About this home

This is the one you've been waiting for! Don't miss your chance to own on one of Darien's most sought-after streets. This beautifully updated Colonial truly has it all-just over 3,600 sf, 4 BR, and 4.5 BTH, with a bright, open layout and fantastic flow that makes everyday living and entertaining effortless. A welcoming foyer leads to a flexible office/playroom then opens into spacious open concept living and dining area designed for gathering. Hardwood floors, high-end appliances, recessed lighting, designer fixtures, and freshly painted interior and kitchen make this home completely move-in ready. The walk-out lower level is a game changer-fully renovated with a full bath and wet bar, perfect for a media room, gym, guest space, movie night or whatever your needs. Plenty of storage throughout, plus, brand new HVAC systems (2025) with warranty mean peace of mind from day one. Outside, the home continues to impress. Enjoy a new fully fenced yard (2023), a tiered blue stone patio, flat usable lawn, sprinkler system, and upgraded landscape lighting-all surrounded by mature landscaping for total privacy. Even better, the protected land behind the property means no future building-this peaceful setting isn't going anywhere. All this less than 10 mins from the Noroton Heights Train (55 mins to GC), Heights Crossing, schools, daycare, restaurants, coffee shops, Equinox, grocery stores, beaches, and the train - this home checks every box.
