About this home

14 Windermere Lane - Elegance and modern comfort converge in this comprehensively renovated 6-bedroom, 5.5-bath residence, where every detail has been thoughtfully curated. A full-home renovation includes beautifully updated bathrooms throughout and a stunning open-concept kitchen designed for both effortless daily living and refined entertaining. The home also offers the convenience and flexibility of a desirable **first-floor primary suite**. Inside, freshly refinished floors, newly painted interiors, and thoughtfully selected lighting fixtures create a bright and sophisticated atmosphere. Convenience has been elevated with two sets of new washers and dryers, including a newly added second-floor laundry ideally located near the bedrooms. Beyond the interiors, the property is framed by professionally manicured landscaping that enhances the home's curb appeal. The two-car garage has been upgraded with 220-volt wiring to support dual electric vehicle charging stations. Additional improvements include a new boiler, new 80-gallon hot water heater, whole-house generator, and an external security system-providing both comfort and peace of mind. With every system and finish carefully considered, this move-in-ready home offers a seamless blend of luxury, efficiency, and modern convenience.
