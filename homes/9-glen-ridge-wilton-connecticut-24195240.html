About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated two-story townhouse in the highly desirable Glen River community, ideally located just moments from the heart of Wilton Village. Enjoy the convenience of walking to shops, restaurants, the library, parks, the Norwalk River Valley Trail, and the Metro-North train. Inside, the open-concept main level features gleaming hardwood floors, a spacious living and dining area, an updated kitchen, and a convenient powder room-ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, the sun-filled primary bedroom boasts a vaulted ceiling and a generous walk-in closet. A versatile second room is perfect as a guest bedroom, home office, or study, while a full hall bath and an oversized hallway closet provide excellent additional storage. Central air conditioning ensures year-round comfort. Residents of Glen River enjoy a community pool, beautifully landscaped grounds, and the convenience of a covered parking space just steps from the unit. Offering a low-maintenance lifestyle in a welcoming community, this move-in-ready home combines comfort, convenience, and an exceptional Wilton location.
