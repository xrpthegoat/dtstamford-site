About this home

A timeless Colonial residence in the highly desirable North Mianus school district, 98 Hillcrest Park Road blends classic charm with thoughtful updates and comfortable, elegant living. This four-bedroom, three-and-one-half-bath home offers approximately 2,464 square feet of well-balanced interior space, and a fully finished lower level perfect for both everyday living and gracious entertaining, in a peaceful setting on a generous one-third acre parcel. Light-filled living spaces feature rich hardwood floors, a welcoming fireplace, and an intuitive layout that flows seamlessly from formal to casual areas. The heart of the home is defined by a kitchen and dining area designed to accommodate both intimate dinners and larger gatherings, while adjacent rooms provide flexibility for relaxed family living or refined social occasions. Upstairs, the serene primary suite and generous secondary bedrooms offer privacy and comfort. The unfinished lower level is ideal for a media room, home gym, or additional living space. Situated on a beautiful, maturely landscaped lot in a coveted neighborhood with top-rated schools nearby, this residence offers a rare opportunity to enjoy classic architectural presence with modern ease and lifestyle appeal.
