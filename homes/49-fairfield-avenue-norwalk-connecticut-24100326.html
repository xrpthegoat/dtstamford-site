About this home

Minutes to SoNo Collection Mall, train and downtown SoNo from this turn-of-the-century charmer! Renovated in 2010, this classic colonial includes an eat-in kitchen with cherry cabinets, granite counters and stainless-steel appliances. The living room has a wood-burning fireplace, large dining room and den. Hardwood floors and a wonderful walk-up 3rd floor. Warm & inviting front porch. Walkout basement perfect for storage w/access to backyard. Recent updates include newer roof, newer Bosch washer & dryer, newer hot water heater, newer plantation blinds on 1st floor windows, newer wood blinds on 2nd floor windows, newer stone fence & gate in front yard w/newer bluestone walkway, and newer retaining wall w/outdoor terrace & stone staircase in back yard. A commuter's paradise!
