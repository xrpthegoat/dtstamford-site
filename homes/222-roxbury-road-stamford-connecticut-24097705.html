About this home

Discover luxurious living in this exquisite Center Hall Colonial nestled on a pristine acre in desirable Westover, near the Roxbury Swim and Tennis Club. Updated in 2022, this stunning residence features soaring ceilings and sunlit spaces throughout. The heart of this home is the all-white gourmet kitchen with granite counters, thermador range, and Sub-Zero refrigerator, seamlessly flowing to a breakfast nook with sliders leading to an expansive deck, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. Entertaining is effortless with an open floor plan connecting the kitchen to a butler's pantry and elegant dining room, while the bright family room showcases an impressive 11' stone fireplace. The home includes five bedrooms, one currently used as an office, offering flexibility in living arrangements. The private loft wing could serve as an additional bedroom, ideal for guests, in-laws, or a game room. Upstairs, retreat to the elaborate primary suite with a gas fireplace, dual walk-in closets, and a sumptuous all-white marble bathroom. The lower level offers an additional 2,500 square feet ready for customization into a media room or gym. Outdoor living shines with a flat, tree-lined yard, underground sprinklers, and professional landscaping. Modern conveniences include a SONOS indoor/outdoor sound system, and a three-car garage. Conveniently located off Merritt Pkwy, yet moments from dining and shopping, this extraordinary home blends luxury and comfort!
