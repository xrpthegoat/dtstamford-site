About this home

Welcome to 16 Black Twig Place where pride of ownership shines throughout this move-in ready colonial set in a cul-de-sac in the Mayapple neighborhood of North Stamford. Sitting on over an acre, this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers exceptional flow and functionality, with thoughtfully proportioned rooms and over $173,000 in capital improvements. The first floor features a classic layout with an eat-in kitchen equipped with new stainless-steel appliances, granite counters, and direct access to a large back deck that overlooks the professionally landscaped backyard. The adjoining family room with fireplace, formal dining room, and spacious living room create ideal spaces for hosting and entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms, including a generous primary suite with walk-in closet and full bath. Additional features include hardwood floors on both upper levels, French doors, a finished lower level with bonus storage room, and a main level 2-car attached garage. Must see!
