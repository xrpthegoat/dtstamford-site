About this home

There's still time to enjoy summer in Rowayton! This adorable cottage built in 1860 is located in the heart of the village within walking distance to all Rowayton amenities. Enjoy sun and sand at Bailey Beach or picnics at Pinkney Park while watching the boats go by on the Five Mile River. Experience fine dining at Rowayton Seafood, a little more casual dining awaits at Sales or sit outside at Rowayton Pizza where for 50 years they have been satisfying Rowayton's pizza needs. The Rowayton Market and Aldens also provide food and community gathering places, while the shops and Art Center on Rowayton Avenue provide pleasant diversions, Brimming with charm this welcoming home has one bedroom, 1 and 1/2 bathrooms along with an updated kitchen, spacious dining area and large living room. An attached screen porch, water views and a small enclosed garden area with outdoor shower only add to the feeling this is where you want to be.
