About this home

Fantastic end unit townhouse conveniently located at the hearth of Norwalk, with only a few minutes drive to the spectacular SONO collection which offers shopping, dining and entertainment. Enjoy Calf Pasture beach, it's concerts, mini golf and car shows. Built in 2002, with a sunny and bright open concept first floor, this unit features: living room, dining room, kitchen, 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, central A/C, washer and dryer on 2nd floor and 2 car garage. This unit is in prestige condition located in a small condo complex of only 8 units with low common charges.
