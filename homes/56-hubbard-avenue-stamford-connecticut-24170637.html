About this home

Charming vintage Cape Cod in sought-after Hubbard Heights, where timeless character meets everyday comfort. Set on a beautifully landscaped and fully fenced 1/3-acre, this lovingly maintained home offers exceptional indoor-outdoor living with space to relax, gather, and entertain. Inside, a welcoming living room with stone fireplace sets a warm and inviting tone, flowing to the formal dining room--ideal for holiday meals or casual evenings at home. Original details including crown moldings and hardwood floors add character throughout. The updated kitchen features a center island, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and generous storage. The screened porch creates a perfect extension of the living space for morning coffee and summer dinners. Two bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a true retreat with an updated ensuite bath featuring dual sinks, heated floors, and a striking copper tub. An additional bedroom, renovated hall bath, and laundry complete the upper level. An expansive backyard offers the perfect balance of sun and shade, with three apple trees and ample lawn space for play, gardening, or entertaining. Additional highlights include a detached garage, gas utilities, 200-amp service, and numerous updated mechanicals and appliances. Ideally located close to shopping, dining, parks, and commuting options this special home offers convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods.
