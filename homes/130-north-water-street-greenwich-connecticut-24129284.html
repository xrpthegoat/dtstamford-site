About this home

Enjoy river-front entertaining in the peaceful and relaxing yard, complete with dining, grilling and lounging areas, at this shingle-style, Greenwich home. Upon stepping through the gate, the front courtyard welcomes you to a custom 3 bedroom, single family home that features high quality materials throughout. A cozy living room with wood-burning stove opens to the gourmet kitchen with custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances and an extra large island that seats up to 6 guests. Gleaming hardwood floors, custom shades and updated millwork showcase the upscale details and flow into 3 bedrooms, a laundry area and the modern baths that complete this level. Take the spiral staircase to the fully-finished lower level, which can be used for entertaining, an exercise room or office; and it comes complete with an authentic wood bar and beverage sink. Additionally, there is a spacious, heated 2 car garage and plenty of off street parking on the fully paved driveway. Offered unfurnished (or furnished at an additional cost). This waterfront gem is like a respite in a most convenient location. Also available furnished for an additional cost.
