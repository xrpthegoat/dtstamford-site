About this home

Welcome to 29 Hunt St, a beautifully updated and renovated post and beam colonial home in the heart of Rowayton! This residence seamlessly blends classic charm with modern luxury and is move in ready. The upper level living space features abundant windows flooding the home with natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The completely renovated kitchen is a culinary dream come true; marble counters, oversized island with seating, premium La Cornue range, Sub-Zero fridge. The spacious living room boasts stunning cathedral ceiling, wood burning fireplace and built-ins, creating a welcoming sense of openness. The adjoining dining area with beamed cathedral ceiling naturally lends itself to entertaining and socializing. The primary suite features a completely renovated bath with radiant floor, soaking tub, zero entrance shower, and walk in closet. Lower level offers den/workout space, laundry area, plenty of closets and storage. Enjoy the beautiful private outdoor patio, complete with fireplace and seating area, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing at the end of the day. Ample parking and storage in freshly painted two-car garage and oversized driveway. This ideal location is a few minutes walk to the train station and the quaint village of Rowayton. Experience all Rowayton has to offer with Bayley Beach, 5 Mile River, tennis, pickleball, dog park, library, shops, restaurants, Shakespeare on the Sound and so much more.
