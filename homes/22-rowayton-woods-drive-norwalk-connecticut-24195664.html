About this home

Experience quiet sophistication in this ground-floor, end-unit condominium located within the highly regarded Rowayton Woods complex. Offering three bedrooms and two full bathrooms. With a direct walkout patio, this residence provides a private layout surrounded by nature. The interior is fully turn-key, featuring brand-new flooring and fresh paint throughout. A commitment to quality is evident in the comprehensive mechanical updates, including a newly installed HVAC/AC system and a new hot water tank. Thoughtful structural improvements, such as added insulation to exterior wall piping, ensure year-round efficiency and peace of mind. Further enhancing this seamless ownership experience, the seller will pay off the current HOA assessment in full prior to closing. Rowayton Woods offers an exceptional environment with well-maintained grounds featuring a serene lake, an inground pool, tennis courts, and scenic walking trails. The location perfectly balances tranquility with everyday convenience. Situated just one mile from the coastal charm of Rowayton Village, the property is also under two miles from the extensive shopping options on Connecticut Avenue and I-95. With significant capital improvements already completed and premium amenities just outside your door, this pristine property delivers a refined, low-maintenance lifestyle in a highly convenient setting.
