About this home

189 Rowayton Ave, a private sanctuary on .56AC next to the beloved Pinkney Park, steps from town & w/ water access to 5 Mile River. Lush, mature landscaping creates a serene barrier from the outside world, allowing one to enjoy modern amenities in peace & privacy. From your 1st step in the entry w/ terracotta floors & bespoke lights, you know you're in for a treat. The thoughtful design exudes warmth & character w/ wide plank oak floors, 4 fireplaces, cathedral ceilings, & French doors out to an unparalleled backyard. The 1st floor flows w/ ease as you walk from the entry to the right, into the living room, followed by the dining area & kitchen. The state-of-the-art kitchen features a new Viking range, a chef's dream come true. Walking from the entry to the left is a grandiose primary suite w/ a large walk-in closet, expansive ensuite bath, & doors to the yard. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms share a full bath & include a stacked washer/dryer. Adjacent to the kitchen is a powder rm, laundry rm, mudroom, & staircases leading up & down. Upstairs, discover a bonus room, bedroom, & full bath. The finished basement offers ample space for a home media room, as well a gym, & full bath. The meticulously landscaped yard boasts a pool, fireplace, & outdoor dining area situated under a pergola adorned w/ wisteria & an elegant chandelier. Summer living, just got a lot more glam! A separate structure makes for a great WFH office or gym space and let's not forget the 3 car garage.
