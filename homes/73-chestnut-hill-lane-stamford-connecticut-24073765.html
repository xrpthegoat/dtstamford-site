About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in North Stamford, this classic colonial highlights stunning views of McManus Lake and exceptional curb appeal. Elegantly perched atop a hill, the 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home features a spacious living room and family room, both with a fireplace and French doors, and a formal dining room - all showcasing beautiful water views. The eat-in kitchen provides ample space for casual meals, perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite boasts breathtaking lake views, a walk-in closet, and an en suite bath. Three additional bedrooms, full bath, large linen closet, and additional walk-in-closet complete the upper level. The finished basement offers flexible living space and abundant storage. Outdoor features include a fenced-in backyard, a large patio with lake views, a leveled area with a cozy fire pit, and access to the lake, ideal for fishing and boating, via your private dock. There is also plenty of room for a garden or playset. Additional highlights include an oversized garage, along with recent updates such as a newer roof, boiler, driveway, and 1st floor AC unit, ensuring comfort and peace of mind. Ideally located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, with convenient access to Metro-North and just a short walk to the scenic Chestnut Hill Park. The ultimate lakeside retreat, this home seamlessly blends peaceful surroundings and a convenient location with expansive indoor and outdoor living spaces.
