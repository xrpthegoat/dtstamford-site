About this home

Charming direct waterfront Shore Colonial with private dock and 1st floor primary suite. This home is located just over the Old Greenwich line in the sought after Davenport Point Waterfront Association. House has been completely updated and modernized with a fabulous open floor plan, chef's kitchen and professionally landscaped grounds. The real "wow" factor here though is the amazing panoramic Long Island Sound water views! Bring your boat/kayak/fishing pole and love for the sea- great family neighborhood with private roads and real sense of community. Walk to Southfield park with tennis courts, playground, sandy beach and waterfront boardwalk with water taxi to Harbor Point restaurants. Bike to Old Greenwich village or Tod's Point. Commuter's dream with Stamford & OG train stations less than 5 minutes away. Private association dues just $800/year. Come live the waterfront dream!
