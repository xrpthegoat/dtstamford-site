About this home

Welcome to 32 First Street, a beautifully designed two-family home in highly coveted East Norwalk. Thoughtfully crafted to complement the charm of the neighborhood, this exceptional property offers the perfect blend of style, flexibility, and investment potential. The front residence lives like a single-family home, featuring 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and three levels of living space. The main floor offers a spacious living room, dining room, and kitchen, while beautiful hardwood floors run throughout the first and second levels. The primary suite features a private en-suite bath and walk-in closet, and the finished third floor provides the perfect space for a bedroom, home office, or bonus room. Split-system air conditioning and a dedicated washer and dryer add everyday comfort. The rear unit has its own private entrance and offers 2 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, hardwood floors throughout, spacious bedrooms, and its own washer and dryer, making it ideal for rental income or extended family. A detached two-car garage, separate entrances, and a thoughtfully designed layout provide privacy and convenience for both residences. Whether you're an investor seeking strong rental potential or an owner-occupant looking to offset your mortgage, this is a rare opportunity in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods, just minutes from Metro-North, beaches, parks, restaurants, shopping, and everything East Norwalk has to offer.
