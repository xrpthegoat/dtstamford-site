About this home

You'll fall in love with this adorable 1940s craftsman bungalow! This home oozes charm both inside and out! With its high ceilings, it lives bigger than its square footage suggests! The kitchen boasts quartz countertops, an extra large sink, great storage with secret drawers, and a brand new gas range. In the living room, enjoy the wood burning fireplace framed by custom bookcases. Upstairs, there is a spacious bathroom with an oversized tub. The large basement features laundry and recently upgraded electrical amps. Additional storage can be found had with the pull down attic. This sweet home is set up for comfort with a Navien gas water heater, brand new furnace, and central air. Outside, relax on the cozy front porch or enjoy the generous, completely fenced in backyard. There is even a separate fenced-in dog run! Walk to restaurants and enjoy an easy commute to NYC, a true gem in the middle of Norwalk!
