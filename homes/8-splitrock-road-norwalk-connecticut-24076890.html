About this home

PRICE DROP - WATERFRONT COMMUNITY - NO FLOOD INSURANCE NECESSARY! Welcome to the beautiful private beach association of Village Creek! Nestled along the coast of Norwalk, CT, Village Creek is a community on Long Island Sound with its own private beach, a playground, a dog park and a marina. 8 Splitrock Road is a pristine 4 bedroom, 3 bath house with modern amenities. As you enter the bright, welcoming foyer, there is a convenient spot to store your things. The living room features a fireplace with a stone surround and wooden mantel. The cheerful office has a custom bookcase. The kitchen is a wonderful spot for cooking, with a gas range, granite countertops, farmhouse sink and a picture window to watch the changing seasons in the wetlands. Adjacent to the kitchen is a lovely dining area. There is a cozy family room as well, and a hall bath for guests. To complete the first floor is the primary bedroom with en-suite bath. Upstairs are three additional bedrooms and a full bathroom. On the back of the house is a spacious patio for entertaining and a screened-in porch for enjoying summer evenings. At high tide, the inlet becomes a watery paradise, full of egrets and wildlife. The fully fenced backyard features a built-in fire pit, and has abundant trees and plantings. Only five minutes to the vibrant nightlife of South Norwalk and the Metro North train station. Come enjoy this cheerful home and its wonderful neighborhood perfect for walking, biking and water activities!
