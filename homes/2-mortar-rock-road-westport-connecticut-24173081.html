About this home

Better than new, this pristine 2016 custom-built residence offers a rare standard of refinement and sophisticated excellence. With 4,000+sqft and an elevator accessing all four finished levels, the home blends modern architecture with beautifully executed transitional design. The open-concept main level features a magnificent chef's kitchen, spacious dining area, and expansive living room with gas fireplace, custom built-ins, beams, and tray ceiling. Four-inch white oak flooring runs throughout. A wall of glass doors opens to a breathtaking private setting with a remote-controlled awning, ideal for effortless indoor/outdoor living. Four bedrooms include the luxurious primary suite with tray ceiling, built-ins, and serene garden views, plus a spa-like bath with heated floors, soaking tub, double vanity, and walk-in closets. Two additional en-suite BRs and an exceptional home office with dual built-in desks, double-height bookcases, library ladder, and automated shade complete the level. The third floor offers a skylit fourth bedroom with full bath and cedar closet. The finished lower level includes a playroom/gym, office, and half bath. A 2car garage, elevator to all levels, full-house generator, security and sound systems elevate the offering. Ideally set on a desirable cul-de-sac minutes to the beach, train, town, library, and farmers market, making this an incredible location from which to enjoy all of Westport's most coveted amenities. Move-in ready and truly exceptional.
