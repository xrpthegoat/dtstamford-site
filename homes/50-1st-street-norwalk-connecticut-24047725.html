About this home

Welcome to 50 1st Street, located in the East Norwalk Shoreline Community. This charming 4 bedroom, 2 bath home is ready for your personal touches and updates. It features spacious rooms including an Eat-in Kitchen, Dining Room, Living Room, enclosed Sun Porch, 2 Bedrooms & Full Bath, which complete the first floor. Up to the 2nd floor where you'll find 2 additional Bedrooms & 2nd Full Bath. Don't overlook the finished attic on the third level, which provides additional living space. The partially finished basement includes a large laundry room with hookups. Investors, take note: opportunity knocks...this property is zoned Residential C (two-family zone), and has numerous possibilities. Buyer to do their "due diligence". A fenced in large, level backyard and a one-car garage with a carport. The property is an estate and sold "As-Is." Walk or bike to all East Norwalk amenities, including train station, beaches, dog park, shops, restaurants, or take a 5 minute ride to SoNo for a visit to the Maritime Museum & Aquarium, SoNo Collection Mall and many restaurants & nightlife spots. This is a commuter's dream, located a short 55 miles north of NYC, with easy access to Metro North, I-95 and Merritt Parkway.
