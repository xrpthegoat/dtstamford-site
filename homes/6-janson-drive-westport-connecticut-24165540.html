About this home

Exceptional new construction by AR Luxury Homes with 7,500+/- square feet of thoughtfully designed living space across four finished levels in a prime Westport location. This 6-bedroom, 6 full and 3 half bath residence blends refined craftsmanship with modern functionality, featuring an open-concept layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The heart of the home is a custom-designed kitchen equipped with premium appliances including Wolf and Sub-Zero, expansive island, and seamless flow to the family room with fireplace. Three fireplaces throughout the home add warmth and architectural interest. White oak flooring, custom millwork, and high-end finishes elevate every space. The upper levels offer a luxurious primary suite with spa-like bath and custom closet, along with well-appointed secondary bedrooms. A fully finished lower level with walkout access provides flexible space for recreation, fitness, or additional living. Set on a level .50-acre property, the outdoor experience is complete with a 15' x 30' heated gunite pool, bluestone patio, and professionally planned landscaping-perfect for enjoyment and relaxation. Additional features include city water and sewer, propane gas, 400-amp electrical service, 24 KW generator, and wiring for audio, security, and smart home systems. A rare opportunity to own a new construction home with exceptional scale, quality, and amenities. Still time to customize finishes.
