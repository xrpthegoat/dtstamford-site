About this home

Charming 1815 Colonial with Character and Endless Potential Step back in time while envisioning the possibilities in this charming 1815 Old-Style Colonial, offering timeless character, versatility, and an exceptional opportunity to create a home uniquely your own. The inviting main level features a welcoming front porch, comfortable living room, dining area, kitchen, and bright sunroom overlooking the private backyard. Upstairs, two bedrooms and a full bath provide a cozy and functional living space. The lower level adds valuable flexibility with a recreation room, half bath, and walk-out access to the backyard. A detached one-car garage and private yard further enhance the property's appeal. Ideally situated near the exciting Noroton Heights development, the property is just minutes from restaurants, shops, and the train station, providing convenient access to shopping, dining, transportation, and the many amenities of this desirable location. Bring your vision and imagination to this special property and transform it into your dream home. With its historic character, private outdoor space, and excellent location, this is an outstanding opportunity for a builder, investor, or buyer looking for a property with tremendous potential.
