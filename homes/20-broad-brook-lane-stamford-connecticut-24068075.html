About this home

6 Month Rental in great location close to Springdale Village, offering a blend of charm & convenience w/amenities such as a train station, bars, restaurants & shops. Additionally, conveniently situated near New Canaan, the Parkway & Downtown Stamford. Upon entering through the front door, you are welcomed by a stunning 2-story cathedral ceiling foyer & an elegant staircase adorned with wainscoting & secret closet-a mere glimpse into the exquisite details this young colonial presents. Chef's kitchen, equipped w/high-end appliances, serves as the focal point for gatherings, boasting a spacious breakfast nook w/windows on 3 sides. This inviting space seamlessly connects to the great/family room, featuring a fireplace, wainscoting, hardwood floors, 2-story cathedral ceiling & a 2nd enchanting staircase leading to the bedrooms. Family room, kitchen & breakfast nook open up to a deck, creating a perfect setting for indoor/outdoor entertaining. The main level further includes a formal DR & LR & a distinguished study/office adorned with beautiful built-in shelves. The primary suite is a luxurious retreat, complete w/splendid bathroom & large his/hers walk-in closets. The remaining 3 bedrooms are generously sized, each w its own walk-in closet, accompanied by 2 additional full baths. 1300sqf lower level offers finished space featuring a large wet bar, full bath, sauna, & ample storage. Ideal escape for relaxation, w/the sauna offering a tranquil space to melt the stress away
