About this home

An exceptional rental offering in South Wilton, 5 Deerfield Road pairs the architectural drama of a historic barn home with the comforts of a distinctive Connecticut retreat. Set on a cul-de-sac, the residence offers soaring 11-foot ceilings, walls of glass, and monumental timber beams reclaimed from Manhattan's iconic Ruppert Brewery-a striking connection to its New York heritage. Built in 1969 with portions dating to the 1700s, the home blends rich texture and historic character with generous spaces for entertaining and everyday living. A chef's kitchen anchors the main level, complemented by impressive living and dining rooms, an office, an ensuite bedroom, and a powder room. Upstairs, the primary suite features an expansive walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms include one with a loft. The lower level provides flexible space for recreation, fitness, or a studio, along with a half bath, laundry, and wine cellar. South Wilton offers an appealing balance of New England character and everyday convenience. Wilton Center's local shops, restaurants, and cultural offerings give the town a vibrant gathering place, while the Norwalk River Valley Trail and surrounding preserved open spaces invite time outdoors. The artistic legacy of Weir Farm National Historical Park adds another distinctive dimension to a town where history, nature, and creativity remain part of daily life.
