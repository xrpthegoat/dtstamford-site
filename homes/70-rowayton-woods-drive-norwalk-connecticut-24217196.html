About this home

This ground level unit in the desirable Rowayton Woods condo complex is inviting you to step out from your living room to your private patio and enjoy the tranquil views of the ducks swimming past on the pond. Relax at the pool, play tennis, take a stroll through the scenic grounds or get planting in the communal garden if you like. This unit offers a recently updated kitchen and bath. The open concept hallway to the bedroom offers a versatile space for a desk and/or extra storage space. Hardwood floors and crown molding enhance the living space. Room for a dining area and an in-unit washer/dryer finish up this package. Assigned parking spot in front of unit. Cross the convenient footbridge to Rowayton train station, a 15 minute walk, meander the charming village of Rowayton. Minutes to SONO's diverse shopping, dining & entertainment. Ideally located close to Metro North and I-95.
