About this home

Old Hill ranch home available for rent. Tucked away on a picturesque 1.18-level lot acre, this updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath ranch offers a rare blend of privacy, character, and location in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods. Step inside to find original hardwood floors and a bright, open-concept kitchen with modern appliances, marble countertops, and high ceilings. The heart of the home centers around a classic fireplace, creating a warm and inviting focal point. A marble-tiled bathroom adds a timeless touch, while both bedrooms offer generous closet space. Outside, the property is surrounded by mature, thoughtfully maintained landscaping that enhances its natural beauty and creates a sense of serenity. Enjoy peaceful mornings on the private deck overlooking a tranquil rear pond. Additional highlights include an attached rear garage with basement access and a separate storage room to keep things organized.
