About this home

Tucked away at the end of a long private driveway in Wilton's desirable Thunder Lake neighborhood, this charming center hall Colonial offers exceptional privacy and tranquility. Set on 2.45 acres and surrounded by protected state land, the property enjoys a secluded setting while remaining just minutes from shopping, dining, schools, and everyday conveniences. Beautiful hardwood floors run throughout the home. The main level features a spacious living room, formal dining room, and an inviting family room with a wood-burning fireplace and sliders leading to an expansive deck overlooking the private backyard and gazebo. The centrally located kitchen offers views of the private setting and convenient access to the home's gathering spaces and outdoor living areas. Upstairs are four generously sized bedrooms, including a primary suite, along with an oversized great room offering flexibility as a potential fifth bedroom, home office, media room, or guest space. The finished lower level includes a dry bar and additional living space, while a large workshop and storage area with exterior access provides endless possibilities. Recent improvements include a freshly painted exterior, new main roof, new HVAC system, and new flooring. Enjoy easy access to nearby Weir Farm National Historical Park and Wilton Town Forest with miles of scenic trails. A rare opportunity to enjoy space, privacy, and convenience in one of Wilton's most sought-after locations.
