About this home

Located on a great street, close to town, the train,Winslow Park, the library, stores, Longshore and Compo Beach, the home at 46 Park Lane offers a wonderful opportunity for those wanting to renovate or build new. The 0.60 acre has a beautiful ,level back yard. There are no wetlands and the house is serviced by city water, natural gas and city sewers. The house offers an eat in kitchen, living /dining room with a fireplace with brick surround. The living room's full wall of windows opens up to the beautiful back yard,offering a feeling of quiet and serenity. There are 5 bedrooms and 3 full baths and a powder room. A large family room and a laundry/mudroom area that leads to the 2-car attached garage. There are 2,300 SF of living space and room for expansion. 10 years ago the new windows were added, the roof was replaced and bathrooms renovated.Hardwood floors are throughout except for the two bedrooms on the 3rd floor. The house is ready for new owners to bring it to its full potential or the lot is perfect for a brand new home. House being sold as is.
