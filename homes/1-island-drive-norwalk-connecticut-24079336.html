About this home

Fabulous, "to die for" direct waterfront open, light and airy end-unit condo with 35 ft boat slip (#27) located on the Island Drive side of the marina. This unit was gutted and totally remodeled in 2010 with all new walls, windows, doors, floors, central "see-thru" staircase, HVAC system, super soundproof insulation... kitchen 3 baths, hot water system, upgraded electric, central vac and more. The finishes, wall coverings, level of detail and total vibe of this unit cannot be matched. And, its location is one of the best - if not the best - in the complex - with open views of Charles Creek to Cove Marina, the Norwalk Harbor and the Norwalk Islands beyond. This exceptional direct waterfront property is located in the private and gated waterfront association of Charles Cove Condominiums. Built in 1982, Charles Cove is considered a premier property in East Norwalk with an excellent and extremely safe marina. The historic district of South Norwalk (SoNo) is just a stone's throw across the Norwalk River with many varied and fine restaurants, as well as entertainment and local and mall shopping at the convenient SoNo Mall.
