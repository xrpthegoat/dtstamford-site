About this home

Exceptional Outdoor Living in Cranbury Enjoy the best of outdoor living in this inviting Cranbury home, where a thoughtfully designed backyard steals the spotlight. Follow winding garden paths through natural plantings, relax on the oversized patio, and listen to the soothing sounds of the koi pond and birdsong. This private, park-like setting offers a peaceful escape just minutes from all the conveniences of Norwalk. Inside, the home is move-in ready and full of charm. A bright spacious sunroom with vaulted ceilings and walls of windows brings the outdoors in, offering tranquil views in every season. The living and dining rooms feature hardwood floors, a wood-burning fireplace with classic mantel, and a large bay window that fills the space with natural light. Additional features include flexible living space ideal for guests or multi-generational living, an oversized two-car garage, and 4 brand-new split HVAC system for efficient cooling and secondary heating.. Located in a quiet yet convenient area close to shopping, dining, and major commuter routes, this home is also within the district for Norwalk's soon brand-new Norwalk High School and recently completed Cranbury Elementary School.
