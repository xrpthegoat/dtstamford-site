About this home

FrogPond Estate is New Canaan's most striking modern Scandinavian offering-an architectural achievement set on 2.25 private acres just moments from town and train. With 11,836+ sq. ft. of sunfilled, designdriven living space, this 7 bed, 9.5bath custom residence delivers soaring 12ft ceilings, bespoke millwork, designer lighting, and walls of glass that erase the line between indoors and out. A showstopping doubleisland gourmet kitchen anchors the open main level, perfect for quiet daily living or largescale entertaining. The estate's fully independent 1,190 sq. ft. Cottage-with separate address and utilities-adds a private bedroom, office, full bath, island kitchen, laundry, and a flexible loft ideal for guests, extended family, staff, or professional use. Thoughtfully scaled yet beautifully finished, it elevates FrogPond into a true multidwelling compound. Pool is open. Landscaping complete. Showready today. A rare modern sanctuary-private, polished, and unlike anything else in the market. This is the listing buyers will clear their schedules to see.
