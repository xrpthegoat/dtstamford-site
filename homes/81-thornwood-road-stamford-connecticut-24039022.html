About this home

Located in a tranquil cul-de-sac neighborhood, this impeccably maintained 4 bedroom, 3 bath raised ranch sits on a beautiful acre in North Stamford. The main floor consists of an open-concept kitchen, dining area, living room, and family room which create a wonderful living/entertaining space. The family room has a vaulted ceiling and a wall of windows overlooking the property. Also located on this level are the main bedroom with updated ensuite bathroom, three additional bedrooms, plus an updated bathroom with jacuzzi tub. The finished walk-out lower level has additional living space with a family room, game room, beautifully updated full bathroom with laundry, office, storage, and attached spacious two car garage. The lower level has great potential for an in-law suite. The property has a park-like setting which can be enjoyed from both the main level and lower-level decks. Home is being sold as is.
