About this home

Welcome to 43 Hidden Brook Drive, a warm and inviting 5-bedroom, 2-bath single-family home offering versatile two-level living-perfect for extended families or those seeking flexible space. The upper level features three comfortable bedrooms and a full bath with a tub/shower combination, along with an open-concept living and dining area that flows seamlessly into the kitchen. Step out onto the deck-ideal for summer barbecues and easy entertaining-overlooking a private backyard that is beginning to bloom beautifully, a true reflection of the owner's love of gardening. With partial fencing and abundant potential, it's a wonderful space to enjoy the warmer seasons. The lower level expands your living options with an oversized family room anchored by a cozy fireplace, perfect for relaxing evenings. You'll also find a renovated full bath with a shower stall and two additional bedrooms, one of which is well-suited for a home office, guest space, or hobby room. Interior access to the two-car garage and mechanical room adds convenience. Additional features include natural gas heating and central air conditioning for year-round comfort. Ideally designed for both everyday living and gathering with loved ones, this home offers space, warmth, and opportunity in a desirable setting. Sale is contingent upon the seller securing suitable housing.
