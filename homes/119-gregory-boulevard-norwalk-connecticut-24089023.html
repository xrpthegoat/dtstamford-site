About this home

Exceptional Waterfront Living with Private Dock and Sweeping Views Waterfront living like you've never seen before. Welcome to Charles Cove Condominiums, where this beautifully updated, sun-filled 2-bedroom waterfront end unit includes a 64-foot boat slip-visible from the comfort of your new home. Wake up to the tranquil beauty of the water and unwind with glowing sunsets from one of your two private decks. This turn-key property offers a light-filled open floor plan with new kitchen and bathrooms featuring quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and sleek, modern finishes. Spectacular water and side views unique to this home. The spacious primary suite showcases a large walk-in closet, and a spa-like bath complete with double vanities, a soaking tub, frameless walk-in shower, and your very own private sauna. Enjoy the warmth of a gas fireplace, the comfort of central air, and the flexibility of the third-floor loft-ideal as a home office, guest room, gym, or creative studio. As a rare end unit, this home is equipped with **propane-available only to end units-**adding efficiency and desirability. Additional highlights include a one-car attached garage with extra storage, ample closet space throughout, and exceptional privacy. This serene, gated community offers resort-style amenities: pool, tennis and pickleball courts, kayaking, and a scenic waterfront boardwalk. All just minutes from the beach, train, I-95, and every convenience. Live like you're on vacation.
