About this home

Welcome home to this sun-drenched ranch located in the historic Silvermine neighborhood of Norwalk. Situated on a beautifully landscaped 0.41-acre with a large, level yard that is perfect for endless hours of outdoor play, gardening, & enjoyment. This spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers a fabulous layout with great flow for everyday living & entertaining. The inviting front porch leads into a welcoming foyer with a large coat closet. The spacious living room with a wood-burning fireplace flows into the dining area and updated kitchen with new stainless-steel appliances. The sunroom/family room with a cathedral ceiling and skylights is flooded with natural light and opens out to the beautiful wrap-around deck. This home also features a large primary bedroom with a sizable walk-in closet and bathroom. The basement is an additional 1,296 square feet of versatile space with a fireplace. Hardwood floors throughout most of the main living spaces, 2-car attached tandem garage, central AC, first-floor laundry, and freshly painted interior are just some of the wonderful features of this fabulous home. Explore all that Silvermine has to offer with the Arts Guild, Gray Barns, Silvermine Market, and Mercantile just down the road. A beautiful, move-in condition home!
