About this home

Enjoy low-maintenance living in desirable Grumman Hill Village, a beautifully maintained community of 48 homes in sought-after South Wilton. Residents enjoy a carefree lifestyle w/ association-covered grounds maintenance, landscaping, and snow removal, allowing time to enjoy all that Wilton has to offer. Perfectly positioned within the community, this home enjoys one of the most desirable settings, overlooking a spacious greenbelt with sweeping lawn and wooded views beyond. Open floor plan on main level and 3 spacious bedrooms plus office/playroom/extra bedroom completes 2nd floor. The private patio provides a peaceful outdoor retreat for relaxing or entertaining. The freshly painted bright and inviting interior features hardwood floors, natural gas heat, and a functional floor plan designed for comfortable everyday living. Updates over the years include newer roof, refinished hardwood floors, updated kitchen appliances, refreshed interior finishes, and meticulous maintenance throughout. Additional features include underground sprinklers, pull-down attic storage, and extensive built-in shelving in the garage. Grumman Hill Village is known for its attractive setting, strong management, and convenient location close to Wilton Center, Westport, shopping, restaurants, schools, train stations, major commuting routes, and public transportation. Move right in and enjoy the convenience, comfort, and lifestyle that make Grumman Hill Village one of Wilton's most popular communities
