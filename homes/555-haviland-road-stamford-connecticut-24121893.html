About this home

Welcome to this Cape Cod style home in North Stamford offering 4 bedrooms, 3-1/2 baths. From the moment you step inside, the thoughtful floorplan and inviting details make this home stand out. The welcoming foyer opens to a formal living room at the front of the house, featuring a vaulted, beamed ceiling, and fireplace that adds charm and character. A formal dining room with a bay window creates the perfect space for gatherings. The remodeled kitchen is both functional and stylish, with ample cabinetry and counter space, seamlessly overlooking the large family room. The family room is a true highlight, with a cozy fireplace and a dramatic wall of windows framing views of the private backyard and in-ground gunite pool, making it the perfect place to relax or entertain. A convenient laundry room is located as you enter from the attached garage. The first-floor primary bedroom suite provides comfort and convenience with its spacious design, ensuite bath, and access to an adjoining office complete with built-ins. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms, a loft area that could serve as a sitting room or study space, and a full bath. The partially finished lower level adds even more flexibility, with a half bath and walkout. Set on a sprawling 3-acre parcel, providing an incredible sense of space and privacy. The grounds offer plenty of room for outdoor enjoyment. Property being sold in as is condition and the land can not be subdived, it has a conservation easement.
