About this home

Completely renovated and move-in ready single-family rental offering modern finishes, functional layout, and exceptional living space throughout. This beautifully updated home features a bright and open main level with a sleek, designer kitchen equipped with quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a stylish backsplash. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining area and living space, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The home offers 3 well-sized bedrooms and a fully updated bathroom with contemporary finishes. Hardwood flooring runs throughout the main level, complemented by recessed lighting and abundant natural light. The fully finished lower level significantly expands the living space and includes a large additional family room, a full bathroom, and a dedicated laundry area deal for extended living, guest space, or a home office setup. Step outside to a spacious private deck overlooking a level backyard, perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or relaxing. The property also includes a 1-car garage and ample additional parking. Situated in a desirable residential neighborhood with convenient access to shopping, dining, schools, and major highways. Tenant responsible for all utilities and maintenance, including landscaping and snow removal. Strong credit required. Two months security deposit required. Renter's insurance required.
