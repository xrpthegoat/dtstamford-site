About this home

Beautiful 3BR, 2.5BA Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac with a one-car garage. Conveniently located near I-95, Merritt Pkwy, shopping, and dining. Hardwood floors throughout. The main level features a formal living room with a fireplace, a family room, a formal dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, white cabinets, Corian counters, and a breakfast bar. French doors lead to a large deck and backyard. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a full bath with a jetted tub, double vanity, and two walk-in closets. Two additional bedrooms share a full bath. Fully finished basement with a full bathroom and sliding door to backyard.
