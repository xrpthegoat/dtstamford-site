About this home

Step Back in Time with the ultimate charm of yesteryear blended with the modern comforts of today - The Samuel Middlebrook House, circa 1830. This beautifully preserved 5-bedroom antique residence offers timeless character and plenty of charm. Rich in period detail, the home features wide plank wood floors, exposed beams, original fireplaces with hand-carved mantels, and a charming bee-hive oven-classic hallmarks of 19th-century craftsmanship. A welcoming front foyer with fireplace sets the tone, while French doors from the cozy den open to a private flagstone terrace-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The spacious kitchen is complemented by a sunny seating area plus large laundry/mudroom, adding everyday functionality. A true highlight is the attached authentic barn-style family room with fireplace, offering rustic warmth and extra room to gather. Upstairs there's a spacious primary en-suite bedroom plus four more bedrooms. The partially finished walk-up attic provides more bonus space for work, play, or storage. Set on a picturesque 2-acre lot framed by stone walls, a white picket gate, and possible pool sites; the home offers over 4,300 square feet of living space-an impressive scale for an antique. With a coveted location near town schools and train, this unique property blends historic charm with everyday convenience. Not your typical home-this one-of-a-kind treasure is waiting to welcome its next chapter.
