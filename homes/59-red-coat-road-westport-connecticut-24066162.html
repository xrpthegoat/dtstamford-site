About this home

Modern open concept new home nestled on two private acres. Enjoy the quiet sounds of nature from your oversized patio with a natural gas fire pit and a charming Pergola. Lots of space for gardening, playtime or just relaxing. The main level is perfect for entertaining friends and family, there is a custom designed kitchen with a ten foot island, Thermador appliances, including two dish washers, a tall wine chiller and a beverage cooler. A custom designed wine /coffee bar is perfect for entertaining. The dining area has a unique mirrored wall with lighted built-in cubbies your holiday get togethers will sparkle! There are two guest suites, one on the main level and one on the third level. So much more to see, come take a look at this brand new home located in an amazing neighborhood of fabulous estates. Easy access to the Merritt, the Y and downtown. Approved pool site, builder not installing.
