About this home

Welcome to Unit 6C at The Gables on Glenbrook-set privately in the back of the complex, providing a quiet and secluded setting! This meticulously maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse delivers both comfort and convenience, all within walking distance of Stamford's vibrant downtown with its dining, shopping, and entertainment options. Major transportation routes are nearby, allowing for easy commutes to NYC or weekend trips to local beaches, parks, and surrounding towns. Inside, thoughtful updates create a fresh and inviting space. Walls, ceilings, and trim have been recently painted, and the oak hardwood floors are beautifully kept. 9-foot ceilings on the entry and main level add to the sense of openness, while new light fixtures-including a stylish chandelier in the dining room, dual lanterns at the entry, and recessed lighting throughout-enhance the modern appeal. The kitchen features granite countertops and sleek stainless steel appliances, making it a warm and functional centerpiece of the home. A private, updated 2-car garage provides secure parking and additional storage. With numerous updates and a truly private setting, Unit 6C stands out as a rare find at The Gables.
