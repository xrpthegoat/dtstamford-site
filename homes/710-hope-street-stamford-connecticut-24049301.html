About this home

Discover this spectacular tri-level townhome nestled in the heart of Springdale, offering an impressive blend of luxury and convenience. Featuring 3 bedrooms and 3.5 baths, this home boasts a custom gourmet kitchen outfitted with Jenn-Air appliances including gas range, granite countertops, tumbled marble backsplash, and a built-in microwave oven. The kitchen seamlessly opens to a spacious great room with soaring ceilings, a cozy gas fireplace, and a built-in sound system, perfect for entertaining. Adjacent is a charming dining room with a pantry for added storage. On the main level, you'll find a powder room, a convenient laundry closet, and a bedroom with an ensuite marble bath. Each of the three bedrooms features ensuite marble baths, with the primary suite showcasing a luxurious full bath with a Jacuzzi tub, gas fireplace, expansive walk-in closet, and French doors leading to a private patio and fenced backyard. The lower level offers a versatile bonus room that could serve as a gym, playroom, or office. Rich hardwood floors with intricate inlay details, custom built-ins, trim, and moldings enhance the home's timeless elegance, Iotty smart wifi switches, abundant storage throughout including a massive attic. Outside, the exceptional masonry and stonework elevate the exterior's curb appeal. Plus, enjoy the convenience of walking to the Springdale train station, shops, and restaurants-without the burden of association fees. This one-of-a-kind townhome won't last long
