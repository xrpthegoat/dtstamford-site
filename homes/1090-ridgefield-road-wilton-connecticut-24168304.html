About this home

Experience the character of the historic Northup-Scott House (c.1760), thoughtfully updated for today's lifestyle and set on four scenic acres. This well-maintained home offers a flexible floor plan with formal living and dining rooms, a spacious keeping room with original fireplace, a sun-filled family room with dramatic architectural window, private office, and first-floor guest suite. The renovated kitchen features a La Cornue range, 48 inch Pro Sub-Zero refrigerator/freezer, Wolfe double ovens, updated countertops, and opens seamlessly to the main living spaces. The second floor includes the primary suite, four additional bedrooms, three updated baths, and laundry room. A unique loft overlooks the impressive barn/great room, offering exceptional space for recreation, studio, gym, or entertaining. The owners put in $1M+ of improvements including newer roof, 75 thermopane windows, refinished hardwood floors, updated mechanicals, installed central air, interior and exterior painting, and renovated full bathrooms. Also purchased lot on Ridgefield border for $299K to allow seemless flow thru circular driveway. Outdoor amenities include pond, tennis court, expansive yard, gazebo and beautiful porch spaces ideal for relaxing or entertaining. A legal rental apartment provides added flexibility for guests, extended family, or supplemental income. A true Wilton gem.
