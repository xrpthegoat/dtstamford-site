About this home

Come be delighted by the brand new, gut renovated home at 2 Charcoal Lane.It is spotless and the 1 level living you have been seeking. It has beautiful craftsmanship and custom finishes that you would not expect at this price point.Move in ready, if the layout works for you, this will not disappoint! Privacy and nature set in a desirable neighborhood on over 2 acres. Charcoal Lane offers quiet and peace in addition to a convenient location! Open concept and huge windows create a bright home filled with natural light.The first floor features the primary suite with a deck for star gazing and 2 additional bedrooms. In the lower level, again the light is abundant.Here there is a family room and 2 additional rooms for work from home, or overflow company! The laundry room is spacious and well appointed and makes the chore a little less arduous. Premium Bertazzoni appliances, smart-home systems, new windows. All of the heavy lifting has been done. For now or forever this quaint Westport home is in a class of its own.It would be near impossible to find the same quality of finishes at this price point.
