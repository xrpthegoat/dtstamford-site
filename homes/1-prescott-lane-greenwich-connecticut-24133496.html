About this home

Perfection near town. Attractive and well done 4-bedroom with recent updates is situated on a lovely, quaint private lane off lower Lake Ave. Built by a local & award-winning high-end builder, this home has great flow for today's lifestyle. Lovely foyer leads to spacious well-lit living room & formal dining. Also, off the main hall is the family room w/fireplace which is adjacent to the stylish kitchen. The primary suite w/fireplace has a tray ceiling & new luxury bath. Bonuses: playroom, exercise area w/high ceilings, custom windows giving an enormous amount of light. Landscaped level yard has a private terrace for entertaining & a mahogany floored porch for relaxing. 2 car garage w/double height ceilings & access to a huge loft for storage. Generator. Close to schools, town & train.
