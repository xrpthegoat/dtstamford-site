About this home

Nestled atop one of New Canaan's highest points in the esteemed Oenoke Ridge neighborhood, 1052 Oenoke Ridge is set on over 3 captivating acres boasting stunning sunset views. This charming colonial offers refined, open living space. Thoughtfully designed to embrace its natural surroundings, the home has French doors from each room on the first floor; connecting the interior to expansive outdoor living areas-perfect for effortless indoor-outdoor living. An open concept kitchen, complete with a charming breakfast nook, seamlessly connects to the inviting family room. Just off the kitchen, an upstairs playroom with full bath offers additional flexibility, ideal for guests, home office or play. The main-level primary suite is a private retreat, featuring a sitting room adorned with one of two elegant fireplaces found on the first floor. On the second floor, a spacious bunk room expands the home's sleeping accommodations and offers exceptional flexibility-allowing for a variety of lifestyle needs. Two additional bedrooms and a well-appointed bathroom complete the upper level, providing comfort and functionality. This is the opportunity of a lifetime for the savvy buyer.
