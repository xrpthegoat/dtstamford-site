About this home

This Newfield home offers exceptional convenience as it's just minutes away from local dining, shopping, houses of worship and essential transportation links such as the train station and major highways. The main level provides a seamless flow between the living room, dining area, and kitchen, creating a great sense of connection. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms, each featuring hardwood floors and recessed lighting. The lower level includes a well-sized family room and a dedicated laundry room. The screened-in porch is a nice bonus, further expanding the living area. There's a surprising amount of storage space throughout the house. Plus, the 1-car garage has a work bench with additional storage. The yard is fully fenced, delivering a nice sense of privacy. This home is available immediately, so come take a look!
