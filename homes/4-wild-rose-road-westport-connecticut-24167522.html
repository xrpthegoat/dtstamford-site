About this home

Wonderful reimagining of this circa 1880 antique home on a quiet in-town cul-de-sac. Set on a level half acre and beautifully rebuilt and expanded by Further Afield, the home now features 3 levels of brand new living space. An English inspired kitchen features inset cabinetry, marble countertops, Bertazzoni appliances and an 11' island with storage and seating. A separate larder/pantry with oak countertops hides small appliances and includes food storage, microwave, wine fridge and a second sink for functionality. Family Rm with built-ins and wood-burning FPL features a wall of glass doors to the patio and gorgeous private property with room for a pool. 5 BRS on one level include a stunning primary suite with 10' ceilings, two walk-in closets and a marble bath. A well-outfitted mudroom, laundry rm and finished lower level flexible space complete this new home. Amazing in-town location with sidewalk access to Westport's main shopping district, restaurants and the Saugatuck River. Another beautiful restoration from Westport's Further Afield!
