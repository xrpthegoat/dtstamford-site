About this home

Discover this charming 1928 Colonial in the desirable waterfront community of Shippan Point! This elegant home has been lovingly maintained by the same family for over 50 years & is now ready for new owners to infuse their own style. As you enter, you're greeted by a gracious foyer leading into a spacious Living Room, complete w/fireplace, original hardwood floors & crown moldings-ideal for entertaining & family gatherings. The formal Dining Room features beautifully detailed period corner cupboards, while the Eat-in Kitchen awaits your personal updates. The main level also includes a cozy Den with built-in bookcases and powder room. Upstairs, you'll find a generous Primary Bedroom boasting two walk-in closets and access to an ample Jack & Jill bath, easily transformable into a luxurious primary suite. This level also includes three additional bedrooms (one currently serving as an Office/Laundry Room) and hall Full Bath. The third floor features a fifth bedroom, full bath, along with a cedar closet and plenty of storage. The expansive lower level provides ample space for a gym and a recreation room. Outside, enjoy a large screened porch surrounded by mature plantings, a new two-car garage & a generator. All of this is just a short stroll from the Stamford Yacht Club and Woodway Beach Club & beach, with water access for kayaking & breathtaking sunset views. Also, you're only 7 minutes from Metro North trains & vibrant downtown Stamford. Don't miss this opportunity!
