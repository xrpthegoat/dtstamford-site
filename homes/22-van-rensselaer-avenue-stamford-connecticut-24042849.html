About this home

Welcome to your dream retreat in one of the most sought-after water-front neighborhoods of Stamford, CT. Just a block from the yacht club and offering seasonal water views, this charming 3-bedroom, 1-bath ranch sits on a large, level lot, offering you endless possibilities to expand and truly make it your own. Imagine waking up to the scent and sounds of the water, and taking a short stroll to one of many nearby beaches, where as a Stamford resident you have private beach rights. The property is designed for ease and comfort with one floor living, easy out to new, large, Trex-style deck, and other outdoor spaces for outdoor enjoyment. There is even an electronic dog fence to keep your furry friends safe and a generator to ensure you're always prepared. Whether you're commuting to NYC or enjoying a weekend getaway, the Metro North train station is only two miles away, making your journey effortless. This is a rare opportunity to own a piece of prime real estate in a wonderful neighborhood with room to grow. Close proximity to Stamford Yacht Club, Woodway Beach Club, multiple beaches including West Beach, Cummings Park and Cove Island, vibrant downtown Stamford and more! Your perfect home awaits!
