About this home

Nestled by the 5-Mile River and spanning 2.30 acres of mother nature and all that she offers, this 2,767 sq. ft. 4-bedroom house emphasizes privacy and comfort. The open main floor plan, bathed in sunlight, includes easy access to a bedroom with en suite bath, a gourmet eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, living room, fireplace adjacent to a screened patio with views of nature. The second floor features a private bedroom suite with fireplace, walk-in closets, an open deck overlooking the waterfall, a large bathroom with steam shower, whirlpool, heated floors, and scenic views. Two additional bedrooms upstairs, a full bathroom, suitable for family members needing supportive living arrangements. Highly accessible to businesses, hospitals, NYC, and major highways.
