About this home

Tenants have described this charming bungalow as "a thoughtfully-curated 5-star accommodation in a fantastic location". Furnished rentals do not get any better! You can enjoy this sparkling clean and tastefully decorated cottage for 12 to 15 months. WALKABLE TO TRAIN, Saugatuck Center, Levitt Pavilion, our award-winning Westport Library and so much more. Beautiful Compo Beach and Longshore Golf, Sailing & Tennis are all a short bike ride away in one direction, with downtown Westport shopping and restaurants in the other direction. Enjoy dining at many local restaurants or al fresco in your own charming, pea-gravel back yard complete with cafe lights and wisteria above a rustic farm table. Lawn and landscaping included for condo-style, low maintenance living. 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms plus a convenient outdoor shower for rinsing off sand from the beach! Price includes locker at Compo Beach to store your beach chairs; it just doesn't get easier than this! NOTE: current tenant has some limited furnishings of their own in the house, so refer to the photos for decor with a few furniture pieces upgraded since photos taken.
