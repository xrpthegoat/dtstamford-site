About this home

Beautifully updated 4/5 bedroom 4 1/2 bath residence offering a turnkey lifestyle with the perfect blend of modern luxury and timeless character, privately set on 1.95 serene, park-like acres. A circular driveway welcomes you to a fenced yard with a beautifully sited pool, expansive entertaining patio and gently rolling grassy knolls creating a peaceful, private retreat. Distinctive living and dining rooms each feature fireplaces, and the living room is further enhanced by custom built-ins.
