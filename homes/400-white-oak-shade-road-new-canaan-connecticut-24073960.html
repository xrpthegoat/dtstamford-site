About this home

Amazing opportunity to own this beautifully renovated home with a spacious flat yard and oversized barn. This classic colonial has been completely reimagined with a modern open concept with large windows, spacious rooms, beautiful bathrooms and a fantastic chef's kitchen. The upper level offers 4 bedrooms including a stunning primary suite complete with a fireplace, walk in closet and spa like bath. The lower level is finished, with a fireplace, full bath, access to the outside and is perfect for entertaining and overflow guests. The spacious 2,040 sq. ft barn provides endless opportunities for creative use. This is a must see home to appreciate all that it offers.
