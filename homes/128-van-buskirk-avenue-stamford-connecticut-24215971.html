About this home

Located at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this meticulously maintained and fully renovated Colonial offers generous living space, exceptional versatility, and outstanding amenities. Currently configured as a two-family residence with a strong 14+ year rental history, the 1st floor unit is rented for $3,000 per month, with tenants paying utilities. The owner-occupied 2nd unit has an estimated market rental value of $5,500+ per month, offering a combined potential rental income of $102,000+ annually and a 6+% cap rate. This unique property spans three floors and offers exceptional flexibility: whether for use as a two-family rental, an owner-occupied residence with rental income. The first floor features two bedrooms, living room with a fireplace, an updated kitchen, dining room, and full bath. The second floor boasts an open-concept layout, with a well-appointed kitchen featuring a wine fridge and custom-built breakfast bar that seamlessly connects to the family room, complete with an electric fireplace. The luxurious primary suite offers an oversized walk-in closet, a spa-inspired marble bath complete with a soaking tub and rainfall shower. The third floor features two additional bedrooms, a full bath, a kitchen and living area. In the private backyard is a built-in grill, outdoor fireplace, koi pond, and expansive patio. Custom-built detached office provides a private workspace in the backyard. Located minutes from beaches, Chelsea Piers, downtown, Metro-North and I95!
