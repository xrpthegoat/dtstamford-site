About this home

Set on two wooded acres, 94 Vista Road presents a rare retreat where refined country living meets the rhythm of the natural world. Down a drive of stone outcroppings and drawn in from the whimsy of the wooded setting, a gracious foyer sets the tone for style and sophistication through the lens of an artist's eye. Architectural detail and extensive millwork provide a perfect pallet for color and character. A light filled living room extends the depth of the home and leads to the warm and inviting dining room with sliding doors to a stunning screened in area for dining a la fresca. Newly renovated euro-inspired kitchen offers premium chef's appliances, overly generous cabinetry and island designed to entertain while opening seamlessly to the great room grounded by a stately stone fireplace. The second level presents four bedrooms including an expansive primary suite with dressing room and a walk-in closet. A grass cloth hallway leads to a sun-drenched office currently enjoyed as a guest room. Walkout lower level reveals the property's true distinction where a resident barred owl calls softly at dusk while red-tailed hawks circle above the canopy. Hummingbirds hover at hidden nests and robins, blue jays, and cardinals craft cradles in every corner filling mornings with cheerful chorus. Convenient to the shops, schools, and dining of Wilton, New Canaan, and Ridgefield, Vista Road delivers the deep, daily calm that only a true two-acre sanctuary can create.Live here and love life.
