About this home

Masterfully renovated and expanded by renowned architect Chad Nehring, this 1929 Colonial features a chef's kitchen boasting a centerpiece 10' island, heated gunite pool, and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Set on beautifully landscaped, private, and fully fenced-in grounds and thoughtfully designed for both elegant entertaining and everyday comfort. This home offers a gracious living room with wood-burning fireplace, formal dining room with custom paneling and hand-painted walls, and a stunning kitchen with Vermont White quartzite countertops, custom cabinetry, GE Monogram appliances, Viking refrigerator and freezer columns, dual Bosch dishwashers, and a well-appointed bar area. Completing the main level is a family room with custom built-ins, a music room for your grand piano, a private office with three desk areas, and a thoughtfully designed mudroom and powder room with radiant heated floors. French doors from the family room open to an expansive bluestone and granite terrace with custom stonework and outdoor kitchen, overlooking the private yard and heated 16'X32' pool with automatic cover. Upstairs, the primary suite features a spa-like bath with soaking tub, oversized shower, radiant heated floors and custom walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms and two full baths complete the second level. A finished third floor with full bath offers flexibility for a guest suite, office or playroom, while the lower level includes a gym, gaming room, and recreation area.
