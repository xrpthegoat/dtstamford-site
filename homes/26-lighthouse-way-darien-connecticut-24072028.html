About this home

Welcome to 26 Lighthouse Way where the "Darien Dream" of living on the water gets real! This picturesque waterfront property features panoramic water & sky views of the coastal estuary inlet of Holly Pond. Custom built by its owners this 4300 SF stone & shingle colonial has an estate like feeling with quality finishes throughout featuring a two-story entry foyer, 10' high ceilings on the 1st floor extra wide staircase & hallways, extensive solid mahogany trim millwork details & custom doors throughout. The open floor plan provides an easy flow for entertaining with large French doors that lead to the stone terrace with field stone sitting wall, pool, dock and game ready yard.The elegant finishes continue throughout the 2nd floor with 9' ceilings, 4bedrooms, 3 ensuite baths, sun deck balcony, large laundry room & full stair walkup to the 3rd floor attic with potential for expansion.An oversized 3 car garage with 12' high ceiling also offers room for possible interior living area expansion.Don't miss this opportunity to grab the Darien dream in a location that can be your forever home and sunsets a daily ritual to be enjoyed. All conveniently located close to Town Beaches & Great Island, YMCA, Hindley School and the amenities of the Noroton area of Darien. Excluded: Hand carved ship above fireplace in FR, TV's, Chandelier in primary bath & 2 in upstairs foyer
