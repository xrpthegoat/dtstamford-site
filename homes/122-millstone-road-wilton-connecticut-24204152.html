About this home

The most spectacular and unique offering in Wilton! Welcome to "La Barn," a chic estate where timeless character meets exceptional design and resort-style living. Originally a barn, this extraordinary home has been thoughtfully expanded, renovated, and lovingly maintained over the last century, creating a one-of-a-kind property rich in charm, history, and modern comfort. At its heart is a showstopping newly renovated kitchen blending mid-century warmth with clean, contemporary design. The main residence features four bedrooms, three full and one half baths, along with a newly finished walk-out lower level complete with a full bath and walk-in closet, ideal as a guest suite, gym, office, or recreation space. Resort-like grounds invite effortless entertaining and relaxation, highlighted by a recently converted and heated saltwater pool, sweeping lawns, and exceptional privacy. Above the three-car garage, a separate studio offers an additional bedroom, full bath, kitchen, and laundry, providing remarkable flexibility. Adding to the property's rare appeal is an additional barn with horse stalls, offering an exceptional opportunity, with convenient access to Wilton, Ridgefield, New Canaan, and the best of Fairfield County.
