About this home

Renovated 5 BR/3.5 bath home on a quiet cul-de-sac in the North Street school district, close to shops, restaurants, parks and hiking trails, available 8/1/2026. Renovated gourmet kitchen with stainless steel Thermador appliances, double oven, and beautiful quartz countertops. In addition, 4 more bedrooms and 2 hall baths provide ample living and office space for the whole family. Bathrooms are luxuriously renovated with radiant heat floors, marble vanities, Waterworks fixtures, and built-in towel warmers in the primary bath. Enjoy the sunset from the spacious deck off the kitchen area or relax in the private wooded backyard.
