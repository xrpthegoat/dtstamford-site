About this home

Move right into this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home ideally set on a fabulous flat property in one of Westport, CT's most desirable walk-to-town neighborhoods. Located on sought-after Loren Lane, this inviting residence offers the perfect blend of comfort, function, and everyday lifestyle just minutes from Main Street, top-rated schools, Metro North train, Compo Beach, and Longshore Club Park. Inside, sun-filled living spaces are anchored by a charming wood-burning fireplace, while the updated kitchen opens seamlessly for daily living and entertaining. A fabulous oversized den provides exceptional living space for relaxing, gathering, or play, and the mudroom with direct garage access adds the practical convenience today's buyers love. The finished lower level delivers outstanding flexibility with dedicated gym space, private home office, and additional room for recreation, media, or storage. Upstairs, four spacious bedrooms offer comfortable accommodations, while the thoughtful floor plan creates an easy flow for modern living. Outside, the level yard is ideal for entertaining, play, and outdoor enjoyment. Rarely does a move-in ready home offer this combination of updated interiors, flexible living space, flat property, and true walk-to-town convenience close to Westport's vibrant downtown shopping, dining, library, parks, beaches, and all this exceptional coastal town has to offer.
