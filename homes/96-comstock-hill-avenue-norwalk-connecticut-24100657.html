About this home

Enjoy the luxury of a fully renovated 5 bedroom home set on nearly three manicured acres complete with pool, spa, and your own par 3 hole for off-season golf practice! High-end gourmet kitchen with seamless flow to indoor and outdoor entertaining areas creates the perfect house for entertaining. Finished lower level is the perfect hang-out space with TV viewing, pool table, bar, 5th bedroom & full bath and well-equipped gym. Live, Work, Play - this property has it all! And to top it off, a red-light sauna will be a great way to end the day! Offered furnished for the months of Oct 1 2025 - June 1 2026, monthly rent includes all utilities & maintenance costs.
