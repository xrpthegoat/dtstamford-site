About this home

Effortless Luxury Living at the Buckingham Condominium. You can have it all!! Move without compromise in this elegant and meticulously maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath condo at the sought-after Buckingham Condominium. Combining spacious interiors with a prime downtown location, this home has everything you need for a sophisticated yet low-maintenance lifestyle. From the moment you step into the welcoming foyer, you'll feel at home in the thoughtfully designed layout. The open-concept living and dining areas flow seamlessly into an updated eat-in kitchen, complete with sleek stone countertops, premium appliances, and a generously sized pantry. Located in the heart of downtown Stamford, this condo puts you just steps away from everything the city provides. Walk to everything.
