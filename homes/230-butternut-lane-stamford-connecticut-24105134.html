About this home

Tucked away on a serene cul-de-sac in coveted North Stamford, this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath colonial offers privacy, luxury, and exceptional amenities on a full acre of park-like grounds. Step inside to find hardwood floors throughout, two cozy fireplaces, and spacious rooms designed with both comfort and style in mind. The bright, open layout is perfect for everyday living and effortless entertaining-indoors and out. The oversized primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a large custom walk-in closet and a spa-like bathroom with ample space to unwind. Each additional bedroom offers generous proportions and natural light. Outdoors, enjoy an oversized heated in-ground pool, a private putting green, and lush landscaping that creates a peaceful escape just minutes from downtown Stamford. An expansive undercover deck makes entertaining possible in any season or weather, while a whole house generator ensures year-round peace of mind. Located in a quiet, established neighborhood with easy access to NYC, this property is the perfect blend of suburban tranquility and commuter convenience. Truly move-in ready-this is North Stamford living at its best.
