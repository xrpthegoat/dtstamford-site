About this home

Multiple offers have been received, please submit highest and best offers by Wednesday, 8/26 at 10am. Thank you! Set on a private one-acre lot in Norwalk's highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood, 4 Stonehedge Road is a beautifully maintained four-bedroom Colonial that blends New England style with modern comfort. A gracious floor plan, rich hardwood floors, crown molding, custom millwork, and abundant natural light create a warm, sophisticated atmosphere. The renovated kitchen features GE Profile stainless steel appliances and opens to the inviting family room with a charming wood-burning stove. Additional living space, not included in the square footage, includes a bright three-season porch overlooking the private backyard and a partially finished lower level, ideal for a media room, fitness area, home office, or playroom. The spacious primary suite offers a walk-in closet and renovated en-suite bath, while three additional bedrooms and provide ample closet space and flexibility. Central air, a two-car garage, and numerous updates add everyday comfort and convenience. Ideally located within walking distance of award-winning Cranbury Elementary School and Cranbury Park, this exceptional home also offers easy access to I-95, Route 7, the Merritt Parkway, Metro-North, shopping, dining, and Norwalk's vibrant waterfront. Offering privacy, space, and an exceptional location, this is a rare opportunity to own in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
