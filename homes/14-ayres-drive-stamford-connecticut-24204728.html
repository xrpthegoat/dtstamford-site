About this home

Location, location, location! Welcome to 14 Ayres, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2-bath home nestled on a quiet street in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Step inside to find gleaming hardwood floors thru-out, living room with fireplace, Eat-in kitchen, dining room and two bedrooms with a full bath on the first floor. The second floor has two generous bedrooms along with another full bath. Just off the dining room, an enclosed porch with sliding doors opens to a large awning-covered deck overlooking the beautifully landscaped, fenced backyard, perfect for outdoor gatherings and relaxation. Upstairs, you will find two generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath. The large unfinished basement offers abundant storage and additional potential for future use. Additional highlights include gleaming hardwood floors, freshly painted interiors, central air conditioning, energy-efficient features, including solar panels and a Generac whole-house generator for added peace of mind. Conveniently located near shopping, places of worship, parks, public transportation, downtown Stamford, Metro-North, and major highways, this home offers easy access to everything the city has to offer. Don't miss the opportunity to make this wonderful house your next home!
