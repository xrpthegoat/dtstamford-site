About this home

Spacious North Stamford Ranch home in the prestigious North Lake area. Fabulous stone and iron automatic gated entry opens to the majestic, park-like 2-acre level property. Attached 2-car garage with stairs to a huge storage loft. Newer roof and HVAC system, 200amp panel, plus a full house generator. Fantastic opportunity in this super desirable neighborhood.
