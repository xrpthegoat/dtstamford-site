About this home

Welcome to 23 Butternut Place, a charming four-bedroom, two-bathroom home that expertly blends comfort and flexibility. As you enter, you'll be greeted by a formal living room featuring beautiful hardwood floors. The cozy family room, complete with a fireplace, flows into a formal dining area, creating an ideal space for entertaining. This area opens to a serene enclosed porch, perfect for enjoying morning coffee or evening gatherings. The spacious kitchen invites culinary creativity with ample counter space. Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac and conveniently close to town, this home also features a walkout basement that leads to an expansive private backyard, ideal for outdoor activities and entertaining, along with a convenient two-car garage below.
