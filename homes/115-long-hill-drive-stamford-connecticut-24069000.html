About this home

OFFER DEADLINE MONDAY 3/10 AT 7:00 P.M. Unbeatable cape in highly sought after Westover neighborhood. From the moment you walk through the front door of this meticulously maintained home, you'll begin to truly understand the meaning of a hidden gem! The main floor features an inviting sun-filled living room w/ beautiful wood burning fireplace, which flows nicely into the formal dining room area. Gorgeous, modern white kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances, marble countertops, built-in microwave & tons of cabinet space! The Primary Bedroom is sure to please and can also be utilized as a family room/den, and has access to the stunning updated 1st floor bath. As you make your way upstairs, you'll notice 3 additional spacious bedrooms, along with a 2nd full bath which includes a tiled shower, lovely vanity & newer fixtures. Relax and unwind during the summer months on the amazing patio - perfect for BBQing or entertaining guests! Also features recessed lighting, attached 1 car garage, tons of storage, fully fenced in yard, separate laundry room & hardwood floors throughout. Close proximity to Downtown, Ridgeway Shopping Center, Whole Foods, Pepe's Pizzeria, public transportation, shops, restaurants, & all that Stamford has to offer! A TRUE MUST SEE!!!
