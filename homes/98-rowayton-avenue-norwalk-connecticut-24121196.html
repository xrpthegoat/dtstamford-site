About this home

Welcome to 98 Rowayton Ave, in the heart of Rowayton where you can walk to it all! This historic home has been thoughtfully renovated, combining luxury with advanced technology for modern living. The first floor boasts new hardwood floors, a first floor en suite bedroom with radiant heat marble bathroom floors. The gourmet kitchen features Wolf and Sub Zero appliances, heated quartzite countertop, and two Sub Zero wine coolers. Renovations extend to the second and third floors, including a family room with shiplap and a wood burning fireplace, an office, 3 bedrooms and all new bathrooms. A third floor primary suite offers privacy and luxury, with great views of the Five Mile River. The home is equipped with Lutron automated custom drapery and lighting, a Sonos system, and Nest security features. CAT 6 wiring and WiFi extenders ensure seamless connectivity. Outdoors, enjoy a landscaped fenced-in yard with a sprinkler system, outdoor shower, and gas fire pit. Additional highlights include a garage wired for an electric car charger, a 10k kW Generac generator, and an EF-3000 filtration system. Every detail in this home is crafted for comfort and convenience, providing an unparalleled living experience.
