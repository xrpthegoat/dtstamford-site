About this home

Doral Farms is a unique community as it consists of 59 stand-alone homes on 60 acres in the sought-after Westover neighborhood. Carefree living with grounds and road maintenance, club house, pool, and tennis court maintained by the property management. Lush landscaping and flat roadways provide homeowners with the perfect setting for a relaxing stroll. 1 Alfred Lane is situated just a few steps from the pool, tennis courts, and club house. The house is positioned with neighbors on just one side of the property, providing a private setting. This well-maintained ranch-style home has no interior stairs. The floorplan is open with a centralized living and dining area and includes vaulted ceilings and gleaming hardwood floors. There is a den off the main living space leading to a large private deck. The deck overlooks a flat grassy yard and woods beyond. Generous sized primary bedroom includes a deep walk-in closet with professionally designed organization plus a secondary closet and an ensuite fully equipped bathroom. The secondary bedrooms are bright and perfectly situated. The kitchen includes a walk-in pantry, granite countertops, center island, newer stainless steel appliances, and a charming breakfast area. Visit this well-maintained home and you will want to make it your own.
