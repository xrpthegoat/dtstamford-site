About this home

*Please submit offers by Monday 3/23 at 2pm* Welcome HOME to 42 Hastings Lane! Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's highly sought-after Springdale neighborhood, this beautifully maintained and tastefully updated home truly checks every box. The home features four spacious bedrooms upstairs along with a convenient ground-floor bedroom that's perfect for guests or a work-from-home office. The expansive primary suite includes a private ensuite bath. Two walk-in closets provide excellent storage. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the main level offers a seamless flow as the kitchen opens to the dining area and family room, with doors leading out to a private backyard and deck-ideal for gatherings, play, or relaxing outdoors. The finished basement adds even more versatile space, perfect for a family room, gym, playroom, or office, while the walk-up attic presents exciting expansion potential for the future. Set on a peaceful, tree-lined street, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and location, just moments from local shops, restaurants, parks, houses of worship, and the train station for an easy commute. Local sidewalks are currently being remodeled for great walkability! A wonderful opportunity to own in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods-don't miss it!
