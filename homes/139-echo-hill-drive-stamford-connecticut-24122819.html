About this home

This lovely home boasts an amazing North Stamford location, set on a tranquil cul-de-sac among a beautiful enclave of homes. If you love to entertain inside and out, this is it. Set on a park-like and private 1.4 acres, this home has a one-level floor plan that's easy and ideal for entertaining and at-home relaxing. Upon entering the home you'll notice that the living and entertaining space is well separated from the bedrooms, offering privacy to both areas. The rooms are all spacious and well-proportioned. The expansive living room has a wood burning fireplace with stone surround. The 23' x 23' Family Room is accented by many pretty windows for lots of light. The kitchen and dining area are also generous in size. Four ample-sized bedrooms comprise the left side of the home, including a primary suite with walk-in closet and separate bath. Entertaining on the deck is a pleasure, and the yard is fully fenced with plenty of room for backyard activities. Other important features include new Hardie plank siding, new deck, whole house generator, new insulation, and fresh paint. 139 Echo Hill Drive has the best of both worlds...a short drive to vibrant Downtown Stamford as well as the quaint Pound Ridge, NY. It offers an idyllic location and setting to make your new memories here.
