About this home

Step into luxury in this chic and modern, newly constructed townhouse in beautiful Shippan Point. A one of a kind home with gorgeous features & high-end finishes. The open concept is masterful with the Living Room being seamless to the gourmet Kitchen and eating area. The Kitchen was custom designed with a Signature Kitchen Suite Appliance package including a 36" gas range and high performance Zephyr Range Hood. Calacatta Quartz countertops, and marble herringbone backsplash. You will love the oversized island with plentiful counter seating. Also on the main level is a half bath and private Office, perfect for working from home. There are 3 well-sized Bedrooms, all en-suite. The 3 Full and 2 Half Bathrooms have upgraded porcelain tile and marble floor tile, and granite & quartz countertops. The Primary Bedroom is on its own level with 2 walk-in closets and an amazing spa-like Bathroom with oversized shower and separate vanities. The Lower Level has the huge Family Room with wet bar featuring quartz countertop, wine refrigerator, and imported designer wall tile. The flooring is porcelain tile in a wood look. And, another half bath on this level - so convenient. The outside living is just as special - there is a garden patio with lighting and professional landscaping. The rear patio is more private and near the 1 car garage. Some other features are Irrigation System, Security System and new fencing. There are only 4 units in this complex, so very easy to manage and maintain.
