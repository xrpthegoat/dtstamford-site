About this home

Fabulous 4 bedroom colonial set on a spectacular landscaped acre in one of North Stamford's premier locations. Surrounded by flowering perennials and mature plantings, this home will not disappoint. Step inside to a light and bright living room with fireplace and hardwood floors which flows into the formal dining room. The eat in kitchen has sliders to the beautiful deck. The family room, with cathedral ceilings, fireplace and sliders to the deck offers plenty of space to unwind & relax. Upstairs you will find four generous sized bedrooms, including a primary suite with full bath and walk in closets. The full unfinished basement offers endless opportunities. Additionally, there is a whole house, 24KW generator. Located on a quiet cul de sac, this well maintained colonial is just minutes to shopping, restaurants and all that Stamford has to offer!
