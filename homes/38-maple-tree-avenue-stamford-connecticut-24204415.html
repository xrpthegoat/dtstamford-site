About this home

Welcome to this spacious three-bedroom townhouse located in Stamford's Glenbrook neighborhood. The recently painted interior features a generously sized living room with hardwood floors and a fireplace, a dining area, a functional kitchen, and a convenient half bathroom on the main level. Upstairs are three well-proportioned bedrooms, a full bathroom, and ample closet space. The partially finished basement provides flexible additional space suitable for a home office, recreation room, gym, or other uses. The unfinished utility area includes a private in-unit washer and dryer. Additional features include a private outdoor deck and assigned parking. The property is conveniently located near the Glenbrook Metro-North station, providing easy train access to New York City. It also offers convenient access to downtown Stamford, I-95, grocery stores, restaurants, shopping, and Stamford's waterfront parks and beaches, including Cove Island Park, Cummings Park, and West Beach.
