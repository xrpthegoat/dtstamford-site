About this home

One of the most sought-after complexes in Westport, Lansdowne offers the perfect blend of comfort, security, convenience, and luxury. This spacious home with a highly desired first-floor Primary Suite is bathed in natural light and designed to provide the feel of single-family living while enjoying the benefits of a condominium community. The main level features a welcoming foyer, an eat-in kitchen with granite counters, updated appliances, and breakfast area, a dining room with vaulted ceiling, a living room with fireplace, hardwood floors and wet bar, and a sunroom opening to a private terrace with tranquil land views. The first-floor Primary Suite boasts walk-in closets and a renovated full bath, while a half bath and laundry add convenience. Upstairs offers a large bedroom with renovated bath and walk-in closet along with a versatile loft area. Additional features include a one-car garage, beautifully landscaped grounds, a heated pool and pool house, tennis and paddle courts, and a charming gazebo-truly a lifestyle you will love at Lansdowne!
