About this home

Desirable Pepper Ridge location sets the stage for this fabulous Colonial filled with natural sunlight, offering space, comfort and modern efficiency. A dramatic two-story foyer welcomes you into a thoughtfully designed layout, complemented by hardwood floors throughout. The spacious eat-in kitchen features s/s appliances, double ovens, newer windows and flows seamlessly into the living room and separate dining area, with sliders to a deck overlooking the yard & tranquil stream-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Main-level primary bedroom provides true convenience, complete with custom closets and a beautifully renovated bath featuring a double vanity, soaking tub and separate shower. Upstairs, you'll find three additional bedrooms (one with a walk-in closet), a hall bath and a versatile loft-ideal for a home office, playroom or flex space. Newer roof & energy-efficient solar panels add long-term value, with the balance to be paid off at closing. A two-car garage, EV charger ready offers added convenience and storage. Ideally located close to schools, shopping, houses of worship and just minutes to vibrant downtown. A wonderful opportunity not to be missed!
