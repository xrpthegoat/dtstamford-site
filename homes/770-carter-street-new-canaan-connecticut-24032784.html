About this home

Tucked away down a private driveway and situated on 2.25 acres of secluded land, this charming cottage-studio awaits you. The spacious room features vaulted cathedral ceilings and ample closet space. A bay window floods the space with natural sunlight. The eat-in kitchen is equipped with a new stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, and your own washer and dryer. Enjoy relaxing evenings on your private deck. The driveway accommodates parking for two cars, while garages on the premises are reserved for the owner's use. One dog may be considered with the owner's approval; however, cats are not permitted. The tenant is responsible for the cost of heating oil for the cottage and must obtain a P.O. Box for postal services. The landlord covers the electric bill, cable bill, internet, and trash removal. Excellent credit and references are required, along with renters insurance. The tenant is responsible for shoveling the cottage walkway, while snow plowing and grounds maintenance are provided by the landlord. A new central AC wall unit and a generator are in place. Please note that the cottage is available for single occupancy only. Full house Generator. This property is a must-see!
