About this home

Modern Luxury New Construction residence sits on .78 acres combines privacy with elegant design. Moments from Greenwich Ave. built by an award winning builder. This home features soaring 10ft ceilings, light filled rooms, wide white-oak wood-floors throughout, and seamless indoor-outdoor living. A few highlights of many, are a Rooftop deck with Long Island Sound views that crowns the residence, a 3-car garage (+900 sq. Ft.) with 14-ft ceilings, and a serious top-of-the-line chef's kitchen. The Chef's kitchen features, Sub-Zero refrigeration,Wolf cooking, 2 sinks, and 2 dishwashers. A grand center island, Custom cabinetry, and thoughtful storage, providing effortless daily living and elevated entertaining. The primary suite offers a double-sided fireplace, creating a warm ambient enjoyed from both the bedroom and the spa-like bath. A private balcony extends the retreat, while the spa-inspired bathroom features a steam shower and radiant heated floors. 4 additional light filled en-suite bedrooms, share the same level, alongside a full laundry suite equipped with custom built-ins including steam/Iron drawer, a dry rack drawer, and stacked double washers and dryers. A 4-stop hydraulic elevator serves every level-from the versatile finished attic to a finished expansive lower level, you'll find a dedicated room for a gym, guest/au pair en-suite, and pre-wired theater/Golf simulator, ample space for a wine cellar, storage, and protected living space.
