About this home

Set on a 1-acre lot in Old Hill, this 5-bedroom, 4.1-bath Colonial offers 4,035 sq ft of open, light-filled living space. The main level features a welcoming foyer, formal living and dining rooms, and a family room with large windows overlooking the landscaped backyard. The kitchen includes a gas cooktop, wall oven, microwave, refrigerator, freezer, ice maker, and dishwasher, opening seamlessly to the deck with scenic views. The primary suite provides a spacious bedroom and modern en-suite bath. Hardwood floors extend throughout the main living areas. Three fireplaces add warmth and charm. Finished lower level with walk-out access provides living space. The attic and basement offer ample storage. Attached 3-car garage. Includes 3D walkthrough and video tour. Conveniently located near King's Highway Elementary (1.0 mi), Coleytown Middle (1.5 mi), Staples High School (2.0 mi), shops, restaurants, and commuter routes. Exceptional opportunity to combine classic Colonial style with modern functionality.
