About this home

Experience the best of coastal living in this stunning Rowayton retreat, perfectly positioned on the tranquil waters of Farm Creek. Available for rent for a flexible term from September through April, this home offers the rare choice of being delivered furnished or unfurnished to suit your lifestyle. Inside, the vibe is effortlessly chic and "beachy," defined by a fresh palette, new light hardwood floors, and an abundance of natural light that pours through oversized windows. The open-concept layout creates an inviting flow, centered around a beautifully renovated kitchen and modern baths. With four spacious bedrooms and three full bathrooms, there is plenty of room to breathe, including a versatile finished third floor that works perfectly as a home office, gym, or creative studio. The exterior is a true waterfront sanctuary. Step out onto the expansive deck to take in the crisp salt air and panoramic views of Farm Creek-a vista shared by almost every room in the house. Whether you're sipping morning coffee or watching the sunset, the backdrop is spectacular. The property also features a private dock, offering direct water access for kayaking or simply enjoying the rhythmic beauty of the tides. This is an incredible opportunity to settle into a turn-key, upscale home in one of the area's most coveted locations. Home can also be rented furnished for $24,000 per month.
