About this home

Excellent opportunity for owner-occupants and investors alike at 29 Roosevelt Ave in Stamford. This well-maintained two-family home offers flexibility, income potential, and recent updates throughout, making it an ideal option for anyone looking to live in one unit while renting the other, or for an investor seeking a vacant multi-family property ready for its next chapter. The home features 1,982 sq ft of living space with 3 total bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms across two units, plus a finished basement with an additional bathroom. The first-floor unit offers 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom, while the second-floor unit features 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom. Both units have been freshly improved with newly finished hardwood floors, fresh interior paint, and new kitchen flooring, giving the property a clean and refreshed feel. The semi-finished basement adds valuable flexibility, featuring a separate entrance, heat, laundry hookups, and a bathroom. This space can be kept for owner use, storage, additional recreational space, or potentially rented together with Unit 1, depending on the buyer's needs. Set on a 0.11-acre lot, the property also includes a newly sealed driveway and garage parking, offering convenient off-street parking in a highly accessible Stamford location. Separated natural gas utilities add to the home's functionality, and with both units currently vacant, the next owner has the ability to set rents, choose occupants, or move in immediately.
