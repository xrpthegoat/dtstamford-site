About this home

Welcome to this stunning four-bedroom colonial home, ideally situated on a private cul-de-sac in the desirable Silvermine area of Norwalk. This residence features a nearly new, state-of-the-art white kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances, perfect for culinary enthusiasts.The adjacent family room has a wood burning fireplace, vaulted ceiling and access to a private deck and level back yard. A second deck is accessed from the eat-in kitchen. The laundry room with recent front load appliances is conveniently located adjacent to the kitchen. Adding to the perfectly functional modern floorplan are highlights like a dedicated home office, a formal living room and an elegant dining room with tray ceiling. The primary bedroom has a vaulted ceiling and primary bath with Jacuzzi soaking tub. The upper floor is complemented by three additional bedrooms. The finished lower level offers a tiled garage entry mudroom, a versatile recreational space that can serve as a music room, media room or multipurpose area plus a workshop/utility room. Another bonus feature is a three bay garage. In addition, there are Nest thermostats for energy efficiency and smart home convenience. This home combines elegance and practicality as well as a convenient location, making it an ideal home.
