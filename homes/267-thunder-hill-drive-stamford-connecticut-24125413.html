About this home

Prime WESTOVER location of Stamford!!! Fabulous fully updated light and airy 3BD/2BR lovingly maintained ranch style single family home sitting on a flat acre of fully usable land and on a dead end street with minimal traffic. This home offers an open and functional layout with living and entertaining wings nicely separated. Great size eating-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances opens up into the dining/living room with vaulted ceilings, centrally located wood burning fire place, beautiful contemporary light fixtures and brand new sliders that lead onto the deck that is PERFECT for entertaining/family gatherings. Brand new floors were installed last year, recessed lights, year round family room heated and cooled in addition to the living room, great amount of closet space and additional storage is also available in the basement. Parking for a minimum of four cars!!! Propane heating, central air conditioning and large private yard. Close to shopping and restaurants, miles of hiking and biking trails, Stamford Museum and Nature Center, Stamford's Arboretum, golf and tennis, horseback riding, minutes to Greenwich and all Stamford Living has to offer including its beautiful parks and beaches. Great credit and income is a MUST! Don't wait...this kind of property does not come very often.
