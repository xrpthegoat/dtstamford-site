About this home

Furnished academic rental Sept '24-May 31 '25. Embrace the Westport beach lifestyle! Enjoy morning coffee from the balcony of this beautiful home while taking in the endless vistas of LI Sound. This FEMA compliant home is directly across the street from Westport's iconic beaches. Entertain family & friends on the oversized, private terrace with a custom heated plunge pool/spa and waterfall, built-in BBQ grill and refrigerator. Sip wine in front of the outdoor fire pit. It's the perfect spot to unwind at the end of a hectic day. The primary bedroom is off the 3rd floor great room with a balcony overlooking Old Mill beach. Enjoy people watching at it's best! Two additional bedrooms on the main floor flank the chef's kitchen with marble countertops, SubZero and a Viking range. Wipe off your sandy feet in the garage before entering the laundry room where you can conveniently drop your dirty beach clothes, or use the outdoor shower to wash off. It's a great house in a special location! Absolutely NO PETS!
