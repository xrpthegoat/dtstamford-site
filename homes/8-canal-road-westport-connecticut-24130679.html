About this home

Incredible opportunity to own a coastal retreat with sweeping water views of Saugatuck Harbor and beyond. This sun-drenched home is perfectly positioned to capture the beauty of seaside living, offering both tranquility and convenience in one of Westport's most desirable locations. With deeded water rights included, you can enjoy direct access to the shoreline, making it ideal for boating, kayaking, or simply savoring the peaceful waterfront setting. Step inside to find an open floor plan designed for both comfort and entertaining. The interior is flooded with natural light that frame the captivating water vistas, creating a seamless connection between the indoors and outdoors. The main living areas flow effortlessly, allowing gatherings to feel open and inviting, while the patio just outside is perfect for morning coffee, sunset cocktails, or evenings spent soaking in the cool sea breeze. Westport is known for its stunning coastline, and this property fully embraces that lifestyle. Breathe in the salt air, watch the sailboats drift by, and immerse yourself in the calming rhythm of the water. Beyond the home itself, the location offers unmatched convenience just minutes to vibrant downtown Westport, the train for easy commuting, and an abundance of shopping and dining options. For recreation, Longshore Park, Compo Beach, and golf and tennis facilities are all within close reach, ensuring a year-round resort-style experience.
