About this home

Move-in ready is an overused phrase, but this one earns it. The unit has been freshly painted throughout and comes with stainless steel appliances, a new in-unit washer and dryer, a newer water heater, and an updated electrical panel. Those last few are the unglamorous, expensive items most buyers end up handling themselves, and here they're already taken care of. A private balcony extends the living space for morning coffee. One off-street parking spot comes with the unit, as does a private storage unit and pets are welcome, including a built-in nook under the stairs that's already set up as a dedicated dog and cat space. The location adds a lot. Lione Park is a short walk away, E. Gaynor Brennan Golf Course sits around the corner, and downtown Stamford is under a mile on foot, which puts restaurants, coffee, and everyday errands within easy reach. Metro-North is a quick drive when the city calls, with a direct ride into Grand Central. Finished, comfortable, and priced below the Stamford condo median. This is a genuine opportunity to own in a market where that's getting harder to find.
