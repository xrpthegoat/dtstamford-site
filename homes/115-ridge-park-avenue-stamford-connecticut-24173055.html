About this home

Welcome to Stamford's coveted Mid-Ridges neighborhood, where this newly constructed Colonial offers luxury new construction, thoughtfully designed living, and expansive outdoor space in one of the city's most desirable residential settings. Offering nearly 3,000 square feet across three finished levels, 115 Ridge Park Avenue features 3 upstairs bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, 1 half bath, a dedicated private office, and a fully finished lower level with its own full bathroom and direct exterior access. The oversized primary ensuite retreat spans over 400 square feet and features cathedral ceilings with custom wood shiplap detailing, designer lighting, abundant natural light, and a spacious walk-in closet. The spa-inspired bath includes oversized 48" x 48" tile, a frameless walk-in shower with gold fixtures, and a custom made double vanity. At the center of the home is a chef's kitchen with Taj Mahal countertops, a full-height backsplash, handcrafted wood range hood, dedicated coffee area, and custom wood-paneled island designed for entertaining and everyday living. The expansive two-tiered backyard offers privacy, sunrise and sunset views, and over 2,500 square feet of additional flat outdoor space ideal for entertaining, recreation, or future pool potential. Perfectly positioned minutes from Route 15, I-95, Metro-North, Whole Foods, Starbucks, and Stamford's premier dining and shopping destinations, this home delivers elevated living with exceptional convenience.
