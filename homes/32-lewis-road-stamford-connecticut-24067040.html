About this home

Beautiful, fully renovated Turn of River home. A modernized colonial offering 3000+ sq ft of open concept living with 4 bedrooms, 3 full and 2 half baths. Main level boasts a custom modern kitchen including an oversized island with seating in proximity to dining area and sitting room. Adjoined by sunken family room accessing large deck/patio area and backyard. Spacious en-suite bedroom offers option for primary suite on main level. Centrally located powder and laundry room make entertaining and housework a breeze! Upstairs features large primary en-suite bedroom. Accompanied by 2 additional bedrooms with a full bathroom and tub. Finished lower level contains 2 separate multi-use areas with another half bath. Perfect for a home gym, playroom, office, and/or entertaining space. Additional storage, mechanical room, and single car garage complete the level.
