About this home

Welcome to the best of both worlds. This beautifully designed townhouse lives like a single-family home, offering the space, and flow tenants crave without the maintenance headaches. Step inside to an open, sun-filled main level featuring rich hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and an effortless layout perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The spacious living and dining areas flow seamlessly into a well-appointed kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, custom cabinetry, and a stylish tile backsplash. It's functional and inviting. Also, on the main level is a bedroom and half bathroom. Upstairs, generously sized bedrooms offer comfort and flexibility, whether you need guest space, a home office, or a peaceful retreat. The bathrooms feature stone tile, updated vanities, and clean, modern finishes. Every detail feels thoughtful and turnkey. Located in the heart of Stamford's sought-after Cove neighborhood, you're just minutes from Cove Island Park and beach, Chelsea Piers, neighborhood shops, dining, Metro-North, and I-95. Morning beach visits, quick commutes, and everything Stamford has to offer are right at your doorstep. Spacious. Move-in ready. Ideally located. This is townhouse living reimagined.
