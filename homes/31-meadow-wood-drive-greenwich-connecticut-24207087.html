About this home

An extraordinary opportunity to acquire a waterfront residence within the exclusive & highly coveted Belle Haven Association - at an unusually low ''cost of entry'' point. One of just eight residences in historic Quarry Farms - which has its own gated entrance, this gut renovated 3-bedroom. 3.5 bathroom home is privately set on 1.2 acres with 220' of direct Long Island Sound frontage. Unique features include a private kayak launch, woodland trail, charming footbridge, commercial grade chef's kitchen plus a 4-car garage. Stylishly finished chic interiors are elevated by the open floor plan, high ceilings and captivating ''protected'' water views. Ample expansion opportunity and within close proximity to the ultra-exclusive Belle Haven Club. Seize this Belle Haven waterfront opportunity...
