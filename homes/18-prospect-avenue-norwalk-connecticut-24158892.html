About this home

Love where you live (or invest) in this prime, central location! Step inside to a sweet surprise -impressive in both size & condition. The open & airy living room beams with natural light from the extra-large slider windows & borrowed light from the easy to access modernized kitchen. The eat-in kitchen with GE stainless steel appliances has a true breakfast nook. The bedroom is very spacious with ceiling-height double-closets ensuring no negotiation needed. The gorgeous bathroom was recently remodeled & features a refreshingly full-sized, fully-tiled walk-in shower & window for great natural light. Stunning style & colors are integrated throughout this pristine home from the beautiful hardwood floors, to the kitchen's sparkling quartz countertops & subway tile backsplash, to the bathroom's marble hexagon tile flooring & light fixture - beauty abounds. Heat, hot water & very convenient reserved parking included. Also enjoy the perks of private storage, laundry on site & plenty of visitor parking. Within 1-3 miles almost everything you would need or want. From SoNo RR to I-95; to a simple commute to schools & hospital; to restaurants, entertainment & retail (Washington Prime, Donovan's, Kazu, Maritime Aquarium, theaters, Apple, Nordstrom to name a few); to all that is just around the corner (Stew Leonard's, Stop & Shop, Wegmans, Starbucks, parks, beach, golf, tennis etc), settle right in to warmth, comfort & convenience today!
