About this home

Previously featured on HGTV Property Brothers: Dream Home, this newly renovated masterpiece showcases unparalleled craftsmanship and design. A soaring two-story foyer with a stone fireplace sets the tone for elegance. The chef's kitchen boasts stunning quartz countertops, a professional range, and custom cabinetry. The dramatic living and dining areas feature coffered ceilings, a stone fireplace, and oversized windows that bring in natural light. The luxurious primary suite offers a tray ceiling, dressing room, custom walk-in closet, and spa-like bath with a private deck. Two ensuite bedrooms, an office/fourth bedroom, and an oversized family room complete the home. Multiple patios and an outdoor fireplace overlook 1.25 acres with serene Mianus River frontage.
