About this home

Welcome to the tranquil and beautifully manicured grounds of Foxboro! This highly sought after complex is located on the border of Norwalk and Wilton with close proximity to restaurants and shops. This condo is complimented with ample parking, sidewalks and open green areas, along with a sparkling community pool. The private deck off the living room overlooks a green lawn and trees. This home has an open floor plan for the dining and living rooms with plenty of sunlight, plus built-ins and a wood burning fireplace. The large kitchen provides an abundance of cabinets and overlooks the homes entrance. A half bath completes the main floor. Upstairs are two bedrooms each with en-suite bath. Primary bedroom has two large closets. Lower level has additional closet space, access to the one car garage, laundry area and space for storage or workout/playroom.
