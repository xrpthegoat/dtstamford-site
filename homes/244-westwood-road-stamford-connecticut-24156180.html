About this home

Welcome to this spacious and solid 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath raised ranch nestled in the highly sought-after Westover neighborhood. Proudly owned by the same family for more than 60 years, this special home has been the backdrop for decades of cherished memories-and is now ready for its next chapter. This home offers an exceptional opportunity for its next owner to bring fresh vision and updates to truly make it shine. With great bones and generous living spaces, it provides the perfect canvas to create your dream home in a premier location. Recent improvements include a brand new furnace and a two-year-old roof, offering valuable peace of mind. The living room, dining room, and family room have all been freshly painted in a beautiful neutral tone, creating a bright and welcoming atmosphere. The exterior was painted in 2025. Designed for comfortable living and effortless entertaining, the main level of the home features a gas log fireplace in the living room and an expanded dining room with beautiful oversized windows. Just off the kitchen, which has a gorgeous greenhouse window, and cathedral ceiling, you'll find a screened porch with a vaulted ceiling and ceiling fan-an ideal space to relax and enjoy the outdoors. The porch connects to a spacious deck that offers wonderful potential and will benefit from structural repairs to restore it to its full glory.
