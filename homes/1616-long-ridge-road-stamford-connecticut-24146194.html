About this home

Stunning rear yard with in ground salt water pool, hot tub, outdoor kitchen, fireplace and pizza oven plus plenty of level lawn enclosed in fencing. All of this just steps from the tastefully renovated open concept kitchen great room combination. 6 burner Viking gas stove, SubZero and quartz counters. The kitchen with oversized center island is flanked by a breakfast bar and dining area with builtins. Luxury master suite with his/her walk in closets, French doors to private balcony plus a newly renovated spa bath. Formal living room/dining room with two sided gas fireplace. 10' ceilings, main level home office with a wall of built ins and a lower level rec room with a game day worthy bar, ample space for indoor fun and a separate gym. A luxury home of refined taste.
