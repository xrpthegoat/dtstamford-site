About this home

Bucolic, charming and recently remodeled 1 bedroom, 1 bath cottage with loft available for immediate occupancy in West Norwalk! The cottage offers a private, tranquil setting sited on 1.21 acres and features a brand new kitchen with new cabinetry, quartz countertops, ceramic tile backsplash, stainless steel range and island with plenty of storage. The open-concept main level also offers a living room and dining area with new custom built-in banquette. Upstairs, find the bedroom, bathroom with new vanity plus loft perfect for a home office, dressing area or sitting room. There are refurbished hardwood floors throughout and the entire space has been freshly painted and upgraded with designer touches and finishes. In addition to the interior living space, the cottage also offers a fully fenced in front yard plus a private patio area. Washer/Dryer in the under house garage. Excellent credit, proof of income and references required.
