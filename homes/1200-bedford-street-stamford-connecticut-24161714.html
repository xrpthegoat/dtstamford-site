About this home

Welcome to 1200 Bedford Street, located in the heart of downtown Stamford-one of the most vibrant and convenient places to live in Fairfield County. Enjoy walkable access to top restaurants, cafes, shopping, and nightlife, all while being just minutes from the Stamford Transportation Center, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, making commuting a breeze. This beautiful, modern apartment features a full-size spare room, perfect for a home office or guest bedroom, and is situated in a small, quiet luxury building-a rare find in downtown Stamford. If you're looking to be close to all the action, but still value privacy and a peaceful living environment, this is the perfect fit. Unlike larger apartment complexes, this boutique-style building offers a more relaxed and luxury feel, with no crowded lobbies or elevators. The charming pavilion-style outdoor garage adds to its unique character. Originally converted from corporate office space, the units offer exceptionally spacious layouts with a modern, chic design that stands out from typical apartments. Features include: Wide open floor plan High ceilings with track lighting Brand new flooring Modern kitchen and bathroom with updated appliances Abundant natural sunlight through oversized windows A truly unique opportunity to enjoy downtown living without the usual hustle and noise.
