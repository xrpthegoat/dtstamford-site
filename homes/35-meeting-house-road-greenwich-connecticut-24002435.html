About this home

This back-country barn combines old world charm with modern living. Wide-plank floors, sprawling wood beams, and antique fireplaces give this home a quintessential New England feel. The modern layout features an eat-in-kitchen, combo living/dining room, wet bar, and first-floor bedroom with full bath. Upstairs houses the oversized primary bedroom with cathedral ceilings and walk-in-closet, as well as a 2nd bedroom and full bath. Completing the 2nd floor is a large family room with soaring, beamed ceilings and loft. Bonus space in the lower level can be used as an office, gym, or extra bedroom. Above the 2-car garage is a quaint 1-bedroom cottage with living room and full bath. Step outside to the level backyard to find a welcoming entertaining space and pool, just in time for summer.
