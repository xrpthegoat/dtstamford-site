About this home

Welcome to 2 Coldspring Street! Currently zoned (CD2-Community District 2) This home offers a perfect blend of character, comfort and improvements for today's demands. Unique ranch style home with a primary bedroom suite on the second level. It boasts vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet, full bath with jet-tub and separate shower stall. Also French door to private balcony and to the exterior staircase. The main level with an open floor feeling from the living room to an updated eat-in kitchen to a cozy and comfortable step-down family room with a wood burning fireplace. Slider to a deck overlooking the above ground pool (condition unknown)and large side property. Two bedrooms on the main level with a shared common full bath. Semi-finished lower level - a fantastic playroom or exercise room with a full bath and Laundry and utility area. Recent improvements: New Solar System, Eat-in Kitchen w/SS appliances, 3 yrs old boiler and more. Beautiful corner property with fully fenced-in yard that provides outdoor space for all amazing year round events. Off street parking with paved driveway. Zoned for multifamily and buyers are responsible for their own municipal search. This sought after Spring Hill area is extremely convenient to Highways, Route 7 Connector, and to all local amenities such as: Hospital, Restaurants, Sono Mall and Beaches. Property as-is condition
