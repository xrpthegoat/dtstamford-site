About this home

Rich in history is this sidewalk and tree lined neighborhood of homes oozing with architectural character. A cheerful 1929 colonial gem with a darling vestibule entry and many unspoiled period details, has been freshly painted both inside and out. Wonderful first floor living space with oversized sunroom, living room and dining room each with windows that frame a quaint neighborhood picture. The bright skylit kitchen has shaker cabinets, garden window and granite counters with under cabinet lighting. The upper level comfortably accommodates 3 bedrooms that share an oversized bath. A walk up attic with full height at its center point can be finished for a multitude of uses. Clean and bright unfinished basement with ample storage and options for use. Walk to Glenbrook village and Metro North. This neighborhood jewel is move in ready.
