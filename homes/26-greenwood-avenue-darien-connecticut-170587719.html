About this home

Pride of ownership! Sunny ranch on a low traffic street in Darien, CT! Best of both worlds; Darien schools & convenience of Springdale and access to New Canaan parks! Newly finished hardwood floors throughout! Large living room with closet, bay window and wood-burning fireplace. Remodeled eat-in kitchen (2005) with pantry, breakfast bar & dining area. Cosy den with door out to deck and backyard. Light-filled second bedroom to the left of the entry of the home comes with built-ins. Hallway closet and full bath with tub & ventilation window. Sizable primary suite (originally 2 bedrooms) with built-ins, large shower/double vanity, and space enough for a dedicated home office. The lower level of the home could potentially be refinished. Sliders from primary suite head out to back deck. Mature appealing spacious garden with shed. Two dedicated off street parking spots on shared driveway, and the kindest of neighbors! Welcome home!
