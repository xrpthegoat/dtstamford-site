About this home

Three story 1910 Colonial offers a versatile floor plan and many updates by current owner over the past 10 years. These include 4 remodeled bathrooms (one on each floor), updated kitchen, 18 new windows, repaved driveway, upgraded electrical service, new heat, central air and hot water systems. After entering foyer through the new front door you will find on the main floor, a living room with fireplace and french doors that lead to large sun room with walls of windows and an eat-in-kitchen with quartz counters and stainless-steel appliances. Main floor also has what is currently a bedroom but could easily be a large formal dining room. Second floor has three large bedrooms and full bath. Versatile third floor, with skylights to provide natural light, has a full bath offering the options of additional bedrooms, office or playroom. Hardwood floors on first and second floor plus two fireplaces. Newly finished walk-out basement with waterproofing and a full bathroom. Exterior offers a level yard, re-built stone walls, deck, front porch and a 2-car garage. Insulated first and second floors. Conveniently located to beach, shopping, highways and Metro-North.
