About this home

Located in the charming neighborhood of Shippan, this meticulously restored gem epitomizes coastal living at her finest! 4 years of dedicated restoration has transformed this property into an oasis, boasting a fully fenced backyard with an in-ground swimming pool, spa, a cabana & main level deck surrounded by mature plantings. There's a spacious grassy area: Seeing is believing! Upon entering, you're greeted by a stunning foyer. To the left, a spacious living room captures the essence of the 1920s with its marble mantle & wood-burning fireplace, complimented by a large new picture window offering views of the swimming pool. On the right, a light-filled dining room with abundant new windows awaits, perfect for entertaining guests. Through the dining room, the oversized eat-in kitchen beckons. Adjacent to it is a cozy dining nook & a well-appointed coffee bar. A dedicated laundry room off the kitchen adds to the convenience. The family room invites relaxation with its lower seating area & another wood-burning fireplace. Sliding doors open onto the private backyard. Ascend the wide stairs to discover 5 spacious bedrooms, including the primary suite which features access to a private deck, ideal for enjoying moonlit evenings. Minutes away, residents can access a private beach, perfect for kayaking on Long Island Sound. Additional highlights include a detached 2 car garage, ample driveway parking. Metro North 7 mins drive with direct fast access to Manhattan: Paradise!
