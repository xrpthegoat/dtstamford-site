About this home

Impeccably maintained, stunning classic colonial, tucked away on a private road in one of Greenwich's most sought-after enclaves. A combination of traditional New England architecture with spacious interiors designed for comfortable everyday living and entertaining. Enter in through a magnificent, sun filled double height entry. Generously scaled rooms, hardwood floors, multiple entertaining areas and an open floor plan filled with natural light. Large kitchen and family room. Primary suite, one ensuite bedroom and one Jack and Jill with double sink. Finished third floor for office, den, or guests, plus an unfinished attic for storage. Fabulous finished lower level which can be used as an additional family room, gym, or playroom. Large 2 car attached garage. Expansive lawns, mature landscaping, and a magnificent pool offer a private spa like setting that enhances the home's retreat-like atmosphere Quiet, wooded surroundings and elegant curb appeal. A balance of privacy and convenience, with access to top-rated Greenwich schools, country clubs, equestrian properties, and commuter routes to New York City. Minutes to downtown Greenwich, shops and restaurants, beaches and parks!
