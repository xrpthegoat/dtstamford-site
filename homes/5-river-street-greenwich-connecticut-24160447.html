About this home

Renovated rental located at the end of a quiet street, offering a spacious open layout and a bright 3-season sunroom. The home features newer energy-efficient windows and a great setting with views of the Byram River. The primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet with a built-in system. The finished lower level adds valuable living space and walks out to the backyard. It includes a laundry area with washer and dryer, along with additional space that can be used for a home office, gym, or storage. A Murphy bed in one of the rooms provides added flexibility.
