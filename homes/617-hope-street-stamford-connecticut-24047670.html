About this home

Spacious 2-Bedroom Townhouse in Prime Stamford Location - Walk to Train & Shops Welcome to 611 Hope Street #617 in the desirable Springdale neighborhood of Stamford, CT. This recently renovated 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath detached home offers the feel of a single-family home, complete with a private 2-car garage. Built in 2002, this home is flooded with natural light and features a fantastic layout. The spacious living and dining areas boast high ceilings, ideal for entertaining, while the eat-in kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, light cabinetry, a quartz countertop, and a step-out balcony perfect for grilling. The master suite offers a walk-in closet and an en-suite bath with a double vanity. The second bedroom is equally bright and has its own full bath, providing plenty of space and privacy. Additional highlights include: Central air conditioning Central vacuum system Security system 700+ sq. ft. of extra storage in the lower level, including garage space and a laundry room Energy-efficient windows Custom-fitted closets, pull-down attic access for extra storage, and quiet, comfortable living This prime location is a commuter's dream, just a short walk to the Springdale Metro-North train station, restaurants, shops, schools, and more. With easy access to downtown Stamford, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, you'll have everything you need at your fingertips. *** HIGHEST AND BEST DUE MONDAY 9/23 7pm***
