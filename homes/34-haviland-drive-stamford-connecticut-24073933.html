About this home

A Hidden Gem! Step into this stunning colonial, perfectly set on a peaceful cul-de-sac with 1.68 acres of scenic beauty, complete with stone walls, patios, and a tranquil babbling brook. This spacious and sun-filled home blends timeless charm with modern convenience, offering grand-sized rooms, elegant details, and an effortless flow. Go inside-it's so much bigger than it looks! The oversized living room features a fireplace, gleaming hardwood floors, and French doors leading to a heated glass porch. The gourmet kitchen boasts quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a sunny dining nook with a French door opening to a stone patio-perfect for indoor-outdoor living. The formal dining room has a bay window and charming corner cabinets, while the cozy den offers a gas fireplace. The expansive family room impresses with a Palladian window, skylight, and deck access. A full bath completes this level. Upstairs, you'll find four generous bedrooms and two baths, including a serene primary suite with large windows, a sitting area, and a spa-like bath with a jetted tub and separate shower. Extras include a security system, generator hookup, and gorgeous hardwood floors. Don't miss this private retreat with breathtaking views-schedule your showing today!
