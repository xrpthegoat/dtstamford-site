About this home

Welcome to Marvin Beach! This charming Colonial beautifully blends classic New England character with the relaxed lifestyle of coastal living. Main level consists of Living room, dining room with dry bar nook, kitchen, 1st of 3 full bathrooms and a main level bedroom. Second floor consists of the Primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings, full bath ensuite, and double closets. Two additional good sized bedrooms with ample closet space and full hall bath. Enjoy a private yard, covered back porch, one-car garage, and full partially finished basement, offering additional bonus finished space. Freshly painted throughout. Take advantage of access to three beautiful beaches and a welcoming beach association that hosts holiday celebrations with neighborhood gatherings throughout the year. When the day winds down, enjoy breathtaking sunsets over Long Island Sound, just steps from the end of your street. Experience the charm, community, and coastal lifestyle Marvin Beach offers.
