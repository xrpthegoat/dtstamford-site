About this home

Welcome to 10 Holly Lane, a custom modern Colonial built in 2019 by Fox Hill Builders nestled in one of Darien's most sought-after neighborhoods. This exquisite residence offers the perfect blend of elegance and comfort. Its 10' ceilings on the first floor allow natural light to stream throughout the home making it an ideal sanctuary for discerning homeowners. Boasting approximately 6,012 square feet of meticulously designed living space, this home features 5 generously sized bedrooms and 5 well-appointed en suite bathrooms with 2 half baths providing ample room for family and guests. Situated in a tranquil and prestigious area of Darien, the property offers peace and privacy while being part of a vibrant community. The home is conveniently located within walking distance to Darien HS and Middlesex Middle School, shopping centers, dining establishments, and recreational facilities. With easy access to major highways and public transportation, commuting to nearby cities is seamless. Experience the epitome of luxury living at 10 Holly Lane. This property is a testament to refined taste and thoughtful design, offering a lifestyle of unparalleled comfort and sophistication.
