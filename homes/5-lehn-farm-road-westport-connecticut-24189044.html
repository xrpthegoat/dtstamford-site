About this home

In the owners' words... We moved to this home looking for an escape from city life and a place to grow our family. It has offered us all of that and more. Every inch of the home serves a purpose, creating a space that feels intentional and complete. The thoughtful size and open layout means we are never far from one another. We can prepare meals in the kitchen while watching our little ones play in the family room. It truly brings a family together. With an abundance of windows, the living area draws the outdoors in and floods our home with natural light. We love enjoying a cup of coffee in the breakfast nook while looking out at the yard and appreciating the beauty of each season. Outside, we all spend countless hours exploring the spacious yard - racing around the house, crossing the bridges, searching for frogs in the brook. This is a home that lets us embrace nature. As much as this home feels like a private retreat, we love the convenience of its central location and being able to get anywhere within Westport, from town to beaches to schools, in a few minutes. Over our years here, this house became a home, a truly special place to grow our family.
