About this home

Perched high on the bluff of Long Neck Point, this 6680sf custom-built brick estate offers you unmatched privacy and panoramic water views, all in this captivating natural environment where land and sea converge in harmony. Set on 1.38 acres with 175 feet of shoreline, the home enjoys 180-degree views over Long Island Sound, with main floor rooms capturing the stunning scenery and the ever-changing water. The home opens with a dramatic marble foyer and circular staircase. Graciously scaled rooms feature 9-foot ceilings, an oversized arched window and sliding glass door walls that flood the interiors with light. A long flat backyard, with a 20 X 40 ft concrete pool, slopes gently toward the water, leading to a private dock. The home offers five bedrooms, a luxurious primary suite with its own wing, dual bathrooms and two large walk-in closets. Indoor 38x6 ft lap pool in a separate glass room with sliders to the garden and sea. Separate in-law apartment has a private entrance, kitchen, and sitting room. The lower level has a great room with fireplace, home office with built ins, gym, and a newly renovated pool bathroom and dressing room. Three-car garage, a new slate-style DaVinci roof, new furnace, new A/C units, 45 " Viking cooktop, new oil tanks, full property landscaping and a partially finished attic with cedar closet. This is a rare opportunity to own a truly special waterfront home where every detail was thoughtfully designed to elevate daily living.
