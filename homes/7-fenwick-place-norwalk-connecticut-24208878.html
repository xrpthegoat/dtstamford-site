About this home

Space, style and the East Norwalk coastal lifestyle come together at 7 Fenwick Place #2. This exceptionally spacious 4-bedroom, 3-full-bath rental offers a flexible two-level floor plan, sun-filled rooms and an impressive amount of living space in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods. The main living level is bright and inviting, with hardwood floors and an open-concept kitchen, dining and living area designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Three bedrooms are located on this level, including an expansive primary suite with multiple exposures, a private full bath and a remarkably large walk-in closet/dressing room that provides incredible storage. Two additional bedrooms, another full bath and a conveniently located full-size washer and dryer complete the main floor. The entry level adds even more versatility, offering a fourth bedroom, full bath and separate den/home office making it an ideal setup for guests, working from home, a playroom or additional living space. Enjoy a private side patio for outdoor dining and relaxing. Easy access to Long Island Sound, Calf Pasture Beach, Norwalk Cove Marina, waterfront parks, recreation and neighborhood restaurants, while East Norwalk Metro-North is in walking distance and I-95 make commuting equally convenient.
