About this home

Welcome to 2 Scofield Place, a charming waterfront beach cottage in Westport's coveted Saugatuck Shores community. Offered furnished or unfurnished, this exceptional rental is available as a summer rental through Memorial Day for $6,500/month or annually for $5,250/month. Nestled along picturesque Harbor Road, this 2-3 Bedroom, 1 Bath home is filled with classic New England charm and showcases breathtaking Long Island Sound views from nearly every room. Step outside to direct water access and embrace the ultimate coastal lifestyle-kayak to Cockenoe Island, paddle up the Saugatuck River into downtown Westport, or enjoy waterfront dining at the iconic Black Duck. Recent updates include new Pella windows, fresh interior paint, newer second-floor carpeting, a partially renovated Bathroom, and a newly paved two-car driveway with a one-car garage. Both Bedrooms feature spacious walk-in closets, while an additional room is ideal as a Home Office, Nursery, or small third Bedroom. The enclosed heated porch adds approximately 120 square feet of year-round living space. Enjoy access to Compo Beach, Longshore Club Park, award-winning Westport schools, Metro-North, I-95, shopping, dining, and everything that makes Westport one of Connecticut's premier coastal communities.
