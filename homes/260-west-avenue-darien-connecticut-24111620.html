About this home

Move-in ready and filled with natural light, this beautifully renovated raised ranch offers nearly 2,500 sq ft of versatile living space on an expansive, fenced corner lot. The main level features a seamless open-concept floor plan designed for effortless entertaining. Relax by the fireplace in the sun-drenched living room or cook in the chef's kitchen, complete with a center island and professional-grade appliances. The adjacent dining area with sliding glass doors to a private back deck overlooking the vast yard-ideal for outdoor dining and gatherings. Three bedrooms and two full baths complete the main floor. The bright, walkout lower level offers exceptional flexibility, making it an ideal setup for an in-law suite, guest quarters or multi-generational living. It includes a generous family room, a fourth bedroom and full bath, a dedicated laundry room, and adaptable open space suited for a home office, gym, or playroom. Perfectly situated just steps from Noroton Heights train station, dining, and shopping. An exceptional value offering space, convenience, and turn-key living.
