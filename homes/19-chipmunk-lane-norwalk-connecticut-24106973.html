About this home

Tucked away on a quiet West Norwalk cul-de-sac and set on a beautifully landscaped half-acre, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and flexibility-right on the Darien border. The heart of the home is a stunning, light-filled great room that flows effortlessly into the gourmet kitchen with gas range, stainless steel appliances and casual dining area. A large granite island anchors the space, offering ample prep space and seating. Skylights, walls of windows, and sliders to the deck invite the outdoors in, creating a bright and airy space perfect for entertaining or everyday relaxing. Just beyond, a charming living room with a fireplace and bay window is set apart by elegant French doors. Upstairs, you'll find three comfortable bedrooms with hardwood floors, including a primary suite featuring a marble walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms share a full hallway bath with a tub/shower combination. The lower level adds versatility with a bonus room ideal for an office, playroom, or den-plus a dedicated laundry room and half bath for added convenience. Step outside to a spacious deck with a beautiful stone wall, overlooking a lush back and side yard with garden. This is a special opportunity to move right in and enjoy everything the West Norwalk location has to offer.
