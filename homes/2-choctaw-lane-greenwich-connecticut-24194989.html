About this home

Wonderfully updated family Colonial in private King Merritt, set on 1.39 acres in Greenwich. Offering nearly 5,300 square feet of living space, this 4-bedroom, 4-full-bath home features a spacious, light-filled layout with multiple living areas ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen serves as the heart of the home, while a large private office with beautiful custom built-ins provides an impressive work-from-home setting. Generously proportioned rooms, multiple fireplaces and a three-car garage add to the home's appeal, while the beautifully situated property offers exceptional privacy.
