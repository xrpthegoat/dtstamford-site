About this home

Location, Location, Location! Perfectly located in the Hindley Blue ribbon school district, this beautiful Colonial is warm and welcoming from the moment you step through the front door. The formal living room featuring a wood-burning fireplace, is complimented by a formal dining room and cozy office. Steps away, at the heart of the home, the spacious kitchen seamlessly flows into the family room, boasting a wet bar and another wood-burning fireplace. Adjacent to the kitchen is a bonus room ideal for a playroom or additional home office. A convenient half-bath completes the main level. Upstairs, are the home's four bedrooms and three full bathrooms. The primary suite impresses with two large walk-in closets and a luxurious bathroom featuring a jetted tub, steam shower, and radiant heat floors. Not to be missed is the finished lower level, complete with a full bathroom and home gym. Outside, a stunning stone patio with a built-in grill sets the stage for perfect entertaining. Located on one of Darien's most desirable streets, and close to schools, trains and beaches this home is a must see!
