About this home

Located on a quiet private lane just two blocks from Compo Beach, this newly built coastal residence at 22 Appletree Trail offers a rare combination of modern craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and effortless beach living in the heart of Westport. Spanning approximately 4,791 sq ft of living space, the home blends timeless architectural elements with refined contemporary finishes. White oak floors, soaring ceilings, and custom Italian cabinetry create a warm and sophisticated interior. The chef's kitchen features L'Ottocento cabinetry, Thermador appliances, and Compac Quartz surfaces, complemented by a fully equipped butler's pantry with a second dishwasher and wine refrigeration. An open-concept family room with a sleek linear gas fireplace flows seamlessly to the covered outdoor terrace overlooking the heated pool and landscaped grounds-perfect for both quiet evenings and entertaining. Upstairs, vaulted bedrooms include fully furnished custom closets. A sun-filled lounge opens to a private rooftop deck with a gas fireplace and seasonal views of Long Island Sound. The primary suite offers a private terrace, dual walk-in closets with custom Italian millwork, and a spa-inspired bath designed for relaxation. Set moments from the shoreline while offering easy access to Manhattan via train, this newly completed residence presents a rare opportunity to enjoy modern coastal living in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods.
