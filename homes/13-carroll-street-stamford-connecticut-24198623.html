About this home

Updated 2bedroom, 2.5bath, three-level townhouse in the heart of Springdale! The main level features a mudroom with coat closet and a bright, open-concept kitchen, living and dining area perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar, pantry and plenty of storage. Just off the kitchen, enjoy access to an oversized deck, ideal for BBQs, dining al fresco or simply relaxing outdoors. A powder room completes the main level. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms with ample closet space and a full bath. Hardwood floors flow throughout the main and upper levels. The finished lower level offers even more flexible living space with a bonus room perfect for a home office, playroom, gym or guest space, along with a full bath, laundry area and additional storage. Ideally located just 0.6 miles from the Springdale Train Station and close to fantastic restaurants, shopping and everyday conveniences. Move right in and enjoy all that Springdale has to offer!
