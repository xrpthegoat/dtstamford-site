About this home

West Norwalk Condo - One-Level Living in a Park-Like Setting. Enjoy the perfect blend of convenience and tranquility in this desirable 2-bedroom, 2-bath condominium nestled within a beautifully maintained 20-acre community in coveted West Norwalk. This spacious one-level home features a private deck overlooking lush greenery and offers views of the community pool, creating a peaceful retreat just minutes from major roads, shopping, dining, and highway access. Two outdoor parking spaces provide added convenience. Residents enjoy exceptional amenities, including a clubhouse, 2 pools, scenic pond, and walking trails that wind through the property's bucolic grounds. The HOA handles all exterior maintenance and landscaping, allowing for truly low-maintenance living. Whether you're relaxing on your deck, taking a stroll through the picturesque grounds, or enjoying the community amenities, this home offers an ideal combination of comfort, nature, and accessibility in one of Norwalk's most sought-after locations. One or more of this property's owners is a real-estate licensee.
