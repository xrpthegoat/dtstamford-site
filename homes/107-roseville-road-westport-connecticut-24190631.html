About this home

Sweet Cottage with In-Ground Pool on a lovely 1-acre property in Westport. Fabulous location, close to everything! This peaceful 3 Bedroom, 2 1/2 Bath home feels worlds away from the hustle and bustle, while being just minutes to Downtown Westport, Compo Beach, Longshore, and major commuter routes. Enjoy a dip in the pool, then relax on the expansive backyard deck and listen to the birds. You'll love the indoor-outdoor lifestyle and entertain with ease, with access to the deck from every room on the main level. A charming arched entrance leads to a gracious Living Room with cozy wood stove, vaulted ceiling and hardwood floors. The Dining Room connects the two sides of the house, with a wall of windows and French doors leading to the deck. The Kitchen allows room for a kitchen table for casual meals, and leads directly to the spacious, light-filled Family Room with vaulted ceilings, skylights and windows on three sides. There's a Half Bath and Laundry on the main level. Upstairs, the Primary Bedroom Suite is a restful retreat with Full Bath with tub and shower, a fireplace, and tree-house views to the backyard. Two additional Bedrooms and a Full Bath complete the second floor. Fantastic opportunity to enjoy the home as an end-user or build your dream house! Please do not enter property without an appointment.
