About this home

Nestled in the desirable town of Westport and conveniently located near the Merritt Parkway, this charming Cape Cod-style home offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and modern updates. The home features four bedrooms and two and a half beautifully updated baths, providing plenty of space for everyday living. The main level offers a welcoming living room and kitchen layout ideal for hosting family and friends, creating a warm and inviting setting for gatherings. The finished lower level adds valuable living space with a half bath and laundry area-perfect for a playroom, media room, or home office. Outside, enjoy a partially fenced yard offering room to relax, garden, or entertain, while the one-car garage provides added convenience and storage. With its classic Cape charm, thoughtful updates, and commuter-friendly location, this home is a wonderful opportunity to enjoy all that Westport living has to offer.
