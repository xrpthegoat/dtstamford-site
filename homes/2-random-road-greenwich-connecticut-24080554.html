About this home

Welcome to this exceptional and completely renovated 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom masterpiece, perfectly embodying the ultimate beach lifestyle. This 5,300 sqft home is situated in the coveted Village of Old Greenwich and less than a mile from the beach, train, shops and top-rated schools. Enjoy the perfect serene escape just 45 minutes from NYC. Sun-drenched interior featuring 5-inch white oak floors, oversized windows, and soaring 10-foot ceilings. The formal dining room, fabulous eat-in kitchen, formal living room with a graceful marble-surround fireplace, and an office/den with a coffered ceiling create the perfect settings for memorable gatherings. The chef's kitchen is a true highlight, featuring Blue Star appliances, a 10-foot Taj Mahal natural stone island, a built-in bar with a wine refrigerator and beverage cooler, and a stylish coffee bar for your morning rituals. Exceptional comfort and design continue with the luxurious primary suite, complete with a radiant heat spa-like bath and walk-in closet. The finished lower level is perfect for a home gym and family room and wine cellar.
