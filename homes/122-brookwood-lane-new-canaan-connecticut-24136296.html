About this home

Build your home in one of New Canaan's most desirable and picturesque settings. Set on over two acres of gently rolling land with mature trees and exceptional privacy, this special homesite is just minutes from town, train to NYC, and walkable to the Country Club of New Canaan. Part of the former star-studded estate on Brookwood Lane, the property is located along a bucolic and quiet dead-end lane that epitomizes tranquility and convenience. Unlike a typical land offering, buyers have the opportunity to collaborate with an experienced builder/design team to help streamline the process. Options may include building a fully custom residence or working from thoughtfully developed architectural concepts tailored specifically for the property and setting. Part of a limited collection of estate homesites that will ultimately define one of New Canaan's most compelling settings. Select homes and additional offerings within this Brookwood Lane development will be introduced over time, creating a cohesive and highly curated residential setting. The homesite has already been approved for a six-bedroom septic system, and electric utility infrastructure has been installed, providing an increasingly rare level of readiness and reducing uncertainty for future development. An open canvas in a rarely available ultimate location.
