About this home

Charming, four-bedroom, sun-filled Mid-Ridges Cape home is situated in a premium location - close to schools, shopping and transportation. The home features a large living room with wood floors, leading into a recently renovated eat-in kitchen. Two generous size bedrooms and a full bath complete the first floor, and the second floor has two additional bedrooms and a full bath. There is a two-car detached garage and a spacious, level rear yard and deck, perfect for entertaining. Lovingly cared for with many updates, such as a new roof on the home and garage, some new windows, a repaved driveway and Mitsubishi A/C units to name a few. Move right in!
