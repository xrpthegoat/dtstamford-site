About this home

Tucked on a private lane just off Nod Hill and Whipstick Roads, this home is a hidden escape - where life slows, and every day begins with a sense of calm. The approach is quintessential New England: a winding, scenic drive that opens to rolling topography, timeless stone walls, and a classic 4BR colonial gracefully set into picturesque surroundings. Inside, the home unfolds with warmth and intention. Sunlight pours through thoughtfully updated spaces with craftsmanship evident at every turn; a gorgeous eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home and the flow from room to room is seamless and easy. Custom millwork, refined moldings, solid doors, updated bathrooms and beautifully integrated built-ins combine quality and function. No detail is overlooked. The connection to the outdoors is constant and captivating. A stunning family room wrapped in expansive windows invites natural light, with wildlife and the changing seasons always in view. Evenings are best spent on the wraparound deck, while the property itself becomes a backdrop for cherished moments - from winter sledding down the neighborhood hill to summer days spent exploring along the footbridge out front. Life on the private lane offers a true sense of community - where neighbors become friends and impromptu gatherings happen with ease. A property defined by its space and natural surroundings, this home is set in one of Wilton's most peaceful neighborhoods.
