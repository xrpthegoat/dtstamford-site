About this home

Private and peaceful three-bedroom Contemporary with expansive deck and level backyard set back on one well-landscaped acre off a coveted Westover cul-de-sac. This sunlit, multi-level home features an inviting 2,427 sq. ft. interior with vaulted ceilings punctuated by skylights in gracious entryway and striking living room lined with a wall of custom bookcases; formal dining room, eat-in kitchen with back porch access, laundry/mudroom room and half bath. Step down into a relaxed family room with sliding door deck access and adjacent seating area with wood- burning fireplace. Spacious primary suite with bath, deck overlooking the picturesque setting and walk-in closet; two comfortable bedrooms and full hall bath comprise the upper floor. Spacious lower level and attached two-car garage.
