About this home

Nestled on a serene cul-de-sac in the sought-after Old Hill neighborhood, this exquisite 5-bedroom, 4.1-bath home sits on 1.4 acres of beautifully lush, mature landscaping. Ideally located, you're just minutes from town, schools, the train, beaches, Earthplace, and the YMCA, blending convenience with tranquility. Stepping into the heart of this home is a bright, modern kitchen and breakfast room, seamlessly flowing into an inviting family room featuring a cozy fireplace-perfect for gatherings. A stunning stone fireplace creates a charming focal point in the spacious living room, while the large dining room is ready to host memorable get-togethers. Three of the five spacious bedrooms feature ensuite baths, ensuring comfort and privacy for family and guests alike. Generous walk-in closets throughout provide exceptional storage. As a bonus, the finished lower level adds versatility, offering space for recreation, a home office, or a gym. This home also includes a generator, irrigation system, all new windows & additional insulation and convenient city water, all providing peace of mind. Embrace the elegance, warmth, and natural beauty of this remarkable home and savor peaceful pond views in this coveted setting in a location that's truly unmatched. Complete list of special features and upgrades are in the documents.
