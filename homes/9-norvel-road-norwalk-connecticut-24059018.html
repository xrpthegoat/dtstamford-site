About this home

Welcome to 9 Norvel Rd. This beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath Ranch in Norwalk's desirable Broad River neighborhood is move-in ready and full of charm! Enjoy hardwood floors throughout, central air, spacious living and family rooms, and a newly remodeled kitchen featuring quartz countertops, subway tile backsplash, and high-end stainless steel appliances. The primary bedroom includes a convenient en suite half-bath, while the main level also features laundry for added comfort. The partially finished basement offers the perfect space for a rec room or home office with outdoor access through the garage. Step outside to relax or entertain on the wood deck overlooking a large private, fenced-in backyard with a cozy firepit. Conveniently located near Route 7, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, with parks, dining, and shopping just minutes away. Don't miss out-schedule a tour today and make this beautiful home yours!
