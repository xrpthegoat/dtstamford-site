About this home

Experience the ease of move-in ready living in this beautifully reconstructed home, privately situated on a nearly three-quarter acre retreat in desirable Richmond Hill. Enjoy exceptional privacy as your new home sits nestled back from the road, perched on an elevated lot offering a wonderful sense of space and views above the surrounding neighborhood. Completely renovated and modernized with today's discerning buyer in mind, the home welcomes you with a traditional entry leading to a spacious, sun-filled eat-in kitchen. Imagine preparing meals on the stunning quartz countertops with all brand new appliances. The first floor also features an elegant formal living room, where oversized glass windows flood the space with light, and a beautiful stone fireplace serves as a warm and inviting focal point. Adjacent is a comfortable family room, complete with a slider door that opens to your exterior patio - the perfect spot for entertaining guests or enjoying al fresco dining. Just a few steps down, discover a convenient mudroom with laundry facilities and direct access to your two-car garage. This level also offers invaluable flexible space with two additional rooms, ideal for a home office, children's playroom, or a personal home gym. Upstairs, you'll find three well-appointed bedrooms. The primary suite provides a private sanctuary with its own en suite bathroom, while the two additional bedrooms share a tastefully designed hall bathroom.
