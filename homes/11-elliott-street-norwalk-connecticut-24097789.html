About this home

Nestled a short stroll from the shoreline and just one mile from the Metro-North station and I-95, this classic New England colonial offers timeless charm and everyday convenience. This rare 4-bedroom, 2-bath home features high ceilings, abundant natural light, and hardwood floors throughout. Elegant moldings and spacious rooms create a warm, inviting feel with flexibility for modern living. The landscaped yard with accent lighting offers a private retreat perfect for outdoor entertaining. Enjoy central air, natural gas heating, and a vibrant lifestyle in the sought-after Marvin Beach community with access to three private beaches, neighborhood events, and the MBA Yacht Club. Walk to Calf Pasture Beach, Taylor Farm dog park, and Ludlowe Park's pickleball, tennis, and basketball courts. South Norwalk's shops, dining, and entertainment are just minutes away. A perfect blend of coastal charm, community, and commuter convenience.
