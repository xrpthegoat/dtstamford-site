About this home

Dramatic Contemporary Colonial all newly painted interior with soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, sun-drenched large rooms with great open floor plan. Eat-in kitchen has high-end newer Bosch double convection oven & newer cooktop & new frig & newer washer/dryer. Huge living room with wood-burning fireplace, high ceilings, skylights & windows abound! Separate formal dining room & separate family room create easy flow for living & entertaining. Private deck off the dining room allows for quiet relaxation over morning coffee, evening cocktails & BBQing over-looking 1.6 peaceful, quiet, private acres surrounded by beautiful property & stonewalls. Upstairs invites you into the primary en-suite bedroom with 1 walk-in closet plus 2 other closets, some w/built-ins & bath w/whirlpool tub, walk-in shower, glass door & double sinks. Additionally there's an office/computer/library area on the upstairs landing w/separate upper loft. Partially finished lower level provides more lifestyle options, i.e. exercise gym/recreation area/extra office space & storage. Central air plus 2-car garage. Minutes to schools, shopping, commuting, beaches. Fantastic rental home! Owner may consider pets - Ask. New lower level carpet to be installed. New freshly painted color on front door too!
