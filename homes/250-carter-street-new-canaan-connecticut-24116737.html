About this home

Convenience & sophisticated style is what you get with this ultra-chic 6 bedroom, 8.5 bathroom masterpiece perfectly sited on a picturesque 2 flat acres with 20x40 pool and private gate entry. Built with the best finishes In mind, you'll enjoy 10ft ceilings, flexible open floor plan, private office with French doors, formal rooms that flow seamlessly from one to another, and the ultimate dream kitchen featuring a gorgeous island with 2" countertop/ waterfall edge, chefs appliances, two dishwashers, custom walk-in pantry, wet bar, dramatic windowed breakfast area that opens to the family room overlooking the fabulous terrace and pool with spa. Exquisite millwork, wide plank floors, custom staircases, and cubie built-ins shows meticulous attention to detail. Absolutely spectacular primary bedroom with fireplace, vaulted ceilings, huge dual closets and spa inspired bathroom that will take your breath away featuring a 8ft x 4ft curbless walk-in shower, soaking tub, 10ft floating vanity, and heated floors. (All bedrooms have en-suite bathrooms). Gorgeous mudroom with 2nd laundry and elevator that travels from bedroom level to the finished basement. This home has all the features for today's discerning buyer including Smart Home technology, Crestron lighting system, state of the art mechanical room, full house generator and setting the new standard in energy efficiency. Just Minutes to New Canaan's shopping and dining, Train Station, schools, fields, and Merritt Pkwy.
