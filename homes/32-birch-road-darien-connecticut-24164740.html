About this home

Stunning 4-bedroom, 4.1-bath Colonial on .77 acres completely reimagined & renovated by Nordic Custom Builders offers exceptional craftsmanship & modern luxury throughout. Gracious layout features formal living & dining rooms with fireplace & French doors opening to expansive bluestone patio & pool. Spectacular office showcases vaulted shiplap ceiling, marble wet bar with wine refrigerator & French doors to backyard. Custom moldings throughout. Chef's kitchen beautifully appointed with Thermador appliances, Sub-Zero refrigerator & freezer, custom cabinetry, banquette seating & an additional wet bar with wine fridge & ice maker. French doors lead to screened porch with removable plexiglass panels for warmth, ideal for effortless indoor-outdoor living. Sophisticated family room features oversized windows, custom built-ins & fireplace. Integrated audio system runs throughout the home. Luxurious primary suite lined with custom built-ins offers oversized walk-in closet & marble bath with steam shower & heated floors. Three additional bedrooms with custom closets & two marble baths with heated floors complete 2nd floor. Finished lower level includes a full bath and two large flexible spaces for gym, playroom, media room or fifth bedroom. Step outside to enjoy a meticulously landscaped lawn with new Gunite pool, hot tub, & pool house. Every detail in this home is new - all windows, doors, 10" white oak floors, HVAC/furnace, cedar roof, irrigation, landscaping/lighting & more!
