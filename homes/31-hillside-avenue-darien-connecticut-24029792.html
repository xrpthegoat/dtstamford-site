This privately sited 1940s home on a professionally landscaped property has the perfect space to enjoy family and memory making times. Large eat in kitchen with beautiful cabinetry, formal dining and living rooms and a grand family room with high ceilings! Two BRs and full bath complete the first floor with an additional 3 BRs and a full bath up. This home is in pristine condition and ready to welcome its new owners.
