About this home

Experience luxury living in this meticulously updated 4,105 square foot contemporary residence, quietly nestled at the end of a serene cul de sac in North Stamford. Renovated to perfection, the bathrooms feature porcelain floors and walls, exuding a sleek and sophisticated ambiance. Indulge in the luxurious rainfall shower heads, while the master shower goes above and beyond with body jets and a multi-feature shower head for a spa-like experience. Quartz countertops adorn the solid maple wood vanities, offering both durability and elegance. Additionally, the bathrooms boast color-changing backlit mirrors, enhancing the atmosphere and adding a touch of modernity. The chef-inspired kitchen showcases quartz countertops, a matching backsplash, and top-of-the-line appliances, perfect for culinary enthusiasts and entertainers. Solid maple wood cabinets with soft-closing doors and dove-tail joints provide ample storage in the kitchen, while the hardwood-floored spaces throughout the home are enhanced by color-changing recessed lights, adapting to any mood or occasion. With 5 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, multiple balconies framing picturesque wood views, and added conveniences like a two-car garage, this home epitomizes luxurious living while maintaining close proximity to amenities, top-rated schools, and lush parks.
