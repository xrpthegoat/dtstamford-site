About this home

Welcome to 45 Marva Lane, a beautifully appointed Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac street offering the perfect blend of elegance & modern convenience just a stone's throw to shopping/schools/Nature Center/hiking trails w/easy access to downtown & train. Walking through the front door, the grand entryway w/double staircases sets the stage. The spacious EIK w/high-end appliances (Viking gas range/oven, wall oven & warming drawer, Sub-Zero refrigerator & 2 Jenn-Air dishwashers) flows into the formal dining room making entertaining a breeze! The oversized Great Room w/cathedral ceilings & gas fireplace opens to a large deck, perfect for BBQs & outdoor gatherings. The living room w/a 2nd gas fireplace & powder room complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite offers 2 walk-in closets & a spa-like bath w/jetted tub, separate shower & water closet. 2 more spacious bedrooms share a full bath w/laundry. The finished lower level offers endless possibilities, including a playroom, 2 bonus rooms perfect for a gym or office & ample storage w/access to the 2-car garage. The large backyard has plenty of room to play while a new stone wall in front enhances the property's curb appeal. Smart home features including Nest locks/thermostat, Ring camera, security system & sprinkler system provide ease right from your mobile device. Propane heat, C/A & solar panels complete the package. With its prime location, spacious yard & exceptional amenities, this is the perfect place to call home.
