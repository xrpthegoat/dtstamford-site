About this home

It's all about location, and this one checks that box beautifully!! Sitting at the end of a secluded cul de sac in the Wolfpit neighborhood, this updated ranch is turn-key and ready for its next chapter! Step onto the covered porch and enter the expansive living room with wood burning fireplace, oversized picture window and view of the dining area. Updated chef's kitchen with center island, storage and built-in appliance cabinet to keep the granite countertops clutter-free! Stainless steel appliances include Viking gas stove and hood and Bosch double oven. Gorgeous wood cabinets and a view of the landscaping outside will make being in the kitchen a joy, not a job! Primary Bedroom has its own private bathroom. 2 additional bedrooms share an updated hallway bathroom with Bain Air tub/shower. Bonus Room can be used as a family room, home office or gym. Additional Bonus Room can easily become a 4th bedroom, Home Office or first-floor laundry room. Sliders to patio off of dining area allow ample sunlight to flow into the home. Patio is a relaxing place to enjoy a cup of coffee or glass of wine and the professionally landscaped yard. Gas hook-up outside for grill! Newer Roof (2023), Central Carrier A/C (2016), New Hot Water Heater (2025), Energy Kinetics System 2000 Furnace, Underground irrigation system and Shed are included in the sale! Evenings bring amazing views of sunset that pour into this home's oversized windows and create a vibe of peace and tranquility!
