About this home

***Call for Offers: May 12 @ 7PM*** Do you dream of a country setting but still want the in-town lifestyle, paired with a truly design driven renovation? 190 West Lane brings it all together. You get all the in-town conveniences of Stamford, train to NYC, restaurants, shopping, major highways, and more, while still enjoying a private, country-like setting. Inside, it hits right away, a clean, modern organic feel with warm finishes, wide plank flooring, and a layout that actually works. Every detail here feels custom. The kitchen is clean and well thought out, with thoughtfully designed cabinetry, a walk-in pantry, and a layout that flows easily into the rest of the house. The living room has a fireplace with built in bookshelves that actually add something. Off to the side, a separate family room with vaulted ceilings and large windows brings in natural light and connects you to the outside. Upstairs, the primary suite is thoughtfully done, with custom closets and a bathroom that feels elevated without trying too hard. The second bedroom is also an ensuite, giving you flexibility that's hard to find. The additional bedrooms each have their own character, with wallpaper and soft, high quality carpet. There's also a mudroom right off the garage for easy living. The lower level adds usable space with a playroom, laundry, and direct access to the garage. Outside, the fully fenced yard is level and private. Not just renovated, reimagined for today's homeowner.
