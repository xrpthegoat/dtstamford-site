About this home

Embrace the sheer delight of effortless living in a completely re-envisioned and renovated property that lives like a new construction. Feel like you are on your very own farm (no farm hands needed!) with first level living in a sun-filled modern home. Award-winning developer sets a new standard with personality and sophistication offering custom finishes throughout. Enjoy this mini country estate as a wonderful weekender or year round retreat for the lover of life, not afraid to stand out from the broad canvas of neutral pallets. Custom finishes make spaces and places for all to partake in work, play and repose. Exceptional details make this quintessential Connecticut farmhouse a no-brainer offering a new roof on all three buildings, new septic, new town water hook up, new HVAC, new windows, new kitchen, new baths, new flooring, newly rebuilt stone walls, and new pool coping and liner. Heated party barn and adorable Zen Den add substantial usable square footage with the potential to finish the two level barn into an accessory dwelling, office, play space, meditation space and more. Perfectly positioned in a tranquil setting with Smart Home technology adding ease, convenience, entertainment and sustainability features. This elevated enclave caters to a variety of lifestyles and is remarkably commutable to NYC, while being just minutes away from Westport's amenities. Come find your JOY!
