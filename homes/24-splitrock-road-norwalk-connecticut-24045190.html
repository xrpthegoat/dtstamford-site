About this home

Embrace serene water views and vibrant coastal birdlife through a wispy willow in this beautifully updated home, move-in ready and waiting for you! The spacious open floor plan invites tranquility and light, creating an ideal backdrop for relaxation and entertaining. Step into the newly updated kitchen featuring 2024 appliances, quartz countertops and a stunning AGA stove, a true culinary gem valued at over $32K. Enjoy seamless flow from the kitchen to the pantry, dining area, and living room, making hosting a breeze. A generous utility room and large easily accessible two-car garage provide ample storage and convenience. Step out onto the spacious deck overlooking a fenced yard with waterfront access to the tidal basin-perfect for launching your kayak to explore the coastline from Village Creek to Wilson Point and beyond. Upstairs, be dazzled by the lounge room that showcases more expansive views of the tidal basin and an occasional passing boat. Here you'll find an intricate wood patterned wall that adds character and charm. The upper floor also includes a primary suite, two additional bedrooms, and another bath. Village Creek is a unique waterfront community that enjoys its own private sandy beach, marina, ballpark, tennis courts, dog park and access to eight public Pickleball courts just outside the entrance. Residents rave about the very friendly and peaceful environment. Minutes to train and SONO with its vibrant nightlife, dining, aquarium & the Collection Mall.
