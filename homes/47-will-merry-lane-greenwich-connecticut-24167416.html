About this home

Situated on over two private acres in the highly desirable Mid-Country Greenwich area, this sophisticated 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath brick Colonial is a move-in-ready home designed for comfortable living and effortless entertaining. A welcoming foyer introduces the home's spacious layout, leading to both the living and family rooms. The generous family room, featuring a fireplace, flows seamlessly into the formal dining room. The updated, oversized eat-in kitchen opens to a large deck, creating an ideal setting for indoor-outdoor gatherings. A flexible bonus room-perfect as a sunroom, den, or home office-offers sliding doors that open to the expansive, private backyard. The home includes two primary bedroom suites - one on the main level and another upstairs -offering versatility for guests, multi-generational living, or an au pair. Bright, sun-filled interiors, hardwood floors throughout, and a fully finished basement add to the home's comfort and appeal. Additional features include a three-car garage, beautifully updated bathrooms, a whole-house generator, and a fully fenced backyard. Just minutes from the amenities of Greenwich, this exceptional property offers a rare blend of privacy and convenience.
