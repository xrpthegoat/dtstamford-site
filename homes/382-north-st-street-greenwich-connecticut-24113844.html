About this home

Exceptional commanding luxury estate under development in prime mid country location. The 2.39 acre gated & elevated site includes a 13,326 SF main house with 6 bedrooms / 8.3 bathrooms across 4 levels of custom crafted living space with indoor parking for 7 cars - plus an outfitted 800 SF luxury pool house with vaulted ceilings, heated floors, stone fireplace & full bathroom overlooking a large terrace, 50 ft. pool with integrated spa & sundeck plus a sprawling lawn area with sunken fire pit & Bocce court. Designed by the SOHO Group, this striking transitional home with soaring ceilings & tremendous light is perfectly layered in clapboard, hand chiseled fieldstone & oversize glass. A 3-stop elevator plus an amenity-filled lower level complete the offering. Est. completion date: Dec. 2025
