About this home

Where Modern Architecture and Nature Become One. Architect Peter Leventhal designed this residence as his own home, set on 1.21 professionally landscaped acres. Soaring ceilings and walls of windows bring the outdoors in, while the raised setting keeps it private above lush, level gardens. A dramatic staircase anchors the main floor, with fireplaces inside and out. A second-floor gallery connects 3 bedrooms, including a primary suite with sitting room/office, walk-in closet, balcony and updated bath. 2.1 baths, lower-level media room, and an oversized double-height 2-car garage with mudroom. Stone walls, green giant arborvitae, balconies and a terrace. In the highly sought-after Bailiwick Association, minutes to Glenville, Greenwich Ave, schools and Metro-North.
