About this home

Great opportunity in Stamford's desirable Harbor Point area, offering a prime location near the waterfront, Stamford train station, restaurants, shopping, and I-95. This two-family home features 3 bedrooms in each unit, gas heat, hardwood floors, and spacious layouts. The first-floor unit shows well, while the second floor offers an opportunity to update and add value. A walk-up attic provides additional potential, along with extra space in the basement and an additional full bath. A solid opportunity for an owner-occupant or investor looking for a multi-family property with upside in one of Stamford's most convenient locations.
