About this home

Located on a quiet cul-de-sac, this home offers a beautiful blend of thoughtful architecture + effortless everyday living. If you've been searching for a residence that balances impressive entertaining spaces with cozy, intuitive retreats, you've found it. The heart of the home flows seamlessly from formal rooms to casual living areas. The kitchen is the main hub, flanked by a library/office that offers a quiet workspace without feeling disconnected from the pulse of the house. For those who love to host, the butler's pantry-complete with a wine fridge-connects the kitchen to the dining room for seamless service during dinner parties or weekend gatherings. With four finished levels of living space, there is plenty of room for everyone to find their own corner of comfort. The primary suite serves as a sanctuary at the end of a long day, offering the ideal space to unwind. Step outside to discover a perfect back yard setting. Whether you're dreaming of a vibrant summer garden, a sprawling lawn for outdoor activities, or a serene patio setup, this lovely outdoor space is ready to become your private oasis. Living here means enjoying the very best of the Westport lifestyle, from the vibrant downtown shopping + dining to the coastal amenities. This location also places you within the Long Lots Elementary district, where the community is excited about the brand-new school facility. Expertly maintained and ready for its next chapter, this home offers a lifestyle of ease and grace.
