About this home

In this wonderful Old Greenwich home, you will find it has been beautifully maintained, freshly painted & is move in ready. It offers a bright sunny main level w/ an open floor plan w/ great flow. An impressive living room, dining room and family room w/ a fireplace & built in shelving, a half bath, laundry & 2 car garage. A sunlit stairway leads to the 2nd fl to the large primary suite along w/ 3 good sized bedrooms & full hall bath. The finished lower level consists of a playroom, exercise room, office / bedroom & a full bath along w/ walk out access to the outdoors. An oversized deck is perfect for al fresco dining & overlooks the large flat backyard. The home is generator wired. Equipped w/ propane for gas cooking. This home is in excellent condition & in a great location.
