About this home

Welcome to 262 Mountain Road, a beautiful Cape-style home set on two peaceful acres in desirable North Wilton. Inside, you'll find hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and a bright eat-in kitchen with generous counter space and large windows overlooking the expansive backyard. Step out to the spacious deck - perfect for morning coffee or summer gatherings. The cozy family room features a wood-burning fireplace and built-ins, while a first-floor bedroom with a nearby full bath offers flexibility for guests or a home office. Upstairs are three comfortable bedrooms and a refreshed full bath. The finished walkout basement adds extra living space for a playroom, gym, or media area. Outside, enjoy mature trees, a vegetable garden, and a fire pit - a private retreat just minutes from Caraluzzi's Market, Route 7, and Wilton Center. A wonderful blend of comfort, charm, and convenience awaits at 262 Mountain Rd. Offer Deadline, All offers are due Monday, October 13, 2025, by 6:00 PM.
