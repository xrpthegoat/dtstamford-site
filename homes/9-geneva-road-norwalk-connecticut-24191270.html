About this home

Step into a home that feels like a private resort-where wellness, comfort, and style come together in one unforgettable space. Perfectly positioned in prime West Norwalk, near the New Canaan and Darien borders, this Contemporary Ranch offers a seamless blend of luxury and ease. Inside, the open layout features a beautiful living room with fireplace, an oversized family room, a dedicated office, main level laundry, casual dining areas, and a dramatic dining room ready for dinner parties and celebrations. The thoughtfully designed floor plan includes 2 primary suites, a sunroom, a poolside lounge with an extra refrigerator for effortless indoor-outdoor entertaining, and an oversized 3-car garage. A full basement offers exceptional versatility with a partially finished area, a private sauna retreat, and abundant space for storage, a workshop, or hobbies. Outside, your private backyard oasis awaits: a heated saltwater pool, hot tub, and an enclosed herb and vegetable garden. Just 5 minutes to the Merritt Parkway, Oak Hills Golf Course, Wegmans, and all the conveniences of Connecticut Avenue. Floor plans attached.
