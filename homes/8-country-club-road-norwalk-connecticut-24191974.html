About this home

Welcome to this stylish, move-in ready raised ranch offering modern finishes, flexible living spaces, and an outdoor setting designed for everyday living and entertaining. Situated on a quiet residential street, this home welcomes you with attractive curb appeal, manicured landscaping, and a bright, open floor plan designed for today's lifestyle. The main level features newer flooring, recessed lighting, and a seamless flow between the living room, dining area, and beautifully updated kitchen. The kitchen features cabinetry with crown molding, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, wall oven, tile backsplash, breakfast bar, creating the perfect space for everyday living and entertaining. Door in Kitchen leads to a spacious deck overlooking the private, fully fenced backyard. The backyard offers a spacious patio, deck, level lawn, vinyl privacy fencing, and multiple spaces for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply relaxing in a peaceful setting. The finished lower level offers versatile living space ideal for a family room, recreation room, home office, fitness area, or playroom, along with a full bathroom, providing flexibility to suit your lifestyle. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, Metro-North, and major commuter routes, this home offers the perfect combination of comfort, style, and convenience.
