About this home

A piece of heaven in Norwalk. Just move in and enjoy this completely renovated, highly efficient, versatile, well-loved three bedroom home. This gem sits on a beautifully landscaped .85 acre that boasts chic, high-end modern updates, fabulous entertaining on a stone terrace with a built-in grill, fireplace, smoker and shed. This amazing home features an enticing living room with a stone fireplace that seamlessly expands into a striking kitchen with granite counters, Thermador appliances and a commanding island with counter seating. A gracious dining room, walk-in pantry and full bath round out the main floor. A primary suite with walk-in closet, luxurious bath with radiant heated floors, two additional bedrooms and hall bath comprise the second floor. The lower level sports a family room, office, laundry and half bath. There is a heated detached two car garage plus carport with an awesome office and playroom above. This enviable property has an impressive gated entry and completely fenced-in property with ample parking for family and guests. Solar panels will be paid in full before closing. This most impressive property won't last.
