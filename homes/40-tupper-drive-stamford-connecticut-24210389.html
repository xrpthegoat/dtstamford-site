About this home

Welcome to this sun-filled, beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath Cape in Stamford's sought-after Cove neighborhood, where you'll enjoy private beach access and an easy walk to Cummings Park and Beach! Step inside to a fully renovated first floor, with new flooring throughout and a brand new kitchen featuring white shaker cabinets, granite countertops, a mosaic tile backsplash, and a new dishwasher and microwave to go with the stainless gas range and refrigerator. The kitchen opens to the dining room, and French doors connect the large living room to a first-floor bedroom with full-length glass sliding doors providing direct access to the backyard stone patio, a flexible room that has worked well as a home office or playroom. A full bath and laundry are just down the hall. Upstairs you'll find a spacious primary bedroom with two large closets and a cozy window nook, plus another bedroom and a second full bath. Both baths were remodeled in 2024. The home also has central air, solar panels for energy savings and an attached one-car garage. Outside, the fully fenced, level yard has mature shade trees, a fire pit and a storage shed. You're minutes from the train station, I-95 and downtown, and within walking distance of the supermarket and some of the city's best restaurants. Please note that the microwave installation and upstairs paint touch-ups have been completed since the photos were taken. Ready for immediate occupancy!
