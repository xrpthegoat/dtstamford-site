About this home

Welcome to your 5 bed/bath dream residence located in the idyllic Compo neighborhood of Westport. This stunning home offers over 3900 sq ft of modern elegance living space, where sleek design and hand-crafted finishes flourish. The kitchen is a chef's delight with high-end appliances, custom cabinetry, along with a spacious island for your culinary creations and casual family dining. Abundant light-filled home office, dining room, butlers pantry with wet bar, and family room with fireplace open to the kitchen complete the ground floor. On 2nd floor you'll find 3 nice sized bedrooms, plus the luxurious primary suite. There you'll bask in its soaring ceiling, ample closet space, and a true spa-like primary bath experience.The private and finished 3rd floor can serve as a large playroom or 2nd home office.Fully-finished lower level with spacious bedroom/full bath and kitchen/living area offers fantastic opportunities as a family gathering area for viewing sports/movie nights, or a perfect in-law or au pair suite with total privacy. Located 1 minute from Longshore Park public golf/pool/tennis/paddle, and 2 minutes from Compo beach where swimming and sunsets await. Moments from Metro North train for easy/quick commuting, and downtowns wonderful shopping/dining options galore.
