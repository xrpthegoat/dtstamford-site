About this home

This exceptional 2-bedroom, 2-bath first-floor unit with 3 exposures to charming serene courtyards was renovated extensively in 2022 including new flooring throughout and new plumbing and electric wiring in the kitchen and bathrooms. Nestled among lawns and gardens, yet conveniently located just off the main drive, this inviting home features an open floor plan that welcomes both sunrise and sunset. The spacious dining room flows to the shady terrace, perfect for enjoying the outdoors. The kitchen is equipped with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. Additional features include hardwood floors, recessed lighting, onsite parking and an optional garage rental. Set on 12 beautifully landscaped acres, Putnam Park is a tranquil, pet-friendly community adorned with flowering trees, ideally situated in the heart of Greenwich. Enjoy close proximity to Whole Foods, Greenwich Avenue, fine dining, schools, and the train station.
