About this home

Classic Connecticut Colonial beautifully renovated for today's lifestyle! This pristine 4-bedroom, 3.1-bath home offers an inviting open floor plan centered around a fabulous gourmet kitchen flowing seamlessly into a sun-filled family room with custom built-ins. Elegant entertaining spaces include an oversized dining room and gracious living room with fireplace, while a thoughtfully designed mudroom with custom millwork keeps things organized. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite features a marble bath and his-and-hers walk-in closets, creating a private retreat, while two additional beautifully updated bathrooms complete the second floor. The finished lower level, filled with natural light from abundant windows, provides flexible space ideal for a playroom, home office, gym, or recreation room. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the screened porch and deck offer seamless indoor-outdoor enjoyment overlooking the sweeping flat backyard. Stone walls, perennial gardens, and beautifully landscaped grounds complete this picture-perfect family home, ideally located close to town, schools, Waveny Park, and commuting. ***HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY 5PM 6/15/26***
