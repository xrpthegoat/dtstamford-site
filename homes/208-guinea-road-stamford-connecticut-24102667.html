About this home

Pristine and spacious 5-bedroom Colonial for rent near the Greenwich border, perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Recently renovated throughout with fresh interior/exterior paint, brand new bathrooms, updated flooring, kitchen improvements, and electrical upgrades. The classic center hall layout includes a formal dining room, a living room with a fireplace, and a large eat-in kitchen with Corian countertops. Adjacent to the kitchen is a laundry room, a half bath, and a stunning vaulted family room with exposed beams, a ceiling fan, a fireplace, and windows on three sides. A two-tier deck runs the length of the house with access from multiple rooms, ideal for indoor-outdoor living. Upstairs is a serene primary suite with a steam bath, plus three generously sized bedrooms and a full hall bath. The finished lower level offers a flexible den with a fireplace, a full bath, and a 5th bedroom with a private entrance, perfect for guests, an au pair, or a home office. A walk-out terrace overlooks the beautifully maintained property with mature trees. A mudroom connects directly to the 3-car garage. Located minutes from Greenwich Country Day School. Landscaping included!
