About this home

Tucked away in the heart of Cranbury, 1 Wakerobin Road is a corner-lot gem offering .68 acres of space and charm. This 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home blends modern style with everyday convenience, featuring a new roof, sleek quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and soft-close cabinets in a beautifully designed kitchen. The finished lower level adds versatility, perfect for a home office, gym, or entertainment space, while central air keeps things comfortable year-round. A 1-car garage and prime location just minutes from Cranbury Park, local shops, SoNo Mall, and the Merritt Parkway make this home a rare find in a sought-after neighborhood!
