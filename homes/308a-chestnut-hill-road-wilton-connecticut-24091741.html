About this home

Charming, renovated Carriage House in South Wilton with private driveway and two floors of bright living space. So convenient to the towns of Wilton and Westport and to major highways and trains. Such a peaceful place, freshly painted and ready to move-in! Imagine preparing meals in this spacious Kitchen with eat-in area, newer stainless steel appliances, wood-grained tile floors and a picture window to the side yard. When relaxing or entertaining, the Living Room area holds lots of options on how you may want to utilize the space. Also, an attractive half bath is conveniently located on the main floor. Upstairs, gleaming hardwood floors and abundant windows providing natural light welcome you to the bedroom and an office/additional room to use as you'd like. The full bath with tile floor, tub/shower with white subway tile and pretty vanity completes the second floor. Adding to the convenience is the washer & dryer at the top of the stairs, so laundry is an easy task. Plenty of parking on the wide gravel driveway which compliments the Carriage House. Doesn't get better than this! Come for a visit and see what 308A Chestnut Hill has to offer!
