About this home

Location, Location! Beautiful West Norwalk. This 3 Bedroom, 1 Bath Ranch home on 1.05 Acres Abutting Oak Hills golf course, is waiting for your Decorating Ideas to shine this gem into a diamond. Hardwood Floors throughout, Living room with a Fireplace, Eat In Kitchen, 3 Bedrooms and 1 full Bath. Less than an hour to NYC and enjoy all Norwalk has to offer. SONO, Beaches, Shopping, Parks, Concerts, Maritime Aquarium, Restaurants and more.
