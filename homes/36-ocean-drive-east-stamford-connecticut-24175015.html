About this home

Nestled in the desirable Shippan neighborhood of Stamford this beautifully renovated home effortlessly blends modern elegance with classic charm. The bright and updated kitchen boasts quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, and a convenient pantry. The spacious main level flows effortlessly, offering a warm and inviting living room with a cozy fireplace, a dining room perfect for entertaining, a first floor bedroom, and a renovated full bathroom. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms offer plenty of natural light and closet space, accompanied by another fully updated bathroom. The lower level features a large entertainment area, a versatile room that can be used as a bedroom, office or gym, and a third renovated full bath. Enjoy outdoor living in the expansive, flat backyard ideal for entertaining, gardening, or relaxation. A convenient one-car garage and two parking spaces in the front add everyday convenience. Experience coastal charm and comfort in this exceptional home!
