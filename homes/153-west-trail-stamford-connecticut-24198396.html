About this home

There's a particular quiet that settles over 153 West Trail Road - the kind that comes from thirty years of a family calling this 4,000-square-foot classic Connecticut Colonial home, and grounds so thoughtfully planted the outside world falls away as you turn up the drive. Inside, the house unfolds unhurried across three finished levels. Sunlight pours through oversized windows and pools across the eat-in kitchen's island, where morning coffee turns naturally into open French doors and a deck that seems to float in the trees. By evening, the living room fireplace pulls everyone back inside. Upstairs, the primary suite feels less like a bedroom and more like an escape - a dressing room, a marble bath with soaking tub, and a private balcony overlooking the gorgeous backyard. Three more bedrooms (one ensuite), and an optional office/nursery complete the second floor. Downstairs, a spacious rec room and a separate gym give the lower level a life of its own, while outside, the wooded, private grounds unfold into an 800-square-foot Ipe wood deck, a bluestone patio with outdoor kitchen, and a separate pergola-covered gathering spot. Cared for year after year because it was loved - this rare, turnkey home is ready for its next lucky owner.
