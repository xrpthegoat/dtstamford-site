About this home

Welcome to 192 Knickerbocker, a beautifully maintained 3-family home nestled on a prime lot in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood. Perfect for owner-occupants, investors, or those seeking additional rental income. The main floor features a charming 1 bedroom, 1 bath unit with hardwood floors, a bright kitchen, and private access-currently leased at $2,200/month. On the second level, you'll find another similar 1-bedroom unit with separate entrances, currently rented month to month for $1,800/month. The top floor, unit 3, is also a 1 bedroom with an updated kitchen, currently rented month to month for $1,800/month. Lastly, this building features a versatile unfinished basement and usable outdoor space with a large stone driveway. All units have their own electric meter, plus a 4th owners meter. The hot water and heat is fueled by natural gas, currently one boiler, and one tankless water heater serving all units, but three gas meter hook ups are in place and ready for conversion to individual heating systems, with individual gas meters. Located just off Hope Street, you're within walking distance to shops, restaurants, the Springdale train station, and other everyday conveniences. Enjoy the charm of a residential community with the perks of city living minutes to downtown Stamford, highways, and public transportation. Don't miss your chance to own a rare 3-family in this part of Springdale.
