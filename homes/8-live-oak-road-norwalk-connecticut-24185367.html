About this home

Beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home set on a picturesque 1-acre property surrounded by mature trees, offering exceptional privacy and a peaceful connection to nature. Filled with natural light, the open-concept floor plan is designed for both comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. The stunning kitchen features custom cabinetry, a large center island, and beautiful views of the backyard, screened porch, and inground pool. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining area and spacious living room with a fireplace, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Both the kitchen and family room lead to a beautiful screened porch with dual ceiling fans, providing the perfect space to relax, dine, and enjoy the changing seasons before stepping onto the patio and taking a refreshing dip in the pool. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite features a custom walk-in closet, soaking tub, oversized shower, and double vanity. Three additional spacious bedrooms share an updated full bathroom. The lower level offers a generous family room, mudroom with custom built-ins, and flexible space ideal for a home office, gym, playroom, or recreation area. Enjoy beautiful lawn areas, mature landscaping, and a private backyard retreat while being within walking distance of Cranbury Park with its scenic trails and recreational amenities. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, schools, and major commuting routes, this move-in-ready home offers the perfect blend of luxury.
