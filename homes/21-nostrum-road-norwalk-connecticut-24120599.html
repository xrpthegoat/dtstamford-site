About this home

This well-maintained Cape holds tons of potential, as well as value! Located in the heart of Norwalk, close to I-95 and Rte 7 with a 45 minute commute to NYC. Enjoy the peace and quiet that this neighborhood offers! Hardwood floors are brand-new throughout the first floor and both bedrooms on second level also have hardwood floors underneath the carpets. Gas stove, gas heat and thermopane windows add to the home's cost efficiency. Family Room with a fireplace is a great room to gather for celebrations and holidays! Step outside onto the patio which overlooks the expansive, level backyard! Oversized, one-car garage has plenty of space for a workshop or studio. Unfinished dry basement also allows a possible guest room or man cave! The possibilities for this property are endless, but the house is a true find in today's market!
