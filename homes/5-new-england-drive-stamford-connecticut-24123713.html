About this home

Welcome to 5 New England Drive-a spacious and thoughtfully designed ranch set on a level one-acre corner lot in North Stamford, offering the rare advantage of natural gas and public water. The beautifully maintained grounds provide a true retreat with a tennis court, in-ground pool, courtyard, covered outdoor dining area with pizza oven, and a fully fenced backyard. Inside, you'll find 5,474 sq ft of living space with 5 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms. Four bedrooms are located on the main level, including a junior primary suite with en-suite bath. At the heart of the home, the chef's kitchen opens to a glass atrium-style dining and living area, filled with natural light and views of the property. The expansive primary suite features cathedral ceilings, two walk-in closets, spa-like bath, fireplace, and two private balconies. A walk-out lower level offers a bar, half bath, and a third fireplace-perfect for entertaining or extended living. Additional highlights include a large three-car heated garage, oversized driveway, new roof including plywood (2024), and a fully replaced septic system (2012). Ideally located, 5 New England Drive is just minutes to downtown New Canaan, Talmadge Hill train station, and the Merritt Parkway. You do not want to miss out on this property that truly offers it all!
