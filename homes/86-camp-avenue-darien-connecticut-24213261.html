About this home

Enjoy the space, comfort, and tranquility of this inviting ranch, tucked away in a peaceful setting with charming stone walls and a picturesque stream. A long circular driveway welcomes you to this lovely home with attached garage. Inside, the spacious living room features soaring cathedral ceilings and a fireplace, creating a warm and inviting focal point. A large wraparound deck extends the living space outdoors, perfect for entertaining or relaxing amid the serene surroundings. The generously sized eat-in kitchen offers plenty of room for cooking and gathering. The primary bedroom features an en-suite bath, while two additional generously sized bedrooms share a full bath. The full basement offers an additional 900 square feet of versatile finished space, ideal for a home office, exercise room, playroom, or potential bedroom, along with ample closet storage. With 2,955 square feet of total livable space, this home offers room to spread out, work, play, and relax in a tranquil natural setting.
