About this home

Spacious and impeccably maintained Colonial tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Westport's most desirable locations, just moments to town, schools, trains, beaches, and everyday conveniences. Offering four finished levels, this beautifully updated home features 5 bedrooms, 4 full baths, and a powder room. Designed for both comfortable daily living and effortless entertaining, the home showcases sun filled interiors, hardwood floors throughout, and timeless architectural details. The updated kitchen opens seamlessly to the inviting family room with fireplace, while formal living and dining rooms offer additional space for gathering and entertaining. A spacious mudroom with first floor laundry adds everyday convenience. Additional highlights include two fireplaces, a brand new roof, whole house generator, and irrigation system. Set on professionally landscaped grounds with inviting outdoor living spaces and a private level backyard with room for a pool. Exceptional opportunity in a sought after Westport location.
