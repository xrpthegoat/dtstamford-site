About this home

This conveniently located apartment has been completely renovated from top to bottom. The two level unit offers an open floor plan, plenty of sunlight, hardwood floors throughout, one spacious bedroom with additional space for an office/second living space, and a brand new kitchen and bathrooms. The apartment is minutes away from everything Greenwich has to offer including transportation, shopping, schools, the beach and the town pool. There is a pulldown attic that can be used for additional storage. Tenant can utilize an exterior patio off the kitchen for outdoor space. Parking for 2 cars is permitted in the driveway.
