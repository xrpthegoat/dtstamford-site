About this home

Tall and stately, this beautiful home stands proudly at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac. Filled with warmth, charm, and character, this residence welcomes you with a sun-filled living room featuring a cozy fireplace - perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A versatile den or home office adds both style and function to the main level. The beautifully remodeled kitchen is a chef's dream, boasting stainless steel appliances, a spacious pantry, and modern finishes. Step outside to an oversized deck overlooking the fully fenced backyard - an ideal retreat for gatherings, gardening, or playtime with your furry friends. Centrally located and a commuter's delight, this home offers easy access to the train station, shopping, and is just a short drive to scenic Calf Pasture Beach. A rare combination of comfort, convenience, and timeless appeal - ready to welcome you home.
