About this home

Set at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Bulls Head neighborhood, this 4-bedroom Colonial offers space, flexibility, and exceptional convenience. With 2,024 square feet of living space plus an additional 1,100 square feet in the unfinished lower level, this home presents a compelling opportunity to update and add value over time. Freshly painted throughout, it features central air, gas heat, and city water and sewer for straightforward, low-maintenance living. The flexible layout includes the possibility for a main-level bedroom suite, with a full bath, which is well-suited for guests, a home office, or in-law potential, while upstairs offers four bedrooms, a full bath, and a half bath ensuite to the primary. A brand new deck with access from the kitchen and family room overlooks the backyard, providing an ideal setting for everyday use or casual gatherings. Inside, the family room has a vaulted ceiling with a fireplace and sliders to the deck. A formal dining room and spacious living room complete the space. Conveniently located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, downtown Stamford, and Metro-North express trains to NYC in under an hour, the location offers an easy commute and access to a wide range of local shops, parks, restaurants and grocery stores. A solid opportunity to personalize a well-located home in an established neighborhood!
