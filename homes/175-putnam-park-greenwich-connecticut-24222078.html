About this home

Discover this beautifully remodeled 1-bedroom residence in downtown Greenwich's coveted Putnam Park, offering an exceptional blend of modern updates, comfort and convenience. The gorgeous new kitchen features high-end stainless steel appliances, complemented by a beautifully renovated bath, new recessed lighting and gleaming hardwood floors refinished with a water-based finish. Additional improvements include new electrical, many new windows and built-in wall A/C units. The spacious living room opens to a private balcony, while the generous dining area features custom chair rail molding. Ample closets, an in-unit washer/dryer and a separate large storage unit add everyday convenience. Set on 13 beautifully maintained, park-like acres, this pet-friendly community welcomes pets with no breed or size restrictions-a rare find in Greenwich! The $779 monthly HOA includes property taxes, insurance, heat, hot water, landscaping and snow removal. Enjoy ample free on-site parking, with optional garage parking through the HOA for $85/month. Ideally located just moments from Metro-North for an effortless NYC commute, Greenwich Avenue's renowned shopping and dining, Whole Foods and Bruce Park. A wonderful opportunity for move-in-ready living in the heart of downtown Greenwich!
