About this home

This stunning 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom colonial home is located in the highly sought-after Stamford North End community of Davenport Farm. The property sits on a spacious 1.11-acre lot and features a beautiful gunite pool, perfect for summer relaxation. This 4830 sq. ft. home boasts 4 levels of exceptional living space, including a two-story entryway, a fabulous 3rd-floor office with skylights, and a sun-splashed finished basement with direct pool access and fireplace. The home's fantastic floor plan includes a designer kitchen, a dreamy family room with a woodstove insert, and a formal living room with fireplace. The primary bedroom suite is a true oasis, featuring incredible natural light and ample space. The home's exceptional craftsmanship is evident throughout, with hardwood floors, crown moldings, 9' ceilings, and oversized windows. Additional features of this home include a finished basement, a double deck, and a beautiful walkway to the impressive entrance. The property is also equipped with a security system and extensive deer-proof landscaping. This home is just minutes from Stamford's nature center, golf courses, exceptional private and public schools, High Ridge Road shopping, multiple train stations, Merritt Pkwy, and more. The property is in the peaceful and serene Davenport Farm, an exceptional community perfect for those who appreciate stunning homes, spacious living, gorgeous landscaping, and a convenient location.
