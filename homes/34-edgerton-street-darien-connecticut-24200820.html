About this home

Prepare to be charmed by this impeccably maintained, oversized Cape that is sure to delight. Spanning 2,867 sq ft of light-filled living space with 3/4 bedrooms & 2.5 baths. The main level is designed for effortless flow and versatility. An impressive great room w/vaulted ceiling and an ornamental fireplace add a touch of elegance. The functional kitchen is outfitted with high-end stainless appliances-including a Bosch refrigerator, Jennair electric range, and LG dishwasher. Enjoy casual & formal dining in the two dining spaces. A cozy den w/hardwood floors & custom built-ins and a flexible office space provide the perfect setup for remote work or as an additional bedroom. Upstairs, the primary suite boasts a full bath, a spacious walk-in closet, and an additional double-door closet. Two more well-proportioned bedrooms share a full hall bath along with the convenience of an upper-level laundry room. The outdoor living space is as impressive. Situated on a lush .34-acre lot, the private, fenced-in backyard is a landscaped oasis with composite deck & bluestone patio for outdoor relaxation. Practical features include central AC, a 1-car garage and a large driveway for extra off-street parking. Easy access to shopping and dining at Darien Commons and Heights Crossing, and just moments from the Noroton Heights train station. Available October 1, 2026. Estate sale arrangements set for September 12 preclude interior picture taking at this time but come see this beauty now!
