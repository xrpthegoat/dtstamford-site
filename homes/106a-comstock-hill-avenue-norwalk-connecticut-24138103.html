About this home

Welcome to 106A Comstock Hill Avenue, where classic elegance meets modern luxury. This stunning 7-bed, 5.5-bath colonial offers over 5,700 sq ft of refined living space plus a 3,300 sq ft finished lower level for endless possibilities. Step inside to an airy open layout with soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwoods, and sun-filled rooms that flow seamlessly from one to the next. The chef's kitchen features premium appliances, a massive center island, and generous storage, ideal for entertaining or family gatherings. The main-level primary suite is a private retreat with a spa-inspired bath, while additional bedrooms offer en-suite or Jack-and-Jill layouts for ultimate comfort and privacy. The lower level invites your imagination-create a home theater, gym, or playroom with room to spare. Outside, relax or entertain on a spacious deck overlooking a serene, landscaped yard. A three-car garage and accessory apartment add exceptional value and flexibility for guests or multigenerational living. Perfectly situated in one of Norwalk's most desirable areas, this home offers a rare blend of space, sophistication, and functionality.
