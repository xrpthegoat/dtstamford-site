About this home

Welcome to 81 Four Brooks Road. This spacious North Stamford home is filled with character and light and offers the space, layout and features you want for how we live today. This desirable open concept floorplan allows for easy living and entertaining. The stunning kitchen with custom cabinets and overized breakfast bar, quartz counters and stainless appliances will delight the serious home chef. And the elegant livingroom/dining room combo adds to the splendor. Additional living space thru the sliders and steps to the deck overlooking the private, level backyard makes this home a true showplace. Prepare to be captivated with the beautiful primary bedroom suite with abundant custom built wall-to-wall closets and spa-like private full bath with heated floors. An additional bedroom and another full bath complete the first floor. The lower level boasts another bedroom and full bath, den, office and walk-out to lovely outdoor space with ample seating and gorgeous perennial gardens sited on one acre. Nestled in a sought after neighborhood in North Stamford, yet close to everything, (shopping, restaurants, the Merritt parkway and more) this home is ready for you. Make your home ownership dreams a reality!
