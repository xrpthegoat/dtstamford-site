About this home

First time on the market. Builders own home, only the best construction from the highest level of professional contractor. Brick and stone build quality. Effortless one-level living on a coveted 1.01-acre lot, minutes from the heart of New Canaan. This meticulously maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers the single-floor lifestyle today's buyers crave - with expansion potential to grow into a larger home. All just moments from town, train, shops, and dining - a turnkey opportunity for downsizers and growing households alike, in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after towns.
