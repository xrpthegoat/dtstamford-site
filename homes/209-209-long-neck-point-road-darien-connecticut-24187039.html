About this home

This dramatic property offers 200 feet of waterfront frontage with your own private beach in the premier Long Neck Point area of Darien. Once owned by Christopher Plummer of The Sound of Music, the current owners have meticulously renovated and restored this cherished home with the renowned builders, Significant Homes and interior designer, Cindy Rinfret, Rinfret LLC. The architectural integrity has been preserved with comfort and quality for today's lifestyle. A world-class property.
