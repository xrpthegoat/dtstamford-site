About this home

A rare opportunity to buy in the private, gated waterfront Mead Point Association! Set high on 2 beautiful acres, this charming 4 bedrooms/4.1 baths home offers a serene lifestyle in a special community. The main level features multiple living areas, including a cozy living room with fireplace, a formal dining room, a well-equipped kitchen, sunlit family room with fireplace and a wood-paneled executive office with picture window and abundant storage cabinets, perfect for working from home. The first-floor primary suite with luxury bath and walk in closet provides convenience and privacy, making it a perfect sanctuary. The second floor includes three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms, providing comfortable accommodations and an optional 2nd floor primary suite. The expansive outdoor area is the true highlight, offering breathtaking views of the tidal inlet and stunning sunsets. The house offers a glassed-in sun room surrounded by the blue stone patio, allowing you to enjoy the coastal breezes and picturesque water views all year long. The spacious attached two car garage has a separate workshop and additional storage area above. Mead Point Association provides 24/7 guard-gated security and access to a private beach, ensuring peace of mind and exclusivity. Conveniently located near downtown Greenwich, this home offers both tranquility and accessibility. Perfect for those looking to add their personal touch to a well-established property in a desirable location. SH
