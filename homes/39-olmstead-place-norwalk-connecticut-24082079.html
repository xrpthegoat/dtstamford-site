About this home

Nestled at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac, this lovingly maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home is hitting the market for the very first time! Owned by just one family, this move-in-ready gem offers a perfect blend of comfort, character, and charm. Step inside and immediately feel the warmth of a home that has been cared for with pride and attention to detail. With a spacious and functional layout, this residence boasts abundant natural light and timeless appeal. Walk into a spacious open concept living room, kitchen and dining room with a bay window to lookout to your serene backyard. The main level includes three generous bedrooms and full bath with tub, all accented by natural light reflecting off hardwood floors. The finished lower level offers a warm and inviting gathering area around the fireplace. Complete with a bedroom and full bath with steam shower enclosure, heated floor bathroom, laundry room and utility room. The backyard features a Trex deck, mature trees, and is completely fenced. This home offers a perfect retreat, ideal for entertaining or unwinding after a long day. Tucked away in the sought-after East Norwalk neighborhood, this property offers a quiet environment while being just minutes from beaches, parks, shopping, dining, and major highways. This is a rare opportunity to own a beautiful, well-kept home in an unbeatable location. Lower level has potential to be in-law suite or possible Accessory Dwelling Unit.
