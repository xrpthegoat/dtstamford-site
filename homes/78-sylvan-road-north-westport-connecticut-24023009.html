About this home

Step into luxury with this remarkable modern colonial, featuring 5-bedrooms, 6-full bathrooms, and 2 half baths. Meticulously designed and crafted to perfection by Epoch Homes, this home sits in close proximity to major commuting lines as well as downtown Westport. European windows flood the home with natural light. The gourmet kitchen boasts custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, and sleek countertops. Featuring expansive living areas, adorned with tall ceilings and hardwood floors that enhance the sense of grandeur. Indulge in the convenience of a private office and a luxurious guest room with its own bathroom on the first floor of this exquisite home. Each of the five bedrooms in this home provides personal sanctuary, boasting its own ensuite bathroom for ultimate privacy and convenience. The primary suite with a vaulted ceiling and gorgeous oak beams, is complete with a spa-like ensuite bath and a private balcony overlooking the serene surroundings. The second floor features a hidden gem bonus room located over the 3 car garages that could be turned into a playroom, art studio or simply an additional bedroom. The lower level of this home features an open-plan layout with an abundance of natural light, and includes a spacious entertainment room, gym, and ample space for recreation and relaxation. Enjoy seamless entertainment throughout the home with an integrated sound system, which extends from the first floor to the primary bedroom, and lower level and outdoor areas.
