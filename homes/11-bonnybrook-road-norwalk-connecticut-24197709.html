About this home

Welcome to 11 Bonnybrook Road, a versatile split-level home set on nearly 0.8 lush acres in West Norwalk offering 1,606 square feet of main living space plus nearly 700 square feet of additional heated lower-level area. The main level features an open-concept flow with gleaming hardwood floors, a wood-burning fireplace, and a large picture window in the spacious living room that seamlessly connects to a bright eat-in kitchen and dining room. The dining room opens to a private sunroom overlooking tranquil green space-ideal for summer dinners or a cozy den-which steps directly out to the deck, where a staircase leads down to a generous patio, rock garden, and an expansive, level yard functioning as your own private playground. Upstairs you'll find three comfortable bedrooms and two full bathrooms, including a primary suite. The lower levels provide an office, laundry room with a half bath, interior access to the attached two-car garage, and a lower basement level featuring a spacious heated playroom/family room alongside a mechanical room with ample storage. Generous storage throughout, multiple bonus closets, and central air conditioning ensure total comfort in this peaceful, quiet setting. Just minutes from Connecticut Avenue, the Darien Metro-North station, Wegmans, and Costco. Bring your vision to make it your own, or move right in and start enjoying everything this property has to offer-this opportunity won't last!
