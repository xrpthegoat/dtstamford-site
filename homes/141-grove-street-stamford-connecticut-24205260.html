About this home

Forget everything you know about renting downtown. Unit P at Gatewood isn't an apartment - it's a four-level townhouse that lives like your own private home, hiding in plain sight in the heart of downtown Stamford. Hardwood floors run throughout the open main level, where the granite kitchen with breakfast bar and stainless appliances flows straight into a sun-filled living room - and through the sliders, your own private deck for morning coffee or evening wine. Upstairs, the primary suite comes with its own updated bath and a showstopper you won't find anywhere else at this price: a soaring cathedral-ceiling loft with skylight, ready to become your home office, movie room, or private gym. The second bedroom? It has its own full bath too - every bedroom is a suite. Full-size washer and dryer plus serious storage on the lower level. And the everyday luxury that sells this place: 2 covered garage parking under the building with direct entry into your unit - groceries to kitchen without ever touching the weather - includes guest parking for friends. Central air. Pet friendly - bring your best friend. Restaurants, parks, theaters, and shopping are literally steps from your door.
