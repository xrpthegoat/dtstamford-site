About this home

Offer deadline today 2/15/2026 at 6pm. Welcome to 25 Powder Horn Hill Road-a stunning oasis set on 2.79 level, beautifully landscaped acres offering exceptional privacy, sun-filled interiors, and a resort-style backyard just minutes to town. This 4-bedroom, 4-bath Colonial offers 4,144 sq ft with a versatile floor plan and multiple living spaces designed for modern living. A circular driveway creates a gracious arrival. Inside, the slate-floored living room features a cozy fireplace, floor-to-ceiling windows, and French doors that open to the backyard. The dining room is rich in character with hardwood floors, built-ins, and a second fireplace-ideal for entertaining. The eat-in kitchen is wrapped in floor-to-ceiling windows with serene views, and French doors make indoor-outdoor living effortless. Outside, enjoy a stunning pool with attached spa and waterfall, surrounded by mature plantings and generous space for dining and lounging. A large first-floor primary suite with walk-in closet and ensuite bath plus a first-floor bedroom offer flexibility. Upstairs, two vaulted-ceiling bedrooms, two full baths, a den, and an oversized great room complete the home. Other features include central air, a 2-car garage, sprinkler system, shed, and full-house generator. All close to town, train and commuter routes. This home is a must-see-don't miss it!
