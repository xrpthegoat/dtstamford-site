About this home

Tucked on a cul-de-sac in the heart of Long Lots, this light-filled ranch offers the ease of single-level living within a thoughtfully composed floor plan. Well-proportioned rooms and seamless flow create a home that feels both comfortable and refined. The living room, framed by a large picture window, is bathed in natural light throughout the day and anchored by a custom fireplace surround. An open kitchen with granite counters connects effortlessly to the dining room, offering an ideal setting for everyday living and relaxed entertaining. Three bedrooms and two full baths are arranged with privacy in mind. The primary suite includes a spacious bath, generous closet space, and an adjacent office, sitting room, or playroom. Two additional bedrooms with custom closet built-ins and a well-appointed hall bath complete the interior layout. Outdoor living is equally inviting. A flagstone patio extends into beautifully landscaped gardens with dedicated grilling and dining areas, complemented by a fire pit and curated plantings that enhance both privacy and charm. Ideally situated in a central location, 20 Hickory Drive provides convenient access to town, train, beaches, and schools. An exceptionally well-cared-for home offering a quiet, established setting and the ease of a truly connected Westport lifestyle.
