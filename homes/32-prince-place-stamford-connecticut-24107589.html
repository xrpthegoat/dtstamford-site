About this home

Discover this quaint 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home, available for the first time in 44 years! Located in the desirable Hubbard Heights neighborhood, this residence combines historic charm with modern updates. Step inside to find a bright living room with hardwood floors and a formal dining area. The updated kitchen features modern appliances and a cozy center island and the convenience of first-level laundry. The first level is complete with sunroom/mudroom or office off the kitchen, and a large family room in the rear of the home overlooking your spacious backyard, offering endless possibilities for outdoor enjoyment. . The upper level you will find a primary suite with an en-suite bath, ensuring comfort for everyone, in addition to 3 additional bedrooms and a full bath. Do not miss the walk up attic though one of these bedrooms, perfect for additional storage or a creative space. This center hall colonial exudes timeless elegance with classic symmetry, offering tremendous curb appeal that embodies the charm of this historic neighborhood.
