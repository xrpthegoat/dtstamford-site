About this home

Character, charm and LOCATION define this sunny and bright cape colonial on a dead end street in the desirable Springdale area. This tastefully updated home has all the conveniences of today - central air, modern kitchen, newer bathrooms - with the enchanting features of a past era - hardwood floors (second floor original), cozy fireplace, original moldings. Newly remodeled eat-in kitchen with beautiful quartz counters and island with breakfast counter. Newly added half bath with laundry and mudroom on first floor. Newer windows, newer appliances, updated electric. Hardwood floors throughout. Cozy and bright sunroom with skylight and built in desk. Convenient to restaurants, shops, Merritt, I-95, and the Springdale Metro North station. Tiered, private back yard with picket fence, stone fire pit and stone walls.
