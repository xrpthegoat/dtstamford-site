About this home

This is the Wilton home you have been looking for, set on one acre, offering the perfect blend of character and comfort. With 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, this Cape style home is filled with natural light and features hardwood floors throughout. The first and second floor offer great versatility for today's family needs, with flexible living and sleeping areas. The kitchen is a true highlight, boasting quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a seamless connection to the home's gathering spaces. The lower level provides additional finished space to suit your lifestyle. The exterior shines with mature trees and lush plantings that provide both privacy and beauty. The delightful patio is designed for entertaining with friends and family. A separate two-car garage and shed add convenience. With its versatile spaces, modern updates and timeless charm, this home offers endless possibilities for today's lifestyle.
