About this home

INCREDIBLE waterfront 2-family in the heart of East Norwalk! Soak in breathtaking water views from your sizable backyard, two outdoor decks, covered patio, and from all three floors inside the home! - The first floor features a welcoming living room, two spacious bedrooms, and an updated bathroom. The large eat-in kitchen leads directly to a gorgeous sun room with a propane wood stove for year-round usage, and sliders out to the deck overlooking Mill Pond - The finished basement is completely updated, with its own stainless steel refrigerator, tile floors, a separate heating zone, recessed lighting, and a remodeled bathroom - The second floor unit is the sunniest of all, with two skylights, updated vinyl floors, two additional bedrooms, and outdoor access via the eat-in kitchen - The property's two asphalt driveways provide plenty of parking, and the garage offers additional storage. You simply cannot beat the location: walkable to the East Norwalk train station, Vet's Park, and several amazing local restaurants, plus just a few minutes by car to Calf Pasture beach and I-95! A prime location combined with excellent cash flow! Schedule your showing before this unique gem is snatched up!
