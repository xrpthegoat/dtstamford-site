About this home

A Westport treasure, this c.1900 beauty enchants with timeless charm and a magical warmth. Once the homestead of one of Westport's largest onion farms, you may still find one growing wild on the lawn. The wrap-around screened porch is perfect for summer mornings or unwinding at sunset. Inside, sunlit living spaces are filled with original character, and the chef's kitchen offers high-end appliances, wine fridge, and a breakfast nook set in a bank of windows. Listed on Westport's historic inventory (but not registry-restricted), the home offers flexibility for expansion or renovation. Outside, enjoy a large, level double lot on a peaceful cul-de-sac-ideal for lawn games, gardening, or a pool. The detached garage does not currently have living space above, but there's ample room to expand or add a guest cottage, office, or gym. Bordered by stone walls, and just minutes from the beach, train, and town-this is a rare chance to own a piece of Westport history.
