About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in sought-after East Norwalk, this stunning home blends quality craftsmanship with coastal convenience. Just a short walk to the Metro North train, Calf Pasture Beach, and vibrant SoNo, this home offers exceptional access to shoreline living, parks, and dining. Inside, you'll find red oak hardwood floors throughout the main and second levels, elegant crown molding, recessed lighting, and 9' ceilings. The property features a gas fireplace with remote control and first floor wired for surround sound. The finished walk-up third floor impresses with skylights, and plumbing for a wet bat to be installed, -ideal for a media room/playroom or private retreat. A brand-new high-efficiency Carrier furnace and A/C units, new washer and dryer, and new central vacuum system ensure comfort and convenience. Enjoy large windows, custom window treatments, multiple storage rooms, a full basement with 9' ceilings ready to finish, and an oversized 2-car garage. Outside, a flagstone patio, in-ground sprinkler system, and fully fenced yard complete the picture. Walkable to east Norwalk's restaurants and short distance to beaches, restaurants, Shorehaven Golf Club, and more. Sunset views from your front door make every evening unforgettable. This is East Norwalk living at its finest.
