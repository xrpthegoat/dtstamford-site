About this home

Rare Craftsman Colonial in coveted Davenport Farm offers a unique opportunity to own a home rich in character & timeless craftsmanship in this quintessential Stamford neighborhood. Spanning over 5,000 sq ft across three expansive levels, this residence features 3 bedrooms (+1 other) & 4 fireplaces on a private, park-like property. A welcoming foyer opens to a sun-filled living room with a striking fireplace & serene views, ideal for everyday living & entertaining. The gracious dining room with oversized windows flows into an eat-in kitchen with breakfast bar & a charming brick fireplace. The dramatic open-beam sitting room is light-filled with Atrium-style windows, shiplap walls, a built-in bar, & a statement fireplace. A cozy family room, laundry, powder room, & attached two-car garage complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a sitting area, a fourth fireplace, & a spa bath, while two en-suite bedrooms provide comfort & privacy. The finished 1,700+ sq ft walk-out lower level overlooks a nature preserve & offers a playroom, family room, large bedroom or other, full bath, & ample storage-ideal for guests, in-law, ADU or au pair living. Enjoy Stamford Arboretum with gardens & hiking trails, & Stamford Nature Center, plus a diverse selection of nearby restaurants. Conveniently located in lower North Stamford near downtown, beaches, parks, schools, shopping, dining, Metro-North, & major commuting routes. A must see! More pictures & video coming soon!
