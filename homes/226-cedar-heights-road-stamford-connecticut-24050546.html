About this home

Prepare to be wowed by this one-of-a-kind, custom renovated Cape in the highly sought-after Mid Ridges neighborhood. Drive down a hydrangea lined driveway to discover New England charm at its finest, highlighted by beautiful stone walls enhancing the curb appeal. Inside, a show-stopping chef's kitchen boasts a large center island, quartz countertops, designer lighting, & both a range and wall oven to meet every culinary need. An adjoining eat-in area with French doors opens to the front yard, perfect for morning coffee or casual gatherings. Entertain in style in the elegant formal dining room with vaulted ceilings & wall-to-wall windows overlooking the lush back gardens. The oversized front-to-back living room features a wood-burning fireplace, built-ins, and French doors leading to a stone patio and professionally landscaped yard set on .73 private acres. Flexible first floor includes a possible bedroom or family room w/ a renovated full bath. Upstairs features two updated full baths, a primary suite w/ an oversized WIC, 2 additional bedrooms-including one w/ a charming bonus nook. Lower level provides versatile flex space ideal for a playroom, gym, or den. Additional amenities include a 2-car attached garage, ample storage in the basement & attic, & outdoor living spaces designed for entertaining w/ a level backyard, fire pit area, & the signature stonework that enhances the property's beauty. Secluded sophistication near parkway, High Ridge shopping, train & downtown.
