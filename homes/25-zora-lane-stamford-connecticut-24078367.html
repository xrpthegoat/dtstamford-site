About this home

Welcome to 25 Zora Lane, a beautifully updated Colonial-style gem tucked away on a professionally landscaped one-acre lot on a cul-de-sac in North Stamford. With 2,592 square feet, 4 bedrooms, and 2.5 fully renovated bathrooms, this meticulously maintained 9-room home offers space, charm, and improvements throughout. Owned by just one family since it was built in 1964, it is now available for its second owner. The interior and exterior have been freshly painted, and refinished hardwood floors bring a timeless warmth to every space. The main level includes a bright eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, large living room with fireplace, spacious family room, and a dedicated office ideal for working from home. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features its own full bath, a walk-in closet, and an additional closet. Three additional bedrooms and hall bath complete the upper level, offering quiet and comfortable living. Major recent upgrades include a new well pump, septic system mechanicals, tankless water heater boiler, electrical panel, and garage doors. A full, unfinished basement with high ceilings adds storage or future expansion space. Outside, the professionally landscaped yard includes a deck, ideal for grilling, entertaining, or relaxing outdoors. This exceptional home, located minutes from the train and close to parks, trails, shopping, and dining, blends traditional colonial charm with updated features and timeless style. Look no further!
