About this home

Beautifully renovated main-level 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment features hardwood floors, central air conditioning and abundant natural light. The spacious eat-in kitchen comes fully equipped with a dishwasher and built-in microwave and opens to a cozy living room. The bedroom offers two generous closets, and a private same-floor washer/dryer that make everyday living effortless. Enjoy a fenced backyard, perfect for relaxing or gardening. Parking includes a driveway spot plus additional street parking. Directly across from John J. Boccuzzi Park: a 12-acre coastal park with a playground, beach, green space, basketball, and tennis courts. Just minutes to Stamford's vibrant waterfront, downtown dining and shopping, Metro-North, and I-95. The charming village of Old Greenwich is just under 2 miles away. A perfect balance of neighborhood calm and city convenience-don't miss it!
