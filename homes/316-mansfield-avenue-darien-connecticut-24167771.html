About this home

Just in time for summer-this exceptional property offers the ultimate in luxury living and outdoor enjoyment. Imagine relaxing poolside by your new heated pool and spa, surrounded by beautifully landscaped grounds and sweeping views of the golf course. Inside, the home is equally impressive. The expansive primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a private sitting room that opens onto a deck overlooking the pool-perfect for morning coffee or evening unwinding. The suite also boasts a beautifully updated bath and two spacious walk-in closets. The second level is thoughtfully designed with four additional en-suite bedrooms and a versatile playroom, offering comfort and privacy for family and guests alike. At the heart of the home is a stunning new gourmet kitchen, outfitted with top-of-the-line appliances including a Wolf range, two Sub-Zero refrigerators, a separate freezer, and three dishwashers-an entertainer's dream. The lower level is equally striking, featuring an elite gym, a playroom, and abundant open space for recreation and play-perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or staying active without ever leaving home. This remarkable colonial seamlessly blends elegance, comfort, and modern convenience-perfect for making unforgettable summer memories and beyond.
