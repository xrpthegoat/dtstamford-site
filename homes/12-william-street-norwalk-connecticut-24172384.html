About this home

Welcome to this beautiful, meticulously maintained, custom-built home. Walk into an open floor plan with appealing custom woodworking throughout. The living room has a lovely fireplace with built-in, floor-to-ceiling bookcases on both sides. The kitchen has high-end appliances, a farm sink, center island, and floor-to-ceiling custom cabinets. Built-in desk and custom cabinetry are adjacent to the kitchen, a mudroom for coats and shoes, and a half bath. The back door takes you to a peaceful, fenced patio and backyard. The second floor has a primary bedroom with a full bath, double sinks, and a walk-in shower, plus a walk-in closet, and two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a washer/dryer. An additional room for work or play, along with a fourth bedroom, make up the third level. The HVAC system is zoned for three levels, with a propane-fired furnace for heating and electric-powered central cooling. Solar panels installed in January 2025. Leafguard protects all gutters. The house has been meticulously maintained and is convenient to Metro North, parks, and the beach. You will love living here!
