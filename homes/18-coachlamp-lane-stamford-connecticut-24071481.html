About this home

Spacious and bright turnkey colonial located on the western side next to Greenwich. Beautifully renovated home in Westover features hardwood floors & high ceilings. Flexible floorplan has stunning eat-in kitchen, stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops & sliding barn door to dining room and adjacent living room. Fabulous family room has cathedral ceiling, tons of natural light, floor to ceiling brick fireplace & access to deck, pool & level yard. Lovely primary suite has multiple closets & luxe bath. 2 more bedrooms & sparkling second bath on this floor. Lower level boasts playroom,laundry, work & storage spaces. Bonuses: Oversized 2 garages, above ground pool & fenced in yard. Close to hospital, schools, shopping,restaurants & golf course. This convenient location is a huge plus.
