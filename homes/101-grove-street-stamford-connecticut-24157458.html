About this home

Enjoy a prime Downtown Stamford location with rare privacy and tranquility. Tucked away from the main road and overlooking a lush, serene courtyard, this townhouse-style home feels worlds away while remaining just steps from restaurants, shops, and Metro North. This well-maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence features a distinctive multi-level layout where each floor serves its own purpose, creating natural separation between living and sleeping areas and giving the home the feel of a private townhouse while allowing sunlight to move through the space throughout the day. The entry level offers a flexible space ideal for a family room, office, or study with a stylish barn door and convenient half bath. The main living level features an open kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a dining area with refinished hardwood floors, and sliding doors to a private balcony. A bright living room with bay window, hardwood floors, and fireplace creates a comfortable space for relaxing or entertaining. The primary suite occupies its own upper levels, offering a private retreat with a spacious bathroom featuring double vanity, Jacuzzi tub, and skylight. The second bedroom sits on its own level with a full bathroom located nearby. A rare downtown convenience is private garage parking located directly beneath the unit with interior access to the home, making groceries and daily errands effortless in an area where parking is often limited. Red oak hardwood floors were added to both bedrooms
