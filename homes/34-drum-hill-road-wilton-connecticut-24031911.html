About this home

The Carriage House- A sweeping driveway draws you in to this charming home that has been beautifully modernized, while still retaining all of its exceptional character. There is a lovely living room w/fireplace, dining room, updated eat-in kitchen w/outside porch for al fresco dining, spacious family room, fab mudroom & even a secret room over the garage for an office. Potential for nanny/in-law area w/fireplace was created on the 1st floor that is by a kitchenette & partial bth. Upstairs, the primary is oversized and has a great bathroom. A 2nd huge bdrm w/high ceiling is ensuite. Two other bedrooms share a newer bath. Outside is truly spectacular w/heated pool, cabana, & a firepit for S'mores nights. The 2 acre parcel is incredibly private. In the vaulted ceiling garage, a basketball hoop beckons for practicing your free throw or 3 pointers. Close to all Wilton offers its lucky residents, The Carriage House is a world apart. This is a very special offering.
