About this home

Welcome home - a place where cherished memories are waiting to be made! Nestled within meticulous landscaping, this beautiful split-level home is both elegant and practical. Step inside and you're greeted by freshly painted walls that still radiate a sense of newness four years after their application. The heart of the home lies in the kitchen, where granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances gleam. A large island takes center stage, offering ample prep space and also seating for eight guests, perfect for entertaining or casual family meals. When work calls - a dedicated office space provides a quiet retreat where productivity can flourish. And when it's time to unwind, the living room invites you to relax by the wood stove, installed just a year ago, ensuring cozy comfort. For the ultimate in convenience and efficiency, this home is equipped with spray foam insulation in the attic, ensuring optimal temperature control year-round, while the tankless water heater guarantees endless hot water for those relaxing showers. Step outside onto the patio, sheltered by a charming pergola where you can enjoy al fresco dining or grill up your favorite meals. With beautiful landscaping framing the outdoor space, you'll find yourself drawn to the tranquility of the surroundings, whether you're enjoying a morning cup of coffee or hosting a summer soiree. In addition to this amazing property, there is a brand new AC and heating unit just waiting for installation.
