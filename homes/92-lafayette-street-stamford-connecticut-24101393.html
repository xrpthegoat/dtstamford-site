About this home

Prepare to be impressed by this impeccably updated and move-in ready 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhome located in the Glenbrook neighborhood of Stamford. This sunlit townhome offers the perfect backdrop for modern day living. The main level welcomes you with an airy open-concept layout, featuring wide-plank engineered hardwood flooring and an abundance of natural light. The kitchen is a chef's inspired dream-complete with sleek marble countertops, a center island, stainless steel appliances, and shaker style cabinetry-all seamlessly flowing into a dining area and spacious living room with a charming bay window. A chic powder room and a covered private deck with southern exposure complete this perfect entertaining space. Upstairs, two oversized bedrooms offer comfort and serenity, each outfitted with double-door closets and easy access to a beautifully renovated full bath featuring a marble vanity and a tub/shower combination. An additional hallway closet and large attic provides excellent storage. The finished lower level offers incredible flexibility-ideal as a home office, gym, media room, or playroom-tailored to your lifestyle needs. Additional highlights include natural gas forced-air heating, central air conditioning, a private 1-car garage, & 2 reserved parking spaces-ensuring comfort and convenience at every turn. All just moments from downtown Stamford, Glenbrook Metro-North, shopping, restaurants, & parks.
