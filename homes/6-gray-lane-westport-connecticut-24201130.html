About this home

A WATERFRONT offering in one of Westport's most coveted enclaves. This beautifully composed estate is gracefully sited on more than two private, tree-lined acres, encompassing sweeping views of Sasco Creek, Southport Beach, and Long Island Sound while preserving an exceptional sense of seclusion. The home is currently furnished and can be rented unfurnished or furnished. It features direct access to Southport Beach (during high tide, straight from property-inquire). Designed to maximize its remarkable setting, the home captures water views from nearly every room and showcases timeless architectural details including custom millwork, random-width hardwood flooring, three fireplaces, cathedral ceilings, and formal and informal living spaces rarely seen in today's homes. Set amid beautifully landscaped grounds with perennial gardens and expansive entertaining terraces, this private oasis invites alfresco dining beneath breathtaking sunsets and sunrises. The home features five bedrooms including two primary suites, a guest suite, chef's kitchen, great room, sunroom, and more. Located between prestigious Beachside Avenue and historic Southport Village, you are moments from beaches, boutique shopping, acclaimed restaurants, and the enduring charm of Connecticut's Gold Coast. Framed by mature evergreens, flowering dogwoods, mountain laurel, and rhododendron, the property delivers an exceptional combination of privacy, scale, and proximity - just 55 miles from NY. A special offering.
