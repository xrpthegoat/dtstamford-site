About this home

4 Bedroom Colonial + Legal 1 Bedroom Accessory Apartment Rare opportunity in West Norwalk. This well-maintained Colonial offers 4 bedrooms in the main residence plus a separate legal 1-bedroom accessory apartment with its own entrance, full bath, laundry, and private living space. Ideal for multigenerational living, a live-in caregiver, extended family, long-term guests, or a private work-from-home suite. The main home features an updated kitchen, spacious living areas, main-level primary bedroom, central air, deck, patio, and ample off-street parking. Situated on a lightly wooded half-acre setting offering privacy while remaining convenient to shopping, dining, Metro-North, the Merritt Parkway, and I-95. A unique property that provides flexibility many need but rarely find.
