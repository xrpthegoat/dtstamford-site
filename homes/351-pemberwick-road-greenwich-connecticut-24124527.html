About this home

This spacious one bedroom pied-a-terre is in the heart of the Glenville area in Greenwich, CT. This corner unit is bright, private & quiet.Enjoy the open floor plan connecting the kitchen, dining and the living room with a fireplace. Relax on the private terrace overlooking a wooded area. Minutes to downtown Greenwich, Port Chester & MetroNorth stations. Easy access to Merrit Pky/Hutch. A fantastic value.
