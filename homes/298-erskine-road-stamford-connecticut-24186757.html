About this home

This beautiful & modern country home has it all! Privately situated down a country lane, you'll enjoy newer construction featuring a sunny open flow of rooms with hardwood flooring, vaulted ceilings, sliding French doors and patio! An open kitchen with breakfast bar looks on to the living/dining space, lined with windows and enhanced with a stunning stone fireplace & vaulted ceilings. Upstairs you'll find a surprisingly large primary bedroom with room for your home office/exercise equipment, second bedroom & large spa-inspired full bath with separate tub and shower. There are so many places to enjoy the bucolic scenery of trees, rolling hills & birds, come see this very special home. Must have confirmed appointment. Tenant is responsible for lawn maintenance, landlord will manage snow removal.
