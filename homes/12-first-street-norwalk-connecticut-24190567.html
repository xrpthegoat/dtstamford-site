About this home

This property can be described in one word - OPPORTUNITY! First and foremost is the location in the heart of East Norwalk's fine and casual dining area in close proximity to Calf Pasture Beach and Norwalk Harbor. Not in a flood zone. This is an exciting investment opportunity given its multi-family designation in a commercial area. There are many options. Two 2 bedroom apartments provide tenant income. An owner could live in one and rent the other. The property could be converted into a comfortable single family residence. It could be expanded vertically to provide additional apartments. Or it could potentially be used for a commercial venture. A garage is included. The property is sold as is to give an owner their choice of options. Of course diligence on their part is mandatory.
