About this home

Welcome to 28 Houston Terrace, a charming 3-bedroom Stamford home offering a flexible layout and an exceptionally convenient location. The main level features a bright sunroom, a comfortable living room open to the dining room, an eat-in kitchen, a cozy den, a bedroom and a full bath. Upstairs are two additional bedrooms and a hallway with convenient built-in storage. The unfinished basement includes laundry and provides plenty of space for storage. A rare three-car detached garage offers excellent flexibility for vehicles, hobbies, a workshop or additional storage. Ideally situated close to Chelsea Piers, Cove Island Park and Cove Beach, with easy access to downtown Stamford, the Metro-North train station, I-95, Stamford Hospital, restaurants and shopping. A wonderful opportunity to bring your vision so you can enjoy space, convenience and all that Stamford has to offer!
