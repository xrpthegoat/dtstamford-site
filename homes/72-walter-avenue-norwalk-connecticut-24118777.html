About this home

Endless potential awaits. This spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath split-level home with an in-ground pool is full of possibilities and ready for your personal touch. Conveniently located just minutes to shopping, beaches, I-95, and Metro North, this home offers a desirable layout and solid foundation to reimagine as your own. The main level features a welcoming foyer, formal living and dining rooms, and an eat-in kitchen that opens to a sunken family room with wood burning fireplace. A half bath and laundry room completes this level. Upstairs, hardwood floors run throughout, with a primary suite offering a walk-in shower and sliders to a balcony overlooking the backyard and pool. Three additional bedrooms and a full hall bath complete the second level. Potential for laundry on upper level, hooks up in place. The finished walkout lower level provides added flexibility, with space for recreation and a soundproof music room. Outside, the vinyl-lined pool and freshly painted decks create the perfect setting for summer and fall entertaining. Additional highlights include transferable solar panels for energy efficiency, natural gas heat, public water and sewer, and a fire/alarm system. Audio/video cameras on premise. Sold AS IS. This is an Estate sale and subject to probate court approval.
