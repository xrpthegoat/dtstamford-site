About this home

Walk to Beaches, Rail Stations, and GFA from this spectacular Greens Farms Estate. This remarkable home is less than a 10-minute walk to Southport Beach, the Metro North Railroad Station, Post Office, and Greens Farms Academy campus. The 2-acre property offers unequaled privacy bordering 40 acres of Audubon land and has its own renovated pool, tennis court, and guest house. The home received a rare 2011 Westport Local Preservation award and blends historic grace with today's living standards. Generous room layouts, high ceilings, five indoor fireplaces, distributed audio, and a kitchen wine cellar make entertaining easy. All living space is above-grade, allowing natural light to stream in over 130 windows. The separate two-bedroom guest house was completely rebuilt in 2010 and is an ideal home for a nanny or parent. The private grounds include a large patio, fireplace and dining pergola; a beautifully landscaped pool/spa and gazebo, framed by extensive decking and exquisite fencing; a HarTru tennis court and expansive lawn with garden, outdoor shower, and sports fields. This showcase property has been used for fireworks, concerts, and fundraisers. If you are a discerning buyer looking for elegance in a home with the convenience of walking to beaches, trains, and more amenities, don't miss this opportunity to own this piece of Westport history with unparalleled beauty and sophistication.
