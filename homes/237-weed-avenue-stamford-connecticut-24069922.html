About this home

Welcome to 237 Weed Avenue with exceptional views of Holly Pond and beyond to Long Island! The bright, open and flexible floorplan, with living room, dining room and family room offers great space, a fireplace, hardwood floors and access to the sunroom. The spacious kitchen has a large center island, lots of counter space and a built-in dining banquette, perfect for informal dining! Even the view from the kitchen sink is a wow! Upstairs is a terrific primary bedroom suite with sunny sitting room with incredible views, an office, as well as a full bathroom with tub, shower and double vanity. There are three additional bedrooms and another full bathroom on the second floor as well. Out back is a private patio, also with water views, great for outdoor entertaining, as well as a detached and heated two car garage. Just down the street is Cove Island Park, offering beaches, a public marina and ice rink and up the hill is the amazing Chelsea Piers Sports Facility! A terrific place to call home before summer arrives!!
