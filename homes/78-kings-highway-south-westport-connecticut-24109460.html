About this home

Welcome to 78 Kings Highway South - the perfect place to call home in the heart of Westport. This beautifully maintained, move-in ready home offers the ease of one-level living with the added bonus of a spacious, finished lower level. Ideally located just minutes from town, the train, Longshore Club and Compo Beach-perfectly positioned to enjoy everything this vibrant community has to offer. Step inside to find a light-filled living and dining room, seamlessly connected to a bright, updated kitchen featuring newer appliances. The main level also includes two well-sized bedrooms, a full bath, and a lovely primary suite - all with gleaming hardwood floors. The professionally landscaped, private, LEVEL yard is ideal for entertaining, relaxing or enjoying a game of catch on the lawn. An expansive upper deck, off the kitchen, sets the stage for memorable summer evenings. Downstairs, you'll be amazed by the generous lower level, complete with a cozy fireplace, built-ins, beverage center, and sliders to a shaded patio. A private office, laundry area plus half bath, and abundant storage round out this flexible space - ideal for work, play or guests. Additional features include a two-car attached, heated garage with built-in shelving and extra room for storage. New AC in 2023. Come tour 78 Kings Highway South today and discover the comfort, convenience, and charm of your new Westport home.
