About this home

Set at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac and perched high above the neighborhood, 12 Arbor Court offers a beautifully updated 4 bed, 2 bath Cape that balances modern comfort with an easy, flexible layout. Step into a sunlit living room with a bay window overlooking your tiered front yard, flowing seamlessly into an eat-in kitchen with a breakfast bar that keeps everything connected. The kitchen has been thoughtfully updated with new cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and new countertops, then opens to a screened-in porch that feels like an extension of the home, ideal for summer nights, workouts, or relaxed mornings. Two main-level bedrooms provide excellent options for guests or home office, paired with a newly updated full bath. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms include a versatile space currently used as a playroom/media room, along with a second updated full bath with a modern stall shower and new finishes throughout. The basement offers laundry, storage, and a garage currently used as a workshop, adding even more flexibility. Located less than a mile from the South Norwalk train station, SoNo restaurants, I-95, Post Road conveniences and all three schools, this home combines a peaceful setting with unbeatable access. Take a closer look and see how this move-in-ready home quietly delivers more than you expect at every turn.
