About this home

Welcome to this wonderful home on a great location! Walking to this beautiful home you will find a wraparound porch, patio and the deck that is located on the back, everything perfect for relaxation or entertaining. This home has an open concept living room, dining and kitchen. The fireplace that can be used with firewood or electricity. Three bedrooms with ample closet space and a full bathroom complete the main level. From the deck off the kitchen, you can appreciate part of the city's skyline and the beautiful garden blooms. The sunsets are also something that you will enjoy. There is access from the dining area to the patio that connects with the wraparound porch. On the basement there are two bedrooms, a living room and a full bathroom. The laundry and a crawl space for storage are located in the basement. This home is conveniently located to public transportation, close to downtown and highway. The roof and water heater are new, and the driveway was just paved. Don't miss the extra yard area behind the garages.
