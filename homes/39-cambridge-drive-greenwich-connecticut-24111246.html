About this home

Welcome to 39 Cambridge Drive, a stunningly renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom residence located in the heart of Greenwich's highly desirable Glenville neighborhood. From the moment you arrive, you'll notice the brand-new siding and windows that give this home incredible curb appeal. Inside, the main level boasts a fully renovated kitchen with sleek finishes and modern appliances, complemented by newly refinished hardwood floors and sunlit living spaces. The primary suite offers a private oasis with a beautifully updated ensuite bath, while the additional bedrooms provide comfort and flexibility, including one on the lower level ideal for guests or a home office. The fully finished walkout basement offers in-law potential or bonus living space, perfect for multi-generational living or entertaining. With updated electrical, central air, and efficient natural gas, every detail has been carefully considered. Step outside to a private backyard sanctuary - quiet, level, and ready for summer nights or weekend lounging. All of this in an unbeatable location just minutes to parks, shops, Glenville School, and with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and Metro-North, making your NYC commute a breeze. This is Greenwich living at its finest - turnkey, stylish, and perfectly situated.
