About this home

Welcome to 155 N Taylor Avenue, a sun-filled 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath colonial set back from the street with privacy hedges and backing onto a nature preserve. Built in 2000, this 2,770-square-foot home offers thoughtful updates throughout, including a 2015 kitchen renovation with granite countertops, gas stove, Bosch dishwasher, tile backsplash, and bar peninsula opening to a deck with French doors. The large bay window floods the living room with afternoon light. The second floor showcases hardwood flooring installed in 2020. The spacious master suite features a walk-in closet and a luxurious marble bathroom updated in 2018 with a double rain and jet shower, soaking tub, and double vanity. The other 3 bedrooms share an updated bathroom with a rain jet shower system and floor-to-ceiling tile. A huge bonus room adds flexible living space as a possible 4th bedroom. Additional highlights include gas forced air heat (2026), central AC (2026), a gas fireplace, solar panels installed in 2014, and a radon mitigation system. The attached 2-car garage provides direct access, with laundry conveniently located in the tall-ceilinged unfinished basement. Situated on 0.37 acres, this south-facing home offers privacy and tranquility while remaining minutes from SoNo, Costco, Target, Wegmans, and abundant dining and shopping along Connecticut Avenue. Enjoy the low-maintenance condo living with the privacy of a single family home--in a desired West Norwalk location.
