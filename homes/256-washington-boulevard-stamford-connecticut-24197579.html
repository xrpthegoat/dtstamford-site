About this home

A fantastic rental opportunity offering comfort and convenience in a prime Stamford location! This bright and spacious two-bedroom, first-floor end-unit townhouse is just a short walk to the Stamford train station-perfect for NYC commuters-and minutes from shops, restaurants, and the vibrant Harbor Point waterfront. The home features an abundance of natural light, an open floor plan, and hardwood floors in the living room area. Enjoy your own private balcony, an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a large pantry. An in-unit washer and dryer are conveniently tucked away, and two reserved parking spaces.
