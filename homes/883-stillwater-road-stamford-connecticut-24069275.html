About this home

Unbeatable ranch in highly sought after Westover neighborhood. This stunning home boasts over 4,300+ sq. ft. of living space & offers the perfect serene, tranquil setting with waterfront views from almost every room overlooking the Rippowam River. From the moment you walk through the front door of this meticulously maintained home, you'll begin to truly understand the meaning of a hidden gem! The main floor features an inviting foyer, which flows nicely into the cozy living room w/ beautiful wood burning fireplace. Elegant, formal dining room, expansive family room/den, sun-room, & modern eat-in-kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, built in wall oven, microwave, disposal, & tons of cabinet space! The Primary Bedroom is sure to please w/ an updated en suite with tile shower, jetted tub, gorgeous vanity, & massive walk-in closet. There are 2 additional spacious bedrooms on this floor, along with main hallway bath & powder room. Enormous finished basement is the ultimate entertaining spot and even has a bonus room which can easily be used as a 4th bedroom or home office. Relax and unwind during the summer months on the gorgeous patio/terrace or BBQ on the expansive back deck. Close proximity to Downtown, Ridgeway Shopping Center, Gaynor Brennan Golf Course, public transportation, shops, restaurants, & all that Stamford has to offer! Also features central A/C, recessed lighting, attached garage, tons of storage, & separate laundry room. A TRUE MUST SEE!!!
