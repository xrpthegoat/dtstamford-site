About this home

This charming Cotswold home is located in the most coveted neighborhood in Cos Cob, with easy access to schools, trains, shops, dining, and the library. The main floor includes a cozy living room with a fireplace, a powder room, and adjacent sunroom/ office. The bright dining room leads to an eat-in kitchen connected to the family room with views of the patio and terraced gardens. The spacious primary bedroom features an en-suite bath and walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms share a full bath, while the third-floor bedroom offers a walk-in storage closet and window nook. The finished lower level adds 373 square feet of versatile space, perfect for a gym, office, or playroom, and includes a full bath. This home offers comfortable and convenient lifestyle in a prime location.
