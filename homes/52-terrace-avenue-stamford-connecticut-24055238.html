About this home

This charming Cape Cod-style home is as cute as can be and perfectly nestled on a picturesque property in the desirable mid-ridges. Step into the inviting living room, where hardwood floors & a cozy wood-burning fireplace set the stage for warmth & relaxation. The seamless flow into the dining room creates an ideal setting for entertaining. The heart of this home is the spacious eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, where you can enjoy scenic views of the sun-drenched backyard. With direct access from the garage, unloading groceries is a breeze-no stairs, no hassle! Need a private space to focus? The convenient home office is your perfect retreat, offering both privacy and inspiration. A full bathroom rounds out the first floor for added convenience. Upstairs, you'll find 2 generously sized bedrooms & another 1/2 bathroom. Storage is abundant with a cedar closet and charming alcoves. The backyard is a true haven, featuring a patio ideal for barbecues, a lush lawn perfect for fun & games, and a delightful garden area. As a bonus, there's a small studio (as is) with electricity, ready to be transformed into a creative space, workshop, or hobby room-the possibilities are endless. This home comes equipped with 2 wall & 2 window air conditioners for your comfort. Minutes from both the Merritt Prkwy & I-95, as well as lots of shopping and restaurant choices at your fingertips. This little slice of heaven is waiting for is waiting for you to call it home!
