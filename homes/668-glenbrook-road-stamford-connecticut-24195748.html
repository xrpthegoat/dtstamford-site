About this home

Welcome to 668 Glenbrook Road #17, a 3 bed, 3.5 bath townhouse in the tudor inspired Riverwalk community. Spanning 4 thoughtfully designed levels, this residence features over 1,900 sq ft of light-filled open-concept living. The main level boasts an updated white kitchen with marble countertops, decorative tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, farmhouse sink, and a breakfast bar with ample seating. The open concept living and dining area showcases wood flooring, a cozy wood-burning fireplace, recessed lighting, a powder room, and sliders to a private back deck. The upper level offers a primary suite with soaring cathedral ceilings, bamboo flooring, custom blackout blinds, private deck access, and a spa-like en-suite bath with a double marble vanity and custom tile shower. Additionally, on this level is a second bedroom suite with updated full bath, plus a laundry closet in the hallway. Upstairs features a 3rd-floor bedroom/ loft with walk-in closet, providing exceptional flexibility. The finished, heated/ cooled lower level adds a rec room, guest bonus room, 3rd full bath, and a storage/ workshop area. This unit comes with 1 assigned parking spot and multiple guest spots. This unit is located on the Darien border, with a short walk to the Glenbrook train station, local shops, and dining. Do not miss this one!
