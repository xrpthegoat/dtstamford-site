About this home

BEST/FINAL MON 5/18,12Noon. Located in the heart of historic Silvermine, this spacious 3-bedroom, openly expanded ranch offers a perfect blend of modern living and timeless character. The centerpiece of the home is a true WOW, spectacular Great Room with multiple living and dining spaces, defined by soaring cathedral ceiling and walls of windows that flood the space with extraordinary natural light. This open concept, airy and inviting atmosphere is ideal for both daily living and epic entertaining. The thoughtful single-level layout also includes a versatile office space, perfect for a quiet at home work area. There are endless ways to decorate and use this incredible room! Large formal Living Room with a grand wood burning fireplace w/ Heatolater, wide gleaming hardwood and plenty of sunlight. Sparkling white Eat in Kitchen with breakfast bar and plenty of room for island, table and chairs or additional cozy sitting area. Situated in a coveted neighborhood known for its artistic heritage and scenic beauty, this home provides the convenience of ranch-style living paired with impressive and unique architectural drama and picturesque views of the Wilton landscape. Partly finished Basement with extra high ceilings boasts laundry area and plenty of storage. Outdoor Deck is accessible from 2 sliders. Generator, CITY WATER, 4 bedroom septic. This home is perfectly located minutes to Wilton Center, New Canaan, Norwalk, all highways and Silvermine Art Gallery, restaurants ..a gem!
