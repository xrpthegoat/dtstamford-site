About this home

Westport Waterfront Community has exquisite seaside gem on private Saugatuck Island with sparkling waterviews over Long Island Sound. Designer showcase home is completely renovated & expanded from the ground up in 2018 with 3 levels of living space. Boasting 3,000sf, 4 beds, 3.1 NEW Baths & attached 2 car-garage with 3rd garage door for golf cart, this house is incomparable. The living room is spectacular w/vaulted ceiling, exposed beams & gas fireplace. The open floor plan provides easy flow into the dining room w/gorgeous built-in beverage center w/custom cabinetry, quartzite counter & French doors to the outdoor deck. There's a new custom kitchen by NuKitchens w/quartz countertops, quartzite center island & Thermador appliances. Step into the stunning new primary bedroom suite w/Juliette balcony, water views, gas fireplace & 2 new custom walkin closets. The new primary bath is beautiful w/ carrara marble counters & flooring, a soaking tub/sep shower stall. There are 3 additional spacious bedrooms w/2 new full baths & new half bath. Step up to the spacious 3rd fl playroom w/a new rooftop deck! New HVAC, tankless water heater, new roof, ask4list! FEMA compliant, Mins to vibrant downtown, shops & restaurants. 3 mins to Metro North Train.
