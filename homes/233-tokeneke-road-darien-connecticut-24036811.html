About this home

Move right into this newly constructed 4 bedroom, 4 bath home, just minutes to town and train for both Darien and Rowayton. Crisp, light and bright interiors with hardwood floors throughout. Gourmet eat-in kitchen with generous dining space, Living Room, Family room, and Mudroom on the first level. On the second level, there are 3 bedrooms including the primary suite up the main staircase plus a large bonus room, 4th bedroom, and full bath up the secondary staircase. The bonus room is complete with kitchenette and could also be used as an in-law / au pair suite. Generous closet space and spacious attached 2 car garage. Level backyard.
