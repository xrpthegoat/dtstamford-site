About this home

Recent tasteful updates elevate this two-bedroom, two-and-a-half bath Condo quietly situated in the desirable Silo Hill community less than a mile from Riverside shops, restaurants and schools. Inviting main level features chef's kitchen with stainless appliances, dining room open to living room with glass doors to secluded deck overlooking verdant woodlands/conservation land, perfect for entertaining, and a half bath. Two bedrooms with two full baths comprise the second floor. Walkout lower level boasts a family room or versatile space, ideal as a gym, playroom or office, with outdoor patio access. Great storage, in-unit laundry, one designated parking spot and community pool and clubhouse.
