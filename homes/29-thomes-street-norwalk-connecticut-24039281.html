About this home

Welcome to 29 Thomes Street, a charming home nestled on a peaceful cul-de-sac in one of Rowayton's most sought after neighborhoods, just a short walk from the train station and the picturesque coastal village of Rowayton. This recently renovated 4-bedroom home boasts expansive living spaces, a seamless flow, and exceptional outdoor areas, including two decks and a large, beautifully landscaped backyard. The most recent updates feature a serene, spa-like primary bathroom with heated floors and a luxurious steam shower! The separate room and full bath on the lower level provide an ideal space for a home office, guest suite, or au pair accommodations. Offering the perfect balance of comfort, tranquility, and convenience, this home is a must-see!
