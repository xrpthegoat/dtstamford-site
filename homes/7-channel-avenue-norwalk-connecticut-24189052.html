About this home

Great opportunity to create your own direct waterfront dream home in the coveted Harbor View Association on Long Island Sound. The property is comprised of two nonconforming lots (known as 7 and 9 Channel). The oversized lot affords room for expansion of the existing home if desired. The setting is magnificent with everchanging waterscapes viewed throughout the home. There are 3 BRs and 3 Full Baths and a floor plan that would adapt to many family configurations and needs. Enjoy the coastal lifestyle in Harbor View. Activities include clubhouse with dock, tennis courts, playground, community garden,and ball field. Plus, the Association handles private road maintenance, community upkeep, snow plowing, and garbage collection. Launch your kayak or paddle board from your own backyard! All SONO amenities are close by including restaurants, shopping, Maritime Aquarium and convenient commuter connections. Schedule your tour today! Home is being sold"as-is."
