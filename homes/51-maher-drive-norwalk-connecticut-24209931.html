About this home

POOL, FENCED YARD, SCREENED PORCH & FIREPLACE - WELCOME TO 51 MAHER DRIVE! This inviting 3-bedroom, 2-bath split-level offers the perfect combination of indoor comfort, outdoor enjoyment and convenient Norwalk living. Situated on a .20-acre lot, the home features 6 rooms with a versatile split-level layout, a warm and inviting living area with fireplace, and a one-car garage. Step outside and enjoy your own backyard retreat! The fenced-in yard provides privacy and plenty of room for entertaining, recreation and pets, while the above-ground pool is ready for summer fun. The screened porch extends the home's living space and is an ideal setting for morning coffee, outdoor dining or relaxing on a warm summer evening. Location adds even more appeal! Enjoy everything Norwalk has to offer, from Calf Pasture Beach, parks, marinas and boating to the restaurants, shopping and nightlife of vibrant South Norwalk (SoNo). Spend the day exploring The Maritime Aquarium, Stepping Stones Museum for Children and the area's many recreational and cultural attractions. Convenient access to shopping, dining, major roadways and Metro-North makes getting around Fairfield County and commuting to New York City easy. A fireplace for cool evenings, a screened porch for warm ones, and a pool and private fenced yard for summer - 51 Maher Drive offers a lifestyle to enjoy in every season.
