About this home

Set on a private flat half acre and thoughtfully expanded and beautifully maintained, this move-in-ready 4 bedroom home offers a flexible floor plan designed for today's lifestyle, with exceptional space for work, entertaining, and relaxation. Enter to a wonderful sun filled living room with fireplace, a new renovated kitchen with large island and stainless steel appliances and separate dining area. Adjacent to the kitchen is a sun filled great room with vaulted ceilings that opens to a generous stone patio-perfect for endless entertaining inside and out. Finished across four levels, the home offers a wonderful blend of comfort and privacy. The spacious primary suite occupies its own private floor and features vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet, and a luxurious bath. An adjacent oversized room provides the perfect retreat for a home office, gym, or additional living space. 3 additional bedrooms share another large bath. The finished lower level offers even more versatility, with a separate entrance, mudroom/laundry room, full bath, and a flexible playroom, family room, or second home office. Outside, the level, fully fenced yard provides plenty of room for play, pets, gardening, and entertaining. With its exceptional location, thoughtfully designed spaces, and inviting indoor-outdoor living, 104 Old Road is a wonderful opportunity to enjoy all that Westport has to offer-close to town, schools, shopping, & just minutes from the Greens Farms train station.
