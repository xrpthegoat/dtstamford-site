About this home

Beyond the classic white picket fence you will find a tastefully updated 1930's colonial in the heart of Stamford. The main level features an expansive living room with oak flooring and a wood-burning fireplace. The open floor plan has the dining room adjacent to the living room. The star of the home is the newly remodeled sleek designer kitchen with custom cabinets, quartz tops, and stainless appliances. A sparkling new powder room and mudroom complete the first floor and lead to the oversized deck in the back yard. Upstairs there are three bedrooms. The hall bath has been remodeled as has the primary bedroom, en-suite. The lower level has a home office, a playroom with a laundry nook, and a powder room. The sunny back yard has a detached 2-car garage and is completely fenced in. The home is in the desirable Hubbard Heights area, only steps to the vibrant Scalzi Park and shopping & dining at Ridgeway Plaza. Minutes to downtown Stamford, Metro North Train, and public beaches.
