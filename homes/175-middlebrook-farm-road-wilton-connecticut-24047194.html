About this home

Spectacular and special, sited down long, private drive on sought after Wilton address, 5 BR Colonial with 2 story entry and sweeping staircase, primed for casual living or a more formal lifestyle! Kitchen slider to large deck that overlooks NEW, gorgeous Wagner In Ground Gunite Pool w/Spa, decking and beautiful manicured grounds. 5 bedrooms on second floor with poss. 6th in the fully finished Basement that boasts 2 sets of sliders to covered pool lounging area, custom built ins, Half Bath, Exercise Room, Media Room and tons of storage. 2nd Primary Suite on 2nd floor could be in law/au pair w/ separate entry from back staircase or second Family/Media/Home School Room, w/mini optional kitchenette area, closets and built ins. The Eat in Kitchen with granite island, 6 Burner Gas Stove, serious Walk-In Pantry and huge Dining area is the very heart of this home, sun drenched from windows and sliders. Kitchen opens to Family room with fieldstone fireplace, Formal Dining Room with stunning millwork/built ins, and convenient Home Office. Sunken Living Room shimmers with floor to ceiling windows, second fireplace, custom built ins, the perfect size for formal entertaining. So many special features and exquisite details including back staircase, fantastic Mud Room Area, 1st Flr. Laundry Room, 3 car garage, circular 2 story front hall entry, custom high end millwork, inlaid hardwood, brand new 1500 gallon septic, parklike grounds. This is a one of a kind home that has everything. A GEM
