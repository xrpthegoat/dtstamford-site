About this home

Welcome to 418 Hunting Ridge Road, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 4-bath home set on over two private acres in sought-after North Stamford. Designed for both comfort and style, the home features a well-appointed kitchen with custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, and a large center island, complemented by a sun-filled breakfast nook with a bay window overlooking the property. The expansive family room, with cathedral ceilings, a fireplace, and wet bar, creates a striking yet inviting central gathering space. A new TimberTech deck (2025) extends the living area outdoors, overlooking a private, landscaped yard with mature trees and a serene wooded backdrop. The primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with its own fireplace, full bath, and walk-in closet. The finished walk-out lower level adds versatile space for recreation, guests, or a home office. Thoughtful upgrades to systems and interiors, including electrical improvements and a new water heater, reflect consistent ownership care and provide added peace of mind. Set on a picturesque property with refined updates throughout, 418 Hunting Ridge Road presents a compelling opportunity for both everyday living and long-term value.
