About this home

Welcome to 76 Gillies Lane in Norwalk-offering easy, one-floor living in this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo. A private pathway leads you to the entrance, opening into a spacious foyer that sets the tone for the home's inviting layout. Enjoy an open floor plan with hardwood floors, generous laundry/ storage room, and a cozy wood-burning fireplace. The primary bedroom includes a large walk-in closet adding to this home's functionality. And the spacious patio offers peaceful views of the pool and pond, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Convenient parking is located directly in front of the unit. Set within a picturesque community just one hour outside of NYC with lush landscaping, all just moments from Norwalk's shopping, dining, beach and Metro North.
