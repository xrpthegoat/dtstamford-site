About this home

Rare opportunity to own a completely remodeled bungalow in the sought-after Harbor Shores of Norwalk! Just a short stroll to the picturesque Norwalk Harbor, pickleball courts, vibrant SONO, and Metro North, this cottage perfectly blends artistic flair with modern living. Step into a bright, contemporary kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, stylish industrial fixtures, granite countertops, and a spacious peninsula with bar seating-ideal for casual dining and entertaining. The sun-soaked living room invites you in with its ample seating and a dedicated artist's studio that offers seamless sight lines through to the kitchen. Retreat to the primary bedroom, enhanced by light-filled transom windows and generous closet space. The second bedroom is versatile, perfect for guests or as a productive home office. Both rooms share a beautifully designed hall bathroom with a modern shower stall and elegant tile work. Rich espresso hardwood floors flow throughout, adding warmth and sophistication. Situated on a corner lot, this condo alternative features a private side yard complete with a cozy patio, firepit area, mature plantings, and a handy shed for storage. Residents enjoy access to the neighborhoods private beach lot, perfect for relaxing days by the water. Harbor Shores is a serene neighborhood with excellent public transportation, making it easy to explore all that Norwalk has to offer. More pictures and floorplans coming soon!
