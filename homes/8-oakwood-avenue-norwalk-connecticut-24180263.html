About this home

Enjoy below unit garage parking, storage room & in unit laundry with this renovated 1 bedroom condominium. Perfect for commuters! Short walk to the Merritt 7 buildings and train station. This unit stands out with a renovated kitchen (stainless steel appliances, grey cabinets and white granite counters), hardwood floors, full size washer/dryer and sliding doors from dining area to balcony. There is also a large (6'X8') dedicated private storage room adjacent to the garage. Easy access to the Route 7 connector and Merritt Parkway. Just minutes from downtown Historic SoNo shops, restaurants and easy access to Norwalk's Calf Pasture Beach. Utilities are modest, pets are negotiable, tenant must have excellent credit and references. Owned by Co-Agent.
