About this home

Step into a world of charm with this picturesque storybook cottage nestled amidst masterful gardens on a tranquil lot in South Wilton. Boasting 2 bedrooms and 2 baths, this meticulously maintained home combines classic allure with modern comforts. Wrapped in cedar shingles and adorned with flower boxes, this home radiates curb appeal and a welcoming vibe. Inside, discover gleaming hardwood floors, expansive double windows and a cozy wood burning fireplace. The heart of the home features an updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances and quartz countertops, offering both functionality and style. Enjoy seamless flow from the family room to the dining room, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Step through the sliding glass door to a treated deck, ideal for dining al fresco. A dedicated office space offers convenience for remote work or study -or reimagined as an artist/yoga space for passion projects. On the upper level, a sizable primary bedroom with ample WIC, recessed lighting and a wall of windows contribute to this peaceful retreat. A second bedroom with wall-to-wall carpeting, windows that bring natural light, and tree-lined views and privacy. Updated bathrooms offer convenience and modern elegance. Upper-level laundry area makes chores a breeze. Close proximity to conveniences and commuter options lends easy access to work and leisure. Don't miss your chance to own this captivating South Wilton gem.
