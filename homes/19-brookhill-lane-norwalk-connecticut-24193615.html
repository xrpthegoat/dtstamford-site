About this home

This move-in ready custom-built home, located at 19 Brookhill Ln in Norwalk, is a private brook-side retreat featuring two decks, native landscaping, and thoughtfully designed, low-maintenance grounds with a micro-irrigation system. This 3700 sq ft unfurnished contemporary colonial features three bedrooms, three bathrooms, a private office, a den, and a dining room among other spaces with hardwood floors, dual fireplaces, and a custom lighting package that creates warm, sophisticated living spaces throughout. The newly renovated kitchen showcases premium European appliances, quartz countertops, and an island with seating for three. The luxurious primary suite includes a cedar-lined walk-in closet and a newly renovated bathroom. A circular crushed-stone driveway provides an elegant arrival, with a separate entrance leading to an oversized two-car garage featuring custom built-in cabinetry for storage. Owner will provide 3 full tanks of propane at move in. Going forward, tenant is responsible for the purchasing of propane from Gault Energy for new heat pump system, setting up an Electricity Account with Eversource, setting up Cable/Internet, and Water usage will be billed to your resident portal quarterly. Owner takes care of Sewer. Cats and dogs (2 max pets) are welcome with a $50/mo.
