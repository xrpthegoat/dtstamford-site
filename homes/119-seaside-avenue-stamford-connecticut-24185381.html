About this home

Welcome to an exceptional opportunity in The Cove! This rare four-bedroom, three-and-a-half bathroom townhouse offers over 2,500 square feet of beautifully designed living space in desirable neighborhoods. Enjoy the luxury of a custom white kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and natural gas cooking. The open-concept layout is perfect for entertaining, with a private outdoor area ideal for barbecuing and gatherings. Additional features include a one-car attached garage, visitor parking, and modern comforts like natural gas heat and air conditioning. Located just a short walk to the water, scenic parks, and convenient public transportation, this home truly combines lifestyle and location. Opportunities like this don't come often.
