About this home

Better than new! This impeccably designed home, built by Coastal Home Builders, is nestled on a coveted street in the desirable Old Hill section of Westport. Recent upgrades have further enhanced this already exceptional property. From the moment you enter the grand foyer, you'll be greeted by luxurious interiors and a seamless floor plan ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. French doors in nearly every room open to the stone patio and beautifully landscaped grounds, creating a perfect indoor-outdoor flow. The show-stopping gourmet kitchen, featuring a double-sized Viking range and top-of-the-line appliances, is complemented by a breakfast room and family room with a gas fireplace. Upstairs, the sumptuous primary suite boasts a private balcony, expansive dressing rooms, and a spa-like bathroom, creating the ultimate peaceful retreat. Three ensuite bedrooms, a laundry room, and a great room with vaulted ceilings and a wet bar complete the second floor. The finished third floor offers an ensuite bedroom, sitting room, and plenty of storage space. The lower level has a gym, additional storage, and the potential for a home spa, media room, or extra entertaining space. Outdoors, the stone patio, pool, and spa offer a perfect setting for alfresco dining, relaxation, and entertaining. Enjoy the prime location, just moments from downtown Westport, with easy access to the train and beaches. A true masterpiece of design and function.
