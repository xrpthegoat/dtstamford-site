About this home

Contemporary retreat on 2.80+ private acres in North Stamford showcasing modern elegance and comfort throughout. This immaculate home features an open floor plan with soaring vaulted ceilings and walls of glass that frame panoramic views of the natural surroundings. Designed to feel like a year-round escape, the dramatic family room invites relaxation and connection to nature, while the updated kitchen includes quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and seamless flow to the living, dining, and sitting areas-ideal for entertaining or everyday living. Beautiful wood floors run throughout, enhancing the modern aesthetic. Three bedrooms on the main level provide flexibility for guests, offices, or hobbies, while the upstairs primary suite includes a private deck overlooking the backyard along with two additional bedrooms. The finished lower level spans over 1,000 square feet, creating endless possibilities for a media room, gym, playroom, or home office, and with so many versatile spaces the home easily lives like a six-bedroom residence. Outside, the heated pool with connected spa, expansive patio, and landscaped grounds create a resort-like setting perfect for gatherings. Additional highlights include a horseshoe driveway, advanced solar panel system with Tesla backup batteries for minimal electric bills and reliable power during outages, and a location just minutes from shopping, dining, schools, and the Merritt Parkway.
