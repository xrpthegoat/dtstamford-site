About this home

HIGHEST & BEST DEADLINE- MON 5/20 @ 5:00. Imagine a lifestyle where the urban pulse meets tranquil beach vibes - welcome to your dream turnkey home at Harbor Shores! This meticulously maintained home greets you with a bright, inviting floor plan that flows seamlessly, accentuated by sun-filled rooms and hardwood floors. Front to back living room, centered around a beautifully appointed gas fireplace w/an elegant mantle, offers a warm and inviting atmosphere that is a versatile space perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The beautiful, updated kitchen leads you to exceptional outdoor living space. An expansive deck with a sunken hot tub complements the charming patio area and gardens beyond. The Wisteria-covered arbor creates a magical space perfect for relaxing in a saltwater breeze. On a cul-de-sac in a blissful near-water setting, this home stands historically resilient against nature's whims and storms. Experience the enchantment of summer listening to live music echoing across the harbor, visible 4th of July fireworks from your deck, and the serenity of soaking in your hot tub under a starlit sky. Outdoor enthusiasts will revel in the proximity to pickleball courts and marinas- launch your paddleboard/kayak within a block from your doorstep to explore the scenic waters anytime. Top-tier dining options, and SONO train station all just moments away. Upgrades include new roof, beautiful bathrooms, and kitchen.
