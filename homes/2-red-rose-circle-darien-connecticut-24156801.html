About this home

Welcome to 2 Red Rose Circle - a bright and beautifully maintained Four bedroom, Two full one 1/2 bath Colonial nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Darien's most sought after neighborhoods. Set on a flat, sunny half acre, this move-in-ready home combines timeless style with comfortable, everyday living. Natural light fills the spacious rooms, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere throughout. The inviting kitchen opens to a casual dining area and overlooks the expansive backyard-perfect for hosting barbecues, playing catch, or simply relaxing in the sunshine. A flexible first floor layout offers open gathering spaces along with quiet nooks ideal for entertaining or unwinding. Upstairs, you'll find four expansive bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with a private bath. The finished lower level provides additional living space for a playroom, gym, and home office. Don't miss the two car garage which has a place for all your tools and sporting equipment. Enjoy life in a friendly neighborhood close to top rated schools, beaches, parks, and the train-all just minutes away. With its natural light, level yard, and easy flow, this home is the perfect place to settle in, spread out, and make lasting memories in the heart of Darien.
