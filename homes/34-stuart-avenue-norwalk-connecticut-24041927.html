About this home

MINT CONDITION! JUST UNPACK, MOVE-IN AND ENJOY! Light, bright and sun filled, tastefully updated end unit townhouse in a small complex, near Norwalk Hospital, shopping, bus line and highways. Newly painted throughout, refinished oak floors on first floor and staircase, new pre-engineered flooring on 2nd floor hallway, new carpet in both bedrooms, new cordless cellular blinds. Open floor plan design featuring living room and dining area with sliders to private blue stone patio with matured arborvitae for maximum privacy. Upscale cherry cabinets kitchen with granite counters, stainless steel appliances and designer tile. Charming, renovated powder room with wainscoting completes the first floor. Second floor offers two spacious bedrooms with great closets, full size washer and dryer and tastefully renovated hall bath with designer tile. Crown moldings throughout. Plenty of storage in attic plus private storage room in basement. Two reserved parking spaces, too. Only 3 years old efficient heating/central air system. Excellent credit and income verification a must. No pets! No smokers! Owner/Agent. AGENTS PLEASE READ AGENT REMARKS REGARDING REQUIREMENTS FOR OFFER SUBMISSION.
