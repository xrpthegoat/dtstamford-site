About this home

BEST and FINAL Tuesday 11/10, 4PM. Well-maintained Cornell Colonial invites you to step into a world of warmth, comfort, and timeless grace, ready for its next stewards to begin their own treasured chapter. As you approach, a stately circular driveway welcomes you home, setting an inviting tone before you even step through the front door. Inside, standard living spaces give way to extraordinary gathering areas. The sunken Living Room boasts cathedral ceiling, creating an airy, sun-drenched haven by day or cozy nights in front of the fireplace. From the moment you step through the front door, your gaze is drawn through to the formal Dining Room's glass sliders directly out to the back yard. At the heart of the home, the spacious family room, with 2nd fireplace, flows effortlessly into the open eat-in kitchen, creating a seamless pulse for daily life. Tucked away above the garage, a versatile bonus room offers a private retreat-ready for a media room, a quiet home office, or a spacious 5th bedroom suite to suit your family's evolving needs. Beyond 4 bedrooms and expansive 9-room layout, the home's true magic unfolds outdoors. Step out onto the generous back deck and take in the sheer beauty of your private oasis: a stunning, completely flat & dry 1.80-acre backyard wrapped in a dense canopy of mature trees. Here, complete privacy envelops you as you unwind to the peaceful sounds of nature. Sited on a tree lined cul de sac street, come enjoy all that lovely Wilton has to offer!
