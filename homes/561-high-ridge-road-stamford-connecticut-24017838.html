About this home

Come see this special 4 Bedroom/2 Full Bath Colonial gem located in the heart of Stamford. This property has been home to its owners for the past 40+ years and has been lovingly cared for both inside & out. This home boasts exquisite Millwork throughout, Crown Moldings, Coffered Ceilings, Hardwood Floors and Central A/C. You will find Gas Log Fireplaces in the Kitchen, Family Room & Living Room. Skylights in the Kitchen, Family Room and Primary Bathroom provide extra natural light in an already sun filled house. As you enter the home and walk into the Living Room you will find beautiful Hardwood Floors. Gazing through the French Doors you will see lush gardens & plenty of Patio space for all kinds of outdoor living. The Dining Room with its Built-In Hutches is versatile to host intimate dining or large dinner parties. The Eat-In Kitchen has plenty of space for everyone to get involved without getting in each other's way. The Family Room is an unexpected surprise with soaring ceilings, skylights, a wall of windows and access to the tiered Patio. The Primary Bedroom has 4 closets for lots of storage. The 2nd Upper Floor Bedroom has Built-Ins & an attached Dressing Area with plenty of closet space. The 2 Main Floor Bedrooms have ample closets & Built-In Cabinetry. All 4 Bedrooms have protected Hardwood Floors under the Carpets. This property is tucked away for lots of privacy yet accessible to all Stamford has to offer.
