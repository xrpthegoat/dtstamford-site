About this home

Welcome to 31 Hamilton Avenue! Located in Stamford, this charming 2-bedroom home offers comfortable interiors, an inviting outdoor space, and is a convenient five-minute walk to the train station. The main level features a bright kitchen with abundant cabinetry, stainless-steel refrigerator, gas range, tile backsplash, recessed lighting, and space for casual dining. A separate dining room provides a warm setting for meals and entertaining. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms receive plenty of natural light, while the updated full bathroom includes a glass-enclosed shower and modern vanity. The unfinished basement offers laundry, utilities, storage, and flexible space for future use. Outside, enjoy a spacious backyard and elevated wood deck with room for dining, grilling, gardening, and relaxing.
