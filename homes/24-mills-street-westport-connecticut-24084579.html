About this home

Welcome home! Pristine and fully updated yearly, unfurnished rental in the Greens Farms neighborhood of Westport. 1940's charm with 2300 square feet over 3 floors, 3 bedroom, 2.5 bathroom cape- desirable layout with option for primary bedroom on main or second level. Beautiful & bright kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, with a wall of built-in cabinetry/pantry in the adjacent breakfast room. Sliding glass doors lead to the deck with grill, access to the storage shed and one car garage. The private & picturesque yard is lined with stone walls, fencing, pretty landscaping and bluestone walkways-a nice place to relax or play. The lower level has flexible rooms, office and playroom, along with a half bath, laundry and plenty of storage. Bright and sunny den/sunroom, living room with fireplace, and dining room round out the first floor. This is truly a charming home close to everything, including the Greens Farms Metro North train station. Photos were taken before Tenants were in place. Available June, 2025
