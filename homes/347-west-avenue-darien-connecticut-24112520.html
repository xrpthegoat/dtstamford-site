About this home

This stunning colonial in Darien, CT offers 3,472 sq ft of elegant living space with four bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with a full bath, a private ensuite bedroom, and two additional bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bath. Features include beautiful, oak hardwood floors, a finished lower level, a spacious deck, and a lush landscape maintained by a smart irrigation system. The home also boasts recent upgrades including: modern kitchen appliances, finished lower level and an attached one-car garage.
