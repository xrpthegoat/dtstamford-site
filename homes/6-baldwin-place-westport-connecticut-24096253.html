About this home

Welcome to this beautiful 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that blends timeless elegance with modern comfort. Designed for effortless everyday living and exceptional entertaining, the spacious eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into the living room, dining room, and a charming parlor-perfect for enjoying an evening beverage over a game or good conversation. Relax and watch a movie or read a good book in the family room. Start your mornings in the serene sunroom, bathed in natural light and warmth, offering a peaceful retreat with your coffee. The home features generously sized bedrooms, including an expansive primary suite that serves as a luxurious private escape. Step outside to a beautifully landscaped yard complete with a sparkling pool-ideal for relaxing or hosting gatherings on warm summer days. Located just moments from picturesque beaches and offering easy access to town, major highways, and the train, this exceptional property delivers both convenience and a lifestyle of comfort and coastal charm.
