About this home

Step into timeless charm with this beautifully restored 1915 Farmhouse located on desirable East Norwalk Street. This home blends historic character with modern updates, featuring stunning original woodwork, stained glass windows, and classic built-ins that showcase the craftsmanship of the era. The first floor offers a bright, open layout with a updated kitchen that flows seamlessly into the living room and dining area. Perfect for everyday living. The second floor features four bedrooms and updated full bath. Outside, enjoy the manicured flat backyard that is fully fenced and perfect area to relax or entertain. Conveniently located 5 minutes from the train station and just minutes from the beach, local shopping. This home offers the perfect combination of charm, comfort, and location.
