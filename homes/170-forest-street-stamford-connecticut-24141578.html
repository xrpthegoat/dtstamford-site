About this home

Just two blocks from vibrant Bedford Street, this impeccably maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers the perfect blend of character, comfort, and convenience. Enjoy hardwood floors throughout, beautiful crown molding and a charming fireplace that anchors the main living space. This home is filled with natural light, enhanced by a private balcony ideal for morning coffee or evening unwinding. Upstairs you will find two large bedrooms with the primary suite impressing with soaring 14 ft ceilings and a spacious walk-in closet, creating a true retreat. Additional highlights include in-unit washer and dryer, as well as assigned parking and a large private storage space in the basement. Located just a five-minute walk to restaurants, parks, bars, shops, movie theaters, train stations, and more-this is downtown living at its best. Furnished option available upon request.
