About this home

Welcome home and move right in to this picture perfect Colonial bursting with charm in the most convenient location in the mid-ridges neighborhood. Open concept updated farmhouse eat-in-kitchen features stainless steel appliances, subway tile back-splash & butcher-block counter-tops overlooking the cozy and bright dining area with bench seating. Light drenched living room features wood burning fireplace with modern shiplap surround, recessed lighting and french doors that open up to a bonus family room space with another set of french doors leading out to a lush, beautiful fully fenced in back yard with patio. Upstairs is an oversized primary bedroom with enough space for an office area, an additional spacious guest bedroom, an updated marble bathroom as well as a second floor laundry closet conveniently located in the hallway. New roof, converted from oil to gas, new gas furnace, new stove and dishwasher, new A/C motor, newer fencing, efficient thermopane windows. Plenty of storage in the attic as well as the old garage space that has been converted into storage. Driveway entry to side door on Dunn ave. provides easy & quiet entrance. Set back off the road, yet walking distance to High Ridge shopping, minutes to Merritt Parkway, downtown Stamford/train & more. Your dream home awaits!
