About this home

Tucked at the end of a private, tree-lined lane in the coveted Wallacks Point neighborhood, this move-in-ready 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch pairs a fully renovated interior with an expansive 0.94-acre lot and deeded private beach rights on Long Island Sound. Inside, refinished hardwood floors and recessed lighting flow through a bright, open living and dining area anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. The new kitchen has shaker cabinetry, quartz counters, stainless appliances, a wine/beverage fridge, and picture windows overlooking the yard. The primary suite is 16' x 13', and a new bath with a double vanity serves the other two bedrooms. A screened sunroom opens to a stone patio, offering seamless indoor-outdoor entertaining options. The finished lower level with interior and separate entrance adds a 26'x21' family room, new full bath, and a versatile extra room for office, gym or au pair, plus a 2-car garage with plenty of storage space. Outside you'll find charm in the stone retaining walls, a garden shed, and open lawn framed by mature trees. With plenty to enjoy as is, you'll also have the potential to build up, build out or subdivide (subject to zoning approvals). Located within a short distance to Cummings Park, Cafe Silvium, and Chelsea Piers CT and easy access to the train to NYC and downtown Stamford -- just ten minutes away -- this serene oasis combines comfort, convenience, and community for an exceptional living experience.
