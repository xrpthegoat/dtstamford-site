About this home

Sun-drenched and full of character, this beautifully updated English Colonial is set on a quiet, picture-perfect street in one of Noroton Heights' most desirable neighborhoods, a quick walk to the elementary school, a mile to middle school, steps to Woodland Park, and moments to shops, restaurants, and the NYC train. Blending timeless 1920s charm with thoughtful modern upgrades, the home welcomes you with an inviting foyer that opens to a gracious open plan living room with fireplace and dining room, perfect for entertaining, plus a private study that also makes a great playroom. The updated chef's kitchen includes a Wolf range and quartz countertops, and flows to an expansive entertaining patio and fully fenced backyard, complete with an irrigation system. Upstairs are three spacious bedrooms and an additional office. The finished daylight/walkout lower level offers a versatile playroom, along with a recently upgraded mudroom and laundry. New central air conditioning added in 2025. New washer and dryer in 2023. Improvements in recent years also include 2 fully renovated bathrooms, refinished hardwood floors, fresh interior paint, updated lighting, Nest thermostat, and an electric pet fence. Current owners have also added extensive plantings to the landscaping in the home's pretty and private property. City water/sewer/gas. Ask about garage and primary bath plans. Enjoy an easy and engaging lifestyle full of charm, style and easy access to all Noroton Heights has to offer!
