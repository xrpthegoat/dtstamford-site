About this home

Antique Post & Beam. Incredible transformation of a 1787 Vermont Barn. Converted to a residence in 1920 artfully integrating hand-hewn beams, random-width floors and incredible stone fireplace. Striking interior with vaulted ceilings and walls of windows to capture natural light and views. Entry with walls of exposed brick and barnwood, vaulted ceiling and sliding barn door. Incredible Great Room with vaulted ceiling beams, fireplace, seating and dining areas. Chef's Kitchen with granite counters and stainless appliances. Primary Bedroom with vaulted ceiling, wood burning stove and doors to porch. Two additional Bedrooms, both with vaulted ceilings and exposed beams. Two Full Baths. Beautiful, terraced gardens, fieldstone walls and stone terraces for dining and entertaining. Absolutely gorgeous grounds overlooking the East Branch Mianus River. Fabulous stone terrace with firepit at river's edge. Convenient location and just a moment's drive to the commercial area of High Ridge Road with its shops and restaurants. Close to the Merritt Parkway, the bus line, the Metro North train and I-95. A remarkable offering!
