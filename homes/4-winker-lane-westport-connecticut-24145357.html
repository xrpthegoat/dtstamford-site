About this home

Spend July in this idyllic, fully furnished Westport retreat, tucked away on a private cul-de-sac and surrounded by stunning, manicured gardens. This sun-drenched Colonial offers resort-style living with a heated gunite pool, hot tub, guest house, and expansive outdoor entertaining spaces. A sweeping staircase welcomes you into the recently refreshed interior, featuring soaring ceilings and beautifully proportioned rooms. Highlights include an updated kitchen complete with a large center island overlooking a breakfast nook with French doors to the patio and an inviting family room with custom built-ins and fireplace. The luxurious primary suite features a private balcony overlooking the gardens, two walk-in closets, and a spa-like bath. Four additional en-suite bedrooms occupy the second floor, with an exercise room on the third level. The finished walkout lower level includes a generous playroom and an additional en-suite bedroom. Completing the property is a fully equipped one-bedroom apartment above the detached two-car garage. Outdoors, a sweeping lawn, blooming gardens, and expansive patio surround the pool, creating the ultimate summer setting. Just minutes to Westport's beaches, shopping, and dining-this exceptional summer rental is not to be missed.
