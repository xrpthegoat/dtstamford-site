About this home

Charming Mid-Ridges Colonial with Modern Upgrades! This beautifully maintained 3 bedroom, 1 bathroom home offers 1,502 sq. ft. of inviting living space that seamlessly blends classic character with modern convenience. A bright, airy layout features a spacious living room, dining area, and heated enclosed porch, perfect for year round enjoyment. Hardwood floors enhance the warmth throughout, while the upper level includes three comfortable bedrooms and a stylishly updated bathroom. A brand new natural gas boiler with an on demand three zone heating system, located in the unfinished basement, provides excellent energy efficiency. The generous sized fully fenced in backyard offers a private setting ideal for outdoor gatherings, summer barbecues, or peaceful relaxation. Situated just minutes from Stamford's best dining, shopping, and entertainment, this move-in-ready residence delivers the perfect balance of urban accessibility and neighborhood charm.
