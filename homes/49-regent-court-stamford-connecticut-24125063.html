About this home

Highly Coveted & Desirable 4 Bed/ 2 Full Bath Colonial on cul de sac in Woodway Acres neighborhood of Upper Springdale. Move-in ready home features main level open floor plan Kitchen w/ stainless steel steel appliances incl 5-burner Thermador gas cooktop, breakfast bar & lg pantry cabinets, bright Dining Area, large Living Room overlooking rear sun deck, potential first floor Primary Bedroom (or Great Room) & Full Bathroom. Second floor offers 3 spacious Bedrooms w/ warm wall-to-wall carpeting (hardwood floors underneath) including a second Primary Bedroom and Full Bathroom. Beautifully finished lower level rounds out the almost 2,200 sq ft of living space. Situated in a quiet neighborhood bordering the Noroton River (home is not a flood zone!), you're a short walk to school, train, shopping, dining, parks, library, Woodway Country Club and all that Springdale Village has to offer. Just 1-mile to Merritt Pkwy and minutes from Stamford, Darien & New Canaan downtowns. This "hidden gem" will not last, make it yours today!
