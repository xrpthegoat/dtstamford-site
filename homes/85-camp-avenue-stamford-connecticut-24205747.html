About this home

Experience refined, effortless living in this exceptional three-level, end-unit townhouse, offering an abundance of natural light, generous proportions, and a thoughtfully designed floor plan. High ceilings and spacious bedrooms create an airy, sophisticated feel, while abundant closet space provides exceptional storage throughout. The well-appointed eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and direct access to a private balcony - an inviting spot for morning coffee or casual dining. A versatile bonus room offers possibilities as a home office, family room, media lounge, playroom, or creative studio. Attached garage parking, plus an additional reserved space, adds everyday convenience. Built in 2007, this well-maintained community offers an impressive array of amenities, including a swimming pool, clubhouse with fitness room, walking trail, and scenic river views. Ideally situated minutes to the Springdale train station, with convenient access to the Merritt Parkway and Exit 13 (old 36). Enjoy easy access to local shops and excellent restaurants, Twin Rinks, NY Sports Club, CVS and more. Combining stylish interiors, generous living space, resort-inspired amenities, and an exceptionally convenient location, this is a sophisticated opportunity for comfortable, connected living. ***Owner will re-paint unit and install new flooring on 3rd floor before move-in.***
