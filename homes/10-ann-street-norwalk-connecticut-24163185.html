About this home

Built in 2004, the theme of modern sophistication defines this location and continues throughout the building. This fantastic unit showcases a modern kitchen equipped with beautiful streamlined wood cabinetry, a backsplash, frosted glass accents, nickel hardware, and stainless steel appliances, all boasting plentiful storage. Newer tile flooring is found in both the kitchen and bathroom, while gleaming hardwood floors, dramatic 10-foot ceilings, and a wall of 8-foot windows give the living space an open and airy feeling. One secured deeded assigned garage space, designated additional personal storage and fitness center in the building included in your rent. Furthermore, the building offers an elevator and bike storage rooms. The location is steps away from Zagat top rated restaurants, museums, shopping, the hot night life, the train, the water, and I95.
