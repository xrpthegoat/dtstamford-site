About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath condo in the heart of Stamford's sought-after Cove neighborhood. Freshly painted throughout with brand new carpeting, this home is move-in ready and full of natural light-including a spacious living room with a stunning skylight. The fully renovated kitchen features all-new stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, modern cabinetry, and sleek new flooring. The bathroom has been upgraded with a new vanity, ceramic shower backsplash, contemporary tile floors, and a new toilet. Each bedroom enjoys sunrise exposure and full-size closets. Additional perks include 1 assigned parking spot in a gated garage. Unbeatable location-just .3 miles to Chelsea Piers, .5 miles to both Rogers International School and scenic Cove Island Park, and less than a mile to I-95. You're only 1.6 miles from Stamford Train Station, offering convenient Metro-North and Amtrak access. Also located on the same road as KT Murphy School. Enjoy coastal living with urban convenience-this is a must-see!
