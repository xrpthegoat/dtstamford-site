About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in sought-after South Wilton, this expanded 5-bedroom Colonial offers the perfect blend of space, style, and convenience. Beautifully renovated throughout, the home features a stunning chef's kitchen with a 10-foot quartz island, 6-burner Wolf gas cooktop, and sliders that open to a spacious deck overlooking a level backyard, ideal for entertaining and everyday living. The main level boasts generous gathering spaces, perfect for both relaxed living and hosting , along with a dedicated home office. Upstairs you will find a 5 bedrooms, including a well-appointed primary suite with another home office, and a luxurious bath with heated floors, large shower and separate tub. Two more fully renovated full baths and laundry is conveniently located on this floor. Extensive updates include: new AC systems, new windows, completely renovated kitchen and baths, refinished floors, a new portico and more! (Please see full list of improvement in documents) Enjoy move-in ready living in a prime location just minutes from downtown Wilton, top-rated schools, major highways, and easy access to NYC! *Deck to be painted when weather permits.
