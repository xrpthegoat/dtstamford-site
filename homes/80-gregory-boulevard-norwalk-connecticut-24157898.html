About this home

Whether you're looking to make a savvy investment or find a place to call home while someone else helps pay the mortgage, this charming two-family residence in the heart of East Norwalk is a rare find that checks every box. Perched on a spacious corner lot, this property immediately feels like home. It's the kind of place where you can envision slow Saturday mornings and easy coastal living. The first floor features a welcoming open porch-perfect for a couple of rocking chairs, a cold drink, and watching the neighborhood go by. Upstairs, the second unit offers a cozy enclosed porch, providing a private, sun-drenched sanctuary to enjoy your morning coffee or curl up with a good book, regardless of the season. The layout is a dream for an owner-occupant. You can live comfortably in one unit and let the rental income from the other take the sting out of your monthly expenses. It's the ultimate "house hack" in a location that people truly love. East Norwalk is all about community and convenience. You're just minutes away from the train station for an easy commute, the local beaches for summer sunsets, and some of the best restaurants in town. This isn't just a building; it's an opportunity to build equity and a lifestyle at the same time. Come see why this corner-lot gem is the perfect place to start your next chapter.
