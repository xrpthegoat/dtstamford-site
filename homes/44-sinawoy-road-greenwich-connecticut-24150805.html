About this home

Welcome to a fully renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bath apartment that blends classic Victorian charm with modern comfort. This sun-filled unit offers 515 sq ft of well-designed space in Cos Cob. The living room and bedroom feature hardwood floors, large windows, and abundant natural light that highlights the home's classic architectural details. A renovated kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, butcher block countertops, crisp gray cabinetry, and a pantry for extra storage. The updated full bath includes a new vanity, a shower, and sleek vinyl plank flooring for a clean, modern touch. Enjoy the convenience of a private entrance, granting both privacy and easy access. Your dedicated off-street parking spot sits just outside the door-ideal for unloading groceries or a quick commute. The apartment is heated by efficient natural gas radiators, ensuring warmth through New England winters. On-site laundry is available in the lower level (coin-operated, accessed via a side entry), simplifying weekly chores. Immediate occupancy means you can move in and start enjoying this beautifully updated space right away. The location is a commuter's dream: Cos Cob train station is minutes away for Metro-North, and I-95 is easily accessible for drivers. Local shops, dining, and waterfront parks are also nearby, enriching the lifestyle offered by this charming Victorian building in the heart of Greenwich.
