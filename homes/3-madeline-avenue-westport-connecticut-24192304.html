About this home

The beach lifestyle, water views, natural light, and endless possibilities await at 3 Madeline Avenue. Freshly painted and furnished, this charming 2-3 bedroom cottage offers an open and inviting floor plan featuring refinished hardwood floors, expansive windows, and a sun-filled living space one house from the waterfront. The kitchen is equipped with granite countertops and ample cabinetry, providing abundant storage and functionality. The lower level includes a mudroom, full bath, and a versatile bonus room with a skylight and window-ideal for a home office, guest space, gym, or creative studio. Upstairs, soaring vaulted ceilings, skylights, and oversized windows create a bright and airy retreat. Recent improvements include new flooring and a beautifully updated bath with a new vanity and tub. Step outside to a spacious patio, perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply enjoying the coastal setting. Whether you're looking for a turnkey weekend escape, year-round residence, investment opportunity, or the perfect site to renovate, expand, or build new, this property offers exceptional flexibility in a sought-after location. A rare opportunity to secure water views and exceptional potential in one of the area's most desirable coastal neighborhoods.
