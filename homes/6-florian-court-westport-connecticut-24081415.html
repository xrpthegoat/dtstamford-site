About this home

Stunning, Custom Colonial on premier Westport cul-de-sac. Set on 1.1 acres of beautifully manicured grounds, this meticulously maintained home offers the perfect blend of luxury/serenity. Plus it's all set up for multigenerational living with separate, new 973 SF Apt! The private, flat, dry backyard at 6 Florian is ideal for outdoor enjoyment. Out/indoors are well integrated, w/charming blue stone Front Porch, large Screened In Porch overlooking the Backyd, 2 built in sound systems. Full Bath on Main Lvl w/Backyd access. Main Level: Foyer, Powder Rm, Full Bath, Liv/Dining Rms w/Butler's Pantry w/wine fridge, large EI Kitchen open to Family Room w/stone FP, Wet Bar, Screened In Porch and Sunrm. Oversized windows/natural light abound throughout. The luxurious Primary Bedroom Ste: Balcony, Spa Bathroom w/Steam Shower, Air Tub, Radiant Heated Floors, large WI Closet, Sitting Room/Office. Also on the 2nd floor are 3 other, ensuite Bedrooms, all w/WI closets. Lower Level: Large Gym, Family/Game Rm, Wine Rm, Storage. There is also a large, unfinished Attic for additional space. PLUS a brand new, separate apartment w/5th Bedrm, Full Bath, large WI Closet, Kitchen, Liv Room, Laundry, sep power panel (on generator), alarm, private entry and parking space! The craftsmanship/attention to detail in this home & apt are superior. Filled w/ happiness/special memories of 18 years, it's time for the next owners to come along and love it!
