About this home

Welcome to 3 Green Lane, an inviting, move in ready Cape on a peaceful cul-de-sac in Greenwich's Glenville area. Sun filled living and dining spaces set the tone for everyday comfort, with hardwood floors throughout and a refreshed kitchen that keeps things bright and functional. The home offers 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths, plus a finished lower level with 900+/- sq ft of additional flexible space beyond the listed square footage, ideal for a media room, playroom, hobby space, or work-from-home setup. Two fireplaces add warmth and character. Outside, enjoy multiple places to relax, a private patio and an elevated deck, along with a classic stone-walled entry that gives the home standout curb appeal. There's also a fenced side yard area great for contained outdoor space. Central air, natural-gas heat/hot water, and a 1-car garage add everyday convenience. Also, Located in an R-6 zone, the property may offer two-family potential (will need to verify with the town). Close to Glenville School, local shops, parks, and commuter routes/train connections.
