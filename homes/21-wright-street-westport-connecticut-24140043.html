About this home

Welcome to 21 Wright Street, Westport, CT - a charming blend of history and modern living. This classic mid-1800s home offers the allure of high ceilings and wide plank floors, exuding rustic elegance across its 1,507 square feet. Featuring three bedrooms and a rare primary suite with its own bath, this antique home provides both comfort and privacy. The living spaces are thoughtfully designed, seamlessly combining historical charm with modern amenities. Situated on a 6,098 square foot lot, the outdoor area offers a peaceful retreat, perfect for relaxation or entertaining. 21 Wright Street was the home maintained by Polly Wright after the death of her husband Charles. It remained in the Wright family until 1918. Just a short walk to downtown Westport, this home offers convenience and a rich sense of history in a prime location. This and the two other Wright Family properties on this street are an important part of Westport's Kings Highway North Historic District. Discover the unique appeal of this antique treasure and make it your own personal haven. Offers being reviewed Monday at 5 oclock.
