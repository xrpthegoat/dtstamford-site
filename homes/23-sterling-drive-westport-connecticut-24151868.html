About this home

SUMMER RENTAL Overlooking Compo Cove beach. Water views from all rooms. Available for 2 periods: June 25th to July 25th and July 25th to August 23rd (there is some schedule flexibility). This recently built 5-bedroom home has a light-filled gourmet kitchen with 2 sinks, 2 dishwashers, and Sub-Zero/Wolf appliances. The Living area and kitchen open to the pool, fenced in yard (perfect for young children) and patio with a Weber gas grill. The heated infinity pool has an auto pool cover making it 100% safe. Our home easily sleeps 10 guests in 5 bedrooms with 1 King, 3 Queens, and 2 Twins. There is handicapped accessible bedroom with a full bath on the Main floor. There are 4 bedrooms upstairs, one of which is the Master suite with a large bath, 2 sinks, tub and a large steam shower. Downstairs is a sitting area and playroom with a ping pong table, a professional treadmill and floor to ceiling windows opening to the backyard. Play golf, tennis, or rent paddle boards, kayaks or boats nearby. The neighborhood is quiet and safe day & night. The New York train, restaurants, groceries and shopping are 5 minutes away. Photo's are accurate. Exterior inspections welcome. Interior inspections permitted after deposit.
