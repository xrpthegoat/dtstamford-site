About this home

A picturesque setting, timeless architectural character, and gracious living spaces define this inviting residence in one of Darien's desirable neighborhoods. Beautiful hardwood floors set the tone, flowing through an elegant living room with fireplace, formal dining room, and spacious kitchen with center island. Designed for both intimate gatherings and effortless entertaining, the expansive family room features a striking brick fireplace and custom built-ins, while the spectacular sunroom captivates with exposed beams, vaulted ceilings, skylights, and walls of windows framing the natural beauty beyond. Upstairs, four generously proportioned bedrooms include a primary suite with private bath, walk-in closet, and abundant built-in storage. The expansive lower-level recreation room offers exceptional versatility. Outdoors, a sprawling multi-tiered deck with integrated seating overlooks a captivating landscape of mature trees, established gardens, and meandering stone pathways, creating a serene backdrop for alfresco dining and entertaining. Complete with 2.5 baths, a dedicated main-level laundry room, and a two-car garage, this home presents a wonderful opportunity to bring your own vision to an exceptional setting. Enjoy a sense of retreat without sacrificing convenience, just minutes from the train, Weed Beach, boutique shopping, dining and all that makes Darien such a coveted place to call home.
