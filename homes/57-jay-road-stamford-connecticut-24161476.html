About this home

Nestled in a desirable Mid-Ridges neighborhood this beautifully maintained 4 bedroom 3.5 bath colonial offers the perfect blend of comfort, space and convenience. The main level features a bright and inviting living room, a spacious family room, centrally located kitchen with new sink, faucet and half bath. A formal dining room with Anderson sliders to private trek deck. Upstairs there are 4 generously sized bedrooms all with hardwood floor and ceiling fans. There are two updated full baths on second floor. The finished walk-out lower level (with add'l sf), could serve as in-law or nanny suite and has new tile flooring, a playroom, home office, full bath and laundry. There are Hunter Douglas blinds throughout, Guttermaxx. sprinkler system and whole house generator with underground propane tank which can hook up to one's grill. This home is just minutes from shopping, dining, and major commuting routes including the Merritt Parkway, making it a commuter's dream.
