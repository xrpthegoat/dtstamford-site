About this home

Refined living awaits on Boxwood Lane, a prime Hunt Club cul-de-sac featuring tranquil private entrances to stately colonial style homes. From its cobblestone-lined, semi-circular driveway to the backyard pool and bluestone patio oasis, the grounds of 5 Boxwood Lane are simply spectacular. The home itself stands apart, meticulously designed with exquisite details and custom amenities. Four levels, soaring ceilings, and a thoughtful layout offer spectacular living and entertaining spaces. Discover the newly styled gourmet kitchen with top-of-the-line appliances, custom cabinetry, and a generous island. It flows easily to the outdoor living areas, or into the great room with coffered ceiling and stone fireplace. Fireplaces add character and warmth to the already luxurious living room, dining room and home office. The upper levels reveal a sprawling primary suite with two ensuite bathrooms, walk-in closets, and an office/relaxation niche. Four additional bedrooms with bathrooms ensure privacy. Two separate sections of the home includes a great room + ensuite bedroom - a perfect refuge for guest or long-term visitors. The lower level is the ultimate "hang-out space" - an expansive recreational area for games and movie nights. It has been said, with stunning perennial gardens, velvety lawns, and a blissful poolside retreat featuring a charming cabana, the outdoors are hard to beat at 5 Boxwood Lane! Convenient to schools, transportation and all of Westport wonderful amenities.
