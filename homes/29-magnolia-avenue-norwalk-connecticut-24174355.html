About this home

Charming and move-in ready, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial offers 1,560 sq. ft. of comfortable living in a highly convenient Norwalk location. Classic curb appeal welcomes you with a covered front porch, manicured landscaping, mature shrubs, and a flagstone walkway leading to the entry. Step inside to find hardwood floors throughout and a bright, sun-filled front sunroom that adds wonderful extra living space and natural light. The main level features a spacious living room, formal dining room, and a cozy family room anchored by a working fireplace, along with a convenient half bath. Upstairs, the primary bedroom offers vaulted ceilings, a ceiling fan, and a private full bath, while two additional well-sized bedrooms share a second full bath. The full basement includes a utility sink and walkout access to the backyard, providing excellent storage and flexible use. Additional highlights include leafless gutters, a private driveway, a detached garage, and a picket-fenced side yard. Major recent updates bring peace of mind, including a new roof (2024) and new water heater (2024). Ideally situated near Norwalk Hospital with quick access to Metro-North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, making commuting throughout Fairfield County and into NYC simple. Shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences are just steps away, with The SoNo Collection only minutes from your door. A wonderful opportunity to own a well-maintained Colonial in one of Norwalk's most accessible pockets.
