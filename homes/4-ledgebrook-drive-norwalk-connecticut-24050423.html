About this home

Discover a perfect blend of style and sophistication in this chic unit, offering the essence of a SoHo loft in the highly desirable Ledgebrook Complex. Just minutes from the train, highways, the upscale SoNo Collection Mall, and trendy restaurants, this home provides both convenience and luxury. The stunning interior boasts 17-foot vaulted ceilings, sunlit 7-foot windows, and brand-new hardwood floors throughout. Unique architectural details add character, while the fully remodeled kitchen impresses with quartz countertops, a subway tile backsplash, schoolhouse pendant lighting, USB ports, and new stainless-steel appliances. The open layout seamlessly connects the kitchen to the spacious living room, perfect for entertaining. Enjoy custom lighting, a floor-to-ceiling corner wood-burning fireplace, and a private deck offering scenic views. The primary bedroom features double closets, built-ins, and an updated en-suite bath, while the second bedroom/den gives versatility for a home office. The updated full bath shines with elegant finishes. This home includes thoughtful upgrades like custom solar shade blinds, a Samsung front-load washer/dryer, new Anderson windows and front door, and central air. A 1-car garage with extra storage adds convenience. Ledgebrook's resort-style amenities include beautifully landscaped grounds, a pool, walking trails, clubhouse, exercise room, sauna, and jacuzzi. Special assessment of $9k already paid in full. This is a must see!
