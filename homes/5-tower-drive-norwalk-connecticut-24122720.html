About this home

Welcome Home! Step into this stunning single-family condo located in the highly desirable Spring Hill neighborhood of Norwalk. This multi-level home features recessed lighting, marble and original hardwood floors, and plush bedroom carpeting, blending comfort with timeless charm. Enjoy the abundance of natural light streaming through the elegant front tri-bay windows, illuminating the spacious living area, and full bathroom on the first floor level. The kitchen's sliding glass doors open directly to a beautifully landscaped backyard - perfect for relaxation or entertaining guests. The Black Galaxy granite countertops and natural oak cabinetry bring warmth and sophistication to the kitchen. Additional updates include newer siding, windows, and roof, ensuring peace of mind and energy efficiency. Upstairs, you'll find three inviting bedrooms and a full bathroom, complemented by an additional bath on the main level. The outdoor area is a true highlight - ideal for gatherings - complete with a handcrafted brick oven stove for cooking and entertaining al fresco. Conveniently located just minutes from Norwalk Hospital, I-95, Route 15 (Merritt Parkway), The SoNo Collection Mall, shopping plazas, and the Merritt 7 Train Station, this home offers easy access to everything Norwalk has to offer.
