About this home

Welcome to luxury living in the heart of downtown Stamford! Move-in ready 2 bed, 2.5 bath corner unit townhouse in the highly sought-after Cobblestone complex! Open and inviting living+dining room is perfect for entertaining, featuring a bay window, wood-burning fireplace, & sliders leading to a spacious patio common area. Well appointed kitchen includes a reverse osmosis system at the sink. The primary bedroom is a true retreat, boasting dramatic vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet with custom shelving and an ensuite bath w/ walk-in shower. A second spacious bedroom, a hall bath, plus a convenient second floor laundry second room completes the second floor. Pull down stairs lead to an attic and the lower level mechanical room offers substantial storage space throughout. Newer mechanicals include a heat pump and water heater. 1 reserved garage parking space #19 right outside the unit door, and plenty of additional floating garage spaces as well as a visitors parking lot. Outdoors, enjoy the well manicured grounds. This prime location has unbeatable convenience, a short walk to Metro North, public transportation, Stamford's best restaurants, bars, and UConn's campus. Don't miss your chance to own a great investment opportunity in one of downtown Stamford's most desirable locations!
