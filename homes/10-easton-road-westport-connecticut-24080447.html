About this home

This charming Cape Cod Colonial sits on a beautiful, level acre+ lot with a semi-circular driveway. The home features four bedrooms and two full baths, with two bedrooms on the main floor and the primary plus an additional bedroom upstairs. The living room boasts a wood-burning fireplace and an oversized window overlooking the front yard. The updated kitchen, equipped with stainless steel appliances, offers plenty of counter space and has a door leading to the side yard. The spacious dining room is perfect for entertaining. On the main level, the two additional bedrooms are generously sized, with one ideal as a home office featuring a picturesque bow window overlooking the backyard. Upstairs, you'll find the large primary bedroom and a fourth bedroom, both sharing an updated full bath. The expansive, level backyard includes a large stone patio, perfect for outdoor gatherings. An oversized two-car garage provides ample storage. Conveniently located near town shopping, the train, and the Merritt Parkway, this home is a must-see!
