About this home

Welcome to 22 Lyon Avenue, where location, lifestyle, and income potential come together in the heart of Greenwich. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac, this beautifully maintained two-family offers two spacious 2-bedroom, 1-bath residences, each with its own washer and dryer. With incredibly low property taxes, a proven history of strong Airbnb and rental income, and the first-floor unit currently generating $4,500 per month on a month-to-month lease, the numbers are just as attractive as the property. Minutes from Greenwich Avenue, Metro-North, incredible dining, shopping, parks, and everything downtown Greenwich has to offer, this is a rare chance to own a turnkey income-producing property in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after communities. Live in one and let the other help with the bills, or add a Greenwich gem to your investment portfolio.
