About this home

Discover 6 Orange Street, a beautifully updated and moveinready home perfectly situated in one of Norwalk's most convenient neighborhoods. This charming property was completely renovated and updated in 2015 and now provides all the modern upgrades, such as, updated kitchen, renovated bathrooms, and spacious bedrooms with generous closets. A flexible lowerlevel area provides the ideal space for a family room, home office, gym, or guest suite. The large, level backyard with a deck and inground pool is perfect for entertaining, gardening, or play, while energyefficient improvements add comfort and value. Located between Silvermine and Broad River, this home offers quick access to the Merritt Parkway, I95, MetroNorth, local shops, restaurants, parks, and Norwalk's vibrant downtown. As a BONUS, this home offers a full 2-bedroom in-law space or potential rental space with its own entrance and deck which is a perfect addition to what this home has to offer. Whether you're a firsttime buyer or seeking a turnkey property in Fairfield County, 6 Orange Street delivers exceptional lifestyle appeal and convenience.
