About this home

Fully renovated three years ago, this versatile, turn-key two-family property features major updates throughout, including a newer roof, siding, high-efficiency boiler, and appliances. Conveniently located with access to Old Greenwich Metro-North Station, I-95 Exit 6, ShopRite, Home Depot, and everyday conveniences. Each unit features separate electric and gas meters, individual thermostats, and its own washer and dryer. Unit 1 includes 1 bedroom plus a study/den and 1 full bath, while Unit 2 features 2 bedrooms, a flexible extra room, and 1 full bath. The partially finished walk-out lower level features natural light through two windows, generous storage, and an additional full bath area not reflected in public tax records, adding flexibility. Outside, a private rear parking area accommodates multiple vehicles with space for activities and entertaining. Low-maintenance and move-in ready, this property also presents income-producing potential.
