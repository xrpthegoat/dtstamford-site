About this home

Modern Light-Filled Retreat in the Heart of Silvermine Welcome to this spacious modern home, perfectly nestled in the heart of New Canaan's sought-after Silvermine neighborhood on two bucolic acres. Just moments from the iconic Silvermine Arts Center, Silvermine Market, GrayBarns, and The Mercantile, this vibrant and historic community is where neighbors gather and friendships grow. Brimming with natural light and thoughtfully designed for comfortable living, this airy residence offers versatile spaces and timeless appeal. Enjoy everyday living and entertaining in the open-concept layout, with a bright sunroom just off the kitchen that overlooks a peaceful, private setting-ideal for morning coffee or quiet reflection. A dramatic first floor great room with soaring ceilings, a cozy fireplace, and a full bath offers endless possibilities whether as a home office, media lounge, or guest suite. Upstairs, the serene primary suite features a fireplace, walk-in closet, spacious dressing area, and a luxurious bath with dual vanities-your private sanctuary. Another out standing feature is the attached and detached garages that accommodate four cars. This is modern living in one of New Canaan's most charming enclaves offering character, community, and convenience.
