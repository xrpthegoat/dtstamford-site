About this home

LOCATION LOCATION LOCATION! Bright and spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom second-floor apartment in the heart of Cos Cob. Totally renovated with an open-concept layout featuring a kitchen with stainless steel appliances and granite countertops that flows into the dining area and living room. Tons of windows flood the space with natural light. The primary bedroom offers an en-suite bathroom with tub/shower, double closet, and sliding doors to a private outdoor deck overlooking the backyard. The second bedroom is generously sized with a double closet, plus a third bedroom. Hallway full bath with walk-in shower. In-unit full-size washer and dryer in its own closet off the kitchen. Abundant closet space throughout plus a pull-down ladder to the attic for extra storage. Comes with 2 parking spots. Easy walk to Cos Cob village shops and restaurants, and just 1 mile from the Cos Cob Metro-North station. Close to Cos Cob School and all the convenience of the neighborhood.
