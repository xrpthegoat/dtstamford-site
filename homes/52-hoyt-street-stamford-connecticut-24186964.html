About this home

Nestled in an ideal downtown location is 48-52 Hoyt St., an Exceptional 7-unit residential complex with unbeatable upward potential! Unlike any other opportunity you'll come across, here you'll find 2 multi-unit buildings ~ One stately vintage colonial home housing 5 spacious 1-and 2-bedroom apartments, and a separate 2-family duplex w/ two 2-bedroom, 1.5 bath, side-by-side townhomes, plenty of parking and common space. The vintage building features a welcoming front foyer and a variety of apartments with high ceilings, hardwood floors (most), character & charm! The side-by-side 2-family offers traditional townhome style units with walk-up attics and basement access. Excellent investment opportunity to own an exciting piece of downtown Stamford. Current rents are inconsistent with today's market. Six of 7 tenants are on month-to-month terms.
