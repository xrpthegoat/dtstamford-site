About this home

Storybook charm meets comfortable living in this enchanting 3 bedroom, 4 bath home. Located at the end of a quiet block you will find exposed beams, character-rich details, and a warm, inviting atmosphere create a home that feels truly special. Thoughtfully designed spaces blend timeless charm with everyday functionality, offering room to gather, relax, and entertain. A spacious deck extends the living space outdoors, perfect for morning coffee, al fresco dining, or enjoying the surrounding setting. Each bedroom provides its own sense of retreat, while four full baths add exceptional convenience. From its distinctive architectural details to its cozy, welcoming feel, this is a home with personality and a true sense of place. Convenient central location close to trains and the beach.
