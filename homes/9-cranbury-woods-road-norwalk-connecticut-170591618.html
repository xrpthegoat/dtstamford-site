About this home

Located in the beautiful and sought after Cranbury section of Norwalk, this home boasts .55 level acres of professionally landscaped property. Recently renovated 3 Bedroom home features HW flooring & recessed lighting throughout, plus an open floor plan - great for both living and entertaining. Eat-in Kitchen features new island, granite counters & Stainless Steel appliances, and flows directly into the bright and spacious Living Room. Dining Room with sliders is filled with natural light and provides direct access to the deck, featuring a privacy wall and wonderful view of the large, level backyard. Down the hall, Primary Bedroom Suite features entire wall of built-ins & a full bath with oversized luxury stall shower. Two more bedrooms and full bath complete the main level. Downstairs, large Family Room with fireplace provides more versatile space. Office/Bedroom and half bath with laundry completes downstairs. 2 car attached garage. Newer windows. Newer septic. Perfect for commuters - Minutes to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, Cranbury Park, Shopping, Restaurants and more!
