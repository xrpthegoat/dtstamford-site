About this home

This absolutely bright beautiful 4 year young, meticulously maintained light-filled corner unit is on the Springdale/Darien border. It offers an open concept first floor, balcony, three bedrooms on the second level, two and a half baths plus an extra bedroom with a full bath on the lower level that can be used as an office, in-law, guest suite, hobby, play area, gym, and more, for a total of 4 beds 3.5 baths and zoned AC with a two car garage. The primary bath has double sinks and a luxurious steam shower for a spa-like experience. The additional kitchen island, gas fireplace, and perfect flow allow for easy indoor/outdoor enjoyment throughout the seasons... an entertainer's dream. There is a fenced, tiered garden next to the private outdoor patio, specially deeded for this unit. The many conveniences in close proximity include restaurants, banks, library, shopping, schools, parks, Woodway Country Club, downtown Stamford or Darien, and the Springdale train station. The ease of commuting for business or pleasure is met perfectly by the sanctuary feel of this gorgeous oasis. Make it your own!
