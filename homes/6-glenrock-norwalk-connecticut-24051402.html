About this home

Beautiful recently renovated to the highest standards one bedroom ranch style condo in desirable Glenrock Community. Park like grounds, gorgeous inground pool with lounging area and Club House. Elegant kitchen boasts new cabinets, granite counters and stainless steel appliances. Spacious living room with dining area provides plenty of room for entertaining guests. Modern full bath and balcony off slider from the large bedroom. Hardwood floors throughout. Washer and dryer in the condo, as well as garage and plenty of space for storage. This is a commuters dream. Amazing location close to new redone Merritt Seven train station, that leads into Sono for an easy NYC commute, restaurants, shopping, Route 7, Merritt Parkway and I-95. 2 month security deposit, excellent credit and references are a must. Move-in of $300 and move-out of $200 paid by tenant.
