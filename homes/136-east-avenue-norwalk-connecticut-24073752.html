About this home

Welcome to The Landings, a charming riverfront building with Nantucket-inspired design. This five-story, 17-unit residence features a gracious lobby, an elevator, and under-building parking-all set along the scenic Norwalk River. Unit 2A is an end ranch on the second floor, conveniently level with the main lobby. You can enter from the main parking lot and walk straight to your condo without using the elevator. If you park in your assigned spot in the garage, you only need to ride the elevator one floor. Bathed in natural light, this two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath unit has a gas-log fireplace and a flagstone balcony overlooking the river. The primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet and an ensuite bath with a spa tub, separate shower, and double-sink vanity, while the second bedroom has its own full bath with a shower. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances-including a brand-new oven/range and microwave (installed February 2025)-and mahogany cabinets. The combination living room/dining room and foyer have hardwood floors. The kitchen, laundry room and all bathrooms have tile floors. Both bedrooms have wall-to-wall carpeting. Each condo comes with a private storage closet on the garage level. Residents also enjoy a 15-foot riverfront walkway and sitting area at ground level, accessible via an exterior stairway. Please note the condo association is considering a special assessment in the near future.
