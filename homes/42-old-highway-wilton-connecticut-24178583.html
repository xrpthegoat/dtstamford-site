About this home

Easy town and school access with a neighborhood feel. This South Wilton 4/5 bedroom designer-owned home is set way back off the road and has an amazing convenient location. Owners and neighbors have created a wonderful community feeling with trails through the backyards leading to town! Built just nine years ago by Petrucci Builders, this beautifully appointed Colonial delivers the craftsmanship and character that buyers covet without the compromises of an older home. From the moment you step inside, the open, flowing layout draws you forward, where generous proportions, elevated ceilings, premium finishes and an abundance of natural light set the tone for a life well-lived. Whether you're hosting a dinner party that spills from the kitchen to the living room, or settling into a quiet evening at home, every space has been thoughtfully designed to meet the moment. Five generous bedrooms and 4.5 luxurious baths ensure everyone has room to breathe and privacy to retreat. The primary suite is in a category of its own: a true sanctuary, anchored by a fireplace that transforms even the coldest Connecticut night into something intimate and indulgent. And the location? Rarely does a home this polished sit this close to Wilton's beloved town center with its shops, restaurants, and the easy rhythms of a community that knows how to slow down without feeling left behind.
