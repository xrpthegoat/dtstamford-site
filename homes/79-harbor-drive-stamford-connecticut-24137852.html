About this home

Welcome to Schooner Cove - Coastal Living at Its Finest. Discover the blend of style, comfort, and functionality in this stunning three bedroom, two and a half bath townhouse, a sought-after waterfront community in Shippan Point. Just a short stroll to beautiful beaches and scenic parks. Enter inside to a bright, open floor plan that creates a light and airy atmosphere throughout the main living space. The fully renovated kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and ample cabinetry. The inviting living room centers around a cozy wood-burning fireplace and flows seamlessly into the open dining area, great for relaxing or entertaining. Sliding glass doors lead to a spacious deck overlooking the peaceful courtyard - an ideal spot for morning coffee or evening gatherings. The main level also includes a primary suite with a full bath, offering convenience and privacy. Upstairs, the second primary bedroom impresses with a walk-in closet and a spa-like ensuite bath. A third bedroom provides flexible space for a guest room, home office, or gym. Additional highlights include a garage, plus an extended driveway that accommodates an extra car. Easy access to Stamford's vibrant downtown, Metro-North train, and major highways.
