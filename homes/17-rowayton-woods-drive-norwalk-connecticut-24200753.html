About this home

Welcome to effortless, sophisticated living at 17 Rowayton Woods. This beautifully refreshed residence offers a seamless blend of comfort and style, perfectly positioned within one of the area's most sought-after communities. Ideally situated on the main level, the home offers easy, convenient living in a peaceful setting. Inside, the home feels bright and airy, with a fresh, neutral palette and brand-new windows that fill the space with natural light. A new sliding glass door opens to a private balcony, extending the living space outdoors. The spacious living room centers around a cozy fireplace and flows naturally into the kitchen, creating an inviting setting for both quiet nights at home and easy entertaining. A generous entryway creates a welcoming sense of arrival. The spacious primary suite offers a peaceful retreat, while a second bedroom and two full bathrooms provide a thoughtful balance of privacy and functionality. Two private storage areas provide plenty of additional space for everyday needs. Enjoy the serenity of Rowayton Woods while remaining just moments from shops, restaurants, and everyday conveniences.
