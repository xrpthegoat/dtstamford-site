About this home

Nestled atop a tranquil knoll, this elegant colonial offers a harmonious blend of style and comfort, conveniently positioned near Wilton town center, New Canaan, Ridgefield and NY state. Boasting soaring ceilings and generously proportioned living spaces, this residence is designed to accommodate both grand entertaining and intimate gatherings. The heart of the home is a magnificent country kitchen with newly painted cabinets, seamlessly flowing into an inviting family room adorned with a stately and newly refaced fireplace. French doors lead to an outdoor dining and entertainment haven, perfect for alfresco evenings. The luxurious primary suite is a private retreat, featuring a newly renovated spa-inspired bathroom (2023) with a soaking tub and an oversized shower. A large walk-in closet and a cozy fireplace complete this serene sanctuary, ideal for relaxing on chilly evenings. An additional newly updated Jack and Jill bathroom (2023) enhances the home's sophisticated functionality. Designed with both convenience and privacy in mind, the residence offers front and back staircases leading to five well-appointed bedrooms. A newly installed whole-house generator provides peace of mind and to top it off: a new, premium, architectural shingle roof (2023). The expansive lower level & attic present endless possibilities for customization and expansion. This exceptional property is a rare offering in a coveted location, promising an unparalleled lifestyle of luxury and tranquility.
