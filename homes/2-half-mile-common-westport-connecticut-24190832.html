About this home

Meticulously maintained and move-in ready, 2 Half Mile Common offers comforts, flexibility, and convenience. With 4 generously sized bedroom this light-filled home features a finished lower level with a full bath, gym, and recreation space, plus a separate playroom over the garage that's ideal as a second home office, studio, or playroom. Enjoy a fenced backyard with beautifully landscaped grounds, hot tub and outdoor kitchen, perfect for entertaining or play. Need more space? There is a 3rd floor just waiting to be finished! Ideally located on a quiet cul-de-sac, this home is within walking distance to Staples High School and Wakeman Town Farm, and just minutes from downtown Westport, beaches, shopping, dining, and Metro-North for an easy NYC commute. A rare opportunity to enjoy turnkey living in one of Westport's most convenient locations.
