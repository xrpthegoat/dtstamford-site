About this home

Welcome to this beautifully appointed three-level townhouse condominium located in the desirable Cove section of Stamford, Connecticut! Offering approximately 1,580 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this inviting residence combines comfort, style, and convenience. Featuring three spacious bedrooms with one of the bedrooms on the third level, two-and-a-half bathrooms, this home offers the perfect balance of functionality and modern living. Step inside and discover hardwood floors throughout, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The renovated kitchen is a standout feature, showcasing elegant granite countertops, an oversized center island with additional granite workspace, and stainless steel appliances-perfect for everyday living, entertaining, and gathering with family and friends. The living area provides a cozy retreat, highlighted by a gas-powered fireplace that adds warmth and ambiance, while the private balcony offers the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind at the end of the day. Additional comforts include central heating and air conditioning, providing year-round climate control, and the convenience of an attached garage. With three levels of living space, generous bedroom accommodations, stylish updates, and desirable amenities, this townhouse offers an exceptional opportunity to enjoy comfortable, low-maintenance living in one of Stamford's sought-after neighborhoods.
