About this home

This cozy and updated one-bedroom condo unit in desirable Fairway Commons is located in one of Stamford's most convenient locations! It has all one needs to live in comfort and is situated in a private location within the complex. Upon entering, you'll find a spacious living room with attractive dark-stained hardwood flooring creating a warm ambiance. Sliders lead to a nice-sized deck, perfect for morning coffee or dining al fresco. The kitchen is well-equipped with newer stainless steel appliances. The primary bedroom has a generously sized closet and easy access to the updated full bath with tub/shower combination. Six panel doors, custom moldings and recessed lighting are some of the special features. There is plenty of storage space with a large closet in the living room and an entire storage locker in the adjacent building. Laundry is conveniently located in the unit. Parking is easy with an assigned space and plenty of visitor parking. Situated just steps from Scalzi Park, you will have quick access to green space, sports fields, and walking trails. The Ridgeway Shopping Center, restaurants, golf courses, and public transportation are all close by, making everyday living easy and efficient. Just minutes away, you will find bustling downtown Stamford with its theaters, Mill River Park and the Stamford Town Center. This rental is a wonderful option for those looking for a comfortable home with excellent proximity to all that is great about Stamford!
