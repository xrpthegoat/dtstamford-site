Stamford Health Opens New Breast Center in Tully Health Center

Stamford Health’s new Odyssey Group Breast Center opens in 2026, delivering advanced, coordinated care in downtown Stamford.

Uploaded · August 11, 2026

The new Odyssey Group Breast Center at Stamford Health’s Tully Health Center officially opened on August 6, 2026, marking a major expansion in local breast care services. For residents of Lower Fairfield County, this development means greater access to advanced imaging, diagnostics, and coordinated clinical expertise—all under one roof. As a real estate agent in Stamford, I see this as a signal of growing health infrastructure that supports long-term community vitality and property value stability in the downtown area.

What's happening

Stamford Health unveiled the newly transformed Odyssey Group Breast Center on August 6, 2026, following a $10 million philanthropic gift from the Odyssey Group Foundation [1]. The center, located on the first floor of the Tully Health Center, spans 13,000 square feet and represents the final milestone in Stamford Health’s Campaign for Women and Babies [1]. The renovation included relocating and reimagining the entire facility to improve patient flow, increase privacy, and enhance comfort [2]. The project began in January 2023 and was designed to support a projected 35,000 mammograms annually [2]. The center brings together advanced imaging, diagnostics, and clinical expertise in a single, coordinated environment, allowing for faster, more seamless care from screening through treatment and reconstruction [1]. In fiscal year 2025, Stamford Health performed over 33,000 mammograms and diagnosed more than 250 new breast cancers, underscoring rising demand for such services [1].

A community investment with lasting impact

The Odyssey Group Foundation has a long-standing relationship with Stamford Health, dating back to 2008 with a $2 million donation for a robotic surgical system, followed by a $10 million commitment in 2011 for a trauma center, and another $10 million in 2022 for this breast center [2]. The new facility was developed to address increasing patient volume and to provide a more humane, efficient experience. The center is designed to co-locate specialists, reduce wait times, and improve outcomes through integrated care. While the sources do not specify changes to staffing, equipment models, or appointment availability, the emphasis on “person-centered” care and improved infrastructure suggests a measurable upgrade in service delivery [1].

This new breast center is more than a medical upgrade—it’s a marker of sustained investment in downtown Stamford’s health and wellness infrastructure. For renters and buyers, this signals that the city is prioritizing high-quality, accessible medical services in its core. The presence of a major, well-funded medical center like this one enhances the appeal of living near downtown, especially for those who value proximity to comprehensive care without long commutes.

The fact that this facility was made possible through a $10 million private donation also reflects a broader trend: Stamford is becoming a hub for private-sector philanthropy in healthcare. This kind of sustained, large-scale giving suggests long-term confidence in the city’s growth trajectory and quality of life. For real estate, this kind of investment often correlates with stable or rising property values over time, particularly in walkable, transit-accessible neighborhoods.

From a rental standpoint, properties near healthcare hubs like the Tully Health Center may see increased demand, especially among individuals and families prioritizing health and convenience. The center’s opening adds to the list of amenities that make downtown Stamford competitive with other urban centers in the region. While the sources don’t detail changes in nearby housing stock, zoning, or development timelines, the overall trend is clear: healthcare infrastructure is a growing differentiator in the local real estate market.

In short, this new center isn’t just about better mammograms—it’s about a community that’s investing in its future.
