About this home

Tucked away on 1.25 serene acres in Westport's sought-after Coleytown neighborhood, this timeless 5-bedroom Georgian Colonial seamlessly blends grand architectural elegance with comfortable everyday living. Welcomed by an inviting, large front porch this impressive home offers exceptional space both inside and out. Inside, the grand main level flows effortlessly between a large formal living room, a gracious formal dining room, and a designated home office. At the heart of the home, a spacious, sunny kitchen connects directly to a casual den/TV room, while double doors lead from both the kitchen and dining room onto a lovely patio ideal for outdoor dining and entertaining. One of the home's most show-stopping features is the massive bonus room,expertly crafted from real barn wood and complete with exposed timber beams, wide plank wood floors, and a dedicated fireplace, perfect for cozy evenings or hosting large gatherings. Throughout the main house, you will find a total of 3 fireplaces creating a warm, inviting atmosphere. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite serves as a peaceful retreat featuring a massive walk-in closet and a remarkably large primary bathroom. A second spacious bedroom functions as its own suite, featuring a private bathroom and an exclusive balcony overlooking the grounds. The resort-style backyard oasis centers around an inground pool and a fun, fully equipped pool house featuring a private bathroom and its own fireplace for chilly evening gathering.
