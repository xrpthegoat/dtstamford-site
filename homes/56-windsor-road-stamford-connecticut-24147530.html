About this home

Ideally situated, this charming home offers a sophisticated blend of timeless character and modern updates, creating an inviting retreat just minutes from the city's best amenities.The sun-filled interior greets you with gleaming hardwood floors and an inviting living room centered around a fireplace, a formal dining room perfect for hosting, and a gourmet eat-in kitchen elegantly appointed with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. Designed for both comfort and entertaining, the expansive family room features a stunning stone fireplace and custom wet bar, while a dedicated home office and sunroom provide additional flexibility. The upper level serves as a private retreat, featuring two spacious bedrooms and a well-appointed full bath. Outside, the private yard features a large patio perfect for entertaining and a detached garage for added convenience. Perfectly situated near premier shopping, dining, and easy commuter routes, this meticulously maintained home offers an unparalleled opportunity to enjoy a vibrant lifestyle from a quiet, picturesque setting.
