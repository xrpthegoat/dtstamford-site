About this home

Experience coastal living in this beautifully crafted New England home in Old Greenwich's waterfront district adjacent to Greenwich Point Park & Beach. Enjoy traditional architecture with modern comfort and sweeping views of Long Island Sound. Each room maximizes exposure to the tranquil waters, creating a peaceful, airy ambiance. This impeccably updated traditional home seamlessly blends contemporary features with the timeless charm of a shore colonial. With over 4,300 sq ft, this spacious three-story home offers expansive water views from nearly every room. The kitchen showcases modern efficiency with ample counter space and storage, complemented by a charming dining area.
