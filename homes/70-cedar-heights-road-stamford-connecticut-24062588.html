About this home

This beautiful craftsman farmhouse has been reimagined with refined modern elegance. A welcoming warmth is created all year round as the property greets with the charm of a farmer's wall and wrap-around porch in the front, all the way to the large, elevated back deck overlooking an entirely usable yard framed by mature plants. The grand, oversized detached two-car garage includes a built-in workshop with an attic storage space. Inside includes a beautifully remastered fireplace with remodeled floors, walls, and lighting to anchor an open floor plan with an extraordinary eat-in kitchen with a culinary-grade range/stove/exhaust fan, farmer's sink, center island, and abundant cabinetry. Sophisticated finishes complete the entire space. Upstairs includes three bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a walk-up additional room that allows you to create an added space of your dreams. The lower level includes laundry, storage, and a walk-in closet. Location is everything and this home has exceptional accessibility to commuting, restaurants, parks, schools, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, boutique shopping, and Stamford's downtown. This home creates seasonal entertaining with soothing spaces inside and out, all year long.
