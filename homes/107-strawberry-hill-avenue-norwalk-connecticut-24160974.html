About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained Hi-Ranch in Norwalk, CT, offering the perfect blend of space, functionality, and modern updates. The upper level features an open-concept layout with an updated kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances and an oversized island that flows naturally into the living room. From the dining area you can step out onto a generously sized deck, great for entertaining and it also allows for great views of the beautiful sunsets. There are 3 generously sized bedrooms, and a full bathroom with a jetted tub to complete this level. The lower level features a fourth bedroom, spacious family room/playroom with sliding doors to a private patio, full bathroom, and laundry room - well-suited for guests, extended family, or an in-law setup. Additional highlights include hardwood floors throughout, central air, ample storage, 200-amp electrical service, an energy-efficient tankless hot water heater, and an oversized two-car garage with plenty of room for storage. Perfectly located within walking distance to all three schools and just minutes to major highways and the Metro North train station, this home offers both comfort and commuter convenience.
