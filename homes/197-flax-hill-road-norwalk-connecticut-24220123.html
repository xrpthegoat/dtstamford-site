About this home

Location, convenience, and comfort all in one! Welcome to this beautifully updated 1-bedroom, 1-bath end-unit townhouse located in a small complex just minutes from the heart of South Norwalk! Featuring newly renovated flooring, kitchen, and full bathroom, this home offers a fresh, modern feel with the convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer. Generous closet space and additional storage provide plenty of room to stay organized. Enjoy your own back deck overlooking a nice, level yard, perfect for relaxing outdoors. Pet-friendly living, one assigned parking space, and additional visitor parking add to the convenience. Trash pickup and grounds maintenance are included in the rent! Ideally situated near downtown SoNo, restaurants, shopping, Flax Hill Park, neighborhood walking trails, public transportation, and the South Norwalk Metro-North train station, with easy access to I-95. Enjoy the convenience of being close to everything while having a comfortable place to call home!
