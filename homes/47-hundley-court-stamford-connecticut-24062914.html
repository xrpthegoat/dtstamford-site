About this home

Nicely maintained colonial home with versatile floor plan. Spacious living room with hardwood floors open to the formal dining room with sliders to the deck and yard. Updated kitchen with granite counter tops, breakfast bar, tile floors and new stainless steel refrigerator & stove arriving Saturday. There is a den with a door to the backyard and a powder room. The 2nd floor has a master bedroom with 2 closets plus two more bedrooms all with hardwood floors and a full bath with double sinks, granite counters and a tub with a new shower door being put it. The finished walk up attic is great for an additional bedroom. The lower level is finished with a nice room with a door to the back yard, a full bath, laundry and the mechanical room. A large detached garage and a brand new roof being put on are two more benefits of this fine home.
