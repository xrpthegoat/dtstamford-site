About this home

Nestled on over 2 acres, this graciously proportioned 5 bedroom home offers over 5400 sq ft of living space and a well thought out floor plan. The first floor features a formal living and dining room, a large light and bright eat-in kitchen opens to the patio and an additional casual dining room/or den with fireplace. A beautiful family room, office, and sun-room complete the first floor. The second floor has an extensive primary suite with fireplace, two large walk-in closets, and a nursery/gym. There are four additional bedrooms, laundry, plus a bonus room for an au pair or extra office. Peaceful and private, yet just minutes to town, schools and train with an easy commute to Grand Central.
