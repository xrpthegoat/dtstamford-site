About this home

This welcoming home is on a peaceful cul de sac close to the Rowayton border. It's spacious Living Room/Dining Room has a wood burning fireplace and built ins. The recently updated kitchen opens into the bright, airy family room with eating area, sky lit ceilings and a gas fireplace. French Door sliders open on two sides of this room and one accesses the deck. There are five bedrooms, one of which could be easily used as a home office. The three full baths have been updated. This house has been lovingly maintained,.(see list of improvements). The private yard is level and professionally landscaped. Don't miss this conveniently located gem!
