About this home

Thoughtfully updated for the way people live today, this expansive 5-bedroom Colonial offers generous space, natural flow, and flexible living areas throughout. The home opens to a large foyer with high ceilings and a beautiful staircase that immediately creates a sense of openness. The living room flows into the dining room, both filled with natural light and well suited for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen has custom cabinetry, a Wolf gas range, and a Sub-Zero refrigerator, with access to the deck for easy indoor-outdoor living. A first-floor office provides a quiet and functional work-from-home space. Upstairs, the primary suite includes two walk-in closets, along with three additional bedrooms on the second level. The third floor adds even more versatility, offering space that can be used as a bedroom suite, playroom, or entertainment area. The lower level provides additional usable space and works well as a gym, movie room, or recreation area. Located on a quiet street in Norwalk, near Rowayton and close to beaches, shopping, and restaurants, this home provides generous space, a practical layout, and room to grow.
