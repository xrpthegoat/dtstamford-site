About this home

Set on a sunny acre well back from the street, this beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers over 3,300 square feet of light-filled living space surrounded by mature trees, flowering plantings, and expansive lawns. Soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, and oversized windows create a seamless connection to the surrounding property while filling the home with natural light. A long private driveway leads to the two-car garage and generous parking area for guests. Inside, double doors open to a welcoming entry hall and dramatic step-down living room centered around one of two striking stone fireplaces. The updated eat-in chef's kitchen features a large center island, custom cabinetry, premium appliances, and an effortless flow into the dining area where the second fireplace creates a warm setting for entertaining and everyday living. The second floor includes a spacious primary suite with full bath along with three additional bedrooms and a shared hall bathroom. Above, the third-floor loft offers flexibility as a playroom, home office, or media room complete with built-in bookcases and sweeping views over the gardens and wooded landscape beyond. Outdoor living is equally inviting with multiple decks, a firepit area, and expansive lawns ideal for recreation or quiet relaxation. Coveted Westover offers privacy with convenient access to downtown Stamford, Metro North, shopping, and restaurants. A peaceful retreat just minutes from it all.
