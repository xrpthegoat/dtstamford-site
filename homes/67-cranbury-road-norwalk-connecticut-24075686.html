About this home

Poised along a peaceful pond, this charming home enjoys a private setting just minutes from downtown Westport. Inside, tall beamed ceilings and beautifully flowing living spaces filled with natural light create a home you'll love for relaxation and entertaining alike. The open living and dining area features 13' cathedral ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace. The eat-in kitchen enjoys sliders to the deck, perfect for stepping outside with your morning coffee. A sunny home office is poised at the front of the home. Upstairs, four generously-sized family bedrooms, including a primary suite with 13' ceilings, form restful retreats. The back porch invites casual gatherings and great conversation, looking out across the serene backyard with sylvan water views. The property is home to an abundance of wildlife including ducks and blue heron along the pond, white owls, red-tailed hawk, and an occasional eagle. Enjoy fishing, kayaking, taking in gorgeous sunsets and savoring the beauty of each season in your backyard retreat. The gardens were recently redesigned to incorporate native plants of the Aspetuck Land Trust. The property is equipped with an electric dog fence and is plug-in generator ready. Enjoying a coveted location, discover restaurants, shops and parks just minutes away. Walk to Cranbury Market, the local deli and Greek grocery for your favorite provisions. A ten-minute walk brings you to the MoCA museum of contemporary art or Earthplace nature center.
