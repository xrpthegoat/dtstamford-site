About this home

Private and convenient with water views from almost every room! Located on a peninsula overlooking Long Island Sound in the hidden gem of Village Creek with sandy beach, tennis courts, picturesque marina, dog park and playground. The spacious layout of this fully furnished, contemporary home allows for flexible living, working and entertaining spaces. The many windows and vaulted ceilings allow for an amazing light-filled home while the wood burning fireplace will keep you cozy all winter. The streamlined and updated kitchen with quarry stone counters and like-new appliances also takes advantage of the views. 3 bedrooms with baths on different floors offers maximum privacy and convenient flow. The large bonus room can act as an additional bedroom with its queen bed and pullout couch. The deck and patio allow total enjoyment of the beautiful setting with easy access to the kitchen. Village Creek is in close proximity to trains, SONO, the SONO Collection and pickleball. The owner is happy to leave 2 kayaks and a paddle board so you can fully enjoy this unique and fun waterfront lifestyle. Available October 1 to June 30 plus the month of August, 2025. Rates are variable depending on season and duration.
