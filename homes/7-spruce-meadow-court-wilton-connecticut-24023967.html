About this home

Nestled on nearly 2.5 acres of meticulously landscaped grounds in Wilton's most coveted estate cul-de-sac, this gated masterpiece redefines luxury living. With over 10,000 SF of opulent living space, every detail of this home exudes sophistication and charm. From the grand private study adorned with arched doors to the multiple gathering areas and a chef's kitchen equipped with top-of-the-line appliances, this residence offers a seamless blend of elegance and comfort. Entertain in style with a tennis court, heated pool, spa, and a custom outdoor fireplace pavilion featuring a built-in grill area-perfect for hosting unforgettable gatherings with family & friends. Car enthusiasts will delight in the 4-bay garage providing ample storage space, while the stunning stone entry gate & wrought iron fencing ensure privacy and security around the manicured lawns & gardens. Descend to the lower level and discover a state-of-the-art home theater, complete with surround sound both indoors and outdoors, a sprawling game room, and a home gym-offering endless opportunities for relaxation and recreation. With over $650k worth of meticulous improvements, including a whole-home commercial-grade generator, this estate is truly unparalleled. Discover why the current owners cherished raising their family here and are now ready to pass the keys to the next generation of homeowners ready to create lasting memories in this exceptional residence.
