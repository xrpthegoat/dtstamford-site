About this home

Discover the charm of this 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment located on the second floor of a Saugatuck neighborhood two-family home. This ideally situated apartment is only minutes from the train and Saugatuck restaurants. It boasts a flexible floor plan with large, bright rooms, high ceilings, and a finished walk-up attic space perfect for a home office, exercise room, art studio, or storage. The updated kitchen features granite counters, while additional highlights include central A/C, hardwood floors, and a whole-house generator powered by solar energy. Enjoy the convenience of a one-car detached garage bay and your own washer and dryer in the basement. The tenant is responsible for oil/heat, cable/internet, water, and liability insurance, while the landlord covers electricity (within an estimated usage), sewer, grounds maintenance, taxes, and trash service. This prime location is just minutes from downtown, Longshore, and Compo Beach, making it a true Westport gem! No pets please.
