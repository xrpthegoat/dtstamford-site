About this home

At the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable North Stamford, you first glimpse this dramatic contemporary jewel as you approach the private enclave on which it is set. As you draw near the front door, turn before entering and savor the expansive front lawn gently rolling down to meet a sparkling spring fed pond and newly refurbished dock. Once inside, you are instantly elevated by the abundance of natural light pouring in from 2 skylights and expanses of glass filling its soaring 2 1/2 story entrance foyer. To your right is an elegant step down living room with marble surround fireplace, crown moldings, and large windows that seamlessly flows into a spacious formal dining room perfect for entertaining family and friends. To the left is the gorgeous family room with beamed vaulted ceiling, beautiful fieldstone fireplace, 2 new skylights, and a wall of windows overlooking the pond on one side and sliding doors to a wrap around backyard deck on the other side. The open floor plan leads you effortlessly into the breakfast area with two more sets of sliding doors, a large kitchen with center island, walk-in pantry, a butler's pantry, and laundry room. An elegant powder room completes the main level. Upstairs the spacious primary suite boasts a high ceiling, 2 large walk-in closets, and ensuite bathroom with marble topped double sink vanity, shower, whirlpool tub, skylight, and large linen closet. There are 3 additional bedrooms all with hardwood floors under the carpeting.
