About this home

Welcome to this classic Coastal Connecticut colonial, ideally situated in the coveted Tokeneke Association - offering space, comfort, and versatility for every member of the family. With 5 bedrooms - including a convenient first-floor guest suite and four bedrooms on the second level - this home is designed for gracious everyday living. The main floor features a formal living room, elegant dining room, casual dining area, and a spacious office, all flowing effortlessly together. The chef's kitchen opens to both casual dining and a warm, fireplace-lit gathering room, the heart of the home. A large mudroom with garage and back-entrance access, plus a separate laundry room, adds everyday ease. Retreat to the private primary suite, complete with an en-suite bath and two walk-in closets. The second floor also offers a second en-suite bedroom, two additional bedrooms sharing a beautifully renovated hall bath, a versatile sitting/work area, and abundant storage throughout. The finished basement boasts a spectacular wine room, that holds 1600 bottles, and casual sitting area - perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The attached garage holds two-cars plus, and a third bay is in the "barn" - a blank canvas ready to become a guest quarters, entertaining space, second-floor hangout, studio, or whatever you can imagination. Outside, unwind on the beautiful stone patio, set against an expansive, green yard. A rare opportunity in one of the most sought-after associations.
