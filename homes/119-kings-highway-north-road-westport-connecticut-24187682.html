About this home

Don't miss this great opportunity close to town. The .87Acre lot is elevated ,rolling and ideally positioned off Kings Highway. There are distance views of the river and town through the trees. There is an older small 1400 sq ft. single family home that has been a rental for many years. The previous owners in the 30's made each floor into apartments. Each level with its own entrance features a living room , one bedroom, kitchen and full bath. This small house offers a unique opportunity to be a ADU or separate office as part of a new home that can be built.There is a sewer hook up for the new house has been paid in full. Please refer to town hall for required building regulations. This is a rare opportunity for the next owner to create something special close to town. Need permission to walk property and see house.
