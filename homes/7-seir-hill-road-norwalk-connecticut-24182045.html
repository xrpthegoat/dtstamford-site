About this home

Spacious 2 bedroom /2.5 bath end unit townhouse with a bonus room (office) and an attached 2-car garage in Norwalk! The main level features a living room with a wood burning fireplace, dining room with lots of light and kitchen with newer microwave and dishwasher. On the top level there are 2 en-suite bedrooms with huge windows, high ceilings, hardwood floors and renovated baths. On the lower level is a bright bonus room that would be perfect for a home office/playroom, etc and interior access to the attached 2-car garage. The front door faces a charming courtyard and the complex is very convenient to the Merritt 7 train station, major roadways, restaurants and shopping. Please scheduled your private showing before it's gone!
