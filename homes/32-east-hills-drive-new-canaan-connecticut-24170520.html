About this home

Welcome home to this bright and beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath condo, perched at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac for ultimate privacy. The updated kitchen shines with brand new countertops and appliances, while a new washer/dryer makes everyday living effortless. Ample attic storage offers a rare bonus you won't find in most condos - room to breathe, organize, and grow. Add in a coveted New Canaan address just minutes from downtown shops, dining, and the train, and you have a true move-in-ready gem.
