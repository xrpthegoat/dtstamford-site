About this home

Located in the North Street Elementary School District and just minutes to town, this chic updated 5-bedroom 4-bath home is set back from the road on .90 acres and features a private beautifully landscaped yard. Step inside to discover an inviting open floor plan, perfect for modern living and entertainment. The spacious living room flows seamlessly into a den/office, adjacent to the family room and updated gourmet kitchen. Hosting dinner parties is a breeze with the formal dining room conveniently located across from the kitchen. Relax in the sumptuous primary bedroom suite, featuring a large walk-in closet and an updated bathroom. A double bedroom, full bath, and laundry room complete the main level, offering convenience and versatility. Upstairs, three generously sized family bedrooms and another full bath await, providing ample space for family and guests. The finished lower level adds even more living space, with a large office, play/family room, gym, full bath, mudroom, and a well-appointed wine closet. Outside, escape to your own private oasis in the beautifully landscaped backyard. French doors from most of the public rooms lead to the expansive deck, equipped with ceiling-mounted heaters and retractable awnings. Entertain with ease in the outdoor kitchen, unwind in the hot tub, gather around the firepit, or dine al fresco in one of the dining areas.
