About this home

Welcome to 14 Wood Ridge Drive ~ This Colonial home with 4 bedrooms & 3 full baths is located on a quiet, tree-lined street in the very convenient, Mid-Ridges neighborhood! Open floor plan with a centered living room that opens to the spacious eat-in Chef's kitchen. Stainless steel appliances, granite countertops and plenty of cabinet storage. Sliders off the kitchen and dining areas provide easy access to the private backyard & patio space. Two bedrooms are located on the main level and share a freshly painted full bathroom with shower/tub combination. Large multipurpose room on the main level with access to the garage; mudroom, home office or den option. The upper level boasts a primary bedroom suite with vaulted ceilings, large walk-in closet , full bathroom with jacuzzi tub and spacious glass step in shower. Upper level also offers a 4th bedroom, full bathroom, laundry & a walk-in attic with tremendous storage space! NEW roof in 2022! Central Air. 1 car attached garage. Lovely yard space with manicured green grounds and back patio just perfect for entertaining family & friends. Demand location makes fast action a must. Schedule your viewing now!
