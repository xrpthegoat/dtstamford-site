About this home

Tucked away on a peaceful one-acre+ property, this charming summer retreat offers privacy and comfort in a serene setting. The home, optimal for one level living features 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths on the main floor, with sun-filled living spaces, hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and an amazing screened-in porch. Enjoy central air, a finished lower level, and seamless indoor-outdoor living perfect for summer entertaining. Outside, the parklike grounds provide room to play, dine al fresco, and take in the tranquil surroundings. The beautiful gunite, salt water pool is the piece de resistance for this rental - it's heated, has a hot tub, and there is an adorable pool house perfect for changing into your suit or for use as a playhouse. One car garage has additional refrigerator and EV charger. Conveniently located near shopping (Trader Joe's, Costco, and Wegman's are just around the corner), dining, and commuting routes, this seasonal rental offers the perfect blend of quiet retreat and accessibility to all of Fairfield County. Ideal for enjoying a memorable Connecticut summer. 31-day minimum
