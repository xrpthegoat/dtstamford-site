About this home

Timeless Colonial charm meets thoughtful modern updates at 152 N Taylor Avenue. This beautifully maintained 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath home offers the perfect balance of character, comfort, and functionality. Inside, you'll find wide plank hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and a flowing floor plan designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and an open layout connecting seamlessly to the dining and living areas. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious full bath, a dedicated home office ideal for today's flexible lifestyle, comfortable secondary bedrooms, and a private primary suite complete with vaulted ceilings, a walk in closet, and an ensuite bath. Recent improvements, including fresh interior and exterior paint, natural gas conversion, and a newer HVAC system installed at the time of conversion, provide peace of mind while preserving the home's timeless appeal. Ideally located near Oak Hills Golf Course and just minutes from Norwalk's vibrant Connecticut Avenue corridor, you'll enjoy convenient access to shopping, dining, parks, and major commuter routes. A home that offers classic character, modern comfort, and a location that continues to grow in popularity.
