About this home

The dream of waterfront living is officially taking shape-construction is underway by renowned builder The O'Dell Group. This exceptional property offers direct water frontage + sweeping, uninterrupted views of the Sound + the iconic Old Mill Beach. Imagine waking up to the soft glow of a sunrise over the water, spending your afternoons enjoying the refreshing salt air, or relaxing to the rhythm of the tide.
