About this home

Spacious 2-bedroom, 1.5 bath townhouse located in the heart of downtown Stamford, just steps from restaurants, shopping, theaters, and the train station. This move-in ready home offers an ideal layout with beautiful hardwood floors throughout, a bright open living/dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. The upper-level features two large bedrooms with wall-to-wall carpet, ample closet space, and a full bath with a tub and shower. Additional perks include central A/C, a powder room on the main floor, a separate laundry room, and plenty of storage in the attic and basement area. The home also includes an attached oversized garage and a reserved exterior parking space. Located on a quiet street but close to I-95 and the train station, this townhouse is perfect for commuters. Enjoy the best of downtown Stamford living in this lovely tri-level corner unit!
