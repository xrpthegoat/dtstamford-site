About this home

Enjoy the best of Westport living in this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath cottage, just under a mile from both the Greens Farms Train Station and scenic Burying Hill Beach. This move-in-ready converted carriage house offers stylish, modern finishes throughout and sits on a meticulously landscaped private quarter-acre, perfect for relaxing or entertaining on the spacious patio. Inside, you'll find a bright, open layout with kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, in-unit washer/dryer, and ample storage, including a full attic. The landlord takes care of landscaping, snow removal, and water, so you can enjoy low-maintenance living in a peaceful, convenient location. Landlord will provide microwave and window blinds if needed. Whether you're commuting to the city or enjoying a beachside lifestyle, this chic cottage is the perfect place to come home to.
