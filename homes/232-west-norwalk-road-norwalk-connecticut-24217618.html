About this home

Tucked away in a beautiful, secluded setting, this tastefully updated residence is designed for both easy everyday living and effortless entertaining. With an inviting pool, expansive lawn stretching down to the Five Mile River, and wonderful indoor and outdoor gathering spaces, this property offers the perfect balance of comfort, style, and natural beauty. Bright and welcoming throughout, the thoughtfully designed interior features tasteful updates, convenient one-level living, and move-in-ready spaces. The finished walk-out lower level adds an entirely new dimension to entertaining, with direct access to the patio and in-ground pool. Whether hosting summer pool parties, gathering with friends and family, or simply relaxing outdoors, the seamless connection between indoor and outdoor spaces makes entertaining a pleasure. Beyond the pool, the gently sloping lawn leads to the banks of the Five Mile River, creating a picturesque backdrop. Surrounded by mature trees and lush landscaping, the property feels like a private retreat, yet is just minutes from the shopping, dining, and amenities of Darien, Rowayton, and New Canaan. A special opportunity to enjoy beautiful surroundings, exceptional entertaining spaces, and the convenience of a prime location.
