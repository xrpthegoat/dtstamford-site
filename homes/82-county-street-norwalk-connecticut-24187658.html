About this home

Conveniently located in the heart of Norwalk, this spacious 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home is being offered for the first time in nearly 60 years. Lovingly maintained by the same owner since 1966, this property offers exceptional versatility and endless possibilities. Situated on one-third of an acre, the property offers a generous yard with plenty of room for entertaining, gardening, play, or simply enjoying the outdoors. Close to shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, major highways, and everything Norwalk has to offer, the location is ideal for today's lifestyle. The standout feature of this property is the legal accessory dwelling unit (ADU) above the oversized two-car garage, providing the perfect setup for multigenerational living, extended family, guests, a home office, or valuable rental income. The main home offers generous living space, while the large two-car garage provides ample storage and parking. Whether you're looking for extra space for family or an investment opportunity, this unique property combines location, flexibility, and decades of pride of ownership in one exceptional offering.
