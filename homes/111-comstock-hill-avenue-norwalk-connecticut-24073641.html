About this home

A Silvermine Sanctuary with endless possibilities! Nestled in the picturesque Silvermine section of Norwalk, this one-of-a-kind 4-bedroom, 4-bath home seamlessly blends charm, functionality, and modern updates. Step into the expansive eat-in kitchen, where French doors lead you to a stunning bluestone patio and sparkling pool, creating the perfect indoor/outdoor retreat for entertaining. Imagine spending spring and summer days poolside, surrounded by lush gardens and a generous, private yard. Inside, the warmth of a river rock wood-burning fireplace invites you to unwind with an evening cocktail or glass of wine. Upstairs, you'll find four spacious bedrooms and three full baths, including two oversized primary suites-each featuring its own private en-suite bathroom. The fully finished lower level has even more versatile living space with gleaming hardwood floors and a modern 3-piece bathroom perfect for a media room, guest quarters, or playroom. Beyond the main home, this property boasts an incredible barn, offering boundless opportunities for the new owner! Whether you envision a luxury in-law suite, a private home office, a state-of-the-art gym, a creative studio, or an epic game room, this space is ready to be transformed to fit your lifestyle. With mechanicals updated within the last five years and a brand-new GenerLink backup generator connection, you can enjoy total peace of mind.
