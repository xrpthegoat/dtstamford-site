About this home

Effortless Living in Desirable Sylvan Knoll This diamond-in-the-rough 2-BR end-unit townhome offers the perfect blend of comfort, potential, & convenience in the highly sought-after, 100% owner-occupied Sylvan Knoll complex. Whether you're just starting out or looking to downsize, this could be the perfect fit! Step inside to a spacious LR w/hdwd flrs which flows into the generous DR-perfect for everyday living & entertaining. The Kitchen is full of possibility & awaits your personal touch and creative vision. Upstairs, discover a relaxing retreat with a generous Primary BR, an add'l BR, & a Full Bath. The LL offers versatile space ideal for a home office, gym, or rec room, w/direct access to laundry, newer mechanicals, & ample storage. Add'l storage is available in the attic via pull-down stairs. Enjoy a true neighborhood feel in this pet-friendly, owner-occupied complex-where rentals are not permitted, ensuring a strong sense of community. Set on 13 beautifully landscaped acres, the complex features sidewalks throughout, a picturesque courtyard setting, and a playground for all to enjoy. Located just minutes from I-95, Cove Island Park, Chelsea Piers, schools, shopping, and the train to NYC, w/easy access to Downtown Stamford and Harbor Point's vibrant entertainment districts. Add'l highlights include: gas heat & central air, common charges include taxes, water, and sewer and hardwood floors
