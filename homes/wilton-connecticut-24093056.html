About this home

Welcome to 127 Nod Hill Road, Wilton's hidden gem-a bit like finding a unicorn-rare and full of potential! Nestled on a sprawling 2.8 acres, this property boasts unparalleled views overlooking the reservoir, making it an oasis of tranquility in a bustling world. The current 892 square-foot, 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom home is cozy with tenants in place, but truthfully-it's prime for a teardown. This means you have a blank canvas to create your dream abode. And don't forget the charming antique barn that begs to be incorporated into your new masterpiece. It is stored off-site in the owner's barn and ready for the next visionary. Located off the picturesque Nod Hill, this land offers the best Wilton has to offer. Whether you're envisioning a modern marvel or a rustic retreat, this property provides the perfect backdrop.
