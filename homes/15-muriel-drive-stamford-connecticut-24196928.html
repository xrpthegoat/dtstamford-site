About this home

Welcome to 15 Muriel Drive, a charming three-bedroom, two-full-bath home in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood. A welcoming semicircular driveway provides convenient access, while the detached two-car garage offers excellent parking and additional storage. Perfect for first-time buyers, downsizers, or anyone seeking a private alternative to condo living, this home combines comfortable space with an exceptionally convenient location. Enjoy easy access to the Springdale Shopping Center, local restaurants and everyday amenities, along with close proximity to the Merritt Parkway for commuting throughout Fairfield County and beyond. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy the privacy of a single-family home in one of Stamford's most convenient neighborhoods.
