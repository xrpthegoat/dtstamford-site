About this home

Welcome to your coastal haven! This charming 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom Westport Saugatuck Island Beach house offers sunlit living space, perfectly blending comfort and coastal charm. Nestled just a short stroll from Saugatuck Island's sandy shores, this FEMA-compliant home ensures peace of mind and easy beach access. Begin your mornings on the inviting front porch, sipping coffee as the sea breeze greets you. The sunny back porch provides a serene retreat for afternoon relaxation or evening gatherings. Inside, the open-concept layout features a bright living area, a well-appointed kitchen, and ample space for family and guests. Modern amenities include an ev charger, full house generator, unground sprinklers, 2 zones of hydro air, outdoor shower and Thermador cooktop,. Whether you're seeking a year-round residence or a vacation getaway, this beachside gem offers the perfect blend of functionality and seaside allure. Don't miss the opportunity to own this delightful home where every day feels like a beach vacation!
