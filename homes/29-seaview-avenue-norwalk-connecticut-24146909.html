About this home

Start your New Year in this charming 1-Bedroom Apartment with park and water views! This unit offers a cozy eat-in kitchen and bright living room space with all-around large windows that bring in plenty of sunshine, welcoming serene water views right from your bedroom. Enjoy it's ductless central air system that facilitates airflow, it's hardwood flooring all throughout the unit, and free laundry located in the basement. Reserved one parking space and guest parking in the back, as well as on street parking. Furnished pictures show potential layout concept. Live a lifestyle of convenience, Walk to SONO downtown district and 30 minute walk to Calf Pastures beach. Veterans Park across the street for biking or runs. Close to Pickle ball court. Nearby restaurants and quick access to Route 7 and I-95. Minimum 1 year lease. Tenant pays for electric and cable/WIFI.
