About this home

New construction Farmhouse Colonial home situated in a sought-after neighborhood. This stunning home offers a sun-filled open floor plan that is sure to impress with its finishes, size and energy efficiency. The first floor includes a welcoming foyer, an organized mudroom and a gourmet kitchen with a Wolf induction cooktop, large island and pantry. Coffered ceilings and a gas fireplace finish off the formal living room while the family room features sliding doors opening to an outside patio to grill and dine al fresco. The 2nd floor offers 5 bedrooms and a laundry room. The primary bedroom suite is spacious with an oversized walk-in closet and vaulted ceilings spanning 18' in height. The bedroom off the primary bedroom could be a 2nd home office or nursery. There is a 6th bedroom and office on the 3rd floor along with an additional bonus space which could be used as an au pair suite or recreational room. In the basement you will find more finished space to be used as a lower-level playroom or fitness area along with plenty of storage space. The house has roughed in wiring for future solar and two 50amp ports for electric car charging in the garage. An energy recovery ventilator has also been installed to help cycle fresh air from outside to inside the home while keeping the home either warm or cool depending on the season. This location offers convenience to nearby restaurants, both Darien train stations, highway access, schools, town beaches and town parks.
