About this home

Welcome to 49 Newtown Avenue, an impressive, charming 1910 Cape Cod on a .68 acre lot that feels like a nature preserve. This 2,553 square foot home sits atop a rolling property alongside a quaint waterfall, with exceptional outdoor entertaining areas. The home offers 3 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, with thoughtful updates throughout. Enter to find warm hardwood floors, countless quirks, as if it were a designer's home, and abundant natural light flowing through the main level. The eat-in kitchen is stunning, with vaulted ceilings, exposed beams, high-end stainless steel appliances, and a granite island every chef dreams of. The living room breathes in the outdoors with several picture windows and French doors to a stone patio. It features one of two fireplaces, the second located in the den, creating multiple welcoming gathering spaces. The main level is home to the primary suite, with a tastefully updated bathroom and a walk-in closet. Upstairs, two bedrooms share a large, fully updated bathroom. The home is equipped with forced air oil heat and central air conditioning for year-round comfort, with a tandem garage and basement to round out the package. Outside, a deck and patio extend your living space for entertaining and relaxation, while outbuildings provide storage or workshop potential. The expansive lot offers room to play, garden, or simply enjoy the mature landscaping. Located minutes from Norwalk's beaches, shopping, dining, and Metro-North for easy commuting to NYC.
