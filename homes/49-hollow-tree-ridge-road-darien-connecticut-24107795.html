About this home

A rare, unique "Nathaniel B. Weed" historic Antique Federal Colonial home in Darien, CT updated with modern conveniences being offered TURNKEY and tastefully FURNISHED - just bring your suitcase and enjoy! 3 BR, 2 BA townhouse- style unit where all three levels are loaded with charm and character. Features include a Mansard roof, wide plank flooring, hand hewn beams, multiple fireplaces, newer kitchen with stainless steel appliances and washer dryer in unit. French doors lead to bluestone patio overlooking large, private backyard with plenty of level play area. Located in award-winning Hindley School district. Walk to train, schools, beaches, shopping. Convenient to I-95 and ideal commute to Stamford or NYC. House is fully furnished turnkey. Close to Noroton Heights Train, beaches, shops, exercise, YMCA, Equinox, and Whole Foods. Pet considered.
