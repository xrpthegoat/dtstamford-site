About this home

Offered for the first time in over 80 years, this cherished Shippan Point residence has been proudly owned by the same family since 1943. Set on a beautifully landscaped .37-acre lot, this classic 5-bedroom, 2 full and 2 half bath home blends timeless character. Rich hardwood floors extend throughout all five bedrooms, the spacious living room with fireplace, formal dining room, and inviting sunroom, preserving the home's warmth and original charm. A detached two-car garage and expansive driveway provide ample parking, while the private backyard offers endless opportunities for outdoor enjoyment. Experience the best of Shippan Point living at the Downs Avenue community beach. Ideally located just 3 minutes from West Beach and Czesiks Marina, and only 5 minutes to Stamford Train Station, I-95, and Chelsea Piers Connecticut, providing convenient access to New York City and local amenities. A rare opportunity to restore, renovate, or simply appreciate a home that has remained in one family's care for generations. Sold "AS IS."
