About this home

Move right in to this spacious 4 bedroom colonial walkable to all: train, schools, beaches, parks, library and new Corbin and Heights shopping developments. Enjoy the timeless beauty of this classic colonial in the Hindley school district, its updated kitchen and baths, fenced level yard and large bluestone patio. Step into its entryway with coat closet and space for backpacks and more, leading to its welcoming foyer open to sunny living room with fireplace. Find a bright dining room surrounded by windows and family room overlooking the backyard; as well as eat-in kitchen, half bath and convenient laundry room completing the first floor. All 4 bedrooms and newer full bathroom are on the 2nd floor. The private and inviting backyard and patio are perfect for endless play and entertaining guests. Find plenty of storage in the walk-up attic, unfinished basement and attached garage space.
