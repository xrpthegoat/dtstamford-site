About this home

Step into this freshly updated Riverside home flooded with light. The main level has a front to back living room with a bay window which opens to the dining room. Pocket doors from the dining room open to large well-appointed kitchen. The double door pantry can store all things you need to keep a kitchen organized. Upper level offers three bright bedrooms with one full bath. Half a level down is a family room, laundry room, second full bath and a door to the patio and interior garage access. The babbling brook along the back adds a sense of relaxation. Very well maintained, recently updated, professionally cleaned and ready for move-in.
