About this home

Welcome to this sunny and inviting, well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch, where comfort meets functionality! As you step inside, you'll be greeted by an inviting open living and dining room, showcasing gleaming hardwood floors and an abundance of natural light. A French door seamlessly connects this space to the spacious kitchen, which boasts a fantastic walk-in pantry-perfect for all your culinary needs. This home features a versatile bonus room that can easily serve as a playroom, den, or office space, offering flexibility for your lifestyle. You'll appreciate the convenience of the laundry area located near the bedrooms, making chores a breeze. Freshly repainted inside and out, this home exudes a sense of newness and care. Step outside to discover your own private oasis! The completely fenced-in flat yard is adorned with mature trees and shrubs, providing both beauty and privacy. Enjoy relaxing on the beautiful covered patio or gather around the cozy firepit area for memorable evenings with friends and family. With a one-car garage and an oversized driveway, you'll have ample parking for entertaining guests. This delightful property truly has it all-come see for yourself and fall in love with your new home!
