About this home

Discover a touch of New England history in this lovingly maintained 1911 brick Colonial, seamlessly blending timeless charm with modern amenities. Nestled on nearly half an acre, this classic home offers a harmonious balance of period details with contemporary updates. The formal living room, featuring a stone fireplace, the formal dining room, and the adjoining sun room provide an easy flow and inviting space ideal for entertaining. Outside, the private, landscaped, fenced yard offers a picturesque setting with seasonal blooms, mature ornamental trees, and a lovely large patio area for outdoor gatherings. Conveniently located near the quaint Rowayton neighborhood and just a short walk to the Rowayton train station, this property also holds potential for expansion, providing a valuable investment in the Tokeneke school district.
