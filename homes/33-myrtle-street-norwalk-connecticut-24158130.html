About this home

Located in highly desirable East Norwalk, this beautifully updated Colonial offers the perfect blend of charm, modern upgrades, and commuter convenience. A bright & inviting entryway welcomes you into a warm & comfortable living room with wood-burning fireplace that flows seamlessly into the spacious eat-in kitchen, creating an open & inviting layout for living & entertaining. The Fully Remodeled Kitchen (Spring 2025) feat. new tile flooring, quartz countertops, & new appliances. Enjoy Outdoor dining on the new Trex Deck. 1st-floor BR & full bath provide excellent flexibility for guests, extended family, or a home office. A major 2nd-floor addition brought significant upgrades including Hardie Plank siding, a new roof, central air, and an updated HVAC system. The spacious primary suite feat. built-in speakers, a walk-in closet, & a luxurious bath with double vanity, soaking tub, & separate shower. 2 additional bedrooms share a full bath and are conveniently located near the upstairs laundry, washer/dryer included! Completed in March 2026, the new 3rd-floor bonus room adds valuable flexible living space, perfect for a playroom, gym, or additional office. The Lower Level inc. a home office & workshop/storage area. A generator (and hook up) also included. 5-minute walk to the E. Norwalk train, this home is ideal for commuters. Enjoy being just 1.5 miles from Calf Pasture Beach & close to parks, historic SoNo, & more!
