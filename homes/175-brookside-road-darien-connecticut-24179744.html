About this home

Exceptional new construction estate by K&C Builders Inc., set on over 2.5 private acres on coveted Brookside Road. Offering approximately 11,000 SF across four finished levels, this stunning residence features 6 bedrooms, 8 full baths, and 1 half bath. Designed for elegant entertaining and everyday living, the home showcases a spectacular great room, gourmet chef's kitchen, private study, cabana-style room opening to the pool area, finished lower level, and finished attic. Premium Rhea European Windows flood the home with natural light while providing exceptional energy efficiency and modern sophistication. Extensive custom millwork, architectural ceiling details, designer lighting, detailed interior paneling, custom cabinetry, and bespoke finishes reflect the superior craftsmanship and attention to detail that define every K&C Builders project. The luxurious primary suite offers a spa-inspired bath and oversized custom walk-in closets. Outdoor amenities include professionally landscaped grounds, a resort-style pool, outdoor kitchen, and expansive entertaining spaces designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living. Ideally located minutes from downtown Darien, Metro-North, beaches, country clubs, and Darien schools, this extraordinary residence presents a rare opportunity to own a signature home built to the highest standards of quality, design, and craftsmanship.
