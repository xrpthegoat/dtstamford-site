About this home

Elegant 8,794 Sq Ft Custom Colonial with Resort-Style Outdoor Living: Set behind stone walls and approached by a tree-lined drive, this extraordinary 8,794 sq ft custom Colonial blends timeless design with luxurious living. Finished on four impeccably crafted levels, the home showcases thoughtful design with extensive millwork and custom wall covering that add depth, texture and character, as well as soaring ceilings, and grand proportions throughout. Inside, seven ensuite bedrooms with large walk-in closets offer privacy and comfort, ideal for both large families and guests. Expansive principal rooms are enhanced by five fireplaces, creating inviting spaces for formal entertaining and everyday living. A gourmet kitchen anchors the main level, seamlessly connecting to a gracious dining room, stylish living areas, and a private home office. The retreat-like outdoor space is truly exceptional - featuring a sparkling pool and spa, multiple stone patios, an outdoor fireplace, and a pergola-covered dining area perfect for alfresco entertaining. A three-car garage and meticulous landscaping complete the picture of refined, move-in-ready living. This is a rare opportunity to own a masterfully designed home in a coveted setting, offering comfort, elegance, and privacy at every turn. - Updated pictures of the outdoors are coming soon!
