About this home

Nestled into a signature Wilton neighborhood, this pleasing, well-built Colonial is mere minutes to Weir Farm National Park with its rich artistic history and inviting hiking trails. 8 Granite presents a harmonious blend of natural setting and comfortable living, including 3 amply sized bedrooms; 2.5 baths; generous foyer; front to back living room; eat-in kitchen, with custom high-end cabinetry and convenient washer/dryer; formal dining room; well-located office and great room flowing off the kitchen. Beautiful hardwood floors throughout. This home provides a seamless transition to the great outdoors with its screened porch and attached deck for entertaining guests or quiet conversations. The huge great room, with commanding view of the front property, is another highlight. Its Cathedral ceilings create an airy and majestic space for hosting gatherings or enjoying peaceful moments by the fire. The first-floor office, tucked into the northeast corner of the home, is a private retreat for productivity and creativity. The elegant dining room creates the venue for formal gatherings and memorable meals. The home generator adds peace of mind to the mix. As does proximity to North Wilton's favorite market. 8 Granite Drive invites you to experience all the possibilities of life in the idyllic hamlet of Wilton, CT; long-renowned for the serenity of its open spaces, award-winning education and its unique balance of the natural environment and modern lifestyle
