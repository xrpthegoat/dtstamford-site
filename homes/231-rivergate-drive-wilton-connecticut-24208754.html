About this home

Spacious 5-bedroom Colonial set on 2.16 acres in desirable South Wilton, offering an ideal combination of privacy, space, and convenience. The 3,575 sq. ft. main residence features a first-floor primary suite with vaulted ceiling, skylight, private bath, and generous closet space. The open-concept kitchen, dining, and living areas provide a comfortable flow for everyday living and entertaining, complemented by two fireplaces. A separate den/home office and spacious family room add flexibility to the main level. Upstairs are four additional bedrooms and two full baths. The home also features a finished lower level, enclosed porch, patio, and garage. The property also includes a separate 660 sq. ft. accessory dwelling with 1 bedroom and 1 bath, available to the tenant for an additional cost. An excellent option for those seeking additional living space, a private home office, or flexible accommodations. Please inquire for additional details and pricing. Enjoy a large, level property in an established neighborhood with convenient access to Wilton Center, schools, Metro-North, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, and area trails. A rare opportunity to enjoy exceptional space, privacy, and flexibility in a convenient South Wilton location. Available date is 12/01/2026.
