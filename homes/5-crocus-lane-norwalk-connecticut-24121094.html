About this home

Welcome to 5 Crocus Lane. A turnkey, updated and spacious 4 bedroom Cape Cod home in the desirable West Rocks area of Norwalk on a Neighborhood Friendly Cul-de-Sac. The first floor feature's a large light filled living room with easy access to an open chef's kitchen at the center of the home with generous cabinet space, a breakfast and coffee bar, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops and a new Bosch dishwasher. The kitchen flows naturally to the formal dinning room and opens to an oversized deck with wrap around seating that it's perfect for outdoor entertaining, overlooking an expansive, fully fenced in private & pet friendly yard with flowering bushes, a green house and a shed for extra storage. The family room features a wood burning fire place, there are also 2 bedrooms on the first floor, one of which is currently being used as an office, and a full updated bath. The second level features the master bedroom with a large walk in modular closet system, updated full bath and another bedroom with custom built-ins. This home has hardwood floors throughout and features Cat 5e ethernet in every room, smart thermostats upstairs and downstairs, Lutron light switches in most rooms, brand new LG Smart Wash laundry stack unit, video doorbell and a 50 Amp Circuit in the 2.5 car garage for EV charging and is generator ready. New custom window treatments. Enjoy your privacy, a beautiful neighborhood and still be close to all amenities, parks, beaches, highways and train
