About this home

Waterviews!! Welcome to 8 Seagate Road, a rare opportunity to live in one of Darien's most sought after waterfront communities! Enjoy views of Holly Pond from this custom-built home, offering a terrific layout. The large living room offers built-in cabinetry, a cozy fireplace & leads to the formal dining room, both with beautiful water views! The bright kitchen is nicely sized, with quartz counters, stainless steel appliances & breakfast bar. Adjacent to the kitchen is the family room with vaulted/beamed ceiling & wood burning stove. A den, or home office, as well as a full bathroom completes the1st floor. Upstairs is a fantastic primary bedroom with amazing views of Holly Pond and beyond! There is full bathroom with a tub and separate shower, as well as a walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms & another full bath complete this floor. A full basement too! Outside at 8 Seagate is a terrific place to be! Enjoy the hot tub on the back patio, or the elevated patio is also a great place for outdoor entertaining, with beautiful views and a bonus storage area below. Finally, The Seagate Association offers its residents a private cove with dock to access Holly Pond. Nearby are the Darien YMCA, Weed Beach, restaurants and shopping. New HVAC August 2025 and new hot water heater April 2026. A wonderful place for your next home! The patio is over an original storage garage that was built in 1949. The home you see today was built in 1987.
