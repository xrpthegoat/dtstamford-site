About this home

Your chance to call this charming Westport Cape Cod home! This 4-bedroom, 2-full-bath residence offers 1,961 square feet of living space, plus an additional 958 square feet of finished lower-level space. The large kitchen provides plenty of room to cook, gather, and dine, while two recently updated full bathrooms add a fresh, modern touch. The finished lower level offers flexible space for a home office, gym, playroom, media room, or whatever fits your lifestyle. Generous windows bring plenty of natural light throughout the home. Set on a beautiful corner lot at Maple Avenue South and quiet Cottage Lane, the property offers a peaceful setting, an additional driveway, and a two-car garage. The covered porch overlooks the yard and creates an ideal spot for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or enjoying the changing seasons. The location is ideal for both commuters and those looking to enjoy the best of Westport. Greens Farms train station is just over a mile away, making for an easy NYC commute, while I-95 and the Merritt Parkway are conveniently accessible. Shopping, schools, restaurants, beaches, parks, and Westport's vibrant downtown are all close by.
