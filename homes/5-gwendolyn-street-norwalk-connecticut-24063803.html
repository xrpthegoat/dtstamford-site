About this home

This charming cul-de-sac home provides a great blend of comfort, functionality, and convenience, plus the possibility of an in-law apartment with its own entrance. The thoughtfully designed layout provides an abundance of natural light, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere throughout the home. With ample storage options, you'll find a place for everything, ensuring a clutter-free living experience. The large kitchen serves as the heart of the home, boasting stainless steel appliances and plenty of space for cooking, entertaining, and gathering with loved ones. The outdoor area features two sheds for additional storage and a versatile yard space that invites you to relax, garden, or entertain. Inside, the timeless beauty of hardwood floors adds warmth and character to almost every room. Located just minutes from I-95 and the East Norwalk train station, it's great for commuters. You'll also enjoy easy access to a variety of amenities, including shopping centers, restaurants, gas stations, schools, and city hall. The proximity to these essentials, combined with the cul-de-sac lifestyle, makes this home a rare find.
