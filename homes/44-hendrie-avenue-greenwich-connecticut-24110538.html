About this home

Tucked away behind lattice fencing you'll find privacy and charm in this 4-bedroom, 2.1-bathroom home. Perfectly nestled just seconds away from both the Eastern Middle and Riverside Elementary School, it is also less than half a mile from the train station. Inside discover a welcoming living room with an abundance of windows, a wood burning fireplace, and sliders to the flat side yard. An open floor plan family and dining room, abuts an expansive back deck allowing for seamless indoor/outdoor living. The renovated, gourmet kitchen with skylights, and the wood paneled 3 season room are also ideal for entertaining. Main level includes a bedroom/ office option, with the primary suite, and 2 additional bedrooms upstairs. The partially finished lower level offers a playroom or second office option. Hardwood floors throughout, attached garage, with additional off-street parking. Embrace comfort and convenience with this optimal location, close to Riverside shops, Old Greenwich Village and Tod's Point.
