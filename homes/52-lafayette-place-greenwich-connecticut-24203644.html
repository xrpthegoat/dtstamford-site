About this home

Welcome to this beautiful 2-bedroom, 2-bath corner unit offering approximately 990 square feet of comfortable living space. Located on the third floor, this bright and inviting home features a private balcony and excellent natural light. The apartment has been completely refreshed with newly refinished hardwood floors, fresh paint throughout, and a brand-new gas stove. The thoughtful layout provides spacious bedrooms and two full bathrooms, making it ideal for comfortable everyday living. Building amenities include a laundry room, bike storage, pet-friendly accommodations, storage unit and ample outdoor parking. Garage parking is also available for an additional $60 per month. Enjoy an exceptionally convenient location-steps to shops and restaurants along Greenwich Avenue. Close proximity to the Metro-North train makes commuting and getting around effortless. A beautifully updated home in a prime, in-town location-don't miss this opportunity! Small dog or cat allowed
