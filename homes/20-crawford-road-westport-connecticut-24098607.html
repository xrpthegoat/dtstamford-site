About this home

Nestled in Westport's coveted Old Hill neighborhood, this stunning residence blends classic Colonial design w/modern amenities. You're greeted by the centerpiece of the home - an impressive foyer w/soaring ceilings. The spacious office w/lacquer-finished built-ins includes a fireplace and access to the veranda. For more intimate gatherings, go through the French doors to a sitting room, w/pocket-door to the wet bar. Or head to the main open-floor plan for the kitchen, dining, and living rooms. The chef's kitchen boasts an expansive Marble and Farrow & Ball island, Miele dishwashers, ovens, and warming drawer, Thermador fridge, Viking range, large walk-in pantry, and bay window seating. Easily sit ten in the dining area. From the living room, access the veranda, complete w/grill, built-in speakers, and ceiling fans. Plenty of room to play in the beautiful fenced in yard, w/curated plantings in bloom Apr-Oct. Upstairs, the massive primary suite includes a wood-burning fireplace, terrace, ensuite bath w/heated floors and two walk-in closets. The walk-ins include special surprises and numerous built-ins. 3 other large beds, 2 full baths, and laundry room complete this floor. Ascend to the upper level, w/large finished room, huge 5th bed, & full bath w/walk-in closets. Features: 9ft ceilings, 3-car garage, numerous smart-home features, Lutron lighting, irrigation system, custom millwork, whole-house generator, wetlands-free 1.07 acres. Extraordinary, meticulous, move-in ready.
