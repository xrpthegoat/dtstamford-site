About this home

This lovely 2 bedroom townhouse is an end unit which provides extra light and lots of privacy. Special features include: an updated kitchen with wainscoting and fabulous cabinets (pull out shelves for extra storage), newer refrigerator, washing and dryer 2022, stove/oven 2023, all new outlets and switches, central air, spacious lower level room just waiting for your finishing touches, convenient parking behind the unit, patio, attractive back courtyard. This unit has a nice location in the complex. It is a few minutes from the entrance and borders a quiet neighborhood street. Parking is just a few steps from the kitchen door and there is additional street parking. Sylvan Knoll is conveniently located off of exit 9 on I-95. It is walking distance to Cove Island Park, the beach and Chelsea Piers, and just a short drive to town, shopping, and the train station. Located on 13 park-like acres with sidewalks throughout, Sylvan Knoll is a pet friendly community. Units are all owner occupied. They cannot be rented. Real estate taxes are included in the common charges.
