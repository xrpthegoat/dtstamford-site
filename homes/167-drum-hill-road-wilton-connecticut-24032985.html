About this home

Four bedroom, 2.5 bath guest house on a spectacular 18th century 3.5 acre gated country retreat offering a quiet escape in a luxurious setting. All three upper bedrooms open to a vast 40ft upper deck. Newly updated ground level primary bedroom opens onto the first floor deck. French doors on the main level kitchen, dining and living room area lead onto the first floor deck as well. First floor deck is a 40' wood deck overlooking the 3.5 bucolic acres and fountained Koi pond. All rooms have beautiful beautiful natural light, most windows have views overlooking the pond. Primary bathroom has a natural rock wall rain shower appointed with Carrara marble. Upstairs bath has a newly updated Carrara marble double sink vanity. Pets are welcome! Smart home automation system, 2G fiber internet, built-in Bose surround system, stainless Fisher & Paykel appliances, minimum 4 car private driveway and parking. The setting offers a luxurious oasis one mile from Wilton town center, nationally ranked public schools and is centrally located minutes to all major highways. Additional $500/month includes 2G fiber internet, electricity, landscaping, snow plow. Additional au pair or in law suite available as an addition to this rental. Propane heat is Tenant's responsibility. A 10x10 insulated storage unit on the property that is heated and cooled by smart technology is included with this rental.
