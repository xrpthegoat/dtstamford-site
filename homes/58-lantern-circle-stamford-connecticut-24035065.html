About this home

Stunning Contemporary Home in Ideal Location. Welcome to your dream home, where modern elegance meets everyday convenience. This updated and move-in-ready gem is nestled in a picturesque cul-de-sac, offering a serene escape with easy access to the city's amenities. Open Floor Plan: Step inside to a spacious foyer leading to a large living room with vaulted ceilings, skylights, and a cozy fireplace. Perfect for entertaining and family gatherings. Stylish Updates: Enjoy the beauty of wood floors throughout the first floor, complemented by a new powder room and laundry room for your convenience. Gourmet Kitchen: The updated eat-in kitchen features granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, making cooking a delight. Indoor-Outdoor Living: The dining room opens up to a large deck through sliding French-style doors, creating a seamless flow between indoor and outdoor spaces. Luxurious Master Suite: The upstairs loft overlooks the living area, leading to a primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet, and a large bathroom. Indulge in the spa-like experience with quartz counters, a double sink, a separate whirlpool tub, and a shower. Additional Bedrooms: Two more bedrooms share a spacious two-sink bath, providing comfort for family or guests. Convenience: A two-car attached garage, minutes to downtown, train, and major highways.
