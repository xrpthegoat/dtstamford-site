About this home

Stunning two floor turn-key townhouse rental in South Norwalk that truly has it all. Updated Kitchen has granite countertops with newer stainless steel appliances. The oversized family room has cathedral ceilings with ceiling fan. Entire unit has been recently painted with newer laminate flooring on first floor and newer blinds as well for the four large windows. Upstairs has large bedroom with wood detailing on the ceilings and walls and W/W carpeting. There is also a walk-in closet and renovated full bath. On main floor, there is a second renovated 1/2 bathroom. Newer Washer and Dryer in unit. Newer HVAC for condo. Plenty of closet space and storage. Two parking spaces in re-paved parking lot (one reserved and one visitor space). All within steps of downtown vibrant South Norwalk (restaurants, nightlife and shopping), the SoNo Mall, the Maritime Center and the train station.
