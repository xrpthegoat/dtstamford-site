About this home

Welcome to this fabulous 5 BR, 4.5 BA colonial nestled in the heart of Westport. Bathed in natural light, the home welcomes you with a two-story foyer, setting the tone for the elegance within. The chef's kitchen boasts high-end Subzero & Wolf appliances including 2 wall ovens, gas cooktop, & Bosch dishwasher, complemented by granite counters, white cabinetry, & a generous island for both prep & casual dining. Entertain effortlessly as the kitchen flows into a spectacular great room w/vaulted ceiling & a fireplace ensconced in stone. Step through French doors to the serene bluestone patio, overlooking a private fenced yard adorned with mature landscaping that blooms in every season. The main level also features a formal dining room, a gracious living room, an office, & a convenient laundry room. Upstairs, retreat to the primary suite w/2 walk-in closets, a vaulted ceiling, sitting area, & ensuite bathroom w/jetted tub & 2 sinks. Four additional bedrooms & two full bathrooms ensure space for all. The lower level offers a gym w/mirrored wall, a full bathroom, & a spacious family room w/fireplace, wet bar w/wine chiller & refrigerator/freezer drawers. French doors open to a patio - potential for an in-law or au-pair suite. Major mechanicals, including a 22KW generator, recently replaced (ask for full list). Situated at the end of a cul-de-sac, this home combines peaceful seclusion & walking distance to school, the river, many restaurants & just 1 mile to the train. Welcome home!
