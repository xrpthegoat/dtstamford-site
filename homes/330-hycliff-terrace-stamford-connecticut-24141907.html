About this home

Nestled at the end of a tranquil cul-de-sac in the prestigious Hycliff neighborhood, this remarkable five-bedroom Colonial presents a sophisticated blend of timeless elegance and modern comfort. A gracious foyer introduces beautifully scaled interiors, including a refined living room with fireplace, a sun-drenched first-floor office, and an inviting formal dining room designed for memorable gatherings. The gourmet eat-in kitchen showcases rich soapstone countertops, center island, and seamless access to the two-car garage for effortless daily living. The expansive family room opens to a private patio and a fenced, level backyard - an idyllic setting for entertaining and serene outdoor relaxation. The second level features a luxurious primary suite offering three closets and a spa-inspired bath, accompanied by three generously proportioned bedrooms - one enhanced by a whimsical hidden playroom - and a well-appointed hall bath. A versatile third-floor suite or fifth bedroom provides exceptional flexibility for guests, a home office, or creative retreat. The finished lower level further elevates the home with a media/recreation lounge, full bath, guest/nanny suite, and a convenient walk-out laundry room. Residents enjoy access to coveted neighborhood pool, tennis courts, and playground. Ideally positioned near downtown conveniences & Stamford/Old Greenwich Metro North
