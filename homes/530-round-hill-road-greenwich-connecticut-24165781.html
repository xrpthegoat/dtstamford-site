About this home

Set on 4.3 picturesque acres along coveted Round Hill Rd, the original main residence of historic Field View Farm is a distinguished pre-war Georgian Colonial of exceptional quality, long associated with one of the area's notable legacy estates. A long sweeping private driveway introduces the stately brick and clapboard home, thoughtfully and comprehensively renovated to today's highest standards. Offering 6 bedrooms and 6.5 and baths, the residence balances timeless architectural elegance with modern comfort. Designed for elevated living, the grounds offer a resort-style pool, a private gym housed in its own standalone structure, and a heated four-stall horse stable offering flexibility for conversion to a guest cottage or ancillary living space. Expansive terraces and multiple French doors frame sweeping views of open fields and a mature evergreen grove. Abutting 8.5 acres of protected Land Trust property, the estate enjoys remarkable privacy, pastoral beauty, and enduring value in one of Greenwich's most prestigious settings.
