About this home

Experience serene living in this cozy top-floor condo with treetop views from your private balcony. This two-bedroom unit features soon-to-be-installed new vinyl floors in the bedrooms and a modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a convenient breakfast bar that flows seamlessly into the living area. The full bath, complete with a tub, showcases white subway tiles and granite countertops for both style and comfort. The condo also includes a full-size washer and dryer for added convenience and a generously sized storage unit measuring 18x9.5. Enjoy the outdoors in the greenspace out back, ideal for grilling. Parking is hassle-free with two dedicated spots-one in front of the storage unit and another reserved-plus additional space for visitors. Whether you're relaxing on your balcony or exploring the tranquil Springdale neighborhood, you'll enjoy the best of both worlds. Nearby amenities include the Metro-North train station, popular local restaurants such as Amore Cucina and Vinny's Backyard, and convenient access to the Merritt Parkway.
