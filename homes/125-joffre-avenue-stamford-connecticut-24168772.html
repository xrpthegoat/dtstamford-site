About this home

Beautifully maintained Cape in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood, filled with charm and a warm, welcoming feel from the moment you arrive. The stone-accented exterior creates inviting curb appeal and sets the tone for this special home. The main level features a comfortable, well-designed layout with a sun-filled living room anchored by a cozy wood-burning fireplace, perfect for relaxing evenings. A spacious family room and a modern kitchen overlooking the deck and landscaped backyard make everyday living and entertaining easy and enjoyable. Upstairs, there are three well-appointed bedrooms and a tastefully updated bathroom. Hardwood floors throughout add warmth and timeless character. Additional highlights include a detached three-car garage, gas heat, interior basement access, and newer windows (2021) that enhance natural light and energy efficiency. Conveniently located near I-95, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, and parks, this home blends comfort, style, and convenience. Truly move-in ready.
