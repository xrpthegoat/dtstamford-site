About this home

Delightfully renovated cape located centrally in New Canaan. Features a new kitchen equipped with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, alongside a freshly painted, well-lit living room complete with built-ins and a fireplace. The ground floor hosts a master bedroom with an en suite bathroom, along with an additional room that can serve as a den, study, or second bedroom. Upstairs, there are two spacious, versatile rooms. The lower level includes extra storage and another full bathroom. The expansive front and side yard ensures a private, serene setting. Walk, bike, stroll or jog this quiet, yet minutes to town neighborhood. Located conveniently to Merrit Parkway (1-2 min). Downtown New Canaan (5 min). Walk to Talmadge train station and be in NYC in 1 hour and 15 minutes. School bus pickup/drop off in front of the house. Cats ok, dog on a case-by-case consideration, 2 year lease preferred. Tenant maintains grass, fall and spring clean-up. Tenant responsible for all utilities, weeding, snow removal, trash, rental insurance and liability insurance. Good credit a must. No Co-signers or Guarantors. Immediate occupancy. Lease and addendum must be signed. Credit check required. Lease and addendum to lease must be signed. Owner/Broker $12,000 security required with lease signing. 500K Lim lib insurance required.
