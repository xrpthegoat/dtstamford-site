About this home

Just imagine living within 10 minutes of everything you need! Welcome to 10 Prospect Avenue Unit #8, a beautifully updated townhome ideally situated in one of Norwalk's most convenient and connected locations. This 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath residence offers 1,003 square feet of comfortable, modern living with a smart, functional layout. The home features a stylish contemporary kitchen with granite countertops. Recent capital improvements include a new HVAC system (May 2025), a new Honeywell thermostat (November 2025), professional interior painting (January 2021), and newly installed recessed bucket lighting in the hallway and kitchen. The unit includes covered garage parking directly below with two assigned parking spaces, a rare and valuable feature! Ideally located close to both I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, commuting is effortless, with easy access to the South Norwalk train station and the SoNo River Rail Trail for recreation and outdoor enjoyment. A playground located directly across the street adds to the neighborhood appeal. Just minutes from the mall, shopping, dining, Calf Pasture Beach and the vibrant SoNo district, this move-in-ready townhome offers the perfect blend of modern updates, convenience, and lifestyle.
