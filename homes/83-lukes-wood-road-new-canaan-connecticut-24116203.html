About this home

This remarkable 8,000 sq. ft. Georgian-style home sits on over 4 peaceful acres just a mile from Grace Farms. Designed by acclaimed architect Alex Kaali-Nagy, it blends timeless design with modern comfort in a perfect blend of comfort and style-ideal for those looking for space, privacy, and style Inside, you'll find 10-foot ceilings, detailed millwork, and sun-filled spaces throughout. A formal dining room with a fireplace is ideal for special gatherings, while the cherry wood library or billiard room offers the perfect place to relax. French doors line the main living areas, inviting in natural light and views of the grounds. The layout includes a luxurious primary suite plus five additional bedrooms-most with en suite baths-offering plenty of space for family members and guests. The expansive 1,800 sq. ft. finished lower level provides flexible space for a gym, studio, playroom, or whatever suits your lifestyle. Outside, the heated pool and cabana are made for summer days and fun. The fully fenced yard is great for pets, surrounded by mature landscaping that creates a peaceful, private setting. A beautifully designed home in Fairfield County that offers peaceful living with easy access to the city-ideal for those seeking space and comfort.
