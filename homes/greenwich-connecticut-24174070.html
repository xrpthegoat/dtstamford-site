About this home

Charming Cotswold-style home in a convenient Western Greenwich neighborhood, custom built by its builder/owner with exceptional attention to detail and timeless aesthetic. This beautifully designed residence offers 4 bd and 3.5 ba. including a relaxing private sauna. The gourmet kitchen opens to a formal dining room featuring custom built-ins, perfect for entertaining. A spacious, light-filled living room with fireplace and built-ins creates a warm and inviting atmosphere. Upstairs includes a lovely primary bedroom with walk-in closet and French doors and two charming double bedrooms with a hall bath. The large walkout lower level includes a bdrm/rec room, mudroom, sauna, and access to the garage with an additional storage room. A rare blend of character, comfort, and convenience in a sought location.
