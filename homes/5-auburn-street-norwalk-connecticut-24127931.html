About this home

Discover this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse offering the perfect blend of modern comfort and convenience. Step inside to a freshly painted interior with an oversized living room and abundant storage throughout. The home features all new energy-efficient systems and in-unit laundry for everyday ease. The finished lower level provides two versatile bonus rooms, including one with a walkout, ideal for a home office, gym, or playroom. Enjoy outdoor living on the private back deck overlooking a large backyard, perfect for gatherings or quiet relaxation. Located on a peaceful street yet only minutes from daily essentials, this home is just 5 minutes to Trader Joe's in Darien and 2 minutes to the brand-new Wegmans and Target. With easy access to Rowayton and Darien, you'll love the lifestyle and convenience this location offers. Parking is never a concern with a private driveway for two cars and plenty of available street parking. This turnkey property is ready for its next chapter.
