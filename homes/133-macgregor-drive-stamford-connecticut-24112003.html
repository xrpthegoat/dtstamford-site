About this home

Welcome to easy living in this updated, freshly painted Westover Ranch, ideally located on a quiet cul-de-sac. Generously sized rooms and a finished lower level offer plenty of flexible space. The main level features a sunlit Living Room with fireplace, spacious Dining Room, and a refreshed Eat-In Kitchen with new quartzite counters, center island with breakfast bar, and sliders to the rear yard and expansive patio-perfect for outdoor entertaining. A cozy first-floor Family Room completes the main living area. The Primary Bedroom includes a walk-in closet and an updated en-suite Bath with tumbled marble flooring and separate shower. Two additional well-proportioned Bedrooms share a beautifully redone Full Hall Bath with marble tile floor, all on the main level. The finished lower level impresses with a large Playroom with brick fireplace and high ceilings, plus an Office, Den, and a Half Bath with marble tile. The oversized 2-Car Garage houses the Laundry area, complete with newer washer & dryer. Additional highlights include an EV charger and City Water-all just minutes from shopping, schools, and major commuting routes. This is the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and value in one of Westover's most desirable neighborhoods.
