About this home

Extensively and tastefully renovated, this turnkey ranch in Stamford's sought after Newfield neighborhood offers beautifully updated interiors, flexible living space, and an exceptionally convenient location. Major cosmetic remodeling throughout the home has created a fresh, cohesive feel, including refinished hardwood floors, fresh interior and exterior paint, a stunning new kitchen with quartz countertops, and stylishly updated finishes throughout. The sun filled main level features an open, easy flow layout designed for modern living, with the kitchen, dining area, and main living space connecting seamlessly for both casual daily living and effortless entertaining. The primary suite offers a comfortable retreat with a walk-in closet and updated bath, while the additional bedrooms and baths are equally well appointed. The finished lower level provides outstanding versatility with additional family space, bonus space, laundry, mudroom, separate entry, and potential for in-law, guest, or home office use. Outside, the level yard and private parking add to the home's everyday practicality. Ideally situated near the shopping and dining of Newfield and Bulls Head, with easy access to Downtown Stamford, this home delivers the rare combination of move-in ready condition, stylish upgrades, and true convenience.
