About this home

In-Town location. Walk to restaurants, markets and retail with short distance access to highways and train station. Updated ranch-style home with hardwood floors throughout. Open floorplan. Updated kitchen with quartz counters, white shaker-style cabinets, and stainless-steel appliances, with access to back terrace. Kitchen opens to dining/living room with natural light galore. Living room with wood-burning fireplace and bow-windows. Sliders from the dining room to a slate terrace. Spacious primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings, sliders, en-suite bath with soaking tub and separate shower, and a walk-in closet. Hall bath for two secondary bedrooms with tub/shower, and stacking front loading washer/dryer.
