About this home

Set on 4 private acres, this fully renovated 5-bedroom, 6-bath home blends modern design with effortless everyday living. From the moment you enter, fresh, sophisticated interiors set the tone for a turnkey lifestyle. The spacious eat-in kitchen with a large center island flows into inviting gathering spaces designed for both daily living and entertaining. The cathedral-ceiling primary suite features a gas fireplace, radiant heated bathroom floors, a walk-in closet, and a luxurious bath. Large secondary bedrooms include one en-suite, while a first-floor bedroom with private bath offers flexibility for guests or a home office. A finished lower level provides space for play, fitness, or work. A generous screened-in porch invites summer dinners and relaxed evenings overlooking the property's beautiful grounds and water views. Outside, sweeping level lawns framed by mature trees create a private, park-like setting with exceptional space for recreation, entertaining, and play. An attached and detached garage offers endless possibilities for storage, a workshop, boat, Airstream storage, or a future party barn. The perfect blend of turnkey living, private surroundings, and exceptional indoor-outdoor spaces.
