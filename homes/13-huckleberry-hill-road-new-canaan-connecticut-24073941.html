About this home

Rare Rental in New Canaan: Historic Character, Natural Beauty, and Modern Comfort on Two Private Acres. Striking 4-bedroom, 3 full and 2 half-bath residence. Part of a distinguished historic estate, beautifully landscaped, this exceptional property is just 10 minutes from the New Canaan village, train, restaurants, and shopping. The 4,400 square foot home has two distinct wings, united by a light-filled, open-concept kitchen and breakfast room, with two private decks. The main wing includes a formal living room with a classic fireplace, an elegant dining room, a tranquil first floor primary suite, two upstairs bedrooms with a full bath, and a private office. The opposite wing-original to the 1930s-features a stunning vaulted-ceiling family room with a dramatic stone fireplace, a built-in bar, and abundant natural light. Above it is another private bedroom with a luxurious en-suite bath. Recent renovation includes fresh paint and refinished hardwood floors throughout, chef's kitchen, all new bathrooms, spacious utility room with new washer/dryer, and three-car garage with electric charging station. Landscaping and snow removal included.
