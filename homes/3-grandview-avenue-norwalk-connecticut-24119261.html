About this home

This two family nestled in an extra large wooded lot conveniently near downtown Norwalk is great opportunity for a creative contractor! Lots of stone walls with terraced property give the yard a charming country feel! Lots of trees provide shade and privacy. The interior of both units has been freshly painted with two coats of Clean White primer, just waiting for your own imaginative touch to make it your own warm personal space. The two separate long driveways provide a great opportunity and space for a contractor with extra vehicles! There was a garage previously at the end of the upper driveway. The home is adjacent to Jefferson magnet school. It is also conveniently located near Norwalk Hospital. For shopping many popular retailers such as Lowes, Home Depot, ShopRite, Stop N Shop, Costco, and the newly opened Wegmans and Target all on Connecticut Avenue! Norwalk is also the home of Stew Leonard's the Worlds largest Dairy store. And the city boasts lovely Calf pasture and shady beach, as well as beautiful parks for recreation such as Gallaghers estate and Veterans park.
