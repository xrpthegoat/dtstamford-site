About this home

The ultimate Westport lifestyle awaits in this luxurious home that is available for lease on an annual or multi-year basis. Enjoy the convenience of living mere moments from myriad conveniences, schools, amenities and recreation opportunities - while residing in a custom home with pool, tennis court and gorgeous grounds. This resort-like setting offers a country club lifestyle without sacrificing easy access to all that makes the area such a highly-desired place to live. The foyer introduces the comfortable spaces that define this serene retreat. A quiet home office/library is tucked beside the entrance. The open plan Great Room is lined with windows that bring sunlight and views of the surroundings into the home - and the adjacent gourmet kitchen features top appliances, fine cabinetry and is accented by a cathedral ceiling. A short flight of stairs leads to a spacious and inviting recreation/family room. The main level bedroom/guest suite offers flexibility and privacy. The second floor offers spacious bedrooms and a plush primary suite with dramatic ceiling and views of the property. The garage, storage room/workout space complete the offering. The expansive decks, patios and gardens offer a beautiful space to enjoy the outdoors. You will not believe that such a tranquil, bucolic setting could be located so close to Winslow Park, Compo Beach, Metro North Railroad, downtown, and popular stores and fitness studios.
