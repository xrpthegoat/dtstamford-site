About this home

Welcome to 31 Hyatt Avenue, a beautifully updated classic Colonial offering 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and 1,826 square feet of living space on a level 0.19-acre lot. This well-maintained home features a spacious living room with a fireplace and refinished original hardwood floors, a formal dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with tile flooring, a pantry, and sliding doors that open to a private deck-perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining. The kitchen is outfitted with energy-efficient appliances, including a high-performance Monogram commercial-style hood. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms with hardwood floors and a full bath with double sinks. The lower level offers laundry and generous storage space. Upgrades include a conversion from oil and electric to an energy-efficient natural gas system, new insulation, and a centralized heating and cooling system with a natural gas furnace. The basement has been professionally waterproofed, and additional highlights include a new roof, central air, and ceiling fans for year-round comfort. Outside, enjoy a private backyard, a spacious deck ideal for relaxing or entertaining, and a convenient storage shed. Ideally located near schools, parks, shopping, and public transportation, this move-in ready home combines timeless charm with modern efficiency. Don't miss the opportunity to make 31 Hyatt Avenue your new home.
