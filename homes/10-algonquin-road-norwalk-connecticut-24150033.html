About this home

Nestled in the heart of Norwalk's Cranbury area is your Forever Home. This 3 Bedroom, 2 Full Bath Split Level is located on a corner double-lot which gives you an expansive outdoor area, surrounded by a privacy fence, mature trees and tranquility! Enjoy sitting on the well-maintained deck and taking in the view after a long day! Inside this beautiful home is an open floor living area with a living room, dining area and fully-updated kitchen with island that has vaulted ceilings, recessed lighting and pristine hardwood floors and tile flooring. Plenty of natural light from the French doors and oversized windows brightens this house from sunrise to sunset! The upper level consists of three (3) bedrooms, all with hardwood floors and spacious closets, as well as a full bath. The lower level is being used as a fourth bedroom; however, this could be the perfect space for a family room. The lower level also includes a full bath with jetted whirlpool tub. Built-ins and shelves provide additional storage, and interior access to the garage for total convenience. The possibilities are endless, but the location and lot are permanent and amazing!! Brand new hot water heater, oil tank, furnace and newer roof add to the meticulous maintenance and upkeep of this home. A house listed in this area doesn't seem to stay on the market very long!
