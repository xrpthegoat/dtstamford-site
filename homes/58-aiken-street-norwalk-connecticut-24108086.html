About this home

Wonderful updated 2 bedroom, 2 1/2 bath townhouse-style unit with central air-conditioning. Tucked back on a private drive, this two-family home is conveniently located near restaurants, shopping and public transportation. There is a spacious living room with a picture window and charming wood-burning fireplace. The eat-in kitchen has stainless steel appliances, a pantry closet and sliders out to a private patio area with lovely rock outcroppings. There is also a powder room on the main level. Upstairs, the primary bedroom suite has hardwood flooring and vaulted ceilings with large windows to bring in the sun. The primary full bath is updated and has granite flooring. The second bedroom is roomy and also has hardwood floors. There is a 2nd full bath on this level with a tub/shower combination. Laundry is conveniently located on the upper level. Two off-street parking spaces and Norwalk beach rights. Available August 1st.
