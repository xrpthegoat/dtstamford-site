About this home

Charming Rowayton Townhouse -- Nestled in the heart of Rowayton, this fully renovated townhouse offers the perfect blend of modern comfort and coastal village charm. Available beginning October for a 12-18 month lease, this property presents a rare opportunity to experience in-town Rowayton living at its finest.
