About this home

Welcome to Marvin Beach! Classic 1924 Tudor style home located across the street from Charles Creek. As you enter the home you are welcomed into a charming 3 season porch. Living room with wood burning fireplace, converted porch into a sunroom, generous sized dining room, kitchen and half bath. Second story offers 3 bedrooms and full hall bath. Want to expand your living space to the third story?! This home has potential as the staircase in hallway. Flat lot, detached one car garage. This costal community hosts seaside lobster dinners, festive block parties, and friendly gatherings. Beachside living is all part of the lifestyle.
