About this home

Welcome to award-winning Wilton, CT and 79 Pipers Hill Road. This stunning 4 bedroom home will win you over the moment you walk through the door. Thoughtfully maintained and full of desirable upgrades, this property offers the perfect balance of comfort, functionality, and charm. Highlights include a Generac generator, brand new roof, brand new septic, central air, a two-car garage, and a CPS well pump designed to increase water pressure. The spacious eat-in kitchen is ideal for everyday living and entertaining, offering plenty of room for the home chef to create and gather with family and friends. During the cooler months, enjoy relaxing evenings around the cozy fireplace, creating the perfect atmosphere for comfort and connection. Step outside to your expansive deck where you can entertain, unwind, or host summer gatherings. If you're a self-proclaimed Grill Master, you'll appreciate that plumbing is already in place for your grill. Need a dedicated workspace? The finished walk-out lower level offers excellent flexibility and is perfectly suited for a home office, gym, or additional living space. At this market position, 79 Pipers Hill Road truly offers it all-space, upgrades, and the opportunity to create lasting memories in one of Fairfield County's most desirable communities. Come experience all that this wonderful home has to offer.
