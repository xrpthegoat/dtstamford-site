About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 3.1-bath home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and functionality. The welcoming main level features a spacious living room with a wood-burning fireplace, seamlessly flowing into the dining area and updated kitchen. Sliding doors lead to an expansive deck and private backyard, creating an ideal setting for indoor-outdoor living and entertaining. A practical mudroom, powder room, and a bright family room or home office with direct deck access complete the first floor. Upstairs, the generous primary suite boasts double closets, a walk-in closet, and a beautifully renovated en-suite bath with a soaking tub and separate shower. Three additional bedrooms and a well-appointed hall bath provide ample space for family and guests. The walk-out lower level adds valuable living space, featuring a full bathroom, playroom, den, and abundant storage, offering flexibility for recreation, work, or overnight visitors. Ideally located in a peaceful neighborhood setting, this move-in-ready home combines modern updates with timeless appeal.
