About this home

Why Rent When You Can Own? This updated triplex townhome in the desirable Sylvan Knoll community is the opportunity you've been waiting for! Step inside and be captivated by the open, sunlit floor plan that flows seamlessly through the main level. The modern, eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring a sleek quartz countertop island/breakfast bar, custom lighting, top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, a cozy built-in bench, and a spacious pantry. Elegance abounds with beautiful tile floors, crown molding and recessed lighting throughout the main living area, creating a sophisticated ambiance perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find two bedrooms with hardwood floors and a bathroom. The lower level has additional space for a family room, home office, or den, complete with laundry and utility areas for added convenience/storage. Plus, you'll love the ease of access to extra storage with pull-down stairs to a floored attic. Your outdoor patio awaits, perfect for al fresco dining. Sylvan Knoll is a pet-friendly, owner-occupied community featuring a lovely playground for residents. With easy access to I-95, the train station, beautiful beaches, and Chelsea Piers, this townhome puts you right where you want to be. Schedule your visit today! Unit is being sold as-is.
