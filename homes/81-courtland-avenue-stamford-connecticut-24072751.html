About this home

Spacious and close to two trains! Live in this desirable Heywood Heights unit. It has three beautiful bedrooms, and two full baths. Hardwood floors, recessed lights, and balcony with interior lawn view. The large primary ensuite bedroom has a supersized walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms with closet systems. The spacious living room has sliding glass doors that open to a large balcony (no grills or grilling permitted). Kitchen with tile flooring, stainless steel appliances, pantry, and casual dining area with granite table and bench. Tenant pays electricity and cable, credit checks. No Smoking, No Pets, No Grilling. Ideal layout for those looking to work from home, commuters, those new to Stamford, and everyone in between. In unit laundry, garage, very large storage room, pool, and clubhouse included. Close to I-95 and the Glenbrook train station.
