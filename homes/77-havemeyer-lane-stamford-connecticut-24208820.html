About this home

Incredible 3 bedroom/2 bath condo in the luxury, gated community of Palmer Hill. Natural light bathes the entire open floor plan. A gourmet kitchen with marble countertops, Wolf range and stainless steel appliances adjoins the living/dining room with gas fireplace and access to a private terrace. The spacious primary bedroom suite has a walk-in closet, access to the terrace and a luxurious ensuite bath. The second bedroom has its own private terrace and lots of storage. A third bedroom/office and second bath and laundry completes this amazing space. 2 indoor parking spaces, storage unit and bike room are included. Amenities include clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym, yoga room, and business center. Minutes to I95 and MetroNorth.
