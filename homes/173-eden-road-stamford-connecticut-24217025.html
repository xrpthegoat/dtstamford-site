About this home

Opportunity awaits at 173 Eden Road! Conveniently located in a desirable Stamford neighborhood just minutes from King School, this spacious four-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath Center Hall Colonial offers endless possibilities to make it your own. Featuring generously sized bedrooms, hardwood flooring throughout, and a convenient main-level laundry room, this home offers a wonderful foundation for comfortable everyday living. Step outside to enjoy the deck and backyard, perfect for outdoor entertaining or relaxing. Bring your vision and creativity to transform this home into something truly special. With its classic Colonial layout, spacious rooms, and exceptional potential, this is a fantastic opportunity to customize a home to your personal style in a convenient Stamford location. This well-maintained home features a newly installed 2025 roof and a 2015 furnace. This home has great bones and excellent potential with endless possibilities.
