About this home

Just renovated and available for immediate occupancy, this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom second-floor residence is filled with natural light and ideally located in sought-after Shippan. A thoughtfully designed eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into a spacious living room. Both bedrooms are generously sized and offer ample closet space, while the full bathroom is complete with in-unit laundry for added convenience. A newly completed deck overlooks the backyard, and tenants enjoy access to the outdoor space-perfect for summer entertaining and relaxation. Ideally positioned just minutes from I-95, Metro-North, and downtown Stamford, and moments to Cummings Park and West Beach, this home offers both convenience and coastal lifestyle. A rare opportunity to live in one of Stamford's most desirable waterfront communities.
