About this home

Available August 19. Welcome to this charming, sun-filled four-bedroom, three-bath home tucked away on a peaceful Westport cul-de-sac. The inviting main level features a spacious living room with recessed lighting and a wood-burning fireplace, an elegant formal dining room with wainscoting, and an updated eat-in kitchen. A separate family room offers a second wood-burning fireplace, tile flooring, and large windows overlooking the front yard. Three bedrooms with hardwood floors and two full baths complete the main level. Upstairs, a private fourth bedroom features an updated en-suite bath, a generous closet, and wall-to-wall carpeting. Enjoy outdoor living on the screened-in porch and rear patio-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Additional amenities include an attached two-car garage and ample storage space. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Westport, shopping, dining, the Levitt Pavilion, Westport Country Playhouse, and Westport's award-winning schools. Please note that the home does not have central air conditioning; several window units are provided. Turtleback Road is private, with an informal association that shares snow-plowing costs of approximately $250 annually, depending on snowfall. Excellent credit and references are required.
