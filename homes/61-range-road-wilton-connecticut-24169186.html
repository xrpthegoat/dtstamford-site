About this home

HIGHEST & BEST: Please submit all offers by -- Monday, April 27th, at 7:00PM. Welcome to 61 Range Road! A coveted South Wilton home tucked at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, offering the perfect blend of privacy, space, and convenience. Set on over an acre of land, this move-in ready 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home delivers a bright and thoughtfully designed living space. The designer kitchen is a true centerpiece - featuring high-end appliances, a built-in banquette with custom upholstery, brass pendant lighting, slate tile flooring, and a peninsula with bar seating that makes everyday dining and entertaining equally effortless. The sun-filled living room impresses with expansive oversized windows, recessed lighting, and rich hardwood floors throughout. The family room anchors the home with a classic wood-burning fireplace, flowing directly into a sun-filled three-season screened porch and deck overlooking the yard. Upstairs the primary en-suite is joined by three generously sized bedrooms. The finished lower level rounds out the home with bonus space ready for a gym, rec room, or home office. With fresh paint throughout, new water filtration system, and a new AC unit, are among recent updates. Nothing left to do but move in. Minutes to Wilton's town center, top-rated Wilton schools, and easy Merritt Parkway and Metro-North access for commuters - this is South Wilton living at its best.
