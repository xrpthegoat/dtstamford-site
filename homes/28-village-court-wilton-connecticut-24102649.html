About this home

New to the market in South Wilton - you'll love this easy maintenance, free standing home in the beautiful community of Grumman Hill Village! Enjoy living in this wonderful location in a community close to commuter roads, the Metro North, shops and of course, award winning Wilton schools. This home is in great shape with brand new hardwood floors and new 2nd floor carpeting. Lovely open space on the first floor combines the living and dining room areas for easy entertaining. The eat-in kitchen and family room with gas fireplace open directly out to the VERY private patio and backyard. Upstairs the Primary vaulted ceiling suite boasts a large walk-in closet, palladium windows and the en-suite dual sink bathroom. There are 2 additional bedrooms, a terrific loft space (perfect for a home office) and a laundry/mechanical room. Loads of storage in the 2-car attached garage as well as a very large attic easily accessed by the 2nd floor pull-down staircase. Don't miss your chance to live in this fabulous Wilton community!
