About this home

There are waterfront homes, and then there are properties that truly define waterfront living. 41 Rowayton Avenue is one of those rare offerings, bringing together the features buyers search for but almost never find in a single property: direct waterfront, a private dock, sandy beach, waterside pool, and coveted Rowayton Beach Association membership. Sunlight pours through the home's inviting interior, creating a warm and welcoming space for effortless entertaining, boating adventures, afternoons by the pool, and evenings enjoying breathtaking sunsets over the water. Just moments from Rowayton Village and approximately one hour from Manhattan, this is an exceptional opportunity to experience one of Connecticut's most complete waterfront lifestyles.
