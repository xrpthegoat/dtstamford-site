About this home

Welcome to this spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch-style condo offering 1,650 square feet of beautifully updated living space in Norwalk's highly desirable Ledgebrook community. Set against a picturesque backdrop, this light-filled home features tranquil views of the pond and walking bridge from the living room, primary suite, and covered porch-creating a peaceful retreat you'll love coming home to. The inviting living and dining rooms share a charming two-sided wood-burning fireplace, perfect for cozy evenings or entertaining guests. Sliders open to a covered patio overlooking lush landscaping and the shimmering water beyond. The newer kitchen is thoughtfully designed for everyday ease and entertaining alike. Ledgebrook offers exceptional amenities including a pool, fitness center, clubhouse, and impeccably maintained grounds filled with vibrant perennials. Conveniently located close to Norwalk's top shopping, dining, train stations, highways, and even a public golf course, this hidden gem blends accessibility with serenity. Enjoy low-maintenance, one-level living in a setting that feels like a private sanctuary-don't miss the opportunity to make this special property your next home. Special assessment has been paid by owner. Due to multiple offers, the sellers are requesting that all interested parties submit their highest and best offers by Tuesday, May 6 at noon.
