About this home

Beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 2-bath duplex located in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods and just a short walk to Cove Beach! This move-in-ready home offers a bright and airy open-concept layout, seamlessly connecting the living room, dining area, and a stunning brand-new kitchen featuring modern cabinetry, beautiful countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a gorgeous center island perfect for entertaining and everyday living. Freshly painted throughout and featuring elegant hardwood floors, this home combines style and comfort in every room. Reserved parking adds convenience, while the unbeatable location puts you minutes from downtown Stamford, restaurants, shopping, the train station, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway for an easy commute.
