About this home

Beautifully renovated and move-in ready. This turnkey 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home is set in a desirable Cos Cob neighborhood and offers four finished levels of comfortable, flexible living space. Updates include an all-new kitchen, renovated bathrooms, new appliances, and new heating and cooling systems. The home also features a 1-car garage, fenced-in yard, patio, and additional outdoor space for relaxing and entertaining. Conveniently located close to Bible Street Park and Montgomery Pinetum Park, with Cos Cob Elementary School, Central Middle School, and downtown Cos Cob all within walking distance. A thoughtfully updated home offering modern finishes, great outdoor space, and an excellent Cos Cob location.
