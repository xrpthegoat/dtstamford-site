About this home

Immaculate colonial in the Twin Ledge Association. Spacious & bright with an open floor plan. Living room with a bay window and gleaming hardwood floors. The spacious family room has a gas fireplace and built ins and is open to the eat in kitchen. The kitchen has a gas range, microwave, dishwasher & stainless steel refrigerator with a space for a table and a door to the two decks. A half bath completes the first floor. There is a beautiful lower level recreation room with a the utility room and storage area. The second floor has a lovely master suite with two closets, one is a walk in plus a beautiful bathroom. The master bath has double sinks with granite counters, a whirlpool and a separate shower. There is a full bath in the hallway plus a guest room, office / bedroom and a large media room that could be a 4th bedroom. Central and a two car garage are a couple more features of this fine home in private setting.
