About this home

Waterfront Summer Rental in Harborview - Private Dock & Stunning Views Spend your summer by the water in this charming 3-bedroom home located in the desirable Harborview community of Norwalk. This seasonal rental offers direct waterfront access and includes a private dock-perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, or simply enjoying the coastal breeze. A bright sunroom overlooks the salt marsh, where you can watch local wildlife and enjoy peaceful summer mornings. The home is part of a private association featuring two tennis courts and a waterfront clubhouse, adding to the resort-style atmosphere. Available for summer rental-don't miss this unique opportunity to enjoy the best of Harborview living.
