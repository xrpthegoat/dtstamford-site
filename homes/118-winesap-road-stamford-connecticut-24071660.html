About this home

Discover your dream home in the highly sought-after North Stamford area, where tranquility meets stylish living on a serene, private lot surrounded by nature. This spacious, light-filled home is ready to welcome its new owners. As you step inside, you're welcomed by a foyer that flows into the elegant living and dining rooms, providing an ideal setting for entertaining. The chef's kitchen, complete with an island and walk-in pantry, is a culinary dream and opens to a charming breakfast area. The airy family room boasts cathedral ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, skylights, and French doors that lead to a generous deck, seamlessly extending your living space into the picturesque outdoor environment. A standout feature is the great room, with its cozy fireplace, vaulted ceiling, and private balcony, offering a perfect retreat for work or relaxation. The primary bedroom suite serves as your personal sanctuary, featuring a luxurious jacuzzi tub and an entire wall of closets. Three additional generously sized bedrooms and a well-appointed bathroom complete the bedroom wing, ensuring ample space for family and guests. The lower level is a spectacular entertainment space, offering 9-foot ceilings, a kitchenette, a full bath, changing room, and designated living and recreation/game room areas. Step outside to the inviting outdoor oasis, complete with a patio and in-ground pool, perfect for summer gatherings.
