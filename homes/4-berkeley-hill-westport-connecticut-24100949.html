About this home

Perfectly positioned just minutes from downtown Westport, this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bath Cape is nestled on 1.18 serene acres, offering the perfect blend of style, function, and flexibility. The home's thoughtful layout emphasizes convenience, with three spacious bedrooms located on the main level-ideal for guests, home offices, or anyone seeking easy, one-level living. At the heart of the home is a show-stopping chef's kitchen featuring sleek Italian Snaidero cabinetry by Pininfarina (of Ferrari fame), top-of-the-line Viking and Miele appliances, Caesarstone countertops, two sinks, and a large island with seating for six. The open-concept design flows into a cozy family room with a wood-burning fireplace and a sun-drenched dining room with windows on three sides-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, the private primary suite spans the entire second floor and includes a luxurious spa bath, creating a peaceful retreat. The finished lower level adds even more versatility with a large rec room, wet bar, home theater, office/gym, and laundry room. Outside, a generous deck and level yard provide great space for relaxing, playing, or dining al fresco. Just minutes to top-rated schools, beaches, and commuter routes-this move-in-ready home is the total package.
