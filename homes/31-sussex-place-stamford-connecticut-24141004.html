About this home

WELCOME TO YOUR MOVE-IN-READY HOME, WHERE ALMOST EVERYTHING IS NEW, IN THE CONVENIENT NEWFIELD NEIGHBORHOOD! This beautifully updated home features hardwood floors throughout the main level, with luxury vinyl flooring on the lower level. It offers a brand-new Wren kitchen with quartz countertops and stainless-steel appliances, plus two fully remodeled full bathrooms. The home also includes fresh paint, new light fixtures, recessed lighting, brand-new doors, and new windows. Major improvements include a new roof, new siding, a new driveway, a new garage door, and a new electric panel-making this home truly move-in ready with nearly everything new. In addition, the home offers an attached one-car garage and a carport, providing space for one extra vehicle. Located at the end of the street, this home offers plenty of privacy, a spacious backyard with a bluestone patio, and an indoor ground-level pool with a safety fence.
