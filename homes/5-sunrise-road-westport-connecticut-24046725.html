About this home

Welcome to the sought after coastal community of Westport. Perfectly located in the heart of town with easy access to everything Westport has to offer, the award-winning schools, the stunning new library, the compo beach and state parks, local theater at Westport Country Playhouse, Longshore public country club pools and tennis courts, just to name a few. Terrific walk to train location, yet private and secluded. 3BR colonial farmhouse style at the end of a quiet private lane. Beautiful hardwood floors throughout, bright and spacious. Newer windows and kitchen, central air. Cozy sun-porch overlooks quiet landscape. Lit walking path at rear of property leads down to train station. No need for a car or parking pass!
