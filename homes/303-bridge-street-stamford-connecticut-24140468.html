About this home

Best & Final by 5:00 Sunday 11/23. Discover the best of Stamford living in this charming brick tri-level end-unit townhome, perfectly situated for convenience and comfort. Enjoy picturesque views of the E. Gaynor Brennan Golf Course right outside your front door and soak in abundant natural light throughout the sun-filled, front-to-back living and dining area-an ideal space for relaxing or entertaining. The kitchen opens directly to a private deck, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow for evening cocktails or quiet mornings with coffee. Upstairs, you will find two generously sized bedrooms and a full bath. The versatile lower level (electric heat) offers even more living space, perfect for a family room or home office, complete with a full bath and laundry. Additional highlights include hardwood floors on the main level (and potentially upstairs), storage galore, central air and natural gas. The rear of the complex borders the serene Rippowam River, offering a peaceful natural setting while still being close to downtown, shopping, dining, parks, and transportation.
