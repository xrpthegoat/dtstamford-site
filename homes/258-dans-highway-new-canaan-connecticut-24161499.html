About this home

A gated entry introduces this stunning Federal-style Colonial, privately set on just over 2-acres. Bathed in natural light, the home is defined by floor-to-ceiling windows, French doors, and skylights that illuminate every space. The flow is exceptional, designed for elegant entertaining and comfortable daily living. A gracious 23-ft foyer leads to a dramatic living room featuring a fireplace, cathedral ceiling, and a striking wall of windows overlooking the gardens. The formal dining room easily accommodates 12 guests. The kitchen and family room combination offers a vaulted ceiling, oversized windows, French doors, and custom cabinetry. A large central island provides ample seating, a built-in banquette and additional gathering space. The primary suite is a private retreat featuring a beautiful marble bath with double vanities, a soaking tub, and a spacious shower. Upstairs, 3 additional bedrooms include one en-suite, along with a hall bath. The 3rd level presents a large unfinished attic, offering excellent potential for a bonus space. The property is truly bucolic, with mature plantings, curated garden areas, and expansive lawns. Outdoor living is enhanced by 2 stone terraces, a pickleball court, and a new heated Gunite pool with a sun shelf, surrounded by an expansive slate patio. Additional features include a generator and storage sheds. Located just 3 miles from downtown New Canaan & Award-Winning Schools, privacy and convenience.
