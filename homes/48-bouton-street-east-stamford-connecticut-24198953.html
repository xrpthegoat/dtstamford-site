About this home

Welcome to this charming Cape nestled in Stamford's quiet sought-after Springdale neighborhood, near the Darien and New Canaan borders. Just minutes from Springdale Elementary School, approximately 1.1 miles from the Springdale train station, and within walking distance of Woodway Country Club, the home offers a convenient location with easy access to shopping, dining, major highways, and everything Stamford has to offer! Inside you'll find a flexible floor plan, beautiful hardwood floors, and thoughtful updates throughout. Enter through the enclosed front porch into a bright living room featuring French doors and recessed lighting! The remodeled kitchen includes granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The main level offers a bedroom and full bathroom, with two additional bedrooms and a renovated full bathroom upstairs. Other upgrades include waterproofed basement and abundant storage and future possibilities. A Walk-out deck with additional space to entertain while overlooking a serene and private backyard. Space for play and gardening included with a shed and carport. Adding another layer of opportunity, the property is a single-family home located within a multifamily-zoned neighborhood, making its location particularly noteworthy for both owner-occupants and investors. *Buyers are encouraged to perform their own diligence regarding zoning and any potential future uses.*
