About this home

Welcome to Shippan Point, one of Stamford's most sought-after waterfront communities! This inviting center hall Colonial welcomes you with a spacious entry foyer that flows seamlessly into the beautifully updated, open-concept kitchen showcasing Carrara marble countertops, stainless steel appliances, including double ovens and center island which opens in to the dining room, creating the perfect setting for entertaining, while the sun-filled, front-to-back living room offers warmth and charm. The family room features a gas fireplace, custom built-ins and sliders leading to the deck, patio and yard. A powder room completes the main level. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts an updated full bath and walk-in closet, complemented by a newly renovated hall bath and three additional bedrooms. Wood floors throughout, a new roof and full basement offering ample storage and potential for future expansion add to the appeal. Set on a level .26-acre corner lot with a 2-car garage, this home offers the perfect blend of space, comfort and convenience. Enjoy a short walk to the beach and Yacht Club, with the train station and vibrant downtown just minutes away. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy coastal living in a prime location not to be missed!
