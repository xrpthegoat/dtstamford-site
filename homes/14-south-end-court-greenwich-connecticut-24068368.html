About this home

This breathtaking new construction on half an acre along a private road offers tidal waterfront views and the best of coastal living. Located near the village, train, and OG School, the home seamlessly blends elegance and convenience. The open design features oversized windows and inviting outdoor spaces. The first floor includes a gourmet kitchen, spacious entertaining rooms with glass doors opening to a large deck. A separate waterfront deck allows for kayak launches. Upstairs, the primary and junior suites feature private balconies, alongside two additional en-suite bedrooms. The third fl offers flexible space with a full bath. Over 5,500 sq ft with a 2-car garage and custom options like an elevator, first floor bedroom and splash pool, this home is ready to be tailored for you!
