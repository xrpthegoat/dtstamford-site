About this home

Thoughtfully updated and expanded, this exceptional 4-bed, 4 full and 1 half bath home located at the end of a private culdesac welcomes you with an open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area designed for everyday living and entertaining. The chef's kitchen features radiant heated floors, marble top island, propane 6-burner cooktop with wall ovens, 2 dishwashers, and walk-in pantry, while the adjoining living and dining spaces flow effortlessly for gatherings of any size. A convenient half bath and main-level laundry room with sink add everyday functionality. An inviting family room overlooks the expansive backyard with in-ground pool and is anchored by a New England stone fireplace with wood stove insert. The primary bedroom suite features a renovated spa-like bath with a curbless walk-in shower, freestanding soaking tub, double vanity, radiant heat floors, and a large walk-in closet. 3 additional bedrooms and 2 full baths on the main level provide additional versatility. The expansive lower level extends the living space with a large rec room, full bath, and additional room offering potential for a bedroom, gym, office, or media room. Step outside to a private backyard oasis with a pool, flagstone patio, ArmorTec pavers, bluestone coping, and mature landscaping. A large 2-car garage, with workshop and a large storage area complete this meticulously maintained, move-in-ready home, ideally located near parks, shopping, dining, and just minutes from the Merritt Parkway
