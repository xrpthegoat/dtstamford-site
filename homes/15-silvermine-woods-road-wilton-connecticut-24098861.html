About this home

Live a carefree life in this pristine South Wilton home, and become a member at private Silvermine Golf Club, no waiting list, initiation fee or dues. Marvelously low-maintenance, this unit has fantastic views overlooking the 9th fairway, and is drenched with beautiful light. Stately living room with vaulted ceiling and gas fireplace. Stunning KITCHEN BY DEANE with quartzite countertops, Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances and large island, open to the family room. There are endless opportunities for entertaining and sunset wind-downs overlooking the course on the extra-large deck. The adjacent family room includes a 2nd gas fireplace and handsome built-in cabinets. Large formal dining room has beautiful view to the course. Primary Suite with vaulted ceiling is bestowed with gorgeous updated full bath, walk-in closet, and extra storage in the built-in bench. Two additional good-sized bedrooms both with updated full baths are gracious rooms for family and friends. The walk-out lower level with 1/2 bath has another great view and so much storage space you can 'downsize' and bring all your stuff and deal with it later while you enjoy your vacation lifestyle, or 'upsize' and have plenty of room to grow. Whether you play golf or not, this house provides ample space, beautiful views, a private community, and a pristine home to enjoy year round or part time, for years to come. Just 12 min to SONO train, and 4 min to the Merritt Parkway!
