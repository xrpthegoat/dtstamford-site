About this home

Exceptional NEW CONSTRUCTION - Walk to Town - Experience a thoughtfully designed home by V&A Construction, a highly regarded local builder known for exceptional craftsmanship. Just a short walk to town, shops, restaurants and the train, the location is as convenient as it is desirable. The home features handcrafted cabinetry and millwork, wide-plank white oak floors and high ceilings throughout. Imported European windows and doors not only add a beautiful architectural element, but make the home feel exceptionally quiet and comfortable. A welcoming foyer opens to living and dining rooms, a private home office and a designer kitchen with breakfast nook, premium appliances and a fully equipped butler's pantry. The kitchen flows directly into a spacious family room with a wood-burning fireplace, creating an easy, open layout for everyday living and entertaining. The expansive mudroom offers generous storage, connects to a back staircase and leads to a spacious lower level that can accommodate a gym, media room and game room. The second floor includes five generously sized bedrooms, highlighted by a primary suite with dual walk-in closets and a spa-like bath with premium fixtures. A large third-floor loft provides flexible space for a playroom, additional office or a relaxed retreat. This award-winning builder is dedicated to creating homes of the highest quality. Rarely offered and not to be missed.
