About this home

Privately set in Westport's coveted Old Hill neighborhood, this sophisticated residence designed by famed architect Peter Wormser pairs striking architecture with timeless, understated elegance. Soaring ceilings, beautifully curated spaces and abundant natural light define the stylish interiors, creating a home that is at once impressive and inviting. 4 finished levels provide exceptional space for everyday living, entertaining, work and guests. The primary suite stuns with its soaring ceilings, fireplace, 2 walk-in closets and spa bath. 4 additional bedrooms each feature walk-in closets, while 2 private offices offer dedicated work-from-home space. A fabulous 3rd-floor recreation room with full bath adds more flexibility, while the beautifully finished lower level has a home theater, gym, space for play, plus storage. Outdoors, the magical setting unfolds through multiple entertaining spaces, from inviting covered porches to an expansive stone patio with a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen, built-in grill and Big Green Egg, offering an exceptional backdrop for gatherings large and small. Lush, established landscaping creates a remarkable sense of privacy, yet the home is just over a mile to Westport's vibrant downtown. A whole-house generator adds peace of mind, and the NYC-bound train is just 9 minutes away. Distinctive, sophisticated and effortlessly livable, this exceptional Old Hill residence is a rare offering of inspired architecture, privacy and a prime location.
