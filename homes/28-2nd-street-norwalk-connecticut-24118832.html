About this home

Nestled in a vibrant and highly desirable waterfront community with an impressive 90% walkability rating, this charming Second Street residence offers the perfect blend of relaxation and recreation. Just steps from beaches, pickleball courts, and water access for kayaking and boating, you're also moments away from the lively energy of South Norwalk-just across the bridge. Fully renovated in 2024, the home features gleaming hardwood floors, recessed lighting, fresh paint, and brand-new windows throughout. The modern kitchen boasts GE appliances, including a dishwasher, and the convenience of a private in-unit washer and dryer. Enjoy your morning coffee or evening unwind on the private porch overlooking a flat, lush lawn-ideal for entertaining or tending to your raised garden. With ample closet space, dedicated parking, and pets considered and central air conditioning, this urban boating community gem is just minutes from the East Norwalk train station, trendy spots and ready to welcome you home.
