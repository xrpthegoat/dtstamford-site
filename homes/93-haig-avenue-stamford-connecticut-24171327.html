About this home

Welcome to 93 Haig Avenue, a lovingly maintained Cape in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood. Set on a level, professionally landscaped corner lot, this warm and inviting home offers 3-4 bedrooms and 3 full baths with flexible living space to fit today's needs. The heart of the home is a beautifully appointed kitchen with abundant cabinetry and countertop space, opening to a cozy dining area-perfect for everyday meals and gatherings. The living room exudes charm with hardwood floors, built-ins, and a fireplace that creates a comfortable place to relax and unwind. Upstairs, you'll find three sunlit bedrooms and a full bath, while the finished lower level offers additional living space with a bedroom and full bath-ideal for an in-law, au pair, or guest suite. Step outside to enjoy a thoughtfully designed garden area and manicured grounds, perfect for entertaining, play, or peaceful outdoor moments. Recent updates reflect true pride of ownership, including a renovated main bathroom (2024), new boiler (2022), new Andersen windows (2025) throughout the upper level, and hot water heater (2021). The home also features efficient gas heating and solar panels that transfer to the new owner. Enjoy the convenience of nearby shopping and dining within walking distance, as well as close proximity to the Springdale train station-making commuting to NYC a breeze. A special home where comfort, care, and functionality come together in a wonderful neighborhood setting.
