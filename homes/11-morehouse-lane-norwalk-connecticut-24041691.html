About this home

Come home to this well maintained Colonial on a coveted cul-de-sac in West Norwalk. Enter the home thru double front doors. Featuring private property with in-ground pool and expansive entertaining deck. Living room w/fireplace opening up to formal dining room. Kitchen with breakfast nook. Large family room with fireplace and glass sliders to deck. A study with built-ins and closet are directly off the family room. Ascend to the upper level where you will find the primary suite with fireplace. Walk-in closet and new bathroom. 3 other bedrooms with new bathroom complete this floor. This home has been lovingly maintained by the current owners and has the additional feature of a sprinkler system. Within minutes of Oak Hills Public Golf course with restaurant. Close to shopping and minutes from the towns of Darien and New Canaan.
