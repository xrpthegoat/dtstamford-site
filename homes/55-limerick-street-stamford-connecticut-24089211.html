About this home

This recently updated 2nd floor 2-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment showcases an inviting layout, boasting freshly stained hardwood floors, new paint and recessed lighting that accentuate its modern aesthetic. Inviting eat in kitchen also has a full size washer and dryer. Bonus sun room offers flexible space great for a home office. Two spacious bedrooms with nice natural light. Driveway parking ensures convenience for residents and guests alike, while the oversized walk up attic offers ample storage space. Situated at the end of a quiet cul de sac, this residence offers proximity to scenic waterfronts, parks, bustling downtown Stamford, and convenient access to the train station and I-95 for commuting.
