About this home

Located in one of Greenwich's most desirable neighborhoods, this meticulously maintained home offers the perfect blend of timeless elegance and modern comfort. Generous bedrooms provide comfortable accommodations, including a serene primary suite designed for relaxation. Set on beautifully landscaped property and conveniently close to town, schools, parks, transportation, and all that Greenwich has to offer!
