About this home

Set on 8 private acres in one of Greenwich's most prestigious enclaves, 38 Partridge Hollow Road offers luxury and tranquility and the ultimate privacy. Boarded by protected land on three sides, this five-bedroom, 7.5-bath home blends timeless elegance with modern sophistication, featuring a first-floor primary suite with dual baths, separate closets, and access to a private office and gym. Bright, sunlit interiors and a chef's kitchen flow into inviting living spaces ideal for entertaining. Outdoors, enjoy a pool, tennis court, and beautifully landscaped grounds. This exceptional estate offers both convenience and serenity.
