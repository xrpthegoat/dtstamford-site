About this home

Exquisite center hall Colonial presents classic architectural details paired with a thoroughly modern design on two private, peaceful acres with pool, waterfall and spa on Greenwich-Stamford border. Impeccably maintained and recently updated 8,562 SF home delivers inviting indoor/outdoor spaces perfect for effortless relaxation and entertaining. Beautifully appointed with high ceilings, French doors, refined millwork and 5 fireplaces. Chef's eat-in kitchen connects to family room, sunroom, office and elegant formal rooms. Upstairs, stunning primary suite with spa bath, steam shower and sitting room plus four comfortable bedrooms served by two baths and a laundry room. Game/playroom, gym, sauna and full bath comprise walkout lower level. Oversized three car garage, mudroom, two powder rooms. Improvement list attached to listing. Serene!
