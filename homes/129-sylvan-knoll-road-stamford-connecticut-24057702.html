About this home

This bright two bedroom townhouse is located on on a grassy courtyard with mature plantings. The quiet location gives it a sense of privacy. Special features include: a spacious eat-in kitchen with attractive eating area and built-in shelving; hardwood floors in living room and bedrooms; central air; lower level family room with built-in cabinetry and tile floor; a lower level utility room with washer and dryer; an attic with pull-down stairs; a brand new hot water heater; nice patio area; convenient street parking in front as well as 1 parking space in the lot. Sylvan Knoll is conveniently located off of exit 9 on I-95. It is walking distance to Cove Island Park, the beach and Chelsea Piers, and just a short drive to town, shopping, and the train station. Located on 13 park-like acres with sidewalks throughout, Sylvan Knoll is a pet friendly community. Units are all owner occupied. They cannot be rented. Real estate taxes are included in the common charges.
