About this home

Classic Old Hill Charm Where Character Meets Convenience Incredible location in the heart of Old Hill, with the ability to walk to downtown Westport shopping & dining, Kings Highway Elementary School, & Earthplace. This warm & inviting 3 BD, 2 BTH historic home is filled with classic details & thoughtful updates throughout. The expansive living room features a beautiful fieldstone fireplace and a cozy bay-window reading nook, while the spacious dining room sits adjacent to a bright kitchen with updated appliances, including a Viking range. A flexible first-floor bedroom, perfect as a guest room, office, or den, along with a renovated full bath, completes the main level. Upstairs, the large primary bedroom offers wonderful natural light, an updated hall bath, and generous closet space (with potential for a laundry area). Two additional bedrooms provide versatility for guests, family, or workspace needs. The dry, level lot includes a deck off the kitchen & a rear patio, ideal for outdoor dining &relaxation. With potential to add a garage or expand the living space, this home combines timeless charm, opportunity, & one of Westport's most desirable locations. The property boasts the convenience of gas, city water, & sewer. Situated in a fantastic location, it's just a few minutes from the train station, making commuting a breeze. Exciting plans for remodeling, crafted by a local architect known for their work on historical homes, are included to inspire your vision.
