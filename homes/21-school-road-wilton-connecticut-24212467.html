About this home

Welcome to an unparalleled masterpiece of living, nestled within a completely fenced, four-plus-acre private paradise in the very heart of Wilton, Connecticut. This extraordinary estate perfectly balances absolute serene privacy with the effortless convenience of town life. Approached via double-gated entries, the winding driveway unveils a residence meticulously crafted for the epicurean, the entertainer, and the modern visionary. The heart of the home centers around a true chef's kitchen, anchored by a roaring fireplace that radiates warmth and timeless character. Designed for both casual mornings and grand-scale catering, it features premium commercial appliances, a designated coffee bar room for your morning rituals, and endless custom storage to keep the aesthetic flawless. Just off the kitchen, a walk-in wine room awaits your private collection. For seamless entertaining, the formal sitting room features a fully equipped walk-in bar, transitioning beautifully into a stunning screened-in porch and a massive outside deck overlooking the grounds. Offering a total of six luxurious bedrooms, the home's layout is brilliantly engineered for ultimate privacy. The Owner's Suite and Family Quarters are situated in the main expanse of the home, offering opulent retreats with peaceful views of the estate.
