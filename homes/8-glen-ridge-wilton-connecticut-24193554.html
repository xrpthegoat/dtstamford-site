About this home

This beautiful, light-filled townhome is located in Glen River, one of Wilton's most sought-after communities. Offering the perfect blend of style, comfort, and convenience, the home features updated kitchen and baths, fresh hardwood floors, and thoughtful details throughout. The updated kitchen boasts modern finishes and stainless steel appliances, creating a clean, functional space for everyday living and entertaining. Adjacent open-concept living and dining areas are bathed in natural light, highlighted by beautiful custom millwork and a cozy wood-burning fireplace. Need a quiet place to work or relax? The cozy office/den features custom built-ins and a sunlit skylight. Wide sliding glass doors open to a private patio-ideal for morning coffee, alfresco dining, or unwinding at the end of the day. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet with a custom California Closet system and a beautifully updated full bath. The second bedroom features a dramatic vaulted ceiling, a palladium window, and its own walk-in closet. A full-sized second-level laundry adds true everyday convenience. A 1-car carport and plenty of guest parking are a great addition to this home. Residents of Glen River enjoy a private in-ground pool and an unbeatable Wilton location-just minutes to the town center, Metro-North train, local shopping, dining, and scenic walking trails.
