About this home

Nestled on a sought-after cul-de-sac in the coveted Hindley School district, this beautifully updated 5-bedroom, 4 full bath Colonial offers the perfect blend of coastal charm and modern luxury. With direct water access to Holly Pond, enjoy a true waterfront lifestyle complete with a private neighborhood dock - ideal for paddle boarding, kayaking, or simply soaking in the serene surroundings. Inside, the home exudes an effortless beach vibe with sun-filled spaces, tasteful updates, and a relaxed yet sophisticated feel throughout. The stunning updated primary suite bath creates a spa-like retreat, while spacious living and entertaining areas provide flexibility for today's lifestyle. Three private balconies showcase amazing water views, creating the perfect backdrop for morning coffee, sunset cocktails, or peaceful evenings overlooking the pond. Whether hosting summer gatherings by the water or enjoying quiet moments surrounded by nature, this exceptional home offers year-round vacation living in one of Darien's most desirable neighborhoods.
