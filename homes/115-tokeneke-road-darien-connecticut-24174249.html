About this home

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac known as Tokeneke Ridge, this impeccably maintained 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers privacy, space, and easy everyday living. The inviting floor plan features formal living and dining rooms, an eat-in kitchen, and a flexible bonus space just off the kitchen - ideal as a playroom, home office, media room, or teen hangout. The family room opens to a deck overlooking a large, flat backyard, perfect for play, entertaining, or simply enjoying the outdoors. Upstairs, the primary suite is joined by three additional bedrooms, creating a comfortable and practical layout. Set back from the road yet walkable to town and train, 115 Tokeneke Road blends classic charm, generous outdoor space, and a warm, welcoming feel in a truly special setting.
