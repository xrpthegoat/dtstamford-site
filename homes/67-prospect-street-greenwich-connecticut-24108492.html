About this home

67 Prospect is a gorgeous single family home for rent in central-downtown Greenwich with prominent exposures East and West for all day sunlight. Perfect for an in-town lifestyle with quick proximity to Greenwich Ave and for those who need to get to Manhattan you have easy access to Metro North because it's in walking distance. You'll love the modern open design of this home which features high ceilings, a foyer, powder room, gas fireplace in the living room, a gourmet kitchen with marble countertops, island, stainless steel appliances, wine cooler and large walk-in pantry. There is a breakfast area which opens to the deck and ZEN gated back patio where you can see ducks quietly float by in the brook in back of the house. Enjoy dinner and formal meals in your large, windowed dining room. The primary bedroom suite has a large walk-in closet, the guest bedroom features an en suite bath and the 2 additional double bedrooms share a full bath with skylight. Bonus storage in the attic and parking for 4 cars. Near to the TRAIN, the best parks, museums and beaches. Long Term Unfurnished one year or longer or Short Term Unfurnished no less than 6 months. No pets please. Agent/Owner.
