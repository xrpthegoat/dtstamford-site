About this home

Sophisticated Center Hall Colonial totally renovated and expanded in 2008. Arched detailed interior doorways, enormous gourmet kitchen with several chef stations, sinks, 2 dishwashers, calacatta gold marble counters and high end appliances provides the hub where friends & family enjoy gathering. The welcoming great room with coffered ceiling, French doors & fireplace leads to flagstone terrace with outdoor fireplace for indoor/outdoor entertaining. A 2-story solarium offers picturesque seasonal views and overlooks spectacular private property with heated pool, cabana & stream beyond. Luxurious master suite with dressing room & marble bath, rear staircase, finished walk-out lower level, generator, garages for 4 cars and so much more. Located close to restaurants, shopping and commuting. Additional 1,800 sq ft in finished lower level with full bath. Tenant to use landlord's service providers.
