About this home

Two homes in one! Nestled in the heart of Newfield, this beautifully expanded residence offers a perfect blend of modern luxury and classic charm. With its expansive layout, exquisite finishes, multiple skylights, and a resort-like backyard, this property is a rare find in today's market. The main living area boasts 5 bedrooms, 2 1/2 baths and an open-concept living area that seamlessly connects the living room, dining room, gourmet kitchen and family room with cozy fireplace with sun drenched windows. The kitchen features top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, including a double oven, gas cooktop, custom cabinetry and a large center island with seating. A spacious laundry room with modern appliances and plenty of storage separates the two living spaces. The second living space, features another beautiful bedroom, custom modern bath and tasteful eat in kitchen overflowing to plenty of living space. The outdoor oasis features a sparkling in-ground pool & hot tub surrounded by a beautifully landscaped yard and ample patio and deck space. The Gazebo is also a true delight with its outdoor TV and speakers. An attached two-car garage with additional storage space completes the home. This home is a must see for professionals and multi-generational living at its finest.
