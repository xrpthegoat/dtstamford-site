About this home

Welcome to 146 Dry Hill Rd, a beautifully expanded Cape offering 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and 1,940 square feet of light-filled living space on a 0.21-acre lot in Norwalk. Featuring a flexible multi-level layout, the home opens with a bright living room featuring a vaulted ceiling and cozy wood-burning stove that flows effortlessly into the open-concept eat-in kitchen. The kitchen is complete with an island, granite countertops, and an adjoining mudroom, complemented by a separate formal dining room and refinished hardwood floors throughout. Upstairs, a central hallway connects two additional bedrooms and a shared full bathroom featuring dual sinks. The renovated lower level expands your living space with a recreation room, a gym, new flooring, a new ceiling, an added window, and generous storage areas. Step outside to enjoy a large Trex deck overlooking a level, fenced-in backyard with a stone patio and fire pit-ideal for outdoor entertaining. Notable updates include an upgraded furnace, added central air, a new water heater, fiber cement siding, and a roof with added gutters. This amazing home and package is located in Wolfpit area, minutes from Stew Leonard's, Cranbury Park, and only a few minutes from I95 and Calf Pasture Beach. Properties offering this much space, style, and outdoor appeal in Norwalk rarely last--schedule your tour today!
