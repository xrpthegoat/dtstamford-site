About this home

Presenting turn-key coastal living with deeded access to one of Stamford's few PRIVATE ASSOCIATION BEACHES, shared by only a limited number of homes. Just a 4-minute walk from your front door, enjoy Long Island Sound for summer gatherings, sunset barbecues, 4th of July fireworks, and seasonal shell fishing -- a coveted lifestyle few Stamford homes offer. Renovated in 2019, this 3-bedroom, 3-bath home features a sun-filled open-concept main level with gleaming hardwood floors, FIREPLACE/wood stove, custom built-ins, recessed lighting, and GOURMET KITCHEN with twin skylights. A flexible office/sunroom opens to a spacious south-facing DECK, LARGE YARD, and seasonal LI Sound views. Upstairs, the primary suite features cathedral ceilings, skylights, dual closets, and spa-inspired bath. The oversized second bedroom with cedar closet offers potential to divide into two bedrooms. The finished lower level adds a game/media room, BEDROOM/office/music studio, full bath, wine room, laundry, and storage. Updates include newer windows, NATURAL GAS, four-head mini-split A/C and heat, whole-house filtration, Ethernet, and smart switches. Set on 1/3 level acre with mature gardens and newer landscaping. Minutes to Cummings and Cove Island Parks, Chelsea Piers, and I-95. PRIVATE BEACH RIGHTS, seasonal water views, large yard and move-in-ready style make this a rare Stamford offering -- a relaxed coastal lifestyle without sacrificing city convenience. NOT IN FLOOD ZONE.
