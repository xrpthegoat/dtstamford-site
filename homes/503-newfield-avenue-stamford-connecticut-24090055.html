About this home

Beautifully maintained family home that is in move-in -condition. Built in 1930 and renovated over the years this home still has timeless charm yet now with all the modern amenities for today's lifestyle. Enter the front door to the freshly painted first level which has a generous sized living room with fireplace, and dining room conveniently located next to the kitchen. On this level there is also a family room, or office. The heart of the home, the kitchen, recently renovated with top-of-the-line appliances, marble counter tops, great cabinet space, an island and many windows giving the space sunny vibes. Second level has 3 bedrooms, and access to the 3rd floor room, could be an additional family room, bedroom or office. There is a full basement with ample storage.
