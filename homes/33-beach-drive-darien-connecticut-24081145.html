About this home

Step into the best of coastal living with direct water frontage and access to Holly Pond leading to Long Island Sound, perfect for boating at all tides, water sports and enjoying nature. This beautifully-renovated home with an open floor plan provides extraordinary views from every window and French doors opening to an spacious deck overlooking the water, ideal for entertaining. Four bedrooms, two en-suite. Nestled on a quiet, private cul-de-sac in the desirable Noroton Manor Association. Friendly neighborhood, perfect for walking. Tennis and beach at either end of Beach Drive. Landscaping provided by the landlord. First month's rent and two months' security. Rental application and credit check. Pets considered. Furniture negotiable.
