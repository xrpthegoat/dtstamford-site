About this home

Vine Meadows at its best! This spacious townhouse offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and style in the heart of Stamford. The open living/dining room is ideal for entertaining, while the updated white kitchen with stainless steel appliances flows to a private bluestone patio and oversized yard-perfect for grilling, relaxing, or enjoying the sun. The primary suite is a true retreat with a spa-like bath featuring double sinks, jetted tub, walk-in shower, and an enviable walk-in closet. A second bedroom and full hallway bath complete the upper level. The versatile loft adds space for a family room, office, or guest suite, while the finished lower level includes a powder room and laundry. Additional highlights: attached 1-car garage plus 2 reserved spots, gas heat, city water/sewer. Recent upgrades to the complex include sealed driveways, enhanced drainage, and patios. This unit also boasts a new hot water heater, sump pump, refrigerator, stove, and microwave. Minutes to the Merritt, I-95, Metro North, shopping, restaurants, and more-move right in and start living the Vine Meadows lifestyle! Please note: pictures are from when the unit was occupied.
