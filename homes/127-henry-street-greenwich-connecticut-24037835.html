About this home

A wonderful alternative to a condo with plenty of potential, this cozy 2-bedroom, 1-bath home in Byram features a full attic with expansion possibilities and sufficient FAR to customize it to your liking. It includes a one-car vintage garage with electricity, perfect for a workshop or relaxing space. In the basement there is a handy workbench and walkout to the front yard. Excellent commuter location, close to Metro-North and I-95, and just a short stroll to the recently built New Lebanon Elementary School, Byram Schubert Library, shops, restaurants, and the newly upgraded Byram pool, park, beach, tennis, and marina. Great opportunity to buy in Greenwich.
