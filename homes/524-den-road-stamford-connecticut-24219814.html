About this home

Set back on 1.45 acres, this serene 4 bedroom / 3 bath fully renovated to the highest standards home, features an open concept kitchen, with modern Cafe appliances and quartz countertops, flowing into the living room, with a resurfaced wood burning fireplace, and a convenient dining area. An adjacent office/sitting room opens onto a new, low-maintenance deck overlooking the level backyard, complete with an outdoor fireplace and inviting gathering area. The walk-out LL includes a generous bedroom, full bathroom, a cozy family room and a new 480 SF bonus room ideal for a gym or playroom. Sliding doors to a covered terrace with fully equipped outdoor kitchen, dining and entertaining area, all while enjoying the quiet and beautiful views of your wooded surroundings.
