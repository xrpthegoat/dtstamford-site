About this home

Fall in love from the moment you step inside! Timeless character meets sophisticated modern style in this beautifully updated Stamford home, perfectly set on a picturesque, tree-lined street. Rich hardwood floors, graceful archways and glass-paneled doors create warmth and architectural charm, while stunning updates give this home the fresh, polished look today's buyers crave. The showstopping chef's kitchen is a true centerpiece, featuring sleek quartz countertops and beautiful finishes that make cooking and entertaining a pleasure. The flexible floor plan lets you live your way-the den and dining room can easily be interchanged depending on whether you prefer a more intimate dining experience or a larger space for entertaining. Curl up by the fireplace in the Living Room on a chilly evening with a good book and hot cup of coffee/tea. Upstairs, 3 spacious bedrooms and beautifully updated baths continue the stylish feel, while a walk-up attic provides fantastic easy-access storage. The partially finished lower level adds even more versatility-perfect for a media room, gym, game room or play space. Outside, a fenced, landscaped backyard and patio create a private retreat for summer dinners, cocktails and entertaining. Close to Barrett Park, downtown Stamford, Metro-North, I-95 and the Merritt. And walking distance to houses of worship. Style, character, flexibility and location-this one has it all! CLICK ON MOVIE ICON FOR VIRTUAL TOUR
