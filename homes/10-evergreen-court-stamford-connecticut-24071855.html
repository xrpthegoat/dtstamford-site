About this home

Nestled in a sought-after Stamford neighborhood, this 4 bedroom, 2 full bathroom colonial home is brimming with character and opportunity! The kitchen and bathrooms await your personal touch, providing a fab opportunity to modernize while preserving the home's charm. Main level features a spacious kitchen, a cozy fireplace in the living room, 1 bedroom and a den (currently used as bedroom), and 1 full bathroom. Enjoy indoor-outdoor living in the main level sunroom and large yard, perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs features another living room with access to the deck, summer kitchen, 3 more bedrooms, and another full bath. Conveniently located near parks, shopping, dining, bus route, train station and major highways (just minutes to the Merritt, I-95 & NY), this home is ideal for those looking to customize and create their dream home in a prime Stamford location!
