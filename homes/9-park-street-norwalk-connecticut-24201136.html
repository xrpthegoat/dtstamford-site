About this home

Located across from 'The Norwalk Green' and scenic St. Pauls, this tasteful and spacious one bedroom offers high end finishes, great views & abundant natural light. Walk outside and enjoy regular farmers markets, outdoor concerts and so much more. Walk to Wall Street dining or East Norwalk metro north. Inside this condominium unit you will find an open kitchen featuring quality cabinets, granite, SS appliances, breakfast bar and pendant lights. Beautiful espresso colored wood style tile floors - hypoallergenic. Bright updated bathroom. Exceptional closet storage. Well kept spotless building with abundant outdoor parking and optional underground garage parking (may be immediately available or may be short wait). Easy access to I-95, Routes 15 and 7. Pets are negotiable w/strong rental application. Must have great credit and references. Water included. Owner is a licensed CT real estate salesperson.
