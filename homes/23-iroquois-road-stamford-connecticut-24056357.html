About this home

Don't miss out on this rare 2-family home in the sought-after Shippan Point community, close to downtown Stamford and Metro North train, and just a stone's throw from access to Long Island Sound. The spacious first floor unit is bright and welcoming and offers 3 bedrooms, a large living room and an eat-in kitchen. It also benefits from basement access with plenty of usable space and a washer/dryer. The second floor unit features 2 bedrooms, a spacious kitchen with laundry area, plenty of space to relax, and even a "sleeping porch". Both units have decks and garden access. Whether you're looking for a sound investment, a home which also generates some revenue, or a house that suits multi-generational living, 23 Iroquois fits the bill. This is coastal living at its best without compromising on convenience or value.
