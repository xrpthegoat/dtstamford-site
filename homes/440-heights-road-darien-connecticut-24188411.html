About this home

Beautifully designed 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence featuring an open-concept floor plan in the heart of Noroton Heights. Thoughtfully laid out with foyer entry, spacious living and dining areas, and a dedicated full laundry room within the residence. The kitchen is appointed with GE Profile appliances, generous cabinetry, and an oversized island anchoring the main living space. Both bedrooms feature en suite baths and walk-in closets, blending refined finishes with everyday functionality. Residents enjoy secure building access, elevator, garage parking, fitness and wellness center, coworking spaces, golf simulator, fireside resident lounge, landscaped courtyard, and curated ground-level retail. Walkable to shopping, dining, coffee shops, grocery stores, and train access.
