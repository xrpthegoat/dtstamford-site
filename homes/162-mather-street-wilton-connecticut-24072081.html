About this home

Pristine, meticulously maintained home in Cannon Knolls. First time on market since sold as new in 2003. Very privately situated on 4+ acres with swimming pool site. (See plot map) Property backs onto open space in a peaceful, tranquil setting. An idyllic spot to entertain yet only a short distance from train station, shops, restaurants & all Wilton amenities. Spacious colonial home of 4875 sq ft on 1st and 2nd floors, plus 1400 sf +/- of beautifully finished space on lower level. 9 ft ceilings on 1st floor, 8 ft ceilings on 2nd floor. Many great features: Main floor: large gourmet kitchen w/center island, Wolf Range & Sub-Zero fridge/freezer. Family room w/wood burning fireplace & gas starter, wet bar and doors leading to large deck. Study with private patio. Two half baths. Huge Great room with 12 ft ceilings. Upper floor: Primary bedroom w/ wood burning fireplace, private balcony, 2 walk in closets, & spacious bathroom with separate tub & shower. 3 additional bedrooms, 2 additional bathrooms, & laundry room. Lower-level: Movie theatre, large recreation room & full bathroom. 3-car garage, large asphalt driveway w/basketball hoop. Cannon Knolls is a private neighborhood of 9 homes. No Homeowners Assoc, but residents share snowplowing & road maintenance costs. Underground utilities-one of the many positives of the development. Distances: Caraluzzi's Market 1.6 mi, Downtown Wilton 3.5 mi, Georgetown 1.8 mi, YMCA 2.8 mi, Cannondale Train Station 2.5 mi. Trains to Grand Central
