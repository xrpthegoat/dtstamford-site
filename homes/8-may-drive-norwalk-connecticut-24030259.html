About this home

Welcome to 8 May Drive, a distinguished Colonial home nestled on a generous corner lot in the Silvermine section of Norwalk, CT. This elegant residence boasts 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and spacious living space. The property sits on a sprawling lot, offering ample space for outdoor activities and relaxation. Upon arrival, you'll appreciate the convenience of the attached garage and the inviting porch, perfect for enjoying the tranquil surroundings. Inside, the home is bright, cozy and inviting with central AC for comfort throughout the seasons. The kitchen has direct access to a sun room addition which also leads to the stone patio and pool. Once outside discover a private oasis with a below ground pool, fenced yard, and a patio, ideal for entertaining guests or unwinding in style. Experience the epitome of refined living at 8 May Drive, where space and comfort harmoniously converge. Discover your new haven in this exquisite Norwalk residence and in the Silvermine community.
