About this home

Recently Renovated 3-Bedroom, 2.5-Bath Townhouse with Garage and Pool! Welcome to 45 Columbus Place, Unit #17, a spacious multi-level townhouse in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood. This recently renovated home offers 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and a comfortable, functional layout. The main level features an open living and dining area, an eat-in kitchen, and a convenient half bath. The generously sized primary bedroom offers plenty of room to relax, while two additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests or other needs. The finished lower level provides valuable additional living space, perfect for a family playroom, home office, recreation room, or exercise area, along with laundry and convenient access to your private one-car garage. Step outside onto your deck, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or relaxing after a long day. During the warmer months, take advantage of the community's in-ground swimming pool. Location, location, location! Conveniently located near the Merritt Parkway, with easy access to Darien, New Canaan, downtown Stamford, shopping, restaurants, parks, and Metro-North transportation. With recent renovations, a finished lower level, private garage, community pool, and convenient Springdale location, this townhouse offers a wonderful combination of space, versatility, and convenience.
