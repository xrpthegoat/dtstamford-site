About this home

Welcome to this bright and inviting corner unit in a small, well-maintained complex. This single-level, 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath home features brand-new luxury vinyl plank flooring, a new refrigerator and dishwasher, fresh carpeting, and has been recently painted throughout. The open floor plan connects a spacious dining area to the living room, where sliding doors open onto an oversized balcony, perfect for enjoying fresh air or relaxing outdoors. The primary bedroom includes direct access to a second private balcony with exterior entry, adding extra outdoor enjoyment and convenience. Additional updates include a refreshed powder room with new fixtures. Convenient in-unit laundry and ample storage space, including a generous 19' x 15' private storage room adjacent to an attached garage and one reserved parking space, make daily living effortless. Low common charges and a self-managed association ensure easy and affordable ownership. Ideally located near the Merritt Parkway, I-95, schools, parks, and shopping, and just a short walk to the Springdale train station, offering a simple 45-minute commute to NYC. The building is pet-friendly too. This unit truly checks all the boxes.
