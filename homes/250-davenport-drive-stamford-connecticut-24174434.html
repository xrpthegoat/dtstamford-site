About this home

Set on 1.68 extraordinary acres at the end of Davenport Drive, this waterfront peninsula property offers a rare chance to reimagine and transform an existing residence into a custom coastal estate. Offered for the first time in 76 years, the home is surrounded by water on three sides, providing sweeping 180-degree eastern, southern, and western exposures that fill the interiors with natural light and frame panoramic views from sunrise through sunset. The current residence offers a blank canvas for the next owner to bring their vision to life. Whether through a comprehensive restoration or a complete reconfiguration, the opportunity exists to create a home that fully realizes the potential of this remarkable setting. With a completed survey and feasibility study already in place, much of the initial groundwork has been done. The property also offers the best of both worlds - privacy and seclusion at the end of a quiet road in the Southfield Association and extreme proximity to downtown Stamford and Old Greenwich - granting easy access to restaurants, shopping, beaches, and train service. It's a rare opportunity to reimagine a home that is placed on one of the most special pieces of property in Fairfield County.
