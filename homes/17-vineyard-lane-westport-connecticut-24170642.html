About this home

Stunning custom-built residence, newly completed in 2024, set within one of Westport's most coveted enclaves. Impeccably designed, this home offers soaring ceilings, refined finishes, and exceptional craftsmanship throughout. At the heart of the home, a chef's kitchen features an oversized center island, Thermador appliance package, seamlessly opening to the dining room adorned with Thibaut wallpaper. The family room showcases vaulted ceilings, a Carrara marble gas fireplace, and French doors leading to the backyard and patio for effortless indoor-outdoor living. The main level also includes a formal living room, office, powder room, mudroom, and access to a three-car garage. Upstairs, the primary suite features vaulted ceilings, two oversized walk-in closets, and a spa-like bath with double vanities, soaking tub, and glass-enclosed shower. Three additional bedrooms, two full baths, and a spacious laundry room complete the second level. A finished third floor provides a versatile bonus room with full bath. The lower level features a media room, fitness space, second office, and ensuite guest suite. Professionally landscaped and fenced, the grounds offer privacy and a beautiful setting for outdoor living. Just minutes to Westport's beaches, shopping, dining and metro north for seamless access to NYC.
