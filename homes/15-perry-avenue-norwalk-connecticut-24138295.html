About this home

Welcome to Riverbend Common and this beautifully updated 1-bedroom + loft, 2-bath townhome tucked quietly away in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations. Set within a boutique, well-maintained community, this residence delivers modern effortless living: the main level showcases a sun-soaked, south facing living room with dramatic vaulted ceilings, wood-burning fireplace, powder room, and seamless indoor/outdoor flow to a private deck - perfect for morning coffee, evening wine, or laptop work days. The recently renovated galley kitchen features new sleek quartz countertops, new designer tile backsplash, and new stainless steel appliances. Off the living room, a spiral staircase leads to a spectacular loft with two skylights - ideal for home office, den, or guest quarters. The spacious primary suite offers two oversized closets, soaring ceilings, and updated full bath. Lower level includes private in-unit laundry, abundant storage, and direct access to your own private garage. Minutes to Tavern at Gray Barns, SONO, Metro North, I-95, Cranbury Park, Calf Pasture Beach, wellness, retail, The Sono Collection, Whole Foods, Stew Leonard's, Stamford + Westport line. Ultra commutable in every direction. Low HOA. Pet friendly. Incredible value. Turn key. Move right in and enjoy!
