About this home

Welcome to 47 Cedar Street #2, a bright one-bedroom condo in the heart of South Norwalk. Built in 1989 and newly updated, this 609-square-foot home features updated vinyl flooring throughout, new kitchen cabinets, counters, and appliances, and in-unit washer and dryer. Enter through a central hallway that connects to all living spaces. The kitchen sits adjacent to the living room, which opens to a balcony. The bedroom is positioned on the opposite side of the unit with a large closet providing ample storage. The bathroom is centrally located between public and private zones, with additional closet space in the entry and hallway. Baseboard electric heat and wall units for AC keep utilities simple and efficient. This condo is less than a mile to the South Norwalk train station and walking distance from The SoNo Collection mall, with restaurants, shops, and nightlife right around the corner. A rare opportunity to own in one of Norwalk's most vibrant and convenient locations.
