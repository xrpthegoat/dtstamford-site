About this home

This beautiful move-in ready Colonial is tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac in the desirable Riverside neighbourhood. It offers the perfect blend of modern style and everyday comfort. The sun-filled open floor plan is designed for today's lifestyle, featuring a stunning chef's kitchen with quartz countertops, a large center island, stainless steel appliances, and abundant cabinetry. The spacious living and dining areas flow seamlessly together, creating an ideal setting for both entertaining and everyday living. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet and an updated bath. Additional highlights include recessed lights, central air, natural gas heat, main-level laundry, an attached garage, and thoughtfully updated finishes throughout. Enjoy the private deck, patio and fenced backyard, perfect for outdoor dining, relaxing, or entertaining family and friends. Conveniently located just minutes from schools, parks, shopping, restaurants, Metro-North, and I-95, this move-in ready home offers the best of Greenwich living with an easy commute to New York City.
