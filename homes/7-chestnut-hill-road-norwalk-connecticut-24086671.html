About this home

Charming Ranch-Style Gem on Half an Acre - Walk to Cranbury Market! Tucked away on a quiet private lane, this spacious and inviting ranch-style home offers over 2,200 sq ft of comfortable living in one of the area's most sought-after locations. Hardwood floors flow throughout, adding warmth and character to this 3-bedroom home that blends charm, convenience and versatility. The welcoming living room features a beautiful fireplace, creating a cozy space to relax or entertain. A large eat-in kitchen opens to a sunny breakfast room, while the formal dining room is ideal for special gatherings. Bonus rooms provide flexible options for a home office, playroom, gym or creative space. Enjoy beautiful views year-round from the bright sunroom or step outside to a generous half-acre lot perfect for gardening, entertaining or simply enjoying peaceful surroundings. Just a short walk to the local Cranbury neighborhood shops, this home offers privacy with easy access to local amenities. Conveniently located less then 2 miles to both the new Cranbury elementary & middle schools. Don't miss your chance to make this well-loved home your own! Just an hour to NYC by train, Norwalk is ideal for commuters. Norwalk, a vibrant coastal city blending New England charm with an energetic lifestyle. Beaches, Maritime roots and a thriving harbor, Norwalk offers a mix of coastal living, rich culture and urban convenience. SoNo offers the mall, restaurants, waterfront bars & the Maritime Aquarium.
