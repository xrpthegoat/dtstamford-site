About this home

This fully renovated 2 bed, 1 bath first-floor apartment features a bright, open layout with a brand-new custom kitchen and spa-style bathroom. The kitchen offers premium appliances, wood cabinetry, ceramic tile, and stone countertops. The bathroom includes a thermostatic temperature control walk-in shower, wood cabinetry, tile, stone and enamel cast iron finishes. Both spacious bedrooms feature ample closet space, new carpet, and large windows with natural eastern and northern light. Additional upgrades include LED lighting, interior closet lighting, blackout cellular shades with top-down, bottom-up functionality, and a dedicated Verizon Optical Network Terminal. Ideally located within a short distance to Downtown Greenwich parks, restaurants, cafes, Whole Foods, fitness, and shops.
