About this home

Stunning townhouse in the heart of downtown Greenwich. This 3 bedroom, 3 1/2 bath townhome has it all and just a short walk brings you to the Metro, Bruce Park and some of the areas finest restaurants and shopping. This updated and beautifully furnished townhome has the feel of a single family home. High ceilings and fine details are sure to please the most discerning tenant. Spacious bedrooms including a luxurious primary suite with full bath including separate steam shower and whirlpool tub, double sinks and marble finishes. Terrific flow for entertaining on main level. Spacious great room with family area, eat-in kitchen w/center island. Granite and stainless in cook's kitchen. Separate formal dining room. Enjoy dinner and a barbeque on the private back patio. The finished lower level boasts a large finished bonus family room/playroom or exercise area plus another bedroom/office and a full bath. Attached 1 car garaged plus addional off-street parking. Available partially furnished or unfurnished. Pets considered on a case by case basis. There is a $300/pet 1 time non-refundable pet rent.
