About this home

This terrific East Norwalk Victorian has it all!!! A short walk will bring you to Metro North RR, Calf Pasture Beach, Veterans Park, nearby Restaurants or your choice of ice cream destinations. With a total of 9 separate rooms/living spaces this home offers versatility few others do. The 1st level is comprised of a living room, dining room, well appointed renovated cook's kitchen, bedroom & a full bathroom. As this home was once configured as a 2 family home, the 2nd level offers an en-suite primary bedroom w/luxurious bathroom, a 2nd full bathroom and a large family room (could be easily enclosed to create a 3rd bedroom) The 3rd floor is configured as 3 small rooms and could suit the needs of home office or hobby space. The basement is large & offers great storage or potential for future use. Outside you will love the period 1890s front porch, patio w/covered area for sunny days & al fresco dining. Many recent updates/upgrades including brazillian cherry hardwood floors, new natural gas furnace, C/A, appliances throughout, jacuzzi soaking tub, renovated kitchen, 2 asphalt driveways, beautiful fencing & much more!!! Do not miss video virtual tour, floor plans and Public Open Houses this Saturday & Sunday!!
