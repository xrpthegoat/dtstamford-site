About this home

Set at the end of a quiet private cul-de-sac, this beautifully maintained North Stamford contemporary offers privacy, natural beauty, and inviting living spaces. The gently rolling front lawn leads to a serene spring-fed pond and dock, creating a picturesque setting. Expansive windows and skylights fill the dramatic two-and-a-half-story foyer with natural light. The open layout flows from the living room with a marble-surround fireplace to the formal dining room and vaulted family room featuring a beamed ceiling, fieldstone fireplace, and pond views. The kitchen offers a center island, walk-in pantry, adjoining breakfast area, and sliders opening to a wraparound deck. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features two walk-in closets and an en suite bath with double vanity, shower, whirlpool tub, and skylight. Three additional large bedrooms and a full bath complete the upper level.
