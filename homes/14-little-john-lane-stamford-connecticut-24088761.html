About this home

Welcome to 14 Little John Lane, a charming retreat nestled in the heart of Stamford's quintessential Sherwood Forest-themed neighborhood. Situated on a quiet cul-de-sac that backs up to the Woodway Country Club golf course, this delightful home offers a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Step inside from the new portico to discover 1,914 square feet of living space, where three cozy bedrooms and two updated full baths await. The foyer leads to a spacious living area adorned with hardwood floors and skylights that bathe the space in sunshine. The open-concept kitchen boasts granite counter tops, stainless steel appliances, skylight and an island along with a breakfast room with vaulted ceiling. Entertain guests on the expansive deck, complete with an awning, overlooking the lovely flat half-acre lot. The fully fenced backyard is ideal for playful afternoons. Enjoy the practicalities of a whole house generator, an attached garage, central AC, and natural gas heat. Available documents of interest include improvements list (roof replaced in the last year), professionally drawn expansion plans and survey. This home is part of a vibrant Springdale community as well as in close proximity to New Canaan and Darien. 14 Little John Lane is not just a home-it's a lifestyle. Come see for yourself!
