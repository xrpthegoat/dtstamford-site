About this home

Tucked in the highly desirable Broad River neighborhood, this 3 bedroom, 2 bath home with recent updates is one you won't want to miss! The bright and welcoming living room is perfect for entertaining, while the kitchen and dining area flow seamlessly together for easy everyday living. Just off the kitchen, a large sunroom floods the space with natural light, perfect for morning coffee or a cozy place to relax year round. Upstairs, the master bedroom offers custom built-ins and a private full bath, your own peaceful retreat at the end of the day. When warmer weather arrives, head outside to the patio and enjoy the fully fenced in backyard. Rest easy knowing the big-ticket items have been taken care of. The home features a durable steel roof installed just 6 years ago and a brand-new boiler, fully replaced in March 2026. Enjoy plenty of storage throughout, plus exciting potential in the unfinished lower level. All of this, conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and the train station.
