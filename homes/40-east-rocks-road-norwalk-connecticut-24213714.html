About this home

Light, bright and open describe the main living spaces of this 3 bedroom, 3 bath raised ranch in the Cranbury neighborhood! The front foyer welcomes you right into the living room, with a huge picture window in the front and pretty sunlight streaming in from the sides making the space feel calm and relaxing. Flowing directlly into the dining room, and kitchen beyond, the main floor rooms connect easily for day to day funtion and entertaining! Continuing to the bedroom wing, the primary ensuite bedroom plus two additional bedrooms and a full bath are well proportioned and private. The lower level includes a great sized recreation room with fireplace and walkout to the backyard, plus an office/studio room, and a half bath/laundry room. A deck overlooks the pretty yard with privacy plantings, and the property is a half acre. There is a two car garage and plenty of storage space. This home has been lovingly cared for and is ready to be enjoyed!
