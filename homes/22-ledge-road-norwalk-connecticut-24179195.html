About this home

Nestled on one of Rowayton's favorite cul-de-sacs, 22 Ledge Road offers the perfect blend of charm, space, functionality, and outdoor living. Set on an oversized, flat lot, this beautifully updated 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home spans over 3,400 square feet above grade plus finished lower-level space. Originally built in 1954 and fully renovated in 2015, the home features an open kitchen, stunning primary suite with custom walk-in dressing room and en suite bath, beautiful laundry room, wood flooring throughout, and thoughtful updates inside and out. Recent improvements include a professionally designed patio with fire pit, extensive landscaping, exterior lighting, automated irrigation, and flourishing raspberry and blueberry bushes. A welcoming foyer opens to the heart of the home, where the kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining room, living room with wood-burning fireplace, and family room with access to the patio. Four bedrooms are located upstairs, including the primary suite and an additional en suite bedroom. The fifth bedroom and full bath are on the main level, ideal for guests. The finished lower level provides flexible living, recreation, or gym space. The expansive backyard offers ample room for entertaining, relaxation, and future pool possibilities. The train, schools, village center, beaches, and waterfront are all just a short walk away. A rare opportunity to enjoy the best of Rowayton living.
