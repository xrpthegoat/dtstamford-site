About this home

Rarely does a home offer such an extraordinary combination of setting, style, and convenience, while being FEMA compliant. Perched along Long Island Sound in the heart of Saugatuck, this beautifully renovated residence captures sweeping 270-degree waterfront views, with sailboats, paddleboarders, and the ever-changing rhythm of the Sound providing a captivating backdrop from morning through evening. Inside, sophisticated design meets relaxed coastal living. The home was thoughtfully reimagined by its talented local photographer owner, whose discerning eye is evident throughout. A striking new kitchen, beautifully renovated bathrooms, and hardwood floors create a refined yet welcoming interior. The waterfront experience continues outdoors with a private, low-maintenance yard and patio designed for effortless entertaining and quiet enjoyment. A private dock accommodates a small boat or kayak, putting the Sound just steps away and making boating and time on the water an integral part of daily life. Beyond its remarkable waterfront setting, the property offers exceptional accessibility, with the train station only minutes away for a convenient commute to New York City. A truly special Saugatuck offering where design, nature, and an enviable waterfront lifestyle come together.
