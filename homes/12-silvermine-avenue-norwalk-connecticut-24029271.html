About this home

This spacious warm cape cod offers the ease of one floor living and features a large living room, country kitchen with access to deck, two bedrooms, full bath, and attached 1 car garage. The corner lot with deck offers privacy and allows for easy three season entertaining. Additional features include Hardwood floors and large basement that can be finished to accommodate an exercise or playroom. This charming home is located on the corner of Lakeview and Silvermine Avenue and located only 5 minutes from all that Norwalk has to offer including beautiful town beaches, South Norwalk/Maritime Aquarium, shopping/restaurants, I-95, Merritt Parkway, and East & South Norwalk Train Station.
