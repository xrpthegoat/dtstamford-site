About this home

Nestled near the coveted New Canaan/Darien border in West Norwalk, "Sanctuary West" delivers the perfect blend of space, functionality and serene natural surroundings. Thoughtfully renovated and impeccably maintained, this home features over 2,500 square feet of finished living space designed for both everyday comfort and effortless entertaining. The heart of the home is the stunning white kitchen, renovated with timeless style and equipped with stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, and an inviting layout that seamlessly connects to the main living areas. Hardwood floors run throughout the home, while oversized windows fill the space with natural light. A highly versatile floor plan includes a main level bedroom with private full bath; ideal for guests, in-laws or au pair accommodations. Upstairs, you'll find three additional bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite complete with dual walk-in closets and a large bathroom. The finished lower level offers exceptional bonus space perfect for a media room, playroom, gym or home office. Step outside to experience your own private retreat. An elevated deck overlooks beautifully landscaped grounds accented by mature gardens, a charming footbridge, and a peaceful babbling brook that winds through the wooded backyard - creating a setting that feels worlds away while remaining close to everything. A newly installed stone patio with firepit gives you another outdoor entertaining dimension.
