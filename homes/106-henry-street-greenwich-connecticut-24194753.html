About this home

*UTILITIES INCLUDED* Welcome to this renovated 2-bedroom first floor unit. Private entrance, beautiful kitchen with stainless appliances, freshly painted, hardwood floors, and a brand-new bathroom. Lower level offers private laundry room, and an oversized 1 car garage for lots of storage space. 2 additional off street parking spots, and shared green space. All utilities except electric is included in the list price. Ideally situated near Mill Street, you'll enjoy convenient access to a variety of shops and restaurants, offering local dining and retail experiences in both Greenwich and Port Chester. This unit is also close to Byram Beach, the pool/ park, Shubert Library, New Lebanon Elementary School, and the skating rink. Commuting is simple with easy access to the train station.
