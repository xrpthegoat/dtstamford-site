About this home

Charming 4-Bedroom, 2-Bath Cape in Mid-City! This inviting home features a spacious living room and dining room, a kitchen with granite countertops and a brand-new dishwasher, and a large deck overlooking the backyard and river-perfect for outdoor entertaining. Two bedrooms and a full bath are conveniently located on the main level, with two additional bedrooms upstairs. The interior has been freshly painted throughout, with new flooring on the main level and refinished stairs. The finished lower level offers flexible space for a playroom, home office, or gym, complete with a full bathroom and walk-out access to the backyard. Ideally located just minutes from the Metro North station, I-95, schools, shopping, and the dining and entertainment of Downtown Stamford and Harbor Point. Sold As Is.
