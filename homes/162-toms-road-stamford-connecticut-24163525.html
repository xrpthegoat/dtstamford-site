About this home

Charming Colonial that truly has it all! This 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home blends classic character with exciting potential, offering the perfect opportunity to create your dream space. With solid bones and hardwood floors throughout the living room, dining room and bedrooms, this home is warm, inviting, and ready for its next chapter. The living room sets the scene for cozy winter nights, while the sun-filled sunroom is perfect for morning coffee, reading, or working from home. Upstairs features three generously sized bedrooms and a full bath. The lower level offers a versatile room-ideal for an office, music room, or creative space. Step outside to a flat, fenced-in backyard-perfect for gardening, entertaining, playtime, or letting the dogs run free. And a full house generator adds to the perfection. Ideally located in a highly desirable area, within walking distance to houses of worship and just minutes to downtown Stamford's best dining, shopping, and entertainment. Easy access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway makes commuting a breeze. And only minutes away from Stamford's beautiful beaches. A rare blend of charm, location, and lifestyle-don't miss this one!
