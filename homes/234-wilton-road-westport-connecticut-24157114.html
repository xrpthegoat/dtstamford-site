About this home

Wilton Road places you minutes from town and the shoreline. At 234, convenience and privacy coexist. A turn onto a side lane and a set-back approach behind mature trees create a setting that feels distinctly separate from the street. Originally built in 1884, the home has been fully renovated and thoughtfully expanded. A 2024 addition reshaped the main level, enlarging the kitchen and introducing a newly constructed family room anchoring the home. Tall ceilings, open sight lines, and abundant natural light create a sense of volume rarely found in homes of this era. It lives larger than its footprint. The kitchen centers around an oversized island, with a cozy banquette tucked into the corner. A beverage bar and architectural wine display are seamlessly integrated. The flow from kitchen to family room to dining is open and intuitive. Sliders extend to an expansive stone patio with a built-in grill station and fireplace, creating an easy transition between indoors and out. An expansive lawn invites play, set within the safety of the private backyard. Upstairs, the primary suite features a private sitting room, boutique-style dressing room, and a light-filled bath with a soaking tub and glass-enclosed shower. Additional bedrooms are carefully arranged, including a private suite, offering flexibility for guests. Historic at its core and thoughtfully expanded for today. Set where Westport unfolds at your doorstep, and designed with the scale to fully enjoy it.
