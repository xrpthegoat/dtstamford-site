About this home

Welcome to Chesterfield! This sought-after gated community offers 24-hour security gatehouse and a prime location directly across from Ridgeway Shopping Center with shops, gym, and grocery store, plus easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and Metro-North. This bright, spacious unit has been beautifully updated throughout. The living room features gleaming hardwood floors, skylights, a fireplace, and soaring ceilings, with an adjacent dining area and kitchen boasting stainless steel appliances, granite counters, stone backsplash, and in-unit washer/dryer. Step out to your private balcony for fresh air and relaxation. Down the hall are two generously sized bedrooms and two renovated baths, including a primary suite with double closets and an en-suite bath. In summer, enjoy lounging by the pool or take a short walk downtown to experience Stamford's shops, dining, and entertainment. Two unassigned parking spaces are allowed per unit.
