About this home

Welcome to 51 Old Kings Hwy in Old Greenwich! This bright and spacious 2-bedroom, 2 full bathroom condo is located in a well-maintained and desirable complex just minutes from everything Old Greenwich has to offer. The home features very high ceilings in both the living room and primary bedroom, creating an open and airy feel throughout the main living space. The living room and kitchen flow nicely together and are overlooked by a sizable loft area, perfect for a home office, den, or additional living space. The unit offers great natural light, comfortable living areas, and a functional layout. Conveniently located close to Binney Park, the Old Greenwich downtown area, the Old Greenwich train station, and Greenwich Point Park, this location offers easy access to shops, restaurants, parks, and commuting. A great rental opportunity in a prime Old Greenwich location.
