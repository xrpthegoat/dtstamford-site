About this home

This beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 2-bath ranch-style home offers modern living in a peaceful setting. Located on a quiet street, the home features an updated eat-in kitchen and fully renovated bathrooms, blending style and functionality. The inviting living room with a cozy fireplace creates the perfect space to unwind, while the large family room, with sliders leading out to the private back deck and yard, offers a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Enjoy the outdoors with a dedicated gardening area and a storage shed for all your tools. The clean, dry basement and attic provide ample storage space, and newer mechanicals, extra insulation, and a 50+ year roof ensure peace of mind. The 2-car garage, complete with an epoxy-coated floor, adds a sleek finish and easy maintenance. Conveniently located near the Merritt Parkway, local shopping, public transportation, and all that downtown Stamford has to offer, this home combines comfort, style, and accessibility. With stunning curb appeal and thoughtful updates throughout, it is move-in ready and waiting for you! ***All offers are due by 5pm on Monday (10/28)***
