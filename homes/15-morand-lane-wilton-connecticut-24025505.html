About this home

Convenient yet private and renovated for modern living! The main level was totally renovated in 2017, transposing the space into an open floor plan living area that seamlessly extends to the screened in porch and patio. Perfect for outdoor enjoyment! Among the 5 bedrooms and three-and-a-half baths, a spacious in-law suite in a separate wing boasts a dedicated full bath, private entry, and patio. Nestled on 2 acres in a small tranquil South Wilton cul-de-sac, this home enjoys proximity to schools, town and commuting arteries along with the privacy of two acres, lush grounds, handsome stone walls and mature plantings. A three-car garage adds to the unique benefits, practicality, and charm of this special opportunity.
