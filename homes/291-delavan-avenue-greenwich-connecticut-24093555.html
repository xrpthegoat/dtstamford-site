About this home

Step into timeless charm with this spacious 1920s Colonial, ideally located within walking distance to shops, schools, and public transit. A bright and inviting front sun porch welcomes you, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing afternoons. Inside, you'll find stunning period woodwork, generously sized bedrooms, and a dedicated home office on the second floor, plus a walk-up attic offering additional potential. The updated kitchen features modern appliances, while newly installed mini-split A/C units ensure year-round comfort. The full basement boasts high ceilings and ample space for a home gym, playroom, or creative studio. Enjoy outdoor living in the level, fully fenced backyard, complete with a detached two-car garage accessible from Spruce Street. Energy-efficient upgrades help keep utility costs low, making this home as practical as it is charming. With plenty of parking and a commuter-friendly location, this home truly has it all-classic character, modern comfort, and unbeatable convenience.
