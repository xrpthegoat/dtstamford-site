About this home

Welcome to 47-49 Indian Harbor Drive-an exceptional end-unit townhouse offering the perfect blend of space, style, and location in the heart of Greenwich. This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath home features approximately 2,200 square feet of open-concept living on the main level. The inviting living room with a wood-burning fireplace flows seamlessly to sliders leading out to a private deck and fenced, landscaped backyard-ideal for entertaining or everyday enjoyment. The gourmet kitchen is designed to impress with rich cherry cabinetry and sleek black granite countertops. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat with vaulted ceilings, skylights, a spacious walk-in closet, and a private bath complete with soaking tub and separate shower. Two additional sun-filled bedrooms-one with its own private balcony-share a well-appointed hall bath with convenient laundry. The finished lower level offers incredible versatility with a second wood-burning fireplace, full bathroom, workshop, and utility room with sink-perfect for extended living, recreation, or creative space. Additional highlights include a private one-car garage and an assigned outdoor parking space. Very short walk to the MetroNorth and Greenwich Train Station getting you Grand Central. Ideally located just steps from Bruce Park and the Bruce Museum, and moments to the shops and dining of Greenwich Avenue, as well as I-95 for easy commuting. Make your appointment to view today!
