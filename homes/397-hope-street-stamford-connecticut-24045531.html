About this home

Highest and best by 5:00PM, Friday October 4, 2024. Welcome to 397 Hope Street. This two family gem is located on a large corner lot in the heart of Glenbrook. Whether you are looking for a traditional two family, an in law apartment or a rental to offset your mortgage, this property can satisfy your needs!
