About this home

Welcome to 7 Tait Road, an exquisite Colonial home offering superior quality construction and thoughtful modern design in coveted Old Greenwich. Step onto the welcoming flagstone entrance and discover an expansive open-concept living space defined by exceptional interior finishes. Highlights include 5" White Oak hardwood floors, custom glass door separating the mud room from the kitchen allowing tremendous natural light, a striking walnut accent wall along the stair shaft with sleek cable guard rails, and custom decorative fixtures. The main level is designed for effortless entertaining, featuring a living room with a gas fireplace framed by custom millwork and built-in bookshelves. The adjoining family room boasts a custom California Closets entertainment area complete with wet bar, refrigeration drawers, and floating display shelving. Outside, relax or host guests on the private flagstone side patio. On the second level, retreat to the luxurious Primary Suite, showcasing a custom built-in closet by California Closets and a stunning primary bath with a walk-in shower and double vanity. Two additional light-filled bedrooms (one with an ensuite bath featuring a glass-enclosed shower/tub combo) offer designer tile by Walker Zanger, complemented by a dedicated laundry room with full-size stackable units. The finished third floor provides incredible versatility as a fourth bedroom or bonus room, complete with its own full bath, separate mechanical room, and abundant storage.
