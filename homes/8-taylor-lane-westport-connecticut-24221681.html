About this home

Located privately on 4.4+/- acres at the end of a premier cul-de-sac in Greens Farms, this is a remarkable opportunity to live in an extraordinarily convenient yet peaceful estate setting. Many of the area's most distinctive, upscale residences surround this exciting Modern showplace designed by Mario Polizzotti, the celebrated architect of fine homes & public venues. The long driveway with motor court leads to this entertainer's paradise. Step into the foyer, and the sun-splashed dramatic spaces begin to unfold. The home evokes a sense of luxury & grace through extensive use of large windows, wood flrs, gallery walls & soaring ceilings. The centerpiece is a spectacular Great Room w/3-sided fpl & French doors to one of the picturesque porches. The grand dining rm invites holiday gatherings or cozy fireside evenings. Custom gourmet kitchen equipped w/Sub Zero ref/frz & wine chiller, dual dishwashers and leads to both an atrium-like brkfst rm & an exquisite double-height screened-in porch for impressive alfresco dining. The family rm has another fpl and custom built-ins. The flexible layout offers several home office spaces. 6 bedrooms provide space for everyone. An exceptional primary suite has a library/study, king-size BR, expansive sun-filled marble bath & two fantastic walk-in closets. Vast finished LL incls game/play rm, gym, wine cellar & storage. Spend glorious days by the sparkling heated Glen Gate pool & waterfall spa - moments from it all but in your own sanctuary.
