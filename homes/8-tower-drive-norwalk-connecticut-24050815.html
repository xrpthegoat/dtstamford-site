About this home

Beautiful 4 BR 2 Full Bath home- totally updated to today's modern tastes! Set on nicely landscaped property in the Spring Hill area of Norwalk on a cul-de-sac on a quiet street, it features an open floor plan with Living Room that opens to the Kitchen with new SS Appliances, breakfast bar, quartz counter tops and has access to the deck overlooking the fenced backyard. The main level has 2 BRs and 1 full bath with double vanity and the upper level has 2 additional BRs with lots of storage and another full bath with beautiful enclosed glass shower. Finished Walkout Lower Level has Family Room/ Office/ Exercise Room, Mechanical Room and Laundry Room with cabinets and folding counter. It also has access to the garage and backyard. Features include hardwood floors, recessed lighting and Central AC. Convenient to Route 7, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Ready to move in! Do not miss it!!!
