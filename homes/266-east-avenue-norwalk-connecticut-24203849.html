About this home

Bright and inviting third-floor apartment in a convenient East Norwalk location! This charming 1-bedroom, 1-bath home offers approximately 700 square feet of comfortable living space with plenty of natural light and a cozy, private feel. The apartment features a spacious living area with distinctive sloped ceilings, neutral carpeting, and a window A/C unit. The eat-in kitchen offers generous cabinet and counter space, a full-size refrigerator and range, double sink, and an abundance of natural light. The bedroom includes closet storage and the same light, neutral finishes found throughout the home. Fresh, clean, and move-in ready, this apartment offers an efficient layout in a highly convenient location close to East Norwalk amenities, shopping, dining, transportation, and everything the area has to offer. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable living in the heart of East Norwalk.
