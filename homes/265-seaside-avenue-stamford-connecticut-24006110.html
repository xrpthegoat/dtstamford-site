About this home

This is a Church which sits in a multi-family zone in the City of Stamford, south of I-95 and walking distance to Cove Island. The main level has an open floor plan. The lower level is finished with a full kitchen and 2 half baths. Both floors are heated and cooled with hot air heat and A/C. The R5 Zone allows for many uses, including but not limited to use as a residence with 2/3 units, a residence with a day care in lower level, and more. City amenities include water, sewer, gas and electric. Heat is fueled by oil. As a tax-exempt organization, the property tax is estimated at $11,758, which should be verified with the City of Stamford. This building is being sold as-is and the buyer must do their own due-diligence. The piano and organ are excluded from the sale.
