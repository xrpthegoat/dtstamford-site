About this home

Welcome to 17 Thornhill, a beautifully appointed home offering exceptional space, versatility and effortless indoor-outdoor living. Sun-filled interiors feature hardwood floors and an inviting flow from the spacious living room to a warm family room anchored by a striking floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace. The eat-in kitchen offers granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and direct access to the deck, creating an ideal setting for everyday living and entertaining. A first-floor bedroom/office with adjacent full bath and conveniently tucked-away laundry provides wonderful flexibility. Upstairs, the serene primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, custom walk-in closet and spa-like bath with double vanity, soaking tub and glass-enclosed shower. Two additional bedrooms and a beautifully updated hall bath complete the level. A spectacular bonus room above the garage impresses with soaring vaulted ceilings, exposed beams, skylight, hardwood floors and private balcony, perfect for a media room, playroom, gym or office. Outdoors, enjoy an expansive, level and fully fenced backyard with mature landscaping, stone walls, spacious deck, stone patio/fire pit area and garden beds. A rare combination of generous living space, character and exceptional outdoor amenities designed for today's lifestyle.
