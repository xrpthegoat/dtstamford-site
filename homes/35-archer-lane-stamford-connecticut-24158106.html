About this home

Move in ready and set on on a quiet cul-de-sac! Step into refined living in this rare 4 bedroom, 3 bath Colonial/Split located in the highly desirable Turn of River neighborhood. The home sits on a beautifully landscaped, park like .55-acre property with ample space to add a pool. The expansive primary suite offers a private sitting room or office, a spa inspired bath, walk-in closet, and its own walk-out access. Sun filled living and dining spaces flow seamlessly into the updated kitchen and out to a spacious, renovated deck, perfect for easy indoor/outdoor living and entertaining. A separate step free entrance leads to a bedroom with an ensuite bath, ideal for in-law, guest, or home office use. The finished lower level provides versatile space for recreation, a gym, or media room. Additional features include an oversized garage, circular driveway, and beautifully maintained grounds. Conveniently located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway and the Stamford train with express service to NYC, and close to shopping, dining, schools, golf, and beaches.
