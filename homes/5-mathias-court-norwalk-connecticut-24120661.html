About this home

Welcome to 5 Mathias Court, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial built in 2010 that blends modern updates with classic charm. With over 1,900 sq. ft. of living space, this home offers a thoughtful layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The first floor features newly refinished hardwood floors, a spacious living room, dining room, and family room, plus a convenient half bath. The kitchen shines with a new oven/stove, vent hood, and refrigerator, making it move-in ready for your culinary adventures. Upstairs, the primary suite impresses with hardwood floors, a walk-in closet, and an en suite bathroom. Three additional bedrooms with new carpet and another full bath complete the second floor. A full, unfinished basement with laundry walks out directly to the driveway, with high ceilings for abundant storage or future finishing potential. Outside, the oversized detached 3-car garage offers tons of storage and bonus unfinished space above-perfect for a home office, studio, or gym-with the possibility of connecting to the main house. Located just minutes from restaurants, shops, grocery stores, and with easy access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, convenience is at your doorstep. This is more than a house-it's a home with space to grow, inside and out.
