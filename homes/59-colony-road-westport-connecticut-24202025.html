About this home

Experience the ease of one-level living in this private, spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch set on approximately an acre in Westport's desirable Long Lots school district. The home offers approximately 2,366 square feet of comfortable living space, including a beautifully redone kitchen and an open floor plan designed for easy everyday living and entertaining. The first-floor primary suite, two fireplaces, central air, and two-car attached garage add to the home's convenience and appeal. The backyard is your own private retreat, complete with a newer pool added in 2020-perfect for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying long summer days. A full basement provides flexible space for a playroom, recreation area, or storage. Come experience everything Westport has to offer year-round without committing to purchasing a home. Private, comfortable, and easy to live in, this is the perfect opportunity to settle into Westport and see why so many people love calling it home.
