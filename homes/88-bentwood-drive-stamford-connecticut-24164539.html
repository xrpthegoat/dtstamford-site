About this home

Discover this distinctive Colonial, gracefully positioned on a quiet cul-de-sac with 2 acres of stunning natural beauty. From the moment you arrive, timeless elegance and thoughtful design welcome you home. Sun-filled grand rooms flow seamlessly throughout. The impressive living room captivates with a fireplace, gleaming hardwood floors, and a wall of windows. The gourmet kitchen is a true showpiece - granite counters, premium stainless appliances, and an oversized island perfect for entertaining. Step through French doors to your private deck and sparkling heated in-ground pool - outdoor living at its absolute finest. The primary suite is a sanctuary of its own - a private wing complete with a fireplace, personal gym, steam shower, jetted tub, and two oversized walk-in closets. Four generous upstairs bedrooms, including an en-suite second primary, accommodate family and guests beautifully. A main-level office and two powder rooms add everyday convenience. The finished lower level offers a mud room playroom, cedar closet, and exceptional storage - ideal for hobbies or a private workspace. A whole-house generator and EV charging hookup deliver the ultimate in modern luxury and peace of mind. Exceptional space. Exquisite details. Extraordinary value.
