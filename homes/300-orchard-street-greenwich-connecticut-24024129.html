About this home

Quintessential charming 4- bedroom, 3 bath retreat in the North Street School district --at the top of Orchard Street-- that overlooks acres of wooded town-owned property, making it the epitome of privacy and serenity. Around the corner from Central Middle & Greenwich High Schools and Pomerance & Pinetum Parks. Completely updated and meticulously maintained by current owners,, includes 1st floor primary suite with cathedral, wainscoted ceiling, walk-in closet, primary bath and adjoining office. French doors in modern gourmet kitchen (with Hanssem cabinets and quartz countertops) open to decks, rock garden terrace and level lawn. First floor bedroom is currently being used as a Family Room. Hardwood floors throughout. A/C system and hot water heater replaced 2015. This is not a drive by!
