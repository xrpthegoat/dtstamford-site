About this home

Commuters delight! Centrally located in the heart of Saugatuck. Rare opportunity for ownership in a great location for under $1MM in Westport! Perfect opportunity for someone who is downsizing, just starting out or wants a weekend getaway. This very charming colonial home rests comfortably on a level .25 parcel in the Saugatuck neighborhood of Westport. Upon entering, you'll be greeted by the open floor plan and gleaming hardwood floors. The welcoming foyer leads to a large open livingroom with cozy fireplace and large bay window. The dining room has sliders to over sized deck expanding your entertaining space. The kitchen has granite counter tops, white cabinetry and stainless steel appliances. Adjacent to the kitchen is a large family room with bay window and sliders to the tiled floor sunroom. Upstairs you will find three generous bedrooms. The primary bedroom has vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet, hardwood floors and full bath with double sink vanity. 2 additional bedrooms have wall to wall carpets and outfitted closets. A renovated full bath in hallway completes the second floor. The finished basement is ideal for a gym or playroom and there is storage galore both throughout the house and in the 2-car garage. Enjoy effortless access to Metro North and I95 - just stroll through your adorable neighborhood to the train platform or commuter lot! What will you do with the parking fees you save?
