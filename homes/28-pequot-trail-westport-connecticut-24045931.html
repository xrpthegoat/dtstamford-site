About this home

Welcome to the home of your dreams at 28 Pequot Trail! One of the most sought-after streets in all of Westport, walkable to town, minutes from the train and a stone's throw to award-winning restaurants, parks and top-rated schools. This exquisite 5-bedroom, 5 full & 1 half bath home is filled with luxurious finishes & thoughtful updates. Step inside the foyer to be greeted by a stunning living room with soaring vaulted ceiling and dramatic stone fireplace. Next you'll find the sun-splashed dining room with elegant archway & custom dry bar featuring Subzero wine chiller and a striking Timothy Oulton Inception mirror. (It's the details at 28 Pequot -and they're everywhere!) Now, get ready for the kitchen of your dreams. Wolf & Subzero appliances, cabinetry and island finely crafted in rich cherry with sleek Silestone quartz. Flex lounging space or spacious eat-in offers panoramic views to the garden with patio access. Off the kitchen is a brand-new super-pantry! A chef's haven with additional prep sink, second Subzero full refrigerator & quick access to the family room. Beyond you have a generous office with custom built-ins and en-suite bath (bonus 6th bedroom!) Playroom & another full bath in lower level. Upstairs, you'll find 5 beautifully appointed bedrooms, including a show-stopping primary bedroom, updated primary bath with spa shower, and 2 more full baths. Come see this home, it's the sweetest one. Make it yours and start creating memories you'll cherish for a lifetime.
