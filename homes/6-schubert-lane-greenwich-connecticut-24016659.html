About this home

This home features four bedrooms, four full baths, and a powder room. Solid oak wood floors throughout the custom kitchen with a modern flair, including a family room with a fireplace. Other features include the large living room and dining room with sliders out to the front porch. The full-finished lower level has a full bath and French doors that lead out to a large fenced-in backyard. This home is right in the heart of Cos Cob, with everything in close proximity to the house. The lower level offers the ability to have a nanny Suite or extra bedroom.
