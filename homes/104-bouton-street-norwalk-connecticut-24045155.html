About this home

Nestled on a picturesque, private lot, this stunning Colonial-style home has a harmonious blend of classic elegance and modern comfort. The expansive living room, adorned with large picture windows, invites abundant natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The gourmet chef's kitchen, fully equipped and featuring exquisite granite countertops and generous cabinetry, seamlessly connects to a charming dining room bathed in sunlight from numerous windows. Step outside to a spacious deck, perfect for alfresco dining, where you can enjoy serene views of the large, private backyard. The main level is home to a luxurious master bedroom with an en-suite bath, accompanied by 1 possibly 2 additl bedrooms & well-appointed hallway bath. A convenient utility room is also located on this level. Upstairs, the second floor boasts three more bedrooms, all sharing a full hallway bath and a laundry closet. At the top of the stairs, a versatile bonus room awaits, ideal for family gatherings or a home office. Throughout the home, an abundance of windows ensures every corner is filled with natural light. Ideally situated in the historic South Norwalk district, known as SoNo, this residence offers unparalleled access to world-class dining, entertainment, museums, shopping, and beaches. In proximity to major highways, incl I-95 & Merritt Parkway, and easy access to the Norwalk train station, this home combines convenience w/charm of a vibrant, historic neighborhood. Sold As Is.
