About this home

** Highest and Best Tuesday at 5pm ** Discover the perfect blend of space, style, and convenience in this stunning Colonial, built in 2001, offering over 3,100 square feet of living space. Nestled on a generous .52-acre lot, this home boasts 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and 2 half baths, along with thoughtfully designed spaces for every lifestyle. The first floor features soaring vaulted ceilings in the living room, a large formal dining room, a kitchen with granite countertops, a large island, and gleaming hardwood floors that flow throughout the main level. There is also an office, playroom/den, and half bath for guests (and a laundry room). The second level is highlighted by a spacious primary suite, complete with a spa-like bathroom and plenty of space to create your private retreat. Three additional bedrooms and two more full baths ensure comfort and privacy for all. The finished basement is an entertainer's dream, complete with a movie room, play room, and an additional half bath. Host movie nights or poker nights--it's up to you! Outside, the expansive patio and large, level yard offer the perfect setting for outdoor gatherings or peaceful relaxation. Conveniently located near Stew Leonard's, Westport Avenue's array of dining and shopping options, and the natural beauty of Cranbury Park, this home provides a rare combination of size, amenities, and prime location. Move right in and enjoy all that this exceptional property has to offer!
