About this home

Welcome to 1165 Riverbank Road, a charming 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence nestled in the desirable North Stamford neighborhood. Offering a wonderful combination of comfort, functionality, and convenience, this home presents an excellent opportunity to enjoy all that North Stamford living has to offer. Inside, a thoughtfully designed layout provides comfortable living and entertaining spaces, while a beautifully updated primary bathroom, renovated within the past six years, adds a touch of modern sophistication. The partially finished lower level features generous ceiling heights and versatile additional space, ideal for a recreation room, fitness area, or a variety of other uses. Additional improvements include an updated septic system completed in 2019. Conveniently situated near the Merritt Parkway, LaRocca's Country Market, the beloved Lakeside Diner, houses of worship, and a variety of local restaurants and shops, this location offers the perfect balance of neighborhood charm and everyday accessibility.
