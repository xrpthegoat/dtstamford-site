About this home

Beautifully Renovated 2BR/1BA with Private Entrance in Greenwich's Desirable Glenville Neighborhood! This bright and spacious residence offers approximately 820 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, with the flexibility to use the second bedroom as a home office or guest room. Enjoy the convenience and privacy of your own separate entrance. Freshly painted throughout, the apartment features attractive wood-look flooring, recessed lighting, and abundant natural light. The generously sized living and dining area provides plenty of room for entertaining, while the updated kitchen showcases white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, a French-door refrigerator, and ample counter space. The beautifully renovated bathroom features marble-look tile, a glass-enclosed shower, and modern vanity. The exceptionally spacious primary bedroom offers two closets and plenty of room for additional furniture or a workspace. Ideally situated in the charming Glenville neighborhood, just minutes from the newly opened DeCicco & Sons gourmet supermarket at Glen Plaza, offering an exceptional selection of fresh produce, specialty foods, and everyday essentials. Enjoy convenient access to local restaurants, cafes, parks, and Greenwich Avenue's vibrant shopping and dining scene. Just minutes from the New York state line, with easy access to Westchester County, Metro-North train stations in Greenwich and Port Chester for commuting to NYC, and major highways including I-95 and the Merritt.
