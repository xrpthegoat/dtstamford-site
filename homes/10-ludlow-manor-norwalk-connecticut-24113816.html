About this home

Welcome to 10 Ludlow Manor, a tastefully renovated gem nestled in the heart of East Norwalk's sought-after beachside community. Beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home blends timeless charm with modern comforts, offering exceptional convenience and style in one of Fairfield County's most desirable locations. Step inside to discover sun-filled living spaces adorned with gleaming hardwood floors throughout. The thoughtfully designed layout includes a spacious living room, a dining area perfect for entertaining, and a bright, updated kitchen that seamlessly connects the living spaces. Central air ensures year-round comfort, while quality finishes and attention to detail elevate every room. Upstairs, the well-appointed primary bedroom suite is your private retreat, featuring a large walk-in closet and a stylish en-suite bath. Two additional bedrooms provide flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. The partially finished basement adds extra space ideal for a playroom, media room, or fitness area. Outside, the lush, professionally landscaped yard creates a serene backdrop for outdoor living, and the two-car detached garage offers ample storage and convenience. Whether you're relaxing in the garden or hosting gatherings, this home offers the perfect setting. Ideally situated from the SoNo Collection Mall, downtown South Norwalk's trendy restaurants and nightlife, Metro-North, I-95, Merritt Parkway, and Route 1-making your commute or weekend getaway effortless.
