About this home

Private country living meets modern comfort in this beautifully maintained ranch home, perfectly situated on 2 rolling acres of serene, secluded land. Step inside to find an inviting, sun-drenched layout highlighted by gorgeous hardwood floors spanning the main living spaces. The heart of the home flows seamlessly, offering a perfect backdrop for both quiet evenings and lively gatherings. Enjoy year-round comfort and total peace of mind thanks to central air conditioning and recently updated mechanical systems that ensure worry-free living for years to come. The living space doubles with a massive, finished walk-out basement. This versatile lower level features a second full bathroom, a bedroom, and ample flexible square footage, making it ideal for an in-law suite, recreation room, home gym, or private guest quarters. Outside, your personal oasis awaits. The expansive 2-acre lot provides exceptional privacy, mature trees, and endless possibilities for outdoor entertaining, gardening, or simply enjoying the quiet surroundings. Sold in "as is" condition.
