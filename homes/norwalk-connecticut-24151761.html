About this home

Classic four-bedroom Colonial set on a private 1.03-acre lot in Norwalk's highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Located at 14 Half Mile Road, this home offers a traditional layout with generous room sizes, hardwood floors, and abundant natural light throughout. The primary suite features a full bath and walk-in closet, while three additional bedrooms provide ample space, extra closets, and flexibility for guests, a home office, or other needs. Enjoy outdoor living with a large private deck, storage shed, and beautifully maintained grounds with professional landscaping and an irrigation system. Conveniently located just minutes to I-95, Route 7, and the Merritt Parkway. A wonderful opportunity to own in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
