About this home

Sprawling raised ranch on a prime 1 acre lot in this highly sought-after Newfield neighborhood cul de sac. The main level delivers functional everyday living with large, bright open spaces and easy flow leading out to the deck for comfortable indoor/outdoor enjoyment. A totally private rear yard tiered with lantern lit stone walls and highlighted with a Belguin block framed patio that's ready to entertain a crowd. Finished lower level offers a space-perfect for home office, in-law, au pair or extended stay guests, featuring a second fireplace, oversized living spaces, bedroom and newer full bath. Location, location, location!
