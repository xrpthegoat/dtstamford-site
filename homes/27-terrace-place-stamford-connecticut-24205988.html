About this home

Rare downtown Stamford rental! Built in 2021, this detached end-unit townhouse offers the privacy and feel of a single-family home with all the convenience of downtown living. Featuring 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths and a bright open floor plan with high-end finishes throughout. The gourmet kitchen with pantry, high-end appliances, and an L-shaped countertop, opens to the living and dining area. The primary suite offers a spa-like bath with double vanity and two custom walk-in closets. A versatile lower-level bonus room provides additional space, home office/gym etc. along w/storage with direct access to an oversized 2-car garage with epoxy flooring and Level 2 EV charger installed. Additional outdoor visitor parking. Private patio for outdoor entertaining. Just 5 mins walk from downtown Stamford's restaurants, shops, nightlife, and approximately 15 minutes to the Stamford train station on foot. Newer construction, privacy, outdoor space in the heart of downtown!"
