About this home

Welcome to 102 Rose Lane, a perfect oasis in a wonderful neighborhood setting. This classic colonial is full of charm and has been beautifully updated for you to move right in. A covered porch and an adjacent slate patio are great for eating outdoors, a cozy living room with fireplace and built-in bookshelves leads to a bright family room with vaulted ceilings, bay window and more built-ins, a formal dining room is perfect for gatherings, a large breakfast room, adjacent to the kitchen is sun-filled and has vaulted ceilings and a bay window, plus there is a beautiful level, fenced yard. Updates include newer windows, newer roof, new hardwood in the family room and breakfast room, newer oil burner, new patio doors and fresh paint. Close to schools, transportation and all that Darien has to offer!
