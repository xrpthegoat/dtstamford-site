About this home

Welcome to the heart of Noroton Heights nestled on one of the most sought-after streets in Darien. This incredible neighborhood offers the perfect blend of community, convenience, & everyday amenities just moments from your door! Renovated in 2008 with a significant addition, this home combines timeless charm, curb appeal & plenty of room for any lifestyle. Inside, you'll find 5 beds/ 3.5 baths, tons of natural light, hardwood floors, & recessed lighting throughout. The first floor features a formal living room, dining room, guest bedroom & bath, mudroom & office. The open-concept family room flows seamlessly to the backyard w/ sliding glass doors leading to a stone patio, perfect for indoor/outdoor entertaining. At the center of the home is a chef's kitchen, complete w/ a massive island, high-end stainless-steel appliances, granite counters, custom cabinets & a tile backsplash. Upstairs are 4 add'l beds, including a spacious primary ensuite w/ vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet & luxurious bath. The property sits on a large, flat, fenced-in lot w/ eastern exposure & beautiful sunsets-ideal for gatherings, outdoor play, or simply relaxing at home. All of this is less than a 10 min walk to the Noroton Train Station (55 mins to GC), Heights Crossing, Darien Commons, offering numerous restaurants, coffee shops, boutique shopping, Equinox, SolidCore, grocery stores & more! Conveniently located near Darien's schools, daycare & beaches-this is Darien living at its finest!
