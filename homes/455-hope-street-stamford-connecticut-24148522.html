About this home

This uniquely designed multi-level triplex offers expansive interiors, plentiful natural light, and carefully planned spaces suited for both entertaining and easy everyday living. A dramatic cathedral-style ceiling is the center of the main living area, which flows seamlessly into the dining space and is illuminated by bright southern exposure. Sliding glass doors open to a private balcony - ideal for morning coffee or evening unwinding - overlooking lush, park-inspired grounds that offer peace and tranquility while still close to the train, shopping, and key commuter routes. The updated open-layout kitchen pairs modern style with functionality with thoughtful appliances connecting effortlessly to a large laundry and utility room. Two generously sized bedrooms are set on separate levels for enhanced privacy. The primary suite spans its own floor and features a walk-in closet, private full bath, and airy loft perfect for a home office, creative space, or quiet reading corner. The large second bedroom enjoys its own level with easy access to a full bath. A flexible lower-level family or media room adds valuable living space while maintaining a neat, streamlined look. Notable extras include two assigned parking spaces, new carpeting, ample guest parking, and a beautifully landscaped central courtyard - all delivering comfort, style, and low-maintenance. Designated parking spots 43 & 44 come with the unit.
