About this home

OFFER DEADLINE - Wednesday, September 3 @ Noon. Looking for a turn-key multi-family just steps from Downtown Stamford? Welcome to 19 Waverly Place! This well-maintained 2-family home features two spacious, up/down style units, each offering 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, updated kitchens, and comfortable living spaces. The basement provides plenty of storage along with laundry for the building. The second-floor unit boasts a spacious back deck with access to a potential third unit-complete with 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, and a small kitchen-offering additional income opportunities. Parking will never be an issue here-this property offers a detached 2-car garage plus a 2-car carport, giving you 4 covered spaces, a rare find in Stamford. A fully fenced yard adds privacy and is perfect for pets or outdoor enjoyment. Lovingly cared for and owner-occupied for over 30 years, this home is hitting the market for the first time in decades. Perfectly located near everything Stamford has to offer, you'll be within walking distance of the Stamford train station, minutes from I-95, and a short drive to beaches, shops, supermarkets and dining. With its strong income potential, incredible parking, and unbeatable location, 19 Waverly Place is a rare gem that won't last long. See it before it is gone!
