About this home

Exceptional summer rental in the heart of Westport, available July 20 to August 20. This beautifully designed 6-bedroom, 4 full bath, 2 half bath home offers a rare blend of elevated style and effortless family living. Set on a fully fenced, professionally landscaped property, the backyard is a private oasis featuring an in-ground saltwater pool with spa, hot tub, and designer outdoor furnishings. It is perfect for both relaxed days and elegant entertaining. Inside, the home is thoughtfully curated with sophisticated yet comfortable interiors, designed to feel both refined and welcoming. Spacious living areas, a well-appointed kitchen, and multiple gathering spaces provide flexibility for families, guests, and summer hosting alike. Ideally located just a short walk to downtown Westport's shops, restaurants, and amenities, this home offers the best of convenience and tranquility. A truly turnkey summer retreat that balances luxury, warmth, and livability.
