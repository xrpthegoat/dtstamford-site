About this home

An extraordinary waterfront residence in Old Greenwich, offers a rare opportunity for sophisticated coastal living with spectacular, unobstructed water views from virtually every room. Meticulously redesigned by renowned architect Rick Swann, this exceptional home is defined by dramatic architectural details, soaring ceilings, and an open, light-filled floor plan that seamlessly blends indoor elegance with the serenity of Long Island Sound vistas. First-floor rooms are anchored by expansive walls of glass and French doors that open to a wrap-around deck, the ideal setting for refined entertaining against the backdrop of ever-changing waterfront panoramas. The gourmet kitchen showcases marble countertops, a generous island, and state-of-the-art appliances, reflecting both style and performance. Thoughtfully designed living spaces offer flexibility and grace, with a first-floor den that can serve as a fifth bedroom, private guest suite, or executive office. Each room is elevated by exceptional light and continuous water views, creating an immersive connection to the coastal environment. Set on a premier waterfront parcel with private dock access to Long Island Sound, this distinguished property embodies the ultimate in seaside refinement; a sanctuary of light, luxury, and lifestyle in one of Connecticut's most coveted coastal enclaves.
