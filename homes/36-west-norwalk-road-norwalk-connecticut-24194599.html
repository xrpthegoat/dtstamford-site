About this home

Opportunity knocks! If you've been searching for a home with solid bones and room to build equity, this 3 bedroom ranch deserves a look. While the interior could benefit from cosmetic updates, many of the big-ticket mechanical systems are already in good shape, allowing you to focus on making the home your own rather than tackling major infrastructure. The easy, one-level floor plan offers approximately 1,158 square feet of living space, plus a full basement that provides excellent storage or future finishing potential. Situated on a manageable lot, the convenient Norwalk location puts shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, and major commuting routes just minutes away. Bring your vision and unlock the potential this home has to offer. With some sweat equity, you'll have a wonderful place to call home for years to come.
