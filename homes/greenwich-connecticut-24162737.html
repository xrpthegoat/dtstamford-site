About this home

Ideally positioned in the heart of Old Greenwich, this charming residence offers an exceptional blend of timeless appeal, convenience, and coastal lifestyle. Set on .34 acres of beautifully level property, the home features 4 bedrooms and 2.1 baths, with all bedrooms thoughtfully situated on the second level, as well as a versatile bonus space on the third floor. A gracious front porch welcomes you into sun-drenched interiors designed for both comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. The expansive, level backyard provides a rare and inviting outdoor setting perfect for gatherings. Complete with a 2 car detached garage and located just moments from town, train, and Tod's Point beach, this home presents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy the very best of Old Greenwich living.
