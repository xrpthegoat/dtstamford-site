About this home

This approximately 1,991 square foot Cape Cod is located in Wolfpit, one of Norwalk's historical neighborhoods. This home features three bedrooms and one bathroom. The interior showcases vaulted ceilings, creating a sense of openness and abundant natural light throughout. The hardwood floors add warmth and elegance, complemented by the exposed beams that accentuate the property's charming architectural details. The spacious layout offers ample room for comfortable living. The kitchen is generously sized, providing abundant cabinetry and storage space with beautiful bay window. The primary bedroom is well-proportioned, offering a peaceful retreat located on the second floor. Two additional bedrooms provide flexible living options located on the main floor with 1 full bath. This home's ideal layout, thoughtful design, and attention to detail make it a welcoming and comfortable living space. The exterior of the home is characterized by a storybook cape cod design, enhanced by lush landscaping and a brick driveway. The spacious yard offers ample room for outdoor enjoyment and relaxation. Prime Location close to Downtown Norwalk. Short drive to Westport I95 or Merritt Parkway close to Stew Leonard's
