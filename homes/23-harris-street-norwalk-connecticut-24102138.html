About this home

***HIGHEST AND BEST requested by 2pm 6/24.*** Welcome home to this storybook 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath Cape Cod that perfectly blends charm, functionality, and modern living. Thoughtfully designed for today's lifestyle, this home offers the space, layout, and features savvy buyers are seeking. Gleaming hardwood floors run throughout, from the spacious living room to the dining area and all three upstairs bedrooms. Cozy up by the fireplace or sip your morning coffee on the charming covered patio. The open-concept kitchen is a true highlight, featuring Corian countertops, a generous island, and a large pantry-ideal for any home chef. Step outside to your own private oasis: a level backyard with a fire pit and a second large covered patio, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The finished basement includes a CT Basement waterproofing system and an industrial-grade dehumidifier, keeping it dry and comfortable year-round. A detached garage with a newer door and opener adds convenience, just steps from the back door. Located on a quiet street with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, SoNo, shopping, and Metro North, this pristine home offers comfort, style, and unbeatable convenience. All that's left to do is move in and make it yours.
