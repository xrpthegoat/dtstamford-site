About this home

This beautifully updated and bright 3-bedroom Cape Cod home offers modern comfort and convenience in sought-after Springdale. Situated on a lovely corner lot, it's just a short walk to the train and village amenities. The first floor boasts an open layout, featuring a formal living room, dining room, and a stylish kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a breakfast bar. Upstairs, you'll find a primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms, and an updated full bath. The finished basement provides versatile living space, including a guest bedroom/office, full bath, laundry, and a spacious area perfect for a gym or recreation room. Step outside to a private patio, ideal for entertaining. A detached garage has been transformed into a studio space with electricity and a wood-burning stove, perfect for an office, art studio, or extra storage.
