About this home

This beautifully updated Colonial blends character with modern home convenience. Set on a rare, level 0.52-acre lot in one of Norwalk's most sought-after neighborhoods, this 3-4 bedroom, move-in-ready home offers roughly 2,710 total square feet of versatile space designed for both seamless everyday living and effortless entertaining. The heart of the home centers around a gourmet open-concept kitchen, with custom white cabinetry, quartzite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a large breakfast bar that opens directly into the dining area/family room. The spacious family room showcases refined craftsmanship with custom trim-work, crown molding, wainscoting, and a built-in dry bar. A flexible bedroom/den/office is located on the 1st floor. The upper level features three generous bedrooms and a dedicated laundry room for everyday ease. The primary suite serves as a relaxing retreat, complete with a walk-in closet and a spa-like en-suite bath featuring double sinks and heated tile floors. Downstairs, a finished walk-out lower level offers flexible space perfect for a recreation room, home gym, or media space. French doors from the family room open onto a private rear deck overlooking a fully fenced backyard serviced by an in-ground sprinkler system. Additional exterior highlights include a detached workshop shed and 1-car garage. Situated in a prime Norwalk location, this home is minutes from I-95, the Merritt Parkway, trains, local parks, and top-tier dining.
