About this home

Nestled in the prestigious and historic Hubbard Heights neighborhood, this classic New England Colonial seamlessly blends timeless elegance with modern smart home upgrades. The state of the art chef's kitchen is flooded with natural light and features a spacious center island, top of the line appliances, a coffee bar, vaulted ceilings, and sliders leading to an oversized deck overlooking the expansive backyard that backs up to Brennan golf course! Open concept layout flows into the dining room leading to the formal living room, complete with a wood-burning fireplace & built-ins. Cozy den with vaulted ceilings, accessed via dual French doors, also offers backyard access. A private office, powder room, and a welcoming foyer/mudroom complete the main level. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite boasts a walk-in closet and an en-suite bath with a steam shower, dual vanity, and water closet. Two additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the second floor. The third level offers exceptional flexibility with two oversized rooms-perfect for a family room, game room, or a potential fourth bedroom with a walk-in closet and en-suite bath. Additional highlights include a one-car attached garage, a full basement with ample storage, and professionally landscaped grounds with mature evergreen hedges for total privacy. Ideally situated just minutes from downtown Stamford, this home offers convenient access to the train station, shopping, dining, Scalzi Park, Whole Foods, and local beaches.
