About this home

Classic 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial offers the perfect combination of comfort, functionality, and convenience. Featuring an open floor plan with 9+ ft ceilings, hardwood floors, central air, city water, and sewer, this home is designed for today's lifestyle. The light-filled family room with gas fireplace opens to the gourmet kitchen featuring granite countertops, center island, stainless steel appliances including a gas range, and casual dining area with access to the private patio-ideal for entertaining. A formal living room, dining room, half bath, and remodeled laundry/mudroom complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a walk-in closet and spacious bath with soaking tub, oversized shower, and double vanity. Three additional bedrooms and a full hall bath complete the second floor. The finished lower level provides flexible space for a playroom, office, gym, or media room. Notable improvements include new hardwood floors throughout, crown moldings, interior doors and hardware, upgraded lighting, kitchen backsplash, remodeled laundry room, custom closet systems, and finished basement. Major mechanical updates include new furnace and central air system (2023), new hot water heater (2024). Exterior improvements include house painting in 2024 and roof less than 6 years old. Located in well-maintained association w/ ground maintenance and snow plowing included, this home offers easy living just minutes from Wilton and Westport town centers, trains and hwys.
