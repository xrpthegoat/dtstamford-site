About this home

This modern farmhouse was praised for its remodel, receiving the HOBI Award for Outstanding Spec Home in 2017. Set on a secluded + conveniently located Old Hill cul-de-sac, the home has tremendous curb appeal w/an expansive front porch overlooking the wide front lawn. The interior finishes are remarkable, reclaimed + repurposed, the energy-efficient home uses materials such as antique woods, metal, glass + marble to create unique + inviting spaces. Traditional in a sense, w/living + dining rooms flanking the entry, this home is anything but ordinary. The living room can also be a cozy den, as it is now, or function as a 1st floor bedroom with adjacent bathroom. On the opposite side, a barn-style door leads to the dining room, whose focal point is a stunning, custom made chandelier. Flowing easily onto the front porch or into the kitchen, dining guests won't linger at the table too long. Drawn to the center island w/an extended dining table, you will discover the eat-in kitchen is for gathering. Appointed with top-of-the-line appliances + plentiful storage options, the kitchen will delight every home chef. Step into the family room or onto the expansive bluestone patio to further experience this home's welcoming vibe. Upstairs, havens await. From the serene primary bedroom to a second ensuite + two bedrooms w/bath, each person has their retreat. The 3rd floor, lower level + mudroom provide options to meet your needs. Located minutes from all Westport amenities + attractions.
