About this home

Discover the perfect blend of style and convenience in this stunning townhouse-style residence. Boasting over 1,500 square feet of bright, airy living space across multiple levels, this 3 bedroom, 2 full bathroom home offers the privacy and scale of a single-family dwelling. The main level features an open-concept layout designed for seamless entertaining, flowing onto a private balcony. This home is outfitted with brand-new appliances, washer/dyer, and a fresh, neutral paint palette throughout. The second-floor primary suite is a true showstopper, featuring dramatic vaulted ceilings and a private en-suite bathroom to create a serene personal oasis. Remote professionals will particularly appreciate the dedicated office area, which provides a quiet, sun-drenched space for maximum productivity. The lifestyle extends outdoors to a rare retreat, featuring an expansive patio and a lush, professionally manicured grass area, all maintained hassle-free by management. Perfect for summer grilling and outdoor gatherings. Comfort is further enhanced by large off-street parking accommodations. Situated in a prime location, this home is just a short stroll from the scenic trails and beaches of Cove Island Park. A true commuter's dream, you are only minutes from I-95 and the Stamford Metro-North station for a rapid trip to New York City. Whether you're working from home or walking to the shore, this sanctuary retreat offers an unparalleled Fairfield County lifestyle. Schedule your showing!
