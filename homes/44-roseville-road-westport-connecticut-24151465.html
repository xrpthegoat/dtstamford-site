About this home

Charming 1-bedroom cottage with den available for yearly rental, unfurnished. Privately set back from the main house on a nicely landscaped property with a small pond and outdoor patio. The cottage offers a practical, efficient layout with a spacious entryway, dining area, and galley kitchen. The living room features a working wood-burning fireplace, and the den provides flexible space suitable for a home office or guest use. A stacked washer and dryer are located in the den. The bedroom is located upstairs, accessed by a short flight of stairs, and connects directly to the full bathroom with walk-in shower. Built in 1920 and approximately 796 square feet, this home has the character of an older cottage with a simple, functional layout. Conveniently located close to Westport's shops, restaurants, beaches, train lines, and major roadways, including I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Oil heat and window air conditioning. Tenant responsible for utilities. References, credit check, and two-month security deposit required.
