About this home

One of kind, this 4,067 sq. ft. masterpiece is the epitome of luxury living, fully renovated with the finest materials. The stunning cement board siding, backed by a lifetime warranty, sets the tone for the quality you'll find throughout. Step inside to a breathtaking living room with an electric fireplace, seamlessly flowing into a sophisticated dining area with sliders opening to a premium Trex deck. The expansive layout extends into a bright TV/family room and a gourmet kitchen, equipped with Thermador appliances, Quartzite Taj Mahal countertops, and a large island perfect for casual dining. The powder room is a statement of elegance with its marble sink and wall. A thoughtfully designed mudroom, seamlessly connected to the garage with epoxy floors, provides the perfect space for organizing coats and gear. Ascend to the second floor, where the opulent master suite awaits with tray ceilings, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-like bath featuring porcelain tiles, a freestanding soaking tub, and quartz-topped double sinks. Two additional en-suite bedrooms boast heated porcelain-tiled showers and luxurious finishes. The third floor provides a versatile great room, abundant storage, and a fourth bedroom with a full bath, perfect for guests, or an au pair. The walk-out lower level is an entertainer's dream, complete with a stylish rec room, vinyl flooring, and the security of a whole-house generator.
