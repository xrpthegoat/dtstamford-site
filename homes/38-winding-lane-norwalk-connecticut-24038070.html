About this home

Picturesque 3 bedroom, 2 full bathroom split-level home nestled on well over half an acre of lovely manicured property in the coveted Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk. This darling and well maintained home offers the ultimate trifecta: Modern updates with wonderful living space, a fabulous neighborhood on a desirable cul-de-sac, and the convenience of city services! Boasting over 1,700 square feet of stunning living space, an incredible lifestyle awaits you here. A sun drenched foyer with soaring ceilings welcomes you into a fabulous open layout with expansive family room and dining room just perfect for entertaining. Large glass sliders from the living space seamlessly transition indoors to the outdoor deck for perfect for hosting family BBQs or simply enjoy the level backyard and charming babbling brook! The functional eat-in kitchen features new cabinetry, countertops, stainless appliances, & a fabulous custom breakfast nook optimally designed for storage and casual meals. The upper level features a relaxing primary suite with renovated full bathroom, 2 lovely additional guest rooms, and a renovated full bathroom. With so many flexible options for use of space, the bonus room in the lower level offers possibilities: office, rec room, or even an additional guest room!
