About this home

Come see this charming, move-in-ready home in pristine condition! Featuring 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, this inviting residence offers a completely fenced, private backyard perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or gardening. Ideally located just steps from the train station and the vibrant shops and restaurants of Noroton Heights, convenience is at your doorstep. Inside, you'll find eight well-appointed rooms, including a living room, dining room, family room, and playroom, providing flexible space for today's lifestyle. The beautifully remodeled kitchen and updated bathrooms blend modern style with everyday functionality. Additional improvements include a brand-new asphalt driveway accented with classic Belgium block edging. Filled with natural light thanks to its desirable southern exposure, this home offers warmth, comfort, and exceptional curb appeal. A wonderful opportunity in a sought-after location-don't miss it!
