About this home

Welcome to this tastefully updated & impeccably maintained 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath split-level home, located on a peaceful dead-end road. This charming residence offers a perfect blend of modern updates and timeless character, featuring gleaming hardwood floors throughout and a spacious 2-car attached garage. Step inside and be captivated by the thoughtful details and upgrades, including fully renovated bathrooms with heated flooring in the primary bath for a touch of luxury. The unique Scandinavian wallpaper designed by Gocken Jobs adds a special flair to the space, creating an inviting atmosphere. The heart of the home is the updated kitchen, showcasing brand new backsplash, a water filtration system, Miele & Bosch appliances, & granite countertops that perfectly complement the contemporary design. Additionally, the newly renovated laundry room provides added convenience and style. Designed for comfort and efficiency, this home features a brand-new high-efficiency heat pump system with a hospital-grade air cleaner, a new double bottom Ecco Gard oil tank, and French drain system provide added peace of mind. Don't miss the opportunity to own this exceptional home, where every detail has been thoughtfully crafted for a stylish, comfortable, and low-maintenance lifestyle.
