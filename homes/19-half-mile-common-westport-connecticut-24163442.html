About this home

Beautifully renovated split level situated on a centrally located cul-de-sac. This sun-filled residence offers an open floor plan with a bright living room featuring a cozy fireplace, an updated kitchen, and an adjoining dining area. The property includes 4 bedrooms and 3 1/2 renovated baths, highlighted by an oversized primary suite with a spa-like bathroom. The finished lower level provides flexible space for a family room or home office. A spacious sunroom with a large hot tub offers year round enjoyment. The flat one acre lot features a patio, greenhouse, and fire pit. Improvements include: extensive landscaping, new stone walkway, newer roof, solar panels, newer skylights, garage doors, moldings & more. Move-in ready with modern updates throughout.
