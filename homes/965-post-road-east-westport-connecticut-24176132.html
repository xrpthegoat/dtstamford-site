About this home

Position your business in the heart of Westport in this beautifully renovated three-story professional building offering luxury office space with exceptional flexibility. Recently updated with modern finishes throughout, the property features bright interiors, hardwood floors, and a refined contemporary aesthetic designed to impress both clients and employees alike. The first floor offers four private offices totaling approximately 1,604 square feet, while the second floor features four additional offices totaling approximately 1,386 square feet. Individual office suites range from approximately 118 to 483 square feet, with flexible leasing options available - lease a single office, multiple offices, or an entire floor to suit your business needs. Each floor includes thoughtfully designed shared amenities including an inviting common area, kitchenette, and private restrooms, creating a polished and functional work environment. A dedicated rear parking lot provides convenient on-site parking for tenants and visitors. Utilities are billed separately as a proportionate share in addition to base rent. Ideal for professional, wellness, consulting, legal, creative, or executive office users seeking a turnkey luxury workspace in a prime Westport location
