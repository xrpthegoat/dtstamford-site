About this home

This home paints a charming picture of a 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath split-level in a desirable Broad River location. The highlights include an open living and dining area with access to a patio and level yard, a well-equipped kitchen, a bedroom level with a primary bedroom featuring an attached half bath, 2 additional bedrooms, full hall bath, and a newly carpeted lower-level family room with a wood-burning stove. The property also includes an attached 1-car garage and is conveniently located near shopping, commuting options, schools and so much more! Perfect for a those looking for a cozy, well-located home with outdoor space.
