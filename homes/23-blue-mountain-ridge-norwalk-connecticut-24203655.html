About this home

Serenely situated on a quiet cul-de-sac, this detached condo offers an easy sense of privacy and calm. A welcoming foyer leads into a sunlit living room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. Outside, a wraparound porch invites you to enjoy the surrounding greenery. Prepare meals in the kitchen and gather in the dining room-or take dinner outdoors to the private backyard and patio. The second floor features three bedrooms, including a primary suite with vaulted ceiling and ensuite bathroom, a second full bath, and laundry for everyday convenience. Below, the finished basement provides room for a home gym or office, as well as a half bath and storage.
