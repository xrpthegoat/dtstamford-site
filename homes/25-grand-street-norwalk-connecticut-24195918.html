About this home

This impeccable and spacious (669 sq. ft.) condo is located at Clocktower Close, an architecturally grand, historic landmark building - formerly used as hat factory. Totally renovated, brand new kitchen with stylish white cabinets, sparkling subway wall tiles, upscale quartz countertops and stainless appliances. This freshly painted studio offers a large open LR/ Dining/Bedroom area with ceiling fan and windows overlooking the greenery. Generous size bathroom with designer tile accents, large shower and stunning high quality shower door ($1700!!). New efficient hot water heater, too. Laundry facilities located in the hallway. Two permitted parking spaces are included and underground garage parking may be available at extra cost. Within the last year, the owner invested approximately $25,000 for improvements. Amenities include: well-equipped renovated fitness center with saunas, billiard table with lounge area, conference room with wi-fi and fully equipped kitchen for hosting large gatherings. Tranquil gardens with old world character and a special brick garden room are perfect areas for reading, relaxation. Pet friendly building. This unique complex is only minutes away from endless shopping, an array of delicious restaurants, entertainment and medical facilities. Easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, Metro North and public transportation. Every convenience is at your fingertips. A cosmopolitan lifestyle awaits!
