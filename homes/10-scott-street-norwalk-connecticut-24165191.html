About this home

Welcome to 10 Scott Street, a beautifully updated home in Cranbury, that blends classic charm with modern comfort and efficiency. From the moment you arrive, the inviting front porch, complete with a charming swing, offers the perfect place to relax and unwind. Inside, you find stunning flooring throughout, a bright open-concept layout on the first floor that is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The renovated kitchen is a masterpiece, featuring a spacious island with a built-in cooktop, stainless steel appliances, a double oven, and elegant waterfall countertops. The seamless flow between the kitchen, dining, and living areas creates a warm and functional space perfect for hosting guests or enjoying time at home. This home offers four bedrooms and two full bathrooms, with a flexible layout that includes two bedrooms on the first floor and two additional bedrooms on the second floor, ideal for a variety of living arrangements including guests, home office space, or extended family. Step outside to a generous backyard designed for entertaining and outdoor enjoyment, offering plenty of space to host gatherings or simply relax in your own private setting. Additional features include a newer boiler, an efficient solar system that helps reduce energy costs, a whole-house backup generator for added peace of mind, durable vinyl siding, and quality Pella windows. 10 Scott Street is a move-in ready home that offers comfort, style, and thoughtful upgrades throughout.
