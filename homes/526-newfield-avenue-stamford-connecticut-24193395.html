About this home

Rare opportunity to acquire a fully leased, 5,427sqft income-producing medical office building situated in one of Stamford's most established commercial corridors. Held under continuous ownership since 1989, the property provides investors immediate cash flow, long-term lease stability, scheduled rental increases & minimal management responsibilities through its favorable lease structure. Prime location, strong tenant roster & well-maintained footprint, this property appeals equally to passive investors seeking reliable returns & medical owner-users looking for long-term value.
