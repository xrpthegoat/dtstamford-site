About this home

Best and final offers are requested by Tuesday 5/5 at 5pm. Tucked away at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac in one of Lower North Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, this beautifully updated contemporary ranch offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and privacy. Set on a serene, level acre, the property provides a tranquil retreat while still being conveniently located. This spacious home features 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, including a thoughtfully updated spa-inspired primary bath. The current owners have expanded the home to include a stunning family room with an open floor plan, built-in audio and sliders to a spacious deck-ideal for modern living and effortless entertaining. The updated kitchen flows seamlessly into the living spaces, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere for gatherings both large and small. The large island, refrigerated drawers, great appliances and large pantry make cooking a dream. The living and dining room share a dual-sided fireplace and a spiral staircase to a private loft space with skylights and beautiful vaulted beamed ceiling. Numerous recent improvements enhance both aesthetics and peace of mind, including updated carpeting, custom shades, a newer septic system, roof, and driveway. Storage is abundant throughout the home, with generous closets in every room, plus a large dedicated storage room and workshop-perfect for hobbies or organization.
