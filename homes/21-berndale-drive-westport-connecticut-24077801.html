About this home

Bring your creativity and design ideas to this wonderful 5 bed 3 bath colonial and make it your own. One owner has lovingly maintained this property for 62 years. The layout is traditional with a formal living and dining room, an eat in kitchen, and a large family room. Convenient 1st floor bedroom is adjacent to a full bath. Laundry room located on first floor as well. Upstairs are 4 bedrooms, 3 that share a large double vanity bathroom with tub, and a primary suite with its own bathroom. Major mechanicals have been replaced in 2022 (furnace & hot water heater). The roof was replaced in 2018. They added a full house generator and propane tank in 2015. Hardwood floors throughout the upper level and some parts of the main. A screened in porch overlooks over a lovely private yard. Wonderful convenient location in central westport neighborhood. Room for pool. This is an estate sale with no disclosures.
