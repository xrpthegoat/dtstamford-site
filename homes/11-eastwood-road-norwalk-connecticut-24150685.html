About this home

Welcome to this beautiful move-in ready, 3 bedroom, with the option of a 4th bedroom plus 3-full bath home offering one-story living on a flat, yet private/park like 1.11 acres. Step inside to find a completely remodeled (in 2022-2024) open-concept living. The heart of the home is the kitchen, complete with natural stone Quartzite countertops, SS appliances, multiple skylights, black cabinetry + an extra large breakfast bar ROOM; leading out to the oversized deck & screened in porch. The Great Room includes a custom mudroom area with built-in cubbies/bench, & newly rebuilt FLOOR TO CEILING stone fireplace for wood-burning fires that adds warmth & character, while also helping reduce the heating bill. There is a very large Flex Room (that could be used as a game room, play room or even 4th bedroom) completely closed off by two custom barn doors, that allows for so many options. At the end of a long day retreat to either of the primary en-suites! Each has its own fully renovated full bath + large closet; (one walk in, one offers double wall). An additional bedroom & fully renovated full bath w/ tub in hall. Dedicated laundry room- not just a closet. Newly installed engineered wood floors and recessed lighting throughout. Attached 2-car garage w/ just one step into the home. The highly sought-after Cranbury Park Neighborhood location is just 5 minutes to the Merritt 7 Train Station, and even less to Rte 7, The Merritt, Walmart, TJ Maxx. There's nothing to do but move in!
