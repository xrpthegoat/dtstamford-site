About this home

Move right in to this pristine townhome in Westport's exclusive Terra Nova condominium association. Located in one of the most desirable areas within the Terra Nova complex, the spacious home features an open floor plan, expansive rooms and tall ceilings. Highlights include a private elevator to all three levels of the home, two gas fireplaces, five-inch white-oak hardwood floors, SubZero, Bosch and Thermador kitchen appliances and private outdoor space with a natural gas barbecue connection. The fully finished walk-out lower level is great flexible-use space, suitable for entertaining, office, home gym, playroom, family and guests. The townhomes all encircle a beautifully landscaped swimming pool. Terra Nova is located moments from shopping, restaurants, Westport Library, Levitt Pavillion, Westport Country Playhouse, Compo Beach, excellent schools and Metro-North Railroad.
