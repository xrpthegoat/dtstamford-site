About this home

Ranch style home with some fantastic updates. The renovations make a big difference in the overall feel of the space, especially with the new kitchen and bathrooms. You will find a spacious family room with a fireplace and a crafted stone wall which makes it cozy and inviting. There is a large primary suite with plenty of closet space and sliders to the deck to enjoy morning moments. The deck is accessible from the kitchen, master suite and family room, and leads to the private backyard, great for entertaining and games. Conveniently located on the Old Greenwich Stamford line, within 5 minutes drive to I95 and the train station, and 10 minutes to Greenwich Point beach which is a huge perk for easy commute and leisure activities. The lower level offers 5 additional rooms, including an auxiliary unit with separate entrance, perfect for Live-In Au Pair or In-law situation. Final remodeling and landscaping work in progress.
