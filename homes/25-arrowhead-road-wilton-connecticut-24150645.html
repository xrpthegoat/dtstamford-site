About this home

Updated South Wilton home, easy commute to Sono/Stamford/NYC . Raised ranch with 3 bedrooms with a possible 4th, 2.5 baths, and picturesque Norwalk River views. Every detail has been refreshed: all-new bathrooms, recessed lighting throughout, fresh interior paint, new siding, and a redesigned front entry with door and sidelights allowing plenty of natural light. Wood-burning fireplaces in both the living room and family room add warmth and charm. The regraded lawn and professional landscaping frame the home beautifully, while the deck and patio create ideal settings for enjoying the tranquil water views. Has portable generator hookup right next to the garage. Neighborhood includes a great pond to skate on in the winter and fish, canoe on in the summer (voluntary annual association is $250 for access to the pond). Wilton living at its finest-move right in.
