About this home

Welcome to 88 Horton Street. Located in the "Heart" of the Cove, a beautiful & popular waterfront section of Stamford w/in walking distance to Cove Island Park, Beach, walking paths, boat launch, etc. Minutes to Chelsea Piers, downtown Stamford, RR station & I-95. Apartment has 3 bedrooms, living & dining room, eat-in kitchen, bathroom, deck & laundry hookups. Separate Utilities: gas heat, gas hot water heaters (50gal) & electric meters. There is plenty of parking with the private driveway & street parking on both sides of Horton St. This perfectly located home is a great opportunity.
