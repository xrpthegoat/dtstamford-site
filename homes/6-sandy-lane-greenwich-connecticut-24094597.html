About this home

Nestled on four serene acres in Greenwich's coveted Back Country, this private estate offers timeless charm, versatile living space, and exceptional indoor-outdoor flow. Located on a peaceful cul-de-sac just off the Round Hill exit, it combines total seclusion with prime commuter access to downtown Greenwich and NYC. Inside, the home features 5 spacious bedrooms, 6.5 bathrooms, and rich hardwood flooring throughout. The sun-drenched living room and elegant formal dining room each feature a wood-burning fireplace, flowing into a vaulted sunroom framed by glass-ideal as a home office or reading retreat. The updated chef's kitchen stuns with granite countertops, a dramatic vaulted breakfast area, and expansive walls of windows overlooking the resort-like backyard. Two dedicated offices, a fitness room with full bath, and a powder room complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true sanctuary with a spa bath, soaking tub, and separate shower. Additional bedrooms are generously sized, with thoughtfully designed bathrooms including a Jack & Jill layout and private guest access. A separate wing over the garage offers incredible flexibility-perfect for an au pair, in-law suite, or long-term guests-with its own den, playroom, bedroom, and two full baths. Outside, enjoy a heated gunite pool surrounded by stone walls and lush greenery-a picture-perfect escape for entertaining or quiet mornings alike.
