About this home

119 Proprietors Crossing is an entertainer's dream, featuring stunning architecture by James E. Gearity. Nestled on four private acres, this six-bedroom estate blends timeless elegance with modern luxury. A private drive leads to an elegant fountain, setting an unforgettable tone. Inside, a soaring foyer showcases a dramatic Baccarat chandelier and seven fireplaces, enhancing the warm atmosphere. Spacious entertaining areas flow seamlessly to a tranquil terrace. The heart of the home includes a chef's kitchen that opens to a Great Room with a remarkable 35.5 foot vaulted ceiling, centered around a large quartz island with integrated audio. A formal dining room beneath another Baccarat chandelier sets the stage for memorable gatherings. The West wing hosts the primary suite, featuring a cozy retreat and a study with a hidden staircase leading to a gym and an 800-square-foot dressing room. An adjacent Junior Primary Suite has its own entrance and living area. Upstairs, four additional bedrooms include two ensuite and two with a jack-and-jill bath. The finished lower level boasts a media room, fitness center, and wine cellar. Outdoors, curated grounds offer a dining pavilion, outdoor kitchen, and a newly constructed pool and spa, with space for a tennis/pickle-ball court. Engineered for efficiency, the estate features commercial-grade systems, radiant heat, and a rare Welsh slate roof. A four-car garage and an elevator service complete this exceptional offering.
