About this home

Rarely available coastal living in coveted Shippan Point! Tucked behind a private gated driveway, hidden from the street, this 1584 sq ft, 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath cottage at 217 Ocean Drive West combines timeless character with modern turnkey ease and scenic water views. The freshly painted interior features hardwood flooring, anchored by a charming living/ den room accented with charming ceiling beams. The kitchen comes equipped with new appliances, ready for immediate use, and is complemented by a eat in/ dining area. Outside, enjoy a balcony with water views, great for morning coffee. The cottage also has a sizable side yard for your enjoyment. All exterior maintenance and groundskeeping covered by the owner. This home is an exceptional match for downsizers, snowbirds, or anyone seeking simplified, low-maintenance living in Stamford's premier waterfront community. Includes 1 dedicated off-street parking space behind the gate. 217 Ocean Drive W is a short walk to Fairview Beach and minutes to West beach, downtown Stamford, i95, and the Metro-North. Tenant is responsible for electric and natural gas. Move-in ready and available immediately!
