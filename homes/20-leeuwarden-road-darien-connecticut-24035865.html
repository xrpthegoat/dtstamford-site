About this home

This very special Georgian colonial is like new and boasts 5 spacious bedrooms, 3 ensuite plus 2 more full baths. The open floor plan allows a perfect flow from the kitchen to the dining area, to the living room to the patio and beyond. The chef's kitchen is the heart of the home and has a marble-topped center island, Wolf range, twin Subzero refrigerator and freezer, additional Subzero drawer icemaker and fridge. A sunny, window-lined open dining area leads to a large living room with fireplace. Adjacent to the kitchen, is a wet bar, sink, marble countertops, extra dishwasher and wine fridge with plenty of storage for glassware. A sitting room/study, library, office and custom mudroom are also located on the first floor. An open, tri-level center staircase leads to the second floor with a luxe primary bedroom suite which includes a private balcony overlooking the spacious, level and professionally landscaped yard, double walk-in closets, standalone tub, two sinks and large shower. A generous second story Media Room with vaulted ceiling and deck overlooks the yard. A second staircase leads upstairs to the media room and all the bedrooms as well. The third floor adds loads of extra storage. The lower level offers a recreation room and exercise room. Thoughtful details in this home include the gorgeous, custom millwork and wainscoting, marble and hardwood flooring and thick solid interior doors and hardware. Professionally designed and decorated.
