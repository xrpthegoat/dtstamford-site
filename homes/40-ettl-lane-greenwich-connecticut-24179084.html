About this home

Beautifully maintained 3 BR, 3.5 Bath, 2368 square foot home located off quiet cul-de-sac in highly sought-after Greenwich. Main level with hardwood floors, includes formal entry with powder room, a bright eat-in kitchen, beautiful DR and spacious, welcoming LR with FP. Sliders open to the private deck which overlooks the backyard - perfect spot for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs is a generous Primary BR with bath and closets. Two Family BRs share the full hall bath. The lower level is a terrific finished space with fireplace, built-in bookshelves and full bath - ideal family/playroom, guest area and perfect home office. Separate laundry, great storage. One car garage, plus assigned spot. Walk to shops, parks, restaurants, new DeCicco's grocery and the highly-regarded Glenville School. Friendly neighbors! Best choice!
