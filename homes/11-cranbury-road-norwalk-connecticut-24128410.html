About this home

***SHORT TERM FURNISHED RENTAL AVAILABLE 11/1/2025 THRU END OF JUNE 2026*** Only two miles from downtown Westport and minutes to I-95 and the Merritt, this classic four-bedroom, three- and a half-bath home was built in an era known for craftsmanship and gatherings. Lovingly cared for and recently updated while preserving the wonderful details of the period, this home is private and peaceful. Here, you can relax in the living room or linger over a meal together in the spacious dining room. Enjoy entertaining? There's a butler's pantry adjacent to the newly renovated kitchen. Two wood burning fireplaces (1 in the LR & 1 in the MBR) set the tone for winter, along with Adirondack chairs surrounding an outdoor fire pit. During the spring and early summer you will enjoy the outdoor deck and patios. Back inside, the house has a flexible layout. Finally, the lower level is great for the hobbyist and has additional storage. Located on the Westport line means both Norwalk & Westport dining and shopping are just around the corner.
