About this home

Gracious North Stamford Colonial Designed for Today Set on a quiet, private acre in one of North Stamford's most desirable enclaves, this 4-5 bedroom Colonial combines classic design with modern comfort. Sole ownership since construction speaks to the care and pride behind this home. With a flexible layout, natural light, and seamless flow, it's designed for the way we live now. The main level features an inviting living room, formal dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with granite counters that opens to a vaulted-ceiling family room with fireplace and glass doors to the deck, that overlooks a park-like, private, level acre. Your own paradise. A handicap accessible full bath and flexible room-perfect as a home office, library, or guest suite-add to the versatility. Upstairs, the primary suite impresses with soaring ceilings, dual walk-in closets, and a large bath. Three additional bedrooms, two full baths, and an oversized multi-purpose room create options for work, play, or guests. A back staircase and second-floor laundry add convenience. A 3-car attached garage, ample storage, hardwood floors, 9-foot ceilings, central air, irrigation, and security round out the features. This home offers scale, style, and privacy in a coveted North Stamford location.
