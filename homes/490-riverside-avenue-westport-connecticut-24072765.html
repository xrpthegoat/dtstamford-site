About this home

This beautifully maintained Westport studio apartment provides the perfect blend of convenience and charm. Ideally situated within walking distance of trains, buses, restaurants, bars, and coffee shops, it's also just a short drive to Compo Beach, Longshore, and Main Street. Flooded with natural light, this bright and airy first-floor unit features gleaming hardwood floors and an updated kitchen equipped with a refrigerator, electric range, dishwasher, microwave, and sink. Step outside to enjoy your own private patio, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The landlord provides heat, hot water, snow removal, and trash collection. Tenants are responsible for electricity and cable/internet. Additional perks include one assigned parking space and access to a coin-operated laundry in the basement. Must have excellent credit, strong references, and proof of income. No pets, smoking, or vaping allowed. Don't miss this fantastic opportunity to live in the heart of Westport!
