About this home

Beautifully updated 4-bedroom home with 4 full baths, set on a private, park-like acre in one of North Stamford's most convenient neighborhoods. The thoughtfully designed layout features a gracious formal living room with fireplace and an oversized dining room with fireplace-ideal for both elegant entertaining and everyday gatherings. The heart of the home is the expansive family room, filled with natural light from abundant windows and skylights, and offering seamless access to the patio, beautifully landscaped backyard, and pool. The updated eat-in kitchen is a chef's delight, featuring quartz countertops, a high-end stainless-steel gas range, and direct walkout access to the patio for easy indoor-outdoor living. A full bath conveniently located off this area is perfect for pool guests. The main level also boasts a luxurious primary suite complete with two generous walk-in closets and an oversized spa-like bathroom with soaking tub and steam shower. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious bedroom with en-suite bath, two additional well-sized bedrooms, and finished storage space. Outside, the private backyard retreat shines with a heated, saltwater in-ground gunite pool and mature landscaping that creates a serene escape. Additional highlights include a detached two-car garage and a full-house generator.
