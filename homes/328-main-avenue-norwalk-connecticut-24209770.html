About this home

A Norwalk Landmark with Three Ways to Create Value For decades, people have known this property as the home of Shear Cut Salon. Since 1979, the salon has served Norwalk and the greater Fairfield County area, giving the building a name and presence that a new business location would take years to build. The commercial space occupies the lower level, with two residential apartments above. That combination creates a compelling opportunity: a recognizable location for an owner-operator, residential income potential, and a commercial property to own for the long term. Few mixed-use properties come with this kind of established local identity. Whether you envision continuing the salon's legacy or bringing a new venture to a building Norwalk already knows, this is an opportunity to make a familiar address your own.
