About this home

NRES is proud to present 79 Noble Street, Stamford, CT 06902 - a standout investment opportunity in the sought-after Waterside neighborhood! This well-maintained multi-family home offers 2,010 sq. ft. split between two spacious 2-bedroom, 1-bath units-perfect for owner-occupants or investors. The property features a detached 3-bay garage and a private backyard, ideal for additional income or outdoor enjoyment. Set in Stamford's vibrant Waterside community, the location offers the best of both worlds: coastal charm and urban convenience. Just minutes from downtown Stamford, train stations, parks, and waterfront dining at local favorites like Prime and Crab Shell, this neighborhood is a magnet for renters and homeowners alike. First-floor unit is available to show! The second-floor tenant is on a month-to-month lease, currently not granting access. Don't miss this chance to own a versatile, income-generating property in one of Stamford's fastest-growing areas!
