About this home

Nestled in the highly sought-after Westover neighborhood, this hilltop contemporary colonial is a true gem. Step inside to discover a blend of elegance and function that will captivate you from the moment you enter the double-story foyer. The main level featues high ceilings and wood flooring, gracious living room with wood burning fireplace, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen with granite counters, stainless steel appliances and island, and adjacent family room with vaulted ceiling and gas fireplace. French doors from both the living room and family room lead to the private back patio overlooking lush landscaping and majestic trees. On the second level, the primary bedroom features high ceilings with skylights, en-suite bath also with skylights, and 3 closets. Two other bedrooms, each with 2 closets, and hall bath with double sink vanity. The finished basement offers great flex space...use as an office, gym or rec room. Conveniently located - just .2 to Stillmeadow School, and 1-2 miles to shops, restaurants, golf course, parks and downtown Stamford!
