About this home

Welcome to 16 Burwell Street, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath coastal home overlooking a tidal inlet with views of Norwalk Harbor and Long Island Sound. Located in one of Norwalk's most desirable waterfront neighborhoods, this move-in-ready residence combines modern updates, flexible living spaces, and an exceptional setting. Recent improvements include new hardwood floors throughout, new appliances, updated bathrooms, new doors and designer hardware, creating a fresh, contemporary feel. Multiple flex spaces provide endless possibilities for a home office, playroom, fitness area, or guest accommodations. Outside, extensive landscaping, a new bluestone walkway, and a stunning bluestone patio surrounded by mature gardens and beautiful plantings create the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining. A detached two-car garage provides ample parking and storage. Enjoy breathtaking water views year-round while being just moments from pickleball, beaches, marinas, restaurants, parks, the South Norwalk train station, and everything this vibrant coastal community has to offer. For those seeking an extraordinary opportunity, the adjacent property at 18 Burwell Street is also available under a separate listing, offering the rare chance to purchase both properties together and create an exceptional waterfront compound or investment opportunity.
