About this home

Extraordinary property. Exceptional location. A once-in-a- generation offering in one of Greenwich's most desirable in-town neighborhoods. Magnificently sited on 1.17 park-like acres along the sweeping curve of one Greenwich's most coveted cul-de-sacs, this custom-built residence offers an unparalleled combination of privacy, scale and convenience, just moments from top-rated schools, downtown Greenwich, fine dining, shopping, and Metro-North. Designed with timeless architecture and gracious proportions, this elegant six-bedroom, five-bath home showcases exceptional craftsmanship and remarkable flexibility for today's lifestyle. A distinctive feature is the private suite with its own driveway, entrance, and parking area--ideal for an in-law or nanny suite, home office, or multigenerational living. The breathtaking grounds are equally impressive, featuring sweeping lawns and an expansive, level backyard perfectly suited for entertaining, recreation, or future enhancements. An exceptional opportunity to own a legacy property where timeless elegance, extraordinary grounds and limitless potential converge in the heart of Greenwich.
