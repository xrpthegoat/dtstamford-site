About this home

This beautiful 1931 Colonial Revival, designed by renowned architect Nelson Breed, is an architectural gem nestled on 2.02 professionally landscaped acres. Combining timeless charm with modern conveniences, the home also features a 25' x 50' heated Gunite pool with wonderful pool house amenities. The 3,327 sq ft main residence offers four bedrooms and three full baths, highlighting vintage details such as wide plank floors while providing modern comforts including central air, propane heat, and a whole-house generator. The living room is flooded with natural light, offering tranquil views of the pond and expansive natural landscape. A large vintage fireplace serves as a striking centerpiece-perfect for both entertaining and quiet evenings at home. Outdoors, enjoy a two-car detached garage, potting shed, fruit trees, aerated pond, and an expansive flat lawn framed by lush gardens and specimen plantings. The pool house is its own retreat, set back on the property and complete with a kitchen, wet bar, fireplace, half bath, built-ins, and vaulted ceilings-all overlooking the serene, heated Gunite pool. This unique property is a rare find, close to downtown Wilton, top-rated schools, and convenient for commuting.
