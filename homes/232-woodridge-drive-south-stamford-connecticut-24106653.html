About this home

Move right into this beautiful, quiet sought after mid-ridges neighborhood set on private idyllic lot. This 4 bedroom, 2 full bath home offers an open floor plan with updated granite kitchen plus slider to deck overlooking the private yard. 3 bedrooms and an updated bathroom on the main level. The lower level offers a bedroom, large family room, newer full bathroom, and laundry, plus a 1 car garage. Additionally, there is a slider from the family room out to a private patio and level yard. Features and updates include new central air, new roof, new floor in lower level, exterior paint, new driveway, city water, and city sewer. Convenient to shopping, Merritt, downtown, & train station. Absolute move in ready!
