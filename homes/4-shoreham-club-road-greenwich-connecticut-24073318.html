About this home

Experience unparallelled coastal living in this exquisite modern beach-style residence, masterfully crafted by the award-winning Braydan Construction. Nestled in a private association with access to neighborhood beach and small craft launch, this home provides a seamless blend of modern design and coastal tranquility. This 5 bedroom home embodies thoughtful architectural details and luxurious amenities, offering an ideal retreat for those seeking a sophisticated yet relaxed lifestyle. An open concept first floor has a living room, dining area, family room and fabulous kitchen. The back of the house is all glass including nano doors with access to the salt water pool, outdoor kitchen and patio surrounded by glass railings ensuing unobstructed views. The home is just seconds from town beach, Old Greenwich shopping village and Metro North train station.
