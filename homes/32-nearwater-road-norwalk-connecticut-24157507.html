About this home

Nestled on one of Rowayton's most coveted waterfront streets, 32 Nearwater offers an exceptional opportunity to experience the best of coastal Connecticut living. Located within the sought-after Pine Point Association and just four doors from Crescent Beach, the association's private beach, this inviting home blends timeless New England charm with a relaxed seaside lifestyle. Enjoy easy access to Rowayton Village, local shops, restaurants, parks, beaches, and the train, all while being tucked away in a picturesque neighborhood known for its scenic beauty and strong sense of community. Light-filled living spaces, comfortable indoor-outdoor flow, and a welcoming atmosphere make this home ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The spacious primary bedroom suite serves as a private retreat, featuring soaring 12-foot ceilings, a cozy fireplace, a spa-inspired bath, and generous walk-in closets. Additional bedrooms are well proportioned, including one with access to a private deck offering tranquil water views. A versatile third-floor space is perfect for a home office, fitness studio, or guest accommodations and can easily be converted into a fourth bedroom. Whether you're enjoying quiet mornings at Crescent Beach, hosting friends and family, or taking advantage of the area's waterfront activities, 32 Nearwater provides the perfect setting to embrace the Rowayton lifestyle.
