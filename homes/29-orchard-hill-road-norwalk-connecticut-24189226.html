About this home

This beautifully refreshed 5-bedroom, 4-full-bath home sits on a private 1.15-acre lot in Norwalk. Light hardwood floors and abundant natural light create a warm, inviting atmosphere throughout the open living spaces. The updated kitchen boasts white cabinetry, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances, flowing effortlessly into the family and living rooms. Large glass doors from these main areas open directly onto the expansive wrap-around deck with covered section, offering seamless indoor-outdoor living perfect for entertaining or quiet relaxation with views of the lush surroundings. Two primary suites feature generous space and private baths, complemented by 3 additional bedrooms and plentiful closets. Central air conditioning and oil heat provide reliable year-round comfort, with propane service available on site. Outside, the fully fenced yard serves as an ideal playground for dogs and family, with a level lawn framed by mature trees for exceptional privacy. Enjoy the hot tub, a charming front porch that connects to the covered deck, and beautiful landscaping with colorful gardens. Additional highlights include a 3-car garage, attractive cedar siding, and thoughtful updates throughout. This move-in-ready home perfectly combines modern amenities, flexible living space, and an incredible private outdoor oasis in a convenient Norwalk location with easy proximity to the Westport train station. A true retreat ready for its next chapter.
