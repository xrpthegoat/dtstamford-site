About this home

All the character of 1940s construction with all of the amenities of today. This quintessential cape is warm, inviting, and full of natural light. Relax with a book in the living room with built-ins or lounge by the fire in the sun-filled family room that opens to the beautiful, updated kitchen. Dine in the banquet-sized dining room perfect for hosting dinner parties, and you can work from home in the quiet, private office with built-ins and beautiful views of the expansive yard. The lower level has an additional family/media room and an exercise room to sneak in a quick workout. After a long day, retire to the first floor primary suite with full bathroom and walk-in closets or upstairs to a second suite with a sitting/yoga room, walk-in closet, and built-ins. An updated hall bathroom and another large bedroom with built-ins complete the second floor. The outdoor living area has a large, inground pool, patio, and expansive deck. Imagine yourself sitting by the pool, grilling on the patio, or playing games in the yard. This exquisite outdoor living area is surrounded by mature trees creating a private, idyllic atmosphere. Once the cold thaws, this could be your summer backyard oasis. Convenient to the schools, fields, trails, and train to NYC, this home is easy living at its best.
