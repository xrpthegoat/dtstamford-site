About this home

Historic character meets modern comfort on 1.11 acres at this special 1870 home. Listed as a 3-bedroom, but it easily lives like a 5. Original woodwork blends with a light-filled, open floor plan. The living room features a wood-burning fireplace, while the family room offers a decorative fireplace, new built-in bar, and abundant natural light. There's space for formal dining, a breakfast room, and that first-floor study conveniently located by a full bath. The chef's kitchen boasts a Lacanche range, Sub-Zero fridge, 2 islands, 2 sinks, Cambria quartz counters, and breakfast/coffee bars. A 3-season room with kitchenette, ideal for game nights or poolside relaxing, includes a pass-through window to the pool for easy entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite has a tray ceiling, windows overlooking the property, and a large bath with radiant heated floors and a soaking tub. Two more spacious bedrooms one with an en-suite, plus the bunk room ideal as a home office or additional sleeping space and a full hall bath complete this level. Outdoors, romantic patios, lush gardens, and thoughtful landscaping create a private, peaceful setting. Just 5 minutes from the Merritt, restaurants, and shopping, a rare chance to own a home rich in history, character, privacy, and convenience.
