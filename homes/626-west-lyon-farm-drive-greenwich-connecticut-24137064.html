About this home

Located in one of Greenwich's most desirable communities, this West Lyon Farm residence blends classic style with modern comfort. With 3 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, plus a flexible first-floor den that can serve as a 4th bedroom or home office, this home adapts to your needs. Relax or entertain on the expansive deck overlooking a peaceful, private setting. Easy access with minimal stairs from both the front entry and garage. Recent upgrades include a new roof and renovated first floor bathroom. Enjoy the Lyon Farm lifestyle, with newly refinished pool, tennis and pickle courts, and more in a park-like community.
