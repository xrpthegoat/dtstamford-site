About this home

At 17 Stuart, AR Luxury Homes turns staying home into a very persuasive proposition. Start in the wellness center: exercise, sauna, cold plunge. Work on your swing in the golf simulator. Choose a bottle from the wine room. Let dinner spill from the gourmet kitchen onto a 45-foot porch with Ghost Screens and heaters, then surrender the evening to the pool. Inside, warm wood tones, sculptural lighting and sunlit rooms give new construction a softer side. Five bedrooms include a primary suite with a private deck, generous dressing room and a bath worth disappearing into. An office, elevator, pantry, lower-level kitchenette and top-floor playroom handle the practicalities. The only difficult part? Finding a reason to go out.
