About this home

BACK ON THE MARKET. Buyers could not perform. Welcome to 150 Wildwood Road, an exceptional new construction that perfectly blends timeless colonial architecture with modern sophistication. Set on 1.12 private acres in one of Stamford's most private neighborhoods, this 9,000+ sqft masterpiece showcases superior craftsmanship & thoughtful design by respected local builder known for quality & attention to detail. Four fully finished levels of lux living, designed for everyday comfort & entertaining. From the moment you enter, you're greeted by a 2-story foyer, sun-filled open spaces, and exquisite white oak detail trim. The main level features a grand foyer, expansive family room/EIK with custom fireplaces, dining rm, living room w wood burning fireplace. The gourmet chef's kitchen has Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry, & generous center island-all opening to a light-filled breakfast area & spacious fam room lead to a huge deck overlooking backyard. The serene primary suite incl. a walkout balcony, huge walk-in closet, & master bath with massive curbless shower and his/hers sinks. 3 other bedrooms on 2nd floor with private baths. The 2nd fl also offers an office space. Finished 3rd floor offers flex space for a playroom, gym, or guest suite, while the fully finished walkout LL provides endless possibilities includes half bath & fireplace. Irrigation system installed. Potential pool site shown on attached survey
