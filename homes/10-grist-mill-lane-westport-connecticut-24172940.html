About this home

Welcome to 10 Grist Mill Lane, a move-in ready home set on a beautiful, level acre at the end of a quiet Long Lots cul-de-sac. Thoughtfully updated throughout, this 3-4 bedroom, 2.5 bath residence offers an open and versatile floor plan designed for today's lifestyle. The dramatic living room features a vaulted ceiling and fireplace, creating a warm and inviting gathering space filled with natural light. The renovated kitchen opens seamlessly to the main living and dining areas, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A screened porch extends the living space and provides the perfect setting for relaxing or hosting guests while overlooking the private backyard. Flexible living options include a main level den or office that can easily serve as a fourth bedroom. Renovated baths, updated Marvin windows, Hardie Plank siding, and numerous improvements throughout add to the home's appeal. Ideally located on a long, quiet lot in a desirable neighborhood setting, this home combines privacy, comfort, and convenience in one exceptional offering.
