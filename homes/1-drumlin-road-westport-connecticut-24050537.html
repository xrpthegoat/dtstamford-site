About this home

Welcome to 1 Drumlin Road, Westport, CT-a designer's dream come true! This modern 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home presents 1,445 sq ft of perfectly move-in-ready living space. Once inside you'll find a bright and airy layout, including an oversized living area with additional space for a dining room table. The kitchen underwent a complete renovation within the past two years, transforming the home with a more open floor plan and the addition of a stylish island with seating. Every room in the home has been thoughtfully updated, with the full bath off of the hallway receiving a complete makeover. Should you ever wish to venture beyond this meticulously maintained home and forego the tranquility of the outdoor patio, you'll find the convenience of Old Mill Beach (0.9 miles), Compo Beach (1.7 miles), the town center (3 miles), and the Westport Train Station (1.3 miles) to be truly exceptional.
