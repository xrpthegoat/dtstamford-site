About this home

Carefully crafted Connecticut modern farmhouse by award-winning Heine Development, this exceptional 6-bedroom residence blends timeless architecture with refined contemporary living amongst 4 finished levels on 2 private, level acres in one of Westport's most convenient luxury locations. Designed with perfection in symmetry, detail, and craftsmanship, the home pairs classic farmhouse inspiration with sophisticated modern finishes, including wide-plank white oak floors, curated lighting, and elegant interiors by renowned House of Clement. The stunning chef's kitchen features Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, seamlessly connecting to beautifully scaled living and entertaining spaces filled with natural light with Southern exposure and beautiful sunsets. Outdoors, enjoy a resort-style ozone maintained 20'x 50'pool with automatic safety cover, sun shelf, and expansive flat lawns enhanced by organic vegetable beds. The extraordinary lower level offers the ultimate lifestyle experience with a gym, sauna, full bath and a custom treehouse, playroom, and second laundry room, creating flexible spaces for wellness, recreation, and everyday convenience. Additional highlights include natural gas heat, a generator, and exceptional indoor-outdoor flow designed for effortless living and entertaining. A rare offering that combines privacy, luxury, and modern farmhouse elegance just moments from town, train, beaches, and all that Westport has to offer.
