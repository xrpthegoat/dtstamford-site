About this home

Don't miss this investment opportunity! Spacious 2-Bedroom Townhouse in Highview East - Tucked Among the Trees! Welcome to this beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse in Norwalk's desirable Highview East complex. Nestled in a quiet, wooded setting, this inviting home features a cozy wood-burning fireplace in the living room-perfect for cool evenings-and a private deck overlooking the trees, offering a peaceful escape right at home. The bright kitchen flows easily into the dining and living areas, creating a comfortable layout for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a full bath, plus a convenient half bath on the main level. Enjoy low common charges, a community pool, reserved parking, and ample guest parking. Easy access to I-95, Merritt Parkway, Metro-North, SONO Collection, beaches, Oak Hills Golf, and vibrant restaurants makes this an ideal home for commuters and weekend adventurers alike. Just a short walk to Norwalk Hospital-an excellent choice for healthcare professionals! New floors, freshly painted. Brand new water heater with a nine year warranty, Maintained HVAC system with service contract, the fact that the parking lots are scheduled to be redone in July with no extra charge to the community. Fully floored attic for additional storage. Easy to own, easy to rent, current tenant lease ends on March 31, 2026 at $2450 per month. Great investment opportunity.
