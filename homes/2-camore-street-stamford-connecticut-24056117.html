About this home

Welcome to this spacious, nicely renovated 4 bedroom colonial in a terrific, convenient location! The living room with fireplace is of a nice size and the open floorplan offers access to the bright kitchen/dining area with bay window, large center island, granite counters and stainless steel appliances. To complete the first floor, there is a large mud room and generously sized walk-in pantry. Upstairs, is a nice primary bedroom with a full bath with double vanity, tub and walk-in shower and lots of closets. There are three additional bedrooms, another full bathroom and laundry room on this level as well. The finished, walk-up attic level offers great space as a playroom or home office. Finally, there is a private patio, perfect for outdoor entertaining! Just around the corner from shopping, restaurants and minutes to vibrant downtown Stamford!
