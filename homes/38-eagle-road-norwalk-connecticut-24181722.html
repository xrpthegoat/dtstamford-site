About this home

Welcome home to this turn key cottage with a wonderful tiered back yard. Open plan living with feature bay window, bathes this house in natural light. Lovely kitchen with quartz countertops and island/breakfast bar. A living/dining room with a wood-burning fireplace, three bedrooms and an updated bathroom complete the main floor. A finished lower level can be a home office/gym/play room/den - a generous space to be creative. Outside there is a fab backyard (property if almost .4 acre!) with pretty stone steps, a patio and a porch - ready for all your summer entertaining. Garage parking for one car. Pets are allowed. Located in West Norwalk and convenient to shopping, Merritt Parkway and I95, train, etc . Hardwood flooring throughout, and recently painted makes this move in ready and waiting for a new tenant(s) to name this home.
