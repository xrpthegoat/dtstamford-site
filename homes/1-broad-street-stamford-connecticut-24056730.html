About this home

Experience luxury living in the heart of Stamford with this stunning residence offering breathtaking views of downtown, the Long Island Sound, and Mill River Park. This home features three bedrooms and three and a half baths, with carefully selected finishes that enhance the space beautifully. Upon entering, enjoy an abundance of natural light and stunning vistas. The chef's kitchen boasts premium appliances, granite countertops, and a wine cooler. The open-concept design connects the kitchen to the living and dining areas, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows. The master suite is a haven of luxury and comfort with an elegant ensuite bathroom, featuring a walk-in shower, jacuzzi tub, and dual sinks. Two additional bedrooms both with ensuites provide generous space and modern amenities for versatile use. Step onto the balcony for sweeping views of the city and Long Island Sound. Park Tower amenities include a fitness center, 24/7 concierge service, and a heated indoor pool. Located in downtown Stamford, you're a short drive from the Train Station (with shuttle service), I-95, and various restaurants, parks, and beaches. Discover urban living at its finest in this extraordinary home. The onsite storage room completes the package!
