About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condominium located in desirable West Norwalk. Offering the perfect balance of comfort and style, this home is move-in ready. The spacious living room with cozy wood burning fireplace and large dining area are ideal for both relaxation and entertaining. Step out onto the covered deck for spectacular views of the pond and fountain, the perfect spot for unwinding after a long day. With generous closet space and storage throughout, you'll have plenty of room to keep things organized. The dedicated office area creates a quiet space for work or study. The new water heater, 2 updated bathrooms, updated kitchen, new carpeting, and fresh paint ensure your comfort and peace of mind. Ledgebrook offers fantastic amenities including pool, fitness center, clubhouse and beautifully landscaped grounds bursting with colorful perennials. All within close proximity to the best of Norwalk's shopping, dining and transportation.
