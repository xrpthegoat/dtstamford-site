About this home

Classic Center Hall Georgian Colonial in convenient Cos Cob location. Modern 5 bedroom 5.5 bath on a cul-de-sac street. Close to Cos Cob Elementary, Central Middle School, Greenwich High, Pomerance Nature Preserve and Library. The Cos Cob train station is a seven minute drive. Modern functional layout with open kitchen and family room with wood burning gas fired fireplace. Roomy primary bedroom with sitting area and wood burning gas fired fireplace. Primary bath has steam shower and separate jet tub. Wood floors thru out. Finished lower level rooms with walkout and fifth bedroom and full bath. Gas fueled furnace and hot water. Detached garage with attic storage.
