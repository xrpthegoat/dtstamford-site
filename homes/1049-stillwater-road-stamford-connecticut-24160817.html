About this home

Welcome to 1049 Stillwater Road, a refined and thoughtfully built residence nestled in Stamford's coveted Westover neighborhood. Constructed in 2019, this home blends modern design with timeless comfort, offering exceptional quality and peace of mind. Spanning five bedroom and four a and a half bathrooms, the home is designed to accommodate both everyday living and elegant entertaining. A private in law or guest suite provides versatility with and extra sleeping area, and full bathroom, while a dedicated office space, home gym, and generous storage enhance functionality without compromising style. Surrounded by mature and established landscaping, the outdoor setting offers privacy and a serene backdrop for relaxation or gatherings. Ideally located just moments from the Merritt Parkway, this home combines convenience wit the charm of one of Stamford's most desirable residential enclaves. This is a rare opportunity to own a move-in ready home where design, location, and livability come together seamlessly.
