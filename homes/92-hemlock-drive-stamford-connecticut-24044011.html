About this home

Welcome to 92 Hemlock Drive! This move-in ready ranch-style home boasts 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. The updated kitchen flows seamlessly into a generous dining area and a bright, sunlit living room, featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace and a large picture window. The family room, complete with a second fireplace, offers a warm entry from the attached one-car garage and can serve as a versatile space-perfect for a playroom, office, gym, or whatever you envision! The level yard provides ample space for play, gardening, or creating a patio for outdoor dining. The home features meticulously updated mechanicals, along with plenty of storage space in the basement. Enjoy the cooler months with a cozy fireplace and admire the magnolia tree and pretty pink azaleas in the spring! Situated in a private, set-back location, it's still conveniently close to schools, transportation, and the expanding Bull's Head center-offering new dining and shopping options like Starbucks, Frank Pepe's, Shake Shack, and Whole Foods coming soon!
