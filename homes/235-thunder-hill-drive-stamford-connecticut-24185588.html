About this home

Light-filled spaces, an open layout, and a seamless connection to the outdoors define this unique contemporary home on one private acre in Stamford. The main level was designed for everyday living and entertaining, with the kitchen flowing into a spacious great room that combines the living and dining areas around a wood-burning fireplace. Large windows bring in natural light throughout the day, while the screened-in deck creates a comfortable place to enjoy morning coffee, dinner with friends, or a quiet evening overlooking the property. Upstairs, cathedral ceilings add volume and character to the two-bedroom layout. The home also offers two full baths and a convenient half bath. A separate room with direct access to the patio provides an ideal space for a home office, studio, or den. Outside, the one-acre setting offers privacy, mature trees, a detached one-car garage, and access rights to the Mianus River. Originally built as one of the Mid-Century Techbuilt homes, the property combines timeless design with a floor plan that feels just as comfortable today as it did when it was first conceived. If you're looking for something with character, natural light, and a setting that's hard to find, this home offers a refreshing alternative to the ordinary.
