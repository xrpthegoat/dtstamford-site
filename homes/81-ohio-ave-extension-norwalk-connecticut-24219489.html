About this home

You may like this "sweet little studio" apt which has the feel of a cottage and is perfectly nestled amongst the tall trees over a two car garage in a quiet setting with lots of privacy. At the end of the driveway you can enter your garage. Step outside and walk up to your own front door, enter the cozy studio, there are beautiful new just like wood floors, vaulted ceilings, lots of windows, a full bath with tub and shower and a breakfast bar. Because this is a studio, there is no stove, but the owner has supplied electric counter appliances for your morning coffee and more! Or, bring your gas grill and cook up some steaks while enjoying a cocktail on the new beautiful large deck which is conveniently located outside near the front door. The kitchen area has a stack W/D and refrigerator. This idyllic, quiet setting, offers small open space/easy to create separate areas and offers very inviting space. If you are looking to get away from the hustle and bustle of everyday living, traffic sounds etc! With the open floor plan you can create an office area if you work from home. Credit score, employment history and landlord references are important to landlord. A pet will be considered. There is only room on the property for one car which is the garage parking.
