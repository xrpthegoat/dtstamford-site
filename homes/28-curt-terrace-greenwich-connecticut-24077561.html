About this home

Tucked away on a serene cul-de-sac and just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, this stunning colonial home offers the perfect blend of timeless elegance and modern comfort. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring top-of-the-line Viking appliances, quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and a spacious island, all flowing seamlessly into the inviting living room with a cozy wood-burning fireplace. Upon entry, the spacious den greets you with a stylish, yet functional layout, ensuring that each activity-whether working, dining, playing, or relaxing-feels equally at home. The luxurious primary suite boasts a large walk-in closet and a spa-like en-suite bath with a relaxing Jacuzzi tub. Four additional bedrooms, all with gleaming hardwood floors and generous closets, provide ample space for family and guests. (Two of the four bedrooms have wall-to-wall carpet but there are hardwood floors underneath.) Step outside to your private, fenced backyard oasis, complete with a large deck and a stunning in-ground gunite pool-perfect for outdoor entertaining and relaxation. Additional highlights include beautiful hardwood floors throughout, a two-car garage, and a prime location near major roadways for easy commuting. This exceptional home offers the best of privacy, convenience, and sophisticated living!
