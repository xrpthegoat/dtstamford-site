About this home

Walk-to-Water Gem! 28 Old Trolley Way presents the perfect opportunity to enjoy all that Rowayton's waterfront community has to offer. Taking a stroll through your neighborhood means Wilson Cove, Wilson Point, and Rowayton Yacht Club. New sidewalk installation on Witch Lane for easy walk to the train station or Rowayton Elementary. Sitting on .26 acres, this charming two-bedroom, two-bath home features hardwood flooring, a cozy fireplace, spacious walk-up attic, and private, tree-lined views from every window. The lot is deeper than it appears, offering great potential for expansion, accessory dwelling or a fully re-envisioned residence with endless possibilities for personalization. Minutes from the Village area with top-rated restaurants, boutiques and everyday shopping. Nearby Rowayton resident-only town amenities like Bayley Beach and Rowayton Dog Park. Just 45 mi. from Manhattan, or a 64-minute train ride into Grand Central. Schools for 28 Old Trolley Way: Rowayton Elementary, Ponus Ridge MS, Brien McMahon HS.
