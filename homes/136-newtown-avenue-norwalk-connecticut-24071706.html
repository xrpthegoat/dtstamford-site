About this home

A rare opportunity in the heart of Cranbury! Nestled in a smaller, private complex, this beautifully updated townhouse offers tranquility while being just minutes from shopping, dining, and Cranbury Park's scenic walking trails. Set back from the road, this unit provides a private entry and overlooks a picturesque pond, creating a peaceful retreat. Step inside to discover new flooring throughout, enhancing the bright and open floor plan. The updated kitchen features modern finishes, an eat-in area, and sliders leading to a deck with serene pond views-perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The living room boasts a charming bay window, filling the space with natural light. Upstairs, both spacious bedrooms include renovated en-suite bathrooms and California Closets for ultimate convenience. The rear bedroom offers stunning pond views and pull-down attic access. The laundry is conveniently located on the upper level between both bedrooms. Additional highlights include an attached garage with ample storage and a flexible bonus area-ideal for extra storage or potential finished space. As a Norwalk resident, enjoy year-round beach access, seasonal waterfront dining, and mini-golf by the shore. Don't miss this rare chance to own a fully updated home in a peaceful, nature-filled setting with easy access to everything Norwalk has to offer! *BEST & FINAL offers by 12pm, Monday 2/24. *
