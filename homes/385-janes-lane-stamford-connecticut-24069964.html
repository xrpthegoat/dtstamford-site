About this home

Nestled in a picturesque setting with a serene pond in the front and a stunning gunite pool in the back, this beautifully landscaped home exudes elegance and comfort. The open floor plan is light, bright, and airy, with natural sunlight streaming into nearly every room. Perfect for entertaining, the spacious living and family rooms feature tranquil views and cozy fireplaces. The kitchen offers ample cabinet space, making storage and organization effortless. The primary suite is a true sanctuary, complete with a vaulted ceiling, oversized walk-in closet, and a luxurious bathroom. A second bedroom boasts the convenience of an en-suite bath. Many rooms capture breathtaking pond views, enhancing the home's peaceful ambiance. The lower level provides flexible spaces for play, TV, workouts, and more. Step outside to the beautifully landscaped backyard, where you can unwind on the deck or take a refreshing swim in the sparkling pool. A true retreat, inside and out! Square footage is 2412 on the main level, and another 1160 finished lower level. New pool 2011 with new motor 2024
