About this home

Welcome to Ledgebrook, one of Norwalk's most picturesque condominium communities! This beautifully updated home offers a wonderful combination of modern improvements, comfortable living and a peaceful setting. Recent renovations include two updated bathrooms, new flooring and new carpeting in the entrance and stairway. Enjoy relaxing or entertaining on your private deck overlooking the beautifully landscaped grounds. Ledgebrook offers a resort-like atmosphere with a swimming pool, clubhouse, scenic walking paths and a lovely pond with fountain. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, Oak Hills Park Golf Course, Norwalk Hospital and major commuting routes. An exceptional opportunity to enjoy the beauty of Ledgebrook with the convenience of condominium living. Professional photography to follow!
