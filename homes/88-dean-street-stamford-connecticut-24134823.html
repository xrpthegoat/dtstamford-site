About this home

If You've Been Looking for a Beautifully Updated Rental Close to the Beach - Your Search Is Over! Just moments from the Cove, this sun-filled 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom rental offers 1,400 square feet of thoughtfully updated living space in a prime coastal location. Step inside to a bright, airy interior designed with comfort and style in mind. The sleek kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and abundant cabinetry, with space for a dining table-perfect for everyday meals or casual entertaining. The living area provides a warm and inviting atmosphere, ideal for relaxing, hosting guests, or enjoying quiet evenings at home. Natural light fills every room, highlighting the clean modern finishes and thoughtful layout throughout. With I-95, the train station, Chelsea Piers, and downtown Stamford all just minutes away, convenience is at your doorstep. Don't miss your chance to live near the coast in a beautifully updated home that offers both comfort and a vibrant lifestyle.
