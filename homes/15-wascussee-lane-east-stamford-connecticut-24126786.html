About this home

This charming 4 bedroom Cape Cod home is full of potential and ready for your personal touch. This classic property features an inviting layout, hardwood floors, fireplace and abundant natural light. This property offers a fantastic opportunity to build equity and create the home of your dreams. Perfect for investors, DIY enthusiasts, or anyone looking to customize a home in a desirable neighborhood. Bring your vision and make this Cape Cod gem shine again. Close to train, and vibrant downtown Stamford. 47 minutes to NYC by train. The home is being sold "as is."
