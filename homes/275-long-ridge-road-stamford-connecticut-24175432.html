About this home

Investment Opportunity! 275 Long Ridge Road is a legal 4-family, non-conforming property w/ separate structures in the Bulls Head section of Stamford. Offering over 3,700 sq. ft. of living space, this property is the ultimate fixer-upper w/ tremendous "add-value" potential. The front building has 2-units; first-floor (Unit A) is a spacious 2-Bed/1-bath w/ eat-in kitchen, living room & large 3-season porch plus access to basement laundry & 1-car garage; the second-floor (Unit B) is a very large 1-Bed/ 1-bath w/ eat-in kitchen w/ pantry & living room. The rear building has 2-units; left side (Unit C) is a 1-Bedroom/1-bath w/ eat-in kitchen & living room plus access to he/she shed; the right side (Unit D) is a 2-floor unit featuring 3-Bedrooms/ 2-baths w/ large eat-in kitchen, living room & large enclosed porch. Paved parking for 15 cars! Oil heat (3-yo furnace & hot water heater), propane (used for stoves), electric, and municipal water & sewer are all paid by owner/ included in rent. Located on a bus line, walking distance to Bulls Head/ upper Summer St shopping/ restaurants, only 1.5 miles from Merritt Pkwy & 2.5 miles to Stamford Metro Station & I-95. Please be respectful of tenants & their belongings while on the property. Sold As-Is.
