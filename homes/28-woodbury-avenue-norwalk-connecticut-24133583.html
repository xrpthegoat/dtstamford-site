About this home

Beautifully renovated 2BR, 1.5BA townhouse showcasing modern upgrades and thoughtful design throughout. This home features new hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and stunning oversized windows with custom treatments. The kitchen is equipped with white shaker cabinets and stainless steel appliances, creating a clean and timeless look. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms, including a primary suite with vaulted ceilings and a skylight that fills the room with natural light. Additional highlights include attic storage and a large 2-car garage with extra space for storage. Conveniently located just minutes to I-95, Metro North, SONO, restaurants, shops, and more-this is a commuter's dream!
