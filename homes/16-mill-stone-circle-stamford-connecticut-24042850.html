About this home

Privately set at the end of a cul de sac in the desirable Saw Mill neighborhood is this refreshed three bedroom ranch with separate home office, updated kitchen and open concept living area. The spacious living room is open to the dining room and the family room with vaulted ceiling has doors out to the trex deck, all perfect for year round entertaining and relaxing. The primary bedroom has hardwood floors, a walk in closet and a full bath. The two other bedrooms also have hardwood floors. There is a two car garage, lovely landscaped property, a full house generator and brand new central air conditioning. The wonderful amenities await from this fabulous address - minutes to schools, shopping, commuting, dining and more.
