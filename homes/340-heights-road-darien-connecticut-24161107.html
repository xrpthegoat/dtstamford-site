About this home

Welcome to Heights Crossing in Darien, the newly constructed community ideally positioned across from the Metro-North train station. This luxury residence features a foyer upon entrance and thoughtfully designed layout. The chef's kitchen is appointed with a waterfall island, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Wolf appliances featuring an induction cooktop, wine refrigerator, as well as a dedicated dining area perfect for entertaining. The expansive living room centers around a gas fireplace and opens to an expansive private patio, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living. Both bedrooms include generously sized walk-in closets and ensuite bathrooms with well-appointed finishes. A powder room for guests and a separate laundry area are located off the main living space for added everyday convenience. Additional features include wood-style luxury vinyl tile flooring, oversized windows providing natural light, custom cabinetry, and quartz surfaces. Heights Crossing offers secure access, elevators, garage parking, on-site property management, fitness center, golf simulator, coworking spaces, and a resident lounge with WiFi. A landscaped courtyard with fountain and curated ground-level retail complete the setting.
