About this home

Starting out or downsizing? Here's a townhouse style condo with an attached one car garage in the 9 unit Hope Manor complex in the convenient Springdale neighborhood in Stamford. Plenty of space with a fireplace in the large living room, a formal dining room, an eat in kitchen, and two large bedrooms. A sizable den, good storage space and laundry are on the garage level. Primary bedroom has extra vanity closet with some plumbing, could be converted to primary bath. Sliders to decks on both the main and lower levels. Great combination of space and location in today's market. Layout plans in the photo section.
