About this home

Rare opportunity to experience waterfront living at its finest. Perfectly positioned on the picturesque Five Mile River with 240 feet of direct water frontage, this exceptional shingle-style residence offers panoramic water views, an all-tide dock, & direct access to Long Island Sound for boating, paddleboarding, kayaking, fishing, and waterfront entertaining. Designed to embrace its setting, the turnkey 4-bedroom, 3-bath home features a light-filled floor plan with vaulted ceilings, a chef's kitchen appointed with a new Sub-Zero refrigerator, Wolf oven, and gas cooktop, and seamless indoor-outdoor living extending to a private, enclosed waterfront yard, perfect for entertaining or enjoying sunrises over the harbor. Equipped with a full-house generator, underground irrigation, and abundant storage. Ideally located within walking distance of Rowayton Village, Metro-North, and the scenic roads of Tokeneke, this exceptional home offers the best of Fairfield County's coastal lifestyle. Spend your days enjoying Darien's beaches, charming cafes, boutique shops, and local restaurants, while taking advantage of a direct commute to Manhattan, less than an hour away. Combining an all-tide dock, expansive water frontage, and unparalleled access to both nature and amenities, this is a rare offering in one of Fairfield County's most coveted coastal communities. The listing agent can secure your private appointment. Available to move in immediately (before closing).
