About this home

Nestled on a peaceful half-acre lot in the sought-after Cranbury area, this charming Cape Cod-style home is the perfect blend of comfort, character, and convenience. Situated on a quiet cul-de-sac, this 5-bedroom, 2.5 bathroom residence boasts hardwood floors throughout and a spacious, inviting layout, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Upon entering, you'll be welcomed by a bright and airy living room featuring a stunning stone fireplace that brings warmth and coziness to the space. The galley kitchen flows seamlessly into the adjacent dining area, perfect for casual meals or special gatherings. The first floor includes 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and a large, versatile room that can serve as a family room or playroom. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms with ample closet space, along with a charming sitting or reading nook that invites relaxation. Outside, the tree-lined yard and oversized patio create a beautiful private oasis, perfect for outdoor entertaining or quiet retreats.
