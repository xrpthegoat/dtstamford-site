About this home

Spacious 2-bedroom, 2-bath condominium in the desirable River House complex, offering a prime mid-city Stamford location. This second floor unit features sliding glass doors leading to a private balcony with views of the pool below and the Rippowam River, creating a peaceful backdrop. Enjoy the convenience of in-unit washer and dryer, generous room sizes, eat-in kitchen, one assigned parking spot and excellent natural light. River House amenities include a pool and club room, with Scalzi Park just moments away and easy access to downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, and transportation. Property is being sold as-is and presents a great opportunity for renovation and customization.
