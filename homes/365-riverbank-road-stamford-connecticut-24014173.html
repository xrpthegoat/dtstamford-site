About this home

Welcome to La Gentilhommiere, a Fairfield County gem, embodying the epitome of architectural finesse, now open for those seeking unparalleled luxury. Inspired by the Chateau de Givenchy le-Noble, this limestone masterpiece seamlessly blends European elegance with modern comfort. Enter through the gates, flanked by mature linden trees, into a formal motor court, where each sense is awakened by the beauty around. From the flowering trees to the melody of birdsong, every detail resonates with opulence. This is a residence, a sanctuary, a work of art. The materials used to create this treasure are authentically French: centuries-old beams, 18th century parquet de Versailles floors, Remy Garnier hardware, even the La Cornue range. In the tradition of so many period manor homes, the main residence's four ensuite bedrooms are all located on the arrival level of the home, with the public rooms located on the upper level. Engage in quiet contemplation in the parlor or library, indulge in lively discussions in the dining room, or delight in culinary pursuits in the Smallbone kitchen, adorned with two limestone sinks. Natural light floods every space, framing picturesque views of the landscape. Step outside to an enchanting outdoor oasis, complete with a guest house, sparkling pool, and pool house boasting a gas fireplace, magnificent bath and surround sound home theater. Despite its tranquil ambiance, this estate remains conveniently close to Manhattan.
