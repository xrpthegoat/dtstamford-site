About this home

A picturesque circular driveway adorned with cherry blossoms leads you to this thoughtfully renovated contemporary home, where modern sophistication meets timeless appeal. Enter the spacious double story foyer, and immediately feel a sense of luxury and style. A half flight up leads you to the main level, where the living room welcomes you with a marble fireplace and custom millwork, creating a cozy yet refined ambiance. The formal dining room features sliders to a mahogany deck, offering a seamless transition between indoor and outdoor entertaining. A true highlight of the home is the family room, with its vaulted ceiling, sliders to the deck, and walls of windows that flood the space with natural light. The adjacent eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with gas cooking, a full-size fridge and freezer, a beverage fridge, and island with bar seating. The main level also hosts three bedrooms, including the primary suite, which features stylish built-ins, a walk-in closet, and en-suite bath. An additional hall full bath completes this level, making this home truly ideal for those desiring one level living. The lower level is equally impressive, featuring a den with brick fireplace, and access to a large patio with fireplace. A fourth bedroom, currently used as a gym, full bath, and dream laundry room, complete this level. This property offers a unique blend of contemporary design, functional living spaces, and outdoor tranquility, in an ideal location. Truly a rare find
