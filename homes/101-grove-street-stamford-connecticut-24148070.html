About this home

Welcome to 101 Grove Street - a bright and updated townhouse with tons of natural light across each level! This unit offers 2-bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, an office/den, very generous closet space, an extra storage area, and TWO attached garages. In the kitchen you will find granite countertops, newer appliances, a lovely dining area, and access to a private balcony deck. The third level living room provides comfort and coziness with a bay window and wood-burning fireplace. The bedrooms are located on the upper levels, each with wall-to-wall carpeting, spacious bathrooms, and washer/dryer nearby. This stunning unit is conveniently located near restaurants, shops, entertainment, public transportation, and i-95. Schedule a showing today, you do not want to miss out on this! Pets conditional, excellent income and credit required, no smoking.
