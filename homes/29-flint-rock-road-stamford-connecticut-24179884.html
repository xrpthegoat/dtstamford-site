About this home

Welcome to 29 Flint Rock Road. Located on a quiet, newly paved dead-end road in North Stamford, this completely gut-renovated 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom, 2-car garage home spans over 5,600 square feet on a private 1-acre lot. Just 2 minutes to the Merritt Parkway, this turnkey property is ideal for a growing family seeking privacy and space. The home has been fully updated from the outside in, featuring a new roof, maintenance-free fiber cement Hardie Board siding, a freshly paved driveway, and a 26 kW whole-house Generac generator for uninterrupted peace of mind. Additional upgrades include a tankless water heater, a central vacuum system, and smart thermostats throughout. The property is pre-wired for outdoor cameras, and out back, there is a 700+ square foot deck, a 700+ square foot patio, and a future pool site available. Enter through the front door to a two-story foyer, featuring a stunning chandelier and modern staircase. The brand-new kitchen is outfitted with a full Thermador suite (propane cooktop, double wall ovens, fridge/freezer) and refrigerated drawers, all finished with quartz countertops and a casual eat-in dining area. Just off the kitchen, the family room boasts vaulted ceilings, exposed beams, a wet bar with a beverage fridge, and a masonry wood-burning fireplace. The main level is further complemented by a formal dining room, a formal living room, a study, and a powder room, as well as a dedicated laundry room with a new LG washer/dryer
