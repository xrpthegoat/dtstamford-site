About this home

Just off Ponus Ridge and moments from Talmadge Hill Train Station, this exceptional Colonial home offers easy access to the Merritt and downtown amenities. Set on 1.67 acres of beautifully landscaped, gated grounds, the property boasts a newly renovated, resort-style gunite pool and spa, an oasis for outdoor living. The professionally manicured backyard is complemented by a stunning bluestone patio, complete with a dramatic fountain centerpiece, ideal for entertaining. This home welcomes you with a grand two-story foyer, leading to an open-concept Kitchen's By Deane chef's kitchen featuring high-end appliances, including dual refrigerator/freezers, a butler's pantry, and a sun-filled breakfast nook. The adjacent family room offers a cozy gas fireplace and French doors that open to a balcony overlooking the serene pool area. The second floor showcases a sophisticated primary suite with custom built-ins, a spacious walk-in closet, and an updated, spa-inspired bath complete with a heated floor and towel rack. Three additional bedrooms and two full baths provide ample family space. A private staircase leads to a bedroom suite, complete with a sitting room and full bath, ideal for an in-law or au pair suite. The lower level has 2334 addt'l finished SqFt with a gym area, a living space with a cozy fireplace, and a wine cellar.
