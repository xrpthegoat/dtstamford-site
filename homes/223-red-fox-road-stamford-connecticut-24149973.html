About this home

Best and final no later than March 19 at 5PM. Live a serene, picturesque lifestyle in this waterfront retreat. Privately set on a tranquil lake, this open Colonial offers an exceptional setting for relaxing, kayaking, and even swimming. Mature trees and a lush lawn frame the property, while an expansive deck creates the perfect space for outdoor dining, barbecuing, and entertaining. Walls of glass invite the outdoors in, showcasing beautiful lake views throughout the seasons and creating a seamless connection to nature. The main level features a gracious living room, formal dining room, den, eat-in kitchen, and family room that flow easily together for comfortable living and entertaining. The eat-in kitchen with Corian countertops overlooks the lake-a peaceful and inspiring setting for everyday meals. Two fireplaces, located in the family room and den/office, add warmth and charm. Upstairs are three bedrooms and two baths, including a spacious primary suite with Jacuzzi tub, separate shower, skylight, and lake views. The current walk-in closet was originally a fourth bedroom and could easily be converted back. The two remaining bedrooms are generously sized. The lower level with private entrance includes an in-law/au pair apartment. Additional features include central air, security system, and whole-house generator. Enjoy the beauty and tranquility of this magical year-round lakeside oasis.
