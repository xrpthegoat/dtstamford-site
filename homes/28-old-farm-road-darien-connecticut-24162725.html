About this home

Set on a beautifully landscaped and expansive property, 28 Old Farm Road offers a rare blend of refined elegance and modern comfort. Thoughtfully designed with impeccable attention to detail, the residence features five spacious bedrooms, each with its own en suite bathroom. An exceptional primary suite was designed as a private retreat. This serene space includes a separate lounge area, an oversized walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired bathroom with views of a manicured, sprawling lawn and a beautifully designed pool complete with a safety cover, ideal for both relaxation and entertaining. Every outdoor element has been carefully curated to enhance the sense of luxury and privacy. Inside, the home has been recently reimagined by renowned designer Naomi Stroh, whose sophisticated vision brings a fresh, elevated aesthetic to every room. The finished lower level offers versatile space perfect for recreation, a home gym, or additional living, all complemented by ample storage. The interiors seamlessly blend timeless style with contemporary finishes, resulting in a warm yet striking living environment. This is a rare opportunity to own a move-in-ready, design-forward home in one of Darien's most desirable locations in the private Tokeneke Association.
