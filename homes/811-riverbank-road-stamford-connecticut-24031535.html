About this home

Peaceful and private Cape Cod Farmhouse style home on nearly 5 acres in North Stamford. The greenery and tranquility will take your breath away. With conservation acreage behind the home and surrounded by beautiful woodlands, the rippling pond, gazebo and island completes your outside oasis getaway. As you enter the home, you are greeted with vaulted ceilings in the great room and a stone wood burning fireplace perfect for a cold winters CT night. The massive family area flows right into the dining area and with an updated kitchen w/ farmhouse sink and new appliances making this perfect for entertaining while enjoying the panoramic views. The master wing has a large updated primary bath and walk-in closet with gorgeous and private views of the grounds. Two other large bedrooms with full baths complete this home along with a secondary family room/den and sunroom with sliding doors going to the backyard. There is also a fenced in side yard used for bon fires and grilling for those cool summer nights. There is potential to expand on this land and subdivide to add another structure or guest house along with the refurbished 2 car garage also having potential for an upper level expansion. It is hard to find this much land and privacy outside NYC. It is also close to all major shopping and the Merritt Parkway perfect for commuters or just to be a weekend retreat!! Check out this peaceful getaway soon!!
