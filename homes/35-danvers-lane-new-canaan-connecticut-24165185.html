About this home

An exceptional opportunity to own in the desirable South of the YMCA neighborhood featuring four bedrooms & 4 1/2 baths, this home is a perfect mix of elegance and practicality. Taken down to the studs and rebuilt in 2006 - this home has it all. From the moment you step inside, you'll be captivated by the sophisticated details, including elegant moldings and wainscoting, rich hardwood floors and sunlit living spaces. The Living room boasts a wood burning fireplace and both LR and DR feature large bay windows. The library accessed off the LR has a trey coffered ceiling and a door to the terrace. Step through to the incredible oversized family room with gas fireplace which is wide open to an updated kitchen complete with top of the line stainless steel appliances, a walk-in pantry and a huge quartzite island. The breakfast nook opens to a charming terrace with a built-in gas grill and a large pergola with retractable shades. Upstairs an incredible primary suite is accented by another gas fireplace, a gorgeously updated bath and huge walk-in closet. There is a large bonus room with vaulted ceiling and three additional bedrooms, two with walk in closets and their own bath and one with access to a hall bath. The unfinished basement is 1784 square feet and is completely dry awaiting your finishing touches! But the piece de la resistance is the over 1 acre of beautifully landscaped level yard perfect for family gatherings, or peaceful relaxation!
