About this home

Discover the perfect blend of elegance and modern comfort in this newly renovated home nestled on two serene acres in New Canaan. Boasting five spacious bedrooms and three and a half baths, this home offers ample space for relaxation and entertaining. The heart of the home is an open floorplan kitchen and dining area, featuring a large kitchen island perfect for culinary creations and gatherings. The formal living room and the family room each have a working fireplace, creating warm and inviting spaces. Upstairs, a fully finished third floor provides a dedicated office space, ideal for those who work from home. The property also includes a large bonus room above the two-car garage, which can serve as an entertainment area or exercise space. With an additional room on the main floor available for home office use, this property accommodates today's need for versatile work and living spaces. Every corner of this home has been thoughtfully updated with modern finishes, ensuring a luxurious and comfortable lifestyle. Experience the best of New Canaan living in this exceptional rental property, where every detail has been designed for your comfort and convenience.
