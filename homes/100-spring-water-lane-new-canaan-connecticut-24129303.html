About this home

Discover the perfect blend of classic charm and modern living in this exceptional 6-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home nestled on a spectacular 2+acre lot in New Canaan's coveted West Side neighborhood. Originally built in 1955 and enhanced by two significant additions and renovations, this thoughtfully expanded residence seamlessly combines timeless appeal with contemporary functionality. The heart of this home features a gorgeous open-concept kitchen and great room design-ideal for both daily living and grand-scale entertaining. With six generously sized bedrooms and four and a half beautifully appointed bathrooms, this versatile layout offers tremendous potential for multi-generational living, providing privacy and comfort for extended family or guests. The truly exceptional 2-acre grounds feature private walking trails, creating your own personal retreat where you can enjoy morning strolls or evening walks without ever leaving home. This rare combination of privacy, natural beauty, and convenient location offers easy access to Talmadge Hill train station for effortless commuting to NYC-all while enjoying the tranquility and top-rated amenities of New Canaan.
