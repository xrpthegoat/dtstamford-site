About this home

SUBMIT HIGHEST and BEST OFFERS BY MONDAY 5PM. Beautiful well maintained ranch style home situated in a highly sought after William St neighborhood positioned right on the Westport line and next to East Norwalk. As you enter you are greeted by bright open floor plan, formal living room with beautiful picture window, gleaming hardwood floors, cozy fire place. Living room flows into a large formal dining room, Den/office/third bedroom with French doors, Updated kitchen with granite counter tops, stainless appliances opens to the family room with large picture window and French doors to brick patio. Fantastic master bedroom suite with vaulted ceiling, absolute dream walk in closet with custom built-ins and Master bath with jacuzzi tub and double sinks. Additional family bedroom and another full bathroom with tub and separate shower. Large 1100 sqft basement provides ample storage, laundry area ready for many possibilities. Attached two car garage. Easy access to East Norwalk train station, Calf Pasture beach, SONO and many great restaurants. Beautiful fenced in level yard.
