About this home

Welcome to Unit 2B at The Nuovo, a brand-new luxury apartment located right off of Stamford's High Ridge Road. Offered at $2,900 per month, this residence features modern finishes, stylish fixtures, and a thoughtfully designed living space. Residents enjoy convenient amenities, including elevator access, a dedicated Amazon locker, outdoor grill area, and landscaped green space. One parking space is included in the rent, with additional covered parking and on-site storage available. Ideally situated along High Ridge Road, just a short walk from Trader Joe's, Starbucks, shopping, and dining. With the Merritt Parkway only minutes away, this location offers the perfect balance of comfort and convenience.
