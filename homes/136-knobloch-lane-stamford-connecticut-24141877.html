About this home

*All offers are requested by Monday, 12/22 at Noon for seller consideration. Location, Location, Location! An exceptional opportunity in a sought-after Westover cul-de-sac neighborhood, this home sits on 1.4 acres of level, usable land with city water and city sewer. Thoughtfully designed, it offers generous rooms, great flow, and abundant natural light-an ideal canvas ready to shine again with your personal touch. Entertaining is effortless with a hardwood-floor living room that opens to a large dining room. The sun-filled eat-in country kitchen and family room feature a fireplace and sliders to an expansive deck, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living with serene views. A main-level home office/5th bedroom and full bath add flexibility, while the rear hall includes a mudroom and stairs to a versatile bonus room-perfect for a playroom, studio, or hobby space. Upstairs you'll find four spacious bedrooms, three full baths, and a laundry room. Home amenities include: whole house generator, central air, full basement, sprinkler system, a 10x16' shed, and a security system. With outstanding square footage and an unbeatable location, this home offers tremendous value for buyers eager to customize. Embrace the potential and transform it into the dream home your lifestyle desires. Offered "as is," it's a rare opportunity for visionaries, builders, and homeowners alike.
