About this home

At the end of a quiet South Wilton cul-de-sac, this exceptional Colonial pairs timeless architecture with beautifully curated interiors and a private, picturesque setting. From the moment you enter, the quality is unmistakable-exquisite millwork, elegant proportions, and thoughtfully designed spaces create a home that feels both refined and deeply livable. Formal living and family rooms each offer a fireplace, while the banquet-sized dining room is perfectly suited for memorable entertaining. At the heart of the home, a stunning chef's kitchen opens seamlessly to the family room, creating the kind of space where everyone naturally gathers. Custom white inset cabinetry, top-tier stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and an oversized island are complemented by a coffered ceiling, stone fireplace, and a warm, inviting atmosphere that balances sophistication with comfort. A private study offers a polished work-from-home setting, while the exceptional mudroom-with extensive built-ins and an additional half bath-brings effortless function to everyday life. Upstairs, five en suite bedrooms and a spacious playroom offer flexibility for modern living. Each bath is beautifully finished with heated floors, and the primary suite is a true retreat, complete with a fireplace, a luxurious spa bath, and an extraordinary custom closet.
