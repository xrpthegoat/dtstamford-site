About this home

Move right into this pristine 4 bedroom, 3.1 bath home perfectly located off of Long Ridge Road. This expanded Colonial sits on 1.14 acres and truly has it all, including 4 renovated bathrooms, a new 5 bedroom septic tank, and a brand new 50 year roof! The house also has 2 separate entrances which are ideal as an in-law/ au pair suite, or as a designated office space. The main level has an open floor plan which allows for seamless living and entertaining. Each room is light and bright, with lots of windows, and refinished hardwood floors throughout. Two bedrooms are located on the main level, and another two are on the second floor complete with en-suite and vaulted ceilings. Separate new laundry room on the main level, and an oversized 2 car garage with additional closets and attic space. Outdoor oasis with a large backyard, spacious patio, and space for a pool or court. This property is ideal home for commuters, being close to restaurants, shopping, golf courses, and specialty hospitals. A must see!
