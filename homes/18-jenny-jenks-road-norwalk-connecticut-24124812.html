About this home

Unlimited Potential! Welcome to 18 Jenny Jenks Road! Spacious 6 bedroom, 3.5 bath contemporary/colonial sits on corner lot Norwalk's most convenient locations. With 3,208 sq ft. above grade this home offers a comfortable layout w/ hardwood floors throughout, a large living room with a wood-burning fireplace, and a large family room perfect for entertaining. The eat-in kitchen opens to a balcony overlooking the backyard creating a great indoor/outdoor flow. The primary suite features a full bath and sliders to its own private balcony. Generously sized bedrooms provide comfort and flexibility for today's living. Oversized attic space above the garage. The finished lower level adds and additional 1,652 sq ft of versatile space, including options for a guest room, home office, game room, plus a full laundry room and a half bath. With great bones, this property works beautifully for a families who want to update and add their own style. Or a handyman seeking a property with cosmetic upgrades in mind! Large two car garage and spacious driveway offer plenty of parking. Walk to schools, and enjoy being just minutes to the East Norwalk train station, I-95 and more. Pool 'as is" Ready for your custom touches. A truly spacious and well-located property waiting for your touch!
