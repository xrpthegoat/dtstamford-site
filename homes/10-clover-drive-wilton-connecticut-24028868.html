About this home

Welcome to your dream home, a stunning newly crafted residence in lower Wilton, perfectly positioned just over the Westport & Norwalk borders. This architectural gem invites you in with its impeccable design and refined luxury, beginning with the exquisite white oak hardwood floors that greet you at the front entrance & flow seamlessly throughout the entire home.Bask in the light & airy ambiance of single-level living, where every detail has been meticulously curated. The gourmet kitchen is a chef's delight, featuring quartz countertops & a striking backsplash. This culinary haven seamlessly opens to the expansive family room, dining area, and living spaces, creating an ideal environment for both entertaining & everyday living. On winter nights, gather around the elegant gas fireplace, enveloped in warmth & charm. The primary suite is your personal sanctuary, boasting vaulted ceilings and a spa-like en-suite bathroom with heated floors & dazzling tile work. Additional bedrooms offer generous space, perfect for guests, family, or a sophisticated home office.Step outside to your private backyard oasis, where a brand-new patio awaits, perfect for serene outdoor relaxation & alfresco dining. The prime location in lower Wilton offers unparalleled convenience, with premier dining, shopping, & transportation just minutes away.Originally built in 1949 & reconstructed from the ground up, this upscale home ensures you can move right in & experience modern luxury with timeless appeal.
