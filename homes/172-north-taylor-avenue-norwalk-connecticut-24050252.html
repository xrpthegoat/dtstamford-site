About this home

Welcome to 172 N Taylor Ave! Nestled in the highly desirable West Norwalk area. This elegant Colonial home, built in 2003, exudes curb appeal with its intricate roof lines, dog shed dormers, and inviting front porch. Step inside to an open concept main level where an expansive center island and custom cabinetry create the perfect space for both entertaining and everyday living. Over 2,000 sqft of living space! Recently updated bathrooms showcase impressive design details, adding a touch of luxury to your daily routine. The finished lower level, featuring a walk-out, offers additional living space filled with natural light, creating a bright and welcoming atmosphere. A two-car garage provides easy access to the house, adding to the home's convenience. Upstairs, discover three generously sized bedrooms and a luxurious primary suite complete with a cozy fireplace and a spa-like ensuite, your private retreat for relaxation. This home is ideally situated just minutes from a golf course, scenic hiking trails, the Metro North train, shopping, and dining options, blending convenience and leisure seamlessly. Utility-wise, benefit from natural gas, city water, city sewer, and central air - the perfect setup for modern living. Additionally, smart home features enhance your quality of life. This one checks all of the boxes and is a great opportunity to establish 'home' this Fall!
