About this home

Come stay a while ~ This immaculately presented, newer mid-ridges home is beautifully tucked back from the road and offers an ideal blend of bright sunny space + comfort! You'll delight in the ease of living here, an open flow of generous rooms with large windows and polished hardwood flooring. All will enjoy watching the game in the sparking white kitchen/great-room featuring fireplace + sliders to the private patio. The lower level offers abundant finished, flexible space with a full bath. Upstairs, you'll find a luxurious primary bedroom with walk-in closet + full bath, plus 3 extra bedrooms and a walk-up attic, ideal for storage. The attached, oversized 2-car garage, central ac, and peaceful babbling brook add to the joys of living here! Pets considered on a case by case basis.
