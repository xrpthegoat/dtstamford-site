About this home

This well-maintained 2-bedroom, 1-bath condo offers a fantastic opportunity to own in one of Stamford's most sought-after locations. Nestled on a quiet, tree-lined street, the home provides a peaceful setting just minutes from all the shopping, dining, and entertainment downtown has to offer. The unit features a functional layout with a generously sized living and dining area that opens onto a private deck-perfect for enjoying views of the beautifully landscaped grounds and nearby river. Additional conveniences include an in-unit washer and dryer, a reserved garage space, and a large private storage unit. Residents enjoy access to an in-ground pool and are within walking distance of Scalzi Park, as well as close to public beaches, golf, and UConn's Stamford campus. Low HOA and a well-managed complex make this a smart choice for owners and investors alike. A rare opportunity to get into a prime location with room to make it your own.
