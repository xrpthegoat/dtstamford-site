About this home

Welcome to this exquisite country home, set at the end of a long, tree-lined gravel driveway. Thoughtfully designed and crafted, the home features a seamless floor plan enhanced by walls of windows that bathe the interior in natural light and offer captivating views of the gardens. The first floor boasts 10-foot ceilings and an open layout ideal for both intimate gatherings and large-scale entertaining. At the heart of the home is the Great Room, which flows effortlessly into the library, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen, and dining porch. A versatile first-floor office can easily serve as a fifth bedroom. Upstairs, the primary suite impresses with vaulted ceilings, fireplace, balcony, spacious walk-in closet, and luxurious bathroom complete with a large shower and soaking tub. Three additional ensuite bedrooms and a second-floor study area provide ample space. The recently finished walkout lower level is flooded with natural light and offers a large recreation room, dedicated wine cellar, workshop, exercise room, and full bathroom. Outdoors, the meticulously landscaped grounds showcase year-round blooms and ensure the utmost privacy. Town approved 20x40 pool site.
