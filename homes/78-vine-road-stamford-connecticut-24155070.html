About this home

Plant your roots and watch your future bloom at 78 Vine Road, a charming 4-bedroom Cape in the highly sought-after Turn of River and Newfield area. While it sits on Vine Road, this property offers unique privacy on .3 acres, tucked far back from the main road with the driveway flowing naturally off Kane, a peaceful dead-end street. The layout is a dream for entertaining, featuring a convenient one-step entry through a functional breezeway that connects the 2-car garage to the house and a versatile home office. This entrance leads into an open-concept kitchen that flows into a large living space, creating a warm, expansive atmosphere. The kitchen is complemented by a dedicated laundry and pantry room that connects directly to the fully fenced-in backyard, perfect for indoor-outdoor hosting. The main level also hosts two generous-sized bedrooms and a full bath, offering easy and accessible living. Upstairs, you'll find two additional well-proportioned bedrooms where dormers pour in natural light, alongside a floored attic space converted into a cozy sitting room that could easily become a great walk-in closet. The basement has over 1,000 square feet ready to store your things or for you to create that gym you've always wanted. As your vision expands, so does this unbeatable neighborhood; you are just minutes from schools, playgrounds, the JCC, and Sterling Farms Golf Course. Settle in and stay a while at 78 Vine Road, the perfect place to start your next chapter.
