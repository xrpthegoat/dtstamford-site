About this home

Welcome to waterfront living at its finest in sought-after Dolphin Cove. Perfectly positioned on the Long Island Sound, this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath residence offers a rare blend of coastal elegance, modern luxury, and resort-style amenities. Extensively updated throughout, the home features a stunning custom kitchen with Sub-Zero, Wolf, Bosch, and Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry, designer finishes, and an open-concept layout ideal for entertaining. The first floor has been completely reimagined with new flooring, custom millwork, a bespoke bar with wine refrigeration, updated lighting, a redesigned fireplace feature wall, and enhanced sight-lines capturing water views from the moment you enter. The luxurious primary suite was fully renovated and includes a spa-inspired bath, custom closets, tray ceiling with lighting, marble shower, designer tilework, and refined finishes throughout. Additional improvements include new HVAC, interior and exterior paint, updated bathrooms, upgraded electrical systems, new front entry, landscaping enhancements, sprinkler system upgrades, and countless thoughtful upgrades throughout the home.
