About this home

Welcome to 63 Maple Tree Avenue, a meticulously updated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse offering over 1,716 square feet in Stamford's quiet Maple Plaza West. Step inside to discover an open layout, with a tastefully designed living room open to the dining room and kitchen. The kitchen has newer quartz countertops, a breakfast bar, and all-new appliances (2022). Refinished hardwood floors flow through these rooms, and a half bath with custom wainscoting completes the first level. Upstairs, three bedrooms feature hardwood floors, and a spacious bathroom with large tub. The primary bedroom is to die for, complete with a spacious closet. The finished and heated basement provides flexible space ideal for an in-law suite, office, or playroom, plus a dedicated laundry room with new washer and dryer. Recent upgrades include recessed lighting throughout all three floors, electric baseboard heat (2021), new light fixtures, stair railings, window treatments, and AC wall units (May 2025). Enjoy your private patio retreat and 2 reserved parking spaces. HOA fees of $380/month cover grounds maintenance, trash, snow removal, water, sewer, pest control, and insurance. Located a short distance from the train to NYC, shopping, restaurants and the Stamford downtown, this thoughtfully maintained home blends suburban tranquility with urban accessibility.
