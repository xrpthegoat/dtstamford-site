About this home

Enjoy resort-style living with access to the in-ground community pool and a beautifully maintained setting in this spacious top-floor condominium. Thoughtfully updated and filled with natural light, this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers approximately 1,500 square feet of comfortable living space. Located at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, the unit features a private entrance and stairwell, creating a true sense of privacy. The open living area showcases vaulted ceilings, skylights, and a cozy wood-burning fireplace with custom built-in cabinetry. Step outside to your private balcony, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The primary bedroom also features cathedral ceilings, a skylight, and an en suite bathroom. Both bedrooms offer generous closet space. Additional storage is available in the attic and detached garage. The unit includes an additional assigned parking space for added convenience. Whether you're relaxing by the pool, enjoying the bright and airy interior, or taking advantage of the home's updates and abundant storage, this property offers an exceptional combination of comfort and lifestyle all just minutes from the Merritt, I-95, and Route 7.
