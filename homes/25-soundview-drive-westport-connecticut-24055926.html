About this home

For over 100 years, 25 Soundview Drive has been owned by one family and has served as a sanctuary for summer celebrations, cozy winter weekends and endless beach days spilling from the front door right out to the sand. The 4 bedroom, 4.5 bath home has 3,492 sq ft of living space with direct water views on all three floors. On the main floor, the living and dining rooms span the front of the home with walls of windows and lead directly into the kitchen and bar areas. In addition you'll find a bedroom suite, laundry room and powder room. On the second floor, the primary bedroom is front facing to take in the 180 degree water views, it includes a sitting area with a bay window, a custom walk-in closet, and large bath with separate shower and soaking tub. There are two additional bedrooms and a hall bath before leading up to the third floor. A spacious flexible space with full bath graces the top floor - there is a balcony overlooking Compo Beach, tons of space (with gorgeous original woodwork) for work, play or additional bedroom usage. The landscaped private backyard is rare at Compo Beach. There is a two car detached garage, private driveway and of course, a coveted Soundview front yard enclosed by a stone wall, sweet front gate and dozens of hydrangea bushes that bloom brightly through the sunny New England summers. * Renovations by architect Robert Gault. Recent updates: house & garage roofs, exterior paint, appliances,gutters,gas conversion, AC & Water Heater, 3rd fl deck.
