About this home

First Time on the Market! Set on a quiet, sought-after street, this spacious five-bedroom, three-bath split-level home spans 2,911 square feet of bright, comfortable space. The main level features an eat-in kitchen and a large open dining and family room with an electric fireplace, ideal for gathering and everyday living. Sliders off the dining and family area lead to a generous deck overlooking a level yard, well suited for indoor-outdoor entertaining. A sun-filled living room with a bay window adds welcoming charm. The upper level includes a large great room, providing valuable flexible space for recreation, work, or relaxing. A walk-out lower level adds even more versatility, well suited to a playroom, home office, gym, or multi-generational living needs. Central air, a one-car garage, and abundant space throughout complete this desirable opportunity in Riverside.
