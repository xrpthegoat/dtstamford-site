About this home

Welcome home to this modern 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath Contemporary Colonial which sits at the end of a picturesque private driveway and greets you with high ceilings and natural light as soon as you walk through the front door. An open and airy foyer connects a spacious den with a wood burning fireplace to a huge living room and dining room with skylights, both featuring gorgeous hardwood floors and easy access to the expansive back deck. Large kitchen has all the bells and whistles you could want: high end stainless steel appliances, center island with extra cabinet storage, wine fridge, pot filler and recessed lighting. Powder room and laundry conveniently located off the kitchen. Upstairs the spacious primary suite has cathedral ceilings, a walk-in closet and a gorgeous ensuite bath. There are 2 more large bedrooms and a stylish hall bath. Step downstairs to the lower level where you will find a generous 275 sqft playroom plus additional storage space and access to the two-car garage with a tesla charger. C/A and smart home features: Nest Thermostats, Amazon Hue Lights & Ring System. Outside is an entertainer's dream with its large wrap around deck, huge yard for outdoor activities and quiet moments enjoying nature. A stone's throw to all the conveniences on High Ridge Road (Trader Joes, Starbucks, Diner and so much more), 1 mile to Merritt, 4 miles to downtown and 5 miles to RR/I-95! Renting a single-family home never felt this good!
