About this home

Immaculate, Fully Renovated Home in Darien. Welcome to this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home. Completely renovated in 2015 with every modern upgrade for maximum energy efficiency and low-maintenance living. This property offers an unbeatable location-just a short walk to the train, Darien Commons, and all the town's vibrant amenities. Inside, the open floor plan is perfect for today's lifestyle, featuring sun-filled living spaces, a sleek gourmet kitchen, and thoughtfully designed updates throughout. From high-efficiency systems, spray foam insulation, full house generator to durable, low-maintenance materials and finishes, every detail was selected with comfort, style, and functionality in mind. Upstairs, the spacious 4 bedrooms include a serene primary suite with a modern ensuite bath. The finished third floor adds incredible versatility, with a bright space perfect for a playroom, gym, or media area, plus a private office ideal for remote work. Full size basement with work bench & tons of storage. Outdoors, enjoy a great backyard complete with a custom stone fireplace-perfect for easy entertaining all year long. With style, function, and location all in one move-in ready home, this is a rare find in the heart of Darien.
