About this home

An iconic Wilton "Once in a Lifetime" homestead. Every classic detail beautifully tells the story of this gracious property, inside and out. From the welcoming, sweeping manicured lawn as you drive up the driveway to the stately sited residence that is a masterclass in timeless elegance, every space is thoughtfully scaled with abundant natural light and views of the spectacular grounds. The extensive millwork is a hallmark of this home as are the 6 fireplaces. Sunken LR with gas fireplace and 9'+ ceiling blends everyday living and elevated entertaining. The Kitchen is effortlessly grand with marble island, high-end appliances, and custom lighting that flows into a cozy eating area with fireplace. Sunken FR is where you'll casually gather with floor to ceiling fieldstone fireplace and French doors to the patio. Library features cherry woodwork with glass built-ins. Upstairs are 5 Bedrooms PLUS a 2nd floor Family Room with fireplace. Primary suite is a serene oasis with fireplace, sitting area, walk-in closets and en suite Bath. Walk-out Lower Level is a versatile finished space with 6th fireplace, Media Center, billiards, exercise room and Full Bath. The sprawling grounds are the star of the show! Lush green lawns and beautifully landscaped flower beds abound! A charming 1905 Barn awaits your creativity (electric, water and hooked up to septic.) Patio with outdoor fireplace, pergola and dining area. A splendid feast for your senses awaits you! Come experience 125 Cannon Road!
