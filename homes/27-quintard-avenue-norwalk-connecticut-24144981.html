About this home

Lovingly restored and fully furnished 1890 Regal Farmhouse offering a beautifully furnished apartment with water views and timeless charm. This spacious residence features a craftsman style kitchen with coffered ceilings, stainless steel appliances, gas stove, coffee bar, and quartz countertops, opening to a large dining room ideal for everyday living or entertaining. The apartment includes three generously sized bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with walk in closet and vanity. A modern full bathroom with tub, plus a convenient half bathroom and office space, complete the interior. Original wide plank floors, central air conditioning, and tasteful furnishings provide comfort and style throughout. Enjoy private outdoor spaces including a front porch, back deck with grill and picnic table, backyard area, and private driveway. Additional features include in unit washer and dryer, private side entrance, and ample storage. Relax and enjoy bay view sunsets from the primary bedroom and front porch, or take advantage of the unbeatable location just steps from SONO South Norwalk. Walk to the train, restaurants, mall, movies, and nightlife. A rare opportunity to enjoy a move in ready furnished apartment in one of Norwalk's most desirable locations.
