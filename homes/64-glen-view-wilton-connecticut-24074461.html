About this home

Welcome to Glen River, located along the Norwalk River in the heart of Wilton. This wonderful location is perfectly suited to enjoying the benefits of town and country. Enjoy walking to the shops, restaurants, library, Bradley Park, the Norwalk River Valley Trail, Horseshoe Pond and more. Even the train station connecting with Metro North isn't far. The private pool is a delightful amenity. 64 Glen View is in a most desirable location at the top of the complex, with cul-de-sac-like privacy and lovely views. Drive right up to the front door and walk straight in, no steps. The first floor features a full kitchen, sunny living room with fireplace and dining area ,with sliding glass doors opening to an East facing deck with lovely views. Also on the main floor are a half bath and closets. Upstairs features a bright primary bedroom with a vaulted ceiling and walk-in closet, a second bedroom, and a full bath with stacked washer dryer for easy convenience. New hot Water heater Jan 2025. Recently updated by the HOA: New roof, trim, soffit, gutters, skylights, venting, porches, decks, stairs and railings - installed in 2022/2023. No current assessments. Being sold as is.
