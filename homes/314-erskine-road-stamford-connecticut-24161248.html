About this home

Come see this private North Stamford home beautifully situated on picturesque waterfront property and featuring open, airy rooms! Be the first to enjoy the stunning new kitchen with quartz countertops, new designer baths and polished hardwood floors! A 2-story entry greets and leads to the open living room with sliders to the super large entertainer's deck overlooking scenic postcard views. You'll also find a home-office/den with vaulted ceilings & fireplace in addition to the formal dining room. This home offers 2 Primary bedroom suites, one on the main level featuring sliders to a balcony overlooking the water and a walk-in closet/dressing area, indulgent whirlpool tub and separate shower stall. The second level of this home offers another primary ensuite bedroom, 2 additional bedrooms and designer full bath. The basement offers plenty of storage and flexible additional space ideal for exercise or other pursuits. A 2-car garage, central air and postcard setting complete this idyllic package.
