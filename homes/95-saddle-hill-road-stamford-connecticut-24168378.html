***Multiple offers received. Highest and best requested by Monday (5/4) at 12***Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in North Stamford, this beautifully maintained Colonial offers space, privacy, and a layout designed for today's lifestyle. Featuring 5 bedrooms and 3.5 baths, the home boasts a large living room and formal dining room, along with an open kitchen that seamlessly flows into the family room-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Hardwood floors run throughout the main and upper levels, adding warmth and continuity. The main level also includes a dedicated office with beautiful built-ins, ideal for working from home. A sun-drenched sunroom just off the kitchen leads to a stunning stone patio complete with a built-in fireplace, creating a true backyard retreat surrounded by a private, serene setting. The main level includes a convenient first-floor bedroom with its own full bath-ideal for guests or flexible living arrangements. Upstairs, you'll find four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with an en-suite bath and walk-in closet. The walkout lower level is fully finished and features custom built-ins along with ample storage space. Completing the home is a two-car garage, making this a rare opportunity in a peaceful North Stamford location.
