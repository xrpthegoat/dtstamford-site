About this home

Welcome to Harbor Points Luxury Penthouse Collection. Elevated luxury living with floor-to-ceiling windows offers breathtaking 180-degree views across the Long Island Sound. With 3 beds, 3 bathrooms, and an expansive balcony, this 2,716 sq ft. penthouse space is custom-configured, providing an open-concept layout perfect for entertaining and offering incredible light throughout. Featuring beautifully appointed finishes and appliances including wood plank flooring, full-size stainless steel appliances, gas cooktop, and quartz countertops. Complimented with perfect amenities inspired by the world's leading resorts and spas, you will find a luxury resort-style pool, a state-of-the-art fitness center with yoga, and on-demand fitness services. In addition, your days are made better with a golf simulator, and a chef's kitchen for entertaining you and your guests. Outdoor living is enhanced with multiple fire pits, grills, outdoor dining, lounges, and a pool. Experience waterfront luxury living in the most coveted community of the fastest-growing city in Connecticut. Stamford's Harbor Point sits along the Long Island Sound with luxury marina access and views stretching to NYC. This thriving community features award-winning restaurants, nightlife, dock and dine access, acres of parks, boardwalks, retail, and 200+ curated community events a year. Amenity fees included in monthly rent except utility, trash and pet.
