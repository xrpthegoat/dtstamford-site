About this home

This bright and airy 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath condo is a rare find in one of Norwalk's most desirable locations. Perfect for first-time homebuyers, downsizers, or anyone looking for a relaxed, convenient lifestyle in a thriving community. This opportunity won't last long!
