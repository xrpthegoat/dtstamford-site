About this home

Enjoy this beautiful annual furnished rental steps to Rings End Bridge, Great Island and a short walk to Pear Tree Point Beach. This 5 bedroom colonial sits on a beautifully landscaped acre in one of Darien's most sought-after neighborhoods with water views that makes every day feel special. The sun-filled chef's kitchen with island opens to a spacious family room with a stone fireplace and custom built-ins. The stunning great room with vaulted ceilings and french doors leads to a fabulous flagstone terrace. Ideal for entertaining inside and out. A graciously entry welcomes you to a elegant formal living room with fireplace, cozy dining room and wood paneled office. The primary bedroom has vaulted ceilings, walk-in closet and balcony with sweeping water views. With three additional bedrooms on the second floor and the lower-level fifth bedroom is ideal for an au pair or in-law suite. From sunrise to sunset, this home is filled with natural light and serene coastal views. A year round retreat. Walk to restaurants, shops and beaches.
