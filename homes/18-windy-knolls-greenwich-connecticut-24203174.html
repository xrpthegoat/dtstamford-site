About this home

This spacious and immaculately maintained 4 bedroom home with lovely landscaping and large flat rear yard, is located on a quiet street near train, shops, schools, and parks. Main floor includes newly renovated eat in kitchen with cathedral ceiling, formal dining room, living room with wood burning fireplace and bookcases, family room with vaulted ceiling and skylight and sliders to spacious deck, 2 bedrooms and 2 newly renovated baths. Second floor includes spacious Master suite with separate sitting room/office space plus 4th bedroom with skylight. Ground level has direct access to large, level rear yard and includes exercise space, laundry/mudroom and 1 car garage with electric car charger. New water heater. Large deck off family room has a direct gas line. Hardwood flooring throughout main floors. Plenty of storage space. NO PETS. Owner is responsible for landscaping maintenance. Tenant pays all other expenses.
