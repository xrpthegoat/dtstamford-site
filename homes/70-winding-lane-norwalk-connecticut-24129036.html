About this home

Welcome to 70 Winding Lane, a delightful split-level home nestled in the Cranbury area of Norwalk, CT. This inviting residence features 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, blending modern updates with classic charm to offer comfortable and stylish living. As you explore the home, you'll find the family room situated off the kitchen area. This space boasts a vaulted ceiling and built ins and seamlessly opens to a screened-in porch, providing a perfect setting for relaxation and entertainment. The exterior is equally appealing, with a well-maintained level yard and a charming patio, ideal for outdoor gatherings or enjoying peaceful moments surrounded by nature. Sited on .84 acres offering ample room for outdoor activities and personal enjoyment. Located in a serene neighborhood with easy access to local amenities, shopping, and dining, 70 Winding Lane offers the perfect balance of tranquility and convenience.
