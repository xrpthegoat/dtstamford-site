About this home

Fall in love with the perfect blend of timeless character and modern living in this beautifully updated 1925 home. Warm, inviting and filled with natural light, this is a home that simply feels good from the moment you step inside. Hardwood floors flow throughout, while charming original details are complemented by thoughtful updates. The renovated kitchen with island, breakfast bar, and stainless steel appliances open to a bright family room with fireplace and skylight-an inviting space which is the heart of the home. A gracious living room with fireplace, formal dining room and private den/home office complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite features a beautifully renovated bath, plus two additional bedrooms and full hall bath. A walk-up attic offers easy storage possibilities. Outside may be your favorite part: an oversized deck with both open and covered areas overlooks a beautiful, flat yard filled with cherry blossoms and varieties of roses-perfect for entertaining, gardening or simply enjoying the seasons. Two separate basement areas (one with a workshop) offers versatility. All this in a wonderfully convenient location close to town, shopping and everything Stamford has to offer. A special home with soul, style and modern comfort-truly a must-see.
