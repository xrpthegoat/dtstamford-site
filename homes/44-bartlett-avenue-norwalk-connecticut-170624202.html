About this home

Welcome to 44 Bartlett Ave. in Norwalk, a delightful and inviting home. This residence offers a blend of comfort and charm, starting with a cozy kitchen that features ample cabinetry, modern stainless-steel appliances, and a bright window above the sink that fills the space with natural light and offers a pleasant view of the private back yard. Step into the adjacent living room, where natural wood paneling and a classic fireplace create a warm and welcoming ambiance. Large windows flood the room with sunlight, making it the perfect spot for relaxation or family gatherings. The partially finished lower level offers a great flex space that can be tailored to fit your needs. The large backyard is a true oasis, featuring native flower gardens that not only beautify the space but also attract a variety of birds and butterflies, creating a peaceful retreat right at your doorstep. This home comes equipped with central air conditioning and new energy efficient Anderson windows. Additional features include a chimney that was professionally cleaned and recently capped in 2019, ensuring both safety and longevity. Beyond the home's interior, the property is ideally located close to schools and shops, providing convenient access to local amenities.
