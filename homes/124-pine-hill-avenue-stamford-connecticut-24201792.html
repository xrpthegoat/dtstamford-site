About this home

Welcome to this well-maintained and updated townhouse style 2-family in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood, offering an excellent opportunity for owner-occupants and investors. Both units offer bright, comfortable layouts with hardwood floors throughout the main living areas. Living rooms flow into open dining and kitchen spaces featuring white shaker cabinetry, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances. Central air, washer/dryer in unit and separate utilities add convenience to each residence. Finished lower with 3rd bedroom, home office, recreation room, or flexible space. Outside, enjoy separate entrances, a large level front lawn, and ample off-street parking with a wide driveway and additional rear parking. Ideally located within walking distance to the Glenbrook Metro-North station and the restaurants, cafes, and conveniences along Hope Street, with downtown Stamford, shopping, and major highways just minutes away. A versatile 2-family offering updated living spaces, a convenient location, and strong rental or owner-occupant potential.
