About this home

**PLEASE SUBMIT BEST OFFERS BY MONDAY 11/17 AT 5PM.** Charming Storybook Tudor in Norwalk's sought-after Pine Hill neighborhood - on the market for the first time in over 50 years! Step inside to a bright living room with beamed ceilings, hardwood floors, and a beautiful brick fireplace framed by built-ins. The inviting floor plan flows through arched openings to an elegant dining room and a sunroom filled with natural light. A cozy eat-in kitchen with walk-in pantry and convenient half bath complete the main level. Upstairs offers spacious bedrooms, a full bath and walk-up attic. Hardwood floors underneath carpeting. Outside, relax on the stone patio surrounded by mature trees and lush greenery. Ideally located within walking distance to Shorehaven Country Club, Shore & Country Club, Calf Pasture Beach, Ludlow Park and East Norwalk Metro North station. This timeless home is the perfect blend of character, comfort, and convenience!
