About this home

Thank you to everyone for your interest! Seller's respectfully request all offers by 10/20 Monday by 12 pm. Welcome to 17 Redcoat Road, offering 2,462 sq ft of living space set on a flat, park-like one acre in the highly sought-after Fox Run area of West Norwalk. This four-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath home features a great layout with all bedrooms upstairs, including a primary suite with ensuite bath. Hardwood floors run throughout, adding warmth and character to every room. Inside, walls of windows and an open floor plan create a bright and airy feel. The spacious dining room is anchored by a large window with a view of the incredible backyard. The large kitchen is a true centerpiece, featuring granite counters, abundant workspace, and direct access to the backyard for easy entertaining. Recently finished lower level, offers flexible space for a playroom, office, or media room. The roof was replaced in 2021, offering peace of mind for years to come. Outside, the expansive flat yard, mature plantings, and flagstone patio provide a private retreat perfect for gatherings or quiet enjoyment. A two-car garage and room for expansion complete the package. Too many updates to list - please see the property fact sheet for full details. Don't miss the chance to live on one of West Norwalk's most desirable streets.
