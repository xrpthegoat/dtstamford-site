About this home

Settled at the end of a quiet cul-da-sac on nearly 1.5 level acres in historic Silvermine, this inviting and sunlit four/five-bedroom Colonial offers endless opportunity for indoor/outdoor entertaining with large terrace and a heated pool. Enjoy easy terrace/pool access from the living room, with a WB FP, family room + newly updated chef's kitchen with all new appliances. Desirable floor plan provides great flow showcasing dining room with fireplace open to kitchen, extra height playroom, half bath and another room/office. Generous second floor boasts a primary suite with a new bathroom, three comfortable bedrooms w an updated full hall bath + a sitting room/fourth bedroom. Ideally located very close to Art Guild, Tavern + market with just a short drive to schools, shops and highways.
