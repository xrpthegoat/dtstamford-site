About this home

Set on expansive 1 acre lot in a desirable West Rocks/Cranbury neighborhood, this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2 full bath home offers the perfect blend of space, comfort, and refined living. The main level showcases a welcoming living room with a cozy gas log fireplace, Crown lighting and hardwood floor, a spacious dining room, and a stunning remodeled kitchen featuring granite countertops, center island, an abundance of white cabinetry and top-of-the-line appliances. The main level includes a fully renovated bath with a modern stall shower and a 3-season sunroom with stone flooring and large windows creating the perfect space to enjoy your morning coffee, read a book, or enjoy nature. Upstairs you will discover, four generously sized bedrooms, a beautifully updated full bath with tub and a convenient laundry area. The semi-finished lower level offers space for recreation or use your imagination! Designed for both comfort and efficiency, the home features central AC with newer ductless mini-splits, and the fully paid solar panels transfer to the new owner, delivering long-term energy savings and added value. Outdoors, you will enjoy a beautifully maintained yard, private hot tub and swim spa, gardening areas and open space across the oversized lot. A 2-car attached garage plus a newly constructed 1-car detached garage offer ample storage and versatility This home is convenient to shopping, great restaurants, RT15, I-95 and Metro North making it a commuters dream!
