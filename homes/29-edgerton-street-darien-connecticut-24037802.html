About this home

Meticulously maintained 4 bedroom/2 two bath cape is a 10 minute walk to Noroton Heights train station and shopping. Situated on a beautiful 1/3 acre with a large garden and backyard shed with underground electric wiring. Updated kitchen features, Kashmir granite countertops, Under-mount sink, glass backsplash, and stainless steel appliances. Kitchen opens to a sun filled family room with access to a new deck and gas grill. Upgrades include central AC, new roof, new wood shingles, new gas boiler, new hot water heater, new clothes washer. Hardwood floors throughout. Convenient car port to keep you dry when bringing in groceries. Tenant is responsible for trash, utilities, weekly lawn, snow removal. Spring and fall cleanup will be landlord's responsibility. No pets please. Second refrigerator and gas grill included. Move in date September 18, 2024. Lease must be a minimum to mid June 2025, however, Landlord prefers a longer term lease. One month security; First and last months rent.
