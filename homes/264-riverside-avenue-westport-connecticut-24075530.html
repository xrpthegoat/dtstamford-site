About this home

Beautiful 2 bedrooms 2.5 bath unit at River Edge new development in Westport CT. High ceiling 9ft+ and water views from every window. Launch kayaks/canoes, or enjoy afternoon fishing on Saugatuck river. Located in heart of Westport, walkable to Levitt Pavilion, KHS/SES sport fields, playgrounds and Westport Public library. Across Riverside park, farmers market, Gilbertites, Birchwood country club and shopping. Short walk to MTA train station, Saugatuck Rowing club, Rive bistro, and so much more. Minutes drive from Compo Beach and Longshore, and SONO mall. Open floor plan, Central Air, white kitchen and updated baths with Quartz counters, stainless steel appliances, washer/dryer in unit, wired security system. Westport Schools (Kings Highway elementary, Coleytown Middle, Staples High school).
