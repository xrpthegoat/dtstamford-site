About this home

Shippan Point Gem! Just a short walk to the water, this turnkey home in a coveted waterfront community is filled with natural light, vintage millwork, hardwood floors, and modern updates throughout. With its unique open floor plan, the main level offers a remodeled kitchen, spacious living and family rooms, and large dining area. Second floor features three sizable bedrooms, including primary suite with its own sitting area, walk-in closet and luxe bath. The third floor boasts a fourth spacious bedroom with a private bath. French doors open to an oversized deck with a covered sitting area. A backyard patio and manicured, fully fenced yard are perfect for relaxing or entertaining. With its two-car garage, partially finished lower level, and prime location near beaches, yacht club, train and downtown, this home is coastal living at its best!
