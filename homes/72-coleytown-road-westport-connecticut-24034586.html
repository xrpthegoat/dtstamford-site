About this home

The essence of Connecticut grace is captured perfectly at this prime estate property in one of the most prestigious and highly desired locations. Set amid homes of historic importance, record-setting sale prices & a backdrop of pristine natural beauty, this celebrity enclave is less than half a mile to schools. The cinematic splendor of the area reaches a crescendo as you turn through the stone pillars that mark the long, circular drive that makes a grand entrance to this timeless & distinctive Colonial on over two park-like acres. The classic proportions of this elegant home were retained when it was carefully remodeled & expanded under acclaimed architect Jack Franzen. Special improvements to note: new roof, new automatic pool cover, new generator, new Thermador oven, new interior & exterior paint, new fencing, new gutters, new water heaters (2023-2024). The beautiful front-to-back foyer leads to a sunroom. The LR w/fpl opens to the 1st home office. Grand dining rm ready to host memorable occasions. The gourmet kitchen showcases Christopher Peacock cabinetry & Carrara marble counters, leading to a breakfast rm & comfortable fam rm w/fpl & French doors. A spacious office or potential 5th BR, 2 half baths, lrg mud rm & a laundry rm complete this level. Front & rear stairs lead to the 2nd flr w/all en-suite BRs, incl a grand primary suite w/3rd fpl, dressing rm, lux marble bath by designer Mar Silver. Fin LL rec rm. Exquisite stone terrace leads to htd gunite pool/spa.
