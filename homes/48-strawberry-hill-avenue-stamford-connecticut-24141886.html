About this home

Discover this charming 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse, ideally situated just 1.5 blocks from vibrant downtown Stamford. This bright and spacious home offers three levels of living, including a lower-level flex space and an attached garage-a rare convenience in this location. The newly refreshed eat-in kitchen features modern appliances, freshly painted cabinets, and opens to a private balcony-perfect for morning coffee or summer entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, each with walk-in closets, along with a beautifully remodeled full bathroom. The semi-finished lower level provides the ideal work-from-home space, gym area, or playroom. Additional highlights include in-unit washer/dryer and a brand-new central AC and heating system for year-round comfort. All of this in a prime, commuter-friendly location! Enjoy quick access to restaurants, shopping, parks, medical facilities, I-95, and the Metro-North station-making this home an excellent choice.
