About this home

Discover refined elegance at 12 White Barns Lane, located in the beautiful White Barns community within Norwalk's esteemed Cranbury area. This fully better than new residence which was built by the renowned Hobi Award-winning Able Construction offers an unmatched living experience. 4-5 generously sized bedrooms and 4 full/1 half bathrooms, this home spans 3,804 sq. ft. with an additional 1,340 sq. ft. in the beautifully finished walkout lower level. Each aspect of the residence has been meticulously designed to blend high-end finishes with practical luxury, from the custom millwork to the sleek, modern aesthetics that fill the space. The open concept layout is thoughtfully arranged to enhance daily living and entertaining, ensuring seamless transitions between expansive living areas. Ideally suited for buyers looking for a sophisticated, ready-to-move-in home in a tranquil, luxurious setting while being moments to area amenities and commuting. Entertain outside on your deck overlooking the bucolic pond. This property represents a unique opportunity to live in a beautifully developed community where comfort meets high-end living.
