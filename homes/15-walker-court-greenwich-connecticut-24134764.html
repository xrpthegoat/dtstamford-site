About this home

Welcome to this move-in ready split-level home ideally located in the Glenville neighborhood. Set on a quiet cul-de-sac, this residence offers comfort, convenience, and great flow. The main level features a bright living room and dining area with easy access to the kitchen, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs are three bedrooms and two full baths, including a spacious primary suite. The lower level offers a comfortable family room with a fireplace. The private yard has a relaxing hot tub-ideal for outdoor enjoyment. Additional features include central air, public utilities (water, sewer, gas), an attached garage plus a carport. Convenient to schools, parks, shopping, and major roadways, this home combines suburban tranquility with easy access to everything Greenwich has to offer.
