About this home

Space! Over 2,300 sq/ft of combined finished living space! Nestled at the end of a cul-de-sac in sought-after Wolfpit, this fully updated single-family home offers modern comfort and charm. Freshly painted with hardwood floors throughout, it's move-in ready!
