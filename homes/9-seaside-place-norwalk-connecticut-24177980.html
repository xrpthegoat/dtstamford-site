About this home

Discover the extraordinary potential of this majestic WATERFRONT Queen Anne Victorian residence in one of East Norwalk's most coveted and convenient locations. While currently configured as a 2 family residence, it was originally designed as a grand single family home. This rare property offers the opportunity to restore its historic elegance while enjoying captivating water views from nearly every room. Rich in timeless architectural character, the home showcases an imposing turret, intricate woodwork, parquet floors, stained glass windows, sleeping porches and an array of architectural details throughout. Built in the 1890's with exceptional craftsmanship, the residence was thoughtfully positioned to capture the sweeping unobstructed waterfront vistas and beauty of coastal living. Whether you choose to return this historic gem to its original glory or create a custom waterfront masterpiece in an unbeatable setting, the possibilities are truly exceptional. The property is offered "as is" and the price reflects the renovations needed. Ideally located with easy access to the restaurants, shops and attractions of both East Norwalk and South Norwalk, as well as Calf Pasture Beach.
