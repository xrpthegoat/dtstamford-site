About this home

Welcome to 21 Hill Street, a charming 1925 Colonial offering 1,204 square feet of comfortable living space on a 0.18-acre lot in Norwalk. Featuring a possible 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, this home blends vintage character with a functional multi-level layout. The main level welcomes you with an open-concept flow connecting the living room, dining room, and a spacious kitchen. It is complemented by a convenient main-floor primary bedroom, a full bathroom, and a coat closet near the entrance--great for multi-generational living. Upstairs, you will find three additional bedrooms arranged around a central hallway along with a second full bathroom, providing plenty of private sleeping quarters. The lower level adds fantastic utility space, complete with a dedicated workshop, mechanical utility room, and a laundry area with built-in storage--which currently functions as an office. Step outside to enjoy wonderful outdoor living features including a new Trex deck, a back patio space, a babbling brook and large detached garage on a large lot. This home is located centrally in Norwalk, within a short distance of the highway, SONO, and the Norwalk River Valley Trail which is across the street--not to mention the beaches. Complete with a new driveway, new roof, and an unbeatable Norwalk location, this delightful home is ready for its next chapter!
