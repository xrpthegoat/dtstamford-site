About this home

This spacious three-bedroom, two bath Cape Cod located in the highly desirable Cove section of Stamford offers 2504 square feet of living space and hardwood floor throughout. The first floor includes an updated eat-in kitchen, living room, office, full bath, large dining/family room with deck access, and water views of Holly Pond. Enjoy water views from the second-floor primary bedroom w/ bath, plus two additional bedrooms. The lower level is partially finished with access to the large backyard. Great location close to Cove Island Park, beach, and marina.
