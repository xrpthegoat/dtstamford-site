About this home

Bring your vision and make this cherished Bettswood neighborhood home your own! Offered for the first time in nearly 60 years, this classic 1936 Colonial has been maintained by the same family since 1967 and is ready for it's next chapter. Featuring 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and timeless architectural charm, this home presents an incredible opportunity to renovate, restore, and create a space tailored to your personal style. Whether you're looking to preserve its classic character or reimagine it with modern updates, the possibilities are endless. Outside, enjoy a flat, private backyard with a patio-ideal for relaxing, entertaining, gardening, or outdoor gatherings. An attached one-car garage and driveway adds everyday convenience. Ideally situated in the desirable Bettswood neighborhood, you'll enjoy easy access to the historic Norwalk Green, shopping and dining at The SoNo Collection, the Maritime Aquarium and IMAX Theater, nearby parks, local restaurants, and Metro-North Railroad, providing a convenient commute to New York City. Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a home filled with history, character, and unlimited potential in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations.
