About this home

Location! Location! And opportunity in Westover! 4BR, 2.5BA home set far back from road on flat 1.31 acres. With its great flow & generous room sizes, it has all the space you need at a price point that ensures a sound investment. Classic entry opens to warm & welcoming living room w/wood-burning fireplace. Light-filled dining room flows into spacious EIK (the heart of the home - a home well-loved by same family for 45+ yrs!) w/breakfast nook, granite countertops & above-sink window overlooking a peaceful, treed view. Just a few steps to the family room - a comfortable place to gather, w/direct access to patio & backyard (perfect for playdates), powder / laundry room & garage. The PBR w/full bath ensuite (a refreshing find w/full-size tub & window for natural light) also features a double-closet & hardwood floors. 3 more BRs (yes, 3!), each w/hardwood floors & double-closets plus another full bath complete the upper level. Outside, framed by tall trees & lush landscaping, enhanced by western sun exposures, your incredible backyard oasis affords ultimate privacy & space galore to celebrate life events w/friends & family. Just 1/2mi- 3mi to: schools, playground, tennis, golf, parks, trails, preserves, equestrian & neighborhood/High Ridge/Bulls Head restaurants, shops, grocery & conveniences; less than 1mi to Merritt to Manhattan; just over 4mi to downtown & RR; & only 10mi to HPN. Truly an amazing home & location in which to live, laugh & love. Plan your first gathering today!
