About this home

Beautiful, freshly painted end-unit townhouse with amazing in-town location and lots of light. Located on a desirable street, it is walking distance to Downtown, shopping, restaurants, and close to I-95 and Metro North. The main floor of the home has a spacious living room, dining area, and kitchen with stainless steel appliances. Hardwood floors throughout the main level. Upstairs are two spacious bedrooms serviced by a full hall bath. There's a pull-down attic for storage. Other important features include a washer/dryer in the unit, gas heat, central air, and one reserved parking space. This home's location can't be beat for its close proximity to schools, Metro North, I-95, shopping, Chelsea Piers, Cove Island Park and Beach, Harbor Point and vibrant downtown Stamford! Excellent credit and references, please. Sorry, no pets.
