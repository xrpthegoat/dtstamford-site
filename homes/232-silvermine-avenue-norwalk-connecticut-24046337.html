About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST @ 5PM ON TUESDAY 9/17/2024. The Daniel St. John house is a stunning example of colonial post and beam construction, blending timeless charm with modern comforts. This 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home boasts wideboard floors, exposed beams, and two fireplaces, offering a unique and inviting atmosphere. The heart of the home is the spacious eat-in kitchen, featuring a new Frigidaire Professional Series refrigerator and freezer, a Bosch dishwasher, a Dacor double oven, and a large center island. It also includes a walk-in pantry and generous storage and counter space. The downstairs family room, recently updated with a new full bath in 2024, includes a wet bar and is adorned with hand-hewn beams and Vermont-milled wood. It opens to a terrace and an in-ground pool. The primary suite contains a light-filled bathroom with a custom shower. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping and transportation, the property is also within walking distance of the Tavern at Graybarns and Mercantile, Silvermine Arts Center, Silvermine Market, and Silvermine Elementary School.
