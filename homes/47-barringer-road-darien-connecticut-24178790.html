About this home

Wonderful 4 bedroom house located in one of Darien's sought after Cul-Da-Sac neighborhoods. 1/2 acer of usable yard space for entertaining. Full size kitchen with granite counters, electric cooktop, pantry and double wall ovens. The kitchen has a eat in area that opens to the family room. Cozy up next to the wood burning fireplace on a cold night, or open the slider to the flagstone patio and private back yard. Traditional living and dining rooms. hardwood throughout the first floor. Mudroom off the garage with a full bath. Extra room above the garage can be used as an in-law suit (use of 3rd full bath is separate from rest of the house). Primary with full bath and two walk in closets. 3 bedrooms share the 2nd full bath. The full basement is 3/4 finished for another playroom or rec room. Laundry in the basement with walkout to garage. Two car garage attached. A pet is up for discussion. Credit Check a must, no guarantor signers. First month's rent and two months security at lease signing. Lease and addendum must both be signed. 4 hour notice to show, must remove shoes or wear booties during showings. Walk to Talmadge Hill Train station, 5-7 minutes downtown New Canaan, 10 minutes downtown Darien, 3 minutes to Merritt Parkway. Photos are from previous tenant Owner and Broker are related.
