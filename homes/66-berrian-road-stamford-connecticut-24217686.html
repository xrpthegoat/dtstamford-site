About this home

Location, location, location. Welcome to wonderful Pepper Ridge. Sought after by many buyers for its close proximity to town, shopping, schools, places of worship, even a swim club - you will love living here! This is the perfect opportunity for those with a little vision who love a home with a versatile floor plan. Situated on an attractive corner lot, this property features an open-concept main living level with hardwood floors, a beautiful stone fireplace and a spacious dining room ideal for entertaining. The beautifully renovated kitchen is a standout feature, providing an updated and inviting space at the heart of the home. Two spacious bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level while two more bedrooms and two full baths are located upstairs. A large cedar closet is the perfect area to store off-season items. The oversized laundry room can accommodate two sets of washer/dryer or a possible kitchenette for an in law suite (buyer to do his/her own due diligence). The expansive basement has several rooms just waiting to be turned into your playroom, gym, home office, etc. There is even a full bath (in need of renovation) but plumbing is there.
