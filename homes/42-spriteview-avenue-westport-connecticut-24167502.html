About this home

A rare convergence of water, light and privacy, this extraordinary waterfront home offers a lifestyle few ever experience.Perfectly positioned between the open expanse of the Long Island Sound and the quiet rhythm of a private canal, the home is set on a private island just one hour from New York City, yet feels worlds away. Sweeping, unobstructed views stretch endlessly in every direction, with sunsets that transform the sky into a daily work of art. Defined by its clean, modern lines and expansive walls of glass, the home is designed to fully embrace its surroundings.Three spacious decks extend the living experience outdoors, each offering a distinct vantage point from morning light over the water to the stillness of evening reflections along the canal. Inside, newly installed wide-board white oak floors bring warmth and understated sophistication to a home that is entirely move-in ready. Every space is oriented toward water, creating a seamless connection between interior living and the natural beauty beyond. With direct water frontage, a private beach and an ever-changing coastal backdrop, this is more than a home, it is a singular waterfront retreat defined by light, scale and the quiet luxury of complete privacy.
