About this home

This classic New England center hall colonial in the Tokeneke district is in move in condition, ready for you today. Four bedrooms and two full baths are on the second floor. On the first floor, the extended kitchen and family room invite you to relax with family and friends. The wood burning fireplace will keep you warm and cozy. Tons of natural light brighten the spacious living room. Enjoy the private in-ground pool during the summer months. The two car oversized attached garage includes a generator with direct hookup. Full basement with a workshop and slate pool table. This half acre property also presents the unique option to live in one of the two houses Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate Winslet used to film Revolutionary Road. Darien has much to offer with the new Great Island waterfront town park, Corbin District in town, and Heights Crossing and Darien Commons in Noroton Heights. Darien is the place to be two town beaches, easy commute to the City, and an award winning school system. Don't miss this golden opportunity to live in Darien.
