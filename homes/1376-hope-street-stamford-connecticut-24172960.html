About this home

Best of "Old World" Charm with Modern Lifestyle. Located in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood, 1376 Hope Street blends timeless New England charm with modern living. Originally rooted in early 1900s craftsmanship and completely remodeled in 2011, this beautifully reimagined colonial offers over 5,000 sq ft of flexible living space on more than half an acre of private grounds. Features include 9 ft ceilings, rich hardwood floors, welcoming foyer, gracious living room with oversized open hearth fireplace and original antique bar, spacious eat in kitchen with island opening to private deck and serene landscaped views, formal dining room with French doors, and four generous bedrooms including a vaulted primary suite with double sink bath. Walk up attic with high ceilings offers approximately 1,000 additional sq ft, already insulated with electricity in place, ideal for office, studio, playroom or future finished space. Lower level offers another approximately 1,000 sq ft with separate walk out, perfect for apartment, au pair or recreation room. Rare early 1900s stone foundation, full perimeter dog fence, immaculate two car garage, tremendous storage and peaceful natural surroundings complete the property. Convenient midtown location close to Merritt Parkway, Metro North, downtown Stamford, beaches, golf, tennis, shopping, dining and both Springdale and Darien train stations. A rare opportunity!
