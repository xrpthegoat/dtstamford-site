About this home

Located on a very desirable+convenient Darien street, 12 Chester Road is a bright and treasured home, ready for its next owner. This 4 bedroom/2.5 bath home has been beautifully renovated, including a new kitchen and baths, exterior painting and resurfaced driveway--the main projects have been done! Spacious en-suite primary bedroom has high ceilings+balcony. 3 add'l bedrooms, one bath and access to the walk-up attic complete the 2nd floor. For outdoor living, there is a large deck overlooking the private yard, terrace and gardens. Ideal location close to schools, shopping, dining and train.
