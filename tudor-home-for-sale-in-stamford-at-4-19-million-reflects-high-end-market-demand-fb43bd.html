Tudor Home for Sale in Stamford at $4.19 Million Reflects High-End Market Demand

A $4.19 million Tudor home in Stamford underscores the city’s strong demand for architecturally distinctive, historically significant properties.

Published · August 8, 2026 at 8:09 AM ET

A Tudor-style home in Stamford, Connecticut, is now listed for sale at $4.19 million, highlighting the continued presence of luxury properties in the city’s residential market. For local buyers and renters, this listing underscores the premium pricing and architectural distinctiveness available in certain parts of Stamford, particularly in neighborhoods with historic character and established zoning protections.

What's happening

The property, located in Stamford, is described as a Tudor home featuring timeless architecture and luxurious amenities. It is listed for $4.19 million as of August 2026. While the specific address is not provided in the sources, the listing is consistent with Stamford’s broader zoning framework, which regulates land use, building height, density, and open space requirements. The city’s Zoning Board of Appeals and Planning Board oversee development and permit processes, including site plan reviews and special permits for certain uses. The Zoning Regulations, last updated through February 28, 2026, include a range of district types—from residential (R) to commercial (C) and mixed-use (MX)—each with specific standards for building size, lot coverage, and use. Zoning and Development Context

The City of Stamford’s Zoning Regulations emphasize the protection of public health, safety, and welfare through careful land-use planning. These regulations aim to promote adequate light and air, prevent overcrowding, support transportation infrastructure, and preserve environmental and cultural resources. The city’s zoning system includes both general and special districts, such as the R-D (Designed Residential Districts) and ARD (Architectural Review Design District), which are intended to maintain aesthetic and design integrity in specific neighborhoods. While the sources do not confirm whether this Tudor home is in such a district, the fact that it is being marketed as a property with “timeless architecture” suggests it may be subject to stricter design standards or require special approvals for alterations. The Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals are responsible for reviewing applications and appeals related to development, including variances and special permits.

Common questions

What makes Tudor homes valuable in Stamford’s real estate market?

Tudor homes in Stamford command high prices due to their unique architecture, historical character, and limited availability. Zoning restrictions and preservation standards further enhance their rarity and value.

Why are new homes scarce in Stamford despite demand?

Stamford’s zoning regulations prioritize light, air, open space, and design integrity, limiting density and new construction. These rules reduce supply, driving up prices and making existing homes with character increasingly valuable.
