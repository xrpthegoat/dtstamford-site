About this home

Experience true pride of ownership in this beautifully maintained Cape located in the highly sought after Cove neighborhood of Stamford. Just steps from Cove Beach and Cove Island Park, this charming 3 bedroom, 1 bath home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience and coastal living. Inside, you'll find a warm and inviting layout ideal for everyday living. The home features central air, newer mechanicals, and modern "Brilliant" touchscreen switches, adding both efficiency and smart home functionality. Enjoy outdoor living on the newer Azek deck, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Additional highlights include an aromatic cedar custom built closet in the primary bedroom, one-car attached garage, hot tub and meticulous upkeep throughout, making this home truly move in ready. Ideally situated just about 45 minutes from New York City, this home offers an easy commute while allowing you to enjoy the charm of coastal suburban living. With close proximity to parks, beaches, shopping and dining, this is a rare opportunity to own in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Schedule your private showing today.
