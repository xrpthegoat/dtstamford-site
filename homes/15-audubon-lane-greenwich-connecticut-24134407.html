About this home

Welcome to 15 Audubon Lane, a modern sanctuary set on 4.2 serene, private acres in one of Greenwich's most coveted backcountry locations. Quietly nestled at the end of a cul-de-sac and surrounded by mature woodlands and premier estates, this architectural retreat offers a rare blend of tranquility, exclusivity, and turnkey luxury. Redesigned by Stuart Shanks and Frank Dong and fully renovated to the highest standards, the residence features a new standing-seam metal roof and all-new plumbing, electrical, and HVAC systems. Inside, refined living unfolds with white oak select flooring, custom cabinetry and closets, and a gourmet kitchen featuring Wolf and Fisher & Paykel appliances set against sleek granite countertops. A new TimberTech deck creates the ideal outdoor setting for entertaining or unwinding in nature. Located in one of Greenwich's most tranquil and private backcountry settings-just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, Glenville, and Westchester County amenities-this is Greenwich backcountry living at its finest.
