About this home

Welcome to 154 Holmes Avenue! Only the second owner since 1920 this charming colonial is all about blending classic vibes with modern comfort. With 5 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and 3108 square feet, there's plenty of space for everyone to spread out and relax. Step inside to the inviting foyer which opens up to an oversized living room with fireplace, formal dining room and the gorgeous freshly painted white kitchen that's filled with light and flows perfectly into the rest of the house. The first floor's awesome 9-foot ceilings make everything feel even more spacious. Imagine cozying up by the fireplace on a chilly evening or hosting friends in a space designed for good times. Four bedrooms with two baths on the second floor and a finished third floor has you covered with a play room and an extra bedroom and bath-ideal for guests or a home office. Outside, the flat yard is a dream come true, beautifully landscaped and just waiting for backyard BBQs or a game of catch. There's even room to expand if you've got big plans. With a two car garage, professionally landscaped yard and a charming porch and blue stone patio this place ticks all the boxes for easy, breezy living. It's the perfect spot to kick back and enjoy life. Come see it for yourself!
