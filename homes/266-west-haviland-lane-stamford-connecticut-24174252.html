About this home

Great North Stamford rental set beside a peaceful bird sanctuary. Start your mornings in the sunroom with calming natural views. A grand two-story foyer opens to a 4,500 sq ft home designed for comfort and entertaining. Bright, open spaces include a family room, living room, sitting area, and dining room. The chef's kitchen offers granite countertops, center island, Sub-Zero refrigeration, Thermador range, dual Miele dishwashers, and propane cooking. The main level includes a flexible playroom with workstations and ping pong table, two home offices, and a guest en-suite bedroom. The second floor features 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 baths, including a primary suite with spa bath, soaking tub, and separate shower. Outdoor living includes a large patio with outdoor kitchen, fire pit, and heated porch for year-round use. The lower level offers a full fitness area with weights, machines, kettlebells, treadmill, TV, and Peloton bike. The kitchen table and 4 TVs are also included. Conveniently located near downtown Stamford dining, shopping, entertainment, and nearby beaches.
