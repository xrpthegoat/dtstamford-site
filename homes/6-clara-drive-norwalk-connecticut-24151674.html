About this home

Tucked along a quiet cul-de-sac street, 6 Clara Drive offers a setting that feels welcoming, neighborly, and easy to call home. This well-maintained single-family residence has been lovingly cared for over the years, and that pride of ownership is evident throughout. The main level features a full living room filled with natural light, a peaceful den ideal for movie nights or quiet evenings in, and a full-size dining room designed for gatherings both big and small. The functional kitchen opens to a screened sunroom, creating a comfortable space to enjoy morning coffee, warm afternoons, and the changing seasons. A full bath on the main level adds everyday convenience. Upstairs, three comfortable bedrooms and an additional full bath provide a practical and well-balanced layout. An under-house garage and a large basement offer valuable storage and flexible space for hobbies, projects, or future use. Outside, a manageable yard keeps upkeep simple, while the cul-de-sac street encourages a calm atmosphere and a true sense of community. Ideally located near Stew Leonard's, the shops and services along Westport Avenue, and major commuting routes, this home delivers both neighborhood charm and everyday accessibility.
