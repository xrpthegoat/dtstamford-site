About this home

Step inside 12 Thomes in Rowayton, this 4 bedroom home is one side of a two-family home on a very desirable, quiet street in Rowayton. Minutes from downtown, metro-north train and the beach, live the quintessential Rowayton lifestyle in this charming abode. Upon entry into 12 Thomes, you are met with a spacious living room, continue down the hallway that leads to a bedroom or office, full bath and an eat-in kitchen and dining room situated at the back of the home. Off the dining room are doors to a huge outdoor deck that connects to a turf putting green! The ultimate summer playground that makes this house so special and unique!! The second floor includes a primary bedroom with large closets, 2 additional bedrooms and a full bath with laundry. Available for a yearly rental starting January 10, 2027.
