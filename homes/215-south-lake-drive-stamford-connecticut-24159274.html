About this home

Lakefront living! This elegant home features 450" ft of private, pristine lakefront in a coveted area of North Stamford CT! 1 hour from NYC by train, minutes from the vibrant city life of Stamford CT, this custom built, sunlit home has been exquisitely re-imagined in a sophisticated, modern style. The grand entrance opens to a foyer with marble floors, winding staircase, and panoramic lake views! Open floor plan with gallery walls are replete with vibrant paintings and sophisticated photographs. Arching oversized windows draw the eye to the lake front, saturating the interior with natural light. Main level includes a music room/study with a wood burning fireplace, elegant dining room, an oversized 2 story living room with a Onyx surround fireplace, updated kitchen with chef's appliances, primary bedroom with a custom marble mosaic fireplace, private balcony, 2 walk-in closets and a luxurious ensuite bath. Deck with steel cord-Ipe wood railings provides indoor-outdoor entertaining space. The 2nd floor has 3 ensuite bedrooms, (2 currently used for art studios) plus a generous recreation room/office/playroom. Walk-out lower level has a potential in-law/office suite with a separate entrance and kitchenette, a gym area, temperature controlled wine closet, and multiple closets/storage areas. Full house generator. Radon system. Bosch, Wolf, Subzero appliances. Approx 4200 sq ft on main and upper levels-another 3000 sq ft in lower walkout level.
