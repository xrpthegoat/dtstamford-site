About this home

Move right into this stunning, fully renovated 2024 ranch-style home, nestled on 4 picturesque acres on a quiet North Stamford cul-de-sac. This exceptional property offers a bright, open floor plan with a brand-new gourmet kitchen, featuring a spacious marble island and top-tier appliances. The adjoining family room, complete with a cozy fireplace, and the dining room open to a gorgeous patio and garden space, perfect for entertaining or enjoying serene sunsets. The home includes a stylish living room with a wet bar, a solarium which allows you to appreciate the outdoors year-round, a luxurious primary suite with a spa-like bathroom, walk-in closet, and a private outdoor patio. There are three additional bedrooms (one en-suite bath and one hall bath), and a private office. Practical features include a separate laundry room, attached garage and convenient mudroom. Available for rent beginning January 1, 2025 furnished or unfurnished.
