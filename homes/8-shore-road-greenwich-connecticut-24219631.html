About this home

This rare find in Old Greenwich sits on .4 of an acre and abuts tidal Tomac Creek. Creek views abound and a kayak or small boat can travel out to the Sound at high tide. The land is in an R 12 zone and has tidal wetlands that have been marked with pink flags. Because the land is in a flood zone, any additional construction will have to be elevated to comply with flood zone requirements. D'Andrea has done an extensive study of the property that is available to Buyers, builders and Agents to review. The study shows the building envelope with which an approximately 5300 square foot new home can be built. Or, if a buyer wishes to retain the current 1880 home, they can apply for historic status and build out the back of the existing house into the building envelope and possibly add even more buildable square footage. This is a rare piece of tidal waterfront land where the guesswork has been taken out by the extensive research done in the study.
