About this home

Completely rebuilt from the frame in 2015, this beautifully updated home features modern efficiency, quality craftsmanship, and year-round views of Scalzi Park. Features include energy-efficient windows and doors, high-efficiency gas heat and central air, tankless water heater, wide-plank hardwood floors, custom built-ins, and an eat-in kitchen with premium appliances and ample storage. Marble bathrooms include a soaking tub, separate shower, and double vanity. French doors open to a spacious new deck overlooking a fully fenced yard and a gated driveway. Located in historic Hubbard Heights across from Scalzi Park and close to downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, major highways, and the train station.
