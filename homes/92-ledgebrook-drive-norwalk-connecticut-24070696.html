About this home

Fully furnished and beautifully remodeled 2 bedroom 1 bath condominium unit available for short term rental **ALL UTILITIES - ELECTRIC, HEAT, HOT WATER, INTERNET, WIFI INCLUDED** As you step inside the charming entryway with wide plank floors and decorative wainscoting, you immediately feel the sense of warmth and comfort that this delightful home exudes. The adjacent living room provides the perfect setting for relaxation and tranquility with its grand beehive fireplace and large picture windows that offer beautiful views of the wooded backdrop and natural rock garden. The spacious primary bedroom with hardwood floors and large closet shares the same scenic views of the outside space and provides the comfort of a king size bed. The recently remodeled kitchen with stainless steal appliances and granite countertops is fully equipped with today's conveniences, along with the second bedroom which is currently functioning as both an office and bedroom (full sized bed provided). The full bath is tastefully updated with its contemporary design and heated flooring and is located conveniently to the in-unit full size washer and dryer. The private, screened in-porch allows shaded enjoyment of the outside in the warmer months and provides an easy, second access to the exterior of the property. Amenities of the Ledgebrook Condominium complex include pool, clubhouse and fitness room. Conveniently located to shopping, train, and major highways.
