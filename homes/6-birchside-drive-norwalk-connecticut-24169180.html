About this home

Tucked away in a quiet Norwalk setting, this bright and inviting apartment blends comfort with thoughtful updates and an easy, livable layout. Step into a sun filled living room where vaulted ceilings and warm hardwood floors create an open, airy feel that instantly feels like home. The flow continues into a spacious eat in kitchen, complete with tile flooring, ample cabinetry, gas range, dishwasher, and all the essentials for everyday living and entertaining. Just beyond, a dedicated dining area offers the perfect spot for casual meals or hosting guests. Both bedrooms are nicely sized with hardwood flooring and great natural light, while the full bath features clean finishes and a whirlpool tub with shower. Central air keeps things comfortable year round, and the convenience of in unit washer and dryer adds to the ease of living. Off street parking is included, along with a peaceful back patio and yard, and tucked away setting that still keeps you close to shopping, dining, and major routes. Available for immediate occupancy, this well maintained rental offers a balanced mix of space, function, and location. Agent/Owner
