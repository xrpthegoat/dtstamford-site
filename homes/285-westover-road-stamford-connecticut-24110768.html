About this home

Location! Welcome to an exquisite sanctuary nestled in the coveted Westover area. This stunning residence promises an elegant lifestyle with its modern touches and serene surroundings. Boasting 4 bedrooms/3.5 bathrooms this property offers a harmonious blend of sophistication and comfort. Upon entering, you'll be captivated by the chef's dream kitchen, featuring high-end appliances including a Viking gas stove, Sub-Zero refrigerator, dual wall ovens, and a substantial kitchen island, perfect for culinary creations and gathering w/ friends. The open flow leads seamlessly to spacious living room adorned w/ a cozy fireplace, creating an inviting ambiance for entertaining or relaxing. The main level also showcases a formal dining room and a sunroom with large windows, inviting an abundance of natural light and offering picturesque views of the tranquil outdoors. This floor accommodates a convenient bedroom and full bathroom, a harmonious blend of privacy and practicality.
