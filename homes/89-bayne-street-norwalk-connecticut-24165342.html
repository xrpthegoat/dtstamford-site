About this home

Welcome to this lovingly maintained 4-bedroom home in the highly desirable Cranbury section of Norwalk-proudly built and cherished by its original owner since 1960. Filled with warmth, character, and decades of treasured memories, this home is ready for its next chapter. At the heart of the home is a stunning living room featuring a gorgeous stone fireplace-perfect for cozy evenings and holiday gatherings. One can easily imagine Christmas carols being sung around the baby grand piano, creating the kind of memories that make a house truly feel like home. The layout offers wonderful flow for both everyday living and entertaining, with the living room, dining room, kitchen, and a delightful sunroom seamlessly connected. The sunroom provides a bright and inviting space to relax and enjoy views of the outdoors throughout the seasons. Four well-proportioned bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. A huge unfinished basement presents endless possibilities-whether you envision a home gym, playroom, workshop, or additional living space, the potential is yours to explore. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping, major highways, and public transportation, this home combines timeless charm with everyday practicality. Recent updates include a newer roof, adding peace of mind for years to come. Set in an established neighborhood known for its accessibility and appeal, this is a rare opportunity to own a home filled with history, heart, and endless potential.
