About this home

GLENBROOK INVESTORS, TAKE NOTICE! This fully occupied, two-family home is primed for its next owner! Currently rented at market-value rates, this four-story property delivers strong cash flow now, with incredible potential for future growth. Both units are beautifully renovated 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartments featuring spacious layouts that live much larger. Each includes an additional den/office, gleaming hardwood floors, and abundant natural light. The first-floor unit offers a fantastic lower-level space with a walk-out and extra flexible areas-perfect for storage or expansion. Meanwhile, the second-floor unit boasts a walk-up attic/loft, adding even more possibilities. Outside, inviting shared spaces include a deck, stone patio, and a large, level greenspace. Additional perks include two electric meters, separate laundry hookups, an oversized driveway, and a detached two-car garage with its own walk-up loft storage. Tucked away on a quiet street just three minutes from the Glenbrook train station, this property offers tenants easy access to Stamford's fine dining, nightlife, shopping, parks, and vibrant culture. This is the investment opportunity you've been waiting for!
