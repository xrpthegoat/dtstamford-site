About this home

Welcome to 40 Cascade Road, a beautifully appointed and expansive home offering timeless elegance, modern amenities, and a layout designed for luxurious living and effortless entertaining. As you enter through the grand two-story foyer, you're greeted by gleaming hardwood floors and an abundance of natural light that flows throughout. The spacious living room, featuring a gas-burning fireplace, invites intimate gatherings, while French doors lead to a dedicated private office. The gourmet kitchen is a chef's delight, boasting a large center island, top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, a walk-in pantry, and a serene breakfast nook overlooking the backyard. The adjoining formal dining room makes entertaining seamless. Unwind in the spacious family room with a wood-burning fireplace and sliding doors that open to a private backyard oasis, featuring a two-tier deck, patio, and firepit-perfect for summer evenings. Retreat to the luxurious primary suite, complete with its own gas fireplace, expansive spa-like bathroom, and generous walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms, two bathrooms, a large media room, and a top-floor space offer endless versatility. Thoughtfully designed for flexibility, this home also features a main-level private bedroom with a dedicated full bath, a spacious lower level with endless possibilities, and a convenient three-car garage. Ideally located near shopping, dining, and just minutes from the Merritt Parkway.
