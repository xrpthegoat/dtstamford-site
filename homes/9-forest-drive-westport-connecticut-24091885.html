About this home

What more could you ask for - this gorgeous, spacious home is located at the end of a cul-de-sac in a friendly & fabulous neighborhood. Completely remodeled, renovated and expanded in 2017. The beautiful, flat 1.14 +/- acre property with room for a pool & cabana is complete with an expansive patio, firepit and enclosed organic garden for outdoor enjoyment. This home is conveniently located within walking distance to Long Lots Elementary plus Bedford Middle & Staples High Schools, local shopping and dining, tennis, swimming, and horse-back riding at the Hunt Club. The open floor plan includes a huge marble kitchen island, walk-in pantry, mudroom, 2 offices, sought-after 5th bedroom w/full bath on the first level, dining room, family room w/fireplace off the kitchen, formal living room, and wine room - all on the main floor. The double-height foyer leads upstairs, where you will find a study/ loft area with built-ins, 4 additional bedrooms with 3 full baths. The Primary Suite is a dream w/radiant heated floors in the luxurious marble bath for a spa-like peaceful setting. This home is in excellent, move-in condition and will exceed your expectations.
