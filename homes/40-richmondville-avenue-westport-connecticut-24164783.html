About this home

Built in 2022, 40 Richmondville Avenue offers generous scale, a thoughtful layout, and true move-in-ready ease. Bright, open-concept main level connects kitchen, dining, and living spaces, with high ceilings and oversized windows throughout. The kitchen features a full Thermador appliance suite, large island with integrated outlets, extensive cabinetry, walk-in pantry, and mudroom with built-ins. Upstairs includes a spacious primary suite with walk-in closet and spa-like bath with heated floors, soaking tub, and oversized shower. Two bedrooms are connected by a Jack and Jill bath, each with lofted spaces for added flexibility. Additional bedrooms offer options for guests, office, or evolving needs. Third floor provides a private, multi-purpose level, while the finished lower level includes polished concrete floors and a bar/kitchenette area for casual entertaining. Six bedrooms, five-and-a-half baths, and four finished levels offer exceptional flexibility. Located just minutes from downtown Westport, with easy access to shopping, dining, and beaches, in an area experiencing continued growth.
