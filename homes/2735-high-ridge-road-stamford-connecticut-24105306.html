About this home

Classic 4BR, 2.5BA young Colonial in desirable North Stamford! Set on a private, wooded lot, this well-maintained home features a formal living room, dining room, and an updated eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The spacious main level also includes a large family room and a convenient half bath. Upstairs offers a fully renovated primary suite with large walk in closets and en suite bath. Upstairs also features three additonal large bedrooms and a fully renovated bathroom and laundry room. Finished lower level includes a rec room, cigar and wine room, and plenty of storage. Enjoy outdoor living with a deck and tree-lined yard. Attached two car garage, ample parking, and easy access to schools, shopping, and commuter routes.
