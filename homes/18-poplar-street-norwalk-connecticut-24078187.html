About this home

Situated on a picturesque street adjacent to Shorehaven Golf Course, this classic Cape Cod-style home blends timeless character with modern updates. With undeniable curb appeal, this four-bedroom, two-bathroom home features exquisite moldings, arched doorways, built-ins, French doors, and hardwood floors throughout. The sunlit living room with a cozy gas fireplace, leading into the beautifully updated kitchen. The kitchen features sleek granite countertops, white cabinetry and Bosch stainless steel appliances with ample counter space and storage. The dining area, accented with elegant shaker wainscoting, seamlessly opens into the kitchen, creating a natural flow between spaces. The main floor includes a comfortable bedroom and an updated full bathroom featuring radiant heated floors. Upstairs, three additional bedrooms offer natural light and generous closet space, along with another well-appointed full bathroom. The finished basement provides flexible living space, ideal for a home office, media room, or playroom, with ample storage. This home is also generator-ready. Outside, enjoy the new bluestone patio along with a pea gravel area, creating a serene outdoor retreat. Plus the added convenience of a one car detached garage. Located just a 5 minute walk to the train station and a short bike or car ride to the beach, this turn-key home offers easy access to the vibrant SoNo district, known for its many shops, restaurants, and entertainment options. A fantastic opportunity!
