About this home

This charming home is situated within the Downtown North neighborhood. This Westport home sits on a 0.29-acre lot and 2,241 square feet of living space. This well-loved, beautifully maintained home will steal your heart. The spacious rooms boast lovely windows and gleaming wood floors. Custom-built cabinets and bookshelves add charm throughout. A wonderful deck overlooks the flat, private property, perfect for enjoying the tranquil surroundings. The home features attractive accents on the exterior while maximizing space and inviting light and bright natural elements inside, with a primary bedroom on the upper floor. This colonial home is in pristine condition. The standout feature is the large, updated kitchen, which boasts ample counter space and modern appliances, making meal preparation effortless. Conveniently located within walking distance of Main Street and close to schools, Compo Beach, and the train station. Enjoy easy access to the vibrant downtown scene, featuring amazing shopping and dining experiences in downtown Westport, as well as proximity to the YMCA and Merritt Parkway, making this an ideal location. Don't miss your chance to make this wonderful home your own!
