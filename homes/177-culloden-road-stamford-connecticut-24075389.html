About this home

Welcome to this terrific colonial home in a very convenient location! A classic front entry porch leads to the bright living room with vintage trim and moldings. There is a nice dining room as well and a generously sized kitchen with access to the covered back porch, perfect for outdoor dining. On the second floor, there are three bedrooms with hardwood floors, as well as a full bath. A large attic is great for storage. There is an additional full bathroom in the basement, as well as more room for storage. A flat back yard and tool shed completes the picture! A great place to call home!
