About this home

Set on over an acre along a peaceful river in desirable West Norwalk, this inviting 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home offers privacy, style, and convenience. Located on a leafy street close to commuter routes, the property combines a serene natural setting with easy access to everyday amenities. A circular driveway provides an elegant approach, while the spacious interior features three fireplaces and generous living areas ideal for both relaxing and entertaining. The flexible floor plan includes a finished lower level with potential for an in-law suite, guest space, home office, or recreation area. Enjoy the beauty of the riverfront setting, expansive grounds, and the opportunity to make this well-located home your own.
