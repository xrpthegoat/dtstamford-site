About this home

Located in a desirable neighborhood in Old Greenwich, this contemporary home offers a spacious and thoughtfully designed floor plan with five bedrooms and three full bathrooms. The second floor features four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary bedroom, along with two full bathrooms and ample closet space throughout. Thoughtfully designed with well-proportioned living spaces and a functional layout, this home offers comfort, versatility, and an exceptional location.
