About this home

Welcome to 18 Fenway! This delightful home features 3 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, complete with stylish modern finishes. Step inside to an inviting open floor plan that exudes warmth and comfort. The cozy living room, highlighted by a fireplace and beautiful hardwood floors, flows seamlessly into the formal dining area. From there, step onto the spacious hardwood deck-ideal for entertaining guests. Upstairs, the master suite offers a luxurious retreat with double sinks and a stand-up shower. Additional highlights of this home include a newly installed central air system and a fully finished basement with an extra bathroom and wet-bar, perfect for extended family or guests. The kitchen is well-equipped with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a spacious pantry for ample storage. For added versatility, the walk-up attic provides extra space, perfect for a home office or playroom. The detached garage is fully finished and insulated, currently being used as an entertainment studio. Situated in a prime location, this property offers both convenience and comfort. Having operated as a successful short-term rental for the past 3 years, it presents a fantastic turnkey investment opportunity. Don't miss out on making this beautiful home yours!
