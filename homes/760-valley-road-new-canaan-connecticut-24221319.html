About this home

A Historic Masterpiece, Reimagined for Modern Living Originally crafted in 1931 by renowned architect Frazier Forman Peters, this iconic stone residence has been completely reinvented-expanded, redesigned, and redefined for today's luxury lifestyle. The newly completed Architectural plans now offers a home with over 7450 square feet of exquisite living space with 6 bedrooms, 7.5 baths, and every modern comfort imaginable, all while preserving the timeless character of its hand-cut fieldstone construction. Inside, soaring ceilings, a dramatic double staircase, and elegant living and dining spaces create a perfect blend of grandeur and warmth. Five fireplaces anchor inviting gathering areas, while French doors lead to light-filled suites and sweeping terraces overlooking the serene landscape. From its architectural heritage to its meticulous, top-to-bottom reinvention, this residence stands as a true work of art-a rare fusion of history, craftsmanship, and modern sophistication-just minutes from downtown New Canaan.
