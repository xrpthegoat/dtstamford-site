About this home

Beautifully renovated in spring 2025, this bright and spacious one bedroom unit offers a blend of modern finishes and peaceful surroundings in a quiet, well maintained complex. The home features all new appliances with two year warranties, new flooring, updated lighting, bathroom fixtures, fresh paint, and a brand new washer and dryer. The kitchen includes granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and plenty of cabinet space. The living area opens directly to a private slate and marble patio, surrounded by mature trees and landscaping, offering a true sense of privacy and calm. A fireplace adds warmth and charm to the living space, and the oversized windows bring in natural light throughout the day. The bedroom is generously sized and offers ample closet space. This unit also includes a one car garage with additional reserved parking. Conveniently located just minutes from I95, shops, restaurants, grocery stores, and pharmacies. The South Norwalk train station is just a short drive away, with express service to Grand Central in about 60 minutes. Low HOA and thoughtful renovations make this a truly move in ready home and a smart opportunity in today's market.
