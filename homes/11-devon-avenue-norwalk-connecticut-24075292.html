About this home

Nestled in Norwalk's charming Spring Hill neighborhood, this classic Cape Cod offers warmth, functionality, and space to grow. With over 1,000 sq ft, the main level features two bedrooms and a beautifully renovated full bath, plus a cozy living room complete with fireplace. There is a dedicated dining room that opens into the newly renovated kitchen with Butcher block countertops and newly uncovered original hardwood floors--with arches through each room, and classic early-1900's trim and mouldings. From there, step out onto the enclosed porch-a perfect spot for coffee or quiet evenings. Upstairs, a third bedroom with recovered and newly finished hardwood floors and a newly renovated half bath await, along with attic space brimming with potential for a future bedroom, office, or playroom--400+ square feet ready to be finished. The walk-out basement includes garage access and even more finishable space to make your own. Enjoy privacy and room to relax in the new, fully fenced backyard, and take full advantage of the quiet streets just minutes from Norwalk's shops including Angelo's Specialties, restaurants such as Uncle Joe's and Blue Cactus Grill, and commuter options including I95 and the Merritt Parkway. Don't miss the chance to make a smart move into a home with classic bones and room to grow.
