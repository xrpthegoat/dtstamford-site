About this home

Welcome to easy living in the heart of Riverside. This charming three-bedroom ranch offers comfortable one-level living in one of Greenwich's most desirable communities, surrounded by mature trees, neighborhood footpaths, parks, and the waterfront-all just moments from your door. Inside, the home features a bright and inviting layout with three bedrooms on the main level, plus a finished lower level offering valuable additional space for a family room, home office, gym, or playroom. All-new mechanicals provide added comfort and peace of mind. Outside, enjoy a flat, fully fenced backyard and an oversized deck with plenty of room for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply relaxing. The location makes everyday life exceptionally convenient, with restaurants, parks, the water, and local amenities close by. Metro-North and I-95 are also just minutes away, making commuting to New York City easy. Move right in and enjoy everything the Riverside lifestyle has to offer.
