About this home

Bring your vision to this waterfront home in Shorefront Park, a private enclave along Norwalk Harbor. With a little imagination, this property can be transformed into a coastal retreat that reflects your style. The home needs TLC and renovation, but the setting makes the opportunity truly special. In addition to the main living areas, the home features a versatile flex space-ideal for an in-law setup, private office, studio, or guest suite-offering even more potential as you reimagine the property. Positioned on a corner lot, the home provides direct water access for boating, fishing, and kayaking, along with the possibility to expand, subject to necessary approvals. Shorefront Park residents enjoy two private waterfront parks, beach access, and a shared boat and kayak launch. Seasonal neighborhood gatherings and low annual association dues add to the community's charm. Start your mornings paddling on Long Island Sound, relaxing by the shore, or taking in peaceful harbor views. Nearby South Norwalk (SONO) offers restaurants, shops, a boat club, and Water Street marinas, and convenient Metro North service to New York City provide even more to explore.
