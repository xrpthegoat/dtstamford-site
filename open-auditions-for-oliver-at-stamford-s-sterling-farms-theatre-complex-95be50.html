Open Auditions for *Oliver!* at Stamford’s Sterling Farms Theatre Complex

Stamford residents of all ages can audition for the 2026 production of Oliver! at the Sterling Farms Theatre Complex.

Uploaded · August 16, 2026 at 4:37 PM ET

What's happening Curtain Call Inc.

What's happening

Curtain Call Inc. is holding open auditions for *Oliver!*, a large ensemble musical production, at the Kweskin Theatre located within the Sterling Farms Theatre Complex at 1349 Newfield Avenue, Stamford, CT 06905. Auditions are scheduled as follows:

  • Youth performers are welcome at the open auditions on Monday, August 17, and Tuesday, August 18, 2026, from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.
  • General auditions for adult and older youth performers will be held on Monday, August 17, and Tuesday, August 18, 2026, from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. each night

No appointments are required — participants are welcome to show up and be seen in the order they arrive. Those auditioning for a musical are asked to prepare a brief song in the style of the show, bring sheet music in the correct key, and wear appropriate footwear for dancing. For non-musicals, auditioners will be given sides from the script.

Rehearsals are set to begin on Monday, August 31, 2026, and will run Monday through Thursday from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. with possible occasional Sunday rehearsals.

The production is scheduled to run from October 23, 2026, through November 14, 2026, with performances on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday evenings at 7:30 p.m., Sunday afternoons at 2:00 p.m., and two Saturday matinees on November 7 and 14. Tech week begins on Saturday, October 17, with a sitzprobe likely scheduled from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

The full cast includes roles such as Fagin, Nancy, Bill Sikes, and Mr. Brownlow, among others. The production is directed by Nancy L. Meyer, with music direction by Neil Flores and choreography by Jenifer Silverman.
