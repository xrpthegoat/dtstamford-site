About this home

Picture perfect colonial that is immaculate. Spacious living room with a fireplace and a picture window. Cozy sunroom/family room with sliders to the deck, gazebo and in-ground pool. Bright white kitchen with stainless steel appliances with a picture window over looking the deck with an awning/ in-ground pool and a private back yard. The back door off the kitchen leads right to the deck. There are two bedrooms and a full bath on the main floor. The 2nd floor was added 10 years ago and has a master bedroom with a sitting area and 2 closets as well as another bedroom, updated bath with double sinks and a TV room. A basement, central air a one car garage complete this fine home along with gleaming hardwood floors through out. Conveniently location close to shops, schools and restaurants.
