About this home

One of the largest residences at Putnam Hill, this impeccably renovated 3-bedroom, 2-bath apartment is set on a tranquil 10-acre, park-like campus in the heart of downtown Greenwich. Bathed in natural light from three exposures, the home showcases an open floor plan and a gourmet eat-in kitchen designed for both everyday living and easy entertaining. The serene primary suite features an expanded spa-like bath with built-in storage and a walk-in closet. There are two additional bedrooms adjacent to a renovated second bath. Additional highlights include in-unit washer/dryer, private balcony with distant views, basement storage, garage parking as available ($85/month), and an on-site Superintendent. One pet is allowed with restrictions. Monthly maintenance includes property taxes, heat & hot water. Buyer pays a one-time capital assessment of .5 percent of the sales price at closing. Just minutes to Greenwich Avenue shopping & restaurants, Greenwich Library, YWCA/YMCA, Whole Foods, Greenwich Hospital, Bruce Museum and Metro-North. This Putnam Hill gem combines comfort, convenience and an exceptional value in one of Greenwich's most desireable settings.
