About this home

Set on 5 quiet, private acres, this 2027 new construction is customizable through July 2026, with 4 finished levels of thoughtful living spaces. With 6 bedrooms, 8.2 baths, elevator, golf simulator, outdoor kitchen + lge pool, ideal for everyday living + elevated entertaining. A double height foyer introduces the main level, where formal + informal spaces flow seamlessly. The kitchen, breakfast room + family room w/fireplace create a warm central gathering space with indoor outdoor connection. Private office and mudroom lead to a three car garage. Upstairs, the primary suite offers dual walk-in closets, dressing room + spa-like bath. 4 additional en suite bedrooms, laundry + lounge space complete the level. Finished upper + lower levels add rec space, gym, sauna, wine cellar + guest suite.
