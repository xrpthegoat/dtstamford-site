About this home

Best/final offers are due Tues 11/4 @ 12 noon. Welcome to 88 Pine Hill Avenue - a beautifully updated home that blends warmth, style, and convenience in the heart of Glenbrook. Step inside and be greeted by newly refinished hardwood floors that flow seamlessly throughout the home. The spacious living room, anchored by a charming fireplace and framed by a picture window, is filled with natural light and perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The chef's kitchen is a showstopper, featuring brand-new cabinetry, sleek appliances, and eye-catching granite countertops that set it apart. From here, step onto the freshly finished deck-ideal for morning coffee or evening gatherings. With one bedroom on the main level and two upstairs, including an oversized primary bedroom, the layout offers both flexibility and privacy. The fully finished lower level adds an impressive 800 sq. ft. of versatile living space, highlighted by a stylish ethanol fireplace that creates the ultimate cozy retreat. Enjoy year-round comfort with a modern, energy-efficient split heating and cooling system, only a few years old. All this, perfectly located in one of Glenbrook's most desirable neighborhoods-just a short walk to restaurants, shops, and the Metro North train station, making commuting and city access a breeze. 88 Pine Hill Avenue isn't just a house-it's a home designed for today's lifestyle.
