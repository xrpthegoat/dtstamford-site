About this home

Stunning, completely updated 4-bedroom home with flexible floor plan offering potential for a 5th bedroom, home office, or guest space! Set on a beautiful, level 2-acre lot at end of a cul-de-sac in Wilton's historic Cannondale neighborhood, this move-in ready home combines style, space, and location. The gorgeous updated kitchen features quartz countertops, custom tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances, and opens seamlessly to the sun-filled living room with vaulted ceilings, exposed wood beams, oversized windows, and slider to the massive deck overlooking the private backyard. The main level offers hardwood floors, spacious bedrooms and a primary suite with updated stall shower bathroom, while the lower level with luxury vinyl flooring provides incredible additional living space including a large bonus space/family room w/ wood burning fireplace, flex room/possible 5th bedroom, and full renovated bath. Central A/C, 2-car garage, and abundant natural light throughout. Enjoy the convenience of being walkable to Cannondale train station, while just minutes to downtown shopping, restaurants, schools, and major commute routes. Exceptional value and one of the best-prices opportunities in Wilton!
