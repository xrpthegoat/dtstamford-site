About this home

Is this Westport or Provence? Sited high above Greens Farms is this charming and spacious renovated cottage on a prime, flat and open estate property. A water lover and swimmer's dream, with stunning, large heated pool (with kick pads) and spa (seats 8+). All-new gourmet kitchen, newly finished pickled wood floors, fresh paint throughout. Decorated for stylish and comfortable living, it's the perfect place to call home. Sunny living room, spacious family room with fireplace and wet bar. Private setting, away from main house. Walk to Greens Farms train station, quick bike ride to Burying Hill beach. Organic garden onsite with fresh herbs and occasionally vegetables available too! Full pool and spa privileges.
