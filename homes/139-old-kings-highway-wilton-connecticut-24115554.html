About this home

Lovely, spacious Colonial in South Wilton's Silvermine neighborhood, near Town Center and the New Canaan border. The house offers great flow on the first floor with a bedroom that can double as an office. Hardwood floors add a touch of elegance throughout the home, while a fresh coat of paint brightens every room. Three bedrooms on the second floor, including the sizeable master suite with two closets and a beautifully updated master bath. Two more bedrooms and another beautiful bath complete the second floor. The bay window in the kitchen offers a sunny space with large eat-in area and cozy fireplace. The kitchen appliances are new and stainless, the cooktop is energy-efficient induction, and there's a roomy pantry. A bonus room is conveniently situated off the kitchen, up a short flight of stairs. It's a bright versatile room, great for family connection, or as a playroom/study, with its bookshelves and built-in desk. The partially finished lower level serves as a recreation room, TV room, or storage; it opens to the patio with a stone fire pit and private yard. Wilton is a vibrant community with its fantastic library, great dining, YMCA and top schools. Easy access to trains, major highways and amenities.
