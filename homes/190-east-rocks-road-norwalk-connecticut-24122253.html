About this home

Step into this exceptional Cape Cod-style residence, a true gem nestled in the peaceful & highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood, right on the border of Wilton. Whether you're looking to create cherished memories with family, entertain guests in style, or enjoy the convenience of a home office suite. Minutes to the route 7 connector, tons of shops on Main Ave, and minutes to the new Merritt 7 Train station.
