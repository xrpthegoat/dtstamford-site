About this home

Welcome to this Classic Center Hall Colonial in the heart of Norwalk! Lovingly cared for by the same owner for over 40 years, this home offers timeless character, modern convenience, and an unbeatable location. Step inside to find beautiful hardwood floors throughout and a spacious living room anchored by a cozy fireplace, opening to a delightful screened-in porch for warm-weather relaxation. The main floor also features a formal dining room overlooking the backyard, a versatile den/TV room, and a convenient half bath. Upstairs, you'll find three well-proportioned bedrooms plus a bonus room-ideal as a playroom, craft space, or private retreat-along with a full bath. Sitting on a rare half acre of level property, the yard is perfect for play, gardening, and outdoor entertaining. This property combines privacy and space while keeping you close to everything Norwalk has to offer: beaches, golf courses, country clubs, SONO nightlife, shopping, and dining. Don't miss this opportunity to own a lovingly maintained home with plenty of room to grow, just minutes from the best of Fairfield County living!
