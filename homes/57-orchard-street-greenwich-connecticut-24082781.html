About this home

Nestled in the sought-after neighborhood of Cos Cob and sited on a level .19 acres, this well-maintained raised ranch offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience. Inside, you'll find an open and inviting floor plan featuring 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, a bright and sunny living room, a generous kitchen with island and adjacent dining area ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The walk-out lower level provides even more living space, with possibilities for a family room, home office, or playroom or in-law space. The backyard is perfect for outdoor entertaining, or simply relaxing. This home is just minutes away from top-rated schools, parks, shopping, dining, beaches, and the Cos Cob Marina. Commuting is a breeze with easy access to the Metro North train making this the perfect spot for both work and play. Don't miss your chance to own this gem in a prime location with a spacious backyard and access to all that living in Greenwich has to offer!
