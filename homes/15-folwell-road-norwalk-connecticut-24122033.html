About this home

Welcome to this expanded and updated colonial revival cape cod-style home, tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac, within a charming walkable neighborhood in Norwalk's Bettswood area. The home's attractive vestibule leads to the warm and inviting living room, complete with a cozy fireplace, built-in bookcases, and filled with light from a south-facing bay window. The heart of the home is the beautiful, open eat-in kitchen that has a wall of windows and french doors, providing a panoramic view and access to the expansive deck and large backyard. The main level has two bedrooms and a full bath. Upstairs is the primary bedroom, a fourth bedroom, and a second full bath. Hardwood floors in living room and all bedrooms. The unfinished basement provides a workshop area and abundant storage space, including a cedar closet for clothing storage. Central A/C and a new high-efficiency heat pump for year-round comfort. Near Stew Leonard's and other shopping on Westport Avenue, and very convenient to Central Norwalk, the Town Green, and East Norwalk train station. Up to two pets allowed.
