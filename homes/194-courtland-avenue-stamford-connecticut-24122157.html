About this home

Fantastic spacious 3 to 4 Bedroom 2 Bath Ranch is available for rent in the Glenbrook area of Stamford. Upon entering the house, you will see the kitchen has been completely remodeled including a new island, and newer appliances. The open floor plan includes a Spacious Living Room with built ins and a Dining area, both with newly refinished hardwood floors. There are 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. Additionally, there is a large family room with a fireplace, which could be used as a 4th Bedroom, with access to the deck. The house has been freshly painted throughout, and all the hardwood floors have been refinished. There is central air, a washer and dryer, along with a 1 car garage and plenty of parking. The solar panels mean the tenant will have a lower electricity bill. The fenced backyard is flat and has a shed for additional storage. Conveniently located to Metro North, schools, restaurants, shopping, beach and downtown Stamford.
