About this home

This stunning contemporary retreat in Greenwich, CT with pool is nestled on 2.5 acres on a private lane off of Stanwich Road and offers both style and relaxation; the ideal cure for winter doldrums. Perfect as either a long- or short-term rental, the home comes fully furnished and features an enormous chef's kitchen overlooking beautifully maintained grounds. The kitchen flows gracefully to a formal dining room and large living room with expansive views of a nearby pond. The setting is ideal for peaceful mornings and lively gatherings. The thoughtfully designed layout includes two bedrooms and a full bath on the main floor. Upstairs, the primary en-suite bedroom boasts a spacious walk-in closet, alongside another en-suite bedroom. Flexible spaces provide excellent options for living and working from home. Additionally, a lower-level full bath offers easy access to the pool. Enjoy your time in this wonderful, inviting home located close to town and transportation. Long-term rent $15,000/month and Short-term rent $20,000/month.
