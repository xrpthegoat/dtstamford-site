About this home

Welcome to Downtown Greenwich Living. Experience refined elegance and effortless comfort in this stunning 4-bedroom, 4.1-bathroom townhome, ideally situated within a private, gated association in the heart of downtown Greenwich. Every inch has been thoughtfully designed to blend upscale luxury with everyday ease. The open-concept gourmet kitchen featuring a large center island and abundant storage, flows seamlessly into a sun-filled dining area and spacious family room anchored by custom built-ins, creating the perfect setting for entertaining or relaxed everyday living. Retreat to the luxurious primary suite, complete with two custom-designed walk-in closets and a spa-like ensuite bath. Additional well-appointed bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. Outdoor living is equally exceptional, with a private patio perfect for grilling and an exclusive rooftop deck offering a serene escape above it all. Just minutes from Greenwich Avenue, Bruce Park, the Metro-North station, and I-95, this home puts you at the center of it all while offering the privacy and tranquility you deserve.
