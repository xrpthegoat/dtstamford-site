About this home

A rare opportunity to have the best of Rowayton Woods! Perfectly situated with direct views of the pond, this walk out ranch-style unit has a private entrance and features 2 bedrooms and 2 full baths. At 1468 sq ft, it is larger than most 3 bedroom units with separate living room, dining room and eat in kitchen. The spacious living room features a wood-burning fireplace with custom built-ins, and slider doors that walk out (great for pets!) to a covered, private deck area. Ideal for relaxing and entertaining! The assigned parking space (154) is directly in front of the unit, along with plenty of additional visitor parking for guests. Extra storage and separate laundry. Residents enjoy access to pool and tennis courts, as well as a community garden and walking trails, all enhancing leisure and recreation options. Convenient to Rowayton train station, Bayley Beach and Rowayton Village with its restaurants and quaint shops. Not to be missed!
