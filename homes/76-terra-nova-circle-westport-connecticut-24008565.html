About this home

Spectacular end-unit Townhouse at Terra Nova, a premier gated condominium community with pool in Westport. This 3-bedroom home feels like a private residence, set back in a quiet corner of the complex and with expansive rear and side yards. Enjoy spacious luxury and outdoor serenity without the upkeep and responsibility of a house. This sought-after floor plan has a welcoming front porch, leading into a two-story foyer. The formal dining room accommodates entertaining with ease. The great room has a gas fireplace & tall ceilings, leading into the dine-in kitchen with gourmet features including a gas Wolf range and Sub Zero. Plenty of cabinets and a walk-in pantry offer tremendous storage. The kitchen has an open & airy atmosphere, with glass doors in the breakfast area that lead to the patio & beautiful backyard w/a completely private view. A mudroom hall leads to the 2-car garage. A custom-tiled powder room completes the main level. Upstairs, an open and sun-filled landing leads to the 3 spacious BRs, beginning w/the primary suite which has a luxe bath w/dual sinks, steam shower, hydro tub, water clst & linen clst. The king-size BR has dual clsts, each w/quality built-ins. The remaining BRs have fitted clsts & share a marble bth. 2nd flr laundry rm. The finished LL has the 2nd fireplace & tremendous space for rec rm, gym, home office or any other flexible need. Ample guest parking is adjacent to this home. Very convenient to shopping, dining, train, beaches, golf & more.
