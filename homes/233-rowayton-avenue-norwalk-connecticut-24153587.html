About this home

Seasonal: Memorial Day weekend (May 22)- June 30 for $26,000; August 1- Labor Day (Sept 7) for $33,000. Please inquire for additional months. Not available for the full year. Enjoy Rowayton living in the Frederic E. Lockwood House (circa 1897). This designer renovated Victorian has amenities galore - a gorgeous in-ground heated saltwater pool with dramatic landscape lighting and sculpture; an entertaining deck with gas grill and seating overlooking the property; and modern eat-in kitchen/ family room with marble counters; Viking range; and fireplace just steps away. Completing the main level, you'll be delighted by the office, dining room, and living room with door to the front porch, featuring a relaxing swing - the perfect spot for an afternoon refreshment. Upstairs, is a grand primary master suite with fireplace, ensuite bath with double sinks, and walk-in closet. Also on this level, are two additional bedrooms plus a hall bath with laundry. On the third floor/ attic level, there is a sweet bedroom with lounge area. Minutes away from downtown Rowayton and Metro North. Convenient to Pikney Park and Rowayton resident Bayley Beach Park (open access in winter / in summer pass will be supplied for access!). Feel like you're in the Hamptons, with the benefit of a short commute to NYC via Metro North (if necessary).
