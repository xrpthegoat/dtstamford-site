About this home

Discover an exceptional blend of elegance, comfort, and contemporary style in this beautiful 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial, thoughtfully designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. Set against a picturesque backdrop of mature trees, natural rock formations, and beautifully landscaped grounds, this inviting residence offers an impressive combination of architectural character, spacious interiors, and peaceful outdoor retreats. A dramatic two-story entry foyer welcomes you with abundant natural light, gleaming hardwood floors, and an elegant staircase overlooking the main level. The thoughtfully designed floor plan flows seamlessly into a gracious living room with a gas fireplace and a spacious formal dining room, creating the perfect setting for memorable gatherings. At the heart of the home, the beautifully appointed kitchen features rich wood cabinetry, granite countertops, designer stainless steel appliances, a professional-style range, generous prep space, and an oversized peninsula with seating. An adjoining breakfast area provides a comfortable setting for casual dining, while the impressive family room showcases a vaulted ceiling, custom built-ins, and a wood burning fireplace, creating a warm and inviting space to relax or entertain. A dedicated first-floor home office offers a quiet and comfortable workspace, while a convenient powder room and separate laundry room add everyday functionality.
