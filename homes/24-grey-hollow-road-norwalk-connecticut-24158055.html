About this home

*Highest and Best Wednesday 3/18 5pm*Welcome to 24 Grey Hollow Road, a spacious 2,794 sq ft contemporary home on 1.02 acres in the peaceful Silvermine neighborhood. This 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home features an open-concept main level where the grand living room flows seamlessly into the dining area and kitchen, enhanced by skylights and abundant windows that fill the space with natural light. The living room boasts tall ceilings, impeccable hardwood floors, a fireplace, while the adjacent den offers a private retreat. Upstairs, four bedrooms include a primary suite with soaring ceilings, three closets, and a full primary bath. Plus three additional bedrooms sharing a hall bath. The lower level houses a 2 car basement garage, workshop, utility, and storage rooms. The furnace is one-of-a-kind, with capacity to burn wood or coal in an emergency. Outside, enjoy two large decks on this generous lot. Central AC, forced hot water oil heat, well water, a generator hookup, and septic complete this home. The location is convenient, with easy access to I95 and the Merritt three minutes away, and Gray Barns and historic Silvermine 3 minutes the other way. The Silvermine golf course and Wilton are even closer. With excellent bones and lots of potential, this quiet Grey Hollow Road address offers a rare opportunity to create your ideal Silvermine retreat.
