About this home

Tucked away you will find 85 Ledgebrook Dr an updated modern, spacious End-Unit Condo in West Norwalk. With over 1800SF of living space, 9+ ft ceilings throughout adorned with tasteful updates this is the one. Private entry leads you into the foyer. Walk up the main stairs you are welcomed into the unit filled with natural sunlight. Main hall you will find tons of closet space & dedicated laundry area. Updated kitchen with marble counter tops, SS appliances and soft close cabinets. Opening to the dining area with a 2 way fireplace. Large Living room access to your private deck over looking nature not someone else's unit! Full bath in hall with stand up shower. Primary bedroom is HUGE with an entire wall of closets, vanity station and full updated bath with tub. Two additional bedrooms both of gracious sizes one with dbl closets and one with walk-in closet. One car garage space plus ample visitor spots. Landlord prefers 18/mo lease to start. Berber Carpets will be professionally cleaned prior to new tenancy.
