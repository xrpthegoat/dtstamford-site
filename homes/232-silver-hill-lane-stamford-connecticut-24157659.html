About this home

Everybody wants a flat half acre plus in Pepper Ridge with a view of protected woods but nobody can find it. Until now! This one of a kind farmhouse cape w/3 car garage features the yard you've been fantasizing about and it's just a few short minutes away from shopping, restaurants, schools and houses of worship. Plenty of room to cook and dine in the large eat-in kitchen. The first floor also boasts a spacious living room with fireplace, airy family room w/vaulted ceiling, wood-burning stove and loft, two good sized bedrooms and full bath. Upstairs you'll find two ample bedrooms either of which could be the primary. One has a kitchenette and a full bath en-suite and the other is right next to an enormous hall bathroom with oversized luxury tub. Take your pick! Have a swing on the front porch and bring your creative ideas to this definitely non-cookie-cutter cape.
