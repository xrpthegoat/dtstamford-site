About this home

Large Springdale Colonial - partially furnished! Welcome to 56 Barholm Avenue a large 4 bedroom, 3 bath colonial conveniently located close to everything! Open concept living: living room/dinning room wrap around into a large kitchen with SS appliances, gas for cooking provides you the living you have been looking for! Additional living room with gas fireplace providing more space on the main level for home office or playroom. Relax or Dine outside on the patio and private yard surrounded by evergreens for full year around privacy. Head upstairs up the beautiful open air staircase to a spacious landing. Large primary bedroom with walk-in closet and ensuite bathroom. Down the hall past the 2nd floor laundry and full bath are 3 large bedrooms all with great closet space. Additional family room in finished basement. Parking is no problem with large driveway and large two card garage with tons of extra storage space. Furnished bedroom spaces and other large scaled furniture make this an easy transition. Moving out of the city, moving out of your home for renovation? This rental might be the easy next step you were looking for! Great credit, 2.5-3X salary, 2 months security deposit. No pets.
