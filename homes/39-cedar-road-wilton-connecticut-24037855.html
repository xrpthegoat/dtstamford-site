About this home

Welcome to this 4-bedroom, 3-bath Deck House, an example of mid-century modern architecture, nestled at the end of a serene access way. Set on 2.35 acres, this home embodies the highlights of the era: clean lines, expansive windows, and a seamless integration with the natural surroundings. The great room is a standout, featuring vaulted ceilings with exposed beams and a striking floor-to-ceiling brick fireplace. Expansive glass windows and sliding doors blur the boundaries between indoor and outdoor living, with a spacious deck spanning the length of the house and a covered porch perfect for enjoying the setting. The eat-in kitchen includes granite countertops, electric range, center island, open shelving, and a wood stove. A ceiling fan and sliding doors to the deck further enhance the seamless indoor-outdoor experience. Hardwood floors throughout the main level add a touch of warmth, while the primary ensuite bedroom offers direct access to the deck through a sliding glass door. The walkout lower level provides versatile living space with wall-to-wall carpet, a combined full bathroom and laundry room, and bedroom with sliders. Eco-friendly living is embraced with solar panels that help reduce energy costs. This home is ready for its next chapter. Whether you're looking to personalize the existing spaces or explore further updates, this property presents a canvas to create your dream home. Located 10 minutes from Wilton Center, offering shopping and dining options.
