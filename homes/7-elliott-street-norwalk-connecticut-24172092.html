About this home

Welcome to Magical Marvin Beach-where waterfront living is a year-round lifestyle! Spend summers swimming, kayaking, and paddle boarding across three private neighborhood beaches, or take advantage of the members-only marina just steps away for effortless boating access. The community truly comes alive with sunset beach gatherings, movie nights, and a full calendar of seasonal events including an Easter Egg Hunt, Fourth of July parade and BBQ, Steak & Lobster dinners, Ice Cream Socials, Halloween festivities, and holiday celebrations with Santa and caroling. This charming circa 1923 Colonial has been thoughtfully expanded to offer both character and modern comfort. Set on an oversized lot, the property features a private backyard with lush gardens and deck overlooking the grounds. The two car garage has an attached shed. Inside, on the main level there is a living room, dining room, den or home office, eat in kitchen and mudroom. The home offers four bedrooms and a full bath on the second level. A partially finished basement provides excellent storage and flexible space. Ideally located with easy access to the Metro-North train, I-95, and the vibrant dining and shopping scenes of East Norwalk and SoNo. Nearby recreational amenities include Ludlow Park with a pickleball court, basketball courts, ball fields, and playground. A short walk to Calf Pasture Beach and Taylor Farm Dog Park. A rare opportunity to enjoy coastal living in one of Norwalk's most desirable communities.
