About this home

Welcome to 1 Arrowhead Lane in Greenwich CT. Conveniently located across from the scenic trails and botanical gardens of Montgomery Pinetum Park, this beautifully updated and meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom center hall colonial effortlessly combines sophistication with comfort. The first floor features a spacious kitchen that flows seamlessly into a bright family room with soaring vaulted ceilings. French doors open to a secluded rear deck, terrace, and lawn-an ideal setting for unwinding outdoors, playing or entertaining guests. A formal living room and dining room provide elegant spaces for entertaining, while a sun-filled office offers breathtaking views of the meticulously landscaped grounds. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite provides a serene retreat, accompanied by three generously sized bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and an additional family room. This sunlit home with an excellent floor plan in the most convenient location- just minutes from the train, schools, restaurants, specialty food stores and more.
