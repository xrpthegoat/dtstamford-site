About this home

Beautifully maintained 3-bedroom colonial that boasts classic charm. Step inside to find a spacious first floor featuring a sun-filled living room with elegant french doors opening to a cozy outdoor porch, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The main level also includes a warm and inviting den, formal dining room, updated kitchen, and a convenient half bath-ideal for both entertaining and everyday life. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms, a full bath, and access to a finished attic via a central hallway. The primary bedroom features french doors leading out to a large porch with a breathtaking view of the backyard/pool, perfect for watching the sun rise or enjoying a drink. Heading upstairs to the attic you have the perfect versatile flex space, which can be used for a home office, gym, playroom, or guest suite-complete with abundant natural light and plush wall-to-wall carpeting. Step outside to your personal outdoor oasis: a professionally landscaped, fully fenced backyard featuring a pristine in-ground pool with a brand-new filter, surrounded by a perfectly manicured lawn. Whether hosting summer gatherings or enjoying quiet moments, the backyard offers a picturesque setting for every occasion. Additional features include a detached one-car garage with ample storage and a full walk-out basement, ready to be finished to your taste or used for extra storage. Close to all major shopping, grocery stores, and highways!
