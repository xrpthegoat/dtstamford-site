About this home

Set on just over an acre in a convenient Westport location, 2 Quarter Mile Road combines comfortable living, modern updates, and outdoor spaces designed to be enjoyed throughout every season. Professionally landscaped grounds showcase mature specimen and evergreen trees, perennial gardens, and expansive lawn areas that create both beauty and privacy. Inside, natural light fills the home, highlighting light oak hardwood floors and a floor plan that flows effortlessly from room to room. The updated kitchen offers generous workspace and storage, opening to the areas where family and friends naturally gather. Main-level laundry, recessed lighting, fresh interior paint, and solid wood interior doors throughout most of the home reflect the care invested in maintaining and enhancing the property. The family room centers around a wood-burning fireplace with a wood-burning insert, providing warmth, character, and a cozy place to unwind. Upstairs, four bedrooms and two full baths offer comfortable accommodations, including an updated primary bath and a beautifully renovated hall bath. Two additional half baths add convenience for everyday living and entertaining. The finished lower level provides an additional 514 square feet of flexible space, ideal for a playroom, media room, fitness area, or home office. A separate room currently serves as a pantry/office, while the unfinished storage area includes a utility sink and Bilco door access.
