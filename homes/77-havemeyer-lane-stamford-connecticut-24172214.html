About this home

Palmer Hill! The luxury gated community located on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. This is a 1600 sq.ft., Magnificent, End Unit, Mid-rise, condo with 2 bedrooms/2 baths,+ office, 1 large deck + a terrace! This home has been completely customized. Every room is spacious with beautiful views and fabulous light. The gourmet kitchen has SS appliances, an island, and a separate pantry with washer & dryer and mechanicals. The kitchen is open to the separate dining space for your large dining table and connects to a 2nd terrace. The light filled living room is surrounded by glass windows, has a gas fireplace, and French doors to a fantastic, spacious deck to enjoy the outside! The beautiful primary bedroom suite is connected to the 2nd terrace, has a custom walk-in closet, and a marble en-suite bath with 2 sinks, and custom marble shower. A 2nd bedroom, full bath, and office complete this incredible condo. This home comes with 2 parking spaces in the indoor garage, a storage unit, and bike room. Amenities include a newly renovated clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym, yoga room, and business center. Minutes to I95 and Metro North. Dog Friendly! This is a MUST SEE HOME!
