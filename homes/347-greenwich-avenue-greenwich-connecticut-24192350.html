About this home

This spacious three-bedroom apartment located on the third floor of a well-maintained building. Nestled in the heart of the Greenwich business district, this residence offers exceptional convenience to shops, restaurants, cafes, and everyday amenities. The train station is just minutes away, making it an excellent option for commuters, while nearby Bruce Park provides access to open green space, walking paths, and recreational facilities. Residents will also enjoy access to two beautiful Greenwich beaches and two town-owned islands, offering a rare combination of in-town living and coastal lifestyle. With easy access to I-95, this location provides both comfort and connectivity in one of Greenwich's most sought-after areas.
