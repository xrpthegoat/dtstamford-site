About this home

Beautifully maintained 3 BR, 3.5 bath, 2368 sq ft home located off a quiet cul-de-sac in highly sought after Glenville. The main level with hardwood floors includes a formal entry, powder room, bright eat-in kitchen, beautiful formal dining room and large, welcoming living room with fireplace. Sliders open to the private deck which overlooks the backyard - perfect spot for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs is a generous primary bedroom with bath and closets, plus 2 family bedrooms with hall bath. The lower level is a wonderful finished space with fireplace, built-ins and a full bath. It is ideal for playroom/family room, guest area and office space. Laundry room and great storage. One car attached garage and assigned space.The outdoor setting is peaceful with deck and terrace. Excellent location - walk to all in Glenville including parks, shops, restaurants, new DeCicco's grocery and top-rated Glenville School. Friendly neighbors! The best choice!
