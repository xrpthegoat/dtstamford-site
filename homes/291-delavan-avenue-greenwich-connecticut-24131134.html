About this home

Step into timeless charm with this spacious 1920s Colonial, ideally located in the heart of Byram, just moments from shops, schools, and public transit. A bright and welcoming front sun porch sets the stage, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or a relaxing afternoon. Inside, the home showcases beautiful period woodwork, generous-sized bedrooms, and a dedicated home office on the second floor, along with a walk-up attic that offers endless potential. The kitchen balances vintage character with modern convenience, featuring updated appliances while leaving room for your own finishing touches. Recently added mini-split A/C units provide comfort year-round, and the full basement with high ceilings offers abundant options for a gym, playroom, or creative space. Outside, the level, fully fenced backyard is ideal for entertaining, gardening, or play. A detached two-car garage with access from Spruce Street adds both practicality and privacy. With energy-efficient upgrades that help lower utility costs, plentiful parking, and a commuter-friendly location, this home perfectly blends classic character with everyday comfort and convenience.
