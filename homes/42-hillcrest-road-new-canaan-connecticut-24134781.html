About this home

A masterpiece of modern luxury, this 8,400 sq ft estate by Arena Development has been meticulously crafted to elevate everyday living. Thoughtfully designed and majestically sited with a dynamic, light-filled layout, the home unfolds across four exquisitely finished levels, anchored by a spectacular chef's kitchen and expansive family room that form the true heart of the residence. Five beautifully appointed en suite bedrooms offer refined comfort, while the home's position on the coveted West Side places you just minutes from the transportation and village. Every detail speaks to sophistication and craftsmanship-soaring ceilings, four elegant fireplaces, graceful transitions between living spaces, and a stunning walkout lower level designed for recreation and relaxation. Energy Star construction, a 7-zone dual-fuel heating system, and a suite of advanced smart-home technologies ensure effortless comfort in every season.
