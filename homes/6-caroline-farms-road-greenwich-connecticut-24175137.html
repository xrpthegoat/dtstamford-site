About this home

Beautiful townhouse in Quiet, Peaceful Cul-de-sac Setting located a short distance to Cos Cob retail amenities and the Metro-North Railroad. This 2 (3) bedroom condo is in move-in condition with a freshly painted first floor. Open floor plan with living room, dining room and breakfast nook overlooking a large private deck and yard. Oversized Master Suite with walk-in closet, vaulted ceiling, skylight bathroom, and private balcony. Second floor landing area can be converted to a flex space, such as a home office, reading nook, or library area. Finished walkup attic can serve as a bedroom, playroom, or home gym. Fully finished basement with full bath can serve as a bedroom, play area, media room, home gym, and offer spacious storage space. This home has been thoughtfully updated with vinyl flooring (Hardwood floor under it on first floor), upgraded deck, attic HVAC replacement (2024), water heater replacement (2026), and a fully renovated basement (Dec 2023) featuring full perimeter water protection, and dehumidifier system. Basement space has been significantly enhanced for comfortable additional living and recreation use. Attached one garage and 2 additional parking spaces opposite unit. Unit offers substantially more functional living space than the listed square footage indicates.
