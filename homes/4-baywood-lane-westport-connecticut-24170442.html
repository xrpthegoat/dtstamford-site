About this home

Tucked away down a private drive on two serene wooded acres, this exceptional mid-century modern deck house offers the perfect blend of architectural character, privacy, and natural beauty. Bathed in tremendous natural light, the home showcases the signature design elements that make deck houses so timeless and sought after, with expansive windows, soaring ceilings, and seamless indoor-outdoor living. The four-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath layout provides comfortable and flexible living spaces designed to embrace the surrounding landscape. 2 car detached garage to hold all of your extra and outdo0r items! Warm wood tones, open gathering areas, and walls of glass create an inviting atmosphere throughout the home. A spacious deck overlooks the peaceful property, offering the ideal setting for entertaining or quiet relaxation immersed in nature. It feels worlds away while still being conveniently located near all that Westport has to offer. Sold As Is
