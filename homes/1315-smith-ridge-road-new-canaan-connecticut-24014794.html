About this home

Totally renovated with large additions in 2007 2008 by Karen Christensen Architect and built by Michael Byrne & Associates., This custom-built first time on the market home. A well-maintained treasure designed for the original owners. Nestled on 2.86 acres of private land, this residence is a secluded retreat, hidden away from the street, offering a sense of exclusive tranquility. The home's grandeur is discreet from the front approach, yet it boasts over 7,500 square feet of finished living space, complete with an array of ceiling designs that add to its modern flair. This expansive property features 5 to 6 bedrooms, 6 full and 2 half baths, all within an open floor plan that encourages a seamless flow of indoor and outdoor living. The large primary suite serves as a luxurious haven, while the manicured grounds and large deck provide the perfect backdrop for relaxation or entertaining guests. The walk-out lower level adds to the home's spacious feel, and the beautiful hardwood floors throughout are complemented by designer finishes that exude charm and elegance. This modern-style home is a true treasure, offering both privacy and sophisticated living spaces. Invite potential buyers to experience the unique charm and luxurious lifestyle this property offers.
