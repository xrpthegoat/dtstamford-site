About this home

Highest and Best by Monday, Sept 8 @5pm. Welcome to this beautifully maintained and move in ready 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home nestled on a cul-de-sac with a large, flat landscaped fenced yard featuring 2 stone patios-perfect for play, pets, or entertaining. This bright, airy and recently painted home is drenched in natural sunlight with spacious bedrooms, including a primary suite featuring a walk-in closet and a luxurious ensuite. Both full bathrooms are outfitted with heated floors and rain shower heads for a spa-like experience. The heart of the home features a cozy and renovated eat-in kitchen and a warm family room with cathedral ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace-ideal for gathering year-round. Downstairs features another living area perfect for a home office, playroom or guests and a renovated laundry room with a half bathroom and pantry and cedar closet perfect for all storage needs. Recent Upgrades Include: Roof & Gutters (2019), New Skylights (2020), 2 zone HVAC System (2021), Navien Tankless Water Heater (2025), LG Washing Machine (2025) and Liftmaster Garage door opener(2025). Whether you're enjoying a quiet evening by the fireplace or hosting a summer BBQ in the backyard, this home offers comfort and convenience-close to Trader Joes, Whole Foods, schools, public transportation and Pool and Tennis clubs. Last but not least this home features City Water, City Sewer and Natural Gas! Agent/Owner
