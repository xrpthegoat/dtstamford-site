About this home

Introducing a timeless, classic colonial that seamlessly meets the demands of modern luxury living while catering to the discerning audience seeking sophistication and style. Nestled at the end of a long private driveway and situated within a park like setting you are welcomed home to your own personal retreat while minutes to Westport's finest amenities. The gourmet kitchen equipped with high end appliances and exquisite current finishes caters to the culinary enthusiast and entertainer for both intimate and grand gatherings. Open kitchen great room grounds the heartbeat of the home while the expansive deck for outdoor entertaining, the formal dining room, the private paneled office and the inviting living room are all perfect and logical extensions of the central living area offering spaces and places for work, play and repose. The second level homes an intimate seating area overlooking the grounds, along with additional family and play room large enough for a work space, lounge area and reading or game corner as well as five well appointed bedrooms, each with an attached bath. The primary suite boasts high ceilings, fireplace, large walk in closet and a spa like bath with jetted tub and steam shower. The finished third floor could be converted to an au pair suite while the newly renovated walkout lower level is where all staycation fun and recreation occurs. Heated gunite pool, firepit, and covered deck with retractable shades round out the life to live and love here!
