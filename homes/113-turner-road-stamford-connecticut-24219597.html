About this home

Situated on a desirable corner lot in Stamford's sought-after Newfield neighborhood, this charming Colonial offers comfortable living space, classic character, and timeless curb appeal. A welcoming front entry opens to a spacious Living Room featuring a large picture window, cozy fireplace, and built-in shelving. The adjoining Dining Room flows naturally into the functional Kitchen and a sun-filled Family Room, where sliding glass doors lead to the outdoor patio and backyard-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The upper level features three bedrooms and a full bath. Outside, enjoy the patio with its distinctive brick grill, creating an inviting setting for outdoor gatherings and summer evenings. Additional features include hardwood floors waiting to be revealed beneath the existing wall-to-wall carpeting, city water and sewer, an attached one-car garage, and an oil furnace updated approximately two years ago. Ideally located near shopping, restaurants, parks, commuter routes, Metro-North, houses of worship, and Downtown Stamford, this home offers incredible convenience. A wonderful opportunity to own a charming home in one of Stamford's most established neighborhoods. Ready for its next chapter!
