About this home

Welcome to The Chesterfield ... Enjoy easy, carefree living in this spacious, FIRST FLOOR 2BR/2B ranch-style end unit just minutes from everything you need! Chesterfield, a peaceful, sought after community with gated entry, boasts quiet tree-lined streets, 2-community pools and clubhouse, yet is conveniently located directly across from Ridgeway Shopping Center and minutes from your choice of shops, restaurants and Whole Foods. Say goodbye to landscaping, lawn cutting and spend your time relaxing on your roomy deck or at one of the pools that's just steps from your door. Parking is available in the front or back of your unit for easy access. It's only 4-steps into this freshly painted, spacious ranch-style home featuring 2-large bedrooms and 2-full baths on the main level. The welcoming living room features a fireplace and flows to the dining area, where sliders open to the deck - perfect for indoor-outdoor living. This special unit is one of a very few that includes a full basement perfect for use as an office or exercise room or can be simply used for storage. Get to know your neighbors while you participate in fun afternoon or evening activities planned by the community's social committee. This sunny home offers gracious space, newly carpeted bedrooms PLUS HVAC updated in 2023. With its wonderful location minutes to Stamford's vibrant downtown, highways and public transportation this easy living home will win your heart.
