About this home

Welcome to 2 Linden Place, an expanded Cape in the Glenville area of Greenwich. Situated on a dead end street, this home which has been with the same owner for over 30 years, boasts 4 spacious size bedrooms plus an additional in-law or au pair suite. The additional in-law or au pair suite has a living room and separate full size bedroom with full bath. Hardwood floors throughout, the updated kitchen has quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances and off of the dining area you will find a slider that opens up to a generous size deck ready to entertain on. A half bath and 2 bedrooms are on the main level. Upstairs you will find 2 more bedrooms and one full bath. There is an extra 1,000 square feet of unfinished space on the lower level ready for your creativity. A professionally landscaped and level yard that is fenced and as a bonus, the home comes with a generator and propane tank. Situated in a prime location, close to transportation, restaurants, schools and parks. Everything you need is just moments away.
