About this home

Contemporary Ranch ideally situated on Silverbrook Lake, offering a peaceful setting with beautiful water views and a wonderful opportunity to enjoy kayaking and bird watching right from home. The inviting eat-in kitchen features granite countertops and opens to a comfortable family room with a fireplace. The primary bedroom also features a cozy fireplace, creating a warm and relaxing retreat. The finished lower level offers additional livable space along with ample storage. Enjoy the outdoors from the private backyard patio overlooking the lake. Conveniently located near Westport schools, town amenities, shopping, dining, and the Merritt Parkway.
