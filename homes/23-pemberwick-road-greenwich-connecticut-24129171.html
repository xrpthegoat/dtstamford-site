About this home

This storybook Pemberwick Colonial is an ideal condo alternative and oodles of character from the covered front porch awaiting your rocking chairs and seasonal mums and ferns. A .14 acre in the R-6 Zone has potential expansion with off street parking. The main floor has an open living-dining room with hardwood floors and Pella replacement windows, and sun-lit eat-in kitchen with new GE appliances. First floor bedroom with a full bath. Upstairs is a king size bedroom with adjoining room ideal for nursery/office/or expanded closet that share a bath and entry. A walk-up attic and basement provide plenty of storage. Conveniently located to I-95 and NY border.
