About this home

Located in Westport's coveted Greens Farms neighborhood, this light-filled home is perfectly sited high and dry on a private park-like acre with room for a pool and pool house. A renovated kitchen with top-of-the-line appliances and sunny breakfast nook opens to a spacious great room with stone fireplace and expansive deck overlooking the property, ideal for indoor-outdoor living. The flexible floor plan includes a rare first-floor bedroom with full bath, ideal for guests, au pair, or multi-generational living, along with a finished walk-out lower level offering additional space for recreation, media, or work-from-home needs. The primary suite features two walk-in closets and a new spa-like bath. With 5 bedrooms and 5 baths, the home offers ample space for living, working, and entertaining. Modern conveniences include city water, city sewer, natural gas, and an EV charger. Exceptional location just minutes to Greens Farms Elementary, the Greens Farms train station, Burying Hill Beach, and downtown Westport shopping and dining.
