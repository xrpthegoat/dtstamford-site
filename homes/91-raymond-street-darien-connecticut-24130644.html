About this home

5 Bedroom Classic colonial for rent in Darien. This spacious five bedroom offers complete privacy with easy access to town and train. Walk through the welcoming front door into gracious foyer opening up to formal living room with fireplace, built in bookshelves, a bay window and french doors leading outside to a built-in fire pit and expansive deck area great for outdoor activities and socializing. A soaring great room with a stone fireplace is off of the kitchen along with play space and separate sunroom complete with a full wet bar with room for a crowd. This level features an office, and dining room which opens to the central kitchen. The primary bedroom has space for a sitting area around the fireplace and there are three additional bedrooms with three full baths upstairs. On the lower level, there is an additional 800 sf in-laws or nanny apartment fully renovated with a living area, den, bedroom, bath, kitchen, and separate entrance. Plenty of room for everyone. A basement with high ceilings is the perfect space for exercise, workshop, studio or entertainment. Two laundry rooms make cleaning a breeze. This is a must-see!
