About this home

Large 2 family home just 1 mile from shops, restaurants, and entertainment in downtown SONO. This house is freshly painted, has shining wood floors, storm windows and thermal pane window all with screen. There are new mini blinds on all the windows. The kitchens are fully applianced, have gas stoves and the appliances are newer. Many of the rooms have new light fixtures and ceiling fans, updated bathrooms, spacious bedrooms each with a closet. There is a large open front porch and the 2nd floor has a bright 4 season enclosed porch. There is a walk up, floored attic, great for storage or other possibilities. The basement is full with interior and exterior access with laundry connection and also great for storage. The 2 car detached garage is oversized and has an option for additional storage and has a newer roof. The yard has been well maintained and stretches from Summitt back to Allview. The cement acorns are not included in the sale however may be purchased separately.
