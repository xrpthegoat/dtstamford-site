About this home

***Highest & Best: Tues 5/19 10AM*** Move right in and start living! This renovated Newfield-Pepper Ridge ranch combines modern finishes, smart functionality, and a location that makes everyday life easy. Walk to coffee, Beldotti's pastry, groceries, pizza, and more, while still enjoying the mature trees and neighborhood feel that make this area so desirable. Inside, the open-concept layout feels bright and connected with oversized windows and natural light throughout. The kitchen was thoughtfully designed with quartz counters, oversized island seating, stainless appliances, pot filler, and a custom shiplap hood that gives the space personality without trying too hard. Built-ins and mudroom storage make the home as practical as it is stylish. The main level offers 2 bedrooms and a renovated full bath. Downstairs adds valuable bonus space with a large rec/playroom, office, bedroom, renovated full bath/laundry room, and excellent storage. The backyard is what truly separates this home from others in the area: a large multi-functional flagstone patio with space for outdoor dining, lounge seating, firepit nights, and BBQ area, all surrounded by beautiful stone retaining walls, mature privacy trees, and planter boxes that give the space warmth and character. Just a few steps up is a rare private level yard perfect for entertaining, relaxing, pets, lawn games, gardening, or simply enjoying the outdoors, plus a large storage shed tucked quietly in the back.
