About this home

Welcome to this classic Colonial, ideally situated on an idyllic street in South Wilton! As you enter, you are greeted by a spacious foyer, flanked by a gracious living room with a fireplace on one side & a formal dining room on the other, conveniently located just off the kitchen. This thoughtfully designed layout is ideal for entertaining & gathering with family & friends. The sun-filled, spacious kitchen features an inviting eat-in area, granite countertops, & stainless steel appliances. Just beyond, a cozy family room with its own fireplace provides the perfect setting for movie nights, relaxing evenings, & everyday family living. The second floor offers a spectacular primary suite complete with a walk-in closet & recently beautifully remodeled primary bath, along with 3 generously sized bedrooms, a full hall bath, & the convenience of second-floor laundry. The finished walk out lower level provides even more flexible living space, complete with another fireplace! Whether used as a playroom, rec room, home gym, or media space, the possibilities are endless. There's great storage & garage access. Step outside to enjoy the beautiful outdoor setting, featuring a fabulous oversized deck, patio, & lovely yard, perfect for entertaining, play, or enjoying nature. Numerous recent improvements add to the appeal, including Hardie board siding, new roof, many replacement windows, & more! A wonderful opportunity to enjoy classic Colonial charm, This is a MUST SEE!
