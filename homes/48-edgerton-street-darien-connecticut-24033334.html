About this home

This is your opportunity to have a turn-key modern colonial property as your own that includes 4-5 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and over 3800 SF of living space. This is where you can experience the best of modern living in a prime Darien location. Built in 2017, this well maintained residence has great curb appeal starting at the covered front porch and custom entry door. Interior features that include 9' ceilings welcome you and the separate living room with fireplace, formal dining room, inviting eat-in kitchen and family room with fireplace offer a great flow for day-to-day living and entertaining. The second floor has 3 bedrooms plus the primary suite with full bath and a proper laundry area. The upper level offers flexibility as either a bonus area with full bath or 5th bedroom. Looking for an exercise and rec room the lower level has plenty of space to accommodate including interior and exterior access. Additional amenities include central A/C, a 24 KW Generac Generator that is propane fueled, lawn irrigation system, outside patios, ample lawn areas and security & fire detection system. Situated on 0.36 acres that have been professionally landscaped with a detached garage this property will impress you with its appeal. Located just 3 blocks from the Noroton Heights Metro North train station and the new vibrant amenities of Darien Commons and the soon to be completed development Heights Crossing.
