About this home

SELLER REQUESTS ALL OFFERS By 1PM 8/5. Clover Cottage. Fall in love with the style, convenience & amenities offered at this delightful 4 bedroom cape on a most convenient cul de sac in lowest Wilton. Relax & unwind in this beautifully decorated, spacious 1940's charmer with gorgeous heated salt water pool, new professional landscaping, hardscaping & welcoming terraces for entertaining. The front to back living room with fireplace opens to an oversized deck for entertaining or relaxing. The formal Dining Room is simply beautiful with wainscoting & ample room to entertain a crowd. The well appointed kitchen offers white cabinetry, updated appliances & the most inviting breakfast nook with a wall of windows that overlook the pool & private property. The generous family room with newly carpeted floors offers sliders to the terrace & pool beyond. Additional 1st floor bedroom & FULL bath can be enjoyed as an office or guest space. There are 3 additional bedrooms on the upper floor, including a captivating primary bedroom with fireplace & brick hearth. There are wood floors throughout most rooms, &Central Air for your comfort. Served by city water & sewer - a rarity in Wilton! Welcoming white picket fence out front a fully fenced back yard for safety and privacy. Relax & cool off at the pool, or have fun at home around the fire pit, or tending your garden. You might never want to leave, but when you do - restaurants & shopping are all immediately accessible in both Wilton & Westport
