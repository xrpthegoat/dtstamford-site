About this home

Embrace the rhythm of coastal living in this stunning, fully furnished retreat on Saugatuck Island. Spanning 4,200 square feet across three thoughtfully designed levels, this residence is more than just a home-it's a sanctuary defined by light, salt air, and sophisticated design. The experience begins in the striking foyer, where an open lighthouse cupola soars overhead, bathing the interior in a warm, natural glow. Every corner of this 5-bedroom home reflects an eye for detail, featuring exquisite finishes and curated decor that blend luxury with a relaxed, beachside soul. Outdoor living is a cornerstone of the lifestyle here. Step out onto the screened deck to catch the sea breeze or retreat to the private, fenced backyard. Whether you're hosting a summer cookout at the built-in grill on the stone patio or enjoying the lush green space, the grounds are designed for effortless enjoyment. Best of all, two private beaches are just moments away, offering easy access to swimming, boating, or simply lounging by the shore. While it feels worlds away from the daily grind, this island gem is incredibly convenient. You are just minutes from the Metro-North, major highways, local yacht clubs, and the vibrant dining and shopping scene that makes Westport so desirable. Whether you're looking to spend your days biking quiet roads or watching the sunset over the sound, this property offers a rare opportunity to live the ultimate beach retreat lifestyle.
