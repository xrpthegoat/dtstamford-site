About this home

Sunny, updated townhouse condo on the Mill/Mianus River, move-in ready and perfectly placed between Scalzi Park, Ridgeway shopping, and downtown Stamford. This bright 2-bedroom, 2-bath home offers gleaming hardwood floors, renovated kitchen and baths, in-unit laundry, and a private balcony with water views, a peaceful spot to sip coffee or unwind. Reserved covered parking and no one above or below make this unit feel like a house with condo conveniences. Notable recent upgrades include a new central A/C system for reliable, whole-home comfort. Inside, enjoy an open, airy layout with great natural light, a cozy fireplace, freshly updated finishes, and thoughtful storage. Low-cost HOA dues, pet-friendly policies, and easy access to walking trails at the Mill/Mianus River and nearby parks make this a perfect option for commuters, downsizers, or investors.
