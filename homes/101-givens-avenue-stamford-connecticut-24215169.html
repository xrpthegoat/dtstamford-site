About this home

Space. Privacy. Beachside convenience. Welcome to this beautifully refreshed townhouse on Givens Avenue, tucked away on a quiet residential dead-end street within walking distance of Cove Beach. With three spacious levels, two bedrooms, and two-and-a-half bathrooms, this home gives you room to live comfortably. Freshly painted interiors set the stage for generous living and dining areas, whether you're hosting friends or enjoying a relaxed night in. The kitchen features a dishwasher, range, and refrigerator, while the back deck invites you to take your morning coffee outdoors. The spacious primary suite delivers your own private retreat, complete with an en-suite full bathroom and two closets. A second bedroom, another full bathroom, and a convenient half bath round out the home. Downstairs, the partially finished basement adds valuable flexible space, generous storage, and an in-unit washer and dryer. A peaceful residential setting. Room to spread out. The beach within reach. Come experience your next chapter in Stamford.
