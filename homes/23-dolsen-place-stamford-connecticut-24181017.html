About this home

Enjoy the convenience of downtown Stamford living in this charming two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath townhouse just moments from restaurants, shopping, Metro-North, and Scalzi Park. The main level offers an open living and dining area that flows into the kitchen, along with a powder room and direct access to the private parking area. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms, each served by a full bath, plus the convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer. Additional features include gas heat, central air conditioning, one assigned parking space, and the option to rent an additional space. A great opportunity to enjoy low-maintenance living in a highly convenient location. Approximate square footage.
