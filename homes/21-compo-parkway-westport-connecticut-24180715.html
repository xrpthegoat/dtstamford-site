About this home

A rare offering in Westport's coveted Compo Beach neighborhood, this extraordinary estate sits on a prized double lot just moments from Compo Beach, Longshore Club, train & vibrant downtown. Featured in Westport Magazine, the property masterfully blends the charm of a beautifully preserved historic residence from 1928 w/ a remarkable 6,000 sq ft addition completed in 2008. The result is a seamless fusion of timeless New England architecture, modern luxury and exceptional craftsmanship. Spanning 9,000 square feet across 4 finished levels, the home offers 7 bedrooms, 6 full & one half baths, 6 fireplaces, soaring 10-foot ceilings, plus a 1st floor in-law/au pair suite. Sunlight pours through an abundance of windows, enhancing the home's gracious proportions & creating a bright, welcoming atmosphere throughout. A dramatic limestone & marble foyer w/ spiral staircase introduces elegant and inviting living spaces enhanced by custom hand-carved moldings and millwork, including a gracious living room with floor-to-ceiling arched windows, library, music room & private offices. The spectacular 900-square-foot chef's kitchen and great room, anchored by a reclaimed limestone fireplace from Burgundy, France, is the heart of the home-an extraordinary gathering place where family & friends come together effortlessly for intimate evenings & grand-scale entertaining.
