About this home

Enchanting Private Country Estate offering classic design and exceptional lifestyle flexibility. Privately set at the end of a country lane among prestigious estates on one of New Canaan's premier streets, this property delivers rare seclusion just minutes from town and train and acclaimed award-winning schools. Recently refreshed throughout, the home features a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings and a Juliette balcony overlooking beautifully landscaped grounds with multiple patios, a pool with waterfall spa, and mature perennial plantings. The suite is complemented by a spa-like bath and an expansive walk-in dressing room. Four additional bedrooms include a first-floor guest suite or flexible office. The gourmet kitchen is appointed with high-end appliances and opens to a bright, airy layout with seamless access to the patio-ideal for entertaining. An oversized family room with a fieldstone fireplace and French doors enhances the indoor-outdoor flow, while a large library/playroom with built-ins adds versatility to the main level. The lower level provides a relaxed recreation space, perfect for a game of pool. The expansive grounds also feature a large yard with a playground and ample open space for recreation and outdoor enjoyment. Surrounded by serene, private landscaping, this home offers a true sense of tranquility. A rare offering combining an ideal layout with every modern amenity-completely move-in ready.
