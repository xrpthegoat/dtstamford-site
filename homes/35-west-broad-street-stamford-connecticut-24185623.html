About this home

A fantastic opportunity to own a modern business condo in the heart of Stamford's corporate hub and its lively downtown! This 1st floor office condo is perfectly suited for a variety of service businesses such as medical, financial, attorneys, marketing & sales, real estate etc. This space offers generous sized rooms inc: reception/waiting area, 3 individual offices, a conference room, workspace room, kitchenette and bathroom w/shower. Situated in a primarily residential condo development just minutes to and from restaurants, shops, Mill River Park,UCONN, Stamford Hospital and public transportation. Employees can take advantage of a common exercise room for a quick workout or relax in the spacious club room with TV or outside in the scenic courtyard with picnic tables. There is both excellent foot and auto traffic with convenient access for clients/patients. Extra storage is available if required. Parking is no problem with 6 dedicated covered parking spaces! One can't easily find a well managed commercial condo that offers all these benefits so consider checking it out!
