About this home

Charming cape in a very central location. This sale is for both parcels - #8 and 10 and the acreage reflects both lots. 10 Murray Street offers 4 bedrooms, 2 baths, living room, dining room and eat-in-kitchen which leads to the back deck. If you don't need four bedrooms one room can be used for an office, exercise room or den. There is a large basement with 8-foot ceilings, laundry and mechanicals. Since the home was purchased several costly renovations were made; stone patio, hook-up to public sewer and water, hardwood floors installed on the second floor and outdoor fencing was installed. This home is sold with parcel #8 Murray Street. This extra land can offer an amazing outdoor space for parties, events or sports. This home is close to shopping, transit, highways and schools. This listing includes #8 Murray Street along with #10 Murray Street. The acreage for #8 Murray is .18. The acreage for #10 is .17. Taxes are $5,917 for #10 Murray Street and $2,998 for #8 Murray Street. A survey has been done, however, the current owner did not pursue the possibility of building on lot #8. If that is something your client is interested in please do your own due diligence.
