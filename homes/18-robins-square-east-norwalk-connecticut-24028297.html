About this home

This well-maintained Colonial, nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac, features hardwood floors throughout the main level and hardwood floors under the wall-to-wall carpeting upstairs. It offers 3 spacious bedrooms, 1.5 newly renovated baths, central air conditioning, and a security system. The cozy living room boasts a fireplace, and the kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, custom cabinetry, and granite countertops. The heated & cooled lower level can be used as a family room or as an extra bedroom. Outside, you'll discover a serene backyard with a fruit tree, a storage shed, and ample parking provided by a large driveway accommodating up to 5 cars. Conveniently located near all major highways. This home offers both comfort and accessibility.
