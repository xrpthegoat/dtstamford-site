About this home

Rich with classic character this 1918 Colonial sits on one of the most coveted streets in Old Greenwich. With an expansive 3,670 square feet, this charm filled home pairs timeless architectural details. A quintessential wide wraparound front porch greets you, opening into a spacious entry hall that connects the formal living room with high ceilings and into the warm flexible layout., coffered ceiling in the dining room and inviting family room with elevated half cathedral ceiling and skylights. At the center of the home, the family kitchen offers informal dining, a built in family office space and casual seating area, all flowing effortlessly onto an expansive back deck and stone patio. The Perfect private space for indoor-outdoor entertaining and to escape the day. The second floor hosts four light-filled bedrooms and a full bath, while the third level provides a private fifth bedroom suite with its own bath. Set on a .3 acre lot backing up to the scenic grounds of Innis Arden Golf Club, the property offers exceptional privacy. Unbeatable coastal lifestyle offering. Work in NYC? it's a 7 minute walk to the Old Greenwich Train Station or 2 minute drive. Effortless stroll to the village, local favorite King's Market, Binney Park and the seasonal outdoor concert series, living at 14 Highview, including short 9 minute bike ride to Tod's Point beach. Location is everything, start your next chapter of your story here in the heart of Old Greenwich at 14 Highview Avenue!
