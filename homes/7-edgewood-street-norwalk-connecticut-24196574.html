About this home

MASSIVE, renovated, private 3,000+ sq. ft. townhome available in the lovely Brookside neighborhood of Norwalk. This modern home is located just a few blocks from Rowayton and one street from Brookside Elementary and Brian McMahon High School. The home features 4 generously sized bedrooms, including 3 with elegant vaulted ceilings, 2 updated full bathrooms, and 2 half bathrooms. The eat-in kitchen features Samsung appliances, 10+ foot ceilings, and a modern transparent gas fireplace. Additional features include a private fenced-in yard, second-floor laundry, a primary suite, central vacuum, central air, on demand hot water, refinished hardwood floors, 2 car attached garage, and more. If you are looking for spacious, modern, low-maintenance living in one of Norwalk's most convenient neighborhoods, look no further. Available 10/1 move in. Pets are allowed with owner approval and a $50/month pet rent. All applicants must have a 700+ credit score and provide proof of employment, pay stubs, references, and consent to criminal and eviction background checks. 12-24 month lease, tenant pays electric and gas meter (Eversource), tenant required to maintain their grounds (weeding, snow shoveling, de-icing), Tenant required to have $300,000 renters liability insurance, no smoking on premise, pets allowed by case with additional pet rent per animal. Owner pays- water, sewer, trash, recycling.
