About this home

Experience the pinnacle of in-town living in this meticulously reimagined New Canaan residence. Situated in the heart of the village, this home has undergone a complete rebuild and expansion, resulting in a stunning transformation that mirrors the quality of new construction. Spanning three finished floors, the interior offers five bedrooms and 4.5 luxurious baths. The floor plan was thoughtfully designed to balance elegant entertaining with modern functionality. Outside, the property boasts a rare, large backyard and deck-a private oasis perfect for relaxation or play. Enjoy the ultimate convenience of "walk-to-everything" capability, with sidewalk access leading you directly to the schools & the Y, Waveny Park with town pool, local shops, world-class dining, library, and metro north express train to NYC, This turn-key beauty offers the perfect blend of sophisticated design and an unbeatable location.
