About this home

A hidden gem on Sound Beach Avenue - this 1927 cape with 3 bedrooms and 3 baths is filled with modern amenities while maintaining its charming feel. This home features hardwood floors, original ceiling beams, an eat-in kitchen, a living room with gas fireplace, and a separate family room, with natural light at every turn. French doors lead to private patios, as well as a screened porch off the primary bedroom suite, making this house ideal for outdoor entertaining. Enjoy living near Binney Park, Perrot Library, Old Greenwich Village, Metro North train station, and the beach at Tod's Point.
