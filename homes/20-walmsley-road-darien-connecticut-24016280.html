About this home

Nestled in one of Darien's most coveted neighborhoods, this exceptional light-filled 5 bedroom 3.5 bath home with 2 car garage offers the perfect blend of comfort, storage, convenience and timeless appeal. Thoughtfully designed for todays lifestyle with open floor plan for everyday living and effortless entertaining. The chef's kitchen with high end appliances, wine refrigerator, white quartz countertops & oversized island extends seamlessly to the outdoor expanded bluestone patio surrounded by mature plantings, professionally curated perennial gardens and elegant outdoor lighting - perfect for entertaining. Additional main floor highlights include both a living room and family room with fireplaces, and a welcoming mudroom with custom built-in storage and shiplap detailing. The primary suite offers a generous walk-in closet and spa-like marble bath. The versatile third floor provides incredible flexibility with a fifth bedroom, full bath, and spacious additional family room-perfect for guests, home office, au pair or nanny suite. While the finished lower level playroom space provides an additional 504 square foot of living space ideal for recreation, media/playroom or fitness area. A rare opportunity to move right in and embrace the best of summer in Darien. Ideally situated just moments from the train for easy NYC commuting, top-rated schools and the vibrant Darien Commons, this beautifully appointed home offers the rare combination of turnkey living and a premier location.
