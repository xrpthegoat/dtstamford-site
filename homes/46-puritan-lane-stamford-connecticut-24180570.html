About this home

Nestled in the desirable Glenbrook neighborhood of Stamford, this beautifully updated Tudor-style colonial home is a true gem. Step inside to an inviting hallway leading to an open-concept layout, featuring a sunlit living room, seamlessly flowing into a dining area and brand-new kitchen. The kitchen boasts new cabinets, a central island, quartz countertops, and stainless steel appliances-perfect for memorable gatherings and entertaining. Hardwood floors on the main level add timeless warmth. The kitchen opens to a spacious mudroom with a large pantry and a full bathroom with a tub, offering views of a private fenced backyard and access to a newly renovated two-car garage. Upstairs, unwind in a modern, newly renovated full bath and explore three bright, spacious bedrooms, each with ample closet space. A semi-finished walk-out basement adds flexible space for storage or hobbies. Not to be missed!**Please see Agent Notes**
