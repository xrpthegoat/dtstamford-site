About this home

The Sea Breezes will Restore and Soothe you in this Super Charming, Remodeled, Vintage Cottage in Saugatuck Shores, Westport! This is Truly Beach Life! Walk to the Beach for a Swim, or go Kayaking or Sailing! The Screened Front Porch Greets you with Water Views of Saugatuck Harbor! Enjoy your coffee here in the morning and watch the sunrise over the water in the east or have a cocktail on the porch at the end of your day to the sounds of gently lapping waves! The First Floor is Open Plan, a Surprisingly Spacious Living Room has enough space for everyone to gather and lounge. The Dining Area has terrific open organized shelving for all your pantry needs and windows overlooking and access to the rear deck and garden. The remodeled kitchen has a newer stainless steel, electric, convection range and microwave hood, granite counters, white subway tile backsplashes, a granite breakfast bar and a cute white fridge that matches the freshly painted kitchen cabinets! The first floor bathroom is also remodeled with white marble mosaic tile floors! Three Bedrooms and another remodeled bathroom are upstairs! New energy efficient windows replaced 20 of the 26 windows. All of the charming window treatments will remain!
