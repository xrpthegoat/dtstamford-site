About this home

Welcome this spacious 4-bedroom colonial, situated in North Stamford. Tucked away at the end of a long, picturesque driveway surrounded by lush greenery and mature trees. With its impressive curb appeal, this home offers the perfect blend of classic charm and modern comfort. Step inside to experience the generous living spaces, recently refinished floors and abundant natural light, creating a warm atmosphere to enjoy by all. Surround sound system throughout. The first floor with a double height entry leads to a sun-drenched dining and living room connecting the butler's pantry to the kitchen. The office with built-in bookcases, great room with stone FP leads to the generously sized and well-appointed eat in kitchen that opens up to the back patio making it the perfect place to BBQ and entertain. A laundry room and mud room off of the 2-4 car garage complete this floor. The garage has 2 lifts that can be sold with home. The secondary back staircase leads to one of the 2 enormous bonus spaces this home offers. A few steps up are the 4 bedrooms complete with a primary en-suite as well as a hall bath attached to one of the additional 3 bedrooms. The 3rd floor offers the 2nd bonus space with an additional 680+ sq footage not included in the sq footage with a full bath. This is an incredible space with endless possibilities. An additional unfinished 2200 sq feet in walk out basement complete this home. Your dream home awaits.
