About this home

Welcome to Casa Nossa at 91 Clovelly Road, a beautifully reimagined residence nestled in Stamford's highly sought after Glenbrook neighborhood, where timeless elegance meets modern comfort. This stunning home offers an exceptional layout where the spacious living room sets the tone with warmth and sophistication, while the oversized dining room provides the perfect backdrop for memorable gatherings. And just beyond, the area flows seamlessly into a newly updated sunroom or private office. Thoughtfully updated throughout, the home features modernized bathrooms and freshly painted interiors, delivering a clean contemporary aesthetic with a refined touch. The lower level presents a flexible bonus space ideal for a home gym, office or custom expansion. Step outside to a generous backyard retreat with newly installed stone patio and enjoy dining al fresco or quiet relaxation while savoring the outdoors. The property is further enhanced by two private driveways and a detached garage with electricity that is currently utilized as an artist's studio - a unique and inspiring bonus space perfect for creatives, a workshop or yoga studio. At Casa Nossa, every detail has been curated to create a warm yet elevated living experience, all within a prime location close to downtown Stamford.
