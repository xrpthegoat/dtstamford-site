About this home

Experience the beauty of North Stamford in this immaculate Cape with CITY WATER, set on a private acre with endless expansion possibilities. The main level offers comfortable living space with three bedrooms, an inviting living room with fireplace, and a formal dining room that opens to a bright, updated kitchen with granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. The primary bedroom includes a classic bathroom with an elegant clawfoot tub and marble tile floor and a modern hall bathroom with a walk- in shower. The second floor opens to a spacious room that could serve as a fourth bedroom or playroom, along with a half-bath and unfinished area that could be finished to add more living space. Relax on the patio overlooking a gorgeous level yard with mature plantings, plus a detached two-car garage with loft for additional storage. Other highlights include central air, 200-amp service and more!
