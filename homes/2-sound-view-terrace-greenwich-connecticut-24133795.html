About this home

Renovated Single-Family Home on Private Drive in Central Greenwich-The open floor plan features 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and a dedicated office. The formal living room includes a fireplace and French doors that lead to a bluestone patio-perfect for entertaining or quiet evenings outdoors. The chef's kitchen is a standout, with a quartz island, Thermador appliances, wine fridge, and access to a second patio overlooking a level, professionally landscaped yard with gardens and a full irrigation system. Upstairs, the primary suite opens to a private deck and includes a luxurious bath with heated floors, rain shower, and spacious walk-in closets. The finished lower level is perfect for a home gym or recreation space. A charming pebble driveway leads to a one-car garage, adding the perfect finishing touch to this exceptional home. Located just minutes from Greenwich Avenue, the train, restaurants, and shopping, this home offers the rare combination of privacy, elegance, and unbeatable convenience
