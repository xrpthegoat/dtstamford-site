About this home

Wonderful opportunity for some lucky buyer, this sunny 3 bedroom 2 bath ranch has been well loved by original owner and is waiting for a new chapter to begin. Enjoy water views of Cove Marina over your neighbors rooftop while drinking your morning coffee. Living room with fireplace, dining area and eat-in-kitchen. Primary bedroom with full bath plus two additional family bedrooms. Hardwood floors on main level except kitchen and baths. The walk-out lower level includes family room with laundry facilities, retro bar and large one car garage. New furnace (approx. 1 yr old) and new oil tank. Slider leads to patio. This home has central air and is less than 1/2 mile from Cove Island Park and all it has to offer. 2.2 Miles to Stamford Train Station/2.5 miles to Noroton Train Station. Estate Sale/As is.
