About this home

A private retreat designed for easy, modern living. Tucked away from the road on 1.86 beautifully landscaped acres, this bright and airy ranch combines a sleek, clean aesthetic with an open concept floor plan and vaulted ceilings throughout. Thoughtfully maintained and move in ready, the main residence features an updated kitchen and baths, fresh interior and exterior paint, an enclosed porch, and a spacious primary suite with new wall to wall carpeting. Two additional generously sized bedrooms and a flexible layout easily accommodate a variety of lifestyles and needs. Adding exceptional versatility is a separate, legally rentable accessory apartment with an updated kitchen and full bath, studio living space, office or flex room, private entrance, patio and dedicated parking. Ideal for supplemental income, guests, an in law suite or private home office. Peaceful and private yet convenient to town, schools, shopping and commuting, this turnkey home offers the rare combination of serenity, flexibility and effortless living. A portable generator is included. Estate Sale.
