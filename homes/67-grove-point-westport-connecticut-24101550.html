About this home

Charming beach cottage nestled in a serene Compo Beach cul-de-sac, this delightful home offers a perfect blend of comfort and coastal living. Featuring vaulted ceilings throughout that enhance its spacious feel, a cozy stone fireplace, and elegant wood floors, the open floor plan creates an inviting atmosphere. The sunroom leads to a stone patio, ideal for gatherings, overlooking a fenced, meticulously manicured backyard, providing both privacy and a picturesque outdoor retreat. Enjoy the convenience of walking or biking to Compo Beach, just moments away, and the proximity to town amenities adds to the appeal of this idyllic seaside retreat. Perfect for those seeking a relaxed lifestyle in a sought-after beach community, bring your paddle board, kayak and bike!
