About this home

Secluded Cottage Retreat in Silvermine - A Rare Rental Opportunity! Nestled in the heart charming Silvermine neighborhood, 69 Comstock Hill Avenue offers the ultimate in privacy and tranquility. This delightful two-bedroom, two-bathroom cottage sits at the end of a long driveway, surrounded by nature for a truly serene escape. Step inside to a spacious family room, where a grand fireplace with a rustic mantle and exposed ceiling beams creates a cozy ambiance. Just beyond, a screened-in porch invites you to relax and enjoy the fresh air-without the bother of bugs. The versatile second bedroom can easily transform into a home office, perfect for those who work remotely. Meanwhile, the retro-style kitchen adds a touch of charm and nostalgia to your everyday routine. The walk-out lower level provides generous storage space and direct access to the backyard, offering a tucked-away hidden retreat. If you're looking for a home that feels like a peaceful hideaway yet remains close to everything, this Silvermine cottage is a rare find.
