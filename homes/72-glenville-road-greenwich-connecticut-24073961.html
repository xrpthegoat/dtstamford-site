About this home

Summer rental- Gracious stone Georgian that has a fabulous blend of indoor and outdoor relaxing and entertaining spaces. This stunning home has spacious and bright rooms with high ceilings and beautiful furnishings and details. Formal living and dining rooms leads to paneled library. The great room has vaulted and beamed ceilings, pool table and French doors to patio and pool. The eat-in kitchen, opens to family room with vaulted and beamed ceiling. Upstairs the primary suite has a walk-in closet and lux bath. There are four more bedrooms, four baths and study/office. The garden level has a theater and gym space. Outside is perfect with a stunning pool and spa, pool house, beautifully landscaped grounds and large terrace. Level yard is great for play. Close to town, train and ferry boats.
