About this home

Privately situated overlooking a picturesque pond, this meticulously maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bath home combines space, comfort and tranquility in desirable North Stamford. Set back in its own private, serene setting, the property offers a peaceful, retreat-like feel that makes every day feel like a getaway. A skylit foyer with double staircase welcomes you into the bright interior. The sun-filled living room with fireplace and formal dining room showcase beautiful pond views. The spacious kitchen features a center island, 6-burner Dacor cooktop, double ovens, Sub-Zero refrigerator, wet bar with wine cooler, abundant cabinetry, built-in pantry organizers and pull-out storage. The light-filled primary suite includes a walk-in cedar closet and skylit bath with heated floors, whirlpool tub and separate shower. Both main-level bedrooms feature distinctive varied-width plank hardwood floors. The walk-out lower level includes two additional bedrooms, an office, full bath and family/entertainment room with wood-burning stove and wet bar. French doors open to the patio and yard. Additional highlights include a whole-house generator, UV purification system and newer water heater. Just a quick car ride to shopping, dining, parks and everyday conveniences.
