About this home

This beautifully renovated Ward French custom home offers elegant formal spaces, a chef's kitchen, flexible guest and office accommodations, a luxury primary suite, fitness studio and sitting room with balcony. The home's 2,442 square foot lower level offers tremendous untapped potential, providing an opportunity to create customized luxury amenities such as a home theater, golf simulator, wine cellar, wellness spa, or guest retreat. Outdoors a spectacular 20' x 40' pool and spa, multiple entertaining patios, and lush private landscaping create a true retreat. Moments from schools and town amenities, this exceptional Westport residence perfectly balances luxury and everyday living.
