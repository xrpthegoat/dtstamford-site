About this home

If you're looking for walkability in a lively urban boating community this is the place to be! One block from Norwalk Harbor, surrounded by award winning restaurant's, sport facilities, parks, coffee shops and only two blocks from the South Norwalk train station. This is the first time this main floor apartment has come to the market. Only one of two units. Noteworthy highlights include high ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, large windows, generous sized bedrooms, private laundry and NON-SHARED use the full basement. INTERNET and RING CAMERA INCLUDED. Plus 95 channel TV use - all rooms including the front porch are set up to plug a TV right in and enjoy. The basement is unfinished however a perfect place for gym/yoga space, home office, gaming nook with plenty of room for kayak, bike and paddleboard storage too. Shared with the 2nd floor tenant: delightful backyard for BBQ's and gardening, covered vintage front porch. 2-car tantum parking also included!
