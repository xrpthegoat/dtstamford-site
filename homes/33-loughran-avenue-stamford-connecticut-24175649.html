About this home

This spacious 4 bedroom, 3 full bath raised ranch offers a perfect blend of comfort and style with beautiful river views from multiple vantage points. Located in a serene and convenient neighborhood, the home boasts 2 patios overlooking a private backyard, ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The upper level features beautiful hardwood floors and an open, airy floor plan. The kitchen, living and dining room are perfect for hosting guests or enjoying peaceful moments. The lower level, with durable laminate flooring, includes a large family room with fireplace and sliders to the patio, a 4th bedroom, a full bath and a large laundry room with loads of storage. 2 car oversized garage with newer doors. Recently painted. Perfectly situated with easy access to all Stamford amenities, this home is a true gem. Don't miss the chance to make it yours. Must have excellent credit and references. Tenant responsible for all utilities, landscaping, seasonal turn on/off of sprinkler and snow removal. No smoking or vaping.
