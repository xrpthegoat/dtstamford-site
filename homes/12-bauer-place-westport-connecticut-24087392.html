About this home

Expected for completion summer 2025, this new construction by AR Luxury Homes is situated on a sought-after cul-de-sac in Long Lots. This 5/6 bedroom, 6.5-bath residence combines thoughtful design, exceptional craftsmanship, & modern luxury across four beautifully finished floors. This open-concept home is designed to impress, with generously proportioned living spaces perfect for today's lifestyle. Expansive windows and 10ft ceilings on the first floor will bathe the interior in natural light, & premium finishes have been carefully selected to create an environment of sophistication. The chef's kitchen will feature Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry, & a large center island, flowing seamlessly into the living and dining areas. The walk-out lower level offers areas for entertaining, fitness, and an additional bedroom, while the upper floors will provide private retreats for rest and relaxation. The primary suite with vaulted ceilings includes a spa-like bathroom & custom walk-in closet. The secondary bedrooms are well proportioned and all en-suite. Step outside to a backyard designed for relaxation & play with room for a pool. Whether enjoying quiet mornings on the patio or hosting summer gatherings, this outdoor space will elevate your lifestyle. Located in the desirable Long Lots neighborhood, 12 Bauer Lane offers proximity to Westport's top-rated schools, vibrant downtown area, stunning beaches, and all of the amenities that make this town special.
