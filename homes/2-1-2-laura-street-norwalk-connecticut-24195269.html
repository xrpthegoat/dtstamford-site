About this home

Why rent when you can own? This inviting 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse condo in the heart of South Norwalk offers the perfect opportunity for first-time buyers, commuters, or anyone looking to build equity while enjoying a low-maintenance lifestyle. The spacious living room flows into an eat-in kitchen with sliders leading to your own private patio -ideal for morning coffee or relaxing outdoors. Upstairs, you'll find new carpeting, two generously sized bedrooms, a full bath, and the convenience of in-unit laundry. The kitchen is ready for your personal touch, with no appliances included, giving you the freedom to design the space exactly the way you envision it. Additional highlights include two reserved parking spaces directly outside your front door, low HOA fees, and a location that's hard to beat-just minutes from The SoNo Collection mall, the South Norwalk Train Station, restaurants, shopping, and beach. Don't miss this chance to make homeownership a reality in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations!
