Privacy

The uploader requests permission to upload videos and read basic YouTube account information. It checks the authorized channel ID before publishing and reads back the video ID and visibility to confirm the result.

The worker stores its OAuth grant, original video files, and upload receipts on the channel operator’s Mac. It sends the clips, titles, descriptions, and selected visibility to YouTube. Google processes sign-in, consent, and video hosting under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google account data is used to operate the uploader. The uploader does not sell it, provide it to advertising services, or send it to a model. Local lesson generation uses public release notes. This informational site does not have sign-in, forms, or analytics scripts.

The operator can disconnect the uploader through Google Account connections and remove its local authorization data. Google controls data held by YouTube.
