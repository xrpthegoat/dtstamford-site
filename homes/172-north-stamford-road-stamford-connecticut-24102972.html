About this home

Vermont in Fairfield County, Connecticut! This Country House in prime North Stamford offers 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms along with numerous modern updates for how we truly live life today. A gated driveway and stone steps lead to the charming foyer, front-to-back living room, formal dining room and great room/family room featuring restored wide-plank floors, exposed beams, and three antique fireplaces. The comfortable kitchen, sunny breakfast nook, updated powder room and porch overlook gently rolling lawns framed by rock walls, perennial gardens, and a picturesque pond with floating dock in the backyard. Spacious primary suite with wood-burning fireplace and a fully renovated limestone bathroom on the second floor. There are three more bedrooms upstairs (one with fireplace) and a hallway bathroom that is ideal for weekend guests or family. Perfect harmony of smart, high-efficiency systems, Ecobee climate control, propane clean energy, multi-zone HVAC & A/C plus artistic lighting throughout the home. New roof, new irrigation system, and freshly painted lower level with storage areas for a move-in-ready experience-- all completed within last 2-3 years. Coveted North Stamford is 20 minutes from Westchester Airport and downtown Stamford train station with service to Manhattan in less than an hour; boutique shops and fine dining in both downtown, New Canaan, and Bedford, NY just minutes away. Live your dream, surrounded by nature's beauty at every turn.
