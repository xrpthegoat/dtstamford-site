About this home

Step into this magical home and be transported to the South of France-Provence in Rowayton! Bathed in natural light, the residence blends refined, understated elegance with intimate yet open living spaces. Throughout the home, French doors open to serene outdoor settings including a koi pond, private sitting areas, and tranquil pebbled gardens. The main house features a beautiful living room with fireplace and soaring two-story ceiling, a charming Provencal-inspired dining room, and a thoughtfully designed kitchen. A 1st floor primary suite offers comfort and convenience, while the 2nd floor loft provides Five Mile River and treetop views along with an additional bedroom and full bath. "The Smithy," originally built in 1775 and completely rebuilt in 2002, is a charming detached structure offering a versatile office or studio space with full bath and its own private patio -perfect for guests, work-from-home, or creative pursuits. A delightful barn/garage features a lattice wall that welcomes natural light and frames views of the garden and wisteria vine. The entire property was thoughtfully designed by Barbara Garfield, celebrated for her distinctive attention to detail. Special features include wrought iron accents, random-width flooring, reclaimed wood and beams from historic barns in Connecticut and the South of France. All in the heart of Rowayton. Walk to shops, restaurants, beach, town dock, tennis, pickleball, library, dog park and train to NYC. Truly a special offering
