About this home

HIGHEST and BEST offers by 3pm Tuesday March 10th. The open property and location can't be beat! This home is perfectly situated in a highly sought after neighborhood within walking distance to schools and the NRVT. Train and town center are also close by. This 4 bedroom 2.5 bath home has great flow with a large living room, fireplace, and picture window leading to the dining area with sliders out to the deck and expansive back yard. Freshly painted throughout, this sunny home is move in ready. A convenient mudroom off the garage entry also contains the washer, dryer - perfect for dropping off shoes, jackets or muddy clothes and gear before entering the large downstairs rec room highlighted by an appealing bar and open shelves on the far end of the room. There is also a half bath and bedroom on this level. Two sets of staircases lead to the main level with an inviting modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a sleek countertop with barstools. Three additional bedrooms and two full baths round out this level. Outdoors, the expansive cleared and open grounds offer a veritable playground for young and old. Wilton has a wonderful sense of community and reverence for nature and this comfortable Catalpa home is anchored in the heart of it all.
