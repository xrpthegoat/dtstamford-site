About this home

What happens when an architect and an interior designer craft a bespoke sanctuary for themselves? The result is that nearly impossible to find unicorn in the world of residential design - a unique, magical space that speaks to those of us that crave exceptional refinement along with livable practicality. Nestled high amid a vibrant New England landscape, this 2023 frame-in restoration project by Wimmer & Wimmer Design is in the home stretch. The organically dark facade adds a sense of intrigue to its classically modern roots. Expansive glass frames up soothing treetop views. The interiors are decked out in the best couture materials: Villeroy & Boch, Dornbracht, Marvin, Ceasarstone, Wolf, Sub-Zero, Robern, Rocky Mountain hardware, and Clopay plus more. The expertly designed kitchen by NuKitchens has every accoutrement a culinary enthusiast could wish for. The bathrooms are sheer perfection - curbless showers, glass enclosures, floating vanities, and artful tile-work. The luscious primary suite is a wellness escape including a stunning 70 inch almond tub. From the glass side stairs to the 6-inch rift sawn hardwoods, the Porcelanosa tiles to the Trustile doors, the architectural trim to the brilliant LED lighting, the entire space radiates minimalist-luxe. This everyday retreat is replete with a gunite swimming pool, stone patios, and clay tennis court. The collection of special homes in and around Greenbrier create an enchanting vibe.
