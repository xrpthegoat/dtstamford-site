About this home

The One You've Been Waiting For! This stunningly renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home is packed with unbelievable upgrades and designed for modern living! From the moment you step inside, you'll fall in love with the open-concept layout, wide-plank white oak floors, and designer details throughout. The gorgeous eat-in kitchen features marble countertops, sleek stainless steel appliances, a spacious island for hosting, and a custom pantry to keep everything organized. The Primary Suite is next-level with a huge walk-in closet and dreamy dressing room and walk in closet. Plus, a bonus third-floor family room and a lower level with flex space give you all the space for work, fitness, or fun. Too many upgrades to list here, including a brand-new boiler and hot water heater. Start your mornings on the expansive front porch, coffee in hand, watching the neighborhood come to life. Out back, the fully fenced-in yard and gorgeous stone patio create a private retreat perfect for outdoor dinners, entertaining friends, or unwinding under the stars. And the best part? You're just steps from shops, restaurants, parks, and the waterfront. Everything you love about charming Rowayton is at your fingertips - a rare find you won't want to miss!
