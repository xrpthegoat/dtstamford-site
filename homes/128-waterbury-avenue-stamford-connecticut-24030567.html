About this home

Embrace the chance to own a piece of Stamford's history while enjoying the conveniences of modern living. This home offers great potential for those with a vision to create their dream space in a highly desirable location. Don't miss the opportunity to make this historic gem your own! With 2,190 square feet of living space, this residence offers a blend of historic character and modern convenience. This spacious home boasts 4 bedrooms and 2.1 baths, providing ample space for family living and guests. Enjoy the perks of living in a prime location, just a short walk from Cove Island Beach, Holly Pond, and the Chelsea Piers sports facility. The home is also conveniently close to highways and downtown Stamford, ensuring easy access to shopping, dining, and entertainment. The property includes a one-car attached garage and features durable vinyl siding. A covered porch provides a perfect spot to unwind on hot summer days. While the property does need work, it presents a fantastic opportunity for those looking to restore and customize a historic home to their liking.
