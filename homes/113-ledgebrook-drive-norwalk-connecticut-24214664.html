About this home

Tucked away in a tranquil private community, this stylish three-bedroom, two-full-bath end unit offers a bright, contemporary open floor plan and beautiful views of the surrounding landscape. An oversized wall of windows fills the home with natural light, while the formal living and dining rooms are connected by a handsome two-sided brick fireplace. Sliding doors open to a spacious deck, creating an ideal setting for entertaining or relaxing outdoors. The beautifully updated kitchen features white shaker cabinetry, top-of-the-line stainless-steel appliances, a wine refrigerator, and a stylish backsplash. The primary bedroom offers a wall of closets and a completely renovated private bath, complemented by two additional generously sized bedrooms and another full bath. Additional features include an in-unit washer and dryer, a utility room, and ample storage space. The professionally landscaped complex is centered around a picturesque pond and fountain, with a wooden bridge leading to the clubhouse and in-ground pool overlooking the water. Conveniently located minutes from Metro-North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, with easy access to South Norwalk's restaurants, shopping, nightlife, and attractions.
