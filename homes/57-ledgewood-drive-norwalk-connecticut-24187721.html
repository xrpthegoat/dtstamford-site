About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST BY 7/15 AT 5PM Discover the flexibility and potential of this spacious ranch-style home in desirable West Norwalk, offering 2,507 square feet of living space, a legal in-law apartment, and room to fit a variety of lifestyles. The main level features an inviting, light-filled layout enhanced by numerous skylights, hardwood floors throughout, and a cozy fireplace that serves as the focal point of the dining room. The kitchen opens to a deck overlooking the private, fenced half-acre yard complete with an above-ground pool-perfect for summer entertaining and outdoor enjoyment. The home offers 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, with the possibility of a 5th bedroom on the lower level. The spacious primary suite includes a large walk-in closet and an en-suite bath, creating a comfortable private retreat. A dedicated home office with a built-in desk that provides an ideal workspace for today's remote professional. The lower level features a legal in-law apartment, offering excellent flexibility for multi-generational living, short term guests, or potential rental income. A two-car attached garage provides convenience and additional storage. Situated on a peaceful half-acre lot in a sought-after West Norwalk neighborhood, this property combines space, versatility, and endless potential.
