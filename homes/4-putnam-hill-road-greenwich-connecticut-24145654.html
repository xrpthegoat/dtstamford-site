About this home

Experience the charm of arriving at Putnam Hill-10 acres of meticulously landscaped grounds in the heart of Greenwich. This unique community features five beautiful brick buildings, ample parking, and a welcoming residential feel, all just steps from the center of town. Leave the car at home and enjoy easy access to dining, shopping, transportation, worship, and countless local activities. This spacious residence offers two bedrooms and two bathrooms, a generous living and dining area, a walk-in closet, and a large private balcony-perfect for relaxing or entertaining outdoors. Co-mingled with the Putnam Park Cooperative Townhomes, Putnam Hill combines classic history with the ease and convenience of apartment-style cooperative living, all within a vibrant and inviting community.
