About this home

Stunning Renovated Home Just Steps from the Beach! This home is ideally located within walking distance to the beach in the desirable Soundview Manor steps away from the Long Island Sound. This pristine property offers the perfect blend of modern updates and classic charm.. Step inside to a sun-drenched interior filled with warmth and character. The chef's kitchen - a true showstopper, featuring stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a stylish tile backsplash, and a breakfast bar perfect for morning coffee or casual meals. Main level has multiple living spaces which creates welcoming space for hosting holiday gatherings or cozy nights in. Main level has a primary bedroom and fully renovated first-floor bath complete with gorgeous marble tile flooring, a contemporary vanity, and a spa-inspired shower-round out the main level. Upstairs, 2 spacious bedrooms with sweeping views of the manicured gardens provide a true retreat. The lower level is fully finished which offers plenty of flexible space, including a full bathroom, laundry area and 2 large living spaces which can be used for a 2nd family room, gym, or flex space. Additional highlights include a new HVAC/Gas Furnace (2025), driveway/sidewalk resealed, 1-car detached garage, and a large deck overlooking the meticulously landscaped, level backyard-perfect for outdoor entertaining. Enjoy the ultimate coastal lifestyle with close proximity to Cove Island Park, West Beach, or Cummings Park, Metro North, and I-95!
