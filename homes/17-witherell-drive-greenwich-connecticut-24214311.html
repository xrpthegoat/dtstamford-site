About this home

Unique opportunity to rent this newly updated residence in the heart of the coveted Rock Ridge Association. Set on an exceptionally private, level 2 acre park-like property, this prime mid-country location offers estate-like privacy just minutes from downtown. Enjoy peaceful views of Horseneck Brook and adjacent conservation land. The charming 5-bedroom home features a primary suite plus four additional bedrooms, including an oversized bedroom ideal for an au pair/in-law suite with its own private entrance as well as access from the second-floor hall. Highlights include three fireplaces, a screened-in dining and lounging porch, and a lower-level family room. A rare combination of privacy, space, convenience and natural beauty.
