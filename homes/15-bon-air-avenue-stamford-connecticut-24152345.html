About this home

Short walk to the Springdale Train Station from this 3 bedroom 2 bath rental, and also close to the Merritt Parkway. Enjoy the Panoramic view from the Living Room picture window that looks out over Deck and rear of the property. You can even see LI Sound. Primary Bedroom with en suite bath. The two other bedrooms share the hallway full bath. Eat-in Kitchen, 1 Car Garage, partial basement and attic storage. This is in the Heart Of Springdale with fine restaurants, shopping, skating rink, library and so much more. Freshly painted, brand new Dishwasher and Range/Stove. Very good credit score required. Disclosure: During some months of the year after heavy rain, water can seep into the basement through sections of the wall, specifically behind the washer and dryer and below the storage shelves to the right of the staircase. This can result in standing water. I provide a shop vacuum for tenant use, but any items susceptible to water damage should not be placed directly on the basement floor.
