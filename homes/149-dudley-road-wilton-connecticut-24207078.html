About this home

Originally built in 1726 and extensively remodeled, reimagined and re-engineered in 2007, 149 Dudley Road is a rare country estate where nearly three centuries of history meet sophisticated modern living. Set behind gates on more than five magnificent level acres, this exceptional property combines timeless New England character, beautifully considered interiors and extraordinary amenities in an idyllic, park-like setting. The main residence is warm, elegant and filled with natural light, with wide-board floors, handsome millwork, dramatic stone fireplaces, original architectural details and inviting covered and wraparound porches that honor the home's remarkable heritage. Generously scaled rooms and an open, effortless flow are complemented by a stunning kitchen and beautifully appointed baths featuring luxurious finishes that are both classic and timeless. Designed equally for intimate everyday living and memorable entertaining, the home extends seamlessly outdoors. An exceptional outdoor living room with fireplace and pergola, expansive tiered terraces, heated pool and spa create a private resort-like setting surrounded by mature landscaping, sweeping lawns and a picturesque freshwater pond. Adding another dimension to the property is the extraordinary three-story carriage barn-a destination unto itself.
