About this home

*Please submit highest and best offers by 10am Monday 5/12* This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and charm. Ideally located just minutes from both Westport and East Norwalk train stations, shopping, and a variety of local amenities. The lower level welcomes you with a functional entryway, a spacious family room with sliding doors, a dedicated home office, and laundry room. Upstairs, natural light floods the bright and airy living and dining areas which open to a private back patio-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The generously sized eat-in kitchen features updated cabinetry and appliances. The primary suite boasts a renovated en-suite bath, complemented by two additional bedrooms and a full hallway bathroom. Outside, the meticulously maintained, fully fenced yard includes an irrigation system and provides a serene setting for gatherings. Additional storage is available in the outdoor shed and attic. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac yet close to everything, this move-in-ready gem is the one you've been waiting for!
