About this home

Peacefully placed overlooking Signal Lake on a Greens Farms cul-de-sac is 4 Wake Robin Road, a classic Colonial with so much to offer! With over an acre of flat, private and blooming property, the home sits far back from the road with wooded and water views. Filled with historic charm, the main floor is bright and sunny - there are formal living and dining rooms with built-ins and cozy decorative fireplaces. The large family room has plenty of space for gathering and lounging, and connects to the beautiful screened in porch, perfect for 3 season enjoyment. The kitchen is anchored by a large island with cooktop, there are double wall ovens and a dining area. An office and powder room complete the main floor. Upstairs holds the primary suite, another ensuite bedroom, two bedrooms and a flexible bedroom/office space that share a hall bath as well as the laundry room. There is also a walk up attic space for plenty of storage. A pretty brick patio spans the front of the home with views of the rolling lawn, while there is a lush green backyard with access to the lake for a canoe rise or paddle board! There is a two car garage, and double driveway areas for sports and guest parking. Conveniently located to town, schools, the Greens Farms train station, and of course, Westport beaches!
