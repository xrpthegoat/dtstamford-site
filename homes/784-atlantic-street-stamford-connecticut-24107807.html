About this home

Summer by LI Sound in Harbor Point! Stunningly renovated (like new construction) 3BR, 2FBA home w/separate office (almost 1600sf of living space!) 3-5 blocks to restaurants, waterfront boardwalk, Stamford Metro North Train & I-95. And, while it is just as easy to walk to downtown Stamford restaurants, entertainment & shopping, there is also a free trolley connecting all of Harbor Pt w/downtown, too. Step inside to modern, open, airy, light-filled spaces. Expansive living room is an impressive area for all to gather. Sparkling kitchen w/breakfast nook features stone counters & ss appliances. 2 gorgeous FBAs ensure no need to compromise. Wide, bright hallway leads to 3 BRs, each w/beautiful wood floors & large windows for excellent natural light. Brand new C/A & in-unit W/D. Outside, relish amazing greenspace for play & 2 parking spaces. Along the Gold Coast of Fairfield County, Harbor Pt is one of the nation's largest luxury urban redevelopments. From live music, movie nights, farmer's market & fitness events at Commons Park or by the boardwalk, or simply a night out, this incredible community is an extension of your backyard. Walk to dinner at Fortina or Sign of the Whale, hop on the summer water taxi to Prime or Crab Shell, enjoy the views of the Sound & Manhattan in the distance or set sail from the marina. Everything else you could want or need is just 1-3mi away & just over 35mi to Manhattan. Welcome to vibrant, walkable waterfront living & easy commuting in Connecticut!
