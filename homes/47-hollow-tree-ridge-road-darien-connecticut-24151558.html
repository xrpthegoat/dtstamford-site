About this home

Owner will consider Unfurnished or partially furnished for longer term lease. A rare, unique "Nathaniel B. Weed" historic Antique Federal Colonial home in Darien, CT updated with modern conveniences. 3 BR, 2 BA townhouse- style unit where all levels are loaded with charm and character. Formal Living Room with woodstove, formal Dining Room, Updated Kitchen with walk-out to spacious deck. Primary Bedroom Suite with Athletic Whirlpool tub, custom walk-in closet/dressing area, hi ceilings & skylight. Features include a new Mansard roof, wide plank flooring, hand hewn beams, fireplace, newer kitchen with stainless steel appliances and washer dryer in unit. Two-Tiered Deck with gas BBQ Grill off kitchen overlooking large, private backyard with plenty of level play area. Located in award-winning Hindley School district. Walk to train, schools, beaches, shopping. Convenient to I-95 and ideal commute to Stamford or NYC. Other flexible Rental terms are available. Call Agent for details. Close to Noroton Heights Train, beaches, shops, exercise, YMCA, Equinox, and Whole Foods. Pet may be considered. Unfurnished possible with 1+ year lease. Occupancy Negotiable
