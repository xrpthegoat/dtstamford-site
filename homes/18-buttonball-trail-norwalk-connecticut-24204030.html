About this home

Welcome to 18 Buttonball Trail. Space, comfort and a backyard made for entertaining. This home features 4 Bedrooms, 3 baths, newly refinished hardwood floors, spacious great room w/custom build-in shelving adding character, functionality with extra storage. The well-appointed galley kitchen features rich wood cabinetry, ample counter space, tiled flooring and double oven. Kitchen is open to dining room & great room that walk out to a huge deck, perfect for entertaining summer cookouts! Beyond the deck is a large, level backyard offering privacy and plenty of room for gardening and outdoor activities. A detached garage, private driveway, attractive landscaping and welcoming paver walkway complete the property. Fully finished basement provides valuable bonus space that can serve as a recreation room, studio, gym, workshop, etc. Basement also has full bathroom. This home truly has it all! Tucked away in a peaceful, private setting while still conveniently located near shops, restaurants, and everyday amenities.
