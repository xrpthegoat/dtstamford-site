About this home

Welcome home to this bright and beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. Conveniently located just minutes from the Glenbrook Metro-North train station, this home offers an easy commute while being close to shopping, restaurants, parks, and everyday conveniences. Inside, you'll find hardwood floors throughout, abundant natural light, and a thoughtfully updated interior. The renovated bathroom features luxurious heated floors, adding extra comfort during the colder months. The spacious layout provides comfortable living with two well-sized bedrooms and a welcoming living area. Additional amenities include shared washer and dryer in the basement and one off-street parking space included in the rent.
