About this home

This terrific 2-bedroom corner-unit townhouse in Hill-An-Dale Village is filled with natural sunlight and offers a wonderful sense of privacy. One of the standout features is the generous outdoor area with a private entrance through the side gate, creating an inviting space for relaxing or entertaining. The main level features a spacious living and dining area with hardwood flooring and a well-appointed kitchen with new stainless steel appliances, including the refrigerator, dishwasher, and microwave. Upstairs, you'll find two bedrooms and two recently updated full bathrooms, offering added comfort and a fresh, modern feel. The second bedroom also features a cozy loft area, adding a touch of extra character and versatility to the space. Additional features include in-unit laundry, assigned parking, and guest parking. Residents of Hill-An-Dale Village also enjoy access to the in-ground community pool and playground. Conveniently located on Hope Street, the home offers easy access to Downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, parks, and major roadways, providing a great combination of privacy and everyday convenience.
