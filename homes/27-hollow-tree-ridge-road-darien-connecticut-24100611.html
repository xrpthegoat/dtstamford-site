About this home

As good as it gets FURNISHED rental property. Shorter terms rentals (4-6 months) will be considered on a case by case basis. Stylishly updated and furnished, this charming colonial has 3 bedrooms and 2.1 baths and everything else a renter could possibly want. The main floor is configured with a formal dining room and spacious living room with fireplace. The recently renovated eat-in kitchen has stainless steel appliances and a cozy breakfast area. The second floor primary bedroom suite has a walk-in closet and an updated full bath. Two additional upstairs bedrooms share a second full bath. The finished lower level not only has a large family room/playroom but also a 4-season sun room with abundant natural light over seeing the scenic back yard. A deck and stone patio along with an attached two car garage round of this terrific offering. Convenient to town, train, schools and beaches, this one will be snapped up quickly!
