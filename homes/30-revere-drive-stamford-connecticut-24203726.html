About this home

Looking for a beautifully renovated, move-in-ready home? This could be the one! Bright and spacious second-floor, 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment with no one above, featuring two generously sized bedrooms with fantastic closet space. Tenant pays electric, cooking gas, and cable/internet-good credit and income required.
