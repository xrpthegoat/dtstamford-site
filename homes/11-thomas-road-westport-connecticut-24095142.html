About this home

Live the ultimate Westport lifestyle from this serene hilltop retreat just 1700 feet from Main Street. Lounge by your private pool, surrounded by nature, then stroll into town for coffee, shopping, or dinner - all within minutes. It's the best of both worlds: peaceful seclusion with unbeatable convenience. This expanded luxury Cape offers three finished levels with four official bedrooms plus a flexible fifth - ideal whether you're upsizing or downsizing. The main floor provides sought-after single-level living, featuring a spacious primary suite with a renovated full bath and walk-in closet. Living spaces include a classic living room and den, along with a bright, airy open kitchen. The dramatic dining room - with its cathedral ceiling, skylight, fireplace, and French doors - opens to a high deck with sweeping views over the Saugatuck River valley. The mudroom/laundry connects directly to the garage for added convenience. Upstairs, three generous bedrooms share a full bath off a balcony landing with a cozy sitting nook. The finished walk-out lower level offers endless flexibility - ideal for a gym, playroom, home office, or lounge - plus a fifth bedroom and third full bath. The grounds feel like a private park, with a resort-style backyard complete with pool, spa-like decks, and lush landscaping. Quality construction, fine finishes, and excellent condition make this home a rare find just steps from downtown. [INTERIOR Photos Expected Friday 5/16]
