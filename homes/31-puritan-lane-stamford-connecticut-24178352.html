About this home

Nestled in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods with 10 minute walk to the New Canaan line, 31 Puritan Lane blends timeless colonial charm w/modern comfort and effortless commuter convenience. Walk to shops, restaurants, cafes, and enjoy quick access to Metro North and the Merritt Parkway. This quintessential 4 bedroom, 2 full bath colonial is filled with warmth & character featuring craftsman style moldings, inviting living spaces, and a flexible floor plan designed for today's lifestyle. A sought after first floor bedroom and full bath create the perfect setup for guests and in laws. The living room flows seamlessly into a sun-filled rear family room overlooking the beautifully landscaped backyard. An updated kitchen w/stainless steel appliances and pantry opens easily to the dining room, making entertaining effortless. Upstairs offers 3 bedrooms & full bathroom with beautiful hardwood floors throughout. The partially finished walk out lower level gives additional living space for playroom or home office along w/storage, laundry room and a utility area. Outside, enjoy a private backyard oasis complete w/expansive entertaining deck, lush lawn, mature plantings, & an updated charming shed perfect for long summer days & evenings outdoors. Additional highlights include a rustic wine cellar, full attic, & garage. A truly special home offering timeless charm, exceptional outdoor space, & unbeatable convenience. See attached update list.
