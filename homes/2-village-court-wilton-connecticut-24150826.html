About this home

Set within the desirable Grumman Hill Village community, 2 Village Ct offers a rare combination of privacy, comfort, and low-maintenance living. Positioned on a one-of-a-kind corner lot with one of the largest backyards in the complex, this distinctive setting offers unparalleled privacy, creating an effortless blend of refined comfort and turnkey living. From the moment you step inside, the dramatic ceiling in the living room sets the tone, framed by expansive windows and modern lighting that amplify the home's natural light. The living room flows seamlessly into the dining area and updated kitchen, creating an inviting layout for everyday living. Just beyond, the den features built-ins, a gas fireplace, and a sliding door to a private patio and comfortable yard. Upstairs, a loft overlooking the main level adds flexibility, while a convenient laundry closet eliminates trips up and down stairs. The primary suite is a true retreat with a Palladian window, cathedral ceiling, walk-in closet, and en-suite bath with dual vanity. Two additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the upper level. A spacious 2 car garage with a new door rounds out the home. Residents enjoy HOA-managed landscaping, snow removal, and trash pickup in the quiet community. Ideally located near ASML, Wilton Town Center, shopping, train stations, Route 7, the Merritt Parkway, and I-95. Come experience a home that blends thoughtful updates and effortless living in one of Wilton's most convenient communities.
