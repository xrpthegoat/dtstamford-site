About this home

Located on a cul-de-sac in Stamford's Newfield neighborhood, this spacious home features hardwood floors throughout and a versatile layout designed for comfortable living and entertaining. The main level offers an open-concept living and dining room that flows into the eat-in kitchen, complete with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, abundant storage and a breakfast nook overlooking the private backyard. A large, sun-filled family room with built-in speakers and adjoining recreation room provide additional living space with convenient access to the powder room, mudroom and attached garage. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features a walk-in closet and an updated private bathroom with a double vanity, soaking tub and separate shower. Three additional bedrooms, a full bathroom and a conveniently located laundry room complete the upper level. The lower level offers flexible space ideal for a home gym or office, along with abundant storage and additional possibilities. The professionally landscaped backyard is highlighted by beautiful stone walls, a built-in outdoor fireplace, privacy trees and a large patio. Conveniently located near parks, Newfield pool club, shopping, local restaurants and Stamford's many amenities.
