About this home

Welcome to 172 Mather Street spacious updated home offering over 3,000 square feet of flexible living. Upstairs there are two to five large rooms that can be bedrooms, guest rooms, or offices. Newer kitchen with stainless steel appliances and granite counters that flow into a sunny breakfast room with walls of windows. Living room and dining room have hardwood floors and french doors leading to your screened in porch, the ideal place for morning coffee or evening glass of wine. Family room with sliding doors out to the backyard. This turnkey home is freshly painted with many updates. The house is set back from the road with city water, close to shopping, restaurants, and trains. With its versatile space and updates, this home offers endless possibilities.
