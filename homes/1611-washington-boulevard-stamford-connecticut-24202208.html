About this home

Experience elevated mid-city living in this expansive penthouse, where fresh updates meet impressive views. This oversized one-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom condo has been thoughtfully modernized with stylish shiplap accents, new flooring, and recessed lighting, making it feel bright and inviting from the moment you cross the threshold. The unit is designed for effortless living, with a well-appointed walk-in closet, the convenience of in-unit laundry, and a private balcony that serves as your own personal retreat. From here, you can enjoy unobstructed views of the city and the greenery of Scalzi Park - a recreational hub offering everything from tennis and pickleball to bocce and basketball. Positioned to take full advantage of the Stamford lifestyle, this condo puts you moments away from the energy of downtown, the Metro-North station, and major travel routes.
