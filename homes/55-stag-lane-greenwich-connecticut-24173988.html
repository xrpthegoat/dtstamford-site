About this home

Privately sited on beautifully landscaped grounds, this extensively renovated 5-bedroom residence blends modern design with exceptional comfort and energy efficiency. Thoughtfully updated throughout, the home features an open-concept layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, with a designer kitchen, high-end stainless steel appliances, and beautifully renovated baths with premium finishes in 2020. New windows and doors enhance the light-filled interiors, while the lower level offers flexible recreation space with the second powder room and direct access to the property. A separate guest or in-law suite with its own entrance and kitchenette provides excellent flexibility for extended family, guests, or au pair living. The home is equipped with advanced energy-efficient systems, including Tesla solar panels and Powerwalls, spray foam insulation, and upgraded water and air filtration systems. Expansive lawn areas offer endless possibilities for outdoor living, including room for a pool or tennis court, all surrounded by mature trees, natural privacy, and picturesque stone walls woven throughout the property.
