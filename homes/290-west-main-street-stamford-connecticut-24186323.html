About this home

An incredible opportunity to own in Stamford! Located at 21 W. Main Street, this spacious 2 bedroom, 1 bath condo offers 860 sq ft of comfortable living space with beautiful views of Downtown Stamford. Inside, you'll find a bright and functional layout featuring generously sized rooms, an in-unit washer and dryer, and the added benefit of an energy-efficient tankless water heater. Enjoy the convenience of two dedicated parking spaces, a rare and valuable feature for city living. Situated on the fourth floor of a walk-up building, this home offers privacy, natural light, and elevated city views. This condo is a great alternative to renting and a chance to build equity in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after cities. Walking distance to Downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, parks, public transportation, and Metro-North, this home delivers convenience, value, and the opportunity to make Stamford home.
