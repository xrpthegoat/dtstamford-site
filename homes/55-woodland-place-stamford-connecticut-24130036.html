About this home

Tucked inside an intimate eight-unit community, this three-story townhome offers a rare blend of privacy, natural light, and effortless convenience. From the moment you step inside, oversized windows fill the space with sunlight, creating a bright, airy feel throughout the home. The eat-in kitchen is both refined and functional, featuring sleek quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and clean modern cabinetry. Upstairs, both bedrooms offer double closets and comfortable proportions, while a full attic provides generous additional storage. The lower level adds flexibility with an office space along with a dedicated laundry room for everyday ease. Location truly sets this home apart. Enjoy a short stroll to Metro North (approximately 15 minutes) for a seamless commute, or walk just 5-10 minutes to the harbor, waterfront dining, and local restaurants. Practicality meets peace of mind with a one-car garage, two additional parking spaces, and plentiful street parking for guests.
