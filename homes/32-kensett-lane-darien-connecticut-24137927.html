About this home

11 foot ceilings, elevator on all 3 levels, and a primary suite with TWO separate full baths & walk-in closets are just a few reasons 32 Kensett is one of the complexes finest and most desired. Positioned at the rear of the complex, this corner unit backs onto protected land preserve, providing a peaceful setting and heightened privacy for effortless indoor outdoor living. Breathtaking from the moment you enter, you are met by a wall of windows at the back via 4 sets of glass french doors out to the patio. The sundrenched open floor plan allows space for living, dining, family room & large eat in kitchen. The gourmet kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, generous island, extra large pantry & a butlers pantry. Rounding out this level is a spacious office or sunroom, powder room, and 2 car garage. On the 2nd level, there are 3 large bedrooms all with ensuite bathrooms and walk in closets. The stunning primary suite has a gas fireplace, and two separate full baths & closets for ultimate luxury. A large laundry room with sink finishes this level. The lower level is extra large with a full bath, walk in closet, 2 huge storage closets, and space for a 4th bedroom, gym and/or playroom. Luxury community amenities: clubhouse, outdoor pool and spa, fitness center, 16 acre walking trails.
