About this home

Welcome to 15 Summit Place, the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. This home features two equally spacious bedrooms and 1 bathroom. The updated kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, quartz counter tops and plenty of storage extending into a large walk-in pantry with built-ins and an additional sink. Thanks to the bay windows and skylight, tons of natural light streams into the charming living room with stone walls. The enclosed porch adds an additional 3 seasons room to use as you see fit. Enjoy the outdoor space with a beautiful backyard and patio that won't disappoint!! Heat, hot water, basic cable and internet are included! WOW! Laundry located right off pantry. Parking is never an issue with one covered space in the carport and additional driveway parking. Located just one mile from the Glenbrook RR station and nestled between the Merritt Parkway & I-95, your commute is made easy. Daily essentials are close at hand with Shopping & Restaurants just around the corner. Pet Friendly! Do Not Miss Out!
