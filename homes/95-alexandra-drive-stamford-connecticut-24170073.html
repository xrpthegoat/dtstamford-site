About this home

Your first impression! A long dramatic driveway welcomes you to an entrance framed by professionally designed landscaping. The peaceful and impressive setting, with an expanded driveway area, provides abundant parking for family and guests. Originally enhanced with thoughtful additions, the home now features a spacious layout, designed for modern living. A bright first-floor office provides ideal work-from-home flexibility, while the enlarged kitchen offers improved flow and functionality for everyday living. Above the garage, a stunning primary suite addition creates a private retreat. This spacious sanctuary includes a beautiful bathroom, generous sleeping area and exceptional closet space-delivering the comfort and style today's buyers seek. Throughout the home, an abundance of skylight fills the interior with natural light, creating a bright and airy atmosphere rarely found in colonial-style homes. The current owners chose to use the original living room as their dining room, but others could easily restore the layout. The finished lower level increases the home's living potential with two additional large rooms each with storage space, a full bath, laundry room, large pantry/storage area and a large cedar closet. The home reflects consistent pride of ownership. Major updates include: Exterior recently (July 2025) stripped to bare wood, sanded, and professionally painted. Updated roofing, exterior shingle and windows. Multiple interior renovations (in docs).
