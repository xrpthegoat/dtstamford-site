About this home

No other property on the market offers this much space in proximity to downtown Greenwich. This exquisite, recently completed Colonial features 10-foot ceilings and 10,646 square feet of new construction, smart-home luxury living, all within walking distance to the library, Greenwich Avenue, the train station, and downtown amenities. A dramatic two-story foyer is nestled between the formal living and dining rooms, unfolding into an expansive family room. This space sits adjacent to a custom chef's kitchen and butler's pantry bar, which walk out to a 20'x40' saltwater pool. A first-floor bedroom with an ensuite bath, double mudrooms, and a magnificent library complete the main level. The extraordinary primary suite boasts a tray ceiling, a fireplace, a private outdoor deck, a sitting room, and spectacular two-story closets. Its luxurious amenities include separate bathrooms equipped with Toto toilets and radiant heated floors. Five additional bedrooms with ensuite baths and a spacious laundry room round out the second floor. The top level features a wet bar, recreation areas, and two dedicated offices. Highlighting walk-out access to the level backyard, the garden level offers multiple flexible spaces. These include a media room with surround sound, a pre-wired gym with mirrors, a game room, a wine room, an additional bedroom with an ensuite bath, a second full bathroom, and a second laundry room complete with a pet shower.
