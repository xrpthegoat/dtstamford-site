About this home

Discover this wonderful 4-bedroom, 1.5-bath home nestled in Greenwich's Glenville section. Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, it boasts a private backyard. A double-height entry leads into an inviting open floor plan with hardwood floors. The spacious living room, bathed in natural light, flows seamlessly into the dining room. The kitchen's island offers seating, and there is ample storage plus stainless steel appliances. Just off the kitchen is the deck with access to the serene backyard, a perfect spot to enjoy morning coffee. The primary bedroom and two additional bedrooms, along with a hall bath, are situated on the main level. Just steps away, the lower level features a fabulous family room complete with a fireplace, built-ins, and a fun wet bar area. A generously sized fourth bedroom offers flexibility for family, guests, a home office, or gym space. Super convenient with a one-car garage and proximity to downtown Glenville's shops, dining options, schools, and community center. Nearby is Greenwich's Byram Beach & Pool and the Port Chester MetroNorth train station, enhancing this property's appeal. Don't miss out on this excellent opportunity to own a home in this sought-after Greenwich neighborhood.
