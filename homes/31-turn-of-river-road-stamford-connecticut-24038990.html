About this home

Picturesque New England farmhouse with open floor plan and a deep, flat backyard in a fantastic location near every amenity imaginable. Feel welcomed from the minute you pull up, with the inviting front porch and charming curb appeal. Enter through the beautiful front door and get ready to be seized by the open floor plan and abundance of natural light. The home has been updated from top to bottom featuring brand new gourmet kitchen with cascading island, solid core doors with Baldwin hardware, 10' ceilings on 2nd level and custom trim/baseboards. There is a possible 4th bedroom on the main level which includes a full bathroom. On the second level there are two large bedrooms w/generous closet space and the primary bedroom screams luxury with full bath & walk-in closets. Detached one-car garage has bonus room in the rear which is perfect home office, gym or storage space. Good credit and references are required. This is a remarkable home in a convenient location just off High Ridge Rd and seconds to Merritt Parkway, Trader Joe's, fine dining and retail outlets.
