About this home

Impeccably maintained 3 Bedroom 3.5 Bath Contemporary Colonial! Nestled on a tree lined street in one of Bull's Head most desirable neighborhoods. Walk into a perfectly constructed open-concept design, accentuated by beautiful hardwood floors, vaulted ceilings with skylights & lots of windows. Gourmet kitchen with generous cabinet space, stainless steel appliances, gas cooktop, double oven & large peninsula island, blends seamlessly into this design. Large living & dining room area with your very own fireplace which will bring warmth during those cold winter months. Sliders to massive private two tiered deck make entertaining a breeze. Master Suite with double closets, & luxury full bath equipped with jacuzzi tub & stand alone shower. Upper level includes two additional bedrooms and one full bath. Enjoy extra comfort with efficient Central Air & Gas Heating. Full walk-out finished basement with full bath. An abundance of storage space & access to 2 Car Garage. Minutes to Downtown Stamford, Restaurants and Shopping.
