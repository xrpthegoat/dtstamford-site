About this home

Welcome to 22 Roxbury Lane! This home is a lovely Classic Colonial Split, ideally situated on a highly sought-after cul-de-sac in South Wilton. Offering 5 bedrooms and 3 full baths, this inviting home combines a wonderful setting with a convenient location close to town, commuter routes, schools and the Norwalk, River Valley Trail. The light-filled main level features a beautiful living room with a fireplace and oversized bay window, along with a gracious dining room conveniently located off the kitchen. The eat-in kitchen offers granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and lots of cabinets for storage. A special highlight is the adjoining sunroom which boasts views of the picturesque and private yard, cathedral ceilings with beams and skylights making it a perfect gathering place for relaxing or entertaining. The lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a spacious family room, 5th bedroom, full bath along with its own separate entrance which is ideal for guest, extended family or private home office. Upstairs, 4 generously sized bedrooms and 2 full baths provide comfortable accommodations with walk-in closets adding to the appeal. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy the best of South Wilton living, with a peaceful cul-de-sac setting, versatile living spaces, and easy access to town, commuting, Wiltons award-winning schools, and outdoor recreation.
