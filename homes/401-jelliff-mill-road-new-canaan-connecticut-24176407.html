About this home

Move-in-ready, renovated New Canaan charmer. This three-bedroom home is a true gem, featuring 1930s vintage architectural details alongside extensive recent updates. Easy access to outdoor patio. Second Floor: An owner suite with hardwood floors and a large walk-in closet, plus two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a large linen closet. Exterior: A level backyard with a two-car garage and a private courtyard patio, perfect for relaxation and entertaining.
