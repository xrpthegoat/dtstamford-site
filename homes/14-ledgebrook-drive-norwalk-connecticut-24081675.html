About this home

Welcome to your dream home! This beautifully renovated upper-level 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo boasts 17-foot vaulted, beamed ceilings, creating an open and inviting atmosphere that enhances the sense of space everyone desires. Unique architectural details add character, while the fully remodeled large eat-in kitchen impresses with brand new shaker- custom cabinets, quartz countertops, a tile backsplash, and new stainless steel appliances. The open-concept layout seamlessly connects the kitchen to the spacious, sunlit living room, featuring a floor-to-ceiling corner wood-burning fireplace-perfect for entertaining. A private deck offers a peaceful retreat. The nicely- sized primary bedroom features generous closet space and a new elegant en-suite bath, while the second bedroom provides versatility for a home office or guest room. Beautiful new engineered wood flooring, two panel doors run throughout, and in-unit laundry adds convenience. This condo comes with a garage and a storage room. All new mechanicals: heating, cooling systems and hot water heater. The sought-after Ledgebrook community offers meticulously maintained, park-like grounds and a wealth of amenities, including a clubhouse, pool, indoor hot tub with sauna, and a fitness center. Enjoy a prime location with easy access to highways, trains, shopping, dining, and the vibrant SoNo area. Just move in and enjoy! Subject to seller finding suitable place to live!
