About this home

The chic and stylish summer rental you've been waiting for! 3 bedroom / 2 bath newly renovated and tastefully designed beach house in a quiet and private location near Westport's Compo Beach and Old Mill Beach. This home includes all the amenities you would expect: a chef's kitchen with breakfast bar, a full dining table for eight, a relaxing and zen-like living room, and even a den that can double as a guest and/or office space. Relax in a newly designed main bedroom with queen-sized bed and en-suite bathroom. Two additional well-appointed bedrooms each have a full-sized bed, ample closet space and share the other updated bath. Grab the beach chairs and towels (included) and take a stroll to the beach, or just stay home to enjoy your book in the fully furnished backyard, with beautiful flowering plants, manicured lawn and an inviting patio and hammock. The converted shed in the backyard, dubbed the Little Casa, is furnished with a desk and daybed, with space to enjoy your morning yoga or use as an inspiring workspace. Experience the amazing beach town of Westport with its bustling downtown and restaurants, its beaches, sailing, kayaking. Enjoy the multiple free summer concerts at the Levitt Pavilion, weekend farmers markets, Wakeman town farm, hiking trails, art shows, and more to make enjoyment of beach, pools, golf and tennis easy! June: (RENTED), July 10-31: $10,500, August: $15,000. Potential to extend monthly beyond summer. Weekly rate is $5,000 including utilities.
