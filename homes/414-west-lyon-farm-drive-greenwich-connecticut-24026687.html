About this home

Experience the ease of condo living in this stunning 3/4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home located in the West Lyon Farm Association of Greenwich, CT. This completely renovated modern residence has nearly 3500 sq ft of living space. Exciting features include a fully equipped spacious Kitchen, formal DR with wraparound deck, and a skylight that bathes the spaces in natural light. The first floor has the advantage of a large bedroom with ensuite bath, while second floor holds two add'l bedrooms and bath, along with the spacious Primary Suite, complete with walk-in closet & spa-like bath. An expansive walk-out lower level provides space for gym and family room/add'l bedroom. This pet-friendly community with a range of amenities, including a club house, pool, tennis court, and guest parking. Area J D Layout
