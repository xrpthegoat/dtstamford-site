About this home

Welcome to 8 Ledge Road, a beautifully updated coastal home in the heart of Rowayton. This 4-bedroom, 3-bath residence offers bright, open-concept living with expansive windows and thoughtfully designed spaces ideal for today's lifestyle. The sun-filled chef's kitchen anchors the home, seamlessly connecting to the main living and dining areas for easy everyday living and entertaining. The spacious primary suite features a walk-in closet and a well-appointed bath. Additional living space includes a finished attic and a walk-out lower level with a playroom, office space, full bath, laundry, and separate entrance- ideal for guests, extended family, or au pair use. Extensive updates completed in February 2025 include new hardwood floors, energy-efficient windows, exterior siding, HVAC, water filtration system, solar panels, propane for cooking and fireplace, WiFi-enabled underground sprinklers, and a Ring security system. This turnkey home is move-in ready and situated on a quiet street in a convenient location close to the train, downtown Rowayton, and Darien, with easy access to the beach and local amenities.
