About this home

Welcome to 41 Valley Road in desirable Cos Cob! This spacious and inviting four-bedroom, three-full-bath residence is available for either a short-term or yearly lease, making it an ideal choice for anyone between homes, relocating, renovating, or looking for flexible accommodations. The home features a comfortable and functional layout with beautiful hardwood floors, newer appliances, granite countertops, and the convenience of laundry in the unit. Enjoy cozy evenings by the wood-burning fireplace or relax and entertain outdoors on the private rooftop deck. One-car parking is included, and small dogs are welcome. Located within walking distance of local shops, restaurants, parks, and the center of Cos Cob, this home offers the perfect combination of convenience and comfortable living. Clean, well maintained, and move-in ready. A wonderful rental opportunity in a highly desirable Greenwich neighborhood-this is a must-see!
