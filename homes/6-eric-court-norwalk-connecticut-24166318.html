About this home

Welcome to 6 Eric Court, a stunning contemporary raised ranch tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in popular Norwalk CT! This turn-key ready home features gleaming hardwood floors throughout, 2 beautifully updated full bathrooms with elegant tile choices, & custom light fixtures paired with recessed lighting creating a warm & modern ambiance. The highly inviting living space is highlighted by a brick floor to ceiling wood-burning fireplace, perfect for cozy evenings, whilst the stunning 4-season sun porch provides an ideal private setting for year-round relaxation overlooking the homes' peaceful surroundings. Step outside to take in a comfortable stone & gravel patio perfect for entertaining, dining, or simply unwinding outdoors. The spacious Chef's Kitchen offers both style & functionality with quality finishes & an ideal layout for everyday living & hosting alike. Conveniently sighted just minutes from shopping, dining, parks, beaches, & major commuter routes, this home offers easy access to The SoNo Collection, Main Avenue Shopping Plaza, & scenic waterfront destinations including Oyster Shell and Mathews Parks. Enjoy the close proximity to vibrant South Norwalk restaurants, Metro North train access, Calf Pasture Beach, & everything Norwalk has to offer. A rare turn-key opportunity combining modern upgrades and comfort, in an unbeatable location!
