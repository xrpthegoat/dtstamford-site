About this home

Designer's Retreat...nestled on a quiet lane in Westport yet convenient to all, this aesthetically pleasing 1930's vintage property with exposed stone and architecturally interesting lines welcomes you to a chic and idyllic oasis. The home has been extensively updated without losing any of its original charm. The first floor boasts an open and expansive layout with over 2400 sf of flexible living spaces for creating, entertaining, and working. Relax by the fireplace in the family room or enjoy views of the lush property from the year-round sunroom. Brand-new, gleaming quartz countertops and high-end appliances make the open kitchen the heart of the home. Exceptional features include a brick floor in the sunroom, oversized or deep-set windows, a front porch, a flagstone terrace and secluded rear patio, marble bathrooms, hardwood floors, oversized skylights, French or glass doors, crown moldings, two laundry hookups, central AC and more. Upstairs are two nicely sized bedrooms and two full baths. The ensuite primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings and skylights and the 2nd bedroom featuring a sizeable loft area and second bath finish up the second level. The level, open, sunny .87 acre lot has room for expansion or even a pool! This home has been curated to the very highest level, and will impress even those with the highest expectations. Truly a dream come true.
