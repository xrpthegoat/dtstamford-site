About this home

A rare opportunity to enjoy turnkey living in one of Stamford's premier neighborhoods! Ideally situated within the sought-after Laurel Ledge Association, featuring a community pool and year-round social events. Set behind a picturesque spring-fed pond, this meticulously maintained and expanded Colonial has been renovated to the highest standards. At the heart of the home is a gourmet eat-in kitchen featuring custom cabinetry, stone countertops, a coffered ceiling, and professional-grade Sub-Zero, Viking, & Bosch appliances. Designed for modern living, the home offers both a spacious great room & family room, highlighted by hardwood floors, detailed millwork & abundant natural light. Formal living & dining rooms provide elegant spaces for entertaining, w/a gas fireplace & custom architectural accents. Upstairs are four-plus bedrooms, all with hardwood floors, including a vaulted-ceiling office w/private balcony overlooking the serene backyard. The luxurious outdoor living space features an oversized stone patio with a built-in chef's grill, surrounded by professionally landscaped grounds offering exceptional privacy. The home's systems have been thoughtfully upgraded & maintained, including an automatic whole-house generator, whole-house surge protection, reverse osmosis water system & automated LED landscape lighting.
