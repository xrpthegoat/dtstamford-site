About this home

Welcome to this charming, bright, and open home in the sought-after Newfield neighborhood, situated on a tranquil cul-de-sac. Step inside and be greeted by sun-filled rooms with newly refinished light hardwood flooring, creating a vibrant and inviting atmosphere. The open-concept living and dining area seamlessly flows into a kitchen adorned with granite countertops, providing ample storage and workspace. Sliding doors from the dining room lead to a charming deck with a cabana and a private outdoor space, perfect for relaxation and entertaining. The main floor boasts an updated designer full bath and three sunlit bedrooms. The lower level expands your living options with a mudroom/gym, an office/bedroom with an open kitchenette, a cozy living room, a full bath, and sliders to the outside. This versatile space can be tailored to suit your needs, whether as a home office, in-law suite, family room, or exercise area. Additionally, the property features a delightful shed resembling a small house, ideal for storage, a playroom, a studio, or any creative use you envision. The generously sized, private, and fenced backyard offers an oasis for relaxation and entertainment. Conveniently located near shops, restaurants, and with easy access to major highways, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, this home is in move-in condition and ready for you today. Don't miss the chance to make this perfectly presented home your own!
