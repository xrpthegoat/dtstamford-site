About this home

Walk to town, train, Binney Park and bike to beach and come home to a private gated setting. Located on a quiet lane in Old Greenwich, this recently renovated 5 bedroom residence with exquisite light interiors is set back from the road on a .32 acre level lot with fenced-in backyard. Main Level: living room, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen and powder room. 2nd level: primary bedroom w/new primary bath, plus 3 additional bedrooms w/new hall bath. Garden Level: large famiy room w/woodburning fireplace, 5th bedroom w/new full bath, gym, laundry room, storage room and direct access to 2 car garage w/ electric car charger, as well as access to the backyard and terraces. Backyard was recently redesigned to enhance outdoor living with new level lawn and terrace surrounded by specimen trees and plantings creating a beautiful private oasis. A pleasure and easy to show
