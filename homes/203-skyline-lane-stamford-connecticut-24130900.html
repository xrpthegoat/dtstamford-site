About this home

This stunning renovated North Stamford home is available to rent furnished or unfurnished and offers immaculately presented bright, modern space on a beautiful, landscaped acre. Enjoy the beautiful open kitchen featuring sea glass island, white cabs and quartz countertops. A window-lined alcove provides sliders to the maintenance free deck which overlooks the perfectly manicured lawn! Spend cozy winter nights by the fireplace in the family room and the easy flow of rooms makes for ideal entertaining! Upstairs you'll find an accommodating primary bedroom with updated bath, as well as 3-other generous bedrooms and another renovated full bath. A main level laundry room, attached 2-car garage, full basement for storage, central air conditioning and new systems add to the good-life here! Located in a delightful neighborhood, private yet in close proximity to the Merritt Parkway, High Ridge shopping and New Canaan Village, you can also enjoy the country attractions offered at the Stamford Nature Center and Bartlett Arboretum. Pets are considered on a case-by-case basis. Come see!
