About this home

Totally rebuilt 5 bedroom, 4.5 bath home on a cul de sac includes many special features. Gourmet kitchen w/ Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances, 3 ovens, warming drawer, walk in pantry, island with prep sink and built in dining area. 1st fl. suite could be Primary or guest. Possible 6th bedroom in the lower level, currently used as a home gym, could be au pair or in-law suite. LL has kitchenette & full bath and can access separate entry. Large family room off of the kitchen with doors to rear patio, pool and outside kitchen/bar & level, fenced yard makes for great entertaining. Primary suite includes extra height tray ceiling, sitting area, multiple walk in closets and luxurious bath. Formal dining room can open into home office to seat large gatherings. Extra height garage can accommodate lifts.
