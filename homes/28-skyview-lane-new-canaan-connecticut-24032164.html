About this home

Located on a serene, picturesque street on the west side of New Canaan, this 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home provides an exceptional opportunity to create a personalized haven in one of the area's most coveted locations. Enjoy the spacious rooms throughout this 3,000 square foot home, beautiful natural light, and expansive 1.14 acres of sweeping lawn, ample space for outdoor living play, or expansion. A gracious foyer welcomes you to the home and invites you to explore the open living spaces, which flow easily from room to room. The large living room features a wood fireplace and ample space for multiple seating areas - perfect for family gatherings. Enjoy spending time in the cozy den, bright sunroom, and kitchen, all of which look out over the property. The primary bedroom is conveniently located on the main level with two additional bedrooms on main floor and two upstairs. The layout and beautiful setting are ready for a new owner to refresh and revitalize. Embrace the chance to invest in a fantastic location with outstanding potential.
