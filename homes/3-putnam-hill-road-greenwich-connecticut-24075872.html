About this home

Perched just two blocks from Greenwich Avenue and moments from renowned shopping and dining, this spacious one bedroom offers an exceptional opportunity to reside in Greenwich. Bruce Museum, Bruce Park, Greenwich Harbor, Whole Foods Market, YMCA and public transportation are within a mile. Enjoy the convenience of in-town living, while surrounded by the tranquility of a 10 acre park-like setting. Additional benefits of this pet friendly community include ample off street parking, refreshed landscaping, new mechanicals, exterior lighting, and on-site superintendent. Enjoy as-is or transform into your perfect pied-a-terre. Hardwood floors beneath carpet. W/D permitted in-unit. Monthly payment covers property tax, grounds maintenance, heat, hot water, and refuse removal.
