About this home

Charming Colonial in Stamford's desirable Cove neighborhood, just a short walk to beautiful Cove Island Park. Enjoy one of the area's most popular waterfront destinations featuring scenic walking, biking, and rollerblading loops, along with tennis courts, basketball courts, an ice skating rink, and a sandy beach perfect for relaxing by the Sound. This delightful home offers 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, with hardwood floors and timeless architectural details that add warmth and character. The flexible floor plan includes a dedicated office or craft room and a spacious family room that could easily serve as a first-floor bedroom if needed. Every room reflects the charm and comfort that make this home truly special. Ideally located for commuters, with quick access to I-95 and the Metro-North train. Just minutes from downtown Stamford's vibrant restaurants, shopping, and entertainment. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy classic charm, coastal living, and unbeatable convenience all in one location. House and garage being sold "As is".
