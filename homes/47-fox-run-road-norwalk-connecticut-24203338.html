About this home

Welcome to 47 Fox Run Road, a meticulously maintained four-bedroom Colonial set on a picturesque 1.05-acre lot in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods. This home showcases quality construction featuring hardwood floors, new windows, central air, a whole-house generator, and much more! Filled with natural light, the home is designed for everyday comfort and easy entertaining. Brand-new bluestone front steps lead you to a freshly painted interior with a generous main-level layout. A spacious formal living room and elegant dining room flow naturally into an inviting family room complete with a fireplace. The kitchen serves as the heart of the home, complete with a Sub-Zero. Completing the main level is a laundry room & formal powder room. Upstairs, you will find four spacious bedrooms, including a primary suite featuring a newly renovated en-suite bath. A second newly renovated full bathroom serves the remaining bedrooms with crisp, modern finishes. Outdoor living is a highlight with a brand-new, low-maintenance Trex deck overlooking a pristine, manicured yard-a private oasis ideal for outdoor dining, play, and gatherings. Enjoy versatile year-round space in the light-filled four-season porch equipped with its own ductless split heating and cooling system. Additional features include a full 1,040-square-foot basement providing abundant storage and future expansion potential, an oversized garage, and new doors.
