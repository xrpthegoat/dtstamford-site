About this home

Set on a picturesque cul-de-sac, this elegant Colonial offers timeless charm and modern amenities. Nestled on a level two-acre lot, the home boasts lovely curb appeal and a private, fully fenced backyard perfect for entertaining, play, and recreation. Inside, you'll find four spacious bedrooms with the option for a fifth bedroom or private home office on the main level, complete with a full bath. The front-to-back living room features custom built-ins, while the generous formal dining room is ideal for hosting gatherings. The heart of the home is the oversized eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a large island, and a convenient mudroom entry. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the welcoming family room with a cozy fireplace and sliders that open to a serene screened porch that overlooks the stunning heated gunite pool and spa, complete with soothing waterfall feature. Enjoy outdoor living on the stone patio, surrounded by beautifully landscaped grounds. The finished lower-level great room offers endless possibilities-whether for a playroom, media center, gym, or additional living space. Additional features include a two-car garage, central air, a pull-down attic for extra storage, and a portable generator. Located just minutes from shopping, top-rated schools, town amenities, and train access, this beautifully maintained home offers tranquility and convenience - just in time for summer!
