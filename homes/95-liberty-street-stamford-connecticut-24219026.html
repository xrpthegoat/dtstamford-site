About this home

Welcome to easy, comfortable living in this spacious 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse located in Stamford's convenient West Side neighborhood. Offering a thoughtful layout with room to spread out, this inviting home offers the space and privacy of townhouse living. The bright and airy living and dining area features beautiful hardwood floors, elegant crown molding, and sliding glass doors that fill the space with natural light and provide access to a private outdoor balcony. The kitchen offers ample cabinetry, stainless steel appliances and a bonus eat in dining area. Two generously sized bedrooms provide comfortable retreats, while a versatile bonus den offers valuable additional space for a home office, hobby room, or whatever suits your lifestyle. Everyday conveniences include a full bathroom, an additional half bath, in-unit washer and dryer, and an attached one-car garage offering both parking and additional storage possibilities. Enjoy the comfort of a spacious townhouse while remaining close to Stamford's shopping, dining, parks, public transportation, and major commuter routes. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy flexibility, functionality, and convenience in one inviting place to call home!
