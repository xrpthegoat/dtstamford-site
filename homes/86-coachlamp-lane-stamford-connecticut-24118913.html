About this home

Westover Elegance - Sophisticated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath + loft Colonial Contemporary home set on a quiet cul-de-sac in coveted Westover. This home blends timeless architecture with refined modern living. A grand two-story granite foyer welcomes you to a sun-filled living room with soaring cathedral ceilings and a marble fireplace. The designer eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and custom cabinetry, opening seamlessly to a family room with sliders to a spacious deck and a private, landscaped backyard with an additional flagstone patio. Upstairs, the serene primary suite offers custom wallpaper, multiple closets, and a spa-inspired marble bath. 3 additional bedrooms and a versatile loft with built-ins provide flexible living space. Hardwood floors and recessed lighting throughout. The fully finished lower level includes a large recreation room with Pergo floors and abundant storage. Luxury amenities include an oversized two-car garage with built-in storage, a full-property sprinkler system, and a security system. Just 8 minutes to Metro-North and close to Stamford's premier dining, shopping, golf, and parks - this meticulously maintained home is truly turn-key. MULTIPLE OFFERS RECEIVED.
