About this home

Filled with natural light and impeccably maintained, this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2-bath Cape is located in the highly desirable Glenville School District and combines timeless charm with modern comfort. The inviting layout features a gracious living room with a stone fireplace and custom built-ins, a stunning renovated kitchen with a quartz island and GE Cafe stainless steel appliances, and a beautifully designed dining area with exceptional built-ins--ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The main level also offers an updated full bath, a bedroom, and a flexible office or nursery. Upstairs are two additional bedrooms with generous storage and a full bath featuring radiant heated floors. The lower level includes a cozy family room with access to the professionally landscaped yard, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestlye. A one-car attached garage adds convenience. Ideally situated near schools, parks, and transportation, this home offers the perfect balance of comfort, style, and sought-after Greenwich living.
