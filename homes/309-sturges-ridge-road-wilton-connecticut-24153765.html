About this home

Gracefully blending classic architecture with modern updates, this exceptional 4/5 bedroom Colonial offers timeless elegance and everyday comfort in a premier Wilton setting. Generously scaled rooms, soaring ceilings, and refined custom millwork create a sophisticated backdrop throughout, while multiple flexible spaces-including a dedicated home office and a sun-filled secondary office/sunroom-provide ideal work-from-home versatility. The heart of the home is the updated kitchen, seamlessly opening to a warm and inviting family room with a striking stone fireplace and French doors that lead to an expansive deck-perfect for indoor-outdoor living and entertaining. Formal living and dining rooms offer classic appeal, with abundant natural light, hardwood floors, and a fireplace enhancing the living room. A richly detailed den with wide-plank pegged floors and an additional fireplace provides yet another welcoming gathering space. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a fourth fireplace, dual walk-in closets, and a beautifully updated spa-like bath with soaking tub, stall shower, double sinks, and generous counter space. Three additional bedrooms-including an oversized room with potential for an en-suite bath-and a large bonus room with vaulted ceilings offer exceptional flexibility for a variety of lifestyles.
