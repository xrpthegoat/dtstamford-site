About this home

Located in one of Westport's most desirable and convenient neighborhoods, this beautifully updated home offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and functionality. Designed for today's lifestyle, the open-concept floor plan features a recently renovated kitchen with an oversized island that seamlessly connects to the main living and entertaining spaces. The upper level includes three spacious bedrooms and two full baths, while the main level offers an inviting family room and a half bath. Just beyond, a spectacular four-season sunroom is flooded with natural light year-round, providing the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, work from home, or take in the changing seasons. The finished lower level adds exceptional versatility, offering space for a playroom, home gym, media room, or office. Ideally situated just minutes from all that downtown Westport as to offer, top-rated schools, and the Merritt Parkway, this home combines an unbeatable location with modern updates and everyday convenience.
