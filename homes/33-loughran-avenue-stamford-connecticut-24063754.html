About this home

Light and bright and in an incredibly convenient location. This home has it all! A spacious raised ranch with a neutral color scheme. The main living level has an open floor plan with hardwood flooring. Newer eat-in kitchen with granite counters and tile floor. Spacious dining room open to the living room creating a great entertaining space. Master bedroom with updated en-suite bath. Two additional bedrooms with hardwood flooring share a second updated full bath. On the lower level you will find a spacious family room with a cozy brick fireplace and sliders that lead to an oversized patio overlooking the Rippowam River. There is a fourth bedroom / office and a third updated bathroom as well as a separate laundry room. Attached oversized 2 car garage with newer doors. Close to the Merritt Parkway, restaurants, shopping, schools and many other Stamford conveniences. Must have excellent credit and references. Tenant to pay for all utilities, landscaping / lawn care including seasonal turning on/off sprinkler system and snow removal. Landlord prefers an 18 month lease to begin with opportunity to extend after that.
