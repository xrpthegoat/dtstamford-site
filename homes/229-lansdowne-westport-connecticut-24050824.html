About this home

This spacious 2,050sf condo offers comfortable living in the heart of Westport, situated within a well-maintained gated community. Built in 1985, this 2-bedroom, 2.1-bathroom unit has been thoughtfully updated with modern touches while retaining its cozy charm. The home features engineered hardwood flooring throughout, lending a sleek and contemporary feel. The new custom kitchen is a standout, boasting elegant white cabinets, durable quartz countertops, and top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances. A fireplace in the kitchen adds warmth and ambiance, making it a perfect space for both casual meals and entertaining. The living room is a bright, welcoming space with skylights that flood the room with natural light. A second fireplace creates a cozy atmosphere, and the built-in wet bar is perfect for hosting guests. The adjacent sunroom, just off the living room, opens onto a private patio, providing a peaceful space for relaxation or outdoor dining. The primary suite is conveniently located on the first floor, offering privacy and ease of access. It includes a spacious en-suite bathroom and ample closet space. The gated community offers resort-style amenities, including a beautifully maintained inground pool, tennis and pickleball courts, and lush common grounds. This home provides the ideal balance of privacy, luxury, and convenience, all within a short drive to downtown Westport and nearby beaches.
