About this home

Tucked away at the end of a cul-de-sac in sought-after Cranbury, this thoughtfully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath ranch offers privacy, flexible living space, and effortless indoor-outdoor living on a park-like .75-acre setting. Warm hardwood floors flow throughout the main level, where an open-concept layout creates an inviting space for both everyday living and entertaining. The living and dining areas connect seamlessly to the kitchen, featuring white cabinetry, new stainless steel appliances, gas cooking, and a breakfast bar perfect for casual meals or gathering with guests. On cooler evenings, relax beside the wood-burning fireplace while enjoying peaceful views of nature. The main level includes a primary bedroom with private bath, plus two additional bedrooms served by a full hall bath. The finished lower level adds approximately 600 square feet of versatile living space, ideal for a family room, fitness area, or recreation space, along with laundry and additional storage. Step outside and enjoy multiple decks overlooking a large, private backyard, creating the perfect setting for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply unwinding in nature. Additional highlights include central air, attached 1-car garage, and storage shed. Ideally located just minutes from shopping, schools, Metro North, Merritt Parkway, and Cranbury Park. Excellent credit and references a must. Dogs will be considered on a case by case basis. No cats or birds.
