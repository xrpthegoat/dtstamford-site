About this home

Steeped in history and rich with original character, the Samuel Fitch House (c. 1800) presents an opportunity to own an authentic piece of Wilton's past. Just 2 miles from Wilton Center, this 2,121 sf Colonial offers 3 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, surrounded by lush perennial gardens & mature fruit trees. Inside, the home showcases many of the architectural details that antique home enthusiasts seek, including wide-plank floors, hand-hewn beams, original fireplaces, period millwork, built-in cabinetry, and handcrafted doors and hardware. More than two centuries of thoughtful evolution have allowed the home to retain the warmth, craftsmanship & character that simply cannot be replicated. Whether you're searching for a full-time residence or a peaceful weekend retreat, you'll appreciate the easy commute to New York City by train or car, nearby walking trails & convenient access to both Wilton Center and Westport. Located within one of Connecticut's most highly regarded school systems, the setting offers the perfect balance of history, convenience & natural beauty. The Samuel Fitch House is being offered as-is and is best suited for buyers who genuinely appreciate the unique qualities of a historic home. Continue the stewardship of a residence that has stood for over two centuries. Buyers should expect the character, imperfections & ongoing care that accompany a home of this vintage while embracing the privilege of preserving an important piece of Connecticut history.
