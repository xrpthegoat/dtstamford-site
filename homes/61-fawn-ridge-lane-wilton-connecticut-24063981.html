About this home

Move right into this condo in the sought after Fawn Ridge condo complex! Enjoy the Wilton amenities and schools! This beautiful sun filled end unit features an updated eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and a wine fridge with sliders to a deck overlooking the tennis courts and pool. The spacious living room has crown molding and a fireplace. Upstairs is a bedroom with a vaulted ceiling and lots of windows! In the primary bedroom there are two closets with one being a walk-in. The full bath has a jetted tub as well as a shower and two linen closets. In the lower level there is a finished room that could be used as an office or exercise room, as well as an entrance to the garage. There is also a carport. The assessment for the paving has been paid in full by the owner. Enjoy the good life in this bucolic complex close to everything! Just pack your bags and move in.
