About this home

This charming Westport home, ideally located just minutes away from shops, dining, entertainment, cafes, the library, parks, schools, and the beach, is a perfect blend of vintage charm and modern living. The moment you step inside this sweet Antique, you'll be charmed by its warmth and brightness. The inviting front-to-back living and dining room, with hardwood floors, flows seamlessly into a spacious eat-in kitchen with updated stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, granite countertops, and a kitchen island. A versatile front room right off the kitchen can serve as a home office, TV room, playroom, etc. A renovated powder room completes this level. Upstairs, the primary suite offers hardwood floors, two walk-in closets, and an ensuite bathroom with dual sinks and a luxurious shower. Two additional spacious bedrooms share a hallway bath with a tub/shower combo and plenty of storage. The lower level, with interior and exterior access, features a carpeted living area and a separate laundry/storage room. Outdoors, enjoy a private patio off the kitchen, a fully fenced yard, two raised garden beds, and a detached two-car garage. This home truly accommodates modern needs and desires with timeless appeal.
