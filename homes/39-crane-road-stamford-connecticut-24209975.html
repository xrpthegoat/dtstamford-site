About this home

Welcome 39 Crane Road, move-in ready 3-bedroom, 2 full bath home located on a cul-de-sac desirable Newfield neighborhood. The open-concept main floor features a spacious living room with fireplace, recessed lighting, new flooring, and a stunning new eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances and easy access to the rear deck and fenced backyard. The upper level offers recessed lighting, a new full bath, and three generous bedrooms. Exterior highlights include new windows, doors, siding, roof, new deck. Enjoy this quiet, walkable neighborhood just off Newfield Avenue, it offers both comfort and convenience for today's busy families. -- The location is unbeatable: stroll to Belltown Park, shops, houses of worship, the Tully Center, or hop on public transportation right on Newfield Avenue.
