About this home

Welcome to this stunning 5,552 sqft, 5 bedroom, 5/1 bath new home located in the heart of Longs Lots on a quiet, highly desirable cul-de-sac. Close to everything Westport has to offer, this beautiful home offers an open floor plan with graciously sized, sun-filled rooms with ample space to gather and also spread out making it ideal for today's lifestyle. Finished on four levels using high end finishes throughout such as Thermador appliances, 5" white oak floors, quartz countertops, custom closets, a generator, and a pool site, this home will fulfill every wish on your list. House newly built on existing foundation. Builder offering one year warranty.
