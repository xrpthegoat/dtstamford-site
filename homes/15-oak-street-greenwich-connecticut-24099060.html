About this home

Step inside this beautifully designed home and experience the perfect blend of modern elegance and everyday comfort. The open-concept layout offers a seamless flow, enhanced by sophisticated finishes and abundant natural light throughout. At the heart of the home is a show-stopping kitchen featuring a large center island with quartz countertops, custom white cabinetry, and stainless-steel appliances. The spacious dining area with sliding glass door to a private patio and yard framed by stone walls and flower beds -your own peaceful retreat. The expansive living room showcases an elevated view of the river, and marina, with gleaming hardwood floors throughout that add warmth and style and an elegant 1/2 bath. Upstairs, the primary suite offers generous closet space and a luxurious designer bath. Two additional light-filled bedrooms and a full modern designer bathroom. The lower level includes a laundry room and an oversized two-car tandem garage with plenty of additional storage. Built with attention to detail and quality craftsmanship, this exceptional home offers stylish living in an unbeatable, ultra-convenient location-just minutes from the train, I-95, parks, beaches, shopping, and dining. A rare find that truly checks all the boxes!
