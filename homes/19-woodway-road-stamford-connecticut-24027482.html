About this home

Charming Townhome in a Serene Community Welcome to this beautifully maintained townhome in a peaceful and well-manicured community. The inviting exterior features lush landscaping and a cozy porch. Inside, the bright living room with hardwood floors and a fireplace opens to a private balcony. The adjacent dining area is perfect for gatherings, with a stylish chandelier and easy kitchen access. The updated eat in kitchen boasts modern stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, ample cabinetry and tile flooring. The main level's hardwood floors extend throughout, adding warmth and elegance. The spacious bedrooms, located on the bedroom level with hardwood floors, include a serene primary suite and a large second bedroom, both offering plenty of natural light and ample storage. Bathrooms are tastefully updated with contemporary finishes, including a fully updated full bathroom and a stylish half bath. The property also features a versatile upper-level space perfect for a home office or additional living area, and a convenient in-unit laundry room with extra storage. Additionally, the windows and sliding doors have been updated to triple pane, ensuring energy efficiency and enhanced comfort throughout the year. Enjoy the convenience and charm of Springdale Village and its many nearby amenities, including shopping, dining, and recreational facilities in this amazing community.
