About this home

Fantastic opportunity to rent a wonderful single-family, ranch-style home in North Stamford. There is charm galore with a handsome stone fireplace, gleaming hardwood floors, custom trim work and glass-paned doors. Enter to find a tremendous living room with high ceilings, wood-burning fireplace and doors out to the patio area. There is plenty of room to entertain your guests! The kitchen has been updated with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. Walls of windows provide a wooded view from the dining area. Two of the bedrooms share a large full bath with dual sink vanity with granite countertops and a tub/shower combination. The third bedroom has its own en-suite bath with shower. There is generous closet space and a laundry area off of the kitchen. The basement below is accessed through the garage and has lots of room for storage. There is parking for a small to mid-sized car in the garage and another parking area right in front of the home. If the sound of birds and wooded views are your idea of Zen, then this is the spot for you!
