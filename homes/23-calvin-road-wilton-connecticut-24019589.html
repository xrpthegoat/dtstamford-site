About this home

HIGHEST & BEST OFFERS DUE 11AM TUESDAY, 5/28. Tucked away down a private drive on 2.43 peaceful acres, this gracious Wilton Colonial is an ideal location near Ambler Farm and Weston center. Views of the pretty property from every window are a nature lover's dream. Expansive deck with new Azek decking overlooking the yard along with a screened porch right off the kitchen are perfect for outdoor meals, entertaining or simply reading a good book and listening to bird songs. Renovated and well-maintained, the bounty of improvements include new Marvin windows, new clapboard siding, central air, central vac, decorative moldings and a finished, walk-up third floor. Enter the home to the sunlit front-to-back Living Room with wood burning fireplace, hardwood floors and french doors to the deck, opening to the Dining Rm accented with chair rail. Enjoy working from home in the Office with a wall of built-in bookcases, hardwood floors and bright bay window. Prepare meals easily in the chef's Kitchen with custom cabinetry, new cooktop with pot filler, hardwood floors and direct access to the screened porch. Upstairs provides an ensuite Primary BR with walk-in closet & updated bath, three additional BRs and hall bath. New third floor with beautiful windows & hardwood floors adds bonus space for a playroom, office or guest area! Walk-out LL can be finished and expansion potential on the second floor. Convenient to Wilton town center, schools & Weston with easy drive to highways & trains.
