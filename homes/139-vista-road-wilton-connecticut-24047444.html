About this home

Experience the best of Connecticut country living with charm, seclusion, privacy and tranquility. A striking entryway with custom molding and pillars leads into a living room with a cathedral ceiling and fireplace. The eat in kitchen features granite counters and a picture window to enjoy your backyard view year-round. The large dining room boasts wainscoting, while a cozy office/library is a perfect spot to relax with a book. The family room, with cathedral ceiling, skylights, built ins, and a library ladder, opens to the patio. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom includes a cathedral ceiling, fireplace, and palladian window and an updated bath ideal for unwinding. A second large bedroom completes the upper floor. The finished lower level with flexible space for a home office, playroom and or gym. Outdoors, your private oasis includes a charming shed, and a large patio perfect for entertaining, morning coffee or a evening glass of wine. The rear hall includes laundry and a mudroom, providing additional functional space. A front circular drive welcomes guests. This home showcases quality craftmanship throughout, with new furnace, freshly painted exterior, generator & hardwood floors.
