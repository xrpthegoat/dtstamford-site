About this home

Welcome to this charming three-bedroom, two-bath Cape-style home located on a peaceful, tree-lined street in Stamford's coveted Glenbrook neighborhood, just minutes from Cove Island Beach, Metro-North, downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, and more. Filled with natural sunlight, this inviting home features a recently renovated kitchen that opens seamlessly to the family room, creating a bright, comfortable space ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The flexible floor plan includes two bedrooms upstairs, a bedroom on the main level, and a separate home office-perfect for today's work-from-home lifestyle. Step outside to enjoy the rear deck overlooking a fully fenced backyard with a classic white picket fence, offering plenty of space for relaxing, entertaining, or outdoor play. Additional highlights include a heated two-car garage with convenient overhead storage and a newer roof, replaced approximately a year and a half ago. With its sun-filled rooms, inviting layout, thoughtful updates, and convenient location, this Glenbrook home offers the perfect combination of comfort, charm, and accessibility.
