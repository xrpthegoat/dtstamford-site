About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's highly sought-after Newfield neighborhood, this beautifully updated 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath center hall Colonial offers the perfect blend of elegance, comfort, & everyday functionality. Designed for both modern living & effortless entertaining, the home features a seamless flow with spacious formal living & dining rooms, ideal for gatherings. The stunning, brand new eat in kitchen with stainless steel Bosch appliances & center island opens to a sun drenched family room with vaulted ceilings, skylights, & a wood burning fireplace. A convenient main level laundry room adds to the home's thoughtful layout. Two sets of sliders lead to an oversized IPE deck & bluestone patio, creating an exceptional outdoor setting overlooking the expansive, .46 acre property framed by lush, mature plantings. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, complete with vaulted ceilings, custom millwork, heated floors, & a spa inspired bath with dual vanity, soaking tub, & walk in shower. Three additional spacious bedrooms & a beautifully renovated hall bath complete the upper level. A walk up attic offers excellent storage & future expansion potential, while the expansive finished lower level provides incredible flexibility for a playroom, gym, and/or home office. A large two car garage completes the home. Enjoy the convenience of city water, sewer, & gas, all just minutes to shopping, dining, parks, houses of worship, & Metro North for an easy commute.
