About this home

Welcome to this beautiful turn-key Colonial with serene views and ultimate privacy at the end of a cul-de-sac. Located in highly desirable Lower Wilton just minutes from local shops, top-tier restaurants, and everyday amenities, with the bustling town centers of Westport, Wilton and Norwalk. With easy access to Metro-North train stations for commuting to New York. This home offers the ultimate blend of tranquility and unmatched convenience. The inviting floor plan includes a spacious living room, dining room perfect for holiday gatherings, and a cozy family room anchored by a warm fireplace-with direct access to the spacious deck for effortless entertaining. The heart of the home is an updated eat-in kitchen with granite counters, stainless appliances, and versatile cabinet storage. Upstairs are four spacious bedrooms and tastefully updated bathrooms, including a private front to back primary suite. Looking for more space? The finished walk-out lower level serves as the perfect bonus room, home office, gym, or guest suite. Potential first floor or lower level apartment with separate entrance offers versatility. Complete with a full-service automatic whole house generator (Briggs & Stratton) for your peace of mind. The home is fully equipped with the Google Smart Home ecosystem which services the interior home lights, climate controlled thermostat and security system. Don't miss the video tour link!
