About this home

Storied and superbly restored, 'The Lookout' at 249 Greens Farms Rd blends the grandeur of a 1907 Victorian with todays modern luxury. Privately set among perennial gardens and antique copper beeches, this iconic 16-room residence offers a gracious floor plan with elegant formal spaces, a chefs eat-in kitchen, media room, wine cellar, and inviting family areas. Original architectural details shine alongside impeccable updates. The primary suite features a spa bath and custom closets; three additional bedrooms offer flexible space. Outside, relax by a sparkling pool or host gatherings on the lush, landscaped lawn. An adjacent lot for added privacy or future expansion is also available. A true Westport landmark, steps from town, schools, and rail, this is a rare opportunity to own an estate rich in character and comfort. Shown by appointment
