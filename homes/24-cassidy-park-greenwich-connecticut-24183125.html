About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated Colonial offering spacious living and exceptional comfort throughout. The main level features a large eat-in kitchen, inviting family room, formal dining room, and an expansive living room with a cozy fireplace. Gleaming hardwood floors flow throughout the home, adding warmth and elegance. The fully finished lower level provides an enormous living space complete with a full bathroom, perfect for a recreation room, home office, gym, or guest/au pair. Step outside to the deck through the walk-out lower level-ideal for entertaining and outdoor gatherings. Upstairs, you'll find four generously sized bedrooms, each offering ample closet space. The primary suite features a private bath with a relaxing tub. Additional highlights include abundant storage, a two-car garage, and ample driveway parking. Conveniently located near downtown shopping and dining, the train station, playgrounds, and hospital, this home offers the perfect combination of space, comfort, and accessibility.
