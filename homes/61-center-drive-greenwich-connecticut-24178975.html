About this home

Welcome to 61 Center Drive, a move-in-ready Old Greenwich home on a dead-end lot. Thoughtfully updated across two levels, this 5-bedroom, 3-full bath home offers approximately 2,300 square feet with bright gathering spaces, renovated finishes, and multiple areas to work, host, relax, or spread out. The main level features hardwood floors, a newly updated kitchen that easily flows into a spacious dining and living room overlooking the foyer with generous natural light. Three bedrooms and two newly renovated full bathrooms complete this level. The finished lower level adds meaningful versatility with a spacious bedroom, renovated full bathroom, fireside recreation room, and an additional flex room that can function as a bedroom, office, gym, or playroom. With its own sense of privacy, this level is well suited for extended-stay guests, multigenerational living, work-from-home needs, or simply extra room to unwind. Outside, a manicured front lawn and garden provide welcoming curb appeal, while the private backyard stone patio offers an inviting setting for summer dinners, weekend grilling, and easy outdoor entertaining. Set in a highly convenient Old Greenwich location, 61 Center Drive is just minutes from Metro-North, Old Greenwich Village, Binney Park, and the town beach, Tod's Point. The home also offers access to sought-after Greenwich public schools, including Eastern Middle School and the International School at Dundee, an authorized International Baccalaureate school.
