About this home

Nestled in the coastal community of Rowayton, CT, this stunning residence offers seaside living on a private acre of mature landscaped grounds. This sanctuary features multiple terraces, a covered porch for enjoying ocean breezes, lush gardens, a sparkling gunite pool with potential for a charming pool house, and an expansive lawn for outdoor enjoyment. Spanning approximately 6,000 square feet, the home boasts an open floor plan with high ceilings and sun-drenched rooms, exuding airy coastal charm. Designed for seamless indoor-outdoor integration, the main floor caters to both intimate gatherings and grand events. Custom built for the current owners, the home includes exquisite trim work and built-ins throughout, enhancing its coastal elegance.The chef's kitchen, with ample cabinetry, gourmet appliances, double sinks, and a generous island, echoes seaside serenity. It connects to the family and living rooms, both opening to the terraces and pool area, promoting a harmonious flow. The second floor features a tranquil master suite with walk-in closets and a spa bath with heated floors and a steam shower. Four additional bedrooms, a playroom, and a fully-equipped laundry room complete this level. The third floor offers a versatile space, ideal for an extra bedroom or office. An outbuilding offers potential for a guest/in-law cottage, enhancing coastal appeal.
