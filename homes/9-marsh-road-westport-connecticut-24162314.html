About this home

Don't miss this rare opportunity to wake up every day to the serenity of breathtaking water views & the charm of coastal living at 9 Marsh Rd, nestled in the coveted Saugatuck Shores of Westport. Perfectly positioned on a private 0.23-acre lot within a tranquil waterfront community, this FEMA-compliant home has been meticulously remodeled to offer modern elegance & everyday comfort. Step inside to find an open-concept layout featuring 2 cozy fireplaces, gleaming hardwood floors, a bright, updated eat-in kitchen complete w/ a spacious island, walk-in pantry & top-of-the-line appliances. With 4 bedrms & 3 full bths, this home provides plenty of room for family & guests. The primary suite is a true retreat, w/ an oversized walk-in closet & a spa-like bathroom w/a soaking tub, stall shower & double sinks. The heart of the home is the sunlit family room, where soaring ceilings & walls of windows frame sweeping water views. Step out onto the expansive Ipe deck w/sleek marine railings, the perfect spot for al fresco dining, birdwatching, or admiring stunning sunsets. The private, fenced yard features an outdoor shower, a whole-house generator, a 2-car garage, & enclosed storage. Embrace the coastal lifestyle w/ ease: stroll to nearby Canal Beach, launch your kayak or paddleboard, or set sail from the Cedar Point Yacht Club, a short walk from the home. And, when you're ready for a change of pace, Westport's vibrant downtown, gourmet dining, & boutique shopping are just minutes away.
