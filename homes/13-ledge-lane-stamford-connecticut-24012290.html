About this home

Bright and charming three story colonial home on convenient cul-de-sac. Great flow on first floor. Living room plus den/office, kitchen, mudroom and bath, dining room with French doors that lead into the family room. Three bedrooms and full bath on second floor. Third floor is a finished attic that can be a playroom or guest bedroom. Hardwood floors, wide baseboard moldings. 200 amp electric. Detached garage.Beautiful fenced in level yard on a cul de sac in walkable area. Patio off family room, detached 1 car garage with ample parking.
