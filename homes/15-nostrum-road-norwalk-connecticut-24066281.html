About this home

Charming and well-cared-for ranch home nestled in the desirable Spring Hill neighborhood, available for the first time ever! This single-level gem is ideal for first-time buyers, downsizers, or anyone seeking the ease of one-floor living. Highlights include three comfortable bedrooms, a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, two full baths, and a bright eat-in kitchen. Beautiful hardwood floors flow through most of the main level, adding warmth and character. The full basement features a small workshop, a convenient half bath, and plenty of storage space, offering endless possibilities for your needs. Designed for low-maintenance living, this home is perfect for creating new memories and room to grow. Enjoy the convenience of walking to nearby schools and a quick drive to all the dining, shopping, and entertainment that vibrant Norwalk provides.
