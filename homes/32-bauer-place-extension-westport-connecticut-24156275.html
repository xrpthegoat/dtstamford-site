About this home

Live effortlessly on a quiet cul de sac in picturesque Westport's highly desirable Long Lots neighborhood. This beautifully updated and meticulously maintained Cape style home offers 1,752 sq ft of light-filled living space- The inviting main level, all with newly refinished hardwood floors, is designed for easy one-level living and includes a serene first floor primary bedroom with en-suite bath, a kitchen equipped with a Wolfe range and stainless steel appliances, generous granite counter space, and a 5.5' center island/breakfast bar. The kitchen flows effortlessly to the dining room and a bright family room anchored by a fireplace. Completing the first floor is a large, open living room with custom built-ins and oversized window, a central front to back foyer with direct access to enjoy the spacious bluestone patio and fully fenced back yard, and a shed for extra storage. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms and a flexible office or study provide room to grow, work, or unwind. A new boiler & HW tank. Absolutely impeccable in and out.
