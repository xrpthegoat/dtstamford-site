About this home

Come see this fabulous Colonial ideally located in the vibrant downtown Stamford area. This sun-filled home features a bright living room and dining room, along with a kitchen that opens to a beautiful stone patio and fenced side yard, perfect for BBQs, outdoor entertaining, and relaxing. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms plus a dedicated office, ideal for working from home. Stunning hardwood floors run throughout the home, adding warmth and character to every room. Additional features include central air conditioning, a charming front porch, a one-car garage, attic storage, and a full walk-out basement with washer and dryer. Conveniently located just minutes from public transportation, restaurants, shopping, and all that downtown Stamford has to offer. Don't miss this opportunity to enjoy comfort, charm, and convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable locations! Public Schools are: Elementary-Hart Magnet School, Middle-Rippowam, High School-Westhill.
