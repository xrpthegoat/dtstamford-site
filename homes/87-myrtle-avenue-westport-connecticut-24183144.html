About this home

Just when you thought all the good summer rentals were gone! Charming New England Colonial literally steps to everything downtown Westport has to offer yet offering your own private oasis. Some of Fairfield's best shopping can also be found here while you stroll along the scenic Saugatuck river! This home delivers all your summer dreams and you will feel completely relaxed the moment you step inside. 1 month minimum stays with longer visits welcome.
