About this home

Distinctive Dream Home in Coveted Westport, CT! Colorful gardens and a circular drive welcome you to a home with its own personality on an established cul-de-sac lane. Within walking distance of neighborhood eateries and award-winning schools and minutes of all that Westport has to offer, enjoy primetime living year-round. Double glass entry doors open to a gracious Foyer and impressive Great Room with a dramatic stone fireplace, vaulted ceiling, original beams and captivating garden views that surround the living and dining spaces. The culinary hub in the renovated Kitchen features white granite counters, an oversized stainless refrigerator and freezer, and an enviable spice pantry. The sunny Breakfast Area and Family Room flow to the Office, Screened Porch, and Deck. The private Primary Suite with a renovated bath and double walk-in closets completes the main level. Upstairs are four Guest Bedrooms and two full baths, including access to the front balcony and a partially finished walk-up attic.
