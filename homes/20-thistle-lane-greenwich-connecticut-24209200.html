About this home

Welcome to 20 Thistle Lane, a beautifully updated raised ranch home set on a tranquil Greenwich cul-de-sac. A private one acre lot directly abutting Pemberwick Park, the property is partially bordered by a peaceful pond with a charming small fountain. The main level features warm wood floors, a welcoming fireplace, and a fully renovated kitchen with quartz countertops, ss appliances, and white shaker cabinets. Direct access to the private backyard is located in the kitchen for convenience and entertaining. Included in this level are a full bath, 2 bedrooms and the primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and an ensuite bathroom. Lower level provides new lvp flooring, a second fireplace, built-in's, and a walk-out to the bluestone patio for entertainment. A newly renovated full bathroom, separate laundry space, 1 bedroom, plus another bonus room that can be used as a guest room, office space, or playroom, complete this level. The entire interior of the home has been freshly painted. This home is conveniently located near public transportation, train stations, and major highways, while still providing the serenity of nature and park-like privacy.
