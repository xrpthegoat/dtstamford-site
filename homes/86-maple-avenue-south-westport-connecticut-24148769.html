About this home

Renovated, refreshed, and move-in ready, this sunfilled Greens Farms gem offers the perfect blend of charm, style, and modern comfort. Set on a beautifully landscaped, level acre with gunite pool and expansive patio, the outdoor living experience is exceptional. Fresh paint inside and out, a new pergola, and thoughtful landscaping create irresistible curb appeal. Step inside and feel instantly at home. The inviting Dining Room and elegant Living Room with fireplace flow seamlessly toward a private office. The updated eat-in kitchen features gleaming new countertops and opens directly to the dramatic Great Room, where soaring ceilings and French doors frame views of the tiered patio, lush grounds, and pool. Upstairs, you'll find four bright bedrooms and two full baths. The spacious Primary Suite is a true retreat, complete with a sitting room, two walk-in closets, a well appointed bath, and striking vaulted ceilings. The heated lower level offers flexible bonus space, ideal for a playroom, gym, or creative studio. Two car garage with new mudroom. A home that feels as good as it looks. You're going to love it.
