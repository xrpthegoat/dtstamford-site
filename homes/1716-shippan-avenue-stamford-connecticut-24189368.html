About this home

Enjoy the distinctive coastal lifestyle of Stamford's sought-after Shippan neighborhood in this distinguished Tudor home, offering approximately 6,000 square feet of timeless architectural character, sophisticated modern updates, and a thoughtfully designed 2016 renovation by Granoff Architects. With 5 bedrooms, 3 full and 2 half baths, the home is rich in detail, including original stained glass, oak paneling, unique solid oak arched interior doors, and three wood-burning fireplaces. Custom millwork, a Vermont slate roof, and new Marvin windows throughout further showcase the home's exceptional craftsmanship. Beautifully renovated second-floor bathrooms feature custom cabinetry, marble showers and countertops, and heated floors. In addition to the generous living and entertaining spaces, the home also includes a finished lower level, a third-floor loft with water views (currently used as a home gym), and an attached greenhouse that adds to the home's classic charm. Outside, a 40' x 20' gunite pool and spa, expansive patio, and mature garden create a picturesque private oasis. Beautifully landscaped and exceptionally private, the grounds provide an idyllic backdrop for sun-filled summer days, evenings spent poolside and memorable gatherings with family and friends. An exceptional Shippan offering with remarkable space, character and versatility. ** Also available for purchase with the adjacent lot at 1688 Shippan Avenue (MLS #24189415).
