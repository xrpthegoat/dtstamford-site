About this home

Set on 1.66 level, lushly wooded acres along Stony Brook, 6 Blueberry Lane is the rare offering in central Darien: a storied 1950 home beautifully reimagined for today's lifestyle. The bones of a quiet, classic property - generous lot, brook frontage, and a coveted location moments from town, train, and the Sound. Ceilings have been raised throughout, giving the principal living spaces the volume and light buyers expect at this tier - an instant departure from the typical mid-century cottage feel. The heart of the home is a stunning kitchen built for cooks who actually cook. Sub-Zero refrigerator and Wolf oven/range anchor a space tailored for both daily family life & entertaining at scale, w/custom cabinetry, generous prep surfaces, and a layout that flows seamlessly into the adjacent gathering rooms and out to the property. The original wood-burning fireplace remains - one of the few period notes intentionally preserved, and the room you'll instinctively reach for on a cool fall evening. Three bedrooms and two-and-a-half baths are configured for ease and privacy, with the addition of a brand-new powder room solving the single most common gripe buyers have with vintage Darien homes of this size. 1.66 level acres on Stony Brook is an exceedingly rare lot profile in this pocket of Darien w/room for a pool, a sport court or simply privacy & birdsong. Minutes from Darien schools, Metro-North stations, town beaches & downtown. Turnkey!
