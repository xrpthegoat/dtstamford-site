About this home

Well maintained contemporary with a mid-century modern flair! This home sits majestically up off a cul-de-sac in a coveted Westover neighborhood near Mianus River Park. First time on market since it was custom-built by owners. This home offer's an open floor plan, high ceilings and walls of windows that fill each room with natural light. Seamless indoor/outdoor living with a wrap-around deck overlooking a private back yard. The generous sized primary bedroom suite includes a private deck outfitted with an outdoor shower, an updated en-suite bathroom, and walk-in-closets that will impress. A guest bedroom with en-suite bath and two additional light-filled bedrooms are perfectly situated. The office includes a private entrance for flexible use. The exterior is enhanced with mature plantings highlighting the interesting angular lines of the house. The neighborhood is an enclave of custom-built contemporary homes each with its own distinct personality. This is a unique property that is designed to be lived in and enjoy.
