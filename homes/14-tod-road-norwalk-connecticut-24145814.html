About this home

A complete gut renovation in 2023 has transformed this property into a gorgeous, transitional ranch home. Enter the great room with soaring ceiling, inviting electric "fireplace" open kitchen and dining area. A large Primary Bedroom suite with full bath, and 2 additional well sized bedrooms plus full hall bath complete the main area of the house. A large family/bonus room is on the new upper level. In addition, there is a small mud room/laundry near the garage entrance. Everything in this house was brand new in 2023: floors, walls, windows, heating system, central AC, kitchen and baths. Spray foam insulation was used to ensure energy efficiency as well as thermal windows. New propane heating system. Partially finished attic space for storage. There is also an inviting back yard with patio to enjoy in the warm weather. It doesn't get any nicer than this!
