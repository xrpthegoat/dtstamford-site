About this home

A city oasis awaits the next owner. 3 bedroom, 2 full bath, and study or office, single family home in the heart of Glenbrook. Open floor plan for easy living. Lovely kitchen renovation sparkles with soft white cabinets, granite counter tops and newer appliances . Newly painted throughout. Wood floors have just been refinished. The Living room dining room combination features a wood burning fireplace and sliders that lead to a generous deck covered by and arbor with trailing Wisteria and built in benches. Leading to a lovely flat welcoming yard. One car garage just off the partial basement/laundry/ storage area. Plenty of natural light in every room. Close to Stamford High School, Tully Health Center, transportation, shopping, restaurants, movies.
