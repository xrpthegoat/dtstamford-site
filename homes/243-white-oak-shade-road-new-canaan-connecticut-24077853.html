About this home

Quietly tucked away on ~2.5 private acres in the highly coveted South of the YMCA neighborhood, this stunning colonial estate offers an unparalleled lifestyle. Located just minutes from the heart of downtown New Canaan, Waveny Park, and top-rated schools, this one-of-a-kind home is a rare gem. The home has been thoughtfully transformed inside and out, blending timeless charm with modern sophistication. This meticulously updated residence boasts 4 spacious bedrooms, each with its own private bath, providing luxury and privacy. Every detail has been considered - from the newly installed white oak hardwood floors to the exquisite Farrow & Ball paint, Viking / Subzero appliances, updated baths, brand new roofs on home & pool house and the finest lighting fixtures... just to name a few! Bright and sun-drenched, the home's interiors exude warmth and character, creating a welcoming space for both relaxation and entertaining. Step outside and discover a true private oasis: lush manicured grounds, stunning flower beds and mature plantings. The sparkling pool and pool house complete this outdoor paradise, offering the perfect setting for leisurely days or vibrant gatherings. This home is not just a property, but a lifestyle. It is the perfect combination of luxury, comfort, & convenience, and it's ready for you to move in & enjoy. Experience the best of New England living-bring your toothbrush & get ready to immerse yourself in the unparalleled beauty of this extraordinary property.
