About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Darien's sought-after Tokeneke School District, this beautifully renovated and expanded six-bedroom home offers exceptional living inside and out. Renovated in 2015, the home features a stunning gourmet eat-in kitchen with top-of-the-line appliances, two oversized islands with quartzite countertops, soaring ceilings, and skylights and oversized windows that fill the space with natural light. The adjacent family room with a gas fireplace creates the perfect setting for both everyday living and entertaining. Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living with a spacious deck, outdoor fireplace, and private fenced backyard. Conveniently located just minutes from the Darien train station, shopping, restaurants, Costco, Wegmans, and all the amenities Darien has to offer.
