About this home

Tucked away at the end of a private lane, this Nantucket-style Colonial sits on 1.21 level acres with city water, offering privacy, flexibility, and space rarely found in Westport. Rebuilt in 2006 on an existing foundation, the property combines classic shingle-style architecture with thoughtful modern living. The main residence features 5 bedrooms, 4.5 baths, and a dedicated home office. The chef's kitchen with professional appliances opens to a spacious deck overlooking the expansive backyard-ideal for entertaining or everyday living. Multiple gathering spaces include a dramatic skylit den/playroom with vaulted ceilings, perfect for a gym, media room, playroom, or recreation space. A standout feature is the full accessory apartment with 2 bedrooms, kitchenette, full bath, and its own garage, offering excellent flexibility for extended family, guests, au pair, or potential rental income. Set on flat, usable land at the end of a quiet private lane, the property provides a peaceful setting while remaining convenient to Westport amenities, schools, and commuting routes. A rare opportunity to own a spacious and versatile home in a desirable private setting. No wetlands or floozone. Agents see attachments
