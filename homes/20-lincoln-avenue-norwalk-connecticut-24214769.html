About this home

Dinner in SoNo, a walk along the waterfront, and evenings by the fireplace. This 3 bedroom, 2 full bath Cape at 20 Lincoln Avenue puts you near Norwalk's waterfront, parks, and restaurants. The main level has hardwood floors, freshly painted rooms, and a living room with a fireplace. The primary bedroom has its own full bathroom, with a second full bath also on the main floor. Upstairs are 2 nice size bedrooms. Laundry is in the basement with an attached garage. Nearby are SoNo's Restaurants, Maritime Aquarium, Norwalk Cove Marina, Calf Pasture Beach, and Oyster Shell Park, with convenient access to Metro North and I-95. Electric heat in the upstairs bedrooms. Fireplace never used. Sale subject to Probate Court approval. Home sold as is.
