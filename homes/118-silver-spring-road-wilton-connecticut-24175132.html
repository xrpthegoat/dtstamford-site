About this home

Refined Silver Spring colonial offering rare 4 bd 3.5ba functionality, expansive living space, open grounds, and seamless room flow for modern family life. Minutes to town, schools, and train. Set within a sought after cul de sac once part of the Firestone Family Estate, this property delivers a peaceful setting while still feeling connected and convenient. Inside, the main level is built for easy living. The open kitchen and eating area flow directly into an inviting family room-perfect for casual nights in or hosting friends. Hardwood floors run throughout the main level, and large windows bring in beautiful natural light from morning to evening, giving the home a bright, welcoming feel in every season. Upstairs, the refreshed primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a spacious updated bath, two walk-in closets, and a versatile bonus room that can flex to fit your needs-home office, yoga studio, nursery, or a quiet reading space. Three additional bedrooms with new recessed lighting are served by two full hall baths. Downstairs, the finished walk-out basement adds valuable living space and offers interior access to the garage-ideal for a media room, play space, workout area, or guest setup. Step outside to the large deck made for entertaining, and enjoy a yard that feels like your own private slice of nature. Even the location of this home within the community can't be beat. Bus stops right out front!
