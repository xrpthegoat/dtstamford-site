About this home

Beautifully updated, sun-drenched 3 level townhouse in the heart of Glenbrook, offers an open floor plan with 2 bedrooms and 2.5 baths. Primary suite boasts walk-in closet and ensuite bath with Calcutta marble and jacuzzi tub. Features hardwood floors, gas fireplace, designer finishes throughout including upscale lighting, wallpaper and carpeting. 2 flat screen TVs, surround sound, wine-cooler, private balcony and gas grill. Washer/Dryer in unit, attic and basement storage, heated 2 car garage. Convenient to shopping, dining and Glenbrook Metro North Station. This home combines modern elegance with ultimate convenience.
