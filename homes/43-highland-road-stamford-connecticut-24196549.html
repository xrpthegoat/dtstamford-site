About this home

Updated 985 square foot condo in sought after Forest Mews, tucked into Stamford's Mid City neighborhood. The living room centers on a fireplace with sliders out to a private deck, and newer hardwood floors run through the living room, dining room and bedroom. Granite kitchen with stainless appliances. Primary bedroom with en suite bath and generous closet space, plus an updated powder room with granite counter for guests. Full size stainless washer and dryer in the unit. Energy efficient heat pump and water heater keep costs down, and central air handles the summer. One assigned garage space included. Walk to shopping, restaurants and public transportation, with quick access to I-95, the Merritt and the Stamford train station. Yearly unfurnished lease, available now.
