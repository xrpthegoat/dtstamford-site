About this home

Classic Colonial in Sought-After Bettswood Area Welcome to this charming 4-bedroom,4-bath Colonial, perfectly situated in the desirable Bettswood neighborhood of Norwalk. Built in 1920 and beautifully maintained, this home blends timeless character with modern updates.Step inside through the beautiful Dutch front door into a sun-filled interior featuring hardwood floors throughout, impressive moldings, and a spacious living room with a built-ins, bench seating and stone fireplace. French doors lead to a versatile office, while the updated kitchen boasts marble countertops and double sinks, a laundry room, mudroom, and full bath conveniently located nearby. Upstairs, the dramatic primary suite offers a renovated bathroom with soaking tub and walk-in shower. Additional bedrooms are generously sized, including one with a cozy dressing or study area. Enjoy outdoor living with a private backyard, a charming stone patio, and a unique terrace with a rail walkway above - the perfect place to relax and take in the view. The oversized detached garage includes a spacious walk-up loft with a side entrance, ideal for storage, a studio, or potential finished space.This classic home shines in the evenings with thoughtful exterior lighting and has been recently painted. Also note there is a lvl 2 ChargePoint EV charger in garge. Located just off Historic Norwalk Green, you'll love the convenience of being close to the beach, train station, I-95,Merritt Parkway, shopping, and restaurants.
