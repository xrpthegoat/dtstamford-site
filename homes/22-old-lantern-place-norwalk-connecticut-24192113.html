About this home

On a level corner lot, this raised-ranch style is nestled in the desirable Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk. With an expansive main level and the finished lower level, this home has over 2,500 sq ft of living space. This home has several luxury features including hardwood floors, central air, cherry cabinets, high-end stainless steel appliances, a full primary suite, a fireplace in the family room, and a two-car garage. The sunroom exits to a large deck, perfect for entertaining! The location offers convenient access to Route 7, the Merritt Pkwy, local parks and trails, and tons of downtown Norwalk amenities. This gem is perfect for anyone seeking open space and comfort in a peaceful community.
