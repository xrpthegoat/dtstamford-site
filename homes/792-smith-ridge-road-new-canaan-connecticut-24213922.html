About this home

Charming Cape Cod Retreat Tucked away on six secluded acres of lush woods with a picturesque pond, this delightful home offers the perfect blend of privacy and convenience. Located on prestigious Smith Ridge Road in New Canaan, you're just minutes from the Village, Irwin Park, Waveny Park, and the Merritt Parkway. Enjoy serene strolls along a scenic path bordered by historic stone walls that lead to your very own pond - a perfect escape from the everyday. This delightful home features two spacious bedrooms, three full baths, a home office or study, a cozy family room, a welcoming living room, and a bright eat-in kitchen. An attached garage, ample attic and storage space provide practical living, while a large back patio invites outdoor entertaining and special moments with friends and family. Live the exceptional New Canaan lifestyle, known for its vibrant community and top-rated schools. Lots of possibilities are here for you to make this home your own and really enjoy the New Canaan lifestyle or build your own dream home in this peaceful, private setting.
