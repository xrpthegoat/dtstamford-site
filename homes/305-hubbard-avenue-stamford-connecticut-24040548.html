About this home

Fantastic 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath, single family house for rent in the historic Hubbard Heights, Mid City Stamford area. Located on a quiet cut-de-sac, residential street, moments to Ridgeway Shopping Center, Scalzi Park, E. Gaynor Brennan Golf Course and Stamford Hospital, various buses, and vibrant downtown. 3 upstairs bedrooms and 1 full bath plus 1 bedroom downstairs with its own full bath and independent heating/cooling. Living room, formal dining room, sunroom, full kitchen with seating nook and stainless-steel appliances. House has wood-burning fireplace, central air and 3 heating zones with Smartphone compatible thermostats (Nest). Solar panels mean lower electric bills! Other features include hardwood floors throughout, washer/dryer in basement, ample storage, fully fenced in backyard, detached garage, long driveway suitable for multiple cars and ample street parking. Equidistant from I-95 and Merritt Pkwy. Must have good references, credit check and proof of current employment.
