About this home

Welcome to Chesterfield, Stamford's premier gated community! This beautifully maintained tri-level townhouse offers the perfect blend of comfort, privacy, convenience, and what a view!! NOTICE, the tax card for town is incorrect, this is a 2 full bathroom and one half bath, 3 level 1600 sq foot townhome, with second bedroom on lower level. Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac this home features a bright, open floor plan with soaring vaulted ceilings, skylights, and abundant natural light throughout. The spacious living room with fireplace flows seamlessly to a private deck with a beautiful view to all of downtown. The upper level features a generously sized primary suite with ample closet space and a full bath and laundry. The versatile walk out lower level offers additional living space ideal for a family room, guest area, or second bedroom with full bath. Residents enjoy 24 hour gated security, 2 swimming pools, a clubhouse, beautifully landscaped grounds, and a location just minutes from downtown, Metro North, shopping, dining, and major commuter routes. A rare opportunity to enjoy living in one of Stamford's most desirable communities. The selling agent is the owner of this property.
