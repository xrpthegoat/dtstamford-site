About this home

Discover luxury living on Saugautuck Shores in this stunning contemporary home. Year round water views meet meticulous craftsmanship and sophisticated design to create an extraordinary space. The captivating curved cypress ceiling and elegant blonde bamboo floors set a refined tone. The open floor plan seamlessly integrates the living room, dining area, and gourmet kitchen, featuring reclaimed California redwood, cypress, English elm, and mahogany. The main level also includes a chic office, a full bath, and a powder room. The well-appointed laundry/mud room, with extensive storage, connects directly to the 3-car garage. Ascend to the second level to find a luxurious main bedroom suite with a Poliform walk-in closet and a teak and slate spa bathroom. Two additional bedrooms share a Jack & Jill bath, while a fourth bedroom has its own concrete ensuite bath. The family/media room, with surround sound, promises endless entertainment. The third floor offers a tranquil retreat with a second primary suite, home office, lounge area, full bath, and a treetop terrace overlooking the lush outdoor space. Step outside to an entertainer's paradise with a Gunite saltwater pool and custom loungers wired with lighting, power and Wi-Fi. The outdoor teak counter, and grill make alfresco dining a pleasure. The beautifully landscaped grounds feature a lush lawn, raised irrigated beds, and over 8,000 spring bulbs, creating a vibrant and inviting atmosphere.
