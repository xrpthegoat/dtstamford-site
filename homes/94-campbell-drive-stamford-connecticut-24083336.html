About this home

Serene and sophisticated retreat, this exceptional 4-bed,3-bath home is discreetly nestled at the end of a private drive in an desirable North Stamford cul-de-sac. Surrounded by lush mature foliage, it blends modern elegance with natural beauty. Designed for both grand entertaining and intimate relaxation, the striking contemporary architecture features bold geometric lines, abundant natural light, and a versatile layout. A dramatic two-story foyer with a wall of windows leads to a living room with vaulted ceilings, a stone fireplace, and floor-to-ceiling windows. The space flows effortlessly into the family and dining rooms, where French doors open to the wraparound Timber Tech French White Oak deck. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is a culinary masterpiece, featuring a sleek center island with matte black granite, high-end five-burner propane cooktop, wet bar, granite countertops, and abundant custom cabinetry. An office/media room, Exercise room, first-floor bedroom, full bath, laundry, and garage access complete this level. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite offers, loft sitting area w/stone fireplace, private deck & spa-like marble bath with radiant heat, soaking tub plus dual walk-in closets. Two additional bedrooms with Juliet balconies share a stylish bath. A vast unfinished lower level offers endless future potential-just imagine a home theater or private wine cellar!Secluded yet convenient to all, this extraordinary home is a masterpiece of contemporary luxury
