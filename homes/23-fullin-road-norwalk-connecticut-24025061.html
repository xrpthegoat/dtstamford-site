About this home

Welcome to your own piece of paradise! This stunning ranch-style home offers a perfect blend of elegance & comfort. Nestled in a picturesque setting. Truly a meticulously maintained ranch home. Unwind on the expansive outdoor patio completely fenced in, perfect for home entertainment gatherings or your own private oasis. With front and back yard sprinkler systems and custom RTF grass which will feel like you're walking on a cloud. The home offers 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths. The large gorgeous kitchen has granite countertops and granite backsplash with stainless steel appliances, stove is an electric induction range. The bathrooms have marble stone and all new marble floor in the shower. Recessed lighting throughout with dimmers and hardwood floors throughout. Currently the home is set up to function as a two bedroom with an additional large laundry room space for convenience. Should an additional bedroom be needed, consider relocating the laundry room back into the spacious lower level. The living room features a wood burning fireplace and is open to the dining area. There is an additional bonus room. All new windows. The spacious basement is sealed insulated from the outside, very functional. Driveway is 6 years old. New electrical 200 amp service. Under garage night lamps. Generator ready setup that comes with a generator also. Oversized attached garage with an 8 foot garage door. This home offers functionality for your everyday needs. It has all of the bells and whistles!
