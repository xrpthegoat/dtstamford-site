About this home

Welcome to Sylvan Knoll, where coastal living meets convenience. This freshly painted, bright and inviting three-level townhouse offers 2 bedrooms 1 full bath & a flexible layout designed for comfortable everyday living. Hardwood floors extend throughout the first and second levels, while the sun-filled living area opens to a patio overlooking a courtyard. The finished lower level provides valuable additional living space, perfect for a family room, home office, fitness area, or hobby room. It also features a separate laundry room with washer and dryer included, a mechanical room, and ample storage. A new furnace adds to the home's value and efficiency. A walk up attic provides additional storage space. Set on 13 beautifully landscaped park-like acres with sidewalks throughout, Sylvan Knoll is a pet-friendly, owner-occupied co-op community where rentals are not permitted. Located just off Exit 9 of I-95, you'll enjoy nearby Cove Island Park, the beach, marina, Cummings Park, Chelsea Piers, and Terry Conners Rink, with downtown Stamford, shopping, restaurants, and the Metro-North train station just minutes away. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy the Sylvan Knoll lifestyle in one of Stamford's most desirable communities. Sale is subject to Probate Court approval and is being sold in as-is condition. Co-op board approval is required. Taxes are included in the monthly common charges.
