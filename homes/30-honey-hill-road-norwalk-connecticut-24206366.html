About this home

Set at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Norwalk's desirable Honey Hill Road area, this three-bedroom, two-bath shingle style Ranch offers privacy, property and potential. Situated on .60 acre-the largest lot in the neighborhood--the home enjoys a broad, level lawn, mature trees and a serene, staycation ambiance. The existing residence offers convenient one-level living with attached garage and a classic ranch footprint that provides an execellent starting point for renovation, expansion or reimagination. The Primary bath is under construction. The oversized property is the real standout, offering room to create your private oasis including swimming pool, gardens or extended property. Located in neighborhood with extensive renovations and improvements, this is a compelling opportunity for an end user, builder or investor looking to capitalize on a substantial lot in an established neighborhood. City sewer and water available.
