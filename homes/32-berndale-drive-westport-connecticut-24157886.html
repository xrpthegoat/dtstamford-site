About this home

OFFERS DUE AT NOON ON MONDAY APRIL 13TH! In the heart of Westport, where charm meets modern living, the expanded cape at 32 Berndale Drive delivers the perfect blend of space, style, and location. Nestled on a quiet, sought-after street just moments from Bedford Middle School and Staples High School, this 5-bedroom, 4-bath residence offers exceptional versatility for today's lifestyle. A welcoming foyer leads into inviting living spaces-including a sun-filled living room with a fireplace, a spacious dining room perfect for gatherings, and a cozy den anchored by a second fireplace. The updated kitchen impresses with top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, plus ample workspace and storage. Upstairs, the primary suite is a serene retreat featuring a spa-inspired bath with radiant heated floors and a luxurious steam shower. Additional bedrooms, including three on the main floor, provide plenty of room for family, guests, or dedicated office space. The lower level expands your options with a recreation area, workout room, bedroom, laundry, and a full bath-ideal for guests or extended family. Additional highlights include a 2-car attached garage, whole-house generator, and mudroom entry with thoughtful built-in cabinets for easy daily living. An adjacent sunroom makes a perfect home office or studio, overlooking the private backyard. This exceptional home combines comfort, style, and one of the best locations in town.
