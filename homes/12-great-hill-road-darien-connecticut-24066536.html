About this home

This spacious home features 5 bedrooms and 3.5 baths. The 6th bedroom on the lower level includes its own private bath and entrance would be perfect for guests or a nanny. The main level boasts a large family room with a wet bar and fireplace, an eat-in kitchen, an office, and a living room with a fireplace that opens to a screened porch and dining room. A large deck spans the entire back of the house, providing ample outdoor living space. The large primary suite includes a balcony, and the home also offers a 2-car garage with an EV charging station, an alarm system, and central air conditioning. Located in proximity to the school, train station, and town, this home is situated in a wonderful neighborhood known for its block parties and holiday events.
