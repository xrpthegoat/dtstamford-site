About this home

SONO Vintage Colonial - Opportunity to create your dream home with this vintage colonial, located a short distance to the Norwalk Harbor and train. The wrap around porch is a great spot to enjoy down time and just watch the world go by. Good bones and a blank canvas to imagine and create your perfect home near Norwalk Harbor. Original hardwood floors and stain glass windows add architectural interest to this home. The first floor features the kitchen, dining room and living room plus an enclosed back porch/pantry. Second floor includes 3 spacious bedrooms with large closets and high ceilings. The walk-up attic provides expansion potential. Conveniently located just a short distance to vibrant South Norwalk restaurants, shops, parks, train station and several boat clubs. Don't let this great opportunity slip away.
