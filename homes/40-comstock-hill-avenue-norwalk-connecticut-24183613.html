About this home

Tucked away on a private, secluded lot in Norwalk's coveted Silvermine neighborhood, this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath Ranch offers the perfect combination of privacy, comfort, and opportunity. Surrounded by mature trees and natural beauty, this property presents a rare chance to own a home in one of Fairfield County's most desirable communities. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the sun-filled interior features an inviting floor plan enhanced by two stunning stone wood-burning fireplaces, creating warmth and character throughout. The spacious eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home, offering cream-colored cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, tile flooring, and abundant space for gathering with family and friends. Step directly from the kitchen onto a large deck overlooking the expansive backyard-an ideal setting for outdoor dining, weekend barbecues, morning coffee, or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings. The level, fenced backyard provides endless possibilities for recreation, gardening, pets, and future enhancements. Evenings can be spent relaxing around the fire pit under the stars, creating the perfect backdrop for entertaining and making memories. The finished lower level adds tremendous flexibility and value, featuring a recreation room, fourth bedroom, full bath, and potential for in-law, guest, or multi-generational living.
