About this home

Welcome to a desirable Fairfield House!!!... centrally located complex offering charm and convenience. This spacious light and bright 1BD condo comes with a dining area/living room combo that has access to an oversized corner balcony with lovely southern exposure. Kitchen and bathroom have been fully updated to meet today's modern demands.
