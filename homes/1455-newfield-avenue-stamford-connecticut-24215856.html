About this home

Welcome to 1455 Newfield Ave, a beautiful estate residence situated on two lovely park like acres in one of North Stamford's most desirable residential settings. Offered by the estate of the late Nicola Tarzia, this spacious home presents a wonderful buying opportunity for buyers seeking privacy, generous living space, and the charm of an established New England setting. The home is directly across from the prestigious, Kings private school, Featuring four bedrooms, three full baths, four half baths, three fireplaces, and a three-car garage with additional storage/workshop in the rear. This residence offers an exceptional combination of comfort and functionality, features a large updated kitchen, renovated bathrooms, newer windows, three welcoming fireplaces that add warmth and character. Generously proportioned living areas provide an inviting setting for everyday living as well as entertaining family and friends, including a bar area and wine cellar. Set on just over two acres the expansive park like grounds offer room for outdoor enjoyment, recreation, gardening, with possibilities for a swimming pool, or a private retreat.
