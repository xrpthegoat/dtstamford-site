About this home

Privately set on 3.2 picturesque acres, this distinguished 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath residence blends classic architecture with sophisticated living in a peaceful, private setting. A double-height foyer welcomes you into sun-filled interiors with elegant architectural details and generously proportioned rooms. At the center of the home is a beautifully designed gourmet kitchen that opens to a bright breakfast room and an impressive family room with cathedral ceilings, skylights, and a fireplace. French doors lead to an expansive deck, extending the living space outdoors. Gracious formal living and dining rooms, each with a fireplace, provide refined settings for both intimate gatherings and larger-scale entertaining. The finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a family/bonus room, playroom, guest bedroom or fitness room, and wine cellar. Modern upgrades include a new generator, updated heating and cooling systems, and smart home automation. A three-car garage with loft space above provides additional storage or flexible use. The property features open lawn areas, a heated pool and spa, and an inviting deck for outdoor entertaining-creating a peaceful retreat for everyday living and gathering with family and friends.
