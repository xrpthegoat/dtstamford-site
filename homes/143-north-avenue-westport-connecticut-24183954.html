About this home

Set on over an acre of beautifully landscaped, level property, this freshly updated Westport home offers the perfect balance of everyday comfort and effortless outdoor living. A welcoming front porch leads inside to hardwood floors throughout and a thoughtfully designed layout where the open kitchen flows seamlessly into the family room, formal dining room, and living room with a wood-burning fireplace. Sliders off the family room open to an expansive deck overlooking the private backyard featuring an in-ground pool, spa, fire pit, and generous lawn with plenty of space for recreation and entertaining. The bedroom wing of the home provides privacy and functionality, highlighted by a spacious primary suite with a full wall of closets and an en-suite bath, along with well-proportioned secondary bedrooms sharing a hall bath. The finished lower level expands the lifestyle with a media room and additional family room with direct pool access, a full bath, dedicated home office, flexible gym space, laundry room, and access to the attached two-car garage. A newly paved driveway provides plenty of parking.Perfectly positioned just moments from Westport's schools and centrally located for everyday convenience, you'll enjoy easy access to the train, downtown shopping and dining, Wakeman Town Farm, parks, beaches, and everything that makes Westport such a special place.
