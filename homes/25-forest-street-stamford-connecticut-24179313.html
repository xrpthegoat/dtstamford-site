About this home

Welcome to The Classic one of Stamford's most sought-after downtown high rises, located in the heart of vibrant downtown, just minutes to shopping, theaters, fine dining and Metro North Train Station. This sun filled light bright and airy 1,342 sq. ft. residence was newly redesigned and remodeled to perfection. The open concept floor plan boasts new gourmet kitchen with breakfast counter, dining area and living room with sliders to private balcony. Thoughtfully redesigned hall bath, adjacent laundry room with full size washer and dryer, nearby 2nd bedroom or home office. Primary bedroom features generous sized walk-in closet and stunning new bath. Heat, hot water and cooking gas are included and 2 indoor garage spaces. Pets possible on a case by case basis. Owners would prefer longer term lease. Enjoy recently renovated "Classic Club" complete with Lounge, rooftop pool, nicely equipped fitness center, 3rd floor dog park and community area with grills and gazebo. The Classic amenities include 24 hour concierge service. Just move right in and get ready to enjoy a carefree city living!
