About this home

Discover the charm of this meticulously maintained home situated in a sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood of Norwalk, near the Westport town line. This Colonial-style residence, cherished by its original owner, showcases quality and pride of ownership. A well appointed newly added paver walkway connects the front and side entrances, while mature landscaping and a prominent picture window enhance the overall curb appeal. With vinyl siding and replacement windows, the house is bathed in natural light and offers a spacious main level living area alongside three generous bedrooms on the upper floor. Ample closets & storage space! The level backyard provides ideal space for outdoor activities. The eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into the formal dining room. Main level features a laundry / mud room area. Multiple entry points into the home enrich the attractive layout, adding to the home's convenience. Enjoy central air cooling in the summer months! Just 2 miles from the East Norwalk Train Station, the new SoNo Collection, and merely 1 mile from Tracey Elementary School / Woods Pond Park, this location is unmatched. You will quickly appreciate the proximity to Whole Foods on the Post Road in Westport (just a 5-minute drive!) and walking distance to Stew Leonard's. Easy access to both the Merritt Parkway and I-95 ensures a great commuter location. 3 Harvard Street is ready for you to establish 'home' and create your lasting memories!
