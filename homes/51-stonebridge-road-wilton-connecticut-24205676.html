About this home

Welcome to 51 Stonebridge Road, a charming four-bedroom, 2.5-bath home set on 1.52 beautiful acres in a peaceful South Wilton setting. Lovingly cared for by the same family for more than five decades, this special property is tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac abutting Aspetuck Land Trust protected open space, offering exceptional privacy. Inside, hardwood floors, abundant natural light and a fireplace add warmth and character. A French door from the living room opens to a spacious patio and level backyard, while the dining room leads to a deck and additional patio, perfect for morning coffee. The sun-filled kitchen features a skylight, ample cabinetry and granite-topped island. A heated, finished walk-out lower level with half bath and separate entrance offers flexible living space, while the bonus room above the garage is ideal for a home office. A whole-house generator and new furnace installed in 2025 add peace of mind. Minutes to Wilton Town Center and Metro-North, approximately 2.5 miles to the Merritt Parkway, with convenient access to Westport's train station, shopping and dining. A rare opportunity to enjoy the privacy, natural beauty and timeless Wilton charm of this special property.
