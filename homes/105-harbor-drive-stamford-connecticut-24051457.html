About this home

Schooner Cove, located in the coveted Shippan Point neighborhood is Stamford's premier waterfront community. This First floor unit has direct access to marina and association pool. The main level features a large combination Living Room / Dining room with a wood burning fireplace. Sliders access a covered patio overlooking the harbor and marina. Both levels have engineered white oak flooring throughout except for the kitchen which is tiled. This unit has 2 large bedrooms with en suite full bathrooms. Either would be suitable as the primary bedroom. One is on the main level and the other upper level. There's an additional main level room suitable for use as a 3rd Bedroom, nursery or office. The main level also features a separate 1/2 bath and full size laundry. The modern kitchen has updated appliances, a dedicated wine cooler and ample storage / cabinetry. HOA includes heat, hot water, water, sewer, garbage, flood insurance, grounds maintenance, swimming pool and a nearby clubhouse. Dock space is available (managed separately through the Schooner Cove Yacht Association.) This is a great way to enjoy coastal living with lots of privacy but also easy access to the train station as well as vibrant downtown Stamford and Harbor Point with lots of shopping and entertainment options. Public beaches are an easy walk.
