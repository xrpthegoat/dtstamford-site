About this home

Nestled in a prime location within a beautifully maintained complex, this exceptional home offers access to premier community amenities-including a pool and tennis courts-alongside the luxury of your own completely private patio. Harvest Commons is ideally situated just moments from fine dining, local shopping, and the Greens Farms train station. Inside, the elegant formal living room features soaring vaulted ceilings, large windows, a cozy wood-burning fireplace, and sliding glass doors that open seamlessly to the outdoors. The main level also boasts a full formal dining room-a rare feature not found in all models-and an exceptionally spacious kitchen and family room combination with a impressive wall of glass and convenient direct access to the attached two-car garage. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite serves as a peaceful retreat, complete with vaulted ceilings, two generous walk-in closets, and an en-suite bath with both a soaking tub and separate shower. A second well-appointed bedroom, a separate full bath, and a dedicated second-floor laundry room complete the upper level. The finished lower level provides versatile additional living space ideal for a recreation room, home office, or guest quarters, along with a rare third full bathroom. Seller would like a January closing.
