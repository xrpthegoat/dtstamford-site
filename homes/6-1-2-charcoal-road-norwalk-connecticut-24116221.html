About this home

Ideally located in the heart of Norwalk on a quiet, quintessential cul-de-sac, this picture-perfect Colonial offers the rare combination of peaceful suburban living and unbeatable convenience. Built in 2014, this home places you minutes from everything, vibrant dining, shopping, beaches, parks and top commuting options. For NYC commuters it doesn't get easier, both the Rowayton and South Norwalk train stations are just a short drive away offering a quick and direct route into the city. Enjoy the charm of a residential neighborhood with the benefit of easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway and South Norwalk's lively downtown. Inside, the home features a wide open floor plan designed for modern living and effortless entertaining. A welcoming living and dining space flows into the stylish kitchen and cozy family room with a beautiful fireplace as the focal point. Glass sliders lead to a spacious patio and private, landscaped yard-perfect for outdoor dining, weekend barbecues and year-round enjoyment. Upstairs, the second floor offers a serene primary suite with a spa-like bath and a generous walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms share a full hall bath and the conveniently located laundry room adds everyday ease. The finished third level is an ideal flex space perfect for a home office, gym, playroom or media room to fit your lifestyle.
