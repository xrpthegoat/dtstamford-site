About this home

Classic American 1928 Tudor. Move-in ready colonial-style home featuring six rooms, over 1,300 sqft of living space plus large dry basement, storage attic, 1-car detached garage & large bluestone terrace within private fenced Courtyard. Elegant two-story foyer welcomes all into the large Living Room w/ wood-burning fireplace, generous Dining Room & updated Kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, wood cabinetry and hardwood floors plus first floor powder room. Upstairs you'll find 2 large bedrooms plus a nursery or home office (you choose) & an updated Full Bathroom w/ original tub. Home improvements include Andersen windows (2019) throughout, Mitsubishi AC/ heat Mini-splits (2016), Garage door, electric door opener & remote (2020) plus bathrooms, floors and much more! Dry basement has laundry & plenty of storage space. Corner lot situated on 0.12-acre dead-end street in Norwalk's most-convenient location. The property is an oasis of perennial gardens, with colors for every season. Make it yours today!
