About this home

Welcome to your dream rental! Nestled in the highly convenient and desirable Westover neighborhood of Stamford, this lovely 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch-style home boasts a seamless blend of modern amenities and classic charm. The spacious layout offers an exceptional flow, ideal for both entertaining and everyday living. Beautiful hardwood floors run throughout the home, adding warmth and sophistication. The recently updated eat-in kitchen features contemporary cabinetry, top-of-the-line appliances. The new master bath is a haven of relaxation, featuring radiant heated floors and marble vanities. Enjoy the luxury of space with large bedrooms and living areas, providing ample room for relaxation and storage. The expansive living room, complete with a charming fireplace, is perfect for cozy evenings and lively gatherings. Host unforgettable dinner parties in the large dining area, designed to accommodate both intimate meals and large celebrations. Situated on a perfect level corner lot, this home offers a picturesque setting with plenty of outdoor space for gardening, play, or relaxation on a two-level custom deck. This home has been meticulously maintained and tastefully updated, making it move-in ready for the discerning renter. Located close to parks, top-rated schools, shopping, and dining, it provides both tranquility and convenience. Don't miss the opportunity to rent this beautiful Westover ranch.
