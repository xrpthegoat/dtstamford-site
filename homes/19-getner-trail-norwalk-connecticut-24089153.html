About this home

Welcome to 19 Getner Trail, a gracious stand-alone colonial farmhouse-style home nestled in the unique Getner Farms community. This charming property offers 3,255 square feet of beautifully designed living space with 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths and 1 powder room. Entering the complex gates, you will drive past the charming horseback riding training center. As you approach the home at the end of a cul-de-sac, you are greeted by a welcoming front porch. Step inside to find a tasteful foyer that introduces you to this smart and spacious residence. The first floor boasts a formal dining room, a modern island kitchen with breakfast area and bay window, along with a comfortable living room with gas fireplace and adjacent to a cozy family room with sliders leading to a delightful composite floored deck and private back yard. The second floor features three airy bedrooms, including a luxurious primary bedroom suite complete with a full bath and a generous walk-in closet. Venture to the third level to discover an additional bedroom and a versatile office space, both with sky lights. The partially finished lower level provides a large rec room and added storage. Keeping you warm in the winter and cool in the summer is the new (2024) HVAC system. Situated on the Darien/Norwalk border, Getner Farms offers close proximity to town beaches, shopping, and all transportation hubs. Experience the peaceful ambiance of this vibrant community making it a perfect country retreat!
