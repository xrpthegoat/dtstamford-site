About this home

Charming and well-appointed, this home combines comfort, versatility, and thoughtful updates throughout. The spacious living room, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, offers a warm and welcoming setting for everyday living and entertaining. An updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances flows seamlessly to a dining area just off to the right, creating a functional layout. The primary bedroom suite offers a peaceful retreat with its own private bath and a spacious walk-in closet. The additional bedroom provides a comfortable and inviting space, complemented by a well-appointed full bathroom. The lower level adds valuable living space with a large rec room that can also serve as an office or guest area, along with laundry and generous storage. Outside, a large backyard with a patio, play area, and shed provides the perfect setting for outdoor enjoyment, all in a convenient location near parks, shopping, dining, and commuter routes.
