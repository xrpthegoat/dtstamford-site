About this home

Renovated & Spacious Apartment with 2-3 Bedrooms and 3 Full Baths! This beautifully renovated and oversized apartment offers 2-3 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and an abundance of comfortable living space. The updated kitchen features newer appliances, including a premium Viking gas range and hood, extra-large stainless-steel refrigerator, dishwasher, and full-size washer and dryer. The thoughtfully designed layout offers excellent flow, with polished hardwood floors throughout the main level and a versatile tiled lower-level family room or potential third bedroom, complete with its own full bathroom. A bright alcove with a floor-to-ceiling window provides the perfect home office or flex space, filled with natural sunlight. The primary bedroom features a private en-suite bathroom, while an additional full bathroom is conveniently located in the main-level hallway. Additional highlights include ceiling fans in all main-level living spaces and bedrooms, a new mini-split system in the living area, off-street parking for two cars, plus plenty of additional on-street parking. Located in Stamford's desirable Belltown neighborhood, you'll be just a short walk from grocery stores, CVS, restaurants, and everyday conveniences. After a long day, relax on the charming covered front porch, the perfect spot to unwind and enjoy your surroundings. This spacious, updated home offers the ideal combination of comfort, convenience, and flexibility.
