About this home

Set on a spacious .48 acre corner lot in the highly sought-after Silvermine area, this stylish antique farmhouse offers four bedrooms and 2.1 baths, blending timeless character with modern convenience. A splendid kitchen and mudroom addition was completed in 2022. The kitchen features a large island, quartz countertops, and stainless-steel appliances. The graceful open floor plan includes custom built-ins, wide plank hardwood floors, and two fireplaces. The primary suite with a vaulted ceiling, large closet and double vanity primary bath provides a peaceful retreat. The exterior is designed for entertaining, with a wraparound porch, large patio, stone fire pit patio and a barn that houses an art studio/entertaining space.
