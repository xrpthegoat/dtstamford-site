About this home

Stylish new renovation and addition enhances this classic four-bedroom colonial on one quiet acre with easy access to hiking trails in Mianus River Park located in coveted Westover neighborhood. Exquisite architectural details complement the sun-filled interior with center hall entry, remodeled and expanded chef's eat-in kitchen open to sunroom with private wraparound deck; living room with fireplace, family room with gas fireplace, built-in cabinets; office with bookcases, dining room, two half baths and mudroom. Second floor primary suite, part of major renovation, features Cathedral ceiling, California Closet and deluxe bath. Additional bedrooms are spacious with en-suite bedroom, two bedrooms and full hall bath. Lower level boasts playroom, gym, wine room and storage. Very close to Greenwich border.
