About this home

Highest and Best Bid deadline is 12 noon on Monday, 1/12. Rare find! Updated 4 bedroom, 2 full bath Ranch with 2,041 square feet of versatile living spaces. Nestled on a 0.28 acre lot at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this home meets the brief of modern life, blending a highly-functional layout with the advantages of living in Greenwich. A flexible floor plan adapts to meet one's lifestyle needs with an expansive family room, an elongated dining room with skylights, a sunny living room, cheerful kitchen, four bedrooms, plus a private, lower-level home office. Hardwood floors in most rooms add warmth and character. The inviting kitchen has a high ceiling, a wall of windows, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and quality cabinetry. The partially-finished, newly-carpeted lower level offers extra spaces for work, gym or playroom, as well as a mudroom/laundry area and wood workshop.Outdoor spaces include a flat front yard with a fire pit and backyard with a wrap-around stone patio with sliders from both the dining and living rooms-perfect for alfresco meals or parties. Dense three-season foliage provides privacy screening during the warmer months of outdoor living. Extensive stone masonry further enhances the property's curb appeal.Maintenance-free vinyl siding, attached oversized one-car garage, attic with pull-down stairs. Conveniently located near Metro-North train stations, I-95, top-rated schools, shopping, and parks, this Greenwich gem delivers!
