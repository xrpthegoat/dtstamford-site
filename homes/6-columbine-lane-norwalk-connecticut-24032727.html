About this home

What could be better than living on a quiet street in the heart of the desirable Cranbury neighborhood? Possibly having a stunning pool with spa on a private, level half-acre lot? How about a long list of updates (see below)? Or the fact that the neighborhood Cranbury School is just over a mile away and has recently re-opened in a brand new building? Nearby Cranbury Park offers 227 acres with wooded trails and a dog-friendly environment. With so much to offer, it's no wonder that Realtor.com recently named 06851 to its list of the Top 10 hottest zip codes in America! Not to mention the easy access to the many amenities in the rest of Norwalk, such as the SoNo Collection mall, too many great restaurants to mention in South Norwalk/SoNo, Calf Pasture Beach, and three Metro North train stations for a one-hour ride into Grand Central Terminal in NYC.
