About this home

** Highest and best by 5pm Tuesday ** Welcome to 250 Strawberry Hill Ave, a versatile and spacious home ready for some lucky buyer. Featuring over 3000 square feet of living space, including 5 beds, 3.5 baths, a finished basement space and a finished attic. There is a large in-law suite above the garage which is separate and private from the rest of the home. The main home has a living room, kitchen, and dining room, with an office, half bath, and a first floor suite. Upstairs there are 5 bedrooms and two bathrooms, which has been used by the seller as another suite for their extended family to live in. There are hardwood floors in much of the space. The basement has a playroom and is home to the mechanicals. There are so many ways to live in this home, with plenty of extra bedrooms, bathrooms, and spaces, to create exactly what you might want for your particular situation. The possibilities are endless! The back yard is level, with a patio and an awning, perfect for entertaining and backyard barbecues. All of this is located close to schools, parks, Stew Leonard's, and all that Westport Ave has to offer. See it quickly before it is snatched off the market!
