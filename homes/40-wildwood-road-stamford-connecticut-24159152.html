About this home

Step into timeless elegance with this 6,034 sq. ft. Colonial masterpiece, featuring a legally finished 2638 square foot walk-out basement over lower level. Set behind stately stone pillars, the home welcomes you with a sweeping driveway, manicured gardens, and a pool site ready for your dream oasis. Inside, a sun-drenched, two-story marble foyer sets the stage for stunning architectural details throughout, including exquisite millwork, rich herringbone-patterned hardwood floors, and three fireplaces. Expansive windows flood the interior with natural light, while dual staircases add both grandeur and convenience. Perfect for entertaining and everyday living, the home offers an effortless flow between generously scaled rooms, all crowned by dramatic ceilings. Upgrades include a brand-new $40,000 HVAC system, a Well Pump and Wire (2022), a Hot Water Heater (2021), a Bosch AC System (2021) and Smart Home features (thermostats, lights, doors, cameras, garage doors) that provide year-round comfort in this extraordinary, move-in-ready residence.
