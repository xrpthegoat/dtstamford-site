About this home

Experience timeless elegance in this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial located near shops, downtown conveniences, and just minutes from train stations for an easy commute to NYC. The main floor invites you in with rich hardwood floors, arched doorways, and a spacious living room featuring crown molding, a cozy wood-burning fireplace, and custom built-ins. The dining room flows effortlessly into the marble-floored kitchen, where a 5-burner gas range, stainless steel appliances, farmhouse sink, and custom pantry elevate everyday cooking. Step into the inviting screened porch, complete with vaulted ceilings and ceiling fan, a perfect retreat for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The porch leads to a spacious deck, offering serene views of the landscaped backyard, ideal for gatherings or quiet moments. Upstairs, discover three generous bedrooms, each with hardwood floors and thoughtfully designed closets, including the primary suite with two large closets and a cedar closet. The upper-level bathroom showcases a spa-like ambiance with radiant heated floors, a jetted tub, and a chic vanity. The lower level extends the living space with a stylish family room, a full bath, ample storage, a putting green, and an office desk, creating versatile spaces for work and play.
