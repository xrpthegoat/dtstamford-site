About this home

Charming first-floor end unit located in the Glenbrook area of Stamford. This well maintained first-floor end unit offers an ideal combination of convenience and comfort. This residence is a commuter's dream, short distance from the train station and with easy access to I-95, making travel throughout Fairfield County easy to do. This unit features two nicely sized bedrooms and a full bathroom, all on one level for effortless living. The inviting living room offers natural light, with hardwood floors that add warmth and character to the space. The well-appointed kitchen, equipped with all essential appliances, is perfect for daily living and meal preparation. Both bedrooms continue the theme of hardwood flooring, providing a cohesive and comfortable environment. Situated close to Stamford's vibrant shopping and dining scene, this home ensures you're never far from the best the city has to offer. Welcome home to a blend of convenience and charm!
