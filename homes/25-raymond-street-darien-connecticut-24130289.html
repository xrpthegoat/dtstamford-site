About this home

Fantastic opportunity to rent in beautiful Darien! Enjoy top-rated schools, beach access, walk to train and the village of Rowayton. Charming 3 bedroom, 2 full bath Victorian home on a level lot. Occupancy can be immediate and can be rented through 6/2026. Pets are welcome.
