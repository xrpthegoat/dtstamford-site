About this home

Stunning 4 Bedroom 2 Full Bath Colonial with in prime commuter location! The foyer opens up to a spacious living room that flows seamlessly into the large renovated eat-in kitchen with updates that include quartz counter-tops, stainless steel appliances and white shaker cabinets. Entertaining made easy with sliders off private sunroom to a level backyard, perfect for hosting large gatherings with friends & family. A formal dining room, a spacious bedroom w/ adjacent full bath and a bonus playroom/office/flex space rounds out the first floor. The 2nd level has your primary bedroom that can easily fit king size bed & other furnishings. Two additional generously sized bedrooms, a full bath and plenty of extra closet space round out this floor. You will find a private laundry room with full size washer/dryer. 1 Car Garage. Conveniently located close to New Whole Foods, CVS, Metro North Train & all Downtown Stamford has to offer - Restaurants, Nightlife & Shopping! Don't miss this opportunity on Oaklawn avenue!
