About this home

This great sunny and well-maintained single-family ranch home features 4 bedrooms and 2 baths and is located in a quiet area of South Wilton. The home boasts an easy, open flow that makes everyday living and entertaining a breeze. Step inside to a inviting family room showcases a beautiful mosaic-tiled fireplace, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Enjoy meals in the formal dining room and seamlessly connecting to a updated kitchen complete with a seating island and granite countertop. Large windows frame views of mature trees, creating a serene backdrop throughout the home. All bedrooms are comfortably sized, primary bedroom including a full bathroom, equipped with a Jacuzzi bath. The lower level provides additional living space with dedicated office and entertainment areas, plus a fun nook under the stairs. Beautiful hardwood floors flow throughout, and there's ample attic for added storage space. The spacious two-car garage includes a portable generator and a dedicated subpanel for easy connection. Step outside to enjoy the outdoor space, featuring a wooden deck, play area, garden shed, and sand pit. This home offers exceptional convenience and comfort.
