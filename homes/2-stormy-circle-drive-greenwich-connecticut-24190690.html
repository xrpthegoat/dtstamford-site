About this home

Spacious furnished/unfurnished rental on a cul-de-sac close to beach, pool, schools and neighboring shops, offering the perfect combination of comfort, convenience and coastal living. A dramatic entry foyer welcomes you to an elegant living room featuring a fireplace. The formal dining room is ideal for hosting large family gatherings and special occasions, while the eat-in kitchen provides ample space for everyday. Adjacent to the kitchen, the 2-story family room with fireplace, offers a generous and comfortable setting. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite features a fireplace, walk-in closets and en-suite bath designed for comfort and relaxation. Two (possibly 3) additional bedrooms, 2 additional bathrooms complete the second level. Walk-out Lower level has laundry, and full bath.
