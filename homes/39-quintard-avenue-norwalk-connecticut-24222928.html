About this home

Opportunity in South Norwalk! This classic two-family home sits on 0.23 acres, one of the largest lots on Quintard Avenue, where most neighboring lots are about 0.14 acres. Built in 1898, the home pairs period character, including shingle-style gables and hardwood floors, with a layout that works well for investors or an owner-occupant.The first-floor unit offers 2 bedrooms and a full bath. The second unit spans two levels, with 3 bedrooms and a full bath on the main level of the apartment and another bedroom and full bath on the upper level, for 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths in all. Outside, the property offers a parking setup that's hard to find in this neighborhood. A very large private driveway on the left leads to a detached barn structure at the back of the lot. You'll also find an enclosed front porch, a rear deck with a covered patio area below, and a level backyard with wood privacy fencing. The location is excellent: less than a mile to the South Norwalk Metro-North station and close to SoNo's restaurants, Veterans Park and the waterfront. With the lot size, the second structure and this location, 39 Quintard Avenue has a lot of potential, whether you're adding to your portfolio or living in one unit and renting the other. Property to be sold 'as-is'.
