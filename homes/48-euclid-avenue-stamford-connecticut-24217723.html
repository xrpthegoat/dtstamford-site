About this home

Discover an exceptional opportunity at 48 Euclid Avenue, a beautifully renovated two-family home offering modern comfort, versatile living arrangements, and an outstanding coastal Stamford location just steps from Cove Island Park and the waterfront. Ideal for owner-occupants, extended families, and investors alike, this property features two updated apartments with a combined five bedrooms and three full bathrooms. Both residences offer solid-surface flooring, central air conditioning, renovated interiors, generous living spaces, and the convenience of separate in-unit laundry. The first-floor apartment features two bedrooms, a full bathroom, an updated kitchen, an inviting open living area, excellent storage, and a dedicated laundry area. The second-floor residence offers three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, living room, eat-in kitchen, and separate laundry room, along with pull-down attic access providing valuable additional storage. Outside, a large private backyard offers wonderful space for outdoor entertaining, recreation, or relaxation. A detached one-car garage and three additional driveway parking spaces provide convenient off-street parking. The location is a standout feature, with Cove Island Park's beaches, marina, ice rink, tennis courts, and scenic waterfront recreation just moments away, along with convenient access to public transportation. The two-apartment configuration provides exceptional flexibility to live in one residence while generating rental income
