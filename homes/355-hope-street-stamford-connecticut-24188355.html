About this home

Terrific income-generating potential meets massive equity upside in a highly desirable commuter location. Located at 355 Hope Street, nearby the Glenbrook train station, this 2-family home offers two large 3-bedroom units featuring separate utilities and strong rental potential ($2,000/mo first floor, $2,500/mo second floor). The crown jewel of this property is the oversized lot boasting abundant parking and a detached 3-car garage with a full walk-up loft-ideal for renting out as premium contractor storage or keeping for personal use. The house needs interior updates, offering a blank canvas to renovate, maximize market rents, and build serious equity in a fantastic neighborhood.
