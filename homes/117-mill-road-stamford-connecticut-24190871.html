About this home

Nestled on a quiet, tree-lined street in desirable North Stamford, this classic New England Colonial offers the perfect blend of timeless charm and modern updates. Set on a private one-acre property surrounded by mature trees, lush landscaping, and a peaceful backyard retreat. Offered fully furnished this beautifully maintained four-bedroom, three-full-bath home is designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The heart of the home is the beautifully renovated kitchen, featuring a stunning oversized center island with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, custom cabinetry, and beautiful hardwood floors that flow throughout the home. Sun-filled rooms, crown molding, recessed lighting, and a welcoming family room anchored by a beautiful fireplace create a warm and inviting atmosphere. Upstairs, you'll find four spacious bedrooms, including a generous primary suite with its own private en-suite bath. A newly finished lower level significantly expands the home's living space and is complemented by a beautifully renovated full bathroom, making it ideal as a recreation room, media room, home office, gym, or guest retreat. Additional highlights include a heated two-car garage equipped with a Tesla charger and a dedicated fitness area featuring a squat rack and free weights. Outside, enjoy your own private backyard oasis with professionally landscaped grounds and an expansive patio complete with an outdoor TV perfect for relaxing or entertaining year-round
