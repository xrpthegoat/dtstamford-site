About this home

This bright and spacious end-unit townhome is tucked away in a quiet, six-home community just minutes from the heart of Stamford. Offering the space of a single-family home with the ease of low-maintenance living, this rental has everything you need for comfort and convenience. Inside, you'll find over 3,200 square feet of living space, including 3 large bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a finished lower level perfect for guests, a home office, or extra living space. The open-concept main floor features hardwood floors, high ceilings, and plenty of natural light. The living area includes a cozy gas fireplace and sliding doors that lead to your private patio. The kitchen is fully equipped with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances (including gas range and convection microwave), a large pantry, and a sunny breakfast nook. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite includes a walk-in closet and en-suite bath with a whirlpool tub. Two additional bedrooms offer plenty of space and storage, one with vaulted ceilings for added charm. Downstairs, the finished lower level has its own full bathroom, making it a great space for guests or to use an additional bedroom. You'll also enjoy the convenience of an attached two-car garage with an EV charger, central air, and large laundry room. Located just a short walk to Glenbrook train station and minutes from shops, restaurants, and downtown Stamford, this home puts everything within easy reach.
