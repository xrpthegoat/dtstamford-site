About this home

Nestled on a private level acre in desirable North Stamford, located at the end of a cul-de-sac this beautifully updated five-bedroom executive colonial offers the perfect blend of timeless elegance and modern comfort. Thoughtfully renovated throughout, the home features spacious sun filled rooms, hardwood floors on the main level, and an ideal layout for both everyday living and entertaining. The heart of the home is the stunning updated kitchen with premium finishes, seamlessly flowing into inviting family and dining spaces. A generous primary suite provides a peaceful retreat, while four additional bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or home office needs. Step outside onto a private deck to enjoy the expansive acre of professionally landscaped grounds, providing exceptional privacy and plenty of room for recreation and gardening. Located in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, this move-in-ready home combines a tranquil setting with convenient access to downtown Stamford, parks, shopping, dining and commuting routes. Note: Dining room chandelier is excluded. Tax records show 4 bedrooms when there are actually 5.
