About this home

This is truly the premier unit at Perry Green. Enjoy the convenience of a first-floor primary suite and a kitchen that opens to a spacious great room, all with stunning nature views and glass on three sides. This beautifully situated end unit offers ample parking and privacy, with a welcoming front garden. The expansive kitchen and great room are filled with natural light and boast high-end finishes, and beautiful millwork. The chef's kitchen, featuring Thermador and Sub-Zero appliances, is sure to impress. The great room, with its fireplace, walls of glass, and balcony, provides an inviting space for relaxation and entertaining. The luxurious primary suite includes a large cedar lined walk in closet and a spa-like bath. This level also offers a second bedroom with a full bath, a powder room, and a laundry area. The lower level features a spacious play room with sliders that open to a gorgeous yard with scenic wooded views. You'll also find a third bedroom with windows and a full bath, plus an additional room that could be used as an office, exercise room, or guest space. Experience the best of single-floor living with a finished lower level for accommodating guests. New boiler and freshly painted. Walk to Wilton Center and Train Station.
