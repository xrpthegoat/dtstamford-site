About this home

Welcome to 8 Steeple Top Road, a charming Rowayton home that is ready for immediate occupancy. Furnished rental available for both short term and long term. This meticulously maintained 3 bedroom, two and half bath home, is nestled on a quiet cul de sac and offers modern comforts of smart technology and timeless charm. The home features high ceilings and an open floor plan, with wonderful natural light streaming in throughout. The family room, complete with a gas fireplace and sliders, opens onto a large rear deck that overlooks the tranquil rear yard, which is perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The primary suite offers a spa-like bath, private balcony and walk in closet, creating a true retreat. Additional highlights include a spacious third floor bonus room, ideal for a home office and a lower level recreation space that's a perfect space for a gym or play. The home also boasts a two car garage with new epoxy floor. Conveniently located to town, train, shops, restaurants and beach.Year-round landscaping and snow removal included.
