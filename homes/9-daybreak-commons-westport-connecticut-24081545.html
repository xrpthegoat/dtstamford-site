About this home

This is an incredible opportunity to purchase a luxurious, mint-condition condominium at Daybreak Commons, offered to the market for the first time. This is only the 2nd re-sale to become available in this boutique community of high-end townhouses built in 2020. This attractive, manicured cul-de-sac of nine upscale homes is located within walking distance of Westport's vibrant downtown area with shopping, dining, cultural attractions & more. Set peacefully at the end of the community, w/incredible privacy & a tranquil backyard. The present owners selected this home during the building process, working closely w/highly-respected Able Construction to customize the entire residence to their exacting specifications, informed by an impeccable sense of style in collaboration w/a renowned design team. This elevator-ready home begins w/a double-height foyer lined w/windows that fill the entire home with light. The custom kitchen w/waterfall quartz island, Thermador appls, excellent storage, walk-in pantry & more is part of the great rm space that spans the rear. The seamless flow links the DR to the cathedral ceil LR w/gas fpl. Three sets of French doors lead to the patio, w/lrg awning &private views. Plush primary suite is the only one featuring a vltd ceil in the cmplx, has a lrg dressing rm & expanded bath w/htd flrs & upgraded quality. 2nd BR suite w/w-i-c & cstm bth. Fin LL has extra-high ceil, wet bar, office, storage. Natural gas, generator, 2 car garage. One owner must be 55+
