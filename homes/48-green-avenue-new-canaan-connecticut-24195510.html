About this home

The Best of Both Worlds: In-Town Ease, Home-Sized Living Experience the perfect blend of in-town convenience and the privacy of single-family living. This spacious end-unit townhouse offers the scale and comfort of a standalone home, featuring three generously sized bedrooms, including a primary suite with a full bath. The expansive main level includes an oversized living room, formal dining room, and a sunlit eat-in kitchen, providing a seamless layout for both daily life and entertaining. Additional highlights include a cozy fireplace, efficient gas heating, and the remarkable potential of a high-ceilinged lower level with 1,064 square feet of space ready to be finished to your specifications. Combined with the existing 2,128 square feet of living area, this residence offers incredible versatility and room to grow. Outside, enjoy the convenience of a detached one-car garage and additional driveway parking. Ideally located in the heart of New Canaan, you are just steps away from the train station, vibrant restaurants, local parks, the library, and schools. With low condo dues and significant potential to add your personal touch-including hardwood floors waiting beneath the existing carpeting-this is a rare opportunity to own a home that offers the ease of townhouse living with all the feel of a single-family house. Don't miss the chance to make this fabulous property your own.
