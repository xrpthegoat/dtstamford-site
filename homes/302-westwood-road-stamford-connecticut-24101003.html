About this home

Unbelievably Large and Spacious Home in the desirable area of Westwood Neighborhood in Stamford. This Home provides 2250 Square Feet of Living Space! Enter the home through the front door and head up to the Living Area and living Room with Hardwood floors throughout and terrific light through the Bay Window. An oversize opening leads through to the Formal Dining Room with a beautiful Brass Chandelier and a picture window out to the Wooded Back yard and Deck. The Kitchen is fully equipped with Stainless appliances, 2-pantrys and access to the Deck. Down the Hallway is the Master Bedroom Suite with a large walk-in Closet and Master Bathroom with stall Shower and Linen closet. The Three other bedrooms are a great size, and the hallway Bathroom has a stall Shower, Jet Tub and tiled flooring. Downstairs you will find a space that can be used for In-Law Living or just additional square footage for the Family. There is an additional Kitchen and entertainment space as well. A 2-Car Garage, Fenced Garden Area, Shed and large Deck and yard round out the picture and allow for outdoor Fun. Close to the Merritt and all things Stamford!
