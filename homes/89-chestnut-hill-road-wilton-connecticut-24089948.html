About this home

New Listing! Originally built in 1825, this distinguished Colonial Revival estate was significantly expanded in the 1920s by McCall's Magazine, which transformed it into their elegant country retreat. Now offering over 5,600 square feet of beautifully remodeled living space, the home has been thoughtfully restored to honor its historic roots while offering every modern comfort. A true sanctuary from the pace of everyday life, this property invites you to vacation at home. Enjoy morning coffee on the private master terrace, sunbathe on the south terrace, or relax with a book on the charming porch. The expansive, level grounds are ideal for outdoor living and feature a sun-drenched site perfect for a future pool. Inside, the home balances grandeur with warmth. The stunning Carrara marble kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with high-end appliances and a spacious island for gathering. A full butler's pantry and porch provide the perfect setup for entertaining. Host unforgettable dinners in the banquet-sized dining room or dine al fresco on the breathtaking stone terrace. The generous living room and adjacent den/bar feature three fireplaces and multiple sets of glass doors that open to the terrace and porch, blending indoor and outdoor living seamlessly. Work from home in one of two quiet offices, then unwind in the cozy family room by the fire, enjoy a workout in your personal gym, or retreat to the luxurious primary suite.
