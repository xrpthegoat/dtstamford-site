About this home

Built at the height of the Mad Men era in a town known for its moderns, this classic 1960 contemporary home with its soaring ceilings, walls of glass, and great flow is the epitome of modern homes built in New Canaan, CT. A glorious array of trees marks the changing seasons, from the spring blossoms of fruit trees along the driveway to the fall splendor of mature trees that fill the backyard and shade the home in deep summer. The living room's soaring wall of windows provides an intimate perspective on the natural beauty outside and becomes an efficient source of passive solar in the winter as the leaves fall to the ground and the sun drops in the sky. Recent updates include new hardwood floors, refinished HW floors thru-out the main living spaces, freshly painted, two of three bathrooms have been fully renovated. Space, light, and functional utility define the indoor space, with plenty of open areas for large family gatherings and dinner parties, yet enough separate spaces that serve as sanctuaries for private activities like office work or play. The home's iconic lines and modern aesthetic harken to New Canaan's history as an urbane settlement perfectly situated between the big city and the sprawling outdoors. Ahead of its time, 101 has a timeless energy and a very current vibe - perfect for today's lifestyle.
