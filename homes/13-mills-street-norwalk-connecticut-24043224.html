About this home

Charming gem in Silvermine neighborhood, 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom ranch with fully finished basement making the home 1,800 SqFt finished/livable space plus a 1 car garage and bonus shed. Basement boasts a beautiful wood bar, built in hutch and full bathroom. Relaxing Trex deck off of the kitchen provides the perfect spot to sit and enjoy the sunny days. Walk up attic for storage or renovation, make it into your dream office or additional bedroom. Long driveway provides space for ample tandem parking. Freshly painted, hardwood floors just refinished, and new carpeting installed. So convenient to shopping, parks and transit. Nothing to do here but move in and make it home!
