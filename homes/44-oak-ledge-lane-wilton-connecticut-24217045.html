About this home

Nestled in the woods in South Wilton, this lovely three bedroom home offers an abundance of space for family, friends and everyday living. The welcoming main level features a cozy living room with fireplace, dining room, kitchen with adjoining breakfast room, an oversized family room and a full bath. One of the home's true highlights is the 410 sq. ft. screened porch - a wonderful three season extension of the living space with plenty of room to dine, lounge, entertain friends or simply relax and enjoy the beauty of the surrounding landscape! Upstairs are three generously sized bedrooms, a full bath and convenient access to attic storage above the family room. The lower level adds even more versatility with an enormous recreation room, a large storage area, laundry and direct access to the two car garage. Outside, the beautifully landscaped property has been thoughtfully and meticulously cared for by the current owner, creating a peaceful setting that feels tucked away while still enjoying the convenience of South Wilton and the close proximity to Miller Driscoll school, downtown Wilton Center and train.
