About this home

Welcome to Applecrest, an enchanting Backcountry retreat available for occupancy in early November. Set within 28 acres of park-like grounds, this beautifully updated five-bedroom Colonial overlooks sweeping lawns, a picturesque pond, and a cascading waterfall. A grand front-to-back foyer welcomes you into this beautifully updated home. The main level features a formal living room with fireplace, dining room, new eat-in kitchen, wood-paneled office, screened porch with sensational views, and a first-floor bedroom with full bath. Upstairs, four spacious en-suite bedrooms complete the home. Ideally located just minutes from Glenville, downtown Greenwich, top schools, Westchester Airport, and the Merritt Parkway for easy NYC access. A truly magical oasis not to be missed!
