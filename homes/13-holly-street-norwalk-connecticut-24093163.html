About this home

Welcome to 13 Holly St, do not miss out on this stunning, recently renovated 4-bedroom, 2-bath raised ranch located in a Prime location!! There is nothing to do but move in. Featuring new tiled and LVP floors throughout, this home offers both style and functionality in a fantastic location just moments from the maritime aquarium of norwalk, the beach, train station, I95, shopping, dining, and more. The main level welcomes you with an open living and dining area, perfect for entertaining or relaxing with family. Large windows flood the space with natural light, enhancing the bright and airy feel. The updated kitchen offers ample counter space and modern appliances, ideal for preparing meals with ease. The home includes four spacious bedrooms with plenty of closet space. Two well-appointed bathrooms provide convenience for all residents and guests. The finished lower level features additional living space, perfect for a family room, home office, or recreation area. The attached garage adds convenience for parking and extra storage. Step outside to enjoy the large deck and backyard with plenty of outdoor activities or gardening space. This property is ideally situated near everything you would ever need, making it a perfect blend of tranquility and accessibility. Don't miss the chance to buy this beautifully maintained home in a prime location. Schedule a showing today and make this raised ranch your next home. Listing Agent has an interest in the property.
