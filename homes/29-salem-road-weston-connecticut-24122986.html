About this home

Welcome to 29 Salem Road, where modern design and timeless elegance converge in a home that makes one-floor living effortless. Completely renovated to the highest standards, this light-filled residence is designed for comfort, flow, and connection. Walls of glass bathe the interiors in natural light, while soaring ceilings enhance the sense of openness throughout. At the heart of the home, the chef's kitchen boasts quartz counters, dual oversized islands-one for prep and one for casual dining-custom cabinetry, and top-tier appliances, creating a hub for both everyday living and entertaining. The adjoining living and dining rooms, anchored by a dramatic two-story fireplace, provide seamless spaces for formal or informal gatherings. The sunroom, wrapped in windows, frames serene views of the pool, stone patio, and lush landscape-an ideal spot for year-round relaxation-while the cozy den offers a more intimate retreat for quiet evenings. The bedroom wing ensures privacy and convenience, featuring a stunning new primary suite with spa-like bath and walk-in closet, plus two additional bedrooms with a shared full bath. A separate wing with bedroom, bath, and flexible living area offers endless options: home office, gym, in-law suite or guest quarters. Set on a cul-de-sac in coveted Lower Weston, just minutes from schools and the town center, this property blends the ease of single level living with style, sophistication and an ideal location.
