About this home

This bright and spacious 3 BR, 2.5-bath townhouse offers the ideal blend of comfort, style, and convenience, all in turn-key condition. Perfectly situated in Glenbrook near the Stamford/Darien border, this rare find is part of a charming four-unit, stand-alone complex that combines privacy with easy access to the best Glenbrook and Stamford have to offer: shopping, dining, parks, schools, major highways, Metro-North, downtown Stamford.The open floor plan on the main floor is designed for today's more relaxed living, featuring an updated kitchen with granite counters and top-of-the-line appliances, a welcoming dining area, and sliders to a private patio-perfect for seamless indoor-outdoor entertaining. A cozy gas fireplace in the living room and gleaming hardwood floors throughout add warmth and elegance, while central A/C and natural gas heat ensure year-round comfort. Enjoy the convenience of an attached one-car garage, a finished lower level offering flexible space for a home office, playroom, or gym, and a pulldown attic for extra storage. With a brand-new roof, low monthly HOA fees, and a pet-friendly policy, this home truly checks every box. Low-maintenance living in a prime Stamford location-don't miss the opportunity to make this move-in ready townhouse yours!
