About this home

Welcome to this Completely Renovated Home, ideally situated in a quiet walkable desirable Bettswood neighborhood. A charming front porch invites you into the new mudroom featuring a wall of closets & two built-in benches. The open-concept main level offers a stunning new kitchen w/large island, quartz countertops, subway tile backsplash & stainless steel appliances. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining & living areas w/wood-burning fireplace & gleaming hardwood floors throughout. This home offers three bedrooms, including one on the main level-perfect as a guest room, office, or den & a newly renovated full bath w/honeycomb tile flooring, brass fixtures & a subway-tiled tub/shower. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms feature hardwood floors, custom wainscoting paneling, and another fully renovated bath w/honeycomb tile floor & subway-tiled stall shower. Additional highlights include approximately 1,430 sq. ft. of finished living space plus a 100 sq. ft. mudroom addition, a level .22-acre lot w/fully fenced backyard, & large flagstone patio ideal for outdoor entertaining. The tandem two-car attached garage provides stair access to storage above, offering future expansion potential.The lower level includes a comfortable lounge area, home gym space, laundry & abundant storage.
