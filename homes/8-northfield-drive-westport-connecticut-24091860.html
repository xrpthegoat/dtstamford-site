About this home

This is the perfect house in the perfect location-just minutes from top-rated schools, playgrounds, ballfields, and houses of worship. Set on a picture-perfect acre of lush, manicured lawn, this Colonial style home offers serene views of a large pond while overlooking 8+ acres of private, protected meadowland. A gas-fired, whole-house generator provides seamless backup power for year-round peace of mind. Step through the front door into a grand two-story foyer that introduces a seamless layout of expansive rooms exceptional for formal entertaining or everyday use. The gourmet kitchen features all granite countertops, double Bosch wall ovens and Subzero refrigeration. The first floor also offers the convenience of a full bath with an oversized shower. Once beyond the kitchen, you flow effortlessly into the spacious family room with vaulted ceilings, a cozy fireplace, and sliding glass doors to help you enjoy the picturesque views. Outside, a large deck overlooks beautifully landscaped gardens and a heated in-ground pool-your own private oasis. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a generous California Closets walk-in as well as a custom-built, luxurious spa-style bath. Three additional bedrooms share a beautifully appointed custom bath with double sinks and thermostatic shower controls for consistent, comfortable water temperatures. The finished lower level adds ample storage and versatile living space, perfect for a playroom, home office, or gym.
