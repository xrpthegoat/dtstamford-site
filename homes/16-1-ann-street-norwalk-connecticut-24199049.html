About this home

Turnkey SoNo Office Condo | 5,952 SF | 5 Reserved Garage Parking Spaces & 12 Surface Parking Spaces| $999K OWN YOUR SoNo HEADQUARTERS! Exceptional opportunity to own a fully finished and furnished 5,952 SF commercial condominium in the heart of vibrant SoNo. Designed for immediate occupancy, this first-floor professional office offers an outstanding combination of space, functionality, parking and location. The flexible layout includes 8 private offices, 2 large bullpen/open work areas, multiple conference rooms, a fully updated kitchen and private bathrooms, with abundant windows providing excellent natural light. The space is fully finished and furnished, allowing an owner-user to move in and begin operating with minimal disruption. Parking is a major advantage: 5 reserved garage spaces plus use of 12 surface parking spaces during regular business hours. The building offers 24-hour access, elevator service, ADA accessibility, brand-new HVAC, wet sprinklers, security system and smoke detectors. Located in the heart of SoNo, the property is walkable to the Metro-North train station, waterfront, restaurants, shopping and the area's vibrant downtown amenities. The building is a three-story condominium with residential units above and this is the only commercial unit in the building.
