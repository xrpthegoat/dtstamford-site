About this home

This completely renovated, sunny 2 bedroom, 2nd floor apartment is a short distance to Greenwich Avenue and the train. It features a contemporary open floor plan with gleaming hardwood floors in the living area/kitchen. The kitchen is modern with stainless steel appliances, white cabinets and solid surface countertops. The two bedrooms are carpeted in a neutral gray and provide generous closets. The main bedroom has a walk-in closet. Laundry and a large, assigned secure storage room are in the lower level. Parking for one car behind the building accessed by the back kitchen door, which opens to a small deck/sitting area.
