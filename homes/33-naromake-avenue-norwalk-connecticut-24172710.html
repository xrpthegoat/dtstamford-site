About this home

Set in the coveted Harbor Shores community, 33 Naromake Avenue offers a rare blend of refined design and true, direct waterfront living. Fully updated with a clean, open floor plan, this home has been thoughtfully reimagined with high-end finishes throughout, creating a space that feels both elevated and effortless. The main level is designed for how you actually live-light-filled, connected, and centered around the water. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the living and dining areas, all oriented to capture sweeping views across the water toward Harborview Island. Just beyond, step outside and you're instantly immersed in the setting-launch kayaks or paddleboards right from your backyard and take full advantage of this unique, accessible shoreline. A first-floor primary suite offers comfort and convenience, complete with a beautifully updated en-suite bath. Upstairs, a second bedroom with its own renovated bath provides an ideal space for guests, family, or a private retreat. For those who enjoy boating, private dock rights are available, while multiple nearby marinas offer additional flexibility-making it easy to pick up and drop off guests directly from your own backyard. A detached two-car garage provides ample room for storage, gear, and all the extras that come with an active coastal lifestyle. Zoned for award winning Rowayton Elementary School.This is more than a home-it's a lifestyle defined by water, light, and effortless living.
