About this home

Welcome to this spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch in one of Norwalk's highly desirable Wolfpit neighborhoods. Thoughtfully updated over the past five years, this home boasts a new kitchen, HVAC system (boiler, central air, and ductwork), a renovated main-level bathroom, and more. The heart of the home is the stunning kitchen with white shaker cabinets, quartz countertops, floating shelves, stainless steel appliances, and a sun-filled dining area. The main level also offers three comfortable bedrooms, an updated hall bath, and a convenient laundry room. The finished lower level features a private ensuite bedroom-perfect for an au pair, in-laws, or guests. Off the primary bedroom, a spacious four-season sunroom opens to a serene patio and park-like backyard, where mature trees provide shade in the summer and a natural sledding hill creates wintertime fun. The newly planted vegetable garden is ripe for the picking! The neighborhood has no through traffic, making it an ideal setting for walks, bike rides, and outdoor play. For added value, the home comes with architectural plans to expand into a 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath layout, offering flexibility for future growth. A versatile and inviting home in a sought-after location-just minutes to grocery stores, restaurants, shops, parks, the train station and more-don't miss this Wolfpit gem before it's gone!
