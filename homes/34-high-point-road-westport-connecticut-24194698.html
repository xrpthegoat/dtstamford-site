About this home

Refined living awaits on one of Westport's most coveted cul-de-sac settings, nestled within the sought-after Long Lots neighborhood. The exceptional residence unfolds across four finished levels, offering six ensuite bedrooms, two additional half baths, and a thoughtfully designed layout that balances grand scale with everyday functionality. A glass-doored entry introduces interiors defined by opulence and impeccable craftsmanship. Formal living and dining rooms are elegantly appointed, complemented by a butler's pantry for seamless entertaining. The chef's kitchen is both stunning and highly functional. Flowing effortlessly into a sunlit breakfast room and family room with fireplace and wet bar, all have a front row view to the spacious backyard. A private office and well-designed utility room enhance daily living, while direct access to the three-car garage ensures convenience. The second level hosts five beautifully appointed ensuite bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite complete with fireplace, bespoke walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath. An additional study or lounge space provides flexibility for work or relaxation. The third floor offers a sixth ensuite bedroom along with generous bonus space, ideal for guests or extended stays. The finished lower level is designed for recreation and wellness, featuring a media room, gym, game room, and ample storage. Comprised of park-like acre with room for a pool, the property offers both privacy and possibilities.
