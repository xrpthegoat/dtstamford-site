About this home

Tucked in the Delafield Island Association and moments from the beach, town, train, & schools, this custom built 5-bedroom residence offers an ideal blend of timeless elegance & modern comfort. Thoughtfully designed with soaring ceilings, an open floor plan & sun-filled interiors, nearly every main living space enjoys beautiful views of the pool & private backyard. A dramatic two-story foyer welcomes you into gracious dining room and parlor, creating a dramatic first impression. At the heart of the home is a spectacular eat in chef's kitchen featuring two expansive islands, abundant custom cabinetry, & seamless flow into the spacious family room & opens to a covered terrace with built-in heaters, creating the perfect setting for year-round indoor/outdoor entertaining. 2 home offices, breakfast nook, 2 powder rooms, & a large mudroom, complete the 1st fl. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, highlighted by a vaulted ceiling, lg walk-in closet & spa-inspired bath w radiant heated floors, soaking tub, double vanity, & oversized shower. An adjoining sitting room offers the perfect space for a private lounge, gym or nursery. 4 addl generously sized BRs & 3 full baths provide room for all, while the spacious 2nd fl landing is an inviting area for reading/relaxing. Finished huge basement w high ceilings has versatile space to add a golf simulator. 3rd fl is framed & plumbed, offering an outstanding opportunity to create a bedroom suite w bath tailored to your needs
