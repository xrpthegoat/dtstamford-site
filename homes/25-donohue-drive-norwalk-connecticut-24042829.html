About this home

***HIGHEST AND BEST BY SUN 9/8 at 7PM!!! More than meets the eye in this well-maintained, Raised Ranch providing almost 2200sqft of living space! Great Room addition features vaulted ceiling and a gas-log floor-to-ceiling fireplace... the focal point of the home. Sliders out to the rear deck make entertaining a breeze. Located right off of the open dining room/kitchen which has newer stainless steel appliances and loads of cabinet space with customized pull-out drawers. The original living room is now being used as the formal dining room which features a wood burning fireplace. Hardwood floors throughout this main level and full attic storage with plywood floors. In the walk-out lower level, the laundry room is also set up as a small kitchenette space, perfect for an in-law set up with a bedroom, family room and half bath (with space for an added shower). Off the rear patio is the wood shop/utility room; also great space to store lawn equipment and yard furniture, leaving more space in the one car garage. Central air, level backyard, large paved driveway and street parking make this home a win. Not to forget the PRIME LOCATION... Right off of Main Ave, within close proximity to tons of shopping, dining, Metro North and all major highways access. Set up your appointment to preview today!
