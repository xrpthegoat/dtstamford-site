About this home

** Highest and Best by Monday, July 28, 2025 at 12:00 PM (Noon) ** Lovingly owned by one family for 63 years, this expansive custom-built Westover ranch offers incredible space, versatility, and untapped potential! With over 3,000 SF on the main level and an additional ~1,423 SF in the walk-out lower level, this home is ideal for multigenerational living, entertaining, or future in-law/guest quarters. A circular driveway welcomes you, while the backyard offers a private oasis with a heated in-ground pool and patio. Enjoy two fireplaces, hardwood floors, first-floor laundry, 3-car garage, and a front porch perfect for morning coffee or greeting guests. The privately located primary suite features ample closet space and quiet comfort. Investors and design-savvy buyers will see the opportunity to modernize and customize throughout. Whether you're looking for a forever home, a smart investment, or a space to accommodate generations under one roof-this Westover gem delivers. Prime location just minutes to Metro-North, downtown Stamford, shopping, and dining.
