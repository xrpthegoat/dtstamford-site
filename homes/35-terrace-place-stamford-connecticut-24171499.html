About this home

Luxury. Location. Lifestyle. Welcome to this stunning 3BR/3BA detached luxury townhome in the heart of Stamford. Completed in 2021 and elevated with curated, bespoke high-end finishes throughout, including gorgeous whitewashed hardwood flooring, this sun-drenched modern residence offers a seamless open-concept layout designed for everyday living, effortless entertaining, style & functionality. The designer gourmet kitchen features top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, generous custom cabinetry, large center island, wine fridge, and a dedicated coffee bar all flowing seamlessly into the sophisticated living and dining area, anchored by a gas fireplace. Step outside to an expansive wraparound deck and private patio - the largest in the development - ideal for entertaining, relaxing, or taking in the stunning downtown city views. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true showpiece featuring vaulted ceilings, a spa-inspired ensuite bath, and massive custom closets spanning the full length of the bedroom on both ends. Two additional generously sized bedrooms, half bath and dedicated laundry complete this level. Additional highlights include an oversized 2-car garage with sleek epoxy flooring and additional parking. A versatile flex space offers the perfect setup for a home office, gym, media room, or additional space.
