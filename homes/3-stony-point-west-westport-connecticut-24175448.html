About this home

Your Summer vacation, elevated. Walk to the train. Walk to dinner or coffee. Wake up to waterfront views. This isn't just a summer rental, it's the retreat you've been dreaming about. Sprawling across six bedrooms and nine baths, this gem was built for the art of summer living. Soaring 9ft+ ceilings, walls of natural light, and water views set the stage. The house was designed for entertaining with an open living room/kitchen/DR concept for mingling, with French doors opening to a beautiful covered wood patio. Not one, but two giant family rooms - one on the main level leads to the rear patio/yard, and one on the third floor complete with wet bar and bath. Outside, an elaborate covered patio spills into the lush landscaped backyard where a heated pool, spa, and slide await. The patio boasts an outdoor kitchen and a giant ceiling fan for cool summer cookouts. And for little ones...a secret playroom they'll never want to leave. Add a sauna and this is summer, perfected. Price is per month. Flexible dates available through labor day.
