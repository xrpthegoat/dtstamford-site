About this home

Perfectly positioned between conservation land and one of Greenwich's most prestigious roads, this five-bedroom home combines exceptional convenience with refined privacy. A tree-lined sidewalk provides direct access to downtown, while the property offers a serene, retreat-like setting. French doors open to a spacious terrace with outdoor lighting and a fully fenced yard, adjacent to an oversized two-car garage with bonus space above. The elegant family room showcases a grand stone fireplace and views of the manicured lawn, and don't miss the lower level complete with a playroom, laundry, and wine cellar.
