About this home

Welcome to this quaint and welcoming 3-bedroom ranch situated on a level lot in a peaceful cul-de-sac neighborhood. The main level features a cozy living room with hardwood floors and fireplace, an open kitchen that overlooks the dining area, and a spacious family room with sliding doors leading to the deck. This floor also boasts three generously sized bedrooms, two with hardwood floors and a primary bedroom suite complete with walk-in closet and half bath. Outside, you'll discover your own private oasis featuring a large, level yard perfect for gatherings and entertainment, complemented by the serene tranquility of a gently flowing river at the rear of the property. A detached garage provides additional parking and storage space. This home is conveniently located just minutes from town amenities, shopping centers, restaurants, the police station, MetroNorth train station, Merritt Parkway, and I-95. Being sold "as is."
