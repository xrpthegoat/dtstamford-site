About this home

This house is as cute as a button! A modern yet classic cape style that lives large! The welcoming and open main floor has a generous sized eat in kitchen, a living, a dining room and a half bath with rich hardwood floors and tall (approx 8ft) ceilings. The upper floor has 2 bedrooms and a full bath. Large closets. A detached garage and private backyard add to the must haves for anyone who wants to live close to the beach and be within walking distance of all the amenities of the Cove/Shippan area. Being sold strictly AS-IS, no ifs and buts!
