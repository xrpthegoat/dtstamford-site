About this home

Welcome to 1 Raymond Terrace-an East Norwalk Colonial full of charm, potential, and convenience. With 3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, and over 1,300 square feet of living space, this home offers a fantastic layout that starts with high ceilings, hardwood floors, and generously sized rooms. The main level features a welcoming living room, a formal dining room, and a half bath with laundry. Upstairs, you'll find three comfortable bedrooms and a full bath, with even more potential waiting in the full basement. The backyard is level and easy to maintain-ideal for relaxing, entertaining, or a summer BBQ-and the detached one-car garage and ample off-street parking are rare bonuses in this neighborhood. Located in East Norwalk, you're just a short ride from the beach, train station, Stew Leonard's, I-95, and everything else Norwalk has to offer. This 1910 classic is powered by natural gas and packed with original character. With the right vision, it could truly shine. Come see why this location continues to be one of Norwalk's best-kept secrets.
