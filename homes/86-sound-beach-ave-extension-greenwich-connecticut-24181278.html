About this home

Located minutes from schools, parks, and shopping in the North Mianus school district, this home delivers on both location and livability. The open floor plan centers around a vaulted-ceiling living room, with hardwood flooring flowing throughout and a kitchen outfitted with brand-new stainless steel appliances. Three bedrooms plus a flexible office/nursery and a family room make for room to spread out. Outside, an oversized deck overlooking a lush, expansive yard is tailor-made for entertaining -- and with .35 acres, the potential for expansion is very real.
