About this home

Welcome to 10 Elliott Street, where classic 1924 Colonial charm meets the ease of coastal living in Norwalk's coveted Marvin Beach neighborhood. Just moments from the water, this home invites a lifestyle defined by morning walks along the shoreline and relaxed afternoons at home. Thoughtfully and meticulously maintained by the same owner for over 53 years, it offers a rare sense of care and continuity. The inviting exterior, warm interior spaces, and overall condition reflect true pride of ownership. Move right in and enjoy, or personalize over time-either way, this is an opportunity to become part of a close-knit waterfront community known for its charm and convenience.
