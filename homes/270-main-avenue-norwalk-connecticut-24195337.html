About this home

Approximately 1,400 SF of modern office space on the second floor of a recently renovated building. The suite offers a bright, open layout with four glass-walled private offices, a dedicated conference room, an open work area, and a full kitchen and lounge with island seating and stainless appliances. High-end, energy-efficient windows on all exterior walls and floor-to-ceiling glass partitions throughout the suite provide abundant natural light and clear sightlines across the entire space. Additional features include polished concrete floors, high exposed ceilings with modern LED lighting, private bathroom in the adjacent hallway, and both front and rear stairwell access. Four assigned parking spaces are included at the rear of the building. Offered at $2,700 per month on a modified gross lease. Tenant is responsible for their own utilities. Located on Main Avenue, the property offers high visibility and excellent accessibility, with close proximity to the Merritt Parkway, Route 7, restaurants, retail, and major corporate campuses. Ideal for a variety of professional office uses.
