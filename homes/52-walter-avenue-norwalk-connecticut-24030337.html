About this home

Expanded Cape on oversize and deep lot filled with beautiful plantings. Inside you'll find a large eat-in kitchen with an adjacent step-down room that could serve as an office or family room. There are 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath on the first floor and 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath upstairs. The lower level is partially finished and includes two large cedar closets and ample storage space. Enjoy summer evenings grilling (gas grill hooks are available to the propane tank) on the large stone patio with mature trees for privacy as well as a detached garage that fits two cars tandem. Recent updates include roof replaced 5 years ago, new furnace installed 4 years ago and the entire home was converted to gas heat. You will love the convenience of East Norwalk minutes from schools, houses of worship, parks, and shopping.
