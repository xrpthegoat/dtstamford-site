About this home

Settle into comfort and convenience with this raised ranch offering 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths. The heart of the home is a tremendously spacious kitchen, seamlessly connected to the dining room and a sunny deck via sliding doors-an ideal setup for gatherings and everyday meals. The primary bedroom features its own full bath and a generous walk-in closet, providing privacy and extra storage. Downstairs, you'll find a versatile lower level with a bedroom, a welcoming family room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, and easy access to a stone patio and fenced yard-complete with a cozy firepit area perfect for evenings outside. Access to the 2 car garage is via lower level. This home is ideal for anyone seeking extra space, whether for a larger household, guests, or hobbies. Enjoy all this in a location just minutes to local highways, shopping, golf courses, parks, the train, and public transportation, making every commute or weekend outing effortless. Ready to make this inviting, functional home yours?
