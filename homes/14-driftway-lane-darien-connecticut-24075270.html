About this home

TENNIS ANYONE? Welcome to this stunning residence located on the sought-after Driftway Lane in Darien, CT. This spacious and light-filled home offers everything you need for comfortable living and entertaining. Nestled on an expansive 1.51-acre lot, the property features a private tennis court, perfect for both avid players and those seeking an active lifestyle. With 2,860 square feet of living space, this home artfully combines elegance and practicality. Step into the bright and airy living room, where a cozy fireplace invites relaxation. The formal dining area seamlessly transitions to an eat-in kitchen, ideal for both intimate gatherings and larger celebrations. The family room boasts a vaulted ceiling and its own fireplace, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The sunroom offers a serene space filled with natural light, providing a perfect retreat for reading or enjoying the view of the lush surroundings. A den with built-ins adds versatility for work or leisure. The finished basement offers additional space for recreation or hobbies. The primary suite is a true sanctuary, ensuring privacy and comfort with its spacious layout. With three additional bedrooms and two more bathrooms, there is ample room for family and guests. Experience the perfect blend of elegance and functionality in this exceptional home, where every detail is designed to enhance your lifestyle.
