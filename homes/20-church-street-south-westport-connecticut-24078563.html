About this home

Welcome to a truly pristine residence in the Greens Farms area of Westport, where sophistication and comfort blend seamlessly in a like-new Colonial built by SIR. This exquisite home, finished on 4 levels, promises an unparalleled living experience with its thoughtfully designed spaces and top-of-the-line features. Step into the heart of this residence-a beautifully appointed chef's kitchen that flows effortlessly into the inviting family room. From here, step outside to the patio and enjoy your private backyard, perfect for entertaining or playing. The dining room, featuring elegant details, sets the stage for memorable gatherings. The 2nd floor offers a perfect primary suite with 2 generous walk-in closets and a spa-like bath that invites relaxation. An en-suite bedroom provides privacy, while 2 more bedrooms share a stylish Jack & Jill bathroom. The convenience of a 2nd-floor laundry room enhances everyday living. The 3rd floor unveils a hidden gem-another bedroom, a full bath, and a bonus space complete with a homework station. The lower level is a haven of leisure and fitness, featuring a top of the line gym, rec room, bedroom, full bathroom, and abundant storage space. This extraordinary home is perfectly situated just minutes from Westport's beaches, vibrant shopping, award-winning schools, and transportation options. In the heart of Westport, this Greens Farms treasure invites you to experience a life of elegance and convenience, with every amenity at your fingertips.
