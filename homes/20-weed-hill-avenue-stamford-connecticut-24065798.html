About this home

Welcome to this stunning, fully renovated 3-bedroom, 2 1/2-bath townhouse, located in the heart of the sought-after Springdale neighborhood. With a perfect blend of charm, modern upgrades, and unbeatable convenience, this home is truly a must-see! Step inside to find beautiful bamboo hardwood floors that flow throughout the spacious living room, leading to a private sundeck-perfect for outdoor relaxation. The completely remodeled kitchen boasts brand-new appliances, elegant stone countertops, and an open layout-ideal for any home chef. Each of the three bedrooms has generous closet space and plenty of natural light. On the lower level, you'll find a versatile additional living area that can be used as a playroom, home office, or guest space, along with a large laundry room equipped with a brand-new washer and dryer. Additional upgrades include high-efficiency baseboard heating, a newer water heater, and fully renovated bathrooms. The home also includes a one-car garage, plenty of outdoor parking, and access to Springhill Terrace amenities, such as a pool and playground. Available immediately. Don't miss this incredible opportunity to own a beautifully updated home in one of Springdale's most vibrant locations!
