About this home

Charming Classic Cape in the sought after area of Cranbury set on over a flat quarter-acre +of professionally landscaped property, featuring rustic exposed beams, a cozy fireplace & gleaming hardwood flooring throughout. This well maintained home offers numerous updates,including a newer roof,new hot water heater, new blue stone from step,new Bosch dishwasher & more. A welcoming front door leads into the flawless floor plan offering ease of living & flow. In the front to back living room you will find a wood burning fireplace, rustic beams, two built-ins flanking a window that over looks the backyard and a door that leads out to the screened in porch. The spacious dinning room will seat many for a dinner gathering. From the back door you enter into the kitchen with an abundance of cabinetry, granite counter tops, stainless steel appliances, breakfast nook, vaulted ceiling with skylight and a sliding door out onto the deck. Up to the second floor are three lovely bedrooms, two with walk-in closets and a new renovated full bath. The basement offers storage & more potential for living space. A exceptional quiet location close to everything! A Wonderful blend of charm & modern improvements -ready for you to move right in!
