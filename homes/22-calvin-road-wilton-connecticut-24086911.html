About this home

Beautifully sited gracious Colonial in great South Wilton location. Drive up the elegant entrance and enjoy the beautiful grounds creating a stately presence to this four bedroom home. Lovely updated kitchen with its own eat in area/seating. Kitchen opens to great family room with second fireplace and access to deck for excellent outdoor entertaining. Large room over garage can serve multi purposes including home office, playroom, movie room..... Large unfinished basement with high ceilings has great potential to finish for additional living space. An abundance of windows and light make for a very sun filled happy home!
