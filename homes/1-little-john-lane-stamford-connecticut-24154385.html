About this home

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Sherwood Forest, one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, this charming home offers a rare blend of privacy, character, and natural beauty. The true centerpiece of the property is its extraordinary backyard retreat. Surrounded by nature, it features a tranquil stream and a one-of-a-kind private island, creating a serene setting perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings with family.This 3-4 bedroom home offers endless potential to personalize and transform it into your dream home. Inside, you'll find updated bathrooms, hardwood floors with distinctive parquet detailing throughout the main level, and a welcoming wood-burning fireplace with a granite hearth. The spacious eat-in kitchen provides ample room for everyday living.The upper level features hardwood flooring throughout, including a primary suite with a walk-in closet and private full bath, along with two additional bedrooms and a second full bathroom.The lower level of this split-level home expands the living space with a generous family room featuring sliding doors to the backyard patio, a full bath, and a kitchenette-offering excellent potential for an in-law suite, guest accommodations, or flexible living arrangements. Combining a prime location, a truly unique natural setting, and timeless warmth, this special property presents a rare opportunity to own a one-of-a-kind home in Stamford.
