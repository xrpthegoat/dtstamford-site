About this home

Lovely home with unique blend of historic character and modern convenience. Gourmet eat-in kitchen tastefully renovated with large dining area, center island, gas range, large pantry and laundry. Exposed beams and 2 fireplaces in living and sunny family room. First floor has possible bedroom or office and full bath, while there are 3 bedrooms and an additional office on 2nd floor with large closets and full bath. Spacious back yard w/ beautiful stonework and patio, 1 car garage, storage. Cottage with water and electricity can be rented for an additional $1,000 per month. Tranquil setting, huge back yard, all within minutes drive to Rt. 1, Merritt, I95, Rowayton or Darien train stations, shopping and restaurants on the Post Rd. Pets considered on individual basis. Full credit report and references a must. No smoking. Tenant to pay all utilities and lawn care. Lease now through May 31, 2025 with possible 12 month renewal.
