About this home

Spacious light filled 2 (possibly 3) bedroom colonial will have interior rooms freshly painted prior to occupancy August 1st. Painting is underway! It sits proudly on 1.3 level grassed acres, maintained by the landlord while the tenant takes care of the snow removal. It is on a quiet lane yet is so convenient to shops, restaurants, Metro train into NYC and commuting highways. The eat-in kitchen has tile counters and tile backsplash plus newer appliances. The large living room with fireplace (a new mantle is ordered & facing will be repaired) has a spacious dining area. There is a full tub bath off the family room which could also be used as a primary or guest bedroom. Upstairs are 2 large bedrooms and a small office as well as a hall tub bath. There is good storage in the basement and the walk-up attic. There are 2 convenient small entry porches at the front and back of the house. Relax on the private 26' by 10' deck just off the back porch overlooking the play yard. Good credit & references are an absolute must! Lease renewal potential exists for good tenants. A small well-trained pet will be considered on a case-by-case basis with additional monthly rent. Landlords are very conscientious! The home is being repaired where needed and rooms painted, All new oil burner, oil tank in basement and roof were installed recently. FURNISHED ROOM PHOTOS ARE FROM PREVIOUS TENANTS
