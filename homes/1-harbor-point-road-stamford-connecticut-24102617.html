Experience Luxury Waterfront Living at The Beacon at Harbor Point Discover Stamford's premier luxury waterfront high-rise, The Beacon at Harbor Point. Offering beautifully appointed finishes, each residence features stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, quartz countertops, a full-size in-unit washer and dryer, and spacious open layouts designed for modern living. Enjoy resort-style amenities, including a 24-hour concierge, a state-of-the-art fitness center with cutting-edge cardio and weight-training equipment, a game room with billiards, a swimming pool, and outdoor fire pits for relaxation.
