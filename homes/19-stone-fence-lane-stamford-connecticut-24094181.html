About this home

Like-New Construction! Exquisitely renovated from top to bottom, 19 Stone Fence Lane is a masterpiece of design & craftsmanship, offering 7,000+sf of living space across 3 meticulously finished levels. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated, showcasing the finest materials & high-end finishes - from custom kitchen cabinetry & Sub-Zero/Wolf appliances to Taj Mahal stone countertops, intricate millwork, & stunning chevron-patterned hardwood floors. A dramatic 17ft foyer welcomes you into an expansive first level featuring a coffered-ceiling living room, elegant dining room, sunlit private office, & family room with a fireplace framed by custom built-ins. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, complete with an oversized island, inset-designed pantry, & high-end finishes. A marble-clad powder room & custom-designed laundry room complete this level. The 2nd floor boasts a luxurious primary suite with 11-foot tray ceilings, a spa-like en-suite bath, & a fully outfitted walk-in closet. 3 additional guest bedrooms, with their own beautifully appointed bathroom, provide comfort and privacy, while a vaulted-ceiling bonus room offers versatile space for a great room, playroom, gym, or media room. Designed with 10ft ceilings, the LL offers endless possibilities for customization. Plenty of space exists for a theatre, gym, wine cellar, & more. The 3-car garage features extra height, epoxy flooring, & brand-new doors.
