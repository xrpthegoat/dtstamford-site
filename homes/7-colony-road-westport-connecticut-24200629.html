About this home

ALL UTILITIES INCLUDED-electricity, natural gas, heat, and water! Enjoy easy living in this bright and charming two-bedroom, one-bath garage apartment in the heart of Westport, just minutes from the train station, schools, shopping, dining, and town beaches. The apartment features a private entrance, private driveway, and oversized one-car garage. The open-concept living area flows into a modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, and a wine refrigerator. Vaulted ceilings, a bay window, and a skylight fill the home with abundant natural light, while hardwood floors run throughout. Additional features include in-unit laundry and generous storage. Surrounded by beautifully maintained grounds, the property offers a peaceful and private setting with convenient access to everything Westport has to offer. Simply bring your furniture and move right in-ALL UTILITIES ARE INCLUDED! Available unfurnished; a two-year lease is preferred.
