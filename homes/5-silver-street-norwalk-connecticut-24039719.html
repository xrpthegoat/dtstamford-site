About this home

Located in Norwalk's Light Industrial Zone (SD-LI), this commercial/industrial building is ideally situated just off Rt 7 and offers flexibility for many uses (subject to P&Z approval) such as Distillery/Brewery, Printing Shop, Farmers Market, Storage facility, Research & Development Facility, Warehouse, Gymnasium, Industrial Office & Showroom, Sports Court, Personal Services Establishment. The building is bright with abundant natural light, offers clearance of 10' at overhead door & below structural beams with added height in between. HVAC provides heat (natural gas) and C/A for cooling. Electricity is supplied by a three phase 100 amp panel. As a corner lot, there is abundant parking on two sides of the building and covered storage on the rear. This would be an excellent workshop, storage center for classic cars or suit many additional uses. Speak to listing agent for more details and to view this great offering. The space is presently configured to offer 3 offices and shop space but the offices can be eliminated if sought by Tenant. Rent is NNN with Tenant paying modest real estate taxes and property insurance (combined roughly $8000 per year). Tenant responsible for all property and building maintenance. Two year or longer lease is sought. Owner is seeking good credit and a proven track record for prospective tenant.
