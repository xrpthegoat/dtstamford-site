About this home

Beautiful, light-filled corner apartment in nearly new construction that is close to everything Darien has to offer. Spacious 1-bedroom, 1-1/2 bath apartment features 9ft+ and elevator access. Kitchen boasts top-of-the-line Bosch appliances, soft-close drawers ad Quartz countertops. Open concept kitchen/dining/living room floor plan is perfect for entertaining, with oversized windows and gas fireplace to keep space inviting. Stunning primary bedroom includes large closet and modern, en-suite bathroom with oversized shower. Additional amenities include 1/2 bath located in entry, washer/dryer in-unit, dedicated storage space in basement, and 1 assigned parking space with plenty of parking for guests. Tenant responsible for credit/background/eviction check, liability and personal property insurance, natural gas, electricity, water, and cable/internet. 1st month's rent and 1 month security deposit due at lease signing. Additionally, non-refundable $500 deposit for all pets due at lease signing. Please ask about pet restrictions and fees. No smoking allowed. No more than 3 occupants allowed.
