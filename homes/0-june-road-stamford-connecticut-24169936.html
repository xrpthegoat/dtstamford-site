About this home

Set on 31.76 acres and thoughtfully subdivided into three distinct lots, this exceptional offering sits along the border of Greenwich, delivering a rare blend of scale, serenity, and convenience. Each parcel offers its own unique character: Lot 1 captures sweeping vistas and breathtaking sunrise views 6.22, while Lots 2 and 3 feature over 18.82 acres and 6.71 acres of beautifully level land. The property is further enhanced by bordering the protected 900+ acre Mianus River Gorge Preserve, ensuring enduring privacy and a stunning natural backdrop. Whether you envision a private compound, equestrian estate, or a refined country retreat, this is a canvas of remarkable potential. A walk of the land reveals the true scale and beauty an opportunity that must be experienced to be fully appreciate Perfectly positioned just 12 minutes to downtown Greenwich and 20 minutes to Stamford, you'll enjoy effortless access to premier dining, shopping, and Metro-North train service--while returning home to complete tranquility. A rare chance to create something extraordinary in one of Fairfield County's most coveted settings.
