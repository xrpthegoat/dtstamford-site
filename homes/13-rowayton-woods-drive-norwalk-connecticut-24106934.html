About this home

This charming and elegant garden-level end unit is thoughtfully updated and full of character. It offers the rare combination of privacy and convenience, tucked away just steps from the heated pool with both a private and shared entrance. Enjoy outdoor living on your spacious patio with a private perennial garden, a peaceful spot for morning coffee or evening entertaining. Inside, the space is a visual treat, with gleaming bamboo floors, crown moldings, and high-end finishes, as well as built-ins and a stunning entry niche. The kitchen features a breakfast bar, appliance garage and lovely detail, while the primary suite includes a generous ensuite bath. Whether you're lounging by the pool, enjoying a match on the tennis courts, or exploring nearby Rowayton, Darien, and great local shopping, this is a home that blends comfort, style, and lifestyle in one delightful package.
