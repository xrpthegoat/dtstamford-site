About this home

Welcome to 13 Suburban Drive in the highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk. This stunning four-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath ranch offers exceptional one-level living with over 1,800 square feet of meticulously maintained space. Completely renovated in 2018, this home exudes modern charm and comfort. Step into the bright and airy living area, where sprawling high vaulted ceilings and elegant French doors lead to an oversized patio with custom masonry-perfect for seamless indoor/outdoor entertaining. The large eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar for casual dining. The primary suite offers a tranquil retreat with engineered wood floors, a walk-in closet, and a luxurious en-suite bath. The expansive, flat yard is beautifully landscaped with new plantings and offers plenty of space for a pool. Situated on a peaceful half-acre lot, this home is just minutes from shopping, restaurants, the Merritt Parkway, and the train station, combining convenience with suburban tranquility. Don't miss the opportunity to own this exceptional property in one of Norwalk's most desirable areas.
