About this home

Step into a serene sanctuary nestled amidst the natural elegance of North Stamford. This contemporary home features an inviting floor plan spread over 2866 square feet, showcasing four cozy bedrooms and 3.5 modern bathrooms. Two of the star attractions are the majestic stone fireplaces that promise warm, story-filled nights and a screened porch that extends a quaint invite to enjoy the lush, wooded views. Venture outside onto the spacious decks, where leisure meets beauty. It's the perfect backdrop for both tranquil morning coffees and vibrant evening gatherings. Cathedral ceilings inside amplify the sense of space, setting a dramatic stage for both living and entertaining. Curated with potential for personal touches, this home calls out to those with a visionary eye for renovation. It's a canvas awaiting your unique brushstrokes to enhance its inherent charm and character. Location is just the cherry on top! With proximity to cultural gems like the Stamford Museum & Nature Center, the art and nature lover in you will be continually inspired. LaRocca's Country Market is just a stone's throw away, adding convenience to this delightful mix. Perfect for those who appreciate privacy yet value connectivity to community amenities, embrace the ambiance of a neighborhood that is as inviting as it is well-positioned. Here, life slows down a bit-just enough to savor each moment. Welcome home to a place where every day feels like a retreat from the ordinary.
