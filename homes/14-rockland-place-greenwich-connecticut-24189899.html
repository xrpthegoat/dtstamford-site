About this home

Spacious Colonial with nearly 4,000 sq ft on a quiet cul-de-sac in the heart of Pemberwick. The bright main level offers a living room with a fireplace, an open eat-in kitchen with an island and breakfast bar, a pantry, a dining area, one bedroom, and a full bath. Upstairs features the primary suite with a walk-in closet and en-suite bath, two additional bedrooms, a hall bath, laundry, and a flexible office/study area. The walk-out lower level adds a recreation room, full bath, and two finished rooms ideal for a home office, guest space, gym, or hobbies. Deck overlooking the backyard. Close to Pemberwick Park, playgrounds, library, shopping, and neighborhood amenities.
