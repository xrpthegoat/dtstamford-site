About this home

Seller are asking for HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY MONDAY 7/14/25 at 5PM Welcome to this charming Contemporary Colonial in the sought-after Long Lots area. Nestled on a serene cul-de-sac, this bright home offers the trifecta of natural gas, city water, and sewer. 3/4 acre lot extends all the way to Long Lots Rd! Enjoy the convenience of being just minutes from town, the train, beaches, and major highways. The open floor plan features Andersen Windows and hardwood floors throughout. Relax by the custom wood-burning fireplace in the family room, or entertain in the dining room or eat-in kitchen. The lower level provides versatile space for a gym, office, and guest accommodation, complete with a full bath. With proximity to beaches, shopping, dining, and top-rated schools, this home offers an exceptional lifestyle. Don't miss your chance to make it yours!
