About this home

Nestled in the highly sought-after Cranbury section of Norwalk, this charming 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom home sits on a generous 0.87-acre lot, offering a rare combination of space, privacy, and location. Just a short walk from scenic Cranbury Park and the historic Gallaher Mansion, this property provides easy access to natural beauty and local landmarks. Inside, you'll find sun-drenched living spaces with a comfortable flow between the living and dining areas-ideal for everyday living and entertaining. A versatile bonus room just off the dining room adds flexibility, whether you're in need of a home office, playroom, or potential third bedroom. The expansive backyard is perfect for gardening, relaxing, or hosting gatherings, with plenty of room for future expansion. Additional features include a detached garage and a storage shed, adding both function and convenience. Located close to major commuter routes, this home offers the perfect blend of suburban tranquility and city accessibility. Don't miss this unique opportunity to enjoy the best of both worlds-comfortable living with a spacious outdoor lifestyle.
