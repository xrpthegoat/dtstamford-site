About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in the highly coveted Allwood Neighborhood, this charming 5-bedroom home is set on a beautifully maintained 1.61-acre property and offers over 4,000 sq ft of thoughtfully designed living space. Classic yet comfortably casual, the home features high ceilings and generous living areas throughout. The welcoming foyer opens to a step-down living room with vaulted ceilings, a cozy library with fireplace, a formal dining room, and a sunken family room-perfect spaces for everyday living and elegant entertaining. Upstairs, the spacious primary ensuite is accompanied by four additional bedrooms, offering flexibility for family, guests, or home office needs. The lower level expands your lifestyle with a large playroom, game room, bar, and wine cellar-ideal for gatherings or quiet relaxation. With its exceptional layout, timeless style, and serene setting, this is a must-see home in one of the area's most desirable locations.
