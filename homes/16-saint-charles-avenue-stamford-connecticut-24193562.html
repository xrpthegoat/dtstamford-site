About this home

Stunning Springdale gem completely modernized ready for you to unpack, hang your hat & move right in. Step inside to an open, light-filled layout w/an unobstructed, sweeping front-to-back view, gorgeous finishes & high ceilings. Exquisite style combined w/quality workmanship presents throughout all of the living spaces, including kitchen & bath w/quartz & marble countertops; GE S/S appliances (gas cooking!); Kohler & California Faucets; oak/maple/teak wood; & porcelain & ceramic tile. XL shower stall w/frameless glass & window affords great natural light. Classic architectural details & rich millwork are also integrated throughout, infusing the home w/the warmth, charm & vibe of a quintessential New England cottage. Outside, incredible greenspace! Enjoy every inch of this unprecedented property: host a perfect picnic, gather for dinner by the garden, meditate in the morning under the trees, watch the sun rise peacefully under the portico & set from just about everywhere. Truly your own private wonderland! For avid gardeners or those developing a green thumb, historically rich soils have yielded delicious squash, zucchini, corn, beans & peppers; turnips & tomatoes, too. And the fruit trees bearing fresh apples, cherries & raspberries are a sweet surprise. 2/10ths of a mile to Metro North RR; less than 1mi to shops, restaurants & conveniences; 2mi to golf; 3mi to I-95 or Merritt. Unwind, relax & rejuvenate - come home to comfort in Connecticut today! Lawn maintenance included!
