About this home

First time on the market in 39 years! This charming 1930's 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers 2211 sq ft of living space on 2.96 acres of flat, beautiful, park-like property. Set back from the road, this property offers privacy, space, and endless possibilities while remaining conveniently located in a community with award-winning schools. A welcoming foyer leads to a spacious formal front-to-back living room featuring a fireplace and attractive moldings, along with a formal dining room perfect for entertaining. The kitchen offers stainless steel appliances and opens to a dining area and comfortable family room space. An enclosed porch provides additional versatile living space and overlooks the expansive grounds. The second-floor features three bedrooms and a full bath, while the fourth bedroom is privately located on the third floor and includes an ensuite bath. Hardwood floors throughout add warmth and character. With a generous layout, beautiful property, 2-car garage and tremendous potential, this is a wonderful opportunity to bring your vision and create the home you've always wanted. Close to town, train and major highways. Property is being sold "as is."
