About this home

This stunning Mid-Century Modern home combines striking architecture, sophisticated designer finishes and exceptional indoor-outdoor living in a private setting with sweeping views and spectacular sunsets. Thoughtfully expanded and extensively rebuilt in 2015, the home features a light-filled open floor plan, walls of glass and high-quality finishes throughout. At the heart of the home, the dramatic living and dining areas feature soaring vaulted ceilings and expansive windows that frame breathtaking vistas and flood the interiors with natural light. The first-floor primary suite offers a private sanctuary with a spacious walk-in closet and dressing room. Three additional bedrooms provide generous accommodations, while the fabulous lower-level great room opens directly to the terrace and pool area and features an entertainment bar-perfect for relaxed gatherings and entertaining. Multiple outdoor living spaces take full advantage of the home's beautiful natural setting. A stunning infinity-edge pool with waterfall creates a resort-like focal point, complemented by an outdoor shower, expansive terraces and gorgeous perennial gardens. A truly special home offering privacy, dramatic views and exceptional modern living.
