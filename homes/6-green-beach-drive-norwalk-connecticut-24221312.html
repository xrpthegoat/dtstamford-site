About this home

Experience effortless coastal living in this chic, sophisticated furnished Rowayton retreat, available January 1 through May 31, 2027, at $12,500 per month. Offering 3,093 SF with 3 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, this beautifully renovated designer home is situated on a quiet, traffic-free street within walking distance of downtown Rowayton and Bayley Beach. The stunning eat-in kitchen features custom cabinetry, soapstone countertops, and high-end appliances, opening to a dramatic family room with gas fireplace, cathedral ceiling, rich wood paneling, and distinctive barn doors with pulley systems. French doors from the kitchen and family room lead to a private, professionally landscaped backyard oasis. A spacious formal dining room and inviting living area provide exceptional entertaining space. The luxurious primary suite occupies the entire second floor, featuring a spa-inspired bath with seamless glass shower, marble accents, soaking tub, and dual vanities. An upstairs laundry room with coffee bar and mini refrigerator adds convenience. Two additional spacious bedrooms on the main level share a beautifully renovated hall bath in a separate wing. The lower level offers a versatile studio, second laundry room, and attached two-car garage. Enjoy SONOS sound throughout the home and rear patio. Fully furnished. Utilities not included. No smoking. Dogs considered. References required. Note for navigation: Green Beach or Beech.
