About this home

Nestled in the Cos Cob neighborhood of Greenwich at the western side of the Mianus River. This fully renovated apartments offer partial views of the Mianus River, alongside some beautiful homes hugging the shoreline. These two-bedroom apartments include newer stainless-steel appliances, and include a combo living/dining room, primary bedroom, a smaller bedroom that can also be used as a home office, full bath, and laundry room with some storage. This apartment has a private side patio, and a patio in front. Just 35 miles from New York City, close to highways, train station, and public transportation, Greenwich and Stamford shopping, restaurants, parks, library and so much more...These apartments have off street parking and on street parking.
