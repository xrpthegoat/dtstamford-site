About this home

Palmer Hill Townhouse! Located on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. "LIKE NEW", END UNIT, custom 3 bedrooms/3.5 baths + office/bedroom or playroom and 2 car garage. This private, light filled home has custom maple floors throughout and many special upgrades. The eat in kitchen has custom cabinets, glass backsplash, white marble counters, SS appliances, a large island and custom lighting. The 27 foot Living Room/Dining Room has custom lighting, a white marble gas fireplace, and French doors to your private patio. A powder room completes this floor. The upper floor has the primary bedroom suite, with a tray ceiling, 2 custom walk-in closets, a marble en-suite full bath with a makeup table and special soaking tub. There are 2 more bedrooms and full marble bath on this floor as well as a separate laundry room with a washer and dryer. Bring your decorating ideas and move right into this special, impeccable home. Amenities include a newly renovated clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym, yoga room and business center.
