About this home

Welcome to this classic 3-bedroom Colonial tucked at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Stamford's most convenient neighborhoods. This sunlit home offers timeless style with flexible living spaces designed for today's living. A generous living room with hardwood floors and a picture window sets a warm tone, while the dining room flows seamlessly into the updated kitchen, creating a natural hub for living and entertaining. The main level family room adds space for everyday activities. Upstairs, three comfortable bedrooms include a spacious primary with walk-in closet and plenty of natural light. The full bath is thoughtfully updated, and an attic with pull-down-stairs provides excellent storage. The walkout lower level offers potential for a game room, office, or recreation space. Outdoors, the privately situated yard is framed by mature plantings, offering space for gardening, play, or relaxed gatherings on the patio or screened in porch. The 2-car garage has attic space with PDS, and is one step into the family room for ease of unloading groceries. 2 attic accesses: PDS in garage, shuttle in BR. The cul-de-sac location combines a peaceful setting with amazing proximity to shopping, restaurants, schools, parks, Merritt Pkwy, and Metro-North for easy commuting. Plant your roots at the end of a cul-de-sac-38 Lancaster Pl, where home begins.
