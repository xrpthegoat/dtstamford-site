About this home

Welcome to this beautiful 5 bedroom, 4.5 bath home in the heart of Old Greenwich and the coveted Dundee International school district. Incredibly convenient to Binney Park, Perrot Library, Metro-North train, and all that OG village has to offer, this transformative, to the studs renovation and expansion was designed by award winning Greenwich architect, Krist Dodaro. The open-concept main floor features a stunning new kitchen flowing seamlessly into the dining and family room, with beautiful limestone-clad fireplace and French doors leading to the stone patio and expansive, fully fenced in back yard. A light-filled office, living room & half-bath complete this level. A gorgeous primary suite, 3 additional bedrooms and laundry room make up the 2nd floor while the versatile 3rd floor includes a play room, 5th bedroom and full bath. A rare opportunity to own a turn-key home in one of Greenwich's most beloved neighborhoods.
