About this home

Tucked away in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood, this fully renovated 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath home sits on over 3 beautiful acres with a pond-a rare and peaceful setting. Walk right into a bright, sunny living room with a gorgeous stone wood-burning fireplace, perfect for cozy nights. The home flows into the dining area and family room, featuring a wall of windows that look out over the property and a wood-burning stove that adds warmth and charm. The kitchen offers plenty of counter space and a door that leads straight to the backyard-great for grilling, entertaining, or just stepping outside with your morning coffee. On the main level you'll find two spacious bedrooms, one with an en-suite bath, along with an additional full bathroom with double vanity, tub, and shower. Laundry is also conveniently located on the main level. Upstairs is the third bedroom, ideal for guests, a home office, or extra living space, with tons of storage. Outside is where this home really shines-relax by the pond, fish, watch wildlife pass through, toss a ball in the level yard, or enjoy the privacy and open space that make this property feel like a true retreat. A perfect rental for anyone looking for comfort, space, and a peaceful setting-while still being close to everything Norwalk has to offer.
