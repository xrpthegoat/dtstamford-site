About this home

Perfect home blend of comfort, style, and privacy, having been lovingly maintained and thoughtfully upgraded over the years. Private setting with beautifully landscaped grounds, you'll enjoy complete tranquility and the ideal backdrop for hosting your next family gathering in the spring. Upon entering, you're greeted by a bright living room, cozy family room, featuring a charming wood-burning fireplace-perfect for cozying up on chilly nights. The formal dining room adds an elegant touch, providing the ideal space for hosting dinner parties or special occasions. At the heart of this home is the open-concept kitchen, which has been beautifully updated with brand-new white cabinetry, stunning quartz countertops, and a sparkling backsplash. This dream kitchen is both functional and stylish, offering abundant space for meal prep and casual dining. A charming breakfast area opens directly onto the patio, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow-perfect for enjoying morning coffee. Gleaming hardwood floors run throughout the house, adding warmth and character to every room. Upstairs, you'll find three fabulous bedrooms, each offering ample space and comfort. The primary bedroom, featuring walk-in closets and a ensuite bathroom. The two additional bedrooms are perfect for family, guests, or a home office, and they share a beautifully updated full bathroom. Conveniently located just minutes from the town center, public transportation, and the Merritt Parkway.
