About this home

Welcome to Heights Crossing, a newly constructed, luxury apartment community in Darien, offering a rare balance of refined living and immediate access to New York City via the train station across the street. This 2-bedroom, 2.5 bathroom residence is thoughtfully composed with a seamless, open layout. The kitchen features GE Profile appliances, quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and a bar-height island suited for both casual dining and effortless entertaining. The living space extends to a Juliet balcony overlooking the train station, introducing a subtle urban edge while maintaining the privacy and calm of a suburban setting. Both bedrooms are ensuite and well-proportioned. The primary suite offers dual closets and a spa-like bath with a stall shower, while the second bedroom includes a custom walk-in closet and a full bath with a tub and shower combination. A powder room and in-unit washer and dryer are discreetly integrated for everyday ease. Heights Crossing offers secure access, elevator service, garage parking, on-site management, a fitness center, golf simulator, coworking spaces, and a resident lounge with WiFi. A landscaped courtyard with a central fountain and curated ground-level retail complete the experience.
