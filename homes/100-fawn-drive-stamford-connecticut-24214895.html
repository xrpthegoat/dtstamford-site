About this home

Welcome to 100 Fawn Drive, a beautifully appointed ranch-style home tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac. Offering the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience, this inviting home features a bright, open floor plan designed for easy everyday living and effortless entertaining. The spacious living room, complete with a cozy fireplace, flows seamlessly into a gorgeous white kitchen featuring a center island and an adjoining dining room with a dramatic vaulted ceiling. The thoughtfully designed layout offers a wonderful sense of openness while maintaining a warm and welcoming atmosphere. The bedroom wing has a primary suite plus two additional bedrooms and an updated hall full bath. Step outside to a spacious deck overlooking the pretty backyard, an ideal setting for outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply relaxing and enjoying the surroundings. A newly paved driveway leads to the convenient under-house garage. Ideally located close to schools, houses of worship, commuter lines and Stamford's vibrant downtown with its wonderful restaurants, shopping, and entertainment. A delightful opportunity to enjoy easy one-level living in a desirable Stamford setting!
