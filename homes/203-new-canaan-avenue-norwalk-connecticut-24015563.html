About this home

Welcome to this charming ranch in desirable Silvermine location. This delightful residence boasts 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, and comfortable living space. Inside, you'll find hardwood floors throughout, adding warmth and character to the home. Enjoy fires in the cozy living room that open up to the dining room. Off of the dinning room you will find a great luminous sun room. Multiple Sliders from the sunroom lead to large private level back yard, ideal for entertaining and outdoor activities. This house sits on over a half of acre. Attached garage. Expansion possibilities. Large unfished basement provides plenty of storage. Conveniently located on the New Canaan border. Minutes to Merritt, I95, shops and restaurants. Don't miss out on this great opportunity!
