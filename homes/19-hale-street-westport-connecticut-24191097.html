About this home

Nestled near the beach, this inviting apartment offers a relaxed lifestyle with easy access to local amenities and coastal attractions. Move right into this furnished haven. Newer maple floors in the kitchen, thoughtful storage space, and mini-spilt unit installed in 2022. Modern, thoughtful touches throughout. The kitchen showcases a propane stove, granite counters and ceiling fan. Private entrance through an external staircase. Designated storage space in the home's shared basement, where the shared washer & dryer are located. Enjoy private use of the deck along with a shared backyard and additional storage beneath the deck. Owner will keep Compo Beach Kayak rack membership, inquire for further details! Fixed utilities make life effortless. Very-walkable location: just over a mile to the Westport train station, the vibrant restaurants of Saugatuck and Longshore. Meet friends for an Americano at the amazing Old Mill Market. Enjoy a delicious dinner at the club overlooking the river. The perfect place awaits at 19 Hale Street!
