About this home

This graceful 1920s colonial with large windows, high ceilings and wood floors throughout has been completely renovated for modern living. Enjoy the new kitchen open to dining area with flow into the living room, and the light filled sunroom. New mudroom and 1/2 bath make a great back entrance. Upstairs the primary bedroom with en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet is complemented by two large bedrooms with a new jack and jill bath. The lower level boasts a dedicated office space, new laundry area and work out room that can be converted back to garage if preferred. A new outdoor patio surrounded by lush landscaping area makes entertaining a breeze. Walk to Woodland Park.
