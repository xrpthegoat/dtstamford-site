About this home

Welcome to Blau House, a stunning mid-century modern masterpiece nestled in the tranquil Coleytown neighborhood of Westport. This architectural gem, located at 8 Bayberry Ridge, provides a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the essence of sophisticated living with 3,300 square feet of exquisite space. The open-concept Kitchen/Living Room, with its charming, heavy timber beamed ceiling, forms the heart of the home. Expansive windows seamlessly connect the indoors with the spectacular gardens (maintained by owner) creating a serene and picturesque setting. Indulge in the luxury of a Great Room with soaring beamed Cathedral ceilings and unwind in the solarium or take a refreshing dip in the indoor pool-ideal for year-round relaxation, fitness, and entertaining. The modernized Kitchen, equipped with high-end appliances, caters to both casual cooks and culinary enthusiasts alike. Relax in the spacious Screen Porch, surrounded by lush nature, or retreat to one of the three tranquil bedrooms, each with stunning garden views. The finished Lower Level features a Sauna, mirrored wall for dance or exercise, and a quiet Patio space, perfect for unwinding or enjoying private moments.Blau House is a sanctuary of peace and elegance. This rental is perfect for those who cherish the enduring allure of mid-century design, the warmth of natural materials, and a seamless blend of indoor and outdoor living. $167/month in addition to rent for landscaping and snow plowing.
