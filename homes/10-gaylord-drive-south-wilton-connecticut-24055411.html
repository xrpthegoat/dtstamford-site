About this home

Charming, private retreat in the heart of Wilton! Discover this sprawling ranch, offering convenient single-level living tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac. The home sits on 1.1 acres bordered by conservation land, with direct access to the scenic NRVT trail from your backyard! Minutes from town, train stations, and highways, this home is perfect for commuters. Inside you'll find an updated kitchen with an oversized island, a wood burning stove, and sliding glass doors leading to an expansive deck. The kitchen blends seamlessly into the dining and living areas, all flooded with natural light. Pristine hardwood floors, a cozy wood burning fireplace, and custom built-ins add warmth and elegance to the main level. The fully finished 1,400 sq ft lower level offers versatile additional living space ready for entertaining, complete with a built-in bar, full bathroom, laundry, and sliding doors leading outside. This home boasts a flat, private backyard ideal for outdoor enthusiasts and nature lovers! It features a large vegetable garden, two sheds, and a fire pit surrounded by deep woods and natural stone walls. Don't miss your opportunity to rent in one of Wilton's most desirable locations!
