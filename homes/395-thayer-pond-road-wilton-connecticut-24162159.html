About this home

A quiet country lane near the Lake Club (pool lake and tennis club) introduces this lovely Colonial set back on a beautiful, level property. The desirable open floor plan encompassing a warm and inviting kitchen and family room w/ a stone fireplace encourages lingering and relaxation. The gracious living room also with a fireplace and an expansive dining room are perfect for more formal entertaining. A large bonus room, 1st floor office, laundry and mudroom complete this level. The master suite enjoys a full bath and walk-in closet. Down the hall are three additional bedrooms and large hall bath. Finished walk-out lower level. Convenient location.
