About this home

Welcome to 27 Owenoke Park, a timeless direct waterfront retreat, offering sweeping views where history, nature and Long Island Sound converge. Tucked away from NYC's hustle, yet close enough to commute, Owenoke Park has always been one of Westport's most coveted neighborhoods. Set directly on the Long Island Sound shoreline with over 80' of water frontage, including private access to the tidal beach for boating and swimming, this New England Coastal Gambrel style home offers front-row seats to mesmerizing panoramic sunrises and sunsets, Compo Beach fireworks and beautiful existing gardens. Year-round, you'll marvel at the magnificent sights of birds soaring, fish jumping, and sailors and boaters gliding across the water. Take a stroll to Westport's Compo Beach or Marina to board your yacht at the dock, or paddle around Westport's Longshore Country Club for golf, tennis or sailing lessons. Nestled in a welcoming and picturesque community, you'll enjoy access to top-ranked schools and Westport's vibrant downtown, known for its exceptional dining, shopping and cultural experiences. 27 Owenoke Park isn't just a home-it's a lifestyle defined by natural beauty, a tight-knit community and the endless allure of Long Island Sound. Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a piece of Owenoke Park's legacy.
