About this home

PLEASE SUBMIT ALL OFFERS BY MONDAY 6/1 12pm. You will fall in love the moment you pull up to this picture-perfect, sun-filled Cape featuring the most charming teal blue front door. Completely renovated in 2017, this 4-bed, 2 full bath home blends warmth, style, & functionality in all the right ways. The inviting living room features a beautiful fireplace & sits adjacent to a flexible first floor bedroom ideal as a guest room, office, or playroom. The spacious open kitchen offers abundant storage, stainless steel appliances, a large farmhouse sink, center island, & flows seamlessly into the dining area w/ bay window & access to the backyard patio. Just off the kitchen is a fantastic mudroom w/ exterior access along w/ a beautifully updated full bath. Upstairs, the lovely primary suite features vaulted ceilings & a walk-in closet, accompanied by two additional bedrooms, a laundry room, & an oversized bath complete w/ double sink vanity, soaking tub, & large shower. The fully fenced, level backyard is truly exceptional w/ a large flagstone patio, terraced garden area w/ stone wall, & endless room for entertaining, gardening, pets, & play. The insulated & heated garage is currently used as a gym but can easily convert back to traditional garage space. Hardwood floors throughout, central air, natural gas, and an unbeatable Springdale location close to Sleepy Hollow Park, train, buses, Twin Rinks, shopping, restaurants, and more. This one is not to be missed!
