About this home

This glowing waterfront townhouse is a perfect sanctuary complete w/ 3 waterfront outdoor living spaces & the ability to launch a kayak from your own back yard! Whats better is that you are only steps to the DELAMAR Hotel, MetroNorth & all that downtown living offers (dining, shopping, parks & more). Inside you will enjoy tall ceilings, abundant natural light, top of the line fixtures & finishes including an elevator to whisk you from underground parking to an impressive rooftop deck w/ water views. The living/dining room features views, views & more views in addition to a gas fireplace, architectural detail & wonderful proportions . The kitchen is equipped for a serious cook looking to spread out & prepare a special meal. You will love the professional Wolf stove, Sub Zero refrigerator & near endless storage. The second level offers a waterfront Primary bedroom suite w/ home office as well as a luxurious bathroom & closet. A guest bedroom w/ luxurious en-suite bath & spacious closet finishes the second level. The top floor, equipped w/wet bar is ready to house guests or function as entertaining space for memorable gatherings - 4th of July fireworks? Town Party after party? Holidays? This is one of a kind! Pets may be considered on case by case basis. The lower level features a 16 by 13 workspace for hobby activities or potential a home gym plus a 3/4 bathroom, storage & access to the covered parking. Do not miss the virtual tour link for a video showing. Come see!
