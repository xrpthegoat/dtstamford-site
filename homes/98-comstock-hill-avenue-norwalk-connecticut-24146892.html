About this home

Set on an incredible 1.45 acres of flat, private land in the heart of the historic Silvermine community, 98 Comstock Hill Avenue offers a rare blend of space, character, and thoughtful updates. The home opens to a generous living room with a fireplace and a separate dining area, creating a welcoming flow for both everyday living and entertaining. From there, the beautifully updated kitchen features a mix of butcher block and quartz countertops, a breakfast bar, and a modern pantry with a wine cooler, opening to a cozy den anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. Adjacent to the kitchen is a light-filled space currently used as an art gallery, where French doors lead to a screened-in porch overlooking the expansive grounds. The main level also includes a dedicated office and a newly updated full bath. Upstairs, the primary bedroom impresses with soaring ceilings, exposed beams, and walk-in closet space, while two additional bedrooms offer flexibility for guests, work, or creative use. The upper-level full bath features Carrera marble tile, high-end Bender dual vanities, and a modern shower stall with a rainfall showerhead. A large basement provides abundant storage, and outside, a detached two-car garage, long private driveway, outdoor fireplace with grill, and open acreage invite endless possibilities. Enjoy modern comforts and room to expand in historic Silvermine, near Silvermine Farm & Market and Gray Barns-all in a setting that feels both private and connected.
