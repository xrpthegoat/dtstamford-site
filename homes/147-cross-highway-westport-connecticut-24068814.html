About this home

Nestled on an extraordinary 1-acre property close to town & walking distance to schools, the observatory, & Wakeman Park, this one-of-a-kind home offers a seamless blend of modern construction with timeless charm; function with character. REBUILT (& addition) IN 2000 & meticulously maintained. 6,000+ square feet of well-designed living space including 5 BR, 4-full & 2-half BTHS, & built-ins throughout. Stepping into the foyer you'll notice impeccable details. The LR boasts wide-planked floors, striking wood beams, a gas fireplace & light from nine windows. The elegant DR, anchored by a stunning oversized fireplace, promises conviviality. The open-plan kitchen features a dedicated dining nook, breakfast counter, built-in desk, & a brick oven! Adjacent: a warm & welcoming family room with a fireplace & sweeping views of the lush grounds. A tiled mudroom connects with a laundry room, a 3-car garage, & a giant bonus room, ideal for games night or a dedicated kids' lounge. A walk-out LL with Italian tile would serve as a terrific media room or gym. Up the front staircase, the primary suite offers true retreat, with vaulted ceilings, cozy fireplace, 2 walk-in closets, loft, and stunning bath. 4 spacious BRs provide privacy and comfort, with 2 sharing a Jack-Jill BTH & 2 featuring en-suite BTH. One BR stands out with beams & vintage chestnut floors. Perfectly sited on flat, expansive grounds with room for a pool, this home perfectly blends character, convenience, & timeless appeal!
