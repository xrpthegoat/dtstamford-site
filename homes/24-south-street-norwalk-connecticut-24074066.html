About this home

This Colonial style three-bedroom, three-bathroom home sits on approximately 0.14 acres of land with 1,680 square feet of living space. The primary bedroom features ample space for a king-size bed with a sizable closet and an en-suite bathroom. The other two bedrooms are well-proportioned with space for queen-size beds and additional furnishings. The main bathroom is conveniently located near the secondary bedrooms. The floor plan checks all boxes with a half bath and spacious living room and dining room providing a versatile space for relaxation and entertaining. The kitchen is equipped with essential appliances and offers access to the deck perched high over the surrounding houses. The property features a private outdoor area with potential for recreational use. A new roof was just installed on the home, and the basement has high ceilings and a walk out to the back yard perfectly situated to be finished.
