About this home

A rare find! The curb appeal of this grand victorian in Bettswood begins with the oversized front porch - a perfect spot to relax and enjoy the view of the other beautiful homes on this dead end street. The detailed craftsmanship throughout begins in the large foyer with extensive millwork. Formal living room and dining room each with a fireplace. The remodeled eat-in-kitchen features quartz counter tops, a center island with breafast bar, a six burner gas stove and wine cooler. Half bath completes the main floor. Second floor offers primary suite has a remodeled bathroom with both a large stall shower and soaking tub. Primary bedroom also has a walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms, an office and hall bathroom complete the second floor. Large finished 3rd floor is a perfect family room and also offers a large bedroom and full bath. Hardwood floors and high celings throughout all three floors of this special home. Private level yard with patio and shed. Dry unfinished basement provides ample storage. Conveniently located to schools, highway, shopping & Metro North.
