About this home

Immaculate modern bright and sunny LUXRY home with open floor plan inside and amazingly revamped Front & Backyard, ideal for entertaining in the Newfield area. This luxurious and spacious 3 Bedrooms and 3 full bath Raised ranch style home with finished basement has everything. Tastefully Renovated Kitchen and bathrooms, new Central AC, gas heat, City water and public sewer. It is perfect for first time home buyer or a professional couple. The main level has 3 bedrooms, E-I-K, spacious living room that brings in a lot of light and charm. Dining room with sliders to walk out on the deck overlooking the backyard. Downstairs, the cozy family room can be used as a 4th Bedroom with full bath, fireplace, and laundry Room. Family room has additional sliders leading to a wonderful back yard with charming covered patio, New above ground swimming pool, New Shed and much more. With a large two-car garage and mature landscaping, this property combines beauty and functionality. Conveniently located across from a park and close to amenities. Easy commute to the Merritt, train station, shopping and restaurants.
