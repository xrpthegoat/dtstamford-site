About this home

Charming Three-Bedroom Cape in Prime East Norwalk Location Welcome to this inviting Cape Cod-style home nestled in the heart of East Norwalk. Conveniently located a short distance to the East Norwalk Train Station, and just minutes from Calf Pasture Beach, local parks with playgrounds, ball fields, and tennis courts. Enjoy easy access to a variety of nearby restaurants and major roadways. Inside, you'll find a cozy living room with hardwood floors and a fireplace, and two main-level bedrooms (one currently used as a dining room), all featuring hardwood floors. The eat-in kitchen offers a comfortable space for everyday meals. The second floor includes a third bedroom with an adjoining sitting room or study, wall-to-wall carpeting, and useful storage.
