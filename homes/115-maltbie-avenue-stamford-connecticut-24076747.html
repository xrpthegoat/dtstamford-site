About this home

Experience the allure of 115 Maltbie Avenue-a renovated riverfront ranch featuring 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, all set on a nearly 1-acre flat lot. With 2,448 square feet of expertly designed living space, this home seamlessly blends modern comforts with timeless charm. Inside, you'll find a renovated eat-in kitchen with vaulted ceilings, black stainless appliances, a farm sink and views of the Rippowam River. The kitchen is seamlessly connected to the living room which also sports vaulted ceilings, as well as hardwood floors, skylights, and a welcoming central fireplace. Adjacent to the living area, the riverfront dining space provides breathtaking views and direct access to an outdoor deck perfect for relaxing by the water. One of the 3 bedrooms in this home is riverfront with wrap-around views of nature. The remaining two bedrooms can both be considered "Primary Bedrooms", both of ample size, with spacious closets, and access to spa-like bathrooms. Additional highlights include a practical mudroom with soaring ceilings that connects to a well-equipped laundry room featuring an oversized utility sink and workbench. Modern upgrades, such as a new propane furnace + tankless water heater, an upgraded 200 amp panel, and a connection to the city sewer, bring this timeless home up to today's standards. This home is conveniently located in the Mid-Ridges neighborhood of Stamford. Just a short drive to exit 34 on the Merritt Parkway, and minutes to Whole Foods and downtown Stamford
