About this home

Experience the perfect blend of privacy and convenience on a quiet dead-end street in the heart of Stamford. This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath home is an entertainer's dream. A warm and inviting living room with fireplace. The eat-in kitchen boasts granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. A spacious family room and deck overlook a lush, landscaped yard - your own private oasis ideal for entertaining or relaxing by the in-ground pool. A first-floor office provides an ideal work from home setup and leads to a breezeway connected to a heated and air-conditioned garage, which gives endless possibilities for additional living space. Enjoy movie nights in the lower-level media room, complete with surround sound. Additional features include beautiful hardwood floors throughout, 200 Amp electrical service, new pool equipment, a one-year-old pool heater, a new Aqua Booster and a recently updated oil tank.
