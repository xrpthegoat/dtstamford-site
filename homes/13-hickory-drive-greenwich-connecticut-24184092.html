About this home

This stunning 4 bedroom home offers an exceptional blend of elegance, comfort, and beautiful design. Featuring gorgeous finishes throughout and a fantastic floor plan, it is perfectly suited for both everyday living, and entertaining. The chef's Kitchen serves as the heart of the home, showcasing lots of cabinetry and counterspace, quality craftsmanship, and a vaulted ceiling with beams that add character and an architectural twist. The main level office/den is very comfortable with vaulted ceiling and lots of windows for natural light. A recent renovation has transformed this beautiful home into an expanded Cape by seamlessly incorporating the walk-up attic into an amazing 4th bedroom, Rec Room or guest quarters, complete with gorgeous full bathroom, added storage and customized walk-in-closet. The lower level provides outstanding expansion potential, offering flexibility for future living space, recreation, fitness, and mudroom. Outside, enjoy a private outdoor retreat with beautiful stone walls and mature trees. The patio and firepit area create the perfect setting for relaxing evenings and gatherings with family and friends, while landscape lighting enhances the property's ambiance after sunset. The stone facade and paver driveway add a unique flair to the curb appeal. Ideally located, this exceptional home is just minutes from town, train, parks, shopping, and all the conveniences the area has to offer.
