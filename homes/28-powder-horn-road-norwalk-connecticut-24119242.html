About this home

Gracious and full of charm, this High Ranch offers a prime location near the Darien border and minutes to New Canaan. Perfect for entertaining or unwinding, the outdoor space features a pool and a generous yard at the end of a quiet, private cul-de-sac. Inside, enjoy abundant natural light, hardwood floors throughout, fresh interior and exterior paint, and newer windows and doors-ready for your personal touch. The versatile ground level is ideal for an in-law suite or guest accommodations. Convenient to shopping, top restaurants, and local beaches, with easy access to the Darien train station, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway for a smooth NYC commute.
