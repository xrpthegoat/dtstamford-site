About this home

OWNER REQUESTING BEST/FINAL OFFERS BY 5 PM SUNDAY. Postcard-pretty 5-bedroom home on level park-like acre in sought after South Wilton neighborhood, with many recent updates and great layout. "Right-sized" formal rooms and renovated country kitchen/family room with french doors to large stone patio and outdoor entertaining area. Serene primary suite, with three other ample bedrooms in main upper level. Separate studio off the back stair. Recently-finished lower level playroom and office, large mudroom/laundry off kitchen with entry from covered side porch too. Newer mechanicals, custom built-ins and stainless steel appliances, great commutability, city water, and nearby access to neighborhood cul de sac make this one your winner.
