About this home

A perfect harmony of traditional style and fresh contemporary living in this sun filled and beautifully renovated classic Cape Cod. Nestled in a lovely neighborhood on a no-through street and set on a corner lot, this home offers so many possibilities! Inside, discover quality craftsmanship and timeless finishes including beautifully refinished hardwood floors, a custom kitchen featuring solid wood cabinetry, quartz countertops, Samsung stainless-steel appliances, plus two completely redesigned bathrooms. The practical upgrades making life almost care free are a new roof, windows, garage door, oil tank, water heater, duct work and a 2021 furnace. Smart Wi-Fi-controlled A/C units provide comfort, efficiency and saves money w/remote access. A finished enclosed porch invites relaxed mornings and cozy evenings, while the finished walk-out lower level offers flexible space for a playroom/office/workout space and/or media room. The property is enhanced by new landscaping with lush lawn, shrubs, and flowering gardens. A freshly paved driveway accommodating up to six vehicles and an attached garage provide plenty of room for everyone and everything. The Norwalk River Valley Trail is only .3 mile away, offering endless opportunities for outdoor fun and exploration for fitness enthusiasts, nature lovers, and anyone seeking an active outdoor lifestyle. This location is sure to invite daily walks, invigorating runs, and peaceful moments in nature.
