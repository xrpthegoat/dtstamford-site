About this home

Welcome to this large 2-bedroom, 2 bath home located in the sought-after Ledgebrook condominium complex. As you enter, the spacious foyer leads you into an open concept living and dinning area. The sliding doors at the end lead you to the beautiful covered deck that can be enjoyed in all seasons. The kitchen has newer stainless steel appliances and a barn door between the kitchen and dinning area. The primary suite has a full bath, walk in closet and an extra storage area off of the closet. The second bedroom features an additional loft area that can be used as an office or gym. The unit comes with a 1 car covered garage and two additional permitted parking spots. New HVAC installed in Aug 2025. New Washer dryer and a barn door separating the kitchen and dining area was installed in 2024. HOA includes Snow removal, Trash removal, Water, Pest control, Grounds maintenance, Pool service and Road maintenance.The complex features amenities such as an in-ground pool, clubhouse, fitness center, library, sauna, and hot tub. Conveniently located near I-95, Route 1, and the Merritt Parkway and Sono.
