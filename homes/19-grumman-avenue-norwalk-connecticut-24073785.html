About this home

Charm abounds in this antique farmhouse located in Norwalk's sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Built in 1840, this two bedroom colonial has been fully updated for today's modern buyers. The newly renovated kitchen with stainless steel appliances, gas range, and a farmhouse sink opens to a bright and airy dining room. Custom-built cabinetry serves as a "mudroom" by the door and makes coming inside during inclement weather seamless, and without tracking in the elements. The living room has a stylish slate fireplace and is an ideal space to relax or entertain guests. Tucked off this room is the first-floor bedroom. Currently being used as an office, this room is a quiet and peaceful space with a beautiful view of the spacious backyard. Upstairs is the primary bedroom suite complete with a bright, modern bathroom and roomy walk-in closet. The basement has tons of room for storage and showcases the home's original craftsmanship, including its antique log construction. The outdoor space is truly magical. The gently sloping lot features stone walls, a babbling brook, and a large vegetable garden. Be on the lookout for monarch butterflies too as they are attracted to the native plantings in the pollinator-friendly garden. There is even a charging station so you can charge your Tesla or EV. The location is great too; you'll be minutes away from Cranbury Park and shopping and just a short drive to Merritt Parkway. Own a piece of Norwalk history in this one-of-a-kind home!
