About this home

Step into sophisticated city living with this beautifully appointed 2BR 2BA residence in the desirable Hayes House, one of Stamford's most distinguished condo buildings. The home welcomes you with expansive open-concept living and dining areas, where oversized windows and sliders frame breathtaking western sunset views. A spacious private balcony extends your living space, offering the perfect setting for entertaining or unwinding. The remodeled kitchen is outfitted with brand new stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and rich cabinetry, seamlessly blending style and function. Throughout the home, timelessly refinished parquet flooring, elegant crown molding, and abundant storage closets create a warm, refined atmosphere. The primary suite boasts a walk-in closet and ensuite bath, while the generous second bedroom provides flexibility for guests or a home office. Hayes House residents enjoy a newly redesigned marble lobby with concierge, a fitness center, community room, garage parking with abundant guest spaces, private storage, and 24-hour security. With heat, hot water, and cooling included in the HOA, residents enjoy both comfort and convenience in every season. Ideally situated in the heart of Stamford, this residence is just moments from dining, shopping, nightlife, and Metro North, with easy access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. A rare opportunity to pair elevated design with unmatched convenience.
