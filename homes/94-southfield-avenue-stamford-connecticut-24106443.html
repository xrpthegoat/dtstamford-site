About this home

Be prepared to be wowed by this spectacular fully renovated designer 1 bedroom end unit w/loft down by the water in Stamford. Natural light pours into the fully open concept living space w/soaring 2 story vaulted ceilings and palladian windows. Modern gourmet kitchen with granite counters, marble backsplash, & a large peninsula that overlooks the dining and living room w/wood burning fireplace. Spacious bedroom with oversized walk-in closet and en-suite bath. Bright and airy loft w/skylights can serve as a guest room, office, or den. Privately situated towards the back of the complex, this end unit condo also has a deck w/views of the harbor! There is a 1 car attached garage along with additional reserved owner parking spaces, and a generous visitor parking lot at the entrance of the complex. Don't miss the pool and jacuzzi overlooking the manicured courtyard and harbor! Minutes to Old Greenwich, Stamford train station, and a short water-taxi ride across the marina to restaurants and parks at Harbor Point. Walk down the boardwalk to Prime Steakhouse or The Crabshell for al fresco dining along the marina. Your permanent vacation home awaits!
