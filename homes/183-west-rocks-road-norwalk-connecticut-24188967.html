About this home

Exceptional Value on 1.5 acres with potential of over 3,200 sf of living space! This 5-bedroom, 2-full-bath home is located in the highly sought-after West Rocks neighborhood of Norwalk, offering privacy and peace of mind. Recent improvements include a 200-amp electrical service, whole-house Generac generator, tankless water heater, new 500-gallon propane tank, dual zone heating and central AC hydro air system, Connecticut Basement Systems waterproofing and flood prevention system with a transferable lifetime warranty. Too many improvements to list! One of the home's most exciting features is the expansive lower level, offering more than 1,200 square feet of unfinished space with excellent ceiling height and endless possibilities. Whether you envision a family room, home office, gym, media room, guest suite, or in-law setup, this level provides a tremendous opportunity to significantly increase the home's finished living area and overall value. A rare opportunity to own a well-maintained home with room to grow in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
