About this home

Nestled in Stamford's highly sought-after Pepper Ridge neighborhood, 122 Berrian Road offers the perfect blend of timeless charm, thoughtful updates, and exceptional outdoor living. Set on a beautifully landscaped .65 acre property in one of the city's most picturesque residential enclaves, this move-in-ready home has been extensively improved by its current owners. The house is being completely painted. Recent upgrades include a newer roof, central air conditioning, newer windows, gas fired hotwater heater, refinished hardwood floors, updated masonry and landscaping, garage doors w wall-mounted motors, renovated hall bath, refreshed primary bath with re-glazed tile, updated fixtures, and newer lighting fixtures. The beautifully remodeled kitchen features quartz countertops, a pot filler, expanded cabinetry, and newer appliances. The screened porch has been completely restored with new paint, new screens and treatments. A spacious patio addition creates the ideal setting for outdoor entertaining and relaxation. Pepper Ridge is beloved for its tree-lined streets, rolling landscapes, and welcoming neighborhood atmosphere. Residents enjoy convenient access to Stamford's vibrant downtown, houses of worship, schools, Metro-North, parks, shopping, dining, and top recreational amenities, while still enjoying the tranquility of a classic suburban setting.
