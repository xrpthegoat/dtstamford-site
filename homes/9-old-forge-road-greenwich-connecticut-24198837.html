About this home

Privately set on two magnificent acres at the end of a quiet Mid-Country cul-de-sac, this beautifully updated four-bedroom residence offers an exceptional blend of understated elegance, comfort and tranquility. Sun-filled interiors feature newly refinished hardwood floors and gracious living spaces, including an inviting eat-in kitchen with premium appliances and a dramatic family room with vaulted ceiling, fireplace and custom built-ins. The expansive primary suite is a serene retreat, while three additional bedrooms offer generous accommodations for family and guests. Designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living, French doors open to a fabulous cedar deck and impressive three-season screened porch overlooking sweeping, level lawns. Mature specimen trees, established plantings and natural rock outcroppings create a remarkably private, park-like setting. A spacious finished lower level provides versatile recreation, office and fitness spaces, plus a cedar closet. A rare Mid-Country offering where privacy, beautiful grounds and relaxed sophistication come together just minutes from the best of Greenwich.
