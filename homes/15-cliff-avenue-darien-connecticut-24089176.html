About this home

This stunning, fully updated four-bedroom Colonial seamlessly blends modern luxury with timeless charm. The main level, with 9 ft ceilings, boasts multiple living spaces, including a formal living room with gas fireplace, a cozy family room with wood-burning fireplace, and a spacious dining room-perfect for entertaining. The eat-in kitchen features radiant heat flooring and ample storage.Upstairs, the primary suite offers a fully renovated spa-like bath with radiant heat flooring and a custom-designed oversized walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms include renovated en-suite baths with radiant heat and custom closets. A newly converted walk-up attic serves as a flexible space for a playroom, office, or gym. The lower level features a mudroom, a climate-controlled 1,000-bottle wine cellar, and spray foam insulation for energy efficiency. Outside, enjoy a new stone patio, a full irrigation system, and professionally landscaped grounds with evergreen privacy plantings. Conveniently located in the highly desirable Tokeneke Elementary School district, near Rowayton village and Darien town center. Short walk to the Rowayton train station. This exceptional home is not to be missed! Finished third floor not reflected on field card.
