About this home

Experience refined living in this beautifully renovated townhouse-style condominium, ideally situated in the heart of New Canaan. Offering four bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a finished third floor, this exceptional residence combines timeless elegance with modern comfort and effortless functionality. Beautifully renovated bathrooms, pristine hardwood floors, and an abundance of natural light throughout.
