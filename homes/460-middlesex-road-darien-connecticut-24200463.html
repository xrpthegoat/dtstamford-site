About this home

Built in 2023 and stunningly refreshed in 2026, this five-bedroom, eight-bath home is set on two landscaped acres framed by handmade stone walls. Soaring 9' to 12' ceilings, oversized simulated divided light windows and Pella doors enhance the light-filled interiors. The open living and breakfast areas center around a spectacular eat-in chef's kitchen with high end appliances and a waterfall-edge marble island and a formal dining room with butler's pantry. A double-height vaulted great room features a wood-burning fireplace, wet bar and French doors out to the Gunite pool with auto cover. The first floor also offers an office with powder room, a sunroom/pool cabana room and en-suite bedroom with exterior entrance. Upstairs, a luxurious primary suite includes a cathedral ceiling, balcony, four closets and spa bath with dual showers, plus three additional en-suite bedrooms with walk-in closets, a homework room, sitting area and a playroom / game room. The beautifully finished lower level is complete with a theater, a bar, a mirrored gym, full bath, a sauna and a steam room. 3 car oversized garage. Generator. This stunning build by Ritzzo Development has everything!
