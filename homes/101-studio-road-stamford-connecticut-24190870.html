About this home

Spacious and beautifully updated, this move-in-ready ranch offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and future potential. From the moment you enter, the oversized foyer with stunning gray Foussana stone flooring sets the tone for the thoughtful updates throughout the home. The open-concept floor plan flows effortlessly from room to room, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Rich hardwood floors extend throughout the main living spaces, while the inviting living room features a cozy wood-burning fireplace with a newly renovated onyx stone surround, creating a warm and sophisticated focal point. The beautifully updated kitchen, complete with a walk-in pantry, opens seamlessly to the spacious eat-in dining area and family room, creating the perfect gathering space for meals, entertaining, and everyday life. Dramatic black-painted French doors separate three of the four bedrooms, creating a private and peaceful bedroom wing. The home offers four generously sized bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a chic renovated powder room featuring a striking wall-to-wall Belvedere stone vanity. Meticulous attention to detail is evident throughout, with thoughtfully curated designer lighting, including fixtures from Visual Comfort and other premium design sources, many on dimmers, along with sleek screwless switch plates and outlets for a polished contemporary finish.
