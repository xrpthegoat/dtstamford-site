About this home

A rare opportunity to live in the exclusive Wilson Point Association, this stately Georgian Colonial is privately sited on 1.4 acres with sweeping views of Long Island Sound. A true secluded oasis, the home features a sophisticated open floor plan, vaulted ceilings, light-filled rooms, and exceptional custom millwork and architectural details throughout. The luxurious primary suite offers a fireplace, spa-like bath, and private terrace overlooking the water. An impressive pool house with a full kitchen extends the home's entertaining and living space, while the expansive flagstone patio, pool, and spa create an exceptional setting for relaxed outdoor living. Residents of Wilson Point enjoy access to a private beach, clubhouse, and tennis court. A truly special year-round retreat, offering privacy, resort-like amenities, and an idyllic waterfront setting-all just an hour from New York City.
