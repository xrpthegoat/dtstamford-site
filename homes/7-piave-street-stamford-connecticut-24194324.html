About this home

Welcome to this well-maintained 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse offering the perfect blend of comfort, space, and convenience in Stamford. With a thoughtfully designed multi-level layout, this home provides the privacy and feel of single-family living while remaining close to everything the city has to offer. The bright and inviting living areas create a warm and welcoming atmosphere, while the spacious bedrooms provide a comfortable retreat at the end of the day.
