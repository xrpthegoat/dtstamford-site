About this home

Impeccably maintained 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath end-unit townhouse offering approximately 2,400 sq ft of refined living space. Rich custom moldings, wainscoting, and built-ins add timeless character throughout. When entering the home, one is greeted by an inviting living room which features a wood-burning fireplace and an elegant bay window. Venturing further inside, the gourmet kitchen showcases granite countertops, double ovens, a wine cooler, and custom cabinetry, with sliding doors opening to a private deck with an electric awning-ideal for outdoor enjoyment. A striking open staircase leads to a beautifully finished lower level with a spacious family room, full bath, and ample storage, plus walk-out access to a serene seating area and landscaped common grounds. On the upper floor, the expansive primary suite is highlighted by a dramatic arched window and a luxurious custom bath with separate stall shower and large whirlpool bathtub. Two additional bedrooms and an additional full bath complete the upper level. There are gleaming hardwood floors that extend throughout. This home has the feel and space of a single-family home, with the peace of mind that comes with Condo living. Many restaurants, movie theaters, and the Sono Collection for shopping are just minutes away.
