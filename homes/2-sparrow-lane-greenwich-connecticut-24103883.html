About this home

Redesigned in 2019 by renowned architect Doug Vanderhorn, this 6-bedroom, 6.5-bathroom home blends exceptional craftsmanship with natural light throughout. The main level features formal living and dining rooms, a private office, and an open-concept kitchen and family room, all opening to a patio and level grounds with a playground. Upstairs, the second level features 5 en-suite bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with a marble-clad bathroom and a custom dressing room. The fully finished lower level offers a 6th bedroom and full bath. Pool site approved. Just minutes to Greenwich Avenue, schools, trains and available for you to move before the school year starts!
