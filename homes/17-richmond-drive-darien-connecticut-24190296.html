About this home

Highest and Best offers are due Monday, July 13th at 12:00 PM. Welcome to your perfect suburban retreat! Thoughtfully renovated throughout, this four-bedroom, three-bathroom home seamlessly blends timeless style with everyday comfort. Inside, you'll find bright, inviting living spaces, a formal dining room, and a beautifully renovated eat-in kitchen with breakfast nook that opens seamlessly to the backyard. Upstairs, the new spacious primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet, office and a gorgeous en-suite bath. Two additional bedrooms, a recently renovated bathroom and a conveniently located laundry room complete the second level. Set on .65 beautifully landscaped acres, the fully fenced backyard is perfect for relaxing or entertaining, with a detached studio that's perfect for a home office or gym, fire pit, large playset, and storage shed for added versatility. Ideally located just minutes from downtown Darien, the train, beaches, parks, and major commuter routes, this turnkey home offers the perfect balance of convenience, privacy, and exceptional indoor-outdoor living.
