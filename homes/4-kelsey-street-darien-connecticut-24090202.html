About this home

A Commuter's Delight! Beginning from the lower level & up, this terrific new listing is as close to brand new as you can get! The unique floor plan offers great flexibility w/open entertaining space on the main level in addition to a bonus den/office/potential 5th BR & full bathroom. The 2nd floor delivers a stylish Primary bedroom suite w/plentiful closet space & a luxurious en-suite bathroom. Also featured on the 2nd floor is a sun-drenched rear facing guest bedroom, hall bathroom & front facing guest bedroom w/surprise bonus office/gameroom/nursery. The 3rd floor offers a spacious 4th bedroom, possible alternate master bedroom, w/en-suite full bath & abundant storage. In the lower level you'll find a terrific recreation space, a laundry center & plenty of storage. Benefit from efficient living w/energy saving closed cell foam insulation, insulated Andersen windows, natural gas sourced heat, LED lighting, brand new appliances & more. Modern conveniences include integrated Wi-Fi, cable, security systems and generator ready hookup if needed. The yard is easy to care for and shows as it features a bluestone patio, large driveway & circular parking area. Located on a private dead-end street while being only steps to Noroton Heights train station & new shopping/dining district! This home is move-in ready and will provide years of ease & comfort!
