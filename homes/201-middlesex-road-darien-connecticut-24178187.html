About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac setting just off a private road, 201 Middlesex Road offers a rare combination of turnkey luxury, convenience, and timeless family living in one of Darien's most sought-after neighborhoods. Extensively renovated and beautifully maintained, this 4-bedroom home is ideally located close to town, Metro North, shopping, parks, and Darien's award-winning schools, including highly coveted Ox Ridge Elementary. Designed for both modern living and effortless entertaining, the home features sun-filled interiors, an open layout, and gracious entertaining spaces. The chef's kitchen serves as the heart of the home with a stunning Carrera marble waterfall island, custom cabinetry, Viking appliances, granite countertops, and French doors opening to the private backyard. The inviting family room features a wood-burning stone fireplace, custom wine bar, and oversized gathering space overlooking serene pond and fountain views. A built-in sound system and alarm system provide additional comfort and convenience. The property offers exceptional year-round enjoyment, from backyard games, tree swings, and firefly-filled summer evenings to winter ice skating during colder months, all within the warmth of a quiet, close-knit neighborhood setting. Just minutes from Darien Commons, Noroton Heights Metro North, Highland Farm, and local favorites including Palmer's Market and Raphael's Bakery, the home combines everyday convenience with a true neighborhood feel.
