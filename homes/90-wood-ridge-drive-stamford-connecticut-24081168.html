About this home

Welcome home to this beautifully updated split-level in sought-after Mid-Ridge! Enter the bright and airy living room which flows seamlessly into the dining area where sliders open to a deck and private yard, framed by charming stone walls, an ideal setting for outdoor enjoyment. The open-concept kitchen boasts quartz countertops, farmhouse sink, stainless steel appliances and breakfast bar. Just a few steps up, you'll find the primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms and a stylish hall bath with double vanity. Lower level family room offers a versatile space with newer tile flooring, half bath and easy access to the garage and yard. The basement presents expansion potential along with ample storage. Additional highlights include gleaming hardwood floors, propane heat, central air, recessed lighting and an irrigation system. Minutes to shops & vibrant downtown. This move-in ready home is not to be missed!
