About this home

Welcome to the Historic Hill Bridge House! Where style, sophistication and the utmost convenience in location is what this brand new 2 bedroom, 2 bath gorgeous unit has to offer! No detail has been left behind from the kitchen and bathrooms down to every light fixture! You get the best of both worlds of Harbor Point without having to live in a high-rise building. The open concept kitchen / living room is the heart of this unit. Imagine cooking in the kitchen while your guests chat around the center island, relaxing on your sofa with a good book while the natural light pours in, or working from home with ease in a little nook at your desk. You will swoon over both bathrooms, each with its own style. Plenty of closet space and laundry in unit complete this picture-perfect package. The icing on the cake is that this unit is completely energy efficient with its zoned heating and cooling systems. 1 reserved parking space and plenty of street parking. Pet friendly! 3/10ths of mile to train station and a quick walk to all that Harbor Point has to offer with its great restaurants, boardwalk, park and so much more! Minutes to I-95, downtown or hop on the Harbor Point trolley! This beauty is not to be missed!
