About this home

Welcome to 130 Wood Ridge in the sought after Mid Ridges in Stamford. Walk into the living room featuring an open concept and is highlighted with a beautiful brick fireplace. Off the living room is a spacious dining room for gatherings, and generous sized kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and custom cherrywood cabinets with ample storage space. The upper level includes the primary bedroom with a full ensuite, closets with built-in organizers, and hardwood floors and additional bedrooms and bath. Downstairs features a family room showcasing another fireplace with a custom mantle plus French doors opening to a slate patio. A striking rock wall adds character, and just off the family room you'll find a dedicated office with a half bath. Large laundry room and separate storage located in the lower level. Throughout the home, enjoy ductless A/C. Conveniently located in the Mid Ridges area, close to both High Ridge and Long Ridge Roads.
