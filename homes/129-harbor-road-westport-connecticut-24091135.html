About this home

Bring your Boat! DIRECT WATERFRONT LIVING at Channel Marker 16 W/ DEEP WATER DOCK and Stunning 24/7 views of Saugatuck harbor activity. Fully rebuilt home w/ sweeping water views, an open floor plan, 4700 SF total, with 4 bedrooms, 4.5 baths (3400+/- SF of living space on 2 floors and a bonus 1400 SF of large lower level consisting of mudroom, 2 garage bays and a spacious recreation FLEX-space w/ water views). Located on Saugatuck Island, a truly unique Utopia, offering beaches for residents, quiet streets, exceptional sunrises and sunsets and an over-abundance of outdoor waterfront activity! Fish off your dock, Kayak, paddle board or just sit back, relax and take in the sights of the vast assortment of wildlife, rowers and sailing regattas. The home has a coastal and casual aesthetic throughout. The Living/ Dining/Great Room and Gourmet Kitchen w/ 13' Island, all look out to the Harbor. In addition to a stellar location, the home offers: Wolf and Subzero Appliances, a Family room w/ fireplace, Master suite w/ private deck, Master bath w/ soaking tub, Separate home office, Dual Tv/Entertainment areas, outdoor shower and a very large Rec area. Enjoy all Westport has to offer in way of fine dining, boutique browsing, Arts, Music and Cultural Events. A quick and easy NYC commute. Please note: Agent is an owner.
