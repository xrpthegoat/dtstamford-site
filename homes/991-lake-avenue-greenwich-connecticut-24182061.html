About this home

A truly exceptional Greenwich estate combining privacy, sophistication in a coveted backcountry setting. Set on four secluded acres, this classic European-inspired residence offers timeless elegance and exceptional living spaces. The main level features a gourmet chef's kitchen,charming family room with fireplace, formal dining room, dramatic beamed living room with stone fireplace,and double bedroom with full bath. Upstairs are a primary suite and three additional bedrooms. French doors open to wonderful outdoor entertaining space which is adorned by a gunite pool and spa. Formal gardens and expansive grounds complete this spectacular outdoor space. The finished lower level offers a spacious game room with a stone fireplace, extensive office space, with a private entrance.
