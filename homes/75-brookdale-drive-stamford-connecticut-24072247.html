About this home

Fabulous opportunity on a desirable North Stamford cul-de-sac featuring a handsome colonial with stunning inground pool. Character and light fill this home, featuring an ideal design with sweeping views of picturesque backyard. Oversized kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, lovely backsplash, and abundance of classic cabinetry and huge pantry. The floorplan flows effortlessly-gracious large living room with beamed ceiling leads to family room with stately fireplace. And dining room right off the kitchen adds to the splendor. A captivating porch off the family room leads to a sparkling, inground pool for months of enjoyment. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts 2 closets and designer renovated full bath. 3 additional bedrooms and another renovated bath complete the second floor. Need to work from home? Want extra space for media, playroom or gym? The versatile, bright, finished lower level can accomodate all of these needs. Nestlled in one of the most sought after neighborhoods, and walking distance to the Bartlett Arboretum, nature trails & Stamford Nature Center, this pristine home is ready for you. So many improvements: repaved driveway, hardwood floors refinished, new refrigerator, first floor interior painted, newer windowos and so much more. Enjoy the peace & solitude of North Stamford while being minutes to the Merritt, shopping and restaurants. The dream of home ownership is right here-simply unpack and move right in!
