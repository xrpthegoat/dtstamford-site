About this home

Absolutely charming and situated in the heart of the waterfront village of Rowayton, this delightful plaqued antique home is available from October to May 1st! Relish the end of summer with a fire pit on one terrace and a dining area on another. The kitchen opens up to a sunlit porch, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The private landscaped yard, along with the cozy living and dining rooms, provides a warm and inviting atmosphere. Whether you're looking to launch a kayak, savor fresh seafood, dine out, participate in local community events, explore the arts center, or take a short train ride on Metro North to Manhattan, this home offers easy and convenient living. Experience the charm and lifestyle of Rowayton and this beautiful Rowayton home for yourself!
