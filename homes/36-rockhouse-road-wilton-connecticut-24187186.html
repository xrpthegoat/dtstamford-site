About this home

Please submit highest and best offers by 5pm on Wednesday, July 1st. Set on 2.71 private acres at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom center hall Colonial offers the perfect blend of privacy, space, and timeless appeal. Lovingly cared for by its original owner since 1998, this home welcomes you with a dramatic two-story foyer and a thoughtfully designed floor plan. The inviting living room features French doors to a wraparound porch, while the formal dining room is ideal for entertaining. A private home office also opens to the porch, creating the perfect work-from-home space. The sun-filled eat-in kitchen with center island and stainless steel appliances flows seamlessly into the spacious family room with a wood-burning fireplace. Upstairs, the generous primary suite offers an expansive bath and abundant closet space. Three additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a conveniently located laundry room complete the second level. The partially finished lower level provides flexible space for a recreation room, playroom, gym, or media room. Additional highlights include a whole-house generator and over 3,400 square feet of living space. Conveniently located just minutes from Wilton's award-winning schools, town center, train, and commuting routes, this special property offers the peaceful lifestyle today's buyers are seeking.
