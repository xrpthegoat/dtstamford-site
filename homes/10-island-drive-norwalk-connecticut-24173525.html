About this home

Life on the Sunset Coast. Some addresses are just addresses. This one is a lifestyle. Nestled on the coastal community of Charles Creek, this impeccably maintained ranch is the perfect "right size" home - ideal for those ready to simplify without sacrificing a thing. One level, turnkey, and move-in ready, it's an equally perfect full-time retreat or seasonal escape. Walk to Ludlow Park for tennis, pickleball, basketball, and a playground. Let the dogs run free at historic Taylor Farm Dog Park. And just down the road, Calf Pasture Beach delivers Long Island Sound coastline packed with activity all season - volleyball, sailing, kayaking, paddle boarding, outdoor movies, concerts, and the best seat in the city for 4th of July fireworks. Inside, hardwood floors run throughout. The kitchen features stone countertops, a breakfast bar, and pantry storage. The dining area walks out to a deck complete with a gourmet grill, hot tub, and landscape lighting. A cozy fireplace and built-ins anchor the family room. The spacious primary suite offers a full ensuite bath and excellent closet space. Two-car garage plus driveway parking. Outside, stunning flower gardens surround the meticulously maintained, private fenced yard. Welcome home to Island Drive.
