About this home

The best unit at Compo Beach Area's (only!) condo association, 46 Edgewater Commons is a beautiful two (ensuite) bedroom, open floorplan townhouse with fully finished lower level. It has a back deck made private with high end fencing and shade from a rollable awning. The first compelling aspect of this property is the space. With 2240sf, the unit approaches the roominess of a private home. The first floor has a double height foyer, large eat-in kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances, and an open living space combining dining and living room areas. Two sets of french doors lead out to the wood deck. The fenced area is perfect for privacy, pets, or little ones. The motorized awning (rare) combines with the fencing to create an outdoor room in the warmer months. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has a full bath with double sinks. The second bedroom is also en suite with two closets. From the second bedroom there is access to a balcony sitting area/dedicated office space. An open landing conveys the overall roominess offered. Down on the lower level, where many units have only crawl spaces, #46 provides two full rooms - owners choice of rec room, gym, office, or more - as well as offering an efficient laundry area. The 1 car garage is detached and sits in front making the entry courtyard pleasantly private. Life here is about enjoying coastal ease and joy - from #46 it is only a gentle, non-intersectioned roadway to the delights of beach, sand & sea. Near longshore club and train!
