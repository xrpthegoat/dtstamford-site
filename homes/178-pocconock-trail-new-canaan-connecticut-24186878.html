About this home

Nestled on a picturesque 1.83-acre natural setting, this spacious 5-bedroom, 4-full-bath home offers privacy, comfort, and convenience. Set back from the road with a long driveway and ample off-street parking, the property enjoys beautiful frontage and a serene atmosphere. The main level features a gracious formal living room with fireplace, an elegant dining room, and a beautifully renovated kitchen that opens to a welcoming family room with a gas fireplace and access to the backyard deck, creating an ideal space for everyday living and entertaining. The primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, walk-in closets, and full bath. Three additional bedrooms are located on the second floor, while a first-floor bedroom with a full bath and separate living area offers flexibility for guests, extended family, or multigenerational living. A four-season sunroom overlooks the pond and surrounding nature, with large windows that fill the space with natural light-perfect as a sitting room, home office, or creative retreat. A spacious lower-level recreation/playroom provides additional living space for entertaining, hobbies, fitness, or play. Enjoy a neighborhood setting ideal for walking and biking, while remaining conveniently located near the train, Merritt Parkway, downtown shopping, and restaurants.
