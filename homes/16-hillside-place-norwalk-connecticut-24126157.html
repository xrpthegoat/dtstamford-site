About this home

End unit, tri-level townhouse with private entrance. This Victoria Lane Condominium has 3 levels of living space,and an additional full basement level - basement with high ceilings, storage, and newer laundry appliances. Generously sized ensuite bedrooms. Each bedroom spans an entire floor, has a private bath and 2 closets, and are large enough for "flex" space for home office. Wood floors in kitchen, dining and living areas - complemented by a full-length French door to the patio from the living room. "Pass through" window between kitchen and dining area. Half bath on 1st floor for guests and everyday ease. Pull down access to floored attic storage. 2 reserved parking spaces as well as visitor parking. Efficient gas heat. This pet friendly complex is well designed with garden sidewalks and ideally situated near everything - including a new walking path thru the historic "Elmwood" area. Small, quiet condo complex near beaches, shops, restaurants, museums, commuter routes. Central air, security system, and newer appliances.
