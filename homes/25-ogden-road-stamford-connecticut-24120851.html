About this home

This charming 4-bedroom cape will be the perfect place to call home! Walking through the front door, you will be greeted by a cozy living room with a wood burning fireplace and multiple window exposures. Next, the large eat-in-kitchen that opens up to a bright sunroom with sliders and views of your backyard will be sure to WOW you! 2 bedrooms which can be as versatile as you want (office, dining room, playroom the choice is yours) full bathroom, hardwood floors thru-out and recessed lighting complete the first floor! Upstairs, you have 2 large bedrooms and a beautifully updated full bath (don't forget to check out the awesome shower)! Head on down to the basement where you will find storage galore, laundry and access to your 1 car attached garage. Your private and spacious backyard is perfect for summer BBQs on the large patio, playing in the snow or just enjoying a bit of peace and quiet. Natural gas and central air make this the perfect find! All centrally located to get you downtown or North Stamford in minutes. Walk to Newfield Shopping Center, Barrett Park, houses of worship, shops, restaurants & easy access to public transportation. Welcome home!
