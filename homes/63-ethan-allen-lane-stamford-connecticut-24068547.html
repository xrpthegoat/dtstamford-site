About this home

Luxury and Comfort in North Stamford. This exquisite eco-friendly home seamlessly combines modern luxury, eco-conscious upgrades, and thoughtful design. Fully renovated, it features hydronic radiant-heated porcelain tiles throughout, ensuring unmatched comfort in every room. These upgrades are complemented by a new boiler, water heater, and ductless mini-splits for efficient year-round climate control. A 400-amp electrical service powers dual EV chargers, a whole-house gas generator, and buried power lines, offering uninterrupted living even during New England's toughest weather. Accessibility and style blend effortlessly with widened doorways and leveled floors. Beautifully remodeled bathrooms showcase modern fixtures, spacious showers, and dual-flush toilets. Convenience is enhanced with a relocated laundry area in the primary bedroom, a whole-house water filtration system, and custom storage solutions. The revitalized finished lower level has a versatile guest suite with a dual-entry bath, a multi-use room, a workshop, and expanded living areas, all with ample storage. Outdoors, a motion-activated lighting system, invisible fencing, and Nest smart devices provide security and peace of mind. With a new septic system (2023), this home is perfectly equipped for contemporary living. Experience a harmonious blend of elegance, innovation, and sustainability. Schedule a viewing today!
