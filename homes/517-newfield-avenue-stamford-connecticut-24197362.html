About this home

Welcome to this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home, situated in the desirable Belltown neighborhood of Stamford, CT. Step inside to discover an inviting open-concept layout, featuring a living area filled with natural light and elegant finishes throughout. The stunning, fully updated kitchen boasts sleek cabinetry, quartz countertops, and stainless steel appliances. With its central location, this home offers easy access to shopping, dining, parks, and major commuting routes, making it perfect for today's lifestyle.
