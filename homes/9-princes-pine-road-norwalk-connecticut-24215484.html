About this home

Welcome to 9 Princes Pine, a beautifully restored 3-bedroom, 2-bath Mid-Century Modern home that charms you from the moment you pull into the drive. Tucked away on a quiet, neighborhood street, this bright sanctuary was been completely transformed and is meticulously maintained. Step inside to experience a true "jewel box." Sunlight streams through every window, highlighting an open-concept layout designed for effortless indoor-outdoor flow and seamless entertaining. This home is 100% turn-key with a new roof, mechanicals, windows, siding, lighting and more. Outdoors, the professionally landscaped yard rolls down to the relaxing banks of The Five Mile River. Located in the sought-after West Norwalk enclave, you're perfectly positioned for convenient commuting and just minutes from the vibrant downtowns, dining, and shopping of Darien, New Canaan, Norwalk, and SoNo.
