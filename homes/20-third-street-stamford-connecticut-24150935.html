About this home

Welcome to refined living in Stamford's desirable Ridgeway-Bulls Head neighborhood. Built in 2015, this modern condominium offers 1,683 sq ft of well-designed living space with 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms. The open, airy layout features high ceilings, hardwood floors, contemporary lighting, and oversized windows that provide abundant natural light. A standout feature is the rooftop deck, offering beautiful city views. This pet-friendly home includes central heating and air conditioning for year-round comfort. Conveniently located near major shopping centers and within walking distance to Scalzi Park, Mill River Park, downtown Stamford, restaurants, movie theaters, and more. Low common charges include trash removal and yard maintenance, making for easy, low-maintenance living. A fantastic opportunity to own a spacious, move-in-ready condo in one of Stamford's most convenient locations.
