About this home

Tucked away on a quiet West Norwalk cul-de-sac, this raised ranch provides space, comfort, and an ideal in-law setup. The open layout features new windows and a slider to a large deck overlooking a private, fenced backyard-perfect for gatherings or quiet evenings. The kitchen includes newer appliances, quartz countertops, a spacious island, and tile flooring. Three bright bedrooms with hardwood floors and an updated full bath complete the main level. The lower level provides a family room with patio access, a kitchenette, a carpeted bedroom, and a new full bath with a tub and shower. Conveniently located near shopping, transportation, and trains, this home also includes a heated garage. More Photos Coming Soon!
