About this home

Remarks: Indian Harbor House. Direct waterfront on Greenwich Harbor. Western Exposure with wonderful sunsets. The perfect Pied-a-Terre. Walking distance to the Indian Harbor Yacht Club, Bruce Museum, Bruce Park, Delmar Hotel, Island Beach Ferry, train station and Greenwich Avenue's wonderful stores and restaurants. All rooms excluding baths have stunning water views with large windows and abundant sunlight. Primary bedroom connects via outdoor private waterfront terrace to a 24'x14' living room. Also included is a rare separate studio apartment with full bath on lower level. Great for an office, art studio, guest or live-in help. There are 2 elevators, full time superintendent and a secure indoor parking garage with reserved space. No pets allowed as per bi-laws. A/C included in common charge.
