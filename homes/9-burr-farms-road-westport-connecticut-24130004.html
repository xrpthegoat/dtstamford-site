About this home

The home of your dreams that exceeds every expectation awaits at this spectacular, like-new residence built w/the finest quality & elevated design. Perfectly sited on one of Long Lots' top cul-de-sacs behind a stone wall, the manicured acre w/saltwater gunite pool/spa offers a serene backdrop for this exceptional property. The impressive, soaring foyer w/exquisite millwork introduces interiors w/ 10-ft ceilings on the main level incl the grand LR w/modern marble fpl & DR w/architectural ceiling. Well-appointed library/home office w/built-ins, fireplace & French doors to side & rear porches. The show-stopping chef's kitchen dazzles w/custom cabinetry, marble counters & island, backsplash w/subtle bronze inlay, Sub Zero & Wolf appliances, walk-in pantry, butler's pantry to DR & sunlit dining area, flowing into the stunning fam rm w/French doors & gorgeous fireplace. Expansive rear hall w/full pool bath, mudroom w/ample storage, 3-car garage access, back stairs to 2nd & 3rd flrs. With 7 BRs, 8 full & 2 half baths, there is space for all. Luxe primary suite w/spacious BR, sitting area w/fpl, dressing rm, coffee bar, 2 incredible walk-in clsts, marble bath. Bonus rms on 2nd flr w/built-ins & 3rd flr w/climbing structure. Incredible LL w/media rm, gym, wine cellar, bar, game rm w/fpl, sauna. Resort-style property w/covered porch, pergola, firepit, outdoor kitchen & level lawns framing the 45-ft pool. Generator, Savant smart home system & much more. Impeccable & move-in ready.
