About this home

Located in the sought-after waterfront community of Noroton Bay, this charming 4-bedroom, 2-bath Cape Cod-style home blends classic design with modern comfort. The inviting living room features paneled molding and a cozy gas fireplace, while the eat-in kitchen offers marble counters and backsplash, Sub-Zero refrigerator, La Cornue Range, wine cooler, dishwasher, and peninsula with sliders leading to the backyard patio-perfect for entertaining. The spacious family room includes custom built-ins and storage. A main-level bedroom and full bath provide flexibility for guests or home office use. The mudroom with built-in cubbies and laundry adds everyday convenience. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features custom built-in drawers and dual closets, complemented by two additional bedrooms and a full bath. Additional highlights include hardwood floors, recessed lighting, central air, and an attached 1-car garage-all within steps of the water and Noroton Bay amenities. Living in The Bay is a lifestyle with multiple celebratory events throughout the year. This golf cart community offers membership at a private beach association where you can enjoy paddle boarding, kayaking or a picnic with family and friends.
