About this home

Welcome to the charming private community of Marvin Beach! Established in 1914 The private Association is located on the Norwalk Harbor and offers a member's only marina and three private beaches for kayaking, sailing, swimming, or sunset walks. Enjoy the quiet lifestyle, warm and friendly neighborhoods, and outdoor activity all year. This vintage 1925 colonial features a large living room with fireplace, dining room, kitchen area, den/office with fireplace, and 3 Season porch. On the second level there are 4 bedrooms, 1 bath, and stairs to the spacious attic. The lower level basement includes storage, high ceilings, and access to the backyard. Additional features include custom molding, oversized windows, transom windows, partial views of the harbor, and 1 car detached garage. Don't miss this rare opportunity! A wonderful community within walking distance to the private beach, Taylor Farm, Calf Pasture. A 5 minute drive to the East Norwalk Train Station, SoNo, I-95, and Merritt Parkway.
