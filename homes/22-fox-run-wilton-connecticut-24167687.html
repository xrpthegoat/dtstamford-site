About this home

THIS is the home you've been waiting for! Beautiful, stately home set on gorgeous level property located in a quiet, private enclave of 17 homes. Enjoy your morning walks in this little slice of heaven in North Wilton. This home abuts the Woodcock Nature Preserve - a wonderful offering right in your backyard! NEW gorgeous quartz state of the art kitchen is the heart of the home - wide open to a fabulous family room with wood burning fireplace. The 1st floor offers a spacious layout with the light filled sunroom/den & gas fireplace & slider doors opening to the outdoor bluestone patio and beautiful level yard. Private Home Office and Formal dining room round out the first floor. The 2nd floor showcases 5 bedrooms, including the lovely Primary bedroom, and the very large Bonus Room for play/media OR it can serve as a terrific in-law/ guest suite complete with the full kitchenette, full bath/laundry and separate entrance with private back staircase. Sparkling hardwood floors throughout this home, spacious closet space, 2nd floor laundry & newer mechanicals, ... this home has been well cared for! Enormous unfinished basement with 2 newer oil tanks and brand-new state of the art $20,000 full house water treatment system. Fox Run Road has been newly paved showcasing a lovely entry approach to the home. Of course.... Award Winning Wilton schools, quaint Wilton Village Center and beautiful downtown Ridgefield are just minutes away. FABULOUS home!
