About this home

This well-loved 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath Cape Cod offers a fantastic blend of cozy living and flexible space. The main level features two comfortable bedrooms, along with a generously sized living room anchored by a fireplace, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. An eat-in kitchen provides a functional layout for everyday living, while the sunporch adds a bright, relaxing space to enjoy year-round. Upstairs, you'll find two additional finished rooms that offer great potential for extra bedrooms, a home office, or creative space-tailor them to fit your needs. Hardwood floors lie beneath the carpeting on the main level, just waiting to be revealed and restored. The partially finished basement expands your living options even further, complete with a second fireplace-perfect for a recreation room, media space, or home gym. A dedicated laundry room in the basement adds convenience and practicality. Step outside to a private, fenced-in backyard with plenty of room for entertaining, gardening, or simply enjoying the outdoors in peace. Ideally situated for commuters, this home provides quick access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and nearby train service, making daily travel a breeze. A great opportunity to personalize a home with solid bones in a highly convenient location-come see the possibilities!
