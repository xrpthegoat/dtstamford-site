About this home

This desirable Newfield neighborhood offering is completely redone & expanded w/top grade materials, fine craftsmanship & backyard oasis w/heated pool+spa. The tremendous curb appeal is your first introduction to the many improvements in this home. New exterior windows & siding w/copper accents, stone facade, stone walls, lighting & more, exude the quality in construction. Upon entering the foyer you will be greeted with the many updates of this open layout. Handsome hardwood floors throughout, LED lighting, custom built-ins, tray ceiling, gas fireplace & skylight are just a few of the interior updates. The cook's kitchen has abundant cabinetry, top grade Thermador appliances & stone countertops. A cozy den features a gas cast iron stove-the perfect multi purpose space. Sliders to an awning covered stone patio offer alfresco dining & views of the pool & professionally landscaped property. Upstairs features 3 guest bds. w/hardwood floors, 1 en-suite w/new bath & 2 sharing updated hall bath. The spacious primary suite includes French doors to a deck, extra large walk in closet & modern bath w/tub & shower. The 1,200SF heated garage accommodates 3+ cars-the ultimate toy enthusiasts/gym & features a bonus office space. Outside, there are several dining/entertaining spaces around the resort style salt water pool & spa that features a waterfall, gas fire pit & grilling area. This energy efficient all natural gas run home even has an auto generator & spray foam insulation.
