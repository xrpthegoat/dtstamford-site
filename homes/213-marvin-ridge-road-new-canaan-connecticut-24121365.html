About this home

Significant price improvement! It's all about the garden! Imagine having your very own park and a house that comes with it! Beautifully laid out 4 bedroom, 3 1/2-bath Colonial with high ceilings sits on a 2+ acre corner lot with stunning mature plantings. Situated minutes from the Merritt Parkway, 213 Marvin Ridge Rd invites you to experience the perfect balance of convenience & serenity! Step outside, & you're moments away from New Canaan's vibrant cultural scene, including Grace Farms, the iconic Summer Theater, & weekly Farmers Market. As you approach the house via the private asphalt driveway, you're greeted by a professionally landscaped yard that reflects years of care & attention: something close to a botanical garden, a little bit of paradise. Stunning mature trees & vibrant plantings create a peaceful, private oasis-perfect for relaxing, entertaining, & enjoying the changing seasons. Upon entry to the house you'll see a powder room, a welcoming living room with fireplace, a dining room with bay window, an eat-in kitchen with den, a laundry room, a cozy study, another dining space which leads out to a beautiful screened in porch, a great room with an incredible vaulted ceiling & fireplace & built-ins. To the left of the entry is a main level primary suite with ample closet space. The 2nd floor offers three bedrooms (1 ensuite), & hallway bath. The walk out lower level is huge & offers versatile space - a library, a gym, plus a home office. Welcome Home!
