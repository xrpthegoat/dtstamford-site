About this home

Set in the coveted Saugatuck Shores enclave, this 5BR/4BA Colonial offers timeless design & modern amenities for today's living. The open floor plan includes a sun-filled living room with gas fireplace & dining area, highlighted by the heart of the home-a chef's dream kitchen with abundant storage, granite countertops, Sub-Zero refrigerator & 2 refrigerator drawers, Wolf six-burner double oven, microwave/convection, Miele espresso machine & kitchen island. The well-planned first floor also features a fifth bedroom with convenient access to a full bath w/radiant heated flrs, perfect for use as a den, study, guest room, or for one-level living. French doors open to a serene patio & private yard, ideal for dining, entertaining, or quiet evenings surrounded by nature. On the second level, the private quarters are anchored by a tranquil primary suite with fireplace, walk-in closet, & spa-bath w/radiant heated flrs. Three additional bedrooms with generous closets are complemented by two full baths, including one en-suite. A laundry room & pull-down attic with generous storage round out this level. Brazilian Chestnut hardwood floors extend throughout, adding warmth & elegance. Energy-efficient systems enhance comfort. Just minutes to town, train, and I-95, this is the ultimate Westport coastal retreat, blending everyday comfort with refined sophistication for today's modern lifestyle.
