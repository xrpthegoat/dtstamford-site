About this home

Nestled on 2.5 impeccably landscaped acres with Silvermine River views, this charming 3 bed, 3 bath home is a perfect blend of historic charm and modern elegance. From the foyer, step into a bright, formal living room with fireplace and vaulted ceilings. The light filled room includes an oversized picture window that showcases stunning property views. Adjacent, a warm family room / library offers equally beautiful views and a calming area to read and unwind. The heart of the home is the showstopper Small Bone kitchen, with vaulted ceilings, antique wooden beams, and fireplace. The open kitchen includes ample counter space, casual dining area, and second family room. Adjacent is a formal dining room with access to a lovely exterior space overlooking lush gardens. The primary bedroom is a luxurious retreat with beautiful views, two large walk-in closets, and en suite marble bath with shower, tub, and double vanity. The second bedroom includes double height ceiling and lofted play area. The third bedroom / office offers an en suite bath and private exterior entrance, ideal for guests or a home office. Completing the home is a lovely solarium, perfect for dining, socializing, and enjoying the property. From here, the peaceful stream, large dogwood and maple trees, beautiful gardens, and charming CT wildlife are on display. This home is a perfect blend of charm and history, elegantly updated for today's buyer. Come experience the tranquility and beauty of this country oasis.
