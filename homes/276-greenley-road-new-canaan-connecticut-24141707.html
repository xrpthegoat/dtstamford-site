About this home

Nestled in one of Connecticut's most sought-after estate neighborhoods and just minutes from town, this spectacular 1939 Classic Colonial blends timeless elegance with every modern amenity. Set on nearly 5 bucolic acres of rolling lawns, perennial gardens, and mature trees, this pristine property offers unmatched privacy and beauty. The grand approach includes a stately driveway with a central fountain leading to an inviting paneled foyer. Inside, the gracious living room with fireplace and built-ins opens to a formal dining room that overlooks the magnificent grounds. The chef's kitchen, complete with eat-in area, is perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Unique lifestyle features abound: a spacious game room with cigar ventilation ideal for cards or gatherings, a cozy wood-paneled office with extensive built-ins, and a secondary office featuring a charming circular window with a view of the sprawling front lawn. The luxurious primary suite offers a fireplace, dual walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath. Four additional bedrooms provide comfortable accommodations for family and guests. A modern gym and a bonus bunk room with sleeping quarters for up to eight make this home as functional as it is elegant. Outdoors, enjoy an expansive patio perfect for entertaining, a swimming pool, and a tennis/pickle ball/sport court-all surrounded by meticulously manicured landscaping that recalls a bygone era of quiet sophistication. A true Connecticut gem,
