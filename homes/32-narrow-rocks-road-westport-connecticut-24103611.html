About this home

Welcome to 32 Narrow Rocks Road, an exceptional ranch-style home nestled in the highly desirable Compo Beach neighborhood of Westport, CT. This 1,883-square-foot residence sits on a generous 0.74-acre lot, with no wetlands, offering expansive outdoor space and endless possibilities. Located just steps from Longshore Golf Course, Compo Beach, and the train station, this home presents a unique opportunity for buyers ready to reimagine and upgrade a well-located property with classic charm and solid bones, while enjoying prime access to premier amenities and coastal living. Step inside to discover a spacious living room featuring a large fireplace and original custom-built hardwood floors that flow throughout the home. The updated kitchen balances function and style with pristine white cabinetry, granite countertops, and modern appliances, opening seamlessly into a cozy dining area. The primary suite boasts two closets and a fully renovated en-suite bath, while three additional bedrooms provide ample space and access to two additional full bathrooms. Enjoy relaxing and entertaining on the large screened porch that opens to a serene stone patio, ideal for outdoor gatherings or a peaceful retreat. With its ideal setting and generous lot, 32 Narrow Rocks Road invites your vision to transform it into a truly exceptional residence.
