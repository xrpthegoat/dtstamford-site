About this home

Come be a part of Rowayton's vibrant seaside community in this Classic 4-5 bedroom colonial in the heart of town. This lovely sun-filled home offers great space and flow for easy living and is within walking distance to the beach, village, parks and train. Updated and spacious with just under 4000 SF on 3 levels. Hardwood floors and deep moldings throughout. Gracious formal rooms, a fabulous updated kitchen by Deane, separate office and mud room and large vaulted great room with fireplace, opening to terrace and yard. White kitchen is a chef's delight and boasts a center island, Wolf, Sub Zero and charming dining area. Vaulted primary suite has luxurious new bath and walk in closet. Laundry on second level. Walk out lower level has another large family room plus a full bath that can be 5th bedroom, as well as another mudroom and copious storage. Tandem 4 car garage offers room for boat/kayak storage. Fully fenced yard for privacy and safety has charming gate access to Witch and Crest Lane.
