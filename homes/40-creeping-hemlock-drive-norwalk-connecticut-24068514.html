About this home

Welcome to this impeccably maintained, freshly painted and thoughtfully updated turn-key ranch-style home, perfectly situated in the heart of Cranbury. From the moment you step inside, you'll be greeted by a bright and inviting living and dining area that flows seamlessly into the kitchen, offering direct access to a charming, covered porch. The main level features three spacious bedrooms and a full bathroom. Downstairs you will find a finished basement featuring a room that can serve as an office or guest room. This level also includes a full bathroom, a laundry room with built-in storage and shelving, and a pantry. An oversized two-car garage provides ample storage opportunities. The expansive 1.38-acre lot is a true outdoor retreat, and it features fruit trees, a vegetable garden, a cozy fire pit area, and a relaxing outdoor hot tub - perfect for unwinding or entertaining. Additionally, two generously sized sheds totaling over 500 square feet provide ideal spaces for storage, a workshop, or a creative studio. Nestled in a peaceful and highly desirable neighborhood, this home provides the perfect balance of tranquility and accessibility. It is located just moments away from schools, parks, highways and local amenities. Combining functionality, charm, countless updates such as newly refinished hardwood floors (2025), this property is move-in ready for its new owners. Photos coming soon!
