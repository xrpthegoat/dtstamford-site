About this home

Set on a sweeping acre in Rowayton, this renovated home offers sunlit interiors, a private pool, and modern updates. A welcoming entry opens into a bright, open-concept layout with new hardwood floors throughout. The updated kitchen features a 4-seat quartz island and Bosch stainless steel appliances, flowing seamlessly into the dining area, living room, and large family room. Sliding glass doors lead to a new deck overlooking the pool, extending the living space outdoors. Recent upgrades include a new roof and gutters, new floors, updated bathrooms, central air, heating, plumbing, deck, and a crushed stone driveway with Belgium block accents. Located within walking distance to downtown Rowayton, schools, and just 60 minutes to NYC by train, this residence combines updated living space with coastal convenience. Nearby amenities include beaches, marinas, parks, and the village center. The property also offers one of the area's larger private lots, providing ample outdoor space.
