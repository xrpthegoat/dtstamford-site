About this home

Welcome to 44 Ambler Dr- a picture-perfect ranch tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations. This amazing condo alternative offers true one-level living with 2 spacious bedrooms and a full bathroom on the main level, plus a finished basement that adds even more flexibility. The open layout centers around the kitchen, which features granite counters, ample cabinet/counter space, and easy access to the patio. Off the kitchen and living room is a flexible bonus room with vaulted ceilings and a slider to the patio. The lower level includes a large playroom or den, a laundry area and a full bath for added convenience adding over 500 sqft of space. Whether you need a home office, guest suite, or playroom, there's plenty of room to make it your own. A staircase leads up to a large attic, offering excellent potential for future expansion. Outside, you'll find a detached one-car garage and a level backyard with a full patio-ideal for entertaining, gardening, or relaxing year-round. Central air, 2 driveways, and sidewalks round off this great home. It's the perfect blend of privacy and comfort while still being close to everything you need. Just around the corner from Stew's and all the shopping, dining, and entertainment along Westport Ave, you'll love how effortless life feels here. 44 Ambler Drive checks every box for easy, comfortable living in a prime location.
