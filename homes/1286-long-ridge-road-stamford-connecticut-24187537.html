About this home

Come live in beautiful North Stamford where the dear, squirls and chipmunks will be your friends and neighbors. Wake up every morning in a unique home with a large outside deck and where the birds chirping will be your alarm clock. Right off the main drag of Long Ridge Road. Minutes aways from the Merit Parkway. The home has a total of nine rooms, including three bathrooms and at least three bedrooms. You can convert one of the additional rooms into a fourth bedroom. Rooms have tall ceilings and lots of storage space throughout the house. The master bathroom recently renovated, includes a brand new whirlpool tub with jets, and plenty of space for bathroom storage amenities, for optimal relaxation. The home invites an abundance of natural light, with the main living and family room areas lined with large windows, with views of the outdoor grounds. The kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, and newly installed skylights, and large cupboard storage area. The washer and dryer are on the first floor, for easy use and access. For the nature lovers, enjoy the beautiful trees, greenery and regular bird watching. Don't forget the back deck for backyard barbequing and outdoor hosting, or the front porch to enjoy your morning coffee. Don't leave your vehicles outside, as the home boasts a spacious two car garage. For your guests, there are an additional two outside parking next to the garage. Close to city bus access and minutes away from local public schools.
