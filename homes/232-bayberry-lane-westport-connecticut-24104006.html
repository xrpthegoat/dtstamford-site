About this home

Set in Westport's prime Coleytown neighborhood on a sun-filled 2-acre property is this striking, recently-built home with a stylish blend of transitional and modern elements. Architectural highlights include a graphite-colored exterior with oak trim, sleek Marvin casement windows, artisan-style galvanized gutters and smoked-glass garage doors, plus a sprawling, level property with Gunite pool and spa surrounded by stunning new low stone walls and bluestone terrace. The gorgeous glass front door leads to the dramatic foyer, floating staircase, oversized windows, custom wood finishes and wide-plank oak floors, enhancing the sun-filled layout. The gourmet cook's kitchen is outfitted with premium appliances, an oversized island, sleek quartz counters, a walk-in pantry and custom cabinetry with a built-in three-zone beverage fridge. Designed for entertaining, this space flows into a spacious living room featuring a two-sided fireplace that connects to a sunroom/dining room and an ensuite bedroom/office. Upstairs are four additional spacious bedrooms, including the primary suite, with a luxurious bath with soaking tub, offering tranquil views of the grounds. A beautiful 2nd floor office is tucked behind glass doors. The walk-out lower level boasts 9-foot ceilings and a media room, gym and playroom. This energy-efficient home features a 5-zone HVAC system, 19kW solar, 240V Level 2 EV charger and generator. Centrally located and just 1.3 mile to schools!
