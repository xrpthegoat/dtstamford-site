About this home

Rare opportunity on one of Norwalk's most charming streets! This beautifully renovated Cape perfectly blends timeless character with modern comfort. Light, bright, and meticulously maintained throughout all 1,898 sq ft, the pride of ownership is evident from the moment you arrive. Soaring first-floor ceilings create an airy, open feel that sets this home apart - generous height that amplifies the natural light and gives every room a sense of gracious space. The main level offers exceptional flexibility with a first-floor bedroom ideal as a guest suite, home office, or private retreat. The stunning kitchen features gas cooktop, wall oven, microwave, range hood, refrigerator, and dishwasher - designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. Original hardwood floors preserve the home's historic charm while contemporary updates feel cohesive throughout. Convenient second-floor laundry adds everyday ease. Three full baths serve four spacious bedrooms across two well-appointed floors. Outside, the nearly 8,700 sq ft lot provides room to relax and entertain through every season. Located just off Newtown Ave, enjoy walkable access to parks, shopping, transit, and top schools - all the best of Fairfield County living.
