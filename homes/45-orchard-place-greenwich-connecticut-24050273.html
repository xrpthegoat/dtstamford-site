About this home

Luxury living in an one of two condo units, nestled in the heart of Greenwich. This turn-key home is just a stone's throw from the vibrant town center, offering easy access to shopping, schools, historic Bruce Park and Museum, and convenient transportation. The residence boasts two opulent en-suite BDRs with private balconies - the primary featuring a spacious walk-in closet. An expansive rooftop terrace provides a panoramic view of the surroundings. The living and dining room flow seamlessly into a gourmet kitchen, complete with a cozy fireplace and French doors that open to a lush private backyard patio and garden. Extra high ceilings and layered moldings, paned windows throughout add a touch of elegance throughout. A generously sized finished basement features a family room with a wet bar, an additional bedroom, a full BTH, and a LAUN. A two-car garage and a reliable generator, ensuring comfort and peace of mind. Experience sophistication and convenience in this exquisite Greenwich haven.
