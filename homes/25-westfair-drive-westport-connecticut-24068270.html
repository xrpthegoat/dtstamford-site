About this home

Pristine/LIKE NEW! Welcome to The Westporter! Get ready to fall in love with this incredible family home that combines privacy with ultimate convenience! This stunning 2016 H&P Builder masterpiece boasts custom craftsmanship and designer finishes in the charming Long Lots neighborhood. As you step inside, you'll be greeted by breathtaking dark hardwood floors that set the stage for today's modern family lifestyle. The spacious family room features a cozy gas fireplace, perfect for gatherings, while the elegant formal dining room with coffered ceilings seamlessly flows into a magnificent gourmet eat-in kitchen. With an island and French doors opening onto a gorgeous terrace, entertaining has never been more delightful! The lower level basement is a treasure trove of fun, boasting 9-foot ceilings, a media room with a fireplace, and a versatile gym/dance room, all perfect. Embrace the love and care that has gone into this home, where every finish and design choice reflects a commitment to comfort and elegance.
