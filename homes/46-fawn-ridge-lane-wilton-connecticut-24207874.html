About this home

Ideally situated on the Norwalk/Wilton line, this beautifully maintained condominium offers privacy, comfort, and an unbeatable commuter location. Set toward the back of the complex, the unit enjoys a peaceful, tucked-away feel, with the kitchen window, dining area, and private deck all overlooking natural surroundings rather than passing traffic or neighboring units. Inside, the living room's floor-to-ceiling windows fill the space with natural light, while the multi-level layout creates distinct living zones: a welcoming entry, garage access, and laundry on one level, an open kitchen and living area with a spacious island offering abundant prep space and storage, and bedrooms upstairs for a cozy, private retreat away from the main living areas. The upper level features a double-sinks in the primary bedroom, allowing two people to get ready at once with ease. Generously sized rooms throughout and abundant closet space provide comfortable, flexible living. An attached garage adds everyday convenience. Residents enjoy access to the pool and tennis courts, and the location cannot be beat: close to shops and restaurants, yet quiet within the complex, with easy access to major commuting routes and New York city.
