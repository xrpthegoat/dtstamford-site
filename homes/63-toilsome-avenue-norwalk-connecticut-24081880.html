About this home

Located in the sought-after Cranbury neighborhood, this stunning contemporary colonial offers exceptional curb appeal, soaring ceilings, and a thoughtfully designed open floor plan perfect for modern living and entertaining. The inviting living room with a fireplace sets the stage for warmth and elegance upon entry, while the formal dining room, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows, showcases breathtaking views of the resort-style pool and professionally landscaped yard. A custom deck extends the living space outdoors, creating the perfect setting for relaxation and gatherings. The private primary suite serves as a tranquil retreat, complete with a spa-inspired bath and a spacious walk-in closet. Step outside and breathe in the fresh air as you take in the beauty of the expansive 1.22-acre backyard, a true sanctuary offering lush greenery, a resort-style pool, and serene spaces to unwind. Whether hosting lively gatherings or enjoying quiet moments, this exceptional outdoor setting is designed to be enjoyed year-round.
