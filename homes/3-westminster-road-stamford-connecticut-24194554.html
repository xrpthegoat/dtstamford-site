About this home

Shippan Waterfront Community! Pristine home with water views just a short walk to the beach! Updated 3-BR, 2.5 bath home on quiet cul de sac. Large eat in kitchen, living room with vaulted ceiling and fireplace. Family room and half bath complete the first floor. Hardwood floors throughout. Upper level has primary BR with full bath plus two additional bedrooms and full hall bath. Many recent updates--new HVAC system, new washer/dryer, built-ins in primary BR, renovated upstairs bath, custom closets and recessed lighting. Main Level laundry. This home is heated by natural gas and has central a/c. 2 car attached garage. Lots of storage. Private fenced-in back yard with slate patio. Only 8 minutes to Metro North!
