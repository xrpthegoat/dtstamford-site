About this home

This spacious and modernly built home is located in the sought after Silvermine neighborhood. Nestled privately, this home features four bedrooms and 2 1/2 baths of which the largest bedroom can be used as a great room. The backyard patio can be accessed from the both the eat-in-kitchen and living room. The basement is partially finished with electric and plumbing containing a full bath and three rooms, as well as outside access, and can be easily converted to an in-law apartment. Don't miss this opportunity!
