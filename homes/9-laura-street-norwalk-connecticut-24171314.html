About this home

"This Old House", built in 1900, has been transformed to a very inviting almost new home which has been painted throughout, the floors beautifully refinished, a new gas furnace and central air, stainless steel kitchen counters, new stove, dishwasher and great microwave. There are two skylights on the first and second floors; with the abundance of windows, this is a very happy sunny home. The landscaped yard is lovely, and the new crushed stone driveway will be the perfect place to park your cars. It is located only steps to the kitchen entrance! The first and second floors each have a full bath with tubs and showers.The basement is not heated, but could be an exercise room, playroom part of the year, workshop or for storage. It can not be used as a bedroom. GARAGE NOT INCLUDED
