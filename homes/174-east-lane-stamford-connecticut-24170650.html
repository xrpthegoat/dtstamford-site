About this home

***HIGHEST & BEST 5/12 @ 12P***174 East Ln is one of those homes where you immediately understand why people want to live in the hidden gem that is Revonah Woods. Set on 1.12 acres, it offers a private, nature filled setting that feels like North Stamford, but with all the amenities and convenience of being just minutes to everything Stamford has to offer. When you pull up, you will see why the neighborhood talks about the lawn that looks like it belongs on a PGA course. This classic New England Colonial has been fully restored, expanded, and modernized over the past 5 years. A curved stone walkway, mature trees, and seasonal plantings create standout curb appeal. Inside, hardwood floors, crisp millwork, shiplap detail, and sun filled spaces balance classic character with a clean, updated feel. The main level offers six rooms that are open where you want them to be, kitchen, family, and dining, but still defined enough to keep real character, including a formal living room with wood burning fireplace, den, and office. The kitchen anchors the home with a Wolf gas range, pot filler, wine chiller, oversized island with second sink, and great sightlines throughout. Multiple French doors open to a spacious composite deck, creating seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Upstairs offers three bedrooms, including a well-designed primary suite with a soaking tub and curbless walk-in shower. The lower level adds storage, gym space, and a one-car garage.
