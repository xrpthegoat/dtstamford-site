About this home

Sun-filled, Beachy Colonial ideally located beside a coveted private cul-de-sac just moments from the Darien train & Darien Commons. Thoughtfully updated with numerous improvements and upgrades, including a stylish, top-of-the-line eat-in kitchen designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining.The open, flexible floor plan offers the rare option of a primary suite on either the main level or the second floor. Four bedrooms and two full baths provide comfortable living, complemented by a formal living room and a bright, elevated office/playroom - perfect as a work-from-home space or Peloton room - set among the treetops with beautiful views. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced yard and inviting patio ideal for gatherings, outdoor dining & relaxed afternoons. The charming private court is known for its welcoming sense of community, hosting block parties and cozy fireside evenings. Enjoy exceptional walkability to the train, restaurants, beaches & town. Located in the award-winning Blue Ribbon Hindley School district. Additional features include a basement with new French drains and sump pump.
