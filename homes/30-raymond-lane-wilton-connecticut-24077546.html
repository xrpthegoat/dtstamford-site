About this home

This sprawling 5 bedroom ranch with potential in-law is beautifully sited on a quiet South Wilton street, set peacefully behind stone pillars. Approach the home along its large horseshoe driveway, with unlimited parking for any gathering. This home is gracefully nestled into its 2.7 acre very private and meticulously landscaped grounds, only steps from Wilton's outdoor treasure; The Norwalk River Hiking Trail. Also this home is a mere 5 mins to all Wilton schools & 5 mins to the train or Merritt Pkwy. Once inside, the interior welcomes you with an airy open floor plan with gleaming hardwood floors and vaulted ceiling, exposed beams and 2 cozy fireplaces. The huge kitchen has high-end GE Monogram appliances, a large center island, wet bar, double sink and wine cooler and overlooks the full-size indoor swimming pool for year-round enjoyment and pool parties! There are 5 bedrooms on the main floor, including the primary suite with fireplace, full bath and 2 walk-in closets. Room for everyone on the main level, plus, on the walk-out lower level, there's an independent Potential In-Law (has its own entry, kitchenette, laundry, 'sleeping space' & living area). There is an enclosed 3-season porch (w/new roof and skylight), a four-car garage, newer furnace and new $4K washer/dryer. This very special home is set on an idyllic level open property, with perennial gardens, and total privacy! Only 1 hr to NYC Home being sold as is.
