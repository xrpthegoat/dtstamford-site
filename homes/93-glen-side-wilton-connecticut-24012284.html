About this home

Wonderful opportunity to live in the desirable Glen River community in this 1,127-sf unit. The layout is perfect for entertaining offering a spacious living room with woodburning fireplace and sliders to a private deck overlooking the woods. Bright kitchen opens to dining area. Upstairs features a primary bedroom with vaulted ceiling and a walk-in closet. There is also an additional 2nd bedroom/den. Laundry is conveniently located on 2nd level and there are pull-down stairs to attic. Enjoy in-town living in this townhouse which offers central a/c, detached garage, crawlspace for additional storage and an inground pool. Ideally located within walking distance to Wilton Center restaurants, shopping and close to library and park. Unit has tremendous potential and is being sold in "as is" condition. Offer is subject to probate court approval.
