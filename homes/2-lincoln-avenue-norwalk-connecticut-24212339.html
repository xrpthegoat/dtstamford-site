About this home

Excellent South Norwalk 3-Family Investment Opportunity! Located on a desirable, quiet one-way street, this income producing property offers three fully rented units with excellent rental income and strong investment potential. Recent improvements include newer siding and roof installed in 2021. The partially finished basement features high ceilings, bathroom and laundry hookup, offering additional usable space and storage. A detached garage provides even more storage, ample off street parking for residents. Ideally located just minutes from the Norwalk Train Station, major highways, The SONO Collection Mall, downtown SONO restaurants and entertainment, nearby parks and waterfront amenities. A convenient location with easy access to transportation, shopping, dining, and recreation. All units are currently rented, an excellent opportunity for investors looking for immediate income in a highly desirable South Norwalk location.
