About this home

This 4 Bedroom, 2 1/2 bath colonial home is perfectly sited on half an acre on cul-de-sac. The home is situated in the Hindley School District, a nationally acclaimed Blue Ribbon School. Bright sunny kitchen with large eating area and access to spacious deck and beautiful flat back yard. Just off the kitchen is the Family Room with vaulted ceiling. The first floor also features a Formal Living Room and Dining Room as well as laundry. Upstairs the Primary Bedroom is en-suite bath with walk in closet. The finished lower level provides another area for play or relax. There is a two car attached garage as well. Conveniently located to vibrant Darien Commons with it's upscale shops and restaurants, Noroton Heights Metro North Station, beaches, YMCA!
