About this home

Classic Ranch on Expansive Park-Like Acre+ in one of Darien's Most Sought-after Neighborhoods. Nestled on a prime 1.08-acre property, this home offers the perfect backdrop for your dream lifestyle. The light-filled formal living room, highlighted by a wood-burning fireplace and custom built-ins, flows seamlessly into the inviting family room with rich wood paneling and a second wood-burning fireplace, ideal for both relaxing and entertaining. The spacious eat-in kitchen features a center island with granite countertops and casual dining area, opening to the formal dining room or the bright sunroom and rear deck, perfect for dining al fresco overlooking the expansive private property. The primary suite provides a true santuary, complete with walk-in closets and primary bath. Three additional spacious bedrooms, all with hardwood floors, share a well-appointed hall bath with double sink vanity and tub/shower. The walk-out lower level features a generous recreation room with bay window and full bath, opening to a back patio for endless options and hours of fun. A workshop, mudroom and abundant storage areas provide exceptional flexibility and opportunity for additional finished space. Two-car attached garage, new HVAC (2023/2024), and a full house generator. Opportunities abound to renovate, expand, or build the home of your dreams on this exceptional property. Close to schools, trains, Great Island, beaches, clubs, downtown, shopping/dining, golf courses.
