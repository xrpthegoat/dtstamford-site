About this home

The perfect little, stand-alone, big house, w/2-3 bdrms, in the center of it all and with privacy!! (The home cannot be seen from the road or by a drive by. This house sits behind the front main house at 146 Imperial Ave.) Within walking distance to the Saugatuck train station, shops, restaurants, Long Shore Club, Compo Beach, Farmer's market and Westport's downtown center. A truly charming retreat complete with vaulted Ceilings, beams, skylights, channel-board walls, handsome fir hardwood floors, Limestone tile, granite countertops, SS appliances, a butcher block breakfast island, updated baths, walk-in closets, built-ins and storage. There is radiant-floor heating throughout along with highly efficient mechanics, and a lovely private backyard and patio perfect for lounging or entertaining. The house sits on the rear parcel in back of the front Farmhouse. It comes with 2 parking spots next to the cottage side door. Broker is part owner. Tenant is responsible for mowing only the rear yard.
