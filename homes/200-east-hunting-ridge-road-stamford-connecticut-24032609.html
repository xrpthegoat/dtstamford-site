About this home

PLEASE SUBMIT HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS BY TUESDAY, JULY 23 AT NOON. Welcome to this delightful and inviting Colonial home, nestled in a picturesque natural setting in North Stamford. This 4-5bd home offers a versatile floorplan with the opportunity for an in-law suite on the main floor. The sunny, expansive living room features a wood-burning fireplace, while the bright and cheerful kitchen with a Dutch door leads to a lovely sunroom with skylights providing a perfect serene setting to enjoy your morning coffee. The large family room boasts a lovely bay window, and the library/study includes built-ins and another Dutch door to the sunroom. The formal dining room provides a sophisticated setting for entertaining. Upstairs, the beautiful primary suite features an expansive walk-in closet, complemented by two additional bedrooms, an office, and 3rd bath. Meticulously maintained with beautiful hardwood floors throughout. Embrace the opportunity to make this wonderful and charming property your new home!
