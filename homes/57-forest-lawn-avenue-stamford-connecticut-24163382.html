About this home

Exceptional investment and lifestyle opportunity in the highly desirable Bulls Head neighborhood of Stamford! Tucked away on a quiet dead-end street just minutes from vibrant downtown, this rare three-unit, legal two-family property offers versatility, strong income potential and modern updates throughout. The front residence (Unit 1) is a charming Cape Cod-style home featuring 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths. The main level offers a classic layout with a spacious living room, dining room, eat-in kitchen and a primary bedroom with convenient in-unit laundry. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms and a half bath. The rear residence (Unit 2), built in 2005 and located above the three-car garage, offers a bright and open floor plan with 1 bedroom and 1.5 baths. This thoughtfully designed space includes a kitchen with granite countertops and center island, dining and living areas, central vacuum and a laundry room. The primary suite features cathedral ceilings, skylights, a walk-in closet, and an oversized bath with jacuzzi tub. Sliding doors lead to a private deck overlooking a level backyard. (Unit 3) has been newly constructed in 2024 and serves as a fully independent in-law/studio apartment. With its own private entrance, kitchen, full bath and open layout, it provides excellent rental or multigenerational living potential.
