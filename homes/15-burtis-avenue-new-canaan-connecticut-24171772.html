About this home

Effortless Luxury: The Modern Flat at The Quartier - timeless luxury through 21 bespoke residences. Redefine your standard of living in this nearly 2,140 sq. ft. bespoke sanctuary, where high-end architectural design meets the seamless ease of single-level luxury. Located in the vibrant heart of New Canaan, this 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence welcomes you through a private terrace entrance into an expansive open-concept great room with gas fireplace, illuminated by a gallery of oversized windows and grounded by 7" wide-plank white oak flooring. The epicurean kitchen-a masterclass in style featuring German-engineered Leicht cabinetry, quartzite counters, Watermark fixtures, and professional-grade appliances-flows perfectly for entertaining, while the primary suite serves as a secluded retreat with its spa-inspired bath and generous walk-in closet. As a cornerstone of a boutique community committed to sustainable innovation through solar and Geo-thermal energy, this residence offers a curated "white-glove" lifestyle including a full-time concierge, state-of-the-art golf simulator, and professional fitness studio-all mere steps from elite boutiques, world-class dining, and the NYC train.
