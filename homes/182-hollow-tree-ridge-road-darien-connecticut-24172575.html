About this home

Welcome to 182 Hollow Tree Ridge Road, a delightful, sunlit home meticulously maintained and thoughtfully updated. Nestled on a .33-acre lot, the property is adorned with mature trees and lush landscaping, featuring a spacious backyard perfect for recreation and gardening. The bright and inviting living room, complete with a fireplace, seamlessly connects to a charming dining area that overlooks the patio and backyard, ideal for entertaining. The dining space naturally transitions into the turn-key, eat-in kitchen. A generously sized office provides versatile functionality. The serene primary suite boasts his-and-her walk-in closets, while three additional bedrooms on the same level offer comfortable spaces for family guests or remote work. The attached garage and finished lower level add extra flexibility to suit your lifestyle. Located in the heart of it all, this home is just a short stroll to Noroton Heights vibrant restaurants, shops, train station, and top-rated schools.
