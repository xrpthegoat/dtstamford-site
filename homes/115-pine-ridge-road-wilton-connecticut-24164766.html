About this home

This stunning fully renovated 5-bedroom, 4 full bath home offers the perfect blend of modern design and comfortable living, set on a private 2.03-acre lot. Featuring an open concept layout with 9-foot ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors, central air, and fresh paint throughout, the home is both stylish and move-in ready. The inviting living room with a cozy fireplace flows seamlessly into the gourmet kitchen, complete with a large center island, Bosch refrigerator, gas range, dishwasher, and built-in microwave. Ample cabinetry is complemented by a wet bar/coffee station and a spacious walk-in pantry, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The expansive dining area overlooks a private deck and serene backyard, creating a perfect indoor-outdoor connection. The generous primary suite is a true retreat, featuring two walk-in closets, a gas fireplace, and a private office. Three additional bedrooms provide plenty of space, including one with its own ensuite bath. The fully finished lower level offers exceptional versatility with a mudroom, wine cellar, and a large recreation room complete with a fireplace and built-in bar with quartz countertops. A 3-car garage and beautifully landscaped grounds complete this exceptional property.
