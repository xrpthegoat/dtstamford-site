About this home

This 4 bedroom,3.5 bath tastefully renovated vintage home has an open floor plan,walls and windows with divided light and high ceilings, creating an instant sense of peace and well being. A stone hearth at the entry is surrounded by walls of bookshelves and a reading corner. The beamed living room and adjacent sun room overlook a romantic tiered garden and flat lawn. The fireplace's stone wall frames the dining space, and French doors open onto the garden patio and pergola. The warm polished wood island featuring a copper bar sink is the centerpiece of a perfect chef's kitchen. Down a private hall is the walk out first floor bedroom with full bath, walk in closet and adjacent laundry room. Upstairs an exercise/ gym space is conveniently located outside the primary suite with it's private office, high ceilings, walk in closet, and elegantly kitted bath (soaking tub, double sinks, walk in shower) Two additional generously proportioned bedrooms share a large hall bath. Convenient to charming Scott's Corners and just a few minutes to New Canaan town center, this is a special spot. Swing on the front porch, have morning coffee on the patio, wander the charming garden features, plant the enclosed garden with vegetables, flowers and herbs. Breathe. Zen living behind a stone wall on a rural street.This one is just right.
