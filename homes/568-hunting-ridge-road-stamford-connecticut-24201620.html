About this home

Tucked away on a tranquil 1.2 acre lot in desirable North Stamford, this classic 4-Bedroom, 2.5 Bath Colonial offers a rare opportunity to create rhe home of your dreams. Enter the Foyer of the home and be welcomed by the traditional layout and generously sized rooms. The Main Level features the Living Room, Kitchen, Formal Dining Room, generously sized Family Room with a Fireplace and a sliding glass door to an enclosed Porch, Powder Room and Laundry Room. The Upper level features a Primary En-Suite Bedroom, over-sized Bedroom/Office, 2 additional Bedrooms and a shared Hall Bath. The partially finished Lower Level, Deck, and pristine wood floors complete the picture. Enjoy the peace and serenity of North Stamford living while being just minutes away from the Merritt Parway,Downtown Stamford, and Metro North. Home is equipped with a whole house generator and central AC for your convenience.
