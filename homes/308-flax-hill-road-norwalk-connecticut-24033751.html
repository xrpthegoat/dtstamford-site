About this home

Welcome to 308 Flax Hill Rd Unit A located in Norwalk's charming Brookside Neighborhood! This Unit offers 2 bed 2.5 Bath with finished walkout basement. Walk right into the living room where you can relax after a long day with your family. The floor plan offers a large kitchen and dining area with plenty of counter/cabinet space & stainless-steel appliances. The main level has a half-bath on the first floor. The dining area has sliding doors with a view of your deck and treed back lot. The second floor has 2 large sized bedrooms and a full-bath & laundry room. The finished basement also has a full-bath, 2 rooms, and walk out to the back yard. This unit is conveniently located with close distance to parks, historic downtown SONO, theaters, public transportation, train station (5 min), restaurants, Maritime Aquarium, schools, easy access to I95 & much more.
