About this home

Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated, 50 Crestview offers a highly functional layout with 2 bedrooms and 1.5 baths plus two additional flexible rooms currently used as a bright home office and a playroom just off the kitchen-ideal for everyday living and visibility. This home is in excellent condition with major system upgrades including a high-efficiency tankless heating and hot water system (2016), multi-zone Mitsubishi ductless A/C (2022), and a leased solar panel system (2020) currently outperforming production estimates. Additional improvements include updated windows, a newer roof (2017) and numerous interior and exterior updates throughout. The home offers exceptional storage including a spray foam insulated attic with easy pull-down access, an unfinished basement and a detached two-car garage with an attached rear shed. Outdoor features include a welcoming front porch, private back patio, irrigation system and dog run to help maintain a beautiful yard, along with a spacious driveway ideal for playing, entertaining and easy turnarounds. Ideally located in the Springdale neighborhood with sidewalks and close proximity to the train, local shopping and parks, this home offers a great combination of comfort, efficiency and convenience.
