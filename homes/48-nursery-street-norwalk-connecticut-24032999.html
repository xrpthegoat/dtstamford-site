About this home

Nestled on the picturesque West Norwalk - New Canaan border, this charming 1948 Cape sits on a sprawling 1.92-acre lot, offering a serene and private retreat. This well loved home boasts a huge living room with space for a grand piano, pool table and sitting area; dining room with fireplace and home office on the main level. The kitchen, ready for a complete renovation, presents a blank canvas for creating your dream culinary space. On the second floor there are four spacious bedrooms, primary suite with sitting room and 2 full bathrooms, each brimming with potential and awaiting your personal touches. Screen porch could be incorporated into kitchen and interior living space. Hardwood floors throughout all freshly refinished and all rooms are newly painted. Basement offers laundry area and a bonus room for home gym. With 2 separate 2 car garages and a detached storage shed, this property is perfect for those looking to house a car collection. A great location with easy access to the Merritt Pway., minutes to I95 and Rt 1. Embrace the opportunity add value and to transform this home into your ideal sanctuary amidst a beautiful, verdant setting in beautiful Norwalk.
