About this home

Experience loft-style living at its best in this beautifully updated penthouse in the heart of downtown Stamford. With soaring 18-foot ceilings and dramatic walls of windows, this spacious home is filled with natural light and offers an open, airy feel throughout. The versatile floor plan features 2 spacious bedrooms, a loft overlooking the main living area, and an additional room that can be used as a home office, fitness space, or guest area. Freshly painted with new flooring and thoughtful updates throughout, this home is truly move-in ready. The living and dining area opens to a private balcony overlooking Mill River Park and the downtown skyline, creating the perfect spot for morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. The bedrooms feature large walk-in closets, and in-unit laundry provides added convenience. Residents enjoy exceptional building amenities, including weekday concierge/doorman service, a fitness center, a beautifully landscaped courtyard with gazebo and gas grills, and a resident lounge on the floor. Two parking spaces are included. Ideally located just steps from Mill River Park, restaurants, shopping, entertainment, parks, and playgrounds, and only a short walk to Metro-North, this exceptional penthouse offers comfortable, stylish living in the heart of downtown Stamford. Access to the gym and courtyard is through the door across from Unit 108.
