About this home

Welcome to this stately center hall colonial with a gracious 2 story entry hall nestled on a private 2-acre lot. Offering timeless charm and generous sized rooms, this home features large, light filled rooms perfect for both everyday living and elegant entertaining. Recent upgrades include a brand new roof, all new HVAC system, and a new on-demand hot water heater, so you can move in worry-free and enjoy this beautiful home. At the heart of the home, the custom-designed kitchen is a chef's dream. Outfitted with top-of-the-line appliances, Thermador and Viking, and custom cabinetry offers ample storage along with granite counters and a wine-chiller. The second floor has 4 generous sized bedrooms with large closets and the hall bath has been completely renovated. The finished lower level offers a possible private in-law suite with it's own entrance perfect for multi-generational, or au pair living. Designed with ease this home features 2 laundry rooms and a 3 car garage which provides ample space for vehicles, storage, or a workshop. Close to the Merritt Parkway and shopping.
