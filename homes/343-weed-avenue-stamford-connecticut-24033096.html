About this home

This airy 2 bedroom, 2 bath, upper level apartment, has lovely views of Holly Pond. It is short walking distance to Cove Island Park, which has beautiful beaches and many activities. This home was completely rebuilt in 1991 and is in excellent condition. There are 2 ample sized bedrooms with the primary having a private bath, and an amazing balcony. The kitchen is open to the expansive living room which fully takes advantage of the expansive views. This unit has a separate driveway and garage. There is central air, and a washer/dryer in the unit. This unit will be professionally cleaned before new tenant occupation.
