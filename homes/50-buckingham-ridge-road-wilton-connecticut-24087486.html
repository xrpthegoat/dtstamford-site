About this home

Welcome to 50 Buckingham Ridge Road - a timeless 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial set on a quiet, tree-lined street in one of Wilton's most desirable neighborhoods. Situated on a serene and private 2-acre parcel, this beautifully maintained 2,308 sq ft home offers a perfect blend of character, comfort, and convenience. Freshly painted throughout, the interior is move-in ready and brimming with warmth. Hardwood floors, sun-filled rooms, and a traditional floor plan create an inviting atmosphere ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The spacious eat-in kitchen flows into a formal dining area, while the cozy family room and living room each feature their own fireplace for relaxed evenings at home. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a private bath, and three additional bedrooms offer flexible space for guests, a home office, or playroom. Step outside to enjoy the expansive back deck surrounded by natural beauty - the perfect spot for morning coffee or summer gatherings. Located moments from the Norwalk River Valley Trail (NRVT), this home is a dream for outdoor enthusiasts with miles of scenic walking and biking trails right outside your door. All this, just minutes from Wilton's charming town center, top-rated schools, shopping, restaurants, and the train - making commuting and everyday errands a breeze. **HOME NOT IN A FLOOD ZONE!!**SEE ATTACHED FEMA MAP
