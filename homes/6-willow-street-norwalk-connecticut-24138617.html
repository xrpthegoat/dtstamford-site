About this home

Step inside 6 Willow Street and enjoy an open, airy layout that offers great flow and room for all. This four bedroom East Norwalk Cape provides 1,936 square feet of flexible living space. The home offers central air, a NEW ROOF, and a level backyard ideal for outdoor entertaining. The main level centers around a bright open concept layout where the kitchen connects directly to the family room, with windows that bring in excellent natural light and doors that lead out to the deck, creating an easy setup for everyday living. The living room includes a fireplace and offers a comfortable setting for gathering or relaxing. Two bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. The second floor includes the primary bedroom and an additional bedroom, both with vaulted ceilings and ample storage, along with an upstairs bathroom featuring a jacuzzi tub. The lower level provides excellent potential for storage, recreation, or a possible in-law setup. A detached garage is accessed from Colony, a cul-de-sac, and 6 Willow Street itself is also located on a cul-de-sac, offering a uniquely private setting at both access points. Enjoy close proximity to Norwalk's beaches, parks, dining, shopping, the East Norwalk train station, Stew Leonard's, and surrounding amenities. List of upgrades available in Supplements.
