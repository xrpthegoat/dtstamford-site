About this home

Easy living awaits you in this spacious ranch home on beautiful level and private full acre. Four bedrooms each have full ensuite bathrooms with walk in shower. Fifth bedroom has full ensuite bathroom and sitting room. Cozy up to the fire in the family room which also walks out to a large deck or enjoy entertaining or more formal time in the spacious dining room and living room.
