About this home

Step into the effortless elegance of 23 Greenwood Ave., Darien, CT - a fully renovated 5-bedroom, 21/2-bath home offering approximately 2,200 square feet of light-filled living space designed to be felt as much as seen. From the moment you enter, the warmth of brand-new oak flooring flows underfoot throughout the home, creating a natural sense of comfort and continuity. At the center of it all, the custom chef's kitchen invites you to gather, cook, and connect. Wrapped in stunning quartz countertops with a dramatic full quartz backsplash, the space is anchored by a striking quartz island-perfect for morning coffee, casual meals, or lively evenings entertaining friends and family. Upstairs, everyday living feels effortless with a conveniently placed laundry area just steps from the primary bedroom, adding ease and practicality to your daily rhythm. Step outside and take a deep breath as the backyard opens to the peaceful waterfront of the Noroton River. Here, the setting feels calm and restorative-an ideal backdrop for outdoor dining, quiet mornings, or simply unwinding as the water gently flows beyond your yard. All of this is just a five-minute drive to the train, making your commute to New York City seamless while allowing you to return home each day to the tranquility and charm of Darien living. Come experience a home that invites you to slow down, settle in, and truly enjoy where you live.
