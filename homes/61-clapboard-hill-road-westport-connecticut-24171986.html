About this home

Located in prestigious Greens Farms, this European-inspired Colonial offers a rare opportunity to enjoy immediate charm while planning for the future. Set behind private gates on expansive, picturesque grounds, the property is surrounded by multimillion-dollar estates-making it an exceptional canvas for new construction or a significant estate expansion. The existing home is beautifully renovated, offering flexibility with gracious living and dining rooms, a sun-filled sunroom, cozy family room, and a curated Chef's kitchen with Calacatta marble, bespoke cabinetry, and top-tier appliances. A serene Primary Suite and additional bedrooms upstairs, the home functions perfectly as-is-or as a future guest house or accessory residence with thoughtful modifications. The sweeping land provides ample room for a new main residence set further back on the property, while maintaining the current home for extended family, guests, staff, or as a pool house. Outdoor living is equally compelling, with an expansive covered stone terrace, fire pit, and open meadow-like grounds offering endless possibilities for a pool, tennis court, barn, or gardens. Whether you choose to embrace the home's timeless appeal or create a legacy estate tailored to your vision, this is a unique chance to live comfortably now while building for tomorrow-all in one of Westport's most coveted locations, just minutes to Westport's vibrant downtown with award winning restaurants, premium shopping, beaches, and train.
