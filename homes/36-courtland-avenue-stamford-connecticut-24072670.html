About this home

Discover this stylish and well-appointed 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom apartment in the sought-after Courtland Green cooperative community, ideally situated on the Stamford/Darien border. This move-in-ready home features modern updates throughout, including a sleek kitchen and blank canvas to really make this home yours. The open-concept living and dining area provides plenty of space for entertaining, while the generously sized bedroom ensures comfort. Additional perks include having a washer/dryer already in the unit, access to storage on the lower level in the same building, and assigned parking. The co-op fee covers heat, hot water, taxes, and grounds maintenance, leaving owners responsible only for cooking gas, electricity, and cable/internet. This pet-free, owner-occupied community offers a peaceful living environment, with an 80% carpet coverage requirement for second-floor units to help with noise control on this first level unit. Enjoy easy access to local dining, shopping, beaches, and transportation, making this home an excellent choice for convenient and comfortable living in Stamford.
