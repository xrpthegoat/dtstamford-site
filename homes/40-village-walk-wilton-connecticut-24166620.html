About this home

Village Walk, Wilton's most-popular in-town/ pet-friendly community features a magnificent private pool (opens May 22) & meticulously manicured grounds in a park-like setting. This second floor townhouse style home (3-levels) has 2 Bedrooms & 2 Full Bathrooms. The spacious main level features a large open living & dining area, kitchen, first-floor bedroom & full bathroom perfect as a home office or guest/second room. Primary Bedroom suite on the upper floor features private full Bath, cozy new carpeting and oodles of closet/ storage space! Below finds your own 1-car garage & large, 12x10 storage room. Central AC, in-unit washer & dryer and new Life Proof flooring throughout. This location presents unparalleled in-town living; walk-to Wilton Center's fabulous restaurants, shops, library, train station, grocery stores, pharmacy, public transportation plus Schenck's Island Park and the Norwalk River Trail across the street. Add in Wilton's top-rated schools and this home incorporates a convenient, comfortable community set in a thoroughly private, natural country setting.
