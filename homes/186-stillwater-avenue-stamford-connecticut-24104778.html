About this home

Welcome to this well-maintained, end unit condo nestled in the Liberty Square complex. Boasting 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath across 890 sq ft, this home offers light-filled, easy living in a superb Stamford location. Hardwood floors throughout the living room and bedrooms add warmth and character. Both bedrooms have installed closet systems. A sliding glass door leads from the living area to a private patio and outdoor space. Freshly repainted. the low taxes and common charges ($300/mo) make this a great opportunity for investors!
