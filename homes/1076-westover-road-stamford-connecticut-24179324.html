About this home

Completely rebuilt and reimagined, this exceptional Westover residence blends timeless design with the quality, efficiency, and comfort of new construction. Privately set on over an acre, the property offers an unparalleled indoor-outdoor lifestyle featuring a Gunite pool, expansive 1,500 SF patio, and 400 SF entertaining deck. Thoughtfully designed living spaces are enhanced by wide-plank 4" Red oak flooring, custom millwork, Andersen windows & doors, and abundant natural light throughout. The chef's kitchen serves as the heart of the home, showcasing custom cabinetry, Taj Mahal quartzite, a grand center island, and premium High-End appliances. The luxurious primary suite provides a private retreat with a custom walk-in closet and spa-inspired bath featuring heated floors, soaking tub, and oversized shower. Additional highlights include a finished walk-out lower level, two gas fireplaces, multi-zone HVAC, whole-house generator, city water, and a new four-bedroom septic system. An extraordinary offering that combines sophisticated luxury, exceptional craftsmanship, and one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods.
