About this home

This sunny, happy house is as sweet as a frosted cupcake! Walking distance to the Westport/Saugatuck train station and all the great restaurants in the Saugatuck section of town. Short bike ride or walk to Longshore and Compo Beach! Close to downtown Westport too. 3 (possibly 4) bedrooms and 2 1/2 baths with lots of flexible bonus spaces for home office, playroom, extra bedroom, and storage galore (check out the floor-plans). Hardwood floors throughout, everything freshly painted. Landscaping is included and the gardens are just lovely in spring and summer. Private outdoor patio with comfy outdoor furnishings to lounge on. Plenty of room to throw a ball in the front and side yard. Furnished or Unfurnished and available immediately! The owner prefers a year or nearly year rental with some flexibilty. A small dog might be considered with a pet interview and the landlords approval.
