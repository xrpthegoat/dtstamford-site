About this home

Spacious, sun-filled one-bedroom duplex condo featuring elevated ceilings and a large primary bedroom with a versatile bonus loft, ideal for a home office, reading nook, or additional flex space. The contemporary eat-in kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a peninsula with barstool seating. The spacious living room opens through a sliding glass door to a private deck. Past improvements, completed in 2017, include kitchen and bathroom updates, updated flooring, replacement windows, a new sliding glass door, and a replacement deck. Freshly painted in 2026, with a new refrigerator and stackable washer and dryer. Additional features include a soaking tub, individual storage unit, and deeded garage parking (Unit 20). Currently tenant occupied, with an existing lease through July 2027. Offered subject to the existing tenancy, this property presents an excellent opportunity for investors seeking rental income or end users willing to wait until the lease expires to make it their own. Enjoy peaceful, park-like grounds near transportation, Old Greenwich Village shops and restaurants, and area beaches.
