About this home

Discover the perfect blend of location and charm at this stunning 3 bedroom/2 full bath home in Norwalk. This residence effortlessly combines the elegance of traditional craftsmanship with modern conveniences, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. As you step inside, you'll appreciate the open floor plan accented by tall ceilings and exquisite woodwork details. The sun-filled front porch is an idyllic spot for your morning coffee, offering a tranquil start to your day. The large living room, complete with a cozy fireplace, serves as the perfect gathering place for friends and family. If you enjoy cooking and entertaining, you'll fall in love with the spacious dining room bathed in natural light, seamlessly connected to a kitchen outfitted with modern appliances including a warming draw. The main floor hosts a fabulous primary ensuite w/heated flooring, featuring sliders that open to a private deck-ideal for enjoying the serene outdoor setting. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a full bathroom with a double vanity, ensuring comfort for family or guests. Additionally, the walk-in attic provides convenient access to ample storage space. Private driveway parking. Experience the perfect harmony of old-world charm and modern living in this exceptional Norwalk home. Additional features, new oil tank, new hot water heater and Fig Tree!
