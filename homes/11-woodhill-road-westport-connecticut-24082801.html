About this home

Majestically sited on over an acre of land is this transitional center hall colonial, offering 6 bedrooms with 5 full baths and 3 half baths. The main entrance opens to a grand double height foyer with a sculptural staircase with lighting by a grand sputnik chandelier. Formal living and formal dining rooms flank the foyer. The open flow of the kitchen and family room take in views of the back yard and grand entertaining spaces. An office, bar, 2 half baths and a great mudroom finish the main floor. Ascend the stairs to the second level and you are greeted with 5 full bedrooms. The primary suite is oversized with a modern Dolomite Marble bath and large walk in closet. All the bedrooms have vaulted ceilings making each one grander than the next. The lower level features an expansive playroom, gym, bedroom, full bath and a second laundry room. The storage in the lower level is beyond belief. Closets galore!! The real piece de resistance is the outdoor pool and entertainment spaces. Built in kitchen with outdoor sink and pergola makes a nice spot for grilling. Expansive patio for lounging or dining. Covered patio area with fire pit and television for 3 season enjoyment. Salt water pool for summer fun. If you love to entertain then this is the new home for you.
