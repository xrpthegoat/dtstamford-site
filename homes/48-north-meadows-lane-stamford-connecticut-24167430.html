About this home

SELLER'S ARE RESPECTFULLY REQUESTING ALL OFFERS TO BE SUBMITTED BY SUNDAY 4/26 @ 5 pm. Situated on a private level acre in the prestigious Upper Newfield area of Stamford, this exceptional 4 bedroom, 3.5 bath designer home spanning over 5,000 sq ft sits on a coveted premier cul de sac. Approach via the stately circular driveway and grand double height foyer, where you're greeted by pristine newly refinished hardwood floors and a library with built-in bookshelves that sets an elegant tone. Meticulously renovated by the designer owner with high end custom finishes including vintage Italian Murano sconces, that you won't find in new construction. The formal dining room with exquisite textured finishes and connects to a stunning gourmet kitchen with custom cabinetry, professional grade appliances, and marble countertops. The breakfast area with French doors opens to the level backyard overlooking serene green space, while the family room captivates with soaring vaulted ceilings and a dramatic floor to ceiling fireplace. A marble laundry room with custom mudroom cabinetry off the garage completes the main level. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, a spa like marble bath with radiant floors, soaking tub & walk in shower, plus a bonus sitting/dressing room. Three additional bedrooms include one en suite with marble bath. A finished walk-out lower level is ideal for media, recreation, potential in-law suite, or a home gym.
