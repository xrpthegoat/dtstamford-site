About this home

Welcome to this unique cottage retreat-a beautiful single-family home nestled on a private, wooded lot, offering proximity to all that Silvermine and Norwalk have to offer. Perfect for those seeking serenity, the open kitchen and living room, complemented by a wood stove, radiate charm. The full bath conveniently houses laundry, while the spacious bedroom boasts vaulted ceilings, a bay window, a walk-in closet, and access to a secluded patio. Ascend the spiral staircase between the kitchen and living room to discover a bonus office/living space-an ideal haven for creative pursuits. With Silvermine's Gray Barn nearby and New Canaan Ave just down the road, this home offers both privacy and proximity. Enjoy the best of both worlds with private living and minimal maintenance, all at an affordable price. Welcome home to tranquility! Excellent credit required, please don't bother sending without it. High income to support rent price a must. No pets, no smoking, one year lease minimum.
