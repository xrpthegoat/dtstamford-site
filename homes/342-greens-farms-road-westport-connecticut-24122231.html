About this home

From city politics to select suburb - this home offers you the desired change with NYC still within reach. Nestled in Westport's prestigious Greens Farms enclave, this Victorian estate blends timeless character with modern sophistication. Originally built in 1861 and expanded in 2008, it offers over 6,600 sq ft of refined living space on 2.02 serene acres. Enjoy lush lawns, a private pool, outdoor kitchen, and hot tub-perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or quiet reflection. Inside, historic charm meets modern comfort with original fireplaces, soaring ceilings, and exquisite millwork. The gourmet kitchen, anchored by a large island, opens to an expansive great room and includes a butler's pantry with a walk-in commercial refrigerator. The lower level features arched doorways, natural light, and flexible space for a gym, playroom, office, or guest suite. A luxurious bar and theater-style entertainment area with mahogany tray ceilings adds sophistication, while a rooftop balcony makes every evening special. A six-car garage plus rear access for two more vehicles ensures convenience. Just minutes to Metro-North, I-95, beaches, and Westport's amazing restaurants and shops, this home offers the perfect blend of New England charm and modern luxury-all with NYC within easy reach.
