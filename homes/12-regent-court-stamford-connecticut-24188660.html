About this home

Welcome home to this fabulous Springdale Colonial, ideally situated on a quiet double cul-de-sac on the Darien border! The updated eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a center island and convenient garage access, flowing seamlessly into the dining room. The sun-filled living room with French doors opens to the impressive family room with vaulted ceiling, custom built-ins and sliders leading to the deck, patio, and fully fenced backyard where you'll enjoy the peaceful sounds of the Noroton River. Upstairs offers a spacious primary bedroom with a walk-in closet, two additional bedrooms and an updated hall bath. The partially finished lower level provides flexible living space, plus abundant storage and a 2nd access to the garage. Just minutes to Springdale Village shops, restaurants and train station. Recent improvements include a new driveway, front walkway, 200-amp electrical service and exterior paint, making this move-in-ready home not to be missed!
