About this home

Secluded Mediterranean inspired estate located on prestigious Bluewater Hill South with tennis courts and private beach rights to Compo Beach located steps away. Magnificent historical gardens designed by F.L. Olmstead of Central Park fame in the early 1900's. A crescent shaped pool and pergola over patio crown the front lawn and gardens. The upper living level open floor plan boasts water and garden views from the over sized windows throughout the rooms. The living and dining rooms feature a gas fireplace. A balcony off the living room has seating for many guests. Renovated kitchen with custom Maple cabinets and flooring, built in cabinets, stainless steel appliances and dining area. The den located off the kitchen with the laundry can function as an additional bedroom if needed. Master Suite has large renovated bath with custom built in cabinets. Enter on the garden level through the front door and see a sweeping staircase with mahogany railing or take the convenient elevator if needed. This level features three additional bedrooms, two full baths, family room and sun room. The attached two car garage entrance is through the sun room. This home is truly turn key, decorated and furnished with all the comforts needed to enjoy living this resort lifestyle! The upper garden is shared with the owners.
