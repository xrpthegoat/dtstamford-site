About this home

Picture-perfect vintage Colonial in a location that can't be beat! Oozing with charm and character, this freshly painted and beautifully maintained home features warm hardwood floors throughout and a living room with built-in cabinetry surrounding a wood-burning fireplace. The white kitchen boasts granite countertops and stainless steel appliances and flows into the adjacent formal dining room, with both offering direct access to the oversized deck overlooking the private backyard. A dedicated main-floor office provides the space needed for today's lifestyle. Recent improvements include exterior painting, a new HVAC split system, new lattice deck enclosure and more. Best of all, sidewalks along Vine Road lead to High Ridge Road, putting shopping, dining and everyday conveniences within easy reach. A charming home in an exceptionally convenient Stamford location.
