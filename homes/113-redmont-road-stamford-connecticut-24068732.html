About this home

Welcome to this delightful home nestled in the charming neighborhood of "Not So North" Stamford. Set on a truly exquisite half-acre of level grounds, this residence exudes a warm and inviting glow. The home features a spacious living room with an open floor plan that seamlessly connects to the dining area, complemented by a brick fireplace, perfect for cozy gatherings. The eat-in kitchen with a breakfast bar and two sinks provides ample space for culinary adventures. With three bedrooms and two full bathrooms on the main level, this home provides comfortable living for all. The lower level of the home adds even more versatility and functionality. It includes a second fireplace, creating a cozy atmosphere for additional living space. There's a spacious laundry area, and plenty of room for a gym, office, or whatever your needs may be. Plus, you'll find an abundance of storage space to keep everything organized. Many updates have been done to the house, making it a turn-key property ready for you to move in and enjoy. Don't miss the opportunity to make this cherished property your own!.
