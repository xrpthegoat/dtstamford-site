About this home

This ranch style home with contemporary flair offers tranquil lake setting along with a stunning gunite pool overlooking breath taking views of the lake. Situated on a private 2-acre oasis, this 4-bedroom, 2 full 2 half bath home features large picture windows and pristine hardwood floors in the spacious, open living and dining areas. The sunlit eat-in kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, high-end appliances, a separate Sub-Zero refrigerator and freezer, a fireplace, and sliders leading to a deck overlooking the lake. The bright family room combines comfort and style with a brick fireplace, built-in cabinetry, hardwood floors, a wet bar, and sliders which also open to the oversized deck overlooking the lake. The primary suite boasts stunning lake views, a generous sized walk-in closet, and a luxurious marble master bath with a Jacuzzi tub, separate shower, and double sink. Three additional bedrooms, a full bath & half bath, along with laundry/mudroom complete the main level. Head down to the walkout lower level, featuring picture windows and exposed brick, offering versatile space for a den, playroom, home office, library, or gym. Sliders leading to a covered patio. Nestled among the trees, enjoy the beautiful stone deck, heated pool, and lake views. Additional amenities include a generator, ample storage space, basement workshop, & newer roof & heat pump. This secluded North Stamford retreat is on a culdesac conveniently located just 3 miles from the Merritt Parkway
