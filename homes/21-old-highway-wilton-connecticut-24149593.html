About this home

BEST AND FINAL, TUESDAY 3/10, 5:30PM - Mary Gannett House Circa. 1931, charming South Wilton Opportunity - Perfect for Builders or Ambitious Renovators! Located on a desirable 1.70 acre lot on a picturesque street in one of South Wilton's most notable and central locations. This 3 Bedroom Cape/Colonial Revival-style is Registered with the Historic Resources Inventory and boasts a side-gabled roof that extends in a saltbox style. The addition of a free standing one car garage to the house provides endless potential as either a custom-built dream home or a major renovation project. This 1930s classic has been a beloved family retreat for years, and while it now requires significant work, the stunning setting and unmatched location provide a rare chance for builders and investors to create something truly special. Residents will enjoy an elevated panoramic view of the natural surroundings, while convenient access to Wilton's award-winning schools, shopping, trains, and local amenities. Please note that this is an Estate, House and Garage being sold AS IS; the buyer is to perform all due diligence as no property condition disclosure is available. However, a pre-inspection report for the chimney is available, along with all relevant paperwork. Updates include: New oil tank 2019. Increase Electric Service to 200 amps-1990, Septic Inspection-12/25, tank pumped and riser installed. Water Purification System inspected-1/26.
