About this home

Built by the renowned Kellogg Brothers, this exceptional custom home showcases timeless craftsmanship, architectural elegance, and meticulous attention to detail. A circular driveway leads to a dramatic two-story foyer with Bordeaux-style Brazilian cherry parquet flooring. The gourmet kitchen is appointed with a Sub-Zero refrigerator, Viking six-burner range with griddle, pot filler, warming drawer, two dishwashers, custom cabinetry, walk-in pantry, and sliders opening to the expansive deck. The spectacular great room features soaring ceilings, a fireplace, dramatic two-story windows, extensive custom millwork, refinished hardwood floors, a brass chandelier, and sliders that seamlessly connect to the outdoor living spaces. Additional highlights include a sun-filled living room with fireplace and bow window, elegant dining room with window seat, and a richly paneled office with French doors and access to the screened porch. Outside, enjoy a heated infinity-edge Gunite pool, grotto seating area, mature landscaping, and manicured privet hedges creating an exceptional private oasis. The property also offers direct access to scenic walking trails connecting to 93-acres of the Wilton Land Trust and Harris Smith Preserve. The home offers six bedrooms, including five upstairs and one on the walk-out lower level. The finished lower level features a recreation room with a wood-burning fireplace. Grey Rocks truly depicts luxury, comfort, and timeless design.
