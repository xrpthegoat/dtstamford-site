About this home

Beautifully maintained single-family home offering 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, with hardwood floors throughout, perfectly move-in ready, this home provides comfortable living with a thoughtful layout, over 1100 sq ft, a great condo alternative. Located in the heart of Westport, you'll enjoy convenient access to all the town's amenities-shops, restaurants, beaches, and more-while commuters will love the quick connection to I-95, the nearby train station. and excellent Westport school system, consistently ranked among the best in the state. This home is designed for easy living, featuring bright and airy living spaces, a functional kitchen, and well-proportioned bedrooms. The home has many improvements including improved crawlspace, new electrical panel, roof (with 30-yr warrantee), newer Central cooling and heating system and water heater, thermopane windows, hardwood floor, Wi-Fi thermostat. Don't miss this opportunity to own a turnkey property in one of Fairfield County's most desirable communities.
