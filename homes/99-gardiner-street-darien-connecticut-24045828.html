About this home

Welcome to this charming Cape Cod home designed for both comfort and style. The bright, sunny rooms create a welcoming atmosphere throughout the house, enhancing the excellent flow from one space to another. The lovely living room with fireplace, dining room and renovated kitchen and baths with screened porch on the first floor has wonderful ample spaces! All three bedrooms are conveniently situated on the upper level, providing a private and peaceful retreat. The eat-in kitchen has been thoughtfully renovated, boasting white cabinetry and sleek stainless steel appliances, making it a delightful space for both cooking and casual dining. Step out onto the pretty screened porch, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or relaxing in the evening, while overlooking the professionally landscaped property that adds a touch of nature's beauty to your everyday life. Both bathrooms have been tastefully updated, offering modern amenities and a fresh, clean look. Additionally, the finished basement provides great additional space, ideal for an office or a playroom, catering to your varied needs. This home combines classic charm with modern updates, making it a perfect place to be home! Easy walk to Darien Commons and Noroton Heights train station. Don't miss this gem!
