About this home

AVAILABLE JULY 1ST! Charming 3BR, 1.5BA Victorian blending classic character with modern updates. Relax on the inviting front porch. Gracious foyer with high ceilings leads to a spacious, light-filled living room and formal dining room-ideal for entertaining. Updated kitchen features center island, stainless steel appliances, gas range, dishwasher, wine fridge, decorative fireplace, and separate workspace. Upstairs offers a generous primary bedroom with two large closets, two additional bedrooms, and a full bath with claw-foot tub. Walk-up attic provides abundant storage. Lower level includes washer/dryer. Level backyard with large deck for seasonal entertaining. Private off-street parking for 2 cars. Hardwood floors, central air, and natural gas heat. Pet friendly. Excellent credit, references, and proof of income required. Prime location-walk to South Norwalk train, SONO dining, shopping, nightlife, and the Maritime Aquarium.
