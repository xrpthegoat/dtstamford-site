About this home

This terrific property, recently renovated from top to bottom with beautiful finishes, is ideally located on a quiet cul-de-sac in East Norwalk and just a quick walk to Metro North, great restaurants, Calf Pasture Beach and much more! Enjoy the benefits of residing in a small condominium complex where all exterior maintenance is taken care of (at no extra cost) while living in the closest equivalent to a single family home which includes a full basement and a private deck just off the kitchen. The first floor features a front to back living room with wood burning fireplace, a large modern eat-in-kitchen (featuring quartz countertops, stainless appliances and abundant storage), powder room and a flex room which could serve as a formal dining room, home office, den or even a cozy guest room. The second floor features two well proportioned bedrooms and a bright new full bathroom. Recent improvements include fresh paint, new floors, new baths, new kitchen, new energy efficient windows, new furnace and so much more. There are 2 dedicated off street assigned parking spaces. This is a quality home and doesn't feel like a rental property. Come and see for yourself. Excellent credit and references required. Pets may be considered on a case by case basis with a strong offer. Available 9/1/2026
