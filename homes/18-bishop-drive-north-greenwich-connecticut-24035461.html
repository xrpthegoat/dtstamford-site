About this home

This extremely well cared for and improved expanded ranch style home, has been customized with outstanding features. Living spaces with remolded kitchen in 2013 with tray ceilings, cherry wood cabinetry, with newer appliances, granite counters, double sinks, center island seats 6, mud room entry & sliders, adjacent family room, energy efficient laundry room, pantry, tiled floor, and closets galore. An outstanding size IN-Law apartment on 1st floor grade level entry. Bright light beams into the living room & dinning room with hardwood floors through-out the main floor, with spacious primary bedroom w/full bath & walking-in closet. Remolded in 2023, hallway full & half baths. The special bonus room of the finished attic lends itself to many uses, finished with insulated A/C & heated, new windows, bamboo floors and hardwood staircase. The serene natural fencing in the lush back yard, all exterior improvements in 2018 to 2023 for the roof ice/snow shield/ to the walkways, to new cedar siding, and heated garage, new door & opening system. Utilities upgrades are numerous. Commercial grade Mitsubishi hyper heat pump, programmable humidifier, and generator too. So much more in this desirable neighborhood location, close to parks, planes, trains and shopping & schools. The Merritt Parkway near by for an easy drive into NYC. Do not miss this exceptional opportunity for a comfortable lifestyle living in the outstanding community of Glenville in Greenwich, in coveted Fairfield County.
