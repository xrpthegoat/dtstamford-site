About this home

18 Ivy Place provides a premier opportunity to secure a high-demand multi-family asset in one of Norwalk's most accessible neighborhoods. This well-maintained two-family residence consists of two distinct 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom units, presenting an ideal configuration for a low-maintenance investment or an owner-occupant looking to leverage rental income. The property shines with recent capital improvements that ensure a move-in-ready experience. The first-floor unit has been refreshed with brand-new carpeting and a crisp, modern paint palette, while the exterior benefits from newly installed front steps. Each residence provides a comfortable and efficient layout designed to meet the needs of today's urban renters. Logistics are a standout feature of this property, which includes a large, private parking area and a level backyard-rare and highly valued assets in such a central location. Buyers can do their own due diligence of potentially expanding backwards. Situated just moments from the vibrant energy of Downtown Norwalk, you are perfectly positioned near premier shopping, diverse dining, and major commuting routes.
