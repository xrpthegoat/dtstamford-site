About this home

Stunning waterfront building lot in the private downtown Indian Harbor Association with 24-hour security. This gated community has many luxury waterfront homes in the district. The expansive lot has 1.9 acres in a 1/2 zone, allowing for a substantial home to be built. This lot is not subdividable. There is 290 feet of waterfront on Long Island Sound with its own pier and dock. The owners had spent over a year with their architect Charles Hilton to design a home. The prior home has been taken down and there is a plan in place for a new septic system (approved and permitted). This remarkable location is close to downtown Greenwich with restaurants, shops and the train. Truly an amazing property on the water.
