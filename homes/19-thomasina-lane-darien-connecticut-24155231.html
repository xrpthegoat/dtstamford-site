About this home

Meticulously maintained and built in 2016 by Doherty & Deleo Development, this five-bedroom colonial offers four levels of thoughtfully designed living on a highly desirable in-town cul-de-sac. The home features a bright open layout, 9' ceilings, hardwood floors throughout, and a spacious kitchen with walk-in pantry that flows seamlessly into the main living areas. An oversized mudroom, powder room, and two-car garage complete the first floor. Upstairs the primary suite includes a vaulted ceiling, walk in closet, and oversized bathroom. The finished third floor and lower level with wine cellar provide flexible space for work, recreation, or guests. Energy-efficient features include Andersen Energy Star windows, high-performance insulation, high-efficiency HVAC, and recently added solar panels delivering significant cost savings. Set on a large, flat property, the backyard offers wonderful outdoor living space with a flagstone terrace and site plans for a future pool. Located with convenient access to nearby parks, shops, beaches, and other town amenities in the coveted Hindley school district - all just 60-minutes to NYC.
