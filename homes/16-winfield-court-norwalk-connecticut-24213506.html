About this home

A compelling opportunity in East Norwalk! Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac, this home offers solid bones, hardwood floors beneath the existing carpeting, and the flexibility to move right in or update to suit your personal style. Being sold as-is, this property presents an excellent opportunity for buyers looking to add their own touches and build equity in a desirable neighborhood. Location, location, location! Enjoy a private backyard with a deck, ideal for relaxing or entertaining, and take advantage of the walkable lifestyle with the beach and neighborhood favorite Spot Deli nearby. The home is also within walking distance of the Norwalk Train Station, making NYC commuting a breeze, with convenient access to the Westport Train Station as well. Enjoy close proximity to Shorehaven Golf Club and Long Island Sound, plus an easy 10-minute drive to vibrant South Norwalk (SoNo), known for its outstanding restaurants, lively nightlife, and waterfront attractions. Stew Leonard's is also just 10 minutes away! Whether you're looking to make cosmetic updates over time or create a home that reflects your own vision, this is a wonderful opportunity to unlock the potential of this property and make your mark in East Norwalk. With its desirable setting, convenient commuter access, and proximity to beaches, dining, and local amenities, this home offers an exciting opportunity for homeowners and investors alike!
