About this home

Welcome to 57 Meadowpark Avenue North! Located in the Mid-Ridges neighborhood, this property sits on a private lot with mature plantings and a babbling brook in the back yard. The Mid-Ridges location has easy access to shopping, restaurants, highways and public transportation. One owner for nearly 50 years this home is a rare opportunity for a new homeowner to make it their own. The expansive dining room is perfect for holiday get-togethers and family entertaining. The deck off of the dining and family rooms is perfect for summer barbecues. The light-filled family room includes a cathedral ceiling, three walls of windows and a door leading to the deck. There is a main level bedroom and full bathroom. The walk-out lower level is partially finished with in-law possibility.
