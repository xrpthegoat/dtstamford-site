About this home

Welcome to 29 Weatherbell Drive, a classic 1960 ranch-style home located in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood, offering 2,044 square feet of comfortable living space with 4 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms on an expansive 0.9-acre lot. One-level living doesn't get more spacious than this. The main floor showcases an open-concept living area where the kitchen, dining room, living room, and family room flow together seamlessly, complemented by a warm family room featuring vaulted ceilings with skylights and a large deck overlooking the property. Tucked away are four spacious bedrooms and two full bathrooms, perfectly separated from the active living areas. The finished lower level adds incredible flexibility with a sprawling playroom with a second fireplace, a bonus room, half bathroom, laundry and pantry, and a convenient basement garage. Step outside into your own private backyard oasis, complete with an inviting in-ground pool and a spacious stone patio for the ultimate summer barbecues and weekend lounging. All of this is located in desirable Cranbury, only a few minutes from Cranbury Park, the Merritt Parkway, and more-complete with access to Calf Pasture Beach. This spacious gem is ready to welcome you home!
