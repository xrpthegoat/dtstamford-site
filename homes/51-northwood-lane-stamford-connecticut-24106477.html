About this home

Spacious & Updated North Stamford Ranch with In-Law Potential! This updated Ranch offers generous living spaces, flexible layout, and easy in-law or au pair potential-perfectly situated in a prime lower North Stamford location! Freshly painted throughout with gleaming refinished hardwood floors, this home also boasts updated bathrooms and an impressive eat-in kitchen. Step into the spacious Entry Foyer that flows into a large, light-filled Living Room with a classic fireplace and picture window. The separate Dining Room easily accommodates holiday gatherings and dinner parties. The expansive Eat-In Kitchen features two skylights and a large adjacent Laundry Room for added convenience. The Bedroom Wing includes a well-sized Primary Suite with a Walk-In Closet and Full Bath featuring a separate shower. Two additional Bedrooms and a Full Hall Bath complete the main level. The walk-out Lower Level offers incredible versatility-with a Family Room (fireplace and sliders to the patio and yard), Playroom with wet bar, Full Bath, additional Bedroom, and a large workshop that connects directly to the oversized 2-car Garage. Fantastic location just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping centers, and a quick ride to the train, I-95, and the vibrant Downtown Stamford & Harbor Point districts. A must-see!
