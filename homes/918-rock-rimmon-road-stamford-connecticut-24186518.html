About this home

Nestled on nearly two acres at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable North Stamford, this lovely Colonial offers nearly 4,000 square feet of elegant living space. A welcoming foyer leads to sun-filled formal living and dining room, ideal for entertaining. The spacious eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, abundant cabinetry, and a bright breakfast area overlooking the scenic property. The adjoining family room boasts a fireplace and French doors that open to the outdoor living space, creating the perfect setting for indoor and outdoor gatherings. A first-floor bedroom offers private bathroom for guests, extended family, or a home office. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features hardwood floors and a luxurious bath with a whirlpool tub, separate shower, and double-sink vanity. Three additional generously sized bedrooms and full bath complete the upper level. Additional highlights include hardwood floors, two wood burning fireplaces, central air, main-level laundry, a full basement with endless potential, and an attached three-car garage. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, and major commuter routes, this exceptional home offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience.
