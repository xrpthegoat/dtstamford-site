About this home

Just in Time for Summer! Ideally located minutes from Binney Park, downtown Old Greenwich, and the beach, this recently renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers easy, comfortable living in a prime location. The open kitchen features a generous dining area that flows seamlessly to a private deck-perfect for summer entertaining. The cozy living room with a fireplace invites relaxation, while the main-floor bedroom offers a great option for a home office. Downstairs, the walk-out lower level adds an additional 557 sq ft of living space, including a family room, mudroom, large laundry room, ample storage, and access to a beautifully sized yard. A detached shed with electricity adds even more versatility. Welcome home to Old Greenwich living at its best.
