About this home

Rare one-story gem! Discover this beautifully designed ranch home nestled on a flat 1-acre lot in the desirable Cos Cob neighborhood in Greenwich, CT. Offering 2,751 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this home provides an ideal layout for both entertaining and everyday living. Step inside to find a gourmet kitchen with custom cabinetry, granite countertops and Wolf appliances, a spacious center island, and ample storage-a dream for any chef! The home features four generously sized bedrooms, along with a living room, family room, office, and formal dining room, creating flexible spaces to fit your needs. Outside, enjoy outdoor living on the beautiful stone terrace or unwind on the covered back porch. The full walk-up attic offers exceptional potential to customize and expand. This home is a find-offering convenience, style, and endless possibilities. Located in the North Mianus and Eastern Middle School area. Don't miss this incredible opportunity to own a piece of Greenwich luxury!
