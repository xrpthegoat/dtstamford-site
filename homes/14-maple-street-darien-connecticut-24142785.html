About this home

Prime Darien lifestyle awaits at this updated Colonial, perfectly situated for the commuter or a local downsizer, as it's just steps from the Noroton Heights Train Station and a short stroll to the vibrant shops and restaurants of Darien Commons. This well-maintained home seamlessly blends original charm, gleaming hardwood floors and elegant crown molding, with modern luxury. A stunning Primary Suite serves as a true sanctuary with a vaulted ceiling and a spa-like ensuite featuring an oversized soaking tub and a therapeutic steam rain shower. Beyond the three additional bedrooms, a finished attic provides flexible space, ideal for a home office or a magical play area as well as a partially finished basement. The private backyard "oasis" boasts a spacious deck with a hot tub overlooking a level yard that backs directly onto Baker Park. Spend cold winter nights curled up by the living room fireplace and warm summer nights on the spacious deck. Great entertaining spaces and tons of storage!
