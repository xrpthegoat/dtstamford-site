About this home

Seller is asking for Highest and Best Offer by Tuesday 10/14 by 5PM. This stunning Mid-Century Modern home, thoughtfully renovated by award-winning architect Rob Sanders, blends timeless design with modern comfort. Walls of glass invite natural light and showcase the wooded surroundings, while exposed roof rafters throughout the living space add warmth and architectural character. The open-concept kitchen features stained cherry cabinetry, sleek black granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances-perfect for both entertaining and everyday living. The home offers excellent functionality and flow, with four spacious bedrooms including a large en-suite primary, plus a flexible den/office/playroom to suit your needs. A convenient mudroom off the garage is located on the lower level, ideal for managing daily comings and goings. Nestled on a private lane, the property offers peace and privacy just minutes from the town centers of Wilton and Weston. Seller is asking for Highest and Best Offer by Tuesday 10/14 by 5PM.
