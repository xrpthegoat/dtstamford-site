About this home

Nestled on one of Rowayton's most desirable streets, 20 Indian Spring Rd stands on 0.8 acres, exuding charm and sophistication. This impeccably maintained home, built in 1940, blends old-world elegance with modern amenities. Inside, discover a stunningly renovated kitchen by nuKitchen, all-new bathrooms and updated mudroom, offering a turnkey experience in the heart of Rowayton. The main level features an inviting entry with a powder room, a spacious living room with 1 of the home's 3 fireplaces, a cozy den, and an impressive dining room that flows from the updated kitchen. A screened sun-porch offers views of the flagstone patio and lush backyard. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom includes a fireplace, complemented by two additional bedrooms. The lower level boasts a 4th en-suite bedroom, expansive family space, and a mudroom leading to a two-car garage. The vast backyard is ideal for outdoor entertaining. Just minutes from Rowayton's vibrant amenities, this home marries elegance with modern living. Additional square footage on the lower level.
