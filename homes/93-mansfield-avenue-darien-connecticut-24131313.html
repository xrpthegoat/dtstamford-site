About this home

Fabulous five bedroom home in sought-after, in-town location. Originally built in 2006 this impeccably maintained home offers it all. Gourmet kitchen which opens to a spacious family room, inviting outdoor patio and private backyard perfect for entertaining. Professionally-designed landscaping with outdoor lighting, built-in grill area, fencing and driveway gates. Includes a sunny second floor family room as well as a third floor playroom/office area with a guest suite. Custom millwork throughout, generous closet and storage space, a beautiful back staircase leading to the kitchen and mudroom, a large unfinished basement with 10ft ceilings, approximately 1500 square feet and a Foresight powered golf sim. A truly move-in ready home with many high-end amenities.
