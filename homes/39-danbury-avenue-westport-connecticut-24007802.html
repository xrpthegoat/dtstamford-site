About this home

Introducing this exceptional home just a block away from water's edge in the popular Compo Beach area of Westport. Custom-built by J Tallman Builders and designed by architect Jonathan Wagner, this gem is privately nestled at the end of a cul de sac. The stunning exterior showcases cedar and clapboard siding, stainless steel accent tiles, a metal roof, and energy-efficient windows. The nearly half-acre property, an oversized lot for the beach area, boasts a bluestone terrace, built-in grill, and a cozy fire pit area, creating the perfect outdoor retreat. Inside, the entrance hall features elegant imported porcelain tiles with radiant underfloor heating, leading to an open plan living area with a granite surround fireplace and access to the outdoors. The gourmet kitchen, complete with chef's appliances and Maistri imported cabinetry, serves as the heart of the home, where all gatherings are savored! The first floor also includes a fun three-season room, a den or fifth bedroom with a full bathroom, and a stylish powder room. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a wooden-paneled sitting room, a hidden dressing room/walk-in closet + a relaxing spa bathroom, while three additional bedrooms, each with private bathrooms, provide comfort. The home is equipped with geothermal heating, solar panels, and professional sound wiring, making it a modern coastal living haven. Get ready to host your summer soirees and embrace the beach lifestyle!
