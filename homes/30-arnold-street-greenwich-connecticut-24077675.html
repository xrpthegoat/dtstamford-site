About this home

High quality renovation and addition was done to this beautifully crafted home in 2015, adding 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 3 full baths and laundry to the newly built 2nd floor, while the main level and lower level were thoroughly gut renovated to create sophisticated open flow living spaces. Top of the line Kitchen with custom cabinetry has an oversized island for food prep, storage and seating. It opens to the living room with wood burning fireplace, the dining room with a dry bar, and a large family room, as well as to the rear deck, yard and heated pool. Gorgeous master suite has 10' ceilings, walk-in closet, and sumptuous bath with oversized shower. 4 double bedrooms, 1 with an ensuite bath, offer ample bedrooms or options for a home office or exercise space. The finished lower level has a spacious rec room and a full bath, which is well located for the heated pool. There is beautiful custom millwork throughout. The level yard and heated pool are fully fenced. Wired for up to a 20kw generator, which could be installed on the south side of the house.
