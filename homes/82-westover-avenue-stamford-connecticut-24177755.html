About this home

Welcome to this charming Colonial nestled in Stamford's highly desirable Westover neighborhood. Set on over an acre of landscaped property, this spacious and well-maintained home offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and classic appeal. The main level features a living room with fireplace, formal dining room, powder room, and a large eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and sliders leading to a deck-ideal for entertaining or relaxing outdoors. A warm and inviting family room just off the kitchen adds additional living space and everyday comfort. The second floor offers a spacious primary suite with walk-in closets and a beautifully appointed bath featuring double sinks, whirlpool tub, and separate shower. Three additional generously sized bedrooms and an updated full bath complete the upper level. Finished third floor provides flexible extra space perfect for a home office, gym, or playroom. Additional highlights include central air, central vacuum, security system, generator, laundry room, attached two-car garage, and a full basement offering abundant storage. Conveniently located just minutes from Downtown Stamford, Old Greenwich, shopping, dining, parks, and major commuting routes. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy space, privacy, and convenience in one of Stamford's most sought-after locations.
