About this home

Welcome home to this spacious, bright and sunny townhouse offering a wonderful combination of comfort, flexibility, and convenience. The inviting floor plan features two generously sized bedrooms plus a versatile loft-perfect for a home office, guest space, den, or quiet retreat. Hardwood floors throughout most rooms add warmth and character, while abundant natural light creates a bright and welcoming atmosphere. The living and dining areas both feature sliders opening to the stone patio, making it easy to enjoy indoor-outdoor living, entertaining, or simply relaxing outside. Upstairs, the bedrooms each offer sliders to the balcony, bringing in even more natural light and providing additional outdoor space to enjoy. With plenty of storage throughout, this home offers both space and functionality. The well-maintained complex features attractive green spaces, an in-ground pool, and clubhouse-perfect for enjoying the warmer months. All this in a great location close to shopping, dining, transportation, and everyday conveniences. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy easy townhouse living with the space and amenities you've been looking for!
