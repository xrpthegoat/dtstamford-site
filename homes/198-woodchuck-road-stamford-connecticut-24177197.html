About this home

Completely rebuilt and reimagined, this exceptional Mid-Century Modern ranch lives like new construction and offers a rare blend of architectural sophistication, privacy, and lakefront serenity. Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, the home is surrounded by mature trees and immersed in nature, with approximately 300 feet of private lake frontage and walls of glass framing breathtaking water views from nearly every room. Designed for modern living, the home features a sleek contemporary kitchen open to a spacious family room made for gathering, where abundant natural light, soaring ceilings, and stunning lake views create an unforgettable backdrop for everyday living and entertaining. Adjacent to the kitchen, the inviting living and dining room is anchored by a striking stone wood-burning fireplace, offering a warm and intimate retreat. Three spacious bedrooms plus a private office or optional fourth bedroom with en-suite bath provide exceptional flexibility. Integrated smart-home technology and a built-in audio system enhance comfort and convenience throughout the home, while direct lake access invites kayaking, fishing, or simply enjoying the peaceful natural setting. Wildlife glides overhead and dances across the lake, creating a setting that feels worlds away-yet shopping, dining, and transportation are just five minutes away. A truly one-of-a-kind waterfront sanctuary where modern design, complete privacy, and nature exist in perfect harmony.
