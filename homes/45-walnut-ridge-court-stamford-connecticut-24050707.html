About this home

In the limited NEW HOME construction inventory, 45 Walnut Ridge Court is ready for its owner. Transitional in look and feel both in and out, this home provides comfortable open spaces, clean lines and designer selected finishes on all three levels. The finished lower level will include the 4th bedroom with over sized windows, full bath and a rec room. This lot is gently perched right in the cul de sac and provides a picturesque rear yard. A premium mid city Stamford location tucked well off the beaten path for quiet enjoyment yet putting you minutes from every convenience you will need. City water and sewer, natural gas for heating and cooking plus underground utilities. Traveling distance from Walnut Ridge Court to the Stamford Train Station is a mere 2.5 miles and CT Transit has a stop practically at the front door. A grand opportunity for NEW construction.
