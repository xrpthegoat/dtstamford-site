About this home

Impeccably updated 5 bedroom colonial with a modern feel, convenient to town, yet private and quiet. Beautiful hardwood floors, 9 ft ceilings on the main level. Gourmet kitchen has custom cabinetry, high end appliances and a large center island. Floor to ceiling glass doors provide easy access to a large deck and brilliant sunlight. The 2nd level features primary bedroom, 2 generously sized closets and spacious primary bath. Two other bedrooms, a full bath and laundry complete the 2nd floor. 3rd floor offers 2 additional bedrooms, full bath and sitting area. On the lower level is a large family/rec room, 2nd powder room, mudroom and access to the 2 car garage. Full house generator. Adjacent to 135 acre Fairchild Wild Flower Sanctuary with 8 miles of nature trails.
