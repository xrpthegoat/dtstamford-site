About this home

Modern Luxury 1-Bedroom Rental with High-End Finishes in Prime Stamford Location. Available for rent, this impeccably renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bath unit combines sophisticated style with everyday comfort. Just two years ago, the space underwent a full transformation, featuring a designer-inspired eat-in kitchen outfitted with custom shaker cabinetry, sleek brushed gold hardware, quartz counters, stainless steel appliances, open shelving, and upscale finishes throughout. The spa-quality bathroom is wrapped in shiplap and a premium shower system with four body sprays, a rain shower, a waterfall feature, and a handheld wand-perfect for starting or ending your day in relaxation. Beautifully refinished hardwood floors run throughout the apartment, and every inch of the unit has been thoughtfully upgraded-from new windows and solid-core doors to custom hardware and fresh paint. Smart home touches include all-new electrical, dimmable recessed lighting, stylish fixtures, and app-controlled switches and outlets for modern convenience. The spacious bedroom offers warmth and functionality with hardwood floors, smart lighting, and a custom-designed walk-in closet-an uncommon bonus in a one-bedroom rental. Located just minutes from Stamford's vibrant downtown, the beach, and I-95, and only a five-minute stroll to the Stamford Dog Park and Courtland Avenue Park, this unit offers the perfect blend of elegance, convenience, and lifestyle.
