About this home

Coffee in hand strolling out onto the balcony offering wooded views encapsulated in one's own private sanctuary is the optimal way to start the day. 271 Butternut Ln perfect confluence of location, condition and amenities. This 4 bedroom 2 full bath home is located in North Stamford not far from the Merritt Parkway. The home's natural light traverses throughout to create a warm feeling. The culinary landscape's recent renovation includes a newer refrigerator, dishwasher and stove, and a newer quartz countertop. The sizable living and dining rooms offer up enough space thanks to sliders to the front balcony. The primary bedroom and two other bedrooms complete the main level. The 1st floor's level presents a family room with fireplace and bedroom / bathroom and laundry room with newer washer and dryer. The lower level features an open layout that can serve as a rec/fitness room, with a dedicated office setting or whatever satisfies your household needs. The home boasts a number of esthetic and mechanical upgrades including newer furnace and hot water heater and both garage doors. Moreover, the oil tank is approximately 5 years old. Welcome home!
