About this home

A meticulous renovation and expansion has transformed this quintessential New England Colonial into a refined retreat on 1 beautifully landscaped acre with pool, set along one of Mid-Country's most premier streets. Thoughtful design and attention to detail create over 6,000 sq. ft. of light-filled living space with sweeping views of the lush grounds. Elegant formal rooms are complemented by a sun-drenched family room, custom kitchen, breakfast room, and 2 offices. French doors from nearly every room allow for a seamless connection to the outdoor entertaining spaces. The luxurious primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, private balcony, dual walk-in closets, and a marble-clad spa like bath. Four additional bedrooms and three full baths complete the second level. The finished lower level features a media room, gym, cedar closet, and generous storage. Outside, a bluestone terrace with built-in grill and outdoor fireplace overlooks the 20x40 pool, spa, and park-like setting. An exceptional opportunity just minutes from downtown Greenwich.
