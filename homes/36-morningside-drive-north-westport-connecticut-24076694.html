About this home

Located on a full acre of property and set back at the end of a quiet enclave off the main road, this beautiful home with a pool was built in 2005. Featuring 12 rooms, which include 5 bedrooms, 5 full and 1 half baths, a full walk-out lower level, and a spacious atmosphere, there is flexible and well-appointed space for everyone. The open gourmet kitchen/family room leads out to a large stone patio with perimeter walls. The pool has an automatic cover and a pool patio. Tall ceilings, bay windows and more give this home a wonderful atmosphere. Natural gas heating, town water & sewer. The convenient location is near schools, town ball fields, shopping, and 1.5 +/- mile to the Greens Farms station with Metro North railroad service to Grand Central in NYC. Westport's beaches and Longshore Club Park are nearby for summer fun. The sought-after Fairfield County lifestyle awaits.
