About this home

Set in the North Stamford neighborhood near the New Canaan line, this vintage Colonial offers character in a peaceful, private setting. Enjoy seasonal views of Laurel Reservoir from this well maintained and thoughtfully updated home. The remodeled kitchen showcases recent stainless-steel appliances, a greenhouse window and an adjacent powder room. The kitchen is open to a generous breakfast and seating area, complete with sliders leading to a deck overlooking private surroundings. A large formal dining room and inviting living room, both with beamed ceilings and built-ins, provide comfortable living spaces with a striking two-sided stone fireplace with gas inserts. The upper floor has three bedrooms plus a sunny office. The spacious primary bedroom has an ensuite bath and walk-in closet. The third/largest bedroom could also be suitable as a family room. Hardwood floors run throughout the home, enhancing its warmth and appeal. A favorite unique feature is the screened-in porch with a stone floor, connected to the home and perched above the two-car garage-a special multi season retreat for entertaining or relaxing. Additional highlights include perennial gardens, stone patio, four-zone heating, whole house generator and paved off-street parking. This very special property combines privacy, convenience, and classic New England style in an unbeatable location.
