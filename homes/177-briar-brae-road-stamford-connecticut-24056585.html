About this home

Discover a truly unique home, nestled privately on over an acre of serene land in North Stamford. This remarkable property has been generously donated by its former owner to benefit the Midwest Food Bank, turning this house into a home with heart. This custom-built home seamlessly combines historical elements with modern comforts, creating a truly unique living experience. The main floor features a primary bedroom with en-suite bath, providing convenience and comfort. The living room boasts a vaulted and beamed ceiling, complemented by a gorgeous Vermont soapstone fireplace, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Wide plank flooring graces most rooms, adding to the home's rustic elegance. A standout feature is the walnut wood balusters, originally from General Grant's home in Washington D.C., adding historical significance and character. The unique tube elevator provides easy access across three levels, ensuring accessibility for all. Step outside to enjoy the tranquil outdoor gazebo, perfect for unwinding amidst the natural beauty of the surroundings. This home is a rare find, offering a distinctive blend of history, character, and privacy. Don't miss the opportunity to make this home your own, and in the process, make a lasting impact.
