About this home

Welcome to this Manhattan style 3 bedroom 2 bathroom apartment in Downtown Greenwich. As you go inside, you'll notice the beautiful hardwood flooring and custom millwork.The centralized gourmet kitchen is adorned with custom cabinetry, complemented by stunning quartz countertops and a stylish backsplash along with Bosch and Thermador kitchen appliance package, and a full-height wine refrigerator. Sophistication extends to the baths, featuring Phylrich plumbing fixtures. A double-sided linear water vapor fireplace and custom glass and steel frame doors connect spaces. Visual comfort decorative light fixtures grace the interior, providing both illumination and style to every corner of this remarkable apartment.
