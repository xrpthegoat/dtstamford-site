About this home

Private and serene, 58 Arrow Head Drive offers a rare combination of character, openness, and a spectacular North Stamford setting. Set high above a tranquil pond on two lush, wooded acres, this unique country home is surrounded by mature trees, beautiful gardens and exceptional privacy. A dramatic great room with hardwood flooring, skylights, and a magnificent stone fireplace anchors the home. It opens to the expansive kitchen, dining, and family room areas. The kitchen features custom pine cabinetry, serpentine marble and teak countertops, and two large islands. Jerusalem stone flooring extends throughout the kitchen, dining, and family room spaces. French doors lead to an oversized deck overlooking the parklike grounds. The home offers four bedrooms including a spacious primary suite with walk-in-closet and en-suite bath, two additional full baths, and a dramatic powder room. The lower levels feature a large rec room with fireplace, storage areas, an exterior plant room and a two-car garage. A wonderful blend of warmth, character, and natural beauty just minutes from downtown Stamford and the Merritt Parkway.
