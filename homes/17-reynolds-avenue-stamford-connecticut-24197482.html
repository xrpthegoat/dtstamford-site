About this home

Behold this Gem nestled in one of the most desirable neighborhoods in Stamford! This spacious Cape wonderfully located a respectable distance from downtown is a great fit for those who enjoy everything Stamford Malls/Shopping centers have to offer with the perfect Mix of suburban lifestyle as the property is located on a cul de sac. The location speaks for itself for work opportunities conveniently located in the second largest city in the state of CT. To boast the proximity of the Big Apple would be an understatement with the Merritt & I-95 being just a hop and a skip away. The home is equipped with a newer Electrical panel, Furnace & Water Heater. The enclosed porch gives you a beautiful view of the street and the city upon entrance many will notice the open living room with a bar connection to the kitchen( with Newer appliances). Each bedroom being decently sized with 2 of them being on the main floor the third bedroom being almost the entirety of the home from front to back also includes a lot of storage space in addition to the walk-in Closet. The Full Bathroom being recently updated .The basement which is also a 1 car garage. Offers decent potential to be finished or for those who need more storage. The exterior of the property offers not only an Apple and a Pear tree but a beautiful red deck and a Cabana for the summertime cookout spot.
