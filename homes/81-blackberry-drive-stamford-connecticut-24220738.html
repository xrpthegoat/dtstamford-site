About this home

Welcome to 81 Blackberry Drive, a beautifully maintained contemporary home offering approximately 3,417 square feet of living space, plus a fully finished walk-out lower level. Featuring 5 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, this spacious residence offers a versatile floor plan ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The home's contemporary architecture creates a bright, open atmosphere, complemented by an updated kitchen and thoughtfully maintained interiors. Recent mechanical improvements, including an updated furnace and water systems, provide added peace of mind. The finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional flexibility for recreation, a home gym, office, or additional entertaining space. Set on approximately 1.05 acres of beautifully manicured grounds, the property is surrounded by mature trees, established plantings, and lush landscaping that create a peaceful, private setting. Located in desirable North Stamford, this home offers a wonderful combination of space, privacy, and contemporary comfort.
