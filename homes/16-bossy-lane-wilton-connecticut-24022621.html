About this home

Nestled in South Wilton on a tranquil cul-de-sac, this timeless colonial residence exudes charm. Boasting access to acclaimed schools and proximity to two train stations, as well as shopping in both Wilton and Westport, convenience is a hallmark of this location. Step inside to discover a spacious interior designed for comfortable living. The heart of the home lies in the inviting eat-in kitchen, complete with a freplace for cozy gatherings. Adjacent, a warm family room beckons relaxation, while a three-season screened porch with skylights offers a serene retreat overlooking the in-ground pool. Formal living and dining rooms provide elegant spaces for entertaining. Throughout the home, gleaming hardwood fooring adds a touch of sophistication. The kitchen has beautiful terracotta fooring. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite awaits, accompanied by four additional bedrooms for family or guests. The walk-up attic presents excellent expansion possibilities for future endeavors. This property seamlessly combines classic elegance with modern amenities, offering a haven for all who call it home.
