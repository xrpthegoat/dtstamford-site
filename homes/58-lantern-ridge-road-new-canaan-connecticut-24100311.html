About this home

This Mid-Century classic sited on just over two acres has spectacular water views and vistas of the Milner Reservoir. Located on a quiet cul-de-sac, this home is notable for for its great curb appeal, open floor plan, multiple fireplaces and large-beamed great room with huge windows to capture the beautiful setting at every turn. Featuring five bedrooms and multiple family gathering spaces there is plenty of room to relax and enjoy this special home. The expansive fenced-in back yard is nearly entirely flat, a true rarity for a ridge home in New Canaan with ample space to add a pool. Wildlife abounds with many birds enjoying the expansive trees and mature landscaping. The location is all about the tranquility and beauty within a quick 10 minute drive to the center of New Canaan and it's Top-Rated Schools and Award Winning restaurants. Recent upgrades include Central A/C, new oil tank, new water heater, new appliances, new washer & dryer, generator and the beautiful, fenced garden is a true showstopper! A truly special offering!
