About this home

Simply gorgeous modern contemporary raised ranch on a one-acre wooded lot in West Norwalk. Enjoy fabulous views from every window and an open, airy layout with floor-to-ceiling windows, vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors throughout, exposed beams, and two fireplaces. The large deck/ patio is accessible from sliders in both the kitchen and family room, ideal for indoor/outdoor living. The kitchen features stainless-steel appliances including a gas range. Two bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with sitting area/ office, en-suite bath and private deck. Raised bed/ enclosed garden, electric dog fence. Two-car attached garage completes this house. AMAZING VIEWS BUT PLEASE NOTE STEEP SLOPE IN REAR! Prime location close to the Merritt Parkway, with easy access to parks, golf course, public transportation, and shopping.
