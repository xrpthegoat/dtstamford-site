About this home

Rarely available end-unit townhome at Greenwich Court featuring a light-filled, spacious, and functional floor plan. This well-maintained townhome is located in a pet-friendly complex and offers a generous living and dining area with a wood-burning fireplace, hardwood floors, and access to a private balcony. The main level also includes an updated kitchen with pantry, Viking stove, and newer (2023) dishwasher, along with a versatile breakfast nook with high ceilings that functions well as a home office/playroom/library. A half-bath completes the main level. Upstairs, two en-suite bedrooms feature vaulted ceilings, including a primary suite with its own private balcony, walk-in closet, and a spiral staircase leading to a spacious loft-ideal for a secondary workspace or quiet retreat. The secondary bedroom features a recently updated bathroom and ample closet space. Significant recent updates include a newer furnace, HVAC, and hot water heater (all 2023). Additional highlights include second-floor laundry with newer (2023) washer/dryer, two reserved underground garage spaces with elevator access, and a storage room. Situated in the heart of Cos Cob, convenient to the Metro-North station and close to parks, schools, restaurants, and all that Greenwich has to offer. A move-in ready opportunity offering space, privacy, and convenience.
