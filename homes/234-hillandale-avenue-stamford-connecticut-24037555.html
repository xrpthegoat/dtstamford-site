About this home

Charming 4 bedroom, 2 bath cape with finished basement, large driveway and flat, fenced in backyard! Fabulous location - walkable to downtown - in a lovely neighborhood. Open plan kitchen/ living area. Large eat-in kitchen features beautiful granite countertops & stainless steel appliances. Hardwood floors throughout. Four bedrooms, two on the first floor and two on the upper floor, offer great flexibility and can be easily adapted to work-from-home office space. Finished basement provides even more living space and could easily be a home office, media room, man cave, craft room, home gym, etc. Laundry room is downstairs and there is plenty of room for storage as well! The backyard is accessible from the kitchen and the basement, and is fully fenced and flat with a large patio (patio furniture stays with the house!) Perfect for a table and chairs, a firepit, and easy entertaining. Driveway is paved and can easily accommodate 4 or more vehicles. Come see this adorable property before it's gone! Property is also listed for rent.
