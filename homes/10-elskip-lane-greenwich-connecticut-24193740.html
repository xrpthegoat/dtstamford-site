About this home

Welcome to 10 Elskip Lane, a timeless Colonial set on a picturesque one-acre property in one of Greenwich's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering 4,539 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this elegant home features four spacious bedrooms and four full bathrooms plus a convenient powder room. The well-appointed floor plan is designed for both comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. Sun-filled living spaces flow seamlessly throughout the home, while the first-floor laundry room adds everyday convenience. The generous primary suite and well-proportioned secondary bedrooms provide comfort and privacy for family and guests alike. Set on a beautifully landscaped one-acre lot, the property offers exceptional outdoor space with endless possibilities.
