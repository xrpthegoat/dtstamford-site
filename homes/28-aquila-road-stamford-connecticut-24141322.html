About this home

LIVE THE COVETED COASTAL LIFESTYLE IN STAMFORD'S SOUGHT-AFTER COVE PARK COMMUNITY. Just one block from the beach, this picture-perfect Cape Cod home sits on a quiet cul-de-sac with a large, flat lot and views of the Long Island Sound right from your front door. Whether you're ready to move right in or envision expanding, this property offers rare flexibility in an unbeatable location. Enjoy premium access to Cove Island Park, where you can walk to two sandy beaches, a scenic one-mile walk/run trail, a playground, rollerblade and cycling paths, BBQ areas, expansive lawns, two softball fields, a tennis court, a basketball court, and a picnic pavilion. It's the ultimate blend of recreation and relaxation - all just steps from home. Inside, this well-maintained, quintessential New England Cape features 3 bedrooms with a flexible dining room/4th bedroom option, two modern bathrooms, gorgeous hardwood floors, and a bright kitchen with new appliances. The layout offers both charm and versatility, making it easy to tailor the home to your lifestyle. Commuters will love the easy express train access to NYC, while everyone will appreciate being moments from a wide variety of restaurants, shopping, and community amenities. Buyers are encouraged to perform their own diligence regarding potential expansion opportunities and room designations on the field card.
