About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Westport's most convenient locations, 6 Twin Circle Drive offers luxury living with exceptional indoor and outdoor spaces. Just moments to Bedford Middle School, Staples High School, downtown Westport, trains, shopping, restaurants, Longshore, and Compo Beach, this property combines privacy, recreation, and convenience. The resort-style backyard features a pool with spa, expansive lounging areas, full outdoor kitchen, and a rare post-tension concrete tennis court - the premier surface used at elite clubs and college facilities. A truly extraordinary amenity rarely found in residential homes. The home offers an open floor plan filled with natural light and thoughtfully designed spaces. The gourmet chef's kitchen features high-end appliances, abundant workspace, and two dishwashers. Main-level highlights include elegant entertaining spaces, a private meditation room, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The primary suite is a retreat with a spa-inspired bath, steam shower, and dressing room with abundant closets. Upstairs, an incredible playroom with six built-in beds creates the perfect sleepover space or playroom. The walk-out lower level offers versatility with a media room, gym, office, and guest suite ideal for extended family, au pair, or guests. Rarely does a Westport luxury home offer resort-style amenities, premier tennis facilities, flexible living space, and walk-to-school convenience in such a private setting.
