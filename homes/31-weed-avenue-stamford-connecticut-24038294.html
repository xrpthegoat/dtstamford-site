About this home

Discover 31 Weed Ave, a versatile and charming two-family home located just under a mile from Stamford's scenic Cove Island Park. This property is ideal for investors, multi-generational families, or homebuyers seeking an unbeatable location with abundant amenities. Each unit offers a flexible layout with two spacious bedrooms, a full bathroom, kitchen, living room, dining room, tall ceilings, and hardwood floors. Private decks for each unit allow you to enjoy ocean breezes and serene views of Holly Pond. The upper floor boasts extra living space and a full bathroom, ideal for an additional bedroom, home office, or in-law suite. The basement includes separate washers, dryers, and ample storage. Each unit has its own utilities, ensuring privacy and convenience. The large, private backyard is perfect for gardening, relaxation, or play, and the property includes four parking spaces in addition to a two-car garage with extra storage and a workbench. Enjoy outdoor activities just steps away-launch your kayak on Holly Pond or take a quick bike ride to Cove Island Park for fishing, sailing, boating, and more. The park also offers trails, beaches, a playground, and educational opportunities at the Sound Waters Education Center. With close proximity to downtown Stamford's shops, restaurants, and transportation, this home offers the best of both convenience and charm. Don't miss out-schedule a tour today to explore the unique potential of 31 Weed Ave!
