About this home

Looking to enter the competitive market in the town of Westport? Check out this wonderful 5-bedroom colonial with Westport schools and town amenities! It's bright and perfect for anyone looking to settle down in a vibrant area. You're just a short walk from the train and all the action at Saugatuck Center, where you'll find a fantastic mix of restaurants and unique shops to explore. Inside, the home features a spacious, open living and dining area that's ideal for hosting friends and family. The kitchen sits in the center of the home, offering a cozy dining space that opens up to a patio-great for al fresco dining. The fenced backyard is perfect for BBQs or just relaxing, and it borders an adjacent cul-de-sac, providing extra privacy. For those chillier nights, there's a cozy family room with a fireplace. On the first floor, you'll also find a handy half bath, a laundry room, and a mudroom area that leads to the two-car garage. Upstairs, you'll discover a spacious primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and a roomy bathroom featuring a double vanity. There are four more bedrooms and another full bath, offering plenty of space for bedrooms, gym or even a home office setup. Enjoy all the fabulous amenities Westport has to offer, with easy access to NYC for your commuting needs or to other coastal attractions along Long Island Sound. This home combines comfort and convenience, making it an ideal choice for anyone looking to experience the best of Westport living.
