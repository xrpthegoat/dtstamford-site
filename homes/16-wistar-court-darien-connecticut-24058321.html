About this home

REMODELED and READY! Open floor plan ranch home boasts a sleek and stylish, all new kitchen featuring breakfast bar, stainless steel appliances, including a double oven and gorgeous granite countertops. Sparkling new bathroom provides combination tub with shower. New floors run throughout the easy, no steps interior! The remodeling goes beyond looks alone and includes new systems, mechanicals and appliances: Central Air Conditioning, Heating, Electrical, Recessed Lighting, Additional Insulation, Washer & Dryer and more. The unfinished lower level has plenty of storage space. Relax on the gated rear deck overlooking a delightful rear yard - ideal for gardening, pets and backyard fun. The exterior is freshly painted and includes a newly sealed driveway with ample parking. Set on private cul de sac convenient to Noroton train station and the brand new Darien Commons where you can shop and dine. Walk to Baker Park, Jake's Place and other amenities... A fabulous alternative to restrictive condos. Enjoy the Darien lifestyle.
