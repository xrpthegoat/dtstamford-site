About this home

Welcome to 18 Own Home Avenue - a classic white Cape Cod-style home nestled on a peaceful and level lot in the Georgetown neighborhood of Wilton. This well-maintained 4-bedroom, 1-bathroom residence offers a warm and inviting atmosphere with hardwood floors flowing throughout the main level. The first floor features a bright living room, a cozy dining room, and a well-equipped kitchen with a propane stove - perfect for home cooking. Two generously sized bedrooms are conveniently located on the main level and an two additional bedrooms upstairs for guests or home office. Enjoy the ease of an in-unit washer/dryer, and take advantage of a one-car garage for parking or extra storage. The level backyard provides a great setting for outdoor activities, gardening, or simply relaxing in a private natural setting. Ideally located close to Wilton's amenities, you're just minutes from shopping, grocery stores, restaurants, coffee shops, and the train station - making commuting or weekend outings a breeze.
