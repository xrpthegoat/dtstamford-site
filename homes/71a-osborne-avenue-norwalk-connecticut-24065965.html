About this home

This amazing River Watch 2 bedroom condo is recently renovated and shows better than brand new! In addition to 1,232sf, this unit comes with 1 assigned garage space, 1 assigned outdoor parking space, a storage room (roughly 6'X8') and an outdoor deck space for sunbathing or entertaining. Inside there are hardwood floors throughout, high end hardware and light fixtures, built-ins, victorian details and new appliances. Each room is very large starting from the kitchen, dining room, living room, guest bedroom and into the primary bedroom suite which includes a large walk-in-closet. Natural light is abundant and the ceilings are a generous height. Natural gas heats this condominium and you will comfortable in the summer with central air conditioning. The complex is waterfront and features a boardwalk on the river for residents to enjoy. A short walk will bring you to the East Norwalk Train station. Also nearby are great restaurants, ice cream, Veterans Park, South Norwalk, Calf Pasture Beach and much more. Easy access to I-95, Rt 7 and Merritt Parkway. Tenant must have excellent credit and references.
