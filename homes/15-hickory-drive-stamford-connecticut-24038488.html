About this home

Welcome to Southfield Point- perfectly situated on a peninsula stretching into Long Island Sound between charming Old Greenwich and vibrant downtown Stamford. This lovely Colonial with in-ground pool is ready for some summer fun! Large and spacious rooms with high ceilings throughout compliment this home. Huge basement with high ceilings could be finished adding an additional approx. 1000 sq/ft. of living space. Private roads with barriers mean it's a great place to play safely and take long walks with dogs. Southfield Point is a water lover's paradise and comes complete with a private beach and boat docks/slips. Besides just a passion for the sea, neighbors here enjoy time together- whether it be our Holiday Parties, Summer Picnic or Friday Night Beach Parties. Amazing price point for this prestigious neighborhood where homes are upwards of $6 million. Come and see!
