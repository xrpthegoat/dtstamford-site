About this home

Space, privacy and potential in the heart of Stamford. This nearly 1,700-square-foot end-unit townhouse at 9 Old North Stamford Road offers a flexible multi-level layout and a wonderful opportunity to create a home that reflects your own style. The main living level features a spacious, light-filled living room with fireplace and access to a private balcony, along with a separate dining area and an easy flow for everyday living and entertaining. Two generously sized bedrooms are located on separate levels, offering exceptional privacy and flexibility, ideal for creating a dedicated home office, guest suite or separate retreat. Two-and-a-half baths provide added convenience. A private entrance and end-unit location enhance the feeling of space, while the community pool offers a welcome place to relax during the warmer months. Conveniently located in Stamford's Mid City neighborhood, the home offers easy access to downtown, shopping, dining and all that Stamford has to offer, while providing a quieter residential setting. Ready for its next chapter, this townhouse presents an opportunity to update and personalize over time while enjoying nearly 1,700 square feet of versatile living space from day one. Bring your ideas and imagine the possibilities. N.B. the tax card for town is incorrect, this is a 2 full bathroom and one-half bath, 3 level nearly 1,700 sq foot townhome, with second bedroom on lower level.
