About this home

Sophisticated Living in Sought-After Heatherwood. Enjoy luxury, comfort, and privacy in this exceptional home in the highly desirable Heatherwood community. Surrounded by peaceful woodlands and featuring a clubhouse, pool, and tennis court, this residence offers a resort-style lifestyle in a convenient location. A dramatic two-story entry welcomes you with soaring ceilings, skylights, and walls of glass that fill the home with natural light. The spacious living room with fireplace and wet bar flows seamlessly into the formal dining room and four-season sunroom, where tranquil wooded views create a relaxing retreat. The custom kitchen features granite countertops, premium appliances, and a sunny breakfast area, perfect for enloying morning coffee. The main-level primary suite offers two walk-in closets, a private deck, and a beautifully renovated bath with dual vanities, soaking tub, separate shower, and private water closet. A den with custom built-ins and its own deck completes the first floor. Upstairs, two generous bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bath, while an expansive loft provides ideal space for a home office, library, or sitting area. A private deck leads to additional storage above the attached two-car garage. The walk-out lower level includes a spacious family room, fitness area, half bath, and abundant storage. Exterior maintenance is included, with shopping, dining, and commuter routes just minutes away.
