About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST AT NOON 2/12. Discover this charming New England Cape nestled in a serene and desirable Springdale neighborhood. This 1950's home boasts level, landscaped grounds with a fully fenced backyard, new bluestone terrace and pathway, and a completely rebuilt screened-in porch, creating a perfect outdoor retreat. Inside, the sunlit spaces feature beautiful hardwood floors throughout. The cozy living room, complete with a wood-burning fireplace, seamlessly flows into an office/bedroom area with ample closet space and stunning yard views. The kitchen opens to the dining room and also provides access to the porch, ideal for entertaining and afternoon naps. The main level is completed by a full bath. Upstairs, a spacious primary bedroom and a second bedroom with an upper level renovated bathroom with marble floors and countertop. The partially finished lower level offers a flexible hangout space, a full bath, and substantial unfinished storage. There is also a one car attached garage! Located in a delightful neighborhood, this home is convenient to amenities, including shopping, dining, tennis and golf facilities, and is less than a mile from the train. Additional updates include a new washer/dryer, new dishwasher, new boiler, and newer water heater. A true blend of prime location and impeccable condition!
