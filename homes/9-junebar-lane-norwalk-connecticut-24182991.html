About this home

Move-in ready 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom split-level on a quiet cul-de-sac in the Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk, Connecticut. This well-maintained home features hardwood floors, central air conditioning, sprinklers, and a layout designed for flexible living with separate living room, dining room, and family room. The updated kitchen includes granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. Three bedrooms and two full baths provide comfortable space across the split-level floor plan. There is tons of room for storage in the expansive, clean basement, as well as the pull down attic. The private backyard is fenced with mature flowering landscaping, an above-ground pool, and a deck for outdoor dining and entertaining - a ready-made retreat without leaving home. Located on a cul-de-sac in Cranbury, Norwalk, CT, with convenient access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and Norwalk shopping and dining. 9 Junebar, Norwalk, CT 06851.
