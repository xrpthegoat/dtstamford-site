About this home

Tucked away down a long private driveway, this spacious 4 BR, 2.5 BA home offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort & convenience. Located just minutes from the vibrant shops & restaurants of both Stamford and New Canaan, this home is ideally situated for easy access to everything you need while enjoying a peaceful, secluded setting. Inside, you'll find a functional layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. The elegant living room and formal dining room provide inviting spaces for gatherings, while the cozy family room features a fireplace, perfect for relaxing evenings. The eat-in kitchen opens directly onto a lovely deck-ideal for enjoying your morning coffee or hosting summer dinners outdoors. Upstairs, the generously sized bedrooms offer plenty of space for rest and relaxation. The primary suite includes two walk-in closets and a private bath, offering a true retreat. A large walk-out basement presents exciting potential: finish it to create additional living space, a recreation room, or even a private in-law suite. Set on over an acre of beautifully lush property, the grounds feature a mix of wooded areas and vibrant flowering plants, including stunning azalea bushes and rhododendrons. While the home does need some cosmetic updates, it has great bones and endless potential-bring your vision and make it your own. With a new furnace installed last year, many of the essentials are already in place for you to move in and start creating your dream home!
