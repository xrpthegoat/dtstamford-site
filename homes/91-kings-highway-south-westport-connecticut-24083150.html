About this home

Discover a remarkable home that perfectly balances suburban serenity with urban accessibility. If the suburbs appeal but living central to trains, restaurants, shopping, theaters, beaches and convenience is a must, this is your home! This sun-filled property offers an exceptional living experience with spacious, thoughtfully designed rooms that flow seamlessly. The main level features an inviting living room with a fireplace, connected to a paneled dining room creating an ideal space for both everyday living and entertaining. An updated kitchen opens to a family room with a stone fireplace, providing a central gathering space. Upstairs, a large primary bedroom with a luxurious walk-in closet is complemented by three additional bedrooms and a central bathroom. The lower level offers a bright recreation/multi-use room, a full bathroom and another space perfect as an office or exercise room. A wonderful offering with so much flexible living opportunity. The peaceful outdoor space offers multiple areas for gathering and entertaining and is enveloped in a beautifully professionally designed oasis with magnificent lush plantings that bloom throughout the year. Strategically located, this home provides immediate access to Westport's best amenities - from renowned shops and restaurants to the Westport Country Playhouse, Levitt Pavilion, Compo Beach and Train. An exceptional turn-key opportunity for those seeking a home that combines comfort, style, and prime location.
