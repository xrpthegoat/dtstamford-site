About this home

This enchanting Tudor style home was built in 1931 and sits in a coastal enclave of charming "beachy homes". Located across the street from Long Island Sound, this home has water views and beautiful light. Step into the foyer and fall in love with the open flow, generous ceiling height (9'), refinished rich hardwood floors, beautiful stucco walls, and a sweet library with stonework and built-ins. Enjoy sitting by the fireplace on winter evenings OR step out through French doors to the covered screened porch, you'll find a private oasis of specimen plantings, stone and pebble walk ways, trees and bushes. The garage has been set up as a studio that can be used as a home office/yoga retreat, etc (or easily turned back into a garage if you want to park). There is so much to love about this home: 3 Bedrooms, 1.5 baths, great laundry/mud room, updated kitchen and newly redesigned 2nd floor full bath. Need more space? The expansion possibilities are endless: add a new roomy primary bedroom suite & bth (over the covered porch), build out the 3rd floor and create a magical space with water views; or, if you add a front facing dormer, just imagine a recessed roof top deck overlooking the harbor! 61 Quintard was not affected by hurricanes Sandy or Irene and has never flooded. You will find this home to be smart, charmingly stylish, sunny and soulful! Walk to Historic SoNo, Metro North Station, Maritime Center, shops, dining, cycle to the beach or an hour-drive to NYC!
