About this home

Coastal Retreat in the coveted Harborview community and steps to Long Island Sound. Completely rebuilt and elevated in 2019, this stunning home blends timeless coastal charm with modern design. It offers an open-concept layout ideal for entertaining, anchored by a chef's kitchen with designer shiplap accents and Brazilian teak hardwood floors throughout. The generous first-floor primary suite features a spa-inspired bath with soaking tub. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms each enjoy their own modern bathrooms, perfect for family or guests. Set within the sought-after Harborview Beach Association where residents enjoy resort-style amenities including a waterfront clubhouse with year-round events, tennis courts, playground, ball field, dock, beach and moorings-all in a vibrant, dog-friendly community. With South Norwalk's Metro North station and the trendy SONO dining and nightlife district just 5 minutes away, and only 55 minutes to NYC, this home is ideal for year-round living or the ultimate weekend getaway.
