About this home

This newly re-built 4-bedroom, 3-bath Colonial offers 2400 sq feet of finely crafted living space on a level .25-acre lot. Set on the corner of Anthony Lane and a cul-de-sac, the property pairs neighborhood charm with privacy. Enjoy the outdoors in a newly fenced yard with professional landscaping and a full irrigation system. Inside, the open layout is defined by natural light and superior craftsmanship. White oak floors run throughout the home, complemented by custom millwork and oversized windows. The living room's fireplace, framed by built-in cabinetry, provides both warmth and architectural interest.The chef's kitchen impresses with custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, and high-performance energy-efficient appliances. A large mudroom with integrated laundry is designed for durability and organization. A first-floor bedroom offers versatility as an office, guest suite, den or playroom. The full bath is finished with frameless glass shower ensuring comfort and style. Upstairs, three bedrooms feature ample closets and abundant light. The hall bath includes a glass-enclosed tub/shower, dual windows, heated floor. The primary suite features two large closets and bath highlighted by a walk-in shower, heated floors, double sink vanity and polished finishes. With zoned propane heating, central air, and a balance of classic proportions and modern amenities this home combines elegance and functionality less than one mile to shops, dining and transportation of Noroton Heights.
