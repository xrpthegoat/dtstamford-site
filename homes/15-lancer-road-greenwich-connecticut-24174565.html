About this home

4 Bedrooms w/ huge bonus room in basement within homeowners' association! Located in the highly desirable private Dogwood Park Association in Riverside, this beautifully updated single-family home includes all association costs AND monthly landscaping in the rent for easy, low-maintenance living. Enjoy access to a peaceful pond and grassy area in a tightly knit community known for quiet streets, friendly neighbors, and very limited through-traffic. The home is freshly painted throughout and features refinished hardwood floors, updated kitchen improvements, new lighting fixtures, and new door hardware across the main level. The primary suite offers a walk-in closet and full private bathroom, while the main level includes both an additional full bathroom and half bath for guests and everyday convenience. The lower level includes a large bonus room with a clothes closet, ideal for additional living space, a home office, gym, or guest area, plus a second refrigerator located in the nearby mechanical room. Outside, enjoy two separate stone patios perfect for entertaining or relaxing, along with a fenced backyard ideal for pets and outdoor enjoyment. Conveniently located near top-rated public schools, Riverside Commons shopping, I-95, Metro-North commuter trains, Stamford, and everyday conveniences, this is a rare Riverside rental opportunity.
