About this home

Beautifully updated and well maintained contemporary style home on a park like acre in Westover. Soaring ceilings, two stone front wood burning fireplaces plus hardwood floors & recessed lighting throughout. The many windows and sliders are all new and allow natural light to flood each room. Enter large foyer with half bath, livingroom with access to outdoor deck, large formal dining room, family room with 2 story ceiling and access to outdoor deck, eat-in-kitchen with granite counters, stainless steel appliances including a double oven & center island. Mudroom room with laundry and second half bath plus a private office complete the main floor. Second story offers 4 bedrooms and two full baths. Primary bedroom suite has two walk in closets. Primary bath is large with double sinks, stall shower and a jetted soaking tub. Three additional bedrooms all have walk-in closets. Two of them share a covered outdoor balcony/deck. Second floor also has easy access "attic" storage. Huge basement adds enough addtional space perfect for a gym and playroom. A large unfinished portion adds a tremendous amount of storage space. Both interior and exterior have been recently painted. Enter the property on the newly repaved circular drive. Outdoor deck runs the full length along the back of the home with interior access on each end allowing a great flow for indoor/outdoor entertaining. Central air and a two car garage complete this home. Walking distance to the hiking trails of Mianus River Park
