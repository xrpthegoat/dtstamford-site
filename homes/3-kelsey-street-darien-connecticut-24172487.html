About this home

Welcome to the wonderful 3-bedroom (with a potential 4th BR), 5-bath rental located on a quiet cul-de-sac. Designed for easy living, this spacious rental offers a flexible layout with a finished lower level and a fantastic upstairs game room-perfect for work, play, or entertaining. Enjoy unbeatable convenience with a short walk to the Noroton Heights train station, new shopping center, Equinox, post office, and the schools. The rental is also just an 8-minute drive to Weed Beach, making it easy to enjoy Darien's beautiful coastline. Set in a friendly, sought-after community, this home combines comfort, space, and location-ideal for today's lifestyle. Landlord prefers mid-August move-in date. Offered Unfurnished or Furnished. Excellent credit score and referrals are required.
