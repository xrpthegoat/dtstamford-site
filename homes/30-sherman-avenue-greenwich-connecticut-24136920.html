About this home

Welcome to this inviting 3-bed, 1.5-bath home offering 1,699 sq.ft. of comfortable living space in one of the area's most desirable neighborhoods. The entry features a convenient vestibule leading into a warm and welcoming living room - perfect for keeping out the chill on cooler days. The first floor includes a spacious, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen, family room, full bath and primary bedroom, offering the ease of main-level living. Upstairs, you'll find 2 additional bedrooms, a large closet area with ample space to add a 2nd bath and laundry, if desired. A generous attic provides great potential for future expansion. Enjoy outdoor living on the deck, overlooking a nice backyard - ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Close to schools, parks, Town pool, beach and tennis courts as well as the Dorothy Hamill Skating Rink. With easy access to major highways, this home combines comfort, convenience and opportunity in a prime location.
