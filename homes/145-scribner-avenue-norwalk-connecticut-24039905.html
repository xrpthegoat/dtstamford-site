About this home

Nestled in a peaceful neighborhood close to shopping, restaurants, and schools, this recently updated ranch-style home is a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. The home features 4 bedrooms, 2 updated full baths, and newly redone floors throughout, including new flooring in the lower level as well as new roof. The spacious, fully-applianced eat-in kitchen flows into a welcoming living room, making it ideal for family gatherings. Enjoy the outdoors from the sunroom, which opens to a level backyard perfect for entertaining. The lower level boasts a cozy family room, a second kitchen, and a possible 4th bedroom, along with a laundry area and ample storage. A 1-car garage completes this lovely home. Available for sale or rent.
