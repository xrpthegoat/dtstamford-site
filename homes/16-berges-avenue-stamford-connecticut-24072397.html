About this home

Welcome Home! 4 generous sized bedrooms, 2 full baths - this spacious, bright and cheerful house has it all. Set in quiet mid-city neighborhood, offering four large bedrooms (two upper, two on the main level), two full baths with tubs (one upper, one on the main level), with a sun-filled living room and dining room area. The updated kitchen with granite counter tops and island and beautiful cabinetry is a cook's delight with plenty of counter space & cupboards for storage. Crown molding adds the finishing touch. Hardwood floors throughout. Bonus sunroom / mudroom with access to a private lush yard (partially fenced) is idyllic for summer BBQ's and entertaining with room for all! Multiple bedrooms offer a great oasis to work from home. The lower-level basement has a laundry room and so much storage area. Off street parking for 2 completes this wonderful property. Excellent credit.
