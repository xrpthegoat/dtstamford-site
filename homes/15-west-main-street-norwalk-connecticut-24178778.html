About this home

Welcome to this spacious, sun-filled 2-bedroom, 2-full-bath townhouse in a quiet Norwalk neighborhood, offering comfort, convenience and a commuter-friendly location. Built in 2012, this inviting home features a bright kitchen with abundant cabinetry, a generous center island and recessed lighting, creating a functional space for everyday living and entertaining. The standout primary en suite is impressively sized, complete with its own entry foyer, walk-in closet and private bath with dual sinks. A second bedroom and full bath offer flexibility for guests, a home office or additional living space. Additional highlights include elevator access within the building and a rare private two-car garage, providing exceptional storage and parking convenience. Located in a pet-friendly community, this attractive townhouse combines the ease of low-maintenance living with the space and amenities that are hard to find. Enjoy easy access to commuting options, shopping, dining and all that Norwalk has to offer.
