About this home

NEW CONSTRUCTION BY RENOWNED LUXURY BUILDER MATTERA CONSTRUCTION - STEPS TO COMPO BEACH! A rare opportunity to own a brand-new, architecturally refined home in one of Westport's most coveted coastal locations-just steps to Compo Beach, Longshore Club, downtown Westport, the train, and schools. Currently under construction, this impeccably designed 6-bedroom residence spans four finished levels and blends timeless elegance with modern living. The main level offers light-filled, open living spaces ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring a custom chef's kitchen with breakfast area, expansive family room, formal living and dining rooms, private office, mudroom, and thoughtfully placed powder rooms. The second level showcases a luxurious primary suite with two generous walk-in closets and a spa-inspired bath, along with four additional ensuite bedrooms and a dedicated laundry room. The walk-out lower level includes a spacious recreation room, gym, guest bedroom with full bath, and flexible space for today's lifestyles. A finished third floor provides additional living space-perfect for a playroom, media room, or home office-with a half bath. Designed with Mattera Construction's signature attention to detail, craftsmanship, and integrity, this home offers the ultimate Westport lifestyle-coastal, convenient, and effortlessly elegant. New construction. Premier beach-area location. A home that rarely comes to market.
