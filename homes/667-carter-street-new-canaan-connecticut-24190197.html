About this home

Welcome to this pristine, move-in-ready 4-bedroom, 3-bath Center Hall Colonial, ideally situated in the highly desirable Hoyt Farm Association. Thoughtfully updated and meticulously maintained, this exceptional home offers a modern floor plan designed for comfortable living and effortless entertaining. The stunning chef's kitchen features top-of-the-line appliances, a granite center island, and hardwood floors, with new sliding glass doors opening onto a mahogany deck overlooking a park-like, level backyard. The gracious family room with a fireplace creates a warm gathering space, while the expansive living room boasts a second fireplace and an oversized picture window. A generous dining room is perfect for hosting family and friends, and a first-floor office makes working from home a pleasure. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite offers a beautifully updated bath and a spacious walk-in closet, while three additional bedrooms and an updated bathroom provide ample space for family and guests. Hardwood floors extend throughout the home, enhancing its timeless appeal. Outside, lush gardens and mature landscaping create a private backyard retreat on stunning grounds. Ideally located just minutes from town, schools, the train, and major parkways, this beautifully maintained home combines classic Colonial charm with today's modern amenities.
