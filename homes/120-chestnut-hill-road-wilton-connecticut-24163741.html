About this home

Historic charm meets modern updates in this timeless Federal Colonial Revival home set on 1.7 flat, usable acres in desirable South Wilton. This 5 bedroom home blends antique character with thoughtful renovations, including a brand new chef's kitchen. Preserved original details throughout, including wide plank hardwood floors, custom built ins and wet bar, and three wood burning fireplaces that create warmth and an inviting atmosphere. A separate studio with additional wood burning fireplace is ideal for a home office or creative retreat. Flexible layout suited for today's lifestyle with key updates including new kitchen (2024), bathroom renovations (2019), roof (2019), oil tank (2024) and hot water tank (2024). The beautiful, park-like property offers exceptional outdoor potential and privacy while still being very accessible. Convenient to Merritt Parkway and Metro North. Wilton Center 8 minutes, Westport 8 minutes, Cranbury Park (a 227 acre public park with scenic trails) 3 minutes.
