About this home

If you've been looking for a home with privacy and plenty of space for entertaining, look no more. This beautifully maintained home is move-in-ready for you to make it your home. The professionally landscaped yard with stone wall and fence sets the tone for this warm, Connecticut country home and is less than 50 miles from NYC offering a great commute and perfect getaway from the city. This light and bright home features an architecturally designed open floor plan, with vaulted ceilings, French doors and built-ins throughout the home. If you enjoy cooking, the gourmet kitchen will delight, with center island, SubZero refrigerator, double ovens, warming drawer, wine refrig, and dining area. Enjoy formal dining off the living room, cocktails in the living room, and game nights in the great room. The primary suite has French doors to a nursery or office, walk-in-closet and luxurious private full bath with heated marble floor and towel rack, and spacious shower. The lower level is amazing with two spacious bedrooms, full bath with tub/shower, two cedar closets, rec room/playroom with built-ins and fireplace, laundry and in-home office, and two-car garage. This home provides flexibility for any family's lifestyle, live-in family member or au pair, for example. The wrap-around deck and patio are perfect for outdoor enjoyment with plenty of privacy. Heat pump, central air, updated electric panel, apoxy garage floor, whole-house generator, this home is all set for you, 'Welcome Home'
