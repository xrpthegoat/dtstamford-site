About this home

Welcome to Downtown Greenwich Living. Experience refined elegance and effortless comfort in this stunning 4-bedroom, 4.1-bathroom townhome, ideally situated within a private, gated association in the heart of downtown Greenwich. Every inch has been thoughtfully designed to blend upscale luxury with everyday ease. The open-concept gourmet kitchen featuring a large center island and abundant storage, flows seamlessly into a sun-filled dining area and spacious family room anchored by custom built-ins, creating the perfect setting for entertaining or relaxed everyday living.
