About this home

Take advantage of the opportunity to own in the sought after Sylvan Knoll Community! This beautifully maintained, and recently renovated 2 bed, 1 bath home is centrally located, with easy access to the beach, shopping, restaurants, Stamford train station, Darien train station, and I-95.This townhouse features a spacious eat in kitchen/dining room with white cabinets, stainless steel appliances, and sparkling granite countertops. It also boasts a lovely living room drenched with morning sunlight, two bedrooms, one full bathroom (with tub and shower), and a partially finished basement that can be used as a second living space. The master bedroom has a spacious closet, and the second bedroom has ample storage and would make a great second bedroom, guest bedroom, or home office. The townhouse has a washer and dryer in the basement and there is also generous storage space in the pull-down attic and basement. The home also features central air conditioning, gas heat/stove, and hardwood floors throughout the unit (under the carpets). Further, this townhouse is a corner unit which means that you only share one wall with a neighbor and have more natural light from the wonderfully placed windows. The front and back of the unit have fabulous garden beds for flowers and/or vegetables as well as a brick patio out back. Lovely cherry trees and lilacs bloom in the back during the spring, and the large front lawn provides even more space to play or relax.
