About this home

Nestled in the sought-after neighborhood of Old Hill in Westport on .75 acre, this Belgium block aproned property 3-bedroom, 2 baths property situated on a flat lot with mature trees pushed back from the road offers both privacy and convenience. The 2-car detached garage and barn provide flexibility and a very special option for the buyer with the vision to make this property your own. The former home of the late actors' Maggie Williams of The Theater Artists Workshop of Westport, and Warner Brother's actor and author Lawrence Williams. The perfect retreat for exceptional outdoor entertaining. The location is incredibly convenient to Westport Center, the YMCA, Museum of Contemporary Art (MOCA), the Merritt Parkway (Route 15), and the Westport train station for Metro-North. A quintessential country CT escapes just 1 hour from NY. Don't miss the opportunity to own this property and make it your own. Opportunity knocks! A perfect choice for a discerning buyer wanting to create their dream home. THIS PROPERTY IS BEING SOLD AS IS. Agent/Beneficiary Owner
