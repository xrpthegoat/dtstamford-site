About this home

Exceptional HOBI Award-winning shingle-style home by Argus Development on one of Riverside's most coveted streets. Thoughtfully designed for everyday living and entertaining, this home features a chef's kitchen with oversized island and banquette, family room with custom built-ins and fireplace, private study, mudroom, luxurious primary suite, and en-suite baths for every bedroom. French doors open to a covered bluestone patio overlooking a private backyard retreat with pool and spa. Renovated in 2022, the finished lower level offers a bedroom suite, full bath, kitchenette, laundry, two fireplaces, hardwood floors, and cedar closet. Minutes from schools, train, Old Greenwich Village and Tod's Point. Riverside is a lifestyle.
