About this home

With only two owners in the last 54 years, 1 Woodchuck Court West sits high and dry on a corner lot with loads of potential. Three bedrooms plus an office - or possible fourth bedroom - there's room for everyone. Large walk-out basement with half bathroom, laundry, and garage access. Professionally landscaped, expansive Belgium block-lined driveway, and a real two-car attached garage. Quiet neighborhood with sidewalks, not one but two cul-de-sacs, and mature trees. Three minutes to the Rowayton Train, four minutes to Wegmans and Costco, and five minutes to Brookside Elementary and Brien McMahon High School. Generator with 250-gallon propane tank, city water, and city sewer. Great bones and well maintained. Make it yours!
