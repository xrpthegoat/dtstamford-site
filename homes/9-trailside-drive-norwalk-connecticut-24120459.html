About this home

Welcome to 9 Trailside Drive, tucked away in Norwalk's sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood. This 3-bedroom, 2-bath Colonial offers over 2,100 square feet of versatile living space on a private 0.38-acre lot on a dead end street with a cul-de-sac. Step inside and you'll immediately notice the home's thoughtful updates, including a brand-new roof in 2024, a freshly painted wood exterior in August 2025, and a new water heater. The highlight of the home is the primary suite, a true retreat spanning 578 square feet. It features a large walk-in closet, oversized bathroom, and a bonus room that works perfectly as a home office, nursery, or creative studio. The suite also includes a private balcony with elevated views and the peace that comes with being set up on the hill. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility, with space that can serve as a fourth bedroom, in-law suite, or recreation area. Step outside and discover the stunning backyard patio, crafted entirely from cemented-in rock, an ideal setting for entertaining or relaxing in your private outdoor space. With its cul-de-sac location, spacious design, and a blend of privacy and convenience, this home is built for both comfort and versatility.
