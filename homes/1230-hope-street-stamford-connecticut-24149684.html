About this home

Welcome to 1230 Hope St. #7. This is a beautifully updated townhouse that seamlessly blends contemporary elegance with everyday comfort in a highly walkable, commuter friendly location offering modern luxury and exceptional convenience. This residence features a rare two car attached garage and an open, sun-filled layout with hardwood floors throughout. The main level includes a welcoming foyer, half bath, and an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, newer stainless steel appliances and wine fridge, opening to a spacious living and dining area with fireplace and walkout access to a private deck and small backyard. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite offers ample closet space, including a walk-in closet, and an updated spa-like bath with walk in shower and dual vanities. Two additional oversized bedrooms, a hallway bath with tub/shower, and convenient upper level laundry complete the 2nd floor. Additional highlights include utilities, windows, roof, ample storage and a prim location with the park and playground across the street. It's also a 3 minute walk to the bus and just a 4 minute drive to the Springdale train station. HIGHEST AND BEST 1/28 BY 10AM.
