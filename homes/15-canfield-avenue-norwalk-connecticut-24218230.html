About this home

Experience the epitome of coastal sophistication at this meticulously maintained and carefully updated residence where refined design meets the effortless allure of the Shorehaven lifestyle. Set on an acre of manicured grounds w/fenced rear lawns, this ranch-style home underwent a stunning professional renovation which achieved functional & beautiful results. At the heart of this single-story residence are stylish living spaces, each w/a vltd ceil. Designer kitchen features an oversized quartz island that flows into the gorgeous dining rm w/fpl and a den/sunroom. Walls of French doors create an indoor/outdoor flow to the magical, manicured courtyard w/fountain, creating an enchanting private oasis for al fresco entertaining. The light-filled Great Room offers timeless elegance with a fpl & custom built-ins. The primary suite is a true retreat, boasting soaring ceilings, gas fireplace, expansive custom closets, and a spa-inspired bath. Two additional BRs share an updated Jack-and-Jill bath, while a separate wing houses a 4th BR, full bath, and a dedicated home office for privacy and versatility. Every detail, from high-end hardware to the sun-drenched layout, reflects an uncompromising commitment to quality. Complete with a 3-car garage w/ new doors and a flexible bonus studio above, this property offers a rare opportunity to live elegantly in one of the area's most coveted enclaves, moments from train, commuting routes, beaches, marinas. New roof, heating system & much more.
