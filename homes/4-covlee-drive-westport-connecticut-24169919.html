About this home

Enjoy Westport's water community life-style, in this 4-5 bedroom seaside retreat in the Saugatuck Shores area of town. This 3000 square foot home comes with a membership to the Covlee Association, with its private beach, play area and boat slip. Beautifully maintained, the main floor living room has a cathedral ceiling with beams, fireplace and french doors to the blue stone patio. The updated and spacious eat-in kitchen, with fireplace breakfast bar, also accesses the patio. There is a Bedroom and Office on this level. Upstairs the Primary Bedroom boasts a huge walk-in closet and a double-sided fireplace that also opens to the luxurious bath with fabulous soaking tub & steam shower. 2 other Bedrooms and Bath complete this level. The sunny yard, with a second patio, offers privacy and space. Moments to the Westport train and amenities, this home is available in July 2026 for yearly rental. Good credit a must. Pets at owners discretion. No cats. Photos from previous tenancy.
