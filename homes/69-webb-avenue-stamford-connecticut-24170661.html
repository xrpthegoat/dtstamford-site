About this home

Welcome to this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 3-bath Cape, ideally situated in the desirable Cove area just moments from the beach. Blending timeless charm with today's most desirable finishes, this home is truly move-in ready and designed to impress. Features an open-concept layout with a spacious living room and dining area that flows seamlessly into a brand-new kitchen, complete with modern cabinetry, stylish tile, and stainless steel appliances. Step outside from the kitchen to enjoy a fully fenced backyard, perfect for summer entertaining, barbecues, or relaxing in privacy. The main level also offers two generously sized bedrooms and a fully updated bathroom. Upstairs, the primary suite provides a comfortable retreat with recessed lighting, a renovated full bathroom, and a walk-in closet. The partially FINISHED BASEMENT expands your living possibilities with flexible space for a home office, guest suite, or recreation area, along with access to an additional full bathroom, laundry access and direct access to the garage. Recent 2025 updates include a tankless heating and hot water system, new central air conditioning with gas integration, brand-new windows, and a roof that is just five years old.
