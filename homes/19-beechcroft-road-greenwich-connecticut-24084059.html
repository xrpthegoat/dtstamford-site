About this home

This home is quintessential Greenwich, combining classic charm with stylish and sophisticated expansion and renovation, and located on one of the most desirable mid-country lanes. Oversized windows in most of the primary rooms afford tranquil views of this 1.7 acres, including spacious terraces, an in-ground heated pool and Reynolds Pond. A sophisticated Family Room with fabulous views adjacent to a gourmet kitchen and casual dining area, formal Dining Room and Living Room with fireplace, each have direct access to the spacious rear terrace with outdoor kitchen. Den/Office off the gracious entry foyer offers custom millwork and cabinetry, a wet bar and surround sound. The sumptuous Primary Bedroom Suite is privately situated. 4 more ensuite bedrooms, wide hallways and expansive storage completes the 2nd floor. The large finished lower level walks out to the yard and pool and includes another entertainment space with custom desk, a playroom, an exercise area, laundry room, half bath and tons of storage. Mudroom is off of the 2 car garage plus there is a 3rd garage which can accommodate a low profile vehicle. There is a full house generator plus many more special features!
