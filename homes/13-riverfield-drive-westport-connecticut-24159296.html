About this home

Nestled in a country setting with 2.04 acres of flat, private, beautiful property on one of Westport's most idyllic and picturesque walking streets is a warm and light filled Colonial, in a serene and wooded neighborhood setting. Inside and out, this home has been beautifully updated and maintained. The interior has all the right rooms in all the right places. Rooms are spacious. Main Level: Foyer, Formal Liv/Din Rms, Family Rm w/vaulted ceiling, Kitchen, Breakfast Room, Office and access to Screened-in Porch and large, outdoor Deck. The Lower Level has another Family Rm, Office/Storage Rm, Full Bath, Sauna, Mudroom and tons of storage. Outdoors, you will find a beautifully set tennis/pickleball court with basketball hoop. The peacefulness of this property makes the perfect setting for parties and everyday enjoyment. All of this only 1.3 miles to Coleytown Elem/Middle Schools, less than 3 miles to downtown, and less than 5 miles to the Westport Train station!
