About this home

Nestled in sought-after North Stamford near the New Canaan border, this exceptional Colonial, built in 2007, offers over 7,000 sq ft of elegant and versatile living space on a private one-acre property. Thoughtfully designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home combines timeless style with modern comfort. A grand entry leads to expansive, light-filled rooms enhanced by quality craftsmanship and sophisticated finishes throughout. The chef's kitchen is beautifully appointed with luxury appliances, abundant cabinetry, generous counter space, and ample storage, making it ideal for casual meals or large gatherings. The open layout flows seamlessly into the stunning family room, featuring a dramatic stone fireplace, and oversized sliders leading to a massive new deck overlooking the picturesque property. There's even room for a pool! The home offers four spacious en-suite bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite designed as a private retreat. An expansive great room provides endless flexibility for a playroom, media room, office, fitness area, in-law suite, or additional family room. The fully finished walk-out lower level adds even more living and entertaining space with direct backyard access. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, schools, and commuter routes, this remarkable North Stamford home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and functionality in an exceptional setting.
