About this home

Effortless one level living in one of Westport's most sought after communities, Harvest Commons. This sun-drenched corner unit offers a rare blend of privacy, space, and convenience, with no stairs, a spacious two-car attached garage, and two newly landscaped private courtyards. Inside, you'll find high ceilings, hardwood floors, and oversized windows that flood the home with natural light. The generous living and dining areas provide a comfortable, open flow for everyday living and special occasions. The updated kitchen features white cabinetry, new stainless steel appliances, and crisp new countertops. The expansive primary suite includes vaulted ceilings, dual walk-in closets, and a renovated bath with a walk-in shower and separate tub. The second bedroom, also oversized, offers built-ins and versatility as a home office, den, or guest space. Additional highlights include a second full bath, full-size laundry room, finished lower level with half bath and storage space, natural gas heating, and an automatic backup generator for peace of mind. Harvest Commons is a beautifully maintained community offering resort-style amenities including a heated pool and two Har-Tru tennis courts. A truly special opportunity for stylish, low-maintenance living in a prime Westport location.
