About this home

Welcome to 154 Knox Road-an updated and inviting Colonial set on .27 acres in Stamford's Springdale neighborhood. Offering 3/4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and 1,278 sq ft, this home blends comfort, updates, and a highly convenient location. The renovated kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless-steel appliances, and direct access to a private oversized deck overlooking the landscaped backyard. The kitchen opens to the dining and living area, creating a practical and comfortable flow for everyday living and hosting. The main level also includes a bedroom and full bath with laundry. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms with hardwood floors are complemented by a flexible bonus room that can support a variety of uses, along with a renovated full bath. The additional 480+ sq ft lower level provides excellent storage/recreation potential, and the attached 1-car garage adds convenience. City water and sewer. Situated on a quiet and well-established street, the property offers close proximity to Springdale's shops, restaurants, library, parks, and train station, making day-to-day living exceptionally accessible. The home's thoughtful layout, outdoor space, and location combine to create a wonderful opportunity in one of Stamford's most established neighborhoods. With a balanced blend of updates and charm, this home is ready for its next chapter. BEST AND FINAL OFFER DEADLINE MONDAY 2/2/26 @ 12PM.
