About this home

Experience refined living in this exquisitely custom ranch on the coveted Norwalk/Rowayton/Darien border. Thoughtfully redesigned with exceptional craftsmanship and timeless finishes, this turnkey residence offers over 5,200 square feet of beautifully finished living space, including 4 spacious bedrooms, 4 full baths, and a flexible layout with potential for a private 2-bedroom in-law suite with its own separate entrance. Designed for effortless entertaining, the open-concept floor plan features a stunning designer kitchen with custom white cabinetry, premium stainless-steel appliances, a massive center island, and seamless flow into the expansive living and dining areas. A spectacular great room with soaring ceilings and skylights fills the home with natural light. The fully finished lower level offers endless possibilities for recreation, a home gym, office, or guest quarters. Recent upgrades include a new roof, new central air system, rich hardwood flooring throughout, and a spacious laundry room with custom cabinetry and utility sink. Set on a private, level 3/4-acre property, the backyard is a true retreat with a brand-new stone patio, exceptional privacy, abundant parking, and a two-car garage. Ideally located on the Darien border with convenient access to New York, this exceptional home offers luxury, versatility, and modern living in one of Fairfield County's most desirable locations.
