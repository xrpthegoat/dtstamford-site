About this home

Few homes in Darien offer this combination: five bedrooms, five indoor entertainment spaces, a private elevator, gunite pool, hot tub, and pool cabana with wet bar-all on a private .70-acre lot in the Tokeneke School district. Fully updated 2021-2026 and just a walk to Metro-North and downtown. Spanning three fully finished floors, the main floor is built for living and entertaining: a formal living room with fireplace, sunroom with, family room with fireplace, and a renovated chef's kitchen open to the dining room, featuring new cabinet doors, hardware, countertops, sink, faucet, pantry, and updated lighting. The primary suite features a dressing room and a cedar walk-in closet. Upstairs: home office and home gym. The at-grade lower level-built into natural ledge with no below-grade space-opens via French doors directly to the pool and features a pool entertainment room, three bedrooms, playroom, game room, cabana with wet bar, a mudroom, and two attached garages (a two-car garage and a separate third garage bay). Outside: gunite pool, new hot tub (2025), new pool heater (2024), pool cabana with wet bar and fridge, stone patio, large custom playset, full irrigation, fenced yard, and professional landscaping. Updated 2021-2026: two central A/C units, Ecobee smart thermostats, new appliances, refinished hardwood floors, renovated bathrooms, and painted exterior. No deferred maintenance. A private world of its own.
