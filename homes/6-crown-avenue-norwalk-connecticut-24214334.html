About this home

Welcome to 6 Crown Avenue, a four-bedroom Colonial offering generous living space and a convenient location. Freshly painted throughout, the home features a family room with a wood-burning fireplace, a large separate living area, and a spacious eat-in kitchen with an island and access to the deck and sunroom. The second and third levels offer four bedrooms, including a primary with a walk-in closet. A dedicated office and laundry room provide added convenience. Outside, the level, fully fenced backyard features mature trees and a deck, ideal for outdoor dining and entertaining. Conveniently located near SONO, local shops and restaurants, the train station, I-95, the Maritime Aquarium, and movie theaters, this home offers easy access to shopping, dining, and transportation.
