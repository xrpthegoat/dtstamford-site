About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Norwalk's most welcoming neighborhoods, this updated split-level home offers modern living with timeless charm. With 3 bedrooms, 2 beautifully renovated bathrooms, and 2,036 square feet of thoughtfully designed space, this home has been meticulously updated over the last 10 years. The bright, spacious kitchen features contemporary finishes, seamlessly flowing into the dining area and living room-perfect for both entertaining and everyday life. A fully finished basement offers even more living space, ideal for a family room, office, or guest quarters. Step outside to enjoy the recently updated back patio and flat backyard, perfect for outdoor gatherings or peaceful relaxation. With plenty of parking, great neighbors, and easy access to all that Norwalk has to offer, this is more than a home-it's your next chapter.
