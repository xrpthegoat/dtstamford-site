About this home

Located in the Village of Rowayton, this charming Ranch sits on over a 1/2 acre and is completely updated. Meticulously maintained throughout, it offers flexible space to live in as is or to expand, easy to configure to three or more bedrooms. The separate barn/garage holds a beautifully finished bonus space for an office, home gym, rec room studio or a possible ADU. The backyard is an oasis of gardens with perennials and fruit trees, and has a large deck for entertaining or relaxing. Recent improvements include a new AC system in 2024. Walk to all Rowayton amenities: shops, restaurants, train station, beach, library, schools, Five Mile River marinas, dog park and nature preserve.
