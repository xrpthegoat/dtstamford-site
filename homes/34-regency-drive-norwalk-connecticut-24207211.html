About this home

Location, location, location! Set on a peaceful residential street in desirable Cranbury near the Westport line, this classic Colonial offers a wonderful combination of space, comfort and indoor-outdoor living. Beautifully landscaped grounds feature an inviting in-ground pool and expansive wraparound deck, ideal for entertaining and relaxing. A welcoming entry foyer leads to a bright and spacious interior. The heart of the home features an open-concept kitchen, family room and casual dining area with wide-plank cherry floors, cathedral ceilings and a gas fireplace. The well-appointed kitchen offers cherry cabinetry, granite countertops, a breakfast bar and high-end appliances. The main level also includes a formal dining room, living room with natural stone fireplace, laundry, and a versatile guest/4th bedroom with a full bath. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features a gas fireplace, walk-in closet, private bath and adjoining bonus room/den. Two additional bedrooms and another full bath complete the upper level. The unfinished lower level offers generous storage and direct access to the two-car garage. A fantastic opportunity in Cranbury, conveniently located near the Westport line.
