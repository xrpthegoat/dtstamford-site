About this home

SHANGRA LA IN WESTPORT. A rare find! Meticulous, modern, updated, centrally-located home with 2 acres of privacy, at the end of a QUIET cul de sac minutes to downtown. 4 Bedrooms and 3 1/2 Baths with a heated in ground saltwater pool and spa. Vaulted ceiling in kitchen/family room with tons of light and access to deck for entertaining. Primary Suite with freestanding tub, shower and walk in closet. Updated center island chef's kitchen (two wall convection ovens, new Bosch induction cooktop, 2 year old Bosch fridge), and family room with a wood burning fireplace offer elegance and comfort. The Primary Bedroom features a vaulted ceiling, large windows, a luxurious bathroom, and walk in closet. Two other bedrooms are located on main level along with a hallway bathroom and laundry area. The large walkout lower level presents a spacious additional family room, full bath, and 4th bedroom. This house had a nearly total renovation in 2014. The heated gunite saltwater pool, with spa and mechanical cover were built in 2019. As a full-time home, or weekend getaway, this one can not be beat. Surrounded by nature yet convenient to shopping, downtown, beaches and all that Westport offers.
