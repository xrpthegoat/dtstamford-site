About this home

Immaculate Colonial that is perfect condition. Nicely updated and neat as a pin. Great location on the Old Greenwich border right in front of the gold course. Living room with a fireplace and gleaming hardwood floors. Formal dining room off kitchen. The kitchen was updated 5 years ago, has granite countertops, stainless steel micro wave & dishwasher & sliders to the deck. There is lovely enclosed front porch. A powder room completes the first floor. The 2nd floor has three bedrooms and a full bath. Central air, a full basement and a two car garage are few more features of this fine home located on the Greenwich border in front of the golf course.
