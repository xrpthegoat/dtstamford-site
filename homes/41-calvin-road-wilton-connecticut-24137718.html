About this home

Prime Wilton Location. Mere minutes from Main Schools Campus and Both Wilton and Weston village centers. Comfortable and efficient Home Office setup on Main Level, with dedicated Entrance, yet seamless connection with the rest of this lovely Home. Your Private Backyard is Nature's playground. If you have ever considered a move out of the City, this Home offers a very nice blend of convenience, natural beauty, and sheer breathing space! Situated in coveted South Wilton, this 4 Bedroom Classic Colonial provides both traditional New England charm and modern ambiance. The inviting Main Level layout is designed for everyday living, yet Perfect for Entertaining....the Flow is Effortless from room to room. The fabulous Cherry Kitchen with granite counters frames serene views of your retreat-worthy, wooded Backyard. The versatile main level Study equals the ideal Work-from-Home situation. Or potentially, a comfortable and private guest suite. The massively Oversized Garage features a full staircase, leading to a spacious Loft, offering abundant additional storage capacity. The currently unfinished Lower Level Walkout, with baseboard heat, is just waiting for your vision of what it can be. 41 Calvin is mere minutes from Metro-North, the Merritt Parkway; Lilly's Market, popular Orem's Diner; the full service Wilton Y, and the shops and restaurants of Georgetown. Welcome to your new CT home, Delivering award-winning schools and the quintessential Fairfield County lifestyle with ease!
