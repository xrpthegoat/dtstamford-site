About this home

Charming and beautifully maintained Cape set on a secluded, flat, .46-acre lot. This updated home offers four bedrooms and three full baths, including a spacious main-floor primary suite. The kitchen opens to a large, sun-filled family room, creating an easy flow for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen and baths were tastefully renovated in 2016. Enjoy a fully fenced backyard oasis with a screened-in porch and firepit - perfect for summer gatherings and relaxed outdoor living. The basement has additional 800 sq feet of space with full gym. Move-in ready and walkable to school.
