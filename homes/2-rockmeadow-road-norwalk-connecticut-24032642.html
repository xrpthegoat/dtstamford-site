About this home

Welcome to 2 Rockmeadow Rd in the Spring Hill neighborhood of Norwalk. This property is perfectly located at the end of a dead end/cul de sac resulting in low/minimal traffic. While it is a quiet road it finds itself just minutes away from many shops/restaurants, CT AVE, I95 (14,15), Merritt PKWY, SONO, Train Stations & MORE! This Cape Cod style home is situated on .37 acres and has been well maintained both inside & out for the last 20+ years. Major ticket items like the roof, siding & windows (Anderson) were installed in 2018. The entire kitchen including cabinets, backsplash, appliances & living room flooring completed in 2018 also. All mechanicals including oil furnace & electric meter/panel have been recently upgraded. Belgian block lined pathways, newer masonary work, newer vinyl siding & professional landscape work bring great curb appeal. The main level includes a spacious living room (newer bamboo flooring), updated kitchen(stainless steel app/recessed lighting), 2 bedrooms (with ample closet space) & a Full Bath(tub w/ floating glass door). Door from kitchen leads to rear deck PERFECT for summer BBQ's! Second level (partly unfinished) Includes 2 additional beds OR 1 bed - 1 family rm. Lower level basement (recessed lighting/tiled flooring) finished living space (576 sq ft) incl. half bath & initerior/exterior access through garage. Rear yard has a wood deck, ground level patio/slab, above ground pool, 2 car- carport & plenty of space for garden. Come on down!
