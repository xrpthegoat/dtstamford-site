About this home

Welcome to this charming Cape Cod in the heart of Old Greenwich, where timeless character meets modern comfort and style. Filled with natural light and warmth, this inviting 3-bedroom home offers a wonderful blend of classic architectural details and thoughtfully designed living spaces. A welcoming entry leads to a spacious living room featuring rich hardwood floors, a fireplace, and an abundance of windows that create a bright and airy atmosphere. The adjoining dining room provides an elegant setting for both everyday meals and entertaining. The stylish kitchen features crisp white cabinetry, sleek black countertops, brass accents, a farmhouse sink, and a charming built-in workstation, combining functionality with sophisticated design. Upstairs, three generously sized bedrooms offer comfortable and versatile spaces, each with its own unique character, complemented by a full bath. Additional highlights include an attached one-car garage, a full basement offering excellent storage and potential for additional uses, and a lovely level yard with mature landscaping and room to enjoy the outdoors. From its classic curb appeal and inviting interiors to its desirable Old Greenwich setting, this home offers the perfect combination of charm, comfort, and convenience. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy all that Old Greenwich living has to offer.
