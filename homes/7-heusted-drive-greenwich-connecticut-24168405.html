About this home

Set in the desirable Edgewater neighborhood, 7 Heusted Drive offers a flexible layout, multiple living options, and exceptional convenience in the heart of Old Greenwich. The home is designed to adapt to a variety of needs, with open, well-proportioned living spaces, high ceilings, and thoughtful detailing throughout. A central kitchen with a large island connects seamlessly to the main living areas and opens to a private rear patio, creating an easy flow for both everyday living and entertaining. The second level features a primary suite with private balcony and a luxurious bath with steam shower, heated floors and soaking tub, along with additional bedrooms and a conveniently located laundry area. A finished third floor adds valuable flexible space, offering options for work, recreation, or extended living. A separate two-story cottage provides a unique and highly functional extension of the home, complete with a spacious bedroom and full bath. Ideal for guests, private workspace, or creative use, this space adds meaningful versatility. Additional highlights include flexible room configurations, ample storage, and professionally landscaped grounds designed for low-maintenance living. Located just moments from the village, train, shoreline, restaurants, schools, and shopping, the property offers a rare combination of adaptability, comfort, and convenience in one of Old Greenwich's most sought-after neighborhoods.
