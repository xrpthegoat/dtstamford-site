About this home

Ideally located just a short drive from downtown Stamford and close to several public parks and golf courses, this spacious and sun-filled 2/3 bedroom, 3.5-bath townhome offers comfort, convenience, and flexibility in one of Stamford's most desirable areas. Hardwood floors flow throughout the main level, where a bright dining area opens into a comfortable living room centered around an elegant fireplace. The updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances added in 2020, wraparound quartz countertops, recessed lighting, a large refrigerator with freezer drawer, a walk-in pantry, and ample storage within classic white cabinetry. Just off the main living space, a large back patio creates the perfect setting for relaxing, entertaining, or enjoying a quiet weekend at home. Upstairs, the second level offers two bedrooms and a full guest bath, including a generous primary suite with its own private bath, abundant closet space, and a sunroom that fills the space with natural light. The lower level adds valuable flexibility with a garage, laundry room, additional storage, a full bath, and a rec room that can easily function as an additional bedroom, home office, gym, or living area. With a private pool just steps from your front door and easy access to downtown Stamford, parks, golf, shopping, and dining, this beautifully situated townhome offers a comfortable and convenient lifestyle in a highly desirable setting.
