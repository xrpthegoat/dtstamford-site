About this home

Discover the beauty and elegance of this meticulously maintained Colonial home. With it's stunning curb appeal and lush landscaping, this 3 bedroom, 2.5-bath gem welcomes you with warmth and style. Inside, you'll find sun-filled spacious living areas - LR, DR, FR with fireplace, screened in porch, and finished basement. Central A/C, gleaming hardwood floors and a thoughtfully designed layout perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The eat in kitchen offers expansive countertops and abundant cabinetry, providing both functionality and style. The oversized primary includes walk in closets and a generous sized bath. There are two additional bedrooms also with plenty of closet space. The attached 2-car garage enhances everyday living with its convenience and offers practical space for parking and extra storage. There is even a whole house generator. Nestled on a private road in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, this home has easy access to parks, schools, shopping, Glenbrook train Station and major highways - making it perfect for commuters and families. Whether you're looking for timeless charm or modern convenience, this home and location have it all.
