About this home

Rare mixed-use investment/owner-user opportunity on Fairfield County's Route 7 corridor. 387 Danbury Road offers 13,706 SF across three buildings on 1.83 acres, combining 6,055 SF of office space (six suites) with 7,651 SF of industrial/flex space, including a 6,451 SF warehouse/garage structure and a 1,200 SF steel building with two drive-in bays. The property delivers diversified in-place income with immediate upside - a 750 SF second-floor office suite is ready for lease-up with minimal capital, while small office tenants provide steady cash flow. For owner-users, the anchor industrial/office tenant has indicated willingness to negotiate an early lease termination, allowing a new owner to accelerate occupancy of over 6,000 SF well ahead of the scheduled expiration. Located adjacent to Wilton High School, minutes from Wilton Center, with convenient access to the Merritt Parkway and I-95. DRB zoning adds further flexibility. Ideal for investors seeking stable income with reversion potential, or owner-users establishing a presence on one of Fairfield County's most trafficked commercial corridors.
