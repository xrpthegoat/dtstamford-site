About this home

Situated in a sought-after Norwalk neighborhood, this impeccably maintained, meticulous 2-family home presents a rare opportunity for discerning investors or owner-occupants seeking both comfort and income potential. One of the most beautifully preserved early 20th century duplex buildings you will ever witness, with original stained glass windows and wood trim! The current owners have exceptionally updated kitchens, bathrooms, windows, vinyl siding, gas furnaces, laundry rooms, water heaters, front porch, gutters, plus all exterior trim was recently painted. Each unit has two comfortable bedrooms, an updated bath, a beautifully renovated kitchen, gorgeous original hardwood floors, and the convenience of in-unit laundry. A full dry basement offers plenty of private storage for each unit, and a walk-up attic offers exciting expansion possibilities. The detached 2-car garage provides a bay for each unit, with a 2nd spot outside. Additionally, a lovely backyard and a storage shed for garden and maintenance tools. Perfectly positioned near Route 7 and Main Ave., shopping, great restaurants, and public transportation, this property delivers the ideal balance of modern updates, character, and a prime location. It is a standout choice for an investor or owner living in one unit while generating rental income from the other. This kind of opportunity is very uncommon - jump on it.
