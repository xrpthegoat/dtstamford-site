About this home

YEARLY OR FLEXIBLE TERM FURNISHED OR UNFURNISHED LEASE AVAILABLE MID AUGUST! Experience an exceptional coastal lifestyle in the private waterfront enclave of Bluewater Hill in Compo Beach. Set on one of the area's premier streets, residents enjoy access to private association tennis courts & are just steps from Compo Beach & the LI Sound. This sophisticated 6,200-square-foot contemporary residence with 5 bedrooms, 4 full & 2 half baths offers year-round water views along with a rare blend of refined design, relaxed elegance, & effortless indoor-outdoor living. Thoughtfully designed with a flexible floor plan suited to a variety of lifestyles, the home seamlessly balances grand entertaining spaces with comfortable everyday living. Sun-filled formal living & dining rooms flow into a stunning family room & gourmet kitchen, while three private bedrooms on the main level open directly to beautifully landscaped grounds featuring a heated in-ground pool & hot tub, creating the ultimate setting for gatherings & summer entertaining. The serene second-floor primary suite offers a spa-inspired bath, expansive walk-in closet, private office, & private deck with stunning views. An additional bedroom & sitting area provide peaceful spaces to unwind. Finished lower level ideal for recreation, fitness, or media. Moments from Longshore, downtown Westport, train & I-95.
