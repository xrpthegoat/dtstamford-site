About this home

The best of Rowayton comes together at 60 Crooked Trail-timeless architecture, resort-style outdoor amenities, exceptional living spaces offering the ideal coastal lifestyle. Privately set on over an acre at the end of one of Rowayton's most coveted cul-de-sacs, this beautifully updated five-bedroom home offers soaring cathedral ceilings, exposed wood beams, abundant natural light, rich millwork, and a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace. The chef's kitchen, complete with premium appliances and custom cabinetry, is designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. French doors open to a wraparound covered porch and spectacular outdoor living spaces featuring a sparkling gunite pool, renovated pool house, outdoor kitchen, fireplace, bocce court, outdoor shower, expansive stone terraces, and beautifully landscaped level lawns-creating a private backyard retreat that lives like a luxury resort.The first-floor primary suite is a peaceful sanctuary with cathedral ceilings, a stone fireplace, two custom walk-in closets, a dressing area, and a spa-inspired bath. Three additional bedrooms, a flexible first-floor office or guest suite, and a finished lower level with a recreation room, gym, full bath, mudroom, and abundant storage provide exceptional comfort and versatility.
