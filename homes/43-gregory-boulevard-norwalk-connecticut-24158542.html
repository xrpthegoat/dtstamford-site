About this home

Welcome to 43 Gregory Blvd, a tastefully renovated gem nestled in the heart of East Norwalk's sought-after beachside community. This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, newly renovated 2-bath home blends timeless charm with modern comfort, offering exceptional convenience and style in one of Fairfield County's most desirable locations. Step inside to discover sun-filled living spaces highlighted by gleaming hardwood floors and central A/C throughout. The thoughtfully designed layout features a spacious living room, a dining area perfect for entertaining, and a bright, updated kitchen that seamlessly connects the main living areas. A charming three-season porch offers the perfect spot to enjoy morning coffee or unwind with a glass of wine in the evening. A full basement provides ample storage along with a washer and dryer. Upstairs, three well-appointed bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or a home office. Outside, the lush, fenced-in yard creates a private and secure setting for pets or outdoor gatherings, while the detached one-car garage provides added storage and convenience. Ideally located within walking distance to Calf Pasture Beach, Taylor Farm Dog Park, and Norwalk Cove Marina. Enjoy all that East Norwalk has to offer, including local restaurants, golf, shops, and the Metro-North Railroad for an easy commute.
