About this home

Steeped in history and thoughtfully updated for modern living, this charming 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home seamlessly blends the character of its 1884 origins with today's conveniences. Filled with natural light and timeless architectural details, the home offers inviting spaces designed for both everyday comfort and effortless entertaining. Relax on the welcoming front porch overlooking the picturesque tree-lined street, or unwind in the private backyard featuring a peaceful patio-perfect for summer gatherings, morning coffee, or quiet evenings outdoors. The beautifully renovated kitchen, updated just three years ago, features stylish finishes, modern appliances, and a functional layout that opens to the spacious family room. Anchored by a large fireplace and custom built-ins, it is the perfect place to gather and unwind. The fourth bedroom is currently configured as a second-floor laundry room and playroom but can easily be converted back to a bedroom. A large attic and finished basement workshop provide abundant storage and flexible space. Adding to the home's appeal is a detached one-car garage with a studio apartment above-ideal for guests, an in-law or nanny suite, home office, creative studio, or potential rental income. Ideally located just minutes from Rowayton Village, the train station, downtown Darien, and the shores of Long Island Sound, this home offers the perfect combination of historic charm, versatile living space and ideal location.
