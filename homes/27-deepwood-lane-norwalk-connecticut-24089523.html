About this home

Welcome to 27 Deepwood Lane, a charming Cape tucked away on a quiet, family-friendly cul-de-sac near the Rowayton border. This sweet home offers the best of coastal living with beach rights to Calf Pasture Beach - perfect for summer days by the Sound. The backyard is a standout feature, boasting a spacious deck for entertaining, a beautiful lawn, and wooded privacy that creates a peaceful retreat or a perfect spot for play. Inside, you'll find a warm and inviting layout featuring four bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a dedicated dining room, and a large basement that offers plenty of flexibility. Set in a walkable, close-knit neighborhood just minutes from downtown Rowayton, SONO, and the beach, this is a wonderful place to call home!
