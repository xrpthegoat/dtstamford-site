About this home

An exceptional opportunity to rent a luxury home in the heart of Westport and be settled in before the school year begins. Tucked away at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac, this exceptional five-bedroom Colonial offers the rare combination of privacy, expansive living spaces, and an unbeatable in-town location. Set on 1.5+ beautifully landscaped acres, this custom home was thoughtfully rebuilt in 2010 and designed for effortless everyday living and elegant entertaining. Filled with natural light, the home features hardwood floors throughout and a welcoming floor plan centered around a beautifully appointed eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a generous center island, and plenty of room to gather. Spacious living areas open to an expansive deck overlooking sweeping lawns and professionally maintained perennial gardens, creating a serene private retreat for relaxing or entertaining. A spacious private in-law suite, complete with its own kitchen, fireplace, and versatile living space, offers exceptional flexibility for extended family, guests, an au pair, or a private home office. The walk-out lower level provides additional recreation space, while the finished third floor offers endless possibilities as a media room, fitness studio, playroom, or creative workspace.All of this just minutes from downtown Westport's acclaimed restaurants, top-rated schools, boutique shopping, beaches, and parks.
