About this home

Dreaming of the perfect summer escape? Look no further. This exquisite home is an entertainer's paradise, designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living. Enjoy a heated saltwater pool, the expansive terrace, a fully equipped outdoor kitchen, and a cozy gas fire pit-perfect for hosting summer parties. Set on beautifully landscaped grounds, this stunning residence offers a versatile layout with five spacious bedrooms plus a dedicated home office, four full baths, and two powder rooms. The oversized eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream - with honed granite countertops, high-end stainless steel appliances and wet bar. Unwind in the elegant formal living room with a fireplace or enjoy a movie night on the large-screen TV in the family room. A bright summer sunroom with an additional desk makes working from home enjoyable. A dedicated playroom upstairs with built-in projector offers more room for fun, while a private gym and sauna provide an at-home wellness retreat. A potential in-law suite above the garage is perfect for hosting, and a Tesla charging station in the garage adds modern convenience. This opportunity is just steps from town, with an easy commute to NYC by car or train. What are you waiting for?! Your perfect summer sanctuary awaits.
