About this home

Exquisite and luxurious, this completely remodeled North Stamford ranch sits on 1.36 private acres and offers a perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and modern living. Beautiful hardwood floors, crown moldings, and recessed lighting flow throughout, leading to an open, bright, and airy living room with a wood-burning fireplace and large sliding glass doors that connect to an expansive deck and large, flat backyard. The dining room provides an elegant space for gatherings and offers seamless access to the screened-in porch, making indoor or outdoor entertaining effortless. The brand-new chef's kitchen impresses with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and high-end finishes designed for both beauty and function. The primary suite is a true retreat with a spacious walk-in closet and a luxurious marble-finished bathroom with soaking tub, glass shower, and double vanity. A second large en-suite bedroom offers its own full bathroom and direct deck access, while two additional bedrooms and a third full bathroom provide flexibility. The finished lower level adds valuable living space with a family room and second wood-burning fireplace, complemented by an oversized laundry room and a two car garage. Ideally located near shopping, dining, and just minutes from the Merritt Parkway.
