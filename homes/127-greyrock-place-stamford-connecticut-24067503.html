About this home

Luxury living at The Biltmore in downtown Stamford! Large, sun-bathed 1 bed/1 bath unit on the 16th floor with hardwood floors, newer appliances, central air, and updated bathroom, comes complete with beautiful views of the LI Sound. The spacious bedroom has a wall of windows, large walk-in closet and separate bathroom entrance. The building offers a 24 hour concierge, a 2000+sf fitness center, shuttle service to the train station, party/community room, dry cleaning service, laundry room, 4th floor courtyard and garage parking (additional $115/month/car). Walk out the front door to everything Stamford has to offer...restaurants, theater, shopping, library and transportation. And you're less than an hour commute to NYC! Must have good credit and references. Move in/out fee $300 to be paid by tenant. No additional amenities fee!
