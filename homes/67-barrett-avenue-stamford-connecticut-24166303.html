About this home

***HIGHEST & BEST DUE APR 21st @ 12PM*** If you're looking for that perfect mix of convenience and neighborhood feel, this is it in Pepper Ridge. Close to both the Stamford and Glenbrook Metro-North trains for an easy NYC commute, and just minutes to I-95 and the Merritt Pkwy, while still being able to walk to Newfield Shopping Center, Barrett Park, and houses of worship. Set on a quiet street, this classic New England Colonial has the charm you want with a layout that actually works. The main level starts with a bright, inviting living room with fireplace and great natural light, flowing into a proper dining room with built-ins. The eat-in kitchen offers solid cabinet space and connects easily to the back of the house, where a sunroom brings in tons of light and opens out to the patio for easy indoor-outdoor living. A full bath completes the first floor. Upstairs are three bedrooms, including a primary with a newly added ensuite bath, plus an additional renovated full bath for the other bedrooms. The walk-up attic is a major bonus, offering excellent storage and flexible space. The lower level provides additional storage, usable space, and a half bath. Outside, enjoy a level, fenced-in backyard with a great rectangular layout, a walk-out patio, and a one-car garage. A move-in ready Colonial in one of Stamford's most convenient neighborhoods.
