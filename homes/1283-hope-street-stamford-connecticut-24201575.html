About this home

Welcome to 1283 Hope Street, Unit 4, a spacious two-bedroom townhouse-style condominium ideally located in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood near the New Canaan border. This bright two-level home features beautiful hardwood floors, recessed lighting, crown molding, and generous living spaces. The large eat-in kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, and a center island with seating and prep space. It opens naturally to the dining area, while the sun-filled living room provides a welcoming space to relax or gather. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms offer excellent closet space along with a full bathroom. In-unit laundry, basement and attic storage, and two parking spaces add valuable convenience and functionality. Set near the Springdale Metro-North station, shopping, dining, major roadways, and the New Canaan border, this home offers an easy commute and access to the best of Stamford and the surrounding area.
