About this home

Welcome to your dream home in the heart of the charming Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk.As you pass through the arbor gate and into your private,fenced 0.39-acre sanctuary, you'll immediately feel the peace and seclusion of this truly special property. Step inside & be welcomed by an open-concept living space designed with both style and functionality in mind. The updated kitchen is a chef's dream, complete with a stunning center island and luxurious quartz countertops. The adjoining Family Room is a warm, inviting space, where the crackling wood-burning fireplace sets the perfect mood for cozy nights spent with loved ones. Large windows in the Great Room flood the space with natural light, highlighting the newly refinished hardwood floors that connect it to the kitchen and dining area.The Primary Bedroom is your private retreat, bathed in natural light and offering serene views of your lush yard. The en-suite bathroom is a peaceful haven with a sleek walk-in shower and contemporary finishes. Three additional bedrooms and a hall bath round out the second floor.The lower level offers endless possibilities to suit your needs, whether as a playroom,media room or home office, this space is ready to adapt to your lifestyle.With direct access to the backyard, it can easily transform into inlaw or au pair space.Outside, the backyard is an oasis, featuring a durable Trex deck perfect for entertaining, along with a stone patio and firepit area that beckon you to relax and unwind.
