About this home

Welcome to Chesterfield, a highly sought-after gated community in desirable Mid-City Stamford. This sunny and spacious townhome presents an exceptional opportunity for its next owner. Featuring a private front entrance and a dramatic two-story foyer, the main level offers a fully renovated eat-in kitchen illuminated by a skylight with new cabinets, countertops, and appliance alongside a seamless flow between the generous dining area and the cozy living room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. Sliding glass doors lead out to a large private deck, making indoor-outdoor entertaining effortless. Beautifully polished hardwood floors run throughout the principal living spaces. On the top floor, a spacious primary retreat provides comfort, privacy, and convenience. Tons of storage possibilities in attic and crawl space. Residents of this vibrant community enjoy 24/7 gatehouse security, a resort-inspired inground pool, a clubhouse, ample parking, and serene sidewalks beautifully canopied by mature trees - all just steps from local shopping, cafes, eateries, and downtown Stamford.
