About this home

Thank you for all the interest in 11 Inwood Rd! The seller is asking for offers to be submitted to listing agent by 5pm on Monday 5/11/2026. Location, Location, Location! Set on a beautiful one-acre corner lot in desirable Silvermine, this classic Colonial offers endless potential and timeless appeal. Step inside through the welcoming foyer, where you'll find an oversized living room to the left, filled with natural light from multiple windows and anchored by a large fireplace-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. To the right, a formal dining room flows seamlessly into the eat-in kitchen, featuring a Thermador refrigerator, Bosch dishwasher, GE cooktop, and double wall oven. A skylight brightens the dining area, adding warmth and charm. The spacious family room boasts a vaulted ceiling, second fireplace, and wet bar, creating an inviting space for gatherings. From here, step out to the three-season screened porch overlooking the expansive backyard with patio and grassy area and pool. A half-bath completes the main level. Upstairs, the home offers a primary suite with walk-in closet, three additional bedrooms with ample storage - and a full hallway bath with tub/shower, providing ample space for family and guests. With strong bones, high ceilings, and abundant natural light throughout, this home is ready for your personal touch. A rare opportunity to create something truly special in one of Silvermine's most sought-after settings!
