About this home

**Best and final offers are requested by 4pm on Tuesday, 12/17/24. Nestled on a quiet cul de sac in the desirable Castlewood Park neighborhood, this 5-bedroom raised ranch offers a perfect blend of comfort, space, and potential. Boasting 9 total rooms and 2 full bathrooms, this home is ready to be customized with a fresh coat of paint and your personal touch. City records say 3 bedrooms but there are 4 on the main level and one on the lower. Inside, hardwood floors extend throughout much of the living space, while a large bay window in the living room invites natural light and creates a warm, welcoming atmosphere. The layout is versatile, offering ample room for family living, entertaining, and creating the perfect work-from-home space. For added convenience, this home features attic storage, providing plenty of space for seasonal items, holiday decorations, and extra belongings. An oversized two-car garage offers room for parking, storage, and more, adding practicality to this home. Step outside to discover a large deck and a covered patio, ideal for hosting gatherings, relaxing on weekends, or enjoying the serenity of your private yard. The home also features central air conditioning and heat to ensure comfort all year round and durable vinyl siding for low-maintenance living. While this property will benefit from a new roof and some updates, it offers incredible potential for buyers looking to invest in a spacious home with fantastic features and a great location.
