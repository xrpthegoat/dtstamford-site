About this home

Beautifully designed 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home on 1.66 private acres with direct pond access-a rare combination of location, privacy, and natural beauty in North Stamford. Centered around a stunning floor-to-ceiling sunroom with panoramic water views, the layout features formal dining flowing into an open kitchen and two living rooms, with a deck that overlooks one of the largest ponds in the area. A private trail winds down to your own dock, perfect for kayaking, fishing, or evening swims. The backyard enjoys afternoon shade from perfectly positioned trees - while also allowing spectacular waterfront sunsets. Flowering gardens offer seasonal color with roses and hydrangeas, and the elevated positioning ensures remarkable privacy and tranquility, shielded from road noise and street view. Conveniently located with easy access to Greenwich, New Canaan, Bedford, and Stamford via scenic roads, bypassing most rush-hour congestion-ideal for commuters. Quality finishes throughout include hardwood floors, a contemporary staircase, marble bathrooms, white fencing, and flexible lower-level space for office, gym, or storage. A rare waterfront opportunity with exceptional privacy and stunning sunset views in North Stamford.
