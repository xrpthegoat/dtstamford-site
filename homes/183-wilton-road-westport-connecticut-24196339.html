About this home

An incredible opportunity has become available in a prime Westport, CT location. Set off the road on a quiet turnoff at the corner of the Overlook cul-de-sac, this expanded Cape on 0.52+/- acre of enchanting property is ready for your creative vision. This is the only 3 bedroom, 3 full bathroom home available in Westport below $1,649,000 - making this a spectacular chance to add value by updating, remodeling, expanding, or building new construction within moments of downtown, train, the Merritt Parkway, I-95, the Y, & schools. This quintessential New England Cape Cod-style home begins with a larger than expected foyer/center hall. The large living room has the first fireplace, built-ins, and French doors leading to the distinctive family room with a custom-patterned hardwood floor, stone fireplace and bookshelves. Formal dining room for entertaining. The kitchen was remodeled w/quality cabinetry and beautiful granite counters, top appliances including Sub Zero, and leads to the vltd ceiling breakfast room with skylights & glass doors facing both front & back. Private office/bonus room/playroom lined with built-ins. The first of the 3 BRs is on the main level, with built-ins and an adjacent full bath. Upstairs, the 2nd BR has its own full bath and the primary suite is an incredible surprise, with king-size BR, oversized lux-scale bathroom & walk-in clst. Partially fin LL. Patios & charming garden paths add to the character. This home requires work and is being sold-as is.
