About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated colonial, where charm meets modern convenience. Nestled in the heart of Wolfpit, this home boasts an inviting front porch-perfect for relaxing with a morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Inside, you'll find a thoughtfully renovated interior designed for comfort and style. The warm and inviting family room, complete with a striking stone fireplace, flows seamlessly into the updated kitchen. From there, step out onto a gorgeous patio creating an ideal outdoor living space that overlooks a fully fenced, meticulously landscaped backyard. Your own private retreat! The open floor is perfect for hosting gatherings, while a second family room with an attached bathroom on the main level offers flexibility-it can serve as a guest suite, home office, or additional living space. Upstairs, the stunning primary suite boasts soaring cathedral ceilings and a full en-suite bath. Two additional bedrooms(one is being used as an office) and another full bath complete the second floor, offering ample space for family and guests. Additional highlights include newer central air and an updated HVAC system (2020), ensuring year-round comfort. Don't miss the opportunity to own this beautifully updated home in one of Wolfpit's most desirable neighborhoods. We are in a multiple offer situation. Sellers ask that all highest and best offers be in by April 4th at noon.
