About this home

Majestically sited behind a classic stone wall and fence on 1.16 dry flat acres, this exceptional 6-bedroom, 5.2-bath residence offers the perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and modern living. Designed by noted local architect William A. Achilles, the home showcases a dramatic open floor plan filled with natural light and refined architectural detail across three beautifully finished levels. At the heart of the home is a stunning chef's kitchen featuring state-of-the-art appliances, custom cabinetry, and generous gathering spaces ideal for entertaining and everyday living. A private study provides the perfect work-from-home retreat, while the luxurious primary suite offers a serene escape with a spa-like bath and abundant closet space. There are 4 additional Bedrooms on the 2nd floor. The Lower level boasts a legal 6th bedroom and bath, a gym and a perfect spot for an in-home theatre. Thoughtful craftsmanship and high-end finishes are evident throughout, complemented by a 20kw generator for peace of mind. Outdoors, enjoy your own private resort with a gunite pool, spa, and automatic cover, surrounded by expansive lawn, gorgeous patio and mature landscaping with an in-ground irrigation system-perfect for play, relaxation, and entertaining. This is a rare opportunity to enjoy timeless design, modern amenities, and exceptional space both inside and out.
