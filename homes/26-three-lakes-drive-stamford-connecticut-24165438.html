About this home

Completely reimagined in 2020, this turn key residence at 26 Three Lakes Drive offers the perfect blend of modern design, comfort, and flexibility in one of Stamford's most peaceful residential settings. A sun-filled open-concept layout is anchored by a show-stopping chef's kitchen featuring custom white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and an island ideal for entertaining. The seamless flow into the living and dining areas, complemented by gleaming hardwood floors and recessed lighting, creates an elevated everyday living experience. The primary suite is a private retreat featuring a well designed ensuite bath and generous his-and-hers closets. while two additional bedrooms and a beautifully updated full bath complete the main level. The fully finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional versatility with two additional bedrooms, a third full bath, and multiple living spaces ideal for a media room, home gym, guest suite, or multi-generational living. Sliding doors provide direct access to a private, level backyard oasis featuring a brand new deck, patio, fresh landscaping, and a multi-zone irrigation system for effortless outdoor enjoyment. Additional highlights include a full 2020 gut renovation with updated mechanicals, roof, siding, and interiors, a newly paved driveway with ample parking, and professional masonry and fencing enhancing curb appeal. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, and Metro-North, this move-in-ready home delivers.
