About this home

This beautifully renovated single-level home is situated in the sought-after Cranbury neighborhood and features a contemporary open floor plan. The completely remodeled eat-in kitchen boasts LG appliances, a new island with storage & seating, along with a dry bar in the family room...ideal for entertaining. The layout encompasses a living room with a striking corner fireplace, dining area, family room, sunroom with access to the private yard, and an attached garage. The primary ensuite features plenty of closet space and a tastefully redesigned full bathroom with double vanity. There are two additional bedrooms and 2nd updated full bath. The flat backyard is fenced for privacy and safety. A full walk-up attic provides ample storage and potential for additional living space. The property is conveniently located within a mile of Cranbury shopping center, park, elementary school, and easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and train. Recent upgrades include; a new HVAC system, washer and dryer, roof, windows, siding, and much more! Move right in & enjoy! Please submit offers by Monday 3/10 @ 2pm
