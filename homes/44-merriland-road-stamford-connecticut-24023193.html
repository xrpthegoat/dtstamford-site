About this home

***HIGHEST & BEST DUE TUES. (6/18) @ 11A*** Discover modern elegance in this stunning home at 44 Merriland Rd. in Stamford. Upon entering, you're greeted by a sunlit foyer with gleaming hardwood floors and an impressive staircase. Make your way to the sunken living room to relax and entertain with family and friends. Take advantage of the seamless flow to the dining room which has room for a crowd. The kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with stainless steel appliances, ample counter space, huge island and a wealth of cabinetry. A breakfast nook allows for casual meals and leads to the wrap-around deck for al-fresco dining. Transition to the family room with a dramatic fireplace, vaulted ceilings, skylights and access to the deck for true indoor-outdoor living. A convenient laundry room is located off the kitchen adding to the functional layout. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a serene escape with in-suite bathroom, soaking tub, separate shower, dual sinks and walk-in closet. 3 additional bedrooms share a large bathroom with dual sinks. The finished lower level is a versatile space, with direct access to the backyard & oversized 2-car attached garage. This level is perfect for a home office, gym, or playroom. The backyard is a tranquil oasis with mature landscaping, providing the perfect setting for outdoor activities and relaxation. Conveniently located near highways, parks, shopping & dining, this home offers the best of Stamford living.
