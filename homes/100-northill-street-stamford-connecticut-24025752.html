About this home

Step into this captivating vintage colonial nestled in the heart of Springdale, set on a peaceful corner lot. Adorned with period details such as arched doorways, this home boasts a gracious formal living room featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace and French doors that lead to the sunroom, perfect for use as a home office or to cozy up with a book. The formal dining room flows seamlessly into the kitchen, where freshly painted wood cabinets, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances create a delightful culinary space. A breakfast bar and casual dining area opens to the family room, with sliders to the yard. Upstairs, discover three bedrooms, including a primary suite complete with a private bath and walk-in closet. Two additonal bedrooms and hall bath complete this level. The lower level is perfect as a playroom or rec room. The location can't be beat, within walking distance to the Springdale train station, and to diverse dining and shopping options. This enchanting home is a true gem, combining vintage charm with modern comforts for a timeless living experience.
