About this home

The Legacy of Elegance: 1.12 Acres in the Heart of Westport. Some homes are built for a market. This one was built for a lifetime. Set on 1.12 tree-lined manicured acres framed by iconic New England stone walls, this pristine Westport Colonial is the definitive intersection of Classic Taste and Modern Life: Located in one of the town's most coveted walkable enclaves-just an hour from Manhattan-the home delivers both privacy and connection. The exceptional craftsmanship and layout are designed for the modern rhythm: Five bedrooms, seven baths and two dedicated offices allow effortless movement from productivity to play. Custom millwork and flooded natural light ground the home in tradition, while the versatile bonus spaces ensure there is a place for everything-and everyone. The backyard isn't just a lot; it's a resort. A newly renovated pool and spa are anchored by a covered pavilion, creating a private stage for the quintessential Connecticut summer. This is where long summer days, spontaneous dinners, and poolside mornings happen - all within the safety of a neighborhood where you can ride bikes to friends' houses and neighbors still say, "Hello." Minutes from Westport's gold-standard schools and its vibrant downtown, this home offers something incredibly rare: a true lifestyle rooted in connection and ease. While trends come and go, this home will endure.
