About this home

Welcome to 68 Orchard Drive, a distinguished residence nestled within the coveted, gated Milbrook Association. Built in 1928 and reimagined with thoughtful updates, this home seamlessly marries the enduring character of its architectural heritage with the comforts and style of modern living.The home offers five spacious bedrooms and four full baths. The primary suite is a serene retreat featuring radiant heated floors and an instant hot water system in the spa-inspired bath. Hardwood floors throughout, a wood-burning fireplace, and thoughtfully placed French doors create an inviting atmosphere that flows effortlessly from intimate gathering moments to grand entertaining. The gourmet kitchen, complete with center island, and high-end appliances, opens to warm gathering spaces, while abundant natural light highlights the home's craftsmanship and attention to detail. Beyond the home itself, ownership in Milbrook affords a lifestyle without compromise. Just moments from Greenwich Avenue, schools, and the train, 68 Orchard Drive balances privacy, sophistication, and convenience in an extraordinary way. This is a rare opportunity to own a piece of Greenwich history in one of its most sought-after neighborhoods, a residence that embodies timeless elegance and modern luxury.
