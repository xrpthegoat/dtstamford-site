About this home

Located in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood, 38 Stonecrop Road North offers the ease of one-level living paired with exceptional indoor-outdoor flow. Filled with natural light and meticulously maintained, this home is designed for relaxed living, effortless entertaining, and everyday enjoyment. The updated kitchen and main living areas open seamlessly to an expansive patio and private fenced backyard-creating a natural extension of the home and an ideal setting for summer barbecues, outdoor dining, and gatherings of any size. Vaulted ceilings and a striking floor-to-ceiling brick fireplace bring character and scale to the family room, creating a space that feels both warm and open. The thoughtfully designed floor plan offers a wonderful balance of connection and privacy, with inviting living spaces, generously sized bedrooms, and a primary suite with its own bath. Hardwood floors, central air, a whole-house generator, and numerous improvements enhance the home's move-in-ready appeal. Set on .64 acres in the heart of Cranbury, just moments from beloved Cranbury Park and convenient to Westport, Metro-North, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes, this is easy Connecticut living at its best.
