About this home

Charming Ranch-Style Home in Sought-After Brookside Location Nestled on a quiet, tree-lined street in the desirable Brookside neighborhood, this well-maintained 2-bedroom, 1-bath ranch offers comfort, convenience, and potential. Located just minutes from major highways, public transportation, shopping, and more, this home combines peaceful suburban living with unbeatable convenience.
