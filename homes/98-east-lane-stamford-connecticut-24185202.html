About this home

Set on nearly an acre in the desirable Revonah Woods neighborhood, 98 East Lane is a 3-bedroom, 3-bath contemporary offers the best of privacy and proximity-just minutes to downtown Stamford, Metro-North, I-95, shopping, and houses of worship. With 2,528 sq ft of living space, dramatic vaulted ceilings, and expansive windows throughout, the home is flooded with natural light and lush green views from every angle. The open-concept layout features a large living area anchored by a double-sided brick fireplace, a dedicated dining room under a sparkling chandelier, and a spacious kitchen with skylight, ample cabinetry, and counter seating. Multiple flexible spaces can serve as home offices, playrooms, or guest areas. The finished lower level with walk-out access expands your options even more-perfect for movie nights, a gym, or creative studio space. Step outside to a wraparound deck ideal for dining al fresco or simply enjoying the peace and quiet of your wooded surroundings. Central air, gas heat, a one-car garage, and generous storage complete the package.
