About this home

Welcome to 559 Cheese Spring Rd., a sun-drenched and meticulously maintained 4,300 square-foot center hall colonial - a Connecticut classic that is stylish, pristine and truly move-in ready. The first floor offers a gracious living room, large dining room with fireplace and built-ins, and a sunlit sunroom/playroom with cathedral ceiling which opens onto a lovely outdoor deck. The updated gourmet eat-in kitchen features high end appliances and opens to a spectacular oversized family room with a fireplace and built-ins. A new marble powder room and laundry complete the main level. Upstairs, the expanded primary suite addition features a cathedral ceiling, spa-like marble bath and two walk-in closets. A second en-suite bedroom with a walk-in closet, three additional bedrooms and a full bath round out the second floor. The walk-out lower level offers a spacious playroom with built-ins, full bath, bar area, and dedicated exercise space, with French Doors opening to an inviting bluestone terrace. Set on two private, beautifully landscaped acres, there is a fabulous flat open play yard and a built-in firepit for outdoor gatherings. Exceptional in every way, this is a forever home to enjoy and cherish with family and friends.
