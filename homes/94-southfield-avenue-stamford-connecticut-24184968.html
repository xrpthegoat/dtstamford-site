About this home

Enjoy a vacation-like lifestyle at Stamford Landing, where waterfront living meets everyday convenience. This spacious townhouse features an open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area with fireplace and sliders to a private patio. The kitchen offers stone countertops, stainless steel appliances, and generous workspace. Upstairs, the primary bedroom includes an en suite bath and private balcony with views of the pool, park, and marina. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and in-unit laundry complete the bedroom level. Additional features include a half bath, one-car garage with built-ins, guest parking, and large private storage. Enjoy the pool, boardwalk, waterfront restaurants, downtown Stamford, Harbor Point, I-95, and the Stamford Train Station. Lease start date: August 1.
