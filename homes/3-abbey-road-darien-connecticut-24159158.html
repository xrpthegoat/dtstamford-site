About this home

Welcome to 3 Abby Road in Darien, a charming four-bedroom Colonial that blends timeless character with a bright, inviting layout. This beautifully maintained home features lovely moldings throughout and an abundance of natural sunlight that fills every room. The main level offers a wonderful open floor plan, creating an easy flow for living and entertaining. Spacious living areas connect seamlessly, making the home feel both comfortable and welcoming. Outside, the property truly shines with its meticulously landscaped, fenced garden and backyard-an ideal setting for relaxing and entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite provides a private retreat complete with a walk-in closet and en-suite bathroom designed for comfort and relaxation. 3 additional bedrooms and bath complete the second floor. The fully finished lower level adds generous flexible living space-ideal for recreation, hobbies, or a home office. Also features a full laundry room. With bright interiors, modern touches, and classic charm, 3 Abbey Road offers a wonderful opportunity to enjoy easy living in one of Darien's most desirable communities.
