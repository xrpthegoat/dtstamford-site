About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the Hindley School district, 3 Salt Box Lane East offers the perfect blend of timeless charm and modern comfort. This beautifully maintained home features sun-drenched living spaces thoughtfully designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. At the heart of the home, a spacious gourmet kitchen flows seamlessly into a vaulted family room with a cozy fireplace, creating an inviting space to gather. Upstairs, four generously sized bedrooms provide comfort and flexibility, including a serene primary suite with an updated bath. The finished lower level offers additional living space ideal for a playroom, home office, or gym. Step outside to a private backyard retreat complete with a stone patio, outdoor fireplace & hot tub - perfect for relaxing or entertaining year-round. Conveniently located close to town, train, schools, and beaches, this is a home you won't want to miss!
