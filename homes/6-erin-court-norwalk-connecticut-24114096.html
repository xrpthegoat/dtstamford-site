About this home

Tucked on a quiet cul-de-sac near Brien McMahon High School, 6 Erin Court offers flexible living in a location that puts everything within reach. This bright and modern raised ranch rental features 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and a layout that makes everyday living feel effortless. Upstairs, the new all-white kitchen is the heart of the home, with quartz countertops, a center island, and a fully open layout. Sliders lead from the dining room to a sunny deck-perfect for outdoor dining or morning coffee-overlooking a low-maintenance, lightly wooded backyard. Three bedrooms and a newly updated full bath complete the main level, while the finished lower level adds a fourth bedroom, second full bath, and bonus space that's ideal for guests, a home office, or media room. Enjoy the comfort of single-family living with easy access to I-95, Rowayton train station (less than a mile away), and everyday essentials like Costco, Wegmans, and Target just minutes from your door. With a stylish interior, outdoor entertaining space, and a super-convenient location, this home checks all the boxes for flexible, functional living in Norwalk. No pets, excellent income and credit required, no smoking, move in ASAP.
