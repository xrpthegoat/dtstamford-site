About this home

There is so much more to discover here than meets the eye at first glance! The understated outdoor charm of this storybook colonial, sitting on a flat, level .66 acre at the end of a private lane and away from the main road, will delight you with a surprisingly large, open, modern home. Quietly nestled in one of Darien, Connecticut's highly sought-after, friendly, and welcoming neighborhoods, this 11 room property offers inmense value for the discerning buyer. High ceilings, beautiful wainscoting and moldings throughout, with great living spaces to satisfy diverse family needs. With over 5,000 sf, 5 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, a flexible, open floor plan, built in 2001 and largely updated by its present owners, this home has a stunning, remodeled, eat-in kitchen opening to a family room with fireplace, entertainment center, French doors to the patio and outdoor grounds. A welcoming covered porch leads into the inviting foyer and a cozy parlor with fireplace, built-ins, and glass doors. The finished third floor has 2 bedrooms and bath, ideal for in-laws, nanny, guests. An office off the main bedroom, connects to a bonus room, also accessed by a second staircase off the large mudroom. The finished basement is a safe playground for the young and old alike.
