About this home

WATERFRONT W/MOORING~Embrace a serene coastal lifestyle with magnificent & dreamy sunsets from this West facing waterfront home in East Norwalk's sought-after MARVIN BEACH neighborhood. Kayak, swim or boat from your own front yard at the 'River House', with expansive WATER VIEWS. Every day feels like a holiday in this designer's newly renovated home, with a light-filled open floor plan that's artistically designed with coastal themes & distinctive details. The fully fenced corner lot has beautiful native plantings creating an outdoor haven. Stargaze & sunbathe from Primary Bedroom's large upper deck, or your Adirondack chairs at the water's edge. This Cape-Style home has an oversized Living Room with a wood-burning fireplace, a large Kitchen with Dining Area, a main level Bedroom & full Bath, plus a heavenly 3 season screened-in porch for lazy summer days. The Primary Bedroom, with a beautifully appointed Bath includes Radiant Heated floors. An additional Bedroom, Bath and Office/Laundry room complete the second level. Just 60 minutes to NYC and conveniently located near restaurants, shops, beaches, highways and commuter train. RARE chance to own on the water in this special neighborhood where one feels like everyday is a vacation. One of the owners is an agent.
