About this home

A timeless colonial tucked into a Newfield cul de sac makes its debut after 30yrs. Thoughtful 2003 expansion with contemporary amenities. This home exudes functionality. Enter and be greeted by a spacious double-story foyer, 9' ceilings, and generously sized living spaces complemented by gleaming hardwood floors throughout. The state-of-the-art kitchen is a chef's dream featuring top-tier appliances, custom cabinetry, and a sense of space perfect for casual gatherings or grand entertainment. Two main-level bedrooms are ready for guests or an in-home office. Retreat to the upper level where four generously sized bedrooms provide comfort and tranquility. The primary suite is a sanctuary boasting a spa-inspired en-suite bath with radiant flooring and walk-in closets. The private in-law suite on the lower level is a true standout feature, offering its own walkout patio, kitchenette, living area, and full bath. Ideal for extended family, guests, or au pair. Outdoors, a pool with wrap-around deck creates the perfect oasis for summer fun. Modernized for effortless living, this home boasts upgraded heating and electrical systems, ensuring efficiency and reliability, a full house generator, reverse osmosis lower-level kitchen faucet and more. This home is the perfect balance of heritage and innovation, crafted for those who seek the finest in multigenerational living. (Ring Door Surveillance)
