About this home

Welcome to 450 Cascade Road, a distinctive North Stamford mid-century modern home. It is set on a spectacular site along the Rippowam River on the New Canaan border, offering seclusion, beautiful views of protected watershed land, and convenient access to NYC. This four-bedroom, three-bath home offers character, quality finishes and a flexible layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. The main level features hardwood floors, a light-filled living room w/ fireplace, outdoor deck, dining area, and large kitchen. There are three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite w/ architect-designed bath, radiant heated floors, and large custom closet. Two additional bedrooms offer river views and share a full bath. The smaller bedroom is currently used as a home office. The walk-out lower level has a heated marble foyer floor, a fourth bedroom, with a full marble bath & direct pool access. It has a versatile family room with fireplace, ideal for guests, an office, or additional living space. Recent exterior improvements include new front stone wall & entrance, a reconstructed stone driveway, new roof, a new front lawn with stone retaining wall and integrated stone steps leading toward the river. An expansive deck overlooks the river and pool, framed by mature landscaping including many spring-flowering trees. An attached two-car garage and generous driveway provide ample parking. Discover the beauty, privacy & exceptional quality of this riverfront site.
