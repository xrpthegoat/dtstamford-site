About this home

Welcome to 28 Dubois Street, a charming 1940 colonial set on a 0.22-acre lot in desirable Darien. Offering 1,804 square feet of well-designed living space, this 3 bed, 2 bath home blends classic character with comfortable modern flow. The main level features a welcoming foyer, opening directly to a formal dining room and a warm living room anchored by a cozy fireplace. The living room flows seamlessly into a rear family room or den, which connects to a bright eat-in kitchen--the center of the home. A convenient full bath, an attached garage accessed via a practical mudroom, a laundry closet, and a spacious rear deck complete the first floor, making indoor-outdoor entertaining a breeze. Upstairs, you will find a split-bedroom configuration offering privacy and convenience. The spacious primary bedroom is complemented by two additional generously sized bedrooms featuring ample closet space all sharing a full bath. An unfinished basement provides excellent storage and potential. The yard is flat and private, perfect for summer barbecues. This home is ideally located on a quiet street just one mile from Noroton Heights Train Station, near Hindley Elementary School, Darien YMCA, Darien Library, and much more--Palmer's and Darien Commons are just around the corner. A timeless Darien gem ready for its next chapter!
