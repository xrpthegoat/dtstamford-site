About this home

Welcome to this bright and charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath end-unit townhome in the highly desirable Sylvan Knoll community-just a short stroll from the shoreline. Move-in ready, this home features an inviting eat-in kitchen with an open layout, elegant crown molding and beautiful hardwood floors. The finished lower level offers valuable bonus space for a family room, home office, or gym, and the comfort of central air enhances year-round living. Enjoy the outdoor patio and side yard, perfect for relaxing, grilling, or gardening. Sylvan Knoll is a pet-friendly neighborhood set on 13 acres of manicured grounds, complete with sidewalks and individual garden plots for residents to cultivate. Great commuter location, just a short distance to I-95, shopping, dining, the beach, marina, and skating rink, making this the ideal blend of comfort, convenience, and coastal living.
