About this home

Available as a furnished, short-term rental. Move right into this classic colonial home, nestled on over an acre of land on a lovely North Stamford cul-de-sac. This well-maintained 5-bedroom, 3.5 bath home features a formal living room, a spacious eat-in kitchen with pantry closets, stainless steel appliances and granite countertops, a formal dining room and a cozy family room with a fireplace and screened-in deck. The finished lower level has sliders to access the back patio and yard, a large recreation room with fireplace, a wet bar for entertaining and a full bath. Three car attached garage. Whole house generator. Available for flexible furnished rental through beginning August 15, 2026 through May 31, 2027.
