About this home

Furnished home is AVAILABLE FEBRUARY 15, 2026-APRIL 15, 2026. Experience luxury coastal living in this designer-renovated residence, perfectly situated just one block to Compo Beach and tucked away on a private cul-de-sac in Westport's most coveted beach neighborhood. Bathed in natural light, this sun-drenched home blends relaxed elegance with sophisticated design, offering 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms. The heart of the home is a newly remodeled gourmet chef's kitchen featuring custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, a large walk-in pantry, opening to an open-concept family room with a marble fireplace-ideal for everyday living or entertaining in style. The main level also includes a living room with fireplace and French doors, a private office currently used as a playroom, and a formal dining room with an adjoining bar area & wine refrigerator. A mudroom & laundry room complete the first floor. Upstairs, the primary suite is a tranquil retreat with his-and-her closets, joined by three additional bedrooms-one with an ensuite bath and two sharing a Jack-n-Jill bathroom. Outdoor spaces evoke resort-style living with a stone patio, a covered dining area, and a large seating area with an outdoor TV & wood-burning fireplace, roof deck with stunning sunset and water views, and a refreshing outdoor shower. Smart home technology. A turnkey coastal sanctuary offering refined design, privacy, and unmatched convenience in Westport, Connecticut's most sought after beach area.
