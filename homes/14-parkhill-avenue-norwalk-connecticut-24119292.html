About this home

Versatile 2-Unit Home with Endless Potential in Prime Norwalk Location Exceptional opportunity in one of Norwalk's most desirable and quiet neighborhoods, with easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, Routes 1 & 7. This versatile property offers flexible living arrangements and is ideal for multi-generational living or investment. The main residence features a formal living room with a charming fireplace and built-in cabinetry, wood floors (as seen), a formal dining room, family room, a spacious kitchen, 1 full and 2 half baths, plus two stunning great rooms with soaring ceilings, sliders, abundant windows-including a striking Palladian-for maximum natural light. Unit 2 (accessory apartment), located on the upper level, offers 2 bedrooms, a full bath, an open-concept kitchen, and a combined living/dining area with a cozy fireplace. Additional highlights include walk up attic, walk out basement, 3 garages, a generous driveway with space for multiple vehicles, and a beautiful lot ready for your vision. Enjoy Norwalk living at its finest-close to beautiful beaches, vibrant SoNo dining and nightlife, parks, entertainment venues, and the region's largest new SoNo Collection Shopping Mall. Conveniently situated between NYC and Boston. Property needs work and is being sold As Is, LBP-a perfect candidate for FHA 203K eligible financing, FHA, or cash buyers.
