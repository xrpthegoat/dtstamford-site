About this home

Great opportunity to own a lovingly maintained home cherished by the same family for over 50 years! Beautiful freshly refinished hardwood floors throughout the main level. This 4 bedroom, 2-bath home offers wonderful living space across two levels. The main floor features 3 bedrooms, full bath, inviting living room, dining area and kitchen with access to a deck overlooking a level backyard-perfect for outdoor enjoyment. The lower level adds valuable living space with a cozy family room anchored by a fireplace, 4th bedroom, powder room, laundry and convenient access to the attached 1-car garage. Neat and tidy while offering the perfect canvas to add your personal touches. Prime location close to shopping and major roadways. A wonderful opportunity to create lasting memories in this well-cared-for home!
