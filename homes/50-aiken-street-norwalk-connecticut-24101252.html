About this home

Enjoy resort like lifestyle at Rolling Ridge, one of the most desirable condo communities in Norwalk, nestled on park like grounds complete with pool, tranquil natural pond & paddle tennis. This top floor light, bright and airy corner condo is a quiet refuge filled with natural sun light throughout. Featuring open floor plan with spacious formal living room, refreshed kitchen with granite countertops and newer appliances with brand new dishwasher. Adjacent dining room opens onto private deck with gorgeous views to both pool, pond and woodlands. Move-in ready, heat, hot and cold water and private (7 x 7 storage room) are all included in rent. Plenty of parking too. Conveniently located to 95, Merritt Parkway, new Merritt 7 Train station, Route 7, restaurants and shopping.
