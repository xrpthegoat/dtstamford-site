About this home

Dream rental now available on Woody Lane, one of the most coveted cul-de-sacs in Westport! This exquisite custom built 2013 residence offers unparalleled privacy, luxury, and convenience, just a 2 min. walk from Wakeman Town Farm, athletic fields, Bedford Middle School, and Staples High School via a private path. Outdoor Paradise: The property features extraordinary grounds with a tennis, basketball, and pickleball court, and a spectacular pool oasis. Surrounding the 44-foot pool are beautifully landscaped bluestone patios, perfect for peaceful lounging and dining. Enjoy the masonry-clad Hot Tub year-round, and entertain in style with an outdoor cooking center that includes a pizza oven, new Lynx grill, and power burner. Elegant Interior: The kitchen and great room, with stunning outdoor views, serve as the home's natural hub. The bright, spacious kitchen boasts an expansive central island and a highly functional layout, with a butler's pantry leading to the dining room for seamless entertaining. The formal living room, featuring a fireplace, offers a front-to-back view of the property. A private home office and bedroom complete the main level. Upstairs, find four ensuite bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with dual closets and vanities. Additionally, a separate-entry apartment with 16-foot ceilings, its own kitchen, and laundry provides versatile living space for an au pair or family member seeking proximity and privacy.
