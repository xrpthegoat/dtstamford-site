About this home

Set within a quiet, well-maintained 13-unit building, this first-floor one-bedroom condo offers a rare mix of historic charm, modern comfort, and everyday convenience in the heart of Norwalk. Nine-foot ceilings and an arched doorway set a welcoming tone, complemented by hardwood floors, thick walls, and a peaceful atmosphere. The eat-in kitchen features stainless steel appliances and room to dine comfortably, while the living room is filled with natural light from a large picture window overlooking a beautiful historic elm tree--with a great sunrise as well. You'll find classic charm in every room. The bedroom is privately tucked off the hall with generous closet space. Additional highlights include basement storage, on-site laundry, and hot water included in the monthly HOA fee, which also covers snow removal, landscaping, trash service, common area maintenance, and major building systems. The building is known for long-term owners and renters, minimal turnover, and a calm, secure environment. The unit includes an assigned parking space in a protected area of the lot, with ample street parking as well. Ideally located near the Norwalk Hospital, SONO Collection, Metro-North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, this home delivers exceptional access without sacrificing privacy or quiet. Take a closer look and experience why this thoughtfully run building has remained a hidden gem in central Norwalk.
