About this home

***FINAL OFFERS by 10/20 @8p*** Living in another time with all the modern features of 2025, 143 Overbrook Drive is an expanded 1920s cape on one of Stamford's most special hidden streets, an oasis near Glenbrook Station, downtown, and both I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. You can walk to coffee shops, gyms, and more yet feel worlds away once you turn down this quiet, dead-end enclave at the end of the street. The home's front porch feels like a snapshot from another era, setting the tone for a home that honors its history yet feels entirely today. Inside, you'll find a living room with vintage charm, a formal dining room, updated kitchen, family room, first-floor en-suite bedroom, powder room, and practical modern must-haves like a mudroom and laundry room. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a changing room, the second bedroom has its own fireplace, and the third bedroom offers flexible space for guests or office. The flat half-acre yard is open, private, and beautifully maintained, with a detached garage completing the picture. Modern living is fully built in with city water, sewer, and gas, ductless air conditioning, and a waterproofed basement by CT Basement Systems. With nothing left to do but move in, 143 Overbrook Drive is the best of Stamford's past and present, offering timeless character with today's convenience.
