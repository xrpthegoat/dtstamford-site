About this home

Tucked away on two serene, park-like acres in North Stamford, 28 Pony Trail offers a refined blend of modern design and effortless living just minutes from the Greenwich border and Merritt Parkway. Natural light fills the home, enhanced by soaring vaulted ceilings and skylights that create an elevated yet inviting atmosphere with seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The property is set against a tranquil natural backdrop, complete with a gentle brook. A custom chef's kitchen by Deane anchors the home, featuring a large walnut center island, marble countertops, and a custom walk-in pantry, while living spaces extend to a large composite deck with multiple access points-ideal for entertaining or enjoying the peaceful surroundings. The Home offers two luxurious primary suites with spa-like baths, including a main-level suite with dressing area and walk-in closet. Upstairs, a second primary suite features dual walk-in closets, along with two additional bedrooms, an updated hall bath, large closets and custom built-ins. A spacious main-level office/den adds flexibility. Additional highlights include two laundry location options, a large unfinished lower level complete with gym, extensive storage, wine capacity for 500+ bottles and potential for a playroom. Enjoy close proximity to Greenwich horse trails and Greenwich Avenue, along with Metro-North access to NYC and Stamford amenities. Added features for convenience include a whole-house generator and irrigation system
