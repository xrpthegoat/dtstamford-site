About this home

This quintessential colonial sits on a quiet cul-de-sac only minutes from town. The main level consists of an updated kitchen, multiple family/living rooms that serve as functional gathering spaces with three wood burning fireplaces, a dining room, two powder rooms and a large three car garage. Front and back staircases lead you up to six bedrooms which includes a primary bedroom with gas fireplace and four full bathrooms. Finished lower level is perfect for a playroom/theater room with a gym and workshop. French doors lead you out back where you will find a large saltwater pool with a beautifully landscaped lawn, a new fence and an outdoor firepit - the perfect space to entertain. New roof in 2025. Don't miss this meticulously maintained home!
