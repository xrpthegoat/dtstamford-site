About this home

Welcome to this charming, naturally bright Cape Cod home set within the coveted Springdale neighborhood. Upon entering, you are greeted by an inviting living room featuring a wood-burning fireplace that seamlessly flows into the eat-in kitchen, complete with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and convenient access to the expansive deck and backyard. The main level also offers a spacious dining room featuring a built-in buffet nook with a wine refrigerator, a well-appointed bedroom, and a full bathroom. The upper level features two generously sized bedrooms and an additional full bathroom. The lower level provides a comfortable family room with French doors leading directly to the backyard, along with a large bedroom and a dedicated laundry room. Hardwood flooring runs throughout the home. Situated on a sizable corner lot, the home boasts a flat, fully fenced backyard ideal for outdoor enjoyment. This property is conveniently located near Springdale Elementary School, local parks, shops, and restaurants, offering both charm and everyday convenience.
