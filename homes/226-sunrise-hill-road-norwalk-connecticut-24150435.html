About this home

A ready-to-move-in condo with a great floor plan and thoughtful updates, 226 Sunrise Hill Road, Unit 226 is located in the Sunrise Hill Townhomes, a highly desirable area of Norwalk. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, and major commuting routes.
