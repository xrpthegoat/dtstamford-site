About this home

Step into a home where the past beautifully blends with the present. Originally built as a charming barn in 1900, this unique property was completely reimagined and constructed as a new home in 2006. During the renovation, great care was taken to preserve some of the original 1900 charm, seamlessly integrating it with modern amenities. You'll fall in love with the generous room sizes and 10-foot ceilings in the main rooms on the first floor, the well-designed plan, and the vast outdoor space, perfect for relaxation and recreation. Situated on flat land, the location is ideal for everything you need while providing a peaceful retreat. This home truly combines the warmth of its history with the convenience of contemporary living. See for yourself where old-world charm meets modern flair!
