About this home

Welcome to Windermere on the Lake, a premier gated community located in North Stamford and nestled in a majestic neighborhood. This custom-built home has spectacular curb appeal and lush landscaping. The double entry foyer sets the stage for elegance and quality craftsmanship. The living room opens into a bright dining room. The gourmet eat-in-kitchen has a 6-burner Wolf range with griddle, Thermador wall oven, warming drawer, marble countertops, center aisle, subzero refrigerator, wine cooler and sliders to terrace. The adjoining family room has a gas fireplace, perfect for entertaining. On the main level there is a 5th bedroom with a full bath which could easily serve as an au pair suite/den. A generously proportioned, well laid out mud room with a separate entrance completes this level. On the second floor the primary suite has a luxurious bathroom and massive walk-in closet. There are three additional bedrooms with ensuite bathrooms, two attics and a separate laundry room. The unfinished walk out basement has high ceilings offering room for expansion & storage. Hardwood floors and 9' ceilings throughout. Heated garage and 42k auto generator. This upscale property has an array of amenities, including a gatehouse, clubhouse, heated in-ground pool, two lakes, tennis, pickleball courts and walking trails. There are 24 well-manicured homes spread across 74 acres, giving one the ideal blend of privacy & community. Located within minutes to the Merritt, shopping & dining.
