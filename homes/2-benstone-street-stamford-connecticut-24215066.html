About this home

*Best bids due by or before 5 p.m. on Sunday October 25th.* Move right in and settle into the season in this thoughtfully updated three-bedroom, one-bath home, low step ranch. A wood-burning fireplace sets the scene for crisp fall evenings, with chimney repointing and relining completed as part of extensive improvements over the last four years. A fully renovated kitchen and bath, updated double pane windows, and interior paint and a new trim package create a welcoming, cohesive interior. Additional investments include a new 275-gallon basement oil tank and a professionally installed basement waterproofing system. Outside, the manicured yard and level, fenced backyard offer inviting space for dining, entertaining, and unwinding, with the pergola and patio furniture included in the sale. The unfinished basement offers potential for additional usable space and added value. Conveniently located near Whole Foods and a wide variety of shopping and dining options, with easy access to downtown Stamford, Metro-North service, and major commuting routes, this well-cared-for home combines move-in-ready comfort with room to grow.
