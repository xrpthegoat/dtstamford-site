About this home

BUYERS COULD NOT PERFORM. NO FAULT OF HOUSE. Indulge in coastal living at 21 Eno Lane, Saugatuck's neighborhood retreat, nestled on one acre at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac near the Long Island Sound and the Saugatuck River. This spacious home, with an open floor plan, and natural light throughout, features five bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms. The fully finished walk-out lower level includes a large office, a bedroom suite with a large bathroom and jacuzzi, a service bar, recreational area, laundry, and organized storage space. The main floor living space offers tranquil views of the outdoors, including a professional putting green for practicing golf skills, all while enjoying the privacy of acres of protected land trust. Embrace a scenic waterfront lifestyle, minutes from the Saugatuck Shores, Compo Beach, and The Longshore Recreational Park. Enjoy Westport's amenities in close proximity to I-95, the Metro North, Cedar Point Yacht Club, and a vibrant downtown with shops, renowned restaurants, and cultural offerings. Don't miss your chance to own this piece of paradise!
