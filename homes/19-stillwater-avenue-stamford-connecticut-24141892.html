About this home

Built in 2005 and move-in ready, this townhouse offers an exceptional blend of space, location, and value-with low HOA of just $250/month. This sun-filled 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath end unit spans three levels of well-designed living space. The main level showcases hardwood floors, a spacious open-concept living and dining area with access to a private balcony, and a modern, functional kitchen-perfect for both entertaining and everyday living. The upper level features three generously sized bedrooms, including a primary suite with a walk-in closet and ensuite bath. Two additional bedrooms share a full hall bath, providing versatility for family, guests, or a dedicated home office. Additional highlights include: Central air and forced hot air heating (natural gas) In-unit laundry. Attached garage with interior access A beautiful backyard Conveniently located near public transportation, downtown Stamford, parks, golf courses, health clubs, private schools, and more, this home offers comfort and accessibility in one of Stamford's most desirable areas.
