About this home

Spacious Beautiful Detached 4 bedroom/ 2baths/ 1 powder room townhouse. This is a townhouse which lives like a single family home in the heart of Springdale! Handsome hardwood floors throughout. Entertaining and relaxing is a delight in your large light-filled living room opening to the dining room with crown molding, a wall of sliders to the private patio & side yard. Enjoy preparing your meals in the Gourmet eat-in kitchen with granite counter tops, tile backsplash, sleek stainless steel deluxe appliances and gas cooking! The finished lower level family room has 9 ft ceilings with room for gym or home office. Upstair is a stunning Owner's Suite painted in a rich designer color, a walk-in closet with custom closet system, and a gorgeous ensuite bath with oversized shower to complete the suite. There are THREE (3) more generous bedrooms with ample closets and a beautifully updated bath with double sinks. And if more space is needed the light-filled walkup unfinished attic offers opportunity for another large Bonus room. Attached 1 car garage, central air, gas heat. And what a wonderful location! Close to restaurants, shopping, post office, library, school, Springdale RR station and Merritt.
