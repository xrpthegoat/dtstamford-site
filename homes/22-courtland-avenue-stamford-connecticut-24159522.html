About this home

Welcome to this charming 1-bedroom, 1-bath co-op perfectly situated in one of Stamford's most convenient locations. Just minutes from I-95, the beach, shopping, restaurants, theaters, and all the vibrant amenities the city has to offer, this home makes everyday living effortless. Inside, enjoy a nicely updated kitchen featuring granite countertops, tile flooring, and a classic beveled subway-tile backsplash. The renovated bath offers a fresh, modern touch, while the large bedroom includes durable laminate flooring for easy maintenance. Newer windows throughout provide great natural light and energy efficiency. Start your mornings on the private outdoor deck, perfect for coffee or quiet relaxation. Additional perks include a dedicated downstairs storage unit, plus taxes, heat, and hot water all included in the common charges, offering exceptional value. Move right in and enjoy convenient, comfortable living in the heart of Stamford! Reserved Parking #30 and Storage #4
