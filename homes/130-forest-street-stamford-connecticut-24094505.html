About this home

Experience vibrant downtown Stamford while enjoying the tranquility of a secluded community. Meticulously maintained, this 3 bedroom unit opens with an open-concept living room and dining room, perfect for hosting friends and family. Delight in the bright kitchen, boasting gleaming granite countertops and sleek stainless steel appliances. Relish in relaxation on the expansive, private brick patio located just off the Living Room. Unparalleled convenience of city living, just minutes from an array of restaurants, shops, and the countless attractions that downtown Stamford has to offer. Indulge in the epitome of urban living without compromising on privacy and tranquility.
