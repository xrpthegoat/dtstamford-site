About this home

Welcome to "The Village" as this lovely neighborhood in Westport is known by long time homeowners. This cape cod style home sits on a flat .25 acre lot on the cul de sac. Opportunity awaits for builders to take advantage of this great location and build a dream home either for you or for a client that has been looking for a terrific lot. If you are an end user and have been renting and waiting for the right home that you can move into... and remodel into your dream home at your own pace, this could be the opportunity you've been waiting for! This home features great bones and was a family home that has stood the test of time and use! There is a living room and kitchen on the main floor. There are 2 bedrooms on the main floor and one full bathroom. Upstairs there are two bedrooms. The basement is full and unfinished. This is an opportunity for someone to be able to own a home in Westport and enjoy the beaches, highly rated schools, the arts, dining and shopping and beautiful lifestyle Westport is known for! The home is being sold "as is".
