About this home

Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a spacious 3-bedroom corner townhouse in the highly sought-after Sunrise Hill complex! The main floor boasts a generous living area with built-ins and a cozy fireplace. Upstairs, you'll find a large primary suite with two closets, plus two additional bedrooms and a full bath. The lower-level family room is perfect for a quiet time with a book and there is also an extra room for an office or guest space. This end unit is filled with natural light and scenic pool views. Sunrise Hill provides resort-like amenities, including a pool, tennis courts, and a playground.
