About this home

This charming and bright property is conveniently located in a very quiet and desirable private neighborhood, close to just about everything. Tastefully renovated and updated for a contemporary lifestyle. It has two spacious bedrooms with built-ins, new bathroom, eat-in kitchen with pantry and modern appliances, a separate dining room, separate living room and a sunroom. Additional space in the finished attic can be used as a 3rd bedroom/home office/workout room/entertainment. New laundry appliances, hardwood floors throughout and a separate patio and deck to enjoy the quiet and serenity of the 1.5 acre parklike manicured grounds. It offers a two car garage and outside parking for multiple cars, and there is tremendous storage capacity in the basement and attic.
