About this home

Central air conditioning, in-unit washer/dryer, AND a garage- the perfect trifecta for this beautiful sunlit rental. Occupying the entire upper level of a charming farmhouse just north of downtown Westport, this spacious 980-square-foot home combines modern convenience with historic character. Step inside to discover a newly renovated eat-in kitchen with sparkling finishes and beautifully restored antique floors that add warmth throughout. The generous living room provides ample space for relaxing and entertaining, while the large bedroom includes a convenient office alcove-ideal for working from home. Nestled in a lovely neighborhood, you're just over a mile from Westport's vibrant downtown dining and shopping scene-an easy walk or quick drive. Commuters will appreciate the train shuttle stopping right at your door, making the NYC commute effortless. Landlord covers landscaping, water, and trash removal. This welcoming apartment offers the rare combination of character, convenience, and location. Easy to show.
