About this home

Charming East Norwalk Condo - Move-In Ready! Discover this adorable, turn-key 1-bedroom, 1-bath end unit perfectly situated in the heart of East Norwalk. Enjoy an unbeatable location just steps from shopping, Whole Foods, Stew Leonard's, great restaurants, beaches and only minutes to major highways and Metro-North for an easy commute. Step through your private entrance into a welcoming tiled foyer that opens to a bright, spacious living room with beautiful hardwood floors. The updated kitchen features granite countertops, stainless-steel appliances, a breakfast bar, and generous cabinet space - ideal for cooking or entertaining. The modernized bathroom offers a stylish new vanity, lighting, fixtures, tile flooring, and a chic shiplap accent wall. Additional highlights include a full-size washer and dryer, one reserved parking spot (#14), low common charges, and a private 12'x7' storage room in the basement. Why rent when you can own? This East Norwalk gem delivers style, comfort, and unbeatable convenience to all the best local amenities. Owner will paint the unit prior to closing.
