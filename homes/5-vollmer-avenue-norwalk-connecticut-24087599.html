About this home

Welcome to 5 Vollmer Ave. This spacious almost 1800 sqft Colonial home has lots of natural light, unique 2nd floor layout, flexible 1st floor, full basement & under-house garage. The large Eat-in Kit (expanded 2015), has loads of cabinetry & counter space, lots of windows, adjacent heated mudroom & nice size deck. There's a formal dining room or 3rd bedroom, living room with fireplace & wood stove insert, a full bathroom with tub & a convenient 3rd entry from outside. The upper floor has a large primary bedroom with 3 closets, nice size 2nd bedroom, full bathroom with tub & a nice landing/loft, most recently as a sewing area, could be an office. The large basement area is great for storage, includes the under-house garage, laundry area, oil fired boiler, propane hot water heater (kit stove & clothes dryer are propane too). There are beautiful hardwood floors (kit has bamboo). plenty of off street parking both on Vollmer & Lufberry Lane driveways. Very convenient location with short drive to Westport, I-95 or Merritt Parkway & Stew Leonard's.
