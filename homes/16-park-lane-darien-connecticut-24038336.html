About this home

Welcome to 16 Park Lane where old world charm meets modern day living. This gracious colonial set in a storybook setting on a private street is close to everything-covenient to schools, trains, downtown, shopping and so much more. Filled with character and light, this home has the space, layout and features for how we live today. Enjoy your morning coffee on the lovely porch with swing or capture the serene evenings on your private patio with oversized .73 acre lot. Enter into a gracious foyer that leads to an oversized living room. Entertain and host holiday parties in your grand dining room and kitchen with granite counters. A full bath completes the first floor. Upstairs boasts 3 bedrooms and a full bath. Enjoy your primary bedroom suite on the 3rd level with walk-in closet and private full bath with granite topped vanity. Need to work from home or recreation space? The finished lower level can accomodate your needs. Enjoy views of Noroton River from your backyard. Interior and exterior of house freshly painted. Make your home ownership dreams a reality. House and detached garaged being sold "As-Is".
