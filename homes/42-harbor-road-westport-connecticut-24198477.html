About this home

Rare opportunity-direct waterfront (PET FRIENDLY with owner approval) on prestigious Harbor Road! Live a true beach community life, with boating (room for boat mooring), swimming, kayaking, paddle boarding and more just steps from your front door. With incredible, panoramic water views, enjoy breathtaking sunrises and sunsets every single day! This home has a true "beach house" feel, with cozy spots and a fireplaced living room, yet offers great open spaces with soaring vaulted ceilings, exposed wooden beams and wide-plank pine flooring throughout. The home features a newly painted interior, freshly refinished hardwood flooring, with new private balcony overlooking the water, bluestone walkway and private bluestone patio just off the kitchen/dining area leading to an oversized, fenced, private yard. With a surprisingly spacious feel, the home boasts 4 bedrooms and 3 full, 1 partial bathroom. The main level features a kitchen with dining area open to a living room with a wall of oversized windows full water views and a convenient office space, also overlooking the water, a bedroom, laundry and full bathroom. The second level is reached via two convenient separate staircases, with a great room with incredible views, 3 bedrooms and 2 full and 1 half bathrooms. The best in coastal living - all ready for its next lucky resident. This is a truly special offering!
