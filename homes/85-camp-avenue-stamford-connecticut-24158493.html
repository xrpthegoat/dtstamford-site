About this home

Convenient and sophisticated style is what you get with this luxury 2 bedroom, 1.5 bathroom townhouse with a great 3rd bedroom option at sought after Rivers Edge complex on Darien/New Canaan border! Offering hardwood floors throughout the main level, spacious dining/living room, eat-in kitchen with granite counters, SS appliances and sliders that lead out to your private balcony perfect for Grilling/entertaining. Walk-to-all living in Springdale Village!
