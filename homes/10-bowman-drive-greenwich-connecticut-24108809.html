About this home

Welcome to 10 Bowman Drive in the coveted King Merritt neighborhood of Greenwich. Access to all the shops restaurants and amenities of Greenwich, close commute to Mastercard, Pepsico and easy distance to the Merritt. This gorgeous split level house was fully renovated 2025 and features 3/4 Bedrooms, 2 full and 2 half Bathrooms. Vaulted ceilings, huge light open plan living room, Den/office with wood burning fireplace Built Ins and door to the yard. Renovated bathrooms. Brand new eat in kitchen with high end appliances, large quartz island, beautiful cabinetry, sliders out to patio and back yard. Family room with cozy wood burning fireplace, additional finished room for exercise room/guest bedroom/media room and second office space. Beautiful primary En suite with windows on both front and back bringing gorgeous light all day,Brand new primary bathroom, Walk in closet. Two further bedrooms, one bedroom is tiered and could be used for 2 occupants. The large level yard features patio, huge rolling lawn and very private in ground pool with solarium. The house can be rented with or without furniture, Ask agent for details. Tenant to take care of pool running costs.
