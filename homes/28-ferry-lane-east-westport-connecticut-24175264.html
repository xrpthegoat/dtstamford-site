About this home

Looking for a sensational home just a short walk to the beach, Longshore, train, and restaurants in trendy Saugatuck? This exceptional home by Walter Mattera is a masterclass in blending traditional design with contemporary style. Expansive interiors feature grand gathering spaces that flow naturally into intimate retreats, creating a home equally suited for entertaining and everyday living. Thoughtfully designed across 4 floors, the home offers 5 bedrooms, 6 full/3 half baths. A gracious foyer opens to large scale living spaces, including an oversized kitchen with breakfast nook that connects seamlessly to the family room with a striking stone fireplace. Formal dining with butlers pantry, private office, and multiple indoor outdoor connections create a dynamic and inviting environment. The second level is highlighted by the luxurious primary suite featuring a fireplace, spa inspired bath, and boutique caliber closet designed as a calming retreat. Four additional en suite bedrooms, laundry, and bonus room complete the level. Third floor and walk out lower level recreation spaces reflect a modern approach to luxury where work, wellness, and entertainment coexist effortlessly. Outside, the chic and ethereal setting continues with a pool, spa, covered patio, fireplace, and expansive bluestone terrace that seamlessly integrate indoor and outdoor living. Every detail has been carefully considered to deliver comfort, sophistication, privacy, and connection to the best of Westport.
