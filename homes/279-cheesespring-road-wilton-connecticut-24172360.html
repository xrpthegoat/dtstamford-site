About this home

Set on a stunning and picturesque 2.24-acre property, this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom home (3 bedrooms per field card) offers the perfect balance of privacy, comfort, and updated living. Surrounded by mature landscaping and peaceful wooded scenery, the property feels like a private retreat while still offering exceptional functionality for everyday life. The main level features an updated kitchen, renovated bathrooms, and an ensuite primary bedroom, along with two additional bedrooms. A warm and inviting living room with fireplace, cozy den/library, and elegant dining room create ideal spaces for both entertaining and quiet relaxation. The dining room opens to a spacious deck overlooking the serene grounds, while a screened-in porch connected to the deck and den provides the perfect spot to enjoy the outdoors throughout the seasons. The finished lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a converted 4th bedroom, expansive common/family room, office area with direct backyard access, laundry area, workshop, abundant storage space, and access to the oversized 2-car garage. Generous closets and storage areas throughout the home provide ample room for organization and everyday convenience. Adding to the home's appeal are a private wooded walking path, whole-house generator, comprehensive security system with multiple exterior cameras, and seasonal tick treatment for added peace of mind. A truly special property offering space, character, and a beautiful natural setting.
