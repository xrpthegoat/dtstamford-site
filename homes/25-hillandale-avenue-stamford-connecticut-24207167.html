About this home

Welcome Home to this Beautifully Updated Colonial! Rich in old-world charm & complemented by quality craftsmanship, this inviting Colonial offers the perfect blend of timeless character & modern updates. Ideally located within a short stroll to restaurants, parks, shops, train station, and all that vibrant downtown Stamford has to offer. The spacious living/dining room features a cozy fireplace, gleaming hardwood floors, & elegant crown molding, and flows seamlessly into the family room, complete with a pellet stove & sliders leading to a delightful three-season sunroom. Overlooking the lush, professionally landscaped, fully fenced-in yard, the sunroom provides a wonderful space to relax & enjoy the outdoors. The eat-in kitchen is both stylish and functional, featuring custom cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless-steel appliances, and radiant-heated floors. A bright home office and full bath complete the first level. Upstairs, the second floor offers two light-filled bedrooms, with brand new carpeting (hardwood underneath), along with a generously sized primary bedroom featuring a walk-in closet/dressing room & convenient laundry. The gorgeous renovated hall bath offers a spa-like retreat with double sinks, radiant-heated flooring, and an expansive, luxurious shower. A walk-up attic provides excellent potential for future expansion and storage space. A full basement provides abundant storage, a sump pump, and full walk-out. This spotless home is a must see!!
