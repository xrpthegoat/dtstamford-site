About this home

Discover unparalleled luxury in this magnificent 8,000 sq. ft. Georgian estate, nestled on over 4 acres of private grounds just a mile from Grace Farms. Designed by acclaimed architect Alex Kaali-Nagy, this residence offers the highest standards of elegance and craftsmanship. Featuring 10-foot ceilings throughout, the home includes a grand dining room with a fireplace, perfect for hosting formal events. Relax in style around the heated pool and cabana, or enjoy the beautifully detailed millwork, walls of French doors, and four stunning fireplaces. The estate also boasts a cherry wood library or billiard room, a luxurious primary en suite, and five additional bedrooms, most with their own en suites. The expansive 1,800 sq. ft. finished lower level provides ample space for a home gym, studio, or any other needs you may have. The fully fenced property is ideal for pets (note: the fence is not depicted in photos). Enjoy a truly maintenance-free lifestyle with services including sprinkler system upkeep, trash pickup, septic maintenance, grounds care, snow removal, and pool management all taken care of by the landlord. This home is sure to impress!
