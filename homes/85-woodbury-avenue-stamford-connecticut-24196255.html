About this home

Impeccably maintained ranch in desirable Springdale, offering effortless one-level living and exceptional convenience. The inviting living room with built-ins flows into the eat-in kitchen and dining area with fireplace. Four bedrooms and two full baths complete the main level, including a spacious primary with a walk-in closet. Upstairs, a laundry room and a bonus space offer an ideal home office, or studio. Beautifully landscaped grounds feature a front deck, back patio, and fenced side yard for easy outdoor living and entertaining. Attached garage, abundant lower-level storage, hardwood floors, central air, and natural gas. Unbeatable location just moments from Sleepy Hollow Park, train, buses, Twin Rinks, shopping, dining, and more. A true Springdale gem that has been in the same family for almost 45 years, and is ready for its next owner.
