About this home

Welcome to 409 Harvest Commons - a sun-filled, 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse featuring a desirable first-floor Primary Bedroom in a highly sought-after community with a heated pool, tennis courts, and clubhouse. Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac for privacy with convenient access to amenities just minutes to downtown Westport shopping and dining, walking distance to Balducci's and easy access to Metro-North and beaches. Enjoy dramatic double-height ceilings and walls of glass that flood the home with natural light, showcasing hardwood floors throughout and a gas fireplace in the spacious living room. The open-concept floor plan is perfect for entertaining, flowing seamlessly from the gourmet eat-in kitchen with slider access to a private patio. The first-floor Primary boasts vaulted ceilings and dual walk-in closets, while the Main Level includes a Dining Area, Powder Room and Laundry. Upstairs features a versatile den/office space and a second ensuite Bedroom with two walk-in closets. The unfinished Lower Level offers flexible space, and a two-car attached garage provides ample parking. A private courtyard entrance with professional landscaping welcomes you home, leading to a secluded brick patio perfect for outdoor dining and relaxation. Located at the back of the complex ensures a tranquil setting and maximum privacy. This turnkey townhome offers a maintenance-free lifestyle in one of Westport's most desirable communities.
