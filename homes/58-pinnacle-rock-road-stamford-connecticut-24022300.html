About this home

Expansive N. Stamford property offers something for everyone! Updated home on 1.5 acre interior lot boasts 5 Beds & 3 1/2 updated baths, in-ground pool & wraparound deck, plus access to private lake. The Colonial's center hall entry is anything but a stuffy foyer - walls have come down, so you & your guests are immediately welcomed into the great room, where the living room flows into the Designer kitchen. Here, form & function meet: marble counters, stainless appliances, large island w seating, ceramic cooktop, custom cabinets & lighting, a wall of windows, Butler's pantry w. storage, utility sink & wine chiller + deck access. Family room is an elegant take on casual farmhouse aesthetic: oversize FP w. reclaimed barnwood mantel & cross beams, draw the eye up to the cathedral ceiling & gorgeous chandelier, reclaimed wideboard HW floors underfoot & salvaged antique cathedral windows provide views of yard & pool. Private Primary BR is perched on its own upper level, providing large double closet, fireplace & full en suite bath & balcony. With the remaining 4 bedrooms on the other side of the house, primary could be used as in-law/au pair/boarder suite. Outdoor living space makes this house the place to be for hosting parties by the pool this summer! When pool season ends, spend a quiet afternoon fishing on the lake. Just minutes to Merritt Pkwy makes for a Commuter's dream but you'll be inspired to find ways to work from *this* home!
