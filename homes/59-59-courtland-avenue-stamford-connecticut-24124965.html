About this home

Discover the charm of Kingswood. Renovated light filled 1-bedroom unit is on the 2nd floor of Kingswood, accessible by elevator. The unit includes a reserved parking space, common laundry room on the unit's floor. Kingswood boasts impressive amenities such as two elevators, a community room with a fireplace, pool table, and serving bar, a media room, a fitness center, a business room, a patio, and an outdoor grill. This community's perfect location offers easy access to i95, close to shops, the beach, transportation and Darien. All utilities are included in the monthly rent, electricity, heat, hot water, cold water and reserved parking.
