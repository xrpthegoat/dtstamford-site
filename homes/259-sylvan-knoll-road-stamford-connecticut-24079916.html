About this home

Incredible opportunity to own in desirable Sylvan Knoll & enjoy one of the few units in the complex with a finished lower level, full-height ceilings and a full bath! Plus, this tastefully updated two-bedroom, two full bath townhome is well located in a quiet & secluded area within the complex. Enter to find the living room & dining room with rich, dark-stained hardwood flooring & custom molding. The kitchen is equipped with a gas range, stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops and a back door to the large & separate patio area where you can enjoy outdoor dining. Upstairs, the primary bedroom & 2nd bedroom are nicely sized with good closet space. The full hall bath has a tub/shower combination. Downstairs, the family room is the real surprise with full-height ceilings & a large storage closet. There's also a full bath with a stall shower & laundry room. Summers are cool with central air. Just one payment per month for taxes, grounds maintenance, water, sewer, rubbish removal, snow removal, etc. The location is fantastic, as Chelsea Piers & beautiful Cove Island Park & Beach are within walking distance. I-95, the Stamford Transportation Center, Harbor Point & downtown Stamford are also in close proximity to Sylvan Knoll. Hardwood floors throughout. Pet friendly. Playground/Tot Lot. Newer A/C unit. Gas heat. Attic storage. Plenty of parking spaces as unit is nestled between two large lots. Owner occupied complex, no renting. Seller is only moving due to growing family!
