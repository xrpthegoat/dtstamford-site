About this home

This newly constructed Nantucket-Style Colonial, exudes timeless elegance. The 6 bedroom and 6.2 bathroom home is meticulously designed with high-end finishes and exquisite millwork throughout. An impressive 3-story foyer anchors the expansive main level as formal living and dining rooms seamlessly flow to the family room and gourmet kitchen. While French doors lead to a patio overlooking the heated pool and spa. With a luxurious primary suite, and 5 additional bedrooms with en-suite baths, there is abundant living space. A bonus room with loft and a partially finished lower level add further versatility. Situated on 2.99 park-like acres, conveniently located to downtown Darien, this property is ready for its new owners to call it home!
