About this home

Welcome to Saugatuck Shores, where luxury living meets coastal charm in this stunning waterfront contemporary property built by renowned Architect Michael Greenberg! This is not to be missed. This residence boasts breathtaking views that extend beyond the horizon. With 4 bedrooms and 4 baths, the interior is designed for both comfort and sophistication, offering spacious rooms and a seamless flow that caters to modern living. The highlight of this property is the inviting heated gunite Shoreline 22' x 40' pool at 8' 6" deep, a perfect oasis for relaxation and entertaining, set against the backdrop of beautiful grounds that enhance the overall tranquility of the home. Convenience meets elegance as this residence is strategically located, offering easy access to the train, making commuting a breeze for residents. The architectural design of the home embraces a contemporary aesthetic, seamlessly blending with the natural surroundings. Immerse yourself in the serenity of Saugatuck Shores, where every detail has been carefully curated to create a harmonious balance between indoor and outdoor living. Whether enjoying a quiet evening by the pool or entertaining guests in style, this property is a rare gem that defines the epitome of coastal living.
