About this home

Set at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac on sought-after Stephanie Lane, this beautifully updated four-bedroom colonial sits on over an acre of professionally landscaped property and offers the kind of space, flow, and setting that are hard to find. With over 3,300 square feet of living space, the open-concept layout is light-filled and well-suited for both everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen connects to the family room, dining room, and living room - each space distinct yet flowing seamlessly into the next. The family room, complete with a dry bar, and the living room, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, both open directly to the patio, making indoor-outdoor living effortless. A screened-in porch extends the space further, offering three seasons of enjoyment. Rounding out the main level are a dedicated office or den and a well-proportioned mudroom. Upstairs, the Primary Suite offers a walk-in closet and spa-like bathroom with radiant heated floors. Three additional spacious bedrooms are arranged across the second floor, one en-suite and two bedrooms sharing a large hall bath. Located in the Ox Ridge school district, this is a home that checks a lot of boxes without sacrificing character. Classic New England architecture, generous indoor and outdoor space, and a private cul-de-sac setting make this a rare opportunity. 2-car garage and walk-up attic.
