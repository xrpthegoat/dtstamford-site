About this home

Well-maintained and inviting 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment located on the second floor of a bi-level unit within a multi-family home, set in a quiet residential neighborhood. The thoughtfully designed layout offers two levels of living space, with the second bedroom privately positioned upstairs and featuring a spacious walk-in closet. The primary bedroom includes access to a private balcony - ideal for relaxing or enjoying time outdoors. Wood floors run throughout the apartment, complemented by ample closet space for everyday storage needs. The home also includes access to a dedicated washer and dryer located in the basement, reserved for this unit. Conveniently located near the Connecticut/New York border, the property provides easy access to both the Hutchinson River Parkway/Merritt Parkway and I-95, making commuting simple. Two neighborhood parks are just four blocks away, and plenty of street parking is available. A comfortable home in a convenient location.
