About this home

Welcome to 21 Friendly Road in Norwalk's sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood-a home that offers the space you've been looking for and the location you'll love. With 5 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and over 5,100 square feet of living space, there's room here for everyone to spread out, gather together, and truly enjoy being home. Whether you're hosting holidays, working remotely, accommodating extended family, or simply looking for more space to live comfortably, this home offers the flexibility today's buyers are searching for. Use the additional storage space as a sun-filled playroom or home office. This space gives easy access to garage and sliders to outdoor patio. The heart of the home features thoughtful updates, including a new cooktop, new double wall oven, new energy-efficient furnace and Roth oil tank with a 30-year warranty. All of these updates including a new hot water heater will give you peace of mind while adding comfort and efficiency for years to come. Step outside and you'll find one of the property's standout features-a level, fully fenced backyard with endless possibilities! Whether you envision summer barbecues, a play area, gardening, or even installing the pool you've always wanted, there's plenty of room to create your own private outdoor oasis.
