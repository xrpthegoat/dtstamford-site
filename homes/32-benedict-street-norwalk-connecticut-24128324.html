About this home

Be the first to call this designer-owned Colonial "HOME". Newly built by a contractor for his own family, every detail showcases expert craftsmanship and elevated finishes-from custom moldings to curated fixtures that blend modern comfort with timeless style. Four full levels of living space with additional 600 sq' bonus 3rd floor, create flexibility for today's lifestyle. The main floor welcomes you with sunlit sight lines, gleaming hardwood floors, and a chic eat-in kitchen. A quartz waterfall island, stainless steel appliances, and sleek cabinetry open seamlessly to a generous dining area and expansive back deck overlooking your generous grounds. Upstairs, retreat to four spacious bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with a spa-worthy bath featuring double sinks and a glass-enclosed shower. A stylish hall bath with soaking tub completes the second floor. The walk up third floor offers potential for more living space for a playroom, home office, or guest suite, while the lower level provides abundant storage. Outdoors, enjoy a deep backyard with mature trees, a shed, gardens and room for a future garage. Move right in and savor the luxury of brand-new construction with character-minutes to shopping, highways, Metro-North, and Calf Pasture Beach.
