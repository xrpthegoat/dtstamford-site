About this home

Welcome to 21 Soundview Avenue, Unit 1st Floor, where timeless character meets fresh, modern design in the heart of vibrant South Norwalk. Completely renovated inside and out, this move in ready three bedroom, one and a half bath rental offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and convenience. Step inside to discover sun filled living spaces accented by wide plank flooring, recessed lighting, and a bright, airy layout designed for everyday living. The beautifully updated kitchen is the centerpiece of the home, featuring crisp white cabinetry, granite countertops, subway tile backsplash, and stainless steel appliances that make cooking and entertaining a pleasure. Stylishly renovated bathrooms, central air, and the convenience of in unit laundry add to the home's modern appeal. Sip your morning coffee on the charming front porch, enjoy the ease of off street parking, and take advantage of your own private storage space in the detached garage. Perfectly located just minutes from South Norwalk's renowned restaurants, shopping, parks, beaches, the train station, I-95, and Route 1, this exceptional rental offers an unbeatable combination of location and lifestyle. If you've been searching for a beautifully renovated apartment with modern finishes and everyday convenience, this is one you won't want to miss. Available for immediate occupancy.
