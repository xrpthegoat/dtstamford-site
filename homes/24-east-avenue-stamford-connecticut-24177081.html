About this home

Beautifully updated, move-in ready townhouse offering the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable commuter-friendly locations. This light-filled home features newer windows, fresh carpeting in bedrooms, remodeled full bath, updated stylish fireplace, and modern light fixtures. The stunning kitchen is a true highlight, featuring Quartz countertops, custom cabinetry with under-cabinet lighting, attractive backsplash, and stainless steel appliances-perfectly designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Spacious living/dining area with stylish fireplace opens through sliding doors to an exceptionally large private deck, a rare find for Stamford townhome living, and ideal for outdoor dining, relaxing, or entertaining guests. Elegant details including engineered wood flooring, crown molding, recessed lighting, and updated switches/outlets add a refined touch throughout. New water heater adds peace of mind. Includes one reserved parking space plus a reserved guest parking space. Exceptional location just minutes to I-95, Stamford Transportation Center (Metro North, Amtrak & buses), downtown Stamford, and a short walk to Cove Island Park, Cummings Beach, West Beach, and Chelsea Piers. Enjoy serene complex living with unmatched access to Stamford's best amenities, beaches, recreation, and commuting options. A must see!
