About this home

Beautifully updated and packed with charm, 21 Ferris Avenue is a standout two-family home offering versatility, character, and rare amenities for multifamily living. Each of the two spacious 2-bedroom, 1-bath units features its own deck, laundry, and updated interiors, with the lower unit enjoying a large island kitchen, a three-season porch, and full access to a finished basement with new carpeting. Freshly refinished hardwood floors, fresh paint, and newer central air in both units-an uncommon perk-add even more appeal. The property also boasts an irrigation system, newly installed HVAC, stonework details, and ample parking in back--with full privacy screens on both sides. With 1,832 square feet of total living space and potential for a third unit or extended lower-level living (adding 768 square feet), this is an excellent opportunity for investors or owner-occupants alike. The cash flow is already there, with potential to increase it further with a creative buyer. Or live in one unit and keep the 20 year tenant upstairs until their September lease date or longer! Situated near CT Avenue's shops, restaurants, and commuter conveniences, it's an ideal combination of income, comfort, and location. Discover the kind of multifamily property that rarely hits the market-where updates, charm, and income potential all come together.
