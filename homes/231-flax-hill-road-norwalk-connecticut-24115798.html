About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 2 family home on 3 levels, in the heart of SONO w/modern updates and brimming with original charm & character. Perfectly sited abutting the newly refurbished Flax Hill Rd. playground & dog park. This beauty is within walking distance to SONO's Metro N. train station, restaurants, shops and the highly acclaimed Maritime Aquarium & Art fest. Sun drenched interiors, original Pine floors, stained glass accents, interior French doors & vintage brass hardware are some of the features that preserve its historic footprint, while modern baths, fixtures, appliances and mechanics bring it into the 21st century. Apartment 1 consists of the 1st floor and LL walkout. All levels have their own exterior access. The 1st fl and LL have a shared interior access. Apartment 1 consists of: LR w/ ceiling fan, formal DR /or optional 2nd bdrm, lg. Kitchen w spacious separate eating area, sliders to rear deck , 2 full baths w/ stone counter tops, a walkout LL w/ slate floor & stone accent walls, a separate entertaining wetbar with walls of cabinetry, sink, refrigerator and microwave all open to a family hangout area, a large nicely lit office and a beautifully appointed mudroom with builtins and a bank of closets. Apartment 2 is accessed from the front of the house as well. Head upstairs and one will find a nice sized kitchen with room for a table, a very large separate food pantry, a white, neutral and clean full bath with deep shower
