About this home

Tucked back from the road on a quiet street, this updated ranch sits on .33 acres and feels like a private oasis. From the moment you step onto the newly designed flagstone entry, you'll know this home is something special. With 2,127 sq. ft. of living space, the home offers 3-4 bedrooms and 1.5 baths. Custom woodwork is featured throughout, including wainscoting, crown molding, and gleaming hardwood floors. The sun-filled living room, dining room, and kitchen create the perfect flow for entertaining. The chef's kitchen is a true delight with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a wine cooler. The dining room opens to a TREX deck-ideal for gatherings and indoor-outdoor living. The main level also includes a spacious primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and half bath, along with two additional bedrooms and a fully updated bath. The finished lower level provides a large family room, a 4th bedroom or office, laundry area, and abundant storage. Additional highlights include city water/sewer, a convenient West Norwalk location close to parks, hiking and biking trails, major highways, and just minutes to the Metro-North train for an easy commute to NYC. This fabulous home is move-in ready-schedule your showing today and be settled in before the holidays!
