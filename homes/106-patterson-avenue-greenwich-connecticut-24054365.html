About this home

Superbly renovated and totally modernized in 2022, this classic Tudor home is sited on parklike acreage on a premier in-town street only moments to schools, train, town and the hospital. Go inside to a new kitchen, restored hardwood floors, new lighting and beautiful arches that create a seamless flow throughout. Upstairs, the primary suite features a newly renovated bathroom and a spacious dressing room. Four additional bedrooms and two updated bathrooms complete the second and third floor. The pristine lower level has exterior access, laundry, ample storage, a 1-car attached garage, and a second 2-car detached garage. Outside, the stone terrace is an entertainers delight with beautiful views of the lush level lawn, gardens and privacy that are not to be missed.
