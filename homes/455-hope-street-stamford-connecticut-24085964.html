About this home

This spacious, move-in ready, Glenbrook condo is conveniently located close to shopping and the train station. The unit has been nicely refreshed over the last few years - kitchen, bathrooms, flooring, appliances, and mechanical systems. The kitchen features sleek quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances. And, there's even a nice size laundry room off the kitchen. Enjoy the open dining and living areas with easy access to the balcony through sliding doors. The thoughtful floor plan ensures privacy with the two bedrooms smartly placed on different levels. The expansive primary bedroom suite, which is on the upper level, has a full bathroom and a generous walk-in closet. Adjacent to the primary bedroom, the open loft space is ideal for a home office or cozy reading nook. The lower level provides great storage and great potential. This condo comes with two assigned outdoor parking spaces (#3 & #4). Tamarac is a well-maintained complex, featuring a beautiful courtyard and ample visitor parking. Don't miss the opportunity to make this condo your new home!
