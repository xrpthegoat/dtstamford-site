About this home

Location, location, location: Easy 5 minutes away from Cos Cob train station. Discover the beauty of waterfront living in this spacious 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condominium at Palmer Point. Perfectly situated along the Mianus River, Unit 306 offers serene water views, a well-designed floor plan, and effortless access to all that Cos Cob has to offer. The sun-filled living and dining area is anchored by sliding glass doors that open to a private balcony-ideal for enjoying morning coffee while watching boats drift by. This unit has 2 large bedrooms, 2 Full baths, 2 Balconies, a storage room, a large attic, and Garage parking. all utilities are included in rent! Great deal. (renter only pays electric).
