About this home

Location, location, location! Located at the center of Saugatuck. Walking distance to train, restaurants, rowing club, gym, close to Compo Beach, Longshore, Golf, and tennis! Historic Landmark Queen Anne Victorian House c. 1899 with beautiful architectural detail. Well maintained and updated. Grand windows surround the house with light, featuring 9-foot ceilings on the main floor and a wonderfully spaced layout. There are 3 bedrooms, 3 baths, and a den that could be a 4th bedroom. Additionally, there is a large attic and 2-story detached garage with lots of storage. Don't miss this opportunity to call Westport your home and take advantage of the top-rated schools and many fabulous amenities.
