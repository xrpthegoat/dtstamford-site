About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac along the scenic Saugatuck River, this stunning Colonial offers over 4,700 square feet of refined living space. With breathtaking panoramic water views, a private dock, and a brand-new cedar roof, this home is a true waterfront retreat. Step inside to discover gorgeous hardwood floors throughout and a custom-designed kitchen featuring granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The expansive living room, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows, bathes the space in natural light and seamlessly opens to a beautiful stone patio through elegant French doors-perfect for indoor-outdoor living. A formal office with tray ceilings and wainscoting provides a distinguished workspace, while the enormous primary suite is a private oasis, complete with a fireplace, walk-in closet, and spectacular views. All four bedrooms are generously sized, each offering comfort and style. With 4.1 bathrooms, a spacious 2-car attached garage, and high-end finishes throughout, this extraordinary home is designed for both luxury and relaxation. Whether enjoying the tranquility of riverfront living or entertaining on the patio, this Westport gem is a rare and exceptional offering.
