About this home

PRICED TO MOVE. Set along one of Fairfield County's most picturesque and prestigious roads, this vintage Wilton estate offers a rare blend of historic character and modern potential. Thoughtfully expanded and ready for a personal touch, the residence sits on 2.4 acres and is surrounded by 4.5 acres of conservation land, providing unmatched privacy and serenity just minutes from downtown Wilton and approximately one hour from New York City. Step inside to discover Brazilian Cherry wood floors throughout and custom built-in china cabinets that frame a wood-burning fireplace which is the centerpiece of the formal dining room. The heart of the home is a chef's dream kitchen that is fully equipped with a Sub Zero refrigerator, two ovens (1 Viking 6 burner gas range and a Bosch electric oven w/ warming drawer), two dishwashers (Bosch and Miele), 2 full sized sinks, ice maker and a marble island. All bathed in natural light from the adjacent dining area. The first floor also boasts a grand 42' x 25' billiards room, a formal living room and a casual dining area, offering ample space for entertaining and everyday living. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite includes a spa-inspired bath with a steam shower and Jacuzzi tub. Three additional bedrooms, a play/rec room and a guest suite provide flexibility for family and guests.
