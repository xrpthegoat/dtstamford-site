About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in Riverside, this beautifully maintained Colonial offers 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and thoughtfully designed living spaces. A welcoming covered front porch leads into a bright, open floor plan with high ceilings, ideal for everyday living and effortless entertaining. The spacious finished lower level, also with high ceilings, provides exceptional flexibility for a playroom, home gym, media room, or additional family room. The finished third floor is perfect for a home office, guest suite, or extra living space. Additional highlights include a walk-in closet in the primary suite, an attached two-car garage, and a large private deck off the main level, ideal for outdoor entertaining. Beautifully landscaped and set back from the street, the home offers both privacy and curb appeal. Conveniently zoned for International School at Dundee and Eastern Middle School, and just minutes from restaurants, shopping, Metro-North, and Greenwich Point Beach. A rare opportunity to reside in one of Riverside's most desirable neighborhoods.
