About this home

Great location just two blocks from the commuter train and minutes to shopping, restaurants, schools. This beautifully Just renovated tri-level single-family row house offers 2200 Square Feet with 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and comfortable living space throughout. The main level features a bright open-concept layout with hardwood floors, seamlessly connecting the living, dining, and kitchen areas. The renovated kitchen features granite countertops, modern cabinetry, tile flooring, and stainless steel appliances. An electric fireplace adds warmth and character to the living space, and a convenient half bath completes this level. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms and a renovated full bathroom. The finished walk-out lower level offers additional flexible living space with a family room, a second full bathroom, and a washer and dryer. Enjoy a private fenced backyard perfect for relaxing or outdoor entertaining, plus two reserved parking spaces directly in front of the residence. A spacious and inviting home offering comfort, convenience, and easy access to transportation and everyday amenities.
