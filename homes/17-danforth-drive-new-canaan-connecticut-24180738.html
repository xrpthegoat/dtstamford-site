About this home

Nestled on 2+ beautifully landscaped acres with a spectacular heated in-ground pool, this exceptional Colonial has been completely renovated to the highest standards, offering the perfect blend of timeless elegance & modern luxury. Thoughtfully redesigned inside & out, every major component has been updated, including the roof, siding, windows, mechanical systems, & generator, providing true turnkey living. Designed for both everyday comfort & effortless entertaining, the home features a modern, flowing floor plan w/sun-filled living spaces & seamless transitions throughout. At its heart is a beautifully appointed kitchen that opens to inviting gathering areas and outdoor living spaces, creating the ideal setting for family & friends. The lux primary suite serves as a private retreat, highlighted by a spa-worthy marble bath with sophisticated finishes & timeless appeal. Step outside to your own backyard oasis featuring the impressive heated pool, expansive entertaining patios & professionally designed landscaping providing exceptional beauty and privacy throughout the seasons. Whether hosting large gatherings or enjoying a quiet evening at home, the outdoor living experience is second to none. Car enthusiasts, hobbyists, & collectors will appreciate the custom outfitted oversized 2.5-car garage offering for vehicles, storage, & workspace. Rarely does a home of this quality, condition & setting become available. Simply move in & enjoy the very best of New Canaan Living!
