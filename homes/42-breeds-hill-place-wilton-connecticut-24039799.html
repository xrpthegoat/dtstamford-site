About this home

Charming Colonial Home with Pool on 3.5 Acres! Discover this sunlit 4-bedroom colonial, tucked away on a serene road just off the coveted Nod Hill Road. This beautifully landscaped property provides a perfect blend of privacy, elegance and modern living. The heart of the home features a newly updated chef's kitchen, equipped with quartz countertops, a breakfast bar, custom white cabinetry, a 6-burner Viking cooktop, and double wall ovens. Ideal for entertaining. This space seamlessly connects to a formal dining room and a spacious great room, boasting hardwood floors, soaring ceilings and direct access to an expansive outdoor deck, pool and spa. Step outside to your private oasis, featuring a free-form gunitye pool and spa, all surrounded by lush greenery. The generous deck is perfect for alfresco dining or simply unwinding while enjoying the scenic views. Located near the picturesque Weir Farm Preserve and town forest trails, this home at 42 Breeds Hill Place is one of the best locations in Wilton. Experience the charm and serenity of this exceptional property!
