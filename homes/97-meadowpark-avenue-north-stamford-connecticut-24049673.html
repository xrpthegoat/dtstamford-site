About this home

Come home to one of Stamfords most desirable neighborhoods! This 4/5 Bedroom colonial in the mid-ridges is set on a private level lot & close to shopping, houses of worship, schools and many other amenities. Main level boasts a bright spacious kitchen w/island and gray countertops, lots of storage and access to deck & mature private backyard. Living room, dining room, family room w/fireplace and access to deck. An additional bedroom/office also conveniently located on this main level. Second floor features 4 generous size bedrooms, one full bath and separate laundry room. Large primary suite w/full bath and his & hers walk in closets. Finished basement has cedar closet, two additional office spaces, utility room and storage. Hardwood floors throughout, freshly painted, sprinkler system, 1 car garage & mudroom. Lots of beautiful natural light throughout this spacious home!
