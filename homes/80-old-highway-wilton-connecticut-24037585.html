About this home

Charming vintage 1941 South Wilton Colonial farmhouse -3/4 bed, 2.5 baths on 2.2 private acres with seasonal water views and treed landscaping which borders a 6 acre preserve. The owner has lovingly updated this unique home with energy efficient windows, doors, skylights , additional insulation, mechanicals and roof. The home exudes "cozy" with the nail head hardwood floors, oversized colonial cooking fireplace in the living room with a comfy reading nook in the bay window accented with bookshelves and tongue and groove built-ins. The living room opens to a full length 3 season porch with full glass windows and storm doors for comfortable entertaining. The upper level has the primary bedroom suite with a full wall of built-in cabinetry as well as 2 additional bedrooms. Additionally the upper level oversized family room could stay as a home office or convert it for an additional bedroom since it adjoins the updated hall bath. This colonial charmer is situated on the rise at the rear of the 2 acre parcel which not only provides privacy and solitude but magnificent views of the seasonal pond enhanced by thousands of blooming daffodils in the spring planted over the last 20 years. There is no more convenient home location -it is close to RT 7 amenities , Wilton town center shopping , metro north RR, medical facilities , Norwalk River Valley Trail and schools.
