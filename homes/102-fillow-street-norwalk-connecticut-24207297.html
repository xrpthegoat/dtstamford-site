About this home

Opportunity knocks in a highly desirable Norwalk neighborhood! This classic Center Hall Colonial offers incredible potential for investors, contractors, or savvy buyers looking to build sweat equity. The main level features a traditional layout including a spacious living room anchored by a cozy fireplace, a formal dining room perfect for gatherings, a comfortable family room, a kitchen with a charming breakfast nook, and a convenient half bath. Beautiful hardwood floors run throughout the main living areas. Upstairs, you will find bedrooms and a full bathroom. A standout feature of this property is the rare, legal full main-level in-law suite, providing excellent flexibility for multi-generational living or guest accommodations. Complete with a 1-car garage and situated in a fantastic location close to local amenities, this home has great bones and is just waiting for your creative updates. The property needs TLC and is being sold strictly AS-IS. Bring your vision and restore this classic colonial to its full glory!
