About this home

Just in time for fall- 3-bedroom Dutch colonial w/an extra room- perfect for those needing a home office. Located in a sought-after beach community- you'll love the cool breezes coming off the sound. Main level with open floor plan, including living room, kitchen, dining area and extra room. Sliders will take you out to a spacious deck for wonderful outdoor living space and year-round barbecuing! You also have the convenience of a powder room on this level. SS appliances and a stackable washer dryer. Upstairs you will find all 3 bedrooms with water views in 2 of the 3 bedrooms of the beloved West Beach! Economical natural gas for cooking and for heating, tankless on demand hot water. Private off-street parking, roomy enough for 2 cars. NO SMOKING OR VAPING ON THE PROPERTY. Excellent credit required, may ask for paystubs, employment letter, reference.
