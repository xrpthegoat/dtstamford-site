About this home

Wake up to breathtaking sunrises over the Westcot Beach water views. This beautifully renovated 3 bedroom 2 1/2 baths apartment, is perfectly positioned in a quiet Shippan Point's neighborhood. The light filled living area is enhanced by fireplace and dining area. The modern kitchen features quality appliances (new refrigerator and water filter) ample storage and generous counter space. A sliding door leads to private terrace creating a calm and inviting atmosphere for family BBQs and entertainment. Heat, and hot water is included in the rent. Located few minutes from shops, restaurants, marinas, parks and public transportation. 35 miles from NYC, 7 minutes to RR station and less to I95 entrance. Enjoy morning walks along the water front and easy access to what the neighborhood has to offer.
