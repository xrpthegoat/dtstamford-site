About this home

Immaculate two-family home in pristine condition, ideally located in one of Stamford's most convenient neighborhoods. Each unit features 2 spacious bedrooms, 1 updated bath, and a beautifully updated kitchen, making this an exceptional opportunity for both owner-occupants and investors. The second-floor unit offers a charming three-season porch and access to a walk-up attic, providing excellent storage or future expansion potential (subject to approvals). Additional features include a full basement, a one-car garage, driveway and off-street parking. The current owner occupies the property and has maintained it meticulously, using one of the units primarily for guests. Enjoy an unbeatable location just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, Stamford Hospital, and the Gaynor Brennan Golf Course, with shopping, dining, and entertainment all within easy reach. Whether you're looking to live in one unit while generating rental income from the other or seeking a turnkey investment property, this is an opportunity you won't want to miss.
