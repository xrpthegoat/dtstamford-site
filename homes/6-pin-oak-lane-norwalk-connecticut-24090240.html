About this home

Looking for peace and serenity? Listen to the birds while sipping on your morning coffee on the new deck overlooking the 5 mile river and tall reeds. This charming expanded cape is set on a cul-de-sac featuring beautiful gardens and mature plantings, a man made small pond with electric waterfall, a new deck and a wisteria covered pergola. Inside there is a breezeway where you can start your seedlings, a large great room with dining room and living room with a wood stove and windows overlooking the reeds and river, beautifully updated kitchen with soapstone countertops and stainless appliances, first floor bedroom with a stunning full bath as well as a large sunroom with small fireplace/grill overlooking the bucolic grounds. Upstairs you will find 2 additional bedrooms, a stylish full bath and a master suite with a stunning updated bath! In the lower level features a space that could be an in-law or an-pair with a full bath and kitchenette (however not heated) Make this your home and enjoy nature, peace and serenity! A truly special property.
