About this home

BEST BUY IN DARIEN! Sweet and Petite 2 bedroom home on private road in desirable Darien. Easy one level living includes updated appliances. Large sliders in kitchen open to fenced rear yard with storage shed. This is a marvelous alternative to restrictive condominium living so bring your pets! Enjoy "as is" or expand and grow your home on this double lot in the popular Noroton Heights area. Around the corner is Darien Commons where there's an array of fine shops and eateries. It's just steps to the Noroton station with express trains to NYC. The Darien lifestyle offers award winning schools, scenic beaches, gourmet dining and much more. This is a rare opportunity so come visit soon!
