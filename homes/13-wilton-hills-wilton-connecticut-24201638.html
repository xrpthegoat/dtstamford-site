About this home

Located in highly desirable Wilton Hills, this stunning home has been completely transformed over the past six years, offering modern luxury, thoughtful updates, and an exceptional open-concept floor plan. A dramatic two-story foyer welcomes you into a light-filled interior featuring a spacious great room with cathedral ceilings, new mouldings, a new gas fireplace, and a wall of windows overlooking the beautiful and private surroundings. The completely renovated chef's kitchen is a true showstopper, featuring gorgeous quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, a generous 9-foot island, and top-of-the-line Wolf, Bosch, and Sub-Zero appliances. The main-level primary suite provides a private retreat with expansive windows, walk-in closet, tray ceiling and a beautifully updated bath. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms feature new hardwood floors and share a newly renovated full bath. The walk-out lower level offers exceptional additional living space, with new vinyl plank flooring, a versatile office that could also serve as a fourth bedroom, and a renovated full bath. With nearly every space thoughtfully updated, this move-in-ready home combines sophisticated design, modern amenities, and flexible living spaces in one of the most desirable South Wilton settings.
