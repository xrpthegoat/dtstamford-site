About this home

Do you dream of living near the beach? Have panoramic views of Long Island Sound year-round? Luxurious yet casual coastal lifestyle? Sleek, modern lines? A carefully crafted floor plan? Designed for the sophisticated buyer looking for easy living, easy care in a great beach community, this home is ideal for year-round residency or weekend getaways. The first floor open floor plan incorporates the beautiful vistas beyond, and a private library/office allows for quieter moments. The second floor includes the primary bedroom suite, where you can enjoy morning coffee on the balcony while watching the sunrise or sit in front of a cozy fire in cooler weather. Two additional bedrooms and a full laundry room complete the second floor. A bedroom and full bath in the lower level provide additional living space, while the garage has been transformed into a stunning workout area or use as a garage. The expansive rooftop deck with stunning views includes ample area for dining, grilling, or relaxing by the gas firepit, while you enjoy the tranquil scenery beyond. Additional features include Wolf and Subzero appliances, wine chiller, Smarthome technology, Apple Homekit, ample storage, city water, sewer and natural gas and a 22kw generator. Enjoy the remarkably private and serene backyard, or the convenience of strolling to the Old Mill Deli by Romanacci, Old Mill Beach, Compo Beach or even Longshore Country Club. NOTE: Strongest and final offers are due by noon Monday, July 15, 2024.
