About this home

Stunning direct waterfront rental perched on a bluff overlooking Holly Pond on the Darien/Stamford line. Completely renovated in 2018, this home offers a seamless open floor plan designed to maximize both comfort and breathtaking water views. The spacious living room features French doors that open to a covered porch, creating a perfect indoor-outdoor flow-all with sweeping waterfront vistas. The beautifully updated kitchen boasts a center island, quartz countertops, modern backsplash, and top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances. It opens to a generous dining area, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, along with a convenient powder room. Upstairs, the primary suite occupies a private level and includes a renovated bath. Three additional bedrooms offer ample closet space, a Murphy bed, and a shared hall bath with tub/shower. A dedicated laundry area adds everyday convenience. Outside, the covered porch leads down to an expansive flagstone patio with masonry walls and a firepit-perfect for year-round enjoyment of the serene waterfront setting. A private driveway at the rear enhances both access and privacy. Move right in and experience the best of coastal living.
