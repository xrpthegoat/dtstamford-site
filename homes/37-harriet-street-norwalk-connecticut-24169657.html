About this home

Introducing 37 Harriet Street, a beautifully maintained home offering timeless character, natural light, and exceptional livability in one of Norwalk's most convenient neighborhoods. From the moment you enter, warm, sunlit interiors create an inviting atmosphere, enhanced by hardwood floors, vintage millwork, French doors, and thoughtful details throughout. The home blends charm with functionality, featuring a spacious butler's pantry, a practical mudroom, and a walk-up attic offering excellent storage or exciting potential for future expansion. Every space feels both purposeful and full of character. Outdoors, a beautifully curated backyard unfolds as a private sanctuary - lush, serene, and ideal for entertaining or quiet retreat - enhanced by mature perennial gardens and the natural beauty of visiting birds and wildlife. Ideally located, you'll enjoy the ease of walking to grocery stores, restaurants, a pharmacy, and everyday essentials, along with nearby schools and convenient access to transportation. The distant chime of St. Philip's bells adds a subtle, charming backdrop to daily life. Blending location, charm, and lifestyle, 37 Harriet Street presents a rare opportunity to own a home that has been truly cared for - ready for its next chapter.
