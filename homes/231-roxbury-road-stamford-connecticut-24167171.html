About this home

Timeless and charming, this home-originally built as a dairy farm in 1925-has been thoughtfully expanded and updated for modern living, offering over 5,000 sq ft of beautifully finished space on nearly one acre of private, park-like property.A welcoming wraparound porch invites you in, where hardwood floors throughout and fresh interior paint create a move-in ready feel. Generous room sizes and a seamless flow blend classic character with refined comfort. The warm country kitchen features rich wood cabinetry, granite countertops, center island, built-in window seat, and a spacious breakfast area, opening to a step-down family room with gas fireplace, wet bar, and French doors to a skylit sunroom. A separate staircase leads to a private guest suite with bedroom, sitting area, and full bath, while the main staircase leads to four spacious bedrooms, including a primary suite with a wood-burning fireplace and spa-like bath with jetted tub and glass-enclosed shower. From the kitchen and sunroom, step outside to a large deck and screened-in gazebo overlooking lush, private grounds with stone walls on nearly one acre. Completing the property is a four-car garage with radiant heated floors, all set in a desirable Westover location close to parks, shopping, dining, recreation, and with convenient access to the Merritt Parkway.
