About this home

Stunning Old Greenwich gem combines ultimate style with unique flexibility. Built on .48 acres with possible pool site, generator, first floor primary, 3 family bedrooms with optional 4th, plus luxurious kitchen and formal dining room, this rare find provides a retreat fit for today's discerning customer. Additional space on the second floor and finished walk out lower level with full bath allows for multiple uses tailored to individual needs. Beautiful outdoor space leads to the charming cottage with living room, kitchenette, full bedroom and bath perfect for guests, relaxation and parties. Featured in both Cottage and Gardens and at Home Magazines, this is truly a one of a kind opportunity south of the village of Old Greenwich. Square footage includes 726 in the cottage.
