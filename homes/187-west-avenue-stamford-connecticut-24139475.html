About this home

Meticulously updated end-unit townhome offering modern finishes, abundant natural light, and a highly convenient location. This spacious three-level residence showcases newly refinished hardwood floors, fresh interior paint, and recessed lighting that accentuates its bright, open-concept design. The brand-new kitchen features white shaker cabinetry, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, undercabinet lighting, and a sleek subway tile backsplash, opening seamlessly to the dining and living areas - perfect for entertaining. Upstairs you'll find three spacious bedrooms with ample closet space and a fully remodeled bath featuring marble-top vanities and stylish fixtures. A renovated half bath completes the main living level. The main-level family room offers great flexibility - ideal as a home office, workout space, or additional bedroom - and includes sliders leading to a private oversized patio with direct exterior access to your parking space. A 1-car garage and additional outdoor parking space are included. Enjoy central air, gas heat, and low-maintenance living in this move-in-ready home. Conveniently located on Greenwich border just minutes to Stamford Hospital, Metro-North to NYC, I-95, all restaurants & shopping Downtown Stamford has to offer, this property combines style, comfort, and accessibility in one perfect package!
