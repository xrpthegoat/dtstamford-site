About this home

Welcome Home to the Heart of Stamford! Tucked away on a quiet dead-end street, this charming 3-bedroom, 2 full-bath Colonial offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable locations. Situated at 46 Vernon Place, you'll enjoy a peaceful residential setting while being just moments from the vibrant energy of downtown. Offering 1,632 square feet of living space, the home features bright, inviting rooms, a clean and dry full basement providing excellent storage or future expansion potential, and a fully fenced backyard perfect for entertaining, pets, or relaxing outdoors. A private driveway adds to the home's everyday convenience. The location is truly what sets this property apart. Just steps from Broad Street and Washington Boulevard, you'll have easy access to Stamford's thriving downtown with its exceptional restaurants, cafes, shopping, entertainment, and nightlife. Commuters will appreciate the quick access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and the Stamford Transportation Center with Metro-North service to New York City. Nearby parks, schools, medical facilities, and the Harbor Point waterfront are all just minutes away, making this an ideal home for those seeking both convenience and an active lifestyle. Whether you're looking for your first home, a place to grow, or an easy commute with all the amenities of city living nearby, 46 Vernon Place offers an exceptional opportunity to enjoy the very best of Stamford.
