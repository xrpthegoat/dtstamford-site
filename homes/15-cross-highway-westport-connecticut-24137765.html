About this home

*Please submit all offers by 5pm Tuesday, Dec. 2nd. This French Country-inspired gem in Westport's sought-after Downtown North neighborhood is a true delight. From the grand entrance to the marble-clad kitchen and beautifully manicured stone patios, every corner reflects thoughtful craftsmanship and timeless elegance. The main level offers an open, sun-filled layout with rich hardwood beams, soaring ceilings, expansive windows, radiant-heated limestone floors, a stunning dining area, and a beautiful kitchen island - perfect for entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite is perfectly proportioned, featuring a spa-like bath and spacious walk-in closet. A cozy lounge connects two additional bedrooms with an adjoining bath, and a convenient second-floor laundry completes the level. The walk-out lower level provides a welcoming family room with a fireplace and a flexible office, gym, or play space. A private guest or in-law suite - complete with bedroom, full bath, kitchenette, and additional laundry - adds comfort and versatility. Outside, lush greenery surrounds garden-view patios, a pergola-covered deck, and a private drive leading to a two-car garage. Just a short stroll to downtown Westport and moments from new favorites like Hudson Malone and local staples such as Coffee An, 15 Cross Highway blends refined living with walkable convenience - the perfect combination of style, warmth, and location.
