About this home

Enjoy views of Long Island Sound from this renovated Old Greenwich four bedroom spacious stone and shingle home. Located on a quiet cul-de-sac, in Ledge Road Association with area private beach, this home has high ceilings, plenty of light and lovely design and craftsmanship throughout. There is a gourmet eat-in kitchen, adjacent family room with fireplace and dining room with cathedral ceiling and access to an outdoor stone terrace with fireplace and a place to entertain, relax and take in the views. The primary suite has a lux bath and private balcony for enjoying the water views and sipping a cup of coffee. Bonus: First floor huge bedroom (with office space) and en-suite bath. This home is a short distance to Tod's Point, town, schools and Metro North train. Perfect for today's living.
