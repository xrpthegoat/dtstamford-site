About this home

Welcome to a spacious & versatile 4-bedroom, 5 bathroom ranch-style retreat in highly sought-after North Stamford. Set on a picturesque lot with tranquil pond views & a bridge over a flowing stream, this home ideal for multigenerational living, remote work, & indoor/outdoor entertaining. Main level features sunken living room w/ a wood-burning fireplace & large windows that flood the space with natural lighting that highlights the beautiful refinished hardwood floors. Formal dining area w/sliding glass doors opens to deck & heated in-ground pool, offering sweeping views of lush landscape & pond. The eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly to a covered grilling area & patio-perfect for summer barbecues, poolside lounging, & gatherings with friends & family. A private primary suite offers a relaxing escape with ensuite bathroom & walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms & a full hallway bathroom complete the main level. Upstairs, a finished walk-up attic provides a fourth bedroom, full bath w/ skylights, & flexible space ideal for a home office, studio, or playroom.A full lower level expands your options w/ a large finished area, full bathroom, kitchenette, separate entrance, & two-car garage-ideal for an in-law suite, au pair quarters, gym, or media room. Additional unfinished space offers room for storage or future customization. From its flowing layout to its serene outdoor spaces, this home has solid bones & incredible potential.
