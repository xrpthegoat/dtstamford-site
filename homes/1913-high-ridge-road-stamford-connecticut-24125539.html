About this home

Welcome to 1913 High Ridge Road, a charming ranch-style home offering 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and endless potential. Set across from the peaceful Danoff Bruder Family Meadow at Bartlett Arboretum, this home combines natural beauty with everyday convenience. Inside, the main level offers comfortable living space with two bedrooms and a full bath, while the level off the kitchen provides flexibility as a possible in-law suite featuring its own entrance and full bathroom. The basement is partially finished with multiple rooms and laundry, ideal for storage, recreation, or expansion. Step outside to a screened-in deck overlooking the 0.34-acre property, perfect for enjoying quiet mornings or summer evenings. This prime location offers easy access to Stamford's best amenities-just minutes to Bartlett Arboretum's trails and gardens, the Stamford Museum and Nature Center, downtown shopping and dining, and convenient access to the Merritt Parkway for commuting. Move right in and enjoy as-is, or bring your vision to transform this home into something spectacular. With its flexible layout and sought-after setting, it's also a smart opportunity for renovation or investment. Homes in this location rarely become available-don't miss your chance to enjoy all that North Stamford living has to offer.
