About this home

Welcome to your next chapter in this delightful three-bedroom residence that perfectly blends comfort with functionality. This inviting two-story home offers approximately 2,000 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, ready for you to make your own. Enter and discover gorgeous hardwood floors that flow throughout, creating warmth and elegance in every room. The main level features a spacious living room anchored by a cozy fireplace, ideal for those chilly Connecticut evenings when you want nothing more than to curl up with a good book. The adjacent dining room provides the perfect setting for memorable meals with guests. One of this home's clever surprises is a main-level bedroom that could easily transform into a home office, giving you flexibility for today's lifestyle. Upstairs, you'll find the generous primary bedroom alongside a third bedroom, both offering comfortable retreats at day's end. The full bathroom features both a tub and shower, accommodating everyone's preferences. Outside, a covered porch invites relaxing weekends, while the garden area awaits your green thumb ambitions. Situated on a level corner lot, you'll enjoy easy maintenance and excellent curb appeal. Location enthusiasts will appreciate being just moments from Trader Joe's for your grocery runs, with abundant dining and shopping options nearby. Nature lovers can explore the nearby Stamford Museum and Nature Center, and commuters will value the convenient access to the Merritt Parkway.
