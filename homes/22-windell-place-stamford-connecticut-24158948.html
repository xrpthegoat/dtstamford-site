About this home

Welcome home to this bright and spacious 2-bedroom, 1-bath apartment ideally located just a short walk from the Metro-North Railroad at the Glenbrook Station-perfect for an easy commute. Inside, you'll find a welcoming layout featuring a sun-filled sunroom, a comfortable living room, and a separate dining room with charming built-ins that add both character and storage. The large kitchen offers plenty of room to cook and gather, while generous closet and storage space throughout the apartment makes staying organized easy. Additional highlights include two reserved off-street parking spaces (during snow events, one vehicle must temporarily park on the street). Enjoy the convenience of being close to public transportation, shopping, houses of worship, and everyday essentials-all within easy reach.
