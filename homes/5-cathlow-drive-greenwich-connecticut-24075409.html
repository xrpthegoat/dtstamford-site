About this home

A rare chance to own an elegant 5-bedroom Riverside home on nearly 2 acres of level land. This timeless residence blends classic sophistication with modern comfort, featuring spacious rooms, an open-concept kitchen, breakfast area, and family room for seamless living and entertaining. The expansive primary suite offers a luxurious dressing room and en-suite bath. Additional highlights include a private gym, wine cellar, and large playroom. Manicured grounds with specimen trees, lush plantings, a stunning pool, and a cabana create a secluded retreat. Sonos speaker systems provide sound throughout the kitchen, dining room, and patio. A circular driveway and parking court complete this exceptional offering.
