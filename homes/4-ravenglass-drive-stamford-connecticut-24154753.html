About this home

Welcome to Windermere on the Lake, a premier, gated community in North Stamford. Situated on a private, scenic lot, this custom 5-bedroom, 5.1-bathroom home offers year-round views of the lake within a sought-after neighborhood. Upon entry, guests are greeted by a stunning foyer with soaring ceilings. The heart of the home is the gourmet eat-in kitchen, featuring brand-new quartzite countertops, a substantial center island, and plenty of counter space for meal prep. The kitchen flows seamlessly to the outdoor deck (with firepit), offering guests captivating views of the lake. From the elegant, formal dining room to the warmth of the cozy great room with custom built- ins, every corner of this home is a sanctuary. A spacious office is conveniently located just off the great room on the first floor. This spectacular home boasts new decorative lighting inside and out, a new entertainment system throughout, refinished floors, a repaved driveway (2025), and a new modern stair railing. The luxurious primary bedroom suite is a private retreat, featuring a designer, walk-in closet and a large, spa-like bathroom complete with a free-standing tub. This home has so many versatile living spaces, including an expansive multi-purpose room on the second floor, perfect as a playroom or additional guest quarters.
