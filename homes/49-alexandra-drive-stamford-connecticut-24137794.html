About this home

***CALL FOR OFFERS: TUES (11/18) @ 9P*** If you've been searching for that perfect North Stamford home on a cul-de-sac, you may have just found it. With easy access to the Merritt Pkwy and quick drive to the Nature Center & Bartlett Arboretum, this location is ideal for commuters and nature lovers alike. 49 Alexandra is a classic Colonial with a modern addition and thoughtful updates that bring the best of both worlds. Set back on a quiet street, the semi-circular driveway and one-acre flat lot make an immediate impression. Inside, the layout offers the perfect main-level flow for everyday living and entertaining: a welcoming living room with fireplace, a dining room with character, an open kitchen with island seating, and a stunning double-height family room with vaulted ceilings that is something you simply don't see every day, framed by walls of windows overlooking the backyard. A large mudroom connects the living spaces with everyday function, keeping life organized and guests moving easily between indoors and out. Upstairs, the primary suite features multiple built-in closets and a large ensuite with dual vanities and an oversized dual-head shower with a built-in bench. Two additional bedrooms share a well-appointed hall bath. The backyard offers a wide, level lawn, a large deck, and multiple walkouts from the home for seamless indoor-outdoor living. A 2-car garage, hardwood floors (most of home), updated mechanicals, city water(!!), generator and Lutron lights.
