About this home

Nestled on a private rear lot in Wilton's coveted Silvermine neighborhood, this beautifully renovated Colonial Farmhouse offers the perfect blend of New England charm, modern updates, and everyday convenience. With 2,736 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this home is ideal for buyers seeking a peaceful setting without sacrificing access to town amenities, top-rated schools, commuting options, and vibrant communities. A covered Ipe front porch sets the tone, leading into a classic center hall layout featuring a front-to-back living room with a fireplace and bay window, a formal dining room, and a stunning, expanded chef's kitchen. Upstairs, three bedrooms each enjoy their own walk-in closet, while hardwood floors and central air conditioning enhance comfort throughout the home. The walk-out lower level offers exceptional flexibility, currently configured as a private in-law or guest suite with a family room, fourth bedroom, full bath, and large mudroom with exterior access. Existing plumbing allows for an easy kitchen installation should future needs require a fully independent living space. Additional highlights include a three-car garage and generous parking area. Located in the historic and highly desirable Silvermine section of Wilton, known for its artistic heritage, scenic roads, and timeless character, this special property offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the Fairfield County lifestyle at its best.
