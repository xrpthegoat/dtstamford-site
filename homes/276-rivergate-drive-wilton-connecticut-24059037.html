About this home

***OPEN HOUSES SAT 23RD AND 24TH 12-3PM. HIGHEST AND BEST BY TUE 26TH @ 5PM.*** Welcome home! This stately 4 bedroom, 2 bathroom updated home in Wilton presents a unique opportunity for the discerning buyer seeking elegance, privacy, and modern convenience. Nestled on a beautifully secluded 4.5 acre lot, this residence is freshly painted inside and out, with newly installed interior and exterior lighting fixtures, and a brand new deck that extends to a sophisticated blue stone patio. Here, you'll experience true tranquility while enjoying close proximity to the Merritt Parkway, Metro-North, and I-95, making this property the perfect retreat for those who value both seclusion and accessibility. A commuter's dream! The main upper level exudes refinement, featuring hardwood floors, three spacious bedrooms, and a welcoming living room. The dining room opens to the new private deck, offering seamless indoor-outdoor flow for entertaining or serene relaxation. The kitchen, thoughtfully equipped with newer Whirlpool stainless steel appliances, granite countertops and an abundance of space and storage to cater to any culinary enthusiast's needs, combines functional design with modern appeal. On the entrance level, a tiled foyer leads to a versatile family room or playroom complete with a cozy fireplace. This level also includes a full bath and a fourth bedroom, ideal as an au pair suite, guest retreat, or executive home office. This is the ONE you have been waiting for!
