About this home

Located in the heart of desirable Hubbard Heights, this beloved family home is being offered for the first time ever and is ready for its next chapter. Tucked at the end of a cul-de-sac with views of Hubbard Heights (E. Gaynor Brennan) Golf Course, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath Colonial Home offers the perfect combination of space, privacy, and location. The main level features a formal living room that flows seamlessly into the dining room and a spacious eat-in kitchen an ideal floor plan for both everyday living and entertaining. A sun-filled family room with a wood-burning fireplace serves as the heart of the home, with sliding doors that open to a deck overlooking the private backyard. Upstairs, you'll find all four bedrooms, including a primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and ensuite bath, along with three additional generously sized bedrooms and hall bathroom. Additional highlights include abundant storage with an attic and expansive basement crawl space, as well as a spacious two-car garage offering both parking and extra storage. Laundry is located on main level with extra bathroom. Can't beat location gives you proximity to everything - close to shopping, downtown, Scalzi Park, Train Lines, I-95, Stamford Hospital. Come fall in love with the historic Hubbard Heights Neighborhood and turn this loved home into the house of your dreams!
