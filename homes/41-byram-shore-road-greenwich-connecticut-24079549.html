About this home

Waterfront Rental on Long Island Sound. Newly renovated, situated on the third floor with a private elevator going directly outside. Two large picture windows look directly out onto the water and islands. Hardwood floors, high ceilings, and custom molding throughout. 1 bedroom, 1 very large living room with a divider for extra privacy. Contemporary open space, bathroom with tub & washer/dryer. Storage space throughout. Beach access for swimming and kayaking, large yard. Around the corner from Byram Park with Tennis, Beach, Pool, and Boating. This is a great location for commuters - easy access to the metro North Train Station (50 minutes to Grand Central Station) and also to Interstate I-95.
