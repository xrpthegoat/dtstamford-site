About this home

Experience a rare opportunity to own a true waterfront compound at 16 & 18 Burwell Street in South Norwalk. With direct frontage on Norwalk Harbor and views of Long Island Sound, these adjacent properties, totaling more than 1/2 acre, offer exceptional flexibility as a private estate, multigenerational retreat, or investment opportunity. The waterfront home at 18 Burwell Street features 2 bedrooms, 2 full baths, new windows, quartz countertops, and bright living spaces designed to capture breathtaking water views. Step outside to a spacious wood patio surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens, creating the perfect setting for entertaining or relaxing by the shore. Approved dock permits and plans provide the opportunity to build a private dock with direct access to Long Island Sound. The neighboring 16 Burwell Street expands the possibilities with incredible water views, additional living space, guest accommodations, or future development potential, making this a truly unique coastal offering. Located just minutes from beaches, marinas, restaurants, parks, and the South Norwalk train station, this property combines an unmatched waterfront lifestyle with everyday convenience. A once-in-a-generation opportunity to create a legacy estate on Connecticut's coastline. Owner/Agent.
