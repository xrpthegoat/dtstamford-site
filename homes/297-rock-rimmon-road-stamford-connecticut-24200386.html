About this home

Double gates glide open to beckon you down a winding drive revealing this special historical residence. Set in coveted North Stamford, this 5-bedroom, 5.5 bath home combines artisanal craftsmanship with modern appeal. Originally built in 1792, its professional restorations maintain authenticity while newer construction offers numerous options for current lifestyles. From the moment you arrive, the property feels like a hidden retreat. Hand-laid stone walls, mature trees, vibrant gardens and expansive lawns create an enchanted setting. Inside, the home's layout unfolds naturally. A breathtaking great room with an impressive fireplace, hand-crafted millwork, custom built-ins, and soaring 12 ft. ceiling flows through French doors to a light-filled sunroom and on into the timber-framed dining room with its own fireplace...perfect for entertaining. The kitchen and adjacent breakfast room support easy daily living with views and access to surrounding gardens and delightful al fresco eating spaces. A thoughtfully designed (2013) extension complete with a spacious family room, kitchenette, office, 2 bedrooms and baths provide flexibility for extended family, guests, exercise room or playroom. Outdoors, the setting is peaceful and secluded. Lush plantings surround the home, offering total privacy. A hilltop pool and screened, eat-in pool cabin create a special destination.
