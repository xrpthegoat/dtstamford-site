About this home

Welcome home to the prestigious, gated Chesterfield community, where classic townhouse charm meets modern convenience in central Stamford! This beautifully maintained residence features a spacious multi-level layout designed for both quiet relaxation and effortless entertaining. Upstairs, dual en-suite bedrooms each feature their own dedicated full bath, offering exceptional privacy for guests as well as primary suite setup. The main level opens to a light-filled living room with access to your own private deck, while the finished walk-out lower level features glass sliders leading outside-perfect for a home office, media room, gym, or rec space. Storage challenges are solved with an enormous pull-down attic, a rare and valuable feature in condo living. Complete with an attached 1-car garage and access to resort-style amenities, this home offers top-tier security and easy living in an unbeatable Stamford location.
