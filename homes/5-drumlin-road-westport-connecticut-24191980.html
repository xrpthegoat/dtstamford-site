About this home

Offers due by 5PM Monday, 7/27. Tucked into one of Westport's most treasured beach neighborhoods, this sun-filled Colonial is where people ride bikes until dinner, neighbors become lifelong friends, and an evening walk to Compo Beach can become part of your daily routine. From the moment you step inside, soaring ceilings, natural light, and beautifully connected living spaces create a home that feels larger, brighter, and happier than its square footage suggests. Designed for the way families actually live, the open floor plan flows effortlessly from the kitchen to the family room, while the unfinished lower level with soaring ceiling height offers potential for a home gym, golf simulator, media room, or recreation space-adding tremendous future value. Then there's something you won't find in most homes: an owned Tesla Solar Roof with integrated battery backup. Not panels mounted on top, but the roof itself generating power. Enjoy remarkably low electric costs, automatic backup power during outages, and annual income through Tesla's Virtual Power Plant-all without compromising the home's classic architecture. Outside, gather around the fire pit beneath the stars, tend the garden, and simply enjoy the feeling of belonging that makes this neighborhood so special. With beach rights, top-rated Westport schools, Longshore just around the corner, and one of the area's most welcoming communities, this is more than a beautiful home. It's the heart of Compo Beach living.
