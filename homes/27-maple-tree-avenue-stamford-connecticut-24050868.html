About this home

Bright, spacious, end-unit townhouse at Maple Manor Estates awaits its' new owner. Unit features an open floor plan with a terra-cotta tile floor throughout the main level. Kitchen has separate door to the side of complex. A nicely appointed powder room completes this level. The stairs and upper level, with both bedrooms, have had new carpet recently installed. The generously-sized primary bedroom has two closets and is flooded with sunlight. The second bedroom also has plenty of space for guests. The lower level adds solid square footage with a double basement and endless uses, as an office, gym, playroom or storage area. There is also an adjacent laundry room, with plenty of space for shelving, workbench or craft table. Other features include newer windows, two A/C wall units and two assigned parking spaces only a short distance from the front door. The entire unit has been freshly painted. Complex is a self-managed HOA (7 units), pet friendly and has a small common area in the rear of the property. It's located close to Darien, I-95, Hope Street shopping and the Metro North Glenbrook train station. Unit is being sold AS IS. Per HOA right of first refusal rules, the association has a 10-day match, made on the property. Highest and best offers due Monday, January 6th by 5pm. Seller will make a decision on Tuesday, January 7th.
