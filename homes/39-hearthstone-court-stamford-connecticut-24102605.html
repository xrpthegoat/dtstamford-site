About this home

Rare opportunity to own a classic ranch in The Cove, one of Stamford's more sought-after neighborhoods. Lovingly maintained by the same family for 70+ years, this 3-bedroom, 1-bath home is ideally located on a quiet cul-de-sac just minutes from Cove Island Park, the beach, and all the conveniences of downtown. Set on a generous 1/4-acre level lot, this charming home offers an open layout with a dine-in kitchen, large living room, family room, and a wonderful screened porch overlooking the backyard-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. This well-cared-for home is connected to city gas, water, and sewer, with furnace and appliances in very good working condition. Whether you're a first-time buyer, downsizer, or investor, want to renovate, update or expand, this home offers endless potential in a truly prime location. Enjoy easy access to I-95, Metro North, shopping, parks, and Stamford's vibrant downtown. A perfect blend of charm, location, and opportunity-don't miss this chance to make it your dream home.
