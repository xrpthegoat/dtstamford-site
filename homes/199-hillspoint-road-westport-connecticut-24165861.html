About this home

Stunning direct waterfront Nantucket style home overlooking Sherwood Mill Pond at Westport's Compo Beach. Completely renovated with chic coastal style, this special retreat offers an open floor plan, European-style kitchen, dining area, and multiple living spaces, all with panoramic water views. Upstairs offers 3 stylish bedrooms with generous closets, excellent storage and custom baths. Enjoy sunrise, sunset and the soothing sounds of the water from your private deck. From the seawall, launch a kayak or paddleboard and enjoy time on the water. Peace and tranquility are yours to enjoy and included at no extra charge.
