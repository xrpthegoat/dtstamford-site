About this home

Welcome to this beautifully expanded and meticulously renovated Cape Cod-style home, nestled on a quiet, family-friendly street in the heart of Cos Cob. Step outside to a covered deck complete with a cozy fireplace and outdoor TV, creating a true all-season retreat. Enjoy the privacy of a large, fenced-in yard, perfect for play or relaxation.
