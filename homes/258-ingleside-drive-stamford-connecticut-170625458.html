About this home

Step into this North Stamford residence where timeless charm meets contemporary luxury. Situated on over 2 acres of landscaped grounds with access to a picturesque pond, this home has been tastefully updated. The main level features wide plank floors, an updated kitchen/dining area with a stone fireplace, a living room with a masonry fireplace, and a family room with grand glass elements and stone fireplace. The first-floor in-law suite boasts a remodeled bathroom with marble finishes. Two staircases lead to a second floor with a master suite, featuring its own fireplace, a spacious bath with a large walk-in shower, and a versatile exercise room/office space that could easily be converted into a walk-in closet. The lush grounds feature stonewalls, direct pond access, and a sun-filled flagstone terrace surrounded by trees, ivy-covered walls, perfect for summer gatherings. Adjacent high-end homes enhance the neighborhood's value. Recent updates include central A.C., master bathroom, kitchen, detached 3-car garage, well pump, roof, and hot water heater, ensuring modern comfort and convenience.
