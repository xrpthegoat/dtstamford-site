About this home

Welcome to this charming 2-bedroom, 2-bath Cape Cod-style home in Stamford's Cove neighborhood, where comfort and convenience come together. ALL UTILITIES INCLUDED: electricity, heat, hot water, water, and central air conditioning! Freshly painted with updated window treatments (to be installed), this inviting home features hardwood floors, a cozy living room with a fireplace, and an updated kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The thoughtful layout offers one bedroom and a full bathroom on each floor, providing flexibility and privacy, with generous closet space throughout. Additional features include lower-level laundry and storage, a private detached garage, and driveway parking. Enjoy a peaceful setting just a short walk from Cove Island Park, with its beaches, walking trails, and waterfront recreation. Convenient to shopping, dining, and commuter routes. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy the best of Stamford's coastal living!
