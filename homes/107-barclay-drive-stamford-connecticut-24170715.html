About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST BY MAY 5th, 5:00 pm. Tucked away on a quiet cul de sac just over the North Stamford border, 107 Barclay Drive is the fully renovated colonial you've been waiting for. Set on 1.17 acres with charming curb appeal and a welcoming front porch, this move-in ready 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath home blends style, space, and location. Filled with natural light, the home features refinished hardwood floors, a spacious living room with bay window, and fantastic flow throughout. The beautiful eat-in kitchen showcases white cabinetry, quartz countertops, farmhouse sink, stainless steel appliances, and opens seamlessly to the inviting family room with fireplace and backyard access. Upstairs are four generously sized bedrooms, including a stunning primary suite retreat with exposed beams, arched windows, vaulted ceilings, dual vanity, oversized shower, and walk-in closet. Recent updates include two-zone central air with smart Nest thermostats, Level 2 Tesla-compatible EV charger, new lawn, approximately 25 Green Giant arborvitae for privacy, and an expansive 700 sq. ft. patio designed for outdoor living and entertaining. Ideally located near Westover, Greenwich, Stamford's best parks, and just minutes to the Merritt Parkway, this exceptional property delivers privacy, convenience, and beautifully updated living.
