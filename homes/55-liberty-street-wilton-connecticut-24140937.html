About this home

Welcome to 55 Liberty St. Unparalleled modern luxury in this meticulously rebuilt 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath Wilton residence. Set on 1.8 private acres, the home delivers seamless, sun-drenched living across 4,300+ square feet, following a comprehensive 2025 renovation that saw the property rebuilt from the studs up. Step inside to discover European wire-brushed hardwood floors and an effortless, open layout. The heart of the home is a vaulted-ceiling kitchen, featuring contemporary white cabinetry and a full suite of CAFETM smart appliances. Upstairs, the spa-like primary suite is finished with Natural Marble and luxurious gold Kohler fixtures, offering a perfect sanctuary. The lower level is designed for wellness and flexibility, hosting a brand-new sauna and a spacious recreation room. Further enhancing the property is a new detached two-car garage with second-story potential for guest quarters or a private office. Engineered for efficiency, the residence features a Bosch Hyper Heat HVAC system and a new Solar Array. Positioned moments from the Wilton Metro-North Station, this move-in-ready offering combines sophisticated, sustainable design with exceptional convenience. A rare opportunity for a turnkey luxury lifestyle.
