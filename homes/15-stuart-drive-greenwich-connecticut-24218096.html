About this home

The real luxury is how easily your day unfolds in AR Luxury Homes' newest build. 15 Stuart gives everyday life an upgrade: sunlight across the breakfast island, a quiet living room to settle into, and a swim before dinner. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a cathedral ceiling, private deck and soaking tub. White shingles frame the exterior, while floor-to-ceiling windows bring the landscape into view. A double-height entry opens to interiors for gathering. A gourmet kitchen connects dining and fireside lounging; a screened porch takes it outside. Four more bedrooms, a bonus room and elevator accommodate life as it changes. The lower level offers a wellness center with exercise room and cold plunge, golf simulator and recreation room. A pool and outdoor kitchen await outside.
