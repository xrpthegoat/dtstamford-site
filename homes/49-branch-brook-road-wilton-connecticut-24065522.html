About this home

Imagine a home where every day feels like a luxurious retreat, a unique log-cabin home inspired by the elegance of Aspen, Colorado. This private sanctuary boasts architectural features that blend rustic charm with modern sophistication, creating an ambience of perpetual vacation. Soaring cathedral ceilings creating a sense of space and openness with the centerpiece of the living area is a floor-to-celling stone fireplace. Skylights invite streams of natural sunlight to bathe the great room and kitchen. Enjoy the primary bedroom on the main floor with a beautifully renovated full bath and sliders to your own private deck. This area is ideal for morning coffee, evening reflections, or simply soaking in the surrounding natural beauty. Adjacent to the expansive main deck is a generously sized, covered entertaining space equipped with a fully functional bar. This area is perfect for hosting gatherings, providing shelter from the elements while allowing guests to enjoy the natural beauty of the backyard. The walk-out lower level is spacious with a full bath, office/bedroom area and sliders to the private backyard. Integrating geothermal heating in this home promotes economic efficiency and environmental sustainability. Reach out to arrange a private tour of this special property.
