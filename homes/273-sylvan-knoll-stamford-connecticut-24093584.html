About this home

Welcome to 273 Sylvan Knolls - A Bright and Beautiful End-Unit Townhouse! Move right into this updated, sun-drenched 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath home, perfectly situated in a serene and convenient Stamford location. This home truly offers the best of comfort, convenience, and coastal living!
