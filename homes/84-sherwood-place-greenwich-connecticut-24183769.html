About this home

HEAT, WATER, AND LANDSCAPING ALL INCLUDED! Enjoy the best of downtown Greenwich living from this updated first-floor apartment at 84 Sherwood Pl. Just a 4 minute walk from Greenwich Avenue, you'll have easy access to some of the area's best restaurants, boutique shops, Whole Foods, Greenwich Library, and a neighborhood dog park. Recent improvements include updated flooring and fresh paint throughout, and all brand new appliances. The home sits on a level lot with a private backyard perfect for relaxing, entertaining, or spending time with your pets. On-site, shared basement laundry and storage adds everyday convenience, while heat, water, and landscaping are all included in the rent. This is a rare opportunity to enjoy a quiet residential setting just moments from everything downtown Greenwich has to offer.
