About this home

Available for immediate occupancy! Stunning long-term rental available furnished or unfurnished on highly sought-after Stanwich Road in prestigious Mid-Country Greenwich. Fully renovated and set on 2 parklike acres abutting 4.25 acres of conservation land for exceptional privacy. A dramatic double-height living and dining room features a wet bar, wood-burning fireplace, floor-to-ceiling glass, and sliding doors to the rear deck. The gourmet kitchen includes top of the line appliances. The spacious primary suite offers a walk-in closet, luxurious bath, ensuite bedroom, office, and powder room, all on the main floor. Upstairs includes two well-appointed bedrooms, an updated bath, a second-floor family room, and an exercise room. The rear deck overlooks a heated pool & lush level lawn, just moments from town, train & schools.
