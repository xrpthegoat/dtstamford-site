About this home

Beautiful and light-filled 4 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom townhome. Located in Downtown Stamford, one block away from Stop & Shop, Starbucks, and Ridgeway Shopping Mall. It is a short-walk away from Scalzi Park as well (great for running, tennis, or just riding a bike). Close to Metro North train station. Inside you will find spacious bedrooms, walk-in closets, crown molding, and hard wood floors throughout. Plantations shutters. The first floor offers a one-car garage (spacious enough for a large minivan and storage), a family room ideal for watching TV, and a private in-law suite with a full bathroom. The second floor offers a great eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and sliders to a balcony. Great for cooking and entertaining. It also offers a half-bathroom, dining room, and living room. The dining room is large enough for an 8-person dining table. The third floor offers two large bedrooms and one full bathroom. Both bedrooms are quite large with walk-in closets as well. Large capacity washer and dryer adds extra convenience to the home. No more carrying laundry to the basement! And finally, the fourth floor offers a very large master bedroom with large bathroom and walk-in closet. And best of all, town home living means having more time for yourself. Rent includes, snow removal and gardening of the front lawn. Worry-free living! Garage +1 Add'l reserved space, there is option to have more parking at the building next door.
