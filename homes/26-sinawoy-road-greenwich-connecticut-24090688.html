About this home

New Construction!! Spectacular Modern Farmhouse. 4 levels of finished living space. This 4 bed, 4.5 bath is the ultimate family home. Greenwich in town living: steps to dining , schools, shopping, entertainment, parks, library, harbor, highways & train. Easy access to NYC.
