About this home

Tucked away just minutes from Greenwich Avenue, this enchanting 1933 stone and half-timbered Tudor sits on .65 acres overlooking a tranquil brook. With rich architectural detail and surprising spaces, this home offers charm and character at every turn. The sunny, dramatic living room features a soaring beamed cathedral ceiling, striking fieldstone fireplace, and triple exposures through large casement windows. The fully renovated kitchen by Deane includes an adjoining office, while a spacious dining room doubles perfectly as a cozy family room. Upstairs, the entire second floor serves as a luxurious primary suite-complete with a serene bedroom, sitting room, fully renovated marble bath, and new laundry.On the lower level, a guest bedroom with a fully renovated bathroom walks out to a very private stone patio, offering a perfect retreat for visitors or extended stays. Exterior highlights include a handsome slate roof, fieldstone terrace, and whimsical details like carved stone crests and ornamental gargoyles. Who says downsizing can't be a delight?
