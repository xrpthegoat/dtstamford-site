About this home

Welcome to this terrific, spacious, sun filled colonial home on great property with 270K in recent updates! Large living room with fireplace, formal dining room with access to the back patio. A spacious kitchen with island and dining area leads to the bright family room with vaulted ceiling, beams, skylights, built in book cases and cozy fireplace. The family room also offers access to the back patio, perfect for outdoor entertaining. Upstairs is a nice primary bedroom with full bath, three additional bedrooms and another full bathroom. Recent updates including Anderson windows, updated bathrooms, new high efficiency HVAC system, new whole house generator, new interior doors, new garage door and opener, Level 2 car charger in garage, new LED lighting, closet organizers, tree removal, freshly painted interior and a just painted exterior! A fantastic home with a great yard and convenient location!
