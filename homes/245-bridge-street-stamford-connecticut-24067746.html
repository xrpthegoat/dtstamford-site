About this home

Move in ready, this conveniently located two bedroom townhouse features a private deck overlooking a scenic brook. The sunny unit has a living room, dining room and a galley kitchen on the first floor. The second floor has two bedrooms and a full bathroom. The finished lower level family room has another full bathroom as well as a full sized washer and dryer. Included in the sale are two assigned parking spaces (#35 & #45) as well as visitor parking. The unit boasts hardwood flooring throughout. Located close to Ridgeway Shopping Center, and the new Whole Foods. Scalzi Park is within walking distance.
