About this home

Absolutely one of a kind opportunity to acquire this designer updated home & barn in a most convenient South Wilton location. The +/- 1 acre grounds are stunning, with perennial beds & plantings to bring beauty throughout the season. The main house exudes quiet charm and thoughtful updates that blend seamlessly with the abundance of historic features. Front to back living room offers a welcoming fireplace, gorgeous wide board floors in a natural finish & direct access to the inviting brick patio for outdoor entertaining. The updated eat-in kitchen includes stainless appliances, custom cabinetry & an open feel. There's even a pantry & laundry-so rare! Stunning dining room w/vaulted ceiling, & study/sitting room complete the main level. Upstairs, the Primary Suite with plush carpets, two walk-in closets and an updated full bath will delight & enchant you. The 2nd bedroom, adjacent to the Primary & also accessed by the rear staircase could also be a dressing room or nursery. An ample 3rd bedroom and full, updated hall bath cap off the interior of the main home. Oh My Goodness - there's a BARN!! A barn with history! Room for parking or storage underneath then venture upstairs to discover two spacious finished levels suitable for anything your heart desires. This home and property have been lovingly cared for and it shows inside and out. Newer roof & mechanicals throughout. Home has a portable generator. Barn has a separate electric meter.
