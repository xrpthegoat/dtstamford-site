About this home

Enjoy bright, comfortable living in this beautifully maintained, four-level end-unit townhouse. Filled with natural sunlight, this inviting home offers a spacious living room with a fireplace and direct access to a private deck, perfect for relaxing and entertaining. The adjoining room provides an ideal setting for gatherings, while the eat-in kitchen offers everyday convenience. A half-bath completes the main level. The second level features two bedrooms both outfitted with California Closet systems, a hall bath. The primary bedroom features its own bath with two ample closets. The third floor loft provides versatile additional living space, perfect for a home office, den, or cozy retreat. The lower level features a mud-room and access to a large two car garage. Well cared for and move-in ready, this townhouse offers a wonderful combination of space, natural light and functionality. Easy access to vibrant downtown Stamford, with restaurants, shopping and train. Please note: tax card shows address as 124 Hope Street.
