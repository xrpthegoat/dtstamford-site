About this home

Bright and inviting 2-bedroom townhouse-style condo in midtown Stamford, offering comfortable living in a super convenient location. This home features spacious bedrooms and an easy layout that works well for anyone wanting extra space. Two generous upstairs bedrooms with good closet space and natural light, ideal for a restful retreat or a work-from-home setup. Townhouse-style layout provides a more private, home-like feel with living space on the main level and bedrooms tucked away above. Hardwood floors run throughout the unit, adding warmth, character, and easy maintenance in every room. Off-street parking for one car is available in the back of the building, plus plenty of additional street parking for guests or a second vehicle. Laundry is conveniently located in the building next door, so you have on-site facilities without sacrificing space inside the unit. Situated in midtown Stamford, you are close to shops, restaurants, parks, and major commuting routes, making daily errands and city access simple and efficient.
