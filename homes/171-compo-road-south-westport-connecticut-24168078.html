About this home

Tucked away in a peaceful, private setting, Hidden Ledge offers the best of Westport living in an ideal Saugatuck/Compo location. This stunning brand-new 4-5 bedroom home combines thoughtful design, quality craftmanship, and effortless access to the area's most loved destinations. Walk along sidewalks or bike the designated bike routes to Compo Beach, Longshore, Saugatuck, downtown shops, acclaimed restaurants, the train, library and Westport's coveted Levitt pavilion. Designed and built by local HOME Design Build, this 3800 SF residence features a clean, timeless aesthetic with an abundance of light thanks to its southern and western exposures. Every detail has been carefully curated, from vertical channel accents, Visual Comfort Lighting and Thermador appliances to the wide-plank white oak floors, custom closets and over-sized Marvin windows. A must-see home in the heart of one of Westport's most desirable locations. #171 Compo Rd. S is one of two NEW Constructions currently being built on a private rear parcel set back from Compo Rd. S. offering city water, sewer and buried power lines. ** The driveway will be maintained jointly by both 171 & 169 Compo Rd. S. As permitted, the property has been organized as a land condominium to allow for the 2 New Constructions to be sold separately.
