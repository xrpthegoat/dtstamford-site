About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained ranch style home, tucked away on a private cul de sac while still offering convenient access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and South Norwalk's popular shopping, restaurants, and amenities. The property offers 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and approximately 1,616 square feet of combined living space between the main home and attached in law suite. Inside the main home, you'll find hardwood floors throughout, a recently updated galley kitchen and full bath, and a wood burning fireplace that anchors the main living area. The attached in law suite provides excellent flexibility with its own private entrance, full kitchen, full bath, bedroom, and separate access to the backyard. Outside, the property offers plenty of space to enjoy with two decks, a brick patio, covered and electrified gazebo, vegetable garden, outdoor lighting, lawn sprinkler plumbing, and a massive storage shed. Additional features include a generator hookup, outdoor camera system, and underground downspout drainage system. The home has also benefited from numerous major updates, including a new central air system, boiler, roof, stucco siding, and windows. With its versatile layout, extensive improvements, and convenient location, this home could be the perfect fit for generational living and or additional income potential.
