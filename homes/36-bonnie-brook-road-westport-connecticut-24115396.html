About this home

Welcome to your dream home in one of Westport's most desirable and charming neighborhoods. This beautifully crafted residence includes a sparkling pool and is perfectly nestled in a peaceful, walkable community-for bike rides and evening strolls. This home seamlessly blends modern design with a fresh take on the timeless Modern Farmhouse, offering both style and function. A spacious front porch invites you in, leading to an elegant foyer. Off to one side, you'll find a sunlit home office or formal living space with views of the property; on the other, a refined dining room complete with a butler's pantry, ideal for hosting. A chic powder room with a floating stone vanity and ample closet space make a thoughtful first impression. At the heart of the home is a designer kitchen outfitted with high-end Thermador appliances, including a gas range, custom cabinetry, and an oversized island perfect for gathering. The kitchen flows into a cozy breakfast nook and expansive family room-all framed by tall glass doors that open to a stone patio and the backyard oasis with pool.A spacious mudroom offers built-in storage and a full bath with direct access to the backyard, making it the perfect poolside cabana setup.Upstairs, you'll find generously sized bedrooms and stylish bathrooms. The primary suite is a true retreat with a spa-inspired bathroom and walk-in closets that will blow you away. Full walk-up third floor with full bath, offering even more flexibility for 5th bedroom.
