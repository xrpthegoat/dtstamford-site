About this home

Updated colonial split level home in sought after Mid Ridges neighborhood.Convenient to the Merritt, I 95, public transportation, shopping, dining, library and vibrant downtown Stamford. This lovely home has an eat in kitchen with stainless steel appliances and granite countertops.There is a large deck off of the kitchen to enjoy summer grilling and dining. The living room and dining room have a vaulted ceiling and a fireplace. Upstairs the primary bedroom has its own bath with a stall shower. The other two bedrooms share a hall bath. The family room, den/office and a half bath are on the first level. There are hardwood floors throughout. The lower level (basement) has a playroom, laundry room and storage all with tile floors. Pet friendly. Nest thermostat and security system. Please use Showingtime
