About this home

This specious Raised Ranch is located on a quiet cul-de-sac in a charming neighborhood. This house has been completely renovated in the last year, bringing it "in-style" with today's standards. With four bedrooms (on the main level) and three full bathrooms, it boasts an open kitchen, living and dining room area, opening up to a newly renovated deck. The lower level features an in-law apartment consisting of two rooms, one can be used as a bedroom, the other one as a large living/dining room (with a spacious kitchen corner). One of the bathrooms is also here, making it a complete apartment. The apartment opens up to the extensive backyard on the ground level. If you are looking for a great spacious home which is open to your tastes, look no further.
