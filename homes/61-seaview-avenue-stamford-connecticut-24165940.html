About this home

Waterfront living at her best! Beautifully updated 3 bed, 3 and a half bath end unit townhouse in desirable Marina Bay complex. Private waterside community just minutes from Stamford, CT RR (with fast trains into Manhattan), downtown Stamford for pickle ball & restaurants, and i95 for those needing to commute (north or south - exit 7). Incredible sunset views from living room (park-like vista) and primary suite balconies. Stunning eat-in kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances and plenty of storage. Large open plan dining (room for table as well as bar stools) and light-filled living room with elegant gas fireplace. Modern half bath completes the main level! Upstairs there's a spacious primary suite with sliders to a balcony. Two further bedrooms well apportioned bedrooms plus full bath complete the upper level. The lower level makes for a perfect den/family room, gym, au pair suite and offers a full bath (with oversized shower), side by side laundry in dedicated room, and sliders out to a stone patio! Attached one car garage with bonus storage, and further under stair storage room on lower level. One further parking in driveway. Take advantage of the gunite waterfront pool during summer months, and enjoy moments with neighbors/friends in private clubhouse with fitted kitchen. Views of Westcott Cove, Marina and Long Island Sound for those looking to live by the sea: Life doesn't get much sweeter than this! Sold "AS-IS".
