About this home

A charming 4-bedroom Cape Cod style home with an open floor plan. The spacious remodeled kitchen is the hub of the home and opens to the dining area and fire lit family room. The sun-filled living room leads to two bedrooms that could also serve as a den or home office. A full bath completes the first floor. Upstairs you will find the cozy primary bedroom, a full bath, laundry, and a fourth bedroom. The spectacular back yard is anchored by a brick terrace and features a luscious green lawn and verdant rock garden. The attached garage and garden shed afford extra store space to the property. The home as ideally situated on a cul-de-sac in the Bull's Head section of Stamford, close to Scalzi Park, Ridgeway Center, and the vibrant downtown dining and entertainment. Welcome Home!
