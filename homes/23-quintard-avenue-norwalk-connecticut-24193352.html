About this home

Charming and Vacant revitalized first-floor farmhouse in one of South Norwalk's most desirable waterfront neighborhoods! Just a short walk to the SoNo Metro-North station, The SoNo Collection, waterfront parks, and the area's best restaurants, cafes, and shops. This character-filled home offers 2 bedrooms - the second is a walkthrough - perfect for a home office, nursery, or guest room. Beautiful original hardwood floors, soaring 9+ ft. ceilings and timeless farmhouse details throughout. Enjoy a spacious living room with a classic built-in cupboard, a large eat-in country kitchen featuring an industrial-style six-burner gas range, dishwasher and a beautifully updated full bath with walk in shower. Every room blends vintage charm with thoughtful updates. Outside, relax in the lovely shared garden backyard-an ideal spot for morning coffee or unwinding after a long day. If you're looking for charm, convenience, and a walkable lifestyle near the water, this special home is a rare opportunity. Easy commute to NYC and close to everything that makes South Norwalk one of Fairfield County's most vibrant communities. *Heat is included. Shared basement storage space and laundry. Street parking only.
