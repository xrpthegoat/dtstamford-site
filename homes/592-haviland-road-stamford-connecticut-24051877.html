About this home

Introducing an extraordinary property in a prime North Stamford location, offering convenience to Greenwich, New Canaan, Pound Ridge, & Westchester. This one-of-a-kind 4-bed, 4.5-bath ranch with a separate office spans 3,523 sq ft and sits on 2.8 acres of lush, level land, blending contemporary elegance with timeless design. This transitional masterpiece has been beautifully updated to provide luxury & comfort in every detail. Inside, you'll find expansive living spaces designed for both everyday living & grand entertaining. The great room, with its striking fireplace, creates a warm, inviting atmosphere, perfect for gathering with family or hosting guests. The kitchen is a true centerpiece, featuring high-end appliances, custom cabinetry, & generous counter space, making it ideal for entertaining. The primary suite is a sanctuary unto itself, featuring a 2nd fireplace for added warmth & luxury. The spa-like ensuite bath & spacious walk-in closet provide both style & functionality, offering a retreat-like experience within the home. Outside, the property provides a private oasis with a Wagner gunite pool, 2 serene ponds, & professionally landscaped grounds with mature trees & lush greenery. The expensive 2.8 acre lot is perfect for relaxation, recreation, or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings. With cutting-edge smart home technology, a three-car garage, & meticulous attention to detail throughout, this home seamlessly combines modern convenience with classic luxury.
