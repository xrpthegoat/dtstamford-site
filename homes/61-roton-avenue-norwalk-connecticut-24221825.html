About this home

Quintessential Rowayton charm meets idyllic seaside living in this sun-drenched three-bedroom, two full bath Cape overlooking the picturesque Farm Creek Preserve. Beautiful water views and a private dock create a serene coastal setting, perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, or simply enjoying the natural beauty of the surroundings. On the main level, light hardwood floors, a cozy den with built-ins and a gas fireplace, and inviting living spaces capture the home's timeless New England character. The charming kitchen features stainless steel appliances and a quaint center island, while the inviting living room flows seamlessly into the dining area, where sliding glass doors open to a spacious waterfront deck, creating an effortless connection between indoor and outdoor living and entertaining. Two of the upstairs bedrooms feature private balconies to take in the tranquil views. A level, private backyard offers a peaceful retreat surrounded by nature. Just a short stroll to Bayley Beach, Rowayton's charming village, restaurants, shops, and waterfront amenities, this enchanting home offers the perfect blend of coastal tranquility and small-town living. A rare opportunity to embrace the relaxed seaside lifestyle that makes Rowayton so special.
