About this home

Nestled on 1.67 beautiful park-like acres in a charming neighborhood just minutes from town, this classic 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath Colonial offers timeless appeal, comfortable living spaces, and a lovely natural setting. Thoughtfully maintained throughout, the home features generously sized rooms, a gracious living room with fireplace, family room with custom built-ins, formal dining room, and inviting spaces designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The sun-filled walkout lower level provides wonderful additional living space, featuring a large recreation room/playroom with a wall of glass sliders opening directly to the patio and expansive backyard. Overlooking sweeping lawns and mature landscaping, the property provides a beautiful backdrop for outdoor enjoyment. A spacious deck and patio offer ideal settings for relaxing, dining al fresco, or gathering with family and friends, while the level yard provides endless possibilities for recreation and play. Conveniently located close to town, schools, and parks, this special property offers the perfect blend of classic charm, comfortable living, and a picturesque setting.
