About this home

Discover the potential at 77 Lolly Lane-a lower North Stamford split-level contemporary home on a level and private 1.34-acre lot at the end of a cul-de-sac. This four-bedroom, two-bathroom home is ideal for those ready to transform it into a dream home. Features include a screened-in porch, tall ceilings in the dining room/ primary ensuite bedroom, fireplace, and a two-car garage. This is a fantastic opportunity for a renovation or as a prime building lot. Conveniently located very close to the Stamford Nature Center, a short drive to the Merritt Parkway, close to shopping on High Ridge Road/ Downtown Stamford and near the Westchester County Airport. Additionally, the home is apart of the Fairfield Woods Association, which grants you access to the lake on Gary Road ($140 annually). The home is on septic, well, and is connected to natural gas, a rarity in North Stamford. Seize this chance to renovate or build new in the ideal location. Please note, the property is sold as-is and requires repairs due to rot, mold, and termite damage.
