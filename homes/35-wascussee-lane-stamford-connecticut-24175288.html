About this home

Rare standalone townhome in Bishop Meadows with the look, feel, and privacy of a single-family home. Thoughtfully updated and move-in ready, this exceptional home was recently updated with resurfaced floors, fresh paint, updated kitchen, renovated primary bath, and beautifully finished lower-level living space. The main level is filled with natural light and offers an open flow with access to the deck, creating an ideal space for both everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite showcases a beautifully renovated spa-inspired bathroom, and two additional bedrooms complete the upper level. The finished lower level adds additional space ideal for a media room, home office, gym, or playroom. A separate laundry room is on the lower level. Private outdoor space, abundant storage, and a quiet standalone setting make this unit especially unique within the complex. Quiet, well-managed community with pool and clubhouse. Minutes to Cove Island Park, Chelsea Piers, Metro North, downtown Stamford, and I-95.
