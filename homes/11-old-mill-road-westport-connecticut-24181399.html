About this home

Discover the perfect blend of coastal charm and modern comfort in this unique one-bedroom back house, ideally situated in the highly sought-after Compo Beach neighborhood of Westport. Nestled directly on the tranquil shores of Mill Pond, this sun-drenched rental offers breathtaking water views and private access for kayaking, paddleboarding, or simply enjoying the serene setting. Step inside to find an inviting open-concept living area with soaring ceilings and oversized windows that flood the space with natural light. The updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, and ample cabinetry. Located just moments from Compo Beach, Longshore Club Park, vibrant downtown Westport, and Metro-North train service, this rare rental opportunity combines waterfront living with unbeatable access to all the amenities Westport has to offer.
