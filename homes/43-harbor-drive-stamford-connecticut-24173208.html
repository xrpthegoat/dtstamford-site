About this home

Welcome to 43 Harbor Drive in beautiful Shippan, where coastal living meets comfort and convenience. This spacious 2 bedroom, 2 bath residence offers stunning harbor views and an ideal open layout for everyday living and entertaining. The primary suite features a luxurious bath complete with a double vanity, Jacuzzi tub, and heat lamp for added comfort. The inviting living room includes a fireplace and sliders leading to a private balcony overlooking the harbor, the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the water views. The updated kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, wine fridge and a Sub-Zero refrigerator. Additional highlights include an elevator that opens directly to your unit door, a reserved garage parking space, and a private storage unit located on the garage level. Enjoy a premier Shippan location just a short walk to beaches, shops, and restaurants.
