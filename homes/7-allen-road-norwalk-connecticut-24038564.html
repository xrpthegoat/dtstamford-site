About this home

Welcome to your own private oasis in desirable Cranbury. If you like to entertain or enjoy outdoor living this is a must see. Spacious wrap around deck and patio with heated in ground pool, gas fireplace, outdoor kitchen, fire pit, and hot tub overlooking a fantastic pond where you can kayak and relax or ice skate in the winter. Enjoy the sound of the charming waterfalls and stream that runs along the property. Almost one acre, it is professionally landscaped with mature plantings and trees. Step inside and be delighted with the light-filled open floor plan and vaulted ceiling living room with wood burning fireplace. Enjoy the sound of music throughout the dining room, family room, primary suite, and deck for entertaining or a relaxing vibe. The high end granite kitchen with stainless steel appliances is recently done with an island leading to the dining room and family room, both of which have radiant heat floors. The main level primary suite with gas fireplace, built-ins, huge walk-in closet, and updated bathroom walks out to the hot tub. A spiral staircase leads you to an upper level bedroom with custom built-ins and large deck with views of the pond. Great home office or bedroom on the main level. Lower level room currently being used as a gym offers additional space for flexible use. See below for recent upgrades.
