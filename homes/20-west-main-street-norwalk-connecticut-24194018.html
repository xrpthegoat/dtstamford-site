About this home

Sophisticated and beautifully maintained, this spacious 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse offers refined multi-level living in a highly convenient Norwalk location. Hardwood floors flow throughout the home, complementing the bright, open living and dining spaces and creating an elegant setting for both everyday living and entertaining. The well-proportioned kitchen connects seamlessly to the main living areas, while the private primary suite features its own full bath and generous closet space. Two additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, family, or a dedicated home office. Central air, abundant storage, in-unit laundry, and an attached private two-car garage add comfort and convenience. Set within a pet-friendly, professionally managed community near shopping, dining, transportation, and major commuter routes, this polished residence delivers the space, privacy, and low-maintenance lifestyle of a luxury townhome.
