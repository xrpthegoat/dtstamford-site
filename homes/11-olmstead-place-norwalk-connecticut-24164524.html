About this home

Set on a quiet cul-de-sac in East Norwalk, this sun-filled Colonial offers a warm, inviting feel with the ideal balance of charm and everyday convenience. The main level features a bright living room with fireplace, a formal dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with pantry, generous cabinet space, and room for daily living. A powder room, dedicated home office, and a library with built-ins-also well-suited as a sunroom-add flexibility for a variety of lifestyles. Hardwood floors run through much of the home. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom features custom closet organizers and ceiling fans. Two additional well-proportioned bedrooms and an updated full bath complete the second level, with hardwood floors beneath the carpeting in the secondary bedrooms and on the staircase. A pull-down attic provides generous storage space, with shelving racks included. The lower level provides additional space for a gym, playroom, or flexible use. A two-car garage provides valuable everyday convenience and storage. Outside, a flat, usable yard and large deck create an easy setting for entertaining or relaxing, with the added privacy of a cul-de-sac location. Recent updates include freshly painted interiors, a 200-amp electrical panel, new dryer, and custom closet built-ins, offering a move-in ready feel. Ideally located less than 1/2 mile to the East Norwalk Metro-North station, this home combines comfort and convenience in one of Norwalk's most accessible locations.
