About this home

Bring your curiosity and your imagination because you just don't see homes like this anymore! This is a hidden Old Hill surprise, a vintage country house built by the artist and illustrator, Karl Godwin, in 1920. Loaded with character and incredible potential, it now needs new owners. Unique custom details, including wavy edge cedar lap siding and impressive stonework welcome visitors on a beautifully landscaped 1.08 acre parcel, which also has room for a pool. Original floors, wooden beams and built-ins set you up for comfort in all four seasons. The property is being sold as is and was recently inspected by the sellers. Private well, oil heat, septic system and generator. Part cottage, part artist's studio, part bird- watcher's lair...this one is incredibly special. An amazing travel-back-in-time experience or a perfect weekend escape with easy access to downtown and all Westport amenities.
