About this home

Welcome to Schooner Cove, where everyday living feels like a waterfront retreat. This move in ready 2 bed, 2 bath corner unit townhouse combines coastal luxury in a convenient location in Stamford's highly sought after Shippan Point neighborhood. The bright, open concept main level flows seamlessly from the kitchen with stainless steel appliances including wine cooler, granite countertops & ample storage into the spacious dining & oversized living area overlooking the water w/ a wood burning fireplace. Step through sliding doors to your private deck w/ views of the courtyard & marina, perfect for morning coffee. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms offer ample closet space one w/ a WIC, offering comfort and privacy. Don't miss the 3rd floor loft, perfect for a home office or den w/ vaulted ceilings and a skylight. New modern wood look vinyl flooring throughout! Schooner Cove residents enjoy resort style amenities including a sparkling pool, boathouse, beautifully landscaped grounds, and inclusive HOA fees that cover heat, hot water, gas, and more. Conveniently located near West Beach, Cummings Beach, Harbor Point restaurants, downtown Stamford, and Metro North, this residence offers the best of both worlds - tranquil waterfront living less than an hour from NYC. Experience the ultimate in coastal sophistication, welcome home to Schooner Cove. Also available for rent.
