About this home

Compo Beach living at its finest! Sleek & sophisticated design meets state of the art technology in this incredible custom built home. Unparalleled level of style & luxury over 3 levels of finished living space with chic modern interiors & high end finishes & the latest in smart home technology. The chef's kitchen with waterfall center island overlooks the living room with 15 ft ceilings & a double sided gas fireplace. Dining room is surrounded by oversized 7ft windows with views of the landscaped yard, luscious hydrangeas, & fully matured arborvitae's for ultimate privacy. There are 2 well appointed bedrooms & bathrooms on the main level. The primary suite is equipped with automated blinds & a generously sized dressing room. The luxurious primary bathroom has radiant heat flooring, a soaking tub, & steam shower. Adjacent to the primary bedroom is an office with custom white birch built ins & laundry room complete the 2nd level. Lower level is complete with a bonus room, temperature controlled wine cellar, full bathroom & access to the showroom style 2 car garage with epoxy flooring that any car enthusiast would appreciate. Brilliant home system control, full 360 degree ring elite security system, 4 zone irrigation system with app controlled by weather or by phone. Full spec list available upon request. Located only moments to both beaches in Compo, town, highways & train for easy access to NYC.
