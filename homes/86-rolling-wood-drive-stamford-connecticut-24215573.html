About this home

Welcome to 86 Rolling Wood, a home where contemporary comfort meets effortless style. Close to town/train, yet tucked away in a quiet neighborhood, this home represents the best of suburban living. From the moment you pull up the freshly resealed horseshoe driveway, it's clear this property has been maintained with an eye for detail and a commitment to quality. Step inside to discover a bright, inviting interior highlighted by a kitchen that truly shines. With a suite of recent upgrades, including gas range, refrigerator, dishwasher, and refinished floors, this space is as functional as it is beautiful. The thoughtful improvements continue throughout the home, including the convenience of a brand-new washer and dryer. Peace of mind comes standard here, thanks to significant mechanical updates. With a newly installed furnace and a new central A/C system, you can enjoy crisp cooling all summer and cozy warmth all winter. The newly floored basement adds additional flex space for office or recreation. The exterior is equally impressive, with flat yard featuring newly installed rear fencing that adds a clean, polished look to the outdoor space. This is a rare opportunity to move into a home where the heavy lifting has already been done for you. This desirable location between 95 and 15 make for seamless commuting as well as proximity to every amenity you can think of. 86 Rolling Wood is ready for its next chapter-come see how easy life can be when every detail is already in place.
