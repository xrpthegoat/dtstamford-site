About this home

11 Roland Avenue is in the vibrant Marvin Beach Association in Historic East Norwalk. Picture coming home in the summer and going for a swim or taking your boat out to enjoy a beautiful sunset. This Association has something to offer people of all ages, tennis for all, a sunset cocktail party for adults, July 4th. parade and picnic, Steak and Lobster Dinner, eligibility for membership in the MBA Yacht Club or just a beach chair to relax in. It is just a few minutes from all the restaurants in SONO. Commuting? It is close to the station and major roadways. If you prefer being close to home relax and enjoy the beautiful back yard patio or sit on the front porch and look towards the Harbor. Don't miss this three bedroom 2.5 bath Colonial as they don't come to market often. Please call listing Broker for information on lower level rec room and full bath.
