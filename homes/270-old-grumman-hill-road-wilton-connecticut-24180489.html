About this home

ALL OFFERS MUST BE SUBMITTED BY 11AM 6/9/2026. THANK YOU! A Rare Gem in a Serene Setting... This thoughtfully renovated South Wilton home is nestled in a quiet neighborhood on a private, sunlit lot. The beautifully landscaped yard creates a peaceful retreat while conveniently close to local amenities. This turn-key home boasts hardwood floors, high ceilings, large windows and natural light throughout. On the main level, the chef-inspired kitchen features an oversized stone island, perfect for gathering and entertaining. The impressive stone fireplace adds warmth and character to the living space. Down the hall is your ensuite primary bedroom and a second bedroom as well as a full bath. The large and light-filled den, currently used as an office, has a 1/2 bath adjacent. Step outside to enjoy the screened-in porch and oversized deck overlooking breathtaking scenery and a large, fenced-in wooded area. Or step onto the back yard patio to relax in nature. The lower level is a fabulous living space, including an expansive mudroom entry, large family room with wood burning fireplace, full bath and additional room with ample closet space. With generous storage, an oversized garage, and numerous updates, this exceptional home combines comfort, privacy, and convenience in a highly desirable location. Minutes away are wonderful restaurants, shopping, theatre, entertainment, train station and schools.
