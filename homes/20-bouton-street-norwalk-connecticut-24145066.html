About this home

Be the first to live in this brand-new construction townhouse rental, offering modern design, flexible living space, and exceptional proximity to town amenities. Located in a vibrant, diverse, and culturally rich area of Norwalk, this recently built home delivers contemporary living with convenience and style. The main level features a bright, open-concept floor plan ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The beautiful kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, generous storage, and a gorgeous center island with seating. Sliding glass doors open to a private patio, perfect for barbecuing or relaxing outdoors. This rental offers three bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms, thoughtfully arranged across multiple levels for privacy and comfort. Both the second and third floors feature spacious landings, ideal for a home office, study area, or additional seating. A standout feature is the oversized second-floor laundry room, complete with custom cabinetry and a utility sink, providing exceptional convenience and storage. The primary suite features a custom walk-in closet with extra storage and a private en-suite bathroom, offering comfort, style, and functionality. Enjoy close proximity to downtown Norwalk, restaurants, shopping, parks, public transportation, and major commuting routes, making this an ideal rental for today's connected lifestyle.
