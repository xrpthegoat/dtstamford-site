About this home

Opportunity awaits in this expanded Cape-style home located in the heart of sought-after Springdale! Set on a spacious and private 0.35-acre lot, this charming property features a fenced-in yard and an attached garage-offering both space and potential. Inside, you'll find 1,830 square feet of living space with 8 rooms, including 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms. Built in 1940, the home offers timeless character and a versatile layout-ideal for buyers looking to customize and add their personal touch. While the home does need some work and is being sold as is, it presents a fantastic opportunity to build equity and create your dream home in a desirable neighborhood. The location is unbeatable-just a short walk to Toquam Magnet Elementary School, the Springdale Train Station, and an array of local restaurants and shops. With great bones, a flexible floor plan, and a prime location, this is your chance to invest in one of Stamford's most vibrant communities. Come envision the possibilities!
