About this home

Step back in time while enjoying the comforts of the present in this beautifully renovated antique home! This adorable antique offers living areas that effortlessly blend old-world charm with contemporary design, perfect for hosting gatherings or quiet evenings by the fire. Period-appropriate details such as exposed beams, wide-plank wood floors and 2 vintage fireplaces seamlessly blend with todays amenities including vaulted ceilings, eat in kitchen with s/s appliances & quartz counter tops. Main level en-suite primary bedroom, updated 1.5 bath plus 2 additional cozy bedrooms on upper floor. Level .48 acre features slate patio, detached 2 car garage, beautiful stone walls & "petite barn", perfect for whatever you can imagine. Additional updates include: Central air, city water, natural gas & newer roof all ensuring modern comforts & convenience. Built in c.1752, this home is a piece of living history, offering a glimpse into the past!
