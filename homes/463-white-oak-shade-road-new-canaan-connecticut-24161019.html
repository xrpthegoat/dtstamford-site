About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained four-bedroom residence in the heart of New Canaan, where timeless New England colonial architecture is thoughtfully paired with modern comfort and refined living. Privately set on a picturesque property, this inviting home offers an ideal balance of entertaining spaces, everyday functionality, and unparalleled convenience. The sun-filled main level is designed for both comfortable living and sophisticated entertaining, featuring a spacious family room, an elegant formal dining room, and a thoughtfully updated kitchen appointed with high-end appliances, expansive counter space, and a breakfast area overlooking the deck and backyard. The first floor office makes working from home a breeze and the handy laundry room located next to the kitchen create even more ease. An inviting living room provides a warm and welcoming retreat, while oversized windows throughout the home bathe each room in natural light. Upstairs, the serene primary suite serves as a private sanctuary, complete with ample California Closets designed space and a beautifully recently renovated ensuite five piece bath. Three additional well-proportioned bedrooms offer exceptional flexibility for family, guests, or a secondary dedicated home office on the upper level.
