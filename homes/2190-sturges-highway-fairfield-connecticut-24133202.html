About this home

Welcome to Pepperidge Acres - a unique enclave of four homes tucked away on a private cul-de-sac ideally located on the Westport line. Conveniently to both Westport & Fairfield town amenities, this classic home sits peacefully on two level acres, offering a serene and picturesque setting. Step into the grand living room featuring a vaulted ceiling and a dramatic floor-to-ceiling, double-sided fireplace. Flowing into the state-of-the-art kitchen featuring custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and an oversized island that opens to a cozy sitting area. Off the living areas enter to the primary bedroom en-suite, additional bedroom and updated full hall bath. Continuing to the bright and inviting lower level, you'll find an open family room filled with natural light. Off the family room is another bedroom featuring a comfortable sitting area and a custom walk-in closet. A full bath and laundry area with access to the garage completes the lower level. The private backyard features a fenced-in pool area and ample open space-perfect for play, relaxation, or even a friendly soccer match. This prime location offers the best of both worlds-privacy and accessibility-just minutes from both Westport and Fairfield trains, shopping, I-95, and the Merritt.The current owners have cherished this home for over 30 years and are excited to pass it on to the next lucky owner.
