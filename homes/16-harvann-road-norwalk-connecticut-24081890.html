About this home

Bright, Well maintained 4 Bedroom, 1.5 Bath Colonial on cul-de-sac in the Broad River area of Norwalk. Eat-in Kitchen with breakfast bar has pass through to the Formal Dining Room. Mechanical Room off Kitchen with furnace/A/C and newer water heater. Laundry Room and Kitchen both have access to the 3 Season Sunroom which is good for entertaining. Oversized 1 car Garage. Newer Roof and Windows have Lifetime Factory Warranty. Central A/C, Oil Heat (above ground oil tank in garage). Second level has all hardwood flooring and 4 Bedrooms (each with double closets) and full hall bath. Insulated pull down attic for extra storage. Convenient to shopping, Route 7, Merritt Parkway and I-95. Ready for you to just move in!
