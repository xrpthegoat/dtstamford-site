About this home

Immaculate Cape that is charming and bright with an open floor plan. Foyer to Spacious living room with hardwood floors. Formal dining room with a hardwood floors and a closet. The bright white kitchen has a glass tile backsplash and stainless steel appliances open to the Sunroom/breakfast nook with vaulted ceilings and skylights & sliders to the deck. There is a first bedroom and bath. The 2nd floor has a spacious master bedroom with hardwood floors & 2 closets. The hall bathroom is tastefully updated with granite counters, frameless shower door and heated towel racks. A large 3rd bedroom completes the second floor. The lower level has a finished den/office, storage area and door to the garage. This adorable home has a beautiful level fenced in yard. Convenient location in Springdale in walking distance to stores, restaurants and the Springdale train station.
