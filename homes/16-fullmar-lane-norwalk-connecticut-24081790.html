About this home

Impressive and stylish 4-5 Bedroom Contemporary by New Canaan and Darien border including a separate home office with own entry. This home uniquely designed & remodeled in 2001 combines charm and character with all of today's amenities. Formal foyer welcomes you as you enter to an expansive two-story Living Room w/floor to ceiling field stone fireplace (currently a wood burning stove) and walls of windows bringing the outside in. Open floor plan featuring a formal dining room, eat-in kitchen with abundance of cabinets, large center island, eat-in nook, desk area and doors leading to the deck & private patio. Main level with attractive in-lay hardwood floors adding elegance to the home includes 2 additional rooms: den or guest bedroom with slider to rear property and home office with private entry. Spacious upper level with an impressive primary bedroom suite with second field stone fireplace, walk-in closets and a stunning bath with whirlpool & separate shower. Four comfortable bedrooms and laundry room. Full finished lower level is a perfect rec room with wet bar- great and fun room to enjoy and entertain family and friends. This home provides ample of closets and storage areas throughout. Special attention from fireplaces, moldings, C/A, Central Vacuum and more. The exterior home with stone veneer & wood siding, architectural designed shingles, attractive driveway with pavers is protected by handsome stone walls & shrubberies which provides total privacy.
