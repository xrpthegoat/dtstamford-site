Stunning Colonial in Prime Norwalk Location! Welcome to this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial located in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods. This home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience, with spacious living areas and thoughtful updates throughout. Step inside to a bright and inviting layout featuring generous room sizes, excellent flow, and plenty of natural light.
