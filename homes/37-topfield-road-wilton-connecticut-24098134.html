About this home

Welcome to 37 Topfield Road - a stunning 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath contemporary home set on 2.35 beautifully landscaped, level acres in desirable South Wilton. From the circular driveway to the soaring ceilings and dramatic architectural details, this home is designed to impress. Expansive windows, skylights, and an open floor plan flood the interior with natural light, highlighting rich wood floors and two inviting fireplaces. The chef's kitchen is a true showpiece, featuring sleek European SieMatic cabinetry, a massive granite island, a 6-burner Viking stove, an oversized Sub-Zero refrigerator/freezer, and a generous walk-in pantry - ideal for entertaining and everyday living. Walls of windows and triple sliding glass doors connect the main living areas to an expansive granite patio and a gorgeous heated in-ground pool, all framed by lush perennial gardens. The flexible layout includes an in-law/guest suite with a full granite bath. Convenient 3 car attached garage, this property offers excellent commuter access with the perfect balance of privacy, space, and sophistication. There have been many upgrades in recent years that include energy efficient solar panels, newer windows, an updated front porch and patio, and new gutter system installed last year. This is a rare opportunity to own a refined and versatile home in one of Wilton's most sought-after neighborhoods.
