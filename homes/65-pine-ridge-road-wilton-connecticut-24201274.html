About this home

Exceptional Winter Rental Offering Space, Comfort & Privacy. Spacious and beautifully maintained seasonal winter rental set on attractive landscaped grounds in a peaceful residential setting. The bright, open interior offers hardwood floors, abundant natural light and multiple living and entertaining spaces. The large eat-in kitchen features extensive counter space, center island and adjoining dining room. A dramatic two-story great room is a standout with soaring windows, wet bar and stone fireplace. A home office, formal living room, pantry and laundry complete the main level. The spacious primary suite features a fireplace, large walk-in closet and light-filled bath with soaking tub, separate shower and double vanity. Three additional bedrooms, two full baths and a large multipurpose room complete the upper level. The finished lower level features 10-foot ceilings, new luxury vinyl plank flooring, fireplace, renovated home gym, 5th bedroom and full bath-ideal for guests or extended stays. Enjoy the privacy of expansive grounds, patio and in-ground pool. An attached 3-car garage and ample driveway parking add convenience. An exceptional opportunity for a comfortable, well-appointed winter retreat with plenty of room to live, work and entertain. Small dog will need owner approval
