About this home

Welcome to 140 Ponus Avenue, a sun-filled 4-bedroom split-level in desirable West Norwalk. This meticulously maintained 2,200+ sq ft home sits on a peaceful 0.36-acre lot with a fenced backyard overlooking beautiful woods, creating a private retreat. Enter through the central foyer and step into an impressive open floor plan connecting the living room with fireplace and skylights to the dining area, and completely renovated kitchen featuring butcher block countertops, center island with Bosch dishwasher, pull-out pantry cabinets with oak doors, and 2021 Bosch three-door refrigerator. The family room has cathedral ceilings and more skylights and flows from the kitchen seamlessly. It opens to the yard with a large wall of windows and a slider to a 2018 cedar deck. The upstairs has 3 bedrooms and another updated full bath. With a completely unique layout, two lower levels expand your living space with two recreation rooms or a fourth bedroom, a full bathroom with slate flooring, oversized walk-in shower, and Dornbracht fixtures, laundry room/bathroom, and ample storage. This home has been loved and upgraded extensively: 2017 roof, 2019 C/A, 2021 boiler and hot water system with zoned heating, 2023 Andersen windows, 2022 electric car charger, 2024 driveway work, 2025 new fence, and 2026 refinished floors and fresh paint throughout (and more). The circular driveway and extra basement storage add convenience. A rare find that's move-in ready close to everything you may need!
