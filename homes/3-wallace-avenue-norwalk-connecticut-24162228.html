About this home

Rare opportunity to rent in the costal community of Marvin Beach. Just steps from the neighborhood's "Second Beach," Norwalk Harbor, and Long Island Sound, this cottage offers sunset views and a peaceful coastal retreat. This is a rare opportunity to rent in one of Connecticut's most charming coastal enclaves. This charming 1920 Craftsman home is tucked into the heart of the historic Marvin Beach Association, a 120-home seaside village in East Norwalk. Beautifully renovated, preserving original details, this home is filled with light, with water views from its inviting all-season sunporch and front yard. Hardwood floors are complemented by built-in cabinetry, original fixtures, and a signature 1920s door chime.
