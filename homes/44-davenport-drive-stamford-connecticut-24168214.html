About this home

This fully renovated and expanded home offers over 3,300 SF of high-quality finishes, including custom millwork, tile, and closet systems. Enjoy seasonal water views and multiple outdoor spaces to enjoy. The primary suite features a vaulted ceiling and sitting area. The eat-in kitchen opens to a terrace overlooking a fully fenced backyard. Energy-efficient with updated HVAC systems and insulation, the home also offers abundant storage, including an oversized 1-car garage. Eligible tenant must have a minimum credit score of 750. Conveniently located near Stamford and Old Greenwich train stations. The home backs to a 12-acre coastal gem including Soundwaters Marina, sports facilities, and playgrounds.
