About this home

LUX CONDO GREENWICH-Downtown Greenwich at your fingertips! Rare first floor 2 bedroom and 2 full ensuite bathrooms and 1/2 bath pristine condo built in 2018. Do not miss this duplex with beautiful hardwood floors and high ceilings throughout this thoughtful, sun drenched open floor plan. Large great room leads into the dining area and attached gourmet kitchen with 5 burner gas range, coffee bar/wine bar, dishwasher, 3 dimensional subway tile, and beautiful stone countertops. Abundance of cabinets and attached rear door leads to a mudroom/laundry area and extends to an amazing rear covered deck for extended living space and relaxation, guest entertainment. The spacious primary bedroom provides respite from the day with a large ensuite bath with double sinks and multiple custom California closets. Private door in primary bedroom also leads to deck retreat. Additional secondary bedroom with oversized custom closets and ensuite bath as well. Pull into a private driveway and park in your secure garage. Large basement for extra storage is an excellent use of space! Wired for Generator. Enjoy the fantastic location steps away from Greenwich Avenue shops, Equinox Club, train station, and restaurants.
