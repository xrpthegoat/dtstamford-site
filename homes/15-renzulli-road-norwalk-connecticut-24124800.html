About this home

Beautifully updated Hi-Ranch home on the Norwalk/Westport line, tucked away on a private gated cul-de-sac road for extra peace and privacy. This 2,766 square foot spacious and flexible home features four oversized bedrooms-including one so large it's currently used as a living room-three stylish baths, and plenty of options for an in-law suite, home office, gym, or playroom. The open main floor is filled with natural light and offers inviting living and family rooms, a relaxing primary suite, an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, plus the convenience of a main-level laundry room. Gleaming hardwood floors run throughout. Step outside to enjoy your choice of an upper deck or lower patio, perfect for morning coffee or evening gatherings. The fenced yard and cozy fire pit area are ideal for entertaining or unwinding. Set on an end lot overlooking a peaceful brook, this home offers a rare sense of tranquility while being just a short walk to the Westport train station, Saugatuck Village shops, and great restaurants. With quick access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, Historic SONO, and the SONO Collection Mall, everything you need is close by. Move-in ready and full of possibilities-discover the perfect blend of comfort, space, and location in this welcoming home.
