About this home

Set discreetly at the end of a long drive and screened by mature landscaping, this exceptional Hillspoint residence offers a surprising sense of privacy in one of Westport's most convenient coastal settings. Just moments from Longshore Club Park, Compo Beach, town and train, it perfectly blends luxury, lifestyle and accessibility. Inside, soaring ceilings and sun-filled spaces create a light, airy flow enhanced by high-end finishes throughout. Designed for everyday living and effortless entertaining, the expansive chef's kitchen with double refrigerators, dishwashers and ovens anchors the home, opening to inviting dining and gathering spaces. Upstairs, a private family wing with its own library and a finished third-floor retreat provides flexible space for study, play or creative pursuits. The walk-out lower level elevates the experience with a laser home theater, media lounge, gym and game room for year-round enjoyment. Outdoors, a heated saltwater pool and spa, pergola and sweeping lawn framed by roses and grasses create a private resort setting. Savant smart home technology offers integrated music inside and out, lighting controls, automated shades, full irrigation, landscape lighting and comprehensive security, all managed from your phone. Peaceful, refined and unexpectedly serene, this home lives like a retreat while placing you at the center of everything Westport has to offer.
