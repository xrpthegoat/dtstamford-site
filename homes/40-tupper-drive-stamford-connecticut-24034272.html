About this home

Welcome to this charming, beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 2-bath cape in the highly coveted Cove neighborhood! This home offers access to your own private beach through the Neighborhood Association, or walk just minutes to enjoy Cummings Park and Beach. Step inside to discover spacious living areas downstairs, perfect for gatherings and entertaining. The home features bright rooms with stunning hardwood floors and central air for your comfort. Beautiful open kitchen includes state of the art stainless steel appliances, including a gas range and granite countertops with a connected dining room. The recently renovated bathrooms, completed in 2024, add a modern touch to this classic home. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced-in backyard, great for various outdoor activities on the level lawn. The lush garden, equipped with an inground sprinkler system, provides a peaceful retreat, or kick your feet up and relax around the firepit. Newly installed solar panels offer energy efficiency and cost savings. Experience convenient access to parks, beaches, tennis courts, and a carefree lifestyle. Located minutes from the train station, I-95, and downtown Stamford and just a quick walk away from the supermarket and some of the finest restaurants Stamford has to offer! Don't miss out on this exceptional rental opportunity!
