About this home

Welcome to Nor-West, a beautiful property featuring flat, large landscapes, mature trees, and peaceful benches--it's perfect for long walks. This bright and spacious one-bedroom apartment is a fantastic opportunity at an unbeatable price. Located at the front of a complex that offers a pool, clubhouse, large courtyards, and ample guest parking, the location is ideal. HOA fees cover heat and hot water, making your expenses mostly fixed. With one-level living on the second floor, enjoy extended daylight through large, newer windows. Upon entering, you'll find a small hallway with a closet, leading to a bright kitchen and a spacious living room with enough space for a dining area. A large sliding glass door opens onto a generous back balcony, perfect for relaxing or morning coffees. The primary bedroom is sizable, featuring a walk-in closet and cozy wall-to-wall carpeting. This unit is perfectly situated in a highly desirable complex, just moments from everything. The new Norwalk High School will be right across the street, with additional schools nearby, as well as fantastic restaurants, Stew Leonard's, the Westport train station, and more. Don't miss out on this prime opportunity-it won't last long!
