About this home

Welcome home to this adorable 2-3 bedroom, 2 full bath cape on Valley Rd., a super convenient, quiet neighborhood located between Greens Farms and Saugatuck Village. The sunny first floor features a spacious living room with fireplace, family/dining room with windows overlooking the backyard and access to the patio. The bright, spacious kitchen, full bath, bedroom/office, and laundry complete the main level. The upper level has 2 large bedrooms and a full bath with double sinks and a tub/shower. This home is close to shopping, restaurants and walking distance to Longshore, Compo Beach, and all that Saugatuck has to offer, as well as the train and I-95 for an easy commute. Garage on property is for owner's personal use.
