About this home

Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in the prestigious Old Hill neighborhood, this light-filled five-bedroom Colonial offers privacy, space, and exceptional convenience in one of Westport's most desirable locations. Set on a beautiful, level and dry acre, the property is approached by a long private driveway, creating a peaceful and secluded setting with ample room for outdoor play and enjoyment. This lovely home features hardwood floors throughout and a warm, welcoming layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A spacious living room opens to a pretty dining room with built-in cabinetry, while the open flow continues into a generous eat-in kitchen highlighted by a wood-burning fireplace. A large family room overlooks the backyard and connects to a stylish mudroom and attached two-car garage. The upper level offers five bedrooms, including a primary suite with walk-in closet and en-suite bath. An efficient heating and hot water system helps with utility costs. Perfectly located just minutes from vibrant downtown Westport with its upscale shopping, delightful coffee shops and eclectic selection of fabulous restaurants. Also convenient to the incredible town amenities of Longshore Club Park and golf course, Compo Beach, the Westport Weston YMCA, and just 2 miles to Metro-North train service to Grand Central. This home offers the ideal combination of comfort, privacy, and the exceptional Westport lifestyle. AVAILABLE FOR ONE YEAR ONLY or to end school yr 2027
