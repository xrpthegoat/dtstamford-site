About this home

Wonderful opportunity to rent a furnished or unfurnished, updated four-bedroom Tudor with bluestone patio and beautiful, private backyard on a desirable road near schools, shops and trains. Spacious interior blends original details with modern amenities including wood floors and four remodeled full baths. Welcoming front porch leads into living room with wood stove; dining room, chef's kitchen with Bertazzoni range, patio access; family room, full bath and sunroom/office with sliders to patio. Primary suite with dressing room and fabulous bath with tub, shower and balcony; bedroom with loft, bedroom and full hall bath comprise second floor. Lower level provides bedroom suite with own entrance, laundry/mudroom and attached garage.
