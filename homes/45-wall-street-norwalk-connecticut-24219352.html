About this home

Rare investment opportunity in the heart of Norwalk's historic Wall Street District! Located in one of Downtown Norwalk's most up-and-coming areas, this mixed-use commercial property offers tremendous potential for investors and business owners alike. The ground level features four storefronts, two of which can possibly offer kitchen installations, creating exciting possibilities for restaurant or cafe concepts. The second and third floors comprise 10 residential apartment units, featuring a mix of studios, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom layouts. In a vibrant downtown setting surrounded by local businesses, restaurants, and ongoing revitalization, this property offers a unique opportunity to capitalize on the Wall Street District's continued growth and transformation. A rare chance to own a substantial mixed-use property in one of Norwalk's evolving downtown neighborhoods!
