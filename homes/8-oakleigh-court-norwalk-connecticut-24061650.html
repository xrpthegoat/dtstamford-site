About this home

Nestled in a serene cul-de-sac in the picturesque seaside town of Rowayton, this turnkey Coastal Colonial is a rare gem. From the moment you step through the door, you'll sense the warmth and charm of this exceptional home with 4700+ sq.ft. of living space. The airy entry hall, soaring ceilings, & abundant natural light create a welcoming & sophisticated ambiance. Designed w/ both style & function in mind, the main floor features a gracious living room, an elegant dining room perfect for hosting unforgettable gatherings, and a spacious EIK with counter seating and room for a breakfast table. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the family room, complete w/ a cozy fireplace. Sliding doors open to a backyard oasis, boasting a deck and a stone patio ideal for al fresco dining and relaxation. A thoughtfully designed mudroom w/ ample storage & a convenient powder room complete the first level. Upstairs, the Primary Suite is a true retreat, featuring vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, walk-in closet, and spa-like bathroom with dual-sink vanity. 3 additional well-appointed BRs, each with its own bathroom, & a laundry room provide both comfort and convenience. The top floor offers incredible versatility, w/ a generous space for a playroom, home gym, or media room, alongside a 5th BR or private home office, and another full bathroom. This home's location is unparalleled offering both privacy & just a short stroll from Rowayton's vibrant town center w/ restaurants, shops, and even the beach!
