About this home

RECENTLY UPDATED!!! Well maintained charming 3-4 Bedroom, 2 Full Bath rental available for October 1, 2026. The location is ideal for both convenience and access to Westport amenities some of which includes: downtown Westport shopping, Compo Beach, Longshore Club Park, Metro North Train Station, top rated schools and I-95. Highly desirable neighborhood. The open floor plan and abundance of natural light are definite highlights, creating a welcoming atmosphere throughout the home. White Kitchen has newish stainless steel appliances, new countertops and hardware that opens up into the Dining area and the Living Room. There are 3 Bedrooms on the second floor. On the third floor there is an additional room that can serve as a Bedroom or a private Office based on the tenants needs. The lower level Family Room is large and sunny with a full bath, newish vanity and shower. Hardwood floors in all rooms except for the lower level. The screened-in porch has a new tiled floor. The porch and deck offer great spaces for relaxation and entertainment, while the flat spacious backyard adds even more versatility for outdoor activities. Central Air. Lease term is one year or longer.
