About this home

When you need peace, quiet, tranquility and light in wonderful Westport, this charming vintage cottage, once the studio of premier Westport artist, George H. Wright, checks those boxes and more. Enter into a very large room with gas fireplace, vaulted ceiling, and extensive built-ins. It has ample space for dining and living. An enormous window, with precious north light, commands your attention and immediately frames the gorgeous and manicured private property where you can cultivate a garden and enjoy a tranquil pond. The ensuite bedroom, is filled with light and its newly renovated bath has a huge walk-in shower. A rare offering for an artist, writer, or someone seeking a peaceful and inspirational life.
