About this home

Welcome to your next rental home! This beautifully maintained 3 bedroom, 2 full bath Cape style home offers 1,252 sq ft of living space on just under an acre of land. The spacious second floor primary suite is completely separate from the rest of the home and features a walk in closet/dressing room and a private ensuite bath with a pocket door for added privacy. Enjoy recently updated kitchens and bathrooms with stainless steel appliances and stunning tile finishes. The large front yard and extended driveway provide both privacy and ample guest parking. The oversized under house garage is ideal for extra storage, hobbies, or even a home gym, while the backyard tool shed offers additional space for your gear. Conveniently located just steps from local parks and restaurants, less than a mile from the Merritt Parkway, and only an hour from New York City this rental has everything you need to feel right at home!
