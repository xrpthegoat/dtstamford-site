About this home

Welcome to 10 Carissa Lane, a privately situated home on a cul-de-sac street not far from the center of town. With 4,000sf of living space, the home has a perfect flow for entertaining. A high-end kitchen that opens to the family room and dining room is the heart of the home. The living room, outfitted with a second fireplace, offers additional space to retreat. The primary bedroom, with its own bath overlooking the peaceful yard, is joined by 3 additional bedrooms and a full bath on the 2nd floor. A bonus room and full bath on the same floor allow for a lot of flexibility. A walk-out lower level provides ample space to play, work, exercise - outfitted with another full bath and kitchenette. The home sits on over 1-acre of property that offers entertainment during all seasons. In warmer weather you can gather on a recently renovated, expansive deck off the family room/kitchen that has an outdoor TV & sound system, or practice your swing on a putting green. In the winter, a quiet pond off in the distance allows for ice skating. Don't miss the opportunity to see this home - with an abundance of updates - on one of Greenwich's most special streets.
