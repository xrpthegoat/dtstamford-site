About this home

Best & Final Offers due by 12 noon, Monday June 1st.Tucked away in the heart of sought-after Silvermine, this beautifully updated ranch w/NEW ROOF offers the ease of one-level living w/ the feel of a private retreat. Surrounded by mature trees, lush lawns, curated planting beds, & a sparkling inground pool, the property invites you to slow down, unwind, & enjoy every season at home. Inside, sun-filled spaces and an effortless open layout create a warm, welcoming atmosphere designed for both everyday comfort & entertaining. The eat-in kitchen features NEW Stainless Appliances & a generous dining space w/sliders leading to the deck for easy indoor-outdoor living. Stylish formal living & dining areas pay homage to the home's mid-century roots w/distinctive 1960s-inspired details, including a striking stone veneer fireplace wall & architectural metal railing room dividers. The private bedroom wing offers a serene primary suite with a beautifully renovated bath, along w/2 additional spacious bedrooms & an equally stylish updated hall bath. Downstairs, more than 600SF of finished space provides endless flexibility plus 2000SF unfinished of space. An oversized 2 car garage adds exceptional convenience, while a third full bath off the side entrance is perfectly positioned for poolside guests. Benefit from lower utility costs w/energy-saving solar panels. Host summer gatherings, enjoy quiet mornings by the pool, or simply embracing the beauty of Silvermine living.
