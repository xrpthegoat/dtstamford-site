About this home

Charming, renovated Noroton Heights colonial, with an easy walk to train, dining and shopping, awaits! Three bedrooms, renovated bathroom with double sinks, and a walk-up attic (lending itself to expansion possibilities) complete the second floor, while a newly finished lower level with laundry and storage provides extra room to play, work or relax. Great street, great location and move-in ready!
