About this home

Multiple offers. reviewing offers with Trustee on Monday 3:00pm 10/20. Charming 1940s bungalow with endless potential! This solidly built cottage feels larger than its square footage and is filled with character. The entry foyer opens to a spacious living room featuring a wood-burning fireplace and built-ins. The kitchen flows into the dining room, and the vintage black-and-white bath adds timeless appeal. Hardwood floors are present in the living room, foyer, and both bedrooms. There seems to be hardwood in the dining room based on the removeal of a small peice of tile, however, we are can't say for certain. A full walk-up attic offers great expansion possibilities. All rooms have been freshly painted; radiators will be painted by list date. The basement includes a finished room of approximately 216 sq. ft., a large storage area with workbench, and a laundry room with sink and hook-ups. Additional highlights include a 3-season porch, attached 1-car garage, newer furnace, and a level yard. This estate property is being sold as-is with no disclosures-an excellent opportunity to make this sturdy, well-built home your own! More photography coming. Active date is October 15 As-Is. Estate No disclosures. Probate is all set.
