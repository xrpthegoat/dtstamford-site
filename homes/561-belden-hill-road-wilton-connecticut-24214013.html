About this home

Welcome to 561 Belden Hill Road! A beautiful, move-in-ready 4 bedroom 3 bath home set on 2+ acres of beautifully landscaped property in desirable South Wilton with CITY WATER. Unique combination of a convenient commuting location with generous living spaces and an incredible flat, private yard. The perfect balance of everyday comfort, entertaining, and outdoor enjoyment. Inside, gracious well-proportioned rooms with plenty of space for family and friends. The eat-in kitchen opens to a bright expansive sunroom with peaceful views of the surrounding grounds. The elegant living room with custom built-ins and gas fireplace flows into the dining room. A comfortable family room with gas fireplace, along with a full bath and laundry room. Upstairs you will find 4 generously sized bedrooms including the primary bedroom suite w/walk-in closet, and 3 other bedrooms and full bath providing comfortable accommodations for family and guests. The lower level offers a spacious bonus room with endless possibilities for a playroom, home office, media room, or additional recreation space. The outdoor setting is truly exceptional. An attached two-car garage is complemented by an oversized detached garage with a loft. Ideally situated in South Wilton, this home offers easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, shopping, town amenities, and Wilton's award-winning schools. Enjoy the best of suburban living with a convenient commute and a peaceful, private setting to call home.
