About this home

Welcome to this exquisite 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath Colonial home located in the highly sought-after Shippan neighborhood. This sun-drenched residence is truly an entertainer's dream, featuring a gorgeous heated gunite pool surrounded by a meticulously curated backyard adorned with hydrangeas and rose bushes. Freshly repainted with refinished hardwood floors, the main level boasts a cozy living room complete with a wood-burning fireplace and west-facing sun exposure. The sunroom, with its vaulted ceilings, offers serene backyard views, while the library features cathedral ceilings and a convenient half bath. The dining room opens through sliding doors to a covered skylight patio, perfect for alfresco dining. The expansive kitchen is equipped with new stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, & an inviting island bar that seamlessly connects to the eat-in kitchen, which includes a breakfast bar & direct access to the outdoor bluestone patio. The family room impresses with 9+ foot ceilings, a gas log fireplace, & a spacious bar area with kitchen amenities, all enhanced by pocket doors for added privacy. The first-floor primary suite provides direct access to the backyard & pool, complete with a luxurious en suite bathroom and laundry. Upstairs, the second primary suite features an en suite bathroom and a walk-in closet, complemented by two additional bedrooms and a full hallway bathroom. The full basement offers endless opportunities.
