About this home

Nestled behind a butterfly garden and privet hedge, on one of the most coveted cul-de-sac streets in the heart of Rowayton, discover 3 Pond. This very special antique home has been sensitively expanded and updated to blend historical charm with all of the modern conveniences. The light-filled chef's kitchen features top-of-the-line stainless appliances, a fireplace, breakfast room, and French doors to the outdoor courtyard. The perfect setup for indoor/outdoor entertaining. Enjoy alfresco meals amidst mature Hinoki Cyprus and Kwanzan cherry trees. A formal dining room with bay window connects to the inviting living room, both overlooking the wrap-around screened porch. Adjacent to the kitchen, a spacious family room with full wall of sliders leads to the terrace, covered by a professionally installed tent and chandelier. Step outside and discover the fully fenced, lush backyard-a private oasis in the heart of all the action. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms plus a charming office in the four-window turret. Two bedrooms feature views of Five Mile River. The primary suite is a sanctuary with a fireplace, coffee bar, vaulted ceilings, and spacious en-suite bathroom. Don't miss this rare treasure in an idyllic coastal setting. Walk to all town restaurants, boats, beaches, and school.
