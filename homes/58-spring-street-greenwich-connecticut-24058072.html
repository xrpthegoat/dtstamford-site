About this home

Location is key for this in-town recently constructed 4 bedroom 3 1/2 bathroom open lay-out smart home. Large elevator makes packages and grocery shopping a breeze in this 4 story modern gem. Enter into the large great-room/living room with soaring ceilings, oversized chandelier and inviting fireplace. Stunning chef's kitchen is outfitted with Leicht Cabinetry and Miele Appliances. Upstairs the master bathroom includes large soaker tub and is finished with heated floors for cozy winter soaks. Utilize the large room as a gym, studio, office, playroom, whatever suits your needs. Lower level mudroom and large one car garage with plenty of storage space and 4 additional exterior off street parking spaces make coming and going a breeze. Ask regarding pet restrictions.
