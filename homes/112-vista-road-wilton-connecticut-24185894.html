About this home

Located moments from Wilton Town Center, this fully reimagined 6-bedroom, 5.2-bath residence blends timeless character with modern luxury. Set on more than two pristine acres with a designated pool site and overlooking the Silvermine River, the home has been extensively renovated, showcasing exceptional craftsmanship and thoughtful design throughout. The interior features 9-foot ceilings, Brazilian cherry floors on the main level, red oak upstairs, and custom millwork. Designer finishes, modern lighting, and integrated smart-home technology complement a spacious layout ideal for entertaining and everyday living. The lower level offers two large rooms, each with walk-out access, a fireplace, a full bath, and an additional room that could serve as a bedroom, providing flexible space for nanny or in-law use, guest quarters, recreation, or a private home office. Bespoke cabinetry, premium fixtures, and top-tier appliances elevate each room. Additional highlights include commercial-grade mechanical systems, a Kohler true full-house 48 KW generator, advanced home technology and security, and a lifetime shingle roof with warranty and copper flashing. Outdoors, refreshed landscaping, new plantings, a mahogany deck and porch, and upgraded hardscaping create a private, inviting setting for gatherings or quiet enjoyment.
