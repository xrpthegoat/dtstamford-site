About this home

Perfectly situated overlooking the Rippowam River in Westover, this spacious mid-century Cape Cod has been thoughtfully updated throughout. The bright, modern interior features an open floor plan, abundant natural light, recessed lighting, two beautiful fireplaces, and bamboo and acacia hardwood floors throughout. The renovated kitchen features granite countertops with waterfall edges and high-end appliances, including a built-in Miele coffee maker. Step outside to an expansive patio and sanctuary-like backyard retreat featuring a large koi pond, outdoor kitchen, and natural gas grill, creating the perfect setting for entertaining or simply relaxing at home. Newly renovated bathrooms include a spa-like primary bath with a walk-in shower and double vanity. Generous closets provide plenty of storage, while two separate entrances add flexibility. Solar panels help keep electricity costs low, while public water, public sewer, and natural gas add everyday convenience. The location is hard to beat, just 10 minutes to Metro-North and minutes from Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, downtown Stamford, restaurants, shopping, parks, and everyday conveniences. Ideally situated between I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, offering easy access to New York City while enjoying the privacy, peaceful surroundings, and natural beauty of Westover.
