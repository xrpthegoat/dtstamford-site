About this home

BEST AND FINAL OFFERS DUE MONDAY JUNE 1, 2026 @ 6PM. Ideally located in the Wolfpit neighborhood adjacent to the Westport border, this 4-bedroom 2 full, 2 half bath Colonial blends timeless character with comfortable everyday living and exceptional convenience. Set on .53 acres with mature trees, a circular driveway, and expansive patio space with a new hot tub designed for effortless indoor/outdoor entertaining, the home offers a warm and welcoming layout filled with natural light, hardwood floors, thoughtful updates, and custom millwork throughout. A gracious foyer opens to an office and dining room, while the heart of the home is the spacious open-concept kitchen, family room, and music room. The country kitchen features stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinetry, a coffee/beverage bar, flowing seamlessly into the inviting family room anchored by a rustic stone fireplace. Upstairs, four generously sized bedrooms provide peaceful retreats, including a spacious primary suite with a walk-in closet and en-suite bath featuring a steam shower and soaking tub. The lower level offers an opportunity for additional living space. Enjoy easy access to Westport and Norwalk train stations, beaches, shopping, dining, and commuter routes from this desirable and well-connected location.
