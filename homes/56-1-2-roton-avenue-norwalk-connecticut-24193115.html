About this home

Completely renovated, perfectly located Rowayton cottage, thoughtfully designed by Penny Glassmeyer with high-end finishes. Tucked behind the primary residence with a dedicated entrance, the main floor of the home features a renovated kitchen, open living area, half bath, and dedicated laundry room. Upstairs, the sunlit bedroom with en suite bath provides a private retreat. Additional highlights include a private backyard terrace, dedicated parking spot, all utilities included, and an unbeatable location - just across from Farm Creek and a short walk to Bayley Beach and Rowayton village. A rare opportunity to enjoy high-end living with coastal charm. Flex terms.
