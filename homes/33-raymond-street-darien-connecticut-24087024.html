About this home

Sunlit Colonial with extra high ceilings and great light on .36 landscaped acres is set up high on a quiet street near Tokeneke and Rowayton. Professional decorating is apparent throughout the whole house. Double height entryway unfolds to formal living and dining rooms boasting 10' ceilings and venetian plaster walls. Updated sunlit kitchen / family room with breakfast area opens to lovely terrace and yard. Primary suite with luxury bathroom and walk in closets. En suite double bedroom with tray ceiling and 2 additional double bedrooms that share a hall bath. Finished lower level with high ceilings is perfect playroom and exercise room. Cedar closet and storage closets galore finish off the LL. Updated systems, full house generator, 2 car garage. Don't miss this wonderful home!
