About this home

Welcome to 70ClintonAvenue,com - the latest offering by multiple HOBI award winning builder; CCO Habitats. Situated on over 2.5 acres, this home has an elegant, modern vibe to it, with many unique design elements. Finished on three levels, the home is wider; not taller, allowing us to have both an office and guest room on the first floor. A second floor has a second designated office, complete with separate entrance and wet bar. Ten foot ceilings on the first floor as well as oversized European tilting windows add to the feeling of spaciousness. A 20 x 40 salt water gunite pool and granite patio is already installed, but you also have room for a tennis court - or paddle, pickle, basketball or an ADU if you want. Yes, there's a generator and yes, there's an EV outlet in the three car garage. Plus - it's ready for you now!
