About this home

Welcome to this first floor, garden ranch 2-bedroom apartment nestled in the sought-after East Norwalk area, within a lovely four-family home. Enjoy the convenience of being just a short stroll from the East Norwalk train station, Vet's Park, Calf Pasture Beach, and the vibrant offerings of SoNo. The kitchen has been updated with quartz counters, and new hardwood floors, and hardwood floors adorn most of the space. Vaulted ceilings and lots of windows make this bright apartment seem even more spacious. In this inviting space, you'll only need to cover the gas bill and renter's insurance-while the landlord takes care of electric. With good credit required for consideration, you can feel secure in your new home. For your convenience, a dedicated washer and dryer are located in the basement, and parking is available for one car in the driveway, with additional options on East Ave or Cottage Street. Please note, no pets are allowed. Heat/electric paid by landlord! Tenant pays gas/cable including hot water. No offers with poor credit or low income to be considered. Come and experience the comfort and community of East Norwalk!
