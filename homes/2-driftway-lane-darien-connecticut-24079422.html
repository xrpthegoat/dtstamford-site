About this home

Welcome to 2 Driftway, a stunning 5-bedroom, 8,239 sq. ft. New England Colonial set on 2 acres in Darien's sought-after Tokeneke neighborhood. Built with A+ construction and high-end European craftsmanship, this home features cathedral ceilings, a dramatic dining room with two-story windows and radiant floor heating, and 18+ foot vaulted ceilings in the formal living, and family rooms, flooding the space with natural light. Designed for year-round entertaining, the gourmet kitchen boasts a Sub-Zero refrigerator, Diva de Provence stove, Wolf convection wall oven, wine chiller, and water filtration system. The kitchen and family rooms share a dual-sided wood-burning fireplace, and a private office includes a dry bar. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a spa-like bath, sauna, and a large walk-in closet, alongside three generously sized bedrooms and a spacious playroom. The fifth bedroom provides exceptional privacy and could serve as an additional primary suite or ADU with its' own entrance. The home also includes a gym, an expansive basement ready to be partially finished, and a 3-car heated garage. Enjoy direct pond frontage, perfect for ice skating in winter and kayaking in summer. Just a short walk to downtown Darien, the train, schools, and The Tokeneke Beach Club. This home is a rare opportunity. Schedule your showing today!
