About this home

Ideally situated in southern Wilton near the New Canaan border, this beautifully appointed four-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial with a heated gunite pool pairs a private setting with easy access to surrounding amenities. Set on 2.6 acres along one of Wilton's most scenic roads, the property provides a peaceful and inviting setting. Sun-filled and thoughtfully designed, the main level offers an easy flow for everyday living and entertaining. A spacious 30-foot eat-in kitchen opens to the backyard and flagstone patio, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living. The vaulted great room features exposed wood beams, a fireplace, and custom built-ins, and opens through French doors to a charming wood-paneled sunroom with sliding windows and doors, and a herringbone tile floor. Additional highlights include a formal dining room with detailed wood paneling and bay window, a living room with a wood-burning fireplace, and a private upper level retreat over the garage with new carpeting. Outside, enjoy a private in-ground pool and a stone patio ideal for entertaining. An unfinished basement with high ceilings offers excellent expansion potential. Freshly painted with recent updates, newer mechanicals, and a generator, this home offers the very best of Wilton living.
