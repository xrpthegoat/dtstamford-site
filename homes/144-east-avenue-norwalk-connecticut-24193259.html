About this home

This spacious top floor apartment features an open floor-plan with skylights above the dining area. The eat-in kitchen offers granite countertops, a center island, and tasteful finishes creating an inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. Hardwood floors extend through the main living space. The south-facing bedroom suite includes a walk-in closet, a large bathroom with a jacuzzi tub, and a separate stand-up shower. Among the additional conveniences are an in-unit laundry room with a washer and dryer. The amenities at Marquis on the River comprise a fitness center, a billiard room, ground floor storage space for each unit, a convenient elevator, and beautiful landscaping. Ideally located with quick access to I-95 (Exit 16), just a 10-minute walk to the East Norwalk Metro-North station, and minutes from The SoNo Collection, shopping, dining, and the waterfront, this home offers a perfect blend of convenience and relaxed living.
