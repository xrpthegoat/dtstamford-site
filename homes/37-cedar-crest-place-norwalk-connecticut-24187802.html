About this home

Beautifully updated West Norwalk home offering the perfect blend of convenience, comfort, and exceptional outdoor living. Ideally located just minutes from I-95, Metro-North, shopping, dining, golf, and Norwalk's beaches and waterfront attractions. Inside, you'll find updated flooring, tile, fixtures, a renovated bathroom, and a warm, inviting living room with a wood-burning fireplace. The eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and ample cabinet space. Two generously sized bedrooms complete the main level, while the finished lower level offers approximately 720 square feet of versatile living space, ideal for a family room, home office, gym, or guest area. Step outside to a private, level backyard enclosed by a new 6-foot privacy fence with two gates. Over the past year, the property has been thoughtfully enhanced with extensive landscaping, a custom fire pit area, a new storage shed, and a whole-house Aquasana water filtration system, creating an ideal setting for outdoor entertaining and everyday enjoyment. Additional highlights include a walk-up attic and detached garage, offering abundant storage and future expansion potential.
