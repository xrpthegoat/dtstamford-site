About this home

Welcome to Newfield ~ Discover the possibilities in this inviting 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath split-level home in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood. Recently refinished hardwood floors bring warmth and character to the living room, dining room, and bedrooms, while the spacious living room features a wood-burning fireplace, perfect for cozy evenings at home. The dining room opens to a wonderful three-season room with sliders leading to the fenced backyard, creating an easy connection between indoor and outdoor living. The lower level offers additional flexible space and includes a door leading directly outside, providing convenient access to the yard. The kitchen and full bath have been updated, and the home offers a solid foundation for its next owner to make it their own with some cosmetic improvements. Ideally located close to shopping, dining, transportation, and everyday conveniences, this Newfield home offers a great opportunity to create a comfortable home in a convenient Stamford location.
