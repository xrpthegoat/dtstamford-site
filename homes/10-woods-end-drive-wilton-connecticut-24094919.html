About this home

Nestled on a coveted, serene cul-de-sac in Wilton, this 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath colonial combines timeless elegance with modern luxury. Set on a beautifully landscaped lot featuring a heated gunite pool and spa, this residence offers a private sanctuary close to the New Canaan border and just minutes from town center. Inside, you'll find a remodeled, designer chef's kitchen with Thermador appliances, custom cabinetry, exotic granite, and propane cooking-flowing seamlessly into an open-concept family room and conservatory-style breakfast area. The current owners have remodeled the baths and the Primary bathroom boasts radiant floors and heated towel bars. The finished basement includes a home gym, wine nook, and extensive storage. Upgrades abound: surround sound wiring, three fireplaces, French doors, and extensive millwork. The professionally landscaped grounds feature arbors, perennial gardens, a rose island, organic vegetable beds, and a charming footbridge over a babbling stream. Sprinkler system, hardwired security cameras, new driveway seal, and architectural roof ensure worry-free ownership. Beyond the specs, this home is alive with seasonal magic-from blooming lilacs and peonies in the spring to moonlit snowscapes in winter. 10 Woods End Drive isn't just a house-it's a four-season experience of comfort, beauty, and privacy.
