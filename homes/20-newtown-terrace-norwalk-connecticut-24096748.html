About this home

HOME ENTHUSIASTS TAKE NOTE!!! This house is a rare opportunity to own in sought-after Wolfpit!! Surrounded by beautiful blossoms, this cape is the perfect place to make your own!! Two bedrooms, one bath, updated kitchen, oversized living room with built-ins and fireplace, dining room with hardwood floors and a bonus sunroom complete the existing floor plan. But, don't be surprised when you see the spacious walk-up attic and unfinished basement, which can be easily be turned into living space! Two-car garage also has a workshop, which can be a studio, a "man cave" or a "she shed". The spacious deck overlooks the beautiful flowers, and the vinyl fencing provides plenty of privacy. This hidden gem is for buyers who want to make a house their home!
