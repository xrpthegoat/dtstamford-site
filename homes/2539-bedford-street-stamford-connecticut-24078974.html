About this home

Welcome to Chesterfield, one of Stamford's most sought-after gated communities. Experience first-floor living in this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom unit situated in a very private section of the complex. The living room is bright and spacious, complete with a fireplace and hardwood floors throughout. The dining room opens to a deck that overlooks a green common area. A primary bedroom features California closets and built-in storage, while the second bedroom includes a built-in desk and bookshelves. Updated kitchen with granite countertops, a tiled backsplash and stainless-steel appliances. Both bathrooms are modern, with sleek fixtures. Enjoy 24-hour security and a welcoming community atmosphere complete with two pools and a clubhouse. Convenient to shops, restaurants and a health club right in the heart of Stamford.
