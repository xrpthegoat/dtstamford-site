About this home

This 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhome at Elm Street Apartments was built in 2016 and puts all of Stamford within walking distance. It has solid oak hardwood floors, a newly updated kitchen with granite counters and a dishwasher, and a primary suite with its own double-sink bath. Step outside and you're minutes from West Beach and the waterfront paths at Cummings Park. You're also close to local favorites like Elm Street Diner, Colony Grill's hot-oil pizza, and Sunny Daes ice cream. Commuting to NYC? The train station is under a mile away, and buses stop practically at your door. Modern, spacious, and walkable to everything. Rentals like this don't last.
