About this home

Spectacular waterfront 2 bedroom, 2.5 bathroom duplex apartment located at Stamford Landing in Waterside! 1,436 sq ft of luxury living in an open floor plan with a 200 sq ft setback terrace overlooking Stamford Harbor and the Long Island Sound. Hardwood floors along with an updated modern kitchen including stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. Relax in your beautiful family room with a natural gas fireplace. Expansive primary bedroom suite with a marble tiled bathroom. Wonderful amenities include a full size outdoor pool and spa along with a dog run for your favorite pet. Spend your time walking along a beautiful waterside boardwalk located right next door. This apartment also includes two indoor parking spots. Near restaurants, shopping, Metro North, Downtown Stamford and I-95. Not to be missed!
