About this home

Welcome to this charming Colonial home, nestled on a peaceful double cul-de-sac street in a serene and desirable neighborhood. With easy access to I-95, this location offers an ideal spot for commuters. Situated in The Cove, you'll also enjoy the luxury of walking to the beach year-round. Recent updates include a brand new roof, new windows, an upgraded kitchen, a new chimney, and a new gas furnace. The home also features a new 50 gallon water heater, a new washer and dryer (included), and vinyl siding on the exterior. Inside, you'll find beautiful hardwood floors throughout, adding warmth and character to every room. The spacious backyard and two-car detached garage complete the appeal of this lovely home. Don't miss the chance to make this updated gem your new home!
