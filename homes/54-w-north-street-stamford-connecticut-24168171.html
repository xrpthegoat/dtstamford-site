About this home

A rare renovation opportunity offering accessible entry into one of Stamford's most competitive housing markets. This is a large open 2 BR, 1 BTH end unit with abundant natural light-- ideal to create a custom home and let your creative genius run wild. Now is the time to completely transform and bring this 900 sq ft unit into 2026 and beyond. Flexible layout features a well proportioned living room, a future dream kitchen with a very large dining area, two surprisingly large freshly painted bedrooms and the potential to create a modern, personalized imaginative interior with the possibilities being endless. River Haven is an owner-centric co-op residence, uniquely positioned in a residential neighborhood between Scalzi and Mill River Parks hedged by Hart Magnet and Cloonan public schools with Ridgeway Shopping Center just moments away and downtown Stamford at your fingertips. Enjoy the convenience of a prime, amenity rich location close to restaurants, entertainment, shops, schools, parks, and public transportation. This is an estate sale offered in as is condition, no disclosures given, owner occupancy only, no investors. Board requires a maximum 34 debt to income ratio. Taxes incl. in HOA.
