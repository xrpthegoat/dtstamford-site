About this home

Located in the Harbor Shores neighborhood, 134 Woodward Ave is within walking distance to Baxter Park (with water access) and Woodward Park. This spacious unit spans the 2nd and 3rd floors. The main level features a good-sized kitchen with in-unit laundry, a dining room, living room, three bedrooms, and two full bathrooms. The 3rd floor offers a fourth bedroom, an additional full bath, and a versatile flex space-perfect for a home office or den. Great storage and closet space throughout. Natural gas helps keep utility costs low. Two off street spaces. The unit is ready for immediate occupancy. Good credit and references are required. 1st floor unit is owner occupied. No Pets allowed- no exceptions.
