About this home

HIGHEST & BEST OFFERS BY WEDNESDAY 5/6/26 5:00PM Your dream home awaits-picturesque both inside and out. This one-of-a-kind, updated antique colonial is set in the historic Silvermine section of Norwalk, offering timeless charm with a spacious feel. High ceilings, freshly painted light-neutral walls, and wood floors throughout create an airy, welcoming atmosphere. The oversized eat-in chef's kitchen features white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a marble-topped island-perfect for cooking, baking, and gathering. Newer windows flood the home with natural light, while classic details such as a leaded glass feature window, original moldings, and crystal doorknobs preserve its historic character. French doors connect the kitchen and living room for easy flow. The main bedroom offers two generous closets. A semi-finished, freshly painted basement adds approximately 600 square feet of versatile living space, ideal for a gym, recreation, or hobby area. Outdoors, tall pines provide shade and privacy, complemented by original stone walls along two sides of the property. Enjoy a large block paver patio with stone seating, perfect for seasonal entertaining. A detached garage with a metal roof (2018) has been transformed into a serene creative studio, ideal for art, crafting, or meditation. Convenient location, close to shopping, I95, Route 7, and the Merritt Parkway. A rare blend of character, space, and modern comfort.
