About this home

Nestled in one of South Wilton's walkable neighborhoods, this exceptional newer-construction home offers over 5,000 SF of beautifully designed living space on a private, flat, one-acre lot. Gently lived in, impeccably maintained, the residence combines timeless craftsmanship w/modern convenience, presenting in truly immaculate condition. A circular Belgium block lined driveway creates a grand arrival & highlights the home's commanding presence. Inside, exquisite millwork, high ceilings & abundant natural light define the elegant yet comfortable interiors. Designed for both everyday living & entertaining, the spacious floor plan features 6 bedrooms, including 2 primary suites, offering flexibility for guests, extended family, or multigenerational living. The heart of the home is a chef's kitchen w/premium appliances, custom cabinetry, generous workspace & seamless flow to the surrounding living & dining areas. Formal & informal spaces provide the perfect balance of sophistication & comfort. Additional highlights include a walk-up attic w/expansion potential, a walk-out basement offering endless possibilities, propane heat, town water & sewer, a generator & an irrigation system. Every detail reflects quality construction & pride of ownership. Enjoy the rare ability to walk to town, shops, dining & community amenities while enjoying the privacy & tranquility of a beautiful estate setting. Combining exceptional quality, municipal utilities with an unbeatable location!
