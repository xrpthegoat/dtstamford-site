About this home

There's lots to love about this spacious 4-bedroom/3-full bath home beautifully set on 1.37-lush acres. It's part of a coveted North Stamford neighborhood of fine homes where it offers privacy while being just minutes from the Merritt Pkwy, shops, & restaurants. The house has been freshly painted throughout, with large windows, gleaming hardwood flrs on the main level, 2-wood burning fireplaces & comfortable deck off the EI kitchen that add to the home's charm & updates PLUS it boasts a brand new roof! Sunny Living with picture window and raised hearth fireplace has double case opening entry into roomy formal Dining room with lovely property views. EIK has new stainless refrigerator, dishwasher and microwave to complement the 2-stainless wall ovens + has a door out to the deck for easy dining and entertaining. Main level bedroom wing has sunny Primary bedroom with full bath w/tub, 2-add'l bedrooms & a full bath w/skylight & jetted tub. Walkout lower patio level features dynamic Great rm/Family rm that's filled with natural light. It has a floor-to-ceiling brick fireplace, walls of windows and sliders out. 4th bedroom and full bath make it the perfect versatile space for in-law suite, nanny or returning student. The deep, verdant front lawn is now complemented by the backyard that has been built-up to create level play areas all embraced by mature trees. Enjoy terrific versatile space with updates/amenities-feature sheet tells all. There's so much value for the savvy buyer
