About this home

Meticulously restored and reimagined by Fletcher Development in 2023, this classic Tudor sits behind secure gates on 28 magnificent, parklike acres. Beautifully appointed interiors feature a dramatic double-height living room, sunroom, office, formal dining room and gourmet kitchen equipped with Wolf, Sub-Zero and Miele appliances. Four generously sized bedrooms each include a private en-suite bath. Exceptional craftsmanship and thoughtful details honor the home's historic character while introducing every modern comfort. Private, picturesque and remarkably serene, the property offers an elevated country retreat in an extraordinary setting.
