About this home

Welcome to 42 Alan Lane in New Canaan, where modern luxury meets classic beauty. Built by renowned design-build team Gardiner Larson in 2000 and completely reimagined and renovated in 2022-23 to reflect today's modern classic aesthetic, this beautiful estate overlooks almost 5 acres of lush, sweeping Connecticut landscape complete with heated pool and potential tennis court site. Built for entertaining, the first floor features a magnificent kitchen with an oversized island designed to comfortably seat 6 people, opening to a generously proportioned family room and expansive patio - perfect for casual gatherings inside or out. Experience your wine collection from the integrated dining room wine room or enjoy cocktails by the double sided fireplace in the adjacent conversation room. A formal living room flanked by french doors, leads to a beautiful office with a wood burning fireplace and extensive millwork. Every room opens to outdoor wonder - a sunken firepit that seats a crowd, a covered conversation area with firepit, a large patio for dining and built-in spa overlooking the gorgeous property and trees.
