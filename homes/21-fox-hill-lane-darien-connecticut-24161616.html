About this home

Set at the end of a coveted Fox Hill cul de sac on a private 1 acre lot, this spectacular 5 bedroom, 6 bath home offers an exceptional lifestyle defined by luxury, comfort, and effortless entertaining. Completely redesigned, the home is surrounded by open, beautifully manicured grounds with a premium lawn and expansive feel, combining privacy and serenity with a setting that feels both special and inviting. Designed for modern living, the home features four fireplaces, a stunning chef's kitchen with soaring ceilings, a statement center island, and high end appliances, as well as a private home office, theater room, gym with sauna, and a first floor in law suite with full bath. Outside, the resort style backyard is the ultimate private retreat, complete with a pool, hot tub, outdoor fire pit, and expansive mahogany deck for seamless indoor outdoor living. Warm, inviting, and beautifully designed, this is a rare opportunity to own a private oasis in one of Darien's most sought after locations.
