About this home

Circa-1932-Charming Colonial Farmhouse- Light and bright, with gracious public and private spaces. The formal living room opens to a cheery sunroom, at one time a porch, now offers additional living with adjacent full bath & works as a den, office or lounge. The totally transformed kitchen & updated mudroom opens to a formal dining room, perfect for small or large family gatherings. On the second level are 3 generously sized bedrooms & newer full bath. BONUS! There is a full walk-up attic, a perfect area for tween suite, in-law- guest or playroom! There is also a lower-level laundry-gym area that offers great utility of space, while providing ample storage.
