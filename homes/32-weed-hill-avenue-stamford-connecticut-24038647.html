About this home

Welcome to this charming three-level townhome in the sought-after Springdale neighborhood, where community vibes and vibrant nightlife come together perfectly. You'll love the convenience of being just minutes from local schools, parks, restaurants, and a variety of shops. Enjoy the amenities of Springhill Terrace, including a community pool, tot lot, and assigned parking for both owners and guests, all amidst beautifully maintained grounds. As you step inside, you'll be welcomed by gleaming hardwood floors and a spacious coat closet that could easily transform into a cozy mudroom. A convenient powder room is also located on this level. The eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining area and opens up to a versatile living room with sliding doors leading to a delightful back patio-ideal for grilling or simply enjoying the outdoors. Upstairs, you'll find three inviting bedrooms, each with generous closet space, and a full bathroom with a tub. The lower level provides extra living space that can be tailored to your needs, whether as a family room, office, or guest quarters. Next door, the laundry room offers ample storage and direct street access, making moving in a breeze.
