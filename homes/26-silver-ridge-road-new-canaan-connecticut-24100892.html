About this home

Set on a beautifully landscaped flat acre, this 4-bedroom home is updated and move-in ready. You'll love the chef's kitchen with white quartzite counters, plenty of storage and high-end appliances. The sparkling kitchen opens to the light filled great room with vaulted ceiling, built-ins, a custom banquette and access to the large deck ideal for dining and gatherings. This home also offers formal entertaining space with a living room with wood burning fireplace and dining room with bay window and built-in wine cabinet. There is also a second family room with wood burning fireplace leading to a private patio and a dedicated oversized home office, ideal for remote work or study. The bathrooms have been tastefully updated including a fully renovated primary bath with heated white marble floor. Located on a quiet street, in the historic Silvermine section of New Canaan, with easy access to the Silvermine Market, Silvermine Art Center and the Gray Barns on the Silvermine River. This home offers a wonderful combination of convenience and tranquility-close to town amenities, yet serene and private.
