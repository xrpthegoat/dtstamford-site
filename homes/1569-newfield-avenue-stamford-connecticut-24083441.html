About this home

The Benjamin Scofield House, dating back to 1777, has been thoughtfully renovated to meet the needs of modern living while honoring its rich architectural heritage. At the heart of the home, a fully redesigned chef's kitchen boasts top-of-the-line appliances and a stunning oversized quartzite island, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The primary and hall bathrooms have been beautifully updated with high-end finishes, creating a spa-like retreat within the home. Timeless details such as wide plank floors, post-and-beam construction, and two period fireplaces-including a rare beehive oven-preserve the home's authentic character. Natural light fills the sunroom and dining room, the latter of which opens to an expansive outdoor patio, perfect for seamless indoor-outdoor living. The home's flexible layout includes an au pair or guest suite, offering versatility for modern families. Step outside to enjoy flagstone terraces ideal for entertaining, along with a detached garage and shed for additional storage or creative use. This is a rare opportunity to own a piece of history, completely renovated for today's lifestyle, all while being conveniently located close to everything Stamford has to offer.
