About this home

Stunning views, thoughtful upgrades, and exceptional privacy define this beautiful colonial set on 3.8 landscaped acres. A soaring, light-filled entry hall leads to elegant living spaces with refinished hardwood floors, high ceilings, and detailed moldings. The living room with fireplace, an oak-paneled library, and formal dining room offer timeless charm. The custom chef's kitchen features top-of-the-line Gaggenau, Thermador, Miele, and Bosch appliances, including induction cooktop, steam oven, built-in Teppanyaki grill, espresso machine, and wine cooler. The adjoining family room boasts oversized windows, French doors to a mahogany deck, wood-burning fireplace, and full bar - perfect for entertaining. Upstairs provides four bedrooms including a primary suite with his-and-her baths and expansive property views. A bonus room above the garage offers flexible space for work or play. The walkout lower level includes a gym, full bath, and changing room. Outside, enjoy a heated gunite pool, outdoor shower, and stone patio surrounded by mature landscaping and stone walls. Energy-efficient features include Tesla solar panels with two backup batteries and an EV charging station. Convenient to local trails, Woodcock Nature Center, Weir Farm, and shopping in Ridgefield, Georgetown, and Weston. This defines refined country living -elegant, efficient, and effortlessly private.
