About this home

Please submit highest and best offers by 12:00 p.m. tomorrow, Monday, March 30, 2026. Welcome to 23 Sachem Street, a beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath Cape that blends classic charm with thoughtful updates in a convenient Norwalk location. The main level features a remodeled kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, opening to warm and inviting living spaces highlighted by gleaming hardwood floors and a raised-hearth wood-burning fireplace and a main level bedroom. Upstairs you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath. The finished lower level adds valuable extra space for a family room, playroom, home office, gym, or media area, along with laundry and additional storage. Outside, an expansive deck overlooks a private, park-like backyard with mature plantings, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining. With central air, gas heat, and a one-car attached garage, this move-in-ready home delivers comfort, character, and everyday practicality.
