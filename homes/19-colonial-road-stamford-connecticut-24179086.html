About this home

Tucked away toward the back of the Colonial Mews complex for added privacy and quiet, the best part of living at 19 Colonial might just be leaving the car parked, with Metro North, coffee shops, bagel shops, restaurants, shopping, and everyday conveniences all nearby. Located in the heart of Glenbrook with easy access to both I-95 and the Merritt, this updated tri-level townhouse offers the layout, sense of community, and low-maintenance lifestyle today's buyers are looking for. The main level features hardwood floors, an updated kitchen with granite countertops, maple cabinetry, and stainless steel appliances, plus an open living/dining area filled with natural light and sliders leading to a private deck, perfect for morning coffee, summer dinners, or just opening the sliders and enjoying some fresh air. An updated powder room completes the main level. Upstairs are two spacious bedrooms with generous closet space and an updated full bath. The finished lower level adds valuable flex space perfect for a family room, office, gym, playroom, or guest space, along with laundry and additional storage. Enjoy the nicely maintained grassy common area with mature greenery, designated parking, visitor parking, and private entrance. Whether you're commuting daily or simply want everything close by, 19 Colonial makes everyday living easy.
