About this home

Looking for a great home with lots of space? This Cape Cod home is located on a quiet street in the Bettswood area! This 4-bedroom, 3-full-bath home offers a large eat-in kitchen with tons of cabinet storage, great counter space for meal prep, and newer stainless steel appliances installed in 2025. Enjoy the spacious living room/dining room with hardwood floors and large windows that fill the home with beautiful natural light. Easy main-level living includes 2 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and a washer/dryer. Upstairs, you'll find 2 large bedrooms and another full bath. The lower level features a paneled recreation room, excellent storage space, a mini kitchenette, and a mini-split A/C system installed in 2026 for added comfort. Additional updates include a new roof installed in 2026. A natural gas generator also provides added peace of mind in the event of a power outage. Outside, enjoy a lovely patio perfect for barbecuing or relaxing. Great location on a quiet street with easy access to commuting routes, train service, restaurants, shopping, movies, the Town Green, concerts, and the Farmers Market. Don't miss this one!
