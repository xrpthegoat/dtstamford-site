About this home

Newly rebuilt to perfection in 2019 this 4 bed, 3.1 bath Colonial offers the ideal blend of luxury, comfort, and convenience. Just 2 blocks to Noroton Heights train station, Darien Commons, and Heights Crossing, the home provides easy access to shopping, dining, schools, and commuting. Perfect for both everyday living and entertaining, the open floor plan boasts 9+ft ceilings and gleaming hardwood floors throughout. A spectacular chef's kitchen is the heart of the home w/Carrara White quartz countertops, center island w/seating, Viking gas range & beverage drawers. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the family room w/gas fireplace and breakfast area w/wet bar and wine fridge. Sliders open to a private fenced backyard featuring bluestone patio and professional landscaping, creating exceptional indoor-outdoor living. A formal dining room, mudroom w/powder room and attached garage complete the 1st floor. 2nd floor primary suite has a fabulous luxury spa bath w/ soaking tub, stall shower, double sinks & walk-in closet. 2 additional bedrooms w/shared bath and convenient hallway laundry. A finished 3rd floor offers a 4th bedroom, full bath, and expansive media/playroom ideal for guests or an au pair, home office, or kids' retreat. Full basement with ample storage space and access to backyard. Set in a wonderful neighborhood with the ability to walk or ride bikes to Holmes Elementary, Middlesex Middle, and Darien High, this turnkey home offers the very best of Darien living.
