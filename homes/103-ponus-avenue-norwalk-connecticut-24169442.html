About this home

MULTIPLE OFFERS: HIGHEST AND BEST TUESDAY 5/5 AT 5PM Welcome to 103 Ponus Ave, a move-in ready hi-ranch nestled in the highly desirable West Norwalk neighborhood. Offering 2,702 square feet of living space on nearly half an acre, this home combines comfort, flexibility, and a beautifully private, fully fenced backyard. The main level features a sun-filled layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A spacious family room with soaring ceilings creates an inviting place to relax and flows seamlessly into the dining area. The adjoining sunroom bathes the space in natural light and offers versatile use, complete with a new washer and dryer and direct access to the back deck overlooking the serene yard. Down the hall, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms and two updated bathrooms, including a sought-after primary en-suite. The lower level adds exceptional flexibility, currently configured as a spacious accessory-style living area with its own full bath, washer/dryer, kitchenette, and expansive living space-perfect for guests, extended family, or movie nights with the built-in projector setup.
