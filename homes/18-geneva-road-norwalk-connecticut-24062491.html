About this home

Discover the perfect blend of classic charm & modern convenience in this stunning updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial-style home, nestled on a spacious .57-acre lot in the sought-after West Norwalk Association. The inviting front porch welcomes you into an open floor plan that is drenched in natural light & features HW flooring throughout, making it ideal for both comfortable living and stylish entertaining. Imagine hosting family & friends in the large living room & dining room, which flow seamlessly into a well-appointed newly renovated kitchen with Quartz Counters, new cabinets, subway tile backsplash, a newer dishwasher. The adjacent family room/great room, with vaulted ceilings, cozy FP & sliders leading to oversized wooden deck (32x18), providing the perfect setting for both indoor & outdoor gatherings. On the 1st floor, you'll also find a cozy sunroom for quiet moments of relaxation, a convenient laundry area with a new washer & new dryer, and an updated powder room with new sink. Head upstairs to discover all four bedrooms, each recently painted and featuring recessed lighting. Primary en-suite bedroom is a true retreat, complete with a full bathroom and walk-in closet. This home is packed w/additional highlights, including brand new Central A/C, four heating zones, a whole house fan, a new oil tank & updated electrical wiring. Step outside into the fully fenced backyard, where you can unwind by the fire pit.
