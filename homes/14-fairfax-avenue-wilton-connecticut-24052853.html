About this home

**Please submit all offers by 6pm on Tuesday, October 22nd** Welcome to 14 Fairfax Avenue, an artfully crafted vintage Colonial conveniently located on a charming cul de sac in South Wilton. Whether you're a first-time home buyer, weekender, busy professional, or downsizer, prepare to fall in love! This home offers three bedrooms (one can be an office) and two full baths. You'll likely spend all your time in the sunny family room featuring a vaulted ceiling and built-ins with French doors leading you to the enchanting terrace, stone walls, and gardens. The spacious living room, complete with a wood-burning fireplace and formal dining room, adds a touch of elegance, with the country kitchen nearby. The attached garage and unfinished basement provide tons of storage. Your peaceful haven or forever home awaits!
