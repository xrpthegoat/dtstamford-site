About this home

Welcome to Glen Oak! This charming, light-filled 2-bedroom 1.5 bath townhouse is nestled in the heart of Stamford's Glenbrook neighborhood, a vibrant community known for its urban feel and abundance of restaurants, coffee shops, and parks. The residence boasts brand new stainless steel kitchen appliances and engineered hardwood floors in the living and dining areas. The spacious living room features a cozy fireplace and sliding glass doors that open to a brick patio, perfect for outdoor dining. Upstairs, the large primary bedroom offers sliders to a private balcony, while the second bedroom includes a generous closet area. Additional amenities include new in-unit washer dryer, attic storage, a new hot water heater and a reserved parking space right in front of the unit. Conveniently located close to the Glenbrook train station, downtown Stamford, and I-95, this townhouse offers both comfort and accessibility. Schedule your showing today, these homes move quickly!
