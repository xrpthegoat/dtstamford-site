About this home

Welcome to Palmer Point! Experience serene waterfront living in this sought-after condominium community, located just half a mile from the Cos Cob train station. This inviting one-bedroom plus loft/den residence features an open-concept design with abundant natural light throughout. The spacious living room boasts a cozy gas fireplace and sliding glass doors that open to a private balcony, offering a peaceful outdoor retreat. Highlights include: Light-filled loft-style den for added versatility, In-unit washer and dryer, Central air conditioning, Covered assigned parking in a secure gated garage, Private storage room plus ample guest parking. The community provides exceptional convenience with common charges that cover water, exterior maintenance, and trash removal. Whether enjoying the spacious living area or unwinding on your balcony, this Palmer Point home combines comfort, practicality, and a coveted waterfront lifestyle.
