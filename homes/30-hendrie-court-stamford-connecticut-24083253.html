About this home

Enjoy panoramic views of the Innis Arden golf course from this recently renovated home at the end of a quiet cul de sac. An easy living main floor plan offers open spaces, hardwood floors and over-sized windows capturing the spectacular views of the Old Greenwich majestic surroundings. The first floor primary bedroom suite has access to the back patio and offers a luxurious marble tile bath, vaulted ceilings and 2 closets. Completing the first floor is the gourmet kitchen, dining room with fireplace, updated powder room and generous mudroom entrance leading to the attached 2 car garage. The second floor has 2 double bedrooms and an updated shared bath. The over-sized backyard is beautifully landscaped with an in ground sprinkler system. Patios off of both the living room and master bedroom take in the views. Minutes from DT Stamford, train stations, parks and the village of Old Greenwich.
