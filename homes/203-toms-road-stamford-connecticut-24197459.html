About this home

Timeles charm and great location. This Beautiful Tudor is located within walking distance of schools, shops and park. Toquam Magnet around the corner. Dolan down the street. Finished attic with room for for couch. Wrap-around yard. Garage. Superb location. Easy access to the Merritt and I-95. Not to be missed! Good credit, references required. Cats welcome.
