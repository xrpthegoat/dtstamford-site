About this home

Welcome to 31 Thornhill Rd in Greenwich's Riverside neighborhood, a renovated home that feels like new and is backed by a new construction warranty. Rebuilt from the ground up, the property features a reinforced foundation, all new framing and walls, plus a new roof and siding for long-term peace of mind. Inside, enjoy wide white oak floors, a custom kitchen, and a Viking appliance package designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Efficiency and comfort are enhanced with spray foam and soundproof insulation, natural gas heat, a new heating and cooling system, and Davinci aluminum-clad windows with energy-efficient glass. The home is smart-home pre-wired with Control4 automation and built-in speakers. Outdoor living includes a private patio and deck, automated sprinkler system, and garage parking, completing this thoughtfully rebuilt and move-in ready residence.
