About this home

Turnkey 3-bedroom townhouse with garage in Stamford's sought-after Springdale neighborhood! Featuring many new updates, this bright and spacious home in Heritage Commons offers three levels of comfortable living in a quiet setting-all conveniently located near trains, major commuting routes, shopping, and dining. The main level boasts an open floor plan and hardwood floors with a powder room, a dining room, and a kitchen appointed with stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. The living room opens to one of two private decks. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms, including a primary suite with a walk-in closet, along with two additional bedrooms that share an updated hall bath. A major highlight is the fully finished walkout lower level, featuring a second half bath and access to a deck overlooking scenic woods and a quiet creek. Ideal space for a playroom, home office, or guest space. Generous storage throughout! Recent improvements include a new heat pump, a new water heater, new kitchen appliances, fresh interior paint, and updated bathrooms. Offering space, ease, and unbeatable convenience, this home truly has it all-hurry, it won't last long!
