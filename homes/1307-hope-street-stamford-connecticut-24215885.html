About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 1-bath townhouse-style home in Stamford's Springdale neighborhood! Offering approximately 1,320 square feet of living space, this home blends timeless finishes with modern smart-home conveniences. The kitchen features Italian porcelain tile flooring and backsplash, premium stainless steel appliances, hardwood cabinetry, and an under-sink water filtration system with a dedicated drinking water faucet. The bathroom showcases elegant Carrara marble finishes, complemented by quality Moen and Kohler fixtures. Additional highlights include hardwood floors, crown molding, a fireplace with marble hearth, and a versatile office area adjoining the third bedroom. Modern upgrades include Wi-Fi-enabled Mysa smart electric thermostats in five rooms and custom motorized roller shades in five rooms, controllable by remote or Wi-Fi. Additional improvements include upgraded sound insulation and porcelain tile flooring on the lower level. Enjoy a private deck off the kitchen, two assigned outdoor parking spaces, two storage units, and convenient access to shared laundry just outside the lower-level entrance. Ideally situated near Springdale's shops, restaurants, and Metro-North train station, with easy access to the Merritt Parkway. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable, connected living in Stamford!
