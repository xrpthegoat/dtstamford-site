About this home

HIGHEST & BEST TUESDAY @5pm. Nestled in a prime Norwalk location, this charming 4-bedroom, 2-bath colonial, offers a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. With 1,428 square feet of well-designed living space, Newer roof, furnace, water heater, oil tanks & electrical panel (2018), and updates in the kitchen and bathrooms. The home features hardwood floors, a sunny living room with a cozy wood burning fireplace. The updated kitchen provides a welcoming space, a 3 season porch connects to the updated Trex deck for easy grilling or outside dining. The main floor has 2 two options for bedrooms for main floor living or home office. Upstairs, a generously sized primary bedroom, full bathroom, storage and 2nd bedroom fill out the space. The lower level offers a full size basement, ready for storage or future expansion. Outside, enjoy a level yard with a small backyard space, plus a playground practically in your backyard as well as a community garden to grow your favorite flowers and vegetables. Commuters will love the quick access to I-95, shopping and nearby parks and schools. PLEASE DO NOT THE THE CATS OUT.
