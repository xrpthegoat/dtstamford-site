About this home

Steps from Compo Beach sits NICELY UPDATED tri-level post-and-beam resulting in wonderfully open floor plan. Located in the heart of Compo Beach, enjoy beach life year-round. Soaring ceilings, interior and exterior balconys, decking with waterviews, private rear yard with hot & cold showers perfect after a refreshing day at the beach only 200 steps away. Perfect home for variety of lifestyles. Your guests will love their own privacy at street level while primary tenants enjoy the luxurious primary. The upper and lower floors offer privacy, while the large open main level is ideal for entertaining large gatherings of friends and family. With approximately 3400 SF, 4 bdrms and 3.5 baths, plenty of room for all!
