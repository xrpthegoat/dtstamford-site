About this home

Discover the perfect balance of comfort, connection & a prime location in this inviting 4-bedroom home, ideally sited in a highly desirable neighborhood on a corner lot with sidewalk-lined streets. Designed w/both everyday living & memorable entertaining in mind, this home offers great space & flow. The sun-filled home welcomes you with a large living room w/picture window creating a warm & inviting atmosphere for relaxing or hosting guests. At the heart of the home, the beautifully updated Mohawk designed kitchen, featuring stylish cabinetry, skylights, generous counter space, & a casual dining welcome all to gather and linger. Multiple communal spaces provide flexibility for easy living. Enjoy dedicated, additional areas that serve perfectly as a media room, playroom, fitness area, or home office. The primary suite offers a peaceful retreat, while 3 additional bedrooms, conveniently located on the same level, provide comfortable accommodations for family, guests, or remote work. Step outside & experience your own private escape - a flat, picturesque yard paired w/a covered patio, easily accessible from kitchen and dining area, that's ideal for summer barbecues, outdoor dining, or simply unwinding. Two car attached garage & generator. Live at the center of it all-just moments from a vibrant downtown, train, top-rated schools, the popular YMCA, & everything that makes daily life effortless. 6 Field Crest isn't just a home-it's the lifestyle you've been yearning to find!
