About this home

Exceptional contemporary residence set on 2 beautifully landscaped acres of park-like property in one of Wilton's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering 4,347 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this impressive 5-bedroom home features 3 full baths and 1 half bath, combining timeless elegance with modern comfort. Newly refinished hardwood floors flow throughout the home, while soaring 9+ foot ceilings enhance the spacious formal living and dining rooms, creating an ideal setting for entertaining. The sun-filled living room showcases a charming fireplace, complemented by a second fireplace in the inviting family room, providing warmth and character throughout the seasons. The well-designed floor plan offers generous living spaces, comfortable bedrooms, and flexibility for today's lifestyle needs. Step outside to your private backyard retreat where beautifully maintained grounds surround an in-ground pool, offering the perfect setting for summer enjoyment, outdoor gatherings, or peaceful relaxation. Mature landscaping and expansive lawns create a tranquil atmosphere with exceptional privacy. Ideally located just minutes from Wilton Town Center, top-rated schools, train service, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes, this home offers the perfect balance of convenience and serenity. A rare opportunity to own a spacious contemporary home on a spectacular property in a sought-after Wilton location. Full house generator!
