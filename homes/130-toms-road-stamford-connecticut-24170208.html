About this home

Charming and full of character, this impeccably maintained 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers the perfect blend of classic appeal and modern convenience. Beautiful wood floors run throughout, adding warmth and elegance, with perfect flow between the living room, kitchen, and dining room. The kitchen is very inviting and features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a new refrigerator, a breakfast bar, and a free-standing hutch, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Situated in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood, this home enjoys a location known for its tree-lined streets, strong sense of community, and easy access to downtown Stamford, Metro-North, parks, shopping, and dining-making it a favorite for commuters and those seeking a neighborhood feel with city convenience. Set on the corner lot, the property offers exceptional privacy along with generous outdoor space. The yard is professionally landscaped, providing a lush and welcoming setting for relaxation or gatherings. A two-car garage plus a driveway that accommodates two additional vehicles ensures ample parking. Adding to the home's value and efficiency, fully paid-off solar panels keep electric costs exceptionally low which is an outstanding benefit for anyone who is energy-conscious. Be part of the community and own a truly special home in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods.
