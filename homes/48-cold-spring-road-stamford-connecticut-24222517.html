About this home

**Stunning, Fully Renovated 3-Bedroom Home in Stamford's Desirable Bulls Head Neighborhood!** Welcome to 48 Cold Spring Road, Unit A, where modern updates, generous living space, and fantastic outdoor areas come together in one exceptional rental opportunity! **Completely renovated from top to bottom**, this gorgeous 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home offers approximately 1,500 square feet of beautifully refreshed living space. With stylish finishes and thoughtful updates throughout, this home delivers the comfort and appeal of a brand-new living experience. One of the true highlights is the **incredible outdoor space!** Step outside onto your private balcony off the main level, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or unwinding after a long day. On the lower level, a brand-new, oversized deck overlooks a beautiful backyard, creating a wonderful setting for outdoor dining, entertaining, relaxing, or simply enjoying the fresh air. Nestled in Stamford's sought-after Bulls Head neighborhood, the property is conveniently located near **Scalzi Park**, offering access to walking paths, recreational facilities, and expansive green space. Enjoy easy access to downtown Stamford's vibrant restaurants, shopping, nightlife, and entertainment, along with convenient commuter routes.
