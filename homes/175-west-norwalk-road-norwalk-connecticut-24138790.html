About this home

Set back on 1.21 acres, this classic 5-bedroom colonial is located at the cross roads of Darien, New Canaan and West Norwalk. Situated high up on a sprawling lot with an expansive yard and a handsome exterior this 5202 square foot residence captivates with original 1940's details and abundant natural light throughout. The main level has a center hall opening on one side into a spacious living room with a wood burning fireplace and into a large dining room on the other side. The gourmet kitchen with a second wood burning fireplace has custom cabinetry, a large island, a sitting area, and a breakfast nook. Off the kitchen is a half bath, windowed mudroom and laundry. Also off the kitchen is a generous family room. A double-height office, adjacent wet-bar and another half bath complete this level. Upstairs there is a master suite with a windowed bathroom, walk-in closet and three additional closets offering ample storage. Also on the second floor are three more well-appointed bedrooms, one of which with a connected playroom and a full hall bath. A separate fifth bedroom with a sitting room and full bath completes the upper level. The lower level has access to the 2-car garage and a recreation room perfect for a man-cave, exercise room or additional playroom. An expansive backyard with original 1940's plantings offers the perfect serene setting for outdoor living. Enjoy the privacy of the property yet the proximity to Darien transportation and conveniences.
