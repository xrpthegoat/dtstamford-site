About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath townhouse-style half duplex, ideally located in the heart of Stamford. Just a 12-minute walk to the Stamford Main Train Station, this home offers unmatched convenience with easy access to top dining, shopping, and entertainment destinations. The main level features a bright and spacious living room, a dedicated dining area, a breakfast bar, and a convenient half bathroom-perfect for both everyday living and entertaining guests. Upstairs, you'll find a generous primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and full en-suite bath. Two additional well-sized bedrooms, each with brand-new carpeting, share a second full bathroom. Fresh paint and updated finishes throughout the upper level add to the home's modern appeal. The lower level offers in-unit laundry and ample storage space, providing excellent functionality and convenience. Don't miss the chance to live in this move-in-ready home in one of Stamford's most desirable locations. Excellent credit history is required.
