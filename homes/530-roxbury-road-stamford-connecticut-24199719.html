About this home

A home of enduring beauty and remarkable provenance, Roxbury Rose is a rare offering that celebrates the craftsmanship and character of a bygone era. Believed to be one of Westover's original farmhouses, this distinguished 1860 Greek Revival Colonial has been thoughtfully preserved while embracing the comforts of modern living. Rich in authentic architectural detail, the residence is defined by soaring ceilings, five original fireplaces, wide-board pumpkin pine floors, handcrafted millwork, and warm brick passageways that create an atmosphere both elegant and inviting. An award winning, architecturally faithful addition seamlessly expands the home's living spaces, honoring its historic integrity while providing exceptional scale for today's lifestyle. Set amid 1.22 acres of beautifully landscaped grounds with mature specimen trees, Roxbury Rose offers over 5,300 sf of timeless living where every room tells a story. More than a residence, it is a legacy property that preserves the romance of New England's past while offering an extraordinary setting for generations yet to come.
