About this home

Welcome to 402 Harvest Commons-offering effortless 1-level Ranch-style 2 Bedroom,2.5 BA unit has 2 private courtyard-style patios, with freshly painted Kitchen, DR, LR, Hallway, Primary bedroom & Guest room. New w/w carpet in the LR, Primary and Guest BR. This home showcases a custom designed eat-in kitchen w/custom-built cabinets by Domestic Kitchens, granite counters, Wolf gas cooktop, Wolf double oven & a Sub-Zero refrigerator. Hardwood floors flow through the kitchen, hallway & dining room. The hallway leading from the kitchen to the primary suite features Laundry/half bath a full bathroom & a small pantry for additional storage. The Primary BR has vaulted ceiling, AUN suite, with stand up shower & separate tub w access to an outside patio. Perfect for morning coffee! The generous-sized living room & dining room offer a comfortable, open flow ideal for entertaining. The living room, w/vaulted ceiling & 2 stories of windows creates a stunning sun-filled room w/a gas fireplace & a custom built credenza w/granite top & access to both patios. The dining room also has a vaulted ceiling and access to a second patio. The second bedroom offers built in book shelves 2 closets (1 cedar). Main level Laundry/half bath adds convenience. Lower level is ideal for a gym, office or studio. This home also has a full house generator. Harvest commons offers resort-like living with its pool & 2 Har-Tru tennis courts. Close to town, beaches, shopping, restaurants, 95 & Merritt. A true jewel.
