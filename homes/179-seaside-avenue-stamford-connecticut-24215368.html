About this home

A solid investment in Stamford's desirable Cove neighborhood! This beautifully maintained two-family home offers charming interiors, excellent rental appeal and an inviting opportunity for an owner-occupant or investor. The impressive first-floor apartment features hardwood floors, charming arched hallways and a spacious living room with a fireplace. The generous eat-in kitchen offers white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances and direct access to the large backyard. Generously sized bedrooms and a charming updated bath complete the apartment. Direct access to the spacious basement provides a washer and dryer, abundant storage and potential for additional space. Move-in ready, this apartment is ideal for personal use or rental income. The delightful second-floor two-bedroom apartment is also in excellent condition and also features its own in-unit washer and dryer. Both apartments offer the comfort, character and convenience that make a rental property stand out. Set on a large, level .23-acre lot, the property includes a two-car garage and ample additional parking. A newer heating system, new hot water heater and new oil tank further reflect the care this home has received. Enjoy close proximity to beaches, Chelsea Piers, transportation and downtown Stamford's shops, restaurants and entertainment, along with easy access to I-95. A well-cared-for property offering immediate appeal and long-term investment potential!
