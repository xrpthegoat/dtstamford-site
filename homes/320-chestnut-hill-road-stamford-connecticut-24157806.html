About this home

Completely rebuilt from the studs in 2008, this one-of-a-kind Contemporary blends sleek modern design with classic New England exterior charm. Set on 3+ private acres, the home was designed to capture beautiful views from every room, with walls of windows creating a bright, open, and airy feel throughout. The main living space features soaring cathedral ceilings and dramatic two-story windows with water views. A chef's kitchen with expansive island, Viking double ovens, and a sunlit breakfast room surrounded by windows overlooks your own waterfall. The layout flows seamlessly from a formal dining room to spacious living areas and out to wraparound decks, ideal for everyday living and entertaining. Outside, enjoy a resort-style setting with a heated gunite pool, spa, and tranquil natural surroundings. Three private bedroom suites include two updated baths, while the primary suite features vaulted ceilings, a sitting area, and serene pond views. Custom storage maximizes functionality throughout. Ideally located just 2 miles from the Merritt Parkway and LaRocca's Market, and within walking distance to Chestnut Hill Park, this property delivers superior construction, privacy and a lifestyle that you will love.
