About this home

This is a charming, cozy, and spacious one bedroom corner unit that is located on the 5th floor. The owner has taken great care of this unit with custom design flooring and tile works. The unit features quality appliances including a less than 5 years old 50-gallon water heater. The bedroom is very spacious with his and hers closet, ceiling fan, and a wall AC ready encasement. The unit has a semi-open concept design with lots of natural light boasting both living room and dining area. The space is cooled with a wall-unit large size AC. The building is well-maintained, well-lit, with lots of security cameras and is located in downtown Stamford in proximity to all of downtown Stamford has to offer such as library, movie theaters, restaurants, hospital, medical offices, shopping mall, public transportation, park, trail along the river, and more. Don't miss your chance to own this great apartment, and live in downtown Stamford, CT.
