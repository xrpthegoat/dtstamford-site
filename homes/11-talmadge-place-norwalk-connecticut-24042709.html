About this home

A hidden gem! This home embodies the warmth and charm of a traditional colonial with modern updates throughout. The L-shaped porch is perfect for relaxing and welcomes you in through front and side doors. The large living room, dining room, and kitchen are beautifully maintained with hardwood floors and built-ins throughout. The first floor also offers a full bathroom. The kitchen leads to a back porch and fully-fenced in back yard; perfect for entertaining! The dining room's two bay windows drench the first floor in sunlight. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms with hardwood floors and a full bathroom. The third bedroom is tandem to another bedroom. Perfect for using as an office or nursery. Full attic with walk-up staircase grants additional space to expand as needed. The full basement provides laundry and ample storage. Detached one-car garage also provides added space. Conveniently located to the South Norwalk Train Station, downtown SONO, I-95 and Route 7 and Veteran's Park! You won't want to miss this incredible find!
