About this home

Welcome to your own slice of California right here in Greenwich with great potential investment property or room to expand. This picture-perfect home, inspired by the charm of a California Bungalow. Step inside and enjoy the warmth of an open-concept layout with hardwood floors and a stone-clad wood-burning fireplace that anchors the main living space. The updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances, generous cabinetry, and a breakfast bar. Tucked away for privacy is the primary bedroom with the two additional bedrooms and a full bath in the rear of the home. The lower level includes laundry area, clean storage, and direct access to the garage. Outside, the level backyard brick patio, just off the kitchen, is perfect for entertaining. Updates include central air and updated kitchen and bath. Just blocks from Byram Shore Park & Beach, and minutes to shops, schools, and major commuting routes. Don't miss this exceptional opportunity to own a move-in-ready gem!
