About this home

Exceptional coastal living awaits in this elegant 4-bedroom residence, where water views and refined design come together. Thoughtfully crafted with an emphasis on both sophistication and comfort, every detail of this home has been carefully considered to create a truly special offering. The spacious primary suite is a private retreat, featuring serene water views and a luxurious ambiance. Entertain effortlessly in the formal living room with fireplace and gracious dining room, or gather in the chef's eat-in kitchen, complete with a large center island, walk-in pantry, and seamless flow into the inviting family room with its own fireplace. Designed for today's lifestyle, the home also includes a separate office, home gym, rec/playroom, and a versatile bonus space ideal for a fifth bedroom, au pair suite, or en suite guest accommodations. Step outside to the updated ipe deck reminiscent of a treehouse, overlooking beautifully designed gardens with mature plantings and the Long Island Sound beyond-an idyllic and private setting for morning coffee or evening entertaining. Located within a unique waterfront association, residents enjoy access to a private beach, marina with available deep water boat slips, tennis courts, parks (including a dog park), and kayak racks. Just minutes to SoNo, Rowayton, and Metro-North, with an easy one-hour commute to NYC, this exceptional property offers the ultimate coastal lifestyle and the perfect year-round home or summer retreat.
