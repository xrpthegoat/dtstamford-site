About this home

Welcome to this charming Cape Cod-style home! The main floor boasts a fantastic layout, including a beautiful kitchen with stainless steel appliances. The open floor plan seamlessly connects the kitchen to a sunlit family room, which offers access to a spacious deck and private yard. The main level also features two bedrooms that share a hall bathroom. Upstairs, the expansive primary bedroom includes a half bath and provides a comfortable, private retreat. The lower level is ideal for all your storage needs and includes a convenient laundry area, with a new dryer being installed shortly. This wonderful home is situated in a great neighborhood, close to transportation, shopping, restaurants, and much more. Don't miss the opportunity to own this delightful home in a quiet, friendly community!
