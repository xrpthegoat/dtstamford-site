About this home

A rare opportunity to create something exceptional in coveted Shippan Point. 90 Rockledge Drive offers more than 2,150 square feet of existing living space, 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths, a fireplace and two-car garage-along with the increasingly uncommon chance to reimagine a home around your own vision. For the discerning buyer, the possibilities are compelling. Renovate the existing residence, undertake a sophisticated transformation, or explore the potential for a second-story expansion that could take greater advantage of the property's coastal setting and possible water views. Imagine a thoughtfully designed home with elevated interiors, expansive windows, beautifully conceived entertaining spaces and indoor-outdoor living-all tailored specifically to the way you want to live. This is an opportunity to begin with a desirable Shippan Point address and create a home that is distinctly your own. Surrounded by the character and coastal atmosphere that define Shippan Point, the property offers a sense of retreat while remaining just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, dining, shopping and the Stamford waterfront. 90 Rockledge Drive is not simply a home to renovate-it is an opportunity to unlock the potential of an exceptional location and create the next chapter of this Shippan Point property.
