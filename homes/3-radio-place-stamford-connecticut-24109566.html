About this home

Welcome home to this stunning fully renovated townhouse in Glenbrook. The modern kitchen features quartz counters, marble backsplash, stainless appliances, and a large peninsula with seating for four. Enjoy an open-concept main floor with dining area, spacious living room with bay window, sliders to a private balcony, and an updated half bath. Upstairs offers two oversized bedrooms with large closets; the primary includes a walk-in closet and direct access to a stylish full bath. The entry level has a family room opening to an entertainer's patio with covered space and rope lighting. Additional features: fresh paint, premium laminate floors, custom recessed lighting, Decora switches, new baseboards, AC units, windows, and sliders. Includes laundry, storage, mechanicals, 1-car garage plus 2 extra parking spaces. Move right in-nothing to do! Quaint complex, walk to Glenbrook train, mins to I-95, shopping in Darien, and Woodland Park trails.
