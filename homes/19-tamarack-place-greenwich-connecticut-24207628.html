About this home

Cherished by the same family for 30 years, this charming 2989 square foot home (walk out lower level not included in square footage), is set on a private .51-acre lot at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, with a level expansive backyard, large front porch and access to a new gated private association pool shared by just 11 homes. The main level features inviting living and dining spaces, a renovated eat-in kitchen with skylights, plus front porch and rear deck. Upstairs are four bedrooms, including a large primary suite with en-suite bath, walk-in closet and private balcony. The walk-out lower level offers flexible bonus space, a bath, storage and direct yard access. Two-car garage, two fireplaces, central air, large storage shed and minutes from the elementary school, grocery store, dining, parks and transportation.
