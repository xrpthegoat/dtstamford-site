About this home

This spacious 2-Bedroom apartment is located on the lower end of Greenwich Avenue- combining urban convenience with cozy living. The unit, includes a well-equipped kitchen with essential appliances in great condition, including a dishwasher. It features a comfortable living space-- one bedroom has an en-suite bathroom, while the common space features a full bathroom for convenience. Other amenities include: an in-unit washer & dryer, and back patio for outdoor relaxation. Residents will enjoy the proximity to the Greenwich train station and the array of shops and restaurants just outside their door. Note that while water and garbage disposal are included in the rent, the tenant is responsible for phone, TV, internet, electric, and gas bills. The apartment, has both front and back door entrances, does not come with parking spots. This unit is ideal for those seeking a mix of suburban comfort and city hustle.
