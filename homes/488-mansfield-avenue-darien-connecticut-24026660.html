About this home

Step into timeless elegance with this renovated and expanded home situated on over 2.5 acres, offering a perfect blend of historic charm and modern luxury. Spanning over 6,800 square feet, this residence features 6 bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms, thoughtfully designed for easy living. Numerous living spaces on the main floor include: a formal dining and living room, a sunlit family room overlooking the backyard, and a sophisticated study, perfect for remote work. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with top-of-the-line appliances, and a large center island with additional seating. Off the kitchen, which is the heart of the home, there is a playroom with vaulted ceilings and tons of natural light, the family room with french doors out to the backyard, and a bonus room which is a perfect spot to snuggle up and watch a movie. Upstairs, the primary suite is a serene retreat with a luxurious en-suite bathroom, changing room with closets and a balcony overlooking the property. Outdoors, enjoy the private backyard, complete with a stone terrace and built-in grill for entertaining as well as a relaxing pool to enjoy on a hot summer day. Located in a tranquil neighborhood this property offers peace and privacy while being just a short drive from Darien's top-rated schools, shopping, and dining. This property is a rare find. Don't miss the chance to own a piece of Darien's history.
