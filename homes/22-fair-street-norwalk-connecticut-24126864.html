About this home

A house-it's a place where life feels easy and full of warmth. Sunlight pours through wide windows and across hardwood floors, filling each room with a natural glow. Every room also elegantly flows from one to another which is ideal for loved ones congregating and also having your own personal space. The kitchen is the heart of the home with granite counters, stainless appliances, and a cozy breakfast nook where mornings begin with bringing the family close over coffee and conversation. The family room, anchored with a wood burning stove, is made for quiet evenings, perfect for the long winter months ahead. The sliders open to a spacious deck and backyard designed for summer gatherings and simple everyday joy. Inside and out this home is ideal for the homebody who doesn't want to be too far from NYC action. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with its whirlpool tub, while two additional bedrooms provide space for family, guests, or a home office. Outside, a detached garage, private driveway, and fenced yard add both charm and convenience. The neighborhood invites evening walks and a true sense of community, with a playground nearby and SoNo's restaurants, shops, and waterfront just minutes away. 22 Fair Street is a timeless backdrop for creating memories that last a lifetime.
