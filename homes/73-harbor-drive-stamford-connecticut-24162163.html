About this home

Experience an extraordinary level of waterfront sophistication at this completely reimagined residence, where both the living room and primary bedroom showcase stunning waterfront views, exemplifying the pinnacle of modern luxury living. Thoughtfully curated with uncompromising attention to detail, the home blends refined elegance with contemporary design, featuring rich hardwood floors, an expansive light-filled layout, an attached garage, & an atmosphere of effortless sophistication. The designer kitchen is a true showpiece, appointed with striking marble countertops, premium appliances including double ovens, sleek modern finishes, & a stylish combination light & ceiling fan above the island. The kitchen flows seamlessly into sun-drenched living & dining spaces, while the waterfront living room creates a tranquil, resort-like setting ideal for both everyday living & entertaining. The leather living room sofa, which fits the space perfectly, may be purchased separately by the tenant if desired. The serene waterfront primary bedroom offers a peaceful retreat with calming water views, while spa-inspired bathrooms evoke the tranquility of a five-star resort. Exceptional storage includes a substantial attic with custom-built closets & a custom ladder with silicone grips for convenient access. An upstairs office area complete with a waterfront pool & marina clubhouse available for rent for private entertaining.
