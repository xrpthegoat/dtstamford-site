About this home

Old Greenwich living doesn't get better than this. 15 Shorelands Place puts you at the heart of a close-knit community within the Town of Greenwich. Minutes from Tod's Point, Long Island Sound, Binney Park with tennis, duck pond and playground, and a charming village with shops, cafes, restaurants, and Metro-North station. A beautifully renovated Victorian with wrap-around porch, soaring ambient light, modern gourmet kitchen, open-plan family room, and a spacious, private level yard surrounded by mature trees. 4 bedrooms, 3.5 brand-new baths, and finished room over 2-car garage, perfect for an office or studio. Whole-house generator. Easy access to NYC, I-95, Amtrak, all major airports, and award-winning schools. This is the Old Greenwich home you've been looking for!
