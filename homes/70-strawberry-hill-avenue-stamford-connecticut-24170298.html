About this home

Bright and airy top-floor corner unit blending timeless character with modern updates. An inviting foyer opens to an expansive living and dining area featuring 9+ ft ceilings, a unique picture rail, crown molding, classic built-ins, and a charming bay window. The renovated kitchen offers ample counter space, a timeless subway tile backsplash, and stainless steel appliances, including a gas range, dishwasher, refrigerator, and microwave. A versatile nook off the entry provides the perfect work-from-home space. Two generously sized bedrooms and a beautifully updated full hall bath complete the interior. Hardwood floors run throughout, complemented by abundant deep closets and additional storage in the building's basement. Heat and hot water are included in common charges! Pets welcome (up to 35 lbs). Ample parking is available in a large lot, plus a grilling area. Garage parking is available for an additional fee/potential waitlist. Set within a beautifully maintained community with mature gardens and lush courtyards, this downtown residence is truly special. Enjoy the convenience of walking to shops, restaurants, schools, and the train-just minutes to the heart of downtown!
