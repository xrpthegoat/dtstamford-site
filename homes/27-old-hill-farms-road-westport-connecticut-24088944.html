About this home

Classic "Old Hill Farms" Estate. One of a kind custom home completely renovated and restored to perfection. Showcase sun filled home with a marriage of old and new. Exceptional details, all custom millwork and cabinetry set on over one acre in premier Old Hill location. Gracious formal living room with custom built in window seat.Original wood burning fireplace complete this large gathering room with several seating area perfect for entertaining. Formal dining room with custom built-ins, window seat and stunning Phillip Jefferies wallpaper. Custom kitchen with Liebherr refrigerator, Garland professional 6 burner and griddle stove. MIELE dishwasher and plenty of room for a large farm table that seats up to 10. Primary bedroom suite opens to a sitting room and bathroom.Three additional bedrooms with built-ins and plus two additional bathrooms complete the second floor. Third floor walk up with additional bedroom and family room. There is a separate pool bathroom and changing room.
