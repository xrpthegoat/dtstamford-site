About this home

Rarely available 3-bedroom rental at Rowayton Woods! This exceptional, single-level ranch-style home (just 6 steps up) offers effortless living in one of the area's most tightly held communities. Features include a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace and a separate dining room with custom built-ins that flows to a covered deck. The updated eat-in kitchen boasts stone counters, modernized appliances, and a sunny breakfast nook overlooking flowering dogwoods. The private primary suite includes built-in closets, a walk in closet with custom drawers and shoe storage and an ensuite bath as well as a laundry area, accompanied by two additional bedrooms and a second full bath. Enjoy resort-style amenities across 23 landscaped acres, including a pool, tennis courts, garden, and a 4-acre pond. Complete with a reserved parking space and garage space right out front, this private yet central location is an easy walk to the train and minutes from downtown Rowayton, Darien, and SoNo.
