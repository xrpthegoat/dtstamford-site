About this home

Welcome to 29 Bartlett Ave, a beautiful 2-bedroom Cape house with an attached garage located on the borderline of New Cannan Ave. This property has one of the biggest backyards on the street. Move in Ready!!!. Architectural plans were created back in 2019 for the Walk-up attic. Features a full basement with high ceiling that can easily be converted to a family or game room. The driveway can hold 5 Cars. Great location for a family, within walking distance to shops, and restaurants and minutes away from Norwalk's Calf Pasture beach and newly built Mall. Freshly painted and landscapers ready to continue service for the new owner. All plumbing is up to date. Great place to raise a family. *Please note square footage does not reflect the basement, attic or garage.
