About this home

Meticulous 5 bedroom, 5 bath, Colonial at the end of a beautiful cul de sac, in walking distance to town. This modern and well maintained home comes w/many updates. A beautiful eat-in kitchen with SS appliances & granite counters connects to sunny living room with a fireplace as well as a dining room. The upper level features a large family room with a full bath, as well as a master bedroom suite w/walk-in closet and new marble master bath. Three additional bedrooms and a full hallway bath complete the 2nd floor. The third floor offers great flex space that can work well as a playroom, bedroom, office or au-pair suite. The beautiful backyard invites to entertain and offers plenty of space to run around and enjoy the outdoors.
