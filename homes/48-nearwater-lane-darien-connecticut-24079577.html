About this home

This solid 1955 home sits on a sunny, level acre, a short walk to Hindley School, Noroton shops and Churches and a quick bike ride down Nearwater Lane to Weed Beach. The property features specimen plantings, a magnolia, apple, cherry and pear trees and a lush lawn, bordered by a stone wall. Bring your imagination to refresh this wonderful spot or build your dream home in a terrific neighborhood! The spacious Living Room has a bowed window with window seat, a cathedral ceiling and raised fireplace. The Dining Room has French doors to the deck and a built-in serving bar. The Family Room opens onto a flagstone terrace and features a fireplace and built-in bookshelves. There's a lower-level office with outside entrance and a playroom. The Primary bedroom has a large picture window, two room bath and walk in closet. Don't miss this special property!
