About this home

Set on a convenient stretch of Newtown Avenue, this well-laid-out two-family offers flexibility, functionality, and long-term potential in one of Norwalk's most accessible locations. Each level features a 2 bedroom, 1 full bath layout with in-unit laundry, making it ideal for owner-occupants, multi-generational living, or investment use. The first-floor unit benefits from two separate entrances, while the upper unit includes a charming sunroom that adds extra usable space. Light floods both floors with windows everywhere, and large living room and bedrooms. A walk-up attic provides valuable bonus storage today with the potential to be finished in the future, subject to due diligence. Behind the home, four off-street parking spaces offer everyday convenience rarely found on a main road location. Positioned near Stew Leonard's, Starbucks, and the many shops and services along Westport Avenue, this property delivers convenience alongside exceptional commuter access, sitting comfortably between I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Whether you're looking to live in one unit while offsetting expenses, expand your portfolio, or secure a versatile property in a high-demand corridor, this is an opportunity that checks multiple boxes.
