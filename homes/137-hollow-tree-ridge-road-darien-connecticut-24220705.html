About this home

Welcome to Residence 1702 at Everly Darien, a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home in a secluded, tree-lined setting in the Noroton Heights neighborhood of Darien, CT. Upon entering the residence you feel a sense of privacy and space. The living area has sliding doors that open into your own patio, so morning coffee and evening downtime are steps from your living space. Inside you'll find a fully equipped eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and a full-size washer and dryer in the unit. The primary suite includes a walk-in closet and en-suite bath. Two more bedrooms also work well as guest rooms, a home office or a den. Your private patio opens into a lovely grassy area, great for anyone who wants easy access to the outdoors. Community amenities include a resort-style pool, a clubhouse, a fitness center, co-working space and a recently added golf simulator room to relax and enjoy with friends or family. A playground and dog park are also on property. The building is pet-friendly and parking is on-site. Commuting is easy: the Noroton Heights Metro-North station is about half a mile away, with quick access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway and local schools. Noroton Heights Shopping Center and Darien Commons are close by for shopping and dining. This is a lovely community and a great way to experience Darien living with easy access to surrounding towns or commute into Stamford or NYC.
