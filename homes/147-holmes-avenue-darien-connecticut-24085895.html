About this home

Don't miss this sun-drenched and stylish 5 bedroom home in a sought-after quiet neighborhood with endless storage. With hard wood floors throughout, enter to a foyer and gorgeous living room with wood burning fireplace. The chic and newly renovated eat in kitchen has all new modern cabinetry, new quartz countertops, new backsplash, stainless steel hood and appliances and is adjacent to a large eat in area with private covered terrace for entertaining inside and out. The family room, a few steps below the kitchen, offers great space for all with new built-ins. Upstairs you will find a generous sunny primary suite with a newly renovated bath, double sinks, large shower and walk-in closet. A 2nd large bedroom boasts a private renovated bath with shower. 2 additional bedrooms share a 3rd renovated bath. On the lower level you will find a great rec room, 1/2 bath and 5th bedroom. The private flat is amazing for pooch and play! Large Laundry room with sink. 2 car garage. Convenient to everything.
