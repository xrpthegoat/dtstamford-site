About this home

If your wishlist sounds something like: ''Greenwich, updated, bright, great for entertaining, and close to everything'' than 34 1/2 Orchard St. in beloved Cos Cob is what you've been waiting for. Nestled on a 1/4 acre just a short stroll to the best restaurants, shops, and school, this stone and sustainable shingle colonial feels welcoming from the moment you come down the driveway. Whether enjoying a quiet day at home or hosting a party overflowing with friends, you'll find this residence spacious, fun, & easy to live in. Updated in 2022, the oversized Dining Room opens to the fresh Kitchen w/white quartz counters & door to the bluestone patio. The large, light-filled Living Room flows into a cozy Family Room w/a stone fireplace & sliders to the yard. Upstairs are the 4 family bedrooms - you'll love the primary bedroom with walk-in closet and full bath. The lower level, complete w/fireplace offers ''potential'' should you ever need extra room.
