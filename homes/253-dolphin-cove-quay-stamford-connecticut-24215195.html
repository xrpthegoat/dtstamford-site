About this home

Coveted west facing direct waterfront home in Dolphin Cove. This 4 bedroom, 4 bath colonial home features open water views from most rooms. The multi-level backyard with built-in outdoor kitchen, firepit and spa provide private dining with views of the Old Greenwich waterfront homes, Tod's Point and Dolphin Cove beaches as well as Long Island Sound. The open floor plan with gourmet kitchen, dining and living room with gas fireplace connect seamlessly to the outdoor entertaining areas via an accordion door. The 4th bedroom is located on the first floor with ensuite full bath and currently functions as the family room. A beautifully curved staircase leads to the second-floor primary bedroom with luxurious bath, private office, walk in closet, gym and covered balcony overlooking the beautifully landscaped backyard and water views. Two more ensuite bedrooms and a separate back staircase complete the upper level. The 3.5 acre Dolphin Cove Club grounds include swimming pool, tennis/pickle ball, beaches , playground, and deepwater docks available to all residents.
