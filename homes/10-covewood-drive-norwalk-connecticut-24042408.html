About this home

A one-of-a kind rare jewel box with a first floor Primary bedroom, nestled on one of the the most sought after cul-de-sacs in the coastal village of Rowayton. As you enter this 2BR, 2BTH home, you are greeted by spacious interiors w/vaulted ceilings, skylights & an abundance of natural light from ,3 sets of sliders, living rm, dining rm and primary BR which opens onto to a private slate patio..The home's layout is designed for an effortless flow of single floor living without sacrificing elegance or comfort. The inviting step-down living room is the heart of the home, features a fireplace, sophisticated built-ins and a marble entertaining area complete with mini fridge & wine cooler. Entertain in style in the light filled dining room open to a renovated kitchen, boasting stainless steel appliances and chic concrete countertops. Retreat to the primary BR, a sanctuary of tranquility w/vaulted ceilings,w/3dlb sets of closets. The primary en-suite bath features a marble top double vanity , deep soaker tub ,shower w/body sprays & elegant Thassos marble flooring.The second bath is no less impressive, adorned w/marble flooring and a custom-painted vanity by renowned artist "Melissa Barbieri", adding an exclusive touch of sophistication. Additional high-end finishes include polished nickel hardware, kitchen faucet and plantation shutters on all windows. Don't miss this opportunity to embrace not just a home, but a coastal lifestyle choice. Expansion potential & Home warranty!
