About this home

Beautifully renovated rental in the heart of Cos Cob offering the perfect blend of modern style and comfortable living. This home features an open floor plan with soaring ceilings and abundant natural light throughout. The spacious kitchen is a true centerpiece, complete with a large island and seamless flow into the dining area, which opens directly to a patio with a gas grill, ideal for indoor-outdoor entertaining. The inviting family room showcases vaulted ceilings, creating a bright and airy gathering space. The luxurious primary suite offers a private retreat with a beautifully appointed bathroom. Upstairs, you'll find an office, two additional bedrooms and a full bath, providing plenty of space for family or guests. The versatile lower level includes an additional bedroom or office, along with a large bedroom or recreation room featuring a brand-new full bathroom and walk-out access. Set on a professionally landscaped property, the home includes an attached oversized garage and a fenced-in backyard that backs directly onto Pomerance Park, offering privacy and scenic green space. A rare rental opportunity combining space, style, and an exceptional location.
