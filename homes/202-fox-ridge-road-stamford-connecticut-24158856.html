About this home

Set on a beautiful private property with sweeping lake views, this turnkey 4 bedroom, 3 bath home lives like a true 5 bedroom with additional lower level living space not reflected in the recorded square footage. Exceptional indoor outdoor living takes center stage with an expansive heated pool, new patio, and generous gathering areas perfect for entertaining or relaxing all summer long. The nearly 1.5 acre flat yard is a rare feature, ideal for recreation, outdoor games, or simply enjoying the peaceful lake setting. Inside, the open concept layout flows naturally between living spaces and is anchored by a large living room with vaulted ceilings and a cozy cast iron fireplace. Spacious bedrooms provide comfort and flexibility, while the upstairs bathrooms feature radiant heated floors and a whirlpool tub creating a spa like retreat. The walk out lower level includes a family room, full bath, and additional bedroom with direct access to the yard and no steps to enter, making it ideal for guests, extended family, or in law potential. Located in a warm and welcoming walking neighborhood with access to the Foxwood Lake Association, this home captures the privacy and lifestyle that make North Stamford living so special.
