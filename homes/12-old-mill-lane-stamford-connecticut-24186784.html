About this home

Exceptional property offering space, versatility, and resort-style living. Set on a beautifully landscaped lot, this impressive residence combines comfort, functionality, and outstanding outdoor amenities. The main home features 5 spacious bedrooms with generous living space throughout, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. A fully finished basement provides additional flexible space perfect for a media room, gym, playroom, or home office. The attached garage offers convenience and ample storage. Step outside to enjoy your own private backyard retreat, complete with a stunning pool and expansive outdoor space perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying summer gatherings. A standout feature of this property is the detached cottage located in the rear of the property, offering 2 bedrooms and 1 full bathroom.
