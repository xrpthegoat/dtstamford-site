About this home

Stunning Backcountry rental available for immediate occupancy! Set on 28 park-like acres, this classic 5-bedroom Colonial offers sweeping views of rolling lawns, a pond, and a waterfall. A grand front-to-back foyer welcomes you into this beautifully updated home. The main level features a formal living room with fireplace, dining room, new eat-in kitchen, wood-paneled office, screened porch with sensational views, and a first-floor bedroom with full bath. Upstairs, four spacious en-suite bedrooms complete the home. Ideally located just minutes from Glenville, downtown Greenwich, top schools, Westchester Airport, and the Merritt Parkway for easy NYC access. A truly magical oasis not to be missed!
