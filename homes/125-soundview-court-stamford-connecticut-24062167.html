About this home

A perfectly situated 2 bedroom + den, ranch style home on cul-de-sac. This home has gleaming hardwood floors throughout the main level with an open flow floor plan. The updated kitchen, with stainless steel appliances has a peninsula with seating as well as dining area. The full bathroom has a shower/tub, built in shelving and LED mirror. The full size basement has many uses including home gym, office or storage space with dedicated laundry area. The large front and rear yards, as well as rear patio, are great for entertaining. Private paved driveway for multiple cars. Garage not included in rent. Excellent credit, income and references required.
