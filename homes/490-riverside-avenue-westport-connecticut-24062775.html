About this home

This stunning Westport apartment provides the perfect blend of convenience and charm. Located within walking distance of trains, buses, restaurants, bars, coffee shops, and more, it's also just a short drive to Compo Beach, Longshore, and Main Street. Nestled on beautifully maintained grounds, this bright and sunny unit features hardwood floors and scenic views of the Saugatuck River. The landlord provides heat, hot water, snow removal, and trash collection. Tenants are responsible for electricity and cable/internet. The unit includes one assigned parking space and access to a coin-operated laundry in the basement. Landlord requirements: excellent credit, strong references, and proof of income. The unit is located on the 2nd floor of the building - no elevator. Please note: no pets, smoking, or vaping are allowed.
