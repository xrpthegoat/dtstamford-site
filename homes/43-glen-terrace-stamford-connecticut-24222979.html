About this home

This beautifully designed 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath Dutch Colonial is set back on a level, fenced yard along a charming street of period homes. This residence offers the perfect combination of architectural character, thoughtful updates, and an enviable location. Its gambrel roofline, pedimented portico, shuttered windows and window boxes deliver storybook curb appeal. Inside, hardwood floors run throughout, framing exquisite original millwork and abundant natural light. The living room feels both elegant and effortlessly welcoming offering a fireplace, built-in bookshelves, and French doors to a bright sunroom. The updated kitchen includes soapstone counters, stainless appliances, a farmhouse sink, and a butler's pantry, flowing into a formal dedicated dining room perfect for gatherings. Upstairs are three bedrooms, a spacious deck, and a full attic. A finished lower level is ideal for media, office, or a gym, plus ample storage and a two-car garage. Close to Glenbrook and Noroton Heights stations for an easy NYC commute. Historical character, modern updates, a level yard and an unmatched location. Come fall in love with your new home.
