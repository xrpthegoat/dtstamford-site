About this home

Experience unparalleled serenity & beauty at this light filled waterfront home with terrific curb appeal and panoramic scenic views from every room. Vacation each day enjoying the sparkling Saugatuck River, boating activity from the Saugatuck Yacht Club, & stunning green vistas of the lush, preserved wetland marsh. This sophisticated contemporary seamlessly blends laid back charm with modern spaces. The flexible open floor plan is thoughtfully designed to create connected living areas without compromising intimacy. The upper deck offers breathtaking water views & magnificent sunrises (plus a front row seat for Westport and Norwalk annual fireworks) Additional highlights include: unique hewn beams, vaulted ceilings, perennial gardens, ample storage, oversized 2 car garage, underground irrigation, sewer, gas, city water, transferable flood insurance, & mooring rights just steps away. This is coastal living at its best. Revel in this exquisite environment and become part of a vibrant community with access to 2 yacht clubs. Kyack, swim, fish, bike, sail, paddleboard, and more. This exceptional location is incredibly desirable for commuters as it is close to the train, and minutes to shops, restaurants, Longshore Park, Compo Beach and other amenities. Whether you are captivated by the stunning views or unbeatable location, this property guarantees a lifestyle many can only dream of.
