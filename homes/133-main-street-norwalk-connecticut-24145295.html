About this home

Welcome to 133 Main Street, Unit 7, a bright and stylish tri-level townhouse that blends modern comfort with effortless everyday living in the heart of Norwalk. Sunlight pours into the open main level, where warm hardwood floors and crisp crown molding create a polished, magazine-worthy backdrop. The updated kitchen steals the show with soft sage cabinetry, sleek pendant lighting, stone counters, and a breakfast bar that invites slow mornings and relaxed entertaining. Sliding doors lead to a private balcony, the perfect spot to sip coffee in the sun or unwind at the end of the day. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms offer peaceful retreats with generous natural light, skylights, and hardwood floors throughout. The full bath is fresh and clean with updated fixtures, while a convenient main-level half bath adds everyday ease. With a Walk Score of 89, this location places you steps from dining, shopping, parks, and public transportation, making car-free living completely attainable. In-unit laundry, ample storage, and a well-kept boutique complex of just eight units complete this charming and inviting rental. This is the kind of place that feels instantly like home the moment you walk through the door.
