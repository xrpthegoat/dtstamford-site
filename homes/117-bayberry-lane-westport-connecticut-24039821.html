About this home

Newly renovated center hall colonial on gorgeous tree lined Bayberry Lane in Westport. The spacious first floor is open flow and flooded with natural light. The eat-in kitchen and family room both open to an oversized deck, wonderful for entertaining. A first floor bedroom with brand new ADA compliant bath is perfect in-law/guest suite. Upstairs five more bedrooms (or room for an office!), including a generous master suite and a second double-vanity full bath. The deck and upstairs bedrooms overlook this generous two-acre property with beautiful mature trees and new landscaping. Conveniently located near Staples high school/Bedford Middle school, Wakeman town Farm, Fairfield County Hunt Club, with easy access to gorgeous beaches, the Merritt, 95 as well as all the wonderful shops and restaurants Westport has to offer. Short term rental possible, negotiable
