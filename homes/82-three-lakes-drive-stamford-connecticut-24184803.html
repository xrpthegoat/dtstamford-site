About this home

Sun-Drenched Colonial on a Premier Cul-de-Sac - Move-In Ready & Beautifully Updated. Welcome home to this sun-filled retreat, thoughtfully updated with style and quality that today's discerning buyers demand. Tucked near the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods, this home delivers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience. Step inside to a gracious living room with rich hardwood floors and elegant crown molding, flowing seamlessly into an expansive dining room ideal for entertaining. Slide open the glass door to your private deck, overlooking a lush, level lawn framed by a charming stone wall. At the heart of it all is a stunning, newly renovated kitchen featuring crisp white cabinetry, luxurious quartzite countertops, and stainless steel appliances. A picture window frames serene views of the backyard, making everyday living feel like a retreat. The adjoining family room offers flexible space - now a playroom, but easily a media room, office, or den. Upstairs, you'll find four generously sized bedrooms and two beautifully renovated baths, including a spacious primary suite with a private bath. The finished lower level adds even more flexible living space, perfect for a playroom, home gym, or hobby studio. This meticulously maintained home checks every box - hardwood floors throughout, central air, city water and sewer, and abundant storage. Homes like this rarely last.
