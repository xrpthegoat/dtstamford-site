About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained, recently remodeled and spacious 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath rental home located in the sought-after neighborhood of North Stamford. Thoughtfully designed for both comfort and versatility, this home offers an open-concept first floor that seamlessly connects the main living areas-perfect for entertaining or simply enjoying day-to-day life. The inviting living room is anchored by a cozy fireplace, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere during the colder months and offering the perfect place to unwind with a book or gather with loved ones. The main level features not one but two ensuite bedrooms, ideal for those seeking a first-floor primary suite, extended family accommodations, or a comfortable and private space for guests. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms connected by a Jack and Jill bathroom, offering yet another flexible living arrangement-perfect for a full-floor primary suite, hobby room, or a spacious bedroom paired with a dedicated home office. A 2-car garage provides ample storage and convenience, while the beautifully manicured property offers a serene outdoor escape with lush landscaping and room to enjoy nature. Nestled in a peaceful, residential setting, this home provides the best of both worlds-tranquility, and privacy, yet just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, parks, hiking trails, and all that North Stamford has to offer.
