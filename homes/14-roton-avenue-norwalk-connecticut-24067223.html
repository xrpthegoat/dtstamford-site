About this home

Welcome to this meticulous cottage, located in the very heart of Rowayton. This home is the ideal blend of privacy and convenience ( just moments from the beach and town center). Inside, natural light pours in through every window, displaying the beautifully restored interior. The main floor has hardwoods throughout. The first stop is the bright and airy sunroom and dining space, with tranquil pond views. Beyond the Sunroom is the main floor bedroom and full bath, a cozy living room, and a perfect kitchen and covered porch at the back of the house. The master suite is a true retreat, complete with two generous closets and a tastefully updated bathroom that perfectly balances style and function. From the covered porch, to the patio, to the perfect back yard, 14 Roton Avenue is a Rowayton classic. Primely located, loaded with timeless character, and newly refreshed, this is the ideal opportunity to experience the best of Rowayton living.
