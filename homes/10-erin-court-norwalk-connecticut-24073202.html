About this home

This is IT! The one that you have been looking, searching, waiting for: 10 Erin Court has it all from top-to-bottom. Starting with your gorgeous, brand new bespoke kitchen with new stainless steel appliances, custom hood, farm sink, eat-in island with extra storage and quartz counters - it's the kitchen you dreamed of! The living and dining room are open concept with wide board custom finish hardwood floors that shine throughout this magnificent house. An oversized, length of the house, composite deck sits just outside a slider at the back of the house. The remainder of the main floor features three bedrooms, all with hardwood and a completely remodeled bathroom with large chic tile floors and bleached wood vanity with marble tops. The shower also features all new tile and shower trim. Just a few steps downstairs is a whole another living level with hardwood as well. There is a sizeable rec room, gym or whatever your heart desires as well as an office and another full bath, once again, remodeled delightfully. This level also has access to your two car attached garage and another slider to a very large paver patio and a completely fenced in yard for privacy. The property sits at the back of a quiet cul-de-sac while only a couple minutes to everything Center Norwalk and Rte.1 have to offer! In addition, it sits in close proximity to highway access (rt.95), half a mile to sought after Rowayton, and most importantly, only 10 minutes to the beach! Schedule your showing today!
