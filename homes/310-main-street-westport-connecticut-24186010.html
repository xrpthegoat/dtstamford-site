About this home

Spacious sophistication in the heart of Downtown Westport. Perfectly positioned just moments from shopping, dining & recreation, this lovely in-town home offers a rare blend of comfort, flexibility, and location. The inviting living room with wood-burning fireplace sets the tone for relaxed gatherings and quiet evenings alike. The open-concept kitchen features granite countertops and flows seamlessly into the dining area, with French doors leading to a private stone patio - ideal for morning coffee or alfresco entertaining. The main level includes a spacious primary suite with en-suite bath, along with two additional BRs. Downstairs, the finished lower level offers additional space with two more bedrooms and a full bath, offering flexible space for guests, rec/playroom, or multi-generational living. For those seeking a professional setup, the town-approved home occupation designation allows for a separate entrance, two offices, and a waiting room - a turnkey solution for small business owners or remote professionals. With quick access to the Westport Library, Levitt Pavilion, and the town's vibrant dining & shopping scene, this property delivers both lifestyle and convenience. Nearby commuting options include the Metro North Railroad to NYC, Merritt Parkway, I-95. A rare opportunity to live and work in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after communities, or to find a home that offers private space for visitors or extended family.
