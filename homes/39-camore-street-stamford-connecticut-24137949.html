About this home

Welcome to 39 Camore Street, nestled in mid-city and in one of the most sought after neighborhoods in Stamford. This 3 bedroom home, close to everything Stamford has to offer yet a world apart. Gleaming hardwood floors, bay window and brick fireplace greet you as you walk into the livingroom. Oversized kitchen with stainless steel appliances and generous sized dining area enables you to host all of your holidays. Upstairs boasts another bedroom and full bath. Need to work from home? The lower level can accommodate all of your remote work needs, or use it as a media, gym or playroom. And one car garage makes this home a winner. Walk to houses of worship and downtown Stamford. Make your home ownership dreams a reality. House being sold "as-is".
