About this home

Picture-perfect three-bedroom home at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable West Norwalk. Set on .32 acres, this special property offers a rare combination of privacy, convenience, and natural beauty. The professionally created pollinator garden by an award-winning landscape architect unfolds in a carefully orchestrated sequence of blooms, colors, and textures throughout the seasons, creating the feeling of a magical English garden alive with butterflies and natural beauty. Inside, bright and airy living spaces flow effortlessly for easy everyday living and entertaining, while private bedrooms and versatile bonus space offer flexibility and comfort. A peaceful neighborhood setting perfect for strolls and play, just minutes from shopping, schools, trains, beaches, and all area amenities.
