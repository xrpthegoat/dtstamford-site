About this home

Exquisite N Stamford Ranch - A Rare Blend of Elegance & Tranquility. Discover refined living on pristine 1acre oasis in sought-after N Stamford. This meticulously maintained 3-4BR, 4Bath ranch offers an exceptional layout designed for effortless entertaining and serene everyday living. An inviting open floor plan showcases a sunken LR with soaring ceilings and skylights, Plus FR w/bay window & dbl-sided fireplace. The chef's kitchen is a showstopper, featuring a granite island, dbl wall ovens, Sub-Zero refrigerator, 6-burner gas cooktop, & Andersen sliders to a patio w/gorgeous views of backyard & sparkling in-ground pool. The bedroom wing includes a spacious primary suite with dual walk-in closets, luxurious full bath with radiant heat, and pool views. A rare authentic Japanese style Tatami room offers flexible use and tranquility-truly a one-of-a-kind feature. An oversized laundry Rm/pantry, featuring updated full bth w/w-in shower, and attached two-car garage complete the main level. The finished LL expands your living and entertaining space with a newly refreshed rec/play room featuring kitchenette, bar, & blt-ins, plus office & full bth-perfect for guests, hobbies, or a home gym. Impeccably updated w/fresh paint, refinished floors, Toto toilets throughout, generator-ready and city water in street. A rare opportunity to own this special North Stamford gem. Minutes away from Merritt Pkwy, shopping, & everything Stamford & Downtown entertainment district have to offer!
