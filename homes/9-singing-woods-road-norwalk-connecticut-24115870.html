About this home

Welcome to 9 Singing Woods Road, a stunning Silvermine 4-5 bedroom home where lush grounds, elegant interiors, and a resort-style pool create the perfect retreat. Set on a professionally landscaped acre, enjoy a heated in-ground gunite pool and open-concept living designed for both relaxation and entertaining. Inside, host elegant dinner parties in the formal dining room. The living room invites conversation, and the family room is a perfect place to relax. The inviting eat-in kitchen boasts stone countertops and radiant heated floors. A sunroom with French doors overlooks the pool, while a massive stone patio with built-in grill and refrigerator sets the stage for effortless outdoor living. An integrated audio system carries music inside and out. Upstairs, the private primary suite spans the entire level with a spacious bedroom, walk-in closet, and spa-inspired bath featuring a jetted tub, oversized glass shower, and radiant heated floors. A full-size office-equal in scale to the bedroom-opens to a rooftop deck with views of the pool and backyard. Additional highlights include two fireplaces, a large finished basement, and an expanded three-car garage (two in tandem). Freshly painted and meticulously maintained, the home offers Hardie board siding, a new roof, and public water. Just a short stroll from GrayBarns, minutes to both I-95 and Merritt, this exceptional residence blends timeless style with modern comfort.
