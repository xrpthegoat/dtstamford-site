About this home

Experience the good life in this beautifully maintained, conveniently located 2-3 BR 2.5 BA townhouse in the desirable Pinewood Estates complex. Unwind in the open living/dining room with accent lighting, fireplace, and sliders leading to deck. Whip up a delicious meal in the sunny eat-in-kitchen with stainless steel fridge, dishwasher & microwave, pantry, and gas range. You'll fall in love with the spacious master bedroom with nearly new full bath with stall shower, large closets, and deck overlooking the complex heated pool. Plus, 2nd bedroom with great closets and updated bathroom with two points of entry. Finished walk-out lower level can be used as 3rd bedroom, den, office, TV room, or playroom. Additional features include glistening hardwood floors throughout, crown moldings, newer washer/dryer, attached garage with extra storage space, 1 additional reserved parking spot, and plenty of guest parking. All just a short walk to Glenbrook train station, downtown Stamford restaurants & shops, and public transportation, schools, houses of worship and so much more. GREAT LOCATION! NOTE: Owner will install electric vehicle charger at request of tenant.
