About this home

Fully reimagined luxury 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse elegantly positioned at the end of a private cul-de-sac in highly sought-after Glenville, offering a sophisticated blend of refined design, exceptional craftsmanship, and turnkey living. The main level showcases an expansive open-concept living and dining area centered around a stylish wood buring fireplace with custom built-ins and sliding glass doors leading to a private deck with serene woodland views. The designer kitchen features top-of-the-line appliances, sleek cabinetry, quartz countertops that extend seamlessly to the backsplash, and elegant under-cabinet lighting. The tastefully updated powder room is conveniently located just off the kitchen. Upstairs, the stunning primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, a custom walk-in closet, and a beautifully appointed en-suite bath. A spacious second bedroom and a fully renovated hall bath featuring designer textured wallpaper and a beautifully tiled tub/shower combination complete the upper level. The finished lower level provides exceptional versatility with a spacious family room ideal for a media room, home office, or additional living space, along with a dedicated laundry area and abundant storage. Additional highlights include a detached garage, assigned parking space, and a peaceful setting surrounded by nature. This exceptional residence offers luxury, privacy, and convenience in one of the most desirable locations in Greenwich.
