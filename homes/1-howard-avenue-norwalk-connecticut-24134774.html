About this home

Beautiful, like-new, three-bedroom condo in desirable East Norwalk. This bright and spacious condo offers an open floor plan featuring a living room with gas fireplace and dining area; stunning kitchen with center island, quartz countertops, tiled backsplash and laundry area. Primary suite with walk-in closet and attractive bath with oversized shower stall; second bedroom with ensuite bath and third floor bedroom with ensuite bath offers flexibility in lifestyle needs--could be office, media room, etc. plus eave-attic space. Hardwood floors and great natural light throughout. Two-car garage under unit has private entry to condo with mudroom and extra storage space. Easy NYC commute from East Norwalk train, close to beach, popular restaurants and the sought-after East Norwalk community. A beautiful and peaceful place to call home. Close to East Norwalk RR for easy commute.
