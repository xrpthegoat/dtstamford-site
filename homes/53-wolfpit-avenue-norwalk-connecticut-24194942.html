About this home

Move right in to this beautiful 3 bed/2 full bath Colonial in Norwalk's desirable Wolfpit neighborhood! Thoughtfully improved throughout, this home features a beautifully renovated eat-in kitchen, new roof with solar panels, new high-efficiency heating and central air system, new energy-efficient windows, upgraded electrical, and new hardwood floors installed throughout for timeless style and easy maintenance. Step inside to a warm and inviting living room featuring custom built-ins and a charming stone fireplace. The versatile family room/dining room provides the perfect space for entertaining and flows seamlessly into the updated eat-in kitchen, complete with sliding doors leading to the deck-ideal for indoor-outdoor living. A full bath completes the main level. Upstairs, you'll find generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings and walk-in closet. Plus a bonus room in the basement for music, play or exercise. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping, restaurants, parks, major commuting routes, and the train, this move-in-ready home seamlessly blends classic charm with today's most desirable amenities.
