About this home

Explore a stunning rental townhome nestled in the highly desirable Shippan water community! This contemporary and chic duplex boasts 3 bedrooms and 3.5 baths, showcasing unique custom craftsmanship and an unparalleled attention to detail that will leave you in awe. The spacious open floorplan has been designed to fit the demands of modern living, boasting energy-efficient LED lighting, European triple glazed KLAR windows, and spray foam insulation for ultimate energy efficiency. The floor-to-ceiling windows not only add to the breathtaking aesthetic, but also flood the home with natural light. The first floor features a gourmet kitchen, ideal for entertaining guests or enjoying casual family meals. The second floor is dedicated to ultimate luxury, featuring a dazzling primary bedroom suite with a balcony adorned with a glass see-through railing. Two additional generously sized bedrooms can be found on this level. The third floor offers a loft/bedroom with a bathroom and an additional room with French doors leading to a balcony. Take a leisurely stroll to the picturesque West Beach and Cummings Park, where you can indulge in water activities such as swimming and paddleboarding while enjoying the scenic views. Savor delectable dishes at the renowned Brennan's Restaurant and take part in all the activities the area has to offer. The Metro North station is just minutes away, offering easy access to New York City within 50 minutes. Don't let this extraordinary opportunity pass
