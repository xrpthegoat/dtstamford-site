About this home

Welcome to 8 Merrill Road, where classic Colonial charm meets the kind of space, flow, and feel today's buyers are searching for. From the moment you step inside, it's clear this home was designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. A spacious living room, a true formal dining room, and a flexible den create a layout that adapts beautifully to your lifestyle. The kitchen features granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, adding both style and function. Hardwood floors run throughout much of the home and are in good condition, offering the next owner the option to refinish or enjoy as is. Upstairs, four generously sized bedrooms offer comfort and privacy, while the finished basement provides that extra space for a home office, gym, or recreation area. Step outside and you'll find your own private retreat, a slate patio overlooking a beautifully maintained yard, perfect for summer evenings, morning coffee, or weekend gatherings.
