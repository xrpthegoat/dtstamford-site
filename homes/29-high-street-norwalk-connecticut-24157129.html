About this home

Live in the heart of Norwalk at 29 High Street where style, convenience, and downtown energy collide. This sleek 2-bedroom, 2-full-bath condo features an open-concept layout perfect for entertaining, with one bedroom on the main level and a second private retreat downstairs ideal for guests, a home office, or added separation. Hardwood floors flow throughout, central air keeps things comfortable year-round, and in-unit washer and dryer add everyday ease. Spend your weekends enjoying the Norwalk coastline with Calf Pasture Beach and Shady Beach just a short drive away. Commuting into NYC? No problem. This is low-maintenance living with maximum lifestyle modern, walkable, and exactly where you want to be.
