About this home

The Cottages at Wire Mill is a pre-construction 55+ active adult community in North Stamford, CT, offering new single-family homes for sale behind private gates. This luxury 55-plus community features deeded single-family ownership, a first-floor primary suite option, and contemporary farmhouse architecture designed for active adults. Each home offers spacious, open interiors with dual primary suites, one on each floor, prioritizing comfort, accessibility, and easy living. Thoughtfully curated outdoor spaces and low-maintenance grounds let you spend less time on upkeep and more time enjoying life. Minutes from downtown Stamford, the Merritt Parkway, and Metro-North rail, with easy access to Greenwich, New Canaan, and Westchester County. Association fees include grounds maintenance, snow removal, water, road maintenance, and insurance for a true lock-and-leave lifestyle. Reserve now to select finishes during pre-construction.
