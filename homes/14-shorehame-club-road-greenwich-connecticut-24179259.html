About this home

A rare Shorehame Association lot with unobstructed Long Island Sound sight lines offered as an amazing opportunity to build new or reimagine the existing residence in one of Old Greenwich's most sought after waterfront settings. The .28 acre property sits within a private association that includes sandy beach access, a boat launch ramp for paddle boarding, kayaking and boating and a large communal waterfront lawn. The current home features a large living room with direct Sound views, a formal dining room, a kitchen and family room opening to a stone patio facing Long Island Sound, and a primary bedroom suite with private bath. Three additional bedrooms and full bath occupy the upper level, sharing the same water views. Gas heating, central air conditioning and an attached 2-car garage are in place. A beautiful and serene location in a private waterfront association, Long Island Sound views and steps to Tod's Point - all within minutes of the Old Greenwich train station, Old Greenwich shops and dining and schools.
