About this home

Discover classic character in this 3 bed, 1.5 bath Cape Cod. Nestled on a corner lot, this home offers both privacy and curb appeal. Step inside to beautiful hardwood floors, an abundance of natural light, and an open, welcoming layout. The kitchen is ideal for everyday life and entertaining, while the main-level primary offers convenience and comfort. Upstairs, find two bedrooms and a half-bath. The fully finished lower level adds flexibility - use as a family room, guest quarters, or home office with ample storage. Entertain outdoors on a stone patio, overlooking the private backyard. Natural gas runs the hot water heater and can be used for future heating and cooking! With easy access to shopping, public transport, and major commuting routes. Being sold "as-is".
