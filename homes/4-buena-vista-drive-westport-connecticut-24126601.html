About this home

Want to enjoy everything there is to love about the beach area, without the hassle of a flood zone? This home has everything you need. Perched at the top of Compo Hill, 4 Buena Vista Drive is a picture perfect colonial with water views and modern upgrades.This beautifully maintained home offers a traditional layout with high ceilings, arched doorways, and abundant natural light from the oversized windows. Thoughtful details include custom millwork, a butler's pantry, and spacious living/dining room great for entertaining. An open kitchen with high end appliances overlooks the family room with lots of custom built ins and a wood burning fireplace. Upstairs, the primary suite includes dual closets, wonderful water views, and a private deck creating a serene retreat. Primary bath has radiant heat. Three other upstairs bedrooms are spacious and flexible, including one with a Murphy bed for multipurpose use. In the lower level, there is a versatile hangout space and an additional bedroom which has access to a full bath. A generous mudroom for all your belongings, and 2 car tandem garage with lots of storage space rounds out this level. Enjoy the outdoors with a stone patio for dining, and admire the professional landscaping throughout the property. A whole-house generator and countless upgrades ensure comfort and peace of mind.
