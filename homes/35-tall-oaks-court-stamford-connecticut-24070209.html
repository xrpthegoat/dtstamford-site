About this home

Set on a tree-lined cul-de-sac in the coveted Laurel Ledge Park neighborhood, this beautifully-renovated Ranch offers the perfect blend of modern elegance and serene outdoor living. The property invites you to explore its thoughtfully designed outdoor spaces with charming stone walls, a cozy firepit seating area, and raised flower beds. At the heart of the meticulously-updated home is the stunning gourmet kitchen with custom-crafted cabinetry and top-of-the-line Thermador appliances. Handsome Vermont soapstone tops the island, counters, and large peninsula-style breakfast area. The open floor plan flows seamlessly between the kitchen, dining room, living room, family room, and sun room, creating an ideal space for both entertaining and day-to-day living. The primary suite features a fully-renovated bathroom with dual sinks, heated floors, and a towel warmer for ultimate comfort. Three other bedrooms and an office are located on the main level. The walk-out lower level is a treasure trove of possibilities, with a large recreation room, an exercise room, a bedroom with full bath, and even a bar and cedar-lined wine or storage closet. Laurel Ledge Park Association is a welcoming North Stamford neighborhood with a gated swimming pool and social activities for all ages throughout the year. Welcome home!
