About this home

Charming New England Bungalow Near Long Island Sound. Nestled in a picturesque setting, this delightful two-bedroom home offers the perfect blend of classic charm and modern updates. Recently renovated, the home features a brand-new kitchen with sleek countertops, stylish cabinetry, and state-of-the-art appliances, making it a dream for cooking enthusiasts. Enjoy the inviting living spaces with warm hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and cozy New England character. Finished attic and lower level provides more living space for an Office/bedroom/entertainment room or storage space. The beautifully landscaped yard and private patio with pergola provides a serene retreat, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Located in a lovely coastal community near the Long Island Sound, this home offers easy access to scenic beaches, walking trails, and charming local shops and restaurants. Whether you're looking for a year-round residence or a tranquil getaway, this bungalow is a true gem. Don't miss your chance to own a piece of classic New England charm!
