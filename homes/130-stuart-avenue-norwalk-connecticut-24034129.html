About this home

More than meets the eye in this spacious and well-kept Cape, located just moments from all major shopping, highways and local transportation. The main level offers a front foyer which opens to the living room with stone fireplace and hardwood floors. Kitchen with newer stainless steel appliances, opens to an all-season sunroom, which can be used as a dining room/den. Two generous-size first floor bedrooms with hardwood floors and a full bath. Upstairs you'll find the primary bedroom with a second full bath, along with a 4th bedroom, both with hardwood floors as well. The finished lower level makes a great rec room/play room with access to the backyard thru the bilco doors. 1 car-garage and level fenced in front and rear yard with deck, above ground pool and custom made playground. Lots of updates over the years including vinyl siding, windows, heating and hot water heater. Generator ready with portable generator included in the sale.
