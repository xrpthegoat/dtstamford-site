About this home

Completely renovated in 2025, this stunning home offers a seamless blend of modern design, quality craftsmanship, and thoughtful details throughout. Situated on approximately 1.5 acres, the property provides exceptional space, privacy, and outdoor enjoyment. Inside, the home showcases high ceilings and 6" white oak hardwood floors that create a warm and sophisticated atmosphere. The beautifully designed kitchen is equipped with brand-new high-end appliances, including a Wolf range, Sub-Zero wine cooler, Miele coffee system, delivering both style and functionality. The first floor includes a bedroom with its own en-suite bathroom, offering flexibility for guests, multi-generational living, or convenient main-level living. The second floor offers a thoughtfully designed layout, highlighted by a spacious primary suite with a spa-like en-suite bathroom, walk-in closet, and private deck overlooking the heated pool, a perfect retreat to relax and unwind. An additional bedroom features its own en-suite bathroom, while two more bedrooms share a beautifully designed full bathroom. A conveniently located laundry area completes the upper level. Bathrooms have been meticulously designed and feature radiant heated floors, offering comfort and luxury. Additional upgrades include a durable slate roof for long-term peace of mind.
