About this home

Experience exceptional waterfront living in this beautifully restored Victorian overlooking a quiet cove. Blending timeless architectural charm with modern luxury, this remarkable residence offers panoramic water views, wraparound porches, soaring 9 foot ceilings, and exquisite period details throughout. Designed for both elegant entertaining and comfortable everyday living, the chef's kitchen features a dual fuel Viking range, Sub Zero refrigerator, Miele-bosch appliances, and opens to a spacious family room with a stunning stone fireplace, French doors, and a mahogany deck overlooking the harbor. Enjoy launching your kayak from your front lawn. The formal living room with fireplace and private study also capture spectacular waterfront views. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat with vaulted ceilings, fireplace, spa like bath with soaking tub and large shower, generous walk in closets, dressing room, and breathtaking sunrise views. Two additional water view bedrooms, a beautifully renovated full bath, and second floor laundry complete the upper level. The third floor offers exceptional flexibility with a large office and a private bedroom and full bath suite showcasing sweeping coastal views. A separate guest suite including laundry above the attached two car garage with a private entrance provides ideal accommodations for guests, extended family, or a private home office.
