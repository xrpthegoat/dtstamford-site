About this home

Beautiful 1929 colonial in the heart of Shippan offers a spacious 3,700 SF home with 3 bedrooms and 3.5 baths. Sun filled rooms, newly renovated, fabulous kitchen, large deck with access from the kitchen and FR for great flow for entertaining. Primary bedroom has en-suite bathroom with heated floor and double vanities. Two additional bedrooms and full bath complete the second floor. The attic space could be used as a playroom or 4th bedroom. This beautiful home is within yards of the Stamford Yatch Club, Chelsea Piers, Stamford RR, beaches and Stamford down town. A must see!
