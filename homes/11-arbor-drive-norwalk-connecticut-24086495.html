About this home

Charming, Sun-Filled Ranch Home in South Norwalk This bright and lovely ranch-style home is located on a quiet street in South Norwalk and is sited on a level lot. Well maintained and full of charm, the home offers comfortable, convenient living both inside and out. The main level features hardwood flooring throughout and includes a spacious living room that opens to the dining area, two generous bedrooms, and a full bathroom. The kitchen has a breakfast counter that seats 2-3 and leads directly into a large, screened-in sunroom - perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Downstairs, the finished lower level offers a versatile playroom/rec room, a laundry room, ample storage space, and a professionally installed drainage system for peace of mind. The fenced backyard is ideal for outdoor living, featuring a patio area perfect for grilling, dining, and entertaining. Convenient access to I-95, South Norwalk train station, shopping, and vibrant Washington Street in downtown SoNo. This great single-family home is waiting for its new owner!!
