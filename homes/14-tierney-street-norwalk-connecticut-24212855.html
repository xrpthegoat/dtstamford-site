About this home

BIG YARD. BIG CHARM. BIG OPPORTUNITY! A labor of love? Investment opportunity? We say BOTH! This classic New England farmhouse offers an oversized, fully fenced, sun drenched yard with room to explore future expansion possibilities. Inside, discover exposed beams, a cozy brick fireplace, wood floors, vintage charm and a surprisingly spacious first floor. Make no mistake, this home will be a labor of love. It needs more than a fresh coat of paint and a little weekend work. Bringing it back to its full potential will require time, effort and investment. But for buyers who appreciate the charm and character of an older New England farmhouse, here is an opportunity to restore, renovate and create something truly their own. The first floor offers a surprisingly spacious layout with options for comfortable living, dining and gathering spaces. The kitchen provides access to the outdoors, while upstairs bedrooms and existing attic space invite buyers to explore creative possibilities for the future. An oversized, fully fenced, South- facing, sun drenched property with room to garden, entertain, relax and enjoy the outdoors. Imagine vegetable gardens bursting with fresh produce, colorful perennial beds, outdoor dining, barbecues, pets playing or simply enjoying your own private patch of sunshine. This is the kind of outdoor space that invites you to dream! Thinking bigger? The property's two-family zoning designation, generous lot and existing garage pad invite further investigation.
