About this home

A rare opportunity to own the beautiful Wilton estate that blends history, luxury and natural beauty. Just 50 miles from Manhattan, Maple Knoll Farm (4.2acres) is nestled under a majestic canopy of Maple trees and offers serenity, privacy, and an idyllic family retreat. The sophisticated main residence (5,670 SF) radiates timeless elegance, exquisite craftsmanship and meticulous attention to detail, including beautiful crown-moldings. Its large rooms, tall ceilings, abundant natural light, and panoramic garden views, seven bedrooms, six bathrooms, and a magnificent four-season sunroom were designed for intimate living and lavish entertaining. Fireplaces warm the grand living, gracious dining, and tranquil family rooms. A large, unfinished basement includes a wine cellar, billiards room and workshop. The entire home has smart automation features including surround sound, fiber networking, climate control, app based security, and irrigation. A charming one bedroom guest cottage (1,750 SF) with full kitchen, bath, laundry, and its own two car garage suits an extended family and potential rental income. Steps away, the 250 SF cabana sits by a 60foot heated pool, sauna, hot tub, and 700SF gym/fitness studio, turning everyday life into a private resort. Expansive and meticulously landscaped lawns create a tranquil backdrop for gatherings and outdoor activities in a peaceful and private setting rarely found.
