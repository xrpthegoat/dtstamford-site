About this home

This storybook stone cottage is such a perfect retreat - it's hard to believe that access to shopping, restaurants and Route 15 is so conveniently close. Built to complement the Frazier Peters main house, the carriage house boasts modern conveniences with charming details and craftsmanship. The main level includes the living/dining room with fireplace and a bay window overlooking the rear yard. Hardwood floors and 9' beamed ceilings elevate this space. Modern kitchen with washer and dryer plus a full bath complete the first floor. Upstairs you'll find an open loft bedroom with very spacious closet, plus a cozy sitting room or dressing room or office. This is a real jewel. Landlord/Owner pays all exterior maintenance, snow removal and trash. All applicants are asked to submit a Rent Spree Credit/Background report. EXCELLENT credit and references required.
