About this home

BELL ISLAND CONNECTICUT. Experience the best of waterfront living on sought-after Bell Island. Perfectly perched to capture beautiful water views, this exceptional home offers peace of mind and an unmatched coastal lifestyle. Featuring 3 spacious bedrooms, 3 full baths and 1 half bath, the home is designed for both comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining. The expansive great room with cozy fireplace serves as the heart of the home, while large windows capture the scenic water views. Step outside to two private decks, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee, hosting friends, or taking in spectacular sunsets over the water. Whether you are looking for a year-round residence or the perfect coastal retreat, this Bell Island gem combines timeless charm, generous living spaces, and an enviable location just moments from the shoreline.
