About this home

This charming three bedroom, two and one-half bathroom home is now available for sale in the desirable Pemberwick neighborhood. The entrance hall, with parquet flooring and a large closet, leads to a combined living/dining room that is great for entertaining guests. The kitchen, with an island, opens to a family room with a cozy Franklin wood-burning stove, making it the ideal spot for gatherings. A powder room completes the first floor. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom includes a bath and ample closet space. A sitting area with additional closets leads to a full bath and two more bedrooms. The deck off the family room has great outdoor space, ideal for enjoying the beautiful weather. Additional features include hardwood floors and central AC, ensuring that you will be comfortable all year round. The detached one-car garage with loft provides ample storage space. With approximately 1,700 square foot of FAR remaining, there's plenty of potential to customize this home to your liking. This home is conveniently located near parks, schools, shopping, and public transportation, making it a perfect location for anyone looking for a convenient lifestyle. Excellent opportunity to buy in Greenwich.
