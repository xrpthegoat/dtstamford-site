About this home

Welcome to 95 Orange Street. A beautiful, clean, spacious sunlit Colonial home tucked away in the Eastern part of the City; having a versatile floor plan with 4 levels of living space. Newer roof (solar), updated windows, 3/4 fenced grassy yard, detached 1 car garage with power & partially covered & open deck & vinyl siding complete the exterior. The entry foyer has a large closet; there are beautiful hardwood floors, large eat-in kitchen w/ stone counters & S/S appliances. Living room with brick fireplace, formal dining room with an attached den/office/bedroom. Upstairs there are 3 bedrooms, walk-in closet, full bath & finished attic with huge closet - could be a bedroom, playroom or office. The walkout lower level with separate exterior entry has the washer/dryer, bathroom & another finished room. House has natural gas & public water/sewer and is conveniently close to downtown, Cove Island with its beach & amenities. Don't miss this one!
