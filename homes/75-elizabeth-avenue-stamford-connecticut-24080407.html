About this home

Modern, Spacious & Bright Colonial presents an ideal canvas to create your dream home in the the heart of popular Springdale! Inviting curb appeal enhanced with stone walls & a charming front porch opens to an easy flow of generous, sun-filled rooms; the large living room features a gas fireplace and flows into a dining room designed for celebration! The family-size eat in kitchen offers plenty of work/storage space, casual dining area + sliders to the the deck and fully-fenced back lawn offering an area to play, garden, entertain + enjoy a variety of activities! Upstairs, take refuge in this surprisingly spacious primary bedroom with deluxe walk-in closet and full bath. Two additional bedrooms, 2nd full bath + attic access add to your storage, comfort & function. A Full basement, attached 1-car garage, newer HVAC, Windows, Central a/c and economical natural gas heat complete this cheerful Springdale opportunity! Enjoy walking to popular Toquam Magnet Elementary School & Dolan Middle School, as well as neighborhood restaurants, shops, attractions + Metro North Train!
