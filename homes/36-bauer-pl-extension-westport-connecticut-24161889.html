About this home

Better than new! This beautifully appointed 4-level home blends contemporary design with warmth, comfort, and thoughtful upgrades throughout. The heart of the home is a cook's dream kitchen with gourmet appliances, a full pantry, and a massive center island showcasing stunning finishes. Designed for both beauty and function, it opens seamlessly to the bright, welcoming family room. Together, these spaces flow onto the patio for easy indoor-outdoor living. A living room and dining room complete the inviting main level. Upstairs, the fabulous primary suite features custom closets and a spa-like bath. Two bedrooms share a chic Jack & Jill bath, while a 4th bedroom is ensuite. A laundry room is conveniently located on this level. The 3rd floor offers a bedroom, full bath, and a spacious flexible living area-perfect for guests, an office, playroom, or recreation space. The lower level is a true wellness retreat with a fully customized gym, sauna, and full bath. Perfectly set in a sought-after neighborhood, it's just a short walk to Long Lots Elementary and minutes to shopping, dining, and the Greens Farms train. With more than $100K in next-level enhancements, this residence has evolved into a truly spectacular home. Beautifully decorated, warm, and welcoming, this home is ready to enjoy from day one. Modern conveniences include city water, city sewer, natural gas, and generator readiness.
