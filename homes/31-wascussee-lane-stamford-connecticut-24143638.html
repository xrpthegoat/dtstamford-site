About this home

Rare standalone townhome in Bishop Meadows - lives like a single-family home with condo convenience. Private driveway, quiet setting, and an easy walk to the beach. Recent updates include roof (2024), water heater (2023), dishwasher (2024), and refrigerator (2025). Bright main level features a living room with fireplace and sliders to the deck, dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with white cabinets, granite counters, and stainless steel appliances. Upstairs, the primary suite offers double cedar closets and a sleek bath. Two additional bedrooms share a hall bath. Lower level adds impressive space with high ceilings, built-ins, and great storage - perfect for a family room, gym, office, or playroom. Separate laundry room with utility sink. Quiet, well-managed community with pool and clubhouse. Minutes to I-95, Metro North, Chelsea Piers, Cove Island Park, and downtown Stamford.
