About this home

Incredible location on a wonderful cul-de-sac! This spacious warm 4 bedroom 3 bath raised ranch is located on the Norwalk/Westport line and sits nicely on a lovely .31 acre that offers both privacy and convenience to transportation and shopping. The easy open floor plan features a large living room with fireplace, dining room, and eat-in-kitchen with access to outdoor deck and expansive backyard. The private sleeping area includes the primary bedroom with full bath and 2 additional bedrooms and bath. The spacious lower level allows for easy entertaining and includes large family room with fireplace, 4th bedroom/office/exercise room, full bath, and access to the outdoors. Additional features include hardwood floors, generator, central air, oversize 2 car garage with separate work area, and multi generational living space. Enjoy the convenience of this home ready for your personal touch and located 5 minutes from all schools and both Westport/Norwalk shopping, restaurants, Train, parks/beach, and I-95/Merritt Parkway.
