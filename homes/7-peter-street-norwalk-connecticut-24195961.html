About this home

Welcome to 7 Peter Street, a charming and updated home tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in one of the Norwalk&apos;s peaceful neighborhoods. This 4-bedroom gem combines character and functionality, with many major updates already completed for years of worry-free ownership. The upper level features a primary bedroom with a walk-in closet, a second bedroom, and a shared full bath. The mail level offers hardwood floors throughout, a cozy living room, two additional bedrooms (or a dining room) and a renovated full bath. The kitchen, ready for your personal touches, opens to a bright sunroom that can serve as a dining room area or family, room. Just off the sunroom, a brand-new deck with solar lighting overlooks garden beds and a level, fenced-in backyard, perfect for entertaining. Additional space includes a heated lower-level extra room with exterior access, ideal as a private office or quiet retreat, a heated one-car garage and a clean, open basement currently used as a gym, offering excellent potential to create additional living space, a playroom, or whatever best suits your lifestyle. Conveniently located near I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and the train, and just minutes from restaurants, shopping, beaches and schools. Photos coming soon!
