About this home

Designer owned contemporary | Private pool & spa | resort-style living. Some homes are listed-this one was designed. Privately set at the end of a cul-de-sac in one of Stamford's sought-after neighborhoods, this distinctive contemporary was completely reimagined inside and out by its designer-owner. Dramatic vaulted ceilings, walls of glass and skylights create an extraordinary sense of light, space and architectural character. The modern kitchen features custom cabinetry, quartz countertops and premium appliances. A spa-inspired primary suite offers a private retreat, while a separate-entry first-floor bedroom suite provides flexibility for guests or extended stays. The finished lower level adds a gym, entertaining space with surround sound, and another bedroom suite with new bath. The versatile attic lounge features a dedicated refreshment station with a compact refrigerator/freezer, microwave, & open shelving, complemented by a large picture window and private balcony creating an inviting space for relaxation and entertaining; three fireplaces add warmth. Outdoors, an in-ground pool & spa, expansive deck+outdoor kitchen create a private resort-style setting, with whole-house Sonos extending to the pool. Conveys: Sonos, surround sound, light fixtures, drapes and hardware, built-in unit and TV. Minutes to Whole Foods, dining, Scalzi park (new tennis courts) downtown Stamford and Metro-North to Grand Central. A truly individual offering.
