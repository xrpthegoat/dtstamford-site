About this home

Downtown living does not have to mean giving up privacy and luxury. Instead, embrace indoor/outdoor contemporary living in this traditional modern colonial energy efficient smart townhome with AV pre-wiring in gym and patio areas and WiFi controlled hydro air and alarm systems. Enjoy all that downtown Greenwich has to offer with its world class shops, dining venues and walking distance to the Metro North train. Take a stroll in neighboring beautiful Bruce park or a short drive to the charming beach at Tod's Point. This brand new custom built masterpiece showcasing high ceilings, designer lighting, and impeccable craftsmanship boasts approximately 4,000 sf with 6 bedrooms, 6.5 bathrooms and 3 fireplaces thoughtfully designed over 4 sun lit floors with everything you could want in a home, including an elevator that stops on all 4 floors. Matching the superior quality and attentive detail of the interior, the exterior features cedar siding, stone chimneys, composite slate roof and copper gutters with gracious bluestone patio accentuated by outdoor kitchen and green space abutted by two car custom outfitted heated garage with EV charger, cabinetry, bike racks and storage. Lower level hosts gym, second laundry, wine cellar and guest quarters.
