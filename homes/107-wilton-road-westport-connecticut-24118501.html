About this home

Historic Elegance Meets Modern Luxury at 107 Wilton Road. Step into timeless sophistication at this stunning 1740 Historic home, masterfully renovated and expanded in 2024/2025 to offer the perfect blend of old-world charm and contemporary comfort. Located within walking distance to vibrant downtown Westport, this residence places you at the heart of one of Connecticut's most desirable communities. An Outdoor Living at Its Finest. Enjoy a professionally landscaped, fenced backyard designed for relaxation and entertaining. The expansive terrace features an outdoor kitchen ideal for summer gatherings, while the screened porch offers a serene retreat for year-round enjoyment. Gourmet Kitchen & Thoughtful Design are at the heart of this home, a beautifully appointed kitchen with a spacious center island, premium appliances, and a built-in office nook-perfect for today's flexible lifestyle. A Spacious & Versatile Living space, with 4 bedrooms and 4 full baths, plus a generous lower-level space that opens directly to the backyard, you'll have room to suit every need-whether it's a fifth bedroom, media room, or a large home office. Be the First to move in! This is a rare opportunity to live in a piece of Westport history, reimagined for modern living. From its classic architecture to its cutting-edge updates, 107 Wilton Road offers a lifestyle that's both refined and relaxed. Landscaping and snow removal included in the monthly rent.
