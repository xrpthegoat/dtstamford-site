About this home

Beautiful and sumptuous, this totally renovated end unit Charles Cove townhouse features all the "bells and whistles"! From its oversized 1-1/4" Nacarado Quartzite kitchen island which serves to "anchor" the kitchen space... to the spacious seating area enhanced by a gleaming white cathedral ceiling, wood-burning fireplace and sliders leading to the private, upper deck, this open and light condo is "a condo to love". The dining area is delineated by a bright, bay window with southern exposure and built-in "buffet" which provides storage as well as housing two (2) Subzero Refrigerator Drawers plus a separate Marvel Wine Cooler. The kitchen is fully applianced with primarily Wolf products including an Induction Cooktop, Single Built-in Wall Oven, separate Steam Oven and a Microwave Drawer. The dishwasher is by Miele and there is a InSinkErator garbage disposal in the stainless 30" Vigo Farm Sink. There are two refrigerators in the unit - a Gaggenau in the kitchen area, plus an LG Stainless Steel Refrigerator with ice maker and interior water dispenser located in the lower level laundry. A second bedroom is currently being used as a den/family room with built-in desk and oversized closet and is located on the main living level. A full-sized marble hall bath with stall shower is adjacent to the den. ("Remarks" are continued below.)
