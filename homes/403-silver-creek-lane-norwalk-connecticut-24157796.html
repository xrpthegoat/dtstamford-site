About this home

Found in the heart of historic Silvermine, The Falls is Norwalk's premier gated community of free-standing townhomes. The two story atrium window is the focal point of this home. Additionally, there is a wood burning fireplace, sliders to three decks and a patio - step outside from all three levels! The large main floor living room allows for versatile use of the space. There is also a formal dining room, eat-in-kitchen, remodeled powder room and private office. Hardwood floors recently refinished and new tile in the kitchen and in the two story foyer with palladium window. The open second floor landing provides an addiotnal vantage point to enjoy the natural light and view provided by the atrium window. The Primary Suite features a spa-like bath, large walk in closet plus a balcony. Two additional bedrooms, additonal full bath and laundry room complete the second level. Newer wall to wall carpet throughout the second level. Beautifully finished lower level has a wall of full sized windows and sliders to a private patio. The interior and exterior have both been recently painted. The pool will be open before we know it!!
