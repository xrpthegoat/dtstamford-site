About this home

This beautiful five-bedroom farmhouse in the highly sought after Old Hill area offers privacy and timeless curb appeal! Freshly painted with newly installed wood floors, this home is filled with natural light and warmth. A dramatic two story foyer with a sweeping staircase welcomes you inside. The large modern kitchen, a true centerpiece to this home has modern finishes and generous space for both everyday living and entertaining. It flows seamlessly into an adjacent great room/playroom. Enjoy dinners in the charming dining room, relax by the fire in the oversized living room or host in the expansive family room featuring beams, vaulted ceilings and fireplace. Each room truly an inviting space to gather. A three-season screened in porch extends your living space and beautifully brings the outdoors in. The main-level also includes a spacious mudroom and laundry room with separate entrance offering functionality and convenience. A bedroom and full bath on the main level provide flexibility for a home office or guest suite. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features a spa-inspired bath, along with three additional well-proportioned bedrooms. The lower level offers ample storage. Outside, the flat yard is perfect for unwinding, complete with a fire-pit for cozy evenings and memorable gatherings. Just minutes to town, shopping and commuting routes, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Available Immediately.
