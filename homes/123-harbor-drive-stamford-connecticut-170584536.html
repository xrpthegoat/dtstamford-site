About this home

Direct waterfront townhouse at Palmer Landing with beautiful views overlooking the marina. The updated kitchen features granite countertops and new stainless steel appliances and opens to a spacious dining area. This 2-bedroom, 2 full and 1 half bath home is filled with natural light and offers an open layout with hardwood floors on the main level. The living room with fireplace leads out to a private balcony, a great spot for coffee in the morning or relaxing at the end of the day. The first-floor primary suite includes a bathroom with whirlpool tub. Upstairs, the second bedroom has good closet space and its own full bath. There is also a powder room, laundry room, and an attached one-car garage. Additional updates include newer windows and washer and dryer. Rent includes heat, water, and access to the community pool. Conveniently located just minutes to downtown Stamford, Harbor Point, beaches, Metro North, and major highways.
