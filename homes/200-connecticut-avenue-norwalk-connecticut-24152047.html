About this home

Recently renovated Class A office building under new ownership, positioned along Norwalk's premier Route 1 retail and amenity corridor. This 10-story, property features seven floors of premium office space multiple levels of covered parking, with flexible configurations from 590 SF to 26,743 SF including full-floor opportunities. Multi-million dollar recent upgrades include a modern lobby, full-service cafe, on-site fitness center, and three elevators. The building offers central HVAC, full fire sprinkler protection, and public water/sewer service. Premium amenities include a 303-space covered parking garage (2.3:1,000 SF ratio), multiple loading docks, and approximately 50 common-area restrooms. Direct I-95 and Route 1 access with nearby SoNo, Rowayton, and Darien Metro-North stations provide seamless connectivity to Fairfield County and New York City. Surrounded by popular dining and retail destinations, this professionally managed property is ideal for corporate, professional, medical, and technology users seeking scalable, move-in ready space in Norwalk's most connected office environment.
