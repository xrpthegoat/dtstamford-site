About this home

Experience refined living in this serene and supremely private residence, ideally located on one of the quietest streets great for quiet walks and bike rides, while remaining just minutes from Wilton shopping and everyday conveniences. Meticulously maintained and truly turnkey, this exceptional home offers the peace of mind of recently updated major systems-simply unpack and enjoy. The grand family room sets the tone with soaring ceilings, fireplace, and abundant natural light, creating a sophisticated yet inviting space. The Chef's kitchen is a culinary masterpiece, featuring a professional KUCHT gas range, additional wall oven, a generous center island with prep sink, and ample cabinetry designed for both function and style. The fully finished lower level adds significant living space and versatility, showcasing a large recreation room, a private office, a dedicated craft studio, and an expansive pantry with custom built-in closets-perfect for effortless organization. With two fireplaces on the main level, curated finishes throughout, and a setting that blends quiet elegance with convenience, this home offers a rare opportunity to live beautifully. A true forever home located in highly desired Wilton CT.
