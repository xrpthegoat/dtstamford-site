About this home

Turn key & beautiful! This 4-bed, 2.5-bath, 2600+ SqFt oversized ranch is in Norwalk's desirable Silvermine area. With a large level lot in a charming neighborhood, the home offers spacious living, thoughtful updates, and a flexible floor plan. All within walking distance to Mercantile, coffee shops, local businesses, and sought-after restaurants. Enter into the inviting living/dining room featuring hardwood floors, a fireplace, and bay windows. Adjacent, the oversized kitchen is a chef's dream with Corian countertops, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar, island and a large eat-in area with vaulted ceilings & plenty of natural light. Just off the kitchen is a spacious family room with half bath & French doors leading to a freshly painted deck overlooking the expansive backyard-perfect for entertaining, yard games, BBQ's.. you name it! The private primary suite wing features hardwood floors, a vaulted ceiling with fan & a beautiful full bath. The opposite wing offers three additional bedrooms with hardwood floors & a full bath. The finished lower level provides additional media/rec/play space plus a dedicated laundry room w/ utility sink & folding station. Recent improvements include a new oil tank, hot water heater, washer, refrigerator, dishwasher, driveway, walkway, most siding, interior paint, deck paint & mature landscaping. Located 15 minutes from 4 train stations. 1 hour from NYC. Dont Miss Out.. This is a well maintained and updated home. Truly move-in ready
