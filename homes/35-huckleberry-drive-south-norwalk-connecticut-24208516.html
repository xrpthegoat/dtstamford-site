About this home

Set on a quiet, low-traffic street in one of West Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods, this remodeled modern split-level home offers an exceptional combination of space, quality, and flexibility. With 5 bedrooms, 4 full and 1 half baths, the home comfortably accommodates a large family, multigenerational living, guests, or those who simply want room to spread out. The heart of the home is a beautiful kitchen featuring Calacatta gold marble countertops, an 8-foot center island, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Viking gas range, and two dishwashers. The kitchen has ample natural light and opens directly into an open living room/dining room, creating an ideal setting for entertaining and everyday family life. Multiple flexible living spaces add to the home's versatility, including a den on the first floor along with a spacious media/great room that can be used as a home office, playroom, or entertainment area. The rooms accommodate a variety of lifestyles. Step outside to a two-tier deck that features a four-person hot tub, pergola, and fire pit. The 1.5 acre tiered yard is fully fenced and abuts Whispering Oaks open space and walking trails. The outdoor space provides a peaceful setting for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying the privacy of the property. The home combines substantial living space with high-quality finishes and a layout that works equally well for everyday living and entertaining. Fully furnished rental option also available.
