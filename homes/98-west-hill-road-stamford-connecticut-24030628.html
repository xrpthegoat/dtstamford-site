About this home

Highly desirable residence in Stamford's Westover neighborhood (Greenwich line). This gorgeous single-level residence boasts an open floor plan, which enhances the flow & spaciousness of the living areas. A highlight of the home is the stunning custom kitchen featuring quartz counters, a large island, stainless steel appliances, and new cabinets. An oversized family room with multiple windows and high ceilings, new carpet, and a custom built fireplace offers a cozy and inviting space for relaxation and gatherings. French doors lead out to a sun-filled patio and a large landscaped yard, complete with a newer irrigation system for easy maintenance. The living room features a second fireplace and custom built-ins, adding character and additional living space options. Three well-appointed bedrooms provide ample accommodation. This single family home offers amazing convenience and proximity to Greenwich, CT.
