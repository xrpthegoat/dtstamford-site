About this home

Welcome to 201 Cognewaugh Road, a transitional retreat on two acres in mid-country. Step through the door and be greeted by a seamless blend of historic character and contemporary style. The renovated island kitchen is a chef's dream, while the formal dining room and adjacent reading room with wetbar awaits your next gathering. The office offers a quiet spot for productivity, and the guest suite provides flexible space for company or downtime. This sizable property boasts four bedrooms plus guest suite, as well as a two bedroom cottage, and a barn with two additional garage bays. Outdoors, find relaxation at your own private oasis with a heated in-ground pool surrounded by generous flat yard, perfect for coastal Connecticut summers. Enjoy the seasons changing from enclosed porch or warm up near one of the three fireplaces. Amenities abound at this charming abode which must be experienced to appreciate. This compound captures the essence of comfortable, elegant living. Don't miss the opportunity to make this unique home marrying past and present your very own.
