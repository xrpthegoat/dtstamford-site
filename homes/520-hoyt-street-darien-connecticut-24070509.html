About this home

This classic Colonial blends timeless elegance with modern comforts. The sunlit interiors feature a formal living room, dining room, and a cozy family room with a fireplace. The gourmet kitchen, with high-end appliances and a large island, serves as the heart of the home. Upstairs includes a serene primary suite with bath, three additional bedrooms, and another full bath. The finished lower level offers flexible space for a playroom, gym, or media room. Outside, enjoy the private backyard with a stone patio, screened porch, and deck. Conveniently located near shops, schools, and train stations, this home is a must-see!
