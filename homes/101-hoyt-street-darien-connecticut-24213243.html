About this home

Space, warmth and an opportunity to make it your own await at 101 Hoyt Street. Set on approximately 0.4 acres, this home offers a spacious layout and three fireplaces that add character throughout. A generous kitchen connects to the family room, creating a welcoming gathering place for everyday living and entertaining. The spacious primary suite features a private full bath and its own fireplace-a cozy retreat at the end of the day. This home offers flexibility for guests, a home office or creative pursuits, while an attached two-car garage adds everyday convenience. The home also features a new roof. Located within walking distance of Holmes Elementary School, this home combines space and potential with a convenient Darien setting. Bring your vision and make it your own.
