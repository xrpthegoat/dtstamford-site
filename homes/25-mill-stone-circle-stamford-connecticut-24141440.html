About this home

Welcome to an updated colonial style home in the highly sought-after Saw Mill neighborhood! This beautifully maintained 4-bed, 2.5-bath home sits on a quiet North Stamford cul-de-sac surrounded by nature. Enjoy peaceful, private living while being just 17 minutes from the Stamford train station and an easy ride to Grand Central. The hardwood floors flow through two inviting living rooms, one with a wood-burning fireplace, and a bright kitchen with two ovens, granite counters, and the home being newly converted to propane. From the kitchen, step onto the expansive screened porch overlooking a huge, flat, private backyard with a stone patio, raised garden beds, and a hot tub. A first-floor office with built-ins provides flexibility for work or a potential fifth bedroom. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features a dressing area and en-suite bath, with three additional bedrooms completing the level. Move-in ready and located in a fantastic community. Don't miss this one!
