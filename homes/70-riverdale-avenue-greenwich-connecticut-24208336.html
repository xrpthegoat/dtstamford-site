About this home

Elegant and impeccably finished, this 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse blends classic detail with modern comfort - hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, custom millwork, and sun-filled rooms throughout. The heart of the home is a chef's eat-in kitchen with a center island and breakfast bar, stainless steel appliances, and granite countertops, flowing seamlessly into the dining room and out to a private rear deck. The open, flexible floor plan connects beautifully proportioned living spaces designed for everyday living and hosting. Upstairs, both bedrooms live like suites, offering generous walk-in closets and private baths. An attached one-car garage plus an assigned space add everyday convenience. All of this in an unbeatable location just moments from transportation, shops, and dining.
