About this home

Summer in Westport! Clean living at its best. A home prepared with non-chemical, organic products. The owners live here year round with ZERO FRAGRANCE. Owners are extremely sensitive to fragrance so only CLEAN LIVING TENANTS take interest. Organic, non fragrance, non chemical, hypo-allergenic product home. Products will remain in the home for summer tenant to utilize during their stay. This is available June 15th through August 24th. The most spectacular home with all things luxury, set on Nash's pond, with a 3 story elevator AND tremendous "island like" location. Tranquil, organic and calming with a particularly comfortable modern interior. Temperature controlled home, saltwater pool with a changing area and fenced in on the main grounds. Spa-like, walls of windows overlooking the pond and oasis location. Similar to your own island off Woodside Avenue. It is a beautiful setting all around. Gourmet kitchen on the main level with open floor plan living and dining room. Top floor entertainment area, with balcony overlooking Southeastern views. Explore with canoes, kayaks, and all water play on the private dock set on the Pond. 5 minutes to downtown Westport, 1.9 Miles to Metro North, 10 Minutes to Compo Beach, Long Shore, Golf/Tennis/Beach. Cultural treats to the Levitt Pavillon for the performing arts and The Westport Playhouse. 75 Minutes to Mid-Town Manhattan. Only CLEAN LIVING TENANTS. Please see Tenant Responsibility List in docs. Have a wonderful summer!
