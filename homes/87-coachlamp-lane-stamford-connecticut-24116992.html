About this home

Nestled on a peaceful cul-de-sac and set on a flat quarter acre lot, this spacious 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home has an abundance of living space across two floors. As you step in you are greeted by a beautiful entryway that leads to a generously sized living room perfect for gatherings or quiet evenings in. A large and airy kitchen features an abundance of counter space ample shelving with an adjacent formal dining room. Cozy family room boasting a fireplace and a skylight, making it the perfect spot to unwind. An enclosed deck where you can enjoy peaceful, starry nights in the privacy of your backyard. Upstairs, the expansive primary bedroom is a true retreat, offering a large en-suite bathroom and a walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms share a full bathroom. The basement is a versatile space, perfect for movie nights, a home gym, or additional playroom space. Located just a 5-minute drive from downtown Stamford, it's also close to Scalzi Park, a nearby golf course, and several schools. Easy 8-minute to the Metro North Station makes your commute to New York City a breeze.
