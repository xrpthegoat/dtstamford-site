About this home

Built in 2014, this modern farmhouse offers timeless design and an easy flow on a flat, private, fully fenced 1-acre with possible pool site. A welcoming front porch opens to a front-to-back living room with impressive stone fireplace. The sun-filled dining room features a coffered ceiling, paneling & Serena & Lily chandelier. The chef's kitchen is beautifully appointed with high-end appliances, quartz countertops and center island, plus abundant storage, incl. walk-in pantry. Just off the kitchen, a vaulted family room with oak beams and a gas fireplace includes space for everyday dining and flows seamlessly to a stone patio, ideal for outdoor dining and summer grilling. A ground-floor bedroom with full bath provides flexibility, while a custom office with work space and built-ins could serve as a den, creating the option for a main-level suite ideal for in-laws, guests, or an au pair. A shiplap-lined mudroom with cubbies includes a chic powder room and leads directly to the 2-car garage, prewired for EV. Upstairs, a 2nd family room offers separate hangout or study space. The primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, 2 walk-in closets, and a spa-like marble bath with soaking tub and steam shower. Additional bedrooms include one en-suite and two with a Jack-and-Jill bath. Finished lower-level is ready for gym equipment or toys, and a walk-up attic offers expansion possibilities. Set in a biking & dog walker's neighborhood close to town, train, beach, and award-winning schools
