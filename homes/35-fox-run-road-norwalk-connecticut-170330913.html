About this home

This gracious center hall colonial is ready to become your forever home! 4 bedroom colonial in fabulous neighborhood! The interior welcomes you with an elegant foyer. Gleaming oak floors throughout. Formal living room with fireplace, den with French doors leads to screened in porch and deck. Eat in kitchen with granite counter tops and stainless appliances, custom cabinetry and large island. Bright formal dining room completes the lower level. This house has great flow for entertaining inside and out. Master with ensuite bath and walk in closet. Three additional bedrooms one with half bath and walk in closet. Huge finished lower level family/rec room with separate entrance. Enough space to create a bonus bedroom. Two car garage with access to kitchen. Situated on 19 acres of conservation land. Enjoy the privacy in your back yard with stone fire pit. Room for possible pool. Steps to Fox Run Elementary and Lakota Oaks, 66 acres with trails on a park like setting. Only one mile to Oak Hill Tennis and Golf course. Minutes to 95, Rt 7 and Merritt Parkway.
