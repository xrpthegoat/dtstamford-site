About this home

A rare opportunity to experience turnkey living in a private, storybook setting. This four-bedroom, 2.5-bath guest house is set on 3.5 acres of gated, beautifully landscaped grounds, offering a peaceful escape with a refined, luxury feel. Designed to bring the outdoors in, all three upper bedrooms open to an expansive 40-foot deck, while the newly updated primary suite on the main level features direct access to outdoor living. French doors from the primary bedroom, kitchen, dining and living spaces create effortless flow to the deck-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. Overlooking bucolic grounds and a serene, fountained koi pond, the home is filled with natural light and tranquil views from nearly every room. The primary bath features a striking natural rock wall rain shower with Carrara marble, complemented by an updated upstairs bath with a double vanity. Additional highlights include smart home technology, 2G fiber internet, Bose surround sound, stainless Viking appliances, and a private driveway for four or more cars. Pets welcome. Ideally located just one mile from Wilton town center, with easy access to top-rated schools, restaurants, shopping, trails and major highways. Great commuter location. Enjoy the serenity and peaceful setting, updated features and exceptional convenience. Heated/cooled storage included.
