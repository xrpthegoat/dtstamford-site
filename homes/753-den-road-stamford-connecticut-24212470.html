About this home

Welcome to 753 Den Road, a beautifully situated Stamford residence offering an exceptional combination of space, comfort, character, and outdoor living on over one acre of property. This inviting home features 3 spacious bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, with generously sized rooms, ample closet space, and a functional layout designed for both everyday living and entertaining. A welcoming foyer creates an impressive first impression and leads into warm, well-proportioned living areas. The kitchen features a charming breakfast nook for casual dining, while the separate dining room provides an elegant setting for family gatherings and special occasions. The home's inviting atmosphere is enhanced by a wood-burning fireplace, creating the perfect setting for cozy evenings. A spacious bonus family room provides additional flexibility for entertaining, relaxing, a home office, playroom, or media space. The unfinished basement offers excellent storage and additional potential for future customization. Outdoor living is one of the property's standout features. Multiple-level decks create wonderful spaces for dining, entertaining, relaxing, and enjoying the peaceful surroundings. The expansive, level yard sits on over one acre, offering plenty of space for recreation, gardening, outdoor gatherings, or creating your own private retreat. The property also features an in-ground sprinkler system for convenient lawn and landscape irrigation.
