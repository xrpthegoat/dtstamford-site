About this home

Quite possibly the best renovation opportunity this year! This 3-4 bedroom, 2 full bath 1940's cape awaits your vision to polish this home to perfection. Sited beautifully at the end of a most desirable Old Hill cul de sac; enjoy privacy and build equity in a convenient neighborhood of high end homes. The flat, fenced .32 acre property offers room to play and space to entertain outdoors. The antique barn is not only an oversized two-car garage, but the vaulted room above can serve any need you have for additional living space. Served by public water - Natural gas and public sewers are in the street - amenities on every buyers "must have" list. The property is located in a designated "limited historic district", and the sale is subject to Probate Court approval.
