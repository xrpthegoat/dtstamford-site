About this home

Exuding modern luxury and classic architectural charm, this distinctive West Norwalk residence stands as a truly unique offering in today's market. Meticulously maintained and enhanced with extensive recent upgrades, the home features a light-filled open-concept floor plan tailored for both elevated daily living and grand-scale entertaining. At its heart sits a gourmet eat-in kitchen equipped with premium granite countertops and a professional grade gas range for the culinary enthusiast. The primary suite serves as a private sanctuary, complete with a personal balcony, walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired bath featuring radiant heated flooring, a steam shower, and a Jacuzzi tub. High-value additions in recent years include energy-efficient solar panels to be fully paid off by the seller at closing alongside an underground electric pet fence, integrated audio system, updated central AC, newer furnace/ water heater, full security and more. A full walk-out lower level offers over 1,300 additional square feet of potential living space. Ideally situated on the Darien border near Metro-North, major routes, and local dining, this exceptional turnkey property offers effortless access to New York City in about an hour.
