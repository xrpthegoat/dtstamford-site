About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated tri-level townhouse in a well-maintained Glenbrook complex with a pool, ideally located near the train station and just minutes from downtown Stamford, Cummings Beach, and Cove Island Beach. This spacious 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers an open floor plan, hardwood floors throughout, a private balcony, and an attached garage. The newly renovated kitchen is thoughtfully designed with HanStone quartz countertops and backsplash, a Kohler workstation sink with integrated cutting board and colander, pull-down faucet, Miele refrigerator, GE cooktop and dishwasher, built-in stainless steel convection oven, and Frigidaire microwave/air fryer with exterior venting. Additional improvements include GE washer and dryer, Stiebel Eltron tankless water heater, new retractable screen storm doors, and custom basement storage with sliding drawers. Smart-home features include 1 Gbps WiFi with Eero mesh router, Ring security system with exterior and interior cameras, biometric smart locks, Mysa smart thermostats for heat, Cielo BreezeMax controls for A/C, Energy Star-rated dimmable LED ceiling lights, smart Lutron roller shades, and custom blackout shades in the bedrooms. All windows have also been enhanced with 3M Sun Control film, providing UV reduction and helping protect the hardwood floors while improving energy efficiency. A move-in-ready home combining modern comfort, convenience, and an exceptional Glenbrook location.
