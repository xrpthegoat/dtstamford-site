About this home

Prime in-town location meets classic style in this turn-key 2BR/1BA first-floor unit at 39 Church St. Situated steps from local dining, shopping, and everyday conveniences, this home offers unmatched accessibility. Inside, rich dark hardwood floors run throughout the bright, open living and dining spaces. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, stone countertops, tiled backsplash, and a separate pantry. A spacious primary bedroom boasts a walk-in closet, while the flexible second bedroom is ideal for guests or a home office. Complete with in-building laundry facilities and off-street parking, this 988 sq ft residence offers ideal, low-maintenance living in the heart of town.
