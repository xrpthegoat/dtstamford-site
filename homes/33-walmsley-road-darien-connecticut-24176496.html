About this home

Nestled on one of Noroton Heights' most sought-after streets, this sun-drenched coastal charmer seamlessly blends classic appeal with thoughtful modern updates. With newly renovated bathrooms, a new deck, and beautifully refinished hardwood floors, this home captures the essence of Darien living with an enviable walk-to-everything location. The fully fenced, level backyard has been lovingly landscaped, creating a private outdoor retreat ideal for entertaining, play, or simply relaxing. The lower level expands the home's living space with 440 square feet of finished recreational area. An adjoining unfinished space provides a dedicated laundry area and abundant storage, bringing the total lower-level footprint to approximately 800 square feet per the tax card. Enjoy an unbeatable location just a short walk to the Noroton Heights train station, Darien Commons, and Heights Crossing, where you'll find an array of restaurants, cafes, boutiques, and everyday conveniences. With easy access to I-95 and close proximity to Middlesex Middle School and Darien High School, this special property offers the perfect balance of neighborhood charm and modern convenience. Don't miss the opportunity to make this gem of a house your new home.
