About this home

NEWLY RENOVATED INTERIOR, NEW ROOF & FRESHLY PAINTED! Searching for absolute tranquility? Your retreat awaits. This distinctive mid-century modern residence is privately sited on a lush, secluded property designed for both peaceful living & effortless entertaining. The grounds are a true sanctuary, featuring a sparkling in-ground pool, a charming cabana & a relaxing hot tub, perfect for summer gatherings. Step inside and you'll immediately be struck by the home's architectural character and bright, airy atmosphere. The new kitchen & bath along w/soaring ceilings w/exposed beams, oversized windows that bathe the interiors in natural light will delight. Generously sized rooms and abundant closet and storage space ensure functionality meets style at every turn. Enjoy your morning coffee or unwind with a good book on one of several private patios, shaded by mature wisteria. This is a home that invites connection-whether you're hosting a lively BBQ with friends or relaxing in the hot tub on a crisp winter evening. Tucked away in one of Wilton's most desirable neighborhoods, you'll enjoy peace and privacy while being just minutes from the heart of Wilton Village. Everything you need is close at hand-top-rated schools, grocery stores, restaurants, banks, child care & medical facilities. For commuters, the location is unbeatable, with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and Metro North trains to New York City.
