About this home

Welcome to this Charming 3 Bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, Cape Cod Home in desirable Cranbury Neighborhood, offering tranquility and comforts. This Gorgeous House offers 1,799 Sqft + 768 sqft Finished basement of versatile living space on a private 0.56 acre lot. Inside, the home features Hardwood floors, New Ductless air conditionings installed, warm three season porch. This home delivers the perfect layout : formal Dining room, additional family room. Adjacent is the spacious kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry. The second floor has two generous bedrooms, full bath and large attic off the Master Bedroom. The first floor bedroom can also be used as a home office and/ or playroom. Outside, a spacious backyard provides a private oasis for outdoor activities and entertainment. Enjoy Summer Barbecues, s'mores on a fire pit and gardening, this home is ready to welcome you.
