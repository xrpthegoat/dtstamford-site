About this home

Location, Location, Location! This well-maintained unit offers an unbeatable location just off Connecticut Avenue, close to shopping, restaurants, public transportation, beaches, parks, golf courses, The SoNo Collection Mall, Metro-North Train Station, I-95, and everything Norwalk has to offer. With its own private entrance, this spacious unit features a bright living room, a generously sized eat-in kitchen with a pantry, a dining area, and a convenient half bath on the main level. Additional highlights include central air conditioning, hardwood floors, fresh paint, security system wiring, attic and basement storage, plus extra parking spaces.
