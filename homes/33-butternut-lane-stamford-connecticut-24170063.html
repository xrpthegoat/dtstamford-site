About this home

Architect designed Contemporary. Modern Serenity Meets Architectural Charm. Experience the perfect balance of architectural scale with a peaceful, zen atmosphere. This stunning light-filled residence is defined by its expansive open-concept layout, starting with a dramatic living and dining area featuring soaring cathedral ceilings. A series of sliding glass doors creates a seamless indoor-outdoor flow, opening the entire main level to one deck-ideal for tranquil afternoons or large-scale entertaining. The heart of the home features a chef's kitchen that opens directly into a comfortable family room, supported by a butler's pantry for effortless hosting. For ultimate convenience, the primary suite is located on the main level, offering a personal sanctuary complete with a full bath, closets, and another large deck. Upstairs, the airy feeling continues with two additional bedrooms, both featuring en-suite baths to ensure privacy for family or guests. The lower level adds even more flexibility, housing one large and one smaller room-perfect for a home gym, office, or media room. Complete with a roomy three-car garage and a layout that breathes, this home is a rare find for those seeking a modern, light-filled home.
