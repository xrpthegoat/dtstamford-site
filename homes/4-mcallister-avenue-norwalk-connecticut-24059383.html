About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained home located on a private small street neighborhood. Offering 4 spacious bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, this residence is perfect for growing families or anyone seeking additional space. As you step inside, from either the front door or your private single car garage, you find a spacious coat closet and a small entry room. The hardwood floors throughout the house give the home warmth and beauty. Living room features a cozy fireplace, creating the perfect ambiance for relaxation or entertaining. Adjacent to the living room, the dining room walks out to a second-floor deck and provides versatility for formal dining, a home office, or extra living space. Bright kitchen includes cabinetry and additional counter space for all your cooking needs. Main floor bedrooms are generously sized with ample closet space. The upstairs was recently renovated into a light and airy bedroom and new full bathroom. Additionally, this floor has lots of storage or extra workspace. Recent upgrades include a new upstairs shower, new floors in both upper back room and basement, fresh paint in both the garage and basement and a new roof over the garage. Enjoy the benefits of comfortable living while being conveniently located less than 5 minutes away from I-95 and retail shopping along with other nearby amenities, parks, schools, and transportation. The home's design combines comfort, functionality, and style, making it the perfect for your next home.
