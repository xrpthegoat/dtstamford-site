About this home

Set on a quiet Stamford street just minutes from Old Greenwich, this updated 3 bedroom colonial is a fantastic alternative to apartment/condo living that offers something that's increasingly hard to find: a fully fenced backyard, great outdoor living space, plenty of storage, and a location where you can walk to the water. Spend mornings on the front porch, summer evenings on the large back deck, or walk to Boccuzzi Park and the waterfront boardwalk, all while being close to the train, I95, shops, restaurants, and everything you need. Inside, the home is bright, updated, and move in ready, with three bedrooms and plenty of additional storage. A garage, attic, basement and large driveway provide space for cars, bikes, sports equipment, seasonal items, and more. One of the best features is the location. Walk to Boccuzzi Park and the waterfront boardwalk, or head just minutes down the road to the shops, restaurants, and cafes of Old Greenwich. Commuters will love the easy access to the train station and I-95, making trips throughout Fairfield County or into New York City simple and convenient. A rare combination of charm, outdoor space, storage, walkability, and commuter convenience this is a house that truly feels like home. Make your appointment today.
