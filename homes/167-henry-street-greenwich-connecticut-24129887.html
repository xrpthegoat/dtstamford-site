About this home

Welcome to this bright and sunny 3-bed, 3.5-bath home offering comfort, style, and an ideal location. Main level features a spacious living room w/FP and built-ins, formal dining room and EI-Kitchen, complete w/granite countertops, an island, and SS appliances. Off the dining room is a beautiful screened-in porch with ceiling fan-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Powder Room. Upstairs, the primary bedroom includes attached bath, accompanied by 2 additional bedrooms and another full bath. The LL offers exceptional versatility with a family room, featuring custom built-ins, refrigerator, dishwasher, and sink. A full bath and 4th bedroom or home office complete this level. Additional highlights include: One-car attached garage + 2 off-street parking spaces. Large backyard and flagstone terrace. Central air, central vacuum, hardwood floors and abundant closet space. Located close to schools, parks, the beach, tennis courts, and just a short distance to the train and highway, this home combines convenience with charm. Available November 1.
