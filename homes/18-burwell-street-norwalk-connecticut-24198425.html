About this home

Experience the best of Connecticut waterfront living at 18 Burwell Street, a rare direct waterfront property with breathtaking views of a tidal inlet and Norwalk Harbor. Nestled in one of Norwalk's most desirable coastal neighborhoods, this charming 2-bedroom, 2-full-bath home offers an extraordinary opportunity to enjoy life on the water. Thoughtfully updated with new quartz countertops and windows, the home is filled with natural light and showcases stunning waterfront vistas throughout. Comfortable living spaces flow seamlessly to the outdoors, making it ideal as a year-round residence or weekend retreat. New gravel driveway with Belgian block border. Step outside to a beautiful wood patio overlooking the water, surrounded by incredible gardens and mature plantings that create a private, tranquil setting perfect for entertaining or simply enjoying the coastal lifestyle. **A rare and valuable feature, the property includes approved dock permits and plans, providing the opportunity to build your own private dock with direct access to Long Island Sound. Conveniently located just minutes from beaches, marinas, restaurants, and parks, this is a truly special waterfront offering. For buyers seeking an even more exceptional opportunity, the adjacent property at 16 Burwell Street is also available under a separate listing, creating the rare chance to own two side-by-side properties and establish a one-of-a-kind waterfront compound.
