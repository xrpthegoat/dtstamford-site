About this home

CHARACTER, CHARM, FLEXIBLE LAYOUT, 3+ BEDROOM/3-BATH HOME IN LEAFY NEIGHBORHOOD: Beautifully preserved early-century Colonial with inviting wraparound porch, yard with mature trees and patio, offers 2,000 square feet of versatile living space in one of Norwalk's sought-after historic neighborhoods. The living room offers multiple entertaining areas, while the large dining room provides a welcoming space for gatherings. A fourth room connected to full bath could be used as a dressing room, nursery, guest room, or nanny suite. And while the kitchen offers a country feel with cozy breakfast area, the attic features skylights that fill the space with natural light, ideal for home office, studio or, family retreat. Large basement. Within minutes to West Rock middle school, All Saints school, shopping, dining and major commuter routes and train. At $4100, the house comes with 2 parking spots and includes electricity, grounds maintenance, snow removal and water. Owner uses garage with access to yard Monday through Friday. Whether relocating for work, on a sabbatical or just looking for change, this house provides perfect balance between charm and practicality.
