About this home

Welcome to Ninety-Four leaf Clovelly. This beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 1.5 bathroom residence is nestled in the idyllic Glenbrook sub section of Stamford. Blending modern comfort with classic charm, this home has been thoughtfully enhanced with a series of high quality upgrades designed for today's discerning buyer. Step inside to an inviting open concept floor plan showcasing gleaming hardwood floors and abundant natural light throughout. The renovated kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring spacious center island with quartz countertops, sleek cabinetry and stainless steel appliances perfect for both casual meals and entertaining. Recent improvements include a new central air conditioning system, upgraded electrical, new windows and newer boiler and hot water heater ensuring efficiency and peace of mind. Outside, the private, fenced in backyard offers an ideal retreat for relaxation, comfort and convenience. Conveniently located near vibrant downtown Stamford, train stations, shopping and local parks, this home seamlessly combines style, comfort and charm. Truly move in ready and waiting to welcome its next chapter. **Sellers are asking for all offers to be submitted by 5pm Monday, 10/27**
