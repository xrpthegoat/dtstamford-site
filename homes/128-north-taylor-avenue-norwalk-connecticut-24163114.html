About this home

Welcome to 128 N. Taylor Avenue, a beautifully spacious Colonial offering over two thousand square feet of bright comfortable living in a convenient Norwalk location. Step inside to an open main level where warm hardwood floors and nine foot ceilings create an inviting flow from the living room to a generous dining area and into a thoughtfully designed kitchen with granite counters, abundant cabinetry, a center island, and easy access to the outdoors. The main level also features three well sized bedrooms including a primary suite with hardwood flooring and direct access to a luxurious full bath complete with double sinks and a soaking tub. A second full bath with tile and a tub shower serves the additional bedrooms. The fully finished lower level adds incredible versatility with a wide open recreation space featuring tile flooring, bright lighting, a half bath, and a dedicated laundry area. Outside you will find a level yard and private driveway set in a quiet residential neighborhood just minutes from shopping dining parks and major commuting routes. This well maintained home offers comfort space and convenience in a desirable Norwalk location and is available for yearly lease.
