About this home

This sun drenched townhouse is ideally situated in Sylvan Knoll with abundant street parking and private parking in rear. As an end unit you have twice as many windows as others, more views and more natural light. This unit offers blonde flooring (laminate & hardwood) and benefits from a more recently remodeled full bathroom. Opportunities exist for you to add your style if you want to update the kitchen and lower level in the future. A rear entrance offers a shared patio area for outdoor entertaining and access to off street parking. This townhouse enjoys natural gas for heating and has central a/c for summer cooling. Great proximity to Cove Island Park, Beaches, Stamford RR, downtown shops/dining/entertainment and all major roadways. $709/month maintenance charge is inclusive of grounds maintenance, water/sewer charges, real estate taxes and insurance for the structure - this is a great value!
