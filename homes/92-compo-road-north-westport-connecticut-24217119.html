About this home

92 Compo Road N. pairs classic architecture, meticulous craftsmanship, private 1 acre property, pool and spa with an enviable near town location. Moments from Main Street's shops and restaurants, ball fields, tennis/pickleball courts, and 3 miles to Compo Beach, this custom Colonial offers a vibrant lifestyle and strong feeling of "Home Sweet Home". Approximately 5,500SF, this residence is finished on 4 floors and showcases light and bright gathering spaces, exceptional millwork, and bespoke cabinetry. It is complemented by thoughtfully redesigned and upgraded interiors, and substantial investments in exterior improvements and mechanical systems. Beautifully landscaped grounds are alive with color and texture and incorporate stone walls and fencing. The large entertaining patio and deck create an irresistible setting for entertaining and everyday living. 4 bedrooms occupy the second floor, including a spacious primary suite with dual walk-in closets, and a luxurious bath featuring double vanity, soaking tub, radiant-heated floors, and a heated towel rack. A third-floor bonus room offers built-in desks, lounge, attic storage, and rough plumbing in place for a future bathroom. The walkout lower level includes a gym, wine room with refrigeration, and recreation room with full bath (space could serve as a 5th bedroom). A distinctive opportunity to enjoy private outdoor living and exceptional convenience in one compelling Westport address.
