About this home

Hunt Club area colonial on quiet, family friendly cul-de-sac. Beautiful, almost 4K sqft home, close to everything on a private lane. Nothing to do - newer roof, kitchen, furnace, AC, W&D and connection to sewer. The open-concept family room with fireplace, built-ins, wainscoting, dining room and newer chef friendly kitchen with a large island and french doors to multi level deck create an ideal space for entertaining and family. Enjoy views of the garden. Also on main floor is an office/fifth bedroom, living room, half bath, pantry and laundry room. Upper level features four spacious bedrooms and three updated bathrooms, including two en-suite. MBR has a walk-in closet, dual sinks, and modern shower. French doors between the other two bedrooms provide customizable space-perfect for a play area, study room, or sibling suite. A garden-level walkout provides 1,144+ sq. ft. of versatile living featuring kitchenette, ample storage, and large windows overlooking the garden. Ideal for a home office, playroom, studio, gym au pair or guests. Generous attic and garage. Gas & city water.
