About this home

This charming home, completely reconstructed in 2022, located in a prime area of Darien. The expanded cape-style house features three finished levels with 4-5 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms. The main level offers an open living floor plan that includes an eat-in kitchen, dining area, powder room, gym room, walk-in storage room, and living room with patio access.The upper level contains a primary suite with a vaulted ceiling and walk-in closet, as well as two large bedrooms and a full bath. The lower level provides additional living space with a bedroom and office, a full bath, a second laundry set, and an in-law kitchen with a separate access option. The outdoor space features two patios and a backyard for entertainment. The home is conveniently located within walking distance to Darien Middle School, Noroton Heights train station, and Noroton Heights Commons Mall. Schedule a viewing today!
