About this home

Unique "Antique" Expanded Cape, brimming with charm and character, featuring wide-plank wood floors, wooden beams and three working fireplaces. The spacious living room and dining room flow into an eat-in "country-style" chef's kitchen, offering double sinks, double dishwashers, two wall ovens and custom cabinetry; along with a wetbar, just around the corner, perfect for dinner parties and/or your morning coffee station. The vaulted ceiling family room with wood burning stove leads way to a stone patio with six-person hot tub; the perfect end to a long day! Home office with built-ins, first floor bedroom and a full, updated bath make entertaining a breeze. Upstairs, you'll find three generously-sized bedrooms, including a primary suite with its own full bath, double sinks and radiant heated floors. Additional updates include: New C/A (2024), Exterior Paint (2024), Interior Paint (24'/26') Kitchen Appliances (23'/24'/25'), First floor shower (2025). Two-car attached garage, abundant storage space, copper gutters, exterior shed and professionally landscaped grounds with seasonal blooms, make this the perfect countryside retreat. Private access through the rear, fenced-in yard to Cherry Lane Park, a 50-acre town park with walking trails and pond. Conveniently located with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, Route 7 and I95, and minutes away from Wilton Center and Downtown Westport make this a rare opportunity, you don't want to miss.
