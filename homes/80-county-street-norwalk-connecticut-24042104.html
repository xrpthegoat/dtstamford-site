About this home

Move right into this spacious, updated 2-bedroom condo! Sip your morning coffee or enjoy a frosty beverage on your generous balcony which overlooks an expansive grassy courtyard. Nor-West residents love the community pool/clubhouse and playground. This unit features in unit laundry (also community laundry in building), crown molding throughout, granite countertops, recessed lighting in both the living room and kitchen, bamboo hardwood flooring, 2nd bedroom has built in closet, new washer, 2 new a/c wall units. There is one assigned parking spot in directly in front of unit. There are visitor spaces for your guests and additional assigned spots may be purchased for just $15/mo. There is an oversized storage unit in building. Nor-West is conveniently located on the Norwalk/Westport border and is walking distance to all schools, amazing shopping and restaurants, trains and highways. Please note that your heat and hot water are included in the common charges...a great savings! $78 special assessment ends soon in March of 2025.
