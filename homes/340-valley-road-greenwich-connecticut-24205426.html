About this home

Rare opportunity to own this turnkey home in the coveted gated community of Valley Ridge. Beautifully updated and meticulously maintained, it offers over 4,000 square feet of living space across three elevator-served levels. A dramatic double-height great room with a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace and adjoining dining room welcomes you inside. The spacious chef's kitchen, appointed with top-of-the-line appliances, opens to a large family room with a gas fireplace. A separate sitting room with sliding glass doors leads to the patio, newly added barbecue area, and private lawn. The lovely primary suite features two generously sized walk-in closets and a spa-like bath with an oversized shower and soaking tub. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and laundry complete the second floor. The finished walkout lower level offers abundant natural light, a wet bar, bedroom, full bath, and ample storage. Numerous improvements throughout, a private backyard with plenty of room to play, and an ideal location in the North Mianus School District, moments from town and the train.
