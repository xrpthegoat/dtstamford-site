About this home

Walk to downtown shops and restaurants from this stunning, reimagined, & rebuilt 1920 Dutch colonial home, situated on a spacious 1.17-acre in-town property.This is truly a work of art, as 2 properties have been seamlessly combined into one, creating a private oasis set way back from the road.Upon entering the home, you will be captivated by the meticulous attention to detail & the perfect blend of modern amenities and old-world charm.The entire home has been torn down to the studs & rebuilt, ensuring that every aspect meets the highest standards of quality & craftsmanship. The heart of this home is the gorgeous chef's kitchen, complete w/large island & family room that features accordion glass doors leading to a breathtaking patio w/inground pool, perfect for enjoying the outdoors & entertaining guests. Windows & doors throughout the home, imported from Germany, add an element of sophistication & elegance.Not only are they aesthetically pleasing, but they are also multi-functional, allowing for an abundance of natural light & fresh air to fill the space. The 6000 Sq ft home boasts high-end details & finishes on four levels.5/6 bedrooms & 6 full and 2 half baths incl a TO-DIE-FOR Primary Suite w/vaulted ceiling, stunning spa bath, 2 walk-in closets & a balcony overlooking the pool. There is plenty of room for everyone to have their own private retreat. The property includes an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU), ideal for guests, in-laws, or a live-in nanny. Smart home!
