About this home

Walk to beach, Compo Beach neighborhood. Inwood Lane is off of Hale Road, off of Hillspoint Road. The backyard of this home views lovely homes on Loretta Court. Charming, with four bedrooms and a large family room. Lovely deck overlooking the backyard with flowers and fauna. The master suite has it's own wing, with fireplace, walk in closet, and full bath, and is accessible to the large deck overlooking the property. New washer dryer, new dishwasher, new kitchen flooring, and lower level large family room has brand new flooring. Additional highlights include hardwood floors, central air, public water and sewer,and four propane tanks that serve the boiler, generator and deck grill. Minutes to the train station, shopping, restaurants, beach and longshore for tennis, golf and sailing.
