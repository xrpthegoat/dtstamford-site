About this home

Wonderful cottage directly on the Rippowam River with amazing wrap around deck extending over water. So peaceful and private yet minutes to Trader Joe's, shopping/dinning and the Merritt Parkway as well as vibrant downtown Stamford and train station. Completely updated and on 1 level make this home easy to enjoy. Relax outside on the deck or in the level yard while listening to the calming sounds of the river and in the summer you can cool off with a dip in the crystal clear water or chill on your float pad. There is also a 2 car garage + additional car port. Fun fact this little hidden gem of an area used to be an artist retreat in the 1950's and Marilyn Monroe even vacationed here! Such a fun, unique and special setting. This very special home is available unfurnished/short or long term- inquire for additional details.
