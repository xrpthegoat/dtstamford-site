About this home

Nestled on a quiet, tree-lined street in a desirable Cove-area complex, this beautifully updated townhouse offers a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. The main level features a spacious living room and a bright eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances. A half bath and a walk-out patio complete this level, perfect for indoor-outdoor living. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms, offering potential for a craft room or home office, and a neutral full bath. Enjoy the ease of low-maintenance living with a common charge that includes heat, hot water, cooking gas, grounds maintenance, and more. Shared laundry facilities and one assigned parking space (with plenty of visitor parking) are also included. This prime location is just a short walk to Cove Island Park, Chelsea Piers, beaches, and local shops. With easy access to I-95, the railroad, and public transportation, this is a must-see property for commuters and city-dwellers alike. Seller is requesting highest and best offers by 8:00pm on Friday, October 3.
