About this home

Nestled near the end of an upscale cul-de-sac in prime Greens Farms, the newest SIR SIGNATURE Home blends timeless design with modern luxury on beautifully flat, private grounds. A heated gunite pool with auto cover anchors the expansive backyard w/ two connecting raised patios creates a true indooroutdoor retreat. Inside, sunfilled spaces showcase the craftsmanship SIR is known for. High ceilings, custom millwork, wide-plank oak floors, and a stunning chef's kitchen opens to a spacious family room w/ a distinctive beamed ceiling & walls of glass overlooking the property are just some of the highlights which place this fine home in a class of its own. The purposeful layout offer effortless flow for everyday living and entertaining. Six bedrooms include a serene primary suite w/ spa inspired bath incl floating white oak vanity and generous dressing room. The finished third floor provides flexible bonus space w/ bath for work or play, while the fully finished walk-out lower level offers recreation, fitness, and media options. The private office with custom cabinetry provides an ideal place to work in serene surroundings. Thoughtful amenities like designer lighting, a terrific mudroom, pantry, and attached garage complete this exceptional home. Close to Greens Farms school, beaches, walk to Westport Tennis Club and only 1.6 mi to train. A rare opportunity to buy from Westport's most trusted builder with over 300+ completed homes in one of the town's most coveted neighborhoods.
