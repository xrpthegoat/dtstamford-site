About this home

Bright, spacious, and beautifully maintained second-floor apartment located just outside the desirable Dolphin Cove area of Stamford. This freshly painted 5-room apartment features 2 bedrooms, a renovated kitchen, ceiling fans, ample storage space, and the added convenience of in-unit washer and dryer. The flexible floor plan offers the potential for use as a 3-bedroom apartment, subject to negotiation and approval by the landlord. Enjoy a convenient coastal lifestyle within walking distance of parks, marinas, and waterfront restaurants. Just minutes from I-95, Stamford Metro-North Railroad Station, downtown shopping, dining, and entertainment. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable living in a highly convenient location!
