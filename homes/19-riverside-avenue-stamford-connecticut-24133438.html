About this home

Welcome to this timeless 1939 Cape full of charm and detail! Perfectly sited on over one-third of an acre-larger than most in the neighborhood-this home features 3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, and original hardwood floors throughout. The entry foyer, dining room, and staircase showcase beautiful wainscoting, while the sunlit front-to-back living room offers varied-width hardwoods, a wood-burning fireplace, and access to a first-floor bedroom or home office. The dining room, complete with a built-in corner hutch, leads to a cheerful kitchen with butcher block counters, new range, and plenty of cabinetry. Off the kitchen, a bright sunporch overlooks the fully fenced yard and peaceful views of the Rippowam River, and connects to an oversized 2-car garage with extra storage. Upstairs are two well-sized bedrooms, a renovated full bath, and a charming landing area. The dry basement provides abundant storage. Additional updates include central A/C, newer mechanicals (furnace, water heater, A/C 2015), 200-amp electric, newer roof (2008), windows (2002), and fresh paint throughout. Enjoy a new front walk, stoop, and columns, plus natural gas heat for efficiency. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, transportation, and just a short walk to Scalzi Park & Brennan Golf Course. Don't miss this mid-city charmer!
