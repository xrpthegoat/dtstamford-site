About this home

Best and final offers are requested by 7/9 at 5pm. Welcome to Heywood Heights, one of Stamford's most sought-after communities! Lovingly maintained and meticulously updated, this exceptional two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath condominium offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. What is special about this unit? It is one of only 14 in the complex with this desirable floor plan. You will love the private entrance and the peaceful patio overlooking the beautifully landscaped grounds. The custom-designed kitchen is a dream for any cook, offering an efficient layout, abundant cabinetry, and a professional-series range with both conventional and convection cooking. Spacious rooms, hardwood floors throughout, and incredible storage, including oversized closets, a full attic, and a dedicated basement storage room,make everyday living easy. Upstairs, both generously sized bedrooms feature their own full bathrooms, creating the perfect layout for privacy and comfort. Perfectly situated at the back of the complex, this home offers exceptional peace and privacy while being just minutes from the shopping, dining, and entertainment of Darien Commons and vibrant downtown Stamford. It's just a short walk to the beach. Residents enjoy a sparkling in-ground pool, beautifully maintained grounds, and a clubhouse available for private events. This is a rare opportunity to own one of Heywood Heights' most desirable floor plans in an unbeatable location!
