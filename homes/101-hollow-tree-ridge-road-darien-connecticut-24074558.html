About this home

Welcome to 101 Hollow Tree Ridge Road, a stunning home that seamlessly blends timeless New England charm with modern finishes. The sun-filled living areas provide ample space for both entertaining and everyday living, with an updated kitchen featuring beautiful quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances. The kitchen flows into the dining area, family room and elegant formal living room with fireplace. The sunroom, off the main level, is a tranquil space to relax, while the finished lower level offers versatility for a playroom, home office, or additional living space. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat, complete with a walk-in closet and a luxurious private bath. Two additional generously-sized bedrooms and a well-appointed bathroom complete the second floor. The home's exterior has also been upgraded with Hardie board siding, adding both durability and visual appeal. The property's prime location places you just moments away from the Noroton Heights train station, Darien's top-rated schools, and the dining and shopping options at the lively Darien Commons! Finished playroom in lower level with 260 SQ FT not included in total square footage.
