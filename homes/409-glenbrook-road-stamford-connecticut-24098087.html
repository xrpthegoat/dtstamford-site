About this home

Beautifully remodeled and well maintained, light and bright 2BD condo in the heart of Glenbrook. With a short Walk to train, shops and restaurants, public transportation, minutes to the beaches, I-95 and Merritt Pkwy, you cannot ask for more conveniently located home. Upon entrance, you will be wowed by a spacious open floor plan, great size living room with hardwood floors and recessed lights, oversized eat in kitchen with floor to ceiling pantry closet that walks out onto your very own private deck, where you can enjoy peace and quiet while sipping your morning coffee or watching sunsets. This condo has plenty of closet space and comes with a great size storage and access to a shared laundry room in the basement. In addition to one assigned parking spot, if need be you can use one of three visitor spots on a first come first serve basis. Landlord wants good credit and verifiable references. Condo will be freshly painted. Brand new deck! This is one of a few hidden gems that sure will not last...grab it while you can.
