About this home

Designer-owned CT Garden House. A rare North Stamford offering, owned by a classic mid-century modern furniture designer & a gifted gardener-where design & nature live in harmony. Set on a coveted cul-de-sac, a daffodil-lined path leads to striking Art Deco front doors. Inside, a sun-filled Colonial enhanced w/Mid-Century Modern styling blends clean lines with timeless detail. Lovingly maintained, the first floor unfolds beautifully with an inviting eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, & a spacious living room centered around a wood-burning fireplace. Expansive screened-in porch overlooks garden-wrapped pool & large grassy yard. A large home office, family room & full bath give a seamless & flexible layout for today's living. Thoughtfully designed and w/handicap accessibility in mind, featuring a ramped two-car garage, easy flow, & potential for a first-floor bedroom w/adjacent full bath & zero-entry access. Upstairs offers four generous bedrooms. The primary suite includes a sitting room w/cozy brick fireplace, walk-in closet, & a vaulted marble bath w/jacuzzi tub, double sinks, walk-in shower w/body sprays, & private water closet. Full basement is ready to be finished. Full attic & generous storage throughout. Extraordinary gardens w/perennial beds & mature plantings. Large backyard offers room for expansion. Backup generator. Walk to Bartlett Arboretum & Stamford Nature Museum trails. Quick to Merritt Parkway & Scotts Corners, NY. North Stamford at its most refined!
