About this home

Enjoy a vacation-at-home lifestyle year-round in this beautifully renovated and expanded home, located within the private Dolphin Cove association. This sought-after waterfront community offers an exceptional 3.6-acre private club area featuring a swimming pool, tennis courts, sandy beaches, clubhouse, kayak storage, private deep-water dock slips along shoreline of Old Greenwich subject to application. Relax on your backyard terrace or balcony and watch the boats glide by, with easy access to Long Island Sound for boating and waterfront enjoyment. Residents can enjoy tennis, swimming, and an active coastal lifestyle just steps from home. Conveniently located near Innis Arden Golf Club, Old Greenwich Village, and both Old Greenwich and Stamford train stations.
