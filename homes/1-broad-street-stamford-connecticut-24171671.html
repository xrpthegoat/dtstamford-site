About this home

Ideally positioned on the 17th floor, this sophisticated one-bedroom offers an exceptional opportunity to experience luxury living at its finest in downtown Stamford. A grand tiled entry sets the tone for a beautifully appointed interior featuring rich wood floors, elegant marble countertops, and modern stainless steel appliances. The gracious primary suite provides a peaceful retreat, while sweeping panoramic views of Mill River Park create a truly stunning backdrop. Additional highlights include a private balcony with upgraded flooring and generous closet space throughout. Residents of Park Tower Stamford enjoy a premier amenity package with a saltwater pool, fitness center, resident lounge, 24-hour concierge, doorman, and shuttle - delivering effortless, full-service living. Just steps from Stamford's top restaurants, shopping, and entertainment, this is a rare opportunity to own in one of the city's most sought-after luxury buildings.
