About this home

This is a unique opportunity to own a direct waterfront home on the LI Sound in prestigious Shippan Point. This stunning French Chateau style home has 172 feet of water frontage, a private neighborhood beach, on over 1/2 acre of land. It features a stunning kitchen with top-of-the-line appliances, 2 family rooms, 2 offices, dining room, living room with fireplace, and a gym, all with gorgeous water views. There is also rec room over garage with a 1/2 bath. The primary bedroom is a suite, with a sitting area with fireplace, and a private balcony overlooking the water. There are 3 additional bedrooms all that lead out to a huge balcony and one with an ensuite bath. The property is professionally landscaped with many perennial gardens. Enjoy spectacular sunsets and sail boats sailing by from almost every room. Move right in to this turn-key coastal home and enjoy every day as if you are on vacation. Close to the Stamford Yacht Club, train station, vibrant downtown Stamford, and Chelsea Piers.
