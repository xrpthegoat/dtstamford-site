About this home

**OFFER DEADLINE TUES. 7/14 AT 12:00 NOON.** Experience the perfect blend of tranquility and potential in Norwalk's highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, 9 Pocono Road offers an expansive 0.84-acre retreat surrounded by mature trees for ultimate privacy. This 3BR/2BA home with 2,400 sq ft living space, has been refreshed from top to bottom, featuring a neutral palette of fresh paint throughout and beautifully refinished hardwood floors. The heart of the home is a spectacular great room, boasting a dramatic beamed ceiling, abundant natural light, and a cozy fireplace-perfect for gatherings. Peace of mind is built-in with a newer roof (installed 2023) and a fully renovated primary bathroom. Outside, the generous lot invites you to relax in your own backyard oasis, complete with an above-ground pool.(heater available). With built-ins, large basement (historically designated as a fallout shelter) and garage, this home offers unique character and incredible storage. Offered As-Is, this property presents a rare opportunity to add personal touches over time and build immediate equity in a premier location. Minutes away from all Norwalk offers; beach access, Cranbury park, local museums, shopping, golf courses, Maritime Aquarium, and vibrant dining scene in SoNo. Commuting is effortless with easy access to Metro North, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Property in an estate, to be transferred by Trustee; available immediately.
