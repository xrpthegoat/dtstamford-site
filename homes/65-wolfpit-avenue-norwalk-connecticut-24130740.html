About this home

Welcome to your bright and beautifully updated townhouse in Norwalk, where every detail has been thoughtfully refreshed for modern comfort. Step inside to sun-filled rooms and a freshly painted interior that feels brand new. The main level features refinished hardwood floors and a newly renovated kitchen with granite countertops, sleek new appliances, and space that opens effortlessly to a private patio-perfect for morning coffee or dining al fresco. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite offers a fully renovated en-suite bath and brand-new hardwood flooring, while the second bedroom enjoys its own full bath just steps away. A freshly carpeted staircase ties it all together with warmth and style. Practical perks abound: parking for two cars (including one in the garage), a convenient mudroom entry, and a private basement area for extra storage. The grounds of the community are beautifully landscaped with walking paths that create a peaceful, park-like setting. This complex also offers plenty of guest parking. Location couldn't be better-close to Stew Leonard's and all the shopping and dining along Route 1, with easy access to major highways and Metro-North. Pet-friendly too: bring along one dog or one cat to make this house feel even more like home.
