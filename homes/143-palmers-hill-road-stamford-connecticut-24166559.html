About this home

*OFFER DEADLINE 4/20, 5PM* Set on over 1.5 private acres in a sought-after Stamford location near the Greenwich line & surrounded by multimillion-dollar homes, this mid-century modern ranch offers architectural character, generous scale, and a natural flow between indoor and outdoor living. Hardwood and slate floors, recessed lighting, rich woodwork, beams, built-ins, and expansive glass sliders reflect the home's distinctive design and appeal. The living room is the centerpiece, featuring a dramatic cathedral ceiling, floor-to-ceiling fireplace, and expansive glass sliders that open to the enclosed porch, where walls of windows frame the serene backyard and set the tone for morning coffee, quiet moments, or easy entertaining. A spacious formal dining room flows naturally into the eat-in kitchen, creating an inviting layout for both everyday living and larger gatherings. Offering 5 beds, with one perfectly suited for use as a home office, 3 full baths, plus a mudroom/laundry off the kitchen, the layout provides flexibility, while the lower level adds tremendous potential for additional living. Outside, the sprawling private backyard is framed by mature trees and offers the space, privacy, and setting for a true outdoor oasis. All of this is ideally located with easy access to downtown Stamford, Metro-North, major commuting routes, and the shopping, dining, and conveniences of Greenwich, offering a lifestyle that blends privacy, space, & convenience in an exceptional location.
