About this home

Welcome to 59 Fairfield Ave, a prime two-family home located in one of Stamford's most convenient and vibrant neighborhoods! This well-maintained property offers incredible flexibility and income potential. The first unit features 3 spacious bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, while the second unit, spread across the second and third floors, boasts 4 generously sized bedrooms and 3 full baths-perfect for extended families or strong rental returns. detached 2 car garage, and lots of off street parking, 6-8 parking spaces. You're also close to Stamford Hospital, UConn Stamford, and several beautiful parks and beaches along the Long Island Sound. With strong rental demand and nearby communities, 59 Fairfield Ave offers the perfect blend of suburban comfort and urban convenience.
