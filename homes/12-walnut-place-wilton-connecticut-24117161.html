About this home

Welcome to 12 Walnut Place, this classic colonial sits on a two-acre lot in one of Wilton's most desirable neighborhoods close to town, train & schools. This turnkey home has many recent improvements and offers 4 bedrooms, 4 full bathrooms & plenty of living & entertaining spaces over nearly 5,000sq feet of finished space. The interior is filled with natural light & the layout offers the perfect blend of formal and informal spaces. The heart of the home is the stunning eat in kitchen, featuring high-end stainless-steel appliances & a wine refrigerator. The bright eat in area flows seamlessly into the sunroom with sliders to the large terrace. Also off the kitchen is the formal dining room featuring a lovely bay window and charming built-ins. The formal living room is bathed in light where you can relax quietly and snuggle in front of the woodburning fireplace. Work from home in the quietly appointed office or hang out and play in the large family room with the 2nd fireplace. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite offers a walk in closet and ensuite bathroom with double vanity and large shower. Three more bedrooms and a hall bath with double vanity complete the 2nd floor. The finished basement has plenty of room to play, work out & with a full bath and kitchenette is a perfect in-law or au-pair suite. Outside, the level backyard has beautiful landscaping. This turnkey home is ready for its next owners to move in and enjoy this wonderful neighborhood, less than 60 miles to NYC.
