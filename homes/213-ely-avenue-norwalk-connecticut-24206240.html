About this home

RARE SIX-UNIT INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY IN NORWALK! This exceptional income-producing property features two separate residential buildings with a total of six units, offering strong rental potential, operational flexibility, and long-term upside. The front building is a classic four-family property dating back to the 1910s, while the rear building offers two additional residential units. The property features a desirable mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments, appealing to a broad range of tenants. Numerous recent improvements make this property an attractive investment, including a new roof, newer hot water heaters, new A/C units, fully renovated apartments, new driveway, and low-maintenance vinyl siding, stone, and stucco exterior. Additional amenities include natural gas, central air conditioning, basement and attic storage, and laundry facilities. Whether you're an experienced investor looking to expand your portfolio, completing a 1031 exchange, or an owner-occupant looking to live in one unit while generating rental income from the others, this property offers exceptional flexibility and value. Six units, two buildings, significant improvements, and multiple income opportunities-an uncommon investment opportunity in the Norwalk market.
