About this home

Welcome to a home that makes everyday living and entertaining easy. Set on a friendly cul-de-sac where neighbors wave hello, and bike rides and dog walks are part of the rhythm of the street. This 4-bedroom split-level offers space, warmth, and a location that's hard to beat. At the heart of the home is an open-plan living room and modern eat-in kitchen, highlighted by soaring vaulted ceilings with exposed beams plus a fireplace that creates a welcoming focal point. With hardwood floors and updated spaces, the home exudes comfort and convenience. Step outside to a large, sunny deck, perfect for grilling, entertaining or simply relaxing while overlooking the full acre of property. The newly renovated in-ground pool is heated and can be enjoyed for those last days of summer. Other significant improvements include a new roof and a completely new septic system, adding peace of mind. Best of all, enjoy the convenience of walking to Wakeman Town Farm and the playing fields, Gruel Britannia for a coffee or a meal, plus the award-winning Staples High School. This home is a wonderful combination of neighborhood, updated amenities and location. Quick closing available.
