About this home

A WESTPORT SAUGATUCK SHORES COTTAGE RENTAL Gem! A MUST see! Just STEPS from Saugatuck Harbor...Perfect Year Round or just Getaway on Weekends with a constant view of the harbor and moored boats. Throw/anchor your kayak or rowboat in the harbor, jump/swim in the water, sit/relax on the beach/shoreline, walk or bike the neighborhood, go fishing... any time you want...This quaint, clean, airy, updated, 2 Bedroom (or 1 bed/1 Office/Den) Beach Cottage boasts of views of the Saugatuck Harbor, easy access to the harbor, a large yard with outdoor furniture, flagstone patio and completed with fencing for privacy. The kitchen offers Granite counters, SS appliances and Solid Porcelain Floors. The Living Room with fireplace and Large Dining area with French Doors has access to a lovely Stone Patio with view of the harbor. Full Bath has Solid Porcelain Floors and European Fixtures. Tankless Water Heater. Basement & Attic storage. Outdoor round patio table & chairs and 2 wicker chairs & cocktail table furnished. Parking for 4 cars. Less than 2 miles from Westport Center, Longshore Park (Golf/pool/tennis/marina), Compo Beach, Arts, Train, Levitt Pavilion, 2 Yacht Clubs. 55 miles from NYC.
