About this home

Experience timeless elegance and resort-style living in this extraordinary stone estate on 2+ spectacular acres overlooking a serene spring-fed pond with direct water access. Designed by architect Malcolm Robertson with interiors inspired by Rick Livingston Period Design, this home offers 9,369 SF of masterfully crafted living space blending architectural sophistication with modern luxury. Built in 2004, the home showcases Venetian plaster walls, cerused oak details, radiant heated stone floors, soaring ceilings, extensive custom millwork, and dramatic walls of glass framing breathtaking views of the pool, grounds, and pond. At the center of the home is a showstopping 3 story gourmet kitchen-a true culinary masterpiece-seamlessly blending architectural drama with top-tier functionality with limestone counters, oversized gathering island, and premium Miele, Wolf, Viking, and SubZero appliances. Designed for both grand entertaining and comfortable everyday living, the residence features 6 en-suite bedrooms, 8 full and 2 half baths, with spectacular first-floor primary suite with dressing rm and spa bath. Amenities incl media rm, billiard rm, sun rm, gym, professional office suite, Control4 smart home technology, surround sound, heated pool w/automatic cover, separate spa, Har-Tru tennis court, extensive landscape lighting, and beautifully manicured grounds. Rarely does a property of this caliber, privacy, and design become available just moments from town in a coveted location
