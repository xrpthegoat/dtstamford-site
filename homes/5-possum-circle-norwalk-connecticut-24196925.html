About this home

Experience effortless living in this beautifully furnished Rowayton rental, with exceptional craftsmanship and high-end finishes. Meticulously maintained, this stylish home offers open-concept living, dining, kitchen area with generous cabinetry, abundant counter space-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The main-level primary suite features custom closet system and luxurious marble bath. Fully finished walkout lower level includes spacious family room, two additional bedrooms with large walk-in closets, home office/flex space, and laundry room w storage. Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living with a covered patio, deck, and private backyard in a peaceful neighborhood setting. Walk out the back yard and in 5 minutes you are at the Rowayton Train Station, 55 minutes to NYC on Metro North. A few minutes more to all Rowayton has to offer, including Five Mile River, beach, library, nature preserve, dog park, village shops, restaurants, tennis, pickleball and paddle. Available Oct. 1 furnished with flexible lease (6-7 months considered). No smoking. Inquire about pets. Credit check, references, and security deposit required.
