About this home

Your Private Five-Star Resort in New Canaan: Experience the pinnacle of luxury at this 2-acre custom estate, where "vacationing at home" is your daily reality. Tucked behind commanding stone columns near the vibrant town center, this park-like sanctuary is framed by gorgeous stone walls and lush, professional landscaping and sited on a quiet cul-de-sac. The sun filled open-concept interior boasts 9'+ and vaulted ceilings, extensive millwork and gleaming hardwood floors, anchored by four fireplaces that radiate warmth and sophistication. At the heart of the home lies a breathtaking chef's kitchen featuring a legendary island with enough room for a large gathering and a dreamy walk-in pantry. The formal living room with gas fireplace and formal dining room with butler's pantry offer an easy flow for entertaining. With 5 bedrooms, 6.5 baths, a stately library and two additional offices, there is unparalleled space for both high-stakes productivity and total relaxation. The lifestyle continues outdoors with a sparkling pool and a private sports/tennis/pickleball court designed for endless summer afternoons. Whether hosting a grand gala or retreating to your private oasis, this property offers a seamless blend of modern elegance with New England charm.
