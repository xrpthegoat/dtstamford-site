About this home

Welcome to your grand private estate set on a South Wilton cul de sac. Enjoy resort-like living since owner will pay for landscaping, snow removal, & pool maintenance. For the first time on the rental market, this luxury home is replete with perfectly manicured grounds, an in-ground saltwater pool, and complete outdoor kitchen. Sited on nearly three flat acres and exquisitely landscaped grounds that directly abut 30 miles of the Norwalk River Valley Trail, this home is an unparalleled retreat. 87 Buckingham Ridge Rd. is a high-end architectural craftsman colonial boasting a large eat-in chefs kitchen with a 2024 six burner Wolf stove. An open concept first floor sunken family room with coffered ceilings opens to both the kitchen and the formal dining room. Your private office awaits with built in mahogany bookshelves. Arched entryways, a coffered ceiling, and cherry wood floors span the main level. A large mudroom, two half bathrooms and an additional formal living room complete the first floor. Upstairs, the primary suite with a walk-in closet includes a double spa shower with shower jets, a heated floor, and a beautiful soaking tub. A laundry room, five total bedrooms and four bathrooms in addition to a massive bonus room complete the second floor. The fully finished carpeted walk-out basement offers an additional 2,568 square feet with a sixth bedroom, fifth full bathroom, a gym/wine cellar, and rec room--an exceptional home w/ ample space and proximity to town amenities.
