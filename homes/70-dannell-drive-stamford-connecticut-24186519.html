About this home

Classic Raised Ranch in Desirable Pepper Ridge! This charming, well landscaped 3-bedroom raised ranch offers excellent curb appeal, a traditional floor plan, and plenty of opportunity to make it your own. The main level features three comfortable bedrooms, bright living spaces, an eat-in kitchen, and a dining room with sliders leading to the rear deck-an inviting spot for morning coffee, outdoor dining, or relaxing while enjoying views of the beautifully manicured backyard. The lower level, expands the living space with walkout to a lovely patio, a comfortable family room, adjacent den, and an additional versatile room that could easily serve as a playroom, guest space, home office, or hobby area. A wonderful opportunity to bring your personal vision and design ideas to a conveniently located home with a flexible layout and tremendous potential to create a space that truly reflects your style.
