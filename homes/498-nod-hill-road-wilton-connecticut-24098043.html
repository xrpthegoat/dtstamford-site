About this home

Nestled on iconic Nod Hill Road- one of the most coveted streets in Connecticut- this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath colonial blends classic New England charm with modern updates. The sunlit main level features hardwood floors, a white eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a fabulous family room with a stone fireplace. Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living with a screened-in porch and deck overlooking the landscaped yard with beautiful stone walls. Additional highlights include a formal dining room, living room with second fireplace, private office, and a spacious upstairs primary suite with three additional bedrooms. The finished walk-out lower level offers 1,200+ sq ft for a playroom, gym, mudroom, and more. With a two-car garage, Tesla charger, and close proximity to top-rated schools, train, and major routes-this home is perfect for today's lifestyle. Enjoy the best of suburban living with easy access to top-rated schools, vibrant cultural attractions, and all major commuting routes. Within walking distance to Wilton's beloved Weir Farm National Historic Park. This is a place where family and friends will feel right at home. 498 Nod Hill - Welcome Home!
