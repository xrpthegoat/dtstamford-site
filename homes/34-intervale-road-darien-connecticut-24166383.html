About this home

This lovingly maintained four-bedroom, three-and-a-half bathroom home is the ideal blend of comfort, convenience and natural surroundings. Filled with natural light from its north-south exposure, 34 Intervale Road features nine-foot ceilings, refinished hardwood floors, and generously proportioned living spaces throughout. Recent renovations include master bathroom and powder room, quartz countertops in both the kitchen and wet bar, new kitchen backsplash, along with basement flooring. Mechanical upgrades include a full-house generator, new air handler, boiler, and newer hot water heater - all connected to natural gas. Enjoy outdoor living on the deck overlooking a private backyard that abuts Woodland Park, offering more than 60 acres of trails and open space. Located in one of Darien's most adored neighborhoods, it's walkable to all three schools, Noroton Heights train, shopping, and restaurants. A true move-in ready home in a prime Darien location.
