About this home

Welcome to 333 West Avenue, a stunning residence nestled within the Noroton Green Association-a boutique enclave of just six homes. Built in 2012 by the renowned Hertz Construction, this home is a harmonious blend of timeless craftsmanship and modern luxury. From the moment you step inside, you'll be captivated by the soaring ceilings, intricate millwork, and custom built-ins that frame the inviting living spaces. A cozy fireplace anchors the main living area, while the chef's kitchen steals the show with its high-end white cabinets, Thermador appliances, gleaming finishes, and thoughtful design. The home boasts four generously sized bedrooms, including a show stopping primary suite with a spa-like marble bath, a freestanding soaking tub, and an enviable walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms, 3.5 baths, including a third-floor retreat with an extra bedroom and living space provide ample room for family and guests, office space or additional living space. Enjoy the ease of maintenance-free living with HOA-covered perks, leaving you more time to enjoy life. A spacious two-car garage adds convenience, and the location is second to none-stroll along sidewalks to the train station, Darien Commons, and Heights Crossing. This is more than a home; it's a lifestyle.
