About this home

Welcome to this brand-new townhouse in Stamford's exclusive 14 Taylor Street, a thoughtfully designed 13-unit luxury development. This 2 bedroom, 1.5 bath home showcases an open-concept living and dining area filled with natural light, complemented by gleaming hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and a modern kitchen with white shaker cabinetry, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and gas cooking. The upper level offers a spacious primary bedroom, a bright second bedroom, and a beautifully appointed full bath, while the main level includes a convenient half bath. Additional features include central air, efficient gas heating, an attached 1-car garage with the option for an EV charger, and a contemporary design throughout. Ideally situated within walking distance to Metro North for a quick commute to NYC and just seconds from I-95, this townhouse also provides easy access to Downtown Stamford, Stamford Hospital, shops, restaurants, and major highways-delivering modern comfort paired with exceptional convenience.
