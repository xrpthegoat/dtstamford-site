About this home

LIVE IN IT! EARN FROM IT! LOVE EVERY INCH OF IT! Perched gracefully above the street on a prominent corner lot, this timeless Victorian immediately captivates with its expansive wraparound porch, an inviting extension of the home that sets the tone for both everyday living and memorable gatherings. Step inside through the main entrance to a welcoming foyer that opens to a warm and character-filled interior. The living room features a charming built-in window seat with hidden storage, creating a perfect place to relax while adding both function and architectural detail. The main level flows effortlessly with a spacious dining room, well-appointed kitchen, and a conveniently located half bath. A secondary porch entrance leads directly into the kitchen, offering ease of access for daily living. Upstairs, three bedrooms and a full bath provide comfortable accommodations, while the finished attic, complete with built-in shelving and heating, offers a versatile space ideal for a home office, creative studio, or quiet retreat. What truly sets this property apart is its rare legal two-family designation while living seamlessly as a single-family home. The accessory apartment, with its own private rear entrance, includes a kitchen and sitting area, 1-bedroom, full bath, and ample closet space.
