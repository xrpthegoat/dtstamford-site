About this home

Amazing East Norwalk Location! Discover this beautifully remodeled second floor unit, perfect for those seeking comfort and convenience. Featuring a refreshed kitchen, a fully updated bathroom, and two spacious bedrooms, this home has been thoughtfully updated to meet modern tastes. The entire unit has been freshly painted and showcases new vinyl flooring, a stylish shiplap accent wall, and newer carpets in the bedrooms. For added convenience, the unit comes equipped with its own washer and dryer. Situated on a desirable corner lot, the property boasts a large fully fenced backyard, side yards, and a spacious shared driveway. Heat and hot water are included! This prime East Norwalk location offers unparalleled accessibility, just a short walk to the East Norwalk train station, a quick drive to Calf Pasture Beach, and easy access to the lively dining and entertainment options in the historic SONO district.
