About this home

Nestled in the coveted Portal Rock community, this recently updated 2 bedroom, 1 bath condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, style and convenience. This sun-filled, one level unit features a brand-new kitchen with stainless steel appliances, refinished hardwood floors, crown molding and recessed lighting throughout. The spacious open-concept living and dining area is perfect for relaxing and entertaining. It also has room for your home office! Both bedrooms are generously sized and filled with natural light, offering serene views of the beautifully landscaped yard creating a peaceful and private atmosphere. Just outside, enjoy your own private patio and lush green space, complete with gorgeous blooms and a peach tree - perfect for outdoor dining, gardening or simply unwinding in nature. Additional features include in-unit laundry, central air and gas heat. One assigned parking space is included with a possible option for a second reserved space. With just a 12 minute walk to the Cos Cob train station, commuting is effortless. It is also only a few moments to shops, restaurants, parks and all that Greenwich has to offer, including Cos Cob Park and the Mianus River trails. This is easy living at its best! Could also be rented fully furnished.
