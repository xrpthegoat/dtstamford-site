About this home

Perfectly sited on 1.59 acres, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath antique home invites a lifestyle defined by warmth, ease, and understated elegance. Original wideboard floors, an impressive hearth fireplace, and beautifully preserved details create a sense of history, while the open, light-filled layout supports a modern way of living-whether hosting gatherings or enjoying quiet moments at home. The main level is designed for flexibility and comfort, with a first-floor bedroom or office that easily adapts to guests or work-from-home needs. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a calming retreat with a walk-in closet and full bath. Two additional bedrooms, a hall bath and small study provide space for family, creativity or focus. Thoughtful updates, including newer mechanicals and roof ensure a turnkey experience without compromising the home's character. Outdoors, life naturally extends into a private, resort-like setting. A gunite pool and spa invites long summer afternoons, while the expansive, sun-filled lawn sets the stage for entertaining, play or simply unwinding. The detached 2-car garage with heated loft offers a versatile escape-ideal for home gym, studio or relaxed gathering. Moments from town, dining, and all transportation, this is a delightful retreat where every day living feels both effortless and elevated.
