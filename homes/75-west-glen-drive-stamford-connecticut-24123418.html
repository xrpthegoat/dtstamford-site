About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST OFFERS DUE BY 10/27 5PM. Beautifully presented in Westover, this immaculate, sun-filled four-bedroom, five-bath residence offers an open, airy layout defined by window-lined rooms and exceptional craftsmanship. Every detail reflects thoughtful design throughout. Chefs and entertainers alike will fall in love with the custom Balthrup kitchen, equipped with Sub-Zero refrigerator, dual wall ovens, built-in Gaggenau steamer, and two Bosch dishwashers. The kitchen opens seamlessly to a bright living and dining area featuring a modern fireplace and floor-to-ceiling windows. A custom-built reading room with beautifully designed fireplace provides the perfect retreat for cozy evenings at home. The main floor living room features a clever built-in storage wall thoughtfully concealed as a design feature. UpstairThe primary suite is a true sanctuary, blending luxury and function with a spa-inspired free-standing tub, custom walk-in Poliform closet, and a modern, romantic aesthetic. The finished lower level offers remarkable versatility with a gym, lounge area and full bathroom. Set on over one acre of level, park-like grounds, the home invites outdoor living at its finest, featuring a heated in-ground pool, built-in outdoor kitchen, slate patio and professionally manicured yard. A whole house generator ensures year-round comfort and peace of mind. This home is nestled on a beautiful, tree-lined, and highly coveted Westover street.
