About this home

Exquisite 6-Bedroom Colonial on Over 3 Private Acres - This is the one you've been waiting for-a timeless estate designed for gracious living, exceptional entertaining, and the creation of lifelong memories. Set at the end of a private lane on over 3 spectacular acres, this exceptional Colonial blends timeless elegance with modern luxury. A grand foyer with a sweeping curved staircase welcomes you into beautifully scaled rooms featuring soaring ceilings, exquisite millwork, and abundant natural light. Formal living and dining rooms, each with a fireplace, flow seamlessly into an impressive library w/custom built-ins and a sun-filled family room w/French doors opening to an expansive terrace. The gourmet kitchen overlooks the pool and adjoins a charming paneled breakfast room with a fireplace, creating the perfect setting for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, a gracious landing overlooks the dramatic foyer and leads to a luxurious primary suite with fireplace, custom cabinetry, and a spa-inspired bath. Three additional en-suite bedrooms, a spacious bonus room, and a private guest suite provide exceptional flexibility. The fully finished walk-out lower level offers a large playroom, guest suite with full bath, and a dedicated fitness room. Outside, enjoy the private, park-like grounds complete with stone walls, a new pool, fenced yard, and playset. Just minutes from town, train, and award-winning schools, this rare offering combines privacy, scale, and convenience.
