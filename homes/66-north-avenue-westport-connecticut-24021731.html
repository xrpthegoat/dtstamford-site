***LEASE TERM AUGUST 10, 2025 TO JUNE 20, 2026. FURNISHED*** Enjoy an array of seasonal delights at this wonderful, well-appointed home, beginning with the conclusion of summer when you can enjoy the sparkling pool, pool house, gracious patio w/outdoor fireplace & built-in grill, gorgeous lawns, gardens & more. When autumn's splendor arrives, enjoy the timeless grace of the beautiful interiors - beginning w/casually elegant living & dining rms, a quiet office/library, gourmet kitchen w/Sub Zero & Thermador appliances opening into a stunning breakfast rm which is completely surrounded by tall windows & French doors overlooking the grounds, and a wonderful family rm w/cathedral ceiling, built-ins, French doors to the patio. The BRs include a vltd ceil primary suite w/walk-in clst, balcony, classic marble bath w/dual vanities, glass shower & soaking tub. The remaining BRs are well-proportioned and have accompanying designer baths. The guest/pool house is a delightful surprise w/LR w/fpl, sleeping loft, full bath. Additional comforts include a two car attached garage w/EV charger, generator, and more. The lifestyle extends to the amenities of adjacent Staples High School, where tennis courts and the outdoor track & athletic fields are available after-hours. This is an ideal opportunity to utilize Westport's award-winning education or experience this celebrated community while home shopping in the area. 2.1+/- miles to train.
