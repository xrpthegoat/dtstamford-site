About this home

A Rare Rental Opportunity in Exclusive Marvin Beach! Set just four houses from the water in the coveted Marvin Beach Association, this charming coastal home offers classic seaside character with fresh, modern enhancements and a rare chance to enjoy one of Connecticut's most sought-after shoreline communities. Full of natural light, the home features a warm, welcoming living room with gas fireplace, a dining room with built-in window bench seating, and a thoughtfully renovated kitchen with stylish finishes. A beautiful new full bath and four-season sun room add to the appeal and complete the first floor. View sunsets and the water from the front sun room! Upstairs are three bright bedrooms, two with walk-in closets, and a second refreshed full bath with jet tub. Recently refinished hardwood floors and fresh paint throughout lend a breezy, classic and cozy beach house feel. A private level backyard with deck is perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining. The garage with private long driveway offer plenty of convenient parking. Enjoy all the benefits of Marvin Beach living - three private beaches, a resident-only marina, and a calendar of neighborhood events in a tight-knit, coastal setting. This location is convenient to all Norwalk has to offer; SoNo, all transportation, parks, beaches, restaurants, and shopping. Rentals in this exclusive beach enclave are few and far between. Don't miss this rare offering!
