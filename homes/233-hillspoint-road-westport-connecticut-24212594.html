About this home

Experience waterfront living at 233 Hillspoint Road, a striking new construction home with panoramic water views and a sandy beach setting. A private elevator is planned to serve all three floors, providing convenient access from the garage to the living areas and up to the rooftop deck. The planned home combines clean architectural lines with an open floor plan, 9-foot ceilings and expansive walls of glass that capture natural light and showcase the water throughout. The design includes four bedrooms, each with an ensuite bath, highlighted by a spectacular primary suite. The rooftop deck, is an exceptional setting for entertaining, enjoying sea breezes and watching sailboats and fireworks over the Sound. A beach-level covered terrace provides a seamless connection to the sand. A rare opportunity to bring your vision to an extraordinary Westport waterfront setting.
