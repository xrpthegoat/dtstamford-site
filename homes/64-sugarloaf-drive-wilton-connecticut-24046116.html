About this home

Darling three bedroom ranch on one very private level acre with specimen trees and gardens. Beautiful street and very convenient location. Light, bright and good sized living room w/fireplace and kitchen plus heated sunroom w/built-ins and sliders to back patio. 3rd bedroom w/full bath is over the garage and currently set up as an in-law apartment w/separate entrance. Hardwood floors throughout and lots of vintage charm and character. All new interior & exterior painting and refinished floors. Full unfinished attic w/pull-down stairs and full unfinished basement. Incredible potential for updating and expansion. Large in-ground pool not currently in use being sold as is. Minutes to RT 7, schools, trains, Wilton Center, shops, restaurants. A gem!
