About this home

In 1968 the owners purchased a 2 acre building lot and built a beautiful home and raised their family and grandchildren. Now it's time for another family to enjoy years of happiness as they did in the past. Spacious single floor living circular drive. Open front door to a large entrance leading to a formal living room and dining room. Eat-in Kitchen opens to a large family room fireplace with a wall of windows overlooking the landscaped property with pool. Master with full bath walk-in closet plus a wall of closets. Two additional bedrooms main level. Lower level with walkout sliders to covered patio A spacious playroom additional 1550 Sq Ft. with fireplace and bar area. Bath with shower and possible office/bedroom and a storage room. Two private beautifully landscaped acres, flat open play yard. Also included is a Generic generator.
