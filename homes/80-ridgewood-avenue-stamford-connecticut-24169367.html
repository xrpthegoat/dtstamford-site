About this home

Spectacular open concept living in this deceivingly large cape in Springdale on a quiet dead end street. As you enter through the front door, you have views directly into the backyard from the oversized picture window. Spacious living room w/ fireplace flows into the oversized dining/family room area w/ vaulted ceilings and sliders that lead out to the Trex deck. The modern kitchen has Carrara marble counters, st/st appliances, pantry w/pull out drawers and a large peninsula perfect for entertaining. 2 comfortable bedrooms on the main floor as well as a full bath. Upstairs is a spacious primary suite w/ full bath. The finished lower level offers versatile space including a den, office/gym area and a kitchenette/laundry room w/ a full walk out to the backyard. Gorgeous fully fenced yard includes a deck, firepit area, and lush foliage allowing for privacy. Plenty of storage in the lower level, as well as additional storage in the eaves upstairs and storage shed in backyard. Gas heat, new windows, newer ductless a/c units in all bedrooms and living area (a/c units were added after pictures taken), 1 car garage, you don't want to miss this one! Agent is owner.
