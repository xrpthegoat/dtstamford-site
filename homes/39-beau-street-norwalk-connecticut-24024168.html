About this home

ALL OFFERS SUBMITTED BY 5PM TUESDAY 6/18. 4 PAGE STANDARD FORM CONTRACT. Don't ya know, this is 39 Beau! This comfortable cape is located on a flat .19 acre plot in the sought after Broad River neighborhood. This home is complemented with three bedrooms, a possible fourth bedroom, two full bathrooms, home office and over 2,000 square feet of finished space (most of which is hardwood floors)! From the outside you'll notice the classic white picket fence, cozy front porch, professional landscaping, lush rear lawn and spacious back deck. On the first floor, there is a living room filled with natural light via a bay window, a lengthy dining room flowing into an updated eat-in kitchen, a full bath and a bedroom. Upstairs there are another two bedrooms, a small home-office and a full bathroom. But wait there's more! The basement provides an optional fourth bedroom, massive family room and dedicated laundry/storage area! Located only 5 minutes from the Merritt Parkway, I-95 and the SONO train station makes any commute a breeze. Don't miss the chance to own a fantastic cape in a prime location!
