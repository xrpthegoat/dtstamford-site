About this home

Tucked near the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Sherwood Forest, this upper Springdale split sits on the Darien line with Woodway Country Club directly behind the street. It's the kind of neighborhood where you have your own land and privacy but still know the people around you, and you're six minutes from the Springdale train. The house gives you a lot to work with: a true entry foyer, a spacious living room with fireplace and picture window framing the backyard, an open dining room, and a large eat-in kitchen with granite counters and a sunny breakfast room. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has its own full bath and opens to a private deck, with two more bedrooms and a second full bath. The lower level adds a family room, a mudroom and laundry area, a third full bath, and direct access to an oversized two-car garage. Below that, a full bonus level offers serious storage and room to finish a rec room, gym, or workshop. Outside, a bluestone patio looks onto a mature, wooded yard that feels private and leafy in summer. Hardwood floors throughout. A rare chance to get into one of Springdale's most sought-after pockets with the space, layout, and location already in place. Bring your vision and make it yours.
