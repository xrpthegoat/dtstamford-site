About this home

Now available for immediate occupancy, this spacious 4-bedroom, 2-full-bath Townhouse is located in a gated community in South Norwalk, complete with security cameras and a one-car garage within the complex. The first floor features an open-concept floor plan designed for comfortable living and entertaining, along with a convenient first-floor bedroom and a full bathroom that includes a stack-able washer and dryer. The kitchen is well-appointed with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, offering both style and functionality. The second level includes three additional bedrooms, all featuring brand-new carpeting, along with a full bathroom equipped with a tub/shower combination. Residents also enjoy access to a shared outdoor patio area in the rear of the complex, furnished with two tables for relaxing or casual gatherings. Additional features include central air conditioning and electric heat for year-round comfort. The property is also fiber optic internet ready, providing high-speed connectivity for work, streaming, and everyday use. Ideally located in South Norwalk, this home offers convenient access to transportation, dining, shopping, and entertainment.
