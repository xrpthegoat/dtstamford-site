About this home

Now available for immediate occupancy, this spacious 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath Townhouse is ideally located in the heart of vibrant South Norwalk. Situated within a smaller gated community featuring security cameras, the home includes two assigned off-street parking spaces for added convenience. The interior offers 9-foot ceilings, split-system air conditioning, electric heat, and a well-appointed kitchen with stainless steel appliances, including a brand-new stove, refrigerator, and dishwasher. The second-level bedrooms feature brand-new carpeting, while the full bath on this level includes a tub/shower combination. The entire third floor is dedicated to the private primary suite, complete with a full bath featuring a walk-in shower and a walk-in closet located within the bedroom. Residents also have access to a shared laundry room located in the common basement, as well as outdoor patio seating available for community use. The property is also fiber optic internet ready, providing high-speed connectivity for work, streaming, and everyday use. Enjoy all that South Norwalk has to offer with easy access to the train station, restaurants, shops, entertainment, and bus lines-all just moments from your doorstep.
