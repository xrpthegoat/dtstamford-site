About this home

FOR RENT $8200 which includes a yearly membership to the Italian Center across the street with gym, pools, tennis courts, paddle tennis courts, basketball court, heated indoor pool with spa and year around social and recreational activities. One of a kind spacious 4000+Sqf renovated and restored Federal Style Antique Colonial home with high ceilings throughout, ideally situated in the Upper Newfield Avenue Area (North Stamford), conveniently located 5 min to downtown New Canaan, Parks, Merritt Parkway, Transportation, Shopping, Golf Course,Tennis Courts, Houses of Worship (all denominations) and minutes to public and private schools such as King Low Heywood School and New Canaan Country School . This home also offers 2 terraces for entertainment , including one with an outdoor sitting/dining area with stone fireplace, overlooking 2 acres of flat lawns/garden, mature plantings.
