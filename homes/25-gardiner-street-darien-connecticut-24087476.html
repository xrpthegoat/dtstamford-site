About this home

Welcome to 25 Gardiner Street - a stunning modern farmhouse thoughtfully blending contemporary elegance with timeless design. Offering three full floors of living space plus a beautifully finished basement, this 6-bedroom home is ideal for both relaxed living and effortless entertaining. Step inside to find soaring ceilings, abundant natural light, and high-end finishes throughout. The open-concept kitchen and living area features premium appliances, custom cabinetry, and a large island - all flowing seamlessly to the private backyard oasis. Enjoy outdoor living with a heated gunite pool, spa hot tub, and spacious patio, perfect for entertaining or quiet evenings at home. This versatile home includes two dedicated office spaces, a gym, and generous storage on every level. The attached two-car garage is complemented by an additional detached garage, offering extra space for parking or hobbies. Located close to town, train, and schools, 25 Gardiner Street is a rare opportunity to experience luxury, flexibility, and comfort in the heart of Darien.
