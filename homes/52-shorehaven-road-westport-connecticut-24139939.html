About this home

Welcome to Canfield Island....a private waterfront property nestled amongst luxury homes in one of the few gated enclaves in Fairfield County. Rarely available, this home offers an unparalleled sense of privacy and serenity. A charming gated bridge welcomes you to the Island offering sweeping views of Long Island Sound, the tranquil Canfield Inlet, Audubon Society Marsh and the manicured greens of Shorehaven Golf Course. Inside, an open floor plan showcases a stunning chef's kitchen, soaring cathedral ceilings designed to maximize the breathtaking panoramic views. Step outside to enjoy professionally landscaped perennial gardens, kayak along Canfield Inlet to the serene Bermuda Lagoon, swim, sunbathe at the beach, take a steam or simply relax on the expansive deck as the scenery unfolds from sunrise to sunset All this, paired with the exceptional schools and amenities of Westport, makes this property a rare coastal sanctuary. Minutes from downtown Westport and SoNo, this is the ultimate private escape. Full year lease for $22k per month June/July $25k per month February, March, April or May $15k per month
