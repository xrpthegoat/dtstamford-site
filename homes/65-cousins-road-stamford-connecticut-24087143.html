About this home

Come see this amazing lakefront home on a quiet cul-de-sac in North Stamford. Enjoy endless picturesque moments overlooking the lake from inside the house or from the back deck or from the walkout enclosed patio. The main level of this home features a wood burning fireplace, an elegant kitchen and dining area, the primary bedroom with updated bathroom and custom walk-in closet, 2 additional bedrooms with views of the lake and a shared hall bathroom. The finished lower level has a family room with a second wood burning fireplace, another bedroom/office, full bathroom, laundry room and walkout to the enclosed porch with direct lake access. This house has city water. New water heater installed 2020. New Roth 275 gallon oil tank installed January 2025. Brand new generator, never used. 3 heating zones, 2 main level and 1 for lower level. Seller is leaving snow blower, purchased 2022 only used once. Annual cost for the lake maintenance is $450. House being sold "As Is" No escalation clauses. Must see!!!
