About this home

Welcome to 279 Westwood Road, a charming and inviting home nestled in the highly desirable Westover neighborhood of Stamford, CT. This delightful residence offers 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and a spacious 2-car garage, providing ample space for comfortable living. Upon entering, you'll be greeted by a generous 2544 square feet of living space. The home boasts a spacious kitchen with updated appliances. For added comfort, 279 Westwood Road is equipped with central air conditioning to keep you cool in the spring and summer months. Situated on a half-acre lot, the property offers plenty of outdoor space for recreation and leisure. With both a deck and spacious patio, this home offers plenty of entertaining space. Additionally, the home is handicap accessible, accommodating a variety of lifestyles. For added peace of mind, a 5-year-old Generac generator is included, providing assurance during any power outages. The location of this home is convenient with access to the I-95 just 10 minutes away and the Merritt Parkway 3 minutes away. Don't miss the opportunity to make this charming residence your own, where comfort, convenience, and a desirable location converge to create the perfect place to be your home.
