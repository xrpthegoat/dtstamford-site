About this home

This is the beautiful rental house that feels like your dream and your home -- thus your dream home! On a cul-de-sac, a whisker away from being in Wilton's town center, the house comes with the flattest of fenced yards, a swimming pool, a front porch, and all that you could want inside. The main entrance is to a welcoming foyer. An office for head(s) of the household is to the left, the great room is to the right with sophisticated built-ins, an eating area, and a wood burning fireplace. The white kitchen is completely current from 2020 with quartz counters, gorgeous finishes, and chef's appliances. Plus a wine chiller and seating at the island that fits five. Sliders lead to the outdoor deck, eating area, play space, and pool. Completing the first floor is the sizable mudroom, year round sunroom, and a powder room. Upstairs, the current owners have transformed the primary suite, upsizing the walk-in closet and the just redone primary bath. Three other bedrooms and a playroom (that could double as a guest room, studio, au pair suite, or other use) provide the room count on the second floor. The remaining two bathrooms serving this floor have also been newly redone. The laundry room is in the second floor playroom. Tons of storage. Full basement w/ interior and garage access. Solar panels to offset electric bill. Nest Thermostats, Ring doorbell. THIS IS A PHENOMENAL LOCATION WITH SENSATIONAL AMENITIES. ENJOY! 2 year lease preferred.
