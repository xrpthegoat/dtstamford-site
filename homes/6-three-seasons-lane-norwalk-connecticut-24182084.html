About this home

Welcome to 6 Three Seasons Lane, a meticulously maintained Colonial set on a beautifully landscaped lot in Norwalk's highly sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood. Thoughtfully updated for low-maintenance, easy living, this inviting home features a bright, open floor plan, hardwood floors, stainless steel appliances, new siding, brand-new windows, a new roof, newly added insulation, and a high-efficiency heating system. A gas-log fireplace creates a warm and welcoming focal point in the living space, perfect for cozy winter evenings. The flexible main-level layout also offers the potential to create a first-floor primary suite and expand the existing half bath into a full bath. Outside, the manicured backyard feels like a private oasis, complete with an underground sprinkler system and a paved patio area that is ideal for entertaining, with seamless flow from indoors to out. Just minutes from Westport shops, restaurants, commuter routes, and everyday conveniences, this truly turnkey home is ready for its next owner to move right in and start living.
