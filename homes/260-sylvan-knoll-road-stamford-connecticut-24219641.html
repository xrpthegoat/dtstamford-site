About this home

All the hard work has been done! Move right into this two-bedroom townhouse in desirable Sylvan Knoll. On the main level, the living room and dining area are complemented by an updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances and new lighting. Hardwood floors on both levels and a stylish, recently renovated bath add to the appeal. Other improvements include newer windows and doors, updated basement stairs, and a newer boiler and central air system. The partially finished lower level offers potential for additional living space. Enjoy a quiet, park-like setting near Cove Island Park, just minutes from downtown Stamford, shopping, and Metro-North. The community is 100% owner-occupied and allows up to two pets. **No monthly assessment on this unit!**
