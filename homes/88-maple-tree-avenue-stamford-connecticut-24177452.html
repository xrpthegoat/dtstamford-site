About this home

Beautiful free-standing 2/3 bedroom townhouse with two full baths and two half baths in excellent condition on the Stamford-Darien border within walking distance of Glenbrook and Noroton train stations. Laundry on upper level. Third floor has spacious room and deck which can be used as an office, playroom or extra bedroom There is also an attic storage room. Central vacuum. Half bath on lower level and additional room used by owners as movie screening room - movie projector stays. Attached one car garage and one other designated parking space.
