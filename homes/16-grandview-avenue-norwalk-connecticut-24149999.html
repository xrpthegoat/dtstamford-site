About this home

This versatile two-family home offers a rare opportunity for owner-occupants or investors or both! Set on .32 acres, it features two separate units: a larger apartment with 3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths *PLUS* a finished walk up unit with an additional 1 bedroom (possibly 2), a large full bath, and food prep area. The studio apartment provides even more income potential with a full kitchen and full bath with high ceilings. The house features a detached 1 car garage and 2 additional car ports. There is also a 20'x20' approximate area off the driveway that can be used as an office, gym, or storage. Located minutes from the Hospital, Jefferson school, I-95 and merrit connector and downtown shopping. The expansive backyard offers room to enjoy or explore the possibility of a future subdivision (buyers to do own diligence).
