About this home

Opportunity abounds at this stunning custom-built home nestled on over 6 wooded acres in a serene setting. Built by the current owners in 1982, this almost 4000 square-foot home boasts four bedrooms, three full baths, spacious formal living and dining rooms, a first floor office/den, an open eat-in kitchen and a fabulous great room perfect for entertaining or relaxing with family. The upper level offers four bedrooms with three full baths. This is your opportunity to create the primary and hall bath of your dreams. Access to the 3 car garage is through the unfinished, walk out basement which offers endless opportunities. The entire interior of the home has been recently painted, the floor refinished and professionally cleaned in preparation for sale. Outside, the expansive deck and terrace provide the perfect spot for enjoying the tranquil setting, while the wooded acreage offers privacy. Whether you're looking for a peaceful retreat or a place to host gatherings with friends and family, this home has it all. Don't miss your chance to own this one-of-a-kind property!
