About this home

A beautifully updated, move-in ready townhouse condo in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. The recently renovated main level features a bright, open-concept floor plan designed for both everyday living and entertaining, with a refreshed kitchen, custom pantry, spacious living area, convenient powder room and direct access to the private terrace. Upstairs, the primary bedroom suite is joined by a generously sized second bedroom, full bath and versatile loft space perfect for a home office, reading nook or additional guest area. The lower level adds even more functionality with a walk-out layout, garage, additional parking, dedicated office/gym space and separate laundry area. One of the home's biggest highlights is the location. Enjoy easy walkability to neighborhood shops, restaurants, grocery stores and Metro-North, making commuting into New York City and everyday errands incredibly convenient. A turnkey Glenbrook townhouse offering updated living, flexible space and an excellent Stamford location.
