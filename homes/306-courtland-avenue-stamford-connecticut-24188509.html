About this home

Welcome to this brand-new, fully remodeled rental in the heart of Glenbrook. This spacious 1st-floor unit features 4 bedrooms, a large living room, and beautiful hardwood floors throughout. It comes fully equipped with a dishwasher, stove, refrigerator, and an in-unit washer and dryer. Rent includes water, snow removal, trash pickup, and 2 off-street parking spaces. Heating is powered by natural gas. Air conditioning is by window unit; tenant to provide their own window AC units. The location is perfect for commuters and convenient for everyday shopping. You can walk to the Glenbrook Train Station and nearby bus lines in less than 10 minutes. I-95, ShopRite, and local restaurants are all just a 5-minute drive away. Applicants must have excellent credit. No smoking or pets allowed.
