About this home

This charming 2 bedroom 1.5 bath condo in the vibrant Glenbrook neighborhood of Stamford offers an excellent blend of comfort, accessibility and convenience. Perfect for first-time buyers and investors alike, this home features beautiful hardwood floors throughout, a versatile lower-level den and a private back deck for relaxing or entertaining. There is also great storage available in the pull-down attic and a shared lower-level common area for your seasonal decorations or general organization. Commuters will love being so close to the Glenbrook train station, i95 and Manhattan, shopping, dining, and nearby entertainment. Plus, with a low HOA of only $150 per month, this condo offers affordable, low-maintenance living in one of Stamford's most desirable areas. The current tenants are month to month, are open to staying and currently looking for other rentals.
