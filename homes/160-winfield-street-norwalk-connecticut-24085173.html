About this home

Welcome to 160 Winfield Street - where timeless charm meets modern comfort in this beautifully maintained home nestled in the heart of Sasqua Hills minutes from Shorehaven Country Club. Offering 5 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 updated bathrooms, and over 2,800 square feet of living space, this home is ideal for anyone seeking a charming home with modern updates in a superb location. Step inside to find a warm and inviting living room with hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and a wood-burning fireplace-perfect for cozy evenings. The updated kitchen features high end stainless steel appliances, marble counter tops, and charming breakfast nook all blending seamlessly into the formal dining area. Enjoy the extra space of a fully enclosed sunroom-great as a family room, home office, playroom, or retreat. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a fireplace, walk-in dressing room closet and renovated primary bath. Additional bedrooms and bathrooms are generously sized with modern finishes. Outside, the fenced-in backyard is a beautifully landscaped private oasis, complete with a large Ipe deck, built in Sedona grill, and heated gunite plunge pool creating the perfect space for entertaining, gardening, or just relaxing. Full house generator. Located minutes from Shorehaven Country Club, beaches, shopping, dining, parks, with easy access to I-95 and Saugatuck train station, this home blends suburban tranquility with urban accessibility.
