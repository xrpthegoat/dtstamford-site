About this home

This inviting ranch offers a spacious, thoughtfully designed floor plan and is tucked away on a beautiful south Wilton cul-de-sac surrounded by multi-million dollar homes. The charming stone front sets the tone, welcoming you into a home with hardwood flooring and a cozy fireplace. At the heart of the home is the large, fully renovated kitchen with stainless steel appliances, double ovens, and a generous center island, perfect for gathering with friends and family. The dining room flows seamlessly into the living space, creating an ideal layout for entertaining. The bedroom wing includes a serene primary suite, two additional bedrooms, and a fully renovated bath. The lower level offers laundry and a recreation room for hobbies, play, or casual entertaining. Thoughtfully updated over the past decade, the home features motorized window treatments, energy-efficient windows, newer roof, well tank, carriage garage door, new shed, new fence, generator, as well as a freshly painted interior. Enjoy mornings with coffee on the front porch or relax in the large fenced-in yard with deck for grilling. Peaceful and private, yet minutes from award-winning schools, shopping and dining. This turn key home combines comfort, charm, and convenience in one of Wilton's most desirable locations.
