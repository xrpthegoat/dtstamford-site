About this home

Welcome to 22 Linda Lane, an elegant Colonial home located on one of Darien's most sought-after streets. This charming and chic, move-in-ready home has 3,861 square feet of sophisticated living space, featuring four spacious bedrooms and three and a half beautifully appointed bathrooms. Step inside to find a masterfully crafted layout where luxury meets functionality. The heart of the home features a gourmet eat-in kitchen that flows effortlessly into both the family room and dining room. French doors throughout create a seamless connection to the outdoor flagstone patio, perfect for entertaining. A welcoming living room with custom built-ins, and a den-each graced with its own fireplace, and a cozy screened porch provide additional gathering spaces. A spacious mudroom and convenient laundry room complete the first floor's thoughtful design. The upper level reveals an inviting primary suite featuring abundant closet space, a dressing area, and a spa-inspired bathroom. Indulge in heated quartzite floors, a luxurious soaking tub, a separate shower, a custom vanity, and built-in storage. Three additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and an attractive deck round out the second floor.
