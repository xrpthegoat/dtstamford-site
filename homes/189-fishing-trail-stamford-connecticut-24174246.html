About this home

Discover this stunning 4 bedrooms and 2.5 bath Center Hall Colonial in one of North Stamford's most desirable cul-de-sacs. This home offers the rare combination of a private, peaceful setting-bordering a conservation overlay-with the convenience of City Water. The natural sun light-filled interior features a kitchen with granite counters and newly updated good quality appliances and eat-in kitchen, overlooking the deck for seamless indoor-outdoor living. Enjoy a flowing-floor plan including a formal dining room and a fireplaced family room and spacious living room. The primary suite features walk-in closets and full bathroom, and three additional bedrooms featuring ample storage. With newly updated modern bathrooms, fresh paint throughout, and a finished lower level for office or gym... this home is completely turnkey!
