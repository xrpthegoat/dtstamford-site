About this home

Spacious and bright renovated six-bedroom Shore Colonial with fabulous water views, located in the Shorelands Association of Old Greenwich, CT! Enjoy the private association beach and dock just a short distance away. This lovely home has an updated and spacious kitchen with adjacent family room. Also, on the main level is a grand two-story foyer, living room with great views, office, dining room, two bedrooms, bath and powder room. The second level has a large primary suite with sitting area, water views and spacious bath. Three additional bedrooms and two baths complete this floor. Great level yard and huge deck are perfect for play and entertaining. So close to downtown Old Greenwich. Close to train, school, shops and restaurants. Also close to Tod's Point beach, boating and biking.
