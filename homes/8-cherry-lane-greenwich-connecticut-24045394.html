About this home

This elegant Shore Colonial, tucked away on a private lane just south of Old Greenwich village, offers a perfect blend of style and comfort. The open living spaces are adorned with a cozy fireplace and striking beamed ceilings. A flexible first-floor layout features a sun-drenched office or bedroom, a gracious dining room, and a family room that opens onto a spacious patio and yard. Upstairs, you'll find a versatile sitting/play area, three bedrooms, and a full bath. Highlights include skylights, wainscoting, and inviting window seats. The serene, park-like yard enhances the property's appeal, along with a two- car garage and loft, plus expansion potential up to 4,802 sq. ft. Whether seeking a weekend retreat, an investment, or a primary residence, this home is a rare opportunity.
