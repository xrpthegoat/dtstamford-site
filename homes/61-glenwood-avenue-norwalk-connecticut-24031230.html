About this home

Welcome to 61 Glenwood, your soon-to-be new residence! Explore this charming cape cod style home nestled on a corner lot with a warm living room. The spacious kitchen and dining area is ideal for entertaining! From the dining room, you can head directly outside to your private back deck. There are two bedrooms on the main floor including the primary suite. You'll also find one main bathroom in the hallway. Upstairs, there are two more bedrooms. The basement, which is partially finished, could potentially be converted into an in-law suite. There is a bonus room or den situated behind the garage, as well as an additional room or storage space above the garage. You can never go wrong with its spacious backyard lawn. Perfect for outdoor cooking and gatherings! This home is just a 10 mins walk to Norwalk Hospital and to Lowe's Home improvement. Come take a tour and make this home yours! Property being sold as-is.
