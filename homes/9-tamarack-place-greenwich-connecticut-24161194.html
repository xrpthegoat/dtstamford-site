About this home

Meticulously maintained Colonial situated on a quiet cul-de-sac in the highly desirable Glenville section of Greenwich. This exquisite home offers exceptional comfort, functionality, and timeless appeal. Set on a level .47 acre lot with beautifully manicured grounds, the property has access to a private community association pool shared with just 11 homes. The home features a gourmet eat-in kitchen with a spacious center island, double ovens, Sub-Zero refrigerator, and abundant custom cabinetry. Sliding glass doors open to a three-season enclosed porch, stone patio, and Trex deck, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living experience perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The inviting family room has a stone fireplace, while a gracious formal dining room and elegant living room-each enhanced with beautiful window treatments-add to the home's refined character. The second floor offers 3 generously sized bedrooms with ample closet space, along with two full baths. The spacious primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet. a luxurious bathroom with double sinks, a jacuzzi tub, and a separate shower. The finished basement includes a spacious entertainment room with custom cabinetry, a separate private office and generous storage space. The heated two-car garage is enhanced with a polyaspartic floor and custom built-in shelving. Additional highlights include landscape lighting, in-ground sprinkler system, whole house generator and security system.
