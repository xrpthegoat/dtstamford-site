About this home

Welcome to coastal living at its finest in this charming 1910 Colonial, just a short walk from Cove Island Park and beach. Freshly painted throughout, this 2-bedroom, 1-bath home features 864 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space with hardwood floors, a sunny eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and sliders leading to a back deck perfect for morning coffee or evening gatherings overlooking the private, fully fenced yard. Enjoy sunrise strolls, afternoon picnics, and watching boats drift across Long Island Sound at nearby Cove Island Park, where swimming, fishing, ice skating, and scenic waterfront paths make every season special. A full 432-square-foot basement provides excellent storage, while off street parking and easy access to I-95 and the Stamford Train Station create the perfect balance of relaxed coastal living and city convenience, just steps from your front door.
