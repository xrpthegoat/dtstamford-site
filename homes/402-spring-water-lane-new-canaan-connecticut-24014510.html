About this home

This stunning Nantucket-style home showcases exquisite craftsmanship with stone and shingle details outside and gorgeous design inside, perfect for luxurious living and entertaining. Beautifully sited on an elevated, private and lush 3.3-acre lot in a coveted west-side neighborhood, this property offers a serene retreat on a quiet cul-de-sac so close to everything. Mature evergreens and stone walls create a picturesque setting that is conveniently located near both train stations and town, providing an oasis of privacy and accessibility. Step inside through the grand Dutch door into the spacious foyer with soaring ceiling and natural light. The main level features stately and serene public rooms with fireplaces, oversized windows, and cathedral ceilings. The sun-filled kitchen is a chef's dream, with marble countertops, double islands, and top-of-the-line appliances, including a built-in cappuccino machine. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a super-luxe bathroom with double vanities, spacious walk-in closets, and private separate sitting area. Four more bedrooms and a great room on the second level, PLUS fully finished lower level, movie theater, playroom/dance studio, and gym, offers entertainment options for all ages. A sixth bedroom, bath, and office area is versatile main floor space for guests or au-pair suite. Gorgeous patio, pool, hot tub, luxurious flat lawn and basketball court provide outdoor recreation and relaxation in a spectacular setting all year long!
