About this home

A Home with a Sophisticated Flair, this 2-Bedroom 2.5 Bath Condo is located on one of the most Charming Tree-lined Cul-de-sacs in all of Greenwich. This Stylishly Renovated Home contains a Light-Filled Open Floorplan, along with Beautiful Solid Oak floors throughout. Boasting a Large Gourmet Kitchen with Quartz Countertops, Pantry, Stainless Steel Appliances, and abundant cabinetry. Full size W/D. A Primary Bedroom with a LARGE Custom Designed Walk-in Closet, along with a Luxurious Primary Bath. During those warm summer months, enjoy your own Private and Secluded Outdoor Terrace (one of only four units in the building). Also, a large outdoor public terrace on side of building. One deeded indoor parking space and storage. Plenty of shopping and restaurants only a short walk away on Greenwich Avenue. Enjoy the rooftop deck with its spectacular views of Long Island Sound and Manhattan skyline. Heat and Hot Water included. Both combi (a/c & heating) units on main floor replaced approx 2 years ago.
