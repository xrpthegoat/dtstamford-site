About this home

Welcome to this charming ranch-style home located in the heart of Norwalk. Built in 1938, this house offers 936 square feet of living space, perfect for those seeking a comfortable and manageable home. Step into the inviting living room, where the fireplace serves as the focal point, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Adjacent to the living room is a formal dining area, ideal for family dinners and entertaining guests. The home features hardwood floors throughout the living room and bedrooms. While the house is in need of significant updating, it is well worth the investment. The vinyl siding ensures durability and low maintenance. This property is also an excellent alternative to a condo, offering privacy and a sense of ownership. The walk-out basement presents an exciting opportunity to expand the living space. With potential to be finished, it can be transformed into a family room, home office, or additional bedroom. Sold as is.
