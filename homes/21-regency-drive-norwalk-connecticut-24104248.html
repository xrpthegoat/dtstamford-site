About this home

Welcome to this oversized, fully renovated hi-ranch, offering 5 spacious bedrooms, including a luxurious main bedroom with a private full en-suite bath and two additional full bathrooms. This beautifully updated home is move-in ready and designed with comfort, space, and functionality in mind. Step into the expansive living room featuring one of two cozy fireplaces, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The open concept eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, ideal for family gatherings and celebrations. The lower level offers incredible flexibility. It is perfect as a family room, home office, extra bedrooms, or even an in-law or au pair suite. Additional highlights include: Ample storage in the attic, generous closets throughout, and a corner lot with a beautifully landscaped, fenced yard. Finally, two fireplaces for year-round comfort and charm. Whether you're upsizing or looking for multi-generational living, this would be the perfect home.
