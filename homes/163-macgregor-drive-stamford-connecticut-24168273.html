About this home

Nestled at the end of an idyllic, tree-lined cul-de-sac in Stamford's highly sought-after Westover neighborhood, this beautifully maintained four-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and functionality. The main level features a stunning, fully remodeled white kitchen with an inviting eat-in area-ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Just off the kitchen, a fully screened-in patio provides a seamless indoor-outdoor flow, perfect for relaxing or hosting on warm evenings. A convenient mudroom with washer and dryer connects directly to the garage, while a powder room adds everyday ease. Hardwood floors and recessed lighting run throughout, enhancing the home's bright and welcoming feel. A flexible additional room on the first floor can serve as a formal dining room, playroom, or cozy den. Upstairs are four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite complete with a private en suite bath and two walk-in closets. The fully finished, heated basement expands your living space even further, offering endless possibilities for a home office, gym, recreation area, or additional storage. This home is ideally located just minutes from downtown Stamford and popular destinations such as Scalzi Park, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods and dining/entertainment. Commuters will appreciate the convenient access to the Metro-North Train Station, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway-making travel to New York City effortless.
