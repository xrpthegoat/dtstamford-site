About this home

Welcome to this charming antique Cape Cod-style home, offering timeless appeal and endless potential. With classic clapboard and stone construction, this warm and welcoming home invites you to make it your own and create the next chapter in its story. Inside, you'll find hardwood flooring throughout most of the home. The spacious living room features paneled walls and a cozy wood-burning fireplace, flowing seamlessly into the dining room with built-in shelving and French doors that open to a four season room. The flexible floor plan comfortably lives like a four-bedroom. There are two bedrooms on the main level, including one with its own en-suite bath. Upstairs from the main staircase, you'll find a large bedroom with an adjoining office or sitting room, plus a separate guest room or private office with its own bath - accessed by a dedicated staircase off the kitchen-perfect for visitors, remote work, or adaptable living needs. The location is ideal - less than 2 miles to the Merritt Parkway and the shops and restaurants along High Ridge Road, and just 3.5 miles to both Springdale and New Canaan. Enjoy a wide array of dining and shopping options, and Metro North Stations for a convenient commute to NYC. Full of character and nestled in a peaceful private setting, this is a special opportunity to bring your vision to life in a truly enchanting home.
