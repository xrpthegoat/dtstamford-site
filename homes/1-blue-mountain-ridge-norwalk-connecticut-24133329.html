About this home

Welcome home to this 1920's Storybook Cape with separate legal Cottage brimming with character and charm-ideally located in the desirable Lower East Rocks neighborhood. A spacious front deck leads into the skylit foyer and updated eat-in kitchen featuring granite counters, stainless steel appliances, vaulted ceiling and multiple skylights. The cozy living room with fireplace and French doors opens to a private yard, while the formal dining room with French doors leads to a delightful screened in porch. The spacious family room with stone fireplace, two full baths (one updated), and a bedroom complete the main level. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms and an updated full bath, all with lovely treetop views and hardwood floors throughout the main house. Freshly painted interior and a new roof on main house & cottage add to the appeal. The unfinished basement has great storage. BONUS: Cottage (approx. 548 sq ft) offers 1 bedroom, living room, EIK and private deck offering great income potential for the savvy buyer.
