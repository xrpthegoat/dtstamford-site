About this home

Welcome to 7 Curt Terrace - a beautifully remodeled center-hall colonial offering the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and indoor-outdoor living. Set on a peaceful cul-de-sac, this 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home has everything you've been searching for. Completely renovated in 2018, the home features an impressive open floor plan highlighted by a stunning gourmet kitchen with a 16-foot quartz island - ideal for entertaining - that flows seamlessly into the spacious family room and inviting den/office. Upstairs, you'll find five generous bedrooms along with additional bonus rooms on the top level, offering endless possibilities for a home gym, playroom, or guest space. Step outside to your private backyard oasis, complete with a heated pool, poolside bathroom, and an oversized wraparound deck with an outdoor fireplace - perfect for gatherings year-round. The yard is thoughtfully screened for privacy, creating a true retreat. Please note: Square footage does not include the finished lower and upper levels, adding even more living space to enjoy. This home truly checks every box. Don't miss the opportunity to make 7 Curt Terrace your new address!
