About this home

Nestled on the Darien border, this charming townhouse offers a peaceful retreat in a quiet section of Springdale. The updated kitchen features sleek quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a brand-new Bosch dishwasher. The spacious living and dining areas boast beautiful hardwood floors and a sliding door leads to a deck, perfect for outdoor relaxation. The first floor also includes a convenient powder room, ideal for guests and everyday use. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms with ample closet space, complemented by a renovated bathroom. The finished lower level provides a versatile space perfect for a family room, home office, or gym. This level also includes a laundry room with an additional refrigerator, and access to a private one-car garage with extra storage space. For added comfort and energy efficiency, each room is equipped with its own thermostat. Located just minutes from shopping, schools, dining, the library, playgrounds, and public transportation, this home also offers easy access to downtown, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway-putting everything you need within reach.
