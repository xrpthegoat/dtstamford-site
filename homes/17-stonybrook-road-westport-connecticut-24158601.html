About this home

This modern farmhouse was recently completed 2019 with highest level of craftsmanship, stunning interior and exceptional modern design. Covered front porch with seating area welcomes you and leads to grand front door entry with two-story foyer. Sun-filled open floor concept 9ft+ ceilings, white oak flooring and contemporary vibe. Fully furnished with essentials and many extras(bikes, scooters, and more). Custom kitchen/family room with marble counters, shiplap ceilings, a center island with breakfast bar, dining area, and a gas fireplace. Large barn doors from the family area lead to a formal living room that can be closed for quite time or opened for entertaining. The formal dining room, office, mudroom, and two half-baths are located on the first floor. A beautiful master suite with sitting area, x-large walk-in closet, and a luxurious marble bath is located on the second level. There are four additional bedrooms, one with en-suite bathroom and the other two sharing a Jack and Jill bathroom. Fifth bedroom are located on third level with full bath. Lower level is finished and offers over 1,800 square feet entertainment, pool, ping-pong, games, and fully equipped gym with bath. Backyard is fully fenced enjoy heated pool, covered porch with smart louvers that adjust with weather. Located right across "Earthplace," a nature preserve with lots of great walking trails, 62+ acres of open space, including a bird sanctuary, interpretive exhibits and nature inspired playground.
