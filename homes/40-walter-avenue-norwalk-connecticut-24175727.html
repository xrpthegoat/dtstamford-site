About this home

Welcome home to this charming Cape nestled in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations, where classic Connecticut character meets modern updates and everyday comfort. This beautifully maintained 4-bedroom,3 full bath home offers flexible living space, stylish finishes, & the warm, inviting feel New England buyers are sure to love. Step inside to discover hardwood floors flowing throughout most of the main level, where 2 spacious bedrooms and an updated full bath provide easy one-level living. The remodeled eat in kitchen is the heart of the home, featuring modern cabinetry, elegant quartz countertops, & stainless-steel appliances - perfectly designed for both everyday living & entertaining alike. This custom designed kitchen opens to both a dining area & spacious living room offering room for everyone and a versatile bonus room complete with its own full bath and large closet, ideal as a private home office, guest suite, or 4th bedroom. Upstairs, retreat to the serene primary suite showcasing a beautifully updated full bath with porcelain finishes and a sleek stand-alone shower, creating the perfect private escape at the end of the day. The finished lower level adds even more functional living space with a cozy family room, additional office or flex space, plus ample storage and laundry area. You are sure to appreciate the efficient and cost saving natural gas heat along with city water and sewer!
