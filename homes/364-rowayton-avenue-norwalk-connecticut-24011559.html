About this home

NEW PRICE! Tremendous value in an expansive Rowayton Colonial with a nod to its 1800's roots in the best town in Fairfield County! Beautiful stone work, mature & flowering trees, perennial gardens, private patio and level back yard with plenty of room for a pool. Light and bright spacious interior. Entertain in the oversized living room with fireplace, formal dining room, both with French doors to the patio/herb garden. The kitchen and walk-in pantry open to a cozy den with built-ins and a full bath with shower. Accent lighting for art and extra closets throughout. The main level also includes an incredible great room with multiple uses, including guest suite, home office (discretely plumbed for possible kitchen), family room, bedroom loft with built-ins, large window seat, and full bath. This space is designed as an in-law/nanny suite and possible rental, has both interior and outside access. Upper level has spacious primary suite with fireplace, two closets (1 walk-in), full bath, vaulted ceiling and windows on 3 sides. There are two additional bedrooms and a full bath with laundry on the upper level. Large 3 car garage, plenty of parking, full basement with workroom and lots of storage. New roof in 2023. Walk to Rowayton train, village shops and restaurants, Five Mile River and so many fun summer activities.
