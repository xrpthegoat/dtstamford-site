About this home

Just beyond a lovely fieldstone wall is 24 Noroton Avenue - a charming traditional Connecticut colonial in the heart of Darien, close to both Darien beaches, Baker Park, Blue Ribbon Hindley Elementary School and Roost Coffee just to name a few. The new Darien Commons, Heights Crossing along with the Noroton Train Station are also a short distance away with sidewalk access. The first floor features a sunny kitchen with high-end appliances and a granite island that opens to a welcoming family room with built-ins, a formal dining room and front to back living room. On the second floor, there are 4 spacious bedrooms, all with walk-in closets, including a primary bedroom with an en-suite full bath. The finished basement with mudroom cubbies and built-in cabinetry is perfect for movie nights, a work-out area or play/study space with generous storage and laundry. Relax and entertain in your very private backyard with a stone patio. Attached 2 car garage. There is also a recently added new roof with solar panels and water heater for energy efficiency along with a number of additional improvements. Make this easy-to-maintain home with a great layout and a fabulous, fun location your own!
