About this home

Waterfront Living at its best on beautiful Long Neck Point. Panoramic open water views of Greens Ledge Lighthouse and Long Island. Five minute walk to both Pear Tree Point Beach and Great Island, Darien's new 60 acre coastal preserve. This is your vacation home and your primary residence! Totally renovated in 2010 by Fox Hill Builders this house is elegantly redone and ready for someone who wants to enjoy Coastal Living. Windows and doors are hurricane proof and the house sits high above mean high water. Rare opportunity to buy on the water, with a dock, open water views and no flood risk!
