About this home

Discover the charm of 102 Houston Terrace, a delightful cape located in the sought-after Cove neighborhood of Stamford, CT. Just a stone's throw from the waterfront, this well-kept property boasts a functional kitchen, a versatile ground-floor bedroom suitable for a home office, and a brightly lit living/dining area. The second floor houses the master suite, two additional bedrooms, and a bathroom. The partially finished basement offers extra space and laundry facilities. Embodying clear pride of ownership, the property boasts verdant landscaping and thriving herb and raised-bed gardens, providing a source of homegrown produce. A private patio provides the perfect setting for outdoor socials or tranquil backyard retreats. Situated only a few minutes away from Cove Island Park, I-95, and the bustling heart of downtown Stamford, this residence provides accessibility, tranquility, and a quality way of life. Make sure not to miss the opportunity to witness it yourself!
