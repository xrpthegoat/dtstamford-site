About this home

Spacious and beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath Colonial-style townhouse offering over 3,100 sq. ft. of living space in the desirable Springdale neighborhood. Privately set back from Hope Street, this home features a bright and open floor plan, hardwood floors, a spacious eat-in kitchen, dining area, and comfortable living spaces ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The primary suite offers a walk-in closet, fireplace, and private bath. A large finished lower level with a full bath provides flexible space for a family room, home office, gym, or recreation area. Additional features include central air, natural gas heat, in-unit laundry, attached garage, ample storage, and a private patio. Conveniently located near the Springdale train station, shopping, restaurants, schools, parks, and downtown Stamford. A rare rental offering with the space and feel of a single-family home.
