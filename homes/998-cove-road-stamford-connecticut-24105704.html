About this home

This modern, luxurious 4-bedroom, 4.5-bath custom-built townhouse features an office, meticulous craftsmanship, and an open-concept layout. The first floor includes radiant heat, a gourmet kitchen with a center island, black stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and Caesarstone countertops, plus a living room with an LED fireplace. The second floor houses a lavish master suite with a balcony, dual walk-in closets, a double sink bathroom, and a glass shower. The in-home washer and dryer are conveniently located on this floor as well. The ground floor hosts a guest apartment, a full bath and an exercise room that opens to a private patio. Hardwood floors, gas heat, central A/C, LED lighting, and spray foam insulation are featured throughout. In addition to a one-car garage, there's a reserved parking space. The property is moments away from Cove Island Beach and Park, offering beaches, picnic spots, trails, playgrounds, tennis courts, an ice rink and a marina. I-95, Chelsea Piers, the train station and downtown Stamford are easily accessible. The house has external video recording devices.
