About this home

Welcome to this bright and spacious 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment in Westport's sought-after Greens Farms neighborhood. This well-maintained home features finished space and additional storage in the lower level, ideal for a home office, gym, or playroom. Enjoy the convenience of in-unit laundry, two included off-street parking spaces, yard access, and two window A/C units provided at no extra cost. Located approximately 1 mile from the Greens Farms train station and close to beaches, Sherwood Island State Park, Post Road shopping, and restaurants. Tenant responsibilities include electricity, cable, and snow removal from the front steps. Landlord provides water, gas, and heating. Lease term: 12 months or longer.
