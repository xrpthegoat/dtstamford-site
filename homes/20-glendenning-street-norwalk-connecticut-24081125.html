About this home

This charming Cape Cod in Norwalk offers 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and a layout that blends comfort with flexibility. With 1,638 square feet of finished space, including a partially finished basement (adding about 400 sq ft), the home features hardwood floors, a newer kitchen, and a sun-filled living room that flows into an eat-in kitchen. Two bedrooms and a full bath on the main level provide easy living, while two more spacious bedrooms and a second full bath upstairs offer privacy and space. Outside, enjoy a large deck and a flat backyard with a pergola--perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The one-car detached garage, central air conditioning, and generous storage throughout the home make daily living convenient. Located on a peaceful street between East Rocks and West Rocks, you're moments from the restaurants, shops, and conveniences of Westport Avenue, yet tucked away in a quiet neighborhood that feels worlds apart. With its bright interiors, updated features, and move-in-ready condition, this home is an incredible opportunity in a desirable location.
