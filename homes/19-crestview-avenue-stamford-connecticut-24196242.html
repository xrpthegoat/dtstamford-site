About this home

Welcome home to 19 Crestview Avenue - a classic colonial blending timeless architectural charm with modern comfort in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Set on two lots, this home offers over 2,826 square feet of light-filled living space, with warm, inviting interiors featuring gleaming hardwood floors and a cozy fireplace - perfect for everyday living and easy entertaining. The main level offers a seamless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen areas. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms plus a finished loft that flexes as a fourth bedroom, along with two full and one half bath. Need extra room? The finished basement offers endless potential as a home gym, media room, or play area. Step outside to your ultimate summer retreat: a gorgeous, fully fenced-in in-ground pool tucked privately behind the house, surrounded by generous patio space for sunbathing and outdoor dining. A detached two-car garage and driveway provide ample off-street parking. Ideally situated near the Springdale Metro-North station, local parks, and top dining spots, with swift access to downtown Stamford and major commuter routes - move right in and enjoy your own private oasis!
