About this home

Welcome to this delightful ranch-style home in the charming coastal village of Rowayton, CT! Situated on an oversized corner lot, this three-bedroom, two-bath residence offers a blend of comfort and convenience. The sunlit living spaces create a warm and inviting atmosphere, with a wood burning fireplace in the living room and an updated kitchen leading to a screened- in porch. The home sits on over one third of an acre where you can create your own backyard oasis - perfect for gardening, outdoor gatherings, or simply relaxing and enjoying the fresh coastal breeze. There is plenty of storage space in the basement that leads to a one car garage. Beach rights offer easy access to the serene shores of the Long Island Sound, and the convenience of the Rowayton train station makes commuting effortless. Rowayton is known for its vibrant community spirit and rich cultural scene. This home is truly a gem and offers the best of both worlds - a tranquil retreat with easy access to all that Rowayton has to offer. Do not miss the chance to make this charming coastal ranch your own!
