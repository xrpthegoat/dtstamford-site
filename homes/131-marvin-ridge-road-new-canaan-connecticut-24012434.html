About this home

Welcome to a unique oasis in the heart of New Canaan. This 4 bedroom/2.5 bath colonial gem offers elegance, space, and convenience throughout 3,100+ sqft. Enjoy picturesque pond views and abundant natural light throughout the home. The main level features seamless flow for entertaining, with a spacious kitchen, dining area, living room and an expansive family room with a cozy fireplace and wet bar. Upstairs, the master suite awaits, offering a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle of daily life. Complete with an ensuite bathroom and ample closet space, this sanctuary is the epitome of comfort and relaxation. Three additional bedrooms share a full bathroom, each thoughtfully designed and generously sized, providing plenty of space for family and guests. The lower level spans over 1,700 sqft. of unfinished space with many possibilities in addition to an attached two car garage. Outside, the park like grounds on 1.3 acres, offer endless opportunities for outdoor enjoyment and recreation. Whether you're hosting a barbecue, playing catch on the lawn, or simply relaxing in the shade of the majestic trees, you'll find that this property is an amazing extension of your living space. Located in close proximity to schools, renowned restaurants and local shops, this home offers the ultimate in convenience and lifestyle. With easy access to major transportation routes, commuting is a breeze!
