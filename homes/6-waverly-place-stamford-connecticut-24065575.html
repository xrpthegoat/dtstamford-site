About this home

Charming 4 bedroom, 2 full bath Cape home located in the downtown area of Stamford; Walk to train station, public transportation, shopping and more! Pets are welcome with landlord approval. This rental has shared grounds as a tenant resides in the lower level/basement. That unit has no entry access to the house. The house tenant will cover all house utilities which includes the heat & electricity for the lower level (minimal expense). Hardwood floors. Interior freshly painted throughout! Central air. Gas heat. Screened in front porch just perfect for Spring/Summer entertaining. Comfy, light & bright living room. This kitchen makes every inch count - cozy & efficient. 2 bedrooms on the main level with full hall bath - bedrooms on main level can accommodate twin or full beds. Primary bedroom on upper level with additional bedroom and 2nd full hall bathroom. Plenty of space for storage. Full size Washer/dryer units included. Shared yard space & shared off street driveway parking. Tenant responsible for all utilities + lawn/landscaping and snow removal. Credit, proof of income and references required. Available now!
