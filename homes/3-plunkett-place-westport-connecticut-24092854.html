About this home

Built by Mattera Construction and tastefully updated throughout by the current owner, this home exemplifies timeless design, top of the line craftsmanship, and features stunning custom details as well as extensive upgrades throughout. Perfectly sited on a professionally landscaped acre down a private lane in the heart of Westport, this spectacular property offers an ideal location combining both privacy and convenience. The first floor is open and bright and is highlighted by its updated gourmet kitchen w/ large center island, top of the line appliances, & large pantry. A spacious family room w/stone FP ,well appointed formal areas, paneled office w/coffered ceilings & built ins and sizeable mudroom complete this level. The second-floor features 5 en suite bedrooms ,including a serene primary suite complete w/fireplace, 2 closets & Luxurious new primary bath featuring heated floors and soaking tub. The Finished 3rd floor media space offers so many options. The walk out lower level offers an additional family room, 1000 bottle wine cellar, rec space, 1/2 bath & gym. Over 8800 SF of finished space overlooking a stunning outdoor space perfect for entertaining w/ mature plantings, expansive blue stone patios, outdoor kitchen & fireplace, gunite pool, spa & full property irrigation. Addl upgrades include control4 smart home, audio, golf simulator, heated 3 car garage, newer mechanicals and much more. Modern luxury meets flawless design to create a home for a lifetime!
