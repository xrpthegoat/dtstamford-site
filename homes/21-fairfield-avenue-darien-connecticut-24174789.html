About this home

Step into timeless charm at 21 Fairfield Ave, Darien, where classic farmhouse colonial design meets modern luxury. This fully renovated 4-bedroom home features a stunning white high-end kitchen and baths, complemented by an open-flow layout and oversized triple pane windows that bathe the spaces in natural light. Downstairs, a finished basement offers incredible versatility with an additional bedroom, heated floor full bath, and an expansive playroom-perfect for guests or family needs. Practicality shines with abundant storage and a spacious laundry room. Outside, the large, level fenced yard is a private retreat, complete with a serene pebble seating area under a majestic Japanese maple, a huge garden space, a storage shed, and a modern deck ideal for entertaining or relaxing. Located on a sought-after neighborhood street, this home is as mindful as it is beautiful, boasting eco-friendly, green-rated upgrades. Its prime location puts you just steps from the train, restaurants, and local shops, blending convenience with lifestyle. 21 Fairfield Ave offers the perfect balance of style, function, and location-a home designed to enhance your everyday living.
