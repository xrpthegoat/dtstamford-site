About this home

Spend a one-month summer escape in this exceptional 5-bedroom home, located on a quiet cul-de-sac for the perfect blend of privacy and convenience. With a heated pool, hot tub, and expansive yard, this home is designed for effortless summer living. Inside, the open kitchen with a massive island connects seamlessly to the eating area, while the family room, just steps up, offers a private yet accessible space to relax. Additional features include a playroom, sun porch, and an outdoor porch with a fireplace, providing plenty of room to unwind. The finished basement features a guest or au pair suite with exterior access and a full bathroom, offering privacy and flexibility for your needs. Conveniently located close to the beach, town, and train station, this home is the perfect setting for a peaceful summer retreat on a quiet street. Available July 11 to August 11
