About this home

Discover this well-maintained split-level home at the end of a cul-de-sac with a private yard. The living room flows into the dining area, and the eat-in kitchen adds convenience. Hardwood floors are underneath the carpet. There are 3 bedrooms, including a master, and a full bath. The lower level features a den with deck access and a half bath. A roughed-in room with a large window leads to the backyard, ready for your finishing touches. With a full basement and garage, this home is a gem. Major updates in 2008 included windows, doors, roof, heating, and AC.
