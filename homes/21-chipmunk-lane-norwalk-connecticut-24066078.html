About this home

Welcome to 21 Chipmunk Lane, a charming home nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in West Norwalk. This spacious 2,358 sq. ft. home features a versatile open floor plan, perfect for entertaining guests or enjoying quiet family nights. With 5 bedrooms, there's ample space for everyone, whether you need extra sleeping quarters or a home office. The front living room can easily transform into a formal dining area, ideal for hosting dinner parties or cozying up by the fire. The home features 2 well-appointed bathrooms and sits on a generous half-acre lot, providing plenty of outdoor space and a deck off the family room. With a full, unfinished basement, attic and 2 car garage there is plenty of storage. Conveniently located just 10 minutes from downtown Darien and New Canaan, this home combines tranquility with accessibility.
