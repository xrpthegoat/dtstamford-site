About this home

A rare offering in the heart of downtown Greenwich, this 2,500 sq. ft. luxury condominium delivers an exceptional blend of sophistication, privacy and effortless living. Set within a serene, tree-line enclave, the residence offers a refined retreat just moments from Greenwich Avenue. A private elevator from the garage introduces a thoughtfully designed interior where elevated finishes and timeless details define the space. The main level showcases a gracious living room with a gas fireplace and custom built-ins, flowing seamlessly into an expansive dining room designed for elegant entertaining. The chef's kitchen is appointed with high-end appliances, a bespoke breakfast nook, and opens to a private covered terrace overlooking the lush grounds. A powder rooms completes this level. The second floor reveals 2 generously proportioned bedrooms, a full bath and a stunning primary suite featuring a spa-inspired dressing room. A dedicated laundry area enhances everyday convenience. Additional amenities include private garage parking with an electric charging station and abundant storage. Offering the rare combination of tranquility, scale and premier in-town location, this residence of luxury living with the charm of a secluded "treehouse" retreat.
