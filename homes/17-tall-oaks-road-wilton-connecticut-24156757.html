About this home

HIGHEST and BEST by Noon Tuesday, March 10th. Set at the end of a quiet Thunder Lake neighborhood cul-de-sac, 17 Tall Oaks Rd in Wilton, CT offers privacy, polish, and everyday function on 1.81 landscaped acres. Hardwood floors run throughout, leading to a beautifully updated kitchen that flows into a vaulted-ceiling family room centered on a gas fireplace. There is a full bath on the main level with a home office adjacent that could be used as an additional guest room if needed. Upstairs, the primary suite features an exquisitely updated bright and sunny bath and generous closet space. Three additional bedrooms are complemented by an updated hall bath for guests and household needs. A dedicated main-floor office space supports work-from-home, while a generously sized additional rec/playroom with vaulted ceilings adds flexibility for hobbies, media, games or entertaining. Step outside to a screened-in porch with trex deck and a beautiful, open and level backyard. The surrounding garden beds have been lovingly maintained over the years, creating an inviting setting for dining, relaxing, and entertaining. Central air, forced hot air heat, 200-amp electrical service, and a whole-house generator add confidence and comfort. Convenient to Wilton town forest trails and National Park Weir Farm, and served by Wilton's award winning Public Schools. This sun filled, happy home is freshly painted throughout and move in ready!
