About this home

If you are looking for ease, convenience, location, comfort, and an updated home, then this is where you are meant to be. This condo is impeccably maintained and is nestled near the harbor in the South End. Commuter? it doesn't get any better than this, easy access to I-95, Metro North, Amtrak. This location is within walking distance to corporate buildings, harbor, restaurants, markets, parks, dog park, train station, and much, much more. The updates include: new laminate flooring, new appliances - euro refrigerator, range, DW, frontload washer/dryer, water heater, baseboards, windows, sliders, air conditioners, updated bathroom, and new recessed lighting throughout offering nightlight and dimming capability that can be controlled by your smart phone. After a long day, retreat to your home for some well deserved "me time" where your slice of tranquility and nature await you. This condo is nestled on the outskirts of the city surrounded by the LI sound, restaurants, organic market, private clubs, yachting, boat clubs, health clubs. Love to entertain? Lounge on the semi-private patio, grill, and/or play a few lawn games, and when you are ready to hit the city, there won't be any need to plan ahead on transportation. Stamford holds harbors nine Fortune 500 companies, entertainment, museums, theater, shopping, beaches, hiking, and education to name a few more. This home is conducive to any lifestyle.
