About this home

Welcome to 25 Cogswell Lane, a stylish and turn-key residence in Stamford's sought-after Westover neighborhood. Located at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this home offers privacy with easy access to downtown, Metro-North, and the Merritt Parkway-ideal for a modern, on-the-go lifestyle. Inside, you'll find a sleek kitchen with quartzite counters and high-end appliances, flexible living and work spaces, and thoughtful updates throughout. The home includes two dedicated offices for remote work or creative projects, a private home gym, a third-floor bonus room (2025), and a newly finished basement bar and bath (2025)-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Multiple fireplaces-four wood-burning plus a contemporary electric-add warmth and charm. The backyard feels like a private retreat with a heated pool, patio, waterfall, and fountain (2023), new fencing, and lush privacy trees. A greenhouse with thermostat, sink, and radiator adds year-round functionality. Major updates include a new roof (2017), boiler (2023), propane water heaters (2021), updated pool systems, spray foam insulation, and generator wiring. Imported stonework and custom design features bring sophistication while keeping the home comfortable and livable. A rare opportunity to own a Westover cul-de-sac home that blends style, comfort, and convenience-ready for its next chapter.
