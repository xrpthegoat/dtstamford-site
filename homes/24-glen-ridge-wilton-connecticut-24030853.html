About this home

Immaculate and bright Wilton townhouse within walking distance to downtown! Glen River is one of Wilton's most popular complexes and this unit has a lot to offer. The unit also has new windows (2022), roof & siding (2022), HVAC(2020) New water heater(2020). Come check out everything Wilton has to offer!
