About this home

Move right into this beautifully renovated, turnkey North Stamford Cape, where timeless charm meets modern updates. This three-bedroom home has been thoughtfully updated throughout, featuring hardwood flooring, fresh interior paint throughout the entire home, and stylish finishes. At the heart of the home is a stunning designer kitchen featuring a spacious center island, perfect for everyday living, entertaining, and holiday gatherings. The inviting family room offers a beautiful stone fireplace and oversized windows that fill the space with natural light. A separate dining room, main-level bedroom, powder room, and versatile home office that can also serve as a mudroom complete the first floor, offering a flexible layout ideal for today's lifestyle. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, spacious walk-in closets, and a beautifully renovated full bathroom featuring both a soaking tub and separate shower. The lower level provides a dedicated laundry area as well as ample storage space. Outdoor living is equally inviting, with a charming screened-in porch overlooking the expansive backyard, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A two-car garage and newly paved driveway add convenience and curb appeal. Ideally situated in desirable North Stamford, this home is within walking distance of Chestnut Hill Park and three minutes to the Merritt Parkway. The perfect combination of comfort, style, and convenience await. Excellent credit required.
