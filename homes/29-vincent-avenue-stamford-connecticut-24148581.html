About this home

Fall in love with this classic 1930s Colonial, beautifully perched on a hill with wonderful curb appeal on a corner lot. Ideally located within walking distance to downtown, yet tucked away on a quiet street with a private yard-this home offers the perfect blend of charm, comfort, and convenience. The elevated setting fills the home with natural light, with morning sun streaming into the back rooms and afternoon light warming the front. Enter through a welcoming vestibule into gracious living spaces designed for gathering and entertaining, featuring a spacious living room and family room. The updated kitchen offers stone countertops and timeless style. Upstairs are three bedrooms and two fully renovated baths. The lower level provides a cozy playroom with fireplace. Enjoy abundant storage, a two-car attached garage, and a serene outdoor retreat with patio, usable yard, and mature gardens-ideal for relaxing evenings or hosting friends. A truly special home you'll be proud to call your own.
