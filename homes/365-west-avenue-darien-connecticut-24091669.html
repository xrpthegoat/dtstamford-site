About this home

Discover the epitome of modern living at 365 West Avenue. This one-of-a-kind residence boasts a contemporary and stylish aesthetic, highlighted by unique materials and finishes with a distinct Danish flair. This home is thoughtfully designed to blend comfort with sophistication. Step inside to find a fully renovated kitchen featuring a Viking gas range & hood, and a breakfast bar with room for 3 stools. A chic dining room with fireplace and cozy living room provide just the right ambiance. The enclosed front porch reveals two finished flex rooms, ideal for a pair of home offices, a study, sun room or playroom, catering to versatile lifestyle needs. Upstairs find 3 distinctive bedrooms, each equipped with a ceiling fan, and sharing an elegantly updated full bath. Downstairs, a polished finished basement with a full bath offers a haven for both kids and adults alike. Adding to its allure, the finished detached garage has a vaulted ceiling, creating an exceptional space for an office, studio or for movie nights. Entertain with style on the backyard deck, complete with an extra-long breakfast bar and a separate outdoor kitchen patio equipped with a built-in sink, grill, and fire pit. The home is entirely enveloped by lush green giants, ensuring privacy. The expansive lot with a large side yard invites endless play opportunities. Many beautiful furniture pieces available for purchase. Close to Noroton Heights, train, Woodland Park and beaches. A modern yet timeless living space.
