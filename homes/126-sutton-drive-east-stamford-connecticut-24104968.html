About this home

Prepare to be wowed as you enter this beautifully updated home offering the perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and functionality. A spacious open floor plan welcomes you in, featuring a generous dining room and a stunning modern kitchen complete with granite countertops, newer stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, and gleaming hardwood floors throughout. The sun-drenched living room is filled with natural light ideal for entertaining. The first floor boasts a desirable bedroom, full bath and sliding doors leading to a brand-new deck-perfect for guests or multi-generational living. Upstairs, retreat to your spacious Primary Bedroom with direct access to a newly updated full hall bath. The Primary Suite also includes a large walk-in closet and a private 12x9 deck-perfect for morning coffee or quiet reading. Two additional oversized bedrooms with excellent closet space complete the second floor, along with the ultimate luxury: second-floor laundry-no more hauling laundry up and down stairs! The lower level offers a versatile finished space ideal for a home office, gym, or playroom, while the unfinished area provides abundant storage and organization potential. Step outside to enjoy your private, fully fenced backyard with a new deck, firepit area, and lush landscaping-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Additional highlights include a one-car garage, circular driveway , & central location close to Glenbrook, Noroton Heights & Stamford Train Stations, I-95 & Merritt
