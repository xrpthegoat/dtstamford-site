About this home

DRAMATIC PRICE IMPROVEMENT. Classic 1930's style Connecticut Colonial with its own guest cottage in the heart of Holmes School neighborhood. Four bedrooms, three and a half baths, formal living and dining room and freshly painted kitchen overlooking backyard create the perfect home. With a sun room, finished 3rd floor & partially finished basement there is plenty of space for living. At the end of a long driveway there is an an oversized two car garage and cottage built above creating incredible privacy for guests & family to visit. Or also Ideal for an ''at home office'' away from the main house. The BEST commute in Darien! A little TLC will provide the opportunity to make this your FOREVER HOME. Best neighborhood with tons of families an short walk on the sidewalk to Holmes elementary school. Walk to all three Darien Schools as well as train & Woodland Park.
