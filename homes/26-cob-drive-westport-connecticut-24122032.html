About this home

An incredible opportunity has become available to purchase a much-loved, solid home on excellent property in a sought-after location. One of the most exciting new listings to hit the Westport market in months- a scenic, usable 1.25 acres on a beautiful street of high-value homes is the setting for this spacious Colonial. This timeless floor plan offers gracious rooms, hardwood floors in many areas and upgraded thermopane windows. The large front-to-back living room has a fireplace, and an oversized dining room. The kitchen overlooks a gorgeous backyard & flows through to a large family room/alternative dining room w/vaulted ceiling & access to a deck. A main level play/rec room or office is an unexpected bonus, and the full bath on this level adds flexibility to the use of space. Upstairs are four generously-sized bedrooms. The primary suite has large closets and an en-suite bath. The walk-out lower level has an additional finished recreation/play room w/access to the backyard. An attached two car garage, central air, mature trees and landscaping all add to the appeal. Westport is one of the most coveted communities with commuter rail access to NYC, award-winning schools, treasured beaches & recreation, sophisticated shopping, dining, the arts and much more. You have been waiting for a terrific opportunity to become available...and this is the one. *** final offer is due by Monday, November 10, 2025 at 6 PM.*+
