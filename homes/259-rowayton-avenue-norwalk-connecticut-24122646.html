About this home

Discover the allure of 259 Rowayton Avenue-a quintessential Victorian masterpiece that could soon be yours. This enchanting 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home seamlessly marries the elegance of classic architecture with modern sophistication, offering 4,274 square feet of beautifully updated living space. The home's multiple living areas gracefully span three floors, providing abundant space for relaxation and entertainment. Outside, a private, serene yard invites you to unwind with a stunning gunite pool and spa, alongside a built-in outdoor grill-a perfect setup within an expansive 18,295 sq ft lot. An exquisite wrap-around porch graces the exterior, adding a charming touch to this exceptional residence. For car enthusiasts, the property features an expansive 2-car garage and ample parking with two driveways. Positioned in the heart of Rowayton, this home offers more than luxurious living-it presents a lifestyle enriched by the vibrant local community. Experience timeless elegance in a home that truly stands apart.
