About this home

Bring your remodeling ideas and make this home your own! This charming cape is nestled on a lovely street in the highly sought Newfield neighborhood of Stamford. The front of the home features a welcoming covered front porch that takes you into this cozy 4 bedroom single family home. The main level features include a primary bedroom and smaller bedroom with an additional shared bathroom. There is a bright eat-in kitchen with side entrance off the driveway, a sunroom off the living room and a formal dining room. The second level has two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, plus a half story of unfinished attic space with potential for an additional bedroom. This home also features a lovely private yard with a patio, a detached one car garage and an additional under house garage with interior access to a full unfinished basement with plumbing for an additional bathroom which gives even more potential for living space. The windows on most of the main level and the oil tank have been recently replaced. It is conveniently located near schools, parks, shopping, downtown, Stamford train station, I95 and the Merritt Pkwy which also makes it a great location for commuters. Come see all the potential this home has to offer. Home is an estate and being sold "As Is". Buyer to perform their own due diligence.
