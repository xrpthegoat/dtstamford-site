About this home

Incredible opportunity to own a beautiful Nantucket-style colonial with four bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and more than 3,100 square feet of living space. This meticulously maintained home has fabulous curb appeal and is set on a beautifully landscaped, level lot in a prime Darien location. The first floor features 9-foot ceilings and easy flow, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Highlights include a gracious living room with built-ins, formal dining room, inviting eat-in kitchen, family room with fireplace, and convenient first-floor laundry. The second floor offers three bedrooms, including a primary suite with two walk-in closets and a recently renovated, generously sized bath. The third floor provides great flexibility, with a fourth bedroom, full bath, built-ins, and sitting area or office space, perfect for guests, home office, au pair, or private retreat. The finished lower level adds more versatility, with a playroom, abundant storage, and flex space for home office, gym or craft room. Outside, a lovely bluestone patio overlooks the lush, professionally landscaped backyard. A stunning hornbeam and arborvitae hedge frames the property, creating an exceptional sense of privacy and a beautiful backdrop for outdoor living. Ideally located just steps from the Noroton Heights train station, ballfields, playground, shops, restaurants, and vibrant new amenities of Darien Commons and Heights Crossing. This fabulous turnkey home truly checks all the boxes.
