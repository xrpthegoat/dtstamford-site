About this home

Introducing 6 Side Hill Road, a stunning 5 bedroom colonial offering the perfect balance of traditional grandeur and modern casual living. Situated on a quiet cul-de-sac, located in the highly desirable 'Old Hill' neighborhood of Westport, within walking distance to the town center, elementary school, Earthplace nature center - & only minutes to the train station. The 9' main level rooms pivot off the impressive double height entryway with fabulous light. The formal living room with gas fireplace enjoys views of the expansive front lawn, while the formal dining room with ornate tray ceiling overlooks the flat, fenced backyard. Work from home in the office and then relax with family and friends in the casual open-plan spaces. The large kitchen boasts an island, viking stove, subzero, & breakfast nook and opens into a huge family room with shiplap surrounded fireplace & sliders to access the deck - making indoor/outdoor living a breeze. A large bright additional rec/playroom off the family room completes this floor. Upstairs, the large primary suite features the 3rd fireplace, an elegant tray ceiling, a large walk-in closet and a dressing room (could be 2nd office area). The ensuite bathroom with double vanity, large tub and walk-in shower. The 3rd & 4th bedrooms share a hall bath and the 4th & 5th bedrooms share a Jack & Jill bath. The lower level offers a large mud room, gym and also gives access to the 3 car attached garage. Live your best life, less than 50 miles from NYC.
