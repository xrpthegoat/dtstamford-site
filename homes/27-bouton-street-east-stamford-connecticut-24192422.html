About this home

Welcome to Springdale! Enjoy the comfort of single-family living without the maintenance in this spacious tri-level townhouse located in the highly desirable Springdale neighborhood. Conveniently situated near shopping, restaurants, schools, Metro-North, and major transportation routes, this home offers the perfect combination of space, comfort, and convenience. The main level features a bright and oversized living room filled with natural light, an eat-in kitchen with new SS appliances, new countertops, a sliding door leading to a private deck/patio, and a convenient half bath. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms, a full bathroom, and access to a large attic, providing plenty of additional storage space. The fully finished lower level offers outstanding versatility with its own private entrance, full bathroom, and wet bar. Whether you need a fourth bedroom, guest suite, home office, gym, recreation room, or space for extended family, this flexible area can easily adapt to your lifestyle. Freshly painted throughout and move-in ready, this home also includes two reserved parking spaces, making everyday living even more convenient. Please Lease Requirements: Excellent credit, verifiable income, and references are required. Pets will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
