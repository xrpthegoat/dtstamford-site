About this home

Enjoy a wonderful life on Woodside Avenue, one of Old Hill's most popular streets, walking distance to town & Earthplace Nature Centre. This charming ranch home offers sophisticated, easy living & so much more. The open floorplan radiates off the bright kitchen with stainless steel appliances, including a wine fridge & gorgeous granite countertops. The formal living room enjoys a wall of sliding glass doors that allow light to flood in & give access to the outside entertaining space. The dining room is perfect for both formal & informal meals. The stunning sunroom is flooded with light & offers a more informal space to relax & enjoy the landscape and the ease of indoor-outdoor living. The large vaulted family room has a grand scale but feels intimate with a cozy fireplace & built-in bookshelves. The main level primary suite is quietly situated and enjoys the second fireplace, has sliders giving access to the private pool area with hot tub. The rest of the primary suite includes a large walk-in closet & marble bathroom with a vaulted ceiling, elegant double vanity, soaking tub & steam shower. Upstairs there are three additional bedrooms and a full bath. Outside the property is surrounded by a professional landscape with mature specimen trees. As well as the attached two car garage there is another large addition that has huge potential. It is a car collector's dream, an opportunity to add an office, a gym & plenty of storage or even perhaps as an ADU. Live your best life!
