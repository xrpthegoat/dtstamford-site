About this home

Set on over two private acres at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac in Westport, this traditional center-hall Colonial with four bedrooms and 2.5 baths offers a wonderful combination of privacy, space and potential. A circular driveway framed by stone walls, Belgian block and exterior lighting creates a welcoming arrival. Inside, hardwood floors run throughout the main living spaces, including a spacious living room and dining room, while the family room is anchored by a classic brick fireplace and opens to a standout screened porch with a vaulted wood ceiling, exposed beams and skylights. This inviting porch is the perfect place for seasonal dining, relaxing and entertaining while overlooking the expansive backyard and mature landscape. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features an en-suite bath, walk-in closet and additional closet. Hardwood floors are found beneath the carpeting upstairs. With plenty of opportunity to update and personalize, this well-loved home is ready for its next chapter. It's a special place to create your own vision while enjoying all that Westport has to offer on lovely Primrose Lane.
