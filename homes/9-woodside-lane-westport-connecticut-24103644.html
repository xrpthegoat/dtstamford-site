About this home

Welcome to this charming Colonial home in the desirable Old Hill neighborhood of Westport, CT. This 3,683 sq ft residence on a generous 0.95-acre lot blends classic character with modern convenience. The open layout connects living areas to the outdoors, creating an inviting space for relaxation and entertaining. The home boasts attractive exterior accents while maximizing space and inviting light and bright natural elements inside. Step into the backyard to enjoy a private pool with a Jacuzzi spa, surrounded by mature landscaping - ideal for summer gatherings or quiet evenings. The living room includes a cozy fireplace, perfect for family and friends. The layout also offers a spacious family room and formal dining area. The kitchen is equipped with modern appliances, including a wall oven, cooktop, microwave, refrigerator, and dishwasher. The primary suite on the upper level includes a walk-in closet, and an additional office provides a great work-from-home space. The main level features a practical den for extra living space. The lot includes a porch and patio, offering plenty of room for outdoor enjoyment. Enjoy easy access to Westport's beaches, parks, shopping, dining, and train station, providing convenient commuting and lifestyle options. Enjoy the peaceful neighborhood and a true sense of community. Discover your dream home at 9 Woodside Lane - where classic Colonial charm meets modern living in one of Westport's finest areas.
