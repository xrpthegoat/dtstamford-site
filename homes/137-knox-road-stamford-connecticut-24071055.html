About this home

Charming Colonial located on a sought-after street in Springdale. Freshly painted and featuring hardwood floors throughout, the home offers a cozy and inviting layout. The main level includes a spacious living room, a dining area, gorgeous custom new kitchen and new full bath. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a full bath. The clean, unfinished basement presents great potential for additional livable space. The home boasts a newer high-efficiency natural gas boiler, newer hot water heater, new roof, new gutters, new exterior paint & new stainless steel flue liner. Take advantage of the large, fully fenced backyard for wonderful outdoor living complete with beautiful new deck. A must-see!
