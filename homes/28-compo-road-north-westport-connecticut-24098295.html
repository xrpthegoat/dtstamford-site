About this home

Modern, Antique Colonial on .58 acres across from Winslow Dog Park and on the corner of Raphael Rd cul-de-sac. Main level has: Kitchen, Dining area, Living Room w/FP, Office, Family Room, Full Bath and Screened In Porch with wood stove, and Mudroom/Laundry. Upper level has Primary Suite with Full Bath, Walk In closet and Gym. There are also 2 other bedrooms and 1 full bathroom on the Second Floor. The Third Floor has a playroom and storage. Kitchen and all Bathrooms have been updated. One of the most convenient locations in Westport-Walk to Compo Shopping Center, Dog Park, Trader Joes and lots more!
