About this home

This considerately renovated 1880 Victorian is steps to the East Norwalk Green. Its 2019 updates include the bathrooms, kitchen, walls and floors, roof, insulation, central air, tankless water heater, portions of the siding, plumbing, and electrical systems. The modernization provides the benefits of current living while preserving the home's period appeal. Offering three levels of living space, the main level has a foyer/parlor with carved staircase, living room, and dining room connected by classically detailed openings. There are two staircases, front and rear for convenience. The classic kitchen includes marble countertops, stainless steel appliances, chef's range, island w/ second sink, a pantry, and an eating area. Adjacent are a mudroom and a powder room. The main level's gracious rooms feel settled, substantial, and timeless. The second floor includes the primary suite with two closets and a sophisticated ensuite bath. Two additional bedrooms share a double-sink hall bath with tub/shower. The third floor offers two additional bedrooms, including one with an en-suite bath (tub only), plus a double-sink hall bath. Additional features include a full unfinished basement with washer and dryer; detached two-car garage; and level yard. Set on an in-town street of classic homes, think sidewalks and strolls to amenities, while also having this convenient location minutes from train, beaches, shopping, schools, and major transportation routes.
