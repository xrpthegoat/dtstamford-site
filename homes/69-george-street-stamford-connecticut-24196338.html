About this home

Welcome to charming rental located in the Cove, one of Stamford's most desirable coastal neighborhoods, where charm, convenience and community come together. Step into the sun-drenched, spacious tiled entrance, a versatile sanctuary perfect for a home office, garden room or den. Picture evenings spent in the cozy living room with gorgeous hardwood floors. A striking staircase with unique staggered windows welcomes natural light into the home, creating visual interest throughout. The open kitchen seamlessly connects with the dining and living rooms, with sliders leading to an expansive deck overlooking a spacious backyard. Upstairs, the bright, primary bedroom features two large closets, a vaulted ceiling, skylights and a large picture window. Two additional rooms offer flexibility for bedrooms or office space.The walk-out lower level offers abundant usable space, ideal for a recreation room, plus ample storage. Enjoy walk-out access to private backyard. Experience the best of Cove living with easy access to beaches, parks, Chelsea Piers, shopping, restaurants, Metro-North and I-95.
