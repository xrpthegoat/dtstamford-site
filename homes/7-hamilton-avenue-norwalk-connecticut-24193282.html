About this home

Welcome to 7 Hamilton Ave, SoNo's hidden gem and a commuter's dream! Perfectly situated just a short walk from the South Norwalk train station and everything vibrant SoNo has to offer, this spacious top-floor 2-bedroom condo combines convenience, comfort, and privacy. Enjoy peaceful, tree-lined views along with an abundance of natural light throughout the home's open and inviting floor plan. The recently updated kitchen and beautifully renovated bathroom featuring a deep soaking tub add modern style and functionality, while high-efficiency insulated windows and a newer sliding glass door enhance comfort year-round. Additional highlights include a full-size washer and dryer conveniently located in the unit, plus a private balcony off the living area-the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the warmer months. A dedicated storage space in the building's storage room provides added convenience and extra room for your belongings. The building itself has also undergone both interior and exterior improvements, offering added appeal and peace of mind. 7 Hamilton Ave comes complete with 2 reserved parking spaces (#1 and #18), this home offers the ideal blend of location, updates, and lifestyle in the heart of Norwalk. Come create new memories at 7 Hamilton Ave.
