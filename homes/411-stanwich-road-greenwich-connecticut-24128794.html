About this home

Welcome to Mead Farm - exquisitely renovated and transformed midcountry Greenwich estate that blends historic character with modern luxury. Set on nearly 6 acres in a 2 acre zone, this 9,189 SF residence offers 6 bedrooms, 6 full and 2 half baths. A recent multi-year renovation by renowned designer Thom Filicia, paired with award-winning landscaping by James Doyle, elevates the property to a rare level of refinement, harmonizing timeless design with modern comfort. Originally built in 1846, the home features wide-plank floors, copper gutters, 7 fireplaces, and a wraparound porch. The rebuilt water tower adds a gym, wine room, full bath, and dramatic double-height office. Outdoors, enjoy gardens, terraces, pool with autocover, pond and a barn with loft and garages - just minutes from town.
