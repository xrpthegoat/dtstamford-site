About this home

Beautiful 3 to 4 bedroom ranch in desirable, quiet Pepper Ridge neighborhood. Gorgeous eat-in-kitchen with granite countertops, gas range, dual-ovens, and two dishwashers, plus a large sunny dining area. Living room with fireplace and lovely picture window. Spa-inspired primary suite with freestanding soaking tub, stall shower, floating duel-sink vanity, with granite finishes, and a walk-in closet. 4th bedroom separate from the others so it could be used as office or den if preferred. One-car attached garage, huge basement with full bathroom, and a large level backyard with in-ground pool. Convenient access to Newfield elementary school, worship, public transportation.
