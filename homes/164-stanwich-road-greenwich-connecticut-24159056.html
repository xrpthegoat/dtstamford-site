About this home

Welcome to this exquisitely updated Colonial on 1.16 landscaped acres near Greenwich Country Club and minutes from town. A grand foyer with gleaming hardwood floors and sweeping staircase introduces the elegant interior. The main level includes a formal living room, dining room, two studies, and a gourmet kitchen with Wolf appliances, Palissandro marble, Thermador coffee station, a custom wet bar and a 425-bottle wine cellar. The kitchen opens to a family room with fireplace and French doors. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a sitting room, luxurious bath, office, and fireplace. Four additional bedrooms complete the level. Enjoy 3,000+ sq ft of terrace, heated saltwater pool, Viking grill, and a pavilion with sound system. The Heated and air conditioned 4-car garage adds storage and versatility.
