About this home

''Orchard Hill'', an exquisite 6.18 acre gated estate with custom built & newly renovated (2024) stone & shingle style 6 bed/7 bath main house, separate 2 bedroom stone guesthouse, pool w/spa, pergola-covered poolside terrace w/outdoor fireplace, tennis court & 4 car garage. Perched high above the surrounding properties, this gated compound enjoys beautiful views and total privacy. Mature trees, verdant lawns & stone paths that border William Rutherford designed gardens complete this extraordinary exterior.. Newly renovated, light filled interiors have beautifully proportioned rooms w/elegant public rooms, new primary bath, fabulous kitchen/ family room layout, 2 library/office options & 1st floor guest suite. Designated elevator option. Lg gym, wine cellar, bath & mudroom in LL.
