About this home

Welcome to 2 Dogwood Lane, a stunning home nestled in the prestigious Tokeneke Association. This elegant home offers the perfect blend of modern luxury and timeless design, with an expansive 5,265 square feet of living space on a beautifully landscaped 1.5 acre lot. Step inside to discover a tastefully renovated interior featuring high ceilings, tons of light and spacious living areas. The home boasts a welcoming dining room with picturesque views of the flat yard, large living room with fireplace, an office, guest room suite, multiple spaces off the bright white kitchen with marble counters and high end appliances. Don't miss the cozy eat in area complete with a fireplace - a space ideal for intimate gatherings. Five bedrooms and three and a half updated baths, provides ample space for family and friends. The finished basement offers a versatile playroom and extra living space plus an attached two-car garage provides convenience and storage. Enjoy the peace and tranquility of the fabulous private outdoor space, perfect for entertaining on the large blue stone patio or simply relaxing with a book. Enjoy waterfront moments through Tokeneke and enjoy the serene setting while being a short walk from downtown Darien and the train. This home is not just a place to live, but a lifestyle that promises comfort, luxury, and a sense of community. Make 2 Dogwood Lane your new haven in Darien!
