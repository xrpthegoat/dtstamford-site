About this home

RECENT PRICE REDUCTION!! Welcome to this Architectural Gem at 238 Newtown Turnpike. If you're not looking for that "Cookie Cutter Colonial Home", this exceptionally designed and built contemporary home by owner architect is on the market for the very first time. Located in South Wilton you're just minutes away from all major transportation hubs and shopping destinations. You are immediately greeted by oversized rooms with amazing ceiling heights that will wow you upon entering this home. Different levels with original hardwood floors add to the dramatic interiors. The modern kitchen and appliances fit the homes design beautifully and the living/dining area is bathed in light from multiple oversized windows. The impressive large primary en-suite with fireplace is located on the main level, where you will also find another spacious bedroom and full bath. Upstairs you will be impressed with the open bonus areas and two large bedrooms and bathrooms. Additionally there is a terrific room with cathedral ceiling that can meets the most creative of options. This home with 2 large decks sits on 2 plus acres. Enjoy the large back yard for all types of entertaining and a variety of activities. Bring your imagination and desire to make this your dream home for the future.
