About this home

This attractive colonial is tucked away on a quiet street yet close to everything Stamford has to offer. Freshly painted and newly refinished hardwood floors throughout. The first floor boasts a living room with fireplace, formal dining room with china closet, large eat-in kitchen, a bedroom and a full renovated bathroom. Upstairs you'll find two more large bedrooms and another full bathroom. The semi-finished basement is perfect for a playroom or home gym. Park two cars in your garage and enjoy the lovely private backyard with patio. Electricity included in rent! Also for sale at $599,000.
