About this home

Nestled in the heart of Rowayton, sits this historic 1910 home exuding charm while also featuring all modern day conveniences. Renovated and updated by Beinfield Architecture, this home features an open floor plan. Each first floor living space complements the other and is anchored by the state of the art, bespoke kitchen. 11' foot ceilings, oversized windows, french doors out to the stone patio and pool, all add to the unique aesthetic and forward design of this home. Upstairs are two generously sized bedrooms that share a full hall bath along with an ensuite bedroom and gorgeous primary suite. The primary suite has a walk-in-closet, spacious bathroom with a soaking tub and shower. The soaring ceilings and large window with views of the Five Mile River enhance the already unparalleled primary. A staircase leads to a loft that could make for a great office, den or exercise area. Steps to Pinkney Park and town and a short stroll to the beach, you cannot beat the location. This is truly Rowayton at its finest. There will be a delayed closing of 12/1.
