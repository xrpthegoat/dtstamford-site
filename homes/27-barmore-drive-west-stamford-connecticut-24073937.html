About this home

Nestled in the highly sought-after Newfield neighborhood of Stamford, this charming home is being offered for the first time in over 50 years. Set on a peaceful, low-traffic street, it provides the perfect balance of serenity and convenience. The main level boasts three generously sized bedrooms and an open living and dining area with hardwood floors beneath the existing carpet. The kitchen flows seamlessly into a delightful sun porch, ideal for relaxing on warm summer evenings. The lower level features a guest bedroom with a full bath and laundry, a spacious family room with sliding doors opening to a private patio, and a level backyard perfect for outdoor enjoyment. Additional highlights include a newer roof, a high-efficiency gas boiler, central air conditioning and a large garage with abundant storage space. Please note that this home is being sold strictly as-is.
