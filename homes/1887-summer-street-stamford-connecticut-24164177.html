About this home

Welcome to 1887 Summer Street, a beautifully maintained 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom residence located in the heart of downtown Stamford. Set within a charming Victorian-style home, this unit offers a rare blend of historic character and modern living. Step inside and you'll immediately notice the stunning mahogany woodwork throughout, adding warmth, elegance, and timeless appeal to the space. From the detailed trim to the rich hardwood finishes, every corner of this home reflects craftsmanship you simply don't find in today's construction. The layout offers a comfortable flow with two well-sized bedrooms, a full bathroom, and inviting living spaces filled with natural light. The property includes one dedicated parking space. If additional parking is needed, tenants can take advantage of nearby public parking options in the downtown area. The location is truly unbeatable. Situated on Summer Street, you are just moments from Stamford's vibrant downtown, offering an incredible mix of restaurants, cafes, shopping, and nightlife. Whether you're grabbing a morning coffee, meeting friends for dinner, or enjoying a night out, everything is right at your doorstep. For commuters, easy access to the Stamford Train Station, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway makes traveling into NYC or surrounding towns seamless. Living here means enjoying the perfect balance of city convenience and neighborhood charm.
