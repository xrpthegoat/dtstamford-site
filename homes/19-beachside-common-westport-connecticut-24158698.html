About this home

Like-new custom modern home with open-concept living designed for comfort and entertaining. The main level connects the family room, dining room, office, and chef's kitchen featuring Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, invisible speakers, quartzite countertops, custom cabinetry, two large islands and multiple sliding glass doors leading to an outdoor oasis. The spacious pantry includes custom shelving, an ice maker, and built-in water dispenser. The primary suite features cathedral ceilings, gas fireplace, a private balcony, expansive walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired bath with radiant heated floors, steam shower, soaking tub, and wet bar with refrigerator. The second level also includes four additional en-suite bedrooms and a spacious laundry room with double washer and dryer and custom cabinetry. The third-floor lounge with wet bar and roof deck provides additional entertaining space along with another bedroom and full bath. The finished lower level offers a great room with wet bar, temperature-controlled wine room, gym, media/playroom, and en-suite guest bedroom. Outdoor spaces feature bluestone patios, a heated covered porch with fireplace, built-in grill, heated Gunite pool and spa and heated walkways. Additional highlights include Savant smart home system, Lutron lighting, whole-home audio, radiant heat, multi-zone HVAC, generator, and three-car garage with electric vehicle charger. Walk to Burying Hill Beach, Greens Farms Academy, and Greens Farms train station.
