About this home

A rare offering in sought-after South Wilton, this exceptional 2009 Colonial pairs over 5,800 square feet of sophisticated living space with the feel of a private retreat. Surrounded by 1.8 acres of park-like grounds, the property offers a serene, resort-like setting just minutes from Rolling Hills Country Club, Ambler Farm, and major commuter routes. Designed for both elegant entertaining and effortless daily living, the home features 5 bedrooms and 6 baths, including a flexible main-level suite ideal for guests or a private office. Sun-filled living spaces include a gracious sitting room, living room, and sunroom, while two fireplaces bring warmth and character throughout. The kitchen is perfectly positioned to overlook the pool and backyard, with sliding doors opening to the deck and outdoor entertaining spaces for seamless indoor-outdoor living. Upstairs, a spacious great room provides ideal flex space for media, play, or gathering, while the expansive primary suite offers walk-in closets and a spacious bath. A second-floor laundry room, walk-up unfinished attic, and finished lower level add versatility and convenience. Outside, the lifestyle is truly exceptional. A saltwater pool, hot tub, expansive deck, level lawn, and indoor/outdoor sound system create the feel of a private oasis-peaceful, polished, and perfectly suited for both quiet enjoyment and entertaining.
