About this home

Introducing The Green Nook Condominium, a beautifully renovated and redesigned custom condo that offers a sophisticated, contemporary style unmatched in modern expression and luxurious living. The Green Nook is one of two stunning side-by-side condos proudly introduced by our premier builder, GreenDay Construction. This 2024 renovated condo is filled with natural light and features a modern open floor plan interior with elegant, luxurious details. The three-bedroom condo is professionally designed and includes modern comforts while maintaining a timeless and graceful feel due to the classic hardwood floors, paint colors, new lighting, and cabinetry featured throughout. The condo features touches ranging from traditional to contemporary to modern-all perfectly infused with relevant choices that integrate a casual luxury lifestyle, incredible attention to detail as only a top-notch builder/designer can bring to life. Experience this new condo complex as the perfect home with convenient access to shopping, dining, and the metro north.
