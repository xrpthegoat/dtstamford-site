About this home

Set well back from the road at the end of a long, tree-lined drive, this private Contemporary residence offers a rare pairing of seclusion and central Westport convenience. A short stroll from the Metro-North station, Compo Beach, and Longshore Club Park, the home rests on a level, professionally landscaped 1.05-acre lot in the coveted Compo Beach neighborhood - and, notably, sits comfortably outside the flood zone. Inside, natural light pours across 4,449 square feet of light-filled living space. The main level opens through a gracious foyer to a beamed-ceiling living room with fireplace, a warm family room anchored by a second fireplace, and a well-appointed kitchen with a dedicated home office just off it. A first-floor primary suite makes single-level living effortless, complete with skylight, walk-in closet, and en-suite full bath. Upstairs, three additional bedrooms - including a second suite with its own full bath and walk-in closet - surround a versatile loft, with laundry conveniently placed on the same level. Rich hardwood floors run throughout. A rear sunroom invites easy indoor-outdoor living, opening to the deck, porch, and private grounds, while the finished lower level adds flexible space and a further full bath. Move-in ready and ideally positioned for summer at the beach - an A+ location, out of the flood zone, and ready to welcome its next residents.
