About this home

Welcome home to this well-maintained Springdale Ranch, lovingly cared for by the same owners for over 40 years! Offering the ease and convenience of one-level living, this home features a bright entry foyer opening into a welcoming living room with hardwood floors, fireplace and separate dining area. Eat-in kitchen offers access to the patio and private yard. This thoughtfully designed layout features a separate bedroom wing with two bedrooms and an updated full bath, while the primary bedroom is privately situated on the opposite side of the home and includes a full bath and walk-in closet. Finished walk-out lower level adds approximately 400 sq. ft. of additional living space, ideal for a family room, recreation area or home office, along with garage access and storage. A bonus second driveway provides additional parking, complemented by a convenient carport. Ideally located just minutes from Springdale Village, the train station and local schools!
