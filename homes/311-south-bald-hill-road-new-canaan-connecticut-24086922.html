About this home

First time on the market! Regardless of the seasons the property of this renown New Canaan horticulturist is stunning; there is a man-made pond, a large meadow, and flowering trees, shrubs, stone walls and paths that divide the two and a half acres into tasteful, green enclaves. Situated in this oasis is a classic 4-bedroom center hall colonial. Offering exceptional living spaces and great flow the home includes a formal living room with fireplace, dining room and paneled den with fireplace. The eat-in kitchen leads to an enclosed sunroom with wraparound windows which provide views of this breathtaking property. Upstairs, the primary suite has its own dressing room and generous bath. Three more roomy bedrooms complete the second floor, offering comfort and flexibility for every lifestyle. Every season brings new beauty to this stunning two-and-a-half-acre property. Lovingly maintained and offered as is.
