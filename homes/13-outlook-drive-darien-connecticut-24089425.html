About this home

Move right into this completely renovated home on a sought-after street in the Hindley School district. The kitchen and bathrooms have all been thoughtfully remodeled for today's lifestyle while preserving the charm and integrity of this classic Tudor. With a sunny open kitchen-family room, separate vaulted ceiling office/sunroom, and living and dining rooms, the spacious floor plan allows for both relaxed living and formal entertaining. All 4 bedrooms are upstairs and include a primary suite with custom built-ins, and a spa-like bathroom with soaking tub and oversize shower. Other recent renovations include the construction of a brand new heated 2-car garage, with enough height for a car lift, and outfitted with a custom work bench and storage system.The original 1-car attached garage has been converted to a "flex" room (playroom/gym/office), bringing the total living space to approximately 3,000 sq.ft. The meticulously landscaped yard features a stunning freeform saltwater pool with spa, recently resurfaced and retiled, and a large grassy play area. The location in the Noroton Manor Association, with its tennis courts and LIS access, can't be beat - close to both downtown Darien and Stamford, Darien beaches, elementary school, YMCA and Metro North train.
