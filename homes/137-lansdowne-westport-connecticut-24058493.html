About this home

If you've been searching for an opportunity for a maintenance-free lifestyle in one of Westport's most desirable communities, 137 Lansdowne might just be the home you've been waiting for! This 2 bed, 2.5 bath residence offers 2100 of living space. Located in the coveted Lansdowne Community, you'll enjoy breathtaking surroundings, top-tier amenities, & an unbeatable location near shopping, schools, and commuter routes. The home's thoughtful layout is designed for both entertaining & everyday living. The large living room is perfect for gathering & features a fireplace that adds a touch of warmth & charm. There is a formal dining room & for those who need a work space, the office/sunroom is an absolute highlight; bathed in sunlight with views of the backyard & patio. The oversized eat-in kitchen is a true gem, complete with hardwood floors & a brick wood-burning fireplace. Whether you're preparing a casual meal or entertaining guests, the kitchen offers plenty of counter space & cabinets to suit all your needs. Upstairs, you'll find the primary bedroom suite which features a spacious bathroom with both a jacuzzi tub & a separate shower stall. The 2nd bedroom offers a walk-in closet & a large dressing room/storage area. Living in Lansdowne means you'll have access to a wide range of amenities designed for relaxation & recreation. A short walk from your front door is the gazebo, walking paths, pool & tennis/pickleball court. Quarterly District Tax of $1578.25
