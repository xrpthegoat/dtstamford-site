About this home

Heming Way is a premier North Stamford cul-de-sac with underground utilities and a quiet, refined setting. This custom-built Colonial offers exceptional space, craftsmanship, and flexibility for today's lifestyle. The chef's kitchen with stainless steel appliances and sun-filled breakfast room opens seamlessly to the expansive family room with stone fireplace, ideal for everyday living and effortless entertaining. A private office with custom built-ins and a full bath on the main level provides flexibility, including potential for a first-floor primary suite. Upstairs, the vaulted primary suite features a spa-inspired bath with heated marble floors and an oversized walk-in closet, plus three additional bedrooms and a private upper level retreat. The finished lower level with custom cherry cabinetry and wet bar extends the entertaining space and flows easily to the backyard. A statement property offering privacy, versatility, and timeless appeal in one of North Stamford's most desirable enclaves.
