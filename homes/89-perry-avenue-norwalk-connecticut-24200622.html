About this home

Charming SILVERMINE colonial farmhouse! Drive through the stone pillars to admire the lovely expansive leafy grounds as you step up to the welcoming front porch. To the right as you enter is the family room with vaulted ceilings, large stone fireplace and sliders to the back deck. The dining room is distinctively decorated which leads to the light filled eat in kitchen that has deck access as well. Nicely appointed kitchen that flows into the office/den area and a full bath. Three bedrooms upstairs with refinished wood floors; full bath with double sinks. Lower level is great space for a workout area as well as tremendous storage. Professional photos to come Friday.
