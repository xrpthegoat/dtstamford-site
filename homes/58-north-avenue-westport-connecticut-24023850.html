About this home

An extraordinary, once in a generation opportunity to live a life of understated beauty, charm & elegance in a landmark Westport home. The Alfred Mills house; originally constructed in 1890 has been lovingly preserved, expanded & enhanced by the current owners. Completely renovated and updated interiors that honor and elevate the original design, together with a stunning fully landscaped property with dazzling Gunite pool and secret spa. The property & gardens can only be described as magazine worthy, with ample areas to entertain, relax and play in privacy & beauty in every season. The kitchen was designed for chefs and entertainers: Subzero, gas range plus wall ovens, wet bar with icemaker, an oversized island plus a spacious area for casual dining. In addition to the original living room with fireplace & built-ins, the Great Room addition with fireplace is a favorite gathering space that opens to both a private terrace or the enchanting sunporch. The secluded primary suite is a serene & romantic haven with double walk-in closets, private full bath and romantic balcony overlooking the grounds. There are 4-5 additional bedrooms including the original primary with full bath that now serves as a home office. Wide plank hardwood floors throughout the main living area & pumpkin pine floors on the 2nd and 3rd levels. Prepare to be seduced by the original details and timeless style, while appreciating the care the current owners have taken to meticulously maintain this home.
