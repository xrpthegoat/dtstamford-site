About this home

Prime penthouse residence in Westport's premier condo building on the shores of the Saugatuck River is ready for occupancy! Located on the southwest corner of Bankside House, unit 4B provides river and downtown Westport views from the primary suite, living spaces and private rooftop terrace. Walk downtown to enjoy all the fabulous shopping and dining with ease, or dazzle your guests by serving an alfresco meal on the rooftop, with a panoramic backdrop. The open flow living spaces, both ensuite bedrooms and the den enjoy tremendous natural light from custom floor-to-ceiling windows. The living room showcases a linear gas fireplace and blends into the kitchen area by way of a spacious 10' island. Marble countertops, Cesar cabinetry and state of the art appliances make for a dream kitchen. Dining options abound with dining room, private balcony and island seating for six. Take entertaining to a new level by adding an outdoor kitchen/BBQ on the rooftop terrace! Accessible by a stunning retractable skylight, the rooftop terrace also has room for a personal hot tub/spa. Carefree living awaits at Bankside House. Leave the stress behind and let management handle the maintenance. Waterfront living in Connecticut's Gold Coast can be yours!
