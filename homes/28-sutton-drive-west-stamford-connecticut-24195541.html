About this home

Welcome to this charming 3 bedroom ranch style home nestled in the highly sought-after Glenbrook neighborhood offering a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Step into accessible one-floor living with layout designed for effortless functionality. Features sunny, spacious living room with a vaulted ceiling and skylight, seamlessly open to the dining area and a lovely kitchen. French doors provide access to a oversized patio overlooking a level , fenced backyard, the perfect space for outdoor gatherings, play or simply relaxing, complete with a garden and storage shed. Additional features include hardwood floors, a full unfinished basement and attic for extra storage, All this, just minutes from the train, shopping, restaurants, and I-95. Don't miss this opportunity to make this wonderful home your new home.
