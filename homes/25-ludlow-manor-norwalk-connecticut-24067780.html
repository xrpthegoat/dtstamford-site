About this home

This charming 1950s single-family home combines timeless wood finishes with modern updates. Enjoy coffee on your private deck, cozy nights by the fireplace, or hosting in the spacious formal dining room. The kitchen features granite countertops and tiled floors, while wood floors flow throughout the home. The main level includes a sunlit living room with deck access, a high-ceiling dining room, and a versatile bedroom with a separate bath-perfect for an office or one-level living. Upstairs, find two spacious bedrooms, a full bath, an office nook, and a master bedroom with a private balcony overlooking the fenced backyard. Walk to the beach, park, Marvin School, and the train. Close to SONO nightlife, restaurants, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway.
