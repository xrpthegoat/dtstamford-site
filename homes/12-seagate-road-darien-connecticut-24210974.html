About this home

Custom crafted by Fox Hill Builders in 2017, this exceptional home combines impeccable quality craftsmanship, generous proportions and a light-filled, open floor plan designed for modern living. Expansive windows and glass doors bring abundant natural light throughout while creating a seamless connection to outdoor spaces and water views. At the heart of the home, the impressive great room opens to the rolling front lawn overlooking the water, while additional glass doors lead to a private covered deck and expansive patio surrounded by mature privacy plantings-ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The gracious primary suite features spectacular water views, a private balcony and two walk-in closets. Four additional bedrooms, each with a private bath and walk-in closet, plus a dedicated office complete the second floor. The third floor offers a private suite with oversized bedroom, bonus room, full bath, balcony and water views. The finished lower level provides flexible space for a media center, play area and gym, plus a full bath. Enjoy a private mooring, walk to nearby beaches, restaurants and Blue Ribbon award winning Hindley Elementary School. Come experience the highly sought-after Seagate Association-a vibrant neighborhood community in one of Darien's most desirable coastal settings.
