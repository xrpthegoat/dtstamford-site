About this home

*Thank you for your strong interest. Please submit your highest & best offer by Monday, 9/21 @ 4pm* This charming Colonial offers a comfortable and inviting floor plan, featuring a welcoming living room with fireplace, spacious dining room, and a sunroom just beyond the French doors. The sunroom provides easy access to the patio, making it an ideal spot for morning coffee, quiet relaxation, or entertaining. The bright eat-in kitchen overlooks the fenced backyard and patio, creating a wonderful setting for everyday living and outdoor gatherings. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms, a full bath, and generous closet space. Recently refinished hardwood floors bring warmth and character throughout the home, while the walk-up attic provides ample additional storage. Recent updates include refinished hardwood floors, newly installed central air conditioning, and conversion to natural gas. Throughout, the home retains its original charm, complemented by higher ceilings and timeless architectural details. Ideally located with convenient access to shopping, Westport Avenue, the train, and I-95, this wonderful home offers the perfect blend of classic charm, modern updates, and everyday convenience.
