About this home

Private getaway just 1 hour from NYC! Bathed in natural light and surrounded by mature landscaping, this 3-bedroom, 3.5 bath contemporary offers a rare blend of privacy, beauty and lifestyle. Set on 2 level acres with an oversized Har-Tru tennis court, the home features an open, airy floor plan with expansive windows that frame the natural surroundings. Vaulted ceilings and generous entertaining spaces flow seamlessly both indoors and out creating an inviting atmosphere. The kitchen is bright, spacious and perfect for daily life as well as entertaining with ample counter space, eating area and adjacent living space ready for your personal touch. The primary bedroom has a vaulted ceiling, seating area with modern fireplace, a full bath with jetted tub and a walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms, both en suite and a convenient laundry room complete the second floor. A finished lower level adds even more potential with room for recreation and home office. Whether you're a tennis enthusiast or simply seeking a peaceful, light-filled retreat with room to grow, this delightful residence offers exceptional promise in a picture-perfect setting.
