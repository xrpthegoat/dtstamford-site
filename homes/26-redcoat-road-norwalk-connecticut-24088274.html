About this home

West Norwalk Beauty! This meticulously maintained colonial home in a very desirable neighborhood is move-in ready. With great curb appeal, this lovely home offers a covered, front porch and entry foyer. The formal living room, with gas fireplace, is lined with built-ins for all your treasures. Formal dining room with built-in hutch--great storage for your fine dining pieces. The updated kitchen boasts lovely cabinetry, granite counters, tile floor and a spacious eating area with bay window. Good-sized den off kitchen is great for many needs (Office/TV Room/Rec room/Guest room) and it leads to a delightful 3-season room with large windows & screens (depending on weather), tile floor, ceiling fan and wainscotting--a true bonus space to relax and enjoy the peaceful wooded wetlands and privacy it creates. Upper level offers a primary suite w/plenty of closet space, ceiling fan and updated bath with large, tiled shower stall. There are two additional large bedrooms and updated hall bath. LL family room (apprx 600 additional sf) has bay window, 2nd gas fireplace, vinyl plank flooring, great storage in both boiler & laundry areas and walkout to level backyard and gardens, charming stone paths and creek. HW floors (great condition) throughout most of house and high-quality replacement windows throughout. Low maintenance Hardie board siding (2017). Extensive filtration systems for well. All window treatments incl. This special home offers many upgrades and enhancements you must see!
