About this home

Welcome to 95 Foxwood Road! A well loved, picture perfect 5 bedroom, 3.5 bath Colonial situated on a level 1.83 acre corner lot and located in the beautiful Foxwood Lake neighborhood of North Stamford. Main level boasts a formal living room with a fireplace, home office, formal dining room, sun filled eat-in kitchen, updated powder room and screened porch. Family room, bedroom and full bath or possible in-law/ au pair suite is up a few stairs. The upper level consists of an airy primary bedroom, stylishly updated primary bath, 3 additional generously sized bedrooms, and a shared hall bath with laundry. Walk-out lower level is partially finished; can be used as a second family room, game room, gym, or other. Large additional storage space, second laundry room, utility room, and half bath complete the space. The home is located 7.5 miles to the Metro North train and downtown Stamford, 3 miles to the Merritt Parkway, close to shopping, and the towns of Pound Ridge and Bedford. Not to be missed! * Showings to begin on Friday, 10/24
