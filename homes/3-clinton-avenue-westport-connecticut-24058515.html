About this home

Welcome home to this vintage, 1930's walk-to-town Colonial that is perfectly finished with a keen attention to the details. The hallmarks of this beautiful home are its original hardwood floors, moldings, woodwork and high ceilings. The stairwell features a new handmade leaded glass window by Tina Green of Westport. The long list of improvements include a new hot water heater, windows, renovated baths, custom shutters, and Porcelanosa tiles. The entire interior has been professionally painted and powerwashed. The renovated kitchen with white cabinetry and granite counters has new Fridgidaire gas range and Fisher & Paykel refrigerator from Aitoro's. The breakfast bar opens to a spacious dining room. The new sunroom/den adds extra living space to the first floor and leads to the new backyard patio, home gym/office and 1 car garage. A state-of-the-art security system is centrally monitored. On the exterior the newly refreshed front porch, bluestone walkway, patio, and landscaping enhance this home's curb appeal and grace. The newly created office or gym is located just off the garage. This home is perfectly turnkey and ready to be enjoyed.
