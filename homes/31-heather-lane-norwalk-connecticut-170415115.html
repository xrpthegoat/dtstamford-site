About this home

Welcome home to 31 Heather Lane! Move right in to this beautiful, raised ranch in desirable East Norwalk. Step in and you’ll find hardwood floors throughout the main living area, living room with wood burning fireplace, formal dining room, and the freshly painted kitchen with access to the expansive back deck. Three bedrooms and one full bath complete this level. The lower level has a family room with walkout to the back patio, one additional bedroom or office, a full bathroom and access to the one car garage. The private backyard is perfect for entertaining and includes a shed for additional storage. Conveniently located minutes from i95, East Norwalk train station, Calf Pasture Beach, Taylor Farm dog park, local restaurants and SONO Collection Mall.
