About this home

Tucked away on a quiet one-way street, this charming 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers privacy, comfort, and the relaxed lifestyle in a private beach association. With recent updates throughout, the home features an open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area. The wood fireplace centers a cozy space for everyday living and entertaining. The split-level design provides a comfortable separation of living and bedroom spaces, while three bedrooms, two full baths, and the finished basement space offer flexibility for family and guests. Step outside to a private side patio, perfect for morning coffee, summer dinners, or unwinding after a day at the beach. Whether you're looking for a year-round residence or a weekend escape, this hidden gem combines a peaceful setting with the charm of coastal living.
