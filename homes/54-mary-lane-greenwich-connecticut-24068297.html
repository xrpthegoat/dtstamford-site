About this home

Available immediately, this bright and sunny home in the coveted Riverside area offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Located in a highly sought-after neighborhood, featuring an open, airy floor plan filled with natural light. The freshly painted kitchen includes granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a spacious island, perfect for snacks and homework, and a cozy dining area. The inviting living room, with a gas fireplace and large windows, is bathed in sunlight. Upstairs are 3 bedrooms with ample closet space and 1 full bath. The lower level includes a family room, 1 full bath and a separate laundry room. There is a 1-car attached garage and a beautifully landscaped yard which features a brook, and a spacious deck, ideal for relaxation and entertaining.
