About this home

Renovated townhouse, conveniently located within 5 minutes to parks, train, highways, shops and restaurants. Brand new eat-in-kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and access to a private porch and yard. Laundry room is upstairs. There is a walk-up attic for additional storage plus plenty of room in the large attached 2-car garage. Central heating/cooling with Nest thermostats for energy efficiency. Full security system already installed.
