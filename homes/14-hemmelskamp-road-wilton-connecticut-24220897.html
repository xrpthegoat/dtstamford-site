About this home

Experience peaceful living in a nature lover's paradise. This John Heggland masterpiece showcases fine craftsmanship, exquisite millwork, beautiful flooring, custom built-ins, and designer finishes throughout. A gracious foyer opens to elegant living and dining rooms with scenic views. The spectacular chef's kitchen features an oversized island and entertaining bar, flowing into a warm family room with fireplace. Enjoy al fresco dining on the expansive patio overlooking a private pond and professionally landscaped grounds, perfect for fishing, skating, hockey, and canoeing. A beautifully appointed home office, full gym, home theater, game room, and finished lower-level offer exceptional versatility. The luxurious primary suite features a fireplace, spa-inspired bath with oversized shower and two walk -in closets. Two additional en-suite bedrooms complete the second floor, while the third floor offers two flexible rooms with built-ins and a full bath. Charming and beautifully constructed shed is a bonus that offers endless possibiities. Enjoy a vacation-at-home lifestyle in an excellent neighborhood for walking and biking, just minutes to award-winning schools, train, major highways, and town. A truly special offering!
