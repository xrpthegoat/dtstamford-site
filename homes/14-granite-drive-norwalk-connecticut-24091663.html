About this home

What's better than living in Norwalk's coveted Cranbury neighborhood? Calling 14 Granite Drive *home*. Nestled on one of Cranbury's most sought-after streets and just a short stroll to Cranbury Park and the brand new Cranbury Elementary School, this 2770 sqft, 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers the perfect blend of comfort, space, and location. Whether you're biking, walking, or simply soaking in the sounds of nature, the neighborhood invites a laid-back yet connected lifestyle. Inside, the updated kitchen features an eat-in nook ideal for casual meals and weekday breakfasts, while the adjacent formal dining room offers ample space for gatherings or can easily flex into a second living area if desired. The front-to-back family room serves as the heart of the home-a cozy, sun-filled space to unwind with a movie, curl up with a book, or simply relax. Upstairs, you'll find three perfectly sized guest bedrooms and a primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and private bath. The finished walk-out lower level adds almost 900 square feet of additional living space-perfect for a playroom, home gym, or media area-and opens directly to the private backyard, a serene spot for summer barbecues or quiet mornings with coffee. Don't miss your chance to live in one of Norwalk's most beloved neighborhoods-14 Granite Drive is ready to welcome you home.
