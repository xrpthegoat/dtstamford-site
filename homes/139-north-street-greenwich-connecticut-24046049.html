About this home

Privacy and close to downtown Greenwich. This HOBI Award winning home was built in 2020 by Red Canoe Partners and Designed by Clarity Home Interiors with thoughtful details throughout. It boasts sunset oriented perspective from large patio and 20x40 gunite pool with built-in auto-cover. The exceptional features of this 8646 sqr. ft. home include 5 bed, 7 bath, 3-car oversize garage, elevator, Wolfe and Sub-Zero appliances, Marvin windows and doors, plus Control 4 Smart Home technology throughout. the Great-Room features full surround sound capability and 13ft bi-folding door to open directly to the rear patio. Stunning Primary Suite with fireplace and view. Luxury primary bath with heated floors, heated towel bar and soaking tub. Huge basement with gym, 2nd laundry, lots of storage.
