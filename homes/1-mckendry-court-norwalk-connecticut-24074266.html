About this home

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, you'll find this renovated and beautifully maintained cottage with a gorgeous yard set along the Five Mile River. The oversized gourmet kitchen offers a light-filled oasis where you can comfortably entertain. Walk through the French doors onto the stunning bluestone patio surrounded by picturesque landscaping and the tranquil sounds of the gentle river - truly a unique property for Rowayton! The living room envelops you in a cozy atmosphere with a stone wood-burning fireplace. This move-in-ready property includes high-end finishes, beautiful blonde wide-plank floors, Carrara marble floors in the bathrooms, a large California closet in the primary bedroom, and plenty of storage throughout. Enjoy all the advantages and delights of living in this coastal town - from the beaches to the parks and the incredible sense of community. It's an easy walk to town or the train with newly updated sidewalks! This Five Mile Cottage is a gem in Rowayton - not to be missed!
