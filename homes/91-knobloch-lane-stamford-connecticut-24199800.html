About this home

Situated in the desirable Southern Westover section of Stamford, this rare .47-acre level lot offers the perfect canvas to bring your vision to life. Flat, cleared, and ready to build, the property features access to natural gas, city water, and city sewer-an exceptional opportunity whether you're an end user looking to create a custom dream home or a developer searching for the ideal site for a new construction spec project. Enjoy the best of Westover living with a peaceful residential setting while remaining conveniently located near multiple Metro-North train stations in Stamford and Greenwich, downtown Stamford, schools, parks, shopping, restaurants, and major commuting routes. With limited opportunities to build new in this sought-after area, this property offers the chance to create something truly special. Bring your plans, choose your builder, and make your vision a reality! The owner/builder also has permits in place for a custom built home, 4,300 above grade sq ft home with full finished basement, 2 car attached garage. Ask for more details.
