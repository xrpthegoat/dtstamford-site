About this home

Welcome to Chestnut Hill, where this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers comfort, character, and convenience on nearly an acre of private grounds. Freshly painted throughout, the home features gleaming hardwood floors and thoughtful spaces designed for everyday living. At the heart of the home, the kitchen opens to a bright enclosed area with walls of windows overlooking the backyard, creating the perfect spot to relax and take in the scenery. On one side, the dining area connects seamlessly to a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace, ideal for both entertaining and quiet evenings at home. The finished basement provides versatile space for a family room, home office, or gym. Additional features include central air, an attached garage, and tastefully landscaped grounds with sprinklers, highlighted by a charming bridge that enhances the property's unique appeal.
