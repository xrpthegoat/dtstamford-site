About this home

Attractive six-bedroom 4536 square feet (includes finished LL) Colonial in North Street School district. Well laid out with newer additions. Spacious & bright home on a quiet road in mid-country Greenwich. Two primary suites, one on each floor. First floor has a living room w/fireplace, well designed kitchen w/space for a large dining table and island seating, family room, formal dining room, library/office, add. bedroom w/laundry & bath. Welcoming deck off kitchen for grilling/entertaining/relaxing, bordered by lush landscaping & overlooks play yard. Upstairs is another spacious primary suite w/walk-in closet & bath, 3 additional bedrooms & bath. Garden level w/gym area, playroom & plenty of storage, 3 car garage w/workshop area. Minutes to downtown, train & schools. Generator!
