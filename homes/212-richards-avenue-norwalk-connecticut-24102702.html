About this home

Welcome to 6 Tealbrook, a spacious 2400 sq ft Colonial nestled in the highly desirable and private Tealbrook community of West Norwalk. This detached single-family home offers three bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, and the ideal blend of privacy and low-maintenance living within a small nine-home homeowners association. Lovingly maintained by its original owner, the home features numerous recent upgrades including Hardie board siding, new windows, a new furnace, and a new oil tank. With these major mechanical updates already complete, the home is move-in ready and offers an excellent opportunity for a new owner to personalize the space. Inside, the main level boasts hardwood floors, a formal dining room, and a bright living room with a charming double-sided wood-burning fireplace. The oversized two-car garage provides direct access to a large unfinished basement, offering endless potential for future living space. Additional storage is abundant throughout the home, including a full walk-up attic. Situated just minutes from Darien, Metro-North train stations, and Norwalk's vibrant shopping and dining scene, this home combines convenience with peaceful community living. Don't miss this rare chance to own in one of West Norwalk's most sought-after neighborhoods.
