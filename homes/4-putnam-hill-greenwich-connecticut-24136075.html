About this home

Welcome to Putnam Hill, a sought-after, 10 acre complex in the heart of downtown Greenwich. This spacious 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment offers 1,300 sf. of living space with a private garden & patio and is ready to customize to suit your lifestyle. Sunny & gracious entry foyer, expansive living room & the largest kitchen in the complex. You can add a W/D, and enjoy the peace of mind that comes with a well-maintained building, featuring a new 2025 heating system and 2021 roof. On-site Superintendent. Maintenance includes your property tax. Elevator accessed, garage parking is on an as-available basis, $85/month. One small pet is allowed. A one time capital assessment of .5% of sale price is due at closing. Within a mile of downtown Greenwich, shopping & restaurants, Greenwich Library, YWCA & YMCA, the Wallace Center, Bruce Museum & cultural events. Putnam Hill combines comfort, convenience & an exceptional value.
