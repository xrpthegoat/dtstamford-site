About this home

Welcome to 994 Shippan Avenue, Unit B-where spacious living, everyday convenience, and the unmistakable coastal lifestyle of Shippan Point come together. This beautifully designed residence offers three bedrooms, each with its own private en-suite bathroom, plus two additional half baths-one conveniently located on the main level and another in the finished lower level. The versatile lower level provides valuable additional living space, ideal for a family room, home office, gym, playroom, or guest retreat. Living in Shippan Point is about far more than owning a home-it is a lifestyle. Walk to West Beach, enjoy the waterfront paths and recreational amenities of nearby Cummings Park, and take in the coastal character of the neighborhood, home to the prestigious Stamford Yacht Club. From mornings by the water to evenings spent enjoying the sea breeze, Shippan Point offers a relaxed shoreline setting just minutes from the heart of Stamford. Some of the city's most beloved local destinations are also close by. Grab breakfast at Elm Street Diner, lunch at Brennan's, a legendary hot-oil pizza at Colony Grill, dinner at Cafe Silvium, and finish the evening with ice cream from Sunny Daes. Despite its peaceful coastal setting, the home remains exceptionally convenient to downtown Stamford, the Stamford Transportation Center, and I-95-making commuting into New York City easy while allowing you to return home to one of Stamford's most desirable waterfront communities.
