About this home

Nestled on 2.22 acres in Greenwich's beautiful Back Country, this meticulously crafted estate was renovated in 2019 and boasts unparalleled luxury and sophistication. With 6 bedrooms, 5 full baths, and 3 half baths spread over 8,812 square feet, it exudes elegance at every turn. The home's exterior features a blend of stone, cedar shingles, and Marvin windows. Inside, Brazilian Walnut floors complement exquisite custom wall art, moldings, and trim.
