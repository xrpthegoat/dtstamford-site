About this home

Welcome to 23 Casement Street, a unique opportunity to bring your vision to life in the heart of Darien. This 3-bedroom, 1,602-square-foot home, set on 0.17 acres, is ready for a complete transformation. Located within walking distance to the Noroton Heights Train Station and the vibrant Restaurants and Shops in Darien Commons, this property offers unmatched convenience and potential. The main floor features a layout that suits modern living, with a solid structure awaiting your design. Upstairs, you'll find three bedrooms. These rooms offer the space and potential to become charming, private retreats. The third-floor living walk-up space is an untapped resource, currently unheated but brimming with possibilities. This area can be transformed into a home office, additional bedroom, playroom, or creative studio, adding valuable square footage to the home. The property's exterior is set on a 0.17-acre lot, with mature landscaping. Whether you envision a garden, a play area, or a serene outdoor retreat, this yard offers the space to bring your outdoor dreams to life. This home is the perfect opportunity for those looking to renovate and create a custom home in a prime Darien location. Don't miss this chance to turn potential into reality at 23 Casement Street.
