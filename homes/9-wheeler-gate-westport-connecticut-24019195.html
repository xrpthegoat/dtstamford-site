About this home

Welcome to the esteemed Compo Commons neighborhood, where this superbly appointed home awaits its next fortunate owner. This exquisitely crafted residence offers unparalleled luxury and convenience, located just a stone's throw from Westport's town center and various amenities, including Compo Beach, Levitt Pavilion, and superb dining spots. This newly renovated, impeccably maintained 5-bedroom, 3 full and 2 half bath home sits on a privately fenced 1/2-acre oasis. It features a beautifully manicured, professionally landscaped property with a heated saltwater gunite pool, spillover spa, bluestone patio, outdoor kitchen, stone fireplace, outdoor shower, and changing room. Inside, the gourmet kitchen boasts top-of-the-line luxury appliances, ample counter space, and custom cabinetry, seamlessly flowing into a spacious family room with a built-in fireplace. The primary bedroom suite includes its own fireplace, custom walk-in closets, and vaulted ceilings, while other bedrooms and bathrooms offer comfort and modernity. Additional features include a spacious two-car garage with high-speed EV charging, an indoor and outdoor custom sound system, city sewer, water, and natural gas. This remarkable property is not just move-in ready; it embodies gracious living with sophistication, comfort, and modern convenience. Don't miss the chance to experience luxury living in Westport.
