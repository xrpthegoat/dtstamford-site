About this home

This stately Colonial home, featuring six bedrooms and six full bathrooms plus three half baths, offers an impressive blend of elegance and comfort. Upon arrival, you are greeted by beautiful landscaping and meticulously maintained gardens, setting a tranquil tone for the entire property. The entryway immediately captivates with its twin staircases and a soaring ceiling, creating a dramatic and welcoming first impression for guests and residents alike. Designed with ample room for everyone, the home boasts a comfortable living room perfect for relaxation, an inviting solarium filled with natural light, a chef's kitchen ideal for culinary pursuits, and a generous family room that serves as a central gathering space. During the summer months, enjoy leisurely days by the pool or savor picturesque sunsets from the comfort of the screened porch, making outdoor living a delightful experience. With four fully finished floors, this home provides all the space and amenities needed to accommodate contemporary living in style and comfort. Sale is subject to Probate Court Approval.
