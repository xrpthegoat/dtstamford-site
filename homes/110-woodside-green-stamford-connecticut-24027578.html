About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST DUE TODAY 6/29 BY 5PM! Carefully updated corner unit in Woodside Green feels like 2 bedrooms for the price of 1! Enter to the foyer which begins the path of hardwood flooring and high ceilings throughout, complete with two deep closets which can also serve as a pantry. A large, sun-filled living room with south-eastern exposure flows into a flexible bonus space, perfect for an office, nursery or possible second bedroom. An updated galley kitchen shines with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops and wood cabinets. The sizeable bedroom can easily fit a king size bed and offers plenty of storage in the true wall to wall closets. With over 800 sqft and easily accessible on the first level, this unit boasts a unique layout and new windows, air conditioning, heating, paint, dishwasher, gas range and microwave since 2022. The grounds at Woodside Green are second to none with acres of lightly wooded green space, lighted walkways, BBQ area and gazebo. All of this is conveniently located in mid-City Stamford where you can see the likes of the Ridgeway Shopping Center and Scalzi Park from the grounds, plus a short trip to downtown Stamford, beaches, I95 and Merrit Parkway, and Metro-North Railroad.
