About this home

Looking for a summer get away with expansive water views, inground pool and a most bucolic setting? Do you desire a short drive or train ride from NYC? Then this wonderful Wilson Point summer rental is for you! Overlooking Long Island Sound on two green acres this custom designed home is meticulously cared for and offers gracious indoor and out door spaces. First floor offers an over sized kitchen and family room, formal living room, formal dining room, home office/tv room, wrap around covered porch, oversized patio, outdoor fireplace and all a few steps to the pool. Upper floor offers two spacious primary suites with full baths and 3 additional bedrooms with hall bath. Third floor offers a huge playroom and can also serve as a 6th bedroom. There is a great workout room in lower level. All rooms are luxuriously appointed and most with water or pool views. The Gunite pool is heated and fully fenced. There is a 1 bedroom guest suite over the detached garage. The Wilson Point Association neighborhood has fun for all ages including a private sandy beach, tennis courts and club house. Hop on your kayak or paddle board and explore the Norwalk Islands or bike to Rowayton Village and Farmers Market. For night life try SONO restaurants; Sono Collection, Oak Hills Golf Course & cultural attractions for all ages. The Rowayton train station is easy 5 minute drive. Available July 20 to Aug 20, Sept 8-Oct 14. Price is per month. No smoking. Credit check & references requested.
