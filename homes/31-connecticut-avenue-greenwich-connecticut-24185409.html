About this home

Welcome to 31 Connecticut Avenue, where the classic charm of a Colonial meets modern comfort in one of Greenwich's most desirable neighborhoods. Ideally situated just moments from vibrant Greenwich Avenue, premier shopping and dining, and the Metro-North train station, this beautifully maintained home offers the perfect blend of convenience and timeless appeal. Filled with abundant natural light, the home features hardwood floors throughout, a welcoming living room with a cozy gas fireplace, and central air conditioning for year-round comfort. The thoughtfully designed layout offers spacious living areas ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Outside, a detached two-car garage and ample off-street parking provide exceptional convenience. Enjoy all that downtown Greenwich has to offer while coming home to the warmth, character, and enduring elegance of this classic Colonial.
