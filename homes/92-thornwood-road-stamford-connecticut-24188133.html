About this home

"To give all interested parties an opportunity to submit an offer, the sellers have established an offer deadline of Monday, July 13 at 2:00 p.m. Please submit all offers by the deadline." Situated on lush level property in one of North Stamford's most picturesque neighborhoods, this spacious Colonial offers a flexible layout with generous living spaces throughout. The home features four bedrooms and two and a half baths. A welcoming eat-in kitchen with center island and breakfast nook opens directly to a bright family room, where walls of windows surround the French doors leading to the covered deck with Brazilian cherry flooring and skylights, creating an inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. The dining room opens to the covered deck through French doors, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The living room with fireplace features oversized windows overlooking the backyard, providing an additional gathering space with beautiful views. The upper-level primary suite includes an oversized ensuite bath and an additional room ready to become the walk-in closet or dressing room of your choosing. Three additional spacious bedrooms and a large full bath with double vanity complete the upper level, while the partially finished lower level offers additional flexibility and easy access to the attached garage. Recent updates include a new septic system, boiler, and oil tank providing peace of mind for years to come.
