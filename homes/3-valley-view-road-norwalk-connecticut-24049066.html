About this home

Lovely spacious 2 Br 2 1/2 Bath Townhouse in the Kingswood Condos in the Cranberry area. Convenient To Train, Rt 7 And Shopping. Loads Of Storage & 1 Car Garage. Main level features elegant Living room W/Crown Molding, Wood Burning Fireplace and Dining Area. The kitchen has a breakfast nook and sliders to access the deck. Credit check, references, 2 months security. Great commuter location close to highways and train. Ask about pets.
