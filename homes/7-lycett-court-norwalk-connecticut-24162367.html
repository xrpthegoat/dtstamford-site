About this home

This beautifully maintained home, tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac offers a flexible 3-bedroom layout (bonus room lower level currently used as 3rd bedroom). This home delivers the space & flexibility today's buyers are searching for. Perfect for modern living, ideal for in-law suite, guests, multigeneration living & working from home. Main level offers a bright, sun-filled living & dining area w/sliders to deck for enjoyable outdoor entertaining. Hardwood floors on main level. Updated kitchen (2026) w/new stainless-steel appliances, new sink & new countertops. Two spacious bedrooms & a full bath complete the upper level. Recently renovated lower-level lives like its own retreat; featuring 2 bonus rooms, potential 3rd bedroom w/walk-in closet & separate home office. Family room w/woodburning fireplace, full bath & kitchenette. New vinyl flooring in lower level. New roof 2024, new driveway & front walkway 2021 & repainted deck 2025. Many updates, must see entire list. One car garage, ample multi-car driveway parking. This home offers a peaceful setting just minutes from parks, schools & commuter routes. Rare opportunity to own a home that combines privacy & updates along w/true flexible living space in one of Norwalk's most desirable locations.
