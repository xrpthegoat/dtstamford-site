About this home

Discover the ultimate retreat in this exquisite Westport home, ideally located on a sought-after cul-de-sac. This beautifully renovated property blends luxury and convenience, featuring 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths. Gleaming hardwood floors, updated kitchen cabinetry, and modernized baths create a warm ambiance. Outside, enjoy a fenced, in-ground heated pool with an automatic cover for extended summer use. High-end amenities include a whole-house generator, EV charging outlet, sauna, and 1GB fiber internet. The open-concept layout is perfect for entertaining and everyday living, with a sunlit living room, cozy fireplace, kitchen with banquet seating, and versatile dining/family area. The main level flows to an updated deck and patio, perfect for outdoor gatherings. Upstairs, the king-size primary bedroom features a spa-like ensuite bath, plus two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath. The lower level includes a family room, another bedroom, and a sizable bath, ideal for guests or flexible living. Walk to town and restaurants, with tennis courts and baseball fields across the street. Enjoy bike rides to Compo Beach or Longshore for golf and pickleball. This exceptional Westport home offers the lifestyle you've always envisioned warm, welcoming, and ready to make your own.
