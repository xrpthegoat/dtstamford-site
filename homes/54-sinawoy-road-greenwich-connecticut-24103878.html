About this home

Welcome to 54 Sinawoy Road, a picture-perfect colonial on a sunny corner lot in the heart of Cos Cob. Inside, a graceful staircase and sunlit front room set the tone, while French doors connect the cozy living room, fireplace, and patio for effortless indoor-outdoor living. The kitchen is thoughtfully designed with an island, stainless steel appliances, and flows to a formal dining room and powder room. Upstairs offers four bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with a renovated bath and walk-in closet, plus a full bath and laundry area. The finished basement-with high ceilings and a separate den/ flex space-adds even more room to relax or entertain. With solar panels covering most of last year's electricity use, a natural gas generator, and an EV-ready garage, this home blends charm with smart, efficient upgrades.But the best part? The location. In a charming, friendly neighborhood where tree-lined streets, parks, schools, shops, and cafes are an easy walk away, this is a place where life feels a little sweeter-like the good old days, only better. Plus public transportation and I-95 are close-by for convenient commutes to NYC and beyond. 54 Sinawoy is a perfect fit for anyone seeking space, connection, and a sense of place.
