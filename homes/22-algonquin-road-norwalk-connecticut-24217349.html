About this home

Beautifully updated split-level home in Norwalk's desirable Cranbury neighborhood, offering modern finishes, spacious living areas, and an exceptional outdoor setting. The main level features hardwood floors, a spacious living room, and a stunning remodeled kitchen with quartz countertops, a large center island, stylish cabinetry, recessed lighting, and a dining area with sliding doors leading to a covered deck. Three bedrooms are located on the upper level, while the lower level features a beautifully remodeled primary suite with a walk-in closet and a gorgeous full bathroom featuring a freestanding soaking tub and double vanity. Step outside to a nearly 3/4-acre, level lot surrounded by mature trees and lush greenery. Enjoy summer days by the inground pool with a new liner, relax under the gazebo, or entertain on the covered deck. The expansive backyard offers plenty of open space and a peaceful, private setting. An attached two-car garage completes this wonderful property.
