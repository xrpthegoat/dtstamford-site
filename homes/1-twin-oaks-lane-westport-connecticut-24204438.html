About this home

A fresh take on Modern Luxury awaits at this new construction home set on stunning property in the Old Hill area of Westport. Now complete and ready for immediate enjoyment, this dynamic residence is perfectly located for convenience to commuting, the train station, schools, shopping and more. Exhilarating architecture will awaken your senses as you approach this customquality home. Walls of glass blend the natural setting with stunning interiors, and bring sunlight deep into the spectacular rooms. The chef's kitchen is a showpiece, featuring bespoke oak cabinetry, premium finishes, and a substantial walkin pantry. Dual mainlevel offices support today's lifestyle. 5 spacious bedrooms and 7 refined baths create a harmonious balance of comfort and sophistication. The primary suite is a serene retreat with three fitted closets and a spalevel ambiance. Additional bedrooms offer generous scale and thoughtful design. A sunlit upstairs lounge and a finished thirdfloor space provide flexible areas for work, play, or creativity. The finished lower level offers exceptional spaces for fitness, media, or entertaining. Outside, an expansive patio overlooks 1.68 acres of private grounds, ideal for gatherings or quiet reflection. A 3car garage (with a separate bay for a trophy vehicle or storage) and sleek modern detailing complete this remarkable offering. An inspired blend of architecture, setting, and convenience await in a prime location. Pool permit in place.
