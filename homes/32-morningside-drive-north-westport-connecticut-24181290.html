About this home

4 Bedroom 3 Bath Farmhouse in the heart of Westport set on a picturesque level .5 acre. Full of potential, this home awaits your creative vision. Flat, pretty property with city water and sewer available nearby. Detached one car garage. Ideally located in the coveted Long Lots neighborhood, minutes to the train, restaurants, schools, shops, Burying Hill Beach, Sherwood Island Park, Compo Beach, Longshore Park and Golf Course, the Westport Farmers' Market, The Levitt Pavilion, Westport Library, The Westport Country Playhouse, and downtown Westport. Home is being sold "as-is". Amazing opportunity for a builder, designer, investor or buyer who understands the great potential in this most sought-after neighborhood.
