About this home

Situated in a prime location within Regents Park, an award-winning community in Westport offering an exceptional blend of luxury and convenience, this unit is ideally located near the pool, tennis court and guest parking. The grand two-story Foyer, bathed in natural light from a double skylight, opens to a spacious Living and Dining Room combination featuring a wet bar and cozy fireplace. Two sets of sliders lead to a private, expansive brick-enclosed patio adorned with specimen plantings, perfect for outdoor relaxation. The generously sized eat-in Kitchen, also illuminated by a skylight, boasts abundant cabinetry, a pantry and a comfortable dining area. The second floor features a spacious primary suited complete with a large Bedroom, luxurious bath and walk-in closet. An additional Bedroom includes its own en-suite full bath, providing comfort and privacy for guests. Regents Park is maintained to the highest standards and recently renovated the clubhouse with a party room, kitchen and baths. Living at Regents Park provides the perfect blend of privacy, access to all Westport amenities, and a carefree lifestyle. This unit is awaiting those who wantto create their dream home with their own personal touches!
