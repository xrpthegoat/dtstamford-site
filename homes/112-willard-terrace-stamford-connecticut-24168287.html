About this home

Located on a cul-de-sac just north of the Merritt Parkway, this lovely four-bedroom, two bath Cape Cod style home is situated on a level half acre lot and has so much to offer! Enter to find the living room with a picture window and wood-burning fireplace. The formal dining room is open to the living area making entertaining enjoyable and easy. The large kitchen with a center island and stainless-steel appliances even has pass-throughs so the chef can easily speak with and serve their guests. A door from the kitchen leads to the sunroom and from there you can access the deck. Also on the main level is the nice-sized primary bedroom with a wall of closets and sitting area. A full bath is also on this level. Upstairs, you'll find three well-apportioned bedrooms and another full bath. There are hardwood floors throughout. You won't want to miss the walk-out lower-level with full-height ceilings. The possibilities are endless. There is a one car garage, an enormous level backyard and a large deck for outdoor dining. The home is in a prime location less than a mile from High Ridge Center with Trader Joes, Starbucks and more and within walking distance to ACME, Chipotle and Jersey Mikes. Don't miss this opportunity to make 112 Willard Terrace your own!
