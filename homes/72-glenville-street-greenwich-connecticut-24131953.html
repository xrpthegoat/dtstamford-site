About this home

This charming Cape is ready for you to make it your own. On the first floor is a living room with a wood-burning fireplace. The spacious kitchen opens up to a dining area and large family room with sliders to the deck. A full bathroom and two bedrooms complete the first floor. On the second floor are two bedrooms and another full bath. The basement, with a laundry room and garage access, is ready for a home gym or potential finishing for the perfect playroom. The front yard is level and fenced in, and the back yard adds additional space for relaxing and playing.
