About this home

Location! Location! Location! This is the one for which you have been waiting. Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac off Sturges Ridge, this 3-bedroom ranch-style home offers a Generac Generator, heated garage, screened porch, and a deer-fenced area on a flat back yard. Set on 2.2 acres with mature plantings. Inside, you'll find generously sized rooms, hardwood floors, an anti-foyer, foyer, and a spacious living room with fireplace. The unfinished lower level--a blank slate--features a fireplace, ample storage and access to garage and backyard. Whether you are looking to downsize, invest, or create your dream home, 35 Orchard Drive offers an ideal canvas in a coveted South Wilton neighborhood.
