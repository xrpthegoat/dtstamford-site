About this home

AVAILABLE from 8/9/26 - 9/9/26 with possibility of extending through September and into October. Enjoy your summer at this wonderful Greens Farms Colonial. What a perfect spot to come home to, after a day at the beach! Spend your evenings on the screened porch & in the festive, private back yard with a great patio featuring multiple seating areas, fire pit, hot tub (with outdoor smart TV) and swing set. The main level offers a living room complete with a comfortable TV area, a spacious country Eat-in Kitchen plus a Home Office -no need to travel to work from here! 3 sweet Bedrooms upstairs share a bath. The Primary has a king sized bed. One Bedroom has a twin with a trundle bed that opens to a king. The other has two twins and one twin has a trundle. The lower level offers a Home Theater, complete with recliners, plus the laundry. Detached Garage has been converted to an Entertainment/Lounge/Game Room with smart TV, piano and more! Owners reserve walk-up attic for their belongings. Just over an hour by train service from NYC. Enjoy all that Westport has to offer including great dining, Compo Beach, Longshore Country Club, Westport Country Playhouse and concerts under the stars all summer long at the Levitt Pavillion. Owners prefer no pets.
