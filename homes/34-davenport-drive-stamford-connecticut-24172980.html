About this home

This thoughtfully upgraded home in the Davenport Point Association sits on a level .44-acre lot and offers comfort, efficiency, and convenience near Stamford's waterfront. A major highlight is the owned Tesla Solar Roof with three Powerwalls-a sleek, all-in-one system that delivers clean power, cuts electric bills, and provides seamless whole-home backup. Inside, the main level features a warm living room with a large fireplace & wood-stove insert, hickory hardwood floors, and a dining room overlooking the expansive backyard. The custom kitchen includes granite countertops, an island, and a natural gas stove and oven. The primary bedroom has an en-suite bath, with two additional bedrooms and a full hallway bath. An office next to the laundry area offers direct access to the backyard and bluestone patio. The oversized recreation room with bamboo flooring has its own walkout & is perfect for gatherings or extra living space. The waterproofed basement offers potential for a playroom, gym, or studio while providing ample storage space. Outside, a double-wide paver driveway fits six cars, and the landscaped grounds feature a Japanese Maple, roses, lilacs, and multiple fruit trees, including apple, cherry, quince, and pawpaw. Adjacent to John J. Boccuzzi Park with beach access, playground, courts, fields, and SoundWaters, and close to top amenities-0.5 miles to Prime Restaurant and the Harbor Point water taxi, 1.5 miles to Stamford Station.
