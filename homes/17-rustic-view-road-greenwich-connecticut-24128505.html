About this home

This peaceful 5-bedroom home is situated in a quiet cul-de-sac, offering easy access to schools, public transit, and highways. It features two luxurious primary suites, with the first-floor suite boasting a spacious walk-in closet and en-suite bathroom. Upstairs, the second suite impresses with vaulted ceilings and elegant finishes. The bright family room flows into a modern kitchen with updated appliances and a cozy breakfast nook. Step outside to a newly renovated deck that overlooks the beautifully landscaped backyard. The lower level offers a large entertainment area with 10-foot ceilings, a walk-out to a patio, a kitchenette, and a bathroom. Additionally, a well-planned office space on this level provides a quiet and efficient environment for working from home.
