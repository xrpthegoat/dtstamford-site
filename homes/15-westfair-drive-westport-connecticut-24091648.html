About this home

Welcome to 15 Westfair Drive, a lovely move-in-ready home located in a fantastic neighborhood, walking distance to shops and restaurants, and a 5 minute drive to the Metro North train to NYC. This charming home boasts four spacious bedrooms and three full bathrooms, and an expansive deck off the dining room leading to a large yard with mature trees. You'll find the warmth of two wood burning fireplaces, perfect for cozy evenings, and light, newly refinished hardwood floors that add a touch of elegance throughout. The home has been thoughtfully updated with a new asphalt roof ensuring peace of mind with its 50-year warranty and a newer AC system for year-round comfort, new lighting, and is also freshly painted inside. The 0.66-acre level lot provides plenty of outdoor space, and the large two-car attached garage adds convenience. A newer driveway complements this property's appeal. In-law/aupaire area on first floor with private entrance. Staples High School ranks as #1 public high school in the state of CT. Quick closing possible.
