About this home

Beyond Beautiful - Welcome to 10 Twin Falls Lane, a striking property and home filled with natural light from floor-to-ceiling windows. Step into the sun-drenched foyer with terracotta tile. Throughout the years the home has been updated and modernized with soaring ceilings, serene views, and inspiring design. The expansive family room features a dramatic picture window and flows seamlessly into the marble-countered kitchen with dry bar and adjacent dining area-perfect for entertaining. A spacious den, easily a second family room, includes parquet floors, sliders to the patio, and stairs to a cozy loft. Step into the screened-in porch to enjoy sweeping views of open sky and lawn. The outdoors are just as captivating as the interior with a sparkling pool, charming garden spaces, and a backyard span stretching nearly 100 yards. The primary suite is privately tucked away with a large ensuite bath and soaking tub. The three additional bedrooms bathed in soft light whites- one with vaulted ceilings, wide plank floors, and sliders to the expansive deck. Understated and distinctive, this home is an expression of modernist design-full of charm, light, and joyful living spaces. Just minutes to train, restaurants, shops, YMCA, beaches, and more. Come see how effortlessly enjoyable Westport living can be.
