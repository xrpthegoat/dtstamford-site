About this home

Enter into a masterpiece of architectural character and modern refinement in a private resort-style sanctuary. This stately 1937 Colonial, crowned with timeless cedar shingle roofs, is poised on 0.79 level acres of manicured privacy on a coveted no-through lane near the Rowayton border. The interior is a study in sophisticated warmth, featuring beautiful wide-board hardwood floors throughout the main residence and a stunning family room with a beamed ceiling and stone wall. The heart of the home is a sun-drenched chef's eat-in kitchen anchored by travertine flooring, a central social island, dual ovens, gas cooktop, a built-in desk area, and a full wet bar for effortless entertaining. Accommodations include four spacious bedrooms and 3.5 beautifully updated baths, featuring high-end finishes like a soaking tub and a large walk-in shower. The expansive primary suite serves as a true retreat with a voluminous, luxury ensuite. The professionally landscaped grounds offer unparalleled amenities: spend summers by the in-ground pool, entertain on the beautiful patio or find peace in the private meditation garden. Completing this estate is the 3-car detached "Barn" garage, a hobbyist's dream featuring dedicated workshop space with a large attic for limitless storage. Experience absolute serenity just minutes from the best beaches, shopping, restaurants and commuting routes in Norwalk.
