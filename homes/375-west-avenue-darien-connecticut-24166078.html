About this home

Fully renovated Colonial directly across the street from Woodland Park and .5 miles to Noroton Heights Train Station, newly built Darien Commons & Heights Crossing. This 2 bdrm, 1.1 bath has over 1,500sqft of living space, high ceilings, central air & hardwood floors throughout. Remodeled eat-in-kitchen w/ granite countertops, dining area, SS appliances, half bath, pantry & double-sided gas fireplace that looks through to the massive living room. 2nd floor has 2 bdrms w/ remodeled full bath and ample closet space. Lower level w/ playroom, laundry and storage space. Step through the French Doors to the fenced in backyard with patio & deck, 1 car garage, and turf for easy maintenance/pet cleanup.
