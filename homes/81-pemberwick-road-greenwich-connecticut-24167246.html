About this home

Sun-drenched end-unit offering space, comfort, and effortless living in a prime Greenwich location. This beautifully maintained home features two generously sized bedrooms, including a private en-suite, along with a flexible open office/study and 3.5 baths. The thoughtfully designed layout includes an eat-in kitchen, dedicated dining area, and an expansive living room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace. Sliding doors open to a private deck-perfect for morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. A finished walk-out lower level provides exceptional versatility for a home office, guest space, or recreation area. Additional highlights include hardwood floors throughout, central A/C, in-unit washer/dryer, and two assigned parking spaces. Landscaping and trash removal are included for easy, low-maintenance living. Ideally located near Metro-North and major highways for a seamless commute. Enjoy close proximity to parks, beaches, shopping, dining, and town amenities-including Pemberwick Park with its playground and tennis courts-all just minutes away.
