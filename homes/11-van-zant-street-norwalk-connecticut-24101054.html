About this home

Luxury Tri-Level East Norwalk Townhouse with 2 bedrooms + loft, 1.5 baths & a fully finished lower level offering bonus recreation space + a home office! Prime location; walk to water, the East Norwalk Train Station, Calf Pasture & Marvin Beaches, shopping & 5 star dining destinations. Open floor plan with combo living & dining room ~ the living room has sliders that open to the outdoor terrace. A perfect space for outdoor entertainment. Kitchen with granite countertops, plenty of cabinet storage and stainless steel appliances which includes a dishwasher. The upper level offers a spacious primary bedroom with vaulted ceilings, tons of natural light and a loft! Loft currently used as a massive walk up & into closet - multi use space, could be a den or home office area as well. Upper level also provides a 2nd bedroom + a full hall bathroom with dual sinks, marble countertop, a shower/tub combination & full size washer & dryer units. The finished lower level is perfect for recreation (there's a wine fridge!) offers tons of storage space + a home office! Hardwood floors. Central air. 2 assigned parking spaces. Electric car charging available nearby. Cats and small dogs (up to 30 pounds only) are welcome. Excellent credit, references and proof of income required. **Please note, photos are from a prior residency.** Available September 1st!
