About this home

Luxury Furnished Rental in the heart of Greens Farms. This timeless 5 bedroom, 6 bath Colonial is where sophistication meets modern ease designed for how you live, work, and entertain. A circular drive, stone walls and a charming front porch create an inviting first impression. Inside, a sun filled double height entry sets the tone for open and gracious living. Curl up by the fireplace in the Living Room or take your calls from the custom Office with peaceful views of the pool. The Dining Room has stunning millwork and a bespoke wet bar is perfect for holiday feasts or cocktails with friends. The Eat In Kitchen anchors the home with a quartz island, walk-in pantry, and top-tier appliances, flowing directly into the Family Room with coffered ceilings and a wood burning fireplace with French doors opening to a bluestone patio w/ outdoor kitchen. Upstairs, the dreamy Primary Suite offers two walk-in closets, a dressing room, and a luxurious spa bath. Four additional bedrooms include two en-suites and a Jack-and-Jill. The finished third floor brings this home to over 6,000 square feet, is ideal for guests, movie nights, or sleepovers. Outside, the saltwater pool and hot tub designed by Glen Gate, make every day feel like vacation. Recent upgrades are new HVAC, EV charger, exterior home painted, renovated baths and more, in a location minutes to the beach, train, and downtown Westport this is more than a home, it's a lifestyle. Make yourself at home for the holidays!
