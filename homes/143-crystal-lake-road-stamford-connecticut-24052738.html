About this home

*Please submit highest and best offers by Tuesday 10/15 at 11am.** Welcome to 143 Crystal Lake Road! This beautifully maintained home offers a rare combination of charm, modern updates and prime location in a quiet neighborhood, close to shopping, dining and all Stamford has to offer. Step inside this classic colonial and you will fall in love with the warmth and character. The first floor boasts gleaming hardwood floors, fireplace, and an effortless flow from living room to dining room, and into an eat-in kitchen. Enjoy cooking in this spacious and updated kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances and a 36 in. professional range. French doors off the kitchen lead straight out onto a stone patio perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining - overlooking a spacious, private flat backyard. A half-bath powder room and access to the attached 2 car garage complete the first level. Upstairs you will find four generously sized bedrooms, two full bathrooms and a convenient second-floor laundry room. Additionally, the home features a fantastic flexible space with separate entrance, perfect for hosting guests, a nanny suite, or a dedicated home office. The second patio on the side of the home is an ideal spot to unwind with a glass of wine or a morning coffee. This stunning home won't last long. Schedule your showing today!
