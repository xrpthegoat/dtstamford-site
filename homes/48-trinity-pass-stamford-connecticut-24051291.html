About this home

Welcome to this cherished early American Colonial, an antique home offering the post & beam charm of 1793. This historic former general store and post office radiates English cottage appeal, featuring 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, a living room with a fireplace, a spacious dining room, a family room with a fireplace and cut-stone hearth, home office, sun porch and a fine British bespoke deVOL kitchen. The kitchen has been thoughtfully renovated in 2024 to include handcrafted cabinets, handmade tiles, elegant light fixtures, and high-quality faucets, and chef appliances blending seamlessly with the home's historical aesthetics while providing modern functionality. Set on one acre of beautifully landscaped grounds, this property is a true outdoor oasis. It features a welcoming sunroom/porch, a stone patio with a small koi pond, and a two-bay detached garage. The expansive gardens capture the essence of a quintessential English garden, with mature trees, blooming flower beds, and meandering pathways enhancing the property's charm. This rare find in North Stamford is just minutes from Scotts Corner Village, offering both convenience and a tranquil setting. Discover the perfect blend of history and contemporary living in this captivating Colonial home!
