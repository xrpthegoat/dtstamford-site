About this home

Welcome to 354 Oaklawn Avenue, a hidden haven where timeless craftsmanship, sophisticated design, exceptional privacy, and elevated outdoor living come together to create a truly remarkable custom-built Colonial. Rebuilt in 2017, this property offers over 6,500 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space, this home delivers the rare combination of grand-scale luxury and everyday comfort. Privately set behind an electric gate and surrounded by meticulously curated grounds, the property offers an extraordinary sense of seclusion while remaining just minutes from Stamford's vibrant shopping, dining, commuter routes, and everyday conveniences. It's the best of both worlds-an beautiful retreat that feels worlds away, yet remarkably connected to everything. Inside, custom millwork, soaring ceilings, designer finishes, and multiple fireplaces create an atmosphere of understated elegance. The chef's kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring premium appliances, an oversized island, and seamless flow into both formal and informal living spaces designed for entertaining and everyday living. The upper-level primary suite offers a luxurious private sanctuary complete with spa-inspired amenities, while the fully finished walk-out lower level provides exceptional flexibility for recreation, fitness, work, or extended living needs.
