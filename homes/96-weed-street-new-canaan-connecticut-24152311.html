About this home

Classic 4-bedroom center hall Colonial set on a beautifully landscaped property with exceptional outdoor living. Ideally located close to everything New Canaan has to offer, the home features a stunning pool, expansive stone terrace with outdoor fireplace, built in grill and smoker, and a private yard designed for seamless indoor-outdoor enjoyment. Designed for both everyday living and entertaining, the home offers a gracious and well-proportioned layout across three finished levels. The two story entry hall is flanked by a formal living room with fireplace and a private office while the elegant dining room is conveniently located off the spacious eat in kitchen that serves as the heart of the home. A comfortable family room with wood burning fireplace opens directly to the outdoor entertaining areas, the mudroom, three car garage and back staircase. The second level offers four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite, along with well-appointed bathrooms, excellent closet space, and a versatile bonus room. The fully finished lower level is a true highlight, featuring a wine room, gym, playroom, and a custom home theater, creating an ideal extension of the living space for recreation and relaxation. Surrounded by mature landscaping that ensures privacy and tranquility, this timeless New Canaan Colonial combines thoughtful amenities, elegant flow, and outstanding indoor-outdoor living.
