About this home

Welcome home to Sue Terrace. This completely renovated and freshly painted colonial boasts 4BR/3FB and is being offered as a yearly, unfurnished rental. This family home is centrally located in Westport and walking distance to shopping (Fresh Market, Free People, CVS, Trader Joes, CAVA) Winslow Park and restaurants such as Terrain and Sakura. Prefer to entertain at home? 16 Sue Terrace has an open floor plan, high cathedral ceilings, a sunroom, and a private backyard with patio, outdoor kitchen and shared basketball hoop. Sue Terrace is a private cul-de-sac where everyone is welcome and many come to stay.
