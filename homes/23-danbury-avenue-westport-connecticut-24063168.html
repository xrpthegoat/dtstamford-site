About this home

Open the door to this slice of paradise located in the heart of Compo basin, just 300 steps from the beach.... This charming Cape cottage is filled with natural light and has a flexible floor plan. Two bedrooms on the upper level , 2 full baths, front to back living room with built-ins, dining room with fireplace, enclosed sunroom (perfect for a third bedroom or home office) , and a kitchen that opens onto a newly-stained deck in a gracious, fully-fenced private yard + outdoor shower! Great front porch as well! Brand new stainless steel kitchen appliances, (and new quartz countertops in the process of being installed). Mitsubishi split AC systems recently added. This unfurnished, yearly rental is turnkey and ready for occupancy.
