About this home

Timeless charm meets modern comfort in this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, stone-front Colonial set back from the road and rich with quintessential 1920 period details. Step inside to reveal how new triple pane windows throughout create a remarkably quiet and comfortable interior environment. Truly a move in ready residence! Unlike many homes situated on busy roads, driveway access is provided via an access road adjacent to High Ridge Road, allowing for easy, stress-free entry and exit without pulling directly onto the main thoroughfare. The living room with its classic wood-burning fireplace and abundant natural light is a great gathering spot, while the sun porch, currently used as an office provides flexible space for today's lifestyle. Entertain with ease in the spacious formal dining room with bay window, french doors with period hardware, and plenty of room to host large dinner parties. The updated kitchen combines character and functionality with a gas stove featuring a built-in-grill and newer appliances. Upstairs, the primary bedroom offers hardwood floors, a fireplace flanked by sconce lighting, and the most amazing walk in closet (closet system to be installed this week). It includes a beautifully renovated primary bath featuring heated floors and stunning walk in shower. Tremendous storage inside and out with full basement, attic, large closets and garage. Outside enjoy time with family friends and pets in your fully fenced yard and spacious stone patio.
