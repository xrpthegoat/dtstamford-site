About this home

Discover a rare investment opportunity with this exceptional multi-family home, nestled in the heart of the thriving East Norwalk water community. Built in 1985 and just steps from the picturesque Calf Pasture Beach, this property is part of a vibrant water community with access to boating and other water activities, while remaining conveniently minutes from I-95 and the East Norwalk train station. Inside, you'll find two side-by-side townhouse units, each spanning three floors and featuring two bedrooms and one bath. Spacious basements and additional attic rooms provide even more living space. The open and airy layouts feature kitchens that flow seamlessly into the living rooms, and both units have access to a convenient coin-operated laundry area. A generous rear parking area, adds convenience for residents. Sold as-is, this property is a great investment opportunity or a wonderful home, ready for someone to bring out its full potential.
