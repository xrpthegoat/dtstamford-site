About this home

**Highest and Best offers due by Monday, 6/17 at 12pm. No Escalation Clauses. Please provide proof of funds for cash offers. Located in convenient South Wilton, this 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath home has been updated with many improvements. Warm and inviting, this center hall colonial offers a flexible floor plan. Upon entering the home, you will find a spacious living and dining room with stone fireplace. The newly renovated eat-in kitchen is a cook's dream with Thermador appliances, quartz countertops, large center island with beverage refrigerator and eating area with custom built-ins. Just a few steps away you will find the large family room with vaulted ceiling, bay/bow window and sliders to the deck. Mudroom, laundry and half bath complete the first level. Upstairs you will find a large primary bedroom with full bath and plenty of closet space. 3-additional bedrooms and full bath complete the upstairs. The 2+large acres of flat property offers beautiful gardens, stone wall, deck and fenced in backyard. Other features of the home include hardwood floors, central air, newly renovated baths, Hardie board siding and 2-car garage! Located just minutes from Wilton Town center, schools, train and major highways.
