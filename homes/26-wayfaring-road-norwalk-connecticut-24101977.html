About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained Raised Ranch nestled on a serene, level half acre lot, this 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath home offers the perfect blend of comfort, space and convenience. Inside, you'll find a warm and inviting layout with generous living areas, a functional eat -in kitchen open to the dining room and living room....great flow for daily living. The primary bedroom features it's own private bath, while the additional bedrooms are the perfect fit for family, guests or a home office. The finished lower level adds valuable finished living space, perfect for a family room, play space, flexible options and endless possibilities. The lower level also provides an bonus room with ample closet space, which can be used for a home office, guest space or recreation, a half bath and dedicated laundry room. There is direct access into the lower portion of the home from the attached 2 car garage and easy access to the private backyard via sliders - perfect for recreation and entertaining. This home is just minutes from all your daily needs, including lifestyle amenities like Cranbury Park, Calf Pasture and Shady Beach. This home has been lovingly maintained by the same family for over 50 years! Located at the end of the street on a desirable cul de sac, come see all it has to offer today!
