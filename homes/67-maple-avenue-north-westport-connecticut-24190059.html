About this home

Charming Cape house situated on one acre of beautifully landscaped and maintained grounds. Prime location across from the Hunt Club with unencumbered views of serene horse stables. Enter the home through an inviting sun porch and convenient mudroom nook before stepping into the newly renovated kitchen. The spacious living room has an easy flow that is ideal for entertaining friends and spending quality time with family. The office/den area is ideal for those working from home or seeking a quiet space to focus. Off the kitchen, access a large back patio overlooking a heated gunite pool and sweeping backyard. Upstairs the home features 3 large bedrooms, ample closet space and 2 full bathrooms. You'll love the tranquility this private oasis provides.
