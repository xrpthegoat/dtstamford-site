About this home

A styled to please...Colonial Cape in East Norwalk! This 4 bedroom, 3 full bath home has been re-imagined & updated to accommodate today's modern lifestyle; The owners removed a bedroom wall to create a trendy open floor plan. The choice is yours, add your personal decorating touch, reinstate the 4th bedroom wall or utilize the fully finished lower level with full bath as the 4th bedroom space. SO MANY RECENT UPGRADES ~ the heavy lifting has been removed. New roof & new oil tank in 2023 as well as a new furnace in November 2024. Complete list of improvements is available. Main level freshly painted, living/dining/kitchen with new recessed lighting & new naturally sanded & stained hardwood floors. Open kitchen with plenty of cabinet space + new dishwasher in 2024. Renovated 1st floor bathroom with all new flooring, tile, walk-in shower, vanity, mirror + light fixtures. A 1st floor spacious bedroom w lots of natural light & 3 season sunroom complete the main level. The upper level provides a family room or sitting area to gather, 2 additional bedrooms w new carpeting & a full bathroom w large shower/tub combo. Finished LL offers recreation space, laundry & another full bathroom w walk-in shower. Central Air. Private, FULLY FENCED backyard w newly sanded & painted deck 2024. 2 driveways provide ample parking + 1 car attached garage. Excellent location: Walking distance to the beach, Shorehaven Country Club Golf Course, tennis courts, marinas & the East Norwalk train station!
