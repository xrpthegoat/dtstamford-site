About this home

Welcome to this pristine 3-bedroom Colonial in the highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Move-in ready and meticulously maintained, this inviting home features hardwood floors throughout, a formal living room with a fireplace, a dining area, and a well-appointed kitchen with an eating area. The spectacular great room with soaring ceiling creates a bright, airy gathering space at the heart of the home. Enjoy year-round relaxation in the heated sunroom overlooking the private backyard. Outside, a heated swimming pool, deck, and mature landscaping provide a private setting for entertaining or quiet enjoyment. Two full baths and a partially finished lower level with abundant storage complete the home's thoughtful layout. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, and the SoNo train station, this exceptional property combines comfort, functionality, and outdoor living in one of Cranbury's most desirable locations. A must see!
