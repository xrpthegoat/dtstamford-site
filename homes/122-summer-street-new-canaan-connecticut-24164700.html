About this home

Fantastic downtown location within walking distance to the town center, train station, and park. This spacious townhouse-style condo offers the comfort of no HOA needs. The updated kitchen features granite countertops, a Sub-Zero refrigerator, and a Miele dishwasher. The living and dining area opens directly to a lovely outdoor terrace and a private garden-style backyard-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. All bedrooms are located on the second floor along with a full bathroom. The finished basement provides additional living space, including a full bathroom, cedar closet, and laundry room. Enjoy the convenience of an attached one-car garage plus two additional outdoor parking spaces. Recent updates include a 2-year-old roof, freshly painted siding (2025), a new hot water heater, and a renovated second-floor bathroom (2026). Move-in ready and waiting for its new owner!
