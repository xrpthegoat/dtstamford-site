About this home

Welcome to Island Life, a truly coveted experience on Saugatuck Island! This home offers breathtaking water views from nearly every room of this sophisticated yet modern beach house. This spacious and airy home is drenched in sunlight, featuring a great room with an open floor plan. The timeless kitchen boasts professional-grade appliances, quartz counters, and a large outdoor porch perfect for alfresco dining. The third-floor rooftop deck provides breathtaking water views of Long Island Sound, and with an outdoor gas fire pit and grill hook-up, rooftop entertaining is effortless. Inside, the third-floor wet bar is ideal for guest gatherings, or it can be utilized as a family room or even a fifth bedroom. This four-bedroom, five-bath home includes a luxurious primary bedroom suite with a spa bath, two walk-in closets, and a delightful Juliet balcony for lounging and enjoying the water views. Additional features include: FEMA compliant, 9-foot ceilings, full house generator, Lutron Lighting, outdoor shower and elevator ready. Enjoy the private neighborhood beach of Saugatuck Island, just steps away. This home is also conveniently located only minutes from Metro North, I-95, and Downtown Westport.
