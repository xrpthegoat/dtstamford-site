About this home

Beautifully sited on 1.46 acres at the end of a quiet and peaceful westside cul-de-sac, this private oasis is perfection! The impressive and sun-drenched 6,200 SF classic colonial offers a thoughtful and versatile floor plan to accommodate a modern lifestyle for easy living, work and entertaining. With four fully finished floors, highlights include high ceilings, two-story entry, detailed millwork, hardwood floors, 5/6 bedrooms, eat-in-kitchen with cozy breakfast room, dramatic family room with wood slat ceiling and fireplace, gracious living room with fireplace, formal dining room with built-ins and home office. The spectacular primary suite has a luxurious updated bath, sitting room and walk-in closet. In addition, there is a huge third floor bonus room with full bath, and a multi-purpose fully finished lower level with full bath and wine cellar. Enjoy the gorgeous heated pool and spa, park-like setting with flat play yard and expansive bluestone terraces, which are surrounded by lush landscaping and mature trees. This exceptional country estate is conveniently located to town, nationally ranked schools and trains.
