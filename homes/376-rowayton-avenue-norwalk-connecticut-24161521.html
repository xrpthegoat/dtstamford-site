About this home

Welcome to 376 Rowayton Avenue, a charming cape in beautiful Rowayton. The home features a spacious yard and deck, ideal for outdoor dining, relaxing, and entertaining. Inside, you'll find a functional layout with a gracious living room and wood burning fire place, a cozy family room with access to the deck, and a well-proportioned dining room. Upstairs are two large bedrooms, and a full bathroom. Additional lower-level space can easily serve as a gym, home office, or rec room. The home is a quick walk to Rowayton train station and just one mile from village center. Whether you're looking for a starter home, a downsizing opportunity, or anything in between, 376 Rowayton ave is your opportunity to live in one of Fairfield County's most coveted beach towns.
