About this home

Lower North Stamford location close to the border of Westover. Continuous meticulous maintenance by the current owner for the past 45+ years. Large scale family rooms each with generous windows, recessed lighting and hardwood floors. There's an easy option to create an in law suite in the finished, walk out lower level. The unfinished walk up attic, currently floored for storage, adds to expansion potential and the detached garage built in 1994, offers flexibility for vehicle storage separate from the attached garage. A parklike rear yard would likely accommodate an in ground pool in your desired spot. A superb opportunity with unlimited potential in a highly desirable location.
