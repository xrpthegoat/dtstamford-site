About this home

Stunning 4 bedroom, 6 bath Spacious Contemporary with BONUS accessory apartment. Located in a desirable central Wilton neighborhood, this unique and special property exudes a very cool Mid Century vibe throughout. Gorgeous living room with stone fireplace, multiple windows and cathedral ceiling invites you into this custom home. Gracious formal dining room, light-filled Chef's kitchen with large eating area opening to level backyard and cozy family room with fireplace create the perfect entertaining set-up. Flexible floor plan with Primary Suite either on the main floor or 2nd floor as well as two other generously sized bedrooms, 2 1/2 baths, sunny office and mudroom complete the first floor. The fabulous in-law/au pair apartment with a separate entrance is fully updated & a legal rental. Complete with kitchenette, washer/dryer, living room, bedroom, 1 1/2 baths & multiple closets for storage. Sited on 2 acres with fabulous Glengate, gunite pool and gazebo on a lovely flat & cleared park-like setting.....the perfect outdoor and private retreat! Many updates, including newer furnace, Culligan water purifier, lighting, 2 new Splitter units & freshly painted interior. Pride of ownership is evident throughout this exceptional light-filled property. A rare opportunity to own a peaceful oasis offering versatility and convenience on a cul-de-sac neighborhood where you can walk to school, town and NRVT. This gem, full of character and charm, is extraordinary and not to be missed!!
