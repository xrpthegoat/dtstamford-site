About this home

Charming Ranch in Desirable Cranbury Neighborhood! Discover the ease of one-level living in this well-maintained ranch, ideally located on a quiet street in the sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. Just 1/2 mile from Cranbury Market, local bank, and well known chinese restaurant, pizza wine all at your fingertips and 11/2 miles from the Dog Park, this home offers both convenience and tranquility. Step inside to a bright and inviting living room featuring a large picture window and a cozy fireplace. The dining area seamlessly connects to a enclosed porch with a vaulted ceiling-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The primary bedroom includes a private newer full bath, while beautiful hardwood floors flow throughout the home. Additional highlights include central air, a full basement with ample storage, and a level 1/3-acre fenced backyard. The two-car attached garage provides added convenience. Don't miss this fantastic opportunity to own a home in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods! We are currently in a multiple-offer situation.
