About this home

Step into this stunning sun-drenched colonial, where classic architectural character has been lovingly and meticulously restored to perfection. Set on a generous, private .89 acre lot on one of Darien's most sought-after streets, this thoughtfully remodeled home offers five spacious bedrooms and 2.5 baths across a layout designed for both effortless entertaining and comfortable family living. Walk to town and the train station in minutes a rare convenience paired with this level of privacy and space. Gorgeous natural light floods the living room with its inviting fireplace, while the elegant formal dining room sets the stage for memorable gatherings. The heart of the home is the brand new kitchen, featuring a large center island, premium finishes, and stunning French doors that open seamlessly to an expansive deck for morning coffee or sunset entertaining, with sweeping views of the yard. Adjacent to the kitchen, the family room beckons with a striking stone fireplace, while the thoughtfully placed mudroom and laundry room keep daily life organized and streamlined. Downstairs, the finished lower level provides flexible space with a playroom and convenient kitchenette. Additional highlights include a two car garage and a large, flat, private backyard a rare oasis for play, gardening, or simply unwinding in complete tranquility. Ideally located, this exceptional home is just moments from town amenities, the train, and Cherry Lawn Park. Charm, character and location!
