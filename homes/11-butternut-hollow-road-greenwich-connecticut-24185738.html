About this home

Sun-filled Colonial set on 2 private acres with desirable east/west exposures, offering exceptional privacy and natural beauty. Thoughtfully updated throughout, the home features a renovated gourmet kitchen with premium appliances, breakfast area, and seamless connection to the family room. Recent improvements include Andersen windows and doors, generator, updated mechanicals, irrigation, security system and more!
