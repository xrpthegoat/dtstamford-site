About this home

Located in one of the most sought-after CT gold coast communities, this meticulously maintained 3-bedroom, 3 full bath expanded colonial home offers an open, light-filled layout designed for everyday living and entertaining. Highlights include a living room with a gas fireplace, a family room, an eat-in kitchen with center island, abundant cabinetry, and a new Bertazzoni gas range. The primary suite features a private roof deck, dual sinks, a jetted tub, and a separate shower. Two additional bedrooms with full bath are located on the second floor. Additional features include energy-efficient windows, multi-zoned heating and cooling, central vacuum, irrigation system, security system, washer and dryer on the main level, and new architect grade shingle roof (May 2025). The property offers a long double-width driveway and fenced rear yard with terrace. It is conveniently located to all the amenities of Heights Crossing & Darien Commons mixed use developments that include shops, market, multiple restaurant venues, the Goddard School and of course Noroton Heights Metro North train station.
