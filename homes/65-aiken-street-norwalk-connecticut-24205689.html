About this home

Spacious, sun-filled and designed to live like a single-family home, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath end-unit townhouse offers a rare combination of space, privacy and convenience in Norwalk. The open-concept main level features 9-foot ceilings, hardwood floors and a generous kitchen with abundant cabinetry, granite countertops and counter seating, flowing seamlessly into the dining and living areas. A fireplace adds warmth and character, while sliding doors open to a private stone patio-perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a private bath, complemented by two additional bedrooms, a full hall bath and conveniently located second-floor laundry. The finished lower level provides valuable flexible space for a home office, gym, media room or playroom. An oversized attached one-car garage adds everyday ease and additional storage. Ideally situated for commuters with convenient access to Metro-North, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, this special end unit delivers the comfort and functionality of a house with the low-maintenance lifestyle of a townhouse-a combination that is truly few and far between.
