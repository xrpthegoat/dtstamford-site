About this home

Welcome to 1 Driftwood Lane, a 3-bedroom split-level on a rare 1.11-acre corner lot in desirable Cranbury. Enter through the energy-efficient front door (2024) into a foyer with coat closet, flowing to an open-concept kitchen and living room. The kitchen showcases quartz countertops (2025), pantry storage, breakfast nook, and updated flooring (2019), while the living room features reinforced flooring and new sliding doors (2025) opening to an extended deck with Sunsetter awning (2020). A spacious formal dining room sits adjacent with its own deck access. The bedroom wing includes a primary suite with ensuite bath (remodeled 2023) plus two additional bedrooms sharing a hallway bath, all freshly painted (2025). The lower level offers a family room with fireplace, dedicated office, half bath and laundry room, plus utility space housing the boiler and oil tank. Smart home features (2023) and alarm system (2021) add modern convenience. Outside, the expansive and level corner lot provides extended front and back yards with no overhanging trees, upgraded fencing (2024), new shed (2023), and gated pillars with lighting (2021). A 2-car attached garage completes the package. Solar panels (2021) cover household usage. Located in a quiet, multigenerational neighborhood with easy access to shopping, dining, and more. This home combines thoughtful updates with exceptional space and natural light throughout--in Norwalk's hottest zip code.
