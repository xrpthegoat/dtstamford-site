About this home

HUGE PRICE IMPROVEMENT! Your chance to enjoy the Quintessenial Rowayton Lifestyle just steps to waterfront restaurants, boutique shopping and the Farmers Market. This beautifully designed 5 bedroom, 3 full and 1 half bath home perfectly captures the Rowayton lifestyle buyers dream about. The dramatic great room features soaring 12-foot ceilings, a fireplace, and enormous sun-filled windows that create warmth and connection throughout the home. A beautiful kitchen opens effortlessly into the inviting step-down family room with a stunning glass conservatory - the perfect setting for everyday living, cozy evenings, or entertaining family and friends. The spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings and an oversized light filled spa like bathroom feels like a peaceful escape, while the private front porch invites morning coffee, evening cocktails, "porch jam parties" as well as quiet moments surrounded by nature. Additional highlights include a 2-car attached garage and an enormous lower level offering exceptional storage and endless possibilities for recreation, gym space, or future expansion. With a new roof, freshly painted exterior & updated utilities there is not a thing to do but move in. Lovely glass doors that open to your private back patio & garden for entertaining guests. Just a quick stroll to Rowayton's beloved restaurants, shopping, waterfront charm, and community amenities, this is the kind of home - and lifestyle - people move to Rowayton for.
