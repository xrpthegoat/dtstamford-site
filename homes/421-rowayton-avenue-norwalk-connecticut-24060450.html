About this home

This is the ranch you have been waiting for! One step and you are in. Well maintained four bedroom with en-suite primary, spacious living room with wood burning fireplace, dining room off kitchen which opens to sunny family room. Screen porch off family room with access to spacious, level half acre yard. Large two car garage, full basement with potential to finish into a gym, playroom or workshop. Freshly painted throughout and beautiful hardwood floors. Enjoy living in the Brookside neighborhood with easy walk to the Rowayton train station, Sono nightlife, restaurants, shopping on Route 1, golf at Oak Hills, Long Island Sound, Maritime Aquarium and so much more. Only 60 minutes to NYC on Metro North.
