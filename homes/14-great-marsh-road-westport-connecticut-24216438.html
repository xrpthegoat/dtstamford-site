About this home

One spectacular property-endless possibilities. Boasting 175 feet of direct Westport waterfront with a private community beach, this 1.23-acre Saugatuck harborside estate awaits your vision. Enjoy expansive views overlooking the vibrant harbor and a parade of yachts. The property currently features a classic 4,000 sq. ft. Colonial set high on the land, offering a charming canvas ready for a custom renovation. For buyers looking to build anew, this premier waterfront location comes with fully approved plans and permits in hand to construct a bespoke dream home. Whichever path you choose, the outcome is unparalleled: an incredible opportunity to live your waterfront dream adjacent to the coveted Saugatuck Yacht Club. Enjoy the very best of Westport living, with Saugatuck Village's top restaurants and the train station just moments away. Come take a look for yourself and let's chat!
