About this home

FEMA-COMPLIANT and masterfully built in 2018, this extraordinary coastal residence at 24 Spriteview Avenue on Saugatuck Island offers an unrivaled waterfront lifestyle in one of Westport's most coveted enclaves. 4 bedrooms include 2 sumptuous primary en-suites and 3.5 impeccably appointed baths. All rooms are drenched in natural light and showcase captivating water views, creating a seamless connection to the shoreline. Custom stained hardwood floors, solar panels, and a full-house Tesla generator combine refined elegance with modern efficiency and peace of mind. The chef's eat-in kitchen is drenched in sunlight, appointed with premium appliances and an oversized island, flowing effortlessly into the family room where walls of glass open to expansive waterfront terraces designed for sophisticated entertaining. A built-in bar with kegerator elevates every gathering. A private office, bespoke built-ins, and three gas fireplaces add warmth and distinction throughout. Multiple decks and a breathtaking rooftop terrace frame panoramic sunrise and sunset vistas, along with front-row views of July 4th fireworks over the water. Residents enjoy access to three private beaches, a private backyard dock, and proximity to Cedar Point Yacht Club. Just minutes to town, train, and schools-yet a world away-the ultimate turnkey waterfront sanctuary.
