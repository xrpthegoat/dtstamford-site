About this home

Set on one of Darien's most sought-after, multi-million dollar cul-de-sacs, this sprawling wood and brick ranch offers exceptional scale and endless potential for the discerning buyer, renovator, or builder. Positioned on a remarkably private 2.5 acre lot, abutting another 10+ acres of protected wetlands, the setting affords unmatched peace, natural beauty, and exclusivity. The interior boasts a versatile layout. The back of the home features expansive walls of glass, with a fully enclosed glass terrace, which creates a seamless visual connection to the serene, wooded surroundings. Highlights include 3 generously sized bedrooms, 3.5 baths, a sauna, a spacious living room anchored by a beautiful stone fireplace, a formal dining room, and a large eat-in kitchen, with a large breakfast area. Designed with an indoor-outdoor flow in mind, the substantial multi-purpose room anchoring one end of the house, can be used according to any family's needs: a playroom, family room, studio, in-law suite. With its sliding doors that open to the flagstone terrace and the private free-form pool, it shows the flexibility of the design. Whether remodeling, renovating, expanding, or building new, this is a special one-of-a-kind opportunity to create your dream in your new home. Agents please check confidential remarks.
