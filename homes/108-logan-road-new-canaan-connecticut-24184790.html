About this home

Set on six spectacular acres in one of New Canaan's most picturesque settings, this exceptional estate offers timeless elegance, expansive living spaces, and breathtaking natural beauty. Surrounded by rolling lawns, mature trees, and serene water views, the property is a private retreat less than 10 minutes from town. Designed for both grand entertaining and comfortable everyday living, the residence features five/six bedrooms, six full baths. The gracious main level includes a formal living room, elegant dining room, soaring vaulted-ceiling family room, private office, and a stunning open-concept eat-in kitchen that serves as the heart of the home. A sun-filled four season room provides year-round enjoyment of the spectacular grounds. Two powder rooms and two laundry rooms add exceptional convenience. The second level is highlighted by a luxurious primary suite featuring dual bathrooms and abundant dressing and closet space. Four additional bedrooms and three baths provide ample accommodations, while a dramatic coffered-ceiling great room offers flexibility as a sixth upstairs bedroom, media room, or private guest suite. The finished lower level includes a spacious recreation/playroom and full bath, creating additional living and entertaining space. Outdoor living is equally impressive with multiple stone terraces overlooking a tranquil pond fed by the Mill River, providing a magical setting for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying nature.
