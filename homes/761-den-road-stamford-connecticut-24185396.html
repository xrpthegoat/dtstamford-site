About this home

Welcome to 761 Den Road, a charming and character-filled home set on a private, level lot in desirable North Stamford. Offering 3-4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and timeless architectural details, this special property blends classic New England charm with comfortable everyday living. Custom built-ins, original millwork, and a beautiful stone fireplace create warmth and character throughout. The spacious living room flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, making entertaining easy and inviting. One of the home's standout features is the sun-filled family room with cathedral ceilings and walls of windows that bring the outdoors in. The kitchen offers ample space for casual dining and easy access to the patio and backyard. Outside, enjoy a large, flat yard with exceptional privacy, perfect for entertaining, gardening, pets, or play. A detached one-car garage and oversized driveway provide abundant parking for guests and multiple vehicles. Additional features include central air conditioning, forced hot air heat, a full basement with laundry, and numerous built-ins throughout. Conveniently located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping, restaurants, parks, and everyday amenities. Served by Stamford Public Schools, including Roxbury Elementary, Cloonan Middle School, and Westhill High School. Rarely does a North Stamford home offer this combination of charm, character, privacy, convenience, and value. Home has been freshly painted in an off-white/light greige.
