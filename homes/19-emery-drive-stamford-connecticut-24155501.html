About this home

Welcome to 19 Emery Drive, nestled in the private, gated community of Westover Park. This beautifully maintained home offers open-concept living at its finest. The designer renovated eat-in-kitchen features white shaker cabinets, marble countertops, and stainless steel appliances which will delight the serious home chef. The spacious living room is highlighted by a charming rounded fireplace, creating a warm and inviting focal point. The dining area is surrounded by windows, allowing natural light to pour in and creating the perfect setting for everyday meals or special gatherings. Need to work from home? A first-floor office a provide wonderful flexibility and can easily be used as an additional bedroom. Gleaming hardwood floors throught add to the perfection. Upstairs, the primary bedroom serves as a private retreat, complete with a full bath with vaulted ceiling, skylight, and elegant tumbled marble. Two additional bedrooms and another full bath complete the upper level. Step outside to enjoy beautiful perennial gardens and a genrous sized slate patio, ideal for outdoor entertaining or peaceful relaxation. Located just minutes from downtown Stamford, this home offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience.
