About this home

Enjoy breathtaking year-round pond and wooded views from this beautifully updated rare corner-unit 2-bedroom, 2-bath townhouse in the highly desirable Cider Mill Pond community of Old Greenwich. Filled with natural light from three exposures, soaring cathedral ceilings in the living room and primary bedroom enhance its spacious feel. A brand-new Bosch HVAC system, fresh paint, hardwood floors, plus new GE washer, dryer and microwave (May 2026) make this home move-in ready. The expansive loft with full-height ceilings offers two distinct flex spaces ideal for an additional bedroom, home office, media room or living area. The open living and dining area opens to a large private deck overlooking tranquil ponds, mature trees and beautifully maintained grounds. A detached garage with durable polyaspartic flooring and fresh paint offers versatile space for a gym, studio, hobby room or storage, plus an attached ~100 sq ft storage room with built-in shelving. Parking includes a private garage, reserved space and ample guest parking. Minutes to Metro-North, Old Greenwich Village, Greenwich Point Beach, parks, shopping, dining and major commuting routes. Available immediately.
