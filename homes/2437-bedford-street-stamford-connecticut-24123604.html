About this home

Rare Chesterfield condo with deeded, detached garage - with the option to resell separately if desired! This 1BR/1.5BA townhouse offers great potential in one of Stamford's most desirable gated communities. The vaulted foyer with skylight opens to a freshly painted interior and leads to a bright eat-in kitchen. A flexible main level includes a formal dining room/den plus half bath. The spacious living room sets the tone with a cozy wood-burning fireplace and sliders that open to a private deck with landscaping for added privacy. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite features three closets, a dressing area, and skylit full bath. Laundry completes the second floor. Chesterfield amenities include 24-hour security, two pools, clubhouse, and landscaped grounds all in a prime mid-city location close to shopping, dining, Metro-North, and major highways. With endless potential and a rare garage that sets it apart, this is an opportunity you won't want to miss!
