About this home

Located on a quiet street in Stamford's desirable Shippan neighborhood, this spacious 3-bedroom, 3-full-bath home offers a wonderful combination of comfortable living and coastal charm, just a short walk from the water, beaches, and parks. The main level features hardwood floors, an expansive living room, a separate dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with a skylight. The primary suite stands out with its high ceilings and exposed beams, adding warmth and character. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a balcony where you can enjoy your morning coffee while taking in views of the water. The finished lower level provides additional living space with a full bathroom, a separate entrance, and plenty of storage. Enjoy the peaceful neighborhood setting while being close to waterfront walks, outdoor recreation, and everything Shippan has to offer.
