About this home

Tucked away on a quiet, tree-lined street in North Stamford, this charming split-level home sits on a private 1.29-acre lot with mature landscaping that provides exceptional beauty and seclusion. Offering approximately 3,088+ square feet of living space, the home features four bedrooms and three bathrooms with a flexible layout perfect for today's lifestyles. The upper level includes a spacious primary bedroom suite with sitting area and two additional bedrooms. The main level offers a bright living room, formal dining room, large eat-in kitchen, and oversized family room with fireplace and wet bar that opens to a generous deck. The finished lower level adds a versatile suite and extra bedroom, ideal as a guest space or second primary option, along with a two-car garage and laundry area. Outside, enjoy the outdoor kitchen, gunite in-ground pool surrounded by a spacious bluestone patio, and a whimsical hidden treehouse tucked in the wooded backyard. This private oasis combines resort-style outdoor living with comfortable indoor spaces. This well-maintained home is ready for its next chapter-schedule your private showing today.
