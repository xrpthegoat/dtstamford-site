About this home

Charming clapboard & stone home with idyllic gardens and outdoor living. Loaded with character and timeless appeal, this 3 bedroom, 1 bath home blends classic charm with inviting comfort. Nestled on a beautifully landscaped property, it features wide plank wood floors, built-in bookshelves, and a cozy living room with a fireplace. The dining area opens to a bright, functional kitchen, making everyday living and entertaining a breeze. Step outside to enjoy the serene gardens, stone patio, and lovely outdoor spaces ideal for relaxation or gatherings. A detached 2-car garage adds convenience. Perfectly located close to Wilton and Westport town centers, top-rated schools, the train, and major highways-this home offers both tranquility and accessibility in one exceptional package.
