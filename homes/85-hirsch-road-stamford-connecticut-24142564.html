About this home

TOTALLY renovated Newfield colonial within walking distance to the Newfield Shopping Center, public transportation, schools and houses of worship. Entire home was updated within the last 11 years with new kitchen, bathrooms, full paint job, new cement fiberboard siding, roof, C/A, and more!! Laundry was moved from the lower level to the kitchen, half bath was expanded to a full bath with steam shower. New recessed lighting and A/C were added and lower level redone. All renovations were done with quality and style. Home offers City water and sewers plus easy access to downtown and adjacent towns. Loved by the current owners for almost 40 years.
