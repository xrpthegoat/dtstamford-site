About this home

Available now--this amazing move-in ready 3000+ SF home in one of the most convenient locations in Westport! This spacious home is just a stone's throw to the Beach and Greens Farms train station, offering an open floorplan with 4 bedrooms and 3 baths on 1.8 acres of level land. A possible in-law suite (or guest suite) located on the 1st floor, with a large bedroom, full bath, laundry, handy 2nd kitchen, walk-in pantry, and a large family room with fireplace and beautiful sliders opening to the level backyard. A beautiful staircase leads to the upper level through the two-story foyer filled with natural sunlight that opens to the 2nd level eat-in kitchen w/sliders and a beautiful deck overlooking picturesque views of nature at its finest. The kitchen also connects to a large formal dining room and living room for a perfect entertaining arrangement. On the 2nd level there is a primary suite with a sleek updated en suite bathroom, large closets and beautiful views. There are 2 other spacious bedrooms and a full bath with laundry on the upper floor. This home is nestled away and very private, and at the same time is located in the most convenient location in Westport, just minutes away from Westport Center & Greens Farm Elementary. Optimal location for NYC commuters!
