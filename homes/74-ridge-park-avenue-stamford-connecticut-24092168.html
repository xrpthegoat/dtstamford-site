About this home

Welcome to one-floor living in the heart of the mid-ridges! This sunny and meticulously maintained ranch has been loved by the same owners for 51 years. Step into the sun-filled living room with hardwood floors and feel instantly at home. The large eat-in kitchen sits at the center of the house, opening to a spacious family room with a fireplace and a wall of windows-perfect for gathering in any season. Just off the family room, the sunroom offers a peaceful spot to start your day or wind down with the soothing sounds of the koi pond's waterfall. All three bedrooms feature hardwood floors and abundant natural light. The updated full bath and main-floor laundry add comfort and convenience. Outside, the beautifully landscaped yard is a private escape with a stone patio (complete with automatic awning), koi pond, and in-ground sprinkler system. The detached 2-car garage and extra storage shed offer plenty of room for your tools and toys. Central air, natural gas, city services and a whole-house generator, complete the many benefits of this special home. Tucked into a great neighborhood, this bright, easy, move-in-ready home is a rare find. Convenient to shopping, dining, public transportation and highways. Come see it for yourself today!
