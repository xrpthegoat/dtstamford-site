About this home

Beautifully updated, fully furnished, end unit townhouse awaits your arrival. This turnkey home offers 2 bedrooms (second bedroom in attic has permit), 1 full bathroom. The main level features an inviting open-concept floor plan accented by hardwood floors and recessed lighting. A sun-filled living room seamlessly flows into a contemporary full-sized kitchen, which boasts quartz counter tops, white shaker cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and a stylish breakfast bar perfect for casual dining and entertaining. This move-in-ready property is available as a 1-year furnished rental. Located near dining, shopping, major commuting routes, and Metro-North stations.
