About this home

Rare & Unique Opportunity to buy a home in sought-after Wolfpit neighborhood. This ranch-style home comes with something unusual - LAND!!! .66 lot - flat, clear and open - gives buyers a blank canvas to create a stunning backyard oasis! The inside is just as desirable with its open floor living room and dining area, kitchen with island, wood burning fireplace, oversized bay window and beautiful hardwood floors. Sunroom is off of the kitchen and is perfect for a home office. Expansive deck serves as an extension of the home and overlooks the picturesque landscaping of this massive yard. Driveway leads to a 2-car garage and a laundry/drop zone before entering into additional lower level living space with a fireplace. Main level also includes 3 bedrooms, 2 full baths (1 bath has been renovated). Updates inside and out include a new roof, crown molding and built-ins, which enhance this home's character and charm! Starlight Drive is a quiet, tree-lined street - it is the perfect house to call "home".
