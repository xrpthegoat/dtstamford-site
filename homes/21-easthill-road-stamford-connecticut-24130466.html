About this home

Welcome to 21 East Hill Road, a timeless New England Colonial tucked away on a quiet North Stamford cul-de-sac. Perfectly situated in a sought-after location, this home rests on 1.36 acres of parklike grounds, framed by mature greenery and highlighted by a heated pool. The setting is both peaceful and serene, offering a true retreat. Inside, spacious rooms and a functional floor plan are accented by hardwood floors and two fireplaces that add warmth and character. The eat-in kitchen is a standout, adjoining the gracious front-to-back living room on one side and a generous yet cozy family room on the other. A formal dining room adds elegance for entertaining, while casual kitchen dining flows to the screened porch and Trex deck, where sweeping views of the rear yard and pool set the stage for gatherings or quiet evenings outdoors. With four bedrooms plus a main-floor flex room/office, the home adapts seamlessly to today's lifestyle-ideal for a guest suite or work-from-home space. The primary suite features a spacious bath, while the finished walkout lower level provides abundant space for recreation, fitness, or media. All this in a prime location with convenient access to downtown Stamford, local beaches, shopping, dining, and the train station. With New York City within easy reach, the home offers the best of both worlds, elbow room to relax, yet close to everything.
