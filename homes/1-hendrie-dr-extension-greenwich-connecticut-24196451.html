About this home

A once-in-a-generation waterfront opportunity in Old Greenwich, perched above Greenwich Cove with breathtaking sunsets, expansive water views, and direct access to "The Cove" and Long Island Sound, 1 Hendrie Drive Ext offers the opportunity to build or renovate in an exceptional waterfront setting. The property includes a separate tax parcel with a private dock, creating the unique advantage of owning both the residence parcel and a separately deeded dock parcel-a rare offering in Old Greenwich. .Just minutes from the village, train station, schools, and library, this property combines waterfront serenity with exceptional everyday convenience.
