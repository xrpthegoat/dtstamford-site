About this home

Impeccable, effortlessly sophisticated house with lovely water views nestled on private road in idyllic Saugatuck Shores steps to Long Island Sound and Bermuda Lagoon. Designed by top architect VITA Design, the house combines relaxed coastal luxury with an elevated contemporary aesthetic for easy living, enjoyable year round. Features warm beach-inspired woods, high-end finishes, high ceilings, light wood floors, oversized windows and abundant natural light. On the main level the sleek chefs kitchen w/ center island flows seamlessly into the dining area and great room w/ wood-burning fireplace framed by floor-to-ceiling windows. Office/bedroom and full bath complete the main level. Second floor features serene primary suite with water view, custom built-ins and luxurious bath. Two additional bedrooms, full bath, laundry, and stylish reading nook complete this level. The front to back top floor bonus room with water views on both sides, leads out to spectacular roof deck terrace with view of Long Island Sound --a wonderful outdoor living space perfect for entertaining or quiet enjoyment to take in glorious sunsets, sunrises and Fourth of July fireworks over the Sound. The lower level offers additional versatile rec room, mudroom, attached garage. Private back yard. House is FEMA compliant. Lovely neighborhood for walking, biking, boating, kayaking. Close golf/tennis. Quick drive to the train station, Saugatuck shops and dining, and all Westport has to offer.
