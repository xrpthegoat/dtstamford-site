About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated, 3/4 BR fully furnished cape in central Westport. Nestled on a professionally landscaped, fenced lot, this property provides privacy and convenience in a prime location. Step inside to discover an open floor plan designed for both everyday living and easy entertaining. The new kitchen features stone countertops and stainless appliances flowing seamlessly into the dining and living areas with a wood burning fireplace. A first floor bedroom with hall bath and a separate home office complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has a walk-in closet and spa-like bath with double sinks, and the second bedroom has use of the hall bath. The large laundry room has a window, too. Outdoors, the expanded patio is perfect for holding summer grilling and entertaining and is surrounded by private, professionally landscaped gardens and sunny lawn. There is even an outdoor shower! Only minutes from town, train, beaches, shopping and restaurants as well as the Westport Public Library and the Westport Country Playhouse.
