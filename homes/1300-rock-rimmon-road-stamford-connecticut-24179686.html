About this home

Spectacular back-country estate on nearly 4 private landscaped acres with a pool and full-sized tennis court. This palatial 7 BR/8.5-BA family home (completely renovated in 2007) is the epitome of tasteful luxury. The beautiful first-floor master bedroom occupies its own wing and boasts 2 walk-in closets, a huge soaking tub and walk-in shower, and a lovely patio overlooking the beautifully landscaped back gardens (the property has two wells, one for residents and one for irrigation). The first level also features a massive living room with a fireplace and a fully-loaded kitchen with stainless steel appliances, limestone countertops, and a breakfast area facing a third fireplace. Hardwood floors and beautiful tilework throughout lend the space a rustic, welcoming warmth. The second floor has 4 bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms plus a nanny suite and an extra laundry area. The basement is fully finished with an exercise room and movie theater. The grounds are meticulously manicured, with a fenced-in pool and tennis court. Back to market after being completely repainted and refreshed. The finished basement has a private screening room and a large sauna. Your personal oasis awaits!
