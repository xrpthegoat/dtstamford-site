About this home

This adorable beach house is totally redone. Heated sun porch with ceiling fan with door to the Living room with a fireplace. Formal dining room open to the newer kitchen with quartz counter tops and stainless steel appliances & breakfast bar. There is a powder room off the kitchen and a newer mud room with10 foot ceilings. There are two bedrooms and an updated bathroom with tile floors and large shower and marble top vanity. Gleaming hardwood floors. All newer light fixtures, ceiling fans etc. A 2 car garage and a full basement with laundry complete this great cozy home that is a block from West Beach and Brenann's neighborhood restaurant and a 5 minute ride to downtown Stamford, the mall and the Stamford train center. Must have a credit score of 700 or better.
