About this home

Location, location, location! Above ground living space is 924 sq.ft. plus sunroom (not heated) w/ additional space. Much sought after desirable Mid Ridges area offering exceptional opportunity to first time home buyers. A fantastic condo alternative without the HOA. This delightful 2 bedrooms, 1 bath cozy ranch/bungalow home provides a perfect balance of charm and conveniences with potential for updates to suit modern tastes. Step into the bright living room where a wood burning fireplace sets the time for cozy gatherings. The lay-out also includes an eat-in kitchen which leads into a versatile/sunroom that can serve as a study, sitting room or home/office (not heated). The 1 attached garage is convenient with easy access inside the home. Pull downstairs to a spacious floored attic. Wonderful and private back yard, ideal for relaxation or entertaining. Some recent improvements: oil burner, roof and bath. This easy one floor living is freshly painted, however, in time to add your finishing touches from some of the original features. Conveniently located to amazing shopping, dining, recreational amenities and Highway.
