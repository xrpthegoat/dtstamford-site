About this home

Charming Craftsman Bungalow in Prime Location! Welcome to this charming, well-maintained, sun-filled Craftsman Bungalow, full of character, comfort, and thoughtful updates. Set in a neighborhood where you can walk to restaurants and grocery stores, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers the perfect blend of vintage charm and modern convenience. Step inside to find gleaming hardwood floors, spacious living areas, and a classic dining room with original built-ins-ideal for gatherings and everyday living. The first-floor family room (or optional master bedroom) is a standout feature, complete with sliding door access to the back deck-perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. Enjoy peace of mind with newly updated systems throughout and take full advantage of outdoor living with a welcoming front porch, expansive backyard with mature trees, and garden space ready for your green thumb. Upstairs, you'll find two tandem bedrooms, one with a full bath-providing a flexible space that works beautifully for a bedroom with separate home office, rooms for siblings, guests, or creative studio. Recent improvements include: Furnace, Roof, HVAC, Painted Exterior and Chimney Flashing! This well-loved home has all the warmth, charm, and location you've been waiting for. Come see it today!
