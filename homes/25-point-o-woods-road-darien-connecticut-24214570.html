About this home

Classic character, exceptional space, and a setting you'll want to experience firsthand. Designed by architect Ed Wallace and built in 1933, 25 Point O Woods Road is a beautiful example of classic Center Hall Colonial craftsmanship, thoughtfully updated for today's lifestyle. Set on 1.15 beautifully landscaped acres, this inviting home blends timeless architecture with generous spaces and modern comfort. A gracious center hall leads to well-proportioned rooms and an impressive family room with a dramatic two-tier coffered ceiling. The recently renovated all-season sun porch, transformed from the original screened porch, offers a bright and comfortable space to enjoy the beauty of the grounds year-round. The flexible floor plan features five bedrooms, five full baths, and one half bath, with exceptional room for family and guests. Two additional rooms above the garage offer endless possibilities as home offices, a gym, guest spaces, or additional bedrooms. An expansive lower level includes a full bath, exercise room, and abundant storage. Outside, professionally landscaped grounds create a private setting for entertaining and everyday enjoyment. The level backyard is ideally sited for a pool, offering the opportunity to create a spectacular private retreat. With its architectural character, thoughtful updates, generous spaces, and exceptional setting, 25 Point O Woods Road is a home that feels special from the moment you arrive and one you won't want to leave!
