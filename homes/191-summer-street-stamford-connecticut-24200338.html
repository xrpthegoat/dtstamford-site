About this home

Urban Elegance Meets Historic Charm in Downtown Stamford. Step into a rare blend of style, character, and convenience, perfectly positioned in the heart of Stamford's vibrant downtown, just moments from the city's best restaurants, shops, and the Metro-North train to NYC. Tucked inside a beautifully restored historic building with only 7 exclusive loft-style residences, this boutique property offers a quiet, upscale alternative to Stamford's high-rise lifestyle. This fully renovated 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom unit impresses with exposed brick walls, oak floors, high ceilings, and recessed lighting throughout. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated from the Samsung stainless steel appliances and Caesarstone countertops to the Moen fixtures, Pella windows, and in-unit GE washer/dryer. The secured building features an elevator, interior mailboxes, and a welcoming lobby. Parking is available at nearby garages for an additional monthly fee. If you're looking for NYC loft vibes without leaving Connecticut, this one delivers.
