About this home

Located in the coveted Rowayton complex, Thomas Place, this stunning and recently renovated single standing townhouse, boasts 3 bedrooms, 3 full baths and a delightful open floor plan including a bright new kitchen, eat-in area, dining room, family room with a wet bar, living room, office and more. Be welcomed by the gracious 2 story foyer that leads to the many light filled living spaces on the main floor. Upstairs, the main bedroom features a vaulted ceiling, sneak peeks of Long Island Sound and a recently renovated large full bath with a generous walk in closet. Two additional nicely sized bedrooms, sitting room/office and full bath round out the 2nd floor. On the 3rd floor you'll find bright and welcoming space that's perfect for a home office.There's a 2 car attached garage, a large unfinished basement with 15 ft. ceilings. Though it's currently being used for storage - it could be finished. The unit is elevator ready. Amenities include a covered boathouse for entertaining, community dock for launching kayaks, paddle boards, etc. HOA fees cover grounds & road maintenance, water, snow removal, property management, maintenance of roofs and the exterior of the units. A short walk to the community center, beach and the seaside village of Rowayton. Come and enjoy all that Rowayton and Thomas has to offer!
