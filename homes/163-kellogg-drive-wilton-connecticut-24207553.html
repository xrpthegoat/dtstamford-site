About this home

Discover the charm and possibilities of this spacious split-level home nestled on 2 picturesque, tree-lined acres in scenic Wilton, where classic New England character meets everyday convenience. Surrounded by natural stone walls, the property offers a peaceful sense of privacy while remaining just a 10-minute drive to Wilton Center, with its local shops, restaurants, community events and access to Wilton Station and Metro-North. Making commuting to New York City a practical option. With over 2,800 sq. ft. of living space, this home offers 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths, plus an additional 952 square feet of finished lower-level space featuring the fourth bedroom and flexible areas ideal for an in-law suite, home gym, theater, playroom or recreation space. The versatile split-level design creates multiple gathering spaces perfect for entertaining family and friends, while the screened porch provides a wonderful setting for enjoying the outdoors throughout the milder seasons. Hardwood floors extend throughout much of the home. Recent improvements include a remodeled upper-level hall bath, new doors and hardware, updated light fixtures, freshly painted rooms, a newer well pump and new garage doors. The eat-in kitchen is ready for your personal vision, with exciting potential to create an open-concept kitchen, dining and living area-possibly enhanced by a cathedral ceiling for a more contemporary feel. Bring your ideas and make this private Wilton retreat your own!
