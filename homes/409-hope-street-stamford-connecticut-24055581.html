BEST & FINAL offers by Noon on Tuesday, 4/15. Welcome to this perfect townhouse centrally located in Glenbrook! This stunning, turn-key home is brimming with charm, warmth, and modern upgrades. Off the kitchen, find a dedicated dining area to make entertaining a breeze, leading right into the large, cozy living room complete with a fireplace and two sliding glass doors that open up to a spacious deck, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living.
