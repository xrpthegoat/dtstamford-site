About this home

Location! Nestled in a beautiful country setting, Rolling Ridge Condominiums is one of Norwalk's finest places to call home. Enjoy wonderful amenities including an in-ground swimming pool, a picturesque pond with fountains, a handball/tennis court, and a clubhouse with shower facilities. Unit 422 features 1 bedroom, 1.5 baths, and a den that's perfect for a home office or guest room. The spacious primary bedroom offers a full bath and large walk in closets. The bright living room features oversized windows that bring in plenty of natural light, while the kitchen opens to the dining area with sliding doors leading to a private patio terrace. With great closet space throughout, an excellent layout, and a wonderful flow, this unit feels like home. If you're looking for peaceful surroundings with great amenities and a convenient Norwalk location, this is one not to be missed. HOA covers heat, hot water, water, sewer, trash, snow removal, and grounds maintenance.
