About this home

MULTIPLE OFFERS- HIGHEST and BEST MONDAY JUNE 28 at NOON. Welcome to this beautifully maintained Cape in Norwalk's desirable Brookside neighborhood. Filled with natural light and thoughtfully cared for, this charming 3-bedroom home offers a warm and inviting layout with hardwood floors, a classic fireplace, and comfortable living spaces throughout. The eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and overlooks a private patio and backyard, creating an ideal setting for everyday living and entertaining. A spacious family room with an adjacent powder room provides flexible space for a playroom, home office, guest suite, or potential in-law setup. Custom blinds, designer lighting fixtures, and abundant storage throughout four levels of living add both style and functionality. Outside, enjoy a professionally landscaped, fully fenced backyard offering exceptional privacy, along with a storage shed, two-car attached garage, and oversized driveway with ample parking. Conveniently located close to shopping, restaurants, Route 1, and I-95, this move-in-ready home combines charm, flexibility, and a highly desirable location.
