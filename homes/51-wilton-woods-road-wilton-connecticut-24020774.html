About this home

This classic colonial, set on two level acres in a highly sought-after Wilton neighborhood, exudes curb appeal and features a circular and lifestyle friendly flow. The updated kitchen enjoys granite countertops and a Viking gas range, catering to culinary enthusiasts. Hardwood floors run throughout the home, adding a touch of warmth and sophistication. The family room is a standout, featuring beautiful wood paneling, a premium cast iron stove, and built-in shelves. The sun-filled sunroom provides a perfect space for year-round entertaining. Completing the first floor are a lovely living room and a formal dining room, ideal for hosting gatherings. The primary bedroom is a private retreat with a full bath and a large walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms offer ample closet space, ensuring comfort for all. Outside, the spacious deck is perfect for summer BBQs, and the large, level fenced-in backyard provides plenty of room for play and relaxation. BRAND NEW ARCHITECTURAL SHINGLE ROOF IN 2024.
