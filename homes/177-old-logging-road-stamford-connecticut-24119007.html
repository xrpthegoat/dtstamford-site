About this home

Welcome to this meticulously maintained 4 bedroom, 2 bath ranch, perfectly situated on a picturesque acre in one of North Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering the ease of one floor living, this home exudes pride of ownership at every turn. Enjoy the convenience of being within walking distance to the elementary school, Stamford Nature Center-known for its farm animals, holiday events & engaging museum, & the Bartlett Arboretum, with its miles of scenic hiking trails, gorgeous plants, flowers of every kind & inviting Sunday evening summer concerts perfect for a picnic. All this, just minutes from the Merritt Parkway and shopping. Inside this fabulous property you'll find a renovated kitchen with granite counters, stainless appliances & lovely tile backsplash. The large primary bedroom boasts 2 closets & a spa-like renovated primary bath. The spacious living room with a wood-burning fireplace sets the stage for cozy gatherings, while the family room's gas fireplace offers a warm and inviting retreat. The nicely sized dining room features a sliding door opening to a stunning 3-season porch-perfect for morning coffee, quiet relaxation & watching nature unfold around you. A small pond, belonging to a neighboring property, offers a peaceful view from the backyard. Designed with entertaining in mind, this home blends comfort, style & location-ready to welcome its next chapter. Simply unpack and move right in.
