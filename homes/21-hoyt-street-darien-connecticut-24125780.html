About this home

3 BR, 2 BA (*tax card not up to date) Darien rental across from Holmes Elementary and convenient to Middlesex, Darien HS, train, town, and all Darien has to offer. Charming Cape Cod home features an updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances, custom cabinets, and modern countertops; a formal Living Room with a wood-burning fireplace; an office (or 2nd BR) on the first floor with a full bath/tub; and two spacious upper rooms with full baths and showers. Full and dry French basement with French drains, plus laundry, a two-car detached garage, and a mudroom that can also serve as a 4-season sunroom. Level, tranquil backyard for BBQs, sports, and/or relaxing with screening.
