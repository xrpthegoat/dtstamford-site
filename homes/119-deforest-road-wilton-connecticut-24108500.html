About this home

Embrace the epitome of privacy, comfort and convenience in this beautifully appointed residence, every detail meticulously crafted to offer an unparalleled living experience. This home boasts four bedrooms, with the flexibility of an optional 5th bedroom/office in the finished basement, complete with a full bath for added option of in law/au pair w/ private entry. The Kitchen, a true showpiece and heart, featuring high end custom cabinetry, granite, a built-in desk for household organization, and truly exquisite millwork exuding warmth and sophistication. The FamilyRoom is a haven of fun and relaxation, with soaring cathedral ceiling, beams, a fieldstone fireplace, sliders to the deck creating an abundance of natural light. Dining Room boasts eye catching raised panel molding. Formal LivingRoom, 2nd fireplace and gleaming hardwood floors, offers a refined space for hosting guests /entertaining. The Primary Suite with an en suite bath, spacious walk-in closet, provides the perfect escape after a long day. Outdoor living is equally impressive, with a newer deck that invites you to unwind and enjoy nature's surround sound. So many features...new LL full bath, beams, skylights, French doors, central vac, new Washer/ Dryer, new powder room, freshly painted exterior, and select interior updates. Location is key w/easy access to Wilton, Ridgefield and New Canaan centers. Enjoy Award Winning Wilton Schools and the utmost privacy where casual meets luxury in this move in ready home!
