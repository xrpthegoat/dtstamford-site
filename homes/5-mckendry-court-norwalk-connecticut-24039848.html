About this home

Welcome home! Rebuilt in 2018, this stunning, thoughtfully designed home in Rowayton, CT, is a perfect blend of luxurious yet cozy living. The foyer, with its custom millwork, is a showcase, opening to 3 levels of light drenched space yet flowing into a cozy den w/coffered ceilings and sueded walls. The heart of the home is a custom, well designed kitchen w/substantial island, beverage center & high-end appliances allowing gracious interaction w/guests in a generous keeping room enhanced by a fireplace & 9+ft ceilings. You'll entertain for hours in the dining area w/French doors leading to an expansive deck overlooking the private, flat backyard w/river views and professional landscaping. A calming retreat, the 2nd fl primary has a private balcony, walk-in closet w/island, dressing table and spa-like bath w/2 person shower & therapeutic soaking tub. 2 ensuite bedrooms & laundry room with sink plus tons of storage complete this floor. An upper level rec room illuminated by skylights is adjacent to 2 additional bedrooms & full bath. Custom mudroom w/floor to ceiling cabinetry has space for everything. Garage used as gym. Moments to train & town, this home outstanding, raised has it all!
