About this home

Welcome to 24 Harold Street, where style, space, and convenience come together in the heart of Cos Cob. Enjoy easy access to parks, coffee shops, restaurants, the waterfront, Metro-North, and I-95, with Greenwich Avenue less than five minutes away and NYC under an hour. Step inside to a beautifully designed home featuring a chef's kitchen with stainless steel appliances and an oversized island. A first-floor bedroom with a full bathroom offers exceptional flexibility for guests, a home office, or convenient main-level living, with easy access to the garage. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite provides a relaxing retreat with a soaking tub, walk-in closet, and custom California Closets. Front and rear decks offer ideal spots for morning coffee, fresh air, or tending to a herb garden. Outside, the gated and fenced front yard provides added privacy along with plenty of space to enjoy the outdoors. The generous rear yard is perfect for kicking a ball around, relaxing with friends, or hosting the next neighborhood get-together. The finished lower level adds even more flexible living space and can easily serve as a gym, playroom, home office, or media room. With beautiful curb appeal, versatile living spaces, and an unbeatable location, 24 Harold Street truly has it all.
