About this home

**Something Different in North Stamford!** Tucked away near the Stamford Nature Center, this charming and well-maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bath ranch offers privacy, character, and thoughtful updates. The home features a brand-new kitchen and two newly renovated bathrooms, along with updated windows, roof, siding, LeafGuard system, landscaping, well pump, washer/dryer, and more. A sun-filled 18' x 16' gathering room with slate floors, heat, and A/C overlooks the patio, deck, flagpole, and beautifully landscaped yard. Two bedrooms are en-suite, while a private entrance leads to a 575 sq. ft. office above the detached 2-car garage. An attic and large shed provide additional storage. The heated and air-conditioned porch is not included in the City of Stamford square footage but provides additional interior living space. **Fun fact:** The home hosted the Prince and Princess of Nepal on November 27, 1958, adding a fascinating touch of history to this truly unique North Stamford property.
