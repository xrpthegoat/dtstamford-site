About this home

Set on a peaceful cul-de-sac in the coveted Riverside neighborhood, 1 Crawford Terrace offers refined suburban living with exceptional convenience. Thoughtfully renovated in 2022, this space was designed by a renowned NYC architect for both comfortable daily living and easy entertaining, the home combines warmth, functionality, and timeless appeal. Ideally located moments from top-rated schools, parks, beaches, Metro North, and the shops and dining of Greenwich, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy turnkey living in one of Riverside's most desirable enclaves.
