About this home

Bright and spacious rental in sought-after Wilton Crest with an ideal in-town location close to shops, restaurants, the train station and post office. This two-bedroom condo offers one level living in a peaceful setting with abundant natural light and walk walkout to the three private patios and the beautifully maintained grounds. The living room and both bedrooms all feature their own sliding glass doors that open to patios, creating a comfortable indoor-outdoor flow. Inside, there are laminate flooring throughout the living areas along with a well-equipped kitchen offering granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. Additional features include convenient parking, a private storage area and shared new smart laundry located just a few steps from the unit. You will also enjoy access to the community pool and tennis court, offering wonderful outdoor spaces to relax, stay active, and enjoy time with family and friends.
