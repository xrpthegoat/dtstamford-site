About this home

Inviting 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath FEMA compliant coastal cottage offering the perfect blend of comfort, privacy, and seaside lifestyle. Built in 2013, this bright and airy home is tucked down a private road that winds through a salt marsh preserve with stunning views of Sherwood Mill Pond, a serene setting that feels worlds away while remaining close to everything Westport has to offer. Enjoy easy walking or biking access to Compo Beach, the coveted Longshore Club with golf, pool, tennis, pickleball, ice skating and sailing, and waterfront dining at the renowned La Plage Restaurant. The home is ideally located within a vibrant beach community known for year-round traditions including summer beach days, sandcastle contests, Fourth of July fireworks, and sunset picnics overlooking Long Island Sound. Enjoy the spacious deck and backyard, as well as driveway parking for 5 cars. Energy efficient heat pump keeps the home comfortable in all seasons. Convenient to downtown Westport, the Levitt Pavilion, Westport Library, MoCA Museum, summer and winter farmer's markets, and Westport's elevated shopping and dining scene. Top-rated public and private schools, easy commute to NYC, and low taxes complete this rare opportunity to enjoy coastal living in one of Connecticut's most prestigious towns.
