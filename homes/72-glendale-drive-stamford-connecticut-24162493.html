About this home

Bright, sun filled living meets effortless functionality in this beautifully expanded Cape set on a level corner property in the heart of Glenbrook. Thoughtfully designed for today's lifestyle, this 4 bedroom 2 full bath home offers comfort and flexibility with a seamless flow throughout. The main level features a spacious living area filled with natural light and a large eat in kitchen with a breakfast bar, perfect for gathering, dining and everyday living, along with versatile space ideal for a dining room, playroom or home office. Hardwood floors run throughout adding warmth and continuity. Upstairs offers generously sized bedrooms with well positioned full baths for easy living. Step outside to a private back deck overlooking a flat usable yard, ideal for entertaining or relaxing. The corner lot provides added openness and a true neighborhood feel. A full basement with direct garage access offers incredible potential for finishing. Conveniently located just minutes to Glenbrook train, shopping, parks and major commuting routes, this home blends suburban charm with everyday convenience. Move right in and enjoy.
