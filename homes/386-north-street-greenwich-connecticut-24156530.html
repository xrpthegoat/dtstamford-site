About this home

This magnificent 9,974 sq ft estate seamlessly blends grand scale, craftsmanship, and modern luxury. Set on 2.3 parklike acres in coveted Mid-Country Greenwich, just minutes from town, the home makes an immediate impression with a soaring two-story foyer introducing exquisitely appointed interiors defined by custom details and rich materials. Updated in 2026, the show stopping kitchen features top-of-the-line appliances, bespoke cabinetry, and a dramatic quartzite center island, opening to a vaulted great room with fireplace and effortless access to the pool terrace. Six spacious ensuite bedrooms include a luxurious primary retreat with sitting room, 2 private offices, fireplace, spa-like marble bath with steam shower and soaking tub, and expansive walk-in closets. A private in-law or nanny suite provides flexible living options. The finished lower level features a 2,000-bottle temperature-controlled wine cellar with tasting bar, recreation space with putting green, sauna, fitness area and ample storage. Outdoors, enjoy a 55-foot pool, spa, 3,000 sq ft sun deck, BBQ island, and sweeping lawns. Additional enhancements include a redesigned laundry room, upgraded lighting throughout, refinished floors, freshly painted interiors, smart home and automation upgrades, state-of-the art security system, new HVAC systems and a modernized garage. An iconic residence where grandeur meets exceptional craftsmanship and refined living.
