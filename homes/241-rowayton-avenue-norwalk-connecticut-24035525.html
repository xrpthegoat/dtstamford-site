About this home

Welcome to your dream home in the heart of Connecticut's most loved beach town! Sited on a half-acre of flat land, this beautifully updated gem is flooded with natural light, thanks to its expansive windows and airy open floor plan. Step inside to discover a perfect blend of historic charm and modern convenience. The main level features a spacious living area, ideal for entertaining guests or relaxing with family, an open dining room and access to the wrap-around front porch. The gourmet kitchen boasts top-of-the-line appliances, sleek countertops, and ample storage space. Upstairs, you'll find 3 generously sized bedrooms all ensuite and filled with sunlight. The Primary is a tranquil retreat with a luxurious ensuite bathroom, walk-in closet, and a private porch. The third floor, with architectural detail and soaring ceilings, has a family room, bedroom, and beautiful bathroom. Other features include an upstairs laundry room, mudroom adjacent to the kitchen, walk-in pantry, and basement that could be a home gym. Outside, is a new stone patio overlooking the private yard and a separate two-car garage. Located just a short walk from the water, charming village shops, and the train station, this home provides the quintessential coastal lifestyle. With its move-in ready condition and unbeatable location, this beauty won't stay on the market for long.
