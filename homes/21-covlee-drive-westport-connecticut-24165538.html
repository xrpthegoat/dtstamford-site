About this home

Your Westport oasis awaits on one of the most exceptional direct-waterfront properties in town! Make lasting memories in this private, modern estate with sweeping panoramic views of Long Island Sound, Bermuda Lagoon, Peck Ledge Lighthouse and boating channels! Walk a few steps to your private dock and take your boat DIRECTLY out on the Sound! Enjoy your gunite pool and spa overlooking the water. You can also swim, jet-ski, kayak or paddle board from your dock! Ready to head back inside? Rinse off in the outdoor shower, enjoy the sauna or take a steam shower! Enjoy all that living in Saugatuck Shores has to offer. As a Covlee resident, you&apos;ll be a member of the private Covlee Beach Association. This neighborhood enclave offers a sandy beach, additional dock slips, playground, picnic area and fire pit. This flowing, spectacular home enjoys a beautiful floor plan with dramatic walls of windows and soaring ceilings, providing unparalleled water views from almost every room. From the Great Room with its dramatic fireplace and vaulted ceiling, to the spacious, custom Kitchen with fireplace, to the cozy Family Room and Study, there are so many spaces to delight in this waterfront haven! On the upper level, all bedrooms have decks overlooking the Sound. The beautiful Primary Suite has a private deck, vaulted ceiling and luxurious spa bathroom. From 21 Covlee you are just minutes to the train and all of Saugatuck and Westport amenities.
