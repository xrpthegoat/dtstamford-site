About this home

This immaculate and move-in ready home located in the Spring Hill area offers a thoughtful layout with flexible living space throughout. The main floor features a brand-new kitchen with all new appliances, a formal dining room with plenty of space for entertainment, and a primary bedroom as well. Neutral tile flooring runs through the kitchen and dining room, while the rest of the main floor features beautiful hardwood floors. An office or bedroom and a full bathroom complete this level, easily be converted in a large primary bedroom. With second floor flexibility, you will find three additional spacious bedrooms and full bath, making it easy to create a second primary suite if desired. There is also extra space that could be used as a den or family room, offering potential for an in-law setup or income-producing tenant with a separate entrance. A good-sized deck off the second floor provides a bird's-eye view of the neighborhood. A small basement with a garage entrance, contains a laundry room and storage. The landscaped yard features irrigation, perennial plantings, and a lovely fenced-in area. This home is ideally located for commuters, with quick access to Route 95, Route 25, Metro-North and the Merritt Parkway, along with many nearby amenities. Great location for commuting to NYC! This property with five bedrooms must be seen in person to fully appreciate its condition and versatile possibilities. Five bedrooms at this price point is rare. Bring your offers.
