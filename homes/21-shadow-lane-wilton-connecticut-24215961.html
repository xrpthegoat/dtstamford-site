About this home

Welcome to this magnificent 3-4 bedroom Wilton "Villa" on over 3 acres! A rare architectural treasure where classic proportions harmonize with true old-world European warmth, blending timeless artisan details and modern luxury into an inviting sanctuary tailored for both intimate living and grand entertaining. Enter into a light-filled two-story foyer creating an immediate sense of grandeur, featuring gleaming granite tile with a custom inlay design. A sunken living room boasting soaring cathedral ceiling, hardwood, bathed in light. The formal dining room was built to large proportions to accommodate a house full of guests. A saved piece of history lies in the sunken family room-part of the original 1930s home that adds old-world charm with its impressive fieldstone fireplace and hand-hewn ceiling beams. The heart of the home is the huge kitchen, intentionally designed for the chef and baker, featuring granite counters, SS double ovens, 6 burner gas stove and stunning hand-painted Portuguese Azulejo tiles that spill into the adjacent Butler's Pantry and Laundry. An office/poss. 4th bedroom and Full Bath round out the 1st floor. Upper Level you'll enter the Primary Suite through French doors to find a huge walk-in closet and spa bathroom. 2 additional bedrooms offer charm and space, one with original fireplace. Over 2200 SF in LL, ready for whatever you need!!! All utilities underground, new roof, 2nd detached garage, central vac. For the discerning buyer of the exceptional!!
