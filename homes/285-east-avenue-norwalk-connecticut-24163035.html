About this home

Enjoy living in one of East Norwalks prime locations. This home features breath taking views from most rooms and is situated directly on "The Mill Pond" at the East Norwalk Harbor. Enjoy the huge oversized deck that offer tons of entertaining room for lounging and dining. The main level offers a large living space with room for a dining table. Kitchen features all new high end stainless appliances. A main level bonus space can serve as a formal dining room/family room or office. Upstairs enjoy a water view from the on-suite primary bedroom with two large closets and bath featuring a tub, separate shower and double sinks. There are also 2 additional bedrooms and an another full hall bath. This home is close to all of East Norwalk's best destinations. Walking distance to restaurants, shopping, Metro North station, Vet's Park and SONO. This wonderful rental includes all utilities in rent. Tenant to pay renters and liability insurance plus internet/cable. Basement level has a separate tenant. ( photos are from a previous listing and interior furnishings are different)
