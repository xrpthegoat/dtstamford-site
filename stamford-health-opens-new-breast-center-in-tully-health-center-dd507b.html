Stamford Health Opens New Breast Center in Tully Health Center

Stamford Health launches a state-of-the-art breast center at Tully Health Center, enhancing access to advanced care in the heart of downtown.

Uploaded · August 12, 2026

Stamford Health has officially opened the new Odyssey Group Breast Center at the Tully Health Center, marking a significant upgrade in local access to advanced breast imaging, diagnostics, and coordinated care. For residents of Lower Fairfield County, this expansion means faster, more private, and more comprehensive services—all under one roof. The center’s opening in August 2026 is a milestone for community health and a testament to sustained local philanthropy.

What's happening

On August 6, 2026, Stamford Health unveiled the newly transformed Odyssey Group Breast Center, located on the first floor of the Tully Health Center in Stamford, Connecticut [1]. The center spans 13,000 square feet and was made possible by a landmark $10 million gift from the Odyssey Group Foundation [1]. This donation funded the full relocation and renovation of the breast care program, consolidating services that were previously spread across multiple floors [3].

The new facility integrates advanced imaging technology, diagnostics, and clinical expertise into a single, coordinated space designed to improve patient flow, enhance privacy, and increase comfort [1]. It is expected to serve approximately 35,000 mammograms annually and support a growing number of patients as demand for breast care continues to rise [2]. The center also enables co-location of specialists, including medical and radiation oncologists from the Stamford Health Bennett Cancer Center, allowing for more seamless care [3].

This project represents the final milestone in Stamford Health’s Campaign for Women and Babies [1]. The facility’s development began in January 2023, with construction completed in time for the August 2026 opening [2]. The center’s design emphasizes a person-centered approach, reflecting the organization’s mission to deliver compassionate, expert care [1].

A community investment with lasting impact

The Odyssey Group Foundation’s $10 million contribution is part of a long-standing relationship with Stamford Health, dating back to 2008 with a $2 million donation for the da Vinci Surgical System [2]. In 2011, the foundation committed $10 million to build a new trauma center, and the new breast center continues that legacy of strategic, community-focused investment [2].

While the sources do not specify the exact timeline for when the project was approved by the Zoning Board or whether any permits were required, the fact that the center is now open confirms that all necessary approvals were obtained. The project’s completion aligns with a broader trend of healthcare infrastructure modernization in Stamford, particularly in the downtown and Tully areas, where access to specialized medical services is increasingly valued.

As healthcare services become more centralized and specialized, neighborhoods with access to comprehensive medical facilities gain a competitive edge in the rental and homebuying market.

This new center strengthens the Tully Health Center as a regional hub for women’s health, reinforcing the area’s appeal for residents who prioritize access to high-quality, coordinated care. In a city where commute times and access to medical services are key considerations, having a state-of-the-art breast center located within the downtown core—just steps from transit, retail, and residential zones—adds tangible value to nearby housing.

Moreover, the fact that the project was funded by a local corporate foundation with deep roots in Stamford underscores the city’s ability to attract and retain private investment in public health. This kind of sustained philanthropy signals confidence in Stamford’s future, which can influence buyer sentiment and rental demand.
