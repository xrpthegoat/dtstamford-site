About this home

Through stone pillars on 2.39 lush level acres, this special Nantucket-style home in South Wilton blends fine architecture with high-end functionality. A rare home that is both grand and cozy, it's one to enjoy and share generously. Striking exterior-gray cedar and chestnut-stained siding with CT fieldstone, 2 patios and a copper cupola-sets the tone for beautifully sunlit interiors. An impressive foyer frames the spectacular Great Room with 20-foot ceilings and a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace. A chef's kitchen offers a large island, dining area, 6-burner Bertazzoni range, SubZero refrigerator, wall oven, wide plank floors, pantry closet, wine cooler and custom Wood-Mode cabinetry, and adjacent mudroom is entered from the heated 3-car garage. Unique curved windows frame the chic formal Dining Room. Move through the foyer to the beautifully integrated original home with new 1st-floor full bath, a cozy office with large fieldstone fireplace, tranquil sitting room for quiet retreat, and full kitchen. A perfect in-law or guest quarters, as well. The large Primary BR suite includes two walk-in closets, balcony, vaulted ceiling and beautiful full bath. Four additional BR's on the 2nd floor (2 ensuite) and another full bath, plus office/playroom are the epitome of craftsmanship and the definition of 'home'. Current owners absolutely loved the combination of charm and sophistication at their beloved 'compound'. Just 15 min to both Westport and Norwalk Trains.
