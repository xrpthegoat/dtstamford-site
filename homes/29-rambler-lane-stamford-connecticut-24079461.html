About this home

A sprawling ranch on a level acre in a coveted lower North Stamford neighborhood on the border of New Canaan is ready for a refresh. Here you will find a grand opportunity with all the right components to renovate this space to your precise specifications. There's endless potential with this well designed custom home. Generous entry foyer and oversized casual areas including a window walled great room adjacent to an entertainment room complete with full sized wet bar. Two fireplaces, vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, whole house generator with automatic transfer switch and extensive lighting both in and out. The main bedroom suite has sliders to a private terrace. The fenced rear yard with kidney shaped gunite pool and open level lawn is a serene oasis with privacy on all sides. A vintage pool house with his/her changing rooms and kitchenette compliment the outdoor space. Make it your own.
