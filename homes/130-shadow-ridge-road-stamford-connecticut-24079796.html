About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated ranch where modern elegance meets timeless charm. The open-concept kitchen features Calacatta Gold countertops, premium appliances, and flows into a bright living area with recessed lighting and hardwood floors. Step outside to a serene travertine patio and landscaped backyard, complete with a built-in sprinkler system perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The main level offers three spacious bedrooms and a stylish full bath. Upstairs, a private retreat includes a full-slab Calacatta marble shower. Another full-slab shower on the lower level showcases the home's upscale finishes. Located in a quiet, desirable neighborhood, this move-in-ready home is ideal for first-time buyers or those looking to downsize without compromising on style or quality.
