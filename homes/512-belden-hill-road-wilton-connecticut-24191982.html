About this home

Nestled among the trees in desirable South Wilton, this architecturally significant mid-century modern contemporary offers timeless design, privacy, and comfort. Inspired by Frank Lloyd Wright's School of Design at Taleisin. Offering approximately 2,700 square feet, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath residence is filled with natural light from expansive windows and sliding glass doors that seamlessly connect the interior with the surrounding landscape. A dramatic living room features vaulted ceilings, exposed wood beams, built-in shelving, and a striking stone fireplace. The adjoining dining room flows into the kitchen and inviting family room with a wood-burning fireplace and sliders to the deck and private backyard. The spacious primary suite is a true retreat with its own fireplace, sitting area, walk-in closet, private deck access, and spa-inspired bath with soaking tub, oversized shower, double vanity, skylight, and water closet. Two additional bedrooms, hardwood floors, an attached two-car garage, and a full unfinished basement provide flexibility and abundant storage. Conveniently located just minutes to Metro-North, major commuting routes, Wilton Center, and award-winning schools, this is a rare opportunity to own an architecturally distinctive home in one of Fairfield Counties favorite towns.
