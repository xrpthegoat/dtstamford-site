About this home

CONVENIENCE PLUS... This 1950's cape has so much to offer and a great opportunity to join the Darien community.. Three bedrooms, one conveniently on the first floor, living room with fireplace, 2 full baths (one on each floor) and a usable lot with great parking. WALK to Noroton Heights train and Darien Commons with its great shops and restaurants, and to Baker Park with playground and baseball field and visit the nearby top notch Darien Library. And in addition it is only a short distance to both of Darien's fine beaches and the town's fabulous 60 acre Great Island waterfront where you can enjoy nature at its best!
