About this home

This beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath residence seamlessly blends timeless architectural character with refined modern finishes. Rich hardwood floors, soaring ceilings, recessed lighting, and elegant curved archways create warmth and sophistication throughout. The spacious living room flows effortlessly into a sun-drenched sunroom lined with oversized windows-an ideal space for a home office, reading lounge, or creative studio. A dedicated dining area connects seamlessly to the renovated kitchen, thoughtfully designed with stainless steel appliances, a farmhouse sink, center island, and designer tile backsplash-perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The generously sized primary bedroom is filled with natural light and highlighted by a striking accent wall, while the second bedroom offers custom built-in shelving for added functionality and style. The refreshed bathroom features dual vanities, sleek contemporary fixtures, and a jetted soaking tub for a spa-inspired retreat. For added convenience, the unit includes an in-unit washer and dryer. Outside, enjoy access to a shared yard framed by mature privacy trees-an inviting setting to relax and unwind. Ideally located within walking distance to the Glenbrook Train Station and just minutes to Downtown Stamford's renowned dining, shopping, and entertainment, this home offers the perfect balance of charm, comfort, and convenience.
