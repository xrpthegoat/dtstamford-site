About this home

This 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial sits on a beautiful acre in one of Westover's most desirable cul-de-sacs. With great bones, hardwood floors, and plenty of space, the possibilities are endless. The classic layout includes a sunny living room, formal dining room, study, and family room overlooking the private backyard. The eat-in kitchen and finished lower-level are just waiting for a refresh. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has its own bath, and three more bedrooms share a hall bath. Tucked away on a quiet, tree-lined street but still close to everything. Come see the potential!
