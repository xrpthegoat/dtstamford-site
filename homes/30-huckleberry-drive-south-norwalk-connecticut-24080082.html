About this home

West Norwalk Stunner! Prepare to be captivated by this totally renovated and updated single-family gem. This isn't just a house; it's a lifestyle upgrade, offering unparalleled space, comfort, and convenience in the heart of West Norwalk. Step inside and be greeted by an expansive, light-filled great room boasting soaring cathedral ceilings, seamlessly blending the kitchen, dining, and living areas. Imagine hosting gatherings and creating memories in this breathtaking space! This home features 5 generously sized bedrooms, including two with full en-suite baths and a fabulous master suite with a spacious walk-in closet. With 4 full baths in total, mornings will be a breeze for everyone. Need multi-generational living space? The lower level is a private oasis, complete with a bedroom, living room, and full bath - perfect for in-laws, guests, or a private retreat. Cooking is a pleasure in the modern kitchen, equipped with sleek stainless steel appliances and island for easy prep and serving. An abundance of Anderson windows floods the home with natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Enjoy year-round comfort with central air, and appreciate the ample storage space with great closet space throughout. Step outside to your park-like backyard, a true haven for outdoor enjoyment. Relax on the deck or host barbecues on the patio, creating your own private escape. Convenience is key with a 2-car garage and a large driveway offering plenty of off-street parking
