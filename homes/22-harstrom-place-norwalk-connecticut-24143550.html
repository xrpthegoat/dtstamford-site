About this home

Welcome to 22 Harstrom Place, a beautifully reimagined Rowayton classic just a short stroll from the heart of downtown. Originally built in the 1920s and fully gut-renovated in 2022 and with a brand-new roof added in 2026, this home seamlessly blends timeless charm with modern comfort. Tucked away on a quiet dead-end road, the property offers rare in-town perks including a spacious yard and a two-car garage. Inside, natural light pours through every window, highlighting the thoughtful design and elevated finishes throughout. Enter through an airy foyer with an adjacent private office-ideal for today's work-from-home needs. The main level opens into a stunning kitchen and family room featuring high ceilings, a gas fireplace, and a seamless flow for everyday living. A full bath and a flexible 5th bedroom/guest suite on the first floor offer valuable versatility. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with two walk-in closets and a beautifully appointed en-suite bath. Perfectly located and completely updated, 22 Harstrom Place is an exceptional in-town Rowayton opportunity.
