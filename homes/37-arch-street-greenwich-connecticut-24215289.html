About this home

A rare opportunity in the heart of downtown Greenwich! Just two doors from Greenwich Avenue, this charming circa-1815 property offers exceptional versatility for investors, developers, owner-users, or those envisioning a spectacular in-town residence. Situated on approximately 0.19 acres with generous on-site parking and a detached two-car garage, the building features 2,372 SF above grade plus a 718 SF basement. Currently an income-producing office property, its highly flexible CGB zoning offers exciting possibilities for residential, professional office, retail, or mixed-use concepts. Enjoy immediate proximity to Greenwich Avenue's shops, restaurants, and Metro-North. An exceptional location with endless potential! All uses and conversions subject to Town approvals.
