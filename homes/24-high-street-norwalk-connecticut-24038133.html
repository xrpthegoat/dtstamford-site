About this home

24 High Street is a turnkey 1870's Classic Victorian (4) unit multifamily, consisting of one (1) three bedroom/two bath unit, (2) one bedroom/one bath units and (1) two bedroom one bath unit. The property features off street parking and a communal laundry facility. Tenants are responsible for their own electric expense, while heat and hot water are provided by a landlord paid, centrally metered, oil-fired boiler. (Top floor - 2 bedroom/1 bath has independent boiler in basement. Tenant can pay own heat and hot water for this unit.) Located in Norwalk's "Enterprise Zone" and "Central Business District". Easy access to Wall St. and Main St. vibrant shops and restaurants.
