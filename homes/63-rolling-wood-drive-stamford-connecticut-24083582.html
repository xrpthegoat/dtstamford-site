About this home

***HIGHEST & BEST: MONDAY (APR 7) @ 4P*** Craving that suburban neighborhood feel without sacrificing convenience? This is it - in the desirable Pepper Ridge section of Stamford. On a quiet street, this front-back split-level home offers a smart and flexible layout. Just minutes from both highways, trains to NYC, schools, shopping, and more. Off the foyer, the living room gets great natural light, with its bay window and custom built-ins. The vaulted family room with soaring ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace is the perfect heart of the home, plus the open-concept to the dining room makes it perfect for hosting large holiday gatherings. Off the dining room is a screened-in porch - just in time for spring! The eat-in kitchen features quartz countertops, a double oven, gas cooktop, great cabinet space, and another bay window bringing sunny vibes to your morning coffee. The office (or mudroom/playroom) is a flexible bonus space you can make your own. The common area also includes a powder room and direct access to the garage. Upstairs are 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, including a primary ensuite with multiple closets. All the bedrooms enjoy great natural light. Additional features include unfinished basement, central AC, natural gas, public water/sewer, and a generator. If you've been looking for the right one, you might've just found it.
