About this home

Experience luxury living at its finest at Park Tower, in the heart of vibrant downtown Stamford. This spacious penthouse unit features high ceilings and walls of windows that flood the space with natural light, creating an inviting and airy ambiance. The open concept floor plan is perfect for entertaining, seamlessly flowing from the gourmet kitchen, boasting Viking appliances, to the living room with sliders to the balcony with spectacular sunset views overlooking Mill River Park. Relish in the comfort and convenience of three bedrooms, each featuring its own en-suite bath for added privacy and luxury. Park Tower doesn't just offer luxurious living - it provides a lifestyle. As a resident, you will have access to an array of amenities, including a large community room, billiard room, movie theater, indoor pool, fitness center, and rooftop access with grills, seating and breathtaking views of Long Island Sound and Manhattan! No need for a car..enjoy easy access to dining, shopping and entertainment, all within walking distance, plus shuttle service to the Metro North station. Park Tower is the perfect place to call home for those seeking luxury living, and the ultimate urban retreat.
