About this home

SUMMER RENTAL: AVAILABLE JULY ONLY. Enjoy all that Westport provides this summer in this perfect FURNISHED 4-5BR home in one of Westport's most sought-after neighborhoods. This beautiful home is set back on the property with a babbling brook. An inviting front porch will have you sipping iced tea all summer long, watching the idyllic and vibrant neighborhood life go by. A short walk to downtown, with wonderful shops, restaurants, parks and amenities. And bike or a short drive to the beach! The sun-filled first floor has an open layout w/ an impeccably furnished living room w/fireplace, frame tv, large dining area. The chef kitchen with Wolfe, Bosche & Sub-zero appliances has a large island, butler pantry w/wine cooler making this home perfect for entertaining! A bright sunroom w/ entertainment center leads to a secluded fully fenced backyard complete with hot tub, fire-pit, grill, outdoor dining area, and outdoor shower. The 2nd level has 4BRs including the priamry BR w/king bed and a spa-like ensuite bath w/soaking tub & stall shower. The finsihed lower level has a lovely guest quarters w/queen bed, full bath, and a tv area. There is a large windowed office which over looks the backyard with 2 work stations & Fiber Optics cable. Exercise room w/a Peleton treadmill, weights, stretching mat. Washer and Dryer. Tenant responsibility for weekly cleaning cost.
