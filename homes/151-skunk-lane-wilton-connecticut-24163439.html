About this home

Set on 1.1 acres of beautifully landscaped, award-winning gardens, this exceptional country property blends history, scale, and effortless livability. With over 5,000 square feet, five bedrooms, and six baths, the home offers a rare, highly functional layout, including two-bedroom suites on the main level, ideal for guests, multigenerational living, or a flexible primary option. Originally dating to the late 1800s, the home has been thoughtfully expanded to preserve its character while creating a natural flow between expansive entertaining spaces and private retreats. Wide floorboards, exposed beams, and three fireplaces add warmth and authenticity, while a bright kitchen and family room anchor daily life. A fully equipped butler's pantry elevates hosting with ease. Outdoors, multiple stone patios open to sweeping lawns, mature trees, and vibrant, curated gardens, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle. Private, picturesque, and endlessly versatile, this property invites gathering, relaxation, and year-round enjoyment. A compelling opportunity for those seeking charm, space, and a home that truly stands apart.
