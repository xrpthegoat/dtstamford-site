About this home

Welcome to 29 Meadowpark Ave E -a charming 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom split-level home filled with natural light, offering 2,020 sq ft of comfortable living space in the desirable Turn of River neighborhood. The cozy living room on the main floor is complemented by beautiful hardwood floors that flow seamlessly into the dining area and kitchen, perfect for gatherings. The kitchen provides ample storage and features sliders that lead directly to the back deck, creating an ideal setup for indoor-outdoor living and entertaining. This thoughtfully designed home offers 1 bedroom with built-in shelves that can double as a home office, playroom or cozy guestroom and full bath. Upstairs, you'll find a full bathroom and two generously sized bedrooms. The lower level offers a cozy family room with new carpeting and additional flexible space for your needs. Conveniently located near parks, shopping and dining, this home is a must-see!
