About this home

Storybook charm meets effortless living in this beautifully maintained Colonial, ideally situated behind a classic white picket fence on a level quarter-acre lot along a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable lower Newfield. Warm and inviting throughout, the home features a gracious formal living room centered around a wood-burning fireplace and an updated kitchen with Caesarstone countertops and a gas range. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, where sliders open to an expansive two-tier deck overlooking the private backyard-an exceptional setting for outdoor dining and summer entertaining, complete with an outdoor TV and a dedicated natural gas line for the grill, so you'll never have to refill a propane tank again. An oversized family room provides the perfect place to relax and unwind, while upstairs you'll find three comfortable bedrooms and a spacious, beautifully updated full bath. A walk-up attic offers abundant storage, complemented by a full unfinished basement and massive detached two-car garage. Additional highlights include natural gas heat and an exceptionally convenient location just minutes from shopping, parks, houses of worship, major highways, the train station, and vibrant downtown Stamford. Charming, comfortable, and wonderfully located, this special home offers the perfect combination of character, outdoor living, and everyday convenience.
