About this home

Welcome to this captivating artist's retreat - a home that beautifully fuses historical charm with modern convenience. Originally a horse barn transformed in the 1950s; this elegant home exudes warmth with its barn wood finishes, exposed beams, and wide plank floors. This property is not in a flood zone and does not require flood insurance. Spanning 1,194 square feet, it offers two inviting bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms. The property is nestled on a private 1.7-acre lot, providing a serene escape with the convenience of close proximity to Wilton Center, Bradley Park, the Norwalk River Valley Trail and the commuter train. The modern amenities include central AC and a whole-house generator. Ensuring comfort and peace of mind. Outside, you will find a secluded patio ideal for outdoor dining. The shed is finished and has electricity.
