About this home

Historic Charm Meets Beach Community Living - 1249 Shippan Avenue, Stamford, CT Welcome to one of Shippan Point's most historic treasures, built in 1850 and beautifully blending classic New England charm with today's conveniences. This spacious 5-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home is set on a generous 0.27-acre lot and offers the rare opportunity to own a piece of Stamford history - all while enjoying the benefits of modern beachside living. Inside, you'll find timeless character with hardwood floors, original architectural details, and a flexible layout perfect for today's lifestyle. With natural gas, city water, and city sewer, comfort and efficiency are already in place. Step outside to your private backyard oasis, featuring an above-ground pool - ideal for summer fun and entertaining. The property is just a short walk to Cummings Park and West Beach, giving you easy access to sandy shores, walking trails, and waterfront activities. Commuters will love the convenience of being just minutes from the Stamford train station and I-95, while local favorites like Brennan's by the Beach offer the perfect spot to unwind and dine close to home. Don't miss your chance to own a truly special home in one of Stamford's most sought-after coastal communities. 1249 Shippan Avenue is more than just a home - it's a lifestyle.
