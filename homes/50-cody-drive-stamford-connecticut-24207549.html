About this home

Welcome to 50 Cody Drive in Stamford's PepperRidge neighborhood of Newfield, a masterfully renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom residence offering the pinnacle of luxury and functional modern design. From the moment you step inside, you are greeted by rich hardwood floors and an abundance of natural light highlighting the expansive open-concept living and dining spaces. The gourmet kitchen serves as the heart of the home, beautifully equipped with sleek quartz countertops, premium stainless steel appliances, and custom cabinetry designed for effortless meal prep and entertaining. Primary suite with walk-in closet and private bath together with 2 additional bedrooms and hall bath make the perfect second floor retreat. Adding exceptional versatility to the layout, the first floor features a full bathroom and a spacious bedroom, making it the perfect setup for a guest suite, multi-generational family living, or a private home office. Step outside to your private outdoor sanctuary, where a patio overlooks a fully fenced-in rear yard that provides a secure, peaceful setting for dining, relaxing, and outdoor recreation. Not to mention, the gas grill hook for easy grilling. Positioned near Stamford's premier shopping, dining, and commuter routes, this turnkey home offers pristine designer finishes and modern convenience from top to bottom. Centrally located between I-95 and Merritt Parkway. Both Springdale and Glenbrook train stations are about equal distance.
