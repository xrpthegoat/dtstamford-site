About this home

This vintage North Stamford classic Cape exudes refinement and offers a relaxed retreat. A welcoming entry foyer leads to a delightful great room with built ins, beams, skylights, fireplace and a bay window that draws the outdoors in. The main floor primary bedroom includes a luxury full bath with radiant heated floors and multiple outfitted closets. The kitchen, dining room and den guide you easily out to an oversized bluestone patio framed by a masonry knee wall and the private, level verdant rear grounds beyond. Two second floor bedrooms share a full bath with radiant heated floors. The partially finished lower level is outfitted for more storage than you may ever need. A tranquil piece of paradise set amongst homes of distinctive character.
