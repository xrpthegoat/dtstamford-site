About this home

This Charming 4 bed, 2 bath Cape Cod home located in a desirable Cove neighborhood has been owned and lovingly cared for by its original owner for over 70 years and is now ready for new owners to call it home and make new memories. Features include, hardwood floors, central A/C, newer roof and hot water heater and upgraded electric. Range, hw heater and dryer are natural gas. In addition there is a walk-out basement and attached 1-car garage. This home is within close proximity to parks, schools, restaurants, and so much more. Its prime location offers easy commuting access to the train station and I-95. This home must be seen to be appreciated!
