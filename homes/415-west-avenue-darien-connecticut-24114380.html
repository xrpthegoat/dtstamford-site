About this home

Freshly priced to sell, this Darien home offers timeless style and comfort in one of the area's most desirable neighborhoods. With a charming brook alongside the property, enjoy added privacy and no neighbor on that side. It is your own peaceful retreat at a new, incredible value. With its exciting new price, there has never been a better time to make your move. This classic 1927 colonial-style home occupies a lush 0.30acre lot, offering privacy and a beautiful outdoor setting. With 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths across approximately 1,900 sqft, it features original character, including a wood-burning fireplace and a layout ripe for entertaining. Upstairs, three bright bedrooms rest under high ceilings. Downstairs offers a spacious living room, family room, sun room and formal dining room flow into a kitchen along with half bath. Additional rooms include a partially finished attic and basement perfect for storage, office or hobby spaces. Outside, the mature grounds and detached garage complete the picture. Located just minutes from the train station (48 min to Midtown), Darien Schools and local parks. Value-packed opportunity.
