About this home

A Westover Masterpiece of Luxury, Comfort & Style. Set in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, this exceptional residence offers a rare combination of timeless elegance, sophisticated updates, and effortless everyday living. Once owned by renowned basketball player and coach Doc Rivers, the home has been thoughtfully enhanced while retaining its grand architectural character. From the moment you enter the dramatic two-story foyer, the sweeping staircase and stunning crystal chandelier make an unforgettable impression. The chandelier is thoughtfully mounted on a lift, allowing it to be conveniently lowered for cleaning: a perfect example of the home's attention to both beauty and practicality. The recently remodeled kitchen is elegant yet inviting, with three skylights, top-of-the-line appliances, and an open connection to the comfortable family room. A stone fireplace anchors the family room, while expansive views extend to the large deck and patio and the beautiful, heated in-ground gunite pool. Re-plastered in 2025 and equipped with an automatic cover, it creates an exceptional private setting for summer fun. You will love entertaining guests on the expansive deck and patio.
