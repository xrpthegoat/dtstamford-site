About this home

Welcome to 28 Marlborough Road, a spacious & beautifully refreshed Colonial in Norwalk's desirable Wolfpit neighborhood. Set on a half-acre lot, this 3,651-square-foot home offers exceptional space & flexibility with five upper-level bedrooms, 4 1/2 baths, an in-ground pool, and a finished lower level with a private entrance. A welcoming foyer opens to generous living and entertaining spaces with refinished hardwood floors and neutral paint throughout. The refreshed kitchen features freshly painted white cabinetry and elegant marble countertops. The spacious primary suite includes a completely renovated bath with a walk-in shower and separate soaking tub, while four additional bedrooms provide flexibility for family & guests. The finished lower level offers a private guest suite and full bath with a separate entrance-ideal for extended family, overnight guests, an au pair, or a future in-law setup. Outside, enjoy a private backyard retreat with a natural gas firepit, in-ground pool with a brand-new vinyl liner, new fencing, and a newly repaved driveway. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, groceries and entertainment along Westport Avenue and Connecticut Avenue, with easy access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Parks, beaches, and vibrant South Norwalk are also within easy reach. Offering generous living space, flexible accommodations, extensive updates, and a resort-like backyard, 28 Marlborough Road is a special opportunity in a sought-after Norwalk neighborhood.
