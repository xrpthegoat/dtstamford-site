About this home

Expect to be WOWED, by the expert craftsmanship and attention to detail that is evident in every room, from the minute you walk in the front door. Renovated from top to bottom, making every day living feel like luxury, you will find the Chef's Kitchen boasting a Breakfast nook, THOR appliances, granite countertops & heated, tile floors. The LR & DR combination are perfect for large gatherings or intimate, family time. Five, generous BR's includes the Primary, En-Suite with LED mirror/medicine cabinets, heated, tile flooring and Quartz countertops. A first floor 5th Bedroom offers flexibility - perfect for guests/Multi-Generational Living Quarters/In-Law or Home Office. And for life's little moments, a Mud Room keeps things tidy. The pristine, full bath completes the main living area. Walk-out, unfinished lower level is great for storage, or potential to finish for increased square footage. Additional Highlights: European Windows & Doors. TRANE HVAC w/3 zones. NAVIEN tankless water heater. HONEYWELL advanced electrode humidifier. All NEW electric and plumbing. Central Air. Be in time for Summer and enjoy all that this "waterfront" community has to offer!
