About this home

Welcome to this spacious 4-bedroom, 3-full-bath home set on a desirable corner lot with two driveways offering plenty of off-street parking. Inside, you'll find a comfortable and versatile layout featuring hardwood floors, a bright living room, dining area, and two bedrooms conveniently located on the main level. Upstairs are two additional generously sized bedrooms and a full bath. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility with a large rec room, kitchenette, full bath, separate entrance, and plenty of potential for guests or a variety of uses. Recent improvements include a brand-new natural gas heating system with on-demand hot water installed this year, along with a pool heater approximately four years old. The home also includes two dishwashers, two refrigerator/freezer combos, two washers and dryers, and a gas range upstairs. Outside, the home really shines with a rear deck, patio, in-ground pool, storage shed, and plenty of space to enjoy the warmer months. Whether hosting summer get-togethers, relaxing by the pool, or simply enjoying the extra room, this home offers a flexible layout with plenty of room to make it your own.
