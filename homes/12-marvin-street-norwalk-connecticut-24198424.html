About this home

Welcome to 12 Marvin Street, a beautifully renovated golf course retreat just steps from the beach! This move-in ready home has been thoughtfully updated throughout with modern finishes, offering the perfect blend of comfort and style. Cozy up by one of two fireplaces on chilly nights, and enjoy year-round comfort with central air conditioning. The main level primary suite provides ideal single-floor living, complete with a spacious layout designed for convenience and privacy. Step outside to your own private oasis - a custom deck with hot tub overlooking tranquil golf course views, perfect for entertaining or unwinding after a long day. The huge unfinished basement offers incredible potential, ready to be transformed into a home gym, media room, playroom, or extra living space to fit your lifestyle. Location doesn't get better than this: golf course frontage right out front, and the beach just a short walk away. Enjoy easy access to downtown Norwalk's shops, restaurants, and commuter routes, all while coming home to a peaceful, private setting. Turnkey, updated, and ready for its next owner - schedule your private showing of 12 Marvin Street today!
