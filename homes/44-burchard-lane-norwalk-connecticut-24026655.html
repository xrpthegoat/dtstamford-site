About this home

Delightful Rowayton Ranch at 44 Burchard Lane. The home has been tastefully renovated to feature 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. With vaulted ceilings and plenty of natural light, the kitchen is a highlight, offering a spacious center island ideal for gatherings. There's also a charming ship-lapped eat-in area for more intimate dining experiences, and a cozy fireplace to snuggle up by. Additionally, the lower level has been newly finished, including a full bath and access to a large, flat backyard adorned with mature plantings.
