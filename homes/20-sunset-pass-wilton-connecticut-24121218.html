About this home

**Highest & Best due today, 9/5 by 5:00 PM. Welcome Home to 20 Sunset Pass, Wilton, CT. Tucked away on a quiet, private street in the heart of Georgetown, this charming 2-bedroom, 1-bath home offers a perfect blend of comfort, character, and convenience. Whether you're just starting out or downsizing, this thoughtfully designed space offers a low-maintenance lifestyle without sacrificing warmth or style. Step inside to a sun-filled living room featuring a wood-burning fireplace and built-in bookshelves-the perfect setting for cozy evenings, reading, or displaying your favorite decor. The adjoining kitchen and dining area provide ample cabinet storage and functionality, while a full bath and a separate office space offer flexibility for remote work, hobbies, or relaxation. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms feature generous closet space and wide windows that bring in natural light throughout the day.Outside, you'll discover a peaceful garden retreat accented with mature perennials and a cozy fire pit-ideal for quiet evenings or casual outdoor entertaining. A barn/covered parking and additional shed offer bonus storage, rounding out the practicality of this well-maintained home. Set on a quiet street with a strong sense of privacy, 20 Sunset Pass is just minutes from the Metro-North commuter rail to NYC and conveniently located between I-95 and I-84, making travel in any direction a breeze.
