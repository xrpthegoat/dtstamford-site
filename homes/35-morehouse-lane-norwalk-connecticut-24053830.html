About this home

Perfection Plus...This 4 to 5 bedroom, 2.5 bath center hall Colonial home is long acclaimed for its timeless appeal. Corner lot, perfectly located on a cul-de-sac in a popular yet quiet West Norwalk neighborhood of picturesque homes. This home offers flexible living & dining rooms; current owners utilize the very spacious living room as the dining area; revert or adapt the spaces to your lifestyle needs! A centrally located family room with wood burning fireplace leads to the updated eat-in kitchen w granite countertops, plenty of cabinet storage space & a large pantry. Sliders lead from the kitchen to the exterior grounds; the backyard is an absolute oasis! Private, fully fenced, professionally landscaped & even has an adorable koi pond. Entertain family & friends on the spacious deck & patio space. A powder room + an updated mudroom w recessed lighting completes the main level. The upper level offers a sizable primary ensuite w shower/tub combo & a large walk-in closet. 3 additional, generously sized bedrooms + a full hall bathroom complete the upper level. Lower level provides recreation space & a bonus room that can be utilized as a 5th bedroom or home office. Central Air, Propane Gas heating & City Water. Circular driveway w 2 car attached garage. Located on the Darien/New Canaan border, under an hour train ride to NYC & equidistant to I-95 & Merritt Pkwy. Enjoy Calf Pasture Beach overlooking the LI Sound, fine dining & shopping in Norwalk, Darien or New Canaan!
