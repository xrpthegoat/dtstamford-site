About this home

Magnificent Westport, CT estate, recently renovated to perfection, boasts 6 bedrooms, 6.5 bathrooms, and 5,147 sqft of living space in the main house along with a charming 2-bedroom, 1.5 bathroom guest cottage, all found on a sprawling 2.53 acre lot. Approach the property via a private driveway, flanked by stone walls and lush gardens, leading to a stunning residence surrounded by mature trees, a blue stone patio, heated gunite pool, and expansive lawns. Step into the grand two-story foyer of the main house, flooded with natural light and exuding opulence. The chefs eat-in kitchen, featuring a central island and beverage center, seamlessly connects to the family room with a fireplace and French doors opening to the patio and pool area, creating a sense of spaciousness. The main level also includes a formal dining room, butler's pantry, sophisticated library with wet bar, office with private entrance, guest suite, she-nook, well-equipped mudroom/laundry room, and a full bath with pool access. A nanny/guest suite, accessible from a back staircase, offers versatility as a playroom, gym, or theater. Upstairs, the primary suite impresses with a private sitting room, expansive dressing room/WIC, and a luxurious primary bathroom featuring a steam shower, soaking tub, and double vanity. Three additional spacious bedrooms and two full bathrooms complete the second floor. Crafted by Phoenix REID Luxury Home & SLS Construction, with support from a 2024 award winning design team..
