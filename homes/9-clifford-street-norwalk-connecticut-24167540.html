About this home

Welcome to Rowayton! Ideally located just a short walk to the village center, train station, and local amenities, this home offers the perfect blend of charm, convenience, and thoughtful updates. Recent improvements include a new roof, new hardwood floors, and a beautifully finished mudroom. The open-concept floor plan is perfect for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. This 3-bedroom, 3-bath home features a spacious main-level family room and a walk-out lower level that leads to a fully fenced backyard-complete with a back porch off of the kitchen and a gravel firepit area for outdoor enjoyment. Don't miss the opportunity to live in this idyllic neighborhood and enjoy all that Rowayton has to offer.
