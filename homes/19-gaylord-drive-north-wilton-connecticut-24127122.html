About this home

Charming Colonial in Prime Wilton Location! Welcome to 19 Gaylord Dr N, a beautifully maintained and spacious Colonial off route 7 on a dead end road, tree-lined street in the heart of Wilton. This warm and inviting home features 3 generously sized bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and three full levels of living space, offering flexible room for every lifestyle. Enjoy classic colonial charm with a modern layout, highlighted by hardwood floors, and thoughtful updates throughout. The bright and open kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining and living areas-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs, the oversized primary suite offers privacy and comfort with an en-suite bath and ample closet space. Step outside to find four distinct outdoor hangout spots-a cozy deck, two patios perfect for dining al fresco, and a charming gazebo for relaxing or hosting guests. The beautifully landscaped yard is a true retreat. The landlord will maintain the garden unless renters wish to personalize it-she simply asks that her seasonal crops are cared for. Located just minutes from top-rated schools, town amenities, and train stations, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, charm, and convenience. Don't miss the opportunity to make this special Wilton property your home! Home was fully renovated in 2005.
