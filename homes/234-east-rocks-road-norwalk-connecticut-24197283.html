About this home

Location Location Location! This spacious warm 3 bedroom 2 bath ranch is located in the Cranbury area of Norwalk near the Wilton Line and sits majestically on a lovely 1+ acre setting that offers both privacy and convenience to transportation, shopping, restaurants, parks, and beaches. The easy one floor living features a large living room with fireplace, dining room with French doors to the outdoor patio and expansive backyard, and country kitchen. The private sleeping quarters include the primary bedroom with full bath and 2 additional bedrooms and bath. The spacious backyard allows for easy entertaining and the lower level is finished to accommodate exercise room or playroom. Additional features include custom exterior stonework, Hardwood floors, and 2 car tandem garage. Enjoy the convenience of this home ready for your personal touch and located 5 minutes From the Norwalk/Wilton shopping, restaurants, Train, parks/beach, and I-95/Merritt Parkway.
