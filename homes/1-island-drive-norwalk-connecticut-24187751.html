About this home

Resort-style waterfront living awaits at prestigious Charles Cove. This beautifully RENOVATED 2-bedroom, 3-bath townhouse offers 2,032 square feet of elegant living with soaring cathedral ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors throughout, and an open floor plan that perfectly blends comfort and sophistication. The dramatic Great Room is the centerpiece of the home, featuring a stunning wood-burning fireplace, vaulted ceilings, and access to a private deck-ideal for morning coffee or evening entertaining. The renovated open-concept kitchen flows effortlessly into the living and dining areas, creating a bright and inviting space for everyday living. The sought-after first-floor primary suite offers exceptional convenience and privacy, complete with its own private deck, creating a peaceful retreat. A spacious second bedroom with an en-suite bath provides the perfect accommodations for guests or a home office. An attached garage and generous storage add to the home's appeal. Enjoy the exceptional lifestyle that makes Charles Cove one of Fairfield County's premier waterfront communities. Residents have access to a heated waterfront pool, tennis and pickleball courts, marina, scenic waterfront boardwalk, and beautifully maintained grounds. Just minutes from vibrant SoNo, Calf Pasture Beach, Metro-North, I-95, restaurants, shopping, and everything the Connecticut shoreline has to offer. Avail unfurnished/furnished for 1 year lease at $7,950 per month. Rarely does an opportunity arise.
