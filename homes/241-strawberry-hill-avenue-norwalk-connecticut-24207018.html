About this home

Welcome to this terrific cape style home in a very convenient location! The entry portico leads to the bright living room with hardwood floors and adjacent open concept kitchen. There are French doors from the kitchen out to the back deck, perfect for outdoor dining! There are two bedrooms, each with hardwood floors and a full bathroom on this level as well. Upstairs is a newly carpeted primary bedroom space. The lower level offers a newly carpeted playroom area with wet bar. There is additional storage in the basement boiler room, as well as the washer and dryer. This leads to the under house, 1 car garage. Outside is a terrific level back yard! Interior freshly painted! This is an estate sale, home to be sold in "as-is" condition. A great place to make your next home!
