About this home

Enjoy the ease of in-town living at its finest. Ideally located just moments from restaurants, shops, library, farmer's market, movie theater, the Metro-North train and local churches. This home offers exceptional convenience with no car required. Set on a quiet cul-de-sac, the property combines peaceful surroundings with close proximity to all the amenities of town. A rare opportunity, this legal two-family residence offers outstanding flexibility, live in one unit while generating rental income from the other, or rent both units. Perfect for commuters or those seeking a weekend retreat, the home also features a sunny backyard, ideal for gardening, relaxing, or entertaining. Thoughtfully updated, the main house includes a new washer and dryer (2026), new dishwasher and hood (2025), a perimeter fence (2023), a mini-split heating and cooling system (2023), a roof installed in 2021, and a whole house water filtration system in place. The apartment has also been well maintained with a new stove (2026), new dishwasher (2025), and washer and dryer (2023). The property is serviced by public sewer and town water. Properties with this combination of location, versatility, and charm rarely come to market. Come experience all the possibilities this exceptional home has to offer.
