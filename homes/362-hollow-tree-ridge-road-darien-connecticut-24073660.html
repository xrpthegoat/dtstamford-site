About this home

Oversize 5+ acre lot with charming home in 2 acre zone. Home features a 1st floor primary suite and adjacent solarium. Gracious living room with large fireplace, home office with fireplace, formal dining room and step down sun room/family room opens to the terrace and swimming pool. Mud room with laundry and powder room as well as rear entrance from the pool sits adjacent to the cooks kitchen. Upstairs are 3 additional bedrooms and hall bath. The adjacent 2 level barn can be finished to complement the home as you wish. A party barn, artist studio, fitness area could nicely fit.
