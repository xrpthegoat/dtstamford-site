About this home

Welcome to 82 Old Mill Road, the perfect balance of privacy and convenience, along with the luxury of an inground pool surrounded by bucolic landscaping. This well-maintained and spacious 3 bed, 2 1/2 bath home is perfectly situated on a secluded acre just steps from Georgetown's restaurants and shops. With almost 2,400 square feet of move-in ready living space, there is room for all. A light-filled and updated eat-in kitchen features new appliances and sliders to the generous backyard patio. Find a welcoming living room with a wood-burning fireplace and picture window, and formal dining room with built-ins. A convenient bedroom and full bath complete the first floor. Upstairs find a generous, light-filled primary bedroom with a private bathroom, and another spacious bedroom, both with ample storage. The finished basement provides even more living space with a large recreation room, wet bar, and half bathroom-perfect for movie nights, watching sports, or entertaining friends. Your outdoor oasis awaits with beautiful mature landscaping, large patio and heated gunite pool, creating a private setting for relaxing and gathering. Find more storage above the attached 2-car garage that features epoxy flooring and entrance to the sunny mudroom. Set along a private road, this opportunity to feel away from it all while close to both Georgetown and Wilton Center, is not to be missed!
