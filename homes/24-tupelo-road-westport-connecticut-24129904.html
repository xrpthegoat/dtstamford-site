About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac, this private oasis is truly move-in ready! Set on over an acre of beautifully landscaped grounds, this stunningly updated home offers 4 spacious bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms. The open-concept layout features soaring vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors, and three fireplaces that add warmth and character throughout. The fabulous, finished walk-out lower level includes a full bath, wet bar, rec room, bedroom and cozy fireplace-perfect for entertaining or relaxed everyday living. Step outside to a generous stone patio that overlooks a heated gunite pool, creating your own backyard retreat. Don't miss this opportunity to make this picture-perfect home your own!
