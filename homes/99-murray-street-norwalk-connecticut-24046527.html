About this home

Welcome to your new home! This meticulously maintained Cape Cod-style home seamlessly combines charm with convenience. Situated on a professionally landscaped .68-acre lot, this move-in-ready home features 4 spacious bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms. The inviting front porch and expansive deck with a private fenced-in backyard provide an ideal setting for both relaxation and entertaining. Inside, you'll find a spacious living room with a fireplace, eat-in kitchen, pantry, stainless steel appliances, and hardwood floors throughout. Additional highlights include a mudroom, central air conditioning, and a 2-car attached garage with convenient attic storage. The partially finished and heated basement provides flexible space that can serve as a family room, playroom, exercise area, or office, while ample closet space and storage options ensure that your home remains organized and clutter-free. Perfectly positioned on the border of both Westport and Norwalk, you'll enjoy the best of both worlds. Explore the vibrant downtown areas with award-winning restaurants, live music venues, shopping, and scenic waterfront activities all just minutes from your doorstep. This property provides a serene retreat without sacrificing the proximity to local attractions. Enjoy the convenience of being close to both I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, with the Westport train station only a few miles away-offering a less than 90-minute commute to NYC. Don't miss out on this exceptional opportunity!
