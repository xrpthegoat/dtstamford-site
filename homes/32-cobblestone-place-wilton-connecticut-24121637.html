About this home

Step into this reimagined colonial, effectively built in 2025, where timeless design meets modern living. Sited on two private acres at the corner of a quiet lane, the home enjoys an open backyard, exceptional privacy, and an abundance of natural light throughout. The first floor is defined by hardwood floors and expansive windows that open to a chef's kitchen appointed with a GE Cafe 48" refrigerator and a professional 48" Thermador range. A formal dining room and powder room add both function and elegance to the main level, creating a space equally suited for daily living and entertaining. Upstairs, four bedrooms include a serene primary suite with a newly finished en suite bath, designed with clean lines and contemporary fixtures. Every major element of this home has been fully renewed: a new roof with 30-year Timberline shingles, Hardie Board siding, updated 200-amp electrical service, and a complete HVAC system featuring dual air handlers and compressors. Additional enhancements include new garage doors, a modern Hunter irrigation system, a new well pump, and a newly installed septic system-ensuring both convenience and peace of mind. More than a renovation, this is a complete reinvention of a classic colonial, offering the craftsmanship and security of new construction in a timeless design, along with a rare opportunity to own a home that truly lives like new on two acres.
