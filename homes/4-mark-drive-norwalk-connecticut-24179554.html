About this home

Welcome to this charming 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home with attached garage located in Norwalk's desirable Wolfpit neighborhood. Situated on a quiet street with a perfect blend of comfort and functionality, this modern home features beautiful hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and a spacious eat-in kitchen ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The large finished basement provides a home office and valuable additional living space, perfect for a family room or recreation area. Step outside to an expansive deck overlooking the backyard and pool, creating the perfect setting for summer gatherings, outdoor dining, and relaxation. Surrounded by mature trees and established plantings, the property offers privacy and a serene natural setting. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping, dining, parks, and major commuting routes, with easy access to both Norwalk and Westport amenities, this home combines neighborhood charm with exceptional convenience.
