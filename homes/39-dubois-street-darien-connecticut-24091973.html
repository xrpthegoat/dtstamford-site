About this home

Move right into this beautifully renovated three-bedroom Colonial nestled in sought-after neighborhood. Conveniently located near Hindley School, trains, beaches and town amenities. This well-maintained home features a sun-filled living room with fireplace which opens to a stunning kitchen and dining area perfect for entertaining. Sunroom offers additional space ideal for an office, playroom or sitting area. Three bedrooms on upper level with sizable, modern bathroom. Inviting patio overlooks professionally landscaped backyard with detached two-car garage. Unfinished walk-up attic and basement (additional 600 sq ft) provide great options for expansion and storage. Excellent opportunity to get into Darien in a superb neighborhood!
