About this home

Nestled on 1.12 acres in sought-after South Wilton, this spacious Colonial offers an ideal blend of space, light, and livability. A welcoming entry opens to sun-filled living spaces accented by hardwood floors throughout. The formal dining room provides a sophisticated setting for gatherings, while the eat-in kitchen, appointed with stainless steel appliances and generous counter space, flows seamlessly into the inviting family room. Here, a wall of windows frames the landscaped grounds and a gas fireplace creates a cozy focal point for everyday living. Upstairs, the Primary Suite offers a private retreat with its own gas fireplace, full bath, and walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms, a dedicated home office, and a spacious media/rec room with cathedral ceiling and fireplace provide exceptional versatility-perfect for entertaining. The beautifully landscaped property backs up to conservation land, ensuring privacy, scenic views, and a tranquil natural backdrop year-round. Additional amenities such as city water, a whole-house generator, and a full irrigation system enhance both comfort and convenience.
