About this home

Welcome to your next chapter in the heart of the Newfield neighborhood-a charming Cape Cod-style home offering both character and comfort. With 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms spread over 2,676 square feet, there's plenty of space to live, relax, and enjoy. As you enter, warm hardwood floors lead you through a welcoming main level with 9 foot ceilings and a cozy wood-burning fireplace-perfect for unwinding after a busy day. The oversized sun porch is a true highlight, ideal for weekend brunches, peaceful mornings with coffee, or showing off your favorite potted plants. Downstairs, the fully finished lower level offers abundant possibilities: turn it into a recreation space, home gym, or even a hideaway for that drum set someone swore they'd play quietly. Outside, enjoy a spacious lawn for cookouts or carefree afternoons, plus a large patio perfect for friendly gatherings. Minutes away, you'll find convenient shopping options including Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, and Grade A Market, so you can skip the pantry scramble and stock up with ease. Plus, the Bulls Head area and Downtown Stamford are nearby for dining and entertainment. Thoughtfully designed and ideally located, this home offers the kind of everyday comfort that just feels right. Whether you're looking to grow, entertain, or simply enjoy a bit more breathing room, this Newfield gem delivers. Come see why life's better under this roof.
