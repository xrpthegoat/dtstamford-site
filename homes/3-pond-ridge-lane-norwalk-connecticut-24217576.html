About this home

Tucked along an in-town cul-de-sac, 3 Pond Ridge Lane offers light-filled living, inviting outdoor spaces, and the charm of Rowayton's coastal village lifestyle. An open main level connects an eat-in kitchen with a spacious family room, creating a natural gathering place for everyday living and entertaining. A separate dining room and living room with a fireplace provide additional space to host or unwind. Don't miss the reading room, a perfect place to cozy up with your favorite book. Step outside to a newly redone oversized, west-facing deck overlooking the backyard an inviting setting for outdoor dining and relaxed afternoons. Upstairs, the bedrooms are complemented by combination laundry and an office with beautiful built-ins. The finished walk-out lower level adds exceptional flexibility with a separate entrance, full bathroom, generous recreation space, and storage. Currently used as an Airbnb without a kitchen, this space offers the option to continue its current use, or create a wonderful private retreat for guests, in-laws suite or an Au-pair. With village shops, restaurants and so close to everyone's favorite Rowayton Seafood, Bayley Beach, this home brings together comfort, versatility, and easy access to the best of Rowayton. Can also be purchased close to fully furnished. Come see for yourself.
