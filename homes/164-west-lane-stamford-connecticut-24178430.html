About this home

Stunning Center Hall sun-filled Colonial tucked away in the coveted Revonah Woods neighborhood and perched atop 1.27 acres of pure tranquility. The grand 2-story entryway with a sweeping staircase draws you in to the voluminous layout encompassing nearly 9,000 square feet of living space. Entertaining is effortless with large formal spaces including an elegant dining room and living room, both featuring soaring ceilings and dramatic fireplace focal points. The expansive Chef's Kitchen is clad with high end appliances as well as quartz countertops. The French doors and oversized windows off of the kitchen showcase the level backyard while opening up the home to even more light. Playroom/office, in-law suite and a large mudroom complete the main level. The divine primary suite boasts a large walk-in closet, full bathroom with an additional closet, and a bonus room perfect for a nursery, private office, reading room, or additional closet space. Large office, oversized laundry room equipped with a folding bench and sink, and 4 additional bedrooms each adjoined by Jack & Jill bathrooms complete the second floor. Fully finished walk-up attic is multi-purpose. Lower level features a Rec Room with Fireplace plus a Home Gym area! The patio and level backyard is a serene and private setting. Enjoy the Sonos Speakers and Smart lighting and 6 zone Nest thermostats. All TV's included. Amazing secluded location so close to town. Visit your dream home today.
