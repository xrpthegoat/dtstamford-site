About this home

Welcome to 26 Anson Road - a rare opportunity on nearly half an acre in the heart of Norwalk's coveted Silvermine neighborhood (06850). This 2,330 square foot single-level Ranch offers the kind of footprint, character, and lot you simply cannot find at this price point. Featuring hardwood floors throughout, a wood-burning fireplace, attached garage, enclosed porch, and generous open porch and these are just a few reasons for what makes it very unique/special. The layout offers tremendous flexibility for buyers seeking room to grow, with the existing footprint providing clear opportunity for reconfiguration . Whether you are an investor with vision or an owner-occupant ready to put your personal stamp on a home, the potential here speaks for itself - comparable renovated homes in this corridor are commanding well over $900,000. The 0.42 acre lot is the hidden gem - room to add a pool, expand the home, or enjoy outdoor living the way it was meant to be. Anson Road sits in one of Fairfield County's most beloved enclaves, minutes to the Silvermine Arts Center, GrayBarns on the Silvermine River, Silvermine Golf Club, New Canaan, Cranbury Park, the Merritt Parkway, downtown Norwalk, and SoNo. Top-rated schools and a quiet, family-friendly setting make this the kind of property today's buyers are searching for and rarely finding. Priced to sell - opportunities like this in Silvermine don't sit long.
