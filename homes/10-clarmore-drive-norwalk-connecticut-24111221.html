About this home

Welcome to this beautifully remodeled one-bedroom condominium, located in a well-maintained complex in the heart of Norwalk. The spacious living room features brand-new flooring, recessed lighting, and a decorative fireplace that adds warmth and charm. The adjacent dining area, also updated with new flooring, provides a dedicated space for meals and entertaining. The kitchen has been thoughtfully upgraded with modern finishes, including a new stove and refrigerator. The refreshed bathroom includes a convenient in-unit washer/dryer unit. Heat and hot water are included in the rent. Two unassigned parking tags are provided. Ideally situated just minutes from the vibrant Historic South Norwalk (SoNo) district and the growing Wall Street area, you'll enjoy easy access to restaurants, shops, nightlife, The SoNo Collection mall, the Maritime Aquarium, museums, movie theater, ice skating, the SoNo Sports facility, trampoline park, and more! Across from Norwalk hospital, Commuters will appreciate the proximity to the South Norwalk Metro-North station (approx. 1.5 miles), with additional access to the Rowayton and Darien stations. Also convenient to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, with Calf Pasture Beach and Cranbury Park just a short drive away-both free for Norwalk residents with valid Norwalk vehicle registration. Approximately 45 miles to Midtown Manhattan. Good credit required.
