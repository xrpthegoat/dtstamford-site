About this home

Thoughtfully constructed 2BR, 3BA condo offering 1,428 sf of low-maintenance living with 9' ceilings in the desirable Cos Cob neighborhood of Greenwich, CT. The well-appointed kitchen features stainless-steel appliances, stone countertops, breakfast bar and adjacent dining area. The inviting living room with gas fireplace opens to a balcony. Both bedrooms are en-suite; the Primary suite features a separate shower, whirlpool tub and balcony. Fabulous custom closets provide exceptional storage, while 2nd-floor laundry adds everyday convenience. A true highlight is the expansive mahogany deck with built-in benches, ideal for dining, entertaining or simply enjoying the outdoors. Less than 5 minutes to Cos Cob's shops, restaurants, waterfront, parks, schools and Metro-North train station.
