About this home

Welcome to 2 Pheasant Run, a meticulously maintained Cape-style residence set in a picturesque and peaceful setting that offers the perfect balance of privacy, comfort, and convenience. Designed for both relaxation and entertaining, the expansive wraparound deck overlooks a level backyard framed by mature trees, creating an inviting outdoor retreat. Inside, the thoughtfully designed main level features a bright white kitchen that opens seamlessly to a spacious family room with a fireplace, as well as a generously sized dining room ideal for gatherings. A separate music room and private office provide flexible spaces that can easily be customized to fit your lifestyle.The first-floor primary suite serves as a tranquil sanctuary, complete with sliding doors to the deck, dual closets, and a stunning recently renovated bath featuring double vanities and a dramatic glass-enclosed shower. Oversized windows throughout the home fill the interior with natural light. The upper level offers 3 additional bedrooms, a full bath, and 2 large walk-in attic spaces that provide exceptional storage. The finished lower level expands the living space with a large family room featuring a fireplace and bar area, a separate room ideal for a playroom or fitness space. There's also a laundry room & multiple unfinished storage areas. Offering a rare combination of space, privacy, and functionality, this exceptional home is conveniently located minutes from town, train, top-rated schools & highways.
