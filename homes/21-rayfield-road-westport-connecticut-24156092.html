About this home

This enchanting, in-town haven is perfectly positioned in the heart of Westport. The sleek, newly renovated eat-in kitchen opens seamlessly to a vaulted-ceiling family room, highlighted by walls of windows, a fabulous pantry, and direct access to a spacious bluestone patio. Designed for easy one-level living, the home also features a charming living room with fireplace and oversized windows, a serene primary bedroom and second bedroom-both with beautifully renovated en-suite baths-and a versatile third bedroom or office with a Murphy bed that tucks away when not in use. The gorgeous backyard patio provides an ideal setting for entertaining and outdoor gatherings.
