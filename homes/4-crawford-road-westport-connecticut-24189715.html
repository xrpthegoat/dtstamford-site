About this home

Privately nestled in Westport's coveted Old Hill neighborhood, this extraordinary five-bedroom New England residence offers a rare opportunity to own a home where timeless character & modern comfort come together beautifully. Thoughtfully updated while preserving its remarkable architectural details, the residence features sun-filled interiors, beautifully scaled rooms, & an inviting warmth throughout. Original elements reflect the home's storied past, while carefully integrated updates provide exceptional comfort for today's lifestyle. A standout feature is the distinctive pub room with its original wood bar - an unforgettable setting for intimate gatherings, celebrations, or quiet evenings at home. The versatile third level offers endless possibilities as a private guest retreat, second primary suite, or home office. Outside, mature specimen trees, flowering gardens, rolling lawns, and elegant stone terraces create a private retreat that evolves beautifully with the seasons. Completing the property is a charming barn with electricity, water, cable, & convenient parking, offering exceptional flexibility as a studio, workshop, recreation space, or a future vision uniquely tailored to the next owner. Just minutes from downtown Westport, award-winning schools, shopping, restaurants, beaches, & the train, this exceptional property presents a rare opportunity to become the next steward of one of Westport's most treasured homes. A truly special place to call home!
