About this home

A wonderful opportunity to own a beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 2 full bath Cape Cod-style home in the highly sought-after Wolfpit section of Norwalk! The first floor features a bright and spacious sun-filled kitchen that opens to a large family room, complete with French doors leading to an outdoor deck, perfect for indoor-outdoor living. Off the other side of the kitchen, a vaulted-ceiling living room adds to the home's open and airy feel, making it ideal for both entertaining and everyday life. Two well-appointed bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level. Upstairs offers two additional bedrooms and another full bathroom. The lower level includes plenty of storage space as well as an additional bedroom, office or playroom. Hardwood floors, central air-conditioning, attached garage and level lot! A short distance from waterfront restaurants, the upscale SoNo Collection shopping mall and Calf Pasture Beach, where you can enjoy, playgrounds, skateboard park, baseball fields, sand volleyball courts, fishing pier and more! Enjoy the best of both comfort and convenience, located just minutes from the Metro North train station, downtown Norwalk and the center of Westport, close to major highways and approximately 45 miles from NYC. Whether you are a first-time buyer or looking to downsize, this warm and inviting home offers the perfect setting to create lasting memories and make this well-cared for home, your own!
