About this home

Located in Shippan Point on coveted Sea Beach Drive, this exceptional home offers the rare combination of water views, a private deeded beach directly across the street, and the peace of mind of being outside the flood zone with no flood insurance required. The private beach, shared by just 19 homes, creates an extraordinary coastal lifestyle just steps from the front door. Inside, the home is spacious, bright and beautifully designed for both everyday living and entertaining. The expansive family room features a coffered ceiling, fireplace and walls of windows framing views toward the water, while the large eat in kitchen offers abundant cabinetry, an oversized center island and an open connection to the main living spaces. A gracious dining room, separate living room and dedicated home office provide additional space to gather, work or unwind. The impressive primary suite features beautiful water views, a vaulted ceiling with exposed beams, fireplace, two walk in closets and a spacious private bath. The finished lower level offers additional living space with recreation areas, a home gym and wine storage. Outside, a private backyard with pool, patio and gas fireplace creates an inviting setting for outdoor living and entertaining. A full home generator provides added peace of mind. Just five minutes from downtown Stamford and the train station, with express trains to Manhattan in 45 minutes, this is Connecticut coastal living at its finest.
