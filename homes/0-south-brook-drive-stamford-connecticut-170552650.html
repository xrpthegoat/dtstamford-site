About this home

This beautiful Modern Farmhouse is designed with both comfort and style in mind, for a lifestyle of LUXURY living! This "TO BE BUILT" 4BRs 3.5BAs home is located in a prestigious enclave of fine residences in North Stamford, on a quiet cul-de-sac. Nestled on 2 lush acres offering a bucolic natural setting and plenty of privacy. It features gorgeous living spaces with an array of luxury amenities to personalize your home. The first floor offers 10 ft ceilings, open-plan chef's kitchen w/butlers pantry, dining and family room provide ample space for entertaining guests and hosting gatherings, with easy access to the large deck and yard. 1st floor guest suite with full bath, office, powder room, mudroom & storage. This home offers everything you can think of to create lasting memories with family and friends.
