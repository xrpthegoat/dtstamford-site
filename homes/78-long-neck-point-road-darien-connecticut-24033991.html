About this home

Crafted w/the highest quality materials, this CARDELLO ARCHITECTS designed French colonial estate compliments desirable Long Neck Pt w/period-inspired curb appeal while delivering every luxury & convenience sought. Upon entering you are greeted by a dramatic sculptural staircase floating in front of sweeping views to the rear garden, swimming pool w/Goodwives River in background. The elegant LR is warmed by a wood burning FP, leads to a sophisticated home office or expansive covered porch. A 2nd fireplace divides the dining & family rooms while delivering comfort & a bold focal point. Featuring nearly floor-to-ceiling views, the state-of-the-art Boffi kitchen provides abundant work/entertaining areas & the top-of-the-line finishes you expect. Stepping outdoors; you will discover an outdoor kitchen, sprawling patio & gunite pool w/auto cover. The 2nd level boasts an exquisite primary suite featuring a water facing balcony, luxury bathroom, dressing room & 2nd walk-in closet. 4 add'l en-suite bedrooms, a guest suite & 500sf finished attic complete the upper floors. The det garage features a cottage space above, to compliment this unparalleled property. As a member of a 4 home HOA you will have waterfront access, kayak storage & endless fun at a shared 25' dock only steps away. This exemplary residence is the culmination of skillful design, execution & delivers a finished product that is externally commanding while meeting todays living demands for function, beauty & ease!
