About this home

Set on a private, beautifully landscaped two-acre property, 14 Powder Horn Hill Road offers exceptional space, privacy and convenience just minutes from Wilton town center, shopping and the train. A stunning front porch creates a memorable first impression and opens to a grand entry foyer with a sweeping staircase. Inside, rich hardwood floors, generous room sizes, high ceilings and thoughtful architectural details create an elegant yet comfortable atmosphere. The spacious chef's kitchen features a large center island, Sub-Zero refrigeration and abundant workspace, flowing seamlessly into the inviting family room with coffered ceilings, a fireplace and French doors leading to the deck. Formal living and dining rooms provide ideal entertaining spaces, while a private library offers the perfect home office or quiet retreat. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite features a fireplace, luxurious bath and two exceptionally large walk-in closets. Four additional bedrooms and an oversized playroom room with a full bath offer outstanding flexibility for guests, recreation or media. The walk-out lower level is a true blank canvas, featuring impressive 9-foot ceilings, plumbing already in place for a future bathroom and endless potential to create a gym, home theater, game room, guest suite or additional living space. Outside, the flat, fenced backyard offers an ideal setting for entertaining, play and pets, with ample room for a future pool.
