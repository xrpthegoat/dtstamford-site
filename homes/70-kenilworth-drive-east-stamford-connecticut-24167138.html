About this home

Located in Stamford's Cove neighborhood, this exceptional waterfront residence combines striking architecture, high-end construction, and sweeping water views from nearly every room. Set on a professionally landscaped half-acre, the home is designed to maximize its unique setting with walls of Andersen windows and sliders that seamlessly connect the interior living spaces to multiple decks and outdoor entertaining areas overlooking the water. The open concept kitchen and family room are centered around a marble-topped island and appointed with premium appliances including a Wolf gas range, Wolf convection oven, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Bosch dishwasher, wine chiller, and custom cabinetry. Exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail are evident throughout with gleaming hardwood floors, designer baths with radiant heated floors, retractable custom window treatments, and thoughtfully designed living spaces filled with natural light. The luxurious primary suite offers stunning water views, a waterfront balcony, and a spa-like bath featuring a soaking tub, steam shower, marble topped vanity, and radiant heated floors. Exterior features include Hardie Plank siding, stone facade, Trex decking, Azek trim, solar panels, outdoor shower and changing area, full house generator, deer fencing, and a three-car garage with electric vehicle charging outlets. Fully FEMA compliant and ideally positioned to capture the beauty of the waterfront from sunrise to sunset.
