About this home

Perfectly situated in one of Stamford's Beach enclaves, 80 Horton Street presents a rare opportunity to own a fully reimagined ranch-style residence. Every element of this three bedroom, two bath home has been thoughtfully renewed with top-quality craftsmanship, delivering a truly turnkey living experience. Inside, a bright & airy open-concept layout showcases refinished hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and elegant finishes throughout. The eat-in kitchen features new custom cabinetry, granite countertops, and premium stainless steel appliances, opening seamlessly to the main living area anchored by the wood burning fireplace. The home's two beautifully appointed bathrooms include a fully renovated main level bath & a newly created LL bath, both designed with modern fixtures & clean, sophisticated lines. The finished lower level offers versatility with a wet bar, laundry area, and abundant flex space ideal for a media room, home office, or guest suite. Behind the walls, every major system has been replaced. Including all new electrical and plumbing, high-efficiency HVAC, new roof, and spray foam insulation for optimal energy efficiency. Ensuring comfort and peace of mind for years to come. The full scope of improvements is far too extensive to list, this is a home that truly must be experienced in person to be appreciated. Minutes from Downtown Stamford's shops, dining & transit options, a short drive to Chelsea Piers, three beaches, and major commuting arteries.
