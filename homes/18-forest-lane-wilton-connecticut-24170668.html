About this home

Stunning Sun-Filled Colonial with Designer Barn. Set on 1.32 flat acres on the New Canaan border, this bright 4-bedroom Colonial offers over 4,000 square feet of sophisticated living space. Built in 1990, the home perfectly captures "old world" architectural charm-featuring reclaimed beams & timeless New England character-while offering the ease & peace of mind of modern construction. The main level features gracious living & dining rooms with fireplaces & an open-concept kitchen that flows into a family room overlooking the expansive backyard. A glass-lined breezeway connects the house to a spectacular renovated barn. Featured in *New England Home* magazine, this climate-controlled "Great Room" boasts soaring ceilings & sliding doors to a stone patio, creating a premier space for indoor-outdoor entertaining. The primary suite serves as a serene retreat with a renovated bath & dual walk-in closets. 2 additional bedrooms-each with fireplace details-share an updated bath alongside a dedicated home office. The finished walk-out lower level adds a recreation room, gym, & private guest suite. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac that ends at a sprawling nature preserve, the property offers total privacy while remaining incredibly convenient. Situated less than 10 minutes from the downtown centers of Wilton, New Canaan, Ridgefield, & Pound Ridge. This is a rare opportunity to own a distinctive home that balances historic aesthetic with effortless low-maintenance living.
