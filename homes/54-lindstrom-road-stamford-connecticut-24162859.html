About this home

Experience refined coastal living just moments from New York City. Welcome to Harbor View Court, ideally positioned at the gateway to prestigious Shippan Point-one of Stamford's most coveted waterfront enclaves. This townhouse spans over 1,500 sqft across three thoughtfully designed levels, offering 2 bedrooms, 1.5 baths, and an open-concept layout that exudes both elegance and comfort. Generously sized bedrooms feature ample closet space, while the finished lower level provides a versatile retreat, perfect for a sophisticated home office, media lounge, home gym, or private guest space. Step onto your patio and unwind with the gentle coastal breeze, or enjoy a short, scenic stroll to West Beach, one of Stamford's premier shoreline destinations. With Cummings Park, waterfront vistas, and outdoor recreation just moments away.
