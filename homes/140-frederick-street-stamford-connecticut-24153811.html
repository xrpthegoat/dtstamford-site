About this home

This charming Cape Cod-style home is ready to welcome its next owner! Enter through a dedicated entrance foyer with potential for a functional mudroom-perfect for keeping coats, shoes, and daily essentials organized. Featuring three bedrooms plus potential fourth (currently used as office) and two fully renovated bathrooms, this home blends classic character with modern updates. The updated kitchen flows seamlessly into the adjacent living room, creating a warm and inviting space ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Enjoy not one, but two covered patios-one on each level-offering exceptional indoor-outdoor flow. Start your mornings with coffee on the kitchen patio, and unwind in the evening with a glass of wine on the private second-floor patio just off the primary bedroom. The spacious basement offers tremendous potential for additional space, a home gym, or recreation area. Outside, you'll find a large flat yard, a gated driveway, and a private one-car garage-providing both privacy and convenience. Ideally located just blocks from Downtown Stamford, you'll have easy access to the Stamford train station, Chelsea Piers, Harbor Point, parks, beaches, dining, shopping, and more. An opportunity you won't want to miss!
