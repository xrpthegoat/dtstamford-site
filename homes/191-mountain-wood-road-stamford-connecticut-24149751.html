About this home

Privately set at the end of a quiet North Stamford cul-de-sac, this gracious brick Colonial offers timeless architecture, thoughtful updates, and exceptional indoor-outdoor flow on over an acre of level, park-like grounds. Sun-filled living spaces and a seamless connection to the outdoors create a peaceful retreat with room to breathe-just beyond the city. A welcoming foyer opens to a curved staircase flanked by formal living and dining rooms. The beautifully updated kitchen features a large island, generous cabinetry, and stainless steel appliances-including Thermador ovens and gas range, a Sub-Zero refrigerator, and a wine cooler-and opens to the family room with fireplace and access to the expansive deck. Just off the garage entry, a functional mudroom with laundry space leads to a flexible office or guest bedroom with a nearby full bath. Upstairs, the primary suite offers an exceptionally large walk-in closet and a spacious en suite bath with dual vanities and soaking tub. Three additional bedrooms and a hall bath with double sinks complete the second level. The finished lower level provides generous recreation space and ample storage. The private backyard, complete with a large deck and flagstone patio, enjoys southern exposure and is ideal for outdoor dining or relaxation. Additional highlights include natural gas, city water, and an automatic generator. Conveniently located near neighborhood amenities while offering the peace and privacy North Stamford is known for!
