About this home

Perched on the top floor of Harbour Heights at 4 Lowe Street in Norwalk, Unit 401 offers an exceptional blend of comfort and convenience. This bright and airy two-bedroom, two-bathroom corner unit boasts an open layout with generous living space and abundant natural light. Enjoy views of Long Island Sound from your private balcony, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The updated kitchen features modern appliances, while the in-unit laundry room adds everyday convenience. Ample closet space throughout ensures plenty of storage. Residents benefit from garage parking, central air conditioning, and energy-efficient systems in this well-maintained elevator building. Ideally situated near The SoNo Collection, Metro-North, I-95, and an array of top-rated dining and entertainment options, this home offers the perfect balance of tranquility and accessibility.
