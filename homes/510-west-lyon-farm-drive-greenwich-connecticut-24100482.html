About this home

Enjoy the best of West Lyon Farm living in this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 3-bath home offering the ideal blend of comfort, space, and privacy. A rare three-season enclosed porch opens to a lush, landscaped yard-your own peaceful retreat off of the spacious dining room. Inside, cathedral ceilings and hardwood floors throughout the living space create an airy, elegant flow, while a whole-house generator offers practical peace of mind. A den with wet bar on the first level could also be used as a fourth bedroom. Plenty of storage and workbench area in lower level. Well-positioned in a quiet section of the community, this home delivers a low-maintenance lifestyle -without sacrificing charm, space, or outdoor living space. West Lyon Farm amenities include pool, tennis and more.
