About this home

Elegant Colonial set on a private, beautifully landscaped acre in North Stamford. Built in 2005, this 5 bedroom, 5.5 bath home offers timeless design, high ceilings, Brazilian hardwood floors, and detailed custom millwork throughout. A grand foyer leads to formal living and dining rooms, a private office, and a chef's kitchen with high-end appliances, opening to the family room with fireplace and stone patio-ideal for everyday living and entertaining. A main-level bedroom adds flexibility, and an elevator from the garage to the main level adds convenience. Extensively updated with full propane conversion, three new AC systems, new boiler, water system and tank, roof, driveway, and garage doors, plus radon mitigation, fresh interior paint, updated powder room, and a fully fenced backyard. Kitchen features new Danby marble countertops, backsplash, dishwasher, two sinks, garbage disposal, and updated faucets, hardware, and lighting, with matching Danby marble countertop, sink and hardware in the butler's pantry. Additional updates include new dryer and exterior lighting fixtures with timers. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a private retreat with fireplace and spa-like bath. A second suite features a walk-in closet and en suite bath, plus two additional en suite bedrooms. Walk-up attic offers expansion potential. Three-car garage with temperature control and hot/cold water connection. Convenient to High Ridge Rd, New Canaan, and Pound Ridge
