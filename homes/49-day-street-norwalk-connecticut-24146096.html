About this home

This spacious 1BR end unit has been freshly painted and features brand-new luxury vinyl waterproof flooring throughout. Upon entry, you're welcomed by the kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar that opens seamlessly into an expansive living and dining area. The space features soaring 9-foot ceilings and beautiful natural light, creating a bright, open, and inviting atmosphere ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The primary bedroom offers a large walk-in closet and sliding doors that lead directly to the private balcony/deck. The updated bathroom includes a newer vanity, mirror, and light fixture. Recent updates also include a new dishwasher, refrigerator, washer, dryer, and new air conditioning. Enjoy private views from the corner deck/balcony and an abundance of windows that flood the home with natural sunlight all day long. The unit comes with 2 unassigned parking spaces. This modern elevator building features secured entry and is ideally located within walking distance to SoNo, restaurants, the Maritime Ctr, Norwalk train station, and shopping. Building amenities include a common patio with picnic tables and a grill. Good credit a must!
