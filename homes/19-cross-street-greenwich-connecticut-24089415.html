About this home

Welcome to this impeccably maintained and charming 3-bedroom home in the heart of Pemberwick. The sunlit, renovated kitchen flows into a formal dining room and spacious living room with fireplace and bonus office/den. Sliders lead to a fully fenced, beautifully landscaped backyard-ideal for entertaining or play. Upstairs, the primary suite features a walk-in closet and spa-like bath with Jacuzzi. Two large bedrooms share a full hall bath. Located in a family-friendly neighborhood steps to Pemberwick Park, and minutes to Byram Park's beach, pool, and marina. Convenient to I-95 and the Merritt.
