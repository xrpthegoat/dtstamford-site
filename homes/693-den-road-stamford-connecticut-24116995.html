About this home

Enjoy the feeling of being at a resort all year round. This wonderful contemporary home is designed for an active family and entertainment of guests on a grand scale. The house and grounds are beautifully designed and lovingly maintained. The floor plan features a gourmet kitchen, comfortable dining and living rooms plus a large family room with exposed beamed cathedral ceiling. This home is light and bright with large windows and walls of French doors. Situated on over an acre of rolling grass lawns surrounding a beautiful heated, gunite swimming pool with raised spa, all remotely controlled. A grand wood terrace, kitchenette area with gas grill and ample room for lounge chairs and tables.
