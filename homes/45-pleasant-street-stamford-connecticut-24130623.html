About this home

Meticulously maintained and truly unique, this property presents an incredible opportunity in the heart of downtown Stamford. Featuring three spacious residential units plus a grandfathered-in commercial workshop, it is a rare investment property with multiple streams of current and future income. The first floor hosts a 3-bedroom, 1-bath unit with a generous layout. The second floor is a larger one-bedroom, one-bath residence highlighted by large walk-in-closet/office. The third floor provides a cozy 1-bedroom, 1-bath apartment with skylights & storage nooks, perfect as an efficient rental or for Airbnb/short-term rental income given its prime location steps to downtown Stamford & minutes to Metro North. Adding to its distinct appeal is the standalone commercial workshop with a separate shed. This space is ideal for trades, storage, or creative uses-offering additional income streams. The lower level includes office space, storage, utility rooms, two (2) half baths & coin-operated laundry for tenants, creating future opportunities for expansion. Additional highlights include a new roof, freshly sealed driveway, and private entrances for each unit-all tucked away on a quiet dead-end street! This turn-key property offers multiple revenue streams and strong value-add income opportunities for investors. This is truly a one-of-a-kind property that blends convenience, versatility, and strong rental demand -a rare chance to own a multi-use investment in mid-city Stamford!
