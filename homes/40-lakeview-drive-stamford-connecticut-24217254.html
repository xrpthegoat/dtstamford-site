About this home

Wonderful rental opportunity in the desirable Mid-Ridges neighborhood overlooking Cooper's Pond! This beautifully remodeled 4 bedroom, 3 full bathroom home offers an inviting layout ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The spacious living room with fireplace flows into the dining room and updated kitchen featuring granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and gas cooking. The upper level includes a primary bedroom with ensuite bath, two additional bedrooms and a full hall bathroom. The lower level offers a versatile family room or playroom with a second fireplace, access to the backyard and patio, and a fourth bedroom with an ensuite bath perfect for guests or a home office! Enjoy the fully fenced backyard with mature landscaping and direct access to Cooper's Pond. A peaceful setting just minutes from downtown Stamford. Available furnished or unfurnished beginning January 15, 2027. Excellent credit and references required.
