About this home

Welcome to this lovely Cape Cod home located in the desirable Belltown area of Stamford. This inviting home sits on a level, landscaped 1/4 fenced-in acre. Built in 1925, this vintage home features a welcoming living room, formal dining room with access to a spacious deck, kitchen with new stove, 3rd bedroom or home office and the front bedroom has new wall-to-wall carpet. The primary bedroom is upstairs with a full bath. The whole house has just been freshly painted. The location couldn't be more convenient with walking distance to grocery store, pizza, pharmacy, bakery, restaurant and more. A storage shed offers added convenience for lawn and garden tools. Easy access to I95 or the Merritt Parkway and just a short drive to downtown Stamford and the wonderful Stamford beaches. Come see for yourself.
