About this home

Welcome home! Step inside this beautifully cared-for 3-bedroom townhouse condo and enjoy three levels of bright, comfortable, move-in-ready living. The main floor offers an open, sun-filled layout perfect for hosting friends or enjoying a cozy night in with a modern kitchen, dining area, and a welcoming living room that opens directly onto your private deck with peaceful river views. Upstairs, you'll find three inviting bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite complete with a walk-in closet and its own private bath. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility, making it an ideal spot for a family room, home office, gym, or guest area and it features a convenient half bath and walk-out access to the outdoors. Parking is effortless with your detached one-car garage that offers additional space for storage. There is also assigned space for your second vehicle. You'll also appreciate the low-maintenance lifestyle with the monthly HOA covering water usage, grounds maintenance, trash pickup, snow removal, pest control and flood insurance giving you peace of mind and fewer monthly responsibilities. If you're looking for style, space, and convenience in a serene setting, this is the home you'll want to see. Come take a look, you may just fall in love.
