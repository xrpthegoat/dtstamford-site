About this home

Step into a piece of Norwalk history. Reported to date to circa 1730, 374 Flax Hill Road is a distinctive Colonial where nearly three centuries of character meet the space and comforts of today's lifestyle. Wide-plank wood floors, massive exposed beams, stone fireplaces and period-inspired details create an atmosphere that simply cannot be replicated in a newer home. Yet with approximately 3,577 square feet, generously proportioned rooms and a flexible floor plan, this is much more than a historic showpiece-it's a home meant to be lived in. A welcoming covered front porch introduces the home, while the rear opens to a surprisingly spacious, private and level backyard framed by mature trees. Inside, multiple gathering spaces offer warmth and character, with handsome fireplaces providing natural focal points. Upstairs, the bedrooms offer wonderful space and natural light. For the buyer who values authenticity, individuality and a home with a story, 374 Flax Hill Road is a rare opportunity to become the next steward of an extraordinary piece of Norwalk's past.
