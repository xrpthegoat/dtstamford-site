About this home

Live in the private Dolphin Cove association, that feels like vacation all year round, in this charming, newly updated, modern beachy four bedroom home. This waterfront community has an amazing 3.6 acre private club area with swimming pool, tennis courts, beaches, clubhouse, kayak storage, and deep water dock slips along the Old Greenwich shore line. Watch the boats come in from your backyard terrace, easy access to Long Island Sound, enjoy a friendly game of tennis or swim in the pool or at the beach. This home features lovely views, a wonderful floor plan with sunny kitchen, great island seating. It contains a bedroom/bathroom on the first floor. Near Innis Arden Golf Club, Old Greenwich village & the OG & Stamford train
