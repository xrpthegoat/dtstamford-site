About this home

This spacious single floor 2 bedroom 1 bath unit has a large living room with adjacent dining area that leads to a private covered deck overlooking the park-like setting of "Rolling Ridge", a premier Norwalk residential complex. Centrally located with access to I95, Route 7 and the Merritt Parkway. There is ample off street private parking and a large 90 x 80 storage area. Enjoy the convenience of an in-unit washer/dryer and exceptional amenities (in-ground pool, a natural pond, picnic areas, tranquil walking trails, fenced dog park & paddle tennis court). The heat and hot water are included in the HOA. Unit to be sold in as is condition.
