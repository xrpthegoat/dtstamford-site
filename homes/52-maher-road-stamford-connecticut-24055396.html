About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom colonial home, offering the perfect blend of convenience and comfort.** Located just steps from the train station and a short distance from downtown Stamford, you'll enjoy easy access to shopping, dining, and entertainment. For recreational enthusiasts, Chelsea Piers is within walking distance, providing an array of activities. The main level features a spacious living room, dining room, a well-appointed kitchen, a guest room, and a full bathroom. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath, ideal for family living. The freshly painted interiors, with gleaming hardwood floors throughout, add to the home's inviting charm. The lower level provides extra living space, perfect for an in-law suite or a cozy family room. Outside, the lovely backyard is ideal for relaxation or entertaining. Don't miss this incredible opportunity to own a home in one of Stamford's prime locations!
