About this home

Just off South Avenue, with much quieter surroundings, sits a modern farmhouse Colonial almost new construction with innovative design, high-style and unparalleled building quality. The exterior blends classic colonial style with contemporary flair. The interior space is thoughtfully created to provide open concept living with transitional finishing elements that will satisfy the needs of today's sophisticated families. The home offers over 7,800SF of finished space including 5 en-suite designer inspired bedrooms and 5.5 baths. The third floor offers additional recreational space and the lower level is finished with gym, office and rec room.
