About this home

The perfect summertime "stay-cation" featuring a new in-ground pool with huge patio decking and bi-level IPE Brazilian hardwood deck. Plenty of yard space that is fenced in for your pet. Dramatic open floor plan with stunning Great Room featuring a gorgeous stone fireplace And beamed vaulted ceiling. French doors take you right to the deck/pool. Sun drenched large country kitchen with center island and dining area overlooking the back deck/pool. Additional bonus room above the garage offers many options including guest bedroom, office, playroom or gym. Conveniently located in West Norwalk, this home is only moments to New Canaan and Post Road shopping.
