About this home

This custom home lives like a 3 bedroom! Welcome to your dream retreat, a breathtaking and glamorous "treehouse", nestled at the end of a serene cul-de-sac, recently refurbished with 11 new windows, new carpeting, paint, fixtures, HVAC, appliances, and more. This light-filled oasis boasts soaring high ceilings and an open floor plan that seamlessly blends modern luxury with natural beauty. Expansive walls of glass invite the outdoors in, offering uninterrupted views of the lush, tranquil wooded setting that envelops the property. A beautifully level side yard provides ample space for recreation or relaxation, perfect for both energetic play and peaceful moments. The covered back deck is an entertainer's delight, allowing you to host gatherings or simply unwind, come rain or shine. Both ensuite bedrooms are upstairs, including a sumptuous primary with two walk-in California Closets. If work beckons, the private office on the lower level provides a quiet space to focus. With an adjacent full bathroom, this space can also function as guest or au pair quarters. Whether you're seeking a stylish weekend getaway or a chic downsizing option, this enchanting home is a unique sanctuary where every day feels like a retreat, and you'll love the quick access to downtown Westport, the train, and the Merritt Parkway.
