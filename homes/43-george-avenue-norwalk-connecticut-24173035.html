About this home

Welcome to 43 George Avenue, a meticulously maintained 3-bedroom Cape Cod in desirable Bettswood. This 1955 home has been thoughtfully reimagined with an open floor plan and extensive upgrades throughout. Enter through the central hallway to find a living room and dining room with fireplace featuring a custom mantle and side cabinets installed in 2024. The open kitchen anchors the heart of the home with stainless steel appliances, 2022 quartz countertops, and a quartz-topped island added in 2025, plus new flooring in 2025. French doors in the breakfast nook open to a three-level deck spanning the width of the home with an above ground pool. A first-floor bedroom with dual closets and updated full bathroom with new vanity and quartz countertop provide main-level living convenience. Upstairs, two symmetrical bedrooms with closets share a full bathroom with walk-in shower, glass enclosure, and new flooring and tile in 2025. The finished basement offers open recreation space plus laundry room with washer and dryer. Major systems include 2016 roof, 2016 gas conversion, 2025 water heater, and lifetime-guaranteed French drain system. The peaceful backyard includes a pergola built in 2023 and an above-ground pool with 2024 liner and filter system. This home is located in a friendly neighborhood convenient to Calf Pasture Beach and local amenities. Stew Leonards and all the shops of Westport Ave are just around the corner. Come visit this amazing home before it's too late!
