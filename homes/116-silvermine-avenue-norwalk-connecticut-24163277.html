About this home

Sparkling 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom home built in 2021 on Silvermine Avenue in Norwalk, Connecticut. The open-plan first floor features a family room with a gas fireplace and custom built-in shelving, a large formal dining room, living room, and a chef's kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, double wall ovens, a wine cooler, and a breakfast bar - all set on hardwood flooring throughout. The kitchen and living areas open to a large deck overlooking gardens and a water feature with expansive views. The second floor features a large primary bedroom suite with tray ceilings, a gas fireplace, a walk-in closet, and a spa-style bathroom, along with three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms. A large unfinished walk-up attic provides significant future potential. The full unfinished basement features extra-height ceilings - ideal for finishing as a recreation room, gym, or additional living space. Solar panels and a Tesla Powerwall battery system deliver energy efficiency and backup power. A two-car attached garage and meticulous curb appeal complete the property. Located in the sought-after Silvermine section of Norwalk, CT, residents enjoy proximity to the Merritt Parkway, downtown Norwalk, South Norwalk dining and shopping, and an easy commute to New York City. A move-in-ready home in Silvermine, Norwalk, Connecticut. *** Solar Panels are paid off ***
