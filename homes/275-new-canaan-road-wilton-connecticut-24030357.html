About this home

Peaceful, easy living at this nature lover's paradise, located in South Wilton. Numerous updates and amenities showcase a beautifully maintained 4-bedroom home perched on a level waterfront lot. The current owner's attention to detail leaves nothing for you to do but move in. New maintenance free siding ensures durability. New Anderson windows throughout bring abundant natural light. New driveway provides curb appeal and convenience. Kitchen with stainless steel appliances, double wall oven, and granite countertops cater to culinary enthusiasts. Azek deck on upper level, off kitchen, provides the ideal spot for outdoor living/dining complete with ceiling fan and tv. New flooring and new central AC contribute to modern comfort and style. A dedicated office space with outdoor access, ideal for remote work- or use as a workout room and more. Three upstairs bedrooms and one additional downstairs bedroom offer flexibility. Updated bathrooms. Solar panels curb utility costs. Great gathering spaces throughout the home. Oversized 2-car garage with freshly sealed floor and smart garage door openers. Situated in Silvermine, this home offers a great commute location, minutes to Rt7 connector/major highways. Convenient to schools, downtown Wilton and New Canaan with easy access to restaurants and shopping. This move-in ready property combines modern updates, functional spaces, and a desirable location. You will want to make this paradise your home.
