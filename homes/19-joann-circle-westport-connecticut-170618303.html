About this home

Elegant new construction home by Mattera Construction sits in the middle of one of the most centrally located neighborhoods in Westport. Dramatic 6 Bedroom home, features an inground Gunite Pool, while offering 6 full baths and 3 half baths on 4 finished levels. An oversized custom Kitchen with Breakfast area, Family Room, Office, Living Room , Dining Room, Mudroom and 2 half baths complete the main level. On the 2nd level the oversized Primary Suite features 2 large walk in closets and a private bath. 4 more en-suite Bedrooms and Baths plus a Laundry Room are also on the 2nd floor. The Lower Level boasts an additional Family Room, Gym and a Bedroom with Full Bath. The finished 3rd level offers an additional Playroom or Office plus a half bath. Custom details and finishes throughout!
