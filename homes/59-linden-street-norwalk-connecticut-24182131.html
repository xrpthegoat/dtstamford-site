Offers due Friday 6/12/26 by 8 PM. This home is in a Great Location. This 3 bedroom, 1.5 bath home with den and oversized 3 car garage is located in the sought after Cranbury Area! This home has great value and worth some of the updates needed. This home is surrounded by more expensive homes and updating it will be well worth your time. Close to highways, dining, shopping and trains. Less than an hour from NYC.
