About this home

Palmer Hill Condo Rental! Luxury, gated community, rarely available and located on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. Spacious 1832 sq.ft., midrise, 2 bedroom/2.5 bath + den and 2 terraces! Freshly painted and available immediately. Ceilings are 9 1/2 ft, all wood floors, large kitchen, with many cabinets, SS appliances, island and a large adjacent dining area for your full size dining table. The living room is surrounded by windows, has a gas fireplace, and terrace. Both bedrooms are spacious, ensuite, with walk-in closets. 2nd terrace off primary bedroom. There is a powder room and separate laundry room with a washer and dryer. This rental comes with 2 parking spaces in the indoor garage and storage unit. Amenities include a renovated clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym, yoga room and business center. Owner will allow 1 dog. Excellent credit and rent spree application is required. Minutes to I95 and Metro North.
