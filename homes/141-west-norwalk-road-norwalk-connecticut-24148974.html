About this home

This exquisite property provides a luxurious and serene escape with stunning main house, pool and gardens. Just 1 mile from Trader Joe's Darien adds convenience to this tranquil countryside setting. Chef's kitchen, 5 ensuite bedrooms, study space and multiple gathering places for relaxation. Cozy up to one of 3 fireplaces on the main floor and enjoy movies, games (pool table included) or workouts in the expansive lower level with custom sleepover bunk. Geothermal, generator, SONOS, radiant heated floor, SMART security system for comfort and convenience. Custom cabinetry and window treatments throughout. Additional guest house, 4 stall barn and equestrian amenities available at a separate charge. Don't miss this chance to experience the idyllic lifestyle this private rental property offers!
