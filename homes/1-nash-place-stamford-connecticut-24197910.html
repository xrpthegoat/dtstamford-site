About this home

An updated home with lots of character. This 4 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom home in the heart of Glenbrook within walking distance to the train is sure to tick a lot of boxes! An inviting foyer with a coat closet brings you in to a roomy living room with a wood burning fireplace. A den/home office, a formal dining room, powder room and eat-in kitchen with island complete the main floor. The second level offers a generous primary bedroom and private full bathroom, with two good-sized bedrooms across the hallway sharing a bathroom. And, there's more! A third floor guest bedroom/office/playroom has its own full bath too. Hardwood floor throughout, high ceilings, a/c, finished basement with laundry, outdoor patio and backyard. Owner may consider renting garage to tenant for an additional $200 per month. What more could you want! GOOD CREDIT IS A MUST! Don't miss it!
