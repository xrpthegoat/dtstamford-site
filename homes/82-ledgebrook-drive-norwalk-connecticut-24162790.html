About this home

Enjoy the best of both worlds-everyday conveniences just minutes away from train stations, major highways, shopping and dining-paired with a peaceful setting overlooking woods and ponds. This sun-filled 3 bedroom, 2 full bath townhouse in the Ledgebrook complex offers a bright, open feel with vaulted ceilings and hardwood floors throughout. A see-through fireplace creates a natural separation between the living and dining rooms while maintaining an airy flow, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Generous closet space can be found throughout the home. Situated on 20 acres, the community provides a welcoming outdoor setting for walking and enjoying the surroundings, along with a pool and an exercise room for added convenience.
