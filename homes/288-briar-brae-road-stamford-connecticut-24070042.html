About this home

Discover this contemporary 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom residence, nestled on a secluded 1.12-acre lot, offering unparalleled privacy. The pristine, move-in-ready home features expansive floor-to-ceiling windows that frame breathtaking backyard views. A custom-designed, in-ground heated gunite pool enhances the outdoor space, providing a luxurious retreat. Inside, the open floor plan showcases a state-of-the-art kitchen equipped with premium stainless steel appliances. Adjacent to the kitchen, a cozy sitting area offers the perfect setting for relaxation. The dining area effortlessly transitions into a spacious living room featuring a striking floor-to-ceiling wood-burning fireplace. This inviting space further opens into an additional lounge area, thoughtfully designed and perfect for relaxation and entertaing. The primary bedroom is bathed in natural light, featuring an expansive wall of windows and custom-built closets. Its ensuite bathroom offers dual sinks, a luxurious soaking tub, and a shower equipped with multiple jets for a spa-like experience. The second bedroom also enjoys abundant light and includes its own full bathroom. The walk-out basement is a versatile space, featuring a recreation area with a floor-to-ceiling wood burning fireplace, two additional well lit bedrooms, and a dedicated office that can easily serve as a fifth bedroom. A full bathroom enhances the functionality of this lower level, making it ideal for guests or extended family stays.
