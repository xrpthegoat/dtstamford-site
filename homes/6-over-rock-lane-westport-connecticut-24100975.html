About this home

Timeless New England Colonial on 1.3 acres blending historic architecture with modern updates. Built in 1860 and reimagined by designer Dan Barsanti with curated interiors by Rena Levine, this five-bedroom, five-bath residence features refined millwork, wide-plank floors, and bespoke lighting. The open main level includes a bright kitchen with premium appliances, dining area, and living room with French doors to a bluestone patio and heated pool. A first-floor suite provides flexible guest or office space; the upper level hosts a spacious primary suite, marble bath, and dressing area. Systems include upgraded HVAC, EV-ready garage, and smart-home controls. Set on 1.3 acres with mature landscaping and a fenced backyard. Just 1 mi to Compo Beach, 0.9 mi to Longshore Golf Club, 1.0 mi to Levitt Pavilion, and 1.1 mi to Downtown Westport shops and dining. Offered furnished with curated designer pieces for a move-in experience rarely available in Westport.
