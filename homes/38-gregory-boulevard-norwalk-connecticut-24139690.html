About this home

Welcome to 38 Gregory Boulevard, an impeccably maintained two-family home in one of East Norwalk's most desirable coastal neighborhoods. Ideally situated just a short walk to the beach, restaurants, local markets, and the water, this property perfectly combines coastal charm with everyday convenience. The first unit features a nicely sized 2-bedroom, 1-bath layout, filled with natural light and well-kept interiors. The second unit is a spacious 3-bedroom, 3-full-bath across two levels, offering abundant living space - perfect for an owner-occupant or extended family. Both units have been meticulously cared for and show true pride of ownership throughout. A beautifully landscaped yard and inviting outdoor areas make this property ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Whether you're seeking an investment opportunity or a chance to live in one unit and rent the other, 38 Gregory Boulevard offers exceptional versatility, value, and location.
