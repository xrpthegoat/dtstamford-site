About this home

Nestled in a peaceful cul-de-sac in the highly sought-after North Stamford neighborhood, this fabulous mid-century modern home offers the perfect blend of style, space, and functionality. With flexible living options-choose from 3, 4, or even 5 bedrooms-this home adapts to your needs. Step into a sun-drenched space where floor-to-ceiling windows & multiple skylights flood the home with natural light. The beamed ceilings add drama to the living rm and dining rm. A dedicated office with built-ins creates an inspiring work-from-home environment. The heart of the home is a brand-new chic kitchen with state-of-the-art appliances, seamlessly blending modern convenience with timeless elegance. Cozy up by the inviting fireplace or enjoy the warmth of a wood-burning stove on chilly evenings. The primary suite is a true retreat, featuring an expansive walk-in closet and a private en-suite bathroom. A generous mudroom with radiant heated floors off the 2-car garage provides ample storage and can double as a home gym or extra workspace. Need even more space? The full laundry room is a game-changer, boasting tons of storage and a built-in desk, making it an ideal secondary pantry. Surrounded by mature trees, this home offers privacy and a beautifully serene setting. With natural gas heating and central air, comfort and efficiency come effortlessly. Don't miss your chance to own this architectural treasure in one of Stamford's most coveted locations. Will you be the lucky new owner?!
