About this home

A rare offering indeed! Welcome to 4 Charcoal Lane, an unrivaled multi-generational estate mere minutes from top-rated schools, shopping, restaurants and all that Westport has to offer. Sited on 2 private acres, this extraordinary property offers panoramic views of a 7-acre nature preserve, affording permanent tranquility. The meticulous renovations center around a gracious farmhouse-style Colonial with stunning features and superior craftsmanship, plus a spectacular post-and-beam Party Barn. A gourmet chef's Kitchen is the heart of the main residence, featuring a 14-foot Calacatta marble island and a suite of professional-grade appliances with dual Family Room fireplaces nearby. The Living Room's unique tray ceiling was specially designed for a superior piano experience. Upstairs, the Primary Suite is highlighted by a gorgeous spa bath. The property's exceptional appeal is anchored by the Country Carpenters barn, connected by a breezeway w/barn doors that create an inviting welcome to backyard entertaining. This luxury 1BR barn features 20-foot ceilings, heated floors, a full kitchen, and hinge-free retractable doors to seamlessly blend indoor/outdoor living. Resort-style amenities await outdoors with a Pebble Tec pool, waterfall hot tub & blue granite patio that remains cool underfoot on hot summer days. The entertainment porch features built-in heaters, see-thru fireplace and outdoor television. This is more than just a home, it's a legacy waiting to be claimed!
