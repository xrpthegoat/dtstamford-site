About this home

Exceptional Location, Tranquil Setting & Effortless Living at Birchwood Condominiums! A wonderful lifestyle awaits at Birchwood Condominiums, where comfort, natural beauty, and exceptional convenience come together! Ideally situated along the Westport-Norwalk border, this sun-filled, spacious 1,448 sq. ft., 2-bedroom, 2-bath, end-unit, top-floor residence, offers the perfect blend of privacy, peaceful surroundings, and easy access to everyday essentials, including Whole Foods just across the street. Nestled at the top of Hills Lane in a quiet, secluded setting, this inviting home offers a serene retreat surrounded by beautiful treetop views. The expansive living and dining areas provide a wonderful space for both everyday living and entertaining, highlighted by a charming wood-burning fireplace and direct access to a private deck overlooking the lush, picturesque landscape. Whether enjoying your morning coffee, unwinding after a long day, or entertaining friends, the setting is simply delightful. The generously sized eat-in kitchen offers lovely views of the surrounding greenery, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere for everything from casual breakfasts to relaxing evening meals. The spacious primary suite features a dedicated dressing area and closet space galore, while the exceptionally large second bedroom provides outstanding flexibility for guests, a home office, or additional living space.
