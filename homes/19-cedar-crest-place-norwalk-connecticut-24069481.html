About this home

Let your pets romp in this fully fenced yard, a great condo alternative! Charming Cape on a lovely street close to all with potential for a third bedroom upstairs. Updated kitchen with SS appliances and gas stove. The first floor features an open LR/DR combination, cozy fireplace, one bedroom and a full bath with hardwood floors throughout and walkout to an enclosed porch overlooking a large and level fully fenced yard. Beautiful Oak in the yard provides shade in the summer and the specimen Japanese Maple in front gives great color to the home. The top floor has 2 skylights, a full bath, one bedroom with Bamboo floors and a versatile second area that is currently used as a nursery but could be easily configured to another separate room.
