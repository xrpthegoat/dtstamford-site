About this home

Summer escape opportunity to the incredibly charming Rowayton. This seaside village is in its glory during the summer months. Beautifully designed living spaces include on the 1st floor a large living room w/fireplace, a bright white eat-in kitchen that&apos;s open to a spacious family room, a formal dining room, a tucked away office if work beckons, and a mudroom. Right off the kitchen is access to an oversized deck w/grill for your summer BBQs and outdoor dining. The yard is park-like and perfect for a game of catch. Upstairs the sophisticated primary suite w/a King bed is flooded with light and is graced with a soaring ceiling, as well as a Zen-like ensuite bath and a walk-in closet. There are three other bedrooms, two with Queen size beds and one w/twin beds with a hall bath. In the lower level, a second family room offers extra space for friends and family. Rowayton is known for its dynamic downtown, awesome restaurants, including Rowayton Seafood and Sails, pristine beaches and darling shops. A Summer to remember is waiting!
