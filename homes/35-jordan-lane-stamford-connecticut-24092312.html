About this home

Pristine Mediterranean Colonial nestled in quiet North Stamford with 4 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms available! With more windows than you'd like to count to illuminate the updates around the home and being set on over 2 acres, it is a sight to see. Beautifully remodeled Kitchen offers Breakfast Bar, top of the line Stainless Steel Appliances, cabinets and pantry space galore. Induction CookTop works LIKE cooking on GAS!! Spacious Laundry room with built-in cabinets. Huge Primary Bedroom with tons of closet space and dressing area with a panoramic window for serene views. 4th bedroom is converted into an Office with gorgeous views, high ceilings and dark wood beams. This house has so much to offer, top quality finishes and high end gorgeous remodeled Marble Primary Bathroom with shower and Jacuzzi. Den with Fireplace, Wet Bar and French doors opens up to a huge fully renovated Deck providing for family and friends' entertainment. Wine Cellar for the finest taste. New hardwood floors throughout and existing floor refinished. Great commuter location convenient to everything Stamford has to offer including the new Scofieldtown Park, schools, shopping, restaurants, The Merritt Parkway, Metro North Train and Downtown Stamford.
