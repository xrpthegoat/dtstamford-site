About this home

Stunning Modern Masterpiece | Bold Architecture & Bespoke Luxury. Complete Rebuild and Major Addition in 2016. Welcome to a truly one-of-a-kind modern masterpiece, completely rebuilt and expanded in 2016 with striking architectural elements and luxury finishes throughout. This residence offers bold design, spacious living, and thoughtful craftsmanship that redefine contemporary elegance. The main level features a sun-drenched eat-in kitchen outfitted with high-end stainless-steel appliances, sleek quartz countertops, and an abundance of natural light. Stunning hardwood floors flow seamlessly into the oversized dining room, perfect for large gatherings. The living room is warm and inviting with a beautifully designed fireplace, while the massive great room stretches the entire length of the house, offering endless flexibility, including the option to easily create a main-floor bedroom. From here, step out to the screened-in porch with brick paver flooring, ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The first floor also includes a generously sized laundry and mudroom with durable tile flooring and ample storage space. Upstairs, the spectacular 888-square-foot primary suite serves as a private retreat, featuring radiant heated floors in the spa-like bathroom, an oversized soaking tub, a frameless glass shower, and dual walk-in closets. Two additional oversized bedrooms each offer ensuite bathrooms and walk-in closets, providing luxurious accommodations for family or guests.
