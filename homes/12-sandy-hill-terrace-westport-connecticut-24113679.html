About this home

Welcome to 12 Sandy Hill Terrace in Westport, CT! A spacious home nestled at the end of a cul-de-sac, offering spacious and comfortable living space, ideal for both relaxing and entertaining. This home boasts a central location with a charming trail behind the property that that provides an easy walk to school. Walk to town and Saugatuck, along with easy access to the train and I-95...perfect for commuters. 12 Sandy Hill Terrace features the trifecta of natural gas, city water, and public sewer, enhancing both convenience and efficiency. This is a must-see home for anyone seeking a spacious home in an incredible location. Don't miss this opportunity to enjoy the best of Westport living!
