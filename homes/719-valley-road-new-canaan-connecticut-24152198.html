About this home

Perfection on Paradise Pond! Fall in love with this enchanting circa 1900 sun-filled home situated on 3.6 private acres with breathtaking direct waterfront views of trout stocked Paradise Pond and the Silvermine River. The home has been fully updated with modern amenities while preserving its classic charm. Move right into this gem, complete with first floor primary suite and 4 additional bedrooms on the 2nd level. Sophisticated & inviting, the open floor plan brilliantly takes advantage of the water views, and is perfect for easy living and entertaining. The ground floor has a fully finished additional family room/recreational room with access to a two-car garage with substantial storage areas. The two terraces function as outdoor rooms and provide a place for viewing the natural beauty of this tranquil property. Special in every way, this oasis is conveniently located just minutes from the New Canaan town center and 3.1 miles from the train station.
