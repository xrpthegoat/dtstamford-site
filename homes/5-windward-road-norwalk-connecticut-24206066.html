About this home

Nestled in a highly desirable West Norwalk cul-de-sac, welcome to 5 Windward Road. Lovingly owned by the same owner for many years, this cherished home offers comfortable living spaces and plenty of room to grow. The main level features three spacious bedrooms, full bathroom, a large, bright living room with a gas burning stove, and a dining room that opens through sliders to a wonderful sunroom spanning the width of the home. The sunroom provides easy access to the back deck and overlooks the private outdoor space, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or extending your entertaining to the outdoors. The lower level offers a generous additional living area, eat-in kitchen, and full bath-an ideal setup for an in-law suite, au pair, extended-stay guests, or additional family living space. Conveniently located near Kendall Elementary School, Oak Hills Public Golf Course, I-95, and Route 1, this inviting West Norwalk home combines a peaceful cul-de-sac setting with easy access to schools, recreation, shopping, dining, and transportation.
