About this home

Welcome to this exceptional property located in a highly desirable neighborhood, just moments away from the town center, esteemed schools, and convenient transportation hubs. This exquisite home is surrounded by lush greenery and stands as a true work of art. Showcasing unparalleled craftsmanship and an unwavering attention to detail, this residence offers an expansive living space of nearly 8,000 square feet spread across four impeccably designed floors. Boasting 5 bedrooms, 8 bathrooms, and an elegant center hall colonial design, this property epitomizes sophistication and luxury. Nestled within a serene and private setting, this property is adorned with mature trees, a charming fieldstone terrace, and a refreshing pool. Step inside and be captivated by the grandeur of the home. A dramatic dining room, generous living room, and a well-appointed home office await you. The chef's kitchen seamlessly connects to a welcoming family room, creating an ideal space for hosting gatherings and entertaining guests. The primary suite offers a spa-like bath and a stylish dressing area, providing a tranquil retreat within the home. Four additional bedrooms offer privacy and versatility, accommodating various needs and preferences. The finished third floor and lower level, complete with a bedroom and gym, present ample opportunities for recreation and relaxation.
