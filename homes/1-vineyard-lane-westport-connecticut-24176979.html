About this home

Enjoy a convenient lifestyle at this centrally located well maintained Custom Colonial with a gorgeous pool and an expansive covered patio, fabulous yard, in the heart of Westport. Featuring an open spacious floor plan, gourmet kitchen with large center island leading to the dining room, family room and a dramatic two-sided floor to ceiling stone fireplace. Walls of glass open to the stone patio overlooking a private, fenced acre, in-ground pool, green house/barn. An office (or bedroom), full bath, powder room and game room complete the 1st floor. The 2nd floor includes a large primary suite plus 3 generously sized bedrooms (one currently an office) all en-suite. Minutes to all town amenities, train, shopping, beaches, schools.
