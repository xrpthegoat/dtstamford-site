About this home

Experience the pinnacle of Compo Beach living at 9 Pond Edge Road, a rare coastal sanctuary offering immediate beach access with the peace of mind of an elevated, flood-safe setting. Spanning 6,100 square feet across four impeccably finished levels, this 6-bedroom home is designed for effortless entertaining and everyday relaxation. Sunlight fills the open great room (with WBF) and dining area, where a wall of French doors opens to a private backyard oasis featuring a heated gunite pool, stone patio, and built-in BBQ, all surrounded by mature landscaping. A cozy living room with a gas fireplace provides the perfect setting for intimate gatherings or quiet evenings at home. At the heart of the home is the eat-in Chef's Kitchen, complete with luxury appliances, two dishwashers, three ovens, and an oversized center island. A flexible main-level suite offers ideal space for an executive office or guest accommodations. The second floor showcases a serene primary suite with a gas fireplace, dual dressing rooms, and a spa-inspired bath, along with three additional en-suite bedrooms and a laundry room. The third level features a secluded guest suite with private balcony and a skylight-filled bath. The lower level is a destination of its own, offering a home theater, dedicated gym, recreation room, and full bath with direct access to the pool. Complete with a whole-home generator and attached garage, this turn-key property delivers laid-back luxury just moments from the sand.
