About this home

Beautifully renovated vintage home offers timeless character on a professionally landscaped acre in coveted Old Hill. With 5 bedrooms, 5 full and 2 half baths with generous space and classic charm. The formal living room and elegant library both with fireplaces and hardwood floors create a warm and welcoming space for relaxing and entertaining. A spacious kitchen featuring a large center island, high-end appliances and seamless flow to the inviting family room with fireplace overlooks the grounds. The first floor also includes a private home office and a guest bedroom with en suite bath-perfect for visitors or multigenerational living.Upstairs, the serene primary suite includes a sitting area, dressing room and en suite bath with soaking tub. Two additional bedrooms each with bath complete the second floor. There is a large light-filled studio/bedroom ideal for long term guests or a private retreat above an attached 3-car garage. Outdoors, there are patios for sunning, a space for grilling and luxurious gunite pool with pergola. This home has been thoughtfully updated with modern amenities creating a truly special place to call home.
