About this home

A dream rental opportunity in Darien. Fully renovated in 2019, this modern colonial is set on a private road near town and is situated across from Woodland Park. High ceilings throughout, front and back staircases, and wide plank hardwood floors round out this 5000 square foot home. 31 Hazel has 5 bedrooms , 4 1/2 baths, two offices, a partially finished basement with recreational room, energy efficiency and modern amenities throughout. Two bedrooms have en-suite baths. The primary bedroom with large bath has radiant heat and steam shower. Radiant heat is also in the mudroom and first floor full bath. Open great room and modern kitchen with french doors lead out to the patio and large backyard with established plantings and treehouse. Energy efficiency includes solar panels that power the home and charge the backup batteries in the event of a power outage. Less than a mile from the newly developed Heights Crossing and Darien Commons. Noroton Heights train station is also nearby. A MUST SEE! Two months security and first months rent due at lease signing.
