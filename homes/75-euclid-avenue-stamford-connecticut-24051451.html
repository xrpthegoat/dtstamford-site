About this home

This beautifully designed townhouse offers a perfect blend of style and comfort, just minutes from Long Island Sound. The main level features an open floor plan with a spacious living room centered around a cozy fireplace, seamlessly flowing into the dining area and gourmet kitchen. A large sliding door opens to a private outdoor area, ideal for entertaining. Completing the first floor is a versatile bedroom or office and a full bath. Upstairs, the serene primary suite boasts two walk-in closets, a spa-like bathroom with a free-standing tub, a jetted shower, and a double-sink vanity. Two additional generous bedrooms and another full bath ensure ample space for family or guests. The home also includes large energy-efficient European-style windows for maximum sunlight and comfort year-round. Additional features include a full-size laundry room, a large storage attic, and a two-car garage with extra storage space. Situated in a prime location on a quiet street, it's just minutes away from Cove Island Park and Marina, offering convenience and lifestyle in one stunning package.
