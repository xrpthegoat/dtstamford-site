About this home

Only 45 minutes from NYC, this beautifully renovated second-floor unit offers modern living with plenty of space and flexibility. It features a spacious eat-in kitchen, a brand-new bathroom, and two large bedrooms. There's also an additional room that can be used as a third bedroom, home office, or extra living space. The unit includes central air conditioning and provides direct access from the kitchen to a deck, which is currently in the process to been remolded. Conveniently located just a 15-minute walk to the Main Station train station, with easy access to I-95, making commuting simple. The property comes with one off-street parking space, with plans for a second space in the future.
