About this home

Located on a beautiful cul-de-sac this totally renovated center hall colonial available is sited on a bucolic acre overlooking the Mianus River. The main floor boasts an open concept gourmet kitchen with family room and fireplace with French Doors that open to a spectacular deck perfect for outdoor dining. Formal Living Room with fireplace and Dining Room provide opportunities for entertaining and easy living. On the second floor there is a large master suite with walk in closet and a new luxurious bathroom with 2 sinks, shower and separate tub. There are 3 additional bedrooms and 2 additional bathrooms all newly renovated. Completing the house is a lower level, game/family room, full bath, office/gym, laundry and a 2 car attached garage. Available August 15, 2025.
