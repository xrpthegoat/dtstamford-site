About this home

Nestled along the picturesque Silvermine River, sitting high and dry, and set on a private lane, this home offers both privacy and endless potential, minutes from the Merritt Parkway. An inviting foyer welcomes you into this traditional center hall Colonial. A spacious living room spans the entire width of the house, featuring a wood-burning fireplace that creates a warm and inviting ambiance. A formal dining room leads to the kitchen, which connects to a back porch overlooking the serene, flat one-acre property. The cozy family room, also equipped with a wood-burning fireplace and half bath, provides direct access to a screened-in porch, seamlessly connecting to the back deck-ideal for easy living & entertaining. Upstairs, discover four generously sized bedrooms, each with large closets, along with two full baths, including a primary suite. Hardwood floors flow through the bedrooms, with additional hardwood under the carpet in the primary bedroom. The full, walk-out lower level presents opportunities for expansion, providing an additional 1,000 sq. ft. of space. Accessed through the lower level, the two-car garage adds practicality to this home. Enjoy the unique benefit of having the Silvermine River in your backyard, with a private path providing direct access to the water-perfect for relaxing and enjoying nature. Cherished by its original owners since 1977, this beloved home is now ready for new owners to appreciate its enduring charm and ideal location. Sold "as is.
