About this home

Welcome to 13 Elliott Street, a charming Colonial nestled in the highly sought-after Marvin Beach neighborhood of Norwalk. Just a short walk to the water, this home offers the perfect blend of coastal living and timeless character. Lovingly and meticulously maintained by the same owner for over 30 years, this residence showcases consistent care and thoughtful upkeep throughout. From its inviting curb appeal to its warm and comfortable interior, the home reflects a true sense of pride in ownership. Whether you're enjoying morning strolls to the shoreline or relaxing in a home that has stood the test of time, 13 Elliott Street delivers both lifestyle and location.
