About this home

Welcome to 31 Coolidge Ave, a wonderful 3 family opportunity for an investor or owner occupant. Unit 1 is a freshly renovated one bedroom featuring a new quartz kitchen, and bathroom. Unit 2 also features a new quartz kitchen, with new laundry machines, 2 spacious bedrooms, open living/dining room, and new flooring throughout. Unit #3, the top level, is a spacious open floor plan 1 bedroom apartment with a finished attic space. Additionally, a brand new gas boiler & water heater has just been installed, powering all 3 units. Parking for you and your tenants is a breeze with ample driveway parking space, and a 1 car attached garage. Being in a central location, this home is within close proximity to the Glenbrook train station, restaurants, shopping, and Downtown Stamford! You do not want to miss this turn key 3 family!
