About this home

This pristine home is a great opportunity in one of New Canaan's coveted walking community neighborhoods. As you enter the property crossing over the picturesque Silvermine brook you will appreciate that it is sited beautifully on a knoll with great curb appeal accented with stone walls, patios, a large front and rear yard with specimen landscaping all on a mostly flat, 1.90 acres. The main floor has been redesigned to create an open concept living room space adjoined with a dining room / new chef's kitchen. The upper level includes the primary suite with a full bath along with 2 bedrooms and a full hall bathroom. The bright and spacious walk-out 1st level family room includes a fireplace, built-in shelving and nearby is a bedroom suite with a private bath. The attached solarium offers the perfect refuge or workspace. The lower-level recreation / family room is well sized and offers a blank slate of additional finished space. It is partially wired for a generator. This home has been maintained very well, and it has been freshly painted and is move in ready. As is, it is a great opportunity for an easy transition, however, it is also an opportunity to purchase this property in such a great location that it would surely benefit from doubling its size as many other homes in the neighborhood have. Come take a ride by and see.
