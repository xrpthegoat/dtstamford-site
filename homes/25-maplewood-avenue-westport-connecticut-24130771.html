About this home

LOVE IT TODAY, DREAM YOUR TOMORROW! WOW!!! This snuggly 1800sf bungalow is ready for you to move right in with 3-bedrooms, 2-1/2 baths total on 3 levels (which includes the 1 bedroom/1bath Lower Level legal accessory apartment). Guests feel welcomed the minute they step through the 25 Maplewood front door into the light-filled main level where entertainment feels easy-peasy! The open floor plan has wonderful flow between Living Room with built-ins, roomy Dining Room and Kitchen with stainless steel appliances, updated cabinetry and granite countertops. The upstairs top level features an unexpectedly roomy Primary Bedroom and Bedroom #2. Closet built-ins maximize storage and an updated bathroom are wonderful features. The Lower Level holds RARE TREASURE in Westport that yields an income option: a LEGAL ACCESSORY APARTMENT with direct walkout to paved parking at its entry and its own #2 Willowbrook mailing address. You can opt to rent it as an ongoing income supplement (as the Seller did for 14 years and at $1425 most recently) --- OR as your primary residence while renting the upper level for greater cash flow(current owner yielded $5750/month for the recent 1-1/2 years ...see MLS listing #24013621)! Or maybe you will opt to live the whole house as all-yours and use it as a private office or recreation space . Off-street paved parking for 6 cars adds a fabulous advantage on a street where many get by with far less.
