Kill the Busywork Uploader

Kill the Busywork publishes short, practical lessons about automation. The uploader is a desktop tool used by the channel operator to turn original lessons into vertical videos and publish them to YouTube.

The operator uses Google authorization to give the local worker permission to upload videos and check the selected channel. Viewers do not sign in or install anything here.
