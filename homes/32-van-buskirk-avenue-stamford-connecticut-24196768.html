About this home

Welcome to the heart of Stamford's sought after Cove neighborhood, where classic charm meets everyday convenience. This inviting first floor two bedroom rental offers a warm, comfortable feel with beautiful hardwood floors, crown molding, and an abundance of natural light throughout. The kitchen features crisp cabinetry, rich oak countertops, crisp white appliances, and generous workspace, making meal prep both functional and inviting. While the spacious living areas provide the perfect setting to relax or entertain. Step outside to enjoy your morning coffee on the welcoming front porch or unwind in the expansive backyard, a rare bonus for apartment living. This home also includes a detached one car garage, plus electric and hot water are included in the rent for added value and convenience. Perfectly positioned just minutes from Cove Island Park, beaches, downtown Stamford, shopping, restaurants, I 95, and the train station, you'll enjoy the ideal balance of neighborhood charm and commuter friendly accessibility. If you're looking for a well maintained home with character, comfort, and an unbeatable location, this first floor gem is ready to welcome you home.
