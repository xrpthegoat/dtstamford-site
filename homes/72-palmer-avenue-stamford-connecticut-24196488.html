About this home

Charming Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac just minutes from the outdoor recreation and beaches of Cove Island Park! This sun-filled home offers an inviting open-concept layout with a spacious living room featuring hardwood floors that flows seamlessly into the dining area. The expansive eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home, designed for both everyday living and entertaining, with soaring ceilings, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances including a gas range, center island, pantry, generous dining space, hardwood floors, and an updated full bath on the main level. Upstairs you'll find the primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms, and a second updated full bath. A pull-down attic and unfinished basement provide abundant storage, while recent mechanical updates include a 2025 oil-fired boiler and hot water heater, plus inside oil tank. Outside, enjoy a fenced yard offering a patio and room to play, plus a detached garage for added convenience. Ideally located just minutes from downtown Stamford's shopping, dining, Metro-North, and major highways, with approximately a 55-minute commute to NYC, this home offers the perfect balance of neighborhood charm, coastal living, and commuter convenience.
