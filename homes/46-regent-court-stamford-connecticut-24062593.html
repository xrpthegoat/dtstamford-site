About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the desirable Springdale neighborhood. This move-in ready residence features abundant natural light, newly refinished hardwood floors throughout the main level and freshly painted. The spacious eat-in kitchen provides ample cabinet and counter space, stainless steel appliances, and casual dining area. On the main floor, you'll find three bedrooms and an updated full bathroom. The lower level is a true highlight, with a cozy family room, an oversized laundry room, and a fully updated bathroom featuring a luxurious walk-in shower. Step outside to the expansive patio, which overlooks the scenic Norton River-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Additional features include an oversized one-car garage with extra storage space, and an updated Trane HVAC system 2022. The home is conveniently located just 1.5 miles from the Merritt Parkway and Springdale Train Station, with easy access to nearby schools, library, ice rink, Woodway Country Club, and shopping. City water and sewer.
