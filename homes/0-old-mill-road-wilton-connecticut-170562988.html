About this home

Incredible Opportunity in Wilton - 41 Acres. Priced to Sell. A rare chance to own a combined 41 acres in highly desirable Wilton. This very private wooded retreat offers underground utilities and city water at the property line. Just minutes from top restaurants, shopping, and three Metro-North stations, with easy access to the Route 7 corridor. Offered as a single package, these two contiguous parcels-Lot #1 (38+ acres, MLS# 170563180) and Lot #2 (3+ acres, MLS# 170562988)-are listed separately due to state parcel numbers but sold together for one low price. Wilton is known for its award-winning schools, classic New England charm, vibrant town center, scenic parks and trails, and as the home of Cannondale Bicycles and chip giant ASML. Note: A 2.5 acre fenced portion is not buildable. Buyers to perform due diligence. Estate sale - sold AS IS.
