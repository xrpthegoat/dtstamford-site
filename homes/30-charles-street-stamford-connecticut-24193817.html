About this home

***Highest and best offers to be submitted by 7/28/26 at 5pm*** Welcome to 30 Charles Street in Stamford's Cove neighborhood. Whether you're looking to offset your mortgage with rental income or add to your real estate portfolio, this two-family home offers plenty of potential. Each unit features 2 bedrooms and 1 full bathroom with separate entrances and separate utilities. The first-floor unit is move-in ready and offers spacious bedrooms, a dedicated dining area, an updated kitchen, and direct access to the basement, which provides ample storage today with the potential to be finished in the future. The second-floor unit is ready for updates, creating an excellent opportunity to add value and generate rental income. Outside, you'll find a level backyard and a long driveway with plenty of room for multiple vehicles. Conveniently located just minutes from Cove Island Park, downtown Stamford, Metro-North, shopping, restaurants, and major highways, this is a solid opportunity to build equity in one of Stamford's established neighborhoods.
