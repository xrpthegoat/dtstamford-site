About this home

Welcome to 29 Wildwood Drive, 4 bedrooms, 3 and half baths where contemporary design meets nature's serenity. This unique home features an open-concept living area with expansive floor-to-ceiling windows seamlessly integrating with the outdoors. Featuring four bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms, the interior includes a cozy wood-burning fireplace in the main living area. The remodeled kitchen boasts modern quartz countertops, a butler's pantry, and an adjoining laundry room. Elegant hardwood flooring graces the main level, complementing the updated interior throughout. Luxury abounds in the renovated bathrooms, highlighted by a curb less shower and soaking tub in the primary bathroom. Two bedrooms enjoy private ensuite bathrooms, while the others share a well-appointed Jack and Jill bathroom. The exterior features a new EPDM roof, an attached two-car garage, and a nine-car detached barn/garage with independent climate control-ideal for car enthusiasts or extra storage needs(50X42). Outdoor amenities include a renovated gunite saltwater pool with a Wi-Fi-enabled variable speed pump and Trex deck for relaxation and entertainment. The home is equipped with a new high-efficiency Bosch two-zone electric HVAC system, ensuring year-round comfort, along with upgraded utilities for enhanced efficiency. 29 Wildwood Drive it's a blend of modern architecture, luxurious updates, and a deep connection to natural surroundings.
