About this home

In the heart of Darien sits this special, center hall colonial amid a private, 1.22 acre property. A generous, light-filled living room with fireplace and formal dining room are perfect for gatherings and are serviced by an eat-in kitchen that leads to a three season sun porch and dinner party sized deck. The family room with wood burning stove is adjacent to a full bath and has access to the lush and private level yard. 5 bedrooms and 3 full baths provides a place for everyone. 2 car garage. Minutes to schools, train and town. This gem is in the heart of it all.
