About this home

An ideal rental for those seeking a serene & upscale lifestyle! With over 2 acres of beautifully landscaped grounds along the New Canaan border, this home is perfect for entertaining with its large covered outdoor kitchen & eating area and is ready for your summer hangouts. There's also a charming brick patio, a second bluestone patio with pergola, a cozy fire pit, plus a vibrant greenhouse for year-round enjoyment. Upon entering through the private, gated drive, you are welcomed by a grand 2-story foyer w/ elegant staircase & windows offering picturesque views of the lush outdoors. A spacious sunroom flows to living & dining rooms w/ fireplace. The gourmet kitchen connects to an eating area & family room, mudroom and bath w/ shower. There's also a mudroom w/ access to the heated 3-car garage and a unique private office suite/in-law apartment above the garage via large, covered breezeway. The primary suite is a haven of relaxation w/ fireplace, luxurious spa bath, steam shower and expansive walk-in closets. A spiral staircase leads to a private study with 3rd fireplace and a separate sunroom, which also joins the living room. Three additional bedrooms, a full bath & laundry room complete the upper level. The home also features a whole-house standby generator and a full en-suite apartment with kitchenette over the garage. Large driveway area surrounded by fruit trees, planting beds & extra parking. Truly an exceptional place to call home in North Stamford!
