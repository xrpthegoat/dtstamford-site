About this home

Welcome to this lower North stamford upgraded 3-bedroom ranch with additional room ideal for a home office or 4th bedroom. Radiant heat ensures efficient, even warmth throughout. The chef's kitchen showcases premium Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, blending performance with style. Recent improvements include a new roof, updated electric panel, newer hall bath, and repaved driveway. A two-car garage equipped with a Tesla wall charger and two dedicated storage rooms adds exceptional functionality. Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living with two patios with awnings and a flat, usable yard. Gas heating and 1250 gallon septic tank ideal for future addition. Located on a peaceful cul-de-sac, this property offers privacy and convenience with easy access from both Long Ridge Rd and High Ridge Rd in equal measure.
