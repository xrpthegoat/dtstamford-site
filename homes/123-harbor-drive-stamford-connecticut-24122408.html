About this home

Experience the elegance of waterfront living at Palmer Landing, one of Stamford's most prestigious luxury waterfront communities. This 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath large end-unit townhouse offers panoramic views of the marina from every room. With heat and hot water included in the common charges, comfort and convenience come standard. The main level features an open, airy layout that flows seamlessly to a private deck overlooking the water - an ideal setting for morning coffee or evening cocktails. The living room's wood-burning fireplace, rich hardwood floors, and a sleek kitchen with stainless-steel appliances create the perfect blend of style and sophistication. The primary suite is a private retreat with a walk-in closet, ceiling fan, and spa-like bath with a spacious walk-in shower. A second bedroom, complete with its own full bath and water views, is perfect for guests or a private office. Set on coveted Shippan Point, Palmer Landing offers an unparalleled lifestyle - just minutes to sandy beaches, boutique shopping, and Stamford's finest dining.
