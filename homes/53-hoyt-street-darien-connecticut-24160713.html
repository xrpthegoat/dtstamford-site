About this home

Welcome to this absolutely charming bungalow-style cottage set in a desirable and convenient location, where timeless character and thoughtful updates throughout come together beautifully. From the moment you arrive, the inviting front porch sets the tone-perfect for morning coffee, evening unwinding, and everything in between. Inside, the home has been beautifully updated throughout, featuring a chef's kitchen with top-of-the-line Viking appliances, complemented by stunning soap stone countertops and refined finishes. The first-floor primary suite offers both convenience and luxury, with a fully renovated bathroom and generously sized closets. Every detail has been carefully considered, from the new lighting fixtures throughout to the seamless blend of classic charm and modern comfort. One of the home's most versatile features is the finished 2 car garage, offering an additional 600 sqft of flex space-perfect for a home office, gym, playroom, or garage - whatever suits your lifestyle. The lower level adds an additional 200 square feet of finished space for recreation or playroom. Step outside to your private backyard oasis, complete with a beautiful gunite pool-an exceptional amenity that elevates everyday living and makes entertaining effortless. All of this, just moments from town, train, and schools. This is the kind of home that doesn't come around often-where charm, quality, and lifestyle come together seamlessly. Don't miss it!
