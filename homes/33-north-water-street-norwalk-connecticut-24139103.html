About this home

Modern Downtown SoNo Condo located on the L.I Sound with Parking & Prime Location . Welcome to the heart of South Norwalk! This sleek and spacious 5th-floor condo offers the perfect blend of style, convenience, and tranquility-just steps from the best bars, restaurants, and the vibrant waterfront scene. Enjoy a short stroll to the Maritime Aquarium, the new SoNo Collection Mall, and all the cultural charm Norwalk has to offer. Inside, you'll find a modern open-concept living and dining area, perfect for entertaining or relaxing at home. The oversized bedrooms provide comfort and versatility, while large windows invite natural light throughout. You'll appreciate the peace and quiet of this well-constructed building-so quiet, you'll forget you even have neighbors. This condo includes two dedicated parking spots one of which is in a secure parking garage, making city living even more convenient. The building also boasts top-notch amenities, including a state-of-the-art fitness center and a stylish recreation lounge complete with pool tables, TVs, and plush seating. For water enthusiasts, the building is situated right on the Long Island Sound, offering scenic views and endless opportunities for outdoor enjoyment-from kayaking and paddle boarding to sunset strolls along the harbor. Come experience elevated living in Downtown SoNo-where modern comfort meets coastal charm.
