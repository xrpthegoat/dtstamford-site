About this home

Welcome to 35 Fara Drive, in the highly sought-after Pepper Ridge neighborhood. This charming expanded cape is located close to parks, schools, places of worship, shopping, and dining. Easy access to both the train and highway makes commuting a breeze. Step inside this move-in ready home and be greeted by newly refinished hardwood floors that flow throughout. The updated kitchen boasts sleek new countertops and appliances, while the beautifully renovated bathrooms add a touch of modern elegance. Outdoors, a stunning bluestone patio and deck provide an ideal setting for entertaining or simply enjoying the outdoors. The inviting living room features a large bay window, offering plenty of natural light, along with ample seating space and a classic wood-burning fireplace. The adjacent dining room, perfect for hosting gatherings, is conveniently located off the kitchen and sunny family room. On the main level, two versatile bedrooms could serve as a home office and a guest bedroom, or be combined to create a spacious primary suite. Upstairs, you'll find three more lovely bedrooms and another full bath. The partially finished lower level offers additional space that is perfect for a playroom, gym, or extra storage. The current homeowners have updated the floors, kitchen, bathrooms, closets, and patio. (See the attached list of updates). All you have to do is move in!
