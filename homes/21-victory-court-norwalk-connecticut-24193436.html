About this home

Charming Cape Cod home nestled on a quiet no-through street in the highly sought-after East Norwalk neighborhood. Beautifully maintained and full of character, this 4-bedroom, 2-full-bath residence seamlessly blends timeless charm with modern comfort. Freshly painted throughout, the home offers bright, inviting living spaces, a flexible floor plan, and a partially finished basement that provides the perfect space for recreation, a home gym, or additional storage. The spacious living room features hardwood floors, while the eat-in kitchen opens to a delightful three-season sun-room overlooking the beautifully manicured, level backyard. The main level includes two bedrooms and a tastefully remodeled full bath with a walk-in shower. Upstairs, you'll find two additional generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath with a tub/shower combination. Additional highlights include central air conditioning, natural gas heat, a private driveway for off-street parking, and a storage shed for bicycles, seasonal items, and outdoor equipment. For added convenience, the landlord pays for internet, water, irrigation, and lawn care. Cats are welcome. Please note that the garage is not included with the rental and is not accessible to tenants. Ideally located just minutes from Metro-North, Calf Pasture Beach, waterfront parks, restaurants, shopping, and major commuting routes.
