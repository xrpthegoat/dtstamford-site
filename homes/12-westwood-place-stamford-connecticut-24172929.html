About this home

Sought-after Westover cul-de-sac, close to Greenwich border, modernized 4BR Colonial (w/city water/sewer & natural gas) set on .46 acres in one of the most walkable, desirable neighborhoods. Inside: well-proportioned living spaces w/wide entryways, hardwood floors & large windows. Warm & welcoming living room opens to light-filled dining room. Spectacular kitchen - heart of the home - w/gorgeous quartz, sparkling GE & Bosch stainless steel appliances (gas cooking) & breakfast bar (seats 5) flows seamlessly into family room filled w/sunlight featuring wood-burning FP & French doors to patio & backyard - excellent layout for entertaining year round. ML laundry/mud room leads to XL garage. All 4 BRs up, including spacious PBR w/en suite bath & walk-in closet w/blt-ins. 2nd bath w/double-sinks & linen closet complete the UL. 550sf+ of pristine play space in LL w/sep office/gym. Freshly painted, C/A, gas heat & abundant storage. Outside: West-facing backyard, framed by tall trees & lush landscaping - perfect for celebrating life events w/friends & family. Bbq on the back patio, play catch in the yard or unwind on the covered front porch w/tree top views. Walk to schools, tennis, top local restaurant & more; under 1.5mi to Merritt toward Manhattan; 1-3.5mi to golf, parks, preserves, equestrian, 4 neighborhood retail centers (shops, grocery, dining, conveniences), downtown entertainment, I-95 & RR. Enjoy space & comfort of the country w/convenience of the city - Welcome to Westover!
