About this home

Welcome to 34 Hunt Street, a charming 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home in the sought-after beachside community of Rowayton. Filled with natural light, the living room features built-in bookcases and a cozy fireplace. The eat-in kitchen and the dining area opens to a spacious deck - perfect for entertaining and relaxing while enjoying the beautifully landscaped and private back yard. The lower level includes both a fully finished family room with fireplace and office space, and a laundry/utility room, and a large cedar closet. With an attached 2 car garage and prime location within walking distance to the train station and the village, this home offers the perfect blend of convenience and coastal charm. Just minutes to the beach while only one hour by train to NYC.
