About this home

Beautiful & Spacious Townhouse in Sought-After Lambert Common, South Wilton! Welcome to easy and fun living in one of Wilton's most desirable communities! This spacious and beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse offers everything you need-just move right in and enjoy. The oversized living and dining area features a cozy wood-burning fireplace and sliders that open to a large patio-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms and two full baths, while the recently finished fantastic 800 sq. ft. basement provides flexible space for a rec room, office, gym, or 4th bedroom. Ample closet and storage space throughout, plus designated parking right in front of the unit and a large one-car garage just around the corner. Lambert Common is nestled in South Wilton, known for its vibrant community and top-rated schools. With two train stations in town, commuting is a breeze. Three-bedroom units in this complex rarely come on the market. This is an exceptional opportunity in a prime location. Most furnishings can also be sold if buyer desires.
