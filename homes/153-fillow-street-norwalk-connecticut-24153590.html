About this home

Recently renovated and ideally set on a full acre in sought after West Norwalk, this exceptional home borders Oak Hills Golf Course and offers a rare blend of privacy, open space, and sweeping views. A gracious foyer leads to sun-filled living and dining rooms with oversized bay windows overlooking the expansive backyard. The sparkling eat-in kitchen w/new countertops and st/st appliances flows into a gleaming sunroom w/ sliders that lead to a wraparound deck, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or working from home. Upstairs features 4 generously sized bedrooms and 2 full baths. The jaw-dropping primary suite offers a spa-inspired bath with a walk-in shower, soaking tub, and dual-sink marble vanity. The lower level boasts an impressive family room w/exposed wood beams, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliders to the yard. An attached two-car garage provides convenient access through the laundry room and updated powder room. Since 2018, updates include a newer roof and hardie board siding, well pump, water filtration system, septic tank, oil tank, newer heating system, and smart irrigation. Enjoy stunning sunsets over the golf course while remaining minutes to Norwalk's shopping, dining, parks, beaches, highways, and Metro-North. Come and make this your ultimate dream home!
