About this home

Welcome to this beautifully-maintained and well-loved colonial on a quiet Silvermine cul-de-sac! The owner of 38 years has taken care of freshly painting the interior, replacing the roof & siding and adding a generator. This lovely house is ready for you to do the cosmetic updates that will make it your own. Notice that many of the similar neighboring homes have expanded the garage and/or added to the footprint of the house. Zoned as CD-1M with required side setbacks of 10 feet (please do your own due diligence). The picturesque, flat half-acre lot makes it easy to create the backyard oasis of your dreams! Located in historic Silvermine, which offers the Inn at GrayBarns on the Silvermine River and its Mercantile Shop/Cafe, the Silvermine Market and Silvermine Arts Center. Not an artist? There is so much more available for your enjoyment! The Silvermine Golf Club is only two miles away. The vibrant SoNo waterfront area offers a diverse selection of restaurant options plus the SoNo Collection luxury mall and the South Norwalk Metro North station for a one-hour commute to Grand Central Terminal - all just a 10-minute drive away! Don't leave without taking a stroll through the neighborhood, stopping on the quaint one-lane bridge or at the GrayBarns Tavern to admire the view of the waterfall. Then you will understand why this is a special place to call home! "This is Silvermine!" **Don't miss the virtual tour!**
