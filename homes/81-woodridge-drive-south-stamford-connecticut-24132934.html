About this home

Beautifully and completely renovated just a few years ago, this spacious five-bedroom, three-and-a-half-bath single-family home perfectly combines modern comfort with classic Mid-Ridges charm. The residence features an oversized family room ideal for entertaining or relaxing, a bright open-concept living and dining area with vaulted ceilings, and a brand-new gourmet kitchen with granite countertops and high-end finishes - truly the heart of the home. Additional highlights include a two-car garage, ample storage, and elegant design details throughout. Conveniently located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping centers, restaurants, recreational areas, and major employers, this home provides the perfect balance of accessibility and serenity.
