About this home

Beautifully updated and oversized one bedroom with private entrance in highly desirable Rowayton Woods. At 900 sq ft, this unit is larger than the typical one bedroom layout and offers a rare direct walkout to a ground level deck with serene, unobstructed views of the pond. Inside, enjoy a fully renovated kitchen and bath, new luxury vinyl flooring. The home features in unit laundry, reserved parking directly in front, and additional storage space. A long list of upgrades includes: new heating system, new refrigerator, new remote control window treatments in the kitchen, new door hardware, new front door and storm door, and new window sconces-all contributing to a truly turnkey experience. Rowayton Woods residents enjoy a resort-like lifestyle with a pool, tennis courts, and a community garden. This private, beautifully updated unit combines modern convenience with peaceful natural surroundings, offering an exceptional opportunity in one of Norwalk's most sought after communities.
