About this home

Welcome to 39 Depinedo Ave, a well maintained home nestled in the heart of Stamford's West Side neighborhood. This charming property offers a spacious layout. Nice private backyard ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Located in a quiet community, the home is just minutes from downtown Stamford's vibrant shopping, dining, and entertainment scene. Commuters will love the easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and nearby Metro-North train stations for a smooth ride into NYC. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy weekend strolls or summer outings at nearby parks. Whether you're a first-time buyer or looking to settle into one of Stamford's most convenient and connected areas, 39 Depinedo Ave offers the perfect blend of comfort, location, and lifestyle. The field card has this home as two bedrooms but the second floor has a third bedroom already finished and it has space for a fourth bedroom, it could easily be a four-bedroom home.
