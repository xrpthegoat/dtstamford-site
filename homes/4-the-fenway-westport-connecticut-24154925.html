About this home

Timeless elegance in Westport's renowned Old Hill. Set on 2.22 bucolic acres w/a heated Gunite pool & picturesque pond, this expansive 5-6 bedroom,4.5 bath farmhouse blends classic charm w/thoughtful recent enhancements that delivers a renewed sense of style & energy to the home. A renovated kitchen remains the heart of the home, featuring a generous center island & a stunning, glass-walled breakfast nook which allows for basking in nature's splendor while savoring a meal. Fresh interior updates incl. a newly painted family room, updated lighting w/two new chandeliers, a modern backdrop for comfortable everyday living. This precious home has been refreshed throughout to highlight its impressive scale, open flow, & architectural character. The sun drenched primary ste is a true retreat w/a fireplace, spa like bath & generous walk in closet. A flexible floor plan offers multiple living areas & a potential in law suite-ideal for multi generational living, guests, or work from home needs. Four wood burning fireplaces add to the home's timeless appeal. Outdoors, enjoy a thoughtfully landscaped property, the pool & spa, set amidst such soothing natural surroundings. Additional highlights: city water & sewer, a whole house generator, & the exceptional privacy just moments from town. Old Hill is one of Westport's most coveted neighborhoods, known for its quiet, tree lined streets & unbeatable proximity to town, schools, & commuting. A rare opportunity to own a special home.
