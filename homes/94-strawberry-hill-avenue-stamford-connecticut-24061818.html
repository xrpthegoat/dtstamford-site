About this home

Welcome to 94 Strawberry Hill Ave at Hampshire House where tranquility, easy living and amenity all meet. Come experience this well managed & maintained 2BR 1BA 900sqft, light & bright top floor corner unit Co-Op. Drive in to your assigned parking space, steps away from your unit & be greeted by a beautiful courtyard. Take a stroll through its walkways & enjoy the surrounding nature w/ abundant flowers, manicured bushes, mature trees & grass filled fields. Stepping inside you have lovely hardwood floors throughout. A sun soaked living space beams w/ charm while sunlight pours in via windows overlooking the yard space. Enjoy a kitchen perfect for entertaining complete w/ pantry & steps away from the dining room & large coat closet. The kitchen has matching white appliances, plenty of cabinets & counter space, tile floors & access to rear deck. Down the center hall is a linen closet with in unit wash/dry. Perfectly situated among the floorplan is the unit's full bathroom w/ tile, a soaking tub/shower & frosted window. Continuing down the hall is a sizable primary bedroom w/ ceiling fan, large closet & adjacent windows overlooking the courtyard. Finishing off the space is the second bedroom complete with ceiling fan, ample windows & large closet. Heat, hot water, yearly taxes & insurance all included in HOA. Just minutes to downtown Stamford's many amenities including restaurants, shops, public transportation, parks and more. Come see why this property may be perfect for you.
