About this home

Location, location, location! Welcome to this lovingly cared-for ranch nestled in a sought-after neighborhood, offering the perfect blend of comfort, charm, and convenience. From the moment you step through the front door, you are greeted by a bright and spacious living room featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace, gleaming hardwood floors, and an inviting open flow into the charming dining room. Enjoy morning coffee or evening sunsets from the sunny bay window, offering beautiful sunrise views and a warm, welcoming atmosphere. The eat-in kitchen has stainless steel appliances, ample cabinetry, and easy access to the deck-perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, or relaxing while enjoying the peaceful surroundings. The main level features three bedrooms, each with hardwood floors, and a full bath. Plus a cozy den or home office provides the ideal space for working from home, reading, or a quiet retreat, with convenient access to the attached garage. The lower level offers even more living space with a walk-out, oversized family room featuring access to a large patio-perfect for gatherings, recreation, or simply enjoying the outdoors. A second full bath and abundant storage space complete this versatile level. This wonderful home is move-in ready and awaits its next family to create lasting memories. A rare opportunity to own a well-maintained ranch in a desirable location-don't miss the chance to make it yours!
