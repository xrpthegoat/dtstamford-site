About this home

A rare and magnificent architectural offering designed by award-winning architect Paul Haigh, where sophisticated simplicity meets the quiet poetry of Bauhaus influence. Set along the serene Mianus River, this home is a masterful composition of nature, light, and form-where every space is intentionally designed to engage with its surroundings. From the moment you enter the oversized foyer crowned by a striking cupola, there is an immediate sense of volume, light, and quiet drama. This architectural introduction unfolds into a home that is both expansive and deeply connected to nature. Walls of glass frame ever-changing river views, while the great room-with its soaring vaulted ceiling, white brick fireplace, and curated built-ins-offers a balance of openness and intimacy. Light moves effortlessly throughout the day, casting warmth across rich hardwood floors and transforming each window into a living work of art. The kitchen is refined and purposeful, with clean lines, natural materials, and a generous central island-all surrounded by soft, ambient light. It is a space equally suited for quiet mornings or gathering with ease. Just beyond, a screened-in porch floats above the river, offering a serene vantage point for coffee at sunrise or long, lingering evenings immersed in the sounds of nature.
