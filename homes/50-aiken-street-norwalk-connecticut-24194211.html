About this home

Location! Nestled in a beautiful country setting, Rolling Ridge Condominiums is one of Norwalk's finest places to call home. Enjoy wonderful amenities including an in-ground swimming pool, a picturesque pond with fountains, a handball/tennis court, and a clubhouse with shower facilities. Unit 422 features 1 bedroom, 1.5 baths, and a den that's perfect for a home office or guest room. The spacious primary bedroom offers a full bath and large walk in closets. With great closet space throughout, an excellent layout, and a wonderful flow, this unit feels like home. If you're looking for peaceful surroundings with great amenities and a convenient Norwalk location, this is one not to be missed.
