About this home

Welcome to 333 West Avenue, a stunning four-bedroom, four-bathroom home in the Noroton Green Association. Inside, you'll find over 3,600 square feet of beautifully finished living space with high ceilings, custom millwork, and oversized windows that fill the home with natural light. The open-concept main level is perfect for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring a spacious living and dining area that flows seamlessly into a gourmet kitchen equipped with high-end appliances, sleek countertops, and custom cabinetry. French doors lead out to a private stone terrace. Upstairs, the primary suite serves as a peaceful retreat with a luxurious en-suite bathroom and a generous walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms and a bonus room offer ample space for family, guests, or a home office. A two-car garage, beautifully landscaped grounds, and the ease of HOA-covered maintenance complete this turn-key lifestyle. Conveniently located to Darien Commons and Noroton Heights train station, this home is a must see!
