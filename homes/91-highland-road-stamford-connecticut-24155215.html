About this home

Discover urban living at its best with this roomy 1-bedroom condo, ideally situated in the heart of downtown Stamford. Enjoy the convenience of your own private entrance which leads to a spacious sun filled living room featuring an elegant fireplace, beautiful hardwood floors and extra-large sliding glass doors that open to your own private balcony-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The bedroom features elegant hardwood floors, a full bathroom, ample closet space, and large sliding glass doors that open to a second private balcony perfect to enjoy a warm summer evening or to welcome a new day. The kitchen offers granite counters, modern appliances, a breakfast bar, and in-unit laundry. Enjoy the convenience an assigned covered garage. Additional perks include firewood storage and a dedicated storage unit; both located in the garage. Benefit from this prime downtown location with effortless access to transportation, renowned restaurants, shopping, and cultural venues. This condo offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience-an outstanding opportunity for city living in Stamford!
