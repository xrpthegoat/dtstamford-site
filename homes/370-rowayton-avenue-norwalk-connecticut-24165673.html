About this home

370 Rowayton Avenue is a sun-drenched home that perfectly blends comfort, style, and functionality. Throughout the home you are greeted by bright, inviting spaces. At the heart of the home is a beautifully renovated chef's kitchen full of natural light and open to family room with a charming wood-burning fireplace. Large French doors open bringing fresh air and views of the expansive and private property. A formal living room, bar area and dining room with access to porch and patio are divine for entertaining. Four well-appointed bedrooms, including a convenient main-level bedroom perfect for guests or flexible living. Upstairs, the generously sized primary suite serves as a true retreat, complete with vaulted ceilings, a large walk-in closet, and a spa-like bathroom featuring a soaking tub, separate shower, and dual sinks. With its thoughtful layout, modern updates, and serene setting, 370 Rowayton Avenue presents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy both elegance and everyday comfort in Rowayton.
