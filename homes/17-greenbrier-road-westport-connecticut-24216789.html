About this home

Welcome to 17 Greenbrier Rd- an irreplaceable masterpiece that perfectly balances elite, high-end refinement with effortless daily livability. Nestled high in a vibrant New England landscape with expansive treetop views, this 2023 frame-in restoration by Wimmer & Wimmer Design has been taken to the next level by the current owner. A striking, organically dark facade introduces its classically modern roots. Inside, the minimalist-luxe interiors are anchored by 6-inch rift-sawn hardwoods, TruStyle doors, and architectural trim under brilliant Lutron controlled smart LED lighting. The gourmet kitchen by NuKitchens is a chef's dream, meticulously styled with Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Caesarstone. Spa-level bathrooms showcase custom Porcelanosa tile, curbless glass showers, floating vanities and new Toto Neorest bidet toilets. The primary suite is a wellness escape complete with a 70-inch almond soaking tub. Outfitted with the absolute best-Marvin windows, Dornbracht, Villeroy & Boch, Robern, Rocky Mountain hardware, and Clopay doors-no expense was spared. The private resort-style grounds feature a newly resurfaced gunite swimming pool with spa and waterfall, expansive stone patios, and a refurbished clay tennis court. Experience the enchanting vibe and unique community of Greenbrier. Turn-key ready: This home can be purchased fully furnished, including the curated pieces of art and decor.
