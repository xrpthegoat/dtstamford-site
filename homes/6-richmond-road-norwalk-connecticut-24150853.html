About this home

Seeking a primary residence or a weekend retreat, it's hard to imagine a better setting than the Rowayton Beach Association (RBA). This three-bedroom Cape Cod Colonial offers an open floor plan with a wraparound deck that creates an easy, natural flow for both entertaining and everyday living, seamlessly connecting the eat-in kitchen and living areas. The second-floor features three bedrooms, two with en-suite baths, providing comfort and privacy. An oversized one-car garage offers additional space for storage, bikes, and beach gear. Set along the edge of Long Island Sound and the Five Mile River, Rowayton together with the enhanced value of the RBA delivers a distinct lifestyle advantage with private coastal beach access, a close-knit village atmosphere, charming local amenities, and abundant outdoor recreation opportunities-all while remaining conveniently close to restaurants, Metro North train, transit, public golf course and shopping.
