About this home

Discover 78 Trinity Pass, almost complete proejct, awaiting your final touches. This home is a 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom ranch in North Stamford, situated on a 1 acre sprawling corner lot. This home features a handcrafted stone fireplace, radiant heating, 80 gallon hot water tank, and a kitchen primed for an open-plan transformation. The primary bedroom offers vaulted ceilings, and an ensuite bathroom that can be finished easily with tile and fixtures of your choice. This home has 3 more bedrooms on the same level, one more also being ensuite. Three of the 4 bathrooms in this home are roughed in, awaiting your personal touch. Additionally, the basement is finished, with a full bathroom. The property also includes a two-car attached garage, a large detached barn, and a new well pump installed in 2024. While the home requires some updates, many of the big ticket items have already been completed - 2023 roof, 200 amp electrical, plumbing, cedar impressions siding, and much more. This can be a rewarding project for those looking to customize. This home is sold AS-IS.
