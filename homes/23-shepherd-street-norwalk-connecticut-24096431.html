About this home

Welcome to this charming Colonial home in the heart of Cranbury, offering the perfect blend of comfort, style, and functionality! Situated on a beautifully landscaped half-acre lot, this property boasts exceptional curb appeal. Upon entry, you're welcomed into an open-concept family and living room, both filled with natural light. The spacious kitchen and dining area flow together nicely and open up through French doors to a wraparound deck, ideal for entertaining and enjoying the above-ground pool. Upstairs, you'll find three well-appointed bedrooms and a full bath. The partially finished walk-up attic is currently set up as a home office and offers plenty of extra storage. Property updates include siding, roof, gutters, a tankless hot water system, and upgraded electrical and HVAC. An oversized two-car garage with additional attic storage, a shed with electrical service, an irrigation system, electric fence wiring, and a generator hook-up complete this exceptional offering.
