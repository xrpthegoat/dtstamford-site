About this home

Welcome to one of the most desirable locations in the complex! Flooded with natural light, this beautiful top-floor condominium offers peaceful living with a covered private deck overlooking meticulously landscaped grounds filled with lush perennial gardens, the sparkling pool, and scenic views of the Rippowam River. Tucked away from the road, this exceptional unit enjoys a quiet setting free from traffic noise. Inside, the home has been thoughtfully updated and impeccably maintained. The bright kitchen features new countertops and newer appliances installed within the past year. Fresh paint throughout and brand-new carpeting in the primary bedroom create a move-in-ready experience. The spacious floor plan offers three generously sized bedrooms, including walk-in closets in two bedrooms, and two full bathrooms. Additional features include four air-conditioning units (one recently installed), a private 10' x 8' storage room, and two assigned parking spaces. Residents enjoy access to the community's in-ground pool and grilling area, perfect for relaxing and entertaining. Conveniently located close to downtown, beautiful parks, shopping, dining, and everyday amenities, this exceptional home combines comfort, convenience, and one of the finest settings in the community.
