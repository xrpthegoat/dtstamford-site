About this home

This Greenfield hill Charming colonial, sits on a 1.6 acre lot, right at the border of Fairfield and Westport and only 6 away minutes from the Southport train station and multiple beautiful beaches. Cozy and stylish with a twist of French flair, this well maintained property offers, 5 bedrooms, including a nursery right by the primary suite, 3.5 bathrooms and 5 garages, 3 being attached to the house. On the first floor a large living room leads to an open concept dining room and a bright kitchen, perfect for gatherings. On the second floor, the 5 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms as well as a dedicated office space await you. A full house generator has been installed. Be the first to discover this hidden gem. Agent/co owner. Cameras are installed outside. UPDATE: Sellers are thanking all agents and potential buyers for their interest and are asking for Highest and best offers by Tuesday June 9th noon.
