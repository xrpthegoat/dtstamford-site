About this home

Experience the best of Westport living in this beautifully FULLY FURNISHED contemporary retreat, ideally located near Green's Farms Train Station and Burying Hill Beach, and everything the area has to offer. Bathed in natural light, the open, airy floor plan features soaring ceilings, a gourmet eat-in kitchen with professional appliances, and inviting living spaces that flow effortlessly to the outdoors through sliding glass doors and a multitude of windows. Set on a picturesque one-acre property with a lap pool, the home is designed for relaxed everyday living as well as effortless entertaining. A main-level office and small second bedroom with a full bath offer flexibility, while the spacious upstairs primary suite features a private deck overlooking the beautiful grounds, generous closets, an en-suite bath, and laundry. There is a detached one-car garage with a pool room with sliders opening to the pool. Available immediately and fully furnished-simply unpack, settle in, and enjoy the very best of Westport!
