About this home

The spectacular backyard is your private oasis, featuring a 2023/2024 POOL, expansive maintenance-free deck, custom STONE PATIO ,GAZEBO w/TV and sound system-perfect for entertaining or relaxing all season. The stunning renovated kitchen is the heart of the home, showcasing Carrara marble countertops, white cabinetry, bluestone flooring, s/s appliances, beverage chiller, and an oversized center island with seating.
