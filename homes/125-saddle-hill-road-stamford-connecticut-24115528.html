About this home

This gracious Colonial on one of North Stamford's most coveted cul de sacs is the home you have been waiting for. A circular driveway leads to the home's classic and elegant entry. The main floor has so much space - a front-to-back living rm features a fireplace and French doors to the new deck & access to a screened-in porch, an elegant dining room, a wonderful updated kitchen with island, 9ft ceilings and a gas fireplace. The direct access to the oversized 2 car garage will mean no need to lug your groceries up steps when you arrive home. Working from home these days? There is not one, but TWO offices! Bring your ideas here - one of these rooms could be an au-pair/guest bedroom because there is a private full bath and laundry here too. A half bath completes the main floor. Upstairs, the generous Main Bedroom suite has French doors , inviting you to stay in bed, and open them up to enjoy the rustle of the trees beyond. The stylish dressing room with sink is a special treat. Three add'l bedrooms & a hallway full bath finish off the second floor. A partially finished lower level offers flexible space with a cozy fireplace and a bar. Other highlights include : hdwd flrs, newer roof (approx 2020), newer furnace (approx 2022) and AC (approx 2021), freshly painted exterior. Welcome - you have arrived at your new home!!
