About this home

Exceptional custom Colonial offering over 5,000 SF of beautifully finished living space on a private, level 1-acre lot in Norwalk's sought-after Silvermine neighborhood. This move-in-ready home features 4 spacious bedrooms, 3 full and 2 half baths, hardwood floors, skylights, custom millwork, central air, a whole-house generator, security system, cedar closets, dual laundry areas, and an attached 2-car garage. The gourmet kitchen boasts granite countertops, custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, an oversized island, and seamless flow into the sun-filled family room with a fireplace and formal dining areas-perfect for entertaining. Flexible floor plan includes potential primary suites on both the main and upper levels, ideal for multigenerational living or guest accommodations. The expansive finished lower level offers endless possibilities for a media room, gym, recreation space, or home office. Outside, enjoy professionally landscaped grounds, a circular stone driveway, a stone patio, and a peaceful backyard retreat with plenty of room for outdoor living and future pool plans. Conveniently located minutes from the Merritt Parkway, I-95, shopping, dining, golf, and all that Silvermine has to offer. A rare combination of space, privacy, and versatility in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
