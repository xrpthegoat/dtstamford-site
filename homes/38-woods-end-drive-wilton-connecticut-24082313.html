About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST BY MONDAY 3/31 AT 3pm. Welcome to this peacefully situated and beautifully maintained 4-bed, 2.5-bath Colonial located in one of Wilton's most desirable neighborhoods. With nearly 4,000 sqft of living space, this sun-filled home offers the perfect balance of classic charm and functionality. The over-sized family room with vaulted ceiling, fireplace and picture windows flows into a traditional-style eat-in kitchen with updated appliances, and access to the deck and screened-in porch-perfect for seasonal entertaining. With adjoining spacious living and dining rooms, the main floor flows seamlessly. The easily accessible ~800 sqft, heated & cooled, finished lower level is ideal for a playroom, gym, or media space. Upstairs, the tranquil primary suite features a walk-in closet and renovated bath complete with soaking tub, stall shower, double vanity, skylight and heated floors. Three additional bedrooms share a functional and stylish, newly updated hall bath. Additional upgrades include the roof, skylights, full-house generator, garage doors, repaved driveway, and exterior paint. Four-zone heating & cooling, abundant storage (including a fully floored attic) and mature landscaping, all add to the appeal of this wonderful home. A mere 2 minutes from The Lake Club - close enough to enjoy the perks, yet far enough to enjoy nature's tranquility. A not-to-be-missed, true Wilton gem!
