About this home

There is a moment just inside the front door, when the house introduces itself. The entrance hall-original, unhurried, exactly as gracious as 1919 intended-tells you everything. This is a home that was built when details mattered and time was taken. A century later, nothing essential has been lost. The living room and formal dining room carry that same quiet elegance, their proportions generous, their light beautiful through Anderson architectural-grade windows. Then comes the turn- into the 2013 addition, where a stunning chef's kitchen anchors a family room that opens through French doors onto a covered deck. Beyond it, a private landscaped backyard unfolds like a secret: lush, park-like, deeply planted and utterly your own. An outdoor shower with hot and cold water, a detached two-car garage, gravel driveway and an automated irrigation system make it as effortless as it is beautiful. Upstairs, the primary suite earns its name- spacious bedroom, sitting room, a spa bath, place to exhale. Three additional bedrooms, each with the soul of the original house, share a handsome hall bath.A finished third-floor now features two versatile offices and a full bath, offering flexible space ready for your next chapter. New roof, siding insulation, 4-zone A/C, sewer and water mains from 2013. 4300 SF set on .39 acres of one of Westport's most sought-after residential streets -walking distance to town.This home exudes charm and is completely, confidently, move -in ready.
