About this home

BEST KEPT SECRET IN STAMFORD - LOCATED ON A QUIET CUL-DE-SAC IN WESTCOTT COVE WATER COMMUNITY! Come and enjoy a true calmness & connection to the ocean with captivating Long Island Sound views from this very special home. Flexible floorplan consists of four bedrooms and three full baths, kitchen, dining area, living room, family room with fireplace and laundry room with one car garage and plenty of storage space. Enjoy two decks, one off kitchen and lower deck off family room, great for entertaining. The beautiful large backyard completes this oasis! Located only 1 1/2 miles to Metro North and I-95 and quick stroll to Cove and Cummings Beach. Only 35 miles to Manhattan & just moments to Downtown Stamford restaurants, shopping, Chelsea Piers & Harbor Point.
