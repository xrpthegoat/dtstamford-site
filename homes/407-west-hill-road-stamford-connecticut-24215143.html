About this home

Steeped in storybook charm with it's brick chimney and flower covered entrance, this enchanting 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom home beautifully blends timeless character with modern luxury. The open-concept layout welcomes you into a large, sun-drenched living room anchored by a cozy fireplace. The chef's kitchen features white shaker cabinets, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a center island, and rich hardwood floors that flow throughout the home. The expansive primary suite serves as a private retreat, complete with a walk-in closet, an en suite bath, and an elevated private balcony deck overlooking the deep, park like yard. The home is expansive with a few steps from the Master are two bedrooms with a bath and a few steps up are two more bedrooms and a bath The lower level boasts a spacious family room and a versatile bonus/art room equipped with a sink. Modern conveniences include central air, a separate laundry room, a newly paved driveway, and an attached two-bay garage. Broker related to seller.
