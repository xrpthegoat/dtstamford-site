About this home

SHIPPAN! Beautiful and immaculate center hall Colonial in desirable Shippan Point location. A must see! Pictures don't do it justice! Home was completely remodeled in 2000--all plumbing, electrical, heating, windows, siding, roof and landscaping were replaced and central air added. First floor boasts extra-large living room with fireplace, large dining room, eat-in kitchen with walk-in pantry, laundry/butler's pantry, den and mudroom. Second floor has huge primary bedroom and bath, two additional generously sized bedrooms and new full bath. Third floor consists of two bedrooms and full bath. Basement is finished with walk-out; extra-large deck with awning, two-car detached garage, sprinkler system, security system, electric fence and full house generator. Just put your furniture in place! Be sure to see virtual tour!
