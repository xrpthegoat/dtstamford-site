About this home

Set on an elevated 0.46-acre parcel along Ocean Drive East in the sought-after Shippan neighborhood, this distinguished residence offers over 5,100 square feet of sunlit living space, a walk-to-water location, seasonal water views from the main level, and panoramic, unobstructed water views from the second floor. Bathed in natural light and presenting a rare opportunity for customization, the thoughtfully designed main level comprises a formal living room, an inviting den anchored by a fireplace, a formal dining room, and an expansive kitchen centered around a generous island. Seamless indoor-outdoor living is achieved through an airy sunporch-complete with vaulted ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows, and multiple sliders-opening onto a stone patio and custom Gunite pool within a fully fenced backyard. The main floor also accommodates two well-proportioned bedrooms, including a private en-suite. Upstairs, where those panoramic water views take center stage, are two additional bedrooms-each appointed with a walk-in closet-a dedicated art room featuring custom built-ins and bookcases, and a versatile recreation space currently configured as a media room and a walk in closet of its own. Completing the residence is an attached two-car garage alongside extensive storage solutions throughout, including a pull-down attic, an unfinished basement, and two additional dedicated storage areas within the home. This is truly a must-see property with endless potential. Sold as is.
