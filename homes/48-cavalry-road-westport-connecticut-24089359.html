About this home

Idyllically set on 600 feet of the West Branch of the Saugatuck River, this 4 acre property is the ideal setting to create your dream lifestyle. The homeowner/developer of the original 111 acre subdivision chose this site, above all others, to build his legacy compound comprised of two acres in Westport and two in Weston. Enjoy, expand or recreate the well cared for 3,800 square foot Colonial which is sited primarily in Westport and finished on three floors with river views from many rooms. The two bedroom guest house/barn, built over a large garage, is primarily located in Weston and provides the potential for a variety of options. This is a rare opportunity to create an extraordinary waterfront estate on one of Westport/Weston's premier properties.
