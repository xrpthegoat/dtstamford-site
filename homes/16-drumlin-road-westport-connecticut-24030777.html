About this home

Beautifully updated ranch nestled in the sought-after Compo Beach neighborhood of Westport. Positioned within walking distance to Compo Beach, this home is ideally located for commuters, with close proximity to both the Westport and Greens Farms train stations, as well as downtown Westport, where you can enjoy the town's amazing shopping and fabulous restaurants. Experience one-level living at its best with an open floor plan featuring three bedrooms, one full bath, and a beautiful fenced backyard with a patio, perfect for relaxing. Step into a large, sun-filled living room with a fireplace, ideal for entertaining family and friends. A den/office off the living room provides the perfect space to work from home and can also be used as a fourth bedroom, gym, or playroom. The updated kitchen boasts newer appliances, quartz countertops, a gas oven/range, and generous counter space. Completing the main level is a nicely sized primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms, and an updated full bath with shiplap wall. The professionally landscaped fenced backyard is perfect for children and pets. Notable updates include a renovated kitchen, solar panels, siding, Anderson windows, gutters, and an updated bath. This exceptional home enjoys a prime location with close proximity to schools, the I-95, train stations, dining, shopping, and parks, as well as the Longshore Club, offering golf, tennis, and a pool, providing a unparalleled elegance and convenience.
