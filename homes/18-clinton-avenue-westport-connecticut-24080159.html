About this home

Welcome to 18 Clinton Avenue, where timeless charm meets contemporary luxury in the heart of Westport. Perfectly situated near town in a fabulous neighborhood, this expanded Cape Cod home epitomizes effortless indoor-outdoor living, featuring a beautifully landscaped, fully fenced property with an inviting in-ground heated pool. Step inside to a welcoming foyer that sets the tone for the home's seamless flow. At its heart, the expansive living room exudes warmth, anchored by a striking stone fireplace and custom built-ins; an ideal space for gatherings. The serene screened-in porch is an extension of the living room overlooking the pool and bluestone patio, offering the perfect retreat for relaxation. The formal dining room, adorned with elegant paneled walls, provides an intimate setting for entertaining. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring custom cabinetry, artisan hardware, quartz countertops, and a stunning Italia Verona 6-burner range. Large sliding doors open directly to the backyard, perfect for dining al fresco. Upstairs, the ensuite primary bedroom is a private sanctuary, complete with a spa-like five fixture bath with heated floors, custom built-ins and two large closets. Adjacent is a bright, versatile space-ideal for an office or nursery. Two additional spacious bedrooms share a well-appointed full bath. New hardwood floors throughout the upstairs. A conveniently located laundry room completes this level.
