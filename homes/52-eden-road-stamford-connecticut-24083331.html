About this home

Nestled in the highly sought-after Upper Newfield neighborhood, this beautifully updated four-bedroom, four-bath Colonial Split offers a perfect blend of charm, functionality, and modern convenience. The open-concept design seamlessly connects the kitchen, living room, and dining area, creating an inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen boasts elegant granite countertops, top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, and sliding glass doors leading to a spacious deck overlooking a level backyard-ideal for outdoor gatherings and relaxation. The second level features three well-appointed bedrooms and two full baths, while a private wing is dedicated to the luxurious master suite. This retreat includes a cozy sitting room and a renovated en-suite bath, providing the perfect space to unwind. On the lower level, the expansive family room opens to a stunning granite patio complete with a built-in fire pit, offering a warm and inviting outdoor space. Additionally, this level includes a convenient laundry room, a full bath, ample storage, and access to the oversized two-car garage with a separate utility area. With its prime location, thoughtful layout, and modern updates, this home is a rare find that offers both comfort and style in a desirable neighborhood.
