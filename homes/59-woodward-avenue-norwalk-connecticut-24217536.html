About this home

An excellent opportunity for investors to acquire a mixed use commercial property in the heart of South Norwalk, one of Norwalk's most evolving and increasingly desirable neighborhoods. Located at 59 Woodward Ave. This property is offering both 59, 61 and 63 Woodward Ave, creating an attractive investment opportunity with multiple potential income streams. The property features two commercial storefront opportunities, a large 2 car garage with each bay door measuring at 12ft & 10ft in height and 4 residential apartments providing a diversified mix of commercial and residential rental potential. The property also has additional enclosed carport space located in the rear of the building providing ample storage space for equipment or vehicles. The residential units consist of 2, two bedroom apartments and 2, one bedroom apartments, offering a variety of rental options to appeal to different tenant needs. For investors seeking to expand their portfolio, reposition an existing asset, or capitalize on the continued development of the surrounding area, this property offers a compelling combination of mixed-use functionality, multiple rental opportunities, and long term appreciation.
