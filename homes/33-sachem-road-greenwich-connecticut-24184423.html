About this home

Tucked into a parklike half-acre south of the Post Road, 33 Sachem Road is a commuter's dream a block from the train, parks, and shops. This beautifully maintained 3+ bedroom, 3-bath home features an open floor plan, an eat-in kitchen with center island and skylights, and a luxurious primary suite. A rare in-home elevator adds ease across every level, while the finished lower level offers a spacious family room perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Bonus room and bonus full bath compliments the lower level fitting for guests. Complete with a 3-car garage, anyone else thinking golf simulator? Private deck for outdoor dining, and serene tree-lined grounds all within the sought after Greenwich school district and just 35 miles from Manhattan.
