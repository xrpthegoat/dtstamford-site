About this home

Tucked at the end of a cul-de-sac in one of Wilton's most convenient locations, 8 Maplewood Lane is designed for the way you want to live-easy, active, and connected to nature. With 5-6 bedrooms, a backyard pool, and a rare rear border on a protected nature preserve, this home offers something that's hard to find: lasting privacy with a true "no neighbors behind you" setting. Start your mornings with coffee and birdsong, knowing the view beyond your yard will remain untouched. Spend afternoons poolside, grilling, entertaining, or unwinding after a busy day-this is a property that makes it simple to enjoy summer at home. When the seasons change, the preserve backdrop adds year-round beauty, from fresh spring greens to crisp fall color. Inside, the generous bedroom count creates flexibility for a variety of needs-guest space, work-from-home options, a hobby room, or a dedicated play or fitness area-while still keeping everyone comfortable. And when it's time to head out, you're perfectly placed close to Wilton schools, town amenities, and Rolling Hills Country Club, making everyday errands, activities, and meetups feel effortless. If you've been looking for a home that blends neighborhood convenience, outdoor lifestyle, and enduring privacy, 8 Maplewood Lane is one you'll want to experience in person. 2.75% assumable mortgage until 2028 and 40K credit at closing toward a new roof.
