About this home

Set on a lovely property with a tranquil and pretty backyard setting, this charming and updated ranch offers the perfect blend of convenience and comfort. Ideally located just a short walk or bike ride to the train station and the vibrant restaurants, shops, and amenities of Westport's sought-after Saugatuck neighborhood. Freshly painted and thoughtfully refreshed, this sun-filled home features 3 bedrooms and 3 full -newly renovated- bathrooms, including a spacious primary suite with a vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet, and private bath. Hardwood floors, central air conditioning, beautifully updated bathrooms, and generous living, dining, and family rooms create an inviting and functional layout for everyday living. Step outside to a private deck overlooking the peaceful backyard-an ideal setting for relaxing, entertaining, or simply enjoying the surroundings. A rare rental opportunity in one of Westport's most convenient locations.
