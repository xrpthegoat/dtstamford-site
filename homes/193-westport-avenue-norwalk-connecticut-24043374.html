About this home

FULLY RENOVATED 1-Bedroom Apartment with Private Entrance - HEAT and HOT WATER INCLUDED! This bright and sunny 1-bedroom, 1-bath unit features a private entrance and has been fully renovated to provide a modern, comfortable living space. Step into a welcoming entryway/mudroom area with carpeted stairwell leading to an open-concept living space featuring high ceilings, built-in AC, and contemporary lighting. The spacious living room and updated kitchen boast new stainless steel appliances (refrigerator, electric stove, built-in microwave), sleek countertops, and beautiful laminate flooring. The bedroom provides plush carpeting, a ceiling fan, walk-in closet, and its own window A/C unit. The bathroom has been completely updated with new tile work, sleek fixtures, a chic vanity, and modern lighting. Additional features include a dedicated/assigned parking space, easy access to major highways (I-95, Merritt Parkway), and proximity to Stew Leonard's, restaurants, and public transportation. Heat and hot water are included; Tenants are responsible for electric and cable/Internet. ONLY 1 MONTH SECURITY DEPOSIT required. Landlord prefers 2 year lease. Available October 1. No pets and no smoking.
