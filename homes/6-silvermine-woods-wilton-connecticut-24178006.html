About this home

Elevated golf course living on the 15th green. This stunning Silvermine Woods home was completely reimagined with high-end finishes throughout and delivers sweeping golf course and treetop views from every room. The showstopping custom Deane kitchen features honed marble countertops, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Wolf range, and Miele steam oven. The primary suite is a true retreat with a soaring vaulted ceiling and two spacious walk-in closets. All baths have been fully renovated; both upper level full baths feature heated flooring, and the spa-like primary bath adds a deep soaker tub and double vanity. An additional room above the garage adds flexible living space with endless possibilities. Hardwood floors gleam throughout the home while all-new windows enhance the picturesque vistas of the fairway and putting green. Fully finished lower level features a spacious bedroom, full bath and ample storage. A whole-home generator plus propane heating system ensure efficient, year-round comfort. The redone deck is perfect for entertaining overlooking the course. One of just 24 homes at Silvermine Golf Club. Full membership, clubhouse access, and all 27 holes available to members and their guests, no initiation or annual membership dues required. Every detail has been taken care of for you.
