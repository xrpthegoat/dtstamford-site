About this home

2026 Riverside Harborview New Construction by Frattaroli Construction Group. A statement in modern luxury, this 6-bedroom, 6.1-bath residence situated on a desirable cul-de-sac in sought-after Riverside, CT. Embrace the coveted Riverside lifestyle, with the train station within walking distance and easy access to beaches, dining, shopping. Blending contemporary architecture with timeless design and details through-out, this exceptional home offers refined living across four finished levels. A multi-slide glass wall seamlessly connects indoor-outdoor living, opening to an expansive deck's ideal for gatherings and entertaining with harbor and sunset views. A dramatic sweeping staircase, walls of glass and white oak flooring create a light-filled, contemporary aesthetic. Open living spaces feature a Calacatta marble fireplace and custom white oak built-ins. The designer kitchen showcases a 9-foot marble island, white rift oak cabinetry, butler's and walk-in pantries, and a curated wine display. The stunning primary suite offers soaring vaulted ceilings, a custom dressing closet and a spa-inspired Calacatta Gold bath with soaking tub and dual vanities. An en-suite first-floor bedroom, private office with custom built-ins, third-floor lounge with balcony, media/playroom, 2-car garage and level yard complete this striking modern family residence.
