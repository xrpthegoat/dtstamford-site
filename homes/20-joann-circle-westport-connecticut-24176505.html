About this home

Enjoy Summer in Westport CT. Located on a quiet neighborhood street, this 5-bedroom home spans a full acre, offering a tranquil setting for your summer retreat. It's ideally positioned near all town amenities, perfect for enjoying Westport's lively summer scene. The home welcomes you with a spacious living room, large windows highlighting scenic views, and a dining area leading to a sunlit deck-great for relaxing summer evenings. The kitchen features custom cabinetry, stone countertops, a center island, and high-end Thermador appliances, complemented by a bay window that brings in abundant natural light. Next to the kitchen, the family room provides extra space for relaxation and entertainment. The lower level includes a versatile bedroom or office, a full bathroom, large pantry and substantial storage. This floor also has a large living room and playroom leading to a large stone patio, perfect for outdoor dining and activities. Upstairs boasts 3 additional bedrooms, including one with an en-suite. The primary suite is on a separate upper level, providing privacy and luxury with a vaulted ceiling, sitting area, large walk-in closet, and a deluxe bathroom featuring a hydro tub. Choose this home for your summer stay in Westport, where convenience meets quiet comfort! Available July and August 2026.
