About this home

Location!! Location!! Move in Ready!! Charming Colonial beautifully manicured located in a sought-after Mid Ridges. Freshly painted, close to schools, restaurants, shopping and highways. This home offers three bedrooms, and two full updated bathrooms with heated floors and hardwood floors throughout. Large primary bedroom on the main level with a walk-in closet, formal dining room and galley kitchen with some updates. Bright and sunny living room with nicely crafted built-ins. Two spacious bedrooms on the upper level with ample closet space. In the lower level is a freshly painted laundry area with a partially finished basement. Create a space that suits your needs for example: workshop or gym. Great storage area. The breezeway is a great place to sit and unwind. Attached one car garage. The backyard offers a beautiful flagstone patio to enjoy barbequing and gatherings. It offers a nice level backyard with manicured trees and shrubs that border for privacy.
