About this home

Beautiful first floor unit at 260 Riverside Ave new residential development. Private access to large 36ft covered porch. Launch kayaks/canoes, or enjoy afternoon fishing on Saugatuck river. Located in heart of Westport, walkable to Levitt Pavilion, KHS/SES sport fields, playgrounds and Westport Public library. Water views from every window. Across farmers market, Gilbertites, Birchwood country club and shopping. Short walk to MTA train station, Saugatuck Rowing club, Rive bistro, and so much more. Minutes drive from Compo Beach and Longshore, and SONO mall. Open floor plan, new kitchen and bath with Quartz counters, new stainless steel appliances, washer/dryer in unit, wired for internet, wired security system. Check out virtual tour.
