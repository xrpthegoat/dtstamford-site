About this home

Classic 5-bedroom Colonial in the coveted Behind the Y neighborhood-just a short walk to schools, the YMCA, and Waveny Park. With over 3,500 sq. ft. of living space, this home features a front-to-back living room with fireplace, a sun-filled dining room, and a kitchen that opens to a step-down family room with fireplace and French doors leading to the terrace and private backyard. A study with fireplace provides a quiet retreat, while the flexible layout includes an au pair or in-law suite with sitting room and separate entrance. This home also offers a lower-level playroom and attached potting shed. With great bones and a fantastic location, it offers an excellent opportunity for updates and personal touches to make it your own. With sidewalks to town, you cannot beat this location.
