About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST BY MONDAY AT NOON - Move in Ready and Best Wilton Commuting Location. This stunning 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home has been meticulously renovated with high-end finishes and timeless design. Custom millwork and moldings enhance every room, while abundant natural light creates a warm, inviting feel. The chef's kitchen features a striking marble island offering ample seating, custom cabinetry and high-end appliances: Subzero fridge, Wolf gas range with griddle, Miele dishwasher and Shaws sink. Off the kitchen, the mudroom includes custom cubbies, a large closet, and a built-in desk, with access to the attached garage, Ipe wood deck, and the main-floor laundry room with utility sink. Spacious living and dining areas and screened in porch for relaxed gatherings or elegant entertaining. Downstairs a finished basement offers extra living spaces. The private bedroom level boasts a luxurious primary suite with spa-like bath and ample closet space, along with three additional bedrooms. Every detail reflects quality craftsmanship-from copper gutters and copper caps on railings to the durable Ipe deck overlooking the private backyard. Added conveniences include underground utility wires that preserve the property's beauty and reliability, plus wiring for a portable generator to ensure comfort year-round. Located in desirable South Wilton, this move-in-ready home is close to commuting locations, Sono Train and Westport train stations, Rt 7, the Merrit Parkway and I-9e
