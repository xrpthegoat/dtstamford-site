About this home

Turnkey 2013 Contemporary Oasis | 5 Beds | Pool | Prime Westport Flawless execution meets ultimate convenience at 4 Turkey Hill Lane. Built in 2013 and meticulously maintained, this 5,318sqft. contemporary offers the ultimate turnkey experience in Westport's highly sought-after Long Lots neighborhood. The Interiors: An open-concept, light-filled layout designed for modern living. The centerpiece is a spectacular 2-story great room with a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace. The seamless flow leads right into a high-end chef's kitchen featuring Sub-Zero appliances, double wall ovens, a wine chiller, and a massive walk-in pantry. Upstairs, the primary suite delivers with dual walk-in closets, a luxe en-suite, and a private balcony. Three additional upper-level bedrooms feature smart Jack-and-Jill setups, while a massive third-floor rec room handles everything from billiards to a home gym. The Grounds: 1.07 level acres of unparalleled outdoor living. The backyard is fully equipped for summer: a heated gunite pool and spa, a custom stone patio with a pergola, and a dramatic outdoor stone fireplace. Beyond the pool, the expansive flat lawn rolls gently down to a private, scenic brook. The Lifestyle: Active solar features, a 3-car attached garage, and city utilities ensure effortless homeownership.
