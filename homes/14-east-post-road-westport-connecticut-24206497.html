About this home

Enjoy the best of downtown Westport from this bright, spacious studio overlooking the Saugatuck River. The open layout offers flexibility for distinct living, dining, and sleeping areas, while large windows bring in abundant natural light and peaceful river views. The kitchen features ample cabinetry, a tiled backsplash, full-size appliances, and a convenient breakfast bar. A substantial double-door closet provides excellent storage, while the full bath includes a wide vanity and tub-and-shower combination. Step outside and enjoy immediate access to downtown coffee shops, restaurants, boutiques, and everyday conveniences, with Bedford Square just a short walk away. An inviting in-town home combining water views, flexible living space, and a truly walkable Westport lifestyle
