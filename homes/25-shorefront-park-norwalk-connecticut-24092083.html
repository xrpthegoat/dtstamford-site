About this home

A very special offering, the first time on the market in almost 50 years! Don't miss this rare opportunity to create your dream home with abundant water views in coveted Shorefront Park. A quiet community of 80 homes where seasonal gatherings are held at the two private waterfront parks owned by the neighborhood association. Wake up to a spectacular sunrise over the water every morning. Store and launch your kayaks at the "boat lot", just a short walk from your home. The South Norwalk Boat Club and marinas along Water Street are just outside the neighborhood. When you're not enjoying the water, there is so much to explore in South Norwalk! Power walk to the new Woodward Park pickleball courts. Stroll into SoNo for brunch or dinner or to the South Norwalk Metro North station for a trip into NYC. Wander to the soon-to-be-developed Manresa Island Park which will offer walking trails and 1.75 miles of public waterfront access. A year-round vacation lifestyle can be yours!
