About this home

Newly Adjusted/Priced to Sell! A unique Cranbury find ~ This beautifully updated Colonial sited on over 1 acre of meticulously maintained lawn & gardens, offers 4 BRs, 2.5 baths & NOT to be missed is a rare detached 4 bay oversized barn/garage w/high ceilings & doors & an upper level that offers over 700+ sq feet ready to be finished as an income producing in-law apartment or addt'l living space. Step inside to an easy flow of rooms, formal LR w/a wood-burning FP, spacious & sunny window lined FR, formal dining just off the kitchen w/corner hutch & sliders to generous sized deck leading to a lush yard, cheerful kitchen w/stainless steel appliances & breakfast bar. Adjacent to the kitchen is a mud/laundry room w/access to outback, powder room & large attached 2 car garage completes. Upstairs you will find a primary BR with multi-closets, W-I-C & en-suite full bth, 3 addt'l light & bright BRs & a guest bath w/tub. Attic w/pull-down stairs provide for great storage. Some of the home's features include a full house generator, crown molding, security system, recessed lighting, triple pane windows, newer carpet ~ a full list of improvements is available. Room 15+ cars. Simply move in, unpack & enjoy! Life is easy on Granite Drive. Convenient to all area amenities: schools, shops, restaurants & more! A Must See!
