About this home

Set up high in a quiet corner, bathed in light, and overlooking the tranquil pond, this ranch-style unit features 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and an open living/dining area with a gas fireplace and new sliding doors to the balcony, the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the serene view. In turn-key condition, it was meticulously and thoughtfully updated by opening it to the studs, and sparing no expense, rebuilt by its current owner ensuring top-quality materials and craftsmanship. With all updated electrical, plumbing, natural gas heating and hot water, central A/C, and mechanical systems, this unit provides modern conveniences and efficiency. The custom-built kitchen, bathrooms, and millwork add a tailored touch, while a new walk-in closet in the primary bedroom provides enhanced storage. Hardwood floors were installed, and both floors and ceilings were insulated throughout for maximum soundproofing, creating a peaceful, private retreat. Later, all windows and the deck were replaced too. Freshly painted, the property is move-in ready for its next owner to enjoy. A garage with large overhead storage is included, and ample visitor parking is conveniently located near its entrance. Nestled in a prestigious, highly sought-after, and well-maintained complex, homeowners enjoy great amenities, including a tennis court, swimming pool, garden, pet walking areas, and even a shortcut path to the Rowayton train station for easy commuting.
