About this home

Set on a private acre in Wilton, this 2001 Colonial offers 4 bedrooms and 4,228 square feet of finished living space, with the versatility to suit every stage of life. The eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, a center island, double wall ovens, and pantry, opening to the family room and an expansive deck overlooking the backyard and seasonal hillside views. Formal living and dining rooms offer inviting spaces to entertain, with a fireplace and tray ceiling. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a walk-in closet and bath with soaking tub, dual vanities, and separate shower, plus three additional bedrooms and second-floor laundry. The finished walk-out lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a fifth bedroom or home office, recreation room, and full bath with steam shower, along with potential for in-law or au pair accommodations. Above the 3-car garage, a finished bonus room provides even more possibilities for a home gym, studio, playroom, or media room. Enjoy the privacy of North Wilton while being just 0.6 miles from Georgetown's restaurants, cafes, and shops, with convenient access to Cannondale and Branchville Metro-North stations for NYC commuting. Additional highlights include public water, a full roof replacement in 2021, and proximity to parks and hiking trails.
