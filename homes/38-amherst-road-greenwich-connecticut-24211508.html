About this home

Thoughtfully updated and truly move-in ready, 38 Amherst Road offers a fresh, design-forward take on Riverside living. The light-filled main level brings together the kitchen, dining and living spaces in an easy, open flow, with direct access to an expansive deck that extends the living space outdoors. Four bedrooms are thoughtfully arranged across the upper levels, including a private primary suite with a spacious walk-in closet and en-suite bath. A generous family room provides a separate space to relax, work or gather, while the finished lower level offers additional flexibility for a playroom, gym or home office. Recent improvements throughout the home pair a clean, considered aesthetic with everyday functionality, creating a home that feels both polished and comfortable. The home offers multiple spaces for entertaining, playing and enjoying the outdoors. Convenient to schools, train, shopping and everything Greenwich has to offer, 38 Amherst Road is ready for its next chapter. Agent/Owner
