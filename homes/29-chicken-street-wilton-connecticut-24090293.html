About this home

Peaceful and Serene!! An exciting opportunity to enjoy renting a 1950's home built by famous architect Julian Whittlesey . Best known for his New York City's Manhattan House, 240 Central Park South and the "new town" of Reston outside of Washington D.C. This rental offer's use of the "balloon loft " which served as a construction site for hydrogen balloons. The house is completely renovated with new floors, windows ,updated bathrooms, a new kitchen with new appliances and cabinets. The house has a new roof and has been painted inside and out. The loft is separate from the main house and offers an option for a third bedroom with a small kitchen and refrigerator. The main house is about 1,274 square feet and the "balloon loft" is an additional 576 square feet. Enjoy the quiet of nature on this property as it is surrounded by land trust. Tenant will have use to the pool at the main house. Move right in and enjoy the beautiful gardens and privacy!
