About this home

Pristine cape on a quiet street in charming Rowayton. Situated on a corner lot this 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath home is turn key. The first floor boasts a stunning eat-in kitchen, living room with fire place and spacious family room along with 2 bedrooms and 1.5 baths that have been newly renovated. Sliders lead to a beautiful deck area with steps down to the fully fenced, beautifully landscaped backyard with stone walls and a fire pit area. Upstairs is the primary bedroom suite with sitting area and flexible office space. The spacious en-suite bath and walk in closet complete the second floor. Too many updates to list, come see for yourself.
