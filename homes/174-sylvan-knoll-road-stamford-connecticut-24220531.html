About this home

Gorgeous move-in ready 2 Bed 1 Bath in the highly sought after Sylvan Knoll complex. This beautiful townhouse style home is sure to please from the moment you walk through the front door! Inviting, sun-filled living room flows nicely into the updated eat-in kitchen, which boasts high-end SS appliances, granite countertops, tile backsplash, dishwasher, & tons of cabinet space. Enjoy the wamer months relaxing and unwinding on the private patio located right off the kitchen. As you make your way upstairs, you'll find 2 spacious bedrooms w/ ample closet space & full bath with lovely vanity & shower/tub. Lower level is the ultimate flex space to be utilized as a family room/den, workout area, or home office. This unbeatable home also features central A/C, newer LG washer/dryer, gleaming hardwood floors, & recessed lighting. Close proximity to Downtown Stamford, Metro-North Station, Chelsea Piers, I-95, parks, beach, shopping, & top notch restaurants. A TRUE MUST SEE!!!
