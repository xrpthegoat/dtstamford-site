About this home

Commuter's Dream in East Norwalk - Walk to Beach, Park, and Train! Move right into this sun-filled, freshly painted townhome in the highly desirable Cottage Common community! Ideally located on a quiet street, this pristine two-bedroom unit provides the perfect blend of convenience and coastal charm. Step inside to find high ceilings and an open-concept main level featuring a stylish kitchen with ample cabinet space and a huge pantry. The spacious living and dining area is bathed in natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. A half bath is also conveniently located on the first level. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, both with vaulted ceilings and large, lighted closets, along with an updated full bathroom and an in-unit laundry closet for ultimate convenience. Enjoy the ease of private parking, including a one-car garage, an additional reserved parking spot (#203), and a heated storage unit. 2nd floor unit. Need outdoor space? A dedicated BBQ area in the rear is perfect for relaxing and entertaining. Unbeatable Location! 5-minute walk to the East Norwalk Metro-North station - an easy commute to NYC! Steps from Veterans Park & Long Island Sound - enjoy waterfront walks and outdoor activities. Minutes from restaurants, shops, and the beach - embrace the vibrant coastal lifestyle. Don't miss this opportunity to live in a beautifully maintained, move-in-ready townhome in one of East Norwalk's best locations!
