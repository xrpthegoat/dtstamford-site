About this home

Location, location!!!! Beautifully situated in the sought-after area of Newfield, this front to back split level boasts an open floor plan, well proportioned rooms, hardwood floors, a dramatic living room/dining room with a fireplace and vaulted ceilings, gas heat, C/A and City utilities. The eat-in kitchen has newer cabinets, wood-like flooring and Corian countertops. The office/possible 4th bedroom and family room are on the entry level with the half bath. Two staircases lead to the main level with the living room, dining room and kitchen. Upstairs are the 3 nicely sized bedrooms and an updated full bath. Another full bath can be added in the primary bedroom walk-in coset as there is another closet there. The unfinished basement can be finished, too, if more space is needed. The .41 acre wooded parcel is lush and filled with mature trees and plantings. Shed has not been used for many years. Walk to almost EVERYTHING needed for everyday living and enjoy the coveted Newfield School, public and private recreational facilities (including a public golf course), houses of worship and more!! A great opportunity to make this home a true showplace with just a bit of TLC. *** Agents, Final and Bests are to be delivered to me by 5PM on Saturday, 9/29. Thank you.
