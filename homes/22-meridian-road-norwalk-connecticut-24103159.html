About this home

A true seaside gem just steps from Rowayton's Bayley Beach and the Rowayton Beach Association's private beach and clubhouse. This sun-filled, fully updated home offers a flexible 5-6 bedroom layout across three spacious levels. The main floor features a large eat-in kitchen with breakfast nook, dining room, living room, family room, and a first-floor flex space suitable for bedroom/office/playroom/gym with full bath. Upstairs includes three additional bedrooms, a home office or flex 5th bedroom, and laundry. The third floor is dedicated to an oversized primary suite with stunning views of Long Island Sound and Green's Ledge Lighthouse. Outside, enjoy a private backyard with kayak storage, outdoor shower, and a spacious patio perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Sited on one of the largest lots in the RBA, this coastal retreat offers the best of beachside living just minutes from the sand. Best yet, only one hour Metro North train to NYC!
