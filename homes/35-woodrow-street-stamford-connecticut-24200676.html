About this home

Spacious and updated three-bedroom rental in Stamford's Cove neighborhood offering approximately 1,138 square feet of comfortable second-floor living space. Unit 2 features hardwood flooring throughout, an eat-in kitchen with a new dishwasher, and a newly renovated full bathroom. New central air provides year-round comfort, while the convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer makes everyday living easy. The unit also includes two parking spaces in the driveway, a valuable feature for a three-bedroom rental. Water is included in the monthly rent, with the tenant responsible for gas and electric. Located close to Cove Island Beach, this home provides convenient access to one of Stamford's most popular waterfront destinations while remaining well positioned for commuting and everyday needs. I-95 is nearby, offering easy access throughout Stamford and lower Fairfield County. Available immediately at $3,500 per month. One-year lease required. Two-month security deposit. Applicants must have a minimum 680 credit score and are subject to standard credit and background screening. No pets and no smoking permitted.
