About this home

Available immediately for yearly, unfurnished lease. This wonderful, sun-filled Townhouse at Chesterfield II is a spacious, welcoming home. The open layout includes a white kitchen with abundant cabinets, large counter space, flowing into a dining area, and a large great room space. The first bedroom with a full bath, balcony, and walk-in closet is on this main level. Upstairs, a large loft overlooking the great room with a skylight, closet space and laundry room with full-size washer and dryer can be a wonderful home office, den, exercise room or hobby space. The primary bedroom suite has a large full bath and spacious walk-in closet. Professionally cleaned and ready to move in. There is an enclosed parking garage with reserved space for this unit, and a locked storage area. This entire home is in gleaming, move-in ready condition. Experience the comfort of this highly-desirable home available for lease. Tenant must have excellent credit.
