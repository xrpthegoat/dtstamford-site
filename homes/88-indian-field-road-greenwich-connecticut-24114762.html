About this home

Welcome to 88 Indian Field Road, where convenience meets comfort in this updated 3 bed, 3 bath cape. Seconds from the Cos Cob Train station, Greenwich schools, parks, and dozens of dining & shopping options, this home offers unparalleled accessibility. Highlights of the surprisingly spacious layout include the living room's w/b fireplace, renovated kitchen and inviting family room perfect for gathering with loved ones. The floor plan is flexible with baths on each of the home's 3 levels supporting a variety of uses including WFH, in-law or au pair suites. Outside, a relaxing backyard oasis awaits with a deck off the family room with stairs down to the patio and pool areas. Super low taxes, R-12 zoning, & 1,000+ additional buildable square feet only add to 88 Indian Field Road's appeal!
