About this home

Fully renovated caretaker's estate house with 3/4+ bedrooms and 3.5 baths on 2 flat acres on New Canaan's West Side less than a mile from Talmadge Hill Station. First floor has a 2-level foyer with stone fireplace, high-end appliances in kitchen and laundry room with dual hook-ups, family room/living room with second fireplace and large bonus room with back staircase to upper and lower levels. Upper level has 3 bedrooms and a flexible office/nursery room, 2 full baths with heated floors and generous closet spaces. Lower level includes wine room with wine cooler, and private possible 4th large bedroom/playroom with full bath, multiple storage closets with a keyed walkout slider. Lovely first floor outdoor patio includes lighted pergola and privacy screens. First floor entrances are covered with enclosed mudroom off 3 car garage. Fabulous walking neighborhood. Lawncare included. Owner is completing work in garage including new doors and openers and minor painting through the house; garage doors to be installed by August 20th. The front walkways are currently being reconstructed and appointed with new landscaping.
