About this home

Welcome to a masterfully restored 1,683 sq ft ranch with in-law apartment that combines timeless architecture with modern sophistication. 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths! Every surface, system, and space has been thoughtfully renewed-this is home where quality and attention to detail are evident in every room. **EXTERIOR EXCELLENCE** A freshly painted white colonial facade with black shutters anchors a professionally landscaped setting. Five-spot driveway and stone pathways flow seamlessly around the property. The architectural highlight: a dedicated insulated workshop with power -ideal for projects, hobbies, or creative pursuits. New roof provides decades of protection. Mature specimen trees, manicured shrubs, and vibrant seasonal plantings frame the home like a gallery setting. **CULINARY CENTERPIECE** The heart of the home is a stunning kitchen remodel featuring premium Bertazzoni stove and refrigerator, crisp white cabinetry, gorgeous granite counters with subway tile and contrasting band backsplash, and stainless steel fixtures. The design flows naturally from prep to cooking to dining, with abundant natural light and a sophisticated island layout. **REFINED LIVING SPACES** Hardwood flooring throughout the main living areas. A generous living room anchors the upper floor with fireplace, bay window, and abundant light.
