About this home

Beautifully renovated top-floor corner condo featuring two bedrooms and sweeping river views. This sun-splashed 3rd-floor unit offers an open, airy layout with vaulted ceilings, skylights, and a cozy wood-burning fireplace. The spacious living and dining areas each open to a large private deck-ideal for morning coffee or evening wine while listening to the tranquil sound of the river below. The fully renovated kitchen and bath showcase stylish finishes, modern lighting, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances, complemented by gleaming hardwood floors throughout. Enjoy the convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer, a generous walk-in closet, and additional storage with custom built-ins. Located steps from Scalzi Park, tennis courts, and a golf course, and just minutes to shopping, dining, and the train station. Two parking spaces included. Experience the perfect blend of privacy and convenience-a rare opportunity to live in one of Stamford's most desirable and accessible locations.
