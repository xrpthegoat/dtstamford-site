About this home

Move right into this beautifully updated 2-bedroom townhouse featuring a stunning kitchen and a gorgeous renovated bathroom. Recent upgrades include new windows, new HVAC, and a new 40-gallon hot water heater. The partially finished lower level offers flexible additional living space-perfect for a home office, gym, or playroom. Enjoy added privacy and abundant natural light, along with a spacious back patio, a remodeled entry with a charming fountain and Adirondack chairs, plus a garden area overlooking a picturesque grassy courtyard-ideal for summer entertaining. Convenient parking is just steps from the unit. Located in the highly desirable Sylvan Knoll community, just off Exit 9 on I-95, and within walking distance to Cove Island Park, the beach, and Chelsea Piers. A short drive brings you to downtown, shopping, and the train station. Set on 13 park-like acres with sidewalks throughout, this pet-friendly, owner-occupied community offers the added benefit of real estate taxes included in the common charges.
