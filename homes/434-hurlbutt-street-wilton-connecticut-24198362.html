About this home

Charming 18th-century antique Colonial set on 7.25 acres, offering exceptional historic character and an abundance of space. With approximately 3,410 square feet of living area, this distinctive five-bedroom, four-full-bath home features seven fireplaces, hand-hewn beams, wide-plank chestnut floors, and numerous built-ins throughout. Generously sized rooms and impressive ceiling heights enhance the home's spacious feel, while three separate stairways provide access to the second floor. The finished third floor offers two additional rooms, providing flexible space for a home office, recreation, or other uses. The driveway is shared with tenants of the cottage and barn. Landlord provides grounds maintenance and snow removal for added convenience. Ideally located in Cannondale, close to the train station, schools, and area amenities. A rare opportunity to enjoy the warmth, craftsmanship, and character of a historic New England home in a beautiful 7.25-acre setting.
