About this home

Currently under construction at 18 Singing Woods Rd in Silvermine. This contemporary Cottage is available late 2025. Greyrock Homes award winning developer of "The Cottages" at Fullin Road and Richards Ave has taken their successful design to this lovely secluded rear 4 plus acre property. The home features exceptional design, modern amenities,Hardy board siding and energy efficiency. Convenient 1st floor living at its best, with luxurious primary suite with spa feel bathroom, office, laundry, 1/2 bathroom and cozy screened in porch. Dramatic 9' ceilings in most rooms and gorgeous hardwood flooring throughout. The Great room boasts a dramatic fireplace and kitchen with bright white custom cabinetry, large island, deluxe appliances, pantry and laundry room. A separate dining room with cathedral ceiling offers spectacular views of the property. Upstairs are 2 spacious bedrooms, well appointed bath, a spectacular primary suite with large closet and spacious bathroom. The grounds to be beautifully landscaped with irrigation system and approved location for a pool. Historic Silvermine offers proximity to many close by attractions and is conveniently located near shopping and all major highways. This home is in the process of being built.
