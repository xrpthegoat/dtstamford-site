About this home

THE PERFECT SEASIDE ESCAPE IN HARBOR VIEW! Discover the magic of Harbor View. Tucked within this sought-after private waterfront community, this charming 2-bedroom, 2-bath seaside cottage offers the quintessential coastal escape-filled with natural light, classic beach-house character, and an easy, relaxed sense of style. Thoughtfully designed for one-floor living, the cottage offers the ease and convenience so many buyers value. The inviting sunroom and spacious open floor plan seamlessly connect the living room, dining room/family room, and eat-in kitchen. Soaring ceilings, beautiful cabinetry, and a generous kitchen island create a warm and welcoming setting for everyday living and gathering with family and friends. Two comfortable bedrooms and two full baths are conveniently located on the same level, offering both privacy and accessibility. And a 1-car attached garage. Outside, the private backyard becomes an extension of the home during the warm summer months-perfect for morning coffee, afternoon gatherings, or lingering dinners with friends. And then there's the water. Step outside and you're just moments from the beach, where you can swim, launch a kayak or paddleboard, enjoy the docks and moorings, or simply breathe in the salt air and take in the incredible birdlife. But what makes Harbor View truly special is the community itself. Established in 1904, this private waterfront neighborhood has a cherished tradition of friendship, fun, and community spirit.
