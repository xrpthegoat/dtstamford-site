About this home

Location and charm with serene tidal views of seagrass and winged wildlife make this spacious Central Greenwich home a nature lovers paradise. There is a private dock to launch your kyack. Across from scenic Bruce Park with paths, tennis, playing fields and bocce. Deeded walking access to Mead Point Beach in a private gated association. Renovated and new mechanical system. Conveniently located to everything Greenwich has to offer. Just minutes to the train, highway, Greenwich Ave with restaurants and shops, Island Ferries and Bruce Museum and playground.
