About this home

Move right into this beautifully maintained one-bedroom ranch-style condominium, tucked away in a peaceful complex yet conveniently located near restaurants, shopping, parks, the train station, and major commuter routes. This bright and inviting home features hardwood floors throughout and a spacious open-concept living and dining area-perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen offers ample white shaker cabinetry and newer appliances. The generously sized bedroom features recessed lighting and excellent closet space, while a spacious full bath completes the unit. Additional highlights include wall-mounted air-conditioning units, one assigned parking space, visitor parking, and a private 10' 8' storage area. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable, low-maintenance living in a serene and convenient setting. Schedule your private showing before it's gone!
