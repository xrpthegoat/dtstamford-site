About this home

Welcome to this rare 3 bedroom, 3.5 bath townhouse! Located in a highly sought after complex in West Norwalk. Enjoy spacious living and outstanding convenience in this gorgeous unit. Offering 3 full levels of comfortable living, along with a partial lower level that provides ample storage & the in unit laundry. Greeted by a warm and inviting open floor plan on the main level that flows seamlessly from the living room to the dining area and through sliders leading to the back porch. Hardwood floors run throughout the unit, adding to its charm and appeal. Key features include a fully renovated kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances, recessed lighting, and a new central air unit installed in 2022. The 2nd level offers 2 bedrooms, 1 full bath, one is the primary suite featuring a full bath and two closets. The 3rd level provides a private space with its own full bath & a dedicated work area. Additional highlights include an attached 2-car garage with direct access to home, in unit laundry, and low common charges. Enjoy long summer afternoons on the front or in the privacy of the back porch & the convenient location close to dining, shopping, public transportation. This immaculate townhouse is a fantastic opportunity for an owner occupant or investor alike. A truly rare offering you won't want to miss. Seller to find suitable housing.
