About this home

Very spacious and updated 2-bedroom, 2-full bath condo located in the well-maintained Ledgebrook community in West Norwalk. Thoughtfully updated with a refreshed kitchen, recessed lighting, and a newly renovated bathroom, this home offers both comfort and functionality. The oversized main living area opens to a private balcony, creating a bright and inviting space ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The primary bedroom suite features a full bath, walk-in closet, and an additional storage room. The second bedroom includes an adjoining space perfect for a home office, reading nook, or sitting area. Full-size laundry is conveniently located on the main level along with another dedicated storage room. Residents of Ledgebrook enjoy a lifestyle centered around convenience and wellness with access to a clubhouse, pool, indoor hot tub, sauna, and fitness center. Ideally situated close to I-95, the SoNo Collection, restaurants, shopping, and everyday conveniences including Costco, Wegmans, Lowe's, and more. Strong credit, verified income/employment, and references required.
