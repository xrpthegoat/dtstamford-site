About this home

A Vintage Jewel Reimagined for Modern Living! Step into the embrace of timeless elegance when you enter this distinguished Colonial tucked away on a lush, landscaped 1.8 acres in coveted N. Stamford. This stunner weaves the sophistication of arched doorways, built-ins, & cozy fireplaces w/contemporary comfort at every turn. The distinguished living room w/fp & French doors is a beautiful space to welcome guests. You will love the impressive one-of-a-kind family room w/huge fp, vaulted ceiling, & french doors. The style continues into the formal dining rm that opens to a space perfect for your barware. You will instantly feel the warmth of the well-appointed eat-in-kitchen equipped w/luxury appliances by GE Monogram, Thermador & double dishwasher drawers by Fisher & Paykel. Upstairs you will find the primary bedroom w/a primary bathroom that will take your breath away! It's like walking into your own personal spa! Both the primary bedroom and primary bathroom are toasty w/radiant floor heating. 3 additional bedrooms and a hall bathroom complete the upper level. The lower level is used as a gym, however, you can make it whatever you love. Vacation at home in the backyard adorned w/multiple patios, mature trees, stunning plantings and lots of privacy. There's even room for a pool! This gorgeous home offers rare character and presence-a perfect canvas for buyers seeking a home w/personality, space to roam, & room to grow. Welcome to the next chapter in this home's enduring story.
