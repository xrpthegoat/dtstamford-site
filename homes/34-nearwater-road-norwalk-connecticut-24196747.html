About this home

Welcome to 34 Nearwater Road, an exquisite home in the coveted Pine Point Assoc., just steps from Crescent Beach. This turnkey gem offers year-round water views & a seamless blend of luxury and comfort. The light-filled interior boasts soaring ceilings & an open floor plan that feels both elegant and inviting. The living room, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, exudes charm, while the formal dining room shines w/ sophisticated coffered ceilings. At the heart of the home is a chef's kitchen featuring state-of-the-art appliances - including 2 refrigerators and dishwashers, perfect for culinary adventures. Adjacent spaces include a bright office & a chic bar area, making entertaining effortless. A refined powder room completes the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat with a cozy gas fireplace, 2 walk-in closets, and a spa-like bath with a tub. Three additional ensuite bedrooms ensure privacy for family & guests, while the laundry room adds convenience. The 3rd floor, w/ breathtaking water views of LIS & Farm Creek, features a full bathroom & versatile space ideal for work, fitness, or play. On the lower level, a large mudroom features ample storage & the large, extra-deep two-car garage provides easy access. Outside, the oversized front patio w/ stunning Long Island Sound water views, is perfect for morning coffee or evening gatherings around the gas fire pit. Enjoy coastal living within walking distance of the beach, town, & Rowayton's vibrant amenities.
