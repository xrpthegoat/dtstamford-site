About this home

This beautifully maintained 2 bedroom, 1 bath first floor apartment sits in the heart of East Norwalk, just minutes from the beach, Marvin Beach, and all the best neighborhood spots. Commuters will love the quick bike ride to the East Norwalk train station and the easy access to I-95, Route 7, and SONO. Inside, you'll find classic 1920s charm paired with everyday convenience. The spacious living and dining rooms feature hardwood floors and plenty of natural light, with a stained glass detail that adds character. A small but efficient kitchen sits just beyond. Off the living room is a private enclosed porch exclusively for this unit, perfect for morning coffee, extra storage, or simply relaxing. The two bedrooms are positioned with the full bathroom between them, offering a comfortable layout for roommates or anyone seeking privacy. Additional perks include half of the basement dedicated to the tenant along with private laundry, plus two off-street parking spots behind the building. No pets. Excellent credit, references, and verifiable income required. Your East Norwalk lifestyle is calling, and this inviting space is ready when you are.
