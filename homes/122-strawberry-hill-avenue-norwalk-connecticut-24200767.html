About this home

This beautiful and classic colonial has it all and is flooded with natural light, too! Upon entry, the foyer welcomes you in and leads to a generously sized, front-to-back living room with hardwood floors, a wood burning fireplace and plenty of space to entertain. Wile the dining room features a beautiful corner hutch, with glass doors, and is adjacent to the classic kitchen complete with stainless steel appliances, granite counters, and a charming, eat-in area. The family room features heated floors and is surrounded by glass, including sliders to one of the 2 outdoor seating areas. Adjacent to the kitchen is a half bath and generous mudroom that leads to the attached 2 car garage with storage above. On the second floor, there is a generous primary bedroom with 2 closets and 2 additional bedrooms plus a full bath. The full, unfinished basement has plenty of room for a workshop, laundry or future Rec space. Sited on .42 gorgeous and extremely private and manicured acres, the backyard is made for entertaining and enjoying with patios, a playground area, vegetable garden, outdoor storage shed, manicured lawn and mature plantings. This home has been lovingly maintained and is conveniently located near on the Norwalk/Westport border within minutes of all amenities, including shopping, restaurants, and transportation! Enjoy beach rights year round, while having easy access to transportation and to local schools. This house is the one you've been waiting for!
