About this home

Location, location, location. Set on a quiet, private road, this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath split-level contemporary is tucked away on a beautifully landscaped acre, offering rare privacy and a serene setting. Filled with abundant natural light, the home is designed for comfortable living and easy entertaining. Cathedral-ceiling living and dining areas feature four skylights, a striking two-sided corner fireplace, a wall of glass, and French doors to an expansive deck overlooking the grounds. The eat-in kitchen offers skylights and its own deck, perfect for indoor-outdoor living. A spacious family room adds casual living space, and an additional room can serve as an office or au pair suite. The primary suite includes two walk-in California closets and a marble spa-like bath with a jet tub. A marble hall bath offers a tub and steam shower. The lower level includes a bonus recreation room and a dedicated wine closet. Central air throughout. A wonderful opportunity on a quiet, highly desirable street.
