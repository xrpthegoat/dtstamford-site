About this home

Welcome to 29 Three Lakes Drive, a delightful Cape Cod-style home nestled in the heart of Stamford, CT. This charming residence offers four bedrooms and two full bathrooms, providing comfortable and cozy living. The home features an attached garage, new kitchen appliances, and an expanded driveway for added convenience. The well-maintained, grassy backyard is perfect for entertaining guests or enjoying outdoor activities with family and friends. Conveniently situated just off Long Ridge Road, 29 Three Lakes Drive offers easy access to the Merritt Parkway, making commuting a breeze. The home is also close to a variety of shopping and dining options, including the new Starbucks, Frank Pepe's Pizzeria, and Shake Shack, all less than a mile away. Don't miss the opportunity to own this wonderful home in a prime Stamford location!
