About this home

A rare opportunity in space & style in highly desirable East Norwalk. Enjoy morning coffee on the inviting front porch, time at the sandy shore or a quick round of golf moments from your front door. 4 bedrooms, 4 full baths + office/den, 3 bedrooms each with en-suite baths. The 1st floor bedroom includes en-suite bath & walk-in closet, currently used as a kitchenette & can be a possible 1st-floor primary bedroom. Abundant natural light, hardwood floors & open main-floor living area creates a welcoming atmosphere. The eat-in kitchen with vaulted ceiling, newer appliances & quartz countertops, makes a wonderful gathering space. A large side deck off the family room extends the living space outdoors. The impressive top floor primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with a spacious sitting area, vaulted ceilings, walk-in closet, en-suite bath, & private balcony overlooking the golf course-the perfect place to start the day or unwind. Additional highlights: central AC, gas heat, 1st floor laundry, large basement with built-in shelving for abundant storage. New main-floor windows installed in 2021 & summer 2026, a new driveway & freshly painted exterior accents & trim. Wonderful opportunity for multi-generational living under one roof while providing comfortable & private spaces. Buyers check local municipal codes regarding potential two-family conversion. Owner/agent. More photos coming soon.
