About this home

Fantastic Investment Opportunity - Spacious 2-Family Home with Strong Income Potential! Welcome to 11 Windsor Place, a well-maintained two-family property offering exceptional versatility for investors and owner-occupants alike. This oversized multi-family features two spacious units with separate living spaces and excellent rental potential. The lower-level unit offers 2 bedrooms, 1 full bath, electric heat, and is currently rented on a month-to-month lease for $1,700 per month (current rent should be $1,950). The expansive main unit spans three finished levels and features 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and extra space for home offices, playrooms, etc, and high-efficiency gas heating/hot water system. The flexible floor plan provides abundant living space, making it ideal for larger households or multi-generational living. It's currently rented for $5,000/month. Outside, you'll find a large detached storage shed and an oversized paved driveway with off-street parking for 8+ vehicles, a rare convenience in this location. Ideally situated close to shopping, public transportation, parks, Metro-North, and major commuter routes including I-95 and Route 7, this property combines convenience with outstanding investment potential. Whether you're looking to expand your real estate portfolio or offset your mortgage by living in one unit while collecting rental income from the other, this is an opportunity you won't want to miss.
