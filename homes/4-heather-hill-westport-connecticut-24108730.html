About this home

Welcome to 4 Heather Hill in Westport! This charming 4 bedroom, 1.5 bath Cape Cod-style home is ideally located on a private road right in the heart of Central Westport. Nestled on a serene quarter-acre lot that is bordered in the rear by a classic stone wall, this property offers both privacy and promise. This home awaits your personal touches and updates. Several key improvements have already been made, including 200 amp electrical service, newer hot water heater and oil tank, roof and gutters replaced less than 5 years ago with a 50 year transferrable warranty, a new stove, range hood and a dishwasher that are less than 2 years old. With ideal proximity to the train, beaches and shopping, Westport living awaits you!
