About this home

A little more space. A little more privacy. And everything else still close. Tucked away on 3+ acres in Wilton's Silvermine area, this home offers the breathing room people come to Wilton for, with everyday conveniences surprisingly close by. A long driveway winds through the wooded acreage before the property opens to a level lawn framed by mature trees. Inside, there's an easy comfort to the way this home lives. The eat-in kitchen opens to a cozy fireside sitting room with access to the patio-a natural spot for morning coffee, homework, or unwinding at the end of the day. A spacious family room offers another place to gather, while the separate dining room is ready for holidays, dinner parties, and everything in between. Upstairs, four comfortable bedrooms create a natural separation from the main living spaces, including a primary suite with a private bath. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility for movie nights, play space, a home gym, or whatever life calls for next. Move-in ready with room to make it your own, this is a home that can evolve right along with you, with the added benefit of public water, a welcome rarity in Wilton. And perhaps the biggest surprise is just how connected you are-minutes to Wilton Center, schools, the train, New Canaan, and the Merritt Parkway. Maybe Wilton is closer than you think.
