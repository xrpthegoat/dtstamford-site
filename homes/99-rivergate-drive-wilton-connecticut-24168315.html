About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated split-level home in the highly desirable town of Wilton Thoughtfully renovated, this 3 bedroom, 3 full bath home offers a seamless blend of modern style and everyday comfort. The open-concept main level is filled with natural light and designed for today's lifestyle, connecting the living, dining, and kitchen areas with ease. The kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a spacious center island-perfect for both casual dining and entertaining. Upstairs, you'll find generously sized bedrooms, including a comfortable primary suite, along with well-appointed bathrooms that reflect the home's updated aesthetic. Set on a private, level property, the outdoor space offers a peaceful retreat with plenty of room for relaxation, play, or entertaining. Enjoy the best of both worlds-privacy and convenience-just minutes from Wilton's town center, commuting routes, and top-rated schools. This well-maintained, move-in ready home presents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy all that Wilton living has to offer.
