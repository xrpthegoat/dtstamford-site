About this home

Your own private oasis in prime central Greenwich location! Perfect opportunity to enjoy as is or build your dream home. This charming and sunlit ranch sits on a one-acre property, and was completely renovated and expanded in 2003. It includes a very large primary suite with tray ceiling, full bath and double closets, oversized living/dining area with fireplace, eat in kitchen, family room with bay window and den with French doors. Great flow, and with the large bluestone patio, perfect for entertaining. Also includes one car garage, pull down attic and generator. Beautiful millwork throughout. Close to everything - Greenwich Ave, restaurants, schools - yet so very private!
