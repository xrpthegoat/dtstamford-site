About this home

Location, condition and options make this home check all the boxes! This home has 3 bedrooms, one of which is large and runs the whole length of the second floor..there may be potential to put doors up and create a 4 bedroom arrangement. Great layout with a full bath on each floor, and the sun room above the garage may just be your favorite spot in the house; featuring entirely new hardwood flooring and paint, the space is soaked in natural light most of the day and could work as an office, guest room, great room, whatever you'd need it to be. The remodeled eat in kitchen features quartz countertops, stylish tile flooring and new, unused appliances. The neutral color palette of soothing cream, white and gold gives an elegant and timeless appearance. The second floor bedroom has luxurious, thick new carpeting, walls, ceilings, lighting, paint, & new bath with stall shower. The list of improvements is extensive and includes a new ROOF and BOILER. The garage and basement could use a little attention, but most of the heavy lifting has been done, and the value in those things is obvious here. Come see how it might work for you!
