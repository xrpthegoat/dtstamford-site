About this home

COMPO BEACH WATER VIEWS at this picture-perfect beach house. Edgewater Hillside is the best-kept secret at Compo Beach, a private Hamptons-style cul de sac where each home is prettier than the next and water views abound. This exceptional residence combines timeless design with high-end amenities and so much space to live and entertain, walking distance to Compo Beach and Old Mill Beach, and moments to Longshore Club Park&apos;s golf, tennis, swimming and boating, and downtown. The four beautifully-finished levels are highlighted by so much attention to detail: soaring coffered ceilings, built-ins, and oversized windows taking in the views of the Sound, the Mill Pond and walls of blooming hydrangea. A chef&apos;s dream kitchen is equipped with Viking and Subzero appliances, and a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen and stunning stone fireplace make entertaining a breeze in all seasons. The generously-sized primary suite offers a sumptuous bath with soaking tub, walk-in closet and gas fireplace. The versatile floor plan includes a third floor retreat also with jaw-dropping views, balcony and full bath. The fully finished lower level also includes a full bath. The home and road access are not in a flood zone, and city water, sewer and natural gas plus a whole-house generator make life easy. Enjoy privacy, water views and the lifestyle only Compo Beach offers. Even room for a small pool! Also available fully furnished for a turnkey beach residence!
