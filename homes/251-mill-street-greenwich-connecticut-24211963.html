About this home

Heat, water, trash, snow removal, and grounds maintenance are all included in rent! Tenants only pay for electricity and stove gas. This apartment has a semi-private balcony with sunset views, and both the full bathroom and railroad-style kitchen are newly updated. There is in-unit laundry, with a brand-new LG washer and dryer set. Imagine the flex-room off of the primary bedroom suite as a home office, exercise room, huge walk-in closet, or whatever your mind can create! Nearby destinations like Byram Beach Park, Bartaco, Colony Grill, The Kneaded Bread, Whole Foods Market, Costco, CVS, and The Waterfront at Port Chester are all within 1.25 mile. The home is also close to downtown Port Chester, I-95, and multiple bus stops. No off-street parking. All on-street parking is available on surrounding streets or in nearby paid or unpaid lots, as applicable.
