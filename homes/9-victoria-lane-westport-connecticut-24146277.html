About this home

Highest and Best offers to be submitted by Wednesday, 1/14 at 5pm. Perfectly positioned at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Westport's highly sought-after Long Lots neighborhood, this bright and beautiful home offers exceptional privacy while remaining just minutes to the Parkway and Westport's Main Street shops and acclaimed restaurants. Set on a spectacular 1.05-acre property, this well-maintained residence features 5 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, a walk-up attic, and a walk-out basement. The expansive grounds are a true highlight, showcasing multiple patios, a gunite pool with spa, and generous lawn areas ideal for entertaining, play, or peaceful relaxation. Inside, refinished hardwood floors flow through spacious, light-filled rooms. The main level includes a front-to-back living room with gas fireplace, a dining room with views of the beautiful property, and an eat-in kitchen open to the family room with a wood-burning fireplace. A spectacular sunroom overlooks the backyard and pool area. The primary suite features an upgraded bath and walk-in closet, complemented by a remodeled hall bath serving additional bedrooms. A first-floor fifth bedroom with access to a full bath provides excellent flexibility as a guest suite or home office.
