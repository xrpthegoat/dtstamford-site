About this home

With deeded beach rights in Shippan Point's private ALC Beach Association, this sun-drenched coastal home blends timeless character with a stunning studs-out transformation. An expansive double lot offers landscaped grounds, updated pool and spacious motor court. New stone walls and steps lead to a custom arched mahogany entry. The 4 BR, 3.5 BA home features an open great room with oak floors, wood-burning fireplace and Thermador kitchen. A den and versatile living space overlook the pool and grounds. Upstairs, 4 BRs include a primary suite with private deck and spa bath, plus 2nd-floor laundry. The finished lower level adds 800 sq ft with full bath and kitchenette. Refined finishes, all-new infrastructure, Lutron lighting, built-in speakers, networking and Ring cameras complete the home.
