About this home

Short term rental. 6 months, can be flexible on term. Tranquil Post & Beam Retreat in North Street School District. Escape the noise to this charming, unique, & private post-and-beam architectural sanctuary. Originally built as the builder's personal home, this 3,116-square-foot residence seamlessly blends rustic warmth with modern, high-end updates. Situated on 2 sprawling level acres just off Stanwich Road offering an ideal balance of secluded natural beauty & everyday Greenwich convenience. Chefs kitchen Tastefully updated w/professional-grade Wolf range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, custom wine cooler & high-end finishes. Dramatic 2-story family room anchored by a double-sided fireplace, complemented by wide-board flooring & long light-filled windows throughout the main level. A cozy sunroom located just off the mudroom & kitchen area, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing views of the landscape. All bathrooms feature contemporary updates, highlighted by primary suite bath w/marble floor, shower & marble sink. Finished lower level Includes an expansive family room w/exposed timber beams, a secondary fireplace, additional flexible space & convenient walkout access. Nature lovers paradise located directly near the Mianus River Park trails entrance, offering immediate access to hiking, river walks & outdoor recreation. Convenient location in North Street School district with swift access to local roads, Greenwich amenities, and commuter routes.
