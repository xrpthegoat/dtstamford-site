About this home

A truly historic property, "OGDEN HOUSE" features a charming 1875 house set on 1.69 acres in New Canaan. The 1450 sq ft, home includes a spacious living room, a first floor office, kitchen and large dining room. The second floor has 3 bedrooms and additional room and one bath. This bucolic setting features an inviting front porch and sweeping lawns. The house boasts refinished floors and newly painted rooms. Convenient to both the village of Pound Ridge and the town of New Canaan, with a direct train to NYC, the Merritt Parkway and route 95 close by.
