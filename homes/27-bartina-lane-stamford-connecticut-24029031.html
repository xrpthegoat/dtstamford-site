About this home

This sprawling home in the sought-after Westover area is conveniently located near the Greenwich border, offering 6,500 sq ft, of which 5,042 sq ft is finished living space a lower level of 1,551sq ft unfinished, on an acre of flat land. From the bluestone front porch to the outdoor area with a sparkling heated pool, this property epitomizes luxury living with the comfort of a modern-day lifestyle. Meticulously maintained, the bright and open-plan first floor features an oversized living room, dining room, and family room with a fireplace. Rich hardwood floors run throughout, complemented by an eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a large island with granite counters, and a spacious first-floor laundry area. The home office boasts built-ins and custom millwork throughout. At the same time, the first-floor bedroom includes a full en-suite bath and an additional family room that the homeowners use as an entertaining space. This makes it an ideal option for a potential in-law suite offering ample space for everyday living or entertainment. A large three-car garage leads into a large open mudroom with built-in cubbies and storage, providing practicality and convenience. This meticulously designed home offers luxury living in a prime location. Don't miss the opportunity to make it yours.
