About this home

Welcome to effortless coastal living in the heart of Stamford's coveted Cove Beach neighborhood. This beautifully refreshed 2 bedroom, 1.5 bath condo offers rare single level living with an open, airy layout and thoughtfully upgraded interiors. Step inside to discover newly refinished hardwood floors and fresh painting, creating a clean, move-in-ready feel. Every room in the main entertaining area is oversized offering the perfect canvas. The kitchen has updated appliances and upgraded finishes perfect for eating in or customizing further! The spacious living and dining room are ideal for everyday living and entertaining alike. Both bedrooms offer comfortable retreats with ample closet space. A bright bonus sunroom with custom built-in's provides the ideal flex space for a home office or guest space adding valuable extra living area. Storage is abundant with numerous closets on the main level, plus a large private storage area in the basement. The thoughtful combination of updates, space, and functionality makes this home truly stand out. Located just moments from Cove Island Beach, major transit, marinas, and downtown Stamford, this home offers the perfect blend of coastal lifestyle and commuter convenience.
