About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable South Wilton, this beautifully updated New England Colonial offers timeless charm, modern comfort, and 1.2 private, professionally landscaped acres. Thoughtfully refreshed interiors feature refinished hardwood floors, updated lighting and hardware, and a sophisticated color palette. An exceptional floor plan offers generously sized living spaces with effortless flow. The beautifully appointed kitchen boasts Viking appliances, custom cabinetry, a walk-in pantry, wet bar, and expansive counter space. Opening to a welcoming family room with a river stone fireplace, this space is ideal for everyday living and entertaining. A spacious mudroom, main-level laundry, and screened porch add convenience and charm. Upstairs, four generous bedrooms, three full baths, a versatile fifth room, and a spectacular bonus room above the garage offer exceptional flexibility. The finished lower-level features recreation and fitness spaces, plus an impressive temperature-controlled wine cellar. A walk-up attic provides ample storage and third-floor expansion potential.Outside, level lawns, a picturesque stream, and an expansive private driveway create a truly peaceful setting. A charming footbridge crosses the stream to a dedicated gardening area with raised beds. A whole-house generator and two-car garage complete this exceptional home, just minutes from Wilton Center, downtown Westport, and Metro-North.
