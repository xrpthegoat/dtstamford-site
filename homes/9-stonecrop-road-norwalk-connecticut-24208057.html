About this home

Restore, renovate, or rebuild in one of Norwalk's most architecturally significant neighborhoods. Set on a peaceful corner lot in the desirable Cranbury section of Norwalk, this 4-bedroom, 2-bath Mid-Century Modern ranch offers a rare opportunity to create a truly special home. Designed by designer/builder P. William Nathan as part of his celebrated Chestnut Hill Corners neighborhood, the home was featured in both The New York Herald Tribune and Living Magazine in 1954 for the neighborhood's innovative Mid-Century Modern design. Owned by the original family since it was built, the property is ready for its next chapter. Original features include hardwood floors, a spacious family room with soaring cathedral ceilings and skylights, and the clean lines characteristic of Mid-Century Modern architecture. An automatic whole-house generator provides added convenience and peace of mind. Situated on a corner lot surrounded by mature trees, the property offers a private, tranquil setting in a quiet neighborhood ideal for walking, biking, and enjoying the outdoors. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, schools, and major commuting routes, this location combines accessibility with a serene residential atmosphere. Whether you envision a complete renovation of this architectural gem or a brand-new custom home, this is a unique opportunity to bring your vision to life in one of Norwalk's most distinctive neighborhoods.
