About this home

Live like you're on vacation all year round in Old Greenwich with spectacular 200 feet of waterfront with stunning views of Manhattan. Amazing vistas from every room of this 5 bedroom designer house. Sliding doors open to expansive deck, both covered and uncovered overlooking private pier with ramp to floating dock. Dine al fresco with stunningly beautiful sunsets, and enjoy sailing, boating, swimming and paddle boarding in Greenwich Cove. There is a wide lush lawn for entertaining, playing ball or croquet or hosting a wedding. Enjoy the private, exclusive Lucas Point beach and boatyard, close to Greenwich Point beach. At the end of a cul de sac, this home is a private retreat to enjoy. A short distance to the Old Greenwich village with shops and restaurants and the train station to NYC.
