About this home

Welcome to 83 Van Buskirk Avenue in Lexington Square, a desirable condominium community in Stamford's sought-after Cove neighborhood. This spacious 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers 1,390 square feet on the first and second floors, plus approx. 458 sft of finished lower-level space. Freshly painted and move-in ready, this home offers a comfortable layout, exceptional storage and wonderful indoor-outdoor living. The first floor features an open-concept living and dining area with oak hardwood floors and sliding glass doors leading to a private deck. The eat-in kitchen offers custom wood cabinetry, granite countertops and a pantry closet, a convenient coat closet and half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs, the generous primary bedroom includes a walk-in closet, while the second bedroom offers two closets. The full bath features a jetted spa tub/shower with access from both the hall and primary bedroom. Two hall closets and attic storage provide additional space. The finished lower level offers flexible living space, along with the laundry and a utility room with workshop and rear entry. A private one-car garage provides direct interior access to the lower level, and an additional assigned parking space is located in front of the building. Conveniently located close to shopping, dining, transportation, parks, entertainment and the waterfront, this well-appointed home offers comfortable living with exceptional storage, private outdoor space and garage parking.
