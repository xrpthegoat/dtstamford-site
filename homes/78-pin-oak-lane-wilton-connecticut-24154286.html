About this home

A perfect blend of contemporary design meets timeless comfort. This pristine 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home sprawls over an impressive 4,455 square feet, offering a seamless blend of space and style. Nestled on over two acres, the private backyard backs up to over 188 acres of Wilton Town Forest. The heart of the home is undoubtedly the oversized chef's kitchen, a culinary enthusiast's dream. The flow is ideal for entertaining all year round, whether indoors, outdoors, or both. Imagine lazy afternoons by the poolside, with the grill fired up in your outdoor barbecue area, and evenings filled with laughter and joy. This home is not just a place to live but a lifestyle to embrace. With modern updates throughout, including renovated bathrooms and a kitchen, and a thoughtful layout, 78 Pin Oak Lane is a true oasis.
