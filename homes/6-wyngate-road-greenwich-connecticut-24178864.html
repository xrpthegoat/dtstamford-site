About this home

Nestled on a private cul-de-sac in one of Greenwich's most coveted mid-country locations, this beautifully updated five-bedroom residence is set on 1.1 professionally landscaped acres, offering a rare combination of privacy, functionality, and convenience. Thoughtfully designed for modern living, the home blends formal and informal spaces, features abundant natural light, and offers seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The living room with fireplace opens to a sun-filled three-exposure office or conservatory, while a second dedicated office provides exceptional work-from-home flexibility. At the heart of the home, the eat-in kitchen flows effortlessly into the inviting family room with incredible vaulted ceilings, beam work and custom light fixture, which features a second fireplace, vaulted ceilings and direct access to an expansive deck overlooking sweeping lawns and mature gardens. A versatile first-floor bedroom suite offers endless possibilities as a guest-suite, playroom, fitness studio, or additional living space. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite features a walk-in closet and an oversized bath, complemented by three additional bedrooms and a hall bathroom. The finished lower level provides valuable bonus space for recreation, media, fitness, or play, while a dedicated laundry room and an attached two-car garage enhance everyday convenience.
