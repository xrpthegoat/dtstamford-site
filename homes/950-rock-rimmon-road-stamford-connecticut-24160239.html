About this home

Stunning North Stamford California style ranch home on a secluded two-acre wooded back lot. This updated property features a spacious gourmet kitchen with handsome hardwood floors throughout. The main entrance opens to a great room with soaring ceiling, floor to ceiling stone fireplace, and walls of glass overlooking a serene park like private property. Plenty of office space on either the main floor and ground floor. Four full bathrooms, five bedrooms and a large office space, den or extra bedroom. The hardwood finished area below the main floor has its own private walk-out entrance to take advantage of an additional office space with its own full bathroom, wet bar and ample storage. A private and spacious fenced in back yard is perfect for family activities & pets. The attached two car garage and a large rear storage space completes this rental offering. A country getaway near major highways, towns such as Bedford, Pound Ridge, New Canaan and downtown Stamford. Pet deposit required.
