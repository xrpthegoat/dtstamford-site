About this home

A rare rental opportunity in one of Westport's most sought-after neighborhoods-this exceptional stone & shingle home is set on a manicured, level acre on a premier cul-de-sac. Offering nearly 9,000 square feet of refined living, this residence combines classic architectural detail with contemporary comfort, showcasing 9'-10' ceilings, custom millwork, and five fireplaces. The gourmet kitchen opens to an inviting great room with stone fireplace and French doors to the patio. Formal living and dining rooms, plus a handsome wood-paneled library, create a gracious main level. Upstairs, five well-proportioned bedrooms include a stunning primary suite with 12' ceilings, fireplace, and spa bath with double steam shower. A spacious bonus room offers flexible living space and includes a potential in-law or au pair suite with its own bedroom and full bath. The walk-out lower level includes a media room, gym, rec/play room, second family room, and full bath. Outside, enjoy a secluded retreat with 20x50 heated Gunite pool/spa, stone patio with fireplace, and lush landscaping. Walk to Long Lots Elementary, Bedford Middle & Staples HS. Truly an exceptional offering!
