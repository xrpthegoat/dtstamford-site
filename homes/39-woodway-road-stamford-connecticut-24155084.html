About this home

Welcome to Woodland Terrace - a highly sought-after townhouse community offering convenience and accessibility in the heart of the area. This 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse presents a great opportunity for buyers looking to customize and add value. The main level features an open living and dining area, a functional kitchen layout, and a convenient half bath. Sliders lead to a private deck - perfect for relaxing or entertaining outdoors. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a shared full bathroom ready for your personal updates and design vision. With the right renovations, this home offers strong upside potential. Additional features include an attached one-car garage with interior access and in-unit laundry. Ideally located just minutes from shopping, dining, the Merritt Parkway, and the Springdale train station, this property is a smart opportunity for buyers seeking location, space, and the ability to create their own style.
