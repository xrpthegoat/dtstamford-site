About this home

Welcome home to this spacious and sun-filled townhome offering the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and flexibility in Stamford's desirable Springdale neighborhood. Designed for today's lifestyle, this three-bedroom, 3-1/2 bath residence features hardwood floors, and generously sized rooms throughout. The inviting living room is anchored by a fireplace and a beautiful bay window that fills the space with natural light, creating the perfect setting for relaxing evenings or entertaining guests. The eat-in kitchen offers granite countertops, a breakfast bar, walk-in pantry, and direct access to outdoor space. A main-level laundry area adds convenience to your daily routine. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet and private bath, while two additional bedrooms share a full hall bath. The finished lower level with full bath provides exceptional versatility and can easily serve as a home office, fitness studio, media room, guest space, or recreation room. Additional highlights include a one-car garage, two additional parking spaces, central air, gas heat, and fresh paint throughout. Perfectly positioned just moments from Springdale Train Station, local shops, restaurants, and everyday conveniences, this location offers an easy commute while keeping everything you need close to home. Available for immediate occupancy. Cats considered. Tenant responsible for all utilities. Excellent credit required.
