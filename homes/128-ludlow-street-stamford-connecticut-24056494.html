About this home

Spacious Townhouse in convenient location, just s short walk to the train, Harbor Point Restaurants, Shops and vibrant Downtown Stamford. There is also a pull-down attic for storage, a large two car garage with laundry and storage closets. Good credit and references a must! No Pets or smoking. First month's rent plus two month's security.
