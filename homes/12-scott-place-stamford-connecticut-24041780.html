About this home

GLENBROOK INVESTORS TAKE NOTE! This fully occupied two-family home is awaiting its new owner. The first-floor unit has been completely remodeled this year and is an oversized, sun-drenched 2-bedroom, 1-bath gem with gleaming hardwood floors, a custom kitchen with stunning blue painted cabinetry and brand-new stainless steel appliances, an open-concept formal dining room with a breakfast bar open to both the kitchen and living room, a generous primary bedroom, and a private in-unit laundry room. The second-floor unit is also a 2-bedroom, 1-bath unit with an additional office/den and its own washer/dryer in-unit. Both units have their own separate utilities, including two Buderus gas furnaces, hot water heaters, and separate electric panels and meters. The property features a brand new roof, basement storage with separate outside access, and parking for two cars. Centrally located on a quiet street, just 4 minutes from the Glenbrook train station, your tenants will have easy access to all the fine dining, nightlife, shopping, parks and culture that Stamford has to offer. This is the investment opportunity you've been waiting for!!
