About this home

Rare opportunity to buy into Silvermine Woods community and become a member of Silvermine Golf Club without initiation fee or annual dues. The house is just perfect, newly updated and refreshed, with new kitchen, bathrooms, landscaping, and freshly painted interiors. Finished lower level adds more living space, with large storage room as well, in addition to the 2nd floor attic. Lawn irrigation and automatic generator, keep things running without effort. So move right in and be ready for the golf season on the course, out your windows, or on the two decks overlooking Hole #15's fairway and putting green. Such a peaceful and scenic quality of life in a neighborhood of Wilton homes, part of the 27 holes open to SGC members and their guests. Wilton is acclaimed for award-winning schools, charming shopping villages, and scenic semi-rural landscapes. And the Historic Silvermine Community is optimally located for convenience to additional amenities offered by New Canaan and Norwalk's shopping, dining, and best commuting distances.
