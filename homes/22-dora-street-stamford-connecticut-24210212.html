About this home

This bright, spacious end-unit townhouse is in the Cove neighborhood of Stamford. Upstairs, you'll find two bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. The updated kitchen has granite counters, stainless steel appliances, and an island, and it opens to a large family room with a fireplace. On the main level, there's a washer and dryer and two extra rooms that work nicely as a gym, office, or playroom. Outside, a private balcony and patio are a great spot for morning coffee. The secluded backyard is a peaceful place to garden, entertain, or just enjoy some quiet time outdoors. Two parking spaces are assigned to the unit. The Cove offers a quiet, residential feel with the city close by, the best of both worlds. Cove Island Park is nearby, with walks along the water, room for a picnic, and plenty of ways to enjoy the outdoors.
