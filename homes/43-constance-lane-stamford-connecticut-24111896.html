About this home

Charming North Stamford Ranch at the Edge of Greenwich. Welcome to this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 4-bath ranch-style home, ideally situated on a quiet cul-de-sac in highly sought-after North Stamford-just minutes from the Merritt Parkway and nestled right on the Greenwich border. Offering the perfect blend of privacy and convenience, this spacious home is set on a serene, lush lot, ideal for peaceful living and effortless entertaining. The sun-filled interior features an oversized living room, grand dining room, and a designer-renovated eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The kitchen flows seamlessly into a generous family room with central purification system throughout the house, a cozy wood stove insert fireplace with direct access to the deck-perfect for indoor-outdoor gatherings overlooking the private landscaped backyard. The luxury primary suite offers an inspired expansive walk-in closets, and deck access, creating a true retreat. Three additional bedrooms and two beautifully full baths, complete the convenient main-level layout. Enjoy true single-level living, finished lower level, perfect for a media room, playroom, gym, or office.
