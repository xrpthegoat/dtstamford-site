About this home

A true New England Classic- 39 West Meadow Rd was built in 1931 in "The Meadows," a neighborhood of architecturally significant homes. Upon entering the foyer, you are greeted by a sweeping staircase with grand living and dining rooms on either side. Each room offers gracious space for gathering and entertaining, showcasing timeless original craftsmanship, including beautifully carved mantel, fireplace, built-in corner china cabinets, and elegant dentil molding. The kitchen features Viking & LG stainless steel appliances and an eat-in dining area with French doors to a private bluestone patio. A spacious family room/den off the living room is ideal for movie night, game days, or possible home office. Above the garage, an au-pair or in-law suite offers 2 bedrooms, a full bath, and a private entrance, as well as a mudroom and laundry area off the kitchen. Continue to the upper levels of the main house, and you will find 6 additional private bedrooms. The 2nd floor hosts 4 oversized bedrooms, including a Primary en-suite, as well as a hall bath, while the light and bright 3rd floor offers 2 more bedrooms and a full bath. Outside, mature trees and gardens with specimen plantings bloom with color throughout the seasons. Relax on the bluestone patio and enjoy your own private oasis- a rare opportunity to own this Classic Beauty in South Wilton!
