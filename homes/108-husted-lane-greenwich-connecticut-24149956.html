About this home

A rare offering of refined luxury and meticulous craftsmanship, 108 Husted Lane is a stunning estate that has been extensively renovated and enhanced, blending timeless architecture with modern sophistication. Set behind stone walls and surrounded by mature landscaping, this exceptional residence offers unparalleled privacy, scale, and comfort. The home has undergone significant, high-caliber improvements. Every detail has been thoughtfully curated for both elevated entertaining and everyday luxury. Throughout the home, you will find multiple newly renovated bathrooms, including a spa-quality primary bath with steam shower, refinished hardwood floors, custom millwork, updated lighting, and beautifully, newly renovated main living spaces. Exterior improvements are equally impressive, with pressed cedar shingle roofing, six-foot stone walls enclosing the property, extensive irrigation, landscape lighting, paver driveway, rear deck, hot tub enclosure, and re-leveled backyard creating a resort-like setting ideal for both quiet enjoyment and entertaining. This is a turn-key luxury estate offering privacy, scale, and architectural integrity; a truly exceptional opportunity for the discerning buyer seeking a legacy property in a premier location.
