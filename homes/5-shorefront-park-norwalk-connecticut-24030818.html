About this home

Welcome to your coastal oasis in Shorefront Park! This classic Cape Cod-style retreat offers the ultimate in water community living just a stone's throw from Long Island Sound and SONO. Step inside this updated, bright, and beachy cottage with its lush gardens and private treelined backyard. Cook up a storm in the well-equipped kitchen, then dine in the expanded dining room or relax in the open family room that opens onto a generous deck. Cozy up in the sun-filled living room with its open hearth gas fireplace, or retreat to the first-floor primary bedroom with a modern glass spa bathroom featuring a steam shower and soaking tub. A smaller bedroom nearby is perfect for an office or nursery, complete with its own washer/dryer. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms with large closets and a full hall bathroom with a tub. Plenty of storage awaits in the full basement. New boiler and hot water heater. Just a mile away, Metro North, SONO restaurants, and shopping await your exploration. Embrace the Shorefront Park lifestyle where coastal charm meets modern convenience-it's the perfect place to ca ll home. Stay tuned for more pictures and a video tour coming soon.
