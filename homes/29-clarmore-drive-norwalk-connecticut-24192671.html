About this home

Welcome to easy, low-maintenance living in Norwalk's desirable Spring Hill neighborhood! This well-maintained upper-level ranch-style condominium offers one bedroom, one full bath, and a bright, inviting layout designed for comfortable everyday living. The unit features a spacious living room filled with natural light, a functional kitchen with a dishwasher, a separate dining area, and a full bath with a window for added natural light and ventilation. A shared laundry facility is conveniently located within the complex. Heat and hot water are included in the rent, helping to simplify your monthly expenses, and one assigned parking space is included. Ideally situated just minutes from Norwalk Hospital, The SoNo Collection, Metro-North, Route 7, I-95, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, restaurants, parks, and entertainment, this location offers exceptional convenience for commuters and anyone looking to enjoy all that Norwalk has to offer.
