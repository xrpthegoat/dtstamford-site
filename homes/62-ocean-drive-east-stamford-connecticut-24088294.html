About this home

Experience luxury living at its finest in this exquisite direct waterfront property located in the coveted Shippan Point neighborhood. Enjoy unparalleled water views from every room of this expansive multi-level home. The living room features a stone gas fireplace, and opens through multiple sliders to an Ipe wraparound deck, with a retractable awning. This space seamlessly flows into a bespoke chef's kitchen, featuring a walk-in pantry, top of the line appliances, and striking copper hood over the Viking range. Enjoy breakfast or casual meals at the large island with built-in banquette, and entertain in style in the adjacent formal dining room with access to the deck and patio. This home is designed for indoor-outdoor entertaining, with multiple doors to the outdoors, offering breathtaking views and vibrant gardens, plus a private beach to launch your kayak! The separate in-law apartment offers flexibility for extended family and guests. Retreat to the primary bedroom suite, featuring a luxurious bath and a private deck. Located under an hour from NYC, this serene oasis is also just minutes away from parks, beaches, restaurants, and the bustling downtown Stamford. Perfect for those seeking tranquility and convenience! This home truly offers a perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and breathtaking views in a prime waterfront location.
