About this home

Bright and spacious condo nestled away on a quiet cul-de-sac in desirable West Norwalk. This one bedroom condo comes with hardwood floors, central air, crown molding, in unit laundry, and central vacuum system. Enjoy not one but two private porches, one off the living room and one off the bedroom. This top floor unit has plenty of storage space, including a full attic with pull down access and oversized storage closet in the basement. Pet friendly! Minutes from The SoNo Collection shopping mall, downtown restaurants, train stations, highways, and hospital.
