About this home

Welcome to 19 Burwood Avenue, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom home offering 1,608 square feet of comfortable living space in desirable Waterside neighborhood. This move-in ready property combines classic New England colonial charm with thoughtful upgrades, perfect for today's modern lifestyle. Inside, you'll find beautifully refinished hardwood floors that flow throughout, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The spacious layout includes bright living areas ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, along with a well-appointed kitchen designed for functionality and ease. Upstairs, four generously sized bedrooms provide plenty of space for family, guests, or a home office. High-efficiency central heating and air conditioning ensure year-round comfort. The finished basement adds coveted entertaining space, featuring a dedicated projector screen - perfect for movies, game nights, and enjoying a true at-home cinema experience. Step outside to your own private retreat - a fully fenced backyard with sparkling pool, patio, and fire pit area. Ideal for summer gatherings, relaxing weekends, and outdoor fun. Located just minutes from Stamford's vibrant waterfront, shopping, dining, parks, and commuter routes, this home offers the perfect blend of suburban tranquility and city convenience.
