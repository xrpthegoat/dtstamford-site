About this home

Located at the end of a private lane with lots of privacy off of Westover Road, this terrific Saltbox colonial checks all the boxes! This home has a large updated kitchen, oversized master suite with gorgeous bath and dressing area; outside decks/patio/screened porch, terrific for entertaining. There is a family room off the kitchen with fireplace and deck. A separate first-floor bedroom, with its own entrance and bathroom also provides great flexibility for guests or an in-law suite. Entire acre of backyard is fenced. Other features include updated bathrooms, heated floors in master bath, 2nd floor laundry room, and an additional office or playroom on the second floor. Lots of storage throughout and a basement with workroom and overflow closets. Attached 2-car garage and plentiful outdoor parking. Beautiful setting atop Indian Rock provides a peaceful setting yet super convenient to both Stamford and Greenwich. Come and enjoy! Please note: The interior photos are with the homeowners furnishings. Property is currently tenant occupied. Owner/Agent.
