About this home

Rare West Norwalk gem - quintessential New England colonial located on desirable cul-de-sac. This warm and inviting 4-bedroom home blends timeless character with thoughtful, stylish updates, set on over a level acre of land. As you enter the home you are drawn to the elegant living room which features a fireplace and French doors that lead to the large deck. Throw a log on the fire and gather around the piano to sing your favorite songs - this is a great space for entertaining. Host the whole family in the large, formal dining room. In the cozy family room, you will want to gather around the wood-burning fireplace for movie and game nights with family and friends. Morning sun drenches the recently renovated eat-in Kitchen features bright, white cabinets with under cabinet lighting, motorized blinds and stainless steel appliances. Upstairs, you'll find four comfortable bedrooms, all freshly painted, with brand-new wall-to-wall carpeting for a soft, modern feel. The Primary bedroom includes ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet. Outdoor living is just as appealing with a large deck overlooking the flat, private backyard, a perfect venue for summer barbeques, relaxation and fun. The full unfinished basement plus garden shed provide extra storage or hobby space. This home has it all in the serenity of a private setting located on the New Canaan line which offers close proximity to shopping, schools, and commuting routes.
