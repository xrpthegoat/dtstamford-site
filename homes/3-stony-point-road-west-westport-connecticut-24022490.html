About this home

Just in time for summer...stunning yearly furnished rental with water views and a pool. Amazing location, walk to Saugatuck train station and restaurants/shops. Sited on the Saugatuck river in Stony Point association, there are wonderful water views from most of the rooms. The house is renovated and furnished, bright and spacious with 5/6 bedrooms all with full baths. One or two home offices possible on the main level. The massive gourmet kitchen has an island that seats 5, double ovens, and a gas cooktop. Formal DR with pool and patio views. Large playroom on 3rd floor has a wet bar and 1/2 bath. Adorable "hidden" playroom for little ones. Two laundry locations on main and upper levels. The great room on the main floor has vaulted ceilings, a wet bar and wine fridge, and a full bath with a sauna! The massive covered patio features a built in grill, seating and eating areas for the ultimate summer entertaining. Patio opens to pool and spa, expansive lush yard with swing set. Launch a paddle board or kayak or boat from the Stony Point community dock just down the hill. Whole house generator is a bonus.
