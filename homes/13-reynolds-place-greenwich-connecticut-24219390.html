About this home

Welcome to this bright and spacious 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home in Greenwich's Pemberwick neighborhood! Featuring hardwood floors, central air, abundant natural light, a generous living room, formal dining area, and well appointed kitchen with ample cabinetry. The expansive finished lower level offers incredible flexibility for a family room, home office, gym, or recreation space, plus a private laundry room and additional storage. Enjoy direct access to a fenced-in backyard and covered porch, perfect for outdoor entertaining. Two driveway parking spaces add convenience. Ideally located near parks, shopping, dining, Metro North, downtown Greenwich, Port Chester, and major commuter routes. A wonderful combination of space, comfort, and convenience!
