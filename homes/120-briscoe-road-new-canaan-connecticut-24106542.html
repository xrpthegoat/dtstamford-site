About this home

This is YOUR opportunity to build the home of your dreams that you have yet to find or have yet to win that bid on! Welcome to 120 Briscoe Road, a 2 acre buildable lot right around the corner from St Luke's School and an 8 min drive to desirable downtown New Canaan with it's amazing restaurants and shops. This is a wonderful prospect for all end users and builders! Please have confirmed appointment to walk land with listing agent. Easy to show!
