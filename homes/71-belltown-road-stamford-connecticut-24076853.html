About this home

You do not want to miss this 3 bedroom 1.5 bathroom colonial!! You will be greeted by a spacious enclosed porch = extra storage and year round usability. On the main floor you have your family room with a wood burning fire place, dining room, kitchen, and half bathroom. Kitchen has stainless steel appliances, granite counter tops, along with a great deal of cabinet and countertop space. Upstairs you will find the three bedrooms along with a full bathroom. There is over 600 square feet in the basement for a great amount of storage plus laundry. Hardwood floors and crown molding throughout entire home. This home is equipped with central air, natural gas, and includes a 1 car detached garage. Large fenced in back yard with an awesome trex deck. Walking distance to Newfield Shopping Center, Barrett Park, houses of worship, shops, grocery stores, nail salons, restaurants etc.
