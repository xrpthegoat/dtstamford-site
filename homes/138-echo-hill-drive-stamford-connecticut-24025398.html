About this home

Introducing the distinctive property at 138 Echo Hill Drive, situated in the esteemed North Stamford neighborhood, a location which is highly desirable. This residence is situated tranquilly at the end of a cul-de-sac, ensuring a peaceful and secluded living experience. The exterior boasts a large and flat park-like backyard, providing an open canvas for landscaping dreams or simply the perfect setting for serene relaxation. As you enter, be captivated by the family room, amplified by a vintage, beamed ceiling and a heartwarming fireplace offering an inviting ambiance for your evening gatherings. Continuing through the home, a generously-scaled kitchen allows for hosting warm social gatherings and gourmet cooking experiences. And the oversized living room with wet bar adds to the perfection. The house boasts four conveniently located bedrooms on the second floor. The primary bedroom comes complete with its own bathroom, providing a private retreat for the homeowners. Adding to its grandeur is the lower basement level that extends an ample area for leisure and entertainment, the perfect place for a game room or a home theater. An added bonus room could easily serve as a personal gym, a hobby room, or whatever else your imagination desires. In summary, the property at 138 Echo Hill Drive is the embodiment of a finely crafted home set in an idyllic location and bound to create cherished memories for its new owners.
