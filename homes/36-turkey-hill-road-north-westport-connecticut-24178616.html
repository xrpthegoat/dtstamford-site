About this home

Welcome home to this charming 5-bedroom home located in the desirable Long Lots area, convenient to everything. Short walk to schools, shopping and minutes from train and highways. Every room in this home is so inviting, gather round in the kitchen or watch movies in the den, sit by the fireplace in the living room or enjoy the view of the brook from the balcony in the primary bedroom. The walk-out lower level leads to a brick patio perfect for private twilight nights and an expansive backyard to kick a ball around. The home abuts a dead end great for riding bikes and extra parking for large gatherings. This multi-level home lives large, truly a must-see.
