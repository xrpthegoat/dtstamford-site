About this home

Turn-key mid-century modern house by renowned modernist architect Lester Tichy with pool set on 4.5 acres. Poised at the top of a slope and screened by buffer for privacy, the 4-bed, 4.1-bath residence spanning 3,992 sqft overlooks Wildwood Lake with its own storybook private island. Glass curtain walls in living/dining room with built-in wet bar and its original cone-shaped fireplace capture west-facing light opening to a 60-ft lakefront balcony. Eat-in chef's kitchen with updated appliances. Wood-paneled study with fireplace and full bath. Glass-walled primary suite with lake views. Two additional bedrooms, one full and one-half bath, and laundry room also on this level. Walk-out ground floor en-suite bedroom with covered patio. Artfully renovated in the past three years with upgraded systems and interior improvements, this home blends its unique historic appeal with today's contemporary luxury. The current owners purchased adjacent lot to create this oversized parcel.
