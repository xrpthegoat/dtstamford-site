About this home

Welcome to the seaside neighborhood of Shippan Point! Hidden along the sparkling shores of Long Island Sound, Shippan Point is one of Connecticut's historical hidden treasures. This beautiful coastal enclave offers timeless charm, scenic beauty, and a vibrant waterfront lifestyle-just minutes from downtown Stamford and a short ride to Manhattan. This Classic Colonial home offers comfort for both entertaining & everyday living. Enter into a welcoming foyer that opens to the formal dining room & a spacious living room with custom built-ins & stunning natural stone fireplace. Continue on to the bright, relaxing, sunlit family room with its own grand brick fireplace; alternate use for this room are an office/library. The chef's kitchen & breakfast nook are ideal for casual gathering - on through the mudroom to the flagstone terrace & built-in firepit; perfect for outdoor gatherings, backyard BBQs, & firepit starlight nights. The screened-in porch with ceiling fan provides a peaceful sanctuary to relax, gather, & make lasting memories. The second level features three generous bedrooms, including a primary suite with en-suite bath and private deck. The third floor offers a retreat for guests, a playroom, or private workspace, complete with its own bathroom. The finished lower level with its wide pine planked floor expands your living space into a rec room, media room, gym, au-pair or in-law suite, with a full bath and laundry.
