About this home

Looking for the perfect spot in Norwalk with Harbor Views? This sunny, spacious two-bedroom on the third floor of 46 Prospect Avenue might be the one. Forget the stress of street parking. You'll have your own covered garage space plus a second spot in the lot. Even better, you can cross heating bills off your list of worries, as they're included. The building keeps life simple with on-site coin-operated laundry and a dedicated small storage area for your extra gear. Location is everything here. You're within easy walking distance to Norwalk Hospital and just a short trip from the South Norwalk (SoNo) train station, making any commute a breeze. When you're off the clock, you're minutes away from the best of SoNo: browse the SoNo Collection mall, explore the waterfront, or grab a bite at one of the area's famous restaurants.
