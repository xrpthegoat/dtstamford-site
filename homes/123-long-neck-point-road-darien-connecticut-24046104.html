About this home

Experience the finest in coastal living at 123 Long Neck Point Road. This impeccably renovated 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom residence spans a generous 6,214 square feet and is situated on 1.22 acres with 118 feet of direct waterfront. Upon entering, you are greeted by an interior that showcases meticulous craftsmanship and high-end finishes. The open-concept design enhances natural light and stunning views of Ziegler's Cove and Long Island Sound. The custom-designed kitchen, seamlessly opens onto a deck, perfect for al fresco dining. This space overlooks the landscaped grounds, brought to life by elegant landscape lighting. The home office provides an oasis for productivity and tranquility while the great room presents endless possibilities for entertainment and gatherings. The primary suite is a sanctuary of luxury, featuring a sumptuous soaking tub, two walk-in closets, and a private deck with serene water views. A plethora of outdoor amenities designed for leisure and enjoyment including the pool and hot tub beckon you to unwind, while the outdoor kitchen, bluestone patio, and dockside fire pit provide the ideal setting for recreation and relaxation. A private boat dock further enhances the waterfront experience, making this home a true haven for water enthusiasts.
