About this home

**HIGHEST & BEST due Tuesday 8/4 at Noon** Welcome to this beautifully maintained 2 bed, 2.5 bath end-unit townhouse in one of Norwalk's well-kept communities. Offering added privacy and abundant natural light, this inviting home overlooks a lightly wooded backdrop where you can unwind on one of two private balconies and enjoy evening sunsets. The spacious main level features hardwood floors, a cozy gas fireplace, and an open living and dining area with sliding glass doors that create a seamless connection to the outdoors. The bright kitchen offers ample cabinetry, granite counter space, gas range and stainless steel appliances. Upstairs features two bedrooms with soaring vaulted ceilings and abundant natural light. There are two full bathrooms, one ensuite in the primary bedroom, which also features a private balcony overlooking the lightly wooded grounds. The finished lower level is multi-purpose space ideal for a family room, home office, fitness area, or playroom, along with a laundry area and additional storage. Two assigned parking spaces, ample guest parking, and access to the community swimming pool complete this exceptional offering. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping, restaurants, parks, I-95, and Metro-North, this move-in-ready home offers the perfect combination of comfort, privacy, and convenience!
