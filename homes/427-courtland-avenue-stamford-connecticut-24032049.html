About this home

Welcome to 427 Courtland, a captivating blend of antique charm & modern convenience nestled in the Glenbrook area of Stamford. Built in 1907, this distinguished 6-bed, 3-bath home exudes character w/its original hardwood floors, crown molding & tranquil ambiance. Inside discover a home where history meets contemporary living. Main level features 2 spacious living areas, perfect for gatherings, complemented by an oversized kitchen and formal dining room. Three updated full bathrooms, one on each level, ensure comfort & convenience for all residents. A standout feature is the walk-up finished attic, complete with a kitchenette, ideal for use as an in-law suite or private retreat. Recently converted from oil to gas heating, featuring a new Navien energy-efficient boiler for two-zone heating, ensuring year-round comfort and cost efficiency. Property boasts a carriage house, presenting endless possibilities for additional living space, a home studio, gym, or workshop-let your imagination flourish & transform this space to suit your needs. This home offers easy access to the Glenbrook train station for a stress-free commute to NYC, as well as quick access to I-95 for those who travel by car. Nearby amenities such as shopping, dining, and entertainment further enhance the appeal of this location. Embrace the timeless elegance and modern comforts of this Glenbrook gem-schedule your private tour today and envision the possibilities of calling 427 Courtland Avenue your new home.
