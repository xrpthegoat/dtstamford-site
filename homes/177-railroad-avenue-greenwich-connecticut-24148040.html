About this home

Luxury Townhouse in Prime Greenwich Location ~Just 1.5 blocks from the Greenwich Train Sta. & world-class shopping & dining of Greenwich Ave. This stunning & sophisticated 3-BR, 4-bath townhouse condo is quietly nestled behind a charming, tree-lined sidewalk in one of Greenwich's most sought-after locations. Designed by renowned architect Rich Granoff & masterfully built by Thomas Torelli, this residence showcases 10' ceilings, exquisite millwork, intricate moldings & exceptional high-end craftsmanship throughout. The spectacular chef's kitchen, redesigned in 2018 by Putnam Designs, is a true showpiece, featuring Neolith countertops, full-access custom cabinetry w soft-close drawers & hinges, a Rohl fireclay sink, top-of-the-line Viking & Miele appliances, executed w remarkable attention to detail. Offering 3 levels of light-filled living space, the home features a gracious open-concept main floor highlighted by a beautiful gas fireplace & French doors that open to a serene bluestone patio, ideal for entertaining/quiet relaxation. A flexible floor plan provides multiple options for a home office, media room, gym, playroom, or bonus space. Other highlights include Restoration Hardware lighting & custom window treatments throughout, a luxurious primary suite w soaring ceilings, chandelier, spa-like bath w jacuzzi tub, separate walk-in shower, and his/her closets w custom built-ins. A convenient motorized dumbwaiter connects lower level to kitchen. Attached garage +guest pkng.
