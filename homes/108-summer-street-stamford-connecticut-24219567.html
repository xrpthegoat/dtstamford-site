About this home

Discover this chic and inviting one-bedroom condo at Woodside Green! Filled with natural light, this lovely corner unit features two generously sized storage closets in the living room, central air conditioning, and beautiful new hardwood floors throughout. Enjoy the beautifully landscaped community, complete with lush green lawns, mature trees, and a charming central gazebo. Ideally situated between Scalzi Park and Ridgeway Shopping Mall, Woodside Green offers easy access to shopping, recreation, and everyday conveniences. Downtown Stamford and the Metro-North train station are just five minutes away. Located on the main level, this home offers the convenience of stair-free living with no elevators to navigate. Two assigned parking spaces, plus additional guest parking, complete the package. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable, low-maintenance living in a convenient Stamford location!
