About this home

Experience a new standard of modern elegance in this brand new luxury home in the heart of Westport. Spanning over 4,200 square feet of finished living space across four beautifully appointed levels, this residence features stunning white oak floors and premium finishes throughout. The main level features a bright open floor plan that seamlessly integrates a gourmet kitchen with large center island into a spacious dining area and family room anchored by a warm gas fireplace. A dedicated first-floor office offers a quiet, sophisticated workspace, with the convenience of a nearby mudroom and powder room. In the back, the transition to the outdoors is seamless, as the family room opens to a covered patio and a sparkling in-ground gunite pool with bluestone surround. To ensure absolute tranquility, the yard will be professionally completed with fencing, trees, and shrubs. The second level of the home has four bedrooms, including a primary suite with a generous walk-in closet and a spa-like bathroom featuring a double sink, large shower, and soaking tub. An additional en-suite bedroom includes a walk-in closet, while two more bedrooms share a well-appointed bath. A laundry room with a dedicated sink is also located on this level. The third floor offers a bright flex space, while the fully finished basement provides a fifth bedroom, full bathroom, and a spacious playroom. Enjoy the convenience of city water, sewer, and natural gas in a quiet neighborhood close to premier shopping.
