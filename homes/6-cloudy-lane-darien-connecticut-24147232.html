About this home

Nestled on more than two private acres, 6 Cloudy Lane presents an exceptional opportunity to own a classic estate property in one of Darien's most coveted neighborhoods. A thoughtful renovation by renowned designer Louise Brooks introduced timeless architectural elements that continue to define the home today. Expansive rooms, elegant detailing, and walls of windows fill the interiors with natural light while framing serene views of the surrounding property. With approximately 5,900 square feet on two levels, the layout is designed for both sophisticated entertaining and comfortable everyday living. An inviting living room with a fireplace and a light-filled dining room with a bay window create elegant gathering spaces. The generous kitchen, featuring double islands and an eat-in area, flows effortlessly into the expansive family room, where a coffered ceiling, wood-burning fireplace, and French doors open to the outdoors-perfect for indoor-outdoor entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite serves as a private retreat with tranquil views of the property, while four additional bedrooms provide comfortable accommodations for family and guests. The beautifully landscaped grounds include a tennis/pickleball court as well as a charming brick potting shed, offering endless possibilities as a changing room for tennis or a future pool. With its expansive lawn and mature trees, the property offers the potential to create a truly resort-like outdoor setting.
