About this home

Highest and Best by Noon on Saturday, 6/27. Nestled in the heart of highly desirable South Wilton, this beautifully updated Cape offers the perfect blend of timeless charm and modern comfort. Set on 1.13 picturesque acres, the home features over 3,500 square feet of living space, including four spacious bedrooms, two-and-a-half baths, and a flexible floor plan designed for today's lifestyle. Warm hardwood floors, two fireplaces, and abundant natural light create an inviting atmosphere. The kitchen is a cook's delight. It is updated with white cabinets, white granite countertops and gas stove, two sink areas, and a large center island with stools. There is also a breakfast nook, with lots of light and garden views. A garage with a studio above offers exceptional versatility for a home office, creative space, gym, or overflow guest space. Outside, enjoy a private backyard oasis complete with a patio, mature landscaping, and charming stone walls, ideal for outdoor dining, entertaining or simply relaxing in a peaceful setting. Conveniently located near Wilton's award-winning schools, town center, commuting routes, and local amenities, this special property offers the best of country living with modern convenience.
