About this home

ELECTRIC, HEAT, WATER, HOT WATER ALL INCLUDED IN THE RENT!! This sleek and roomy 1 bedroom 1 bathroom apartment puts you at the heart of Strawberry Hill, one of Stamford's most idyllic and beautiful neighborhoods. Perfect for anybody who wants to be close to the best the city has to offer, the prime location is set back along a quiet street less than two miles from the serene running paths of Scalzi Park. Spacious living room and open dining room area with hardwood floors throughout. When you're looking to get out, the location couldn't be more convenient, putting you five minutes from downtown and close to Chelsea Piers as well as several golf courses, country clubs, and other top attractions. Commuters will also appreciate the close proximity to the train station and I-95. Garage spot, door man and exercise room also included.
