About this home

Welcome to 10 Old Field Place, a 4-bedroom split-level on a peaceful cul-de-sac in Rowayton. This spacious home sits on 0.29 acres and offers 2,700+ square feet of living space. A beautiful front deck and large foyer welcome you in. Three large windows pour natural light into the striking two-story living room with built-in shelving, soaring ceilings, and a wood-burning fireplace--with access to one of many decks. Overlooking it is a formal dining room with a bay window. The eat-in kitchen features brand new appliances including a dishwasher, refrigerator, and induction stove, plus a walk-out balcony with awning. The upper level includes 3 beds and 2 full baths, including the spacious primary suite with updated bathroom and a hallway bath with dual sinks. The finished lower level offers flexible space with another bedroom and full bath-ideal for an au pair/in-law space, playroom, or office--plus a new washer/dryer. The basement works as a gym or storage. The home includes central AC, forced hot water oil heat, and an attached 2-car garage. Outside, the yard provides privacy, multiple decks, and entertaining spaces. This sought-after neighborhood is a short distance from the train, town beaches at Bayley, Roton Middle School, Rowayton Elementary, the community center, library, Pinkney Park, and preschools, with Wilson Point and Norwalk Yacht Club nearby. 10 Old Field Place offers a rare blend of small-town charm and commuter convenience-and all the fun Rowayton has to offer.
