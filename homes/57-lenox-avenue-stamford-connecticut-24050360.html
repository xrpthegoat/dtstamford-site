About this home

A quaint 3 bedroom 1927 colonial, on one of Glenbrook's most desirable streets. A tastefully remodeled kitchen is the hub of the home. With stone counters and stainless steel appliances the kitchen has counter seating and opens to the dining area and living room. The dining area has French Doors leading to the sun-drenched deck. The oversized living room is anchored by a cozy wood-burning fireplace. The sun room could double as a first floor home office. A powder room and mudroom complete the main level. Beautiful hardwood floors throughout the home. The second floor has three generous sized bedrooms and a nicely remodeled full bath. There is a finished game room and extra storage closets on the walk up third floor. The full basement is unfinished, with loads of storage space and the laundry area. This home enjoys city water, city sewer and natural gas. The property is a level .25 acre lot on a handsome tree-lined street complete with sidewalks. In addition to the low maintenance Azek deck in the back yard, there is a lovely lawn, detached 2-car garage, and fire pit area for outdoor enjoyment throughout the seasons. Walking distance to the Glenbrook Train Station, and minutes to public beaches and the vibrant downtown Stamford. Welcome home!
