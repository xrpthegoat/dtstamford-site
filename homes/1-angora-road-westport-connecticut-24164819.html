About this home

One of Westport's premiere CUL-DE-SACS, WALKING DISTANCE TO ALL 3 SCHOOLS (Long Lots, Bedford, Staples), and 2.3 MILES TO GREENS FARMS TRAIN STATION, this beautifully renovated (2016/2017) 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home perfectly blends modern luxury with timeless charm. Set on a full acre of manicured grounds, this property offers a serene retreat just minutes from every essential. The open floor plan is designed for effortless entertaining, centered around a gourmet eat-in kitchen with high-end appliances. The main level hosts three bedrooms and 2.5 baths, while the upper level serves as a private sanctuary-a lavish primary suite featuring two expansive walk-in closets, a spa-inspired bath, and a cozy lounge. The light-filled, walk-out lower level is exceptionally versatile, offering a 5th bedroom/gym, full bath, rec room, mudroom, and a dedicated home office with three separate entrances-ideal for remote work. Outside, your personal oasis awaits: a sprawling deck overlooks an inviting in-ground pool/spa, surrounded by lush greenery and a yard perfect for recreation. Unbeatable location complete with natural gas, public water, and easy access to vibrant shopping and dining. This isn't just a home-it's a lifestyle.
