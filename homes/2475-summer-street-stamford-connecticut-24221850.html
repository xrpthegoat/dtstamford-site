About this home

Welcome to Colonial Gardens! This charming 2 bedroom, 1 bathroom condominium is conveniently located at 2475 Summer Street in Stamford. Offering 819 square feet of comfortable, single level living space, this inviting unit features hardwood floors, neutral tones, and an abundance of natural light. The spacious living room provides a warm and welcoming atmosphere with large windows, while the eat-in kitchen offers a dedicated space for everyday dining. Both bedrooms provide flexible living arrangements, whether you need additional sleeping space, a home office, or a guest room. The classic brick exterior, landscaped grounds, and attractive walkways add to the charm of this established condominium community. Additional conveniences include one parking space and access to a shared basement for storage. The monthly HOA fee includes heat, hot water, water, grounds maintenance, snow removal, and property management. Conveniently situated near shopping, dining, parks, public transportation, and Stamford's many amenities, this property offers a wonderful opportunity to enjoy low-maintenance living in a desirable location.
