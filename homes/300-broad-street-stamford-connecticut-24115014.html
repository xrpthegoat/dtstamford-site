About this home

Experience city living in this stunning top-floor 2BR/2BTH corner unit, filled with natural light and offering amazing views of downtown Stamford. The open floor plan features large windows, engineered hardwood floors throughout, a kitchen with granite counters, stainless steel appliances and a breakfast bar open to the living/dining room. The spacious primary bedroom includes a huge walk-in closet and a full bath. Enjoy the convenience of an in-unit washer/dryer to complete the space. Comes with 2 parking spots! New HVAC's throughout. The Windemere offers exceptional amenities, including secured entry, assigned garage parking, an elevator, patio area with bbq's...great for entertaining and a newly renovated fitness center. Enjoy a short walk to all that downtown Stamford has to offer. Please note, NO PETS permitted. Good credit (over 700) and references a must!
