About this home

Don't miss this charming, completely renovated home quietly tucked away, yet exceptionally located in the heart of Cos Cob. A beautiful covered front porch leads to the inviting foyer with interior windows, open plan living room, dining area, and gorgeous kitchen featuring a huge island and French door access to deck and backyard. Main level bedroom with barn door and en-suite bath is also the perfect spot for an office. Serene primary suite offers built-ins, spa bath with radiant floors, towel warmer, and soaking tub. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a great laundry room complete the second floor. The lower level was completed in 2025 and you can customize it to fit your needs; using the large space as a playroom, gym, or family room. Also has outdoor access to the fenced-in backyard. Home was fully gutted in 2018 with new roof, siding, electrical, windows and sprinkler system; driveway and Trex deck were redone in the last few years as well. Close to Cos Cob Village, Cos Cob and Loughlin Parks, and enjoy being just minutes away from Cos Cob school, restaurants, and the train. Total sq. footage including finished basement is about 2,260.
