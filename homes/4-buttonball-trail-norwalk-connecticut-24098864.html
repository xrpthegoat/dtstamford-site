About this home

Welcome to this charming and freshly updated Cape Cod-style home for rent in Norwalk's desirable West Rocks neighborhood! With 3 bedrooms and 1.5 baths, this sun-filled home offers a flexible layout that includes two main-level bedrooms and a spacious primary suite tucked privately upstairs. Step inside to beautifully refinished hardwood floors, freshly painted interior and a welcoming flow throughout. The bright kitchen with new energy efficient dish washer and stainless steel appliances opens to a deck-perfect for your patio set and summer dinners under the stars. An attached 1-car garage, basement storage space round out the home's thoughtful features. Ideal for commuters, this location is just 5 minutes to both I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, and only 10 minutes to the South Norwalk train station-making daily travel a breeze. Don't miss this inviting home with character, convenience, and comfort in all the right places!
