About this home

This stunningly remodeled home built in 1900 seamlessly blends old New England charm with modern amenities. This spacious residence features 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and 2 half baths, nestled on a level lot adjacent to open play fields, perfect for outdoor activities. Upon entering from the beautiful enclosed porch, you'll find a formal dining room and a bright living room, complete with a cozy gas fireplace. The gourmet chef's kitchen is a true highlight, featuring gas cooking, custom cabinetry, a farmer's sink, and a large center island, complemented by a charming breakfast nook. Hardwood floors flow throughout, enhancing the home's elegant feel. The second floor offers a serene primary bedroom with an en-suite bath, along with two generously sized bedrooms, a den, and an additional full bath. Ascend to the third floor to discover the fourth bedroom, accompanied by another full bath, ideal for guests or privacy. The finished lower level is perfect for entertaining and everyday living, featuring a family room with a built-in entertainment center, an office space, a laundry room, and a convenient half bath. The spacious back deck overlooks the beautiful landscaping and provides an inviting atmosphere for outdoor gatherings and relaxation. Additional features include a carport for convenience. One of the most sought after streets in Greenwich. 3 blocks to Greenwich avenue, 1/2 block to Brunswick school one block to Greenwich academy. Make this charming home your own!
