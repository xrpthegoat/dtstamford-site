About this home

This pristine, classic colonial offers 3,562 square feet of quality craftsmanship and modern elegance. Situated on 1.8 acres backing up to open space, this home provides a perfect blend of privacy and community. Inside, discover the chef's kitchen featuring a six-burner gas range, farm sink, and top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances. A Pietra Cardosa honed countertop and island seating 4-6 make it ideal for gatherings. French doors open to a composite deck, perfect for enjoying the landscaped backyard. The main level boasts hardwood floors, a family room with vaulted ceilings and a slate fireplace, a formal dining room, a cozy living room with a gas fireplace, and a convenient mudroom and laundry room. Over the garage, there is the ideal bonus/playroom. Upstairs, the primary suite is a serene retreat with a marble bathroom featuring a frameless glass shower and soaking tub. Three additional bedrooms, including a second ensuite, offer ample space. Additional amenities include a full-house automatic generator, two basements for storage, an attached garage, central AC, and a security system.
