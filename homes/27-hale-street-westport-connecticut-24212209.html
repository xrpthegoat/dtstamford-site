About this home

Prepare to be enchanted by this recently remodeled and utterly charming colonial home. As you step inside, the 1st floor warmly welcomes you in a living room adorned with a wood burning fireplace, creating the perfect ambiance for cozy evenings. The dining room seamlessly flows into the kitchen, providing an open & inviting space that's ideal for hosting gatherings. The kitchen itself boasts newer cabinets, elegant quartz countertops & gleaming stainless steel appliances, catering to both culinary enthusiasts & entertainers alike. Additionally, a convenient half bath & a well appointed laundry room round out the 1st floor. Venturing upstairs, you'll discover 4 generously sized bedrooms, each offering ample closet space to accommodate your storage needs. The full bathroom on this level has been meticulously remodeled to offer both style & functionality. The lower level presents a vast, unfinished space brimming with potential, currently serving as storage but ready to be transformed into your dream living area. Additional highlights include hardwood floors throughout, newer propane furnace, central AC for your comfort & a freshly constructed patio featuring a wood burning fireplace. The perfect spot to enjoy outdoor gatherings & create lasting memories. Situated for convenient commuting & close to vibrant downtown Westport, this home eagerly awaits its new, loving tenants.
