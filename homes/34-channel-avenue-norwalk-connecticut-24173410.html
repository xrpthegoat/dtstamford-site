About this home

Exceptional coastal living awaits in this beautifully appointed 4-bedroom, 3 full bath retreat in the coveted Harborview community. Where a home feels less like a destination and more like an escape. Bathed in natural sunlight, the open-concept main floor is designed for effortless entertaining, with the living room, dining area, and kitchen all capturing unobstructed water views and the ever-changing beauty of the surrounding landscape. French doors open to a large, covered deck that invites you to enjoy morning coffee, al fresco dining, or evening cocktails overlooking the shoreline. A versatile main-floor bedroom with an en suite bath offers the perfect space for guests, a home office, or a family room, complete with French doors opening to a private backyard oasis featuring a stone patio. The Primary Suite is a serene sanctuary featuring sweeping water views and a covered private balcony where you can unwind with salt air breezes. Other features include 2 additional bedrooms, a large mudroom, and a spacious 2-car garage. Located within the sought-after Harborview Beach Association, residents enjoy resort-style amenities including a waterfront clubhouse with year-round events, tennis courts, playground, ball field, dock, beach, and moorings - all within a vibrant, dog-friendly neighborhood. Just five minutes from South Norwalk, the Metro-North Railroad station, and the trendy SONO dining district. The epitome of coastal lifestyle - where every day feels like a vacation.
