About this home

2500 sq ft South Wilton Ranch with High-End Finishes Throughout, this beautiful home has been completely rebuilt down to the studs, offering a seamless blend of modern luxury and thoughtful design. Privately set back from the road, the home is approached by a long driveway and enhanced by newly installed deer fencing and gated entry, creating a serene and secure setting. Ideally located in South Wilton, just minutes to town, shopping, and commuting routes. The spacious primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, oversized dual walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired bath with radiant heated floors and dual vanities. At the heart of the home is a state-of-the-art kitchen equipped with Energy Star appliances, designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The finished lower level offers exceptional flexibility and can be utilized as a bedroom, playroom, or home office, complete with a wine cellar and tasting bar-perfect for hosting or relaxing in style. Step outside to a private outdoor entertainment area featuring a custom built-in barbecue, ideal for outdoor dining, all surrounded by mature specimen plantings. Additional highlights include new garage doors along with a fully automated irrigation and sprinkler system servicing both front and back yards.
