About this home

Discover the perfect blend of historic charm and modern living at 12 Hunt Street, a beautifully updated and expanded 1890s carriage house in the heart of Rowayton. Boasting 3 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and over 2,500 sq. ft. of thoughtfully designed space, this home is a true gem. The home includes a bright and sunny kitchen and living room, a dining room, and a family room that opens to a spacious deck overlooking a private, beautifully landscaped backyard. The oversized primary suite features a spacious changing room, heated bathroom floor, and balcony, while two additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the second floor. The walk-out lower level includes a versatile living space with a full bath, perfect for guests, a home office, or a recreation area, and ample storage. Plus, a walk-up third floor provides even more storage options. The home also includes a Tesla EV charging connector. Ideally located within walking distance to Rowayton's seaside village shops, water, and the train station, this home enjoys easy NYC access. With timeless character and all the modern amenities, 12 Hunt Street is ready for you to move right in.
