About this home

Tucked away on a quiet yet convenient cul-de-sac in sought-after South Wilton, this unique Mid-Century Modern home is a rare blend of character, functionality & serenity. Set on a private one-acre lot, the property exudes peace & tranquility while remaining close to everyday conveniences. Every inch of the 1,260 above ground sf has been thoughtfully designed to maximize useful space, light & flow. The main level is filled w/ natural sunlight streaming through expansive two-story windows, highlighting the hardwood floors & creative architectural details. The living room is anchored by a striking contemporary corner brick gas fireplace and dramatic floor-to-ceiling window bank, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. A cozy dining nook & efficient galley kitchen offer easy access to a gravel patio, making indoor-outdoor entertaining effortless. Upstairs, the generous primary bedroom features 2 walk-in closets and an interior balcony that overlooks the main level, enhancing both the home's character and its abundance of light. A 2nd bedroom provides flexible space, perfect for a guest room or home office. A full bath w/ stall shower completes the second level. Meticulously maintained, the home offers exceptional value. Private, distinctive, and and impeccably cared for, this exceptional property is truly one of a kind. Ideal commuter location. Close to trains, shopping, parks & trails, Wilton Center, Westport and Norwalk.
