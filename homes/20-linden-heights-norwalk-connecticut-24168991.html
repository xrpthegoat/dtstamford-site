About this home

Welcome to 20 Linden Heights, a beautifully maintained detached single-family home in Norwalk's sought-after Linden Heights community. Set in a picturesque and beautifully landscaped setting, this inviting residence offers the perfect blend of comfort, privacy, and easy living. Inside, you'll find a spacious and airy layout with vaulted ceilings, sun-filled living spaces, and a warm, welcoming flow ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The eat-in kitchen opens to a large private deck, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor connection and a perfect place to relax, host guests, or take in the home's wonderful sunset views. The great room with a fireplace adds warmth and charm, while the finished walk-out lower level offers versatile space for a playroom, media room, home office, gym, or guest area. The spacious primary suite provides a peaceful retreat, and the home's thoughtful layout offers flexibility for today's lifestyle. Complete with a two-car attached garage, manicured landscaping, and the benefits of community maintenance, this is a rare opportunity to enjoy a detached home in one of Norwalk's most desirable residential enclaves. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, parks, and major transportation routes, 20 Linden Heights offers an exceptional combination of serenity, convenience, and neighborhood appeal.
