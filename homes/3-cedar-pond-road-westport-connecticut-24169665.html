About this home

Tucked away at the end of a long private drive, this exceptional property offers rare privacy just mins from everything Westport has to offer. Set on 2.1 acres, 3 Cedar Pond Rd is a true hidden oasis in the heart of town. Custom-built by noted architect George White as a 'replica' Colonial, the home reflects the timeless character of early CT architecture & was recognized by the Westport Historic District Commission for its authenticity. The 4-BR residence is beautifully maintained & turnkey, with a host of improvements already in place. The kitchen, featuring marble countertops & updated appliances, flows into the living rm, creating an easy layout for everyday living & entertaining. Both spaces open through doors to an expansive screened-in porch with views of Cedar Pond, while an all-season sunroom wrapped in walls of glass enhances the connection between indoor & outdoor living. The main level also includes a formal dining rm & study, while 4 fireplaces add warmth & character throughout. The lower level offers a fully legal walk-out office suite w/ fireplace & private entrance-ideal for a home business, recreation or guest accommodation. A detached 2-car garage & expansive property provide endless possibilities. Enjoy Cedar Pond year round-skating in winter, fishing & canoeing in the warmer months. With its rare blend of privacy, architectural significance & close proximity to town, schools & Merritt Pky, this is a unique opportunity to own a timeless Westport retreat!
