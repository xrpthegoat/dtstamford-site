About this home

Tucked along one of Wilton's most coveted cul-de-sacs, 86 Middlebrook Farm Road is a home that truly belongs to its setting-quiet, prestigious, and effortlessly beautiful. Architecturally designed with intention and a refined eye, this residence balances timeless elegance with modern livability. Spanning 12 thoughtfully curated rooms, including 5 bedrooms, 5 full baths, and a powder room, the home offers both sophistication and comfort in equal measure. The heart of the home is a stunning kitchen, where an oversized marble island, two dishwashers, a walk-in pantry, and seamless flow create the perfect backdrop for daily living and entertaining alike. Each space feels purposeful, inviting, and easy to enjoy. Outdoors, the property transforms into a private, parklike retreat, bordered on one side by the Wilton Land Trust and on the other by 48 acres of the Wilton Riding Club. Flat, manicured grounds offers exceptional privacy and tranquility. The gunite pool serves as a sparkling focal point, paired with a sauna and lush landscaping, creating a true backyard escape. With flexible living options, including potential for a second primary suite, and a location just minutes to Wilton Center, top schools, and commuting routes, this home delivers a lifestyle that is both refined and relaxed.
