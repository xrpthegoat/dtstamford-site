About this home

Discover this beautifully renovated end-unit condo townhouse offering a spacious and modern living experience. Featuring 2 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a versatile office space, this home has been thoughtfully updated to meet all your needs. The open-concept living area flows seamlessly into a contemporary kitchen, ideal for both relaxing and entertaining. Enjoy the tranquility of your private deck, highlighted by a natural rock formation that creates a lush, green backdrop, offering a serene retreat right off your dining and kitchen area. Additional features include an attached garage, providing convenient and secure parking, as well as access to community amenities such as a refreshing pool and well-maintained tennis courts. This prime location is close to shopping, dining, and beautiful parks, ensuring you have everything you need just minutes away.
