About this home

This beautifully updated home offers bright, open living spaces and a serene, park-like setting. The spacious living and dining area boasts soaring vaulted ceilings, a cozy fireplace, and seamless access to an expansive deck perfect for outdoor entertaining. The recently updated kitchen shines with custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, and new stainless steel appliances. Upstairs, the primary suite features a walk-in closet and spa-like bath with a soaking tub and separate shower. Two additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the second level. A versatile first-floor office can double as a guest space or den, while the finished lower level provides endless possibilities. A two-car garage and community amenities, including a pool, tennis courts, and clubhouse, complete this home.
