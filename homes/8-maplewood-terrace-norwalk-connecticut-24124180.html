About this home

Step into this beautifully updated home featuring 9-foot ceilings on the first floor, new windows with privacy blinds and a modern kitchen with newer appliances and granite countertops. Conveniently located near I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and shopping, this home combines comfort with unbeatable accessibility.
