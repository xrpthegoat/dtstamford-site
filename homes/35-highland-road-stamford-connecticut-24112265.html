About this home

Prime Downtown Stamford Location - Live in the heart of Stamford with easy access to shopping, dining, entertainment, the library, public transportation, and the train station. This spacious 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse offers great views and a comfortable, functional layout. The main level features a bright living room with hardwood floors, a cozy fireplace, and sliding doors leading to a private deck. Ideal for relaxing or entertaining. A large dining area, great size kitchen, and a convenient powder room to complete the main floor. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts vaulted ceilings, walk-in closets, and an en-suite bathroom. The upper level also features a second full bathroom and additional bedroom. Pull down access to attic offers excellent storage space. New AC / Heating unit. Included are two parking spaces: one in a covered garage and one off-street. Don't miss this gem!
