About this home

Available for sale is a beautifully maintained end-unit offering tranquil views of the pond and pool. This special one-bedroom residence features a spacious primary suite with ensuite bath, a separate den ideal for a home office or guest space, an updated kitchen, dedicated laundry room, and a convenient hall powder room. A private deck provides the perfect spot to relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. This unit enjoys close access to both the pool and pond, making it one of the most desirable locations within the community. A storage unit comes with the unit available in the basement for added convenience. The complex has recently completed major improvements including newly paved parking areas and roadways, along with updated water heaters and boilers. The current owners have paid this assessment in full, providing a valuable benefit to the next owner. Not to be overlooked, the heat is included in the common charges for this complex, another great benefit with utility costs on the rise. The property is professionally landscaped and well cared for throughout. Access to the unit requires only a few stairs, making it an excellent option for anyone looking to avoid excessive stair use while still enjoying a private, elevated setting.
