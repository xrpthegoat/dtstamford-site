About this home

Welcome to Glenwood Commons! This bright and inviting 1-bedroom, 1-bath condominium offers easy one level living in a desirable Norwalk location. Situated on the ground floor, this end unit home features an open-concept living room and dining room layout that maximizes space and natural light. The primary bedroom has a walk in closet and private balcony, perfect for enjoying your morning coffee or relaxing outdoors. A rare highlight is an assigned parking space plus a convenient attached one-car garage with additional storage space. Recent community improvements include brand-new siding and newly paved driveways, enhancing both the appearance and value of the complex. Conveniently located near shopping, South Norwalk restaurants, Calf Pasture Beach, the Maritime Center, Metro North, Route 7 and I-95, this move-in-ready condo offers comfort, convenience, and low-maintenance living in the sought-after Glenwood Commons community.
