About this home

Come enjoy this move-in-ready ranch minutes to downtown Stamford! This well-maintained residence has been completely renovated with luxury finishes and modern technology. Upon entry you are greeted with an open concept main level complete with new white oak floors and crown molding. The living room features a wood burning fireplace and a wall of windows overlooking the front cascading lawn. Steps away is the kitchen, a chef's dream featuring high-end Brighton custom cabinets, Cafe appliances, a large island with double thick Caesarstone countertops, and a built-in wine/drink fridge. Just past the kitchen is the sunroom which brings in an immense amount of natural light and showcases the beautiful flat and fully fenced backyard. A brand-new patio and pergola, natural gas BBQ hookup, custom brick pizza oven, and a built-in Argentinian wood-fired grill are also included. The other side of the main floor features three well-appointed bedrooms, one being a primary suite. The primary and guest bathrooms have been fully redone, featuring custom wood vanities, heated tile floors, and upgraded plumbing. A new sliding barn door separates the living and bedroom areas. A fully finished basement completes the interior which features new tile floors, a half bath, and built-in wiring for surround sound speakers. New appliances include the washer/ dryer, hot water heater, forced hot air handler, air condenser, and electric panel. Don't miss the opportunity to view this spectacular property!
