About this home

Timeless Colonial Estate Nestled on a scenic 2.4 acre property, this exquisite six-bedroom, four full and two half-bath Colonial estate combines classic charm with modern potential. Located just moments from the renowned Hunt Club polo fields, this home provides a unique blend of privacy and convenience, with easy access to town, train stations, and shops. Built in 1955, this Center Hall Colonial showcases stunning architectural beauty and timeless elegance. French doors open to a magnificent backyard, where specimen trees, age-old patios, and beautifully weathered stone walls create a serene oasis. Recently acquired by the esteemed Coastal Luxury Homes, known for their innovative designs and unmatched craftsmanship, this property provides a rare opportunity to modernize and personalize your dream home. Collaborate with Coastal Luxury Homes to transform this classic beauty into a luxurious residence that meets every modern need.
