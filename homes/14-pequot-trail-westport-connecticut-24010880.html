About this home

Widely regarded as having the best view on Nash Pond, 14 Pequot Trail will charm you with modern elegance inside and magazine-worthy spaces outside. This immaculate, extensively renovated home on a beautifully manicured acre offers 4,400 sq ft +/- of comfort and style, optimized to fully enjoy the tranquil backyard waterfront. Masterful design with custom millwork encompasses 3-4 bedrooms, including an incredible Primary Suite with picturesque water views, a lovely Bath, a dreamy closet with custom cabinetry and skylights, and a private glass-rail deck. The gourmet eat-in Kitchen seamlessly opens to a breathtaking Family Room with gas fireplace, a dedicated coffee/wine bar, cathedral ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows for year-round water views. Entertain easily in the formal Living Room with wood-burning fireplace and Dining Room with Butler's Pantry and Wet Bar. A beautiful Wagner Gunite Pool & Spa with custom stone surround is further complemented by an exquisite screened-in porch, a cabana patio with outdoor fridge, a grill patio, and a floating dock -- all ideal spaces for outdoor living, entertaining, and relaxing. Convenience is paramount with one-level living and an expansive walk-out garden level with separate Sitting Room with wood-burning fireplace, Guest Suite with full Bath, and multiple additional flexible living spaces for additional bedrooms, gym - you decide. A separate-entrance Office provides seclusion, and a whole-house generator ensures peace of mind.
