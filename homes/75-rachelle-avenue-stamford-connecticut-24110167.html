About this home

Gracious colonial in Hubbard Heights. Inviting front porch. Spacious living room with built in shelves and a non working fireplace hardwood floors that need your touch. Formal dining room that is great for family gatherings. The bright eat in kitchen with newer dishwasher and refrigerator. There is a full bath off the kitchen with a shower done by bath fitters plus a mud room. The second level has the master bedroom with 3 additional bedrooms and a full bath. The finished walk up attic makes a great office space or play room. Partially finished lower level with a bath. Lovely private fenced in yard with a shed and new drive. Wonderful location in Hubbard Heights and close to the golf course. 5 minutes to bustling downtown Stamford, Metro North train station and I 95.
