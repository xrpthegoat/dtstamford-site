About this home

Picturesque North Stamford Cape with incredible curb appeal is located on 1.21 private acres. Gracious entry leads to exceptionally large living room with hardwood floors, woodburning fireplace and views of wooded backyard. Charming dining room with built-ins. Eat-in kitchen with mahogany countertops and newer stainless-steel appliances with access to powder room and screened porch. First-floor bedroom with full bath. Upstairs there are three additional bedrooms and a full bath. Access to two attics and unfinished basement with walk-out. Two separate patio areas. Two car detached garage. Gas heat and solar panels, which reduce electric bills. Newer roof. Instant hot water installed 2018. Whole house generator. A storybook gem! Wonderful potential for expansion. This is an estate sale and being sold "As Is". Subject to probate court approval.
