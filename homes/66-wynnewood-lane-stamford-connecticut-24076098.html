About this home

Discover the beauty of this exquisitely designed center hall colonial on one of the most desirable areas in North Stamford. Set on 2 manicured acres, this meticulously renovated home boasts nearly 5000 sqft of living space, featuring 5 spacious bedrooms, 3 full baths, & new powder room on main level. Step inside to find a gracious entry & stunning foyer w/custom, Farrow & Ball paint here & throughout offering a sophisticated ambiance. The home's layout w/impeccable taste is designed for both comfort & entertaining. Main level features open kitchen w/dining, Wolf & Bosch appl, butlers pantry & laundry. A gorgeous great room w/14-16 ft ceilings & french doors & conveniently located office. Formal dining room leads to screened in porch perfect for alfresco dining. Sneak away to the library for moments of quiet reflection & reading, 2 fireplaces to add addln ambiance & cold weather comfort. On the second floor find the Master Bedroom w/fireplace, hdwd floors, & large ensuite bath w/oversized shower stall, soaking tub & large his/hers walk in closet. Addnl 3 bedrooms w/custom closets, hdwd floors & full bath in hallway also on this level. The upper level's living space is a spectacularly designed 1200 sqft guest suite & media room. Lower level has a versatile remodeled gym w/ample room for equipment, New 3 zone A/C sys, central vac, generator, 12 zone sprinkler sys & new roof. This home combines colonial charm w/ modern amenities & is a must see!
