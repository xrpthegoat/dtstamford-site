About this home

Enjoy one of Westport's most convenient locations from this 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath rental home with two parking spaces and a fenced backyard with patio. Both full bathrooms have just been renovated, many interior spaces have been freshly painted, and first-floor wood floors refinished. Comfortable living spaces extend across three levels, including a finished lower level that works well as a recreation room, home office, workout area, or flexible-use space. All three bedrooms are located together on the second floor. Convenient to Saugatuck Center, Metro-North, I-95, and the many amenities that make Westport such a sought-after place to live, including Main Street shopping and dining, the Westport Library, Levitt Pavilion, Compo Beach, Longshore Club Park, tennis, pickleball, recreational opportunities, and the Saugatuck River.
