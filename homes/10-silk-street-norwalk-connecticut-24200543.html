About this home

10 Silk Street offers a charming, easy-to-manage space with modern finishes. It's an easy way to step into homeownership, with the space you need inside and out and a location that keeps everyday life convenient. Set in an established residential neighborhood, this inviting home features a level backyard with plenty of room for play sets, barbecues, gardening and outdoor games. A spacious deck extends the living space outdoors, while the detached one-car garage provides parking, storage and added flexibility. Inside, the open living room/play area flows easily into the dining room and then to the updated kitchen, creating a casual, connected space for everyday living and entertaining. A back porch/mudroom offers a practical transition from the yard and deck, especially useful for coats, shoes, pets and all the things that come with an active household. There are two bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with its own convenient half bath/dressing room, an uncommon feature at this price point. The second bedroom offers flexibility for guests, young people, a home office or future needs. Convenient Norwalk location just 4 minutes to the Sono Mall and a quick walk to multiple schools and playgrounds. 10 Silk Street is a terrific alternative to condo living both indoors and out for anyone looking for a place of their own.
