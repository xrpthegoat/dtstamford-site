About this home

Enjoy the best of coastal living this summer in idyllic Rowayton. With views of Wilson Cove and the Norwalk Yacht Club, this 3800sf home welcomes all with soaring, cathedral ceilings as you are drawn into the great room with a stone, wood-burning fireplace and floor to ceiling windows where light and water views tickle the imagination. The spectacular great room has a gracious flow for entertaining with multiple seating areas and adjacent dining area that opens to a well equipped, gourmet kitchen complete with honed marble countertops, gas cooktop, double wall ovens, and a convenient wine fridge. For more private moments, retreat to the first floor lounge with French doors opening to a patio and fabulous yard where summer comes alive. Outside is like a mini-country club on 1.4 acres. By day, improve your golf game practicing chipping and putting on the custom golf green. Then, take a dip in the refreshing pool, that entices with a light show as the sun sets. Dine alfresco on the deck and toast s'mores around the stone firepit as the day comes to a close. With 3 bedrooms and 3 full baths, plus a loft with office area, there's plenty of room for all. An additional 1,700 square feet in the lower level has a large laundry room, home gym and access to the 2 car garage. A summer full of enjoyment awaits in this hidden oasis and is complimented with community events like Shakespeare on the Sound, the Farmer's Market, Porch Jam, beach access, and coastal vibes.
