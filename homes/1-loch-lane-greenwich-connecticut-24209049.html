About this home

Privately positioned on 3.5 acres at the end of a quiet mid-country Greenwich cul-de-sac, this distinguished stone and brick estate offers an exceptional blend of timeless architecture, refined interiors and resort-style living. Designed with both grand entertaining and everyday comfort in mind, the residence unfolds across three beautifully appointed levels, all connected by a wood-paneled elevator. Elegant formal rooms give way to a chef's kitchen, coffered-ceiling family room and private office, while multiple doors invite effortless indoor-outdoor living. Five ensuite bedrooms provide luxurious accommodations, complemented by a versatile bonus room. The walk-out lower level is a destination unto itself, featuring a fireside game room, full bar, wine cellar, private theater and sauna. Outside, sweeping lawns, mature landscaping and lush gardens surround the pool, pergola, summer kitchen and covered terrace, creating a serene and exceptionally private setting. A gated entrance and four-car garage complete this impressive Greenwich estate.
