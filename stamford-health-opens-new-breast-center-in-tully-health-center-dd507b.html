Stamford Health Opens New Breast Center in Tully Health Center

Stamford Health opens a state-of-the-art breast center at Tully Health Center, boosting local access to integrated, patient-centered care.

Uploaded · August 12, 2026

The opening of the new Odyssey Group Breast Center at Stamford Health’s Tully Health Center marks a significant advancement in local healthcare infrastructure. For residents of Stamford and Lower Fairfield County, this development enhances access to comprehensive, coordinated breast care—improving patient experience through better privacy, flow, and technology. As a real estate professional in Stamford, I see this as a signal of ongoing investment in the city’s health and wellness ecosystem, which directly influences housing demand and neighborhood vitality.

What's happening

On August 6, 2026, Stamford Health officially unveiled the newly transformed Odyssey Group Breast Center, located on the first floor of the Tully Health Center [1]. The center, a 13,000-square-foot facility, was made possible by a landmark $10 million philanthropic gift from the Odyssey Group Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Odyssey Group/OdysseyRe, a Stamford-based global reinsurance and specialty insurance underwriter [1]. This project represents the final milestone in Stamford Health’s Campaign for Women and Babies [1]. The renovation included relocating and reimagining the breast care services to bring together advanced imaging, diagnostics, and clinical expertise in a single, coordinated space [2]. The redesign focuses on improving patient flow, increasing privacy, and enhancing comfort [3]. Stamford Health performed more than 33,000 mammograms in fiscal year 2025, and demand continues to rise [2]. The gift was announced in 2022 and the transformed center opened August 6, 2026 [2].

The new center co-locates breast radiologists and surgeons, who previously operated across different floors, and will allow specialists from the Stamford Health Bennett Cancer Center, including medical and radiation oncologists, to see patients on-site [3]. The facility incorporates state-of-the-art technology and is designed to support patients from diagnosis through to reconstruction [2]. The project’s completion follows a long-standing partnership between the Odyssey Group Foundation and Stamford Health, beginning in 2008 with a $2 million donation for the da Vinci Surgical System, and continuing with a $10 million commitment in 2011 for a trauma center [2].

A community investment with lasting impact

This new center is not just a medical upgrade—it’s a reflection of sustained community commitment. The $10 million donation from the Odyssey Group Foundation, the philanthropic arm of Odyssey Group/OdysseyRe, a Stamford-based global reinsurance and specialty insurance underwriter, underscores the city’s role as a hub for both healthcare and corporate philanthropy [2]. The fact that the project was completed in 2026, after years of planning and construction, shows that large-scale health infrastructure improvements are still possible in Stamford despite rising development costs and regulatory complexity [2]. The center’s focus on person-centered care and seamless coordination across specialties aligns with broader trends in healthcare delivery—where convenience, dignity, and integrated services are increasingly valued by patients [1].

The location at the Tully Health Center, a well-established medical hub, reinforces the area’s status as a key node in Stamford’s healthcare network. While the sources do not specify changes to parking, transit access, or surrounding commercial use, the presence of a major medical facility like this one typically increases foot traffic and supports nearby retail and service businesses. The fact that the center is fully operational and open to patients as of August 2026 means it is already serving the community, not just in planning stages [1].
