About this home

Fresh and appealing condition in this 3 bedroom, 2 full bath ranch style unit which includes a two car detached garage. Gas approximately $65.00 per month, electric approximately $154 per month. Water and sewer included in monthly rental. Walk up attic for storage only. Excellent credit a must with verification of employment and verification of income. An easy walk to the CT Transit stop and the Glenbrook train station.
