About this home

ELECTRICITY HEAT AND HOT WATER INCLUDED. Enjoy the tranquility of a beautiful West Norwalk neighborhood bordering New Canaan and Darien only moments away from the Route 7 commuter line. Meander along the stone path and mature forsythia bushes to the private entrance. A modern patio with shiplap privacy fencing is a special spot for combined indoor/outdoor living. This legal apartment is situated facing South and West extending the enjoyment of sunny days year-round. Upon entry you will immediately take note of the charm, cleanliness, updates and the overall welcoming open space. Built-in bookshelves, decorative fireplace, handsome new flooring, granite counters, and patio slider door accent the combined kitchen/living concept. Pass through timeless French doors to make your way to the large un-suite bedroom. The full bathroom boasts classic white finishes with beadboard and a tiled stall shower with seamless entry. A private and new stackable washer dryer, oversized closet and large windows complete this warm room. Paved parking included. Credit check (good is a must) and background checks are required. Pets are welcome with some restrictions.
