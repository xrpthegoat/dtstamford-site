About this home

Wonderful opportunity to own this beautiful, historic, investment property in an ideal location on the Rt. 7 corridor. This property offers a large building with over 4,000 sq. ft. on 3 floors with several (9) treatment rooms, 1 full bath and 4 half-baths, kitchen and hardwood floors. There is also a Carriage Barn on the property which allows significant storage/parking possibilities. Approximately 25 parking spaces available. This property is convenient to both downtown Wilton and Cannondale train station and has highway accessibility to both the Merritt Parkway and I-95 within minutes.
