About this home

This beautifully maintained bright and inviting five-bedroom residence offers a spacious open floor plan with exceptional light. The living room with fireplace overlooks the private property. A dining area offers a great space for formal or casual entertaining. The spacious gourmet custom kitchen features, all stainless-steel appliances and many cabinets for storage, a large center dining island all with granite counters. A main level office is located adjacent to the living room. Five bedroom and three and a half baths make this a comfortable home with room for family. The primary bedroom features a walk-in closet and bathroom with whirlpool tub and shower as well as two vanities. A large recreation room features a balcony overlooking the yard below. The property has an automatic full house generator. This home is less than a mile from the train station and walking distance to schools. Come enjoy this well thought out family home.
