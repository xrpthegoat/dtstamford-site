About this home

An absolutely wonderful home located in the charming community of Cranbury in Norwalk. The home has been recently remodeled (kitchen and baths) and sits beautifully on a level half acre plus. This freshly painted, open floor plan home includes a formal living room, dining room with walk-out to expansive deck, stunning new eat-in kitchen with center island and newer appliances. Also located on the main level are 2 hall bedrooms with a full bath and the primary bedroom with a newer bath. The lower level(estimated sq ft) includes a spacious family room with fireplace, bedroom/guest bedroom/office, a half bath and laundry (potential in-law apartment). Other exceptional features include central air, two car garage, newer hardwood flooring, new roof... A Great Location, A Great Home, A Great Value!!!
