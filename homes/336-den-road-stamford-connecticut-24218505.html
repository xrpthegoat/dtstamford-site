About this home

Welcome to 336 Den Road, North Stamford, where comfort and exceptional entertaining come together on a picturesque 1.07-acre lot. This spacious 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath Contemporary Colonial blends classic elegance with versatile living spaces, all convenient to downtown and major parkways. The main level features a large living room with a fireplace, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a generous family room with a second fireplace. Just off the family room is an all-season sunroom equipped with electric heat and a ceiling fan, offering year-round views of the grounds. A flexible first-floor bedroom serves ideally for guests or a home office. Upstairs, the primary suite features an updated bath and walk-in closet, joined by three well-sized bedrooms, walk-in closets, and an updated full bath. A partially finished walk-out basement adds recreation space and a separate laundry room. Outdoor living is a highlight with front and back yards, an entertaining deck, an outdoor kitchen setup, and an inground heated pool. The grounds also feature a mature orchard with apple, peach, pear, chestnut, and other fruit trees. Complete with hardwood floors, central A/C, a two-car garage, and a whole-house generator, this home offers comfortable living in every season.
