About this home

Welcome to 91 W Broad Street, Unit 13, a thoughtfully maintained three-bedroom, four-bath townhouse offering bright, light-filled interiors, large windows, dark hardwood floors, and an open floor plan across three spacious levels. Each bedroom features its own en-suite bathroom, providing exceptional privacy and comfort, while the spacious primary suite includes a large walk-in closet and a convenient coffee bar/kitchenette just outside the bedroom. The second-floor laundry area includes a brand-new washer and dryer, and the kitchen features a brand-new dishwasher. Thoughtfully upgraded throughout, the home offers Lutron Caseta smart light switches, Nest thermostats, a Nest Hello video doorbell with four additional security cameras, custom plantation shutters, upgraded shower doors, Elfa organization systems, bidets in every bathroom, an Insinkerator garbage disposal, attic storage, a tankless water heater, whole-home water filtration system, and a dedicated mechanical room. The attached two-car garage includes a Tesla Wall Charger with a dedicated 125-amp line and a quiet garage door opener with a secure lock. Available furnished or unfurnished, this move-in ready home is offered at $5,000 per month unfurnished or $5,250 per month furnished. Ideally located within walking distance of Stamford Hospital in the sought-after Hart Magnet Elementary School District, and just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North, I-95, restaurants, shopping, parks, and entertainment.
