About this home

Bright, sunny three bedroom colonial on cul-de-sac. Hardwood floors throughout with lovely eat-in kitchen with island, granite counters overlooking the backyard. Vaulted entertainment room has sliders to the patio and fenced-in pool area. Heated in-ground pool for summer fun! The primary bedroom has an ensuite bathroom and walk-in closet, two further bedrooms and a bathroom complete the second floor. There is a formal dining room and a large living room with fireplace. There is a finished basement with two recreational rooms/ office space that is not reflected in the public records. Landlord pays grounds maintenance and pool open and closing. Tenant pays for all utilities, credit check, snowplow, and pool weekly maintenance.
