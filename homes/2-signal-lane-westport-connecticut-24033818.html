About this home

Green's Farms Contemporary. Location, location, location! Situated on a cul-de-sac of homes surrounding a 6 acre lake, the setting of 2 Signal Lane is idyllic, private and convenient - just minutes to Westport's beaches, transportation options and amenities. Contoured to the .75 acre property, the home offers two levels for living and entertaining. The main level is open + spacious, graced by cathedral ceilings and hardwood floors. The great room, with dramatic stone fireplace, is open to the dining area and gourmet kitchen. Featuring white custom cabinetry, granite countertops, a breakfast bar and top of the line appliances, you will enjoy meal prep! Serve your guests indoors or out - a spacious Trex deck spanning the length of the home, overlooks the lake and yard. Laundry room, a half bath and the primary suite, with walk-in closet and luxurious bath, are also on this level. Downstairs a second family room awaits, with stone fireplace and walk-out access to the lakefront. Three bedrooms, bathroom and a generous storage area complete this level. There is much to love about this home! Freshly painted interiors, new and improved lighting in living areas, new air conditioner serving the main level, new GE Monogram convection/microwave oven and repaved driveway.
