WATERFRONT LOCATION | TWO-FAMILY INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITY. Discover an exceptional opportunity at 82-84 Harbor Drive, a two-family property offering an attractive combination of lifestyle and investment potential in one of Stamford's sought-after coastal areas. Ideally located near the water, this property provides convenient access to Stamford's public beaches, parks, waterfront recreation, dining, shopping, and the vibrant Harbor Point district. Enjoy the best of coastal living with nearby access to West Beach and Cummings Park, where residents can take advantage of sandy beaches, Long Island Sound views, waterfront walks, recreational facilities, and outdoor activities. Whether you're looking to expand a real estate portfolio, live in one unit while generating rental income from the other, or secure a property in a desirable waterfront market, 82-84 Harbor Drive presents a compelling opportunity.
