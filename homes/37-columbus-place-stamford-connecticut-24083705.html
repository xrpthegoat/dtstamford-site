About this home

Move right into this beautifully updated three-level townhouse, offering the perfect blend of space, style, and modern convenience. Located in the sought-after Springdale neighborhood, this home is designed for the way we live today. The updated eat-in kitchen will delight any home chef, featuring granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. The dining area flows seamlessly into the living room, which opens onto a private deck-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Hardwood floors add warmth and elegance throughout. Upstairs, you'll find a generously sized bedroom with ample closet space and a sprawling primary bedroom that can be divided into two rooms. Some units in this complex were originally built as three-bedroom homes, making this a flexible layout to suit your needs. A beautifully renovated full bath completes the second level. And there's more! Need a home office? The walk-out lower level features a versatile family room with a half bath-perfect for remote work, a playroom, media room, gym, or additional living space. An attached one-car garage adds convenience, while the complex's swimming pool offers a refreshing retreat on warm summer days.
