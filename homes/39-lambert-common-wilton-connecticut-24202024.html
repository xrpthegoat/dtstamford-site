About this home

Welcome to 39 Lambert Common! This lovely townhouse has been meticulously maintained and cared for by the same owner for the past 39 years, and the pride of ownership truly shows. Located in the desirable Lambert Commons complex, where units are rarely offered for sale, this corner end unit offers over 1,700 sq. ft. of living space and a two-car garage. The bright and open main level features a sun-filled living room, dining room, half bath, eat-in kitchen, and a versatile bonus room-perfect for a den or home office. The living room features a fireplace and sliders leading to a 15x15 stone patio and private yard space surrounded by beautiful perennial plantings. A convenient walkway provides direct access to the garage. The second level offers two spacious bedrooms with double closets, a full bath, & laundry for added convenience. The primary bedroom suite features its own private full bath with a double vanity & new stall shower. There is plenty of storage throughout, along with a partially finished room in the basement-perfect for a playroom, rec room, or home gym-plus a separate storage/utility room. All custom window treatments are included. Recent improvements include newer windows and appliances, a newer roof, HVAC system (2024), water heater (2024), basement sink and pump (2024), and a new garbage disposal (2026). With no assessments and a location in this sought-after complex, this beautiful end unit is a rare opportunity you won't want to miss!
