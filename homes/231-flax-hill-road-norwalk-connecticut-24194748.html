About this home

Welcome to this beautiful 2-bedroom, 1-bath first floor rental! This charming residence blends modern updates with original character, featuring sun-filled interiors, updated fixtures, and new appliances. The spacious eat-in kitchen offers sliders leading to a private rear deck-perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. Enjoy the inviting covered front porch and a lush backyard that backs up to Flax Hill Park, offering both privacy and natural beauty. Included in the rent are heat, hot water, and cooking gas, providing exceptional value and convenience. The home also comes with private in-unit laundry, adding to the ease of everyday living. A detached rear deck provides the ideal space for entertaining, grilling, or simply unwinding outdoors. Conveniently located near SONO's Metro-North station, vibrant restaurants, shops, and the Maritime Aquarium-this location perfectly combines comfort, character, and convenience.
