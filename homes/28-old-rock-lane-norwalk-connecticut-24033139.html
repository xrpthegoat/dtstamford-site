About this home

Nestled in the heart of West Norwalk on a private drive, this home boasts 4 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, this 2897 square-foot residence offers space for both relaxation and entertainment. Step inside to discover a welcoming foyer that leads to a spacious living area, featuring an open concept kitchen and sunken family room complete with fireplace and sliders that lead to an oversized deck and a yard fit for entertaining. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite countertops with ample counter space, and a cozy breakfast nook. Upstairs you'll find a spacious primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and ensuite. The additional 3 bedrooms offer flexibility for guests, residents, or a home office, ensuring everyone has their own space. Outside, the expansive backyard is perfect for outdoor gatherings or simply enjoying the natural surroundings. With a large deck with easy access from the sliding glass doors, you'll find yourself spending countless hours soaking up the sun or dining al fresco. Conveniently located near both New Canaan, and Norwalk, 95 and the Merritt, this home offers the perfect blend of privacy and accessibility. With hardwood floors throughout the home, a new roof, new shed, updated bathrooms, and a generator hook up you don't want to miss your chance to make this delightful home your own - schedule today! Driveway entrance on secluded Lagana Lane.
