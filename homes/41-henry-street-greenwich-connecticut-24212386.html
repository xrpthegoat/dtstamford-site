About this home

Welcome to 41 Henry Street, where a two-level layout meets the convenience of living just off Mill Street in Byram. This bright and spacious, renovated two-bedroom, one-and-a-half-bath side-by-side duplex residence offers 1,208 sqft of living space and two driveway parking spaces. Newly renovated, refinished floors and fresh paint throughout make this home move-in ready! The first floor features a living room, powder room, dining room, kitchen, and private in-unit laundry. A separate flex space offers versatility for a home office, playroom, or cozy den. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms with generous closet space and full bathroom. A walk-up attic accessed through the primary bedroom provides an additional 478 sqft of storage beyond the stated living area, with ample room for seasonal belongings and more. Shopping, dining, and Mill Street amenities are close at hand, while easy access to I-95, the Post Road, and Metro-North makes commuting convenient. A spacious, versatile home with everyday convenience at your fingertips.
