About this home

This beautiful and convenient 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom multi-level condo boasts a seamless flow with an integrated 2-car garage, and is ideally located near downtown Stamford, Ridgeway Shopping Center, and Scalzi Park. The spacious living room opens into the dining area, creating an inviting, open-concept space. The modern kitchen is equipped with a refrigerator, electric range, dishwasher, oven hood, and microwave. Newly refinished hardwood floors grace the main, 2nd and 3rd floors, adding warmth throughout. The master bedroom features a walk-in closet private en-suite with a whirlpool tub, separate shower, and a walk-in closet for ultimate convenience. The 3rd floor offers versatile space for an additional bedroom, office, playroom, or a combination of all three. The washer and dryer are conveniently located just outside the main bedrooms. The 2-car garage is equipped with extra storage and a garage door opener and an outlet for an EV charger. Move-in ready, this condo is waiting for you to enjoy.
