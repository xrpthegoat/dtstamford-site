About this home

Welcome to 806 Hope Street! This spacious end-unit townhome offers high ceilings, hardwood floors and generously sized rooms. The main level features a living room with gas fireplace, eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, walk-in pantry and sliders to a private backyard, plus main-level laundry. Upstairs, the primary suite includes an en-suite bath and walk-in closet, with two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath. The finished lower level offers flexible space for a living room, playroom, gym or home office. Oversized attached 1-car garage plus 2 additional parking spaces. Conveniently located near Springdale train station, shops, restaurants and more!
