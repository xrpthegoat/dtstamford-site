About this home

Set on one of Wilton's most sought-after cul-de-sac streets, this classic, turnkey Colonial offers 4,355 SF of finished living space, 4 bedrooms, and 3 full, 1 half baths. Thoughtfully renovated in 2023, updates include the roof, gutters, windows, kitchen, flooring, and interior and exterior paint. The chef's eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home, featuring a Wolf 8-burner range with double ovens, Sub-Zero, warming drawer, large center island, and generous workspace. Adjacent to the kitchen, the spacious family room features a gas fireplace and built-ins. Formal living and dining rooms flow beautifully for everyday living and entertaining, with gorgeous millwork throughout. Private office with dry bar, built ins and bay window make working from home a pleasure. A spacious mudroom with 1/2 bath, heated brick floors and built-in cubbies adds both charm and function. The inviting primary bedroom features a walk-in closet and luxurious bath with heated floors, spa tub, steam shower and sauna. Three additional bedrooms, a full, and second-floor laundry complete the upper level. Finished walk-out lower level offers flexible additional living space. A large year-round back porch overlooks the level backyard with plenty of room for play and entertaining. Whole-house generator and new, fully paid-for solar panels provide added peace of mind and significant energy savings. A beautifully updated home combining classic character, modern amenities, and a wonderful Wilton location.
