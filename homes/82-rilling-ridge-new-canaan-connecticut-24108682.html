About this home

Newly renovated and ready for immediate occupancy, this pristine, move-in ready home offers spacious one-floor living. The impressive 37 x 20 GREAT ROOM, featuring soaring ceilings and a fireplace, is a true showstopper. A brand-new, generously sized kitchen with a center island and breakfast nook sits adjacent to the great room family room & screened porch making it ideal for entertaining and everyday living. The cozy family room includes built-in bookshelves and a fireplace, creating a warm and inviting space. Just off the kitchen, the screened porch provides the perfect setting for seasonal outdoor dining-bug-free. New appliances include a gas range with hood, dishwasher, refrigerator, microwave, washer and dryer. This 3,300 SF home offers 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and an office that can be used as a 4th bedroom plus a 2 car garage for plentiful storage space. The primary bedroom features a brand-new bathroom, walk in closet and a private sitting room for added comfort. Located at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in the sought-after Silvermine neighborhood. Enjoy a private, park-like setting with mature landscaping and a slate patio-perfect for outdoor entertaining.
