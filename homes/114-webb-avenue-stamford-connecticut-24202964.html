About this home

Luxury living in the Cove! Completely reimagined with style, personality and an incredibly flexible floor plan, this is one of those homes that keeps surprising you. The sun-filled main level features hardwood floors, a spacious living room, bedroom and full bath, plus an updated kitchen with granite countertops, Viking refrigerator and Wolf range. Upstairs boasts 550 sqft of flexible space with endless possibilities - use as an expansive primary suite or split space into two bedrooms, a home office or playroom. The finished walk-out lower level adds another dimension to the home and has an ideal setup for multigenerational living, guests or as additional living space. Outside, the oversized backyard is incredibly private and surrounded by mature evergreens. Gather around the stone fireplace, entertain on the patio, or take advantage of the detached outbuilding with electricity - perfect for a gym, studio, workshop or home office. Even the practical details stand out: fully owned solar panels provide significant electricity savings, while two separate driveways from Webb Avenue and Mathews Street offer added convenience. All of this in Stamford's Cove neighborhood, close to Cove Island Park and the beach. Location is a commuters dream. Thoughtfully updated, unexpectedly spacious and packed with features you rarely find under one roof. 114 Webb Avenue is a home you NEED to see in person.
