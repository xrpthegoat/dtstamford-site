About this home

Experience coastal living in this stunning home located on a private road and serene cul-de-sac in the highly sought-after Compo Beach section of Westport. Just one mile from the sandy shores of Compo Beach and 1.5 miles from the Metro North station, this home offers both tranquility and convenience. Set on a spacious 1.02-acre lot, the property provides plenty of room to play, entertain, or simply relax. The expansive yard is perfect for outdoor activities, while the deck and stone patio offer an ideal spot to dine al fresco or enjoy your morning coffee in peace. This well maintained residence features discreet solar panels (fully paid off), keeping yearly electric bills under $500. With updates completed in 2020-including a new AC system, smart home for lights, fans and garage door new roof, gutters, and leaf guards-you'll enjoy modern comfort and energy efficiency. As a Westport resident enjoy Longshore's golf, pool, tennis and ice rink Westport's award-winning schools, renowned shopping, and fine dining are just moments away. Whether you're exploring the charming downtown, visiting local parks, or enjoying a day at the beach, Westport offers a vibrant lifestyle filled with upscale amenities and coastal charm.
