About this home

Year-Round Rental in Noroton Heights- This stylish three bedroom/two bathroom home has a sun-filled living room with fireplace and a large picture window. Newly renovated eat-in kitchen has ample cabinetry, quartz countertops, a sleek glass backsplash, and new stainless steel appliances. Glass doors open to a private deck-perfect for outdoor entertaining or peaceful mornings with coffee. This home also features: updated bathrooms, central air conditioning, a versatile rec room in the daylight walk-out basement-ideal for a home office, gym, or playroom, an attached garage with durable pebble tech flooring, attic space for additional storage, recessed lighting throughout. One mile proximity to the Noroton Heights train station and vibrant Darien Commons-home to restaurants, shops, and Equinox gym. As a resident, you'll also have beach rights to Weed and Pear Tree Point Beaches. Gas heat, public water and sewer complete the picture for easy, year-round living. A professional Air Duct cleaning service was just done involving HEPA Filters for an enhanced clean environment.
