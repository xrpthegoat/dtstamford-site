About this home

Rarely Available Top-Floor Rental in Desirable East Norwalk. Welcome home to this beautifully maintained, sun-drenched 1-bedroom residence on one of the quietest blocks in sought-after East Norwalk. Why settle for an apartment in a large building when you can enjoy your own private space? This top-floor unit offers an open and airy layout with hardwood floors throughout, a large living room with vaulted ceilings and exposed beams, and sliders leading to your own private balcony-the perfect spot to unwind. The spacious bedroom features a generous walk-in closet and shares no walls with neighbors, providing exceptional privacy and tranquility. Located just one block from the water, this is a dream setting for outdoor enthusiasts, with stand-up paddle boarding, kayaking, and waterfront strolls right at your doorstep (plus, a kayak rack in the backyard for added convenience). Enjoy the ease of being just steps from Harbor Harvest General Store, local restaurants, and an 8-minute walk to the Metro-North train, making commutes to Manhattan a breeze. Additional features include an in-unit washer and dryer, a well-appointed kitchen, and a meticulously maintained property. Just minutes from Calf Pasture Beach, SoNo Place, and downtown South Norwalk, this rental offers the best of coastal living with walkable amenities, a vibrant community, and easy access to NYC. Don't miss this rare opportunity-schedule a viewing today!
