About this home

Set high above western Greenwich, this adorable 2 bedroom pre war colonial, with wood floors throughout, has a vibe that must be seen to be appreciated. The enclosed sunporch is the perfect place to work from home or create your next artistic masterpiece. The backyard is low maintenance and pollinator friendly, filled with meadowscaping, beautiful tall flowers and a fruit producing apple tree with plenty of room for additional plantings and gardens. The roof, clapboard siding, and furnace were all replaced in approximately 2013 and the water heater was replaced in 2021. Zoned for Greenwich Schools and Greenwich Beach access.
