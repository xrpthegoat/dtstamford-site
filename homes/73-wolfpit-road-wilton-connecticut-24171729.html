About this home

Classic Colonial Split with beautiful water views of Horseshoe Pond, ideally set in the heart of Wilton with the rare convenience of walking to town. Enjoy privacy and charm on a beautifully landscaped, fenced-in backyard, while being just moments from the NRVT, shopping, transportation, and major roadways. Sun-filled and thoughtfully designed, the main level offers an easy flow for everyday living and entertaining. The spacious eat-in kitchen features gorgeous new quartz countertops and backsplash and opens to the backyard deck and flagstone patio, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living. Additional highlights include a formal dining room with chair rail and bay window, a living room with a wood-burning fireplace, and a private lower-level retreat with full bath and separate entrance. With 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths, TOWN WATER, and the convenience of Wilton's town electric grid, this much-loved and well-maintained home is ready for its next owner to enjoy as much as the current owners have for the past 31 years.
