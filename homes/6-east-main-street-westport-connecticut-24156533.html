About this home

Bright and spacious second-floor office in a desirable Westport setting. Previously used by an engineer, this inviting suite features high ceilings, abundant natural light, a private entrance and restroom, plus dedicated parking. Situated in a well-maintained professional building with only three other tenants, it offers a quiet and comfortable work environment. Conveniently positioned just off Post Road, adjacent to Compo Shopping Center, and minutes from Downtown Westport, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway. An excellent opportunity for a business seeking privacy, accessibility, and convenience.
