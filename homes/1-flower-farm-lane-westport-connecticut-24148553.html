About this home

Absolutely exquisite colonial is located on one of the most sought after neighborhoods. This home will greet you w/a mahogany front porch that leads inside to a grand foyer. This timeless residence boasts a custom kitchen w/high-end appliances, a Butler's pantry, large island & the convenience of a desk station. The elegant formal living room features a cozy fireplace, while the family room offers a stone fireplace, coffered ceilings with French doors leading to a bluestone terrace, overlooking the gorgeous landscaped property. Gather in the sophisticated formal dining room w/yet another fireplace. The main level graciously accommodates a guest bedroom suite with a full bath, walk-in closet, as well as a study adorned w/Cherrywood & built-ins, leading to a serene blue stone screened-in porch. The main level also has the convenience of 2 powder rooms & a mudroom, as you enter from the 3 car garage. Upstairs, discover the luxurious primary bedroom w/a sitting room, 2 walk-in closets, changing room, & spa-like bathroom w/a claw hydro tub, shower & private balcony access. Additionally, there are 3 more bedrooms each w/a custom full bathroom, along w/a generous bonus room. The fully finished 3rd floor offers versatility with a half bath. Entertain on the lower level, featuring a theater room, gym w/sauna, family room, wine room & powder room. Outside, the meticulously landscaped grounds showcase an inground gunite pool, hot tub, pool house w/a full bathroom overlooking 2 acres.
