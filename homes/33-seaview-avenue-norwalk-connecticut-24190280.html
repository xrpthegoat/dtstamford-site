About this home

Spacious. Stylish. Steps from Everything. Welcome to 33 Seaview Ave. 3A, a beautifully renovated 2-bedroom apartment in the heart of East Norwalk-directly across from Veterans Memorial Park and just a short stroll to the East Norwalk train station, vibrant restaurants, and local nightlife. This is city-meets-coastal living at its finest. Inside, you'll find a newly updated eat-in kitchen with sleek cabinetry, new countertop and sink, new floors and plenty of room to cook a gourmet feast. The large living room offers a comfortable space to relax or host friends, while you glimpse out of the windows at the boats in the harbor. The TWO oversized bedrooms provide ample space for king-sized beds, work-from-home setups, or extra storage. The apartment includes a reserved parking space and private storage area for added convenience. Pets may be considered on a case-by-case basis. If you're looking for a move-in-ready rental that offers both space and a walkable lifestyle-this one checks every box. Don't miss it!
