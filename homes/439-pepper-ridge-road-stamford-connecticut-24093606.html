About this home

**HIGHEST & BEST DEADLINE 5/20 11 am** On a half-acre corner lot in the heart of Pepper Ridge, enjoy total convenience w/ proximity to downtown Stamford, Metro-North to NYC, the Merritt Pkwy, I-95, shopping, houses of worship, a swimming and tennis club, and schools. This versatile house features four bedrooms upstairs, a primary ensuite w/ two walk-in closets, a heated screened-in porch, two-car garage, home office, and woodworking space. Step into the foyer and enjoy an open floor plan from living to dining room, complete with bay window, hardwood floors, and wood-burning fireplace. The layout offers great flexibility to expand your living space for friends and family, and your dining room for memorable holiday meals. The kitchen includes stainless appliances and a perfectly placed window by the sink overlooking the spacious backyard. The heated sunroom offers ideal flex space-whether you need an additional family room, dining area, playroom, or gym-and it's a perfect spot for your morning coffee. Upstairs, the primary suite features a full bath and two walk-in closets (currently used as a home office, but could be a nursery or home gym). Three additional bedrooms and a full bath complete the upper level. The lower level adds even more versatility: a spacious den/playroom w/ access to backyard and garage, half bath, laundry room, and bonus room-plus crawl space storage.
