About this home

Spacious and bright 1,560 sq. ft. three-bedroom loft-style unit featuring a large, open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area with vaulted ceilings making the space feel even larger. The full kitchen offers granite countertops and a generous breakfast bar, creating an inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. Living areas and bedrooms have been freshly painted. The bathroom features a new shower with new tile. The large primary bedroom offers a huge walk-in closet with exceptional storage. A special rental incentive is available-tenant may choose between **one month free or a reduced security deposit**. Please ask for details. Enjoy the convenience of a newer washer and dryer located in the unit, along with two parking spaces included in the rent. A third parking space is available for an additional $100/month. Private entrance and storage space in the lower-level hallway top off this one of a kind rental. Superb East Norwalk location-just a 2-minute drive to historic SoNo to enjoy trendy restaurants, shopping, nightlife, Sono Collection Mall, breweries, and much more. Adding to the convenience of this location, you will be only a 4-minute drive to Calf Pasture Beach. A short walk to the East Norwalk Metro North Train Station and conveniently located near I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and all that Norwalk has to offer. **Agents: Please see addditional remarks.
