About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom colonial nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the desirable New Canaan Way-a private enclave of 16 freestanding homes ideally located on the Norwalk/New Canaan border. Backing up to a serene conservation area, this home offers a peaceful and private setting rarely found. The open-concept main level features a spacious layout perfect for entertaining, with access to a private deck for indoor-outdoor living. The expansive primary suite includes a generous sitting area-your perfect retreat. A bright and finished lower level adds 772 sq ft of flexible living space, ideal for a home office, gym, or media room. Enjoy Norwalk beach rights, and the convenience of a new roof (August 2025). Low-maintenance living with an HOA that covers water, landscaping, and snow removal. Don't miss this rare opportunity for tranquil living with unbeatable access to both Norwalk and New Canaan amenities!
