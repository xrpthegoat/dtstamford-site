About this home

Set within the sought-after Sylvan Knoll community, this beautifully maintained two-bedroom, one-bath end-unit co-op townhouse offers comfort and convenience. The main level features hardwood floors, a comfortable living room, dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with direct access to the back patio for easy indoor-outdoor living. Upstairs includes two well-proportioned bedrooms and a full bath. The lower level provides flexible space for a home office or recreation area (200 sq ft), along with an in-unit washer and dryer. Sylvan Knoll is a pet-friendly, owner-occupied community on 13 park-like acres with common charges including real estate taxes. Conveniently located just off I-95 (Exit 9), it is close to Cove Island Park, the beach, Chelsea Piers, downtown Stamford, shopping, and Metro-North
