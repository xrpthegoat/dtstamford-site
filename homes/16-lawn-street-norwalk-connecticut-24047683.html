About this home

Location and private property. In a quiet Rowayton neighborhood sits this beautifully sited 4 / 5 Bedroom, 3 Full Bath 2-level home, fully renovated in 2015. Ideal floor plan. Front foyer leads to a formal LR with wood-burning fireplace, and the renovated Chef Kitchen with new refrigerator and top-of-line appliances extends to the DR with vaulted ceiling, and to the FR for Flat Screen viewing & entertainment. Main floor Primary Suite with full bath featuring vaulted ceiling / skylight, plus 3 other bedrooms that share a renovated Full Bath. Lower level features a Large playroom, a Bonus or 5th Bdrm, plus a new full bath plus Laundry. Outside is a large deck and private yard for relaxing & barbequeing. Attached 2-car garage. Walk to Train, Bayley Beach access, Rowayton School, near town for shopping.
