About this home

Discover a modern appeal with this generously-sized 2-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom condominium nestled in the heart of Glenbrook. The expansive dining and living area seamlessly connects to a private balcony through sliding doors. The kitchen features new countertops, laminate flooring, and plenty of space for meal preparation. For those working remotely, there's a designated home office area. Abundant closet space awaits your belongings, and the convenience of a washer/dryer upstairs outside of the bedrooms enhances daily living. Parking includes a one-car garage plus an outdoor spot. There is an additional storage closet in the garage that is locked and secured. Perfectly situated near shops, train station, local restaurants, and quick access to 95.
