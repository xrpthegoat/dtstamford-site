About this home

You will be amazed! Nestled into a private and serene site at the end of a cul-de-sac, surrounded by 300 acres of conservation land, this sleek contemporary ranch was completely renovated in 2022. Nothing to do but move in to this 4-bedroom, 3 full bath home with in-ground, heated pool. Kitchen, baths, roof, floors, windows, all 2 years old. Enjoy privacy in your expansive yard overlooking Comstock Brook. Wrap around decks on the front, side and rear of the house allow seamless indoor/outdoor entertainment. The light filled home flows beautifully on two levels with a high-tech garage and high-efficient Bosch Mini-Split supplementary heating/cooling systems installed throughout the house. Exterior home office/pool house added in 2024. Don't miss this unique offering.
