About this home

Experience modern coastal luxury in this custom-built, down-to-the-studs rental residence just minutes from Compo Beach and downtown Westport. Blending the scale and sophistication of a true custom home with effortless single-level living, this exceptional property showcases designer finishes and impeccable craftsmanship throughout. The sun-filled open floor plan features soaring cathedral ceilings, white oak floors, custom millwork, Marvin windows, and a striking porcelain fireplace. The chef's kitchen features custom cabinetry, premium Calacatta porcelain throughout the countertops, full-height backsplash, oversized island, and matching wet bar, complete with a dual-zone wine refrigerator, beverage cooler, and ice maker. Top-of-the-line Bosch appliances complete this exceptional space, designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Expansive tri-fold doors open to a spacious deck and private, fully fenced backyard with professional landscaping. The main-level primary suite offers heated bathroom floors, automated blinds, and spa-inspired finishes. The finished lower level includes a gym or recreation room, five-person infrared sauna, wine room, full bath, laundry room with GE appliances, and abundant storage. Additional highlights include smart home technology, integrated sound, a Tesla Universal EV charger, and an epoxy-finished garage. An exceptional rental offering in one of Westport's most desirable locations.
