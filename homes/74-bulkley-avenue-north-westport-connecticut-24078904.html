About this home

Designed by renowned architect Nancy Thiel, this pristine farm-style residence, featured in "Cottages & Gardens", sits on a prime location directly opposite of the Hunt Club. It boasts high ceilings, an open floor plan, and an abundance of natural light. The main floor encompasses a fabulous kitchen, complete with a large center island, top-of-the-line appliances, and ample storage. In addition, the main floor features a formal living room, an elegant dining room, a cheerful family room, and a private office. All of these rooms feature 9' ceilings and beautiful hardwood floors. The second floor has four generously sized bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet. The luxurious primary suite stands out with its two massive walk-in closets, custom built-in drawers, a dressing area, and a spa-like bathroom that offers a serene retreat. Ascending to the third floor, you'll discover a versatile space that can serve as a second office, or a private getaway. The lower level presents a fifth bedroom with a full bathroom, ideal for accommodating guests or creating a private space for an au-pair or in-law. Additionally, the lower level features a spacious playroom with a fireplace. The finished walk-out lower level provides seamless access to the outdoors and adds to the home's overall living space. The architect's past-meets-present approach when designing this house is topped off by the state of the art geothermal heating and cooling system.
