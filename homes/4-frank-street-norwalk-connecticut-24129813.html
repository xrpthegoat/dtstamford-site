About this home

So Much More Than Meets the Eye! Sought after Cranbury neighborhood Colonial with well over 3,000 sq. ft. of finished living space, offering plenty of room for everyone. The first floor has a comfortable flow from front entry foyer into the living room, formal dining room, and kitchen, leading way to the family room and out to the back deck and yard, making it easy to gather and entertain. A home office or den with direct access to the two-car garage adds convenience for today's busy lifestyles. Upstairs, you will find a primary suite, three additional bedrooms, and laundry access right where you need it. The finished walk-out lower level is full of possibilities featuring a separate room that can serve as a bedroom (or extra home office), a half bath with room to expand into a full bath, and access to the rear yard and patio (in-law apt/au-pair potential). Three zone heating, many mechanical updates and a two year old roof, giving peace of mind. The location couldn't be better! Walk to Cranbury Market and neighborhood shops, with easy access to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and the Route 7 connector. Close to the Wilton and Westport lines as well; you'll have shopping, dining, and family activities just minutes away. Don't let this wonderful opportunity pass you by.
