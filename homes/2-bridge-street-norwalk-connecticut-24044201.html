About this home

Welcome to this charming 3-bedroom, 1-bathroom Cape-style home, nestled in the desirable East Norwalk community. Perfectly positioned near shopping, restaurants, entertainment, and transportation, this home offers both convenience and comfort. Step inside to find beautiful hardwood floors throughout, adding warmth and character to every room. The main level features a spacious open living room, perfect for relaxing or entertaining, a formal dining room for hosting gatherings, and a primary bedroom offering easy first-floor living. The well-appointed kitchen is ready for your culinary creations, and the expansive full bathroom provides comfort and space. The enclosed porch offers additional living space, ideal for a cozy sitting area or home office. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, perfect for family, guests, or even a hobby room. Outside, the level treed lot provides plenty of space for outdoor entertaining, gardening, or simply enjoying the fresh air. Additional features include a full basement, offering potential for future finishing, and the home is wired for sound, ideal for music or home theater enthusiasts. Recently converted from oil to gas heat, this home is more energy-efficient than ever. Ready and waiting for its new owner, this charming home is a true East Norwalk gem!
