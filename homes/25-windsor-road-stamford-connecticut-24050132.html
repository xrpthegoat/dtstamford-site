About this home

FINAL & BEST OFFER BY MONDAY 10/7 @ 12PM! This solid and well maintained 4-bedroom Colonial is nestled on a quiet street only minutes from downtown Stamford. It offers easy access to local amenities while providing a peaceful & serene feel. Set on a beautiful, level lot, the home features great living space, perfect for both entertaining and everyday life. The first floor - eat-in-kitchen/ dining area, living room and family room and a primary bedroom with ensuite renovated bathroom and walk- in closet. Additionally, upstairs there are 3 bedrooms with 2 full bathrooms. Gleaming hardwood floors run throughout the home, full, three room partially finished basement can be used as a gym or bonus room with extra storage space. Beyond the belgium block lined driveway and beautiful walkway is a level fenced property invites outdoor fun and relaxation, making it an ideal space for gardening, play or hosting gatherings. There is also an oversize carport and shed. Easy access to I-95, Metro North Train Station and Merritt Parkway. With a little imagination & updating, you can make this home your own!
