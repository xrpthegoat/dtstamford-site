About this home

Welcome to this stunning 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home, available furnished or unfurnished. Modern elegance meets timeless charm with contemporary updates throughout. The chef's kitchen features sleek countertops, custom cabinetry, and top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances. It opens to a spacious dining and living area, plus a sunroom with panoramic windows and access to a serene wraparound deck-ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The primary suite is a peaceful retreat with ample natural light, a walk-in closet, and a spa-like en-suite with a luxurious tub/shower. Two additional well-appointed bedrooms on the main level offer comfort and versatility. The finished lower level includes a generous family room, laundry room, and abundant storage. Outside, enjoy multiple areas for lounging and dining surrounded by lush greenery and beautifully landscaped grounds. Whether you're seeking a turnkey furnished space or a place to make your own, this exceptional home offers the flexibility and lifestyle you've been looking for.
