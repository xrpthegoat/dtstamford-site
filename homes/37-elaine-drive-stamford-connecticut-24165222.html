About this home

Nestled on a tranquil cul-de-sac in the heart of Stamford's highly sought-after Mid-Ridges neighborhood, this charming 3-bedroom, 2 bathroom raised ranch at 37 Elaine Drive presents a rare opportunity to create your dream home in an unbeatable location. Step inside and be greeted by the warm potential of this well maintained residence. The spacious 1,586 sq. ft. layout is a perfect blank canvas awaiting your personal touch. A significant head start has been made for you: the beautiful hardwood floors throughout the main level have been professionally sanded and are ready for you to select the custom stain color of your choice, instantly tailoring the home to your unique style. The home is equipped with the "big three" of city conveniences: natural gas, city water, and city sewer, offering you peace of mind and hassle-free living. Set on a generous quarter-acre lot, the property provides ample space for outdoor relaxation, gardening, and entertaining combined with the tranqulity of the river in your backyard. Location is everything, and this home delivers. Situated in one of Stamford's best neighborhoods, you are just moments away from everything you need. Enjoy unparalleled convenience with easy access to the Merritt Parkway and I-95, premier shopping destinations, a variety of restaurants, schools, and local houses of worship. 37 Elaine Drive is more than just a house; it's a chance to establish roots in a vibrant community with a location that simply can't be beat.
