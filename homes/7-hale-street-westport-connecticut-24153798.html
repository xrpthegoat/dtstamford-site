About this home

Completely renovated in 2025, this Cape Cod-style home offers effortless coastal living in Westports South Compo neighborhood. Thoughtfully updated and move-in ready, the home features 3 bedrooms and 2 baths across 1,882 square feet of living space. Enjoy the best of the Westport lifestyle-- less than 2 miles to Compo beach, the marina and Longshore Golf Course and approximately 1 mile to the train station and I-95, making commuting a breeze. Spend your days at the shore, your evenings dining downtown and weekends exploring Westports vibrant shops and restaurants. An opportunity to enjoy modern comfort, classic charm and a great location close to everything that makes Westport so special. House includes an EV charger, convenient 1st floor bedroom and plumbing available in lower level for a half bath.
