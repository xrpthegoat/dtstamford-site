About this home

Welcome home to this charming move-in ready colonial conveniently located on a quiet street only minutes from downtown Stamford. Quintessential New England charmer offers the perfect blend of character and modern amenities. Gracious open concept living room w/ wood burning fireplace & built-ins flows into the dining room w/ french door leading to the backyard. Country kitchen features st/st appliances including a gas range, breakfast nook, and large peninsula w/ quartz counters. Cozy sunroom could be the perfect home office or bonus space, & a half bath completes the main floor. Upstairs are two generous size bedrooms with hardwood floors, and an updated full bath. Head out to the backyard featuring a maintenance free trex style deck overlooking the perfectly manicured grounds lined with beautiful flower beds. Beyond the belgium block lined driveway is a space for a garden overlooking spectacular views of the Rippowam river. Sited on a level, fenced property, there is great space to entertain, garden and relax in your own personal oasis. Full unfinished basement, oversized 2 car detached garage, newer efficient natural gas heating system, newer water heater, convenient 2 zone central air system, city water/city sewer. A short drive to downtown Stamford, easy access to I-95, Metro North Train Station & Merritt Parkway, and only minutes to Scalzi Park & the future Whole Foods! This is the one you have been waiting for. *Highest and best due Monday 8/12 at 10am*
