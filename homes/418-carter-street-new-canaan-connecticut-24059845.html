About this home

Magnificent Federal Style Colonial built in 1835 with Red Barn, minutes to downtown New Canaan. The home features 7 bedrooms and 4 full baths, powder room on main level, additional half bath in the basement, large primary suite and a second junior suite with 2 additional bedroom on the second floor, 2 additional bedrooms with full bath on the 3rd floor. fantastic floorplan including a butlers pantry, sunroom porch, den, family room, large entertainment deck/patio and mudroom laundry conveniently located on the main level, full walkout basement with 2 car attached garage and space for workshop. Quick drive to the Merritt Parkway and convenient to Downtown Train.
