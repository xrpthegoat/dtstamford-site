About this home

Be among the first to experience the luxury of a brand-new rental, just completed and ready for you to move in. This stunning property is another exceptional creation by Skyview Builders, showcasing their craftsmanship and unwavering commitment to excellence. The property features three identical townhouses, each meticulously designed for ultimate comfort and functionality. With four bedrooms, four bathrooms, and an additional room perfect for use as an office, each unit offers ample space and versatility. Enjoy a private rooftop with a hot tub, where you can unwind while taking in breathtaking panoramic views of the Long Island Sound. The secluded backyard provides a peaceful retreat, ideal for both relaxation and outdoor entertainment. Natural light pours in through oversized windows, highlighting the expansive floor plan and creating a warm, inviting atmosphere. Rich hardwood floors, top-of-the-line kitchen appliances, generous storage, and chic countertops combine to create a kitchen fit for any chef. The residences are built with Insulated Concrete Forms, ensuring unmatched strength, energy efficiency, and safety. Advanced construction technology is showcased in the KlAR Studio's windows and doors, featuring triple-pane glass, offering a glimpse into the future of homebuilding. Only minutes away from Cove Island Park, tennis courts, Chelsea Piers, a playground, Metro-North, and I-95.
