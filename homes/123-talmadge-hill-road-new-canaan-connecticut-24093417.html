About this home

Bordered by a classic Connecticut stone wall and lined with flowering crabapple trees, this distinguished Colonial offers a picturesque and timeless setting. The property is sited on 2 spectacular -park like acres with a fenced in yard and in-ground pool. Inside, the first floor boasts an open, airy layout that flows effortlessly between the custom kitchen, the family room with fireplace, playroom, and a handsome library with built-in cabinetry. The elegant butler's pantry connects to a grand dining room with a fireplace, while the formal living room, adorned with French doors, opens to serene backyard views-completing the main level.Upstairs, six generously sized bedrooms and five full bathrooms provide ample space for family and guests, ensuring comfort and privacy throughout. A major renovation in 2009 transformed the home, seamlessly blending traditional charm with modern functionality. The former back porch was converted into a stunning chef's kitchen, while the original kitchen was reimagined into a custom butler's pantry and a welcoming entrance area. Enhancements also include a walk-in pantry, a back staircase, an additional bedroom, two full bathrooms, many new windows,and extensive updates throughout the home. In 2018, a spacious playroom was added off the kitchen and family room, featuring new flooring, walls, windows, a closet, and doors. This inviting space offers direct access to the back yard and pool- perfect for entertaining.
