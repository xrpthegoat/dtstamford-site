About this home

Welcome to 116 Mulberry Street, tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in the well-loved Springdale neighborhood. From the moment you arrive, the charming wishing well and Belgium Block path set the tone for a home lovingly maintained by the same owners for over 50 years. Freshly painted, with newer windows and low-maintenance vinyl siding, this inviting Cape is ready for its next chapter. Upstairs are three bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with two cedar walk-in closets and a large ensuite bath. The main level features an eat-in kitchen that flows into a cozy living room with wood-burning fireplace, plus a fourth bedroom or home office and full bath. A generous family room with hardwood floors overlooks the deck and flat backyard, creating a wonderful space for relaxing and entertaining. A large laundry room with backyard access offers great potential to create a functional mudroom. Additional highlights include a one-car garage, natural gas heat and central air conditioning. Enjoy a true neighborhood lifestyle close to the library, school, baseball field, train, local shops and restaurants. Come make your wish on Mulberry!
