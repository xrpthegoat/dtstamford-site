About this home

Owner occupied for the past 50+ years. Front and rear entry for both units. Large eat in kitchens and large living rooms in both units. Kitchens each have access to covered decks with steps to the rear yard. First floor unit is owner occupied and has 2 bedrooms and one bath. Second floor unit has been occupied by the same tenant for 30+ years and has two bedrooms and one bath. Full basement with hatchway door. Baseboard and radiators fueled by oil, one boiler with two zones, one HWH, two electrical meters. Gas line to house for gas stove in first floor unit only. Long driveway and carport for good off street parking in rear of house. Level rear yard.
