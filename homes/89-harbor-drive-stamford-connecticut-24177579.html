About this home

Three-level, 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhouse at Schooner Cove in Stamford, Connecticut - with a deeded garage, and partial water views from two private balconies.This townhouse at Schooner Cove delivers a rare marina lifestyle with low-maintenance living in one of Stamford's most sought-after waterfront communities. The open-concept main level combines kitchen, dining, and living areas around a wood-burning fireplace, with a private balcony off the living room offering water views. A second balcony off the primary bedroom extends outdoor living to the upper level, where vaulted ceilings enhance two of the three bedrooms.The layout includes 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and smart vertical flow across three floors. Two parking passes plus one visitor pass - three parking options total - supplement the deeded attached garage. HOA membership includes access to the community pool and clubhouse. Schooner Cove in Stamford's Shippan neighborhood sits minutes from West Beach, with convenient access to I-95, the Metro-North Stamford train station, and downtown Stamford.
