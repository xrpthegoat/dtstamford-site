About this home

Discover "Long Meadow," a timeless home poised for its next chapter. Situated at the highest point of Wilton's coveted Nod Hill, this custom-built colonial offers a serene and scenic setting defined by classic CT stone walls & rolling landscapes. This is a rare opportunity to own a home with exceptional character and a prime address. Step inside this custom-built residence, on the market for the first time in 50 years, to find 4,000+ SF of finished space across 3 levels. Detailed millwork and classic design elements create a backdrop of timeless elegance, while the home's layout provides the perfect foundation for your personal touch. The home's 4 fireplaces-each with a unique brick surround-provide warmth and focal points for the main living areas. Gather with guests in the formal living and dining rooms, or relax in the combined kitchen and family room. From here, doors open to a sunroom and a patio, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living. The library features custom built-in bookshelves, offering a quiet retreat for work or reading. With 2 primary bedroom options on the 2nd floor or a potential in-law suite, 5-bedroom, 3.5 bath home offers flexible living arrangements. The lower level includes a finished walk-out basement with its own fireplace, providing additional space for recreation, a home gym, or a media room. While traditional, the property is presented as an ideal canvas for a new owner to update and personalize.
