About this home

Completely renovated 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath duplex in desirable Shippan, offering two spacious levels of comfortable living. The main level features a large living room, generous eat-in kitchen with new stainless-steel appliances, new full bathroom, and dedicated washer/dryer room. Step outside to a large patio and level backyard, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Upstairs offers three generously sized bedrooms, a second full bathroom, and access to a Juliet balcony. Additional highlights include a brand-new kitchen, central air, and beautifully updated interiors throughout. Ideally located in Shippan, within easy reach of the marina, beaches, Stamford train station, shops, restaurants, and downtown Stamford.
