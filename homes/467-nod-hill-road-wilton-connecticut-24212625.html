About this home

There are houses that simply have a feeling about them, and this is one of those rare homes. Designed in 1938 by noted architect F. Nelson Breed, this enchanting country home is beautifully sited on three open, picturesque acres along Nod Hill Road, one of Wilton's designated scenic roads. From the moment you arrive, the charm is undeniable. A gracious approach and beautifully landscaped grounds set the tone. Inside, approximately 1,883 square feet is filled with warmth, character and architectural detail, thoughtfully updated while preserving the charm and integrity of the original home. The home offers three bedrooms and two and a half baths, along with additional flexible rooms ideal for a home office, den, studio or guest space. Lovely views of the property and an easy connection to the outdoors enhance the sense of true country living. Open lawns, mature trees and established plantings create a peaceful backdrop. Nearby meadows, trails and adjacent town-protected land further enhance the setting, with Weir Farm National Historical Park just minutes down the road. Ideally located between Wilton and Ridgefield centers, the property offers country living with convenient access to both towns. Branchville Metro-North station is 7 minutes away, South Norwalk station is approximately 20 minutes away, and NYC is an hour by car. Charming, distinctive, and rich in history, this is a rare opportunity to own a truly special home!
