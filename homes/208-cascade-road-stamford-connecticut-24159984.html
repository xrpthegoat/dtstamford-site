About this home

Private & tucked away on the Stamford/New Canaan border, you'll find this enchanting contemporary home perfectly blended with its rich natural landscape. An effortless flow of sun-filled rooms welcome the outdoor scenery and are comfortable and functional for every-day living and also - spectacular entertaining! Beautiful, high-quality updates include the stunning kitchen featuring modern Downsview cabinetry, granite countertops, top-shelf appliances and glass accents. The positively indulgent, spa-inspired primary bath blends nature and luxury with designer tile, expansive shower, soaking tub + towel warmers. Newer windows, hardwood flooring, 2-fireplaces, skylights, a flexible sunroom and high ceilings name a few standout features, along with a deluxe primary suite, featuring separate dressing area. You'll also delight in the accommodating family room, offering a wet-bar, wood-burning stove and access to the idyllic outdoor spaces. An oversized heated lap pool/spa provides outdoor enjoyment, while other gardening spots, including the entertainer's deck and babbling brook complete the postcard setting. A 2-car garage, well-equipped workshop, newer systems and excellent maintenance add to the ease of living here.
