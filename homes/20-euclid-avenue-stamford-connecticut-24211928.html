About this home

Welcome home to this bright and stylish 1,041 sf first-floor unit in a totally renovated 2-family home. Freshly painted throughout, the unit has a private entry that opens to an inviting open floor plan where the living room flows into a generous eat-in kitchen, complete with a large center island and stainless steel appliances. It's a perfect setup for cooking and everyday living. Two well-proportioned bedrooms, including one with its own walk-in closet, are set apart from the main living space alongside a full bathroom, and an in-unit washer and dryer adds everyday convenience. A rear door off the kitchen leads down to the large backyard, great for entertaining or simply relaxing outdoors. Gas heat, separate utilities and two included off-street parking spaces make life easy. The unit sits just minutes from beautiful Cove Island Beach and Park, with picnic areas, walking and running trails, playgrounds, tennis courts and an ice rink. Easy access to I-95, Chelsea Piers, the train station and vibrant downtown Stamford makes this an ideal place to call home.
