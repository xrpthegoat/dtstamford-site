About this home

Nestled on a gorgeous, prime piece of property in one of Wilton's most sought-after locations, this 4 bedroom, 3 full bath ranch offers an exceptional opportunity to create your dream home. Set within one of Connecticut's top-rated school districts and a highly desirable commuter location, this property combines convenience, charm, and endless potential. The home features an easy one-level ranch-style layout with great flow, large sun-filled windows, and bright natural light throughout. A welcoming living space showcases built-in bookshelves surrounding the fireplace, adding warmth and character to the home. The dining room is ideally situated just off the spacious kitchen, creating a wonderful flow for both everyday living and entertaining. Featuring cathedral ceilings and an abundance of natural light, the space feels open, airy, and inviting. The large kitchen offers excellent storage, a center island, and tremendous potential to design the kitchen of your dreams while taking full advantage of the home's functional layout and bright, welcoming atmosphere. The main level of the home offers the ease and convenience of a spacious primary suite complete with a full ensuite bathroom and laundry. Two additional bedrooms with hardwood flooring and another full bathroom complete the main level.
