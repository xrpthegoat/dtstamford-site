About this home

Tucked away towards the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in a prime central Westport location, this beautifully updated home offers the perfect blend of privacy, convenience, and lifestyle. Set on 1.05 acres and just moments from town, close to schools, shopping, and commuting options, this five-bedroom residence is designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The heart of the home is the open kitchen featuring a large sunny dining area, center island, breakfast bar and exceptional flow into the family room-ideal for gatherings and modern living. A charming living room with a warm and inviting fireplace seamlessly flows into the private formal dining room, creating elegant spaces for more intimate gatherings. This residence includes 4.5 baths and a thoughtfully designed layout throughout. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, complete with an adjacent office/dressing/make-up area or baby's room-exceptional custom closets-including a spacious walk-in with built-ins-and a spa-inspired bath with jetted tub. The finished lower level, with private room with large closet, open space and full bath, provides ideal flexible space for storage, playroom, or gym. Outdoors, enjoy a private oasis with an in-ground pool framed by beautiful stone walls and mature plantings. Set back from the road, the property offers both tranquility and seclusion while remaining close to all that Westport has to offer.
