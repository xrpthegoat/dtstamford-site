About this home

Charming stone and shingle four-bedroom home on a quiet, private lane sited on a serene .88 acres is a must see. Enter this beauty into a large foyer and be lured into the extraordinary living room with soaring cathedral ceiling, wood-pegged floors, bay window and stone fireplace. Large formal dining room with wood floor and access to stone patio and large, private yard. There is rustic charm to the galley kitchen with Bosch gas range and LG fridge. Huge family room (newly carpeted) with many built-ins, bookshelves and wood-burning stove leads to a sunroom (possible office or large mud room) with skylight, vaulted ceiling, beams, full bath, sliders to backyard and access to garage, which offers great space for many interests and lifestyles. Three bedrooms and second full bath on main floor. Second floor primary bedroom with vaulted ceiling, wood floor and full bath (stall shower and separate tub) plus new vanity. "Well House" to convey "as is." This truly is a uniquely beautiful home on a very desirable lane convenient to major highways, train station, shopping and restaurants. New Central A/C 2023; New septic pump 2024
