About this home

Set on 2.93 private acres along coveted Long Neck Point, this extraordinary waterfront Colonial offers a rare combination of sweeping water views, privacy, and direct access to the water. Spanning more than 10,000 square feet, the residence is designed to embrace its spectacular setting, with expansive living spaces and breathtaking vistas from nearly every room. The eight-bedroom main residence features seven full and three half baths, eight fireplaces, and an effortless flow between gracious entertaining spaces and comfortable everyday living. A pool and private dock extend the experience outdoors, creating an idyllic setting for waterfront living and entertaining. A charming two-bedroom, two-bath guest cottage offers additional space and privacy for family and guests. Distinguished by its scale, setting, and direct water access, this remarkable Long Neck Point estate offers a rare opportunity to experience the quintessential coastal lifestyle.
