About this home

Two-family property built in 2010 on a corner lot in Stamford's South End. This building is much newer than the typical multi-family homes in the area, many of which are more than 100 years old. The first-floor unit offers two bedrooms and one full bathroom. The second-floor duplex spans two levels with five total bedrooms and two full bathrooms, an uncommon layout that provides flexible living arrangements and strong rental appeal. The property features vinyl siding, quality Weil-McLain boilers, wide staircases, good ceiling height, and an expansive unfinished basement with excellent storage and future potential. The property also includes a cleared side lot used for off-street parking, providing ample space for multiple vehicles. Built to modern codes, this property offers more efficient systems and reduced maintenance compared to older multifamily homes. Located minutes from Harbor Point, the Metro North train station, and downtown Stamford, the property offers both accessibility and convenience. The combination of newer construction, rare unit configuration, corner lot location, and dedicated parking area makes this a strong long-term rental investment. Subject to court approval.
