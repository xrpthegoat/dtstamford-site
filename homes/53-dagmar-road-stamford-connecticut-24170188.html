About this home

Imagine living in Stamford's highly sought-after Springdale neighborhood! This home stands out right away with a unique kitchen featuring designer blue polished cabinetry, an electric cooktop, oven, tons of storage, and a built-in dry bar with an eating area. The main level living room and dining room feel bright and open with a vaulted ceiling, multiple bay windows, and new vinyl flooring. The primary bedroom is oversized with a full seating or dressing area, perfect for a home office, plus direct access to the deck via sliding doors, a walk-in closet with built-ins, and a private bathroom with granite vanity, tile shower, and a warm-water bidet. Downstairs offers a flexible in-law or entertainment space with its own bedroom, full bath, dry bar, built-in audio system, laundry, and walk-out access to the yard. Enjoy a large private deck for relaxing or entertaining, and a peaceful street known for friendly neighbors. Located just minutes from the Springdale train station, Stamford Twin Rinks, Sleepy Hollow (dog) Park, Sterling Farms golf course and tennis club, plus a quick drive to Trader Joe's and the legendary High Ridge Rd restaurant corridor.
