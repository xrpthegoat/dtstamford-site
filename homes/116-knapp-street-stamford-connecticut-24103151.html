About this home

This charming colonial offers the perfect balance of classic appeal and modern conveniences. Step onto the large covered front porch-ideal for relaxing with a cup of coffee or catching up with neighbors. Inside, a renovated kitchen features stainless steel appliances and gleaming newly refinished hardwood floors that flow through the main living areas. The home boasts four bedrooms and three full bathrooms, including a spacious primary bedroom suite with a walk-in closet, providing ample space for everyday living. Most of the interior has been freshly painted. Enjoy year-round comfort with central air and a high-efficiency gas furnace, programmable thermostats, LED recessed lighting and hardwired internet throughout to keep everyone connected. The fully fenced, professionally landscaped yard includes a stone patio, a new front walkway with path lighting, LED flood lights, lawn sprinklers, a natural gas grill, and even an invisible fence for your four-legged family members. An automatic full-house Generac generator adds peace of mind, while the freshly painted exterior gives the home a fresh and inviting look. Located close to public transit, including both train and bus stations, and near local favorites like the Springdale neighborhood, a library, and a major grocery store, convenience is just steps away. This home checks all the boxes-and then some-even if it doesn't pack them for you!
