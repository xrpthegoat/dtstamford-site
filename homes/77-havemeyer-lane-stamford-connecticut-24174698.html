About this home

Palmer Hill luxury 2 bedroom, 2 baths, condo located on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. The vaulted ceilings, updated kitchen, wide-plank hardwood floors throughout, updated primary bath and enlarged entry hall, make this an exceptional unit located on the top (3rd) floor. This light filled unit with two story windows in the living room and both bedrooms features a lovely south facing balcony with city views. The recently updated gourmet kitchen with breakfast bar and granite counters features Wolf gas stove and oven, stainless steel dishwasher/microwave and glass door cabinets. This home comes with an elevator, 2 underground parking spots, a storage unit and bike room and full access to Palmer Hill's luxury amenities, including a newly remodeled clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym, yoga room, and business center. Very dog friendly, minutes to the village of Old Greenwich, trains, grocery stores and shopping.
