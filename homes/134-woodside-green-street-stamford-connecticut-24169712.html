About this home

Avail July 1. Renovated and spacious two bedroom unit w/Din Rm used as possible separate 3rd room for office, gym, playroom etc! The updated kitchen has Quartz counters, tile floor, high end S/S appliances, custom lighting, sunny window and plenty of cabinets. Relax in a luxurious renovated bathroom with amazing modern details, extra hidden storages and natural sunlight. You will be welcomed into the unit with high ceilings through a cozy wide foyer featuring hardwood floors and arched doorways seen throughout the condo. Two convenient closets are in the entrance foyer. Extra large living room with views of the treed courtyard. The living room is large enough to combine a LR/DR leaving the separate DR to be used as an office, gym or playroom if desired. Enjoy the comfort of having two large bedrooms with extra modern wardrobe closets located in each room. 2 unassigned parking spots included in the rent. The complex is situated on a beautiful and scenic tree lined campus with walkways, grills, gazebo, dog area and a secure bike room. This is a pet friendly complex. Condo located in desirable Woodside Green across from Scalzi Park and from Summer Street shopping/dining. Minutes to bus, train, UCONN, library, fitness and major highways. Available immediately. Please note: No weekend move-ins allowed per Association. Landlord requires Credit Score 650+.
