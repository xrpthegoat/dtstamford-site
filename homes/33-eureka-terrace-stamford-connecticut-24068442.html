About this home

Amazing beach and water neighborhood just over the Old Greenwich line in private Southfield Point with beach and dock. This fully furnished (or unfurnished) Mediterranean style beach home has 5 Bedrooms/4.5 baths and is the perfect "vacation year round" home with Serena & Lily furnishings and beautiful finishes throughout. Relax on the front or back patio with plenty of comfy lounging areas and solo stove firepit for that perfect nighttime vibe. Explore the area with bikes provided or take a stroll to Stamford's waterfront boardwalk with outdoor dinning options including Prime & Crabshell just a few blocks away. Feeling more adventurous? Grab a kayak rental while there or the free water taxi over to Harbor Point! Old Greenwich is also a great option that's minutes away where you can enjoy a delicious coffee and treat at Sweet Peas Bakery or lunch or dinner at Le Fat Poodle Bistro. Stamford and Old Greenwich train stations are both under 5 minutes away for easy access to NYC. How do I know so much about this amazing neighborhood? I live here too and this is exactly how I spend my time :) Also available for short term and Summer rental (inquire for rates). So why wait- come and see this annual or short term/summer dream that can actually come true.
