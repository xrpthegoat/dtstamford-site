About this home

Welcome to the magnificent home of a connoisseur of art and design. Masterfully remodeled, floor to ceiling in 2020, every room was appointed with high-end finishes and selections, epitomizing luxury and style. Features include a custom designed open kitchen with white granite counter tops all new appliances and brass hardware that opens to a cathedral ceiling dining room. The living room offers panoramic views of the tennis court, pool and natural vistas with highlights that include porcelain surround fireplace, built-in dry bar with black marble countertop and a custom staircase that leads to a library loft with built-in bookshelves. Each secondary bedrooms includes a loft spaces. The private deck offers two entry sliders from both the dining room and breakfast nook. The master suite features walk-in closets and a marble-clad bath with jacuzzi a Kohler whirlpool soaking tub. Every corner of this bright and airy corner unit invites you to relax in a private sanctuary and revel in the elevated lifestyle it provides.
