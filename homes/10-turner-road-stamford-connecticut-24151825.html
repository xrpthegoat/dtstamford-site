About this home

Custom built Colonial with nine foot ceilings on the first level and many custom features. Located on a sought-after street, this exceptional property offers proximity to two prestigious private schools, just one mile from downtown Stamford and a convenient 10-minute commute to the train station for easy access to New York City. Situated on a rare .40 acre fenced lot, the residence combines privacy with ample outdoor space. The home features premium energy-efficient windows throughout, enhancing comfort and reduced utility costs. This prime location and thoughtfully designed home present an excellent opportunity for families seeking convenience and quality Living Room, Dining/Kitchen are combined for a modern open look. The dining area boasts a built-in cabinet. The kitchen features a large walk-in pantry for your storage needs, a breakfast island with granite counters and stainless-steel appliances. Additionally, there is access to a huge deck and stairs to the rear yard and patio right off the kitchen. There is a guest bedroom on the main level. The upper level features a luxurious master suite with whirlpool tub, private shower with steam, two individual vanities, linen closet, huge walk-in closet and hardwood floor. The three additional bedrooms feature large closets and hardwood floors. The third level features a large room with walk-in closet which could be a great room or additional bedroom. The home has natural gas hot air heat and central air.
