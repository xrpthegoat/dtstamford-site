About this home

Sunny oversized cape on a private back property featuring so many updates! This lovely home welcomes you from the moment you enter its 2-story foyer. Walk to MetroNorth for an easy commute! Recent updates include new roof, new furnace, new Trex deck and new front porch, newly painted exterior and interior.
