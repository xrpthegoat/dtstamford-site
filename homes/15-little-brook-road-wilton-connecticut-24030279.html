About this home

Highest and best at noon on Tuesday, September 10th. Classic South Wilton Colonial with beautifully landscaped grounds in one of the most desired social cul de sac neighborhoods in Wilton. Relax in the oversized family room by the fire or head to the sun room to cozy up and read a book. The large eat-in kitchen allows you to walk out to the deck so you have your choice of cooking and eating indoors or grilling and dining al fresco. After dinner head downstairs to the newly remodeled walk out basement with wet bar, wine and beer fridge with plenty of room for ping pong and poker or just sit by the fire and watch the game. While the weather is nice sit on the deck in your own private oasis overlooking the backyard and gather with family or friends around the fire-pit. The upstairs has 4 large bedrooms and off of the main floor you can walk up a few steps to your own personal gym, home office or rec room. The neighborhood is great for running, walking, biking, trick or treating or catching up with neighbors at annual block parties. Enjoy everything Fairfield County has, as you are minutes to Westport and Norwalk for dining, shopping, train to NYC, beaches, trails and award-winning Wilton schools. Don't miss this opportunity, you will love living here!
