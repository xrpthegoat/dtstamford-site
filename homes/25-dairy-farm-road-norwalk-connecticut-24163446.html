About this home

Spacious and versatile, this quad-level home offers exceptional flexibility, multiple living areas, and a walk-out lower level designed to accommodate today's evolving lifestyle needs. With a layout that provides both separation and connection, this is the kind of space buyers are actively searching for but rarely find at this price point. The main living level features a sun-filled living room with a wood-burning fireplace, seamlessly connected to the dining room and an eat-in kitchen, creating an ideal setting for everyday living and entertaining. Just a few steps away, the inviting family room serves as a true gathering space, complete with oversized sliders leading to a deck, extending your living space outdoors. This level also includes a half bath, laundry room, and welcoming foyer, offering convenience and functionality for daily living. The walk-out lower level adds valuable additional space for recreation, storage, or future customization, providing the flexibility today's buyers prioritize. Upstairs, three well-proportioned bedrooms and a full bath offer comfortable private quarters. Hardwood floors run throughout much of the home, creating warmth and continuity. Recent updates provide both value and peace of mind roof, windows, siding, updated kitchen, Trex deck, stainless steel appliances, double-width asphalt driveway, solar panels, and professionally built retaining walls. Walk to schools. 1mi-E. Norwalk MTA, 1.2mi-Stew Leonard's, 2.2mi-Whole foods.
