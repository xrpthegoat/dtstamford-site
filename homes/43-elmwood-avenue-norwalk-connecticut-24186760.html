About this home

ALL UTILITIES INCLUDED. An oversized 2 bedroom unit in the heart of South Norwalk's Golden Hill District. A perfect blend of the homes original victorian charm and today's modern touches makes this unit a true gem. The oversized kitchen, with brand new quartz countertops and appliances, has a large peninsula as well as a dining area that leads directly out to the private, stone patio and fenced in back yard. The bathroom has new finishes in the tub/shower and the unit has a full washer and dryer located in the lower level as well as storage. Get ready to be impressed with the large primary bedroom with fireplace, beautifully, refinished hardwood floors, oversized windows and 9ft ceilings. Enjoy spending time on the screened in front porch and landscaped yard, all just a short walk to the heart of South Norwalk.
