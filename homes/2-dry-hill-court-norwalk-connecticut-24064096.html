About this home

Fantastic condo alternative. Conveniently located near all major amenities and just a short stroll to Stew Leonard's. Living/dining room combo with sliders leading to the large Trex deck. All 3 bedrooms are spacious and are newly carpeted. Tons of natural sunlight throughout the home. 1 car attached garage plus plenty of off street parking in the large driveway. Central air. Newer roof. Situated on the corner lot of a dead end/cul-de-sac street.
