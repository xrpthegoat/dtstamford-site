About this home

Charming window boxes span the upper level while a covered entrance with easy access welcomes you to this stunning end unit townhome in the highly coveted Palmer Hill Association, a gated community on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border. The first floor has 9 ft ceiling height, wide flooring (just refinished), gracious open living areas, large granite-clad island and high-end appliances, gas fireplace flanked with custom built-ins, private stone terrace with gas hook-up, mudroom/laundry room with sink, powder room, and 2-car garage with extra storage. The wide 2nd level sunlit hallway is infused with an open and airy feeling, with the same hardwood floors. The primary suite with a high tray ceiling detail has 2 walk-in closets and a luxurious bath. There are 2 additional bedrooms and bath. The fully finished lower level offers flexible space as a bedroom suite with bath and sitting room, a spacious family room, gym or office. The impressive clubhouse has a redecorated great room with a grand fireplace, office area, new gym and a stunning pool with a hot tub adding immeasurable value. Palmer Hill is a superb location with quick access to downtown Old Greenwich, Metro North train station, and all the practical conveniences that Stamford has to offer as well. With fantastic triple exposure, this refreshed and move-in ready home affords a wonderful lifestyle.
