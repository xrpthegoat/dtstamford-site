About this home

Nestled in one of Darien's most cherished neighborhoods, this charming Cape Cod home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, & community. Situated on .21 acres, this 4-bedroom, 2 full-bath residence is filled with warmth, character, & endless potential. The main level features a spacious living and dining area, ideal for both everyday living & entertaining, anchored by a cozy wood-burning fireplace. The updated kitchen showcases new countertops, appliances, & backsplash, while two bedrooms & a full bath complete the first floor. Abundant natural light & a functional layout create an inviting atmosphere throughout. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms & renovated full bath provide flexibility for family, guests, or home office space. Step outside to enjoy the level, open backyard perfect for summer barbecues, outdoor entertaining, gardening, or relaxing afternoons with family & friends. This private outdoor space offers plenty of room to play, gather, & unwind. The lower level provides abundant storage & offers excellent potential for future finished space, whether envisioned as a playroom, fitness area or home office. The location is truly exceptional. Set within a highly sought-after neighborhood known for its annual gatherings, holiday celebrations, & strong sense of community, this home is just steps from Holmes Elementary School, Middlesex Middle School, Darien Commons, Heights Crossing, Woodland Park, & the train station. A must see!
