About this home

This beautifully designed residence seamlessly blends timeless charm with modern sophistication, showcasing sun-drenched interiors, inviting fireplaces, and refined architectural details throughout. A secluded stone patio overlooks the sparkling in-ground gunite pool and pool house, all set within a serene, park-like landscape - an idyllic backdrop for both intimate gatherings and grand entertaining. The home's exceptional flow connects the formal living and dining rooms to the newly renovated kitchen, featuring brand new stainless steel appliances, striking quartz countertops, and a sunlit breakfast area. Designed for effortless living, the layout is both elegant and functional. A dramatic family room with soaring cathedral ceiling, wet bar, and impressive stone fireplace offers direct access to the extensive new deck and private yard - a perfect setting for celebrating life year round. The main level primary suite is privately situated in its own wing, complete with spa-inspired bath and dedicated office. A separate wing on the main floor features a remarkable 42 foot media/playroom with custom built-ins and two spacious ensuite bedrooms. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms share a bath and offer abundant storage. Meticulously maintained and thoughtfully updated, this exceptional property is ideally located just minutes from town, train, beaches, and approximately one hour to New York City.
