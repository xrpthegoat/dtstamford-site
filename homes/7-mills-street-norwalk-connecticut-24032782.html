About this home

Multiple Offers Received! Please submit BEST & FINAL offers by Monday, July 22nd at 6pm. This beautiful 1930's colonial in the heart of Norwalk oozes character and charm. Period details abound with classic arched openings, built-in bookcases, an oversized stone fireplace and hardwood floors throughout. Just off the living room, there is an idyllic sunroom, perfect for a playroom or a home office. The kitchen has been fully updated with stainless-steel appliances, granite countertops and plenty of cabinet storage. Enjoy your meals in the cozy breakfast nook or the large, formal dining room. Upstairs you'll find three good-sized bedrooms and a full bath. The large, dry basement has tons of storage and offers plenty of room to create additional living space. Outside, the large, sunny backyard has an oversized patio and firepit - ideal for entertaining guests or enjoying a peaceful morning coffee. Tucked away on a quiet street in Broad River, this home is just a short distance from highways, restaurants and shops. Nothing to do but move in!
