About this home

A single-family home with the potential to be your next great project -- or the perfect alternative to condo living! Set on approximately .22 acres, 15 Ponus Avenue offers the space, privacy, and flexibility of a home without the premium price of a fully renovated property. This 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath residence features hardwood floors, a welcoming fireplace, formal dining room, and a practical kitchen. The primary bedroom offers generous closet space, while the lower level provides additional flexibility for storage, a home office, gym, or hobby space. Step outside to enjoy your private deck and manageable yard, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A one-car garage adds valuable parking and storage. While the home would benefit from updating, its solid layout and desirable single-family setting provide an excellent opportunity to renovate, refresh, and create a home that reflects your style. Ideal for a buyer looking to build equity, a first-time homeowner seeking an affordable entry into Norwalk, or anyone ready to trade condo fees and shared walls for the independence of a single-family home. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, transportation, and all that Norwalk has to offer, this is a chance to bring your vision to life. ABSOLUTELY NO TRESPASSING ON THE PROPERTY.
