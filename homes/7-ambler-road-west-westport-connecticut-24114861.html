About this home

Nestled on a peaceful private cul-de-sac in the heart of Westport, this delightful 3-bedroom, 2-bath home combines comfort, charm, and thoughtful updates! Step inside to find a sun-filled layout with a welcoming flow, perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen is a highlight, offering modern style and functionality with easy access to the dining and living areas. Central air, installed in 2019, ensures year-round comfort, while the 2018 Hardie Board siding adds lasting curb appeal. Other major updates include a new roof (2018) and furnace (2014), giving you peace of mind for years to come. Outside, enjoy a spacious yard with room to garden, play, or simply relax on warm summer evenings. Perfectly located just minutes from schools, shopping, restaurants, beaches, and commuting options, this home offers both privacy and convenience. A rare Westport gem ready to welcome its next happy chapter!
