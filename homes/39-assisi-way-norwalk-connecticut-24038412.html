About this home

Vintage meets modern in this wonderfully bright ranch located in a terrific neighborhood - less than a mile to Stew's and all the retail and amenities of the Post Rd! In addition to being enormous and ideal for gatherings, the public rooms offer a multitude of uses and are really only limited by your creative ability. Excellent one level living, absolutely soaked in natural light. The recently refreshed space includes entirely new kitchen with stylish marble backsplash and new stainless appliances, fresh top to bottom paint along with new flooring, new heating system, air conditioning and oil tank. The huge walkup attic is unfinished but offers more than 800 SF that might be suitable for finishing. There is also a detached shed at the back of the property that is just ideal for reimagining; convert it to guest space, workshop, garden shed, etc. So many options - come see how it might work for you!
