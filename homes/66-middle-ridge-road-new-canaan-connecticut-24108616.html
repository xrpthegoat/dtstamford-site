About this home

Discover the perfect blend of sophistication, space, and serenity in this magnificent five-bedroom Colonial home, a true gem that promises an exceptional living experience. This splendid residence is ideally situated on over two private acres of meticulously landscaped property, offering a peaceful retreat that remains conveniently close to town, parkways, and schools. As you enter this stately home, you are welcomed by an open family room bathed in natural light, with elegant sliders that lead out to an expansive terrace, perfect for al fresco dining and outdoor enjoyment. The heart of the home is the beautifully updated kitchen, featuring a large island for culinary creations and additional sliders that open to the terrace, inviting seamless indoor-outdoor living. The home's thoughtful design includes a separate au-pair suite, providing privacy and comfort for guests or live-in assistance. The great room, complete with a cozy fireplace, vaulted ceiling and access to a sun-drenched sunroom, offers a versatile space for relaxation or entertainment. The spacious dining room, also graced with a fireplace, is ideal for hosting dinner parties and family gatherings. Retreat to the finished lower level, where you'll find a welcoming fireplace, a full bath, and a kitchenette, creating an ideal space for hosting guests or indulging in recreational activities. A first-floor office provides a secluded workspace for productivity and focus.
