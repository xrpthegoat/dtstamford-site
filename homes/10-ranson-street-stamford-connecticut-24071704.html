About this home

Spacious & Private Townhome for Rent Near the Beach. Enjoy the feel of a single-family home with the ease of condo living in this free-standing townhome in the Cove area. Half a mile from the beach, this spacious rental offers privacy, modern comforts, and easy access to everything Stamford has to offer. The main floor is perfect for entertaining, featuring a bright living room with a gas fireplace, an open dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops and stainless-steel appliances. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet and a luxurious bath with a whirlpool tub, double vanities, and a separate shower. Two additional bedrooms are generous in size and share an updated hall bath. A second-floor laundry adds convenience. Hardwood floors run throughout the home, which also includes an attached one-car garage, yard space in front and to the side, and a walk-up attic for extra storage. The lower level, with high ceilings, provides additional space for storage or future use. Move-in ready, this home offers quick access to Metro North, I-95, Chelsea Piers, Cove Island Park & Beach, Harbor Point, shopping, and restaurants.
