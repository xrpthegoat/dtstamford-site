About this home

One-of-a-kind opportunity to transform this lost 1950s Split-Level residence squirreled away at the very end on Putter Drive. Location is everything and what other properties don't have, this one has it all -- half an acre level lot and a sense of space on a quiet, tree-lined street while staying near to the pulse of Springdale's shopping and essentials. Bordering New Canaan and on the back of Sterling Farms Golf Course, so much potential in this estate sale with three bedrooms, 2 full baths, an open entry, skylights, French doors and then some. Someone with vision and a little bit of muscle can transform this once begotten rosebud, what you see is not what you can get, and give this home the glow-up it deserves. Sold strictly as-is/where is, subject to probate approval, no repairs or contingencies.
