About this home

Welcome to this charming 2-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom townhouse condo, perfect for those seeking a move in-ready space, with potential to add personal touches. If you love closet space, this is the property for you. Each large bedroom features hardwood floors and double closets with additional space in upstairs linen closet adjacent to the 2nd floor laundry. A full walk-in closet awaits you on the 1st floor as well with double closets in front hallway. Two reserved parking spaces are provided with this Townhouse in the rear of the building. Restaurants, retail shopping and groceries are within walking distance. Convenient location on a quiet tree lined street. Just a very short drive to downtown Historic SoNo district with a large variety of New York-style classy restaurants, shops and clubs along with a nearby Metro North rail station, post office, bank, cinema and more.
