About this home

Eye catching curb appeal greets you from the white picket fence, stone pillars and Belgium block lined driveway accented by crushed stone. Walk to everything from this quiet side street tucked right off the beaten path in this convenient Newfield area. Main floor oversized bedroom with a king-sized walk in closet. Two second floor bedrooms share a full bath. The partially finished lower level is a blank canvas ready for fresh ideas. Mature plantings frame the rear yard for the ultimate privacy. In total, the oversized or "extra" lot offers a multitude of future uses for the discerning buyer. A much loved home, owned by the same family for more than 50 years, this charming cape is ready for its new chapter. Come and make it your own.
