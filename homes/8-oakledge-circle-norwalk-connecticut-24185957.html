About this home

Discover your own slice of heaven in this charming 3 bedroom, 2 bath Colonial, ideally situated on a beautifully landscaped 0.37 acre lot on a private cul-de-sac. Offering the perfect blend of privacy and convenience, this home has been cherished as a second residence by the same owner for more than 25 years. The main level features a spacious living room, formal dining room, and an eat-in kitchen with access to a wraparound deck. A first-floor bedroom and a full bath add convenience and versatility. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath. The walkout lower level includes a family room with a fireplace, utility room, and an attached one-car garage. Surrounded by mature perennial gardens, ornamental trees, and a tranquil koi pond, the property provides a serene setting for everyday living. Recent updates include central air conditioning and replacement windows. Cosmetic updating will make this home a real showpiece. The finished lower level family room provides an additional 264 square feet of living space that is not reflected on the city field card. Enjoy the best of both worlds with easy access to shopping, restaurants, the Metro-North train, I-95, parks, beaches, and all the area attractions, making commuting and everyday conveniences effortless.
