About this home

Welcome to single-level living at its finest! This meticulously cared-for, renovated ranch home boasts modern updates throughout and is located on a quaint cul-de-sac in the desirable West Rocks area of Norwalk. The humongous yard is spectacular! Fully fenced and spacious enough for family and pets to roam, there's no need to go to the park because you own the park! There's plenty of room for a pool and flowing gardens to complement the existing mature plantings. Two sheds provide ample space for arts, crafts, and storage. This special home features recessed lighting, a pellet stove, hardwood floors throughout, and granite countertops with a HUGE kitchen island perfect for entertaining and family meals. Each room is adorned with lovely crown molding, and the beautiful master bedroom includes an en-suite bathroom. The kitchen is complete with stainless steel appliances, and a dual-fuel oven for efficient baking and high-heat stovetop sauteing. Step outside to enjoy the huge wrap-around decking, which is in perfect condition for hosting parties. Additionally, everything you could ever need is just five minutes away. There's literally nothing to do here but move in, enjoy the warm color palettes, and feel secure knowing all the major mechanicals have been upgraded. A propane generator provides peace of mind during outages, oodles of closet space, and basement for storage. This property has an assumable 2.375% VA mortgage. Please call for details.
