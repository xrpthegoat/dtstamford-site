About this home

It is the luxury rental you have been waiting for... Perfectly positioned at the edge of Mill Pond, this exceptional home offers breathtaking views and fabulous sunrises from every room. Whether you are looking for a summer vacation or considering making Westport your home, this is a rare opportunity to experience the very best of coastal living. Featuring a first floor bedroom and 4 additional bedrooms on the 2nd floor, the thoughtfully designed layout provides flexibility for guests and family. There is a gourmet kitchen perfect for entertaining while endless outdoor living areas create the ultimate setting for relaxation. This home showcases high end designer furnishings- every space feels like an elegant and comfortable retreat. Just a stroll to Compo beach your private oasis includes a heated pool, putting green, pickleball court, in-home gym and kayak.
