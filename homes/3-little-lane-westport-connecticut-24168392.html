About this home

Spend your summer in style in this beautifully decorated 5-bedroom Westport retreat designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living. Price is per month. Set on a flat, fully usable acre, perfect for lawn games and entertaining, this elegant home offers the ultimate seasonal escape. Sun-filled interiors feature refined finishes, gracious living spaces, and a dedicated home office for easy remote work between beach days. The heart of the home opens to a stunning backyard oasis with a sparkling pool, expansive patio, and plenty of space to relax, dine, and soak up the sunshine. A bonus room adds flexibility for play, guests, or movie nights, while the finished lower level with gym equipment makes it easy to stay active all summer long. Additional highlights include a 3-car garage, generator for peace of mind, and spacious, thoughtfully designed rooms throughout. Perfectly located to enjoy Westport's beaches, dining, shopping, and vibrant summer lifestyle, this inviting home is the ideal place to make unforgettable summer memories.
