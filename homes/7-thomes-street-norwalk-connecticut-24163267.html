About this home

Welcome to 7 Thomes Street, a charming home set on one of Rowayton's quiet, picturesque streets. A welcoming front porch sets the tone, perfect for morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Inside, hardwood floors run throughout the home, offering warmth and character across the three bedrooms and 2.5 baths. Downstairs is open, airy, and bright. The living room is large enough for plenty of seating around the fireplace, along with a chic mahjong corner, while the porch off the living room provides incredible additional space. The dining room and bar flow seamlessly into the back of the house, where you'll find a stunning chef's kitchen and a vaulted-ceiling family room. The standout feature is the newly built gunite pool - a rare and exciting find in Rowayton - creating a private backyard retreat for summer entertaining, relaxing weekends, and outdoor living. The fully fenced yard adds both privacy and peace of mind, making it ideal for pets, play, and effortless outdoor enjoyment. Within walking distance to the Rowayton train station, village shops, restaurants, and the waterfront, the location makes it easy to enjoy everything Rowayton has to offer while still feeling tucked away on a charming neighborhood street. *showings begin Monday at noon*
