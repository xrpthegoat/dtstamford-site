About this home

Newly updated single-family rental in East Norwalk only .4 miles to East Norwalk Train Station, Ice Cream, Salons and Restaurants etc., approximately 1.7 miles to Calf Pasture Beach, Summer Concerts, Miniature Golf, Sunset Grill, Kayaking, Marina, SoNo and the SoNo Collection, the location is fantastic. The front door welcomes you into the foyer where you will find your Living Room/Dining Room L Shaped Combo with a fireplace, open floor plan to an updated Kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and the Sunroom where you find your back door entrance to the backyard gravel patio, which is partially fenced in to enjoy the tranquility of home. Home offers a washer & dryer, hardwood floors, large attic space and central air. There are 3 bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms, and the home is on one level. The primary bedroom has 2 closets, a full bathroom with tile shower with body sprays and a bluetooth exhaust fan. The hall full bathroom has a stand-alone shower, soaking tub, heated floor and a bluetooth exhaust fan as well. Driveway parking for 2 cars. Owner lives on site part time and reserves the right to the lower level, garage and shed. Owner pays for oil heat, snow removal (tenants responsible for their stairs and entrance ways) lawn care and security system with audio/video doorbell and exterior video cameras. A potential tenant must have excellent credit, provide proof of ability to pay, background/eviction check, references and no smoking/vaping on site
