About this home

Renovated 4 bedroom 3 1/2 bath modern designed home on 3 levels of living in an ideal cul de sac. Light filled and sited on 2 lush, landscaped acres, the Main level centers around the brand new showstopper Gourmet Chef's kitchen. Subzero & Wolf appliances with Cambria quartz countertops. Center island, custom cabinetry, EIK allow for all entertaining possibilities. The adjacent Dining Room offers space for formal dining. A french door leads to the exterior Patio perfect for morning coffee, or bbq. Living Room with wood fireplace and a wall of windows, 3 Season Room and 1/2 bath Powder Room complete the Main Level. The Upper Level features the Primary Bedroom Suite. Cathedral ceilings, fireplace and custom lighting make for an oasis of comfort. A sumptuous double vanity bathroom has a large shower, two person soaking tub and towel warmer. Walk in Closet with room enough for two. 2 additional bedrooms both with walk in closets, separate full bath and laundry room complement the Upper Level. The Lower level is a multi faceted haven. Family or bonus room, Bedroom Suite with full bath, updated kitchen and home office. Sliders in the lower level walk out to the backyard and fenced saltwater gunite pool, spa and patio. Major renovation includes updated kitchen appliances on Main and Lower levels, new hardwood floors, new HVAC and Well equipment, new plumbing and electrical and so much more. This oasis is close to town, schools & NYC
