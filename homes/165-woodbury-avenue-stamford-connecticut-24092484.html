About this home

This impeccably updated Cape is nestled on desirable Woodbury Avenue, near parks, moments from the Springdale train station, dining, and shopping. Step inside to discover a beautifully appointed living room with a wood-burning fireplace, seamlessly flowing into the eat-in kitchen, dining area, and family room. Sliding glass doors lead to a fully screened-in porch, offering a tranquil space to dine alfresco or unwind in the fresh air. The main level boasts two generously sized bedrooms, a renovated full bath, a well-equipped laundry room, and a spacious mudroom with direct access to the attached one-car garage. Upstairs, the expansive primary bedroom is complemented by a 4th bedroom and a renovated 2nd full bathroom. Outside, the meticulously landscaped, level grounds feature a patio, garden - a picturesque backdrop for outdoor gatherings or peaceful relaxation. Not to be missed!
