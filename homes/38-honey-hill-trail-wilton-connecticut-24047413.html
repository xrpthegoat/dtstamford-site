About this home

Enjoy easy one-level living surrounded by nature at 38 Honey Hill Trail, a wonderful 4 bedroom, 2 full bath, 2 half bath ranch on a private 1.1 acre in Wilton! This sun-filled home boasts a country eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a half bath, large living room and dining room plus a cozy family room with vaulted ceilings and stone fireplace with wood burning stove. A spacious back deck with access from the kitchen and family room encourages seamless indoor/outdoor living and entertaining! 3 generous bedrooms, full bath and primary suite with walk-in closet and full bath complete the main living level. Hardwood floors throughout. The finished lower level allows for additional living space with new vinyl flooring and new wet bar, a new home gym with sliding barn door, half bath and plenty of storage. Potential 3 car garage. Brand new bluestone and Belgian block walkway and brand new Trex front entry steps/deck. 38 Honey Hill Trail is located in a bucolic and tranquil setting with direct access to 150+ acres of hiking trails and town preserves yet still convenient to Wilton Town Center, schools, shopping, Wilton YMCA and charming Georgetown.
