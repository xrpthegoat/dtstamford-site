About this home

PRISTINE, STYLISH, AND MOVE-IN READY. Ideally located in sought-after Canaan Close with exceptional proximity to downtown New Canaan, this beautifully updated interior end-unit offers the perfect blend of sophistication, flexibility, and convenience. Thoughtfully refreshed by the current owners, the home features updated kitchen and baths, fresh paint throughout, and refinished hardwood floors with a light natural water-based finish, creating a bright, modern aesthetic. The eat-in kitchen is both functional and inviting, highlighted by a custom coffee bar and sun-filled breakfast area. The versatile floor plan lives like a three-bedroom home, with a main-level den featuring a full bath and oversized walk-in closet-ideal as a primary suite, guest room, or private office. The step-down living room showcases a 9-foot ceiling, fireplace, and built-ins, flowing seamlessly into a formal dining room. A dramatic floating staircase connects all levels, adding architectural interest. Upstairs, two well-proportioned bedrooms include a generous primary suite with ensuite bath and direct access to a private terrace. The expansive, above-grade lower level offers exceptional additional living space with a second fireplace, built-ins, wet bar, and flexible recreation or media areas, along with access to a private patio.
