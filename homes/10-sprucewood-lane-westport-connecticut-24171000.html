About this home

Tucked at the end of a coveted Hunt Club lane, 10 Sprucewood Lane offers exceptional privacy, architectural refinement, & an enviable setting within one of Westport's most sought-after neighborhoods. Thoughtfully renovated in 2017, this sophisticated Colonial showcases a gracious & versatile floor plan that seamlessly balances classic elegance with modern living. Sun-filled interiors & a curated neutral palette create a refined atmosphere throughout more than 5,000 square feet of beautifully appointed living space. At the center of the home is a stunning chef's kitchen, featuring Carrera marble, stainless steel appliances, & a walk-in pantry - expertly designed for both effortless daily living and elevated entertaining. Formal living spaces & the expansive great room connect seamlessly to an entertainer's backyard, highlighted by a spacious mahogany deck, sweeping lawn, & charming stone walls. A screened-in porch off the living room offers an inviting space for relaxation, casual dining, or gathering with guests. Distinctive highlights include two primary ensuite bedrooms - one featuring a luxurious steam shower, four fireplaces, two mudrooms, an oversized garage, & a dramatic great room anchored by a striking see-through fireplace. The fully finished lower level provides exceptional flexibility with an ensuite bedroom, home office, recreation room with fireplace, and an additional mudroom with abundant storage and direct exterior access. Room for a pool. Welcome Home!
