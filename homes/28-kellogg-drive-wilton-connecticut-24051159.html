About this home

Light, bright and refreshed! Move right in to this charming, updated and private colonial! Hardwood floors, central air, new windows and freshly painted! Beautiful lot on neighborhood street. Four bedrooms up have been refreshed with updated bathrooms. New exterior siding and roof. The work has been done for you! Owner related to agent.
