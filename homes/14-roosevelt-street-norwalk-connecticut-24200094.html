About this home

In the heart of Norwalk, 14 Roosevelt Street pairs updated living with classic charm. This lovingly maintained four-bedroom single-family home is now available for rent. The 1920s charmer spans 2,572 square feet and welcomes you through a classic vestibule into an open floor plan with gleaming hardwood floors and character around every corner, from period sconces to distinctive built-in details. The main level features a spacious living room, formal dining room, cozy study, and farmhouse kitchen with a breakfast area and half bath. A vaulted solarium offers a third entertaining space, wrapped in windows on all sides with hardwood floors and two ceiling fans. Upstairs are four bedrooms - including one converted into an enormous walk-in closet with custom built-ins - plus a spa-like primary bathroom. A full attic and basement provide excellent storage, with laundry located in the basement. Outside, enjoy stunning perennial plantings, a built-in fire pit, a deck, and a two-car garage on a 0.16-acre lot. The home is heated with forced hot water and includes central air conditioning. Offered at $6,500 per month. Credit check, income verification, and references required. Ideally located near Westport Avenue and Stew Leonard's, and just minutes from the beach, Cranbury Park, and everything Norwalk has to offer. See it quickly before it's gone!
