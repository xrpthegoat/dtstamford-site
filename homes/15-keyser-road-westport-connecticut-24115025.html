About this home

Charming in-town single-family home available for a 1 year lease. This inviting, sunlit residence is ideally situated in the heart of town. The living room, featuring a cozy fireplace, provides a warm space to relax and gather. The open, bright kitchen with granite countertops connects seamlessly to the dining area and offers access to a private patio and large backyard-perfect for outdoor entertaining. The upper level features a spacious primary bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, walk-in closet and patio. There are two additional bedrooms and a shared full bathroom on the upper level. There is a full unfinished basement for storage. Located just minutes Compo Beach, the train station, and downtown Westport, this home combines comfort with convenience.
