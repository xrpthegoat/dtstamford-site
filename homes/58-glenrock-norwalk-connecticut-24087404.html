About this home

A commuter's paradise! This generously sized 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse is just a short stroll from the Merritt 7 Metro North train station and only minutes from the Merritt Parkway, Route 7 connector, and I-95, making travel to Stamford, Greenwich, or NYC effortless. The kitchen has been completely updated with white shaker cabinets, stainless steel appliances, Quartz countertops, and a subway tile backsplash. The expansive living and dining area, featuring a wood-burning fireplace, is perfect for entertaining. Sliding doors open to a balcony with views of the pool and beautifully maintained grounds. The upper level includes a spacious primary bedroom with a vaulted ceiling, a fully renovated bathroom, and ample closet space. The second bedroom also has an en-suite full bathroom. There is a laundry area with full-size washer and dryer units and additional storage space. The home is equipped with a high-efficiency gas furnace for heating and central air conditioning. It includes a 1-car garage. This well-maintained and highly desirable complex features a pool, ideal for those seeking a low-maintenance lifestyle and is pet friendly - 2 small dogs or 2 cats are allowed. Recent updates include Anderson windows and slider door, hot water heater (2023), kitchen was completely renovated kitchen in December 2022, removed popcorn ceiling and recessed lighting in the main level.
