About this home

Position your business in this exceptional retail opportunity in the heart of Norwalk. This beautifully renovated 850SF storefront offers a clean, modern aesthetic with hardwood floors, high ceilings, and central gas heat and air conditioning, creating an inviting and comfortable environment for both customers and staff. This is an outstanding opportunity for a retailer or boutique concept seeking visibility, character, and a dynamic downtown presence.
