About this home

Fully renovated, spacious 4 Bedroom, 2.5 Bath home with idyllic water views. Enjoy this serene and bucolic environment while only minutes from both New Canaan and Darien amenities, shopping, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Pond views from most rooms. Don't miss this unique setting. Minimum 12 Month lease, but owner would prefer 15 or 18 Month Term.
