About this home

This exceptional 5-bedroom, 41/2-bath Nantucket-style Colonial blends refined architecture with relaxed coastal living. Thoughtfully renovated in 2012 by Steven Mueller Architects LLC and refreshed by the current owners in 2021, the home welcomes you with a dramatic two-story foyer and beautifully designed interiors filled with natural light. Crisp millwork, shiplap detailing, coffered ceilings, and custom built-ins create a timeless backdrop throughout. The elegant yet inviting living spaces include a serene living room centered around a sleek fireplace with symmetrical built-ins and wide-plank floors, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Water views from many rooms enhance the home's peaceful coastal setting, filling the interiors with light and openness. Outdoors, elegant bluestone patios, an elevated second-floor deck positioned for sunset views, and professionally landscaped grounds overlook the scenic Five Mile River-an idyllic setting for both quiet moments and memorable gatherings.
