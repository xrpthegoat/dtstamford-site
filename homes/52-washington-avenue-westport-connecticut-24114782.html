About this home

Welcome home to this charming sun-filled colonial set on a quiet street within walking distance to the town of Westport's many shops and restaurants. It's the perfect weekend getaway or year round easy lifestyle. First floor has living room with fireplace, dining room, renovated kitchen and a sunroom that offers a great space for an in-home office or playroom and half bath. Second floor has three bedrooms and a full bath. Walk out lower level features a family room with fireplace, full bath and the fourth bedroom. Barn cannot be used for car storage, but has plenty of room for exercise or workshop space. Landlord maintains the lawn and grounds. Easy access to NYC commute. Tenants currently in residence until early August. Must have confirmed appointment with listing broker.
