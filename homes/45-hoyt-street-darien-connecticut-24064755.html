About this home

Welcome Home to Darien: A Bright Beginning Awaits This freshly updated 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath Cape-style home is ideal for buyers seeking charm, comfort, and convenience. Freshly painted interiors and refinished hardwood floors create a bright, welcoming space. The atrium-style family room fills the home with natural light and opens to a spacious deck overlooking a deep backyard. The updated kitchen, cozy living room with a wood-burning fireplace, and open layout bring a fun and inviting vibe. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms: a serene primary suite, a secondary ensuite, and two additional bedrooms with a shared bath. Practical features include a mudroom, first-floor laundry, a second-floor laundry chute, and a full basement perfect for a workout area, playroom, or extra storage. Just one block from Holmes Elementary, this home offers unmatched convenience and access to Darien's beaches, and vibrant downtown. With a quick 50-minute commute to NYC, this home has great light, great bones and endless possibilities!
