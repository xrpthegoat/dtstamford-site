About this home

Tucked away on over seven quiet acres off prestigious Round Hill Road, this private estate offers a serene blend of luxury, space, and style. The main residence is complemented by a beautiful pool/guest house designed for effortless entertaining and resort-style living. At the heart of the home, a Christopher Peacock kitchen showcases statuary marble countertops and backsplash, double islands, and professional-grade appliances, all centered around a custom command station with built-in desk and storage. The kitchen opens seamlessly to a sun-filled family room with bespoke cabinetry, creating the perfect setting for relaxed gatherings. The first floor also features a front-to-back living room, elegant dining room, inviting home office, and private en-suite guest suite.
