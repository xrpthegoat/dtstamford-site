About this home

PETS WELCOME! HEAT, WATER, SNOW REMOVAL, AND WASHER/DRYER GAS INCLUDED IN RENT! Enjoy low-maintenance living in an updated Byram apartment featuring a newly renovated kitchen and bathroom, brand-new in-unit W/D, and a private balcony with sunset views. The updated railroad-style kitchen features granite countertops and backsplash, a farmhouse-style porcelain sink, and a brand-new four-panel refrigerator with exceptional storage. Located near recently renovated Byram Park and downtown Port Chester, with easy access to bartaco, Colony Grill, Kneaded Bread, Whole Foods, Costco, and The Waterfront at Port Chester. Multiple bus stops and convenient access to I-95 make getting around easy. NO OFF-STREET PARKING PROVIDED, but two nearby municipal parking lots offer additional parking options, along with ample on-street parking. Tenant pays electricity and stove gas. Pets welcome!
