About this home

Beautifully constructed and thoughtfully designed, this exceptional 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom single-level home is privately set on a landscaped 0.70-acre cul-de-sac lot just minutes from downtown Wilton and the Norwalk River Valley Trails. Designed for today's lifestyle, the modern open-concept floor plan seamlessly blends indoor and outdoor living. The stunning chef's kitchen features a large quartzite center island, high-end stainless steel appliances, and abundant workspace - opening directly to an expansive outdoor deck ideal for entertaining and overlooking the flat, professionally landscaped backyard. The main living area offers an expansive entertaining space with fireplace and dining area, all positioned to enjoy beautiful backyard views. Adjacent to the kitchen is a cozy family room along with a flexible fourth bedroom or home office featuring stylish barn doors for privacy, a full bathroom, mudroom separate side entrance. The private bedroom wing includes three additional bedrooms, a separate laundry room, and a luxurious primary suite complete with tranquil backyard views, a spacious walk-in closet, and a custom-designed spa-like bathroom with double vanity and oversized shower. The lower level offers a large two-car garage with electric vehicle charger, extensive storage space, and a full unfinished basement ready to become a gym, recreation area, media room, or additional living space tailored to the next owner's needs.
