About this home

Welcome to 50 Cody Drive in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood. This fully renovated 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home is move-in ready with the perfect blend of modern updates and classic charm. Featuring brand-new windows, HVAC system, roof, gutters, insulated attic, and vinyl siding, along with beautiful crown molding and hardwood flooring throughout. The updated kitchen features modern cabinets & countertops with a reimagined floor plan, seamlessly flowing into the rest of the home. The entire second floor has been transformed, boasting two new spacious bathrooms featuring dual vanities and a thoughtful primary en suite inclusive of an oversized walk in closet and relaxing freestanding tub. The outside features a bluestone patio with gas grill hookup, no propane tank needed! Located less than a mile from Newfield Green Shopping Center and the newly renovated Barrett Park, this location is convenient to community amenities and only two miles from downtown Stamford. Centrally located between I-95 & the Merritt Parkway with both the Springdale and Glenbrook train stations only a short drive away.
