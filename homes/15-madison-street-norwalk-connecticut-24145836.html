About this home

Welcome to Sono Gardens, a gated townhouse-style condominium community in the heart of South Norwalk. This spacious townhouse offers 3 bedrooms and 1 full bath with a bright open-concept layout ideal for today's living. Hardwood floors run throughout, adding warmth and character. Enjoy the convenience of in-unit laundry and a large private basement perfect for storage, home gym, or future expansion. Property is being sold as-is and requires some renovation, presenting a great opportunity to customize and add value. The unit includes one assigned parking space and is located within a secure, well-kept complex. Ideally located minutes from SONO restaurants, shopping, Vets park, Calf Pasture Beach, Metro-North, and major highways-this is low-maintenance living with an unbeatable location. **Income Restrictions, no rentals and no pets**
