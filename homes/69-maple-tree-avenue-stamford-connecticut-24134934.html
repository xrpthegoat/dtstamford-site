About this home

Located in the desirable Glenbrook community of Stamford, this charming & freshly painted 2 bed 1.5 bath townhouse offers the perfect balance of convenience and comfort. Enjoy easy access to downtown, shopping, and major commuting routes, all while coming home to a peaceful setting. A welcoming front porch provides an ideal spot to relax and unwind. Enter to find a spacious living room and an adjoining dining area with sliding doors that open to the back deck- perfect for a BBQ. The bright kitchen and a convenient half bath complete the main level. On the upper level you'll find two generous bedrooms and a full hallway bath. The lower level offers a flexible space that can be used as a playroom, home office, or additional family room, with laundry located here for extra convenience. With just a bit of updating, this home offers an excellent opportunity to create your ideal space in a highly desirable community.
