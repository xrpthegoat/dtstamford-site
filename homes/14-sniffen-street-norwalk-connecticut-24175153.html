About this home

A real estate opportunity like this only comes around every few years-a cash cow with great tenants, additional income potential, in excellent condition and well-kept. Welcome to 14 Sniffen Street, a large multi-family centrally located in Norwalk, configured as a 3-family home. You're greeted by a wide lot with a large driveway, a 2-car detached garage, and roses and plantings that make you feel right at home. Two units sit at the front of the original home: a spacious 1-bedroom with front and side entrances (the front features a cute porch), and a second-level unit that spans the third floor as well, offering 3 beds and 2 baths. The tenants clearly take pride in their home. On the side is a vacant 1-bedroom unit with great potential-add a small bump-out to connect the basement staircase to the living room and double the square footage. Above it are two 1-bedroom, 1-bath units with no kitchens to maintain. A large basement, separate from the back basement, is perfect for storage and houses several of the mechanicals. Each unit has its own heating, hot water, and electric meter. There's even a small backyard for warm-weather play and barbecues. With strong cash flow and easy access to Route 7, I-95, the Merritt, and everything Norwalk has to offer, this home is exactly what you've been looking for. See it before it's snatched off the market!
