About this home

This stunning 3-5 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom ranch home has an abundance of natural light and vast nature views. This Cranbury ranch has a newly renovated kitchen, spacious dining area and newly renovated bathrooms including a spa tub and hardwood floors throughout. Convenient full size main level laundry. Custom stone countertops, marble and quartz showers, and a large eat-in kitchen featuring a new island & stainless steel appliances. The family room has a cozy wood burning fireplace. Enjoy the views from the 4 season picturesque solarium just off the kitchen. The open floor plan has plenty of breathing room for everyone. The oversized two-car garage provides extra storage space. The large level backyard features a Party Pavilion, ideal for unwinding, reading, or socializing with loved ones while surrounded by the peaceful beauty of nature. Fully finished basement boasts 1300 sqft with plenty of room for the extended fam, live-in au pair/nanny or the in-laws. The lower level has a large entertainment room, kitchen w/full size appli, dining room table and an additional room that can be a large private office or a 5th bedroom with a full wall of custom closets. Designed with professional Feng Shui principles, enjoy the serene and harmonious atmosphere throughout. Emphasizing simplicity, elegance, and efficiency. This home is conveniently located 1 mile from major hi-ways and has easy access to nearby amenities and park. Westport train station is 3.5 miles away train to NY.Pet
