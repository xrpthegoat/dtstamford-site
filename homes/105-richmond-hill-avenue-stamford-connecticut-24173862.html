About this home

Welcome to 105 Richmond Hill Avenue, Unit #2, a charming fully renovated and well-appointed condo in the heart of Stamford's Westside. This 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath unit offers approximately 810 square feet of efficiently designed living space-perfect for comfortable everyday living. New cabinets and appliances! Inside, you'll find a bright, functional layout with inviting living areas ideal for relaxing or entertaining. Both bedrooms provide a peaceful retreat, and the half bath adds extra convenience for guests. The unit also features in-apartment laundry hookups, giving you the option to install your own washer and dryer, plus access to a shared basement for additional storage. Located in a highly walkable neighborhood, you're just minutes from grocery stores, local dining, and downtown Stamford's vibrant restaurant and entertainment scene. Enjoy nearby outdoor spaces like Mill River Park, as well as Lione Park and Jackie Robinson Park. Commuters will love the quick access to I-95 (Exits 6 & 7) and the Stamford Transportation Center, offering Metro-North service to NYC in about 45 minutes.
