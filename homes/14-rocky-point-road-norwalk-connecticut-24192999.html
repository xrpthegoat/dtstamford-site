About this home

BELL ISLAND CONNECTICUT. Experience the best of waterfront living on sought-after Bell Island. Perfectly perched to capture beautiful water views, this exceptional home offers peace of mind and an unmatched coastal lifestyle. Whether you are looking for a year-round residence or the perfect coastal retreat, this Bell Island gem combines timeless charm, generous living spaces, and an enviable location just moments from the shoreline.
