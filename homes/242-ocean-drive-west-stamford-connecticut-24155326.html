About this home

Please submit all offers by 4pm Monday March 2nd. Welcome to 242 Ocean Drive West, a beautifully maintained and extensively upgraded coastal residence just across the street from the water in sought-after Shippan. Blending classic seaside charm with thoughtful modern improvements, this home offers immediate comfort and lasting peace of mind. Designed for exceptional first-floor living, the main level features an effortless flow between living and dining spaces, ideal for both everyday life and entertaining. Expansive windows capture tranquil water views, while multiple porches create seamless indoor-outdoor living-perfect for summer dinners, sunset cocktails, or quiet mornings with the sea breeze. Just a short stroll to the yacht club, the location invites an active coastal lifestyle with the shoreline moments away. A one-car garage equipped with a charging station adds modern convenience, while a private suite complete with bedroom, full bath, and kitchenette provides flexible space for guests, extended family, or a dedicated home office. Offering refined comfort, water views, and effortless living by the Sound, 242 Ocean Drive West embodies the very best of coastal Connecticut living.
