About this home

At the End of a Coveted Cul-de-sac in Lower Weston - Welcome to 55 Blueberry Hill! A picture-perfect lifestyle awaits in this thoughtfully reimagined and beautifully appointed country home. This stunning 4-bedroom, 3-full and 2-half bath residence spans over 4,500 sq.ft., offering a gracious layout, gleaming hardwood floors throughout, and timeless architectural details. The custom center-hall design features a grand front-to-back formal living room with fireplace, an adjacent sun/playroom, and a gracious dining room with bowed windows. The sleek, brand-new kitchen with top-end appliances, is an epicurean's dream-complete with a mudroom extraordinaire featuring elegant porcelain tile, additional pantry, and work space-all- opening seamlessly to the rear play yard. The expansive primary suite includes generous closets and a luxurious -spa-like bath with soaking tub. Additional bedrooms are generously sized, contributing to a very livable, well-balanced floor plan. The upper level showcases a spectacular vaulted great room/family room with skylights, exposed beams, a charming balcony, and a custom pub-style refreshment center-perfect for entertaining, fun and games or cozy family movie-night! Outside, enjoy glorious western sunsets from the outdoor kitchen and terrace, overlooking a beautifully landscaped, perfectly level one-acre lot. Lower level offers extra storage-The school bus stops right at your mailbox! Just moments to acclaimed Weston Schools, town, train & parkway-
