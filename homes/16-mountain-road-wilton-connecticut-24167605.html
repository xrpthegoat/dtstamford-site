About this home

Perched at the top of a hill at the end of a shared drive, 16 Mountain Road offers a rare blend of privacy and convenience in the heart of Georgetown. This contemporary split-level is designed for easy living, featuring a sun-filled open kitchen and family room that flows seamlessly into a vaulted sunroom with a cathedral ceiling, bringing the outdoors in. The upper level includes four bedrooms, highlighted by a spacious primary suite with en-suite bath. The lower level adds approximately 750 square feet of flexible living space, complete with a second family room with fireplace, a private office or optional bedroom, laundry, and a powder room. Outdoor living is just as impressive, with an expansive 1,000+ square foot deck overlooking a 50-foot pool and flagstone patio-ideal for entertaining or relaxing. Set on beautifully landscaped grounds with mature plantings and seasonal color, this property offers a peaceful retreat just minutes from Wilton Center, schools, and commuting routes.
