About this home

DARIEN LIVING IN A PRIME, CONVENIENT LOCATION! Ideally situated near the new Darien Commons, this well-maintained 3-bedroom, 2 full bath home combines comfort, flexibility, and exceptional value. Located in the desirable Noroton Heights neighborhood, the home features a spacious, level backyard, ample parking, and an oversized garage. The flexible floor plan includes one main-level bedroom-perfect for a home office-along with a full bath on each level. Enjoy a bright, eat-in kitchen, convenient main-level laundry, and hardwood floors throughout. The inviting living room features a fireplace, while the patio just off the kitchen provides the perfect spot for morning coffee or outdoor relaxation. Set between Noroton Avenue and Edgerton Street, this is a commuter's dream with easy access to the train, schools, beaches, shopping, and dining.
