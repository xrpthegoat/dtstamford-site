About this home

Welcome to Sheffield Ridge Condominiums! This bright and spacious one-bedroom, one-bath unit offers 788 square feet of comfortable, low-maintenance living and a garage! The thoughtfully designed layout features an open living and dining area-perfect for entertaining-a well-appointed kitchen with a gas range, abundant closet space, and a generously sized bedroom with excellent storage. A full bath with tile flooring and a tub-and-shower combination completes the unit. One reserved garage space (#5) associated to this unit. Building amenities include elevator access, on-site laundry, a private storage unit, security cameras, and one parking space in the gated lot. The monthly HOA includes heat, hot water, water, and cooking gas, helping to simplify monthly expenses. Ideally located near Norwalk Hospital, The SoNo Collection, Historic South Norwalk, restaurants, shopping, parks, and public transportation. Commuters will appreciate convenient access to the South Norwalk Metro-North station, I-95, Route 7, and the Merritt Parkway. Whether you are a first-time buyer, downsizing, or seeking an investment opportunity, this Sheffield Ridge condo offers an appealing combination of space, convenience, and value. Investor friendly.
