About this home

Incredible opportunity to create your dream home in the highly desirable Springdale neighborhood, just moments from the New Canaan line. This spacious, light-filled 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial offers outstanding potential for buyers with vision. Looking for inspiration? Set on a private 1.09-acre lot, this property combines a peaceful setting with convenient access to shopping, dining, schools, parks, and transportation. Additional features include a wrap around wood deck with views of nature, an attached two-car garage and generous living space throughout. Bring your ideas and make this exceptional property your forever home! Sold "As Is."
