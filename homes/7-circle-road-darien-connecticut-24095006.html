About this home

Quintessential Cape with pool on a desirable private road in Darien. This wonderful four-bedroom, three-and-a half bath home with hardwood floors throughout, is set on a generous .84-acre yard. The first floor includes a spacious living room with fireplaced, separate formal dining room, a large eat-in kitchen and a sun-drenched family room. In addition, the first floor features a convenient separate bedroom and a full bath. The second floor offers a large primary bedroom, with a spectacular view of the yard, and an ensuite bathroom, two ample bedrooms and a full hall bath. The lower level offers a large finished recreation room and a separate office space. Entertain family and guests at your sparkling in-ground pool or on your back-yard deck. Move-in ready!
