About this home

Set atop a hill on one of Nod Hill's most coveted cul-de-sacs, this home is an elegant Connecticut estate that seamlessly blends classic architecture with refined, modern living. Some say the house has a Southern charm, others see it as a quintessential New England residence, but all agree that it is a sanctuary of hospitality and grace. Features include updated kitchen and baths, a cozy office with fireplace and built-ins, large family room, screened porch, bonus/playroom off the kitchen, and a sunlit garden room adjacent to the brick patio overlooking the level property. Primary BR suite includes full bath, walk-in closet, sitting room and deck. Additional three BR's on 2nd floor (one with brand new deck) and two additional updated baths. Walk your dog with the neighbors, visit nearby Weir Farm for a hike or art-inspiration, enjoy the legendary Nod Hill 4th of July Parade. Just 8 minutes from town and Wilton's award winning schools,18 minutes to SoNo train, 13 minutes to Ridgefield and 4 min to the Wilton Riding Club. Majestic and unique, this is one home you don't want to miss.
