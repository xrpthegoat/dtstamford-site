About this home

What if you had waited long enough to find your perfect house, and then it appeared? Crafted as the builder's own residence, every decision was made to create a home of EXTRAORDINARY QUALITY, design and lasting value. The Kitchen features 2" marble countertop on the 8' island, quartz countertops, 48" range, wine refrigerator, abundant natural light, butler's pantry/wet bar, plus a 2nd pantry. Open to the Family room with fireplace connecting to a fabulous covered porch with fireplace. And then...there are THE VIEWS! Rolling lawns on the 1.6 acre property lead to the pond waterfront for the most peaceful setting in all seasons. A mudroom with shiplap, 2nd powder room, and ample closet space keeps mess contained. Two private offices-one on the main level and one on the third floor-offer dual workspaces. The second floor includes a primary suite with two WIC's, three additional bedrooms with ensuite or jack-and-jill bath and a huge bonus/playroom. The newly finished third floor serves as a private retreat with a large sun-filled gym, 5th bedroom, full bath, and large private office for concentration above the fray. The huge 3-car garage is enhanced by a 2nd-level EV charger. Whole-house generator. This one is different--so solid and elegant, in a beautiful quiet neighborhood, and set on land that one just cannot find anymore. Don't wait!
