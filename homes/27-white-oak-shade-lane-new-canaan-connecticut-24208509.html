About this home

Set on charming White Oak Shade Lane, this spacious expanded saltbox features four-bedrooms, three full baths and offers space, privacy, and a location few properties can match. Downtown New Canaan and Darien are each a short drive away, along with convenient access to the train and parkway. Inside, generous room proportions and a practical floor plan support both comfortable living and flexible entertaining. The primary bedroom serves as a private retreat with a full bath and large closet, while three additional bedrooms offer flexibility for family, guests, or a home office all supported by a generously sized hall bath. Outside, the half-acre grounds offer a large bluestone terrace in addition to ample room to cultivate a garden, enjoy a playdate, or expand the current footprint; a canvas ready for its next owner's vision. The cul-de-sac setting invites fun neighborhood events and means minimal traffic and privacy, a combination that's increasingly hard to find in such a central location.
