About this home

Beautifully renovated and meticulously maintained 3-bedroom, 3-bath ranch situated on .63 acres, offering exceptional craftsmanship, modern upgrades, and thoughtfully designed living spaces. Completely transformed since 2020, this home features an open-concept layout, stunning renovated kitchen with updated appliances, new hardwood floors, crown molding, recessed lighting, custom closets, and all-new windows and doors. The fully finished lower level offers incredible additional living space with a playroom, office area, heated tile floors, full bathroom, custom built-ins, surround sound, and mudroom with direct access to the 2-car garage. Major mechanical upgrades include two new HVAC systems with multiple zones, Navien boiler, updated electrical, whole-house generator, advanced lighting controls, and comprehensive drainage improvements with sump pumps and French drains. Exterior enhancements include a newer roof, beautiful stone walls, Belgian block driveway, professional landscaping, irrigation, landscape lighting, and updated masonry walkways. Additional features include smart security systems, automatic water shutoff, and architectural plans for a potential expansion. Ideally located minutes from the Route 7 Connector, Merritt Parkway, I-95, South Norwalk train station, SoNo Collection, and vibrant South Norwalk restaurants and shopping. A truly turnkey home offering exceptional quality, comfort, and convenience!
