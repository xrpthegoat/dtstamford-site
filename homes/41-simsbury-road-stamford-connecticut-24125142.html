About this home

Your own private oasis on a desirable Pepper Ridge cul-de-sac! This lovingly maintained 3 Bedroom, 3 Bath Ranch offers easy, comfortable living with hardwood floors throughout the main level. A spacious Living Room with a picture window and charming fireplace flows into the formal Dining Room, while the updated granite/stainless Eat-in Kitchen makes cooking a delight. A Mudroom with side entry, Laundry, and access to the 1-Car Garage add everyday convenience. The Bedroom wing features a lovely Primary Suite with an updated marble Bath, along with two additional Bedrooms and a refreshed Full Hall Bath with jetted tub. The Lower Level offers a separate entry to a large Family Room, Office, Full Bath, Kitchenette, and Storage-a possible in-law or guest setup. Step outside and enjoy your private yard featuring a beautiful patio with brick fireplace, perfect for entertaining or relaxing. The inviting in-ground pool with new vinyl liner and adjacent gazebo provide the ultimate backyard retreat. The professionally landscaped, level yard will offer hours of fun and entertainment. All this in a fantastic location-close to shopping, schools, houses of worship, commuting, and just minutes to vibrant Downtown Stamford & Harbor Point entertainment districts!
