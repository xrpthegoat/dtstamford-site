About this home

Exceptional Four-Unit Investment Opportunity in the Heart of Norwalk! Rarely does a property like this become available. Situated on one parcel with two separate buildings,. 32 Catherine Street offers an outstanding opportunity for investors, owner-occupants, or those seeking a multigenerational living arrangement with significant rental income. The front building is a spacious two-family home featuring two oversized 3-bedroom, 1-bath apartments, each offering generous living space and strong rental appeal. Tucked behind the main residence is a separate ranch-style building with two additional units, including a 1-bedroom, 1-bath apartment and a 3-bedroom, 2-bath apartment, creating an ideal mix of unit sizes to maximize occupancy and income potential. With almost 4,000 square feet of living space (buyer to verify), this unique four-unit property provides four separate income streams and the flexibility today's buyers are looking for. Whether you're expanding your real estate portfolio, completing a 1031 exchange, or looking to offset your mortgage by living in one unit while renting the others, this property offers exceptional value. Ideally located just minutes from vibrant downtown Norwalk, Metro-North train stations, I-95, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, parks, beaches, and major employers, this is a prime investment in one of Fairfield County's strongest rental markets. Properties with four units on two separate buildings are seldom available. Don't miss out!
