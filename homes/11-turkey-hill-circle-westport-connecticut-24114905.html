About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated home nestled in the coveted Greens Farms neighborhood-offering both charm and convenience in one of Westport's most desirable locations. The home's exterior has been recently painted and features all new windows and a new roof, adding to its curb appeal and energy efficiency. Inside, the renovated kitchen is a standout feature, complete with an induction stove favored by home cooks and professional chefs alike, ample pantry space, and an eat-in area with sliders that lead to a brand-new back deck-perfect for seamless indoor-outdoor living. The home offers a thoughtfully designed layout with a spacious family room featuring cathedral ceilings, a cozy fireplace, and additional access to the back deck. The primary bedroom includes a newly renovated ensuite bath, while four additional bedrooms-one ideal for a home office-share a modern hallway bath with a double vanity. Off the main kitchen, a separate wing with its own full kitchen and private entrance is perfect for use as an in-law or au pair suite. The walk-out lower level includes a bedroom, full bath, and generous storage space, making it ideal for guests or flexible living arrangements. Enjoy the serenity of a beautifully landscaped front and back lawn. A rare rental opportunity in an unbeatable location.
