About this home

Welcome to 8 Leeds Lane, one of just six homes on a private cul-de-sac in Darien. This meticulously maintained Colonial is move-in ready and offers the perfect blend of elegance, functionality, and comfort. Step inside to find sun-filled rooms, soaring ceilings, and generous open spaces ideal for today's lifestyle. Thoughtfully updated within the past decade and in amazing condition, the home has been cared for with the utmost pride. Set on a level, beautifully landscaped property just under 3/4 of an acre, the outdoor spaces are as inviting as the interior. From lush lawns to a charming fire pit area, this home is perfect for both relaxation and entertaining. The lower level is a standout feature-boasting a large finished media/playroom along with spotless, oversized storage space that is as impressive as it is practical. Truly turnkey, this is a rare opportunity to own a home that has been updated and maintained to a very high standard, offering light, space, and quality in every corner.
