About this home

On Schedule for December completion! Stunning Farmhouse on almost 2+ serene acres. Gorgeous landscaping with begian block and stone walls. The home seamlessly blends its open spaces with a more private formal dining room and office. The bright and stunning Kitchen has a large island that is just perfect for gathering. The inviting family room adjacent to the patio features built ins and a gas fireplace for relaxing evenings. When you step outside onto the patio you immediately feel the tranquilty of the surrounding property. The yard is deal for enjoying outdoor activities. Upstairs, the primary bedroom suite is generous, complete with a luxurious closet space and an elegant bath. The wide and open hallway leads to three additional bedrooms and a convenient second-floor laundry room. The third floor offers versatility, allowing the owner to customize the space with its hardwood floors, making it perfect for any purpose. The basement has access from either end of the home and windows to provide natural light and affords even potential for recreational activities, gym, wine cellar, movie theater, youre choice! There is ample room for every need and preference in this beautifully designed colonial farmhouse. What an opportunity! (Interior Similar To Photos)
