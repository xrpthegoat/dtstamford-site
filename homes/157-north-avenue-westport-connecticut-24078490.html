About this home

Set back from the road on a 1.21-acre lot in the desirable Coleytown neighborhood of Westport, CT. This stunning colonial home provides the perfect escape. The living area flows seamlessly into the outdoors, providing an open layout filled with warmth that's ideal for relaxation and gatherings. The pool is a wonderful added outdoor feature. The home boasts attractive exterior accents while maximizing space and inviting light and bright natural elements inside. The updated kitchen provides ample counter and cabinet space, featuring a Sub-Zero refrigerator for added convenience, making meal preparation effortless, with a dining room perfect for enjoying meals together, adding to the home's inviting layout. The living room features a cozy fireplace, and the spacious family room provides additional living space for relaxation or entertainment. On the upper level, the primary bedroom has a walk-in closet and spa bath, there is also a guest room, perfect for visitors. In addition, the main floor includes a dedicated home office, providing a quiet space for work or study. This home also enjoys a convenient location close to Staples High School, Longshore, Compo Beach, and the train. Plus, it has a low flood risk, rated 1 out of 10 on RiskFactor. Don't miss your chance to make this wonderful home your own!
