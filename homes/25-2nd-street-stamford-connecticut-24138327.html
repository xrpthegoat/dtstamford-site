About this home

Situated in one of Stamford's most convenient central locations, this bright and updated townhouse offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and walkability. Just moments from Downtown Stamford, Scalzi Park, Ridgeway Shopping Center, Whole Foods, restaurants and public transportation. Seven minute drive to Stamford train station- gets you to NYC in less than an hour! This home is ideal for commuters and anyone who values easy access to everyday amenities. Inside, the main level features hardwood floors, a welcoming living room with a wood-burning fireplace and a generous private balcony-perfect for outdoor relaxation. The kitchen has been refreshed with new tile backsplash and flooring. The powder room on the main level also features a new vanity. Upstairs are two spacious bedrooms, including a primary suite with a fully renovated en-suite bathroom and a walk-in closet with built-in shelving. The hall bathroom has been updated with a new vanity, and the washer/dryer is conveniently located on the bedroom level. The entire home has been freshly painted throughout for a clean, modern, move-in-ready feel. Additional highlights include central A/C, updated lighting and ceiling fans, two assigned parking spaces, and a large private storage room in the basement. The community is well maintained with a newer roof and low common charges. Offering space, updates, and an unbeatable location-this townhouse lets you enjoy the best of Stamford living.
