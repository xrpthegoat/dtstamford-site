About this home

Welcome to Vibrant Norwalk! Experience the perfect blend of simplicity and modern living located in the Heart of Historic SoNo. The recently renovated top-floor, one-bedroom, one-bath condo is a perfect blend of sleek designer-style comfort, and accessibility. Step inside to discover soaring vaulted ceilings, over-sized windows, hardwood floors and beautiful natural southwest light throughout. A space perfect for today's active lifestyle, featuring an updated kitchen with quartz counter-tops, glass tile backsplash, high-end stainless-steel appliances, wine cooler, and custom cabinetry. The open floor plan includes a large loft for extra sleeping, office and/or storage space. Closets by Design and custom bamboo shades throughout the apartment. Steps away from all that SoNo has to offer: Public Dock on the Norwalk River, Tree-lined path to Oyster Shell Park, Restaurants, SoNo Collection Mall, Beaches, Movie Theater and much more. Newly installed W/D, fresh coat of paint throughout! Highly efficient 2024 hydro gas furnace, AC and SMART NEST thermostat ensures year-round comfort without excessive expense. The quiet Ann Street location provides a dedicated assigned attached garage parking space with remote control and visitor parking. Walk to Train, easy I-95 access.
