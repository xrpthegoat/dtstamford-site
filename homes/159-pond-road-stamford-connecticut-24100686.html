About this home

WELCOME HOME! This beautiful, lovingly and meticulously maintained five-bedroom Colonial is located in the heart of Wonderful Westover! The home has everything you could need or want. Boasts eat-in kitchen with breakfast bar plus large dining area. Large living room with fireplace, built-ins and 2 huge bay windows with fabulous views of nature and the lovely grounds! Ample dining room with bay window with even more views; large family room with fireplace and enormous window with those wonderful views again! Four bedrooms all with hardwood floors and 2 full baths complete the second floor plus an additional bedroom and full bath on first floor. Don't miss the wonderful screened-in-porch--great for hosting cocktail or dinner parties! Hardwood floors redone and entire house freshly painted. Beautiful yard, shrubs, trees and patio. Quiet and peaceful setting. Fabulous location--convenient to everything Stamford has to offer. Generator included. Move-in ready!!
