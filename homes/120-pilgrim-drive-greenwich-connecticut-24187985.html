About this home

Updated home located on a private, quiet, culdesac in the Glenville section of Greenwich. The home's interior & exterior were completely renovated in 2015. A split level which opens to stone tile in the entryway & features a custom mudroom with built-in cubbies, coat hooks, storage baskets, and a bench, offering exceptional organization. This level provides a full bath and laundry. Lower level provides additional square footage for a playroom or family room along with storage. Main level has an open floor plan including eat-in kitchen & living room space. Wood flooring throughout, updated kitchen with stainless appliances, large bay window welcoming plenty of sunlight, and a sliding door which extends the living space to the outside. Upper level has 3 bedrooms and 1 updated full bath. Enjoy the fenced in back yard with mature arborvitae for privacy and an outdoor kitchen area for entertainment. Plenty of space to unwind peacefully with a designated fire pit area matted with turf. Desirably located near shops, schools, train station, and major highways for your convenience.
