About this home

Exceptional 6-bedroom, 6-bath luxury estate in Westport, CT, built in 2008 on over 3 professionally landscaped acres. Features expansive entertaining rooms, a chef's kitchen with sunny breakfast room, sunken family room with stone hearth and built-ins, a screened-in porch, pool, spa, pool house, outdoor kitchen, tennis court, and large recreational yard. Walk-out lower level includes a gym, bar, art-room and home theater. Three home offices. Minutes from Westport's vibrant downtown, award-winning schools, and beautiful beaches.
