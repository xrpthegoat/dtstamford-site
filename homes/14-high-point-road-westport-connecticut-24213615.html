About this home

Classic Colonial 5 Bedroom 5 - 1/2 Bath set on over 1 acre with a Gunite Swimming Pool on Westport's longest and perhaps, most sought after Cul-De-Sac! Almost 4700 sq ft on 1st & 2nd floors (hardwood flooring throughout), Plus 1400 sq ft in the fully finished, daylight, walk-out lower level. On the Main level the Gracious Living Room with Fireplace & Box Bay Window, is so large it easily accommodates the grand piano. Stroll through the Cocktail Lounge with built-ins, wet-bar & wine fridge to the delightful sunny, oversized Dining Room with dozens of windows & French Doors that lead to the rear deck. Circle back to the large Eat-In Kitchen with Island that seats four and a dedicated Dining Area by the Bay Window, this all seamlessly flows into the Family Room with Fireplace, Built-In Bookshelves and French Doors opening to the rear deck with views of the pool below. Rounding out the first floor are a Home Office, Ensuite Guest Bedroom with a Full Bath and Walk-In Closet, Half Bath in hall. The 2nd floor has an Enormous Primary Suite with Spa Bath, 3 Walk-In closets & Office or Dressing Room attached to the suite. The 2nd level has 3 more spacious family bedrooms, including one with an ensuite bath and walk-in closet and two more that have ample closet space and share a hall bath. The daylight, walkout lower level has a full bath, tv/media room, gym/playroom with sink, d/w, and fridge and a bonus storage room. Generac Generator! Walls freshly painted! Newer -Roof, HVAC, Septic!
