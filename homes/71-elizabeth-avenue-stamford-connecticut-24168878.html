About this home

Tucked in the heart of Springdale, 71 Elizabeth Ave is a 3 bedroom plus office, 2.5 bath home that blends charm with thoughtful updates and an inviting backyard. The kitchen was renovated in 2022 and opens seamlessly to the main living areas, creating an easy flow for both everyday living and entertaining. Sunlight fills the home, highlighting the beautiful wood flooring and well-proportioned spaces throughout. Recent improvements include a new roof and driveway, updated HVAC, and additional upgrades that make this home truly move-in ready. The flexible layout offers space for a dedicated home office or guest area, meeting the needs of today's lifestyle. Step outside to a renovated patio and a private, manageable yard ideal for relaxing, dining, or hosting. Additional features include a Level 2 EV charger, sprinkler system, and smart home upgrades including Nest thermostat and security system. Located close to Springdale amenities, shopping, and transportation.
