About this home

Welcome to 50 Michael Road, a spacious colonial nestled in desirable North Stamford on a pretty cul-de-sac. Filled with character and light, this home has the space, layout and features you want for how we live today. Well equipped eat-in-kitchen will delight the serious home chef that features granite counters, stainless steel appliances and opens to a dining room to host all of your holiday parties. Great entertaining flow into the spacious family room with fireplace and sliders leading to private, flat backyard. Large living room, half bath, first floor laundry and ovesized pantry complete the first floor. Upstairs, gracious primary bedroom boasts walk-in closet and renovated spa-like bathroom. 3 additional bedrooms and another renovated bath add to the splendor. Need to work from home? Or extra space for playroom, media room or gym? Lower level can accomodate all of your needs. And there's more...240 amp outlet for electric car in garage, belgian block lined driveway and exterior of house just painted adds to the perfection. Prepare to be captivated and make your home ownership dreams a reality.
