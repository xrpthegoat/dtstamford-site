About this home

Welcome to this quintessential Cape Cod charmer on a serene street in Norwalk. This 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home perfectly blends timeless New England architecture with modern conveniences. The exterior is picture-perfect, with new vinyl shingle siding, black shutters, and a lush, landscaped yard. Inside, the main level features a cozy living room with a fireplace flanked by arched built-ins, adjacent to dining room space. Hardwood floors and abundant natural light create a warm, inviting atmosphere. The bright kitchen, complete with SS appliances, granite countertops, and a breakfast bar, opens to a large family room with wood burning stove, skylights, expansive windows, and door leading to a magnificent, level, fenced backyard.Two main-level bedrooms offer flexibility for a guest room or home office. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts a wall of closets and the second bedroom has ample closets and storage space. Both bathrooms have been tastefully updated with contemporary finishes.Outside, the private backyard retreat features a two-tiered deck, perfect for summer barbecues and outdoor fun. The fully fenced backyard with a lush lawn is surrounded by colorful mature plantings. Basement offers lots of storage space. Located minutes from schools, parks, shopping, and dining, with easy access to highways and public transportation. This meticulously maintained move-in-ready home offers the perfect blend of old-world charm and modern conveniences.
