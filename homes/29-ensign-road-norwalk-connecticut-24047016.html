About this home

Welcome to your own little oasis at Sea Glass Cottage. This artists retreat features the best of Rowayton's seaside, Pine Point community, all year long. This three bedroom, two bath home is the perfect place to unwind. With water views on the back deck and bird watching in the front yard enclave, you will surely enjoy natures offerings. This community is not your traditional summer beach community. Less than an hour from NYC and a train station, restaurants, town, paddle courts, pickle ball courts and more are less than a mile away. There are activities and events throughout the year for all ages and interests.
