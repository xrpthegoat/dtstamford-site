About this home

Resort-style living meets timeless elegance in this completely renovated five-bedroom Modern Colonial set on a spectacular North Stamford property. Meticulously maintained and reimagined with exceptional craftsmanship, this turnkey home showcases soaring ceilings, sun-filled living spaces, designer finishes, and the finest materials throughout. The stunning chef's kitchen flows seamlessly into beautifully appointed living and entertaining spaces, while luxurious modern baths-including a spa-inspired primary suite retreat-offer comfort and sophistication. With five bedrooms, three full baths, three half baths, and a finished lower level, the home provides exceptional space for everyday living and entertaining. Outside, discover your own private resort. The beautifully landscaped, fully fenced property features a sparkling pool and spa, pickleball and basketball courts, an outdoor kitchen pavilion, landscape lighting, fire pit, irrigation system, and expansive areas for relaxing or gathering with family and friends. Every detail has been thoughtfully designed to create an unparalleled indoor-outdoor lifestyle. Perfectly blending luxury, recreation, and privacy, this extraordinary North Stamford home is truly move-in ready and offers the ultimate staycation experience-every day feels like a vacation.
