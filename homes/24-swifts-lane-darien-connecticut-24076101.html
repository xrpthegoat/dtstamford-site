About this home

Discover timeless elegance in this wonderful colonial nestled on 1.39 acres on a desirable private street in the Hindley district. With nearly 4000 sf of living space, this home marries historic charm with modern updates including kitchen and baths. Great flow between rooms, with sunny and well sized spaces throughout, the home has delightful views of the expansive property. Featuring a large entry hall, office with built ins, dining room off the updated white kitchen with high end appliances, spacious family room with fireplace and the most incredible great room with fireplace and vaulted ceiling not to be missed! Five bedrooms and three full baths offer room for everyone. With close proximity to Great Island, restaurants, shops and train, this is the residence with charm, character and lovingly taken care of by the owners for the past thirty years! --
