About this home

Set on a level, corner acre-plus lot in the historic Silvermine neighborhood-bordering New Canaan-this fully renovated Raised Ranch offers elevated living in a timeless setting. Ideally located just minutes from Tavern at GrayBarns, The Mercantile, Silvermine Art Guild, and Silvermine Elementary School, the home blends everyday convenience with neighborhood charm. Designed for effortless family living and entertaining, the main level features a sun-filled open floor plan where the living room, dining area, and renovated kitchen flow seamlessly together. The kitchen offers quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances, with sliders opening to an expansive deck overlooking manicured, private grounds-ideal for gatherings or quiet evenings at home. The spacious primary suite provides a serene retreat with fresh paint & a newly renovated bath with walk-in shower stall. Two additional bedrooms + a full hall bath complete the main level. The lower level adds versatility with a generous family or recreation room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace & sliders to the backyard. A fourth bedroom and full bath offer flexible space for guests, home office, or extended living. Direct access to the two-car attached garage & ample storage enhance daily convenience. Many recent improvements include a freshly painted interior and exterior, newly renovated primary bathroom, and a newly paved driveway-making this move-in-ready home an exceptional offering in a sought after neighborhood.
