About this home

Venture X Greenwich offers 20,000 sq. ft. of professional office space for rent at 200 Pemberwick Road. With 74 private offices, one conference room, a stylish cafe, and secure 24/7 access, you'll get the privacy and flexibility to work on your own terms. Our flexible office rentals range from solo suites to six-person team spaces, all thoughtfully designed for focus and convenience. Each office comes with high-speed Wi-Fi and access to shared amenities, and is located in a serene riverside building featuring a waterfall and internal skybridge. We're just five minutes from Greenwich Avenue and minutes from Merritt Parkway, I-95, and a nearby airport. With plenty of parking and an in-house gym, Venture X Greenwich delivers comfort, convenience, and professionalism all in one. If you're ready for a space that helps your business grow, we're ready for you.
