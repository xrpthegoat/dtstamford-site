About this home

NEWLY RENOVATED!!! Welcome to your dream summer rental. Gorgeous spacious home situated at the end of a long private driveway in a fully fenced professionally exquisite landscaped property on 2.14 level acres with pool and spa. This beautiful colonial with a dramatic two story foyer, new light natural hardwood floors, high ceilings, custom millwork & beautiful details is finished on 4 levels. 2 Home Offices with extensive built-ins is perfect to work remotely. Formal Living Room with fireplace flows into the banquet style formal Dining Room. Entertain with ease in the fully equipped modern Eat-in Kitchen complete with high end appliances including a wolf range, subzero refrigerator, 2 dishwashers and a huge marble island. Family Room with fireplace and french doors leading out to the patio. The Game Room is the perfect place to host with its stunning bar and pool table. Bluestone patio provides relaxed living and easy entertaining. Elegant Primary Bedroom with dual walk in closets and 2 luxe spa Bathrooms and a steam shower. Second floor has 3 more ensuite Bedrooms. The 3rd floor has a media room , work out area including free weights and an elliptical as well as a full bath. The massive patio features a built in grill, seating and eating areas, great for outdoor entertaining. Heated Pool House with Kitchenette, full bath and an extra Bedroom. Full house generator. Your own putting green. Fabulous opportunity to enjoy summer in Westport at its best!!!
