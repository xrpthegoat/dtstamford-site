About this home

Welcome to your recently renovated 2 bedroom, 1 bathroom apartment in this charming, impeccably maintained, multi-family home offering comfort and style in a prime location. Owned and occupied by the same family for decades (landlord lives on first floor). Step inside and be greeted by the stunning hardwood floors that run throughout the living room and both bedrooms. The updated eat-in kitchen is sure to impress with its stainless steel farmhouse sink, refrigerator, microwave, range and dishwasher. Please note: no laundry on site 1 driveway space plenty of street parking as well as a clean laundromat one block away. You will love the fantastic location that is within walking distance of all Springdale Village attractions. Shops, restaurants and entertainment just moments away. Don't miss your chance to make this your new home! Freshly painted and move in ready. No pets, smoking or vaping.
