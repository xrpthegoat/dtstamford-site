About this home

This wonderful custom built home is nestled in the heart of historic Silvermine Village within short stroll to Silvermine Art Guild, Silvermine country market where you can enjoy breakfast or lunch, Grey Barn Tavern and Inn and Mercantile coffee and pastry shop. Setting peacefully at the end of serene cut-de-sac on a beautiful one acre property. Inside you will enjoy spacious open floor plan offers beautiful hardwood floors, crown moldings, wainscoting in formal dining room, new Marvin windows and wood doors thru out. Spacious Living room with custom stone FPL, Eat-in kitchen with stainless appliances, 2 spacious bedrooms on the first floor and an updated full bathroom. 2nd floor consist of two bedrooms plus full bathroom and an additional office ideal for work at home situation. Full walk out basement with finished playroom or exercise room. Enjoy summer evenings on your three seasons screened in porch surrounded by natures tranquil beautiful sound and surroundings. Adding a bottle bubbly would be a wonderful addition! Additional brick patio perfect for summer barbecue gatherings with friends. This home is truly a sanctuary of serenity! This amazing home is minutes to New Canaan, Wilton and all the amenities that Norwalk has to offer.
