About this home

Well-maintained two-family home located on West Avenue, offering good condition throughout. Renovated in 2017, the first-floor unit features two bedrooms, an updated kitchen, a spacious living room, basement laundry, and one-car garage. The second unit offers a large eat-in kitchen, living room, two bedrooms, and an additional room on the third floor that can be used as a potential third bedroom. Both units are currently tenant-occupied on month-to-month leases. Tenants pay their own utilities. The exterior includes a one-car attached garage and additional off-street parking with an expanded paved driveway. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Stamford, train stations, parks, shopping, and dining. Schedule your showing today.
