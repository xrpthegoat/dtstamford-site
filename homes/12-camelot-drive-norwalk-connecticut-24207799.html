About this home

Beautifully updated corner-unit townhouse in the desirable Oak Hill Condominium community on a quiet West Norwalk cul-de-sac. This move-in-ready home offers stunning sunrise and pool views, abundant natural light, and modern upgrades throughout. The renovated kitchen features white shaker cabinets, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a powder room. Hardwood floors extend through the open living and dining areas, with sliding doors leading to a spacious deck overlooking the pool and landscaped grounds. Upstairs are two generously sized bedrooms with custom built-in storage and a renovated full bath featuring heated stone tile floors. The lower level offers a flexible heated-floor bonus room ideal for a home office, gym, playroom, or guest space, plus ample storage and patio access. Additional highlights include an attached garage, newer washer and dryer, brand-new windows with a 20-year warranty, new central A/C, and easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, parks, and Oak Hills Golf Course.
