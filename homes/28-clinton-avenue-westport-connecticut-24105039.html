About this home

Exceptional, remodeled home with a beautiful pool near downtown Westport. The "Everett Fillow House, c 1911" was completely restored & exquisitely updated by the present owner to the highest standards of excellence. The welcoming curb appeal & charming front porch create a gracious approach to this Colonial Farmhouse. Step inside and experience the bright open flow, characterized by high ceilings, large windows, & intuitive custom features for a comfortable lifestyle. The Great Room includes a fireplace in a bay of windows, & opens to a serving bar/butler's pantry space w/ice machine, 2 refrigerator drawers & excellent storage. The gourmet kitchen is a chef's delight, w/extensive cabinetry, gas cooktop & top appliances, a glorious view out to the pool & herringbone-patterned backsplash to the ceiling that adds a stunning accent. The dining area has a built-in marble-topped serving cabinet, & French doors to the deck. Main level mudroom/laundry leads to the attached 2-car garage & out to the backyard. The second level includes a flexible layout w/spacious bedrooms. The main BR suite is an exquisite retreat w/fireplace & bay window, complemented by a stunning primary bath w/marble, glass shower & custom vanity. The finished third floor is a great bonus space, lined w/windows & filled w/light - it includes a clever hidden daybed for extra guests & a refreshment center. The magnificent backyard has a stunning pool with patios, a firepit & level lawns. Full-house Generator.
