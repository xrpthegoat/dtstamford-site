About this home

Vintage Charm Meets Modern Living! This sunny Glenbrook Colonial blends classic character with today's updates - and it's ready to impress! The main floor is bright and welcoming, featuring beautiful hardwood floors, custom moldings, a cozy living room that opens to a formal dining room with a sunny bay window, and a modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a pass thru window into the dining room. Convenient mudroom w/built in cubbies, a stylish half bath, & a heated front porch that's perfect for year-round lounging or bonus/office space. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms, an updated full bath, & an oversized walk-in hall closet. Need more space? The large unfinished basement offers a workshop space, laundry area and is just waiting for your personal touch - think gym, office, playroom, you name it! Outside, enjoy the low-maintenance composite deck & a lush, private backyard with mature plantings, a privacy hedge, and space to grow your own garden. Exterior freshly painted, 200 AMP electric service, efficient gas heating, a detached one-car garage sits on a quiet dead-end street, all on a prime corner lot located in the heart of the desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. This commuter's dream is just a short walk to the Glenbrook train station, shops & restaurants on Hope St, Stark elementary school, downtown Stamford, and more. Whether you're starting out, sizing down, or anything in between-this move-in-ready home offers the perfect blend of charm & updates.
