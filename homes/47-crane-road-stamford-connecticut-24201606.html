About this home

Tucked along a quiet residential street, 47 Crane Road offers an inviting combination of character, space and thoughtful updates. Hardwood floors flow throughout much of the main level, which features two bedrooms and a full bath, dining area with built-ins, an updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a light-filled family room with vaulted ceiling, fireplace and sliders opening to the expansive rear deck. The recently renovated second-floor primary suite is a true retreat, offering exceptional space, two generous walk-in closets and a beautifully appointed bath with heated floors, double vanity and oversized shower. The finished lower level adds valuable flexibility for a rec room, home office, gym, playroom or media space. Outdoors, the large deck overlooks a peaceful backyard surrounded by mature trees and established gardens that welcome butterflies, songbirds and hummingbirds throughout the seasons. A detached garage adds parking and storage. Comfortable, versatile and filled with natural light, this special Stamford home offers room to live, work, entertain and unwind.
