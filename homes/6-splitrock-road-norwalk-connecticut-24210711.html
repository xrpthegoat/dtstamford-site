About this home

Nestled in Norwalk's sought after Village Creek neighborhood, 6 Splitrock Road offers a wonderful combination of privacy, coastal charm, and effortless everyday living. Thoughtfully remodeled by the current owners, this beautiful three bedroom home features light-filled interiors and an inviting, open concept main level. The spacious primary suite offers a beautifully updated en suite bathroom, while two additional bedrooms share a well-appointed hall bath. The walk-out lower level provides exceptional flexibility, featuring a bright and spacious recreation room along with a separate room ideal for a home office, guest accommodations, or additional living space. Set in a peaceful, private setting, the property enjoys access to the exceptional amenities of the Village Creek Association, including a private beach, marina, tennis courts, playground, dog park, and recreational open space. Enjoy the best of coastal living in a welcoming waterfront community, all while being conveniently located near shopping, dining, and everything Norwalk and the surrounding towns have to offer. A special opportunity to enjoy a beautifully updated home in one of Norwalk's most charming coastal enclaves.
