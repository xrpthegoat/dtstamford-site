About this home

This downtown gem offers beautifully proportioned living spaces with 4 bedrooms and 4 full baths, providing flexibility for a variety of lifestyles and living arrangements. The main level layout offers the option of 3 or 4 bedrooms, while the lower level can serve as either a private ensuite bedroom or an additional family room to suit a variety of options. The well appointed kitchen flows seamlessly to the family room and dining room, creating a natural hub for gatherings and daily living. The dining room offers direct access to the patio and backyard, allowing for effortless indoor-outdoor flow. A separate private office is both functional and inviting, featuring two built-in desks and a fireplace. Throughout the home, expansive windows frame lush green landscaping and bathes each room with beautiful natural light. The level, fully fenced backyard is surrounded by mature gardens and offers an ideal setting for relaxation, play, and entertaining. Thoughtfully designed with a flexible floor plan that lives large, this home combines comfort, functionality, and timeless appeal in an exceptional downtown location.
