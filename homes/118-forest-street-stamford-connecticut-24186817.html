About this home

Introducing 118 Forest Street at Forest Mews, a beautifully appointed brick end-unit townhouse in the heart of Downtown Stamford, offering the feel of a classic city brownstone with the ease of modern living. Freshly painted and filled with natural light, this inviting home features warm architectural character, a fireplace, generous storage, sliders to balconies off both the living room and primary bedroom, and a private patio garden that creates a rare outdoor retreat in a vibrant downtown setting. An attached one-car garage, abundant visitor parking, and low common charges add exceptional convenience. Just moments from Stamford's restaurants, shops, parks, entertainment, Metro-North, and less than one hour from NYC, this elegant townhouse offers a polished urban lifestyle in one of Fairfield County's most dynamic coastal cities, with comfort, privacy, and ease.
