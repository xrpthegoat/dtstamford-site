About this home

Beautifully expanded Glenbrook cape in move-in condition! The airy, updated kitchen with white cabinetry, granite counter tops, stainless appliances and huge walk-in pantry is open to a bright dining room and family room perfect for entertaining and family activities. Sliders from the kitchen and family room lead to a welcoming deck with cable railing, and to the huge, private, level, fully-fenced back yard with patio, caged veggie garden that can be easily turned into a swing-set area, and lovingly tended perennial plantings. The front door opens to a formal living room with wood-burning fireplace and charming built-in cabinetry. The FIRST FLOOR PRIMARY BEDROOM boasts an en-suite bath w/ double vanities & walk-in shower and a large walk-in closet. There is 1 additional updated full bath with tub/shower on the first floor. On the second floor you'll find two additional generous sized bedrooms and a newly renovated full bath with tub/shower. The laundry is on the main floor, in the mud room between the attached 1 car garage and the house. Plenty of storage; central air and gas heat. New roof 2024, new furnace 2013; new siding in 2004. Sought-after location - walking distance to Metro-North Glenbrook train station, minutes to shopping and dining in Glenbrook and just a 5-minute drive to downtown Stamford.
