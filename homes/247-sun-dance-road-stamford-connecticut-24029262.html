About this home

Welcome to 247 Sundance Road! Fabulous Colonial in the Mid Ridges of Stamford - located in an idyllic neighborhood welcoming all. The home boasts 3000+sqft, 4 full second floor bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms. The lovely curb appeal of this home welcomes you with a manicured irrigated front lawn and Belgium block lined drive and walkway. Upon entry, the dining room and lowkey den/fitness center and wine cellar draw you to the immaculate and functional kitchen that only a chef would inspire and curate. The large family room and adjacent kitchen radiate a cool casual elegance with wood burning fireplace and access to the deck and patio at ground level connecting you with the beautiful and private fenced back yard. Half bath, pantry, laundry, 2 car garage complete the main level. The upper level is defined by the 3 full well sized bedrooms and the exceptional Primary Bedroom with office/salon entryway, dual walk-in closets, and palatial main bath. Central air, gas heat, and on-demand hot water define conscience economy and comfort. BRAND NEW GAF ROOF. Situated between the Merritt Parkway and I95 - this home is a commuter's dream. Moments from Vibrant Downtown Stamford Restaurants, Nightlife, Shops, and upper High Ridge shops and Trader Joe's. Close proximity to the Stamford Nature Center and peaceful gardens and trails of the Bartlett Arboretum. A short and direct 4 Miles to the Stamford Train Station - 50 min express to Grand Central Terminal. Make this gorgeous home yours today!
