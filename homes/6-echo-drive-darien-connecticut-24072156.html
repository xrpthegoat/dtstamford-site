About this home

Nestled in one of Darien's most sought-after neighborhoods, this beautifully renovated Colonial seamlessly blends classic charm with modern updates. Boasting five spacious bedrooms and three thoughtfully designed bathrooms, this home provides an ideal setting for both family living and entertaining. The owners have meticulously enhanced the home's timeless appeal with contemporary upgrades, ensuring every space is both functional and stylish. The first-floor renovation includes refinished hardwood floors, a refreshed open kitchen with quartz countertops, new hardware, and a remodeled breakfast nook-perfect for gathering over meals. The family room now features built-in shelving, updated lighting, and an open-concept layout that maximizes natural light. Both the primary and first-floor bathrooms have been completely remodeled with new tiling, fixtures, and elegant wallpaper. Additional improvements include fresh interior and exterior paint, refreshed landscaping with newly planted trees and lush greenery, and much more. Adding to the home's appeal is an outdoor shower-an ideal feature for rinsing off after enjoying Darien's beautiful beaches. The spacious backyard is perfect for outdoor activities, gardening, or simply unwinding in a serene setting. Situated on a picturesque street, this home is conveniently located near top-rated schools, local amenities, and all that Darien has to offer. Don't miss the opportunity to make this exceptional property your own!
