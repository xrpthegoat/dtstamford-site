About this home

Welcome to this enchanting home & cottage set on 3 beautiful, level acres on one of Wilton's nicest streets! The spacious main home was built in the 1890s & has been updated for modern living. It features 3BD w/ a 4th versatile room that can be used as a bedroom or a den, along with 3.5BA, as well as a separate 2BD cottage. This home includes a large kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar, & wide plank floors that flow seamlessly into the generously sized dining room. The inviting living room features custom built-ins, a cozy fireplace, & large windows that fill the space with natural light. The home also includes an office with a fireplace, providing a warm and private space for working from home. The large sunroom, a standout feature, offers vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, hardwood floors, abundant windows, and direct access to the outdoors - creating a bright and airy space perfect for relaxing or entertaining year-round. The primary suite offers abundant closet & storage space & is complemented by a large primary bath featuring both a tub & separate shower. Two additional bedrooms share a Jack-and-Jill bathroom. Outside, the property includes a blue stone patio, expansive grounds with mature plantings, a pond & an in-ground pool. A two-car attached garage adds everyday convenience. The light-filled cottage offers wonderful flexibility for extended family with 2BR, 1BA & 864sf of additional living space plus a balcony. This property has it all!
