About this home

Experience a zen-like ambiance in this Frank Lloyd Wright-inspired contemporary home, designed to seamlessly bring the outdoors in. Situated on 1 acre at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac in sought-after Revonah Woods, this 3-bedroom, 3-bath gem was recently renovated to showcase an open floor plan, wide plank hardwood floors, and energy-efficient windows and doors that flood the space with natural light. The entryway greets you with a custom-designed staircase in a striking three-story tower. The open-concept living room features an energy-efficient wood-burning fireplace, large windows, and four glass sliders, bringing the outdoors in. It's the perfect setting for both peaceful daily living and entertaining guests. The main floor also includes a spacious family room, dining area, and a chef's kitchen equipped with sleek stainless-steel appliances and generous storage. An office/den or guest room with a full bath adds flexibility to the space. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite features a walk-in closet and a spa-like ensuite bath with a stand-alone tub in a wet room. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a laundry room complete the second level. Outside, you'll find an entertainer's paradise with a large side yard, a stunning garden with a koi pond, cascading waterfalls, and a gas grill plumbed directly to the house. Enjoy the serenity of this tucked-away retreat, while still being conveniently located to downtown.
