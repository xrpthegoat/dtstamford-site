About this home

HIGHEST and BEST DUE TUESDAY at 5 pm First time ever on market. This light-filled colonial sits high on a prime lot with views on beautiful Keelers Ridge. Custom-built by Kellogg Brothers, this home has 3 fireplaces and exceptional craftsmanship throughout. The property is open, sunny, and features a beautiful pool, outdoor living area, and expansive lawns. The expanded eat-in-kitchen has two islands, a 48" Thermadore gas range, double wall ovens,, and more cabinets than you will need. The kitchen is open to family room with fireplace and a sunroom with cathedral ceiling that opens to outdoor living area. The living room is comfortable, yet has a timeless elegance with fireplace, built-ins, beautiful windows and also opens to outdoor oasis. The dining room has floor to ceiling windows and a beautiful built-in. There is a paneled, tucked-away office with a new full bath and a rec room over the garage, in addition to a sunroom with new full bath convenient to the pool. Upstairs is a luxurious primary suite with spa bath, fireplace, and walk-in closet to relax in at the end of a long day. There are 3 more spacious bedrooms and an extra large hall bath. The lower level has 1500 sq ft that is easily finished. This elegant, comfortable home with a special backyard oasis is located close to Quarry Head Park with scenic hiking trails and a neighborhood great for walking, biking, running, and socializing. This home has it all. You will love living here!
