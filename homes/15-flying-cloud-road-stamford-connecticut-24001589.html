About this home

Direct waterfront in coveted Dolphin Cove. 2004 construction and FEMA compliant colonial with Water Views from every room. Light filled open floor-plan with 9 ft plus ceilings and gracious entertaining spaces inside and out. Stone terrace with built-in grill and separate dinning and lounging areas. Primary suite with gas fireplace, balcony overlooking the harbor, generous walk-in closets and a luxury bath with steam shower and separate tub. Four additional guest bedrooms, all with ensuite baths. Third floor Gym. Private deep-water dock slip behind the home. Dolphin Cove amenities include heated pools, tennis and pickle ball, Cabana and Clubhouse and private beach. Total association dues are $3150/year.
