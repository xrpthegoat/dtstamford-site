About this home

Discover the charm of this mid-century modern home, bathed in natural sunlight thanks to large windows that frame stunning views of the lush landscaping and nature outside. The updated kitchen boasts ample storage, including an updated laundry room/mudroom. The dining room features a wall of closets, perfect for storing linens and fine china. As you enter, you're welcomed by an open living room with floor-to-ceiling windows and a striking stone fireplace. Behind the fireplace, a cozy nook offers an ideal space for a home office. The newly paved driveway provides easy access to the property, adding to the home's private setting. With everything conveniently located on one floor, this home embodies the timeless elegance and functionality of mid-century modern design.
