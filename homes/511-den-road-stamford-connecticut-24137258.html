About this home

Brand-new construction at 511 Den Road offers refined modern living in a peaceful North Stamford setting on 1.09 private acres. This thoughtfully designed 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home features an open-concept layout with hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and a striking living area centered around the fireplace, flowing seamlessly into a chef's kitchen with an oversized island, quartz countertops, custom cabinetry and a butler's pantry-ideal for effortless entertaining. Oversized Marvin windows flood the home with natural light and frame tranquil wooded views, while sliders open to a spacious deck overlooking the private backyard. The luxurious primary suite delivers a spa-like retreat with a soaking tub, double vanity, and elegant finishes. A large mudroom off the 2-car attached garage adds everyday functionality and includes an EV car charger, while a dedicated home office provides the perfect work-from-home setup. Built for comfort and efficiency, the home features spray foam insulation, Hardie board siding, central air, energy-efficient systems, and a walk-out lower level, offering the perfect balance of privacy, luxury, and convenience just minutes from nature preserves, dining, downtown Stamford, and major commuter routes.
