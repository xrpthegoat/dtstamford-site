About this home

Nestled in the heart of Westport, 495 Main Street offers an amazing opportunity for those looking to move in, add on or build new in a prime location. Lovingly cared for by the same family for over 60 years, this home is situated in one of Connecticut's most sought-after coastal towns, known for its scenic landscapes, vibrant arts scene, and top-tier amenities. Westport is celebrated for its picturesque beaches and its proximity to New York City, making it an ideal location for commuters seeking a tranquil yet convenient lifestyle. The layout of this home provides generous living space on one level. Each room has ample natural light adding an inviting warmth to a well loved home. Boasting 3 bedrooms, a well sized eat-in-kitchen for entertaining and a spacious living room which opens to a sunroom that overlooks the perfectly level yard. 495 Main Street offers a unique opportunity to enjoy all that the area has to offer. A beautiful home in an unbeatable location! Don't miss your chance to own a piece of Westport's charm.
