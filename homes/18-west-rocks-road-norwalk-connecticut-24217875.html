About this home

Unique multi-unit property in Norwalk's desirable West Rocks area, ideally situated just moments from Route 7, the Wall Street district, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, with an abundance of nearby shopping and dining. This versatile property features three units, each with its own utilities, offering excellent flexibility for investors or owner-occupants alike. The front single family home features 2 bedrooms and 1 full bath with washer/dryer. The rear building offers two additional apartments, each with the potential to be configured as a one- or two-bedroom unit. Three garage bays beneath the rear building are currently utilized for storage, while ample off-street parking accommodates approximately 5-6 vehicles. Live in one unit while generating rental income from the others, or capitalize on the property's potential as an investment opportunity. Property to be sold as-is.
