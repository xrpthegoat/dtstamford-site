About this home

The landscape is every bit as memorable as the home itself. Set on 1.2 breathtaking acres on a quiet cul-de-sac, this exceptional property is framed by magnificent specimen trees, flowering shrubs, and beautifully established gardens, creating extraordinary privacy and year-round beauty. Just moments from town, schools, and the park, the setting offers the perfect balance of tranquility and convenience. Inside, a glass-enclosed sunroom with skylights and a wood-burning fireplace invites you to relax while enjoying the ever-changing landscape. Designed for comfortable everyday living and effortless entertaining, the home features a gracious living room with a fireplace, a warm and inviting den with custom built-ins, an eat-in kitchen, and a formal dining room. The three-bedroom home also offers exciting future potential, with the property having been perked for a possible expansion to a five-bedroom residence, subject to all applicable approvals. Seller has thoughtfully priced the home to reflect current condition, presenting exceptional opportunity for the next person to personalize and add value.
