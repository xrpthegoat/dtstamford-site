About this home

Well maintained, delightful 3 BR 2 full Bath Cape with hardwood floors set in a great Cove neighborhood where you can walk to the beach, Cove Island and is close to Chelsea Piers. Living Room and Kitchen combination offers an open floor plan with skylight and recessed lighting. Welcoming Granite Kitchen features a breakfast bar and has access to the deck - great for outdoor entertaining in the professionally landscaped and fenced backyard. Primary Bedroom on the main level has sliders to expansive deck. Full Hall Bath. 2 Additional Bedrooms on the Upper Level, both with skylights and recessed lighting which makes them bright and inviting. Large hall closet. 3 Separate rooms in the nicely finished basement offers different possibilities - family room, with another separate room which could be an office, possible another bedroom, or gym plus a storage room - all with tiled floors. Full bath. Separate Laundry area has door to the backyard. Features include New gas furnace, Central A/C and Automatic full house Generator. Parking for at least 4-5 cars. This lovely home is ready for you to just move in.
