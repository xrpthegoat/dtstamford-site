About this home

Beautifully updated 4 level townhouse tucked away in a quiet residential setting just minutes from all the restaurants, shopping, and nightlife of downtown Stamford. Bright, sunny and move-in ready, this spacious home offers an open floorplan, hardwood floors in 3 of the levels, and 3 fully renovated stunning full bathrooms featuring custom tile work. Renovated kitchen opens seamlessly into the dining room and sunken living room, highlighted by a cozy corner wood-burning fireplace and sliders leading to a large, partial covered, private deck perfect for entertaining or relaxing outdoors. Dining room features a stylish mid-century modern railing overlooking the living space below. 2nd floor offers a spacious primary suite complete with a private balcony and stunning spa-like bath w/ custom tile and glass stall shower. 2nd large bedroom, beautifully renovated full bath, and convenient washer/dryer complete this level. 3rd floor features oversized 3rd bedroom w/ vaulted ceiling and large storage closet. Lower level offers incredible flex space ideal for 4th bedroom, family room, gym, home office, plus full bath with rain shower head, custom tile, glass enclosure, and exterior access to an additional deck space. Complex includes 1 car assigned parking, visitor spaces and inground pool for summer fun. Exceptional space, updates, and location make this a must-see, incredible value for the money!!
