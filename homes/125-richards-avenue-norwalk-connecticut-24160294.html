About this home

JUST LISTED!! The Cottages at Richards Avenue offers new, modern construction in a luxury, gated community of single family homes (attached at the garage) with full first floor living. An adult community of 10 homes (5 bldgs) where at least one occupant must be 60 or over. This 3 Bdrm, 2-1/2 Bath Home features an open floor plan with gourmet kitchen expanding into dining room/living room/desk area and sliders to private screened in porch. 1st floor master suite w/ luxurious bath & spacious walk-in closet, private garage, mudroom/laundry. Options for a diverse array of lifestyles. Community gazebo overlooking the river. Located on the Norwalk/Darien border w/beautiful views of 5 Mile River.
