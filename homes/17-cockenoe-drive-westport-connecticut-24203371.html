About this home

Live where the beach is your front yard! This five-bedroom waterfront home on sought-after Saugatuck Island offers front-row, unobstructed views of the private beach and Long Island Sound. Spend your days swimming, boating, paddleboarding or simply relaxing by the water, then head out to enjoy Westport's fabulous restaurants and shops just minutes away. Conveniently located near the train station for an easy NYC commute, this is the quintessential coastal rental for those who love being by the water. Flexible rental terms. Summer rent is 25K for each of July and August.
