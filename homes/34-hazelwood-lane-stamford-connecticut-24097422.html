About this home

Nestled on a quiet, tree-lined street, this pristine home welcomes you with gleaming hardwood floors and a sunlit, open layout. The chef's kitchen features stainless-steel appliances, an induction stove, granite counters, abundant cabinetry and a spacious island ideal for morning coffee or evening gatherings. Adjacent dining and living areas, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, flow into the family room with vaulted ceilings that opens to a private patio and lush backyard retreat. A tucked-away home office offers additional space and garden views. The main-level primary suite includes a walk-in closet and a spa like bath with glass shower and marble accents. Two more bedrooms share a well-appointed full bath, and a main level laundry room adds daily ease. The expansive lower level easily adapts to a media room, gym or play space, and an attached one-car garage with direct entry completes the home. Moments from shops, parks and dining, this Pepper Ridge treasure blends comfort and modern style.
