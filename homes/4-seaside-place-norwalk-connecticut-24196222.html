About this home

Rare direct waterfront opportunity on a private road in the heart of East Norwalk. This meticulously maintained Nantucket-style home offers deep-water frontage with a private dock featuring water and 30-amp power, providing effortless access to Long Island Sound and beyond. Designed for today's lifestyle and tomorrow's needs, the home features a sought-after main-level primary suite, making it ideal as your forever home. Sun-filled living spaces showcase hardwood floors, spectacular water views, and a dedicated home office with fiber optic service. The kitchen is appointed with granite countertops, custom cherry cabinetry, high-end stainless steel appliances. An original cedar closet reflects the home's timeless craftsmanship. Outdoor living is exceptional with a covered lanai, natural stone patios, paved walkways, lush gardens, and an eight-zone irrigation system, creating a beautiful, low-maintenance waterfront setting. Additional amenities include central air, natural gas fireplace and grill, two-car garage, partially finished lower level with abundant storage, natural gas, public water, and public sewer. Located just minutes from beaches, marinas, restaurants, shopping, and Metro-North, this exceptional property offers the perfect blend of privacy, luxury, and waterfront living. A rare opportunity to own one of East Norwalk's premier deep-water homes.
