About this home

Welcome to this enchanting 3-4 bedroom, 2-bath home nestled on .18 acres on a quiet street in the heart of Riverside. This story-book residence boasts a spacious, beamed living room with a brick fireplace transitioning seamlessly to a sunlit family room, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Adjacent is a welcoming dining area with a slider to the back patio, creating a layout ideal for gatherings. Upstairs, discover three bedrooms and a recently updated full bath. The lower level provides versatility with a bright fourth bedroom or office space, a second recently updated full bathroom, a playroom for family fun, and a sizable laundry room for added convenience. Outside, enjoy the fenced-in level backyard adorned with gardens that bloom throughout the seasons.
