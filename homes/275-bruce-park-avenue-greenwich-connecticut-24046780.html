About this home

Walking distance to the train, Greenwich Avenue and Bruce Park, this 4 bedroom home is a must see! Nestled among several newly built homes on the street, this property is ideal for entertaining. Sunshine pours into the living room from three exposures and fills this happy home all the way to the spacious outdoor deck overlooking the wonderfully private backyard. With extra height ceilings in the upstairs bedrooms, hardwood floors throughout, and multiple rooms for office space, nursery, exercise or closets, this home offers maximum optionality. Don't miss the finished lower level with an additional 437 square feet with exterior access complete with a full bath, additional washer/dryer, kitchen sink and a covered porch. Come see this downtown gem before it's gone!
