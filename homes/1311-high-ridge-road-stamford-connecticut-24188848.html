About this home

Unlock incredible value with this classic 1940s colonial perfectly situated in desirable North Stamford. Serving as a fantastic canvas for both handy homebuyers and savvy investors, this 4-bedroom, 3-full-bath property is bursting with character and value-add potential. Step inside to discover beautiful hardwood floors, a living room anchored by a wood-burning stove and handsome built-ins, and a spacious eat-in kitchen that flows seamlessly onto a large deck overlooking a peaceful backyard. Maximizing the home's footprint is easy, thanks to a sprawling second-floor bonus room-the perfect layout for an in-law suite-and a walk-out lower level featuring a massive family room. With peace-of-mind upgrades like a 7-year-old roof and a 4-year-old hot water heater already in place, the home is primed for its next chapter. Located just north of the Merritt Parkway for a commuter's dream, and moments from local treasures like the Stamford Museum & Nature Center and the Bartlett Arboretum, this property is a rare find with extraordinary upside.
