About this home

Storybook Saltbox Colonial in North Stamford Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac, this enchanting 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath saltbox colonial blends timeless character with thoughtful updates. Lovingly maintained, the home welcomes you with formal living and dining rooms that flow seamlessly into a beautifully remodeled kitchen featuring a granite island, stainless steel appliances, and a cozy family room with soaring cathedral ceilings. French doors open to an expansive wraparound deck overlooking a picturesque, level, fenced-in yard-ideal for entertaining or peaceful retreat on 1.65 Acres. Upstairs, you'll find four generously sized bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with cathedral ceilings, a full ensuite bath, and tranquil views of the lush backyard. The full basement offers flexible storage space and access to a rare 4-car-plus garage. With a brand-new roof, central A/C, and a location that combines privacy with convenience, this home truly checks every box.
