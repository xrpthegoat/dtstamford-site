About this home

Completely renovated down to the studs with high-end finishes throughout, 36 Carriage Drive is a 6-bedroom, 3.5-bath ranch with 5,360 sq ft on a flat, level acre located in the desirable Westover neighborhood of Stamford. A wide ipe front deck connects the two wings of the house and leads into a bright main level with oak floors and a soaring cathedral-ceiling living room. The living room opens to a chef's kitchen with Taj Mahal countertops, a large island, paneled Thermador appliances including a 48" range, and a Bosch dishwasher, with sliders out to the rear covered back deck. The main level has four bedrooms, including a primary suite with a walk-in closet and a spa-like bath, plus a hall bath, laundry room, mudroom with bench and powder room. The walkout lower level adds 2,320 sq ft with a large family room, an open office/gym, two more bedrooms, a full bath, a second laundry room and plenty of storage. Everything is new: roof, Hardie board siding, Andersen windows, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, garage door, driveway and exterior lighting, plus spray foam insulation and Rockwool between floors for a quiet, energy-efficient home. City water, city sewer and natural gas. A turnkey "no expense spared" home with nothing left to do but move in.
