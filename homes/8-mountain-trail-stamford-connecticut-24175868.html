About this home

This immaculate, well maintained Colonial features brand new Central AC, Whole House Generator, Newer Boiler, City Water and is located on a quiet, unique lot in the perfect North Stamford neighborhood. The Main level boasts a generously sized living room, family room, eat-in kitchen with walk out deck, formal dining room, powder room, and sunroom. With 5 bedrooms on the upper level there is room for family, guests and office space. Finished lower level offers many possibilities with a large open space for play area, game room or second family room, a separate room for office, and plenty of storage. Backyard has potential for a 30' x 80' cleared yard or tiered, multi-level yard- inquire with agent. Convenient to transportation, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, parks, Pound Ridge, Scotts Corners, and Bedford. Don't miss this opportunity to own a rare gem!
