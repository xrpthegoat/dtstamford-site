About this home

Your search is OVER! Step right into this "City Chic" home with gleaming hardwood floors and large entry foyer introducing you to over 1000 square feet of open concept living. The updated kitchen with plenty of cabinets, granite counter tops and stainless steel appliances flows naturally into the formal dining room. Your furniture will fit perfectly in the oversized living room as well as the primary bedroom with double closets offering space for a king-size bed. The second bedroom is also spacious and is well positioned for a roommate, guestroom or a home office with privacy. On-site laundry, too! Parking, cooking gas, hot water and water are included. Woodside Green offers residents beautiful green space with a Gazebo and picnic area. If you want to be close to downtown, walking distance to shopping at Ridgeway Shopping Center and less than 2 miles to the Stamford Train Station, book your showing today and see why this is one of the best locations in Stamford.
