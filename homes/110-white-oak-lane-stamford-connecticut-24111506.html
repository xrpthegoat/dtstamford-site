About this home

Rare combination of cul de sac living in the coveted Upper Newfield neighborhood, plus the serenity of a private location with a stunning in-ground heated pool and spa, within minutes of King School, Sterling Farms golf, tennis and theater, Davenport Ridge School, The Italian Center, shopping and the Merritt Parkway. This gracious 5 BR colonial with circular driveway offers an inviting floor plan perfect for today's lifestyle and entertaining. Front and back staircases, formal living and dining rooms, large family room with bar, sun porch, den, eat in kitchen with access to extensive two tiered deck overlooking grounds and pool, and powder room complete the main level. Upstairs the master suite includes a walk in closet and full bath. There are 4 additional bedrooms, 2 full baths and laundry. The lower level features a large walk out rec/playroom, lots of storage and 2 car garage. The home boasts significant improvements to the infrastructure and exterior including a generator. Lovingly maintained by its current owner this is an opportunity not to be missed!
