About this home

Modern Sophistication Meets Suburban Serenity in Westover - Tucked away on a tranquil cul-de-sac, this recently built architectural gem offers a masterclass in modern living, blending refined elegance with the vibrant energy of nearby downtown Stamford. The residence unfolds through a dramatic two-story foyer into a sun-drenched, open-concept sanctuary, where a chef-inspired kitchen-adorned with pristine white cabinetry and expansive granite surfaces-flows effortlessly into a fireside living room and adjacent sunroom with French doors leading outside. The outdoor experience is truly peerless, featuring a resort-style saltwater pool with waterfall, a professional-grade basketball/sports court, and a sprawling bluestone patio framed by artisanal rock walls and secure perimeter fencing. With a high-efficiency infrastructure, vast expansion potential in the walk-out basement, and a commute to Manhattan in under an hour, this property represents a rare opportunity to enjoy "like-new" luxury and a private backyard oasis without the wait.
