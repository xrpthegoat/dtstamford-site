About this home

Welcome to this beautifully renovated Colonial in the heart of Darien, where timeless design meets modern luxury. Offering four bedrooms and three full bathrooms across four finished levels, this move-in ready home delivers exceptional space, style, and functionality for today's lifestyle. The home features a bright and airy layout with thoughtfully updated interiors throughout. The stunning renovation seamlessly blends classic architectural charm with high-end finishes, creating inviting living and entertaining spaces on every level. The main floor offers an effortless flow, perfect for both everyday living and hosting, while the upper levels provide well-appointed bedrooms and flexible spaces for work, relaxation, or play. The fully finished lower level adds incredible versatility-ideal for a media room or a gym. Set in a prime, in-town location, this home offers unparalleled convenience. Just moments to the train, top-rated schools, restaurants, shopping, and Darien's beautiful beaches. A rare opportunity to own a turnkey luxury home in one of Darien's most sought-after locations.
