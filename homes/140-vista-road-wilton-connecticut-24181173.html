About this home

Never before on the market! Acreage reflects only 1 of 2 parcels. 1.84(house)+2.90 acres of pristine, lush property with main house, authentic Post and Beam Barn and in ground Gunite Shoreline pool. Once in a lifetime opportunity to create your weekend getaway, dream vacation compound or year round home to your personal specifications. Rarely does a property with this much character, acreage and pure potential hit the Fairfield County market. One conveyance of 2 contiguous parcels totaling 4.74 acres, on the NY border, in one of Wilton's most coveted neighborhoods, this secluded sanctuary is for visionaries, design enthusiasts, or anyone looking for the ultimate living opportunity. This estate is anchored by 3 incredible features: 1. Main House, a huge, mid-century modern feel, 1-2 Bedroom awaiting your imagination with remarkable potential, excellent bones, spacious proportions and a layout begging for modern transformation. Craft a sleek high-end masterpiece! 2: Majestic Post and Beam Barn w/ loft, steeped in character and limitless utility for entertainment space, convert to guest house or studio, or for your car collection, home fitness center or huge home office! Barn wood from the 1800's. 3. Vintage Shoreline Gunite Pool and grounds perfectly situated between house and barn, surrounded by nature, majestic trees and absolute tranquility. Plenty of room to expand the outdoor living space into an entertainer's paradise. Separate tax on vacant parcel. SOLD AS IS.
