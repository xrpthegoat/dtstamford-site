About this home

PICS 10/10! Updated Vintage Gem w/Fairy Tale Charm! Discover the magic of coastal living in East Norwalk, where everything is just a short walk away! Tucked behind mature privacy hedgerow and surrounded by enchanting, layered perennial gardens, this classic early 1900s New England bungalow offers a private setting filled with character and charm. A garden lover's dream, the landscape comes alive throughout the seasons with snowdrops, crocuses, daffodils, tulips, lilacs, peonies, roses, irises, butterfly bushes, ornamental grasses and so many more. Hidden behind cascading wisteria, the west facing front porch is a delightful retreat for afternoon sunshine, morning coffee or simply enjoying the beauty of nature. Inside, this sun filled home lives large! Beautiful hardwood floors, vintage details and a handsome brick fireplace create inviting living spaces. Offering three bedrooms and two full bathrooms, the home features a 2026 updated kitchen w/subway tile and gas range. Step outside to the deck, perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining with privacy kept in mind- fully fenced for any furry friends. The finished walk-up attic w/recessed lighting and a full bathroom offers flexible additional living space. 1.5 yr/old roof, central AC, city water, city sewer, gas heat, and an expanded asphalt driveway provide added convenience. Whether you're seeking a year-round residence, weekend coastal retreat or seasonal getaway, this updated vintage gem delivers on loving where you live
