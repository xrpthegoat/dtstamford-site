About this home

A striking expression of modern luxury, 8 Hickory Drive delivers sophisticated design, exceptional craftsmanship and high-end finishes across four beautifully finished levels. Set on 0.8 acres, the home pairs dramatic, light-filled living spaces with effortless indoor-outdoor flow. A deluxe chef's kitchen anchors the main level with open concept living to family room with fireplace. The primary suite is spacious and romantic with large walk in closet and spa bathroom. Four additional spacious bedrooms offer refined comfort and privacy. The finished third floor provides flexible space for work or retreat, and the lower level impresses with a private theater, gym and recreation spaces. Every detail has been thoughtfully considered, creating a home that feels as extraordinary as it looks.
