About this home

Great waterfront rental on 5 Mile River on the Darien Rowayton line. Watch the tide come and go from this bright and cheery 4 bedroom, 3 full bath home. Beautiful water views from the living room, dining room, kitchen and primary bedroom. Enjoy outdoor living from multiple decks and large level yard. Plenty of closet space, baths and a huge two car garage. Tokeneke School district. Access to Darien beaches and great town amenities. Walk to Darien or Rowayton train for easy commute to NYC. Year lease, unfurnished. No smoking, pet restrictions, credit report and references requested. Available July 1.
