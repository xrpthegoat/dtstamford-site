About this home

Welcome to ''Woodwind,'' a stunning 1925 Colonial-style home, exuding historic charm across almost 5000 sq ft on a 2.28-acre corner lot. The grand foyer welcomes you into elegant interiors with 5 fireplaces. The main level features a formal dining room and a living room with original millwork. The gourmet kitchen flows into a family room and home office. Five bedrooms include a master suite with dressing room. Separate upper-level quarters offer privacy with a bedroom, bathroom, kitchenette, and sitting room. Enjoy a wine cellar, lower-level family room, serene pool, and flagstone patio. Conveniently located near Pound Ridge, New Canaan, and Greenwich. This home is a rare find of timeless elegance.
