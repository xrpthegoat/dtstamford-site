About this home

Welcome Home! This beautifully maintained 1920's colonial is the perfect blend of modern convenience and charm. Thoughtfully updated, this home offers a wonderful opportunity to live close to everything Stamford has to offer. This home features a wonderful and flexible floor plan, including a spacious kitchen that flows seamlessly to the dining room and living room. Kitchen is perfect for those who enjoy hosting- large island, granite countertops, 5 burner range, and stainless steel appliances. First floor also features a full bath. On the upper level, you'll find a spacious primary bedroom and two additional bedrooms with an updated full bath.The lower level of the home features a bonus bedroom, a versatile playroom, gym, rec room area, laundry area, and wet bar with a sink and freezer. Beautiful front porch, and detached two car garage. Level lot and outdoor patio- perfect for hosting summer gatherings. 2 miles from I95, metro north, and walking distance to shopping and restaurants. Don't miss the opportunity to call this home today.
