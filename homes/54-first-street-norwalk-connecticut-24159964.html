About this home

East Norwalk Rare opportunity to own a legal 3-family restored Victorian house with separate barn and 2-car garage. This amazing one of a kind property is perfect for owner occupant or investor. Incredible income and low maintenance with many updates and improvements. Some of the features include Separate updated mechanicals, new roof, off street parking for 6 vehicles, Partly finished basement with washer/dryer/1/2 bath, enclosed front porch, refinished hardwood floors, and just painted on the interior/exterior. The second building on the property is a charming converted studio barn with vaulted ceilings and beams and attached 2-car garage completed in 2008 with separate panel & fully insulated. 5 minute walk to East Norwalk train station, Beaches, parks, neighborhood restaurants, and South Norwalk (SoNo). 5 minute drive to Westport.
