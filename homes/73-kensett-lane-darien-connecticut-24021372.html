About this home

Luxurious Darien Home located in one of the most desirable locations in Kensett, which backs up to conservation land. This free-standing townhouse, known as the "Holly," features an elevator to all three levels and an open floor plan, perfect for both living and entertaining. With 10' ceilings, hardwood flooring, and plenty of natural light throughout, the home offers an inviting atmosphere. All HW floors were recently sanded and restained. The main level features a den/office with French doors & the ultimate chef's kitchen. The kitchen includes high-end appliances, a center island, a Butler's pantry with a sink, a wine fridge, a microwave, and a walk-in pantry. It opens to both the formal dining room & the oversized living room, which has a gas FP and a wall of French doors leading to a private bluestone terrace, perfect for grilling or relaxing. Upstairs, a spacious primary bedroom suite with a gas fireplace and 2 separate walk in dressing rooms and 2 primary bathrooms. Two additional large BR's with walk in closets and full baths, as well as 2nd floor laundry room. Brand New flooring installed in finished lower level provides additional 600 SF of finished space & full bath can serve a variety of purposes - playroom, a second family room, etc. 2 Car Attached Garage, oversized mudroom & plenty of closets and storage throughout. Entire home was Freshly painted. Full access to the clubhouse, fitness center, pool, outdoor fire pit and to walking trails throughout the property.
