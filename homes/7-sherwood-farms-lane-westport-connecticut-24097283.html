About this home

Set in Greens Farms on the premier "Sherwood Farms" cul-de-sac of upscale homes with an Association pool and clubhouse, this Nantucket Shingle-style residence designed by prominent architect, Roger Ferris, is in excellent, move-in condition. Meticulously maintained, this 6-bedroom home offers thoughtful updates throughout the spacious floor plan. The gracious interior begins with a soaring double-height foyer, introducing the bright, well-appointed rooms. Hardwood flrs, high ceilings, large windows & many glass doors combine to create a refined atmosphere. LR w/1st fpl, double sets of doors to porch. DR offers space for large gatherings and steps out to a bluestone patio. Newer custom dine-in kitchen has soft close cabinetry, stone counters, stainless steel appls incl gas cooktop and an open flow to the sunny breakfast rm w/doors to both a porch & patio, and leads to the large fam rm w/2nd fpl & built-ins. Main level 6th BR or office has beautiful views across the level property. Remodeled half bath plus a mudroom leading to the oversized 2-car garage complete this level. A dramatic staircase leads to the open, sunny landing & 5 BRs on the 2nd level. Each BR is spacious & offers distinct architectural appeal, beginning w/a pair that share a Jack & Jill bath, and two lrg en-suite BRs. The grand primary suite will impress w/king-sized vltd ceil BR, sep gym or dressing rm, updated white marble bth w/dbl walk-in clsts. Massive LL offers incredible storage or potential rec space.
