About this home

Welcome to 73 Alvord Lane, Stamford, a beautifully updated two-family home offering an exceptional opportunity for an owner-occupant or investor looking for a move-in-ready property with immediate rental income. The first-floor unit has been completely gutted and renovated, featuring a brand-new kitchen with crisp white cabinetry, quartz countertops, new hardwood floors, and a bright open-concept kitchen and living space with modern finishes throughout. The second-floor unit is currently rented, providing income from day one and creating the perfect setup to live in one unit while renting the other or add a turnkey multifamily property to your portfolio. Set back on a .14-acre lot, the property offers a surprisingly private backyard setting surrounded by mature trees, creating a peaceful outdoor space rarely expected this close to Downtown Stamford. Conveniently located just off West Main Street/Route 1 with easy access to Downtown Stamford, Stamford Metro-North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, plus nearby shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences, 73 Alvord Lane brings together modern renovations, privacy, rental income, and commuter convenience in one outstanding Stamford opportunity.
