About this home

Nestled on a lovely cul-de-sac, this charming colonial home in the sought-after William Street area welcomes you with a bright foyer that sets a hospitable tone as you enter. The lovely living room, with a wood-burning fireplace and built-in bookcases, creates a cozy yet sophisticated ambiance perfect for quiet evenings or hosting guests. The space flows seamlessly into the formal dining room, where memories and meals are waiting to be shared. The heart of the home is the eat-in country kitchen, complete with gleaming hardwood floors and granite countertops. Adjacent to the kitchen, the spacious family room boasts hardwood floors and a second fireplace, complete with a pellet stove, ensuring warmth and comfort throughout the seasons. Experience the beauty of the outdoors from the beautiful enclosed screened-in porch, perfect for relaxation or social gatherings. A paneled office and a powder room round out the main floor, offering convenience and style. Upstairs, there is a primary bedroom with a full bathroom and three additional generously sized bedrooms with another full bathroom. The expansive driveway and two-car garage offer ample parking and storage solutions. Located on the Norwalk/Westport line and just moments from schools, shopping, dining and major highways. This home is a perfect blend of colonial elegance and modern convenience. Home is being sold "As is", inspections for informational purposes only.
