About this home

A Rare Riverfront Retreat at the End of a Cul-de-Sac. Welcome to 79 Robin Hood Road, a truly exceptional riverfront property tucked away at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Springdale/Sherwood Forest area, near the Darien and New Canaan borders. Set directly along the Noroton River, this distinctive home offers a remarkable combination of privacy, natural beauty, and open views. Across the river, the manicured fairways of Woodway Country Club create a picturesque backdrop, while approximately 11 acres of protected nature preserve border the property, providing an extraordinary sense of seclusion and connection to nature. The expansive backyard and charming screened porch create an idyllic setting for morning coffee, outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply enjoying the changing seasons. Inside, warmth and character abound, highlighted by two fireplaces that create inviting spaces for gatherings and cozy evenings. Beautifully established grounds feature mature landscaping, multiple gardens, and distinctive outdoor spaces. A lovely terrace leads to a cascading curved stairway with a built-in, multilevel rock garden, an especially unique feature that adds charm and character to the landscape. A spacious two-car garage with EV charging provides ample room for vehicles, recreational equipment, gardening gear, and storage. This property has been lovingly maintained by the same family for over 52 years.
