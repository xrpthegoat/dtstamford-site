About this home

Turnkey Restaurant Business Opportunity - New Canaan/Norwalk Border This is a business and equipment sale only. Excellent opportunity to acquire a fully equipped Indian takeout and delivery restaurant located near the New Canaan/Norwalk border with convenient access to Route 7 and the Merritt. This space is set up for restaurant operations and offers an excellent opportunity for an experienced operator or entrepreneur looking to launch a food concept without the expense and time involved in building a commercial kitchen from scratch. The sale includes an extensive inventory of restaurant equipment and furnishings, including commercial burners and ovens, refrigerators, freezers, tandoor oven, industrial mixer, dough machine, steam table, three-compartment sink, food preparation/work stations, shelving, dry storage racks, pots, pans, cooking utensils, dining tables and chairs, and additional restaurant equipment.The existing setup is particularly well suited for takeout, delivery, catering, or another food-service concept. Fully equipped commercial kitchen. A great opportunity for a buyer seeking a ready-to-operate restaurant location with significant equipment already in place.
