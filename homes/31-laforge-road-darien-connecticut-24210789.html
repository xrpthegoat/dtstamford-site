About this home

Welcome to this inviting 4-bedroom, 3-bath home featuring an open floor plan, spacious rooms, and high ceilings in a prime walk-to-town and train location. The large living room with vaulted ceilings flows seamlessly into the dining room and kitchen, creating an ideal setting for everyday living and entertaining. An adjacent family room with a fireplace offers a comfortable space to relax and gather. Two fireplaces add warmth and character throughout the home. The eat-in kitchen opens to an adjoining sunroom, which leads to a back deck and private, fenced-in yard-perfect for outdoor entertaining and everyday enjoyment. A private office is conveniently located on the first floor. The home offers four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with walk-in closets and a beautifully renovated bath featuring a large shower. Three additional large bedrooms and 2 full baths allow room for all. On a private half acre and ideally situated in a wonderful walkable neighborhood, this home offers the perfect blend of space, comfort, and convenience, just moments from town, the train, and everyday amenities.
