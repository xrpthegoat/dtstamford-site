About this home

Welcome to 23 Macintosh Rd, Norwalk - a beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath Raised Ranch tucked away on a quiet dead-end street in a prime commuter location just minutes from The Green and I-95. This move-in ready home features a stylish kitchen with quartz countertops, stainless-steel appliances, and completely finished hardwood floors throughout. Enjoy year-round comfort with central air conditioning and peace of mind with a new roof and top-of-the-line electrical panel. The upgraded bathrooms showcase new tile and a frameless glass shower door, adding a modern touch. The fully finished lower level with walk-out basement provides flexible space for a family room, home office, or gym. Step outside to a large Trex deck and an expansive stone patio surrounded by fresh landscaping-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Completely turnkey and ready for you!
