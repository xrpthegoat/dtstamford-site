About this home

Cove Beach Stamford sunny and spacious 2-bedroom, 1.5-bathroom townhome in Stamford's desirable Cove neighborhood. This newly renovated townhome boasts a generous floor plan with stylish updates throughout on three levels. The updated eat-in kitchen w/ stainless steel appliances flows seamlessly into the warm, light and inviting living and dining areas, featuring gleaming hardwood floors. The sliders from the kitchen open to a private deck and fenced-in beautifully landscaped yard that's perfect for outdoor dining or relaxation and all on the main level. Upstairs you'll find nice-sized bedrooms with wall-to-wall carpeting, providing comfort and coziness and a spectacular view of Cove beach which is a short distance away. The large entry level w/ceramic tile has additional space that's ideal for an office, den, recreation room, or playroom. It also has the washer dryer and storage on this entry level. Central air conditioning and off-street parking for two cars. Beautiful yard with deck & mature plantings make this picture perfect. Situated just steps away from Cove Island Park, residents have beach rights and enjoy easy access to the beach, park facilities, tennis courts, and convenient location to trains and highway - a commuter's delight! Don't miss the opportunity to move right-in to this beautiful townhome!
