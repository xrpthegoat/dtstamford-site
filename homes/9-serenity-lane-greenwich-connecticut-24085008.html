About this home

Nestled in one of Greenwich's most desirable neighborhoods, this serene and private 4800 + sq foot 5-bedroom, 5 1/2-bathroom residence offers the perfect combination of luxury, comfort, and sophisticated design. Set on beautifully landscaped grounds, this home boasts expansive living spaces,beautiful finishes, and an abundance of natural light. Ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, it provides a peaceful retreat while being just minutes from the best that Greenwich has to offer. The first floor features an oversized living room with a wood-burning fireplace. The chef's kitchen, complete with a wet bar, is adjacent to a family room with soaring ceilings, making it an ideal layout for both entertaining and everyday living. The second floor boasts a large primary suite with 2 walk-in closets, plus four additional bedrooms. Set on a beautifully flat, fully fenced 1-acre property, this home's outdoor space is nothing short of spectacular. A sparkling gunite pool and charming gazebo create a true summer oasis, offering the perfect spot for relaxation or hosting memorable gatherings. Located on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of the most sought-after neighborhoods, this is a rare opportunity to own a stunning family home.
