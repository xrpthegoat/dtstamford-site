About this home

Experience coastal living at its most exceptional in this exquisitely renovated waterfront residence, ideally positioned on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's coveted Cove seaside neighborhood. Masterfully redesigned with impeccable attention to detail, this home blends refined luxury with effortless seaside elegance. From the moment you enter, sun-drenched interiors and sweeping water views create a breathtaking, ever-changing backdrop. Expansive windows frame stunning sunrises and sunsets, filling the home with natural light and coastal breezes. The open-concept layout is both sophisticated and inviting-ideal for entertaining-featuring a chef's kitchen, beautifully appointed living spaces, two gas fireplaces and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Inside, the home's magazine-quality design showcases curated finishes and a cohesive aesthetic rarely found at this level. A thoughtfully designed garage caters to an active lifestyle, ideal for sports and outdoor enthusiasts. Outdoors, a generous water-view deck sets the stage for unforgettable entertaining, while a private, covered patio with mounted TV offers an intimate setting for relaxed evenings. With beach access just steps away, the lifestyle here is as effortless as it is enviable. A turnkey home for the most discerning buyer, this is a true sanctuary-equally suited as a full-time residence or a luxurious weekend escape. Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a private haven in one of Stamford's most desirable seaside enclaves.
