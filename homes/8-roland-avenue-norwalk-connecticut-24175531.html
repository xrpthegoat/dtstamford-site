About this home

Welcome to 8 Roland Avenue in Marvin Beach. This Impeccably maintained home welcomes you as soon you step on the inviting front porch. 1927 colonial offers a foyer, living room with fireplace, office, a large eat-in-kitchen for entertaining and a full bath complete the first floor. Three bedrooms, Primary with vaulted ceiling and a full bath on the second level. Full basement for extra living space and storage. Fenced in yard with party barn and bluestone patio for outside gatherings lush with perennials. Costal living with association offering holiday parties and neighborhood get togethers. Three beaches to watch sunsets or kayak from also boat slips available. No flood insurance needed.
