About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2-bath single-family home in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. Freshly painted and move-in ready, this home features an open-concept floor plan with a spacious living room, dining area, and updated kitchen that flow seamlessly together, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen offers granite countertops, a breakfast bar, and new flooring, creating both style and functionality. Hardwood floors run throughout the home. The main level includes a versatile first-floor bedroom that can easily serve as a guest room, or home office, along with a full bathroom adjacent. Upstairs, you'll find three additional generously sized bedrooms and a second full bath. Enjoy the convenience of a large detached two-car garage, plus an oversized driveway with parking for up to five additional vehicles. The full basement provides abundant storage space and includes a washer and dryer for tenant use. Step outside to a spacious deck ideal for relaxing, entertaining, and summer barbecues. The outdoor patio set and barbecue grill are included for your enjoyment. Ideally located just minutes from Chelsea Piers, Glenbrook Train Station, shopping, restaurants, and I-95, this home offers the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and location.
