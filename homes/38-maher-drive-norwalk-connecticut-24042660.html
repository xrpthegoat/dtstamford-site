About this home

***Your Next Chapter Starts at 38 Maher Drive! Step into this beautifully remodeled 3-bedroom, 2 full bath split-level home. Nestled in a serene neighborhood in Broad River - where there is easy access to the highway and parkway and is close to wherever you need to go. The main level welcomes you with an open floor plan, featuring a cozy living room fireplace, a dining area, and a stunning kitchen remodeled in 2021 - a dream for any home chef! Ascending the short staircase, you'll find 3 bedrooms and a full bath, tastefully updated in 2017. The first lower level provides a versatile living space perfect for a family room, complete with a second full bath (remodeled in 2021) and sliders leading to the outdoors. The second lower level remains unfinished, offering abundant storage potential. This home has seen numerous updates, including a new roof in 2022, the stylish 2021 kitchen, the refreshed upstairs bath from 2017, and a new deck also completed in 2017. Outside, unwind in your private oasis featuring a deck, patio, and yard. With close proximity to local parks, schools, and shopping, this home truly represents the best of Norwalk living.
