About this home

Located in the heart of Riverside, this five-bedroom home has just been beautifully and significantly renovated by the sellers. The stunning European kitchen has appliances and appointments for the most discerning chef. There are multiple ovens and a fireplace. The adjacent family room has been redone to seamlessly flow to the backyard with all new landscape. New wood floors and exquisite lighting are some of the home's many features.Sunny formal living and dining rooms invite gatherings. The primary ensuite is set off from the family bedrooms and has ample closets and opens to a private deck. Stressless living is now afforded by all the recent improvements and upgrades. The home is near the Riverside Yacht Club, train, schools and shopping. Just move in and enjoy!
