About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated home in one of N. Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods w/city water. Nestled on a highly walkable street & steps from the scenic trails of the Stamford Arboretum, this home offers the perfect blend of nature & neighborhood charm. Once inside, you'll appreciate the thoughtful updates throughout. Living room w/fireplace flows effortlessly into a formal dining room w/custom built-ins, creating an inviting space for both everyday living & special gatherings. Hardwood floors run throughout the main living areas & bedrooms. New laminate flooring enhances the family room & lower level. An updated kitchen opens to a stunning sunroom filled w/natural light - perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. A brand-new 600sf wraparound Trex deck seamlessly blends indoor & outdoor living, making entertaining easy & enjoyable. This home offers four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite w/full bath & walk-in closet. The fam. room leads to a fully finished LL just a few steps down, creating an integrated & expansive living space ideal for a playroom, home office, gym, or media room. Major updates provide peace of mind, including a one-year-old roof and C/A + full-house Generac generator ensures comfort & security, keeping the lights on & the home running smoothly during storms. Close to Merritt & shopping, the best of N. Stamford living is here. This is the one everyone dreams about, but only a lucky one will call it home
