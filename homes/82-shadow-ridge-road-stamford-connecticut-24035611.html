About this home

Conveniently located - close to shopping, public transportation a short drive to the Merritt, train station and downtown Stamford, this lovely spacious and totally updated home in a desired neighborhood is available for rent. With hardwood flooring throughout, the home has a updated fully equipped kitchen, with marble countertops, an island, stainless steel appliances and newer tiled floors. The first level also boasts a generous size family room with fire place, living and dining room and a full bathroom. The second floor offers of generous primary bedroom with full bath and two more great size bedrooms with plenty of closet space. Don't miss the beautiful deck for summer parties and entertainment. Good credit, income and references required.
