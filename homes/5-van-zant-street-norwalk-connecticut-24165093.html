About this home

Unbeatable remodeled 2 BR 1.5 Bath townhome in East Norwalk. This stunning end unit was recently renovated a few years ago & has been meticulously maintained. Nothing to do but move in & start living! The main level boasts a lovely open floor plan featuring a spacious living room with sliders that lead out to your very own private balcony. Gorgeous white kitchen with SS appliances, granite countertops, tile backsplash, dishwasher, tons of cabinet space, & a cozy separate dining area. Also on this floor is a beautiful updated powder room and plenty of closets for storage. As you make your way upstairs, you'll notice the massive Primary Bedroom with stunning vaulted ceilings & skylight, which allows for an abundance of natural light! The additional bedroom is perfect for guests & the beautiful modern full bath is sure to please! This amazing unit also comes fully equipped with central A/C, attached 2 car garage, newer floors, newer hot water tank & air conditioner condenser! Washer/dryer is conveniently located in the unit on the lower level. Walking distance to the Maritime Aquarium, IMAX Theater, Zagat-rated restaurants, SoNo nightlife, & Oyster Shell Park. Also close proximity to the Metro-North Train Station & SoNo Collections mall. Take weekend bike rides or walk along the Norwalk River where you can sit on the benches & enjoy the peaceful views of the Norwalk River! Take advantage of all that Norwalk has to offer & make this beautiful condo yours today! WILL NOT LAST!!!
