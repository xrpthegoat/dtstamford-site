About this home

This newly remodeled home updated in 2025, exudes a clean modern vibe with its open floor plan, sleek finishes and natural abundant light. The light-filled foyer with a double-height ceiling leads to a brand-new eat-in kitchen with custom cabinets, an oversized island, wine fridge, built-in pantry, and new appliances. Designer touches throughout this beautifully refreshed home enhance its appeal. This residence boasts fantastic flow with the L-shaped kitchen connecting to the family room, and the dining room seamlessly connecting to the living room. The upstairs features a sunny Master suite with custom double closets and a brand-new master bathroom showcasing luxurious custom cabinetry. Two additional bedrooms with large closets, a hallway bath, and a den/office with built-ins complete this level. The spacious lower level includes a generous second family room, two bedrooms, a full bath, and a large storage closet. The spectacular, level fenced backyard features a generous outdoor deck-perfect for entertaining! Additional recent upgrades include beautifully refinished hardwood floors, updated lighting, freshly painted interior and exterior, and a newly restored flagstone walkway. Highlights include modern updates, a versatile layout, a quiet high-end whole house generator, and a brand new custom garage floor. Car enthusiast's dream garage with separately zoned cooling and heating! Convenient location on the border of Darien, near beaches, shopping and train stations.
