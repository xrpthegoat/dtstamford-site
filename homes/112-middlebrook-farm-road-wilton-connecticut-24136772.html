About this home

Quintessential New England colonial set back on 3.21 picturesque acres on one of Wilton's most sought after cul de sacs, Middlebrook Farm Road. This impeccable home is warm & inviting with graceful blue stone walkway to the main front entry; 2nd informal covered porch entry; charming screened porch; expansive deck overlooking the private backyard with mature trees; plus additional large open meadow/field, perfect for sports or lawn games. This special home is drenched in natural light & offers clean lines and open flow from the sparkling white kitchen offering stainless steel appliances & bar seating to the adjoining family room with walls of windows to both the back deck & front porch. There are just a few steps up to the sunny bonus room, newly remodeled, with vaulted ceiling & skylights, perfect as a playroom or studio. The pristine main level also offers gleaming hardwood floors, custom millwork including wainscot & crown molding, plus 3 fireplaces - in the living room, dining room, & private library with floor to ceiling built-in bookshelves. The second level offers the cheery landing with more built-in bookshelves; expansive primary suite with private bath & multiple closets; 3 additional generous bedrooms, all with hardwood floors & shared hall bath; plus walk-up attic for abundant storage. Top location, just steps to schools, plus convenient to commuting routes, train station, charming Wilton Center for shops/restaurants, & only 52 miles to Midtown. Truly exceptional!
