About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST BY JULY 14 AT 5:00. Rarely available in the highly sought-after ALC neighborhood of Shippan Point, this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3.5 bath home offers exceptional quality, thoughtful design and a prime location. The home has deeded beach rights to a private beach on LI Sound, which is located just steps away. Completely reimagined through an extensive renovation and expansion, the home was redesigned to enhance function and flow. High ceilings, abundant natural light, and well-proportioned living spaces create a welcoming atmosphere throughout. The renovation included the addition of a 3rd floor, reconfiguration of the 1st and 2nd floors, 3 new bathrooms and a 2nd floor laundry room. Additional living spaces include a family room, a finished lower level and a 3rd floor home office with water views that can also serve as a 5th bedroom. At the center of the home is a custom kitchen by Deane, featuring Wolf, Thermador and Sub-Zero appliances, custom cabinetry and generous workspace. Throughout the home custom millwork, high-end finishes, and professionally curated interiors reflect careful attention to detail and timeless style. The primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with a private deck with water views. Set on a professionally landscaped corner lot, the property offers strong curb appeal and an ideal location within one of Stamford's most desirable waterfront neighborhoods. This beautiful home is ready for its next owners.
