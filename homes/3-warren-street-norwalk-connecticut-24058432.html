About this home

Discover the charm of this 1920s Farmhouse Colonial, freshly painted and ready to welcome you home. With its classic facade and delightful curb appeal, this home invites you in through a covered front porch, perfect for quiet mornings or evening relaxation. Step inside to a spacious living room, complete with a cozy wood-burning stove set against an exposed brick backdrop, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The main level includes an open dining area that flows into a well-equipped kitchen with ample cabinetry and counter space. A dedicated laundry area on the main level adds to the home's convenience. Upstairs, the primary bedroom is generously sized and includes dual walk-in closets. The second bedroom is flexible, easily adapted as a home office, artist studio, or guest room. A well-organized basement provides additional storage. Step out onto a large deck, extending your living space outdoors and overlooking a level, fenced yard-ideal for entertaining, gardening, or play. You're just minutes away from popular shopping centers, including the Norwalk Shopping Plaza and The SoNo Collection, providing a range of retail, dining, and entertainment options. Short distance to Calf Pasture Beach. Easy access to major highways like I-95 and Route 7 makes commuting a breeze. This residence beautifully combines vintage charm with practical updates, presenting a unique opportunity to enjoy classic Colonial style with modern amenities in a location that truly has it all!
