About this home

Welcome Home! That's the feeling you'll have the moment you step into this lovingly maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bath home, ideally located in the heart of Springdale. Enjoy the convenience of being just a short walk to the train, shopping, and many local amenities. This home offers the features today's buyers are looking for, including an open-concept kitchen that flows seamlessly into a spacious family room with sliders leading to a private deck - perfect for relaxing or entertaining! The main level also features a comfortable living room, two bedrooms, a full bath, and a versatile office space just steps from the kitchen. Upstairs, you'll find a stunning primary suite complete with vaulted ceilings, a generous walk-in closet, and a spacious ensuite bath. Additional highlights include a beautifully landscaped yard, level driveway, one-car garage and low-maintenance vinyl siding. Don't miss the opportunity to make this charming home yours!
