About this home

Welcome to 8 Byselle Road. Tucked on a quiet street in Norwalk's Bettswood neighborhood, this beautifully maintained, single-level ranch offers easy, flexible living with room to spread out. The main level is bright and inviting, with hardwood floors throughout, an updated kitchen featuring a new refrigerator, dishwasher, and dual fuel range, and updated bathrooms that make everyday living effortless. Cozy up by the gas-burning fireplace on cooler evenings, or step outside to the professionally landscaped backyard, where mature plantings, garden beds that create privacy, and a spacious Trex deck that set the scene for morning coffee, outdoor dinners, or weekend gatherings. Downstairs, nearly 900 additional square feet of fully finished space opens up a world of possibilities: a family room, home office, gym, guest suite, or extended living area, complete with a full bath, counter space, and storage for true flexibility. Additional highlights include an attached garage, a storage shed, and a level 0.23-acre lot with excellent curb appeal and built-in irrigation system. Located with easy access to shopping, dining, commuter routes, South Norwalk's vibrant downtown, and Norwalk's beaches and waterfront lifestyle.
