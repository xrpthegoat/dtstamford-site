About this home

Convenient, contemporary living! This 2BR 2.5 bath updated home is tranquil and private in one of Westport's most sought after condominium complexes where living is made easy. Move right in and enjoy this fabulous open floor plan on the first floor featuring hardwood and tiled floors throughout. The spacious living room boasts a large fireplace, vaulted ceiling, and oversized windows - light pours in from the glorious courtyard and expansive windows throughout. A separate den space makes a perfect setting for casual relaxation. The eat-in kitchen is equipped with granite countertops, a large island, and doors leading to the private courtyard perfect for relaxing, dining, grilling and entertaining. The second level includes a private primary bedroom with two walk-in closets and a newly renovated ensuite bath fully equipped with a dual sink vanity, expanded shower, and soaking tub. The second bedroom has ample space, with newly renovated hall bath. Conveniently located on this floor is a separate laundry area. The partially finished lower level offers an office/living space and ample storage. Direct entry to the kitchen is an attached two car garage. The complex features a heated pool, two Har-Tru tennis courts, and a newly renovated bathhouse with common room attached and the grounds are professionally landscaped with seasonal blooms. Five minutes to Greens Farms train, this ideally located property is near Westport's excellent shopping, dining, beaches and schools.
