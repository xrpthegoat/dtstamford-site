About this home

Tucked off Gregory Boulevard, sits 5 Elliott Street in the heart of the Marvin Beach Association and neighborhood. This charming 3-bedroom colonial features an open, airy floor plan flooded with natural light, hardwood floors and a layout made for relaxed living and entertaining. A spacious entrance leads into the living room, dining room and sunroom with a well-placed window seat. The bright and inviting kitchen features an eat-in area with access to the rear garden. Retreat to the upper level with bedrooms and spa inspired bathroom. Property includes a 1 car garage adjacent to home, parking for 2 cars, plus off-street parking and ideally located to incorporate the natural beauty of coastal living with the proximity to Norwalk and ease of Metro-North and I-95.
