About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood, this well-maintained raised ranch offers space, comfort, and privacy. Featuring 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths, the home boasts hardwood floors throughout much of the main level and two fireplaces that add warmth and character. The spacious layout includes a renovated primary bathroom and generously sized rooms filled with natural light. Step outside to an oversized deck perfect for entertaining, outdoor dining, or simply relaxing in a peaceful setting. A private side yard provides additional outdoor space for recreation and enjoyment. The lower level offers excellent flexibility with a family room and large storage room adjacent to the garage that presents an exciting opportunity for future expansion, home office space, gym, or recreation room. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, and commuter routes, this move-in-ready home combines a sought-after neighborhood setting with exceptional potential.
