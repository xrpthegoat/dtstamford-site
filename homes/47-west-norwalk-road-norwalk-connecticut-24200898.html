About this home

*Twilight Open House 8/28 from 5- 7 pm. Welcome to 47 W Norwalk Road, a newly built 2026 home in desirable West Norwalk, directly on the Darien border-the property behind you is Darien! This 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home features a finished basement, all-new electrical, plumbing, mechanicals, central air, recessed lighting, and luxury finishes throughout. Wide-plank white oak hardwood floors lead to a show-stopping kitchen with dramatic vaulted ceilings, extensive cabinetry, walk-in pantry, and Taj Mahal quartzite countertops, backsplash, and statement island. The main level is designed for living and entertaining with a dining room, private en-suite bedroom with full bath, dedicated office, and powder room. Upstairs are 3 additional bedrooms, 2 full baths and second-floor laundry. The primary suite features an oversized walk-in closet and spa-like walk-in shower with 3 shower heads. The finished basement provides additional flexible living and entertaining space. A brand-new rear deck overlooks a huge fenced backyard with room for a future pool. Enjoy a large front yard, massive new shed, attached 2-car garage, and spacious driveway with ample parking. Just 1 block from Connecticut Ave and minutes to Wegmans, Whole Foods, Target, restaurants, shopping, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway. Brand-new luxury living in West Norwalk with Darien at your doorstep!
