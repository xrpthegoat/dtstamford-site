About this home

Stunning Modern completely redone colonial set in a quiet North Stamford location. Completely re-imagined in 2025 with well crafted & stylish design elements through out. This designer chic modern home features a first floor plan w/an open concept. A two story foyer welcomes you to the many elements of this sophisticated home. The oversized kitchen is the center of the home; featuring abundant cabinetry, large kitchen island, top grade Thermador & Viking appliances+quartz countertops. Open to the kitchen is the family room w/cozy fireplace & extra flex space ideal for casual living. Glass privacy doors introduce you to the in home office with abundant light & slider access to the deck. The formal space includes a dramatic living room with vaulted ceiling, fireplace & modern chandelier+formal dining room. Upstairs, there is a stunning generous sized primary suite w/vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet+luxurious bath w/oversized shower & soaking tub. Three generous sized additional bedrooms share two sophisticated baths. Not to be overlooked is the added suite over the garage. With separate entrance, wet bar with fridge and private bath, this is the perfect space for extended stay guests. A finished walk out lower level with bath & patio includes additional versatile space. Features include-NEW high efficiency multi zone HVAC, Hardie plank siding & Azak trim, Timberline roof, custom windows & doors+driveway just to name a few of the many improvements!
