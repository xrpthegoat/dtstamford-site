About this home

Welcome to the perfect canvas for your dream home! This ranch-style residence offers the convenience of one-level living in a sought-after cul-de-sac location. The functional kitchen includes an eating area and provides ample cabinet and counter space, perfect for culinary preparation and casual dining. For those needing extra flexibility, the family room-complete with a cozy wood stove-can easily serve as a fourth bedroom or a private primary suite, as it includes a full bathroom and convenient laundry access.
