About this home

Welcome to 1285 Hope Street, a beautifully updated end-unit townhouse conveniently located in upper Springdale near the New Canaan border. This move-in-ready condo offers added privacy, a comfortable layout, and thoughtful improvements throughout. The completely renovated kitchen features new quartz countertops and all-new appliances, creating a stylish and functional space for both everyday living and entertaining. The renovated bathroom showcases updated finishes and fixtures, while updated lighting throughout adds a modern, refreshed feel. Step outside to enjoy a custom-built outdoor seating area, designed by the owner as a welcoming space to relax or entertain. The property also includes two dedicated parking spaces, basement storage and pull down attic storage for added convenience. Ideally positioned on Hope Street, the home offers easy access to shopping, dining, transportation, and nearby amenities. With its desirable end-unit setting, modern updates, and convenient location, 1285 Hope Street is a wonderful opportunity to enjoy townhouse living in one of Stamford's sought after neighborhoods.
