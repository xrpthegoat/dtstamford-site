About this home

Cute as a button! Townhouse style living, but with a yard! Great flexibility! The main upper level features an open-concept living space with the kitchen, dining and living room all in one large open area. Beautiful "natural-looking" laminated floors, brightly lit windows, white kitchen, and sparkly clean! The main bedroom is right off the living room and features two spacious walk-in closets. The main bathroom features a tub and shower. Stroll down the easy-to-use spiral staircase to the lower level and discover the flexibility of this surprise living space. This could be used as a second bedroom, an in-law suite or a fabulous home office. From the lower level you can walk out the separate entrance to a private patio and the spacious back yard, large enough for your favorite outdoor activity. This is a unique property that shouldn't be missed by anyone looking for a two bedroom house or private townhouse-like property with a great yard. Convenient location too! Close to everything that beautiful mid-town Stamford has to offer.
