About this home

Welcome to 2 Finch Court, a lovingly maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bath home set on a quiet Norwalk cul-de-sac with a story as special as its setting. Built in 1964 as the final home in the community, this residence offers a bright, functional layout with hardwood floors, a warm living room with a clean-burning gas fireplace insert, an attached 2 car garage, and thoughtful details throughout, including a cork kitchen floor and a custom tile accent over the kitchen sink. The sunroom creates a peaceful year-round retreat with a glass roof overlooking the beautifully landscaped backyard, where stone walls and stairs were crafted from rocks found on the property, gardens bloom sequentially with low-maintenance care, and a full irrigation system keeps the lawn lush. The finished lower-level space offers flexible use for working from home, homeschooling, hobbies, or a home business. Set on over a half-acre with a private two-level backyard, bluestone patio area, and hot tub. Less than a half mile to Rowayton train station and easy access to shopping, dining, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, this home blends comfort, character, convenience, and a true neighborhood feel.
