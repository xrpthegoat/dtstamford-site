About this home

"Experience all that this exclusive waterfront community has to offer" This beautifully updated home with open floorplan sits just steps away from the community clubhouse complete with dock and stunning views of Long Island Sound. Award winning architect Laurent Dupont of New Canaan designed the 2nd floor addition in 2003. Why not start your day with a morning cup of coffee on the MBR deck as you enjoy the breathtaking water views. Following Hurricane Sandy the whole 1st floor was totally rebuilt, which includes a new kitchen complete with Wolf range and soapstone countertops. After completion the whole house was elevated 14 feet in compliance to FEMA regulations. "This is not just a house, it's a lifestyle" complete with tennis courts, playground, a basketball court and much, much more. "Why not stop by and take a peek, you'll be glad you did"
