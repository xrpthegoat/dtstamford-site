Welcome to 78 Portland Avenue, Wilton This well-maintained single-family home offers a comfortable layout, thoughtful updates, and a location that makes everyday living easy. Just a short walk to the train station and local shops, it's ideal for those seeking both space and convenience. Inside, you'll find an open floor plan with hardwood floors, a chef's kitchen featuring a high-end Wolf double oven, eight-burner cooktop, and stainless steel appliances. A wood-burning fireplace adds warmth and character on chilly winter nights. The primary suite is a spa-like retreat, complete with a soaking tub, double sinks, spacious shower, and a walk-in closet. The finished basement provides a dedicated office, home gym space, full bath, and a laundry room with additional storage. An attached two-car garage adds functionality, while the front porch offers the perfect place to unwind and enjoy the scenery. A special home in a walkable location-ready to welcome its next owners.
