About this home

LOCATION, LOCATION, LOCATION...Fully renovated 2-bedroom, 1-bath rental located just half a mile from downtown Westport, offering scenic views overlooking the Saugatuck River. This unit includes private parking, an in-unit washer and dryer, and a private storage unit. The interior is entirely brand new, featuring new windows, updated flooring and carpeting, an eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, and a full bath with a tiled shower. The layout offers two spacious bedrooms and a bright living room with wall-to-wall carpeting. Additional improvements include spray foam insulation, with professional landscaping and exterior painting scheduled for completion in the spring. Enjoy walking to downtown shops and restaurants, with convenient access to Route 15 and I-95.
