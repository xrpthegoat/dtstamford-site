About this home

Nestled on Valley Rd, this gardener's paradise welcomes you to a 4 bedroom Colonial with heated pool. This sunlit home features a first floor primary bedroom, spacious living room and vaulted family room opening to a lovely pool and terrace. The kitchen leads to an oversized screened in porch. A four season sunroom, den and generous dining room complete the first floor. 3 additional bedrooms are on the second floor. The mature plantings and grounds are beautifully maintained and borders a 20 acre New Canaan Land Trust. Welcome to 378 Valley Rd. *House will be sold in its present condition*
