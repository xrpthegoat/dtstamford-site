About this home

Tranquility and convenience describe this South Wilton home! The family room, with its stunning Connecticut stone fireplace, is open to the eat-in kitchen featuring granite countertops, double ovens and an induction stovetop. The adjacent sunroom overlooks the peaceful property. Enjoy holiday dinners in your banquet size dining room! The primary bedroom has a huge walk-in closet and a gorgeous full bath with radiant heated floors. The main floor also includes a formal living room, second bedroom, and full bath. The beautiful hardwood floors throughout the main level were recently refinished. The lower level includes a recreation/play space with a fireplace, two additional spacious bedrooms, a full bath, laundry room and a large storage/ utility room. The two car garage, shed and attic offer lots of additional storage space! The beautiful, flat property features perimeter stone walls and mature landscaping with an irrigation system.There is plenty of room to play baseball, soccer or even host a grand party! It borders on Cranbury Park, with access to its wooded trails. Convenient to Route 7, I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and Metro North Railroad, as well as local shopping. WILTON SCHOOLS. A small piece of the property is in Norwalk, which allows you to enjoy some Norwalk amenities.
