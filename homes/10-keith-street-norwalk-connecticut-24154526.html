About this home

Unique opportunity to purchase a two-family home on a cul-de-sac which has been in the same family for nearly 70 years. The first floor features a large eat in kitchen, living room, two bedrooms, office, storage area and full bath with tub/shower. The second floor includes a living room, eat in kitchen, one bedroom and full bath. Fabulous location near shops and eateries on the Post Rd, Flax Hill Park, I95, train station, beaches, Stepping Stones Museum, SoNo Seaport and SoNo Collection mall. Great income opportunity for investors, homeowners deriving income from one unit or extended families. Home to be sold "As Is, Where Is". Inspections for informational purposes only.
