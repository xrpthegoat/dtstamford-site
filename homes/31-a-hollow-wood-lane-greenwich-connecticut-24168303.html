About this home

Set on a quiet street and close to all that Greenwich has to offer: beaches, parks, train station, major commuter highways, schools, and Greenwich Ave. A large open floor plan welcomes you to the main level. Sunlight pours into the large windows of the living and dining room both providing hardwood flooring. The adjacent updated kitchen provides stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, plus an entry to the back deck for BBQing and enjoying the large, shared, fenced-in yard. A full bath completes this level. Upper level provides 3 bedrooms all with hardwood flooring plus one full bath. Lower level provides lots of storage, washer & dryer connection and a one car garage. 2 extra parking spaces available in the driveway for convenience.
