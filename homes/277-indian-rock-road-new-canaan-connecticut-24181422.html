About this home

GOLF FAIRWAY VIEWS! Set on nearly 4 private acres abutting the 16th fairway of the Country Club of New Canaan, this charming Colonial offers a rare combination of privacy, views, and location. Sun-filled interiors include a gracious living room with built-ins and a wood-burning fireplace, opening seamlessly to the formal dining room for easy entertaining. The bright kitchen features updated appliances and a lovely bay window overlooking the grounds. A spacious primary suite with full bath, two additional bedrooms, and a large family room with fireplace provide comfortable living spaces throughout. Expansive lawn, mature landscaping, and picturesque golf course views create a truly special setting in one of New Canaan's most desirable locations. A GREAT BUILDING OPPORTUNITY!
