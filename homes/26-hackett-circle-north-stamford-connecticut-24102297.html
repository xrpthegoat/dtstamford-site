About this home

Welcome to this spacious and sun-filled 3-bedroom, 2-bath first floor apartment located on a quiet private street. Featuring gleaming hardwood floors and recessed lighting throughout, this unit offers an inviting open-concept layout perfect for comfortable living and entertaining. The modern kitchen is equipped with stainless steel appliances, abundant cabinet space, and direct access to a private rear deck ideal for morning coffee or summer BBQs. Central air ensures year-round comfort, while the full-size in-unit washer and dryer provide ultimate convenience.A standout feature is the fully finished lower level, providing exceptional flexibility-ideal for a home office, guest room, or 4th bedroom, along with a separate family room and a modern full bath. Nestled in a well-maintained two-family home, this apartment is located just minutes from Stamford's vibrant downtown, Scalzi Park, shops, restaurants, and Metro-North station-offering the perfect balance of space, updates, and accessibility.
