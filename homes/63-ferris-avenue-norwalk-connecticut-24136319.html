About this home

Welcome Home! Conveniently located and lovingly cared for, this charming residence offers comfort, flexibility, and room to grow. A wide driveway and attached garage provide plenty of parking, while the beautiful level backyard features mature plantings, a shed, and a lovely patio-perfect for outdoor entertaining or quiet relaxation. Inside, you'll find an extra-long living room and a dining room that offer a warm, inviting flow for everyday living and gatherings. The light and bright kitchen makes meal prep a pleasure, and the main level also includes a bedroom and full bath for added convenience. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom can easily be converted into two separate bedrooms if desired (see floor plans), accompanied by another full bath. The partially finished basement with walk-out access to a covered patio adds versatile space for recreation, work, or guests. Central air ensures year-round comfort. A wonderful opportunity to own a well-maintained home in a convenient location-ready for you to move right in and make it your own!
