About this home

Flexible short-term UNFURNISHED rental: $8,750 yearly or $9,500 short-term. ALSO AVAILABLE FURNISHED SEE MLS# 24126753. Fully remodeled, turn-key home on a premier estate property at the end of a cul-de-sac. Modern styling blends with a captivating private setting to create a unique oasis. Two acres of park-like property, abutting the Paul Newman-Poses Preserve, offer rare serenity in a convenient location. A respected local builder transformed this home into a Transitional-Style Farmhouse, combining high-end new-construction features with ideal scale and price point. Enter the glass front door to an airy, open main level. Living area with fireplace flows to dining and upgraded kitchen. Step onto the expansive deck with lovely views of the flat backyard and unmatched privacy. Recreation room works as family room, game space, or home office. Upstairs, bedrooms are sleek with ample closets and chic, European-style baths. Lower level offers storage or gym/bonus space. Attached two-car garage. Renovated in 2022; built in 1964.
