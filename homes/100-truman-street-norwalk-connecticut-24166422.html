About this home

Experience elevated modern living in this brand-new luxury townhouse, built in 2024 and thoughtfully designed across three spacious levels. This stunning three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath residence blends contemporary style with everyday functionality. The main level welcomes you with a bright, open-concept living and dining area, perfect for both relaxing and entertaining. A sleek, designer kitchen anchors the space, featuring high-end finishes and a seamless flow for effortless living. A convenient half bath completes this floor. Upstairs, the second level offers two generously sized bedrooms, a full bathroom, and the added convenience of an in-unit washer and dryer-making daily living both easy and efficient. Enjoy your own peaceful sanctuary complete with a spacious layout and a private balcony, ideal for morning coffee or unwinding at sunset with beautiful open views. This is a rare opportunity to rent a turnkey, newly constructed home that perfectly balances style, space, and location.
