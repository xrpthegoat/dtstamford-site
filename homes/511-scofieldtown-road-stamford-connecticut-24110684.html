About this home

Welcome to this stunning 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath contemporary home that seamlessly blends style, comfort, and functionality. As you step inside, you're greeted by soaring high ceilings and an open floor plan that creates a light and airy atmosphere throughout the main living spaces. The fully renovated kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a layout perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Sliding doors from both the kitchen and den lead to a spacious deck that overlooks a generous backyard ideal for family fun or outdoor gatherings. The inviting living room offers a cozy fireplace, skylights, and flows effortlessly into the open dining area, creating the perfect space to relax or host guests. The primary bedroom is conveniently located on the first floor and features dual closets, a private powder room, and a luxurious en-suite bath with a Jacuzzi tub. The partially finished basement provides ample room for a home gym, playroom, or extra storage space - the possibilities are endless.
