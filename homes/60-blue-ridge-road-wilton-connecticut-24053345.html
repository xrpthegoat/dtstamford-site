About this home

Charming, Move-In Ready Home in Lower Wilton. Welcome to 60 Blue Ridge Road-a beautifully maintained, move-in-ready home situated on a LEVEL, CORNER LOT in a desirable walking and quiet neighborhood. This one-level living gem offers an open floor plan and is perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The exterior boasts a large, cleared yard with a spacious deck, ideal for year-round gatherings, outdoor play or quiet relaxation. Inside, the bright living room features a large picture window, flooding the space with natural light. The open-concept layout flows effortlessly into the eat-in kitchen, complete with granite countertops and breakfast bar seating-perfect for enjoying your morning coffee. Adjacent to the kitchen is a dedicated dining area and a spacious family room with vaulted ceilings and sliding doors, on three sides, that lead to the expansive back deck. A wood-burning stove adds a cozy touch for chilly winter nights. Hardwood floors run throughout the home adding warmth and character. The primary bedroom offers an ensuite bath, while three additional well-sized bedrooms share easy access to a full bath. Downstairs, the fully finished lower level provides a versatile recreation room, gym space, laundry room, and a half bath-plus the added convenience of a bonus garage. With a perfect blend of comfort, style, and functionality, 60 Blue Ridge Road is ready to welcome you home. Enjoy all the wonderful Wilton amenities and Fall foliage!
