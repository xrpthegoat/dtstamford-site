About this home

Welcome to refined townhouse living in Old Forge Green, one of New Canaan's most sought-after communities. This beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 3-bath residence offers the perfect blend of comfort and style. The thoughtfully designed floor plan features a generous eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, and an inviting living room anchored by a fireplace and custom built-ins. French doors open to a private terrace with a chic awning-ideal for al fresco dining surrounded by professionally landscaped gardens. Enjoy walkability to New Canaan's vibrant village center, where award-winning restaurants, boutique shopping, and Metro-North access await. A rare opportunity to experience low-maintenance living without sacrificing space or location. --
