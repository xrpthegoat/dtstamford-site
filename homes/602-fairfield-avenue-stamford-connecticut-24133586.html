About this home

OLD GREENWICH LIVING AT A FRACTION OF THE COST! One-of-a-kind gem across from the prestigious Innis Arden Golf Club, nestled between the shorelines, Old Greenwich, and Dolphin Cove. This upscale 4-bedroom, 2-bath colonial on a corner lot offers easy access to beaches, marinas, shops, dining, entertainment, train stations and I-95. Features include hardwood floors throughout, new screened tilt windows, custom-designed Leicht kitchen with marble counters/ backsplash and bespoke flooring, generous storage with hidden drawers, wet bar, Electrolux and other high-end appliances. The home also offers a dry, spacious basement with extensive storage, and a large marble-top Electrolux laundry station. Enjoy bright, open spacious rooms with large closets, a gas fireplace, large sunroom, and spa-style bathrooms. The lush, fenced yard with mature trees, fruit, berries, herbs, and roses- a private sanctuary in a highly sought-after prime location! This home is low-maintenance and move-in ready, freshly painted. Other benefits: city water, sewer, and garbage services. Energy-efficient with low gas and electric bills and no flood insurance required.
