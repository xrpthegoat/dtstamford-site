About this home

Experience luxury living in Stamford's prime location with this stunning 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom townhome, built in 2024. Inside, you'll discover a bright, open-concept floor plan filled with natural light from large windows and enhanced by energy-efficient LED lighting. The first floor features an attached garage leading to the mudroom and a chef's kitchen with modern appliances, quartz countertops, and abundant storage-perfect for culinary creativity. The living and dining area, complete with a cozy fireplace, opens to a balcony, creating an ideal space for entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs, a spacious bedroom suite with generous closet space, an additional bedroom, a full hallway bathroom, and laundry offer convenience and comfort. The expansive primary suite occupies the third floor, featuring a modern spa-like bathroom for the ultimate retreat. Located in the heart of Stamford, this home offers easy access to restaurants, cafes, beaches, and the Amtrak/Metro-North station, providing a 55-minute commute to NYC. Luxury, style, and convenience come together in this beautifully designed townhome.
