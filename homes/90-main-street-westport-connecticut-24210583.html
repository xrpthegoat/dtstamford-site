About this home

Experience the best of Westport living in this modern, light-filled in-town location, perfectly positioned in the heart of it all. Soaring 12-foot ceilings create an open and airy feel, while an assigned parking space adds everyday convenience. Set within an intimate complex of just seven units, this home offers a wonderful combination of style, privacy and ease. Just steps from some of Westport's finest shopping, dining & local favorites, enjoy an effortless walk-to-town lifestyle. Stroll along the Saugatuck River, wander charming tree-lined streets, or take in the vibrant energy of downtown Main Street with its upscale boutiques, cafes and restaurants. Westport's beautiful beaches, golf courses, and renowned arts and entertainment, including the Westport Country Playhouse and Levitt Pavilion, are all within easy reach. With convenient access to Metro-North, NYC is just a 60-minutes away making this an exceptional opportunity!
