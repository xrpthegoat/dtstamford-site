About this home

This expansive 4,400 sf colonial, fully rebuilt in 2013, offers a refined blend of comfort, scale and thoughtful design. A welcoming entry leads to a gracious living room with a fireplace, flowing seamlessly into a formal dining room well suited for both intimate dinners and larger gatherings. At the center of the home, the sunlit gourmet kitchen features marble countertops, a 6 burner range, double wall ovens, and a large island, opening seamlessly to the family room with fireplace, custom built-ins, and sliding doors to the patio. Outdoor living is equally well considered, with a bluestone patio extending into a level, professionally landscaped yard that supports both relaxation and recreation. The first floor also offers a private office (or guest suite) w/full bath and a mudroom designed for everyday convenience. Upstairs, the primary suite features cathedral ceilings, two walk-in closets and a spa-like, marble bath w/soaking tub and separate shower. three additional bedrooms, a library/homework room, and a spacious laundry room provide comfort and efficiency. The bright third floor and finished lower level provides additional living space and invite imagination-movie room, home gym, playroom, or gathering space. Ideally located steps from the 65-acre Woodland Nature Preserve and within walking distance to the train, shopping, and restaurants. This home has exceptional flow and offers the perfect blend of space, function and convenience.
