About this home

Fully updated single family on a quiet dead end street in Norwalk, plus a private studio apartment included in the rent. This is the most complete rental on the market in the 06850 right now, and it is priced to move. The main house has 3 bedrooms and a full bath, with the second floor bedroom opening to a bonus room that works as a 4th bedroom, office or gym. Hardwood floors throughout, central air, high end appliances, prewired for audio and cable, and plenty of closet and storage space. Eat in kitchen opens to the deck and stone patio. Washer and dryer in the basement. Security system available. The lower level is a fully redone studio apartment. Use it as guest quarters, a home office or a playroom. Included in the rent, no sublease. Outside: a private, nicely landscaped yard with garden, sprinkler system, stone fire pit, and two driveways with plenty of parking. Minutes to the Merritt Parkway, Route 7 and Main Ave shopping and dining, with Metro North at Merritt 7 and SoNo's restaurants and waterfront a short drive away. Cranbury Park and the Silvermine Arts Center are right around the corner. Bus service nearby.
