About this home

Set on just over two acres with long views to roughly fifty acres of protected open space, 160 Olmstead Hill Road blends vintage farmhouse charm with a thoughtful 2013 renovation for easy, modern living. A welcoming front porch leads inside to an open, everyday flow: comfortable living and dining rooms, a sun-splashed chef's kitchen with marble work surfaces and premium appliances (including a built-in coffee maker with dedicated water line), plus an adjoining sitting area that frames the landscape. The two-story family room-anchored by a fireplace and custom built-ins-gathers everyone together. Upstairs, a calm private suite sits apart from three additional bedrooms, with two flexible work-from-home spots nearby. Storage is abundant; recessed lighting and a dry basement add daily convenience. Just outside the kitchen, a bluestone terrace invites morning coffee and evening suppers, with broad lawns and room for a future pool (buyer to verify). A standout feature is the renovated, two-story barn offering approximately 900 square feet of finished, highly adaptable space-perfect for studio, music, office, or play (not included in house square footage; buyer to confirm).
