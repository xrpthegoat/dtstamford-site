About this home

In Darien, Connecticut, this gracious colonial is a rare combination of space, light, location and value! Beautifully updated and impeccably maintained, this 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath Colonial offers over 5,000 sq. ft. of gracious living on a welcoming Darien cul-de-sac. High ceilings and abundant natural light create a wonderful sense of openness throughout. The remodeled eat-in kitchen flows seamlessly into a spectacular sun-filled, eating area, two-story family room with soaring vaulted ceilings, built-ins, and fireplace. The spacious first floor also offers formal living and dining rooms, a private home office, and bonus/playroom with fireplace. Upstairs are 5 generously sized bedrooms, 3 full baths and an oversized laundry room. The fully finished lower level adds another 1,000+ sq. ft, with a full bath, mudroom, abundant storage and flexible space for a possible 6th bedroom, gym or recreation room. Enjoy a friendly, welcoming neighborhood, where all play safely outside and neighbors gather and support one another. Ideally located with easy access to the Talmadge Hill train station, Merritt Parkway, and the shopping, dining and conveniences of Darien, New Canaan, Glenbrook, Springdale and Stamford. An extraordinary opportunity to enjoy the space, comfort, and lifestyle of a substantial Darien home, at a truly compelling value, increasingly difficult to find in Darien today, particularly in the sought-after Ox Ridge School District!
