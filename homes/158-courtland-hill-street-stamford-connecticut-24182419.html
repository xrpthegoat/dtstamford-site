About this home

Tucked away on a corner lot in the heart of Glenbrook, this meticulously maintained home delivers the rare combination of stylish finishes, flexible living space, and close proximity to the train station, Cove Island, neighborhood restaurants and coffee shops -all just a stone's throw away. A recently renovated kitchen with all-white cabinetry, quartz countertops, and stainless-steel appliances flows seamlessly into an oversized dining and living room with fireplace, ideal for everyday living and entertaining. Two large bedrooms, a newly renovated bathroom (2023), gleaming hardwood floors, and elegant Waterworks fixtures complete the main floor. The lower level offers a third bedroom, fully updated bathroom, family room, flex space, and dedicated laundry area - perfect for guests, extended family, or home office setup. Above, a walk-up attic provides rare expansion potential for a future primary suite, bonus room, or additional living space. All major systems are new - central air, gas boiler, and gas hot water heater - so you can move in with confidence. Outside, the professionally landscaped, fully irrigated lot creates a remarkably private backyard retreat with a fenced yard, botanical walkways, new patio, lush lawn, and pathway to a one-car garage. With the hard work already done, all that's left is to move in and enjoy.
