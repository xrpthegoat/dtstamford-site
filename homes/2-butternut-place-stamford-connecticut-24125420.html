About this home

Welcome to 2 Butternut Place in desirable North Stamford. This spacious colonial is filled with character and light, and offers the space, layout and features you want for how we live today. The well equipped eat-in-kitchen with breakfast bar will delight the serious home chef with stunning granite counters, stainless appliances and lovely tile backsplash. The oversized family room with gleaming hardwood floors and stylish brick fireplace with wood burning insert is perfect for all of your holiday parties. And the sophisticated living room and grand dining room with large bay window add to the perfection. Need to work from home? A first floor home office can accomodate all of your remote work or au pair/in-law needs. And there's more! A lovely sunroom with another entertaining area, laundry and full bath complete the first level. Upstairs the primary bedroom boasts a walk-in closet and renovated full bath. 3 additional bedrooms and another room currently being used as a craft room can easily be used as another bedroom, den or office. The huge basement has unlimited storage potential. Beautiful and lovely manicured gardens and flat backyard make this home a true showplace. The pristine homes you thought only existed in your dreams is right here. Move in ready! Make your home ownership dreams a reality. Being sold As-Is.
