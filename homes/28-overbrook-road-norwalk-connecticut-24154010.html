About this home

Thank you to ALL that came to 28 Overbrook Rd! Highest and Best by Monday 3/23 by 5 pm. First time on the market in 75 years! Well maintained, well loved Cape Cod home in Norwalk. 2 bedroom 2 full bath with potential for a 3rd bedroom on the first floor, currently a den with built-ins and a wood burning fireplace. Main floor bathtub was modified for easy walk in access. Eat in kitchen with bay window and walk in pantry. (Don't miss the DIY laundry lift in the pantry!)Hardwood floors throughout. Side entrance brings you to what was used as an office however leads to the kitchen as well as the one car garage giving way for expansion if desired. Upstairs, the 2 bedrooms have built in dressers to keep you organized! Potential for extra living space in the basement. Attached shed off the back of the garage adds storage space for lawn toys to maintain your established landscape. Bluestone patio to the left of the garage adds to outdoor entertaining space. Hop skip and a jump to the Merritt Parkway for an easy commute. This home exudes warmth and charm. Live as is or make it your own, the solid foundation is awaiting your personal touch
