About this home

A gorgeously fully renovated end-unit studio offering the ultimate in privacy, no one above you, no one below you, and no neighbors on either side. This unique home features skylights, a huge walk-in closet, and plenty of white shaker-style kitchen cabinetry, providing exceptional storage and convenience. Even better, it includes a private one-car garage with additional storage space, perfect for luggage, skis, golf clubs, and more. This is a feature that is virtually unheard of for a studio in Stamford! Enjoy the convenience of all utilities included, eliminating the worry of surprise monthly bills. This home is exceptionally clean, thoughtfully updated, and designed for comfortable, efficient living. The beautifully appointed kitchen features stainless steel appliances, including a refrigerator, dishwasher, and microwave, along with a stainless steel gas cooktop. A full-size washer and dryer adds even more convenience to this truly unique home. Whether you're looking for privacy, simplicity, or exceptional value, this one-of-a-kind studio truly is a needle in a haystack. Don't miss this rare opportunity to call it home!
