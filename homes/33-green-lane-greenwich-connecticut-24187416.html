About this home

Bright and spacious 3-bedroom, 2.1-bath townhouse ideally located across from Pemberwick Park. The updated eat-in kitchen features stainless steel appliances, a center island, and abundant cabinetry for extra storage. Hardwood floors span the main level, while the spacious primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, an en-suite bath, and generous walk-in closet space. The lower level provides ample storage along with a dedicated home office or flex space. Enjoy central air, a private deck overlooking a level backyard, and a private driveway with parking for up to three vehicles. Just steps from Pemberwick Park's tennis and basketball courts, baseball fields, playground, and hiking trails. Conveniently located just minutes from The Mill, downtown Glenville's shops and restaurants, Glenville Elementary School, three daycares, and all major commuter routes.
