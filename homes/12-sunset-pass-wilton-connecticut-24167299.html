About this home

Summer Rental! Updated, furnished Colonial offering comfortable living in a convenient Georgetown location. Just minutes to dining, shops, supermarket, and Metro-North, with easy access to a growing local restaurant scene. High ceilings, an updated kitchen, and two full baths, one with a premium Toto smart toilet enhance the home's appeal. Two bedrooms plus a flexible additional room provide ideal space for a den, office, or additional bedroom with ensuite bath. The finished lower level offers a cozy lounge, while ductless split air conditioning provides efficient, zoned cooling throughout. Enjoy a private deck overlooking a tranquil river, along with a fenced backyard and a universal electric vehicle charging station. Owner/Agent.
