About this home

Welcome to 38 Silvermine Avenue, a beautifully maintained Cape Cod home located in the heart of Norwalk. This home offers timeless charm and modern conveniences, featuring stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a spacious formal dining room, perfect for entertaining. The dining room contains a spacious pantry for food storage. Enjoy relaxing mornings in the large enclosed porch, or cozy evenings in the living room with gleaming hardwood floors. The main level includes a spacious walk-in closet, ideal for added convenience. Upstairs, you'll find a sunlit bedroom with abundant storage and a versatile extra room, perfect as a home office or guest retreat. The lower level provides ample usable space, a dedicated laundry area with a sink, and additional potential for a gym, hobby area, or storage. Step outside into the fully fenced backyard, offering privacy and space for gatherings, pets, or gardening. Ideally located just steps from a local park and minutes to SoNo Collection Mall, the Maritime Aquarium, and Norwalk's scenic marinas, this home combines comfort with unbeatable access to amenities. Commuters will love the easy access to the Rt. 7 connector, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway. Plus, you're just around the corner from shopping centers, restaurants, and more. Only 30-40 minutes from New York.
