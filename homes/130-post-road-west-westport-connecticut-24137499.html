About this home

Penthouse residence in the premier Westgate luxury complex with elevator access. Enjoy single-level living in the heart of Westport, directly across from Kings Highway and Saugatuck Elementary playgrounds and athletic fields. A short walk brings you to downtown shops, restaurants, the Levitt Pavilion, and the Westport Library. The open-concept layout features a dramatic living room with 20-foot ceilings and a gas fireplace framed in marble. The gourmet kitchen is equipped with Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Bosch appliances. The primary suite includes a spacious walk-in closet and a spa-style bathroom with a soaking tub and separate shower. Additional features include assigned covered garage parking, high-efficiency HVAC with Wi-Fi thermostat, and a video security system. No pets. No smoking. Excellent credit required.
