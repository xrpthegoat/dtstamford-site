About this home

Move right in to this sun-filled, immaculate, renovated home in a great South Wilton location. Tranquil and very private park like setting on 6.5 acres creates the perfect oasis. The main floor features a spacious and beautiful kitchen with stainless appliances, granite counter tops, subway tile back splash and door leading out to the back deck, perfect for easy outdoor entertaining. Generous sized living and dining rooms with hardwood floors and sliders leading to front deck. Three bedrooms and newly renovated full bathroom complete the main floor. The lower level has a fabulous family room with fireplace and an additional bedroom and full bathroom. Recent improvements include freshly painted interior, new vinyl siding on the exterior, new garage doors and new tile on first floor. Wilton award winning schools, NRVY walking trails, easy access to downtown Wilton and Westport and a great commute. This gem will not last.
