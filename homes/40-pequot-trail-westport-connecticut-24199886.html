About this home

Located in the highly sought after Old Hill Neighborhood - set back from the street on a one acre lot. This is a 4 bedroom colonial with a spacious first floor lay out including a formal living room with a lovely mantled fireplace, formal dining room, with French doors to the sunroom, library or office with bookcases, eat-in kitchen with an island and a relaxed family room featuring a fireplace, vintage wooden ceiling beams and a bow window. The entry foyer and adjacent powder room designed with reclaimed antique marble. Four spacious bedrooms on the second floor. The primary bedroom has a walk in closet and its own full bath, plus access to unfinished attic space with a walk in cedar closet. The property needs some TLC.
