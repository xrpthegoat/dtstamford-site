About this home

Experience exceptional coastal living in Harbor View, East Norwalk's socially active private waterfront community. Just 100 feet from the neighborhood beach and small boat launch, this beautifully updated Colonial offers breathtaking views of the clubs and marinas of East Norwalk, Long Island Sound, and the Norwalk Islands.Designed for entertaining and relaxed waterfront living, the home features an elevator serving all four levels, from the garage entry to the spectacular third floor.The show-stopping third floor includes a three-quarter kitchen, dining area, bar, full bath, and oversized deck where you'll enjoy front-row views of passing boats, stunning sunsets, and star-filled skies.The main level is highlighted by 9+ foot ceilings, generous room sizes, and an open, welcoming flow. The remodeled chef's kitchen showcases Calacatta marble countertops, Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, and two Asko dishwashers. Not to be missed is the custom walnut wet bar and an inviting dining area enhanced by the warmth of a gas fireplace.Thoughtful updates and enhancements throughout the home include refinished hardwood floors, designer lighting, windows, Klar deck doors, custom dining room millwork,a redesigned fireplace hearth, and freshly painted interior and exterior. The resurfaced deck and updated railings further enhance the home's seamless connection to its spectacular waterfront setting. From sunrise over the Sound to evenings under the stars, this is waterfront living at its finest
