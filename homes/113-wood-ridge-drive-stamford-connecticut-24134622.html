About this home

Welcome to this gem of a home that has an incredible sense of balance. Located in the sought-after Mid-Ridges neighborhood, it has an inviting front yard, 3+ bdrms, 2 1/2 baths, a large deck with a hot tub and a flowing back yard. Entry is from a versatile front porch which leads into a large sunken Living Rm, great for family gatherings. It boasts a 12 ft ceiling, a gas fplc, and hdwd floors/crown moldings which are found throughout the home. The kitchen offers a breakfast bar, SS appliances and white cabinetry. Off both rooms is a sunlit Formal DR with a picture window and built ins. The UL includes 2 ample bdrms, a full bath, attic storage, and a Primary with an updated en-suite. The LL has multiple rooms including one with its own half bath that could be a 4th bdrm/office. Another room offers the same possibilities and there is a nook for a small gym. The FR is here as well with its own gas fplc. Through French doors is the deck, also perfect for family gatherings. The deck has a newer hot tub, and multiple canopies. The level back yard has a designated firepit area, fencing, and a shed. For those with a green thumb, there is a full-sun garden area. The bsmt includes a W/D, a workbench, storage and is generally a blank slate for future endeavors. A one car garage has dropdown stairs for extra storage. The electrical bill is greatly reduced by 48 Solar Panels. Estate Sale, being sold "as-is."
