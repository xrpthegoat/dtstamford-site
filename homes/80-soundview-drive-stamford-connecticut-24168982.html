About this home

Spend your best summer yet in this stunning 5-bedroom retreat just steps from a private deeded beach, shared with only 12 homes, at the end of a peaceful cul-de-sac. Perfect for families, friends, or a remote working getaway, this fully furnished and beautifully updated home offers everything you need for a luxurious, laid-back summer on the Long Island Sound. Launch the included paddleboards for a quick trip to a nearby sandy island, or enjoy panoramic Sound views from almost every room. The open-concept main floor features a chef's kitchen, casual living space with a wood-burning fireplace, and dining area that flows out to an expansive deck-complete with an outdoor kitchen, dining space, and lounge areas. The backyard is designed for fun and relaxation: soak in the Jacuzzi, play a game of cornhole or volleyball, gather around the fire pit, or watch your favorite shows on the outdoor TV. Bikes, yard games (Giant Jenga included), and beach gear are all ready for your use. Inside, the first floor includes two bedrooms, a full gym, two full bathrooms, a spacious family room, and a dedicated home office set up for two. Upstairs, you'll find three additional bedrooms, including a suite with French doors opening to a private terrace. The spa-like bathroom includes a soaking tub, steam shower, and sauna. Conveniently located just minutes from Cove Island, Cummings Park, downtown Stamford, and the Metro North train station.
