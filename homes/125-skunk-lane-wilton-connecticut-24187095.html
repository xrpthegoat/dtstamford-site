About this home

Completely reimagined in 2022, this stunning center hall colonial offers the rare combination of timeless architecture and newer everything - making it the turnkey opportunity buyers in Wilton have been waiting for. Set on a level two-acre parcel, this 5-bedroom, 6 1/2-bath home features a fully redesigned kitchen, 2022 installed HVAC/AC, roof, hot water heater, and water softener. Soaring vaulted ceilings, a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, and a spa-inspired primary suite with oversized soaking tub set the tone throughout. The finished lower level adds a home gym, recreation room, and full bath. Hardwood floors, 3-car garage, circular drive, and a stunning stone-walled backyard complete this exceptional property. Walk to the Norwalk River Valley Trail and Ambler Farm; minutes to top-rated schools and Wilton/Cannondale train station. Move-in ready - everything's already been done.
