About this home

Exceptional opportunity to own one the finest waterfront properties in Fairfield County! Welcome to 3 Pratt Island, a 1.4 acre lot with a 200' south-facing private beach. The location comes with an oversized, protected deep water dock with multiple slips. Stunning water views are yours from all angles. Enjoy being lulled to sleep with the sound of lapping waves. The property is nestled in a gorgeous cove which allows you to observe the abundance of wildlife up close. If you would like more room to build your dream compound, there is an adjacent 1 acre lot available as well! Taxes will be calculated once property has been subdivided.
