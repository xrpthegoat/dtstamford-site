About this home

Nestled on a picturesque cul-de-sac, this beautiful move-in-ready colonial sits on a full acre of lush, landscaped grounds, offering the perfect blend of privacy and convenience. Surrounded by greenery yet just minutes from downtown Westport, top-rated schools, and the Metro-North station, this home provides effortless access to all the best the area has to offer. Featuring 4 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and an open-concept kitchen/family room with soaring ceilings and abundant natural light, this residence is designed for both comfort and style. A large formal living room offers additional space for entertaining, while the adjacent formal dining room can easily function as a home office or playroom to suit your needs. The freshly painted interior and exterior, complemented by beautiful hardwood floors throughout, create a crisp, welcoming feel. The finished basement adds valuable extra living space with a half bath and additional closets for ample storage. Bonus features include a whole-home surround sound system and underground sprinklers for easy maintenance of the manicured lawn.Two cozy fireplaces invite you to unwind on cooler evenings, while the expansive deck is perfect for enjoying the stunning backyard during warmer months. Experience the peaceful Westport lifestyle you've always dreamed of in this versatile and elegant home.
