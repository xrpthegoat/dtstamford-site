About this home

Stylish and move-in ready, this 3-bedroom, 2-full bath home is in a quiet Riverside neighborhood just minutes away from Blue Ribbon award winning North Mianus Elementary School and Eastern Middle School. The open layout features a bright living room with a fireplace, a dining area, and sliders to a private deck and level yard. The sleek white kitchen with a bay window overlooking the backyard includes a Sub-Zero fridge, gas cooktop, new microwave, and dishwasher. All bedrooms are on the second floor with generous closets. The lower level offers a spacious playroom/family room, full bath, and laundry. Plenty of storage plus a shed. Bike or short drive to Old Greenwich village, Metro-North, beach and library.
