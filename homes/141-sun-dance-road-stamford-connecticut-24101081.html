About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in mid-ridges, this loving and charming 3-bedroom, 2-bath colonial house offers the serene ambiance of North Stamford with a short drive to downtown Stamford The house features a welcoming foyer leading to a cozy living room with a wood fireplace, perfect for family nights. There is a large family room, dining room, small office/study, patio, and a firepit. The open floor plan has tall ceilings and skylights, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere throughout. The master bedroom is bright and airy, boasting a charming deck where you can unwind and enjoy your morning coffee. Surrounded by a serene backyard, you'll wake up to the peaceful sounds of birds, providing a calming start to your day. This home offers easy and quick access to shopping, groceries, schools, and the Merritt Parkway. Experience the best of both worlds with this charming and loving home.
