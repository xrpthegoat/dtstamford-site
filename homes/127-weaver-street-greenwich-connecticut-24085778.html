About this home

Enjoy this tranquil setting on 3+ acres while being only 5 mins from convenient shopping on The Post Rd or in Glenville center. This 4 bedroom, 3 bath home offers the ease of one floor living and a unique design. Enter through a glass filled linear hallway with expansive views of the surrounding nature, flanked at one end by 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, living/dining room and kitchen, and the other end by a sophisticated modern wing, designed by renown architect Ulrich Franzen, which includes a spacious, light-filled primary suite, dressing room and bath, interior living room and covered outdoor living room and 2 car garage. There are multiple stone patios, a wooden bridge to a gazebo, access to a pathway into the Calhoun Association for extended walking. Dogs allowed with restrictions. Landlord pays for grounds maintenance and snow removal. Address at street says 125 Weaver St.
