About this home

First floor unit in two-family home in "easy" East Norwalk. Easily walkable to MetroNorth and public transportation - and bike-able to beaches, parks and marinas! This well-located property sits on the corner of Emerson and Burr Place and features a large, flat and sunny side/backyard with room to grow. This apartment comprises the full first floor (1,093 sf) and has been freshly painted with all new vinyl flooring in the kitchen, living room and all 3 bedrooms. It also has direct interior access to the full basement (1,093 sf). Access to the unit is either through the common entry foyer at the front porch or separate exterior stairs to the kitchen. There are two paved spots for off-street parking. In-unit laundry.
