About this home

Welcome to this fantastic, move-in ready 3-bedroom, 2.2-bath townhouse-style condo, the perfect blend of comfort and modern living. Featuring the main level, an open-concept layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, the elegant, sunny living room, the galley-style kitchen boasts shaker-style cabinetry, marble countertops, and stainless-steel appliances. The adjacent dining area offers sliders leading to a private deck-perfect for relaxing or enjoying outdoor dining. Upstairs, the spacious primary bedroom offers a private en-suite bathroom, while the second bedroom is conveniently located near the hall bath and laundry closet. The third-floor loft bedroom, complete with its own half bath, provides a versatile space that can be tailored to your needs as a home office, exercise room, guest suite, or playroom. Gleaming hardwood floors throughout, washer and dryer in the unit. The ground-level entrance provides easy access to a 2-car garage with interior entry. Enjoy the best of low-maintenance living in a prime location just moments from everything SoNo has to offer! Convenient to shopping, restaurants, public transportation, and I-95. You don't want to miss this opportunity to own this elegant and spacious townhome. Schedule your viewing before it's gone!
