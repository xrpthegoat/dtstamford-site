About this home

Timeless North Stamford Elegance with Modern Updates & Exceptional Living Space. Sun-filled and spacious colonial set on 1.29 private acres, offering 5 bedrooms and 4 full plus 1 half baths with an ideal open floor plan for everyday living and entertaining. A dramatic two-story entry with sweeping staircase leads to generously sized rooms filled with natural light. The living room with marble fireplace flows into the family room with fieldstone fireplace, custom built-ins, and French doors to the deck. The eat-in kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, center island, and excellent storage, with easy access to outdoor living. A standout feature is the first-floor bedroom or office with a fully renovated bath, perfect for guests or flexible living. A renovated laundry room and direct access from the 2-car garage add convenience. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a marble bath with dual vanities, whirlpool tub, and separate shower, along with three additional bedrooms, including one en-suite, plus a renovated hall bath. The finished lower level provides additional living space for recreation, gym, or media. Recent updates include new roof (2022), gutters, updated HVAC components, renovated baths and laundry, refinished flooring, new carpeting, interior painting, LED lighting, and built-ins. Generator-ready with central vac and security system. Beautiful private setting with usable yard. Move-in ready!
