About this home

Bathed in natural light from morning to evening, this beautifully updated, move-in-ready Cape Cod is tucked away on a desirable cul-de-sac where you can enjoy evening walks, bike rides, and wonderful neighbors. Inside, the living room flows seamlessly into the dining room, creating an easy layout for everyday living and entertaining. The updated kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a peninsula with bar seating. Both bathrooms have been renovated with modern finishes. The spacious primary bedroom includes a peaceful seating area, while two additional bedrooms offer flexibility for guests, a home office, or more. The lower level adds a mudroom, multipurpose room, and generous storage. Step outside to the stunning multi-tier Trex deck overlooking a private, flat, fully fenced backyard - perfect for entertaining and outdoor enjoyment. Recent improvements include Rhino Shield exterior painting with a transferable warranty offering approximately 20 more years of coverage, a new driveway and sidewalk, new stone front walkway, and new hot water heater. Walk to schools, restaurants, and shopping. Metro-North and Long Island Sound are minutes away. Meticulously maintained with central A/C, EV hookup, and major updates completed, this home is both practical and beautiful.
