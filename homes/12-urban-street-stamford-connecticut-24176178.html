About this home

This contemporary gem, located in the sought-after Revonah neighborhood, is tucked into a serene enclave above Urban St. The moment you step inside, you'll be greeted by sunlit interiors and effortless flow. The home features comfortable, thoughtfully designed, well-proportioned gathering spaces that just work. The intuitive floor plan easily adapts to your lifestyle, with versatile spaces that can evolve as your needs do. The living room, dining room and main level office all open to the low-maintenance composite deck. Its private setting is perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails while overlooking the backyard. Meanwhile, the kitchen connects to a side deck, making grilling a breeze. Daily routines fall into place perfectly with the home's proximity to all Stamford has to offer. You'll love being moments away from Stamford's vibrant downtown, offering a rich variety of shopping, dining, and entertainment options. Parks and recreation are just around the corner, and with easy access to Metro North, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, commuting and travel are a cinch.
