About this home

This spectacular Bell Island Fema Compliant waterfront home sits high about the Long Island Sound. Expansive and breathtaking views are enjoyed from every room. Especially the sunrises.... The bright spacious interior features an open Kitchen -Living Room area with a beautiful stone fireplace and a wall of sliders opening up to a bluestone patio with heated pool all overlooking the Sound....The lower level has a yard with a Stone fire pit surrounded by hydrangeas and even below there is a storage room for paddle boards and kayaks....plus your own private beach....You could also have your own deep water mooring right outside your door...There are 4 Bedrooms, 3 full and 1/2 bath, and a full dry basement perfect for storage. This house was totally renovated in 2019...A must see!! Vacation Year Around!!
