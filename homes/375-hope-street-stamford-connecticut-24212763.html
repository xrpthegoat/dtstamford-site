About this home

Experience the comfort and scale of single family living in this exceptionally spacious 2,700+ sq ft townhouse, privately set at the rear of a beautifully maintained garden community in Glenbrook. Spread across two expansive levels, this 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath home showcases high end finishes, abundant natural light, and meticulous care throughout. The main level offers a bright, open layout that is always covered in natural light. A gourmet kitchen anchors the space, featuring a granite island, generous cherry cabinetry, and a dedicated breakfast alcove adjacent to a versatile family/media/office area. The formal living room with bay windows and dining room sit just off the kitchen, creating an ideal flow for both daily meals and entertainment. Upstairs, the master bedroom has a large closet, an en-suite bath, and a private dressing room. There are two additional bedrooms, each spacious and filled with natural light that share a well appointed full hall bath. The finished lower level provides valuable bonus space perfect for a gym, office, or hobby room, and includes the in unit washer/dryer and extra pantry storage. Additional features include central air, natural gas heat, ample storage, and two assigned parking spaces. Residents also enjoy a common patio area ideal for grilling or relaxing in the afternoon sun. A dedicated on site property manager adds convenience and peace of mind. Located on Hope Street in the heart of Glenbrook.
