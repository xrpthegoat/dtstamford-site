About this home

Glenbrook Gem! Beautiful and charming Colonial located on a Corner Lot. This home has been lovingly maintained and owned by the same family for 60 years. Refinished hardwood floors throughout. Lots of natural light. Freshly painted interior. New flooring in kitchen and family room. The family room/possible 4th bedroom is located on the first floor. Built-in cabinets in the dining room add to the charm of this home. Wood-burning fireplace in living room. Gas heat, high-efficiency Navien tankless hot water system. Attic with pull-down stairs and a basement with workbench. A lovely level manicured backyard with a deck and stone patio for your relaxation and enjoyment. Great for commuters: Within 1 mile of Glenbrook train station (Metro North). Come take a look, and get ready to make beautiful memories in your new home.
