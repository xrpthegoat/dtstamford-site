About this home

This peerless estate sits less than one short mile from the Banksville shopping center where the acclaimed gourmet & coffee shop entices locals to linger and chat. A 3-story entry is flooded with natural light from the glass dome above, and open plan living flows seamlessly. Formal living & dining rooms give way to the kitchen/great room with massive central island, wide open dining area, & family room with fireplace. It's the space you'll never leave. The primary suite is a welcome escape with dual baths, walk-in closets & a private balcony overlooking the gorgeous grounds. 3 additional very private en suite bedrooms with custom closets & a laundry room are on this 2nd level. Fully finished lower level with 9' ceilings includes theatre, full sized gym, yoga room, 2 beds/2 baths. Breathtaking level yard and saltwater pool offer resort living at home.
