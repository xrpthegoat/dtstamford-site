About this home

Welcome to this beautifully renovated townhouse tucked away in a private, peaceful community-just moments from all that Old Greenwich has to offer. Step inside to find a bright, open layout that blends modern elegance with comfortable living. The stunning kitchen boasts designer cabinetry, marble countertops, and stainless steel appliances, flowing effortlessly into the inviting living room with custom built-ins, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliding doors that open to a private deck with tranquil pond views-an ideal setting for both everyday living and entertaining. The serene primary suite offers a spacious walk-in closet and a thoughtfully renovated bathroom, creating a perfect personal retreat. The fully finished lower level adds even more flexibility, whether you're looking for a family room, home office, or guest space. A detached garage and additional parking add convenience, while the beautifully maintained grounds enhance the sense of privacy and relaxation. Enjoy the best of coastal Connecticut living with beaches, parks, shops, dining, and train access all just minutes from your door. This is the ideal combination of style, space, and location.
