About this home

Escape to your perfect summer retreat in this stunning colonial home, where comfort, style, and tranquility come together seamlessly. Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this spacious residence offers an ideal blend of privacy and convenience. Step inside and discover generous, light-filled rooms with gleaming hardwood floors throughout. Three fireplaces create a cozy ambiance, perfect for unwinding after a day of summer fun. The home boasts fantastic common spaces for relaxing and entertaining, while the lower level features a fully equipped playroom and gym to keep everyone active and happy. Outside, you'll find a heated pool surrounded by a lush, park-like setting-your own private oasis for sun-soaked afternoons and refreshing swims. Located just moments from main roadways, this retreat offers easy access to nearby shops and restaurants while feeling worlds away from the hustle and bustle. Available as a one-month summer rental, this home promises everything you need to create lasting memories. Don't miss the chance to experience this incredible getaway!
