About this home

Nestled on a quiet, private lane in one of the most desirable neighborhoods in Norwalk, CT., this beautifully maintained four bedroom, two and a half bath colonial offers the perfect blend of suburban tranquility and vibrant coastal living. This lovely home is located in the Cranbury section of Norwalk, consistently ranked as one of Fairfield County's most sought-after places to live. Just a short stroll to Cranbury Park, where you'll enjoy 220 acres of scenic walking trails, formal gardens, open green space for picnics, and a dedicated dog park. The home is zoned for the highly regarded Cranbury Elementary School, recently renovated within the last two years, offering modern facilities and a strong educational foundation for your family. Inside, the home blends comfort and functionality with modern updates, a new dishwasher; new dryer, and newly finished hardwood flooring; freshly painted interior; recessed lighting and crown molding throughout; central air conditioning; sliding doors from dining room leading to a lovely, newly painted deck, perfect for entertaining!; professionally landscaped private backyard framed by freshly painted, white picket fencing offering privacy and charm - a lovely space for a garden!
