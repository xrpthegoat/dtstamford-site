About this home

Come and see this colonial style home boasting five bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, and over 1,850 square feet of living space. As you step inside, you will be greeted by the charm of the home, highlighted by hardwood floors, French doors, arched doorways, and an abundance of natural light filtering through. The main level offers a seamless flow with a spacious living room, dining room, and kitchen. The second level features 3 large bedrooms and the full bathroom. Finished attic perfect for an office or bonus room. The fully finished basement includes multiple rooms, a bedroom, and a full bathroom, providing additional living space and ample storage. Outside, there is a patio ideal for entertaining, along with a fenced backyard perfect for outdoor activities and entertaining guests. Parking is convenient with an extended driveway and detached garage. Nearby amenities include Santaniello Park, Lockwood-Matthews Mansion Museum, and various restaurants.
