About this home

Welcome to 63 Downs Avenue, a move-in ready ranch located in Stamford's sought-after Shippan neighborhood, just minutes from the beach. This home combines thoughtful updates with a comfortable, functional layout, all within one of Stamford's most desirable coastal communities. On the outside you will find a well manicured front yard with a freshly paved driveway leading to a detached two car garage. This move-in ready home features an updated eat-in kitchen, two bedrooms, and two full baths. Just off the kitchen, the spacious living room benefits from the home's open-concept layout, allowing natural light to fill the space. The finished basement has interior and exterior entrances and hosts a kitchenette making it a flexible space for a play room, home office, or in-law suite. Recent upgrades have been made to ensure worry-free living. These include a new central air system (2024), furnace (2023), roof (2020) ,and vinyl siding (2020). These updates provide peace of mind and reduce the need for major maintenance expenses in the years to come.The backyard is private and level, offering plenty of room for entertaining and relaxing. A spacious deck extends your living area outdoors, making it an ideal spot for barbecues and summer gatherings. Location is a key highlight of 63 Downs Avenue. Living in Shippan means you're also close to Stamford's other beaches, parks, and recreational areas. The neighborhood is known for its friendly atmosphere and easy access to local restaurants
