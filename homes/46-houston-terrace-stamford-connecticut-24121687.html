About this home

Experience the perfect blend of coastal living and convenience in this beautifully maintained 4-bedroom, 2-bath colonial. Situated in the desirable Cove neighborhood on the Darien border, this home is just a short walk from the beach and minutes from I-95 and Route 1. Inside, the sunny and spacious main level features gleaming hardwood floors that flow through a large living room and a formal dining room, perfect for entertaining. The versatile floor plan also includes a large eat-in kitchen, an adjacent sunroom, and the outstanding convenience of a first-floor bedroom and a full bathroom. Upstairs, three additional spacious bedrooms await, including an oversized primary suite with a walk-in closet, along with the second full bathroom. The finished lower level provides even more flexible space for a home office or gym. For your convenience, this unit includes an in-unit washer and dryer, a dedicated patio, use of a grassy yard area for a grill, and two dedicated off-street parking spots located right at the back door.
