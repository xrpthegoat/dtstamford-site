About this home

Showstopper in Westover! Presenting like new construction, this young Colonial has been completely renovated & transformed. Stunning kitchen w/center island, quartz countertops, subway tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances (gas cooking) & spacious dining area. All new, gorgeous bathrooms, too. Primary BR w/spacious ensuite bath (double-sink vanity) & large walk-in closet (custom built-ins). UL also has great option for home office or 4th BR. Finished LL serves perfectly as rec room or den w/high ceilings, impressive storage area, 1/2 bath & laundry room. Other highlights: new windows, energy-efficient PROPANE heat & C/A, freshly painted, recessed lighting, hardwood floors, plenty of storage, all closets w/built-ins & lighting plus attached 2-car garage. Incredibly central location! 1/2mi to schools, tennis & Table 104 (a local fave); just over 1mi to Merritt & Whole Foods (as well as Starbucks/Frank Pepes/Lennys/The Fed & so many other great restaurants, eateries & conveniences); 1 to 3mi to FIVE main retail centers (w/Trader Joe's/Stop & Shop/Grade A & more, grocer choices abound), several more top-rated restaurants, the city's best parks, 2 public golf courses, equestrian, library, UCONN & downtown entertainment; & just over 3mi to Mianus River trails, RR & I-95! Bbq on the back patio, play in the yard or read a book & unwind on the front porch - settle in today & enjoy the space & comfort of the country w/the ultimate convenience of a city. Lawn maintenance included!
