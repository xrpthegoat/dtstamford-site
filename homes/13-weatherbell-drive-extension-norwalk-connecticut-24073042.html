About this home

Sellers are requesting Highest and Best offers by Monday 3/3 at 12pm. Mid-Century Modern Masterpiece w/Legal In-Law Apartment! Nestled on a cul-de-sac in Norwalk's Cranbury neighborhood, this fully renovated contemporary home blends modern design w/ functional living. Recent updates include brand-new, never-used refinished hardwood floors, a new roof, skylights, new septic tank, solar panels for energy efficiency, plus a recent energy audit to maximize sustainability. The main level features an open-concept design with expansive windows. A vaulted living and dining room offers two-sided access to a wraparound deck w/ cable railing. The chef's kitchen has two full-size Sub-Zero refrigerators, a gas cooktop, large granite island, & pantry, opening to the family room & expansive screened-in porch. The primary suite boasts two walk-in closets & an updated en-suite bath. Three additional spacious bedrooms & full bath complete the main floor. The legal, above-ground in-law/au pair apartment, currently rented for $2,550 per month, includes a gourmet kitchen, living room, full bath, private entrance, and separate driveway, offering an income opportunity or multi-generational living. The lower level features a flexible home office/bonus space with a full renovated bath & access to a slate patio for outdoor gatherings. Conveniently located near Westport & Wilton borders, offers easy access to Merritt Parkway, I-95, & Metro-North, plus top dining, shopping, & CT's shoreline.
