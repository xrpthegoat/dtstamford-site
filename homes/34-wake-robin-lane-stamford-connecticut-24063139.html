About this home

This stunning Colonial-style home offers 3559 square feet of beautifully finished living space, blending style and energy efficiency. Features include Andersen oversized casement windows, multi-lock doors, and a Thermatru insulated front door. The exterior boasts low-maintenance brick/masonry siding, while the interior showcases oak and maple hardwood floors, travertine stone, and ceramic tile. The modern kitchen includes re-finished wood cabinets, new quartz "Cullacata" countertops, KRAUS stainless steel sinks, and high-end Samsung bespoke appliances. The primary suite features a custom walk-in closet, dual vanity, glass shower, and jacuzzi tub. Two natural wood fireplaces, composite maintenance-free decking, and an oversized 3-car garage with maintenance-free flooring complete this luxurious home. Additional features include LED lighting, a 4-zone heating/cooling system, and a Buderus boiler.
