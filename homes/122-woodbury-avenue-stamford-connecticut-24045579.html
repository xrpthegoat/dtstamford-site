About this home

***Highest and Best due by 11am Tuesday 9/17/24*** This beautifully updated and well-loved Cape-style home, nestled in the heart of Springdale, exudes charm and warmth at every turn. Step into the bright, cozy family room, where a wood-burning fireplace creates the perfect setting for those crisp New England evenings. The newly refinished hardwood floors throughout the home add a timeless elegance to the space, inviting you to relax and unwind. Just beyond, the galley kitchen is both functional and welcoming, leading to a separate dining room with ample space to host family celebrations and gatherings with ease. The main level also features a full bathroom and two versatile rooms, ready to adapt to your needs-whether it's a guest room, a home office, or a playroom, the possibilities are endless. Upstairs, the second floor offers a peaceful retreat with a third bedroom and a spacious master bedroom, complete with a generous walk-in closet-perfect for all your storage needs. Additionally, the home boasts a wonderful deck, perfect for grilling or unwinding while you take in views of the private backyard. With an irrigation system already in place, you can enjoy a lush lawn and thriving gardens all summer long, without the hassle of watering. This home offers the ideal blend of charm, comfort, and versatility, waiting for you to make it your own!
