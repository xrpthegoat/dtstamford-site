About this home

Set back from the road and set on two level acres, this stately all-brick colonial feels private yet perfectly connected to everyday conveniences. With nearly 6,300 sq ft of finished living space across four levels, the home offers room for everyone, including a separate entrance to a flexible in-law or guest suite. Inside, the warmth of hardwood flooring carries through every room. The heart of the home is the modern kitchen with a six-seat island, quartz countertops, Wolf range, and French doors opening to the deck and backyard-perfect for easy indoor-outdoor living. The kitchen flows into the family room, where a wood-burning fireplace creates a cozy gathering spot. Upstairs, five bedrooms and multiple flexible spaces adapt to whatever life brings-whether that's a home office, playroom, or art studio. The walk-out lower level expands the possibilities, offering space for exercise, music, hobbies, or simply casual fun. Just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, nearby parks, shops, and dining along High Ridge Road-and a short drive to downtown New Canaan-this home combines privacy, comfort, and convenience, bringing together space, warmth, and location in a way that truly feels like home
