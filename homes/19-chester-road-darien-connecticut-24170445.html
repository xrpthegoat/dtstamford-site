About this home

This storybook charmer sits on a rare half acre of flat, dry land directly abutting Woodland Park, creating a setting that feels both private and enchanting. Summer entertaining comes naturally here; think firepit conversations, dinners on the patio, and long evenings surrounded by nature. The four-bedroom, two-and-a-half bath layout offers great flow, with the kitchen opening to the family room and a classic dining and living room arrangement anchored by a fireplace. All of this in one of Darien's most beloved neighborhoods, walkable to schools, train, and shopping.
