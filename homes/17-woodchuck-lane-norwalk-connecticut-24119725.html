About this home

Welcome to this charming 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath raised ranch in Norwalk, set on over a 3/4 acre of level land with stunning views of Keeler's Brook right in your backyard. Located near Rowayton, SoNo, and I-95, this home provides both convenience and tranquility. Enjoy outdoor living with a spacious deck and a large backyard. Spanning across 2,128 square feet, this home features a unique curved staircase, hardwood floors throughout the main level, and a cozy fireplace on the lower level. With a newer roof, attached 2 car garage, and fully finished basement, this property is perfect for both entertaining and everyday living. Don't miss the opportunity to make this your home!
