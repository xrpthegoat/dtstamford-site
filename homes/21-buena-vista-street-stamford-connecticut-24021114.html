About this home

Welcome to this charming vintage colonial nestled in a peaceful Springdale neighborhood. Step inside to find beautiful wood flooring and arched doorways, adding warmth and character. The inviting living room boasts a cozy gas fireplace, a large bay window, and French doors that lead to a sunroom or office, offering the perfect spot to relax with a book or enjoy your morning coffee. Entertain in style in the formal dining room, complete with built-in corner China cabinets. The eat-in kitchen features wood cabinets, granite counters, and stainless steel appliances, including a Viking gas range. Easy access to the deck makes al fresco dining a breeze. Unwind in the spacious family room, featuring paneled walls, beamed ceilings, wood-burning fireplace, built-in bookcases, powder room and access to the deck and yard, making this space perfect for both relaxation and entertainment. Upstairs, discover four bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with an en-suite bath featuring a glass-enclosed shower. Let's step outside to the deck with its removable awning, offering a tranquil retreat to relax, and enjoy picturesque views of the level, private backyard with splashes of color from the canopy of Wisteria, pink rose shrubs and flowering Rhododendrons. Conveniently located, this home is just a short walk to the Springdale train station, restaurants and shops. Recent upgrades include roof, HVAC, irrigation system and more.
