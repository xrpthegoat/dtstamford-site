About this home

Welcome to this exquisite property situated on a peaceful cul-de-sac, just minutes away from town and the train station. This 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom center hall colonial home is perfectly nestled on 2+ acre setting, offering both tranquility and seclusion. Surrounded by mature trees and overlooking a serene stream, this home provides the perfect sanctuary for relaxation. The backyard is a retreat with its large deck and flat, fully fenced-in space, ensuring your utmost enjoyment and privacy. Step inside, and you'll be greeted by a warm and inviting kitchen with a casual dining area. The oversized living room with a fireplace, creating a cozy ambiance. The bright sunroom boasts high ceilings and large windows, allowing you to immerse yourself in the beauty of nature. Hosting gatherings and creating cherished memories is effortless in the dining room. In need of a versatile space? The office-den, complete with its own fireplace, offers endless possibilities to suit your lifestyle. Additionally, the first-floor 5th bedroom adds even more flexibility to accommodate your needs. Upstairs, the primary suite features a walk-in closet and a full bath, providing a private haven for relaxation. Three additional bedrooms offer ample space for family or guests. The finished lower level, 2 car garage, and abundant storage space ensure that practicality is never compromised. Don't let this opportunity slip away - make this home your dream haven.
