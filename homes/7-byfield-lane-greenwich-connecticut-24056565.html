About this home

Picture yourself in a boat on a pond, with tangerine trees and marmalade skies. 7 Byfield Lane, set on nearly three acres in beautiful Mid-Country Greenwich, with five bedrooms, four and a half bathrooms, a pool, and a hot tub offers you a day in the life every day. In 1973, Yoko Ono and John Lennon rowed their own yellow submarine peacefully along a sea of green when they came to visit this home for about an hour and sailed around the pond in the dingy that sits there to this day. Across the universe, this property stands out like a diamond in the sky. Imagine a cozy living room where you can come together with loved ones. Be a day tripper to the city with the Merritt (and the "Ave") minutes away yet still quiet enough to whisper words of wisdom. In the end, you'll realize, all you need is love, and this house is your golden slumbers. Dream away. While it needs TLC and updating, with a little help from your friends, you can make this house a part of your long and winding road to happiness. If you desired, you could start from scratch and have a glorious 6000+ sqft home or you could move in immediately as it currently stands and just, "let it be."
