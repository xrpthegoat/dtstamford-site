About this home

Welcome to 73 Maitland Road, a stunning 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath home in Stamford that's been completely reimagined for modern living. The main level offers two bedrooms, a brand-new kitchen with quartz countertops and soft-close cabinetry, and sliders that open to a brand-new Trex deck overlooking a private flat backyard-perfect for entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious primary suite with a beautifully renovated ensuite bath, additional bedrooms, and the convenience of laundry nearby. Every major system has been upgraded-new Andersen windows, new roof, new HVAC, propane heating, updated electrical, and new plumbing. With fully renovated bathrooms throughout, public water and sewer, and an ideal location just minutes from Cove Island Park, downtown Stamford, I-95, and Metro-North stations in Darien or Glenbrook, this home delivers the perfect blend of thoughtful design, modern finishes, and everyday convenience.
