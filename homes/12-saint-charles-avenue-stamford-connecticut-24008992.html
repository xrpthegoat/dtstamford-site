About this home

Your dream home awaits! This adorable Colonial has so many wonderful features. Located on an adorable Springdale street close to every convenience. Home improvement enthusiasts will love the opportunity this home presents to customize and modernize to their personal taste. The main living level consists of a formal living room with fireplace, a bedroom, office, eat in kitchen and a massive family room with a second fireplace and cedar closet. There is a full bath on this level as well allowing for one floor living if you choose. Upstairs are two additional spacious bedrooms with great closet space and a second full bath. There is tremendous storage in the attic as well which can be accessed from two different areas of the home. Hardwood floors are believed to be under all carpeting other than the family room. The basement presents additional opportunity for the discerning buyer. Several rooms and components for another full bath are already in place making this an ideal space for a potential in law or au pair suite with access from both inside and outside the home. The yard is fully fenced and there is even a pet kennel beside the detached 1 car garage. Economical gas heat. Home is being sold as is and is in trust therefore no disclosures.
