About this home

Move in this summer, enjoy the pool & be settled in time for the new school year... this wonderful custom home is a pure delight and is ready to welcome you. Set on over an acre of tranquil property in one of Westport's few true neighborhoods of quiet streets, this friendly area is within walking distance of the Elementary & Middle Schools, offers commuting convenience plus easy access to the vibrant downtown. A sweeping circular driveway ushers you to this architect re-designed home, which was expanded & remodeled in 2005. The impressive front entrance brings you into a double-height foyer showcasing distinctive millwork. High ceilings, spacious proportions & abundant natural light create a comfortable atmosphere. DR w/built-ins has the 1st of 3 fireplaces. LR w/coffered ceiling is a beautiful space to entertain or enjoy views through oversized windows. The gourmet kitchen w/lrg center island, vast counter space & a view to the pool has a breakfast area & opens directly to the fam rm w/built-ins & stone fireplace. The sunroom will be one of your favorite areas, w/doors to a covered porch as well as the deck & pool. The grand primary suite begins w/an office, on to a lrg sitting rm w/fpl, & through to the king-sized BR w/vltd ceil and on to a lux bathrm plus two very lrg walk-in clsts. The remaining BRs are each spacious and include a 2nd priv suite. Great laundry rm. Gym, poss. 5th BR or cabana w/full bth & addl space for rec rm. Heating/cooling new (3yrs), generator.
