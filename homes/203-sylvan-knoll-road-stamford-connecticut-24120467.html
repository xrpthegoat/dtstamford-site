About this home

Diamond in the rough! Welcome to this 2 bedroom townhouse in the desirable section 2 part of the Sylvan Knoll complex. Tucked away at the back of a courtyard, this home has a peaceful setting surrounded by lots of green space and mature plantings. With bright east/west exposure, the unit offers plenty of natural light throughout the day. This is the perfect home for someone who is willing to do some updating. The tastefully renovated bathroom is move-in ready, while the kitchen presents an opportunity for the next owner to create their own dream space. Original hardwood floors add character and will shine beautifully when refinished. Out back, there's room to put in a private patio - just remove a few shrubs and enjoy outdoor living at your back door.
