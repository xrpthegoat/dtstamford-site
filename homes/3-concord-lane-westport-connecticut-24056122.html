About this home

Welcome to 3 Concord Lane! This unique and fabulous contemporary home is waiting for you! With 2 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, this entertaining home is perfect for those who love to host guests. The professional Chef's Kitchen is a standout feature, complete with granite counters, a 6 burner Professional Grade BlueStar range, additional electric oven, large pantry, and stainless steel appliances. The stunning porcelain floors throughout the main floor add a touch of elegance to the space. The symmetrical layout of the home is flooded with natural light, making it a bright and welcoming environment. The home overlooks a Japanese garden backyard with a Koi pond and waterfall feature, adding a peaceful and serene touch to the outdoor space. This nautical house features the living room and family room on the second floor, along with the primary bedroom and bathroom that overlook the fully fenced gardens. The location of the home is also ideal, with close proximity to the Westport/Saugatuck train station, I95, Compo Beach, shopping, and restaurants. Don't miss out on this move-in ready home that offers a perfect blend of modern amenities and unique design elements. Welcome to 3 Concord Lane - your dream home awaits!
