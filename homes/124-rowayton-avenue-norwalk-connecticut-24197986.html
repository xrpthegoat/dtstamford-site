About this home

Wonderful Rowayton home in the heart of the village! This sunny and inviting rental is literally steps from the vibrant village and all it has to offer. Open floor plan, newly renovated kitchen, spacious living room, family room and dining on the first floor. The second floor offers a lovely primary suite, full bath w soaking tub and shower, 2 huge closets for the most demanding closet lovers, plus two additional bedrooms and hall bath. The back yard oasis has a beautiful in-ground pool, patio with seating and lush evergreens offering total privacy. Enjoy seasonal water views of 5 Mile River from many rooms. Full basement with workout space and storage, 1 car garage. Come experience the casual Rowayton life style with beaches, restaurants, parks, library, sports and so much more. An easy commute to NYC on Metro North. Rented furnished or unfurnished, credit report and references required.
