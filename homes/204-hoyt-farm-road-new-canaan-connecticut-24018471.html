About this home

Discover this stylish and freshly updated bright and spacious Contemporary/Colonial situated on 2 park-like acres and located in very desirable Hoyt Farm neighborhood. A statement two-story foyer with vaulted ceiling welcomes you to this tastefully appointed home featuring an open floor plan highlighted with a newly updated modern eat-in kitchen that includes quartz counters, a wine/coffee bar, brand new appliances throughout and sliders to an oversized deck. Main level also features formal and informal living, working and entertaining spaces including a step-down living room with fireplace, formal dining room, family room with fireplace plus a separate study. Upstairs features the private master bedroom suite and three bedrooms with two additional full baths. Many opportunities remain for the next owner of this special home with over 2000 unfinished sqft in the walkout lower level and a backyard perfect for a pool, sports court, or putting green . All this convenient to schools, train, parks and New Canaan town center.
