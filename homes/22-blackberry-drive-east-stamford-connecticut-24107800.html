About this home

Welcome home to this award-winning contemporary ranch, perfectly situated on a serene cul-de-sac. Soaring vaulted ceilings and walls of windows through-out flood the home with natural light, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living. Upon entering the dramatic two-story entry foyer, you'll find the spacious living room with fireplace and a formal dining room-ideal for entertaining. The well-appointed kitchen features a Sub-Zero refrigerator, double ovens and generous storage. A show-stopping 1,100 sq ft skylit great room offers the ultimate gathering space with multiple sliders to a deck that overlooks the peaceful natural setting. Separate bedroom wing includes a primary bedroom with full bath plus two additional bedrooms a hall bath and plenty of closet space. The lower level offers a rec/playroom with fireplace, access to the 2 car garage and a large storage area. A truly special home that combines thoughtful design with a tranquil lifestyle.
