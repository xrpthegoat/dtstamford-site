About this home

Highest and best by 3:00 today, 9/8. Discover the perfect blend of vintage charm and modern functionality in this delightful two-bedroom bungalow in the desirable Bettswood neighborhood. This home welcomes you with a warm living room that flows seamlessly into the kitchen and dining room, creating an ideal space for both everyday living and entertaining. The heart of the home features an eat-in kitchen complemented by a unique butler's pantry-a rare find that adds both storage and functionality. Throughout the home, you'll discover delightful nooks and crannies that offer endless possibilities for creative storage or cozy reading spots. The full basement provides plenty of additional storage space, while the detached two-car garage ensures convenience year-round. Step outside to your private sanctuary-a fabulous patio perfect for al fresco dining overlooking a private backyard that offers a peaceful retreat from the bustling world. Bring your green thumb as the garden shed, with electric and water hook up, is perfect for all of your garden needs. Location couldn't be more convenient, with easy access to shopping and major roadways.
