About this home

Welcome to 109 Highland Road, a 1-bedroom, 1.5-bath condo in the inviting Forest Mews community of Stamford, CT. Built in 1981, this 965 sq ft home blends fresh upgrades with a prime location, steps from the city's vibrant core. Step into an open layout with gleaming engineered hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and a fully renovated kitchen featuring stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, custom cabinetry, a stylish custom backsplash, and ample storage perfect for cooking or entertaining. The bedroom includes a fully renovated bath, with a walk-in shower, re-tiled floor and heat lamps. Stay comfortable with all-new HVAC, hot water heater (2023), AC unit, heat pump and furnace (2017). Relax on two private balconies from either the bedroom or living room ideal for entertaining guests or quiet evenings. Included in the living room is a wood burning fireplace where you can cozy up and put on your favorite movie or show! Community perks include off-street parking, gated parking garage, private entry, and well-kept grounds, with HOA covering water, sewer, and maintenance for effortless living. Minutes from Stamford's Metro-North station (40-min to NYC), 5 minute walk to downtown restaurants and shops. Book your private showing today!
