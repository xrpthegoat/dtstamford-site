About this home

Nestled on a quiet street in the desirable Wolfpit area of Norwalk, 27 Fairweather Drive is a wonderfully maintained raised ranch offering comfort, flexibility, and charm. This inviting home features 3 spacious bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms, providing an ideal layout for both everyday living and entertaining. The main level is bright and welcoming, while the lower level offers a versatile basement-perfect for a home office, guest room, or additional living space. A one-car garage adds convenience, and the home's thoughtful upkeep is evident throughout. Step outside to enjoy the great back porch, an ideal spot for relaxing or hosting gatherings while overlooking the peaceful surroundings. A fantastic opportunity to own a well-cared-for home in a sought-after Norwalk neighborhood.
