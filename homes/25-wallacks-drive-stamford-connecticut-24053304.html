About this home

Welcome to 25 Wallacks Drive, Stamford, CT-where luxury meets comfort in this lovely 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom Colonial home. Spanning an impressive 5,700 square feet, this residence is nestled on a quiet street in a small waterfront community. The beautifully landscaped lot offers privacy and sanctuary, as well as a beautiful heated salt water gunite pool and outdoor entertaining area with propane fire pit. Step inside to find elegant hardwood floors, a formal dining room perfect for entertaining, and a chef's kitchen equipped with a cooktop, double oven, range hood, and wine cooler. The primary suite features a spacious walk-in closet and a luxurious en-suite bath. Enjoy the convenience of an attached garage with an automatic door, central AC, and a security system for peace of mind. The over-garage area has a gym and bonus area. House is available furnished. Available for a 12/1/24 move in!
