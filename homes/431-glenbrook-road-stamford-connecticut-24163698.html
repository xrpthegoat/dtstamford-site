About this home

Don't miss this beautifully updated three-level townhouse located in a charming neighborhood just 0.3 miles from the train station and within walking distance to shopping and restaurants. The main floor features a bright and sunny living room and an eat-in kitchen equipped with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and sliding doors that lead to a private deck overlooking the backyard. A full-size laundry area and a convenient half bath complete this level. Upstairs, you'll find generously sized bedrooms along with a full bathroom. The lower level offers a large room with sliding doors that open to a cozy backyard-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Additional highlights include hard-surface flooring throughout, gas heating and cooking, central air conditioning, an attached garage, a reserved parking spot, and visitor parking on the premises. The owner covers trash, sewer, water, landscaping, and snow removal. Tenant is responsible for gas and electricity. Good credit is required. Multi-year lease will be considered.
