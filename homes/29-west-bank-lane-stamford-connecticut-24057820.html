About this home

Welcome to 29 West Bank Lane in desirable Westover. This 4 bedroom/3.5 bath colonial has a wonderful flow for easy living and entertaining. Well equipped designer renovated eat-in-kitchen with stunning cherry cabinets and granite counters will delight the serious home chef. Steps from the kitchen is the oversized dining room to host all of your holiday events. Generous sized living room, family room with fireplace and half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts 3 closets and a private full bath. 3 additional ample sized bedrooms and another full bath add to this home on a sought after street. Need to work from home? The finished lower level can accommodate your remote work needs in addition to a playroom, media room or gym. Another full bath, laundry, and sliders to lovely park-like backyard bordering the Mianus River. Minutes from downtown Stamford and all it has to offer: restaurants, shopping, entertainment, and so much more. Make your homeownership dreams a reality. House being sold "as-is".
