About this home

Spend the summer in beautiful Connecticut by the pool, or enjoy beaches and boating on the Long Island Sound, just 6 miles away from this elegant and stately 7-bedroom New Canaan estate with a refined elegance for sophisticated yet comfortable living. The versatile spaces, unique design elements and thoughtful layout are perfect for casual gatherings or elegant, formal entertaining. The property boasts a brand new pool and gazebo, sweeping lawns and gardens, gracious covered porches, and expansive blue stone terraces. Designed by premier builder Alex Kaali-Nagy and decorated by Lynn Morgan, this exceptional West Road residence has unparalleled attention to detail. Luxurious amenities include a full house generator, three-floor elevator access, seven en- suite bedrooms, large chefs eat-in kitchen, wet bar, main and upper floor family rooms, and a lower-level gym, This impeccable beauty should not be missed.
