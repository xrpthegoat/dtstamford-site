About this home

Custom built and well-maintained Ranch located in the "Old Hill" section of town, just minutes to Downtown Westport & Saugatuck dining and shopping, Route 1, Whole Foods, Stew Leonard's, I-95, the Merritt Parkway and Metro North. Large open living room/dining room combo with fireplace, flows directly into the bright and sunny kitchen/family room combo with second fireplace and sliders to the rear stone patio, making entertaining a breeze. The Primary bedroom suite is handicap accessible with a large bathroom with an open shower concept, dressing room and rear sliders to the patio as well. In the lower level, you will find a large playroom, 5th bedroom (or office) and full bath with built-in sauna.
