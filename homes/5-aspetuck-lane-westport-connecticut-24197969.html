About this home

Please submit all offers by Tuesday (9/1) at 5 pm. Rare Opportunity to own two private acres on the Aspetuck River in Westport. Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this gracious Colonial offers an extraordinary blend of privacy, natural beauty and relaxed country living. Set on two spectacular acres, the property extends to and beyond the banks of the river, creating a truly unique waterfront retreat. A natural riverside setting invites fishing, kayaking, and peaceful moments by the water's edge, where the soothing sounds of the flowing river provide a constant connection to nature. Mature trees frame the landscape, while a private tennis court enhances the exceptional recreational appeal and creates a remarkable park-like setting. Designed for both comfortable living and effortless entertaining, the home features a spacious living room, an elegant dining room with handsome built-ins, a den with dry bar, and a family room with FP. The thoughtfully appointed kitchen includes a farmhouse sink and Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances. There is a generator for your peace of mind. An important recent improvement is a new roof installed approximately two years ago. Whether enjoying morning coffee on the screened porch overlooking the water, entertaining guests on the deck, playing tennis, launching a kayak, or unwinding beside the river at day's end, this home offers an exceptional lifestyle and rare opportunity to experience nature's beauty.
