About this home

Architecturally stunning six-bedroom Colonial graces 2.29 level acres designed for resort-style living with pool, pool house, outdoor shower, stone terrace with kitchen & garden masterfully built in 2014. Beautifully appointed 6,117 sq ft interior brimming with exquisite details including high coffered ceilings, transom topped doorways & box molding. Striking double height foyer introduces ideal floor plan, perfect for everyday living & entertaining, anchored by chef's marble-clad kitchen which opens to large family room with fireplace flanked by doors to magnificent screened porch with industrial grade heaters & fireplace. Elegant dining room with fully equipped butler's pantry, stylish living room & office both with fireplaces, mudroom & two powder rooms round out this level. Sumptuous primary suite with fireplace, his/her walk-in closets, deluxe bath with radiant heated floors; two en-suite bedrooms, two bedrooms with Jack/Jill bath, homework area, laundry room comprise 2nd floor. Generous lower-level boasts media room with projector, game room, wine room with wine refrigerator, bedroom, full bath & generator. Unfinished top floor features ample storage & unlimited potential. Outdoor Kalamazoo gourmet kitchen is fitted with grill, pizza oven, smoker & kegerators. Incredible pool house (add'l 860 sq ft) includes custom kitchenette, half bath with outdoor shower & loft. Ideally located on professionally landscaped, private lot with fenced-in yard near all Darien offers.
