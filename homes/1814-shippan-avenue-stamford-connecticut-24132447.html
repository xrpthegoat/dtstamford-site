About this home

Colonial home situated in Shippan Point, a desirable Long Island Sound neighborhood known for its friendly atmosphere and community engagement. This 3-bedroom colonial home, complete with 2.5 baths, a full basement, and situated on a level .33-acre lot, offers a perfect blend of comfort, space, and charm in Stamford's Long Island Sound neighborhood. Shippan has maintained its charm and timeless beauty for many generations. This home's updated kitchen features Sub-Zero refrigerator, Wolfe Gas Range and Hood, Bosch Dishwasher and Center Island plus dining area. When you visit, you will see the residents walking their dogs, jogging along the avenue and enjoying the vibrant waterfront lifestyle. Shopping centers, restaurants, and local markets are within a short drive, as is downtown Stamford. The second-floor features three bedrooms, family bathroom, and a primary suite with en-suite bath. The unfinished Basement provides extra storage or recreational space. The professionally well-maintained landscaping adds to its curb appeal. The rear level yard is suitable for outdoor activities, gardening, or entertaining. Driveway and garage provide convenient parking. Shopping centers, restaurants, and local markets are within a short drive. Close the Stamford Transportation Hub and I-95, makes commuting to nearby cities or New York City more manageable. Located minutes from The Stamford Yacht Club, Shippan Point's private beaches, Cummings Park Beach, and Cove Island Park.
