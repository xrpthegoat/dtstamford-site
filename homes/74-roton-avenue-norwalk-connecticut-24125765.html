About this home

Welcome to 74 Roton Ave, a stunning, fully renovated home in a prime Rowayton location. Reimagined from the ground up, every detail has been thoughtfully crafted for modern luxury living. Just steps from Bayley Beach and Roton Point, and a short walk to Rowayton's vibrant downtown with waterfront dining, boutique shops, and Pinkney Park - home to the Farmers Market and beloved town events. With serene views of Farm Creek and access to scenic nature trails, this location is truly unmatched. Offering 5 bedrooms and 4 full baths across nearly 3,900 sqft of finished living space, this home perfectly blends elegance and comfort. The main floor showcases a chef's kitchen with marble countertops, a Vermont black granite island, Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, a full butler's pantry, and a sunlit dining area with sliding doors to the back patio. A first-floor bedroom and full bath provide flexibility for guests or added living space. Upstairs, you'll find the primary suite and three additional bedrooms, three full baths, and a conveniently located laundry room. The spacious third floor offers endless possibilities with added space for a sixth bedroom, office, or recreation room. An additional 1,100 sqft of unfinished lower-level space offers versatility for storage, an at-home gym, or other practical uses. A rare opportunity to own a masterfully designed residence in one of Rowayton's most coveted coastal neighborhoods.
