About this home

**All offers due by 5pm Monday, 11/3** Welcome to "The Palace" at 433 High Ridge Road, a cherished Stamford residence lovingly owned by the same family for over 80 years. Steeped in history and warmth, this classic 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath colonial has been thoughtfully refreshed to blend timeless character with modern comfort. Inside, you'll find freshly painted interiors, beautiful hardwood floors and updated bathroom vanities that bring a clean, inviting feel throughout. The spacious layout offers sun filled living and dining areas perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Outside, a detached 2 car garage provides ample storage and convenience, while the on-demand generator ensures peace of mind. The expansive yard is ideal for PICNICS or gatherings large and small - a true outdoor retreat where generations have come together and new memories await to be made. The exterior has been refreshed with new front and back doors and shutters. Perfectly located near Stamford's bustling downtown and commuting routes, "The Palace" is a rare opportunity to own a piece of local history that's ready for its next chapter.
