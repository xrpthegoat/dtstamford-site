About this home

Nestled at the end of a serene cul-de-sac, this elegant Center Hall Colonial offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience. Boasting six spacious bedrooms and 4 1/2 bathrooms, this home is designed for both entertaining and everyday living. The main level features an inviting bedroom with a full bath, perfect for guests or multi-generational living. The heart of the home is the large kitchen that offers picturesque views of the beautifully landscaped backyard. Adjacent to the kitchen, a cozy family room/ den with a fireplace provides the ideal space for gathering on chilly winter evenings. With generously sized recreational rooms, this home offers plenty of options for relaxation, hobbies, or entertainment. The private, flat yard is a true oasis, perfect for outdoor activities, gardening, or simply unwinding. Located in a well-established community, this home is just moments from top-rated schools, shopping, and major highways, making daily errands and commuting a breeze. Whether you're hosting family gatherings or enjoying a quiet evening by the fire, this home provides an ideal balance of space, style, and convenience.
