About this home

Like new and pristine 445 Flax Hill Road, Norwalk, CT-a stunning modern colonial built in 2018. Spanning 3700 sq. ft., this exquisite home offers four spacious bedrooms and four luxurious bathrooms, including a master suite with a spa-style bath and custom walk-in closets. Enter through a dramatic foyer to find a formal living room adorned with detailed moldings and a gas fireplace with granite accents. Entertain in style in the elegant dining room adjacent to a gourmet kitchen, featuring high-end cabinetry, a butler's pantry, and Carrara marble countertops. The Family Room also boasts soaring ceilings and seamless indoor-outdoor living with French doors leading to a sweeping wrap-around deck, perfect for al fresco dining. A tiled mudroom offers ample storage and connects to the two-car garage. The main level also includes a private den/home office. Upstairs, find three additional bedrooms with custom closets, a second-floor laundry room, and an oversized all purpose room which could easily be converted to a fifth bedroom or media room. Situated on a .34-acre lot, the home boasts 9-foot ceilings, enhancing its spacious feel, a full basement plumbed to be finished with electricity and duct work completed. The walk-up attic provides amazing potential for further customization, while forced air heating and central cooling ensure year-round comfort. Ideal for discerning buyers seeking luxury and convenience in vibrant West Norwalk close to all amenities.
