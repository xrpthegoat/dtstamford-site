About this home

Nestled in West Norwalk's highly sought-after neighborhood, this beautifully renovated colonial is a true gem. The first-floor master suite offers breathtaking water views, while the recently renovated kitchen boasts high-end Thermador appliances, soft-close cabinets, and stunning quartz countertops. Luxuriate in the primary bath and en suite bedroom with heated floors, and enjoy the brand new maple hardwood floors throughout the first floor. Upstairs, find three spacious bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a potential office space. Energy-efficient propane heat and central air ensure comfort year-round. Additional features include a cozy living room fireplace, a two-car garage, and the perfect spot to kayak in the lake.
