About this home

Set on a serene cul-de-sac within the coveted Glenville School District,this elegant bright and sunny colonial home seamlessly blends classic charm with modern amenities. Spanning 4,400 sq. ft., it has four spacious bedrooms and four and a half bathrooms on a level, .37-acre lot. The bright sunny gourmet kitchen features premium Thermador appliances (including the must have double wall oven) and large quartz island. Enjoy 9-foot ceilings on the main AND in the large finished walkout lower level with its own private full BA.This home also features large mudroom, custom closets, and hardwood floors. Additional perks include a two-car garage, central vac and a sunny second-floor laundry room with large folding area and plenty of cabinets. The exterior is designed with maintenance-free decking and siding. As landscaping nears completion, this exquisite home is ready to welcome its new owners just in time for the holidays!
