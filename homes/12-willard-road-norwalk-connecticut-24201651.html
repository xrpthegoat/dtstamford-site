About this home

Beautifully updated townhome located in a well manicured community constructed in 2014, just under (or within) 15 mins to 2 Metro North train stations, all major highways, shopping, dining, downtown Westport, Calf Pasture Beach, SONO, several gyms and a 50 meter pool club, right next door! Open floor plan featuring living room with gas fireplace and kitchen with large center island, quartz counter-tops and stainless steel appliances. A separate dining room, half bath and access to the deck complete the main floor and make for easy entertaining. Upstairs offers a bedroom with a large closet and hall bath. The primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, custom closet, full bath with double sinks and walk-in shower, as well as access to the private loft with updated carpeting and custom built ins; making this the perfect dressing room and/or home office. Gleaming hardwood floors and crown moldings throughout. 1 car attached garage, foyer and den with sliders to patio complete the ground level. Additional upgrades include custom closets throughout, kitchen drawer pull outs, custom window treatments, Navien tank-less hot water system, upgraded HVAC with humidifier, 5" media filter & UVC bulb, vented cooking exhaust, under stair storage, keyless entry garage with Whisper Drive opener and fully wired security system. 1 reserved parking space and plenty of guest parking on the cul-de-sac. Set up your appointment today before this gem slips by!
