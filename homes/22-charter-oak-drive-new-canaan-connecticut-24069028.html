About this home

Exceptional NEW CONSTRUCTION - Experience the pinnacle of luxury living in this stunning new construction, designed to blend contemporary elegance with timeless sophistication. Built by a renowned local builder known for meticulous attention to detail, this home showcases exquisite craftsmanship, high-end finishes, and seamless functionality. Ideally situated near top-tier shopping, dining, & schools, this custom-designed residence features handcrafted cabinetry & millwork, wide-plank oak floors, soaring ceilings, imported European windows/doors, and 4 fireplaces. The grand foyer leads to sophisticated living & dining spaces, a private office, and a gourmet eat-in kitchen with butler's pantry. The spacious family room opens to a beautifully landscaped outdoor oasis, complete with a covered porch, built-in grilling area, and a Gunite pool/spa. A 3-car garage connects to a well-appointed mudroom with ample storage. This exceptional home offers 6 ensuite bedrooms, including a 1st-floor guestroom & an opulent primary suite on the 2nd level. The fully finished lower level, bathed in natural light, boasts soaring ceilings, a wood-burning fireplace, & versatile spaces for a home theater, game room, gym, or wellness retreat. A generous 3rd-level loft provides additional space for a playroom, 2nd office, or creative studio. Crafted by an award-winning builder dedicated to creating extraordinary homes, this rare offering is a must-see for those seeking the finest in luxury & design.
