About this home

Bright Corner Unit with City Views in the Heart of Downtown Stamford! Welcome home to this spacious and sun-filled 2-bedroom corner unit, perfectly situated in the heart of downtown Stamford. Featuring beautiful hardwood floors throughout, this inviting home offers an open-concept living and dining area with sliding glass doors leading to a private balcony where you can enjoy picturesque city views. The updated kitchen is fully equipped with a refrigerator, dishwasher, and microwave, providing both functionality and convenience. The generously sized primary bedroom features two large closets, while the second bedroom offers flexibility for guests, a home office, or additional living space. A spacious full bathroom and abundant closet space throughout the unit add to its appeal. Residents enjoy the convenience of on-site laundry, assigned parking (Space #51), and rent that includes heat (up to 55F), hot water, and one parking space. Ideally located just steps from downtown Stamford&apos;s vibrant restaurants, shopping, entertainment, the library, public transportation, and Stamford Town Center Mall, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and city living.
