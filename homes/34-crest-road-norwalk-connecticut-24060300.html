About this home

Village charmer. This 3BR farmhouse colonial with its classic silhouette, is an in-town oasis. Set behind custom gates, a magical setting on a corner lot awaits you. Luscious trees and perennials create privacy and many outdoor gathering and patio areas. Step inside to a spacious LR with fieldstone fireplace, a light-filled DR and a warm and welcoming kitchen with adjacent cozy seating area. The second floor features a lovely primary suite and two additional spacious bedrooms. Just steps to shops and restaurants, Pinkney Park with its many happenings and events, and all of Rowayton's wonderful amenities, a very special lifestyle awaits you at 34 Crest Road.
