About this home

Nestled on a sought-after cul-de-sac in the heart of Greens Farms, this exceptional Colonial offers beautifully finished living space across 4 levels. With 6 bedrooms, 6 full & 2 half baths, & superior craftsmanship throughout, this home is the perfect blend of timeless design & everyday comfort. Just a short walk to Greens Farms Elementary & minutes to Compo Beach, local shops, & the train, the location is as convenient as it is coveted. Inside, the main level is anchored by a dramatic great room w/ oak shiplap ceilings, exposed beams, fireplace, & walls of windows overlooking the private backyard oasis. The chef's kitchen shines w/crisp white cabinetry, walk-in pantry, sunny dining area, & a dedicated office nook. This floor also includes a private home office, a formal living room w/ fireplace, elegant dining room w/ butler's pantry, & 2 refined powder rooms. Upstairs, the serene primary suite features white oak beams, fireplace, dual walk-in closets, & a spa-like bathroom. 4 additional bedrooms include 2 w/en-suite baths, & 2 with a jack-jill bath. The 3rd floor offers flexible space for a playroom, 2nd office, or 7th bedroom w/a full bath. The walk-out lower level includes a home gym, media room, game room, playroom, bedroom w/a separate entrance, and full bath. Set on a meticulously landscaped property, the outdoor living space is just as spectacular-featuring a heated saltwater pool w/automatic cover, lush lawn, outdoor shower, & kitchen for seamless entertaining.
