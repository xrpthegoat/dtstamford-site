About this home

This stunning 6-bedroom, 9-bathroom home is set on acres of beautifully landscaped grounds, offering privacy and luxury just an hour from New York City. The classically inspired design showcases the finest craftsmanship, with vaulted ceilings, custom woodwork, and walls of windows that flood the home with natural light. Inside, you'll find a paneled library, staff quarters, a screening room, and spacious recreation areas. The gourmet kitchen and expansive entertaining spaces combine traditional elegance with modern amenities, providing both style and function. A rare opportunity to rent an estate of this caliber, blending timeless charm with contemporary convenience. Work is being completed on the house and property which will be completed by mid/late October in time for occupancy.
