About this home

Welcome to 94 Unity Road in Stamford offering an opportunity to create a home tailored to your own style and vision. Set in a quiet neighborhood, this home features a flat, spacious backyard perfect for outdoor living. The heart of the home is the granite/hardwood style kitchen designed to appeal to the home chef. The space features professional-grade appliances, highlighted by a Thermador six burner dual-fuel range, an Advantium oven, and a convenient warming tray. A timeless Shaws farmhouse sink and Bosch dishwasher further add charm to the kitchen. A generous breakfast bar and kitchen dining area with heated flooring provides both style and functionality. The hardwood first floor continues with a spacious family room and wood burning fireplace, a dining room, bedroom and a convenient half-bath, with the possibility of converting into a full. Upstairs the primary bedroom offers it own sitting area and 2 full closets. Completing the second floor is a third hardwood bedroom and shared full bath. Additionally, the loft space above the garage offers valuable square footage and possible customization. Whether envisioned as a home office, studio or hobby space. Additional highlights include a newly installed Peerless boiler "2023" and roof "2025."
