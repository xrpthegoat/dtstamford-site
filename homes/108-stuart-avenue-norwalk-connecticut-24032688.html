About this home

You don't see it often these days, but here is your opportunity to build equity in this 3 bedroom Spring Hill Home! The bones are good and this gem just needs a little refresh mixed with some personal touches from its new owners to help it shine. The first floor offers mostly hardwood floors throughout, living room, formal dining room, two of the three bedrooms, one full bathroom and a large kitchen area with tons of potential. The second floor offers an open loft-like area perfect for a play room, home office, family room and more, with its own additional full bathroom and bedroom off of it. There's more room to enjoy on the enclosed front porch, the deck in back, and in the heated lower level as well, offering even more possibilities for the next owner. Central Air & Natural Gas Heat, new HW heater in 2023. Location is prime with easy highway access, close proximity to Norwalk Hospital, shopping, restaurants, and many of the town's beaches. Opportunities like this don't come around often so take advantage of it while you can!
