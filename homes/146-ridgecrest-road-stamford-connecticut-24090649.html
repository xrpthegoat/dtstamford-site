About this home

Nestled on a beautifully manicured acre of level land, this charming 5 bedroom, 4.5 bathroom home blends timeless elegance with modern comfort. Sunlight pours through large windows, highlighting rich wood floors and a expansive living room centered around a sophisticated gas fireplace. The formal dining room is perfect for gatherings, while the gourmet eat-in kitchen boasts white marble countertops, top-tier stainless steel appliances and a sunlit dining area that opens to an expansive terrace. The backyard is a private oasis with a built-in gas grill, heated pool with motorized cover, and granite patio-ideal for unforgettable outdoor entertaining. Unwind in the inviting family room with a wood-burning fireplace, or retreat upstairs to the luxurious primary suite with a spa like bath, walk-in closet, and private deck. Upstairs also offers three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms. Above the three-car garage, you'll find another spacious bedroom, a full bathroom, and a grand room with with a vaulted ceiling-ideal for a home office, creative studio, or guest lounge. The expansive finished lower level offers endless possibilities with room for a gym, play area, media room, or additional workspace-designed to adapt to your lifestyle needs.
