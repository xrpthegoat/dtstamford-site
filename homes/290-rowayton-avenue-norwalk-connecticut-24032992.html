About this home

Designer Victorian Beach House, with a Wrap-Around Porch and French doors that lead out to private stone patios with mature plantings. This historic, modernized coastal residence offers an open floorplan with custom bookcases ,beautiful lighting throughout and has been completely renovated . Features include a 5-burner Bertazzoni gas range, stainless steel appliances, sleek white lacquer cabinets, and an authentic tin ceiling. The library/den/office includes an electric fireplace. Three spacious bedrooms offer ample closet space, with the primary bedroom featuring a dressing room and high ceilings. Conveniently located within walking distance to town, train, and beach." Timeless character with modern style.
