About this home

Casual living at its best in the heart of Westport! This sunny and welcoming 3BR/3BA Cape-style home offers a blend of charm, contemporary style, and loads of potential! As you enter the home you will discover an inviting open floor plan designed for comfortable living and relaxed entertaining. The spacious living room features a charming bay window and a cozy fireplace, flowing into the dining room with vaulted ceilings, exposed beams, and another bay window that floods the space with natural light. Slide open the glass doors to reveal a level, fenced backyard with a patio and inviting in-ground heated pool, perfect for outdoor gatherings and lazy summer days. The well-appointed kitchen, strategically positioned between the living and dining areas, offers convenience and functionality while providing easy access to the outdoor retreat. Hardwood floors add warmth and character throughout, enhancing the home's appeal. The main level's flexible floor plan offers a primary suite option with full bath and sliders to the backyard and pool for refreshing morning swims or evening relaxation. Two additional bedrooms and another full bath await on the second level, along with ample storage space. The lower level features additional living space with a fireplace, full bath, laundry, and storage. Conveniently located just minutes away from town amenities, shopping destinations, renowned restaurants, the library, and the Merritt Parkway. Professional photos coming Monday 6/10!
