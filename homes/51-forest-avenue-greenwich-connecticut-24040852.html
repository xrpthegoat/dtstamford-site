About this home

Discover the epitome of luxury living in the highly sought-after Old Greenwich Gables Community. This stunning 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment spans 1,647 square feet, offering the perfect blend of elegance and comfort. Step inside to find beautiful hardwood floors throughout, leading to a fully renovated kitchen equipped with sleek granite countertops and top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances. The open concept main living area is designed for both relaxation and entertaining, featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace and access to a private covered balcony-ideal for enjoying your morning coffee or unwinding in the evening. The dining area flows seamlessly into the kitchen, making it perfect for hosting dinner parties, while a charming office nook provides a quiet space for work or study. Retreat to the expansive primary suite, where you'll find two spacious double closets and a luxurious en-suite bathroom complete with both a standing shower and a tub shower-perfect for indulging in a relaxing soak after a long day. The second bedroom is equally spacious and versatile, offering ample storage and access to the second full bathroom. The OGG community features an inground pool, personal storage space, as well as a covered assigned garage space. Don't miss out!
