About this home

Soaring ceilings and open spaces define the style of this 4 bedroom home. Bright and open and easy living on one level. The large foyer and living room has a cathedral ceiling. The kitchen/casual dining area/family room is dramatic and spacious, with a stone fireplace, wet bar, sky lights, solarium windows and cathedral ceiling. This is the heart of the house and can open to the patio and pool with spa for a fabulous entertaining flow. There is a spacious formal dining room. The master suite includes his and her bathrooms, one with tub and the other with stall shower, and multiple closets including a walk in cedar closet. There are 3 double bedrooms, each with ample closet space and a separate laundry room/mudroom off the 3 car garage, which has ample storage in the walkup 2nd floor. There are hardwood floors throughout the house. Please avoid showing requests between 1-3pm on Saturday and Sunday.
