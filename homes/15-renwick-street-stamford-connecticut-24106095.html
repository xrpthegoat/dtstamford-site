About this home

Ideal commuter location one mile walk to the Stamford train station. Excellent downtown amenities including nearby Scalzi Park & Mill River Park, restaurants and coffee shops, gourmet shops/groceries, fitness and more. This two-bedroom townhouse style unit has a Zen feel and is in pristine condition with hardwood floors throughout. The first floor has a cozy great room & dining area, foyer, powder room and updated kitchen with maple cabinets, granite counters, and stainless appliances. The second level has two large bedrooms with great closet space and an updated full bath with tub/shower plus a linen closet. Washer & dryer is included in the unit. Absolutely no pets. Two parking spots - one assigned, under unit, one in open parking (first come, first serve).
