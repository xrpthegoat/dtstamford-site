About this home

Welcome to 49 Day Street, Unit 209, a well-kept condo in the heart of South Norwalk (SoNo) surrounded by award-winning restaurants, shopping, and year-round events, including the SoNo Arts Festival on Washington Street. This location is especially attractive for commuters, with the SoNo train station just about a 7-minute walk away, and you're also within walking distance to waterfront parks and recreation like Veterans Park, Taylor Farm Dog Park, and Calf Pasture Beach in East Norwalk. The home features an open-concept layout where the kitchen looks into the living room, plus valuable upgrades including a dishwasher (approx. 5 years old), new washer/dryer (approx. 1 year old), and a new heater/AC split system (approx. 2 years old). The building includes an elevator, making it a great option for investors because it opens the door to a larger pool of renters, and you're close enough to walk to SoNo nightlife in about 10 minutes while still tucked away from the noise; with continued redevelopment throughout SoNo, this is a strong opportunity to own in one of Norwalk's most active and appreciating neighborhoods.
