About this home

Available for quick purchase and occupancy! Welcome home to this charming ranch nestled in the heart of Glenbrook, offering a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Step into accessible one-floor living with a layout designed for effortless functionality. The sun-filled living room invites you into this inviting atmosphere and seamless views into the backyard. The eat-in kitchen provides a central hub for culinary delights and casual dining, making it ideal for both daily routines and entertaining. The spacious dining room is complete with sliders to an expansive back deck which offers a picturesque setting for enjoying meals while overlooking the fenced-in private backyard. This outdoor space is perfect for summer gatherings, quiet evenings or simply enjoying a morning coffee in tranquility. The separate family room serves as a versatile space that can easily be transformed into a bonus bedroom or a productive home office. Accommodations include three generous bedrooms, each with hardwood floors, ensuring a restful retreat for family and guests. These bedrooms are complemented by a full hall bathroom with tub. Conveniently designed with a quick side entrance leading to the driveway and a large detached garage with ample storage space. Home is enhanced with a newer water heater. Enjoy a 5 minute drive to Cove Beach, metro North train, restaurants and shopping. 10 minute drive to downtown Stamford. Property video attached.
