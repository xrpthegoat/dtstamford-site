About this home

Welcome to the highly desirable Sunrise Hill community! This absolutely beautiful three-bedroom, 2.5 half bath end-unit townhouse offers three levels of living space and is truly move-in ready. Beautiful hardwood floors flow throughout the main level, where you'll find a bright eat-in kitchen featuring quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, rich cherry cabinetry, and a seamless connection to the dining area. The spacious living room is filled with character and charm, highlighted by a wood-burning fireplace that creates the perfect place to relax and unwind. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms, a full bath, and attic access offering additional storage. The partially finished lower level adds even more versatility with a family room/den setup, and a renovated full bath, laundry area, plus a separate storage room. Sliders lead directly to a stunning private large patio, an ideal setting for morning coffee, outdoor dining, entertaining, or simply enjoying some quiet time outdoors. Sunrise Hill offers a wonderful community setting with a pool and tennis courts, all while being conveniently located near shopping, dining, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway. Beautifully maintained, thoughtfully updated, and filled with warmth and charm, this special home is a true gem and one you'll want to experience in person!
