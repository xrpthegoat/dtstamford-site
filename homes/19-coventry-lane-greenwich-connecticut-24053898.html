About this home

Classic & Timeless Colonial nestled on a corner lot in a friendly cul-de-sac neighborhood in the beloved Riverside community. This home offers so much more than meets the eye. This spacious 5 bedroom 3 full bathroom Colonial boasts a flexible floor plan & unique main floor in-law apartment or primary suite option including over 2,600 square feet of wonderful living spaces for both family & friends to enjoy! Situated walking distance to Riverside & Eastern Middle school, Parks, & Riverside Metro station to NYC, this home is both a commuter's and convenience seeker's dream! The charming covered front porch is ideal for enjoying throughout the seasons & welcomes you into the lovely classic foyer of this majestic home boasting a wonderful, flexible layout. The spacious formal living room offers a large bright open plan perfect for entertaining & hosting the holidays! The expansive Living room seamlessly flows into the Formal Dining room space offering ample room to gather with the entire family! Beautiful natural light adorns the solid hardwood floors throughout making for an elegant and warm setting. Options abound in the exquisite oversized Family room. This thoughtfully designed open space can be customized to suit every lifestyle. The space lends itself well for a great casual dining area, relaxing sitting room, playroom, or a home office space! The sunny eat-in Kitchen includes stainless appliances & plenty of storage. Move right in and create your own story to enjoy!
