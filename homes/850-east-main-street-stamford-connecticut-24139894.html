About this home

Modern Top-Floor Penthouse with Loft in Eastside Commons where style meets convenience in the heart of Stamford! This pet-friendly penthouse offers an impressive layout featuring 2 spacious bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a versatile finished loft-perfect for a home office, gym, or additional living space. The home also includes in-unit laundry and a dedicated storage room for added convenience. Step inside to find soaring 9-foot ceilings, dramatic vaulted living room ceilings, and oversized picture windows that flood the home with natural light. Enjoy your morning coffee or unwind on your private balcony, ideal for relaxing or entertaining. The open-concept kitchen is a chef's dream, showcasing granite countertops, refinished cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and an expansive island breakfast bar-perfect for hosting friends and family. Both bedrooms offer excellent storage space, with the primary suite featuring a walk-in closet, private balcony access, and an en suite bathroom. Recent updates include new carpeting, freshly painted, a refinished staircase, and a high-efficiency gas furnace for year-round comfort. You'll enjoy resort-style amenities such as a fitness center, meeting room, beautifully landscaped courtyard with grilling areas. The unit includes one reserved covered garage space plus plenty of guest parking. Located just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North/Amtrak, and I-95.
