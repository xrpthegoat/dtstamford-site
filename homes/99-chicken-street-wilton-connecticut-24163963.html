About this home

Privately set on 2 picturesque acres, this charming Col Farmhouse offers the perfect balance of quiet country living & everyday convenience, just mins to town, schools, & train. Welcoming front porch sets the tone, inviting slow mornings with coffee & peaceful views of the surrounding landscape. Inside, a gracious entry leads to a formal Living Rm with fireplace & a sun-drenched den or home office, also with fireplace, framed by expansive windows. The formal Dining Rm flows seamlessly into a thoughtfully designed Chef's Kitchen featuring granite countertops, ss appls, wine chiller, walk-in pantry, & a vaulted dining area overlooking the deck & private backyard, ideal for both entertaining & daily living. The heart of the home continues with a comfortable Family Rm just off the Kitchen, along with a well placed Laundry and Powder Rm for effortless functionality. Upstairs, the spacious Primary Suite offers a true retreat with an updated marble Bath, double sinks, separate shower & tub, & generous closets. Three additional BRs & an updated Hall Bath complete the 2nd level. Finished LL provides flexible living space with a Play or Media Rm, built-ins, & a Half Bath. Thoughtful updates since 2017 include a new roof, well, interior & exterior painting, deck refinishing, & a recently installed wall-mounted boiler ensuring peace of mind for years to come. A rare opportunity for the buyer seeking timeless charm, modern updates, & a setting that feels both private & connected.
