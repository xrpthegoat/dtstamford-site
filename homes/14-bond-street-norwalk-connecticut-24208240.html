About this home

A tastefully remodeled Cape Cod style home in the vibrant East Norwalk area! This inviting 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home features a well-appointed chef's kitchen that flows seamlessly into a cozy, open living space. Step outside to a great back deck that gets amazing natural light-perfect for grillin' or chillin'. This home offers easy access to scenic Calf Pasture Beach, Taylor Farm Dog Park, walking trails, and charming local shops. Convenient to the nearby Metro-North train station. You'll be just minutes from downtown SONO and the Saugatuck section of Westport, known for their great restaurants and nightlife. The yard features colorful perennial plantings and bountiful vegetable gardens, while the home boasts a recently updated, natural gas, HVAC system. A perfect blend of comfort, location, and lifestyle! Welcome Home!
