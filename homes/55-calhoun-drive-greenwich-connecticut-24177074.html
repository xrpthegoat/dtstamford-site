About this home

Majestic six bedroom manor, completely rebuilt with exceptional quality and craftsmanship, and sited on stunning 1.4 park-like acres in coveted Calhoun Drive Association. This distinguished residence is gracefully elevated to offer sweeping views of expansive lawns, lovely gardens and specimen trees. Impressive, sophisticated interiors throughout offer soaring ceilings, gleaming hardwood floors and sunlit living space. A grand foyer opens to a gracious floor plan of spacious rooms perfect for formal entertaining and casual living. Highlights include an airy sun room, coffered ceiling family room & breakfast room, all opening to a expansive terrace with stunning lawn and garden views. Generous space for a new custom kitchen. Luxurious primary suite features two marble Waterworks bathrooms, walk in closets and a sitting room with access to a private balcony. An additional private guest suite provides enhanced comfort for guests. A welcoming garden level offers a media room, wet bar, wine cellar, gym with full bath and playroom with access to a private outdoor covered terrace. The main residence has a three car garage. There is a charming two story carriage house with storage for two more cars. This stately home, located on a quiet cul-de-sac, is close to downtown Greenwich with fine shopping and dining and transportation to NYC.
