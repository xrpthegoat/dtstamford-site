About this home

Welcome to 47 Second Street, an impeccably maintained two-family Colonial in one of East Norwalk's most desirable coastal neighborhoods. Built in 1890 and situated on an oversized 0.25-acre lot, this charming property offers a fantastic opportunity for both homeowners and investors. Each unit features two spacious bedrooms and one full bathroom, providing comfortable and functional living spaces. Live in one unit while generating rental income from the other, or add this well-maintained property to your investment portfolio. The current tenant is on a month-to-month lease, offering flexibility for the new owner. Conveniently located near beaches, marinas, shopping, dining, parks, and commuter transportation, this versatile two-family home combines historic character with exceptional potential in a sought-after East Norwalk location.
