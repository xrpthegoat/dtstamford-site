About this home

Timeless craftsmanship meets everyday comfort in this beautifully maintained Colonial, where rich architectural details set it apart. Exquisite millwork, custom paneling, refined wainscoting, and built-ins showcase the quality and character rarely found today. Sun-filled living and dining spaces, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace and gleaming hardwood floors, create a warm and inviting atmosphere. The thoughtfully updated kitchen blends style and function, while the finished lower level offers flexible living space. Outside, a welcoming front porch, white picket fence, and professionally landscaped grounds complete the home's exceptional curb appeal. All just moments from shopping, dining, Metro-North, and downtown Stamford.
