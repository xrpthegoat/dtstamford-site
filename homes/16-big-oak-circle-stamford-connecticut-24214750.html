About this home

Welcome to this contemporary ranch home, located in North Stamford, just north of the Merritt Parkway. The neighborhood is convenient to all kind of shopping, markets, gas station, pharmacy, medical facilities, tennis courts, restaurants and more! As you enter the front door, you will be awed by the feeling and space of the great room. Areas for living room, dining room and den are easily imagined and can be configured in many ways. The room boasts hardwood floors, floor to ceiling windows, a vaulted ceiling and wood burning fireplace. There is also access to the oversized deck. Adjacent to this great room is the open kitchen, featuring granite counters, stainless appliances, recessed lighting and hardwood floors. Downstairs is a built-in office area, a recreation room, full bath (with shower), laundry, workshop and the third bedroom. The downstairs has recently been carpeted and has outdoor access into the carport, making it easy to get groceries and belongings into the house. Whole house generator runs on natural gas piped in, not propane or gasoline. The exterior is lush, green, private and serene. It's hard to believe you are 45 minutes form Broadway!!! A small man-made pond with fountain sits in the treed area and completes the picture. The current owner has lived in and loved this home for over 26 years. Take a look and see if you might be the next buyer who will love this special home!!
