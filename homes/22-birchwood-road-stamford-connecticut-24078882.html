About this home

Experience the best of Springdale living in this charming storybook colonial, nestled on one of Springdale's most desirable streets. Classic elegance blends seamlessly with contemporary comforts, including timeless details like glass door knobs and a solid wood banister. Enjoy your morning coffee on the enclosed front porch, while the sunny home fills with both morning and afternoon light. Inside, you'll find an abundance of living spaces, including a cozy living room with a fireplace, a spacious kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and a pantry with a 1/2 bath, as well as a backyard perfect for outdoor enjoyment. Freshly painted throughout, featuring updates such as a new gas boiler w/chimney liner and a brand-new roof . Upstairs, the spacious primary suite includes a renovated bath and cozy sitting area. The versatile third floor can serve as a playroom, gym, or home office. Conveniently located minutes from Springdale Train Station, Newfield Shopping Area, and easy access to the Merritt Parkway and I-95. With classic features and recent updates, this is a home you'll love!
