About this home

Embrace the essence of coastal living just steps from Norwalk's picturesque Shorefront Park in this inviting Cape Cod home. Sunlight pours into the airy living room, complementing the modern eat-in kitchen, two generously sized bedrooms, and a rejuvenated full bathroom. Kitchen features glass tile backsplash, quartz countertops, pot filler, and professional Samsung appliances including double door stove with griddle and cabinet style digital fridge. Upstairs, discover the expansive front-to-back primary bedroom with updated bathroom. An additional upstairs bedroom or home office provides flexibility and seclusion. The walk-out finished basement presents endless possibilities, whether as a playroom, home office, or potential In-Law apartment. Throughout the home, classic hardwood floors add warmth and charm, complemented by the efficiency of an on-demand hot water heater and the convenience of a deep garage. Outside, a spacious back patio beckons you to unwind amidst the sea breezes and possible hot tub for ultimate relaxation and ample space for hosting BBQs. The property is equipped with irrigation, ensuring lush greenery and hydrangeas. Perfectly situated for both indoor comfort and outdoor enjoyment, this home offers easy access to SONO and Metro North, promising a harmonious blend of convenience and coastal tranquility.
