About this home

Discover the ultimate blend of privacy, space, and style, with this beautifully maintained 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath home set at the top of a cul-de-sac on New Canaan's sought-after west side. Sunlight floods the home's airy interior, where a gracious layout, gleaming hardwood floors, large windows and recessed lighting, create the perfect setting for everyday living and elegant entertaining. The formal living and dining rooms offer timeless charm, while the eat-in kitchen flows into a dramatic family room with vaulted ceilings, backyard access and a cozy fireplace-just right for relaxing nights and lively gatherings. Upstairs, five well-appointed bedrooms include a serene primary suite, and flexible spaces perfect for a home office, playroom, or guest retreat. Outside, enjoy over 5 acres of nature and tranquility-an idyllic backdrop for weekend play, morning coffee, or evening sunsets. Just minutes to New Canaan's village center, top-rated schools, Metro-North, Pound Ridge, Michelin-starred chef restaurants, parks, and shopping-this one has it all!
