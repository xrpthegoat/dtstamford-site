About this home

Welcome to Gramercy Park! Exquisite, boutique enclave of ten luxury residences nestled in the heart of sought-after New Canaan, CT. Private and pristine, Unit 302 offers turn-key living, complete with elevator access & in-unit washer/dryer. This 3 bedroom, 3.1 bath residence features 2,417 sq. ft. of carefully crafted living space centered around a chef kitchen with top-of-the-line SubZero/Wolf appliances, custom cabinetry & marble countertops, with seamless flow into an elegant main living/dining area. Light & bright primary bedroom with spa-like full bath w/marble finishes & generously-sized walk-in closet; 2nd Main Floor Bedroom and Den. Upstairs, 3rd bedroom & Family Room, open to below. A stunning, oversized covered balcony overlooking the serene & tastefully landscaped community garden for year-round outdoor enjoyment, 2 dedicated parking spaces (one covered), ample guest parking and complimentary use of the community clubhouse with outdoor kitchen, seating area and fitness center w/TV & Internet access complete the package. Offering secure entry and 24-hour package room for added peace of mind, Gramercy Park is a modern sanctuary brimming with charm, steps from exceptional dining, bespoke retail shops, parks, and vibrant local events -- all one hour from Manhattan.
