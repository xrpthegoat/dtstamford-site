About this home

A true GEM!! Don't miss this truly beautiful 5 bedroom/3.1 bath home on a flat, dry half acre of lush landscape on one of Darien's premiere streets! This special home seamlessly blends generous proportions with a long list of new renovations including: roof, hvac/natural gas, and primary bath to name just a few.Step inside to over 3600sf of living space perfect for modern living and large-scale entertaining. The first floor offers an oversized eat-in kitchen with banquette and 4 additional oversized rooms for living and dining that accomodate everyone's needs and offers great flow to the gorgeous outdoor areas. The sprawling primary suite serves as a private sanctuary, complete with 2 walk-in closets and a newly transformed, spa-like bathroom. Generously sized secondary bedrooms offer ultimate flexibility for family, guests, or a dedicated home office. Outside, the property features a beautifully manicured flat yard (.51 acres), 2 tier composite deck with built-in grill to truly enjoy and experience seamless outdoor dining and entertaining. Unmatched for convenience, this premier address places you just minutes from top-rated schools, shopping, dining, train, and Woodland Park. Move right in and enjoy!
