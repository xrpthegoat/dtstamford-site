About this home

Move right in and start living your dream in this fully renovated gem on one of Hindley's most sought-after streets! Bathed in natural light, the open kitchen-family room showcases stunning views of Holly Pond and flows seamlessly to a brand-new deck-perfect for morning coffee or sunset dinners. With 4 bedrooms, 4 baths, a versatile homework room/office, and a walk-out lower level, this .95-acre property offers space for everyone. The expansive backyard is known for hosting the neighborhood's legendary kids games at the annual summer party. As part of Noroton Manor, you will enjoy tennis courts, private beach access, and unbeatable proximity to downtown Darien, Stamford, beaches, YMCA, Metro North, and top-rated schools - where water views and the best of coastal living are right outside your door.
