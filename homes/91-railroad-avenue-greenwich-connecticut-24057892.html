About this home

Prime Retail Space on Railroad Avenue Exceptional opportunity to lease a 1,000 sq. ft. retail space located in the heart of Greenwich on Railroad Avenue, right at the base of the iconic Greenwich Avenue. Positioned directly across from Greenwich train Station. This property benefits from unparalleled foot traffic, thanks to neighboring high-end retailers, salons, and renowned restaurants. This location is set to become even more dynamic with the forthcoming Greenwich Crossing, a $30 million revitalization project that will modernize the train station, introduce a vibrant restaurant anchor, expand retail options, and create an inviting outdoor plaza for gathering and events. This transformative development promises to elevate Railroad Avenue's shopping and dining appeal, fostering a stronger sense of community and attracting an even larger customer base. Currently operating as a cigar variety shop, this flexible retail space offers many possibilities. With its prime location, exceptional visibility, and proximity to major transit and luxury shopping, it's perfectly suited for a variety of retail concepts looking to establish a presence in Greenwich's thriving market. Don't miss this chance to secure a high-visibility location in Greenwich's vibrant retail corridor. Tenant is responsible for utilities and trash services; one dedicated parking space is included in the rent.
