About this home

Generational Compound with all the luxuries & amenities one desires. Cascading waterfall pool, pool house, tennis court with viewing gazebo, barn, guest house, fenced garden and meandering sculpture rock garden. Set off New Canaan's highest ridge and down a private lane this resort-style estate is prime living-just one hour from NYC. This is for the discerning buyer who can spot value & potential. With 5+ bedrooms, multiple living areas, the home is designed to suit a variety of lifestyles. Additional features include a five-car garage, spa and gym suite with a jacuzzi, indoor garden room, and a very spacious in-law or caretaker suite. The beautifully landscaped 5-acres includes lush destination gardens, a fenced vegetable garden, and a dedicated pet area. A gated entry leads down a long, tree-lined drive, setting the stage for timeless charm. Outdoor living is just as impressive, with a resort-style pool framed by cascading waterfalls, a romantic pergola with outdoor kitchen, dining terrace, and a charming pool house-perfect for entertaining.This is a rare opportunity to own a peaceful retreat with mystique that has space, beauty, and versatility - all in an exceptional New Canaan location.
