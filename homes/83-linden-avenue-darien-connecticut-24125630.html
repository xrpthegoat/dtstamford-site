About this home

A charming, cozy historic home with modern amenities and close to everything, including Darien Commons, Heights Crossing and Noroton Heights train station. Country kitchen renovated by Deane Inc. with Viking gas cooktop. 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths plus dining room, den and family room. In addition, there is a 1st floor sun room/gym/office, oversized walk in pantry, laundry area and 2nd floor office space. See floor plans in the picture gallery. Three fireplaces for aesthetics only. Newer high efficiency furnace and water heater. Extensive outdoor landscaping. Close to the local elementary school (Hindley), the middle school, beaches and public library. Tenant pays all utilities, snow removal, grounds maintenance, garbage removal, water usage/sewer tax. References and credit check required. Flexible 8 to 24 month lease term considered. This is an unfurnished rental.
