About this home

Welcome to this distinctive 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath ranch offering 1,830 square feet of living space, filled with warmth, character, and impressive architectural details. The heart of the home is the expansive living room, featuring a dramatic vaulted ceiling with exposed beams, a beautiful stone fireplace, hardwood floors, and an incredible wall of windows that fills the space with natural light while showcasing views of the private, wooded backyard. The home offers a functional kitchen and dining area, hardwood flooring, central air conditioning, and oil heat. Three bedrooms and two full baths provide comfortable one-level living. The walk-out lower level offers excellent additional space and features its own fireplace and half bath, providing plenty of possibilities for a family room, recreation area, home office, or guest space. Outside, enjoy a spacious yard surrounded by mature trees, creating a peaceful and private setting. A two-car garage adds convenience and storage. With its impressive windows, exposed beams, stonework, and natural surroundings, this home offers a unique blend of rustic character and everyday comfort.
