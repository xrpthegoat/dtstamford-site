About this home

Serious inquiries only please. Classically elegant turn of the century Mediterranean is located in a private waterfront neighborhood in prestigious Southfield Point. This home is perfect for entertaining with a completely renovated kitchen and butler's pantry, featuring all high-end appliances, custom cabinetry, marble and quartz countertops. The kitchen leads out to the resort-style outdoor entertaining area which includes a heated pool with quartzite deck/patio, pergola, and privacy hedges. The step-down living room with high ceilings, natural light, intricate custom moldings and fireplace also leads out to the outside entertainment area through french doors. The grand scale dining room can comfortably seat 12 people. The primary bedroom is sizable with a sun deck, fireplace, completely renovated bath with heated floors, and office. The stunning Jack & Jill bath is completely renovated which connects to 2 spacious bedrooms. Separate 600 square foot 1 bedroom cottage is over detached garage with kitchen, dining area, full bath, and living room. Enjoy this serene waterfront neighborhood with a private association beach and private boat slips owned by Association. Minutes to Old Greenwich, vibrant downtown Stamford, 2 train stations, shopping, restaurants, and 50 minutes to NYC by train. Imagine your family gatherings and glamorous entertaining, all summer long...at your own private resort.
