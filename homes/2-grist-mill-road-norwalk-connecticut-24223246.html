About this home

Nestled in a picturesque wooded setting, this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers 2,000 square feet of warm and inviting living space. Highlights include hardwood floors throughout, a cozy gas fireplace, a primary bedroom with a newly renovated en-suite bathroom, central air conditioning, main-floor laundry, and plenty of storage. Enjoy outdoor living on the charming covered front porch, or step through sliding glass doors onto the brand-new deck overlooking the private, partially fenced-in backyard, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Additional features include an attached one-car garage and ample off-street parking. Move-in ready, this home offers a wonderful combination of charm, comfort, and privacy in a desirable Norwalk location.
