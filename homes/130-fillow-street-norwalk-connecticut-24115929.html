About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse offering comfort, convenience, and thoughtful design. This unit also includes in-unit laundry, an attached garage, and additional storage space. Enjoy plenty of parking, well-kept grounds, and a truly welcoming setting surrounded by mature trees. Excellent credit required. Two months' security deposit. No pets or smoking permitted.
