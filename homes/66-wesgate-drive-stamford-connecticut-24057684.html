About this home

Rare opportunity to live on the end of a quiet & peaceful Westover cul-de-sac near schools, train station and shopping conveniences. This sun-filled 5 bedroom classic colonial on 1.09 acres has a well-designed floor plan with lovely views from every room. A gracious entryway flows into the living room with fireplace, dining room, large eat-in kitchen and adjoining family room also with fireplace. Sliding doors from both the kitchen & family room lead to a flagstone patio with views of open lawn bordered by mature trees and established landscaping. There is a convenient custom mudroom, laundry and full bath between the kitchen and 2 car garage and an expansive bonus/play room and office with a separate staircase. Second floor features a primary suite with large walk-in closet and 3 additional bedrooms and full bath. The renovated finished lower level has large game/recreation room and additional bedroom with a full bath. Seamless full-house generator
