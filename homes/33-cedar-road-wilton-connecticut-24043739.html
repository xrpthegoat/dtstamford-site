About this home

Nestled on 2.1 acres of beautifully landscaped property, this Ranch style home has been lovingly updated/maintained and is waiting for its next owner. Upon entering the home, prepare to be wowed by the open concept kitchen and living spaces. The remodeled kitchen has stainless-steel appliances which include a gas range, dishwasher, microwave and refrigerator plus a large center island, marble countertops and plenty of cabinet space. Steps away you will find the primary living space has a vaulted ceiling, skylights, fireplace and large picture window offering an abundance of natural light. The dining room is light and bright with a vaulted ceiling, ideal for gathering with family and friends. The primary bedroom has a full bath and access to the side deck. Two additional bedrooms and full bath complete the main level. Don't miss the walkout, fully finished lower level with an office and finished room with a full bath which can be used as a guest bedroom, plus a family room and laundry. Other features of the home include recessed lighting, hardwood floors throughout, skylights, deck, copper gutters, generator, and barn. The private, professionally landscaped yard has a patio with outdoor fireplace plus plenty of room for a pool! Located minutes away from Westport and Wilton town centers, award winning schools, train and major highways. This home is in mint condition and sure to impress! Schedule an appointment now.
