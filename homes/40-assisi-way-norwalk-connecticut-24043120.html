About this home

4 bedroom, 2 full bath home in the desirable Wolfpit neighborhood. Truly nothing needs to be done to this perfectly re-built home. First floor features an open kitchen and living room concept. Kitchen has been modeled with high end finishes and appliances, and the cozy living room features a wood burning fireplace and brand new hardwood floors. Entertain in your dining room, or grill on your patio. Enjoy the convenience of having two bedrooms (or one bedroom, and an office, den, playroom, etc) a full bath, and your laundry room all on your first floor. The second level of the home features two more spacious bedrooms, and a full bath. Plenty of closet space throughout, and an attached one car garage for extra storage. Home is centered on a level lot, with a fenced in backyard. House is centrally located to shopping, beaches, transportation, schools, and parks. You can truly have it all!
