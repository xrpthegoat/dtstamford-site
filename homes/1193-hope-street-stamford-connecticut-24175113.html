About this home

Fantastic townhouse-style condo located in the highly desirable Springdale area of Stamford. Conveniently situated near restaurants, shopping centers, and the Springdale Metro-North train station, offering an easy one-hour commute to NYC. This charming condo is in excellent condition and features 2 bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms. The spacious kitchen includes a breakfast area and flows seamlessly into the dining and living areas, creating an open and inviting layout. The lower level offers an additional family room that can be used as a guest room, gym, or home office, along with an extra full bathroom and laundry room. The unit also includes 2 reserved parking spaces (#4). Please submit offers using the standard 3-page SMLS contract along with proof of funds or a pre-approval letter and all disclosures (see SMLS).
