About this home

Updated with a Modern flair this four-bedroom home is near the water, Tod's Point & located on a quiet cul-de-sac in Old Greenwich. Spacious & bright, there is an open design, sun-filled rooms & hardwood floors. A flexible floor plan makes living & working at home easy. The first floor has a gourmet kitchen w/stainless steel appliances, living room w/fireplace, formal dining room, primary suite with full bath, additional bedroom and full bath. Garden level has 2/3 more bedrooms, two full baths, family room w/access to stone patio, mudroom and laundry. Level and fenced backyard, perfect for entertaining, grilling and relaxing. This home is a short distance to town, schools and Metro North train. Deeded access to view LI Sound at the end of the street. Perfect for today's living & enjoyment.
