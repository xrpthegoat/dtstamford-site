About this home

Discover quality and elegance in this stunning home. The grand entry hall with soaring double-height ceilings sets the tone for this fabulous residence. Relax in the expansive living room with a cozy fireplace, entertain in the oversized dining room, or retreat to the great office/library. The gourmet kitchen has been updated with new appliances in 2021, features quartz countertops, an oversized island, and Subzero refrigerator. It seamlessly flows into the family room with a fireplace and a charming breakfast area. French doors open to a deck and terrace, perfect for outdoor entertaining. The upper level offers a primary bedroom suite with his and hers custom-designed walk-in closets, along with four additional bedrooms and three full bathrooms. House also features a bonus lower level, newly refinished hardwood floors throughout, and ample storage The property sits on 0.29 acres of level, manicured land. Ideally located, close to the train and schools.
