About this home

Spacious Silvermine Ranch with Legal In-Law Apartment. Enjoy easy one-level living in this expansive ranch on a full, level acre in Norwalk's sought-after Silvermine neighborhood. The main home offers 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths and is leased through September 2026-perfect for an income-producing property. Features include hardwood floors, an open living/dining area, a large kitchen with granite countertops, center island, and ample cabinetry. A charming 3-season porch opens to a wraparound deck, ideal for entertaining. The primary bedroom has an ensuite bath with shower/tub combo, plus two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath. The legal 1-bedroom in-law apartment, ready for occupancy, has a private entrance, vaulted ceilings, skylights, a full kitchen, dining area with sliders to its own deck, and a spacious bath. Separate utilities for both units add flexibility and convenience. Additional highlights include a walk-up attic, full unfinished basement with high ceilings, and an oversized circular driveway with plenty of parking. Prime location close to commuter routes, shopping, and amenities-offering the perfect blend of comfort, versatility, and investment potential.
