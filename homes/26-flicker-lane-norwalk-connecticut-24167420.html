About this home

26 Flicker Lane sits on a sunny corner lot on one of Rowayton's most popular streets. Step inside this charming Farmhouse Colonial and be prepared to be wowed. Filled with natural light, the main floor boasts a lovely fireplace with a sitting area, an open family room, an updated kitchen with new appliances, and a dining area with a custom-built-in banquet. In addition, there is a separate office that doubles as a guest room or library, and a large deck off the dining area provides incredible additional space. Upstairs, the Primary bedroom has a new walk-in closet, an updated ensuite bathroom and a hidden slide-out for working out. The two additional family bedrooms have custom closets and drawers. Outside, enjoy the new stone patio, perfect for a night by the firepit. With expansion potential and a separate garage, this home offers endless possibilities. There are so many upgrades inside and out, too numerous to list here; you simply don't want to miss this one! Ideally located just a short walk to Rowayton's shops and restaurants, Pinkney Park, the riverfront and Bayley Beach.
