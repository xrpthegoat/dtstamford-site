About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 1994 Colonial, perfectly situated on a premier, flat, dry acre at the end of a quiet, Private Cul-de-Sac. The experience begins the moment you turn down the picturesque driveway, offering a beautiful approach and an immediate sense of privacy upon arrival. Boasting 4,423 square feet of above-grade living space, this home is bathed in incredible natural light throughout. The thoughtfully designed layout features 5 spacious bedrooms and 4.5 well-appointed baths, offering plenty of room for family and guests alike. The heart of the home provides a seamless flow and an incredible space for entertaining friends and family, whether hosting formal dinners or casual weekend gatherings. A massive, versatile bonus recreation room sits privately over the 3-car garage-perfect for a playroom, media center, home gym, or an expansive home office. Step outside to enjoy the sprawling, level backyard, a rare find that offers endless possibilities for outdoor fun, gardening, or relaxation. Impeccably cared for - freshly painted inside and out and ready for its next chapter, this exceptional Darien property is just waiting for its new owners to move in and start making memories. Turnkey convenience meets timeless elegance in an unbeatable location!
