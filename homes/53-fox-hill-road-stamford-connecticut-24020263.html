About this home

Tucked away on a bucolic North Stamford cul-de-sac, with access to a private association lake, this spacious and fully renovated home awaits its new owners. The open floor plan is ideal for everyday living and entertaining and is anchored by the massive kitchen with oversized island, dining area, walk-in pantry, 48" gas range and even 2 dishwashers. The family room with gas fireplace is just a few steps up from the kitchen and has a full bath - perfect for overflow guests. The primary bedroom is its own oasis and features a sleek, luxurious bathroom and an entire wall of closets. Three more bedrooms, and a bathroom fit for a home-decor magazine, complete the bedroom wing. Additional features include a 3-car garage, play/rec room, workshop, climate-controlled wine cellar, and plenty of storage space. The house has been freshly painted inside and out, and the long list of other recent upgrades includes renovated bathrooms and kitchen, refinished floors, extensive landscaping and tree removal, upgraded insulation, irrigation and alarm systems, Sonos audio system in the kitchen/living room/dining room, a whole house generator, and more! Set on a landscaped 1.47 acres, this home is truly move-in ready and not to be missed.
