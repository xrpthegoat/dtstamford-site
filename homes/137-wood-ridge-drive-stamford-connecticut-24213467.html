About this home

Location, location, location! Welcome to 137 Wood Ridge Drive nestled in Stamford's highly desirable Mid-Ridges neighborhood. This 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath front-to-back split-level home offers a versatile layout ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The open-concept kitchen features granite countertops and stainless steel appliances and flows seamlessly into the dining room and sunken living room complete with a fireplace. Upstairs, you'll find two bedrooms and a full hall bathroom, along with a spacious primary suite featuring ample closet space and a private ensuite bathroom. The lower-level family room is a true standout, with a second fireplace and built-in bar. Sliding doors open to a patio and flat, fully fenced backyard. An additional flexible-use room with an ensuite half bath offers endless possibilities as a fourth bedroom, home office, au pair suite or in-law space. A finished basement by CT Basement Systems provides valuable additional storage. Enjoy all that the Mid-Ridges has to offer, with convenient access to Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, places of worship, downtown Stamford and more!
