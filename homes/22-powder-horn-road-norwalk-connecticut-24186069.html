About this home

Located on the Norwalk/Darien border, this beautifully styled, move-in-ready 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom home, is perfectly situated on a highly desirable and quiet cul-de-sac in West Norwalk. Blending classic charm with a bright, airy aesthetic, this home offers an abundance of space and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Step inside to a spacious, sun-drenched living room centered around a cozy fireplace and anchored by newly refinished hardwood floors throughout the house. The large picture window floods the space with natural light while the fluid layout guides you into the dining room, complete with an elegant bay window-the perfect backdrop for hosting dinner parties or casual family meals. At the heart of the home is the beautiful kitchen, featuring rich granite countertops, and stainless-steel appliances. The welcoming family room, complete with glass sliders, opens directly onto the expansive back deck, creating an effortless layout for entertaining. The four spacious, versatile bedrooms-all have hardwood flooring and ample closet storage-serviced by two full baths. For those looking for extra potential, the full basement boasts remarkably high ceilings, offering an ideal footprint for a future recreation room, home gym, or extensive storage. Outside, the oversized deck serves as your private sanctuary. Backing up to peaceful, wooded land, the property provides a rare level of privacy. Perfectly positioned for effortless living, just moments from town and local shops.
