About this home

Welcome to this charming Cape Cod home nestled in Stamford's highly desirable Springdale neighborhood, where comfort, convenience, and lifestyle come together seamlessly. This thoughtfully updated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home is truly move-in ready and filled with warmth and character. From the moment you arrive, you'll be drawn to the inviting screened-in front porch, the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee or relax at the end of the day. The spacious deck offers an ideal setting for entertaining, summer nights, and gatherings with family and friends. Inside, the home features a warm and functional layout enhanced by recent updates, including a beautifully renovated bathroom completed just two years ago and a recently installed new water heater. Energy-efficient solar panels, a $44,000 investment by the current owner, provide valuable long-term savings and added peace of mind. Location is everything, and this home truly delivers. Just steps from Springdale's restaurants, shops, and everyday conveniences, with quick access to Metro-North for an easy commute to NYC. Whether you're a first-time homebuyer or looking ahead to your next chapter, this is an opportunity you won't want to miss.
