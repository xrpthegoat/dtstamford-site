About this home

Cos Cob Perfection! Location, location, location! This turnkey 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath rental features an open floor plan, a spacious yard and is a stone's throw from the local Cos Cob Elementary school, shops, the famous Rinaldi's deli, restaurants, and offers easy transportation access! Pull into the two-car garage and enter the home through a large mudroom perfect for storage. This leads to the first floor's open floor plan, which includes a living room with a wood-burning fireplace, a large dining room adjacent to an open gourmet kitchen, and a big family room in the rear overlooking the flat yard. Hardwood floors throughout. French door sliders lead to the patio for easy grilling and outdoor entertainment. There are a few steps up to the second floor, which has three bedrooms sharing a full hallway bath. Half bath attached to master bathroom with heated floors for convenience and comfort. Don't miss the large bonus room downstairs, which is equipped with a beautifully renovated full bath and an additional laundry room. Efficient home with natural gas and tankless hot water. Welcome to Cos Cob!
