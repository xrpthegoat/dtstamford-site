About this home

Welcome to this sun-filled Colonial in the heart of Westport! This beautifully updated 4-bedroom home offers the perfect blend of classic charm and modern convenience. Enjoy peace of mind with city water, city sewer, a generator, and an outdoor sprinkler system. Inside, an expanded Kitchen and Family Room create a bright, open-concept, ideal for everyday living and entertaining. A spacious Screened Porch invites indoor-outdoor enjoyment, while the Mudroom adds functionality and ease of entry from the garage. Upstairs, the freshly added Jack & Jill bath offers smart convenience for busy mornings. A flexible main-level office with an adjacent full Bath can easily convert to a first-floor Bedroom-perfect for guests or multi-generational living. There is even additional open finished space in the lower level for a playroom. Located in a sought-after Westport neighborhood close to town, schools, and commuting options, this home checks every box for comfort, space, and style.
