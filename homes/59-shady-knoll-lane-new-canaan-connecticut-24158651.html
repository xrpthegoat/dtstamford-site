About this home

Ideally located in one of New Canaan's most sought-after neighborhoods, this beautifully maintained Colonial offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and everyday living. The renovated eat-in kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring updated appliances, countertops, flooring, and backsplash, and opens to inviting living spaces designed for both entertaining and daily life. The spacious living and dining rooms are ideal for gatherings, while the screened-in side porch provides a peaceful spot to relax and enjoy the surrounding property. Upstairs are four bedrooms, including a renovated primary suite with a walk-in closet and an updated bath featuring an expanded shower, new vanity, and updated flooring. The finished walk-out lower level adds valuable flexibility, offering space for a home office, gym, playroom, or second family room. Outside, a large patio overlooks the private backyard set on over an acre, creating an ideal setting for outdoor entertaining, play, or quiet evenings surrounded by nature.
