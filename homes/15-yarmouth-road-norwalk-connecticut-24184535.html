About this home

Perched on one of Bell Island's most coveted elevated sites, this exceptional residence commands panoramic waterfront views while offering complete privacy in this community. The property uniquely spans from Yarmouth to Westmere, featuring the highly sought-after convenience of a 2-car garage. This thoughtfully designed home showcases four generous bedrooms and four-and-a-half baths across three well-appointed levels. The first floor's open concept layout creates an effortless flow ideal for both daily living and elegant entertaining, while a private office/study provides a quiet retreat for work or relaxation. The walk-out lower level, complete with full bath, offers endless possibilities for recreation or additional living space. Step onto the covered deck to dine al fresco or simply unwind while taking in the meticulously landscaped grounds and stunning water vistas. As a Bell Island resident, you'll enjoy exclusive access to coveted association amenities, including two private beaches and mooring opportunities coordinated through the harbormaster. This remarkable property delivers the perfect blend of serene waterfront living and refined comfort-your own private sanctuary in one of the area's most desirable locations.
