About this home

Enjoy spectacular views of Long Island Sound from this completely renovated, sophisticated 4 bedroom, 3 1/2 bath home. Located directly across from the beach, in the private Pine Point Association of Rowayton. This home has the best of seaside living. The attention to detail inside the house leaves you feeling like you are inside a ship. There is no 'off season' for enjoying the water views. If you are working from home, the home office will make work enjoyable with views spanning the sound and Long Island. In the evenings, the large patio is the perfect place to soak up the sun while relaxing with friends. Tuck behind the mature plantings if you prefer a bit more privacy, while dining al-fresco. The in-law suite features a private bath and sitting area, allowing everyone some space. This is a special opportunity to experience what luxury seaside living in Rowayton has to offer. Available for academic year, annual or short term. NOT AVAILABLE mid July-mid August 2025.
