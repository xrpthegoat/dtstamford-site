About this home

Don't miss this charming newly renovated cape located in Southfield Point, the beautiful beachside enclave between Davenport Point and Dolphin Cove. The oversized lot tucked in the corner of this magical community is part of a private association which includes a private beach and dock with deepwater slips. Open concept living includes bright kitchen which flows into dining room. Dining room leads into lofted living room with skylights and grand fireplace. Upstairs includes three bedrooms with an option to turn loft space into a fourth. Primary bedroom suite includes a primary bath with steam shower and ample closet space. The two remaining bedrooms share a full bath, also located next to the office. New kitchen, fresh paint throughout, new A/C condensers, new garage, new washer/dryer, landscaping and more. Close to train station and downtown Stamford, and minutes from the quaint town of Old Greenwich.
