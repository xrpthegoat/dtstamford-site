About this home

Privacy, luxury, and effortless indoor-outdoor living define this striking residence, tucked away on a private road. A dramatic double-door, classic portico, and waterfall staircase create a memorable first impression. At the heart of the home is a spacious, modern kitchen designed for both everyday living and entertaining - featuring a Sub-Zero refrigerator, glass cabinetry, statement lighting, beverage cooler, built-in desk, and a sun-filled dining area that feels like a year-round retreat. The formal living and dining rooms with beautiful parquet floors provide elegant gathering spaces, while the family room with stone fireplace opens seamlessly to a covered patio for easy outdoor flow. The expansive 1st floor primary suite offers one-floor living, complete with fireplace, huge custom walk-in closet, and a spa-inspired granite tiled bath. A refined study with built-ins and fireplace creates the perfect work-from-home setting. Upstairs, 3 generously sized bedrooms and 2 baths offer comfort and privacy for family or guests. Outside, the professionally landscaped backyard feels like a private resort, featuring a stunning Gunite pool, landscape waterfall and covered entertaining space. The massive unfinished basement adds immense opportunity for expansion. Minutes to Sterling Farms Golf, the JCC, Italian Center, shopping, dining, and just 10 minutes to Stamford's downtown and Metro-North station - this home delivers space, convenience, and a relaxed, elevated lifestyle.
