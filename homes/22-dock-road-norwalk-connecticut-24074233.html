About this home

SUMMER FURNISHED RENTAL, 3 MINS WALK TO BEACH IN SECLUDED WATERFRONT COMMUNITY! Just bring your suitcase! This quiet completely renovated home is superbly situated, only 3 min walk to beach yet only 5 min drive to South Norwalk train station. It is flooded with light and offers EXPANSIVE VIEWS of Long Island Sound and its ever changing tidal wildlife. Upstairs features an huge sun-filled open floor plan including living/dining/kitchen area with fireplace, and separate windowed family room all creating easy living and entertaining spaces. Enjoy the panoramic views from 2 decks with outdoor shower and gas grill. Garage accommodates 2 smaller cars or 1 large car. This waterfront community boasts a beautiful white sand beach, 2 tennis courts, park, playground and a new deep water dock. The museums, boutiques and restaurants of SoNo are minutes away! New Pickleball courts just outside of Village Creek! Available July 3-Sept 3
