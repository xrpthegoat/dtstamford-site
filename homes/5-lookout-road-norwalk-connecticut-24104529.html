About this home

Welcome to 5 Lookout Road! A charming colonial home nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the desirable Silvermine section of Norwalk. This property has undergone a full renovation, where it was stripped down to its core structure and and thoughtfully redesigned with modern finishes. Every aspect has been meticulously updated to provide a fresh and contemporary living space, ensuring that all systems and features meet today's standards. The inviting living room features a stunning replica of the iconic Delmonico bar, adding a touch of sophistication and unique flair ideal for hosting friends and family gatherings. Imagine entertaining in style with this eye-catching focal point! Step outside to discover the allure of a beautifully screened-in porch with bluestone flooring, offering a serene space to relax and enjoy the lush, professionally landscaped yard. Cozy up in the family room, complete with a gas fireplace, perfect for those chilly nights or casual family movie nights. Retreat to the primary bedroom suite, which features a lovely walk-out porch, ideal for enjoying your morning coffee while basking in the fresh air and serene surroundings. With two additional spacious bedrooms, this home provides ample space for comfortable living. 5 Lookout Road is a rare find, combining style, comfort, and a fantastic location!
