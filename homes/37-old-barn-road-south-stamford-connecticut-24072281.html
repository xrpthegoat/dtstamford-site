About this home

Great 3 Bedroom 3 Bath home in convenient location set on .21 acre level property. Eat-In Kitchen with Dining Area opens to both the Living and Family Rooms allowing for open flow. Family Room has skylight, french doors to the patio and a separate Full Bath. Spacious Primary Bedroom with walk-in closet is also located on the main level and has an ensuite bath. Separate Laundry room with sink and folding area has a door to the exterior. The second floor has two additional Bedrooms with eaves storage and full baths. There is a fenced backyard with stone wall for additional privacy and the shed is also included. One car garage with interior access to the Family Room plus plenty of additional parking in the paved driveway. Very private fenced backyard with Patio great for entertaining.
