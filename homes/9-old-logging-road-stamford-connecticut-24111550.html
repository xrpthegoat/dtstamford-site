About this home

Welcome to this meticulously maintained 3BR, 2.5baths ranch-style home nestled on a serene and level 1.36-acre lot in desirable North Stamford. Perfectly blending modern upgrades with timeless charm, this home offers comfort, style, and convenience just minutes from local amenities. Step inside to discover a thoughtfully renovated interior featuring 10-11" raised ceilings, custom woodwork, and European iron hot water radiators that add character and warmth. The spacious living areas are complemented by all-new plumbing and wiring, ensuring peace of mind for years to come. The heart of the home boasts a seamless flow from the spacious kitchen to the inviting living and family spaces, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Each bedroom is generously sized with new custom closets. Many but not all improvements include a new roof, new hot water heater, walls insulation, and foundation waterproofing. Enjoy outdoor living on the new granite patio, perfect for relaxing or hosting guests in your private backyard oasis with a playground and zip-lines. A whole-house generator adds an extra layer of security and comfort. This move-in-ready gem combines the tranquility of a spacious lot with proximity to shopping, schools, and commuter routes.
