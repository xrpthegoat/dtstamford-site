About this home

This colonial-style home, thoughtfully redesigned and rebuilt by a builder for his own enjoyment, blends classic charm with modern convenience. Boasting five bedrooms and three bathrooms, it offers ample space for family and guests. The main floor features a gracious entryway and sitting room with a wood-burning fireplace. The home office is bright and cheerful. The main floor bedroom is spacious and adjacent to a full bath. You will love living and entertaining in the open concept kitchen, dining area and family room which is the heart of this home. The beautiful gas fireplace creates a cozy gathering space. The dining area can accommodate a large group and offers direct access to the patio and rear yard. The center island in the beautifully appointed kitchen offers additional seating. There are beautiful built ins throughout this area of the home. The finished lower level adds versatility, while the new second floor features three guest rooms and a large hall bath with water closet, custom cabinetry, and double sink. The primary suite stands out with its stunning tray ceiling, expansive walk-in closet, and a spa-like bathroom complete with double sinks, a custom vanity, an oversized shower with dual shower heads and whirlpool. Convenience is key with laundry facilities on the upper level, and an additional washer and dryer set in the basement (pending hookup). Located on a great cul-de-sac, the home is close to shopping, a golf course, and all essential amenities.
