About this home

An impeccable home in an incredible location! Just a stone's throw to Compo Beach, Ned Dimes Marina and Longshore Club Park, this gorgeous 4 bedroom, 4.5 bath modern Colonial with a pool is beach neighborhood perfection. The main floor is beautifully arranged, the family, dining area and kitchen span the back of the house creating a great room - with French doors to the patio and pool just beyond. The gorgeous kitchen is down in white oak, with a massive island, top appliances and smart storage. Gorgeous custom details throughout include built ins, dramatic modern millwork, sleek fireplaces and high end stone surfaces plus an elevator. Upstairs, the primary suite is a dream - a bright and airy bedroom, two walk in closets, and an oversized bath with soaking tub, steam shower and radient flooring. There are two additional bedrooms on the second floor, plus an office and laundry room. The third floor holds a bedroom and full bath. Tucked on a quiet side street steps to the water and all the Compo/Longshore area amenities like beach, boating, golf and tennis, while also being just a quick ride into town or to the train!
