About this home

Welcome home to 31 Longmeadows Road in North Wilton. This private oasis on 2.3 acres backs directly to the Wilton Conservation Land Trust, ensuring views are protected. Features extensive 2024-2025 renovations, an open floor plan, and new hardwood floors on the main level. Anchored by an 8-foot island, the chef's kitchen features quartz counters and smart LG appliances with app control, double ovens with air fry/sous vide settings, and a steam dishwasher. Large bay windows flood the main living spaces with light. The living room features a wood stove updated in 2024 with a stone surround, matte granite, and a 150-year-old reclaimed wood mantle. A standout feature is the gorgeous wood and stone sunroom, boasting a full wall of windows and 2 skylights for year round enjoyment of the grounds, with refreshing breezes in warmer weather and cozy nights by the fire in the 2nd wood stove. The layout includes 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths, including 2 renovated in 2025 with designer finishes. The main floor and piano room were professionally painted in 2025. Outside, the pool is open and ready for summer, surrounded by mature landscaping curated by a master arborist, including numerous varieties of Acer Palmatum (Japanese Maples). For the energy conscious, leased solar panels bring reduced cost. Key updates include a 2018 roof, 2014 boiler, and 100-amp electrical panel. This turnkey home offers a true escape from the chaos in a top-rated school district.
