About this home

SONO Colonial in walking distance to the South Norwalk Boat Club, restaurants and shops on Washington Street. This wonderful home offers 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths. As you enter the home you'll notice the masonry and blue stone front porch. The tiled foyer opens up to the Dining and living room with hardwood floors and tons of light from the bay window. The kitchen has been recently updated with cabinets, granite counters and stainless steel appliances. The main floor offers a master bedroom with office and dressing room along with a full bath. The second floor offers 3 additional bedrooms mostly hardwood floors and 1 full bath. Other features include a long paved driveway for at least 4-6 cars and a rear yard and patio.
