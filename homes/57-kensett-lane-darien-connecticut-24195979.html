About this home

Experience refined living in this exceptional custom townhome, ideally situated in Darien's prestigious Kensett community. One of the largest "Willow" floor plans, this immaculate residence offers over 5,200 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space with 3 bedrooms, 5.5 baths, and a private elevator serving all three levels. Crafted for both everyday comfort and elegant entertaining, the home features soaring 10-foot ceilings, rich hardwood floors, a gourmet kitchen with premium finishes, a butler's pantry, private home office, and a spectacular great room that opens through four sets of French doors to an expansive 36-foot deck. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, complete with a fireplace, dual walk-in closets, and separate spa-inspired bathrooms. The finished walk-out lower level includes a full bath, abundant storage, and flexible space ideal for guests, recreation, or a potential in-law suite. As the only end-of-cul-de-sac residence in Kensett with this unique layout, it offers exceptional privacy and outdoor living with both an oversized deck and private stone terrace. Residents enjoy resort-style amenities including a clubhouse, heated pool and spa, fitness center, fire pit, walking trails, meadows, and a scenic conservation area. Just minutes from downtown Darien, Metro-North, beaches, shopping, dining, and top-rated schools, this home delivers luxury, convenience, and an unparalleled lifestyle.
