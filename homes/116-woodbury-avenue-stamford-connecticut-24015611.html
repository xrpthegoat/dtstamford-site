About this home

Welcome to this sunlit, happy 1946 colonial boasting 4 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, and spacious rooms, all nestled on .19 acres bordered by a tranquil wooded nature preserve, ensuring privacy on your back deck. The large eat-in kitchen features stainless appliances, a gas range, and an island perfect for gatherings. Cozy up by the fireplace in the living room with hardwood floors, while the formal dining room offers ample space for family gatherings. The family room, complete with built-ins and treetop views, opens to a lovely deck for outdoor entertaining. The lower level features a play/rec room, full bath, and an additional bedroom, ideal for guests or au pairs, with laundry facilities and plenty of storage. Key amenities include a gas range, central air, and an electric dog fence surrounding the property. Commuters will appreciate the proximity to Springdale Train Station and village amenities such as restaurants, shopping, and schools. Don't miss out on this gem!
