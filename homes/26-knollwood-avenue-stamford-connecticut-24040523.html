About this home

This home is turnkey, centrally-located, private, bright, and tranquil. Your primary suite is your sanctuary: whether relaxing on your bed gazing at the stars, watching your favorite program or laying by the fire. Your bathroom is spacious & updated, and you have plenty of closet space. You can gather by the fireplace in the living room or enjoy the sunroom that has an outdoor TV & audio system. The rec room with a wet bar for entertaining, laundry room with a refrigerator for extra food storage, and workshop for those home projects are located in the lower level. There's more! Your beautifully landscaped backyard is your paradise. Imagine floating in your own private pool with a nice deck for dining while listening to music from your outdoor speakers. No need to drag the hose to water your yard since this home has a 5-zone irrigation system. You also have 2 storage sheds with electricity. Parking is never an issue with plenty of space on your driveway--and it's equipped with an electric gate to access your detached garage. Your property is fenced in for privacy, has keyless entry front door, 4 Ring security cameras with a Ring chime, and motion-sensor lights in the front & back of the home. Your home also has a gas generator, 20-seer central A/C, spray foam & blown insulation making this home energy efficient--and a UV light air purifier system in the air handlers creating a healthy breathing environment.
