About this home

Are you looking for a home or investment opportunity with beautiful water views? Welcome to 11 Hawkins Landing in East Norwalk, a rare standalone unit within a five-home community. This property combines the privacy of a single-family residence with direct waterfront views, making it an ideal home or rental property. The main floor features an open-concept kitchen and living area, framed by floor-to-ceiling windows that flood the space with natural light and showcase water vistas. Additionally, there is a bedroom and full bath on this level, providing convenience and versatility for residents or guests. A striking wide spiral staircase serves as an architectural centerpiece, leading to the second-floor primary suite. This spacious retreat features vaulted ceilings, a full bath, in-unit laundry, and two sets of sliders that open to a private deck, where you can unwind and take in the view. The top level, accessible by another spiral staircase, offers extra storage space for added practicality. With a private driveway for two cars and easy access to East Norwalk's parks, beach, restaurants, and train station, this property is a beautiful place to call home. Close proximity to I-95 makes it an ideal spot for commuters, offering both convenience and a sought-after location. Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity!
