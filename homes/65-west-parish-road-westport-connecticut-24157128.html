About this home

Bright, beautifully maintained home in Westport's desirable Greens Farms neighborhood. Sun-filled rooms and soaring 91/2-foot ceilings create an open, airy feel throughout the home. The flexible layout includes a bedroom and new full bathroom on the main level, with two additional bedrooms and a full bath upstairs, ideal for guests, a home office, or comfortable family living. The private fenced yard features a small deck and stone patio, perfect for outdoor dining, entertaining, relaxing, pets, or play. Sidewalks lead directly to Greens Farms Elementary School, making it especially convenient for families. Big Y, Greens Farms Church, restaurants, and neighborhood conveniences are just a short walk away. Only a 4 minute drive to the Greens Farms Metro-North train station and I-95 Exit 18 with easy access to Westport beaches, downtown shopping, and dining.
