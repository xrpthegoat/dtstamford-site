About this home

Beautifully remodeled and impeccably maintained, this stunning 3-bedroom, 2-bath home offers a perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and thoughtful craftsmanship. Custom molding and carpentry throughout add warmth and character, while an abundance of windows fills the home with beautiful natural light. The finished basement expands the living space with a spacious family room ideal for entertaining, along with an additional room offering flexibility for a home office, guest room, or other personal needs. The home is equipped with central air, a tankless water heater, whole-house water filtration system, and sprinkler system. Step outside to your own private retreat, featuring a beautiful stone patio and custom stone barbecue perfect for family gatherings, summer cookouts, and entertaining friends. Charming stone walls enhanced by solar lighting create a warm and inviting outdoor ambiance. From the elegant finishes to the carefully designed details throughout, this home is truly move-in ready and designed for comfortable living and memorable gatherings. A must-see Norwalk home that combines beauty, functionality, and timeless appeal. Ideally located with easy access to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway, this home is conveniently close to schools, shopping centers, restaurants, and everyday amenities. Just approximately 5 minutes from the train station, offering an easy commute and excellent connectivity. A wonderful location for those seeking both comfort and convenience.
