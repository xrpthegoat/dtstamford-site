About this home

Tucked away on a quiet street in the sought-after Shippan neighborhood, this home has been lovingly maintained by its current owner for over 50 years. This move-in ready property offers an open floor plan on the main level with plenty of living space, dramatic vaulted ceilings in the living and dining rooms, and access to the large back deck. The kitchen embraces the mid-century modern flair of the home and has plenty of work and storage space. Three bedrooms and two updated bathrooms complete this floor. The lower level, completely above grade, features a large family room with fireplace, a fourth bedroom, full bathroom, an office, laundry, and access to the oversized 2-car garage. Freshly painted throughout, this is the perfect way to get your foot in the door of this amazing neighborhood, within easy reach of Stamford train station (50 minutes to NYC!) and all downtown shops and restaurants. Enjoy the unique traditions and camaraderie of this special enclave, within steps of Long Island Sound yet safely outside the flood zone.
