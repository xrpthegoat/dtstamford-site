About this home

Find Your Springdale Sanctuary. This modern 4-bedroom, 3 full and 2 half-bathroom Colonial-style home in sought-after Springdale is a dream come true. Imagine strolling down tree-lined sidewalks, grabbing coffee at the local cafe, and hopping on the train for a quick commute to NYC - all just a 7-minute walk away. Inside, you'll discover an open and airy floor plan featuring gleaming hardwood floors throughout. A separate dining room, and both an inviting living and a spacious family room with a two-sided fireplace, make this house perfect for entertaining. The gourmet kitchen, complete with a large island, quartz countertops, and top-of-the-line Bertazzoni appliances, is a chef's delight. With four bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with a walk-in closet and spa-like ensuite bathroom, as well as a guest bedroom with an ensuite bathroom, there's plenty of room for the whole family to spread out and work from home. The oversized finished walk-out basement provides extra space for recreation or relaxation. An attached 2-car garage ensures convenient parking. Outside, enjoy the peace and quiet of this charming neighborhood while relaxing on the front porch or on the backyard patio admiring the perfectly manicured yard. Need extra space? There's an unfinished walk-up attic for extra storage. Last but not least the house comes with a portable full-house generator for peace of mind. Schedule your visit today and discover the Springdale lifestyle you've been dreaming of.
