About this home

Contemporary-designed and truly one-of-a-kind, this single-family home sits on nearly two acres at the end of a private cul-de-sac in the sought-after Cranbury neighborhood of Norwalk. The main residence features three wood-burning fireplaces, an in-ground pool, patio, walkways, courtyard, and a separate one-bedroom cottage. The three-bedroom main house includes a primary suite with a private bath. The living room is highlighted by a fireplace, vaulted ceiling, and floor-to-ceiling glass doors that open to a wraparound balcony. The spacious open-concept kitchen features granite countertops, island, wood cabinetry, a breakfast nook, and its own fireplace. The lower level is designed for entertaining, with a family room featuring a fireplace and walk-out access to the courtyard, along with the adjoining entertainment room with bar area. Outside, the pool area creates a private oasis surrounded by a tile and stone patio, offering a tranquil setting for relaxing, entertaining, and enjoying the beauty of the surrounding grounds. The separate one-bedroom cottage with fireplace offers flexible options for guests, in-law living, or potential income. Tucked away in a private setting, yet close to all that Cranbury has to offer, 5 Bayne Street is a distinctive retreat offering tranquility, privacy, and exceptional character. Come experience this unique oasis and make it your own.
