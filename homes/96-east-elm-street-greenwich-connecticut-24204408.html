About this home

This is the one you've been waiting for. Set within the gated Arbor Rose Association, this beautifully renovated condo pairs a serene, manicured setting with effortless access to Greenwich Avenue. Step inside the inviting interior with 10-foot ceilings and an elevator, where natural light moves through the open living and dining rooms into a custom kitchen and welcoming family room with fireplace. The spacious primary suite offers a spa bath, extra large walk-in closet, coffee bar, and flows to an office or sitting room with a private balcony. Two additional bedrooms and laundry room complete the upstairs. The finished lower level provides a second family room, flexibility for a fourth bedroom or gym, and a full bath. Outside, private landscaped spaces with room for a BBQ create a peaceful setting for morning coffee or evenings at home, while the nearby train allows for a seamless commute to New York City.
