About this home

Well-loved family home, first time on the market in many years, and ready to make your own. 3 Bedrooms upstairs with a full bathroom. There is also a full bathroom on the main level with a possible 4 th bedroom /office/ Au-pair room/playroom. Large eat-in-kitchen with ample space for a center island. Bring your designer's eye and make this your forever home. Hardwood floors underneath the wall-to-wall carpets, except in the office. Sold " As Is".
