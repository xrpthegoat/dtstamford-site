About this home

Set on a quiet, private cul-de-sac very close to town and Winslow Park, this custom five-bedroom Colonial offers an exceptional opportunity for a new owner to leave their mark and add significant value. Designed with best-in-class materials and a thoughtfully planned layout, the home retains its gracious scale, natural flow, and exceptional character. Deferred maintenance has created the chance to elevate a well-loved property and return it to the signature splendor that defined it. The main level features a gourmet kitchen with Sub Zero & Viking appliances, stone counters, custom cabinetry and an adjacent breakfast room leading to a large family room, living room with fireplace, formal dining room, and two private offices with built-ins. French doors from most main floor rooms open to a balcony overlooking a heated Gunite pool and a large, level play area. The walkout lower level has a wall of French doors and is an exquisite poolside space that includes a fully-equipped wet bar, rec/game rm, gym and a full guest suite. Upstairs, the spacious bedroom layout provides comfort and flexibility for a variety of needs. Set on 1.16 +/- acres moments from shopping and recreation, the home is being offered as-is. ***Listing photos include a mix of current 2026 images and select photos from 2009 to illustrate the full potential of the property. Please note that showings are welcomed and booked by appointment, and no open houses are planned.***
