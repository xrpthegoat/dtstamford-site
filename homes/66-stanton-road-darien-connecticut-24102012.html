About this home

Welcome to 66 Stanton Rd! Yes, that fabulous home with the beautiful VT slate roof! Sitting proudly atop 1.34 manicured acres, this handsome 1938 clapboard clad colonial demands to be noticed. This house underwent a major renovation and expansion that was completed in 2009. Entertaining spaces abound both formal and casual, perfect for hosting both gatherings intimate and large in scale. Step into the generous entrance hall with a gracious curving stairway that sweeps upwards to the second-floor landing that mirrors the curve of the staircase. The South facing front of the house has formal living and dining rooms that flank the entry foyer. The open plan kitchen/family room features a large pantry space, double ovens, Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances, a walnut island and separate dining area. Step down into the wood paneled family room with vaulted ceiling, exposed beams, fireplace and a wall of windows looking over the back yard. Just off the kitchen is a generous wet bar with a second dishwasher and desk space. The spacious primary suite has a vaulted ceiling, separate dressing/closet area and a reading porch! The sun filled bathroom has an oversized steam shower with rain shower, hand-held, and wall mounted shower heads, as well as a soaking tub. There are 5 additional bedrooms on the second floor, 2 ensuite and 2 share a Jack/Jill bath, one that has a coveted sleeping loft. Also on the second level is a study/lounge area and the walk-up attic has ample storage capacity.
