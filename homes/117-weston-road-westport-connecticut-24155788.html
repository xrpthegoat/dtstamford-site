About this home

Set in a serene natural setting, this custom Colonial offers a comfortable layout with a balance of privacy and convenient access to top-rated schools, shopping, and dining. The main living spaces are bright and inviting, with generous windows that frame views of the surrounding greenery and create an easy flow for everyday living and entertaining. Upstairs, four bedrooms are located on one floor, offering a practical and cohesive bedroom arrangement. The top level features an extra room that can serve as a bedroom, home office, playroom, or media room-flexible to suit a variety of needs. Outdoors, enjoy a private yard retreat with a heated gunite pool and spa, surrounded by mature landscaping for a peaceful setting ideal for relaxing or hosting. This property delivers a tucked-away feel while remaining close to modern conveniences.
