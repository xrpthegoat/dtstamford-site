About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained North Stamford home, thoughtfully designed for comfortable living and effortless entertaining. Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac and set back from the road, the property offers exceptional privacy and a serene setting. Inside, the spacious living room with a classic wood-burning fireplace creates a warm and inviting atmosphere and flows seamlessly into a formal dining room with direct access to the deck and backyard, perfect for gatherings and indoor-outdoor living. The large eat-in kitchen serves as the heart of the home, featuring a dedicated breakfast area, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a dual wall oven, and abundant cabinetry, while the adjoining family room offers a second wood-burning fireplace, a powder room, and additional access to the deck and yard. Upstairs, the primary suite provides a true retreat with a private full bathroom and walk-in closet, complemented by four additional bedrooms, and another full bath. The expansive lower level adds valuable flexibility with a large recreation room, private office, and plentiful storage. A two-car garage with high ceilings completes the home. Ideally located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping, and dining, this property combines privacy, space, and everyday convenience in one exceptional offering.
