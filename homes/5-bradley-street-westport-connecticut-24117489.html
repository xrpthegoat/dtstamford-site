About this home

Completely Rebuilt in 2013 FEMA-compliant 4-5 BR Westport Beach House located in highly sought after Compo Beach Area, offers picture perfect living. The main level features a sunny Kitchen & Family Room w/ gas fireplace & deck; walk-in pantry, front hall closet, Office, Full Bath and three-exposure oversized Flex/Great Room. There are 4 bedrooms on 2nd floor, incl. a spacious Primary Suite w/ vaulted ceilings and sitting area, primary Bath w/double vanities, soaker tub and separate W/C; 3 more bedrooms, Bathroom and Laundry Room complete the 2nd floor. 3rd floor aerie with Full Bath that can be used as a 5th BR Guest Suite. An oversized 2 car garage is just off the Mudroom, storage, whole-house generator & fully fenced landscaped yard. New since Fall 2023: Refinished white oak floors; interior paint; Samsung Pedestal W/D; Bosch DW; upgraded gas FP; centrally monitored alarm system with camera network; LED Lighting & Fans; newly renovated garage with full epoxy-coat floor, storage, & new EV charger; remote whole-house water shut-off. Also new since 2023: front door, storm door, Marvin glass slider; 3 Toto Washlets; New vanities, medicine cabinets, faucets; Outside, new irrigation system, landscaping, and lighting, plus repaved driveway. Walk east 1 block and enjoy a gorgeous swath of quiet sandy beach. Walk south and arrive at the park and marina. With homes on the block commanding more than twice the price, an exceptional value, a joyful space and a move-in ready home.
