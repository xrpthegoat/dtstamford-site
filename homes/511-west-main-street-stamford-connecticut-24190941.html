About this home

Well-maintained 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse conveniently located near Downtown Stamford and the Greenwich border, with easy access to I-95, Route 1, shopping, restaurants, and the Stamford Train Station. The main level features a spacious living and dining area with access to a private balcony, an updated kitchen with remodeled cabinetry and newer appliances, and a convenient half bath. Upstairs, you'll find two generously sized bedrooms, including a primary bedroom with dual closets and a full bathroom. The third-floor loft offers versatile space that can be used as a home office, den, playroom, or additional storage. Additional features include an attached two-car garage, central air conditioning, central vacuum, and an in-unit washer and dryer. This well-maintained home offers a functional layout, tasteful updates, and a convenient location close to transportation, shopping, dining, and other everyday amenities.
