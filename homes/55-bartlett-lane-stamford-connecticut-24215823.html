About this home

Welcome to 55 Bartlett Lane, a charming ranch-style home set on 1.21 acres in the peaceful surroundings of North Stamford. Featuring three bedrooms and two-and-a-half baths, the residence offers comfortable one-level living, a screened-in porch, a two-car garage, and a full unfinished basement offering excellent potential for storage, a workshop, recreation space, or future expansion. A separate studio space below adds wonderful flexibility and can easily serve as a home office, creative studio, hobby room, or quiet retreat. Step outside to a beautiful stone and slate patio accented by natural boulders, creating a wonderful setting for outdoor seating, entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying a summer barbecue. If you love nature, privacy, and the beauty of wildlife, this property offers a peaceful setting to enjoy it all. Mature trees and the natural landscape create a beautiful backdrop throughout the seasons. The home offers tremendous potential for a buyer with vision. Bring your style, imagination, and personal touch to update the spaces and create a home that truly feels like your own. This property is an opportunity to create your own North Stamford retreat, combining flexible living space, natural beauty, privacy, and the freedom to make the home your own.
