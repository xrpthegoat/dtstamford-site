About this home

Easy one floor living in this bright and inviting ranch-style home, nestled on one of Westport's most coveted streets. Located in a fabulous cul-de-sac, this home has endless potential; whether you're looking to move into your forever home or expand and create the home of your dreams in this highly sought after neighborhood. Step into the formal living room, where a wood-burning fireplace sets the tone for cozy gatherings. The room flows seamlessly into the dining area, creating an ideal layout for entertaining. A stunning sunken, four-season family room features a vaulted ceiling, oversized picture windows, and sliding glass doors that lead to an expansive deck-perfect for year-round indoor-outdoor enjoyment. The terraced backyard is a serene oasis with privacy trees and fencing, offering the perfect backdrop for relaxation or gatherings. The kitchen is a chef's delight, featuring a new Samsung stainless steel refrigerator, a Miele dishwasher, a double wall oven, and a bright picture window overlooking the deck. It also opens to a fabulous informal dining area and sitting room. The spacious primary bedroom suite has a luxurious retreat with an ensuite five-fixture bath and dual walk-in closets. Two add'l well-appointed bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. Fabulous finished basement. The seller had intended to build their dream home on this property and have approved plans from the town. Details are available in documents attached.
