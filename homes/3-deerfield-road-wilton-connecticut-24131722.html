About this home

BEST and FINAL 3/3, 12PM. Once Upon a Time....the story of this enchanting Antique begins in 1859. Every magical space combines the charm of history with the ease of modern day. Rustic original hand hewn beams, wide plank hard/soft wood floors, brick fireplace, all drenched in light that adds to the gentle glow and ambience that has welcomed generations. The beautifully renovated Eat in Kitchen is surely the heart of this home, where modern luxury meets timeless charm - gleaming granite island with prep sink and seating, large pantry and counter with appliance garages to help keep surfaces clear. Cozy seating areas to entertain your guests while preparing or for Dining Area. Convenient side door goes directly to powder room and into kitchen for easy grocery unpacking. Brand new Stove/Range. Spacious Living and Dining Room spaces can easily be interchanged, cozy wood fireplace for chilly evenings..flexible living at its best. Doors to a slate patio allow for al fresco dining and morning coffee moments. The Primary Suite with brand new carpet boasts a walk in closet and En Suite Bathroom. Second Floor Laundry is a fabulous convenience. 2 additional Bedrooms with hall bath, one with new carpet offers space for guest or studio, office or nursery. Flat, half acre with storage shed, hot tub, useable backyard and walk to town and train.
