About this home

Originally built in 1769, this beautifully renovated and meticulously maintained antique home blends historic character with modern comfort. Set on two landscaped acres along one of Wilton's most scenic country roads, the four-bedroom, three-and-a-half-bath residence offers approximately 3,758 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space. The renovation included updated electrical systems and a whole-house generator that keeps the home running during power outages. An inviting eat-in kitchen opens to a private patio, while the formal dining room, living room with fireplace, hardwood floors, exposed beams, and period details create warmth and character throughout. The primary suite includes a private bath and walk-in closet, and the finished third-floor offers flexible additional space. A charming antique barn and two-car garage complete the property, with room for a pool. Enjoy peaceful country living close to hiking trails, Weir Farm, schools, markets, the post office, and town amenities.
