About this home

Quiet, end-unit townhouse tucked into a cul-de-sac near the West Norwalk line. Just over 2,000 square feet laid out across three levels for a layout that lives more like a home than a condo. The lower level is a finished family room with custom built-ins, a wet bar, and a recessed media wall built to accommodate a projector setup - ideal as a den, theater, or home office. Upstairs on the main level, hardwood floors run through an open plan: an eat-in kitchen with granite, stainless appliances, and a breakfast bar opens to a combined living and dining area anchored by a wood-burning fireplace and matching built-ins. Sliders lead to a 21-foot deck overlooking mature trees, with steps down to a private lawn - easy access for grilling. An updated powder room completes the level. The top floor holds two generous bedrooms with strong closet space, a full bath, and a side-by-side washer and dryer right where you need them. Direct-entry attached garage, covered front entry, and additional driveway parking. Minutes from Oak Hills Park's golf, tennis, and trails, with a quick drive to Norwalk's restaurants, shops, train, and I-95.
