About this home

Full of character and cottage-style comfort, this farmhouse offers an inviting blend of vintage charm and modern living. The large, welcoming front porch sets the tone-a perfect spot to sip coffee, greet neighbors, or simply unwind. Inside, the main level features an open country kitchen-perfect for gathering with family and friends-while natural sunlight fills every corner. The cozy living room fireplace with vintage tiles adds timeless appeal. With three bedrooms and two full baths, there's plenty of space today and exciting potential to expand into the walk-up attic or extend off the back of the house. Outdoor living and entertaining is a delight - in addition to the front porch there is a large, flat, private backyard that feels like your own retreat. Perfectly located within walking distance to town and with an easy commute to NYC, this home is ready to be enjoyed for years to come. Step inside and fall in love!
