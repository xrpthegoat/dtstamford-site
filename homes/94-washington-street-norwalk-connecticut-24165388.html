About this home

Welcome to 94 Washington Street, Unit 9, a beautifully updated condo offering modern comfort in the heart of Norwalk. Set within the Haviland Arms complex, this light filled residence features 9 ft ceilings and an airy open layout that makes the space feel bright and inviting from the moment you enter. The living area flows seamlessly into the updated kitchen, where clean white cabinetry, sleek counters, and a functional breakfast bar create a stylish and practical space for everyday living and entertaining. Large windows bring in natural light and add to the open feel while also offering city views. The spacious bedroom provides a comfortable retreat with room to unwind, and the full bath is conveniently positioned for easy access. Enjoy the ease of elevator access, low maintenance living, and a location that truly stands out with close proximity to restaurants, shops, public transportation, and everything downtown Norwalk has to offer. Whether you are looking for a primary residence or a smart investment opportunity, this move in ready unit delivers on style, convenience, and location.
