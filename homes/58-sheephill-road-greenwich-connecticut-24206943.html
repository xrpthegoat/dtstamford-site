About this home

Welcome to 58 Sheephill Rd, a thoughtfully expanded and extensively updated home offering exceptional space and flexibility. The first floor features new flooring, updated kitchen cabinetry, backsplash, under-cabinet lighting, newer appliances, and a spacious in-law suite with a second kitchen, living area, bedroom, walk-in closet, and beautifully renovated full bath. The newly added second floor offers three bedrooms, wood flooring, a full hall bath, laundry room, generous storage, and a spacious primary suite with high ceilings, walk-in closet, and luxurious bath featuring double sinks and a large walk-in shower. The finished basement provides additional living space and a half bath. Outside, enjoy a new driveway and Belgium-block walkway, updated deck, fencing, siding, roof, and drainage system, plus beautifully landscaped front and rear gardens. Numerous mechanical and interior updates complete this impressive Riverside offering.
