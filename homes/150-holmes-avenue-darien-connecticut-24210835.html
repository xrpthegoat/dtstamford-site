About this home

Step into a storybook setting at 150 Holmes Avenue, where the timeless elegance of a classic Victorian meets a meticulous, top-to-bottom modernization. From the moment you approach the wrap-around porch, you'll feel the historic charm that defines this Darien gem. Inside, the transformation is striking. The entire interior has been freshly painted in a crisp, sophisticated palette that perfectly complements the brand-new hardwood floors flowing throughout the main levels. The heart of the home is a beautifully updated kitchen, blending contemporary convenience with the home's original soulful character. Every corner of this residence shines with pride of ownership; it is exceptionally clean, bright, and truly move-in ready. Whether you are hosting a formal dinner or enjoying a quiet morning in the sun-drenched living spaces, the atmosphere is one of effortless grace. Beyond the main house, discover a feature that sets this property apart: a detached two-car garage designed for the serious hobbyist. Equipped with a professional grade industrial hook and hoist system for engine work, this space is a dream come true for car enthusiasts or anyone needing a heavy duty workshop. It's a rare find that adds a layer of practical luxury to this enchanting estate. Experience the perfect blend of 19th-century personality and 21st-century peace of mind.
