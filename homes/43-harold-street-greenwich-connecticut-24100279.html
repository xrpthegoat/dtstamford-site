About this home

Fantastic Townhouse with a Movie Theater you would only find in a multi-Million Dollar Home! Fabulous 3 bedrooms/3.5 baths, open plan home. 1st floor has a powder room, spacious kitchen with new SS appliances, inviting dining room, living room with a stone fireplace and home office that opens to a carport in winter, patio for entertaining in warmer months. Bring your popcorn and watch your favorite movie in the custom state of the art home theater in the LL. An additional bedroom and full bath complete the LL. A wonderful home with Greenwich amenities.
