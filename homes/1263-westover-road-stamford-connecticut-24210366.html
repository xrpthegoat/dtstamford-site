About this home

Welcome to Bantam Farm, located in the desirable section of Westover. An inviting stone walkway to the main house welcomes you to the many charming and historic elements you will treasure in this home. 4 bedrooms and 3 baths, wide plank hardwood floors, 3 fireplaces and built-ins are just the beginning. The home's casual space offers unimpeded flow between rooms and terrific design versatility for a vintage property. An optional first floor primary bedroom suite offers a versatility of living options. Upstairs, there are 3 additional bedrooms including a second primary with balcony, and private bath and 2 bedrooms with built-ins. Sitting atop one of the highest points in Westover, with level lawns, expansive gardens and specimen plantings. Upgraded systems and automatic generator provide peace of mind.
