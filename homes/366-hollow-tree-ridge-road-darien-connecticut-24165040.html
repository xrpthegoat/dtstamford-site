About this home

Set along one of Hollow Tree Ridge Road's most desirable stretches, 366 Hollow Tree Ridge Road is a masterfully restored and expanded residence that artfully blends historic character with refined modern living. Reimagined by renowned builder Bo Malpass, the home offers 6 bds, 5.1 baths across 7,356 sq feet. Original character is preserved through antique beams and wide plank wood floors, while modern updates introduce a seamless flow and elevated functionality. Interiors curated by Kristen Fine enhance the home's cohesive aesthetic with understated luxury and timeless finishes. Sun-drenched living spaces unfold effortlessly, creating an ideal setting for both everyday living and elegant entertaining. The custom-designed Poggenpohl kitchen serves as the heart of the home, complemented by multiple inviting gathering spaces. A spacious primary suite and well-appointed secondary bedrooms provide comfort, scale, and flexibility. Set on 1.75 acres of beautifully landscaped grounds, the property features a Gunite pool and a charming guest cottage connected by a breezeway ideal as an in-law suite, private retreat, or dedicated work-from-home space. Ideally located with convenient access to town, train, and Darien's top-rated schools, this exceptional offering presents a rare opportunity to own a turnkey home where historic integrity and modern sophistication converge.
