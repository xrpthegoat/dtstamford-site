About this home

Stunning Ranch in Lower North Stamford! Enjoy easy one level living in this stunning Ranch located on a sought after, quiet cul-de-sac in lower North Stamford, offering 4 Bedrooms and 2.5 Bathrooms. The inviting entry Foyer opens to an elegant Dining Room, large Living Room filled with natural light and featuring a gas fireplace, built-ins and picturesque garden view. The charming "country-style" Eat-In Kitchen showcases stainless steel appliances, Caesarstone counter tops and rich cherry cabinetry. Spacious Family Room is a fabulous space to get together and enjoy the cozy Fireplace and Sliders to Stone Patio. Conveniently located Powder Room and Mud Room/Laundry Room just steps to the 2 Car Garage, complete the main living area. The private Bedroom Wing offers a serene Primary Bedroom with Full Bath, 3 additional Bedrooms; all with hardwood floors and a Full Hall Bath with double vanity. The full unfinished basement offers many possibilities. Step outside to the gorgeous gardens and stone Patio, ideal for outdoor gatherings and enjoying the beautiful country setting. With central air and a portable generator, your comfort and convenience are ensured year-round. Located just a short distance from wonderful Chestnut Hill Park, this home offers the perfect blend of peaceful living and easy access to local amenities. Minutes away from the Merritt Parkway, shopping, and everything Stamford and Downtown entertainment district have to offer!
