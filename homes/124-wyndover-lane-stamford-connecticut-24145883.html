About this home

Charming Westover cul-de-sac retreat on over a half acre! This 3-bedroom, 2-bath split-level home offers warmth, character, and great flow. The main level features a dining room with fireplace, bright kitchen, primary suite, and two additional bedrooms with full bath. A few steps away, the spacious living room showcases a beamed ceiling, second fireplace, and abundant natural light. The private family room off the breezeway opens to a deck and scenic yard with stone walls. Additional highlights include a two-car garage, hardwood floors, gas heat, city water/sewer, generator hook-up, and large unfinished basement. Convenient Westover location near schools, shopping, golf, and just minutes to Downtown and Harbor Point. Property also listed for rent mls #24145884
