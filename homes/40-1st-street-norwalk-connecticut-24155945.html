About this home

Welcome to 40 1st Street in the highly sought-after East Norwalk section of Norwalk. This beautifully maintained two-family home presents an exceptional opportunity for investors or owner-occupants seeking strong rental potential and a prime coastal location. The property features one 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath unit and one 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath unit, both offering central air conditioning and in-unit laundry for modern comfort and convenience. Ideally situated within walking distance to local restaurants, shops, and the waterfront, this home combines neighborhood charm with coastal living. Whether you're looking to occupy one unit and rent the other or expand your investment portfolio, this is a fantastic opportunity in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
