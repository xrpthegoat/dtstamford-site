About this home

A LOCATION THAT CAN'T BE BEAT! Well built home on a beautiful flat 1-acre lot (quiet neighbors!) in the highly desirable Westover area, this property is nicely set back from Roxbury Road, offering a wonderful opportunity to customize, refresh or expand and make it your own! 436 Roxbury Rd features an attached 2-car garage, plus bonus space adjacent to the garage complete with a wood-burning stove, full bath with walk-in shower and laundry, and walk-out access to the patio and backyard. On this level there's a bonus room with a closet. Up a flight of stairs, you'll find a light-filled living room with vaulted ceilings and a wood-burning fireplace, flowing into a formal dining room with sliders to a wood deck as well as an eat-in kitchen. The upper level offers three bedrooms - primary with full bath, and a hallway bath. The basement provides utilities, multiple storage areas (including former kitchen cabinetry), as well as a workbench/workshop area: The possibilities are endless! Whether you're an investor, contractor or buyer looking to create something special, this is an opportunity you won't want to miss in a prime Westover location: Run, don't walk!
