About this home

Tucked away at the rear of a duplex and backing directly to a neighborhood park, this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom condo offers a rare combination of privacy, space, and everyday convenience in South Norwalk. Built in 2007, this newer construction home features a thoughtful, multi-level layout and modern comforts throughout. The main level is bright and inviting with 9-foot ceilings, a comfortable living room, and a private balcony overlooking the park-perfect for morning coffee or relaxing outdoors. Upstairs, the primary suite features its own park-facing balcony and a walk-in closet, along with two additional bedrooms on the same level, ideal for flexible living. A third-level walk-up attic/loft provides valuable space, perfect for a hangout area, guest accommodations, or home office. The lower level basemnet offers 9-foot ceilings, is partially finished, and framed for a future bathroom and additional living space, with a separate entrance, creating excellent potential for extended living, work-from-home, future investment use, or extra storage.
