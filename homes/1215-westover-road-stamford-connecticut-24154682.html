About this home

Private three bedroom country cottage in the sought-after Westover neighborhood, re-built from the studs in 1992, set on a level property with exceptional privacy and mature landscaping. This bright, open-concept home features a welcoming foyer, spacious living and dining areas with large windows overlooking the garden and a white kitchen with stainless steel appliances. The primary bedroom includes vaulted ceilings, skylights, and an en-suite bath. A second bedroom offers its own full bath with granite vanity. A third bedroom or office provides flexible space for a home office or sitting room. Large private deck, ideal for outdoor entertaining. Additional features include laundry, detached two-car garage, storage shed, and loft space. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Stamford, Metro-North train service, and the Merritt Parkway, offering a rare blend of privacy, land, and commuter accessibility. Fully gutted and re-built from the studs in 1992.
