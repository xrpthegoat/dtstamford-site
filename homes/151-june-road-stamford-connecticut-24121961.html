About this home

This stunning property seamlessly blends modern farmhouse charm with a touch of Museum of Modern Art sophistication. Situated on 3 picturesque acres with a tranquil pond, Mianus River frontage, and expansive fields perfect for outdoor entertaining or a potential pool, it's a truly exceptional estate. The interior showcases soaring ceilings, a dramatic two-story fireplace, and floor-to-ceiling windows that frame breathtaking views of the pond and barn-creating an artful, open-concept living space. The recently renovated chef's kitchen features quartz countertops, top-tier Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances, a large island, and smart design details that elevate everyday living. An elegant dining area, cozy TV lounge, and stylish home office complete the first floor. With five bedrooms-including one on the main level-and 4.5 bathrooms, the layout is ideal for family, guests, or entertaining. The property includes an historic post-and-beam barn, rebuilt in 2008, offering endless potential: It includes horse stalls, an artist's studio, and a rustic gathering space with a wood-burning stove. The fenced garden invites vegetable and flower gardening, while direct access to 30 miles of riding trails through the Greenwich Riding & Trails Association makes it a haven for equestrians and outdoor enthusiasts alike. This property masterfully combines contemporary farmhouse warmth with artistic, museum-quality architectural details-offering a one-of-a-kind lifestyle in a serene, country setting
