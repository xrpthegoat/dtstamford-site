About this home

Whether you're relocating, renovating, or still working out your next move, this fully furnished one-level house is the perfect place to call home. An open foyer leads into a grand living room with soaring ceilings, hardwood floors, and sun-filled windows. A cozy wood-burning fireplace anchors the space, and the room is large enough to create several seating areas. The 9'+ ceilings continue into the dining room, where a large table is ready for holiday gatherings or homework sessions.The kitchen offers updated appliances and plenty of working space including a breakfast counter. Three spacious bedrooms include a bright primary suite with a spa-like bath: a separate soaking tub, a stall shower, and a towel warmer. Sliders open to a patio, ideal for morning coffee. An updated hall bath serves the two additional large bedrooms. Outside, enjoy generous yard space in both front and back. You'll also find a basement, a wonderful Playroom space with roomy storage, and a 2-car garage. Minutes to downtown Westport, the Merritt Parkway, I-95, YMCA, and schools.
