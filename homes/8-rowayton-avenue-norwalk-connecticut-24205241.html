About this home

Welcome to this beautiful shingle-style colonial, ideally set within the private Rowayton Beach Association - a coveted enclave of private streets with its own private beach on Long Island Sound right off the village. Inside, 3,825 square feet of thoughtfully renovated living space unfolds across a bright, open floor plan designed for effortless entertaining and functional living. The chef's kitchen features a Wolf range, marble-topped island with seating, and a farmhouse sink, opening to a dining and living area where a wall of French doors floods the room with natural light. Upstairs and throughout, the home offers five well-proportioned bedrooms, three and a half baths, an additional family room, and a separate living space with fireplace, providing ample space for family living or hosting guests. Outside, a bluestone patio offers space for lounging and alfresco dining, framed by mature hydrangea hedges and a natural stone retaining wall for privacy and separation, opening onto a flat, open lawn. Beyond the property, Rowayton's charming village center - with its boutique shops, acclaimed restaurants, marina, and commuter rail access - is just minutes away. Offered fully furnished - simply bring your bags and enjoy turnkey coastal living within one of Fairfield County's most private waterfront enclaves.
