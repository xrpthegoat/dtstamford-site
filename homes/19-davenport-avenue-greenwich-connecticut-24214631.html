About this home

Completely reimagined by J & S Homes, 19 Davenport offers 3 levels of sophisticated single-family living overlooking Smith Cove, showcasing exceptional quality, craftsmanship and attention to detail throughout. Light-filled interiors feature white oak floors, graceful archways, a linear fireplace and chef's kitchen with Viking appliances. The primary suite opens to a private waterfront deck, while the versatile ground level offers a 4th bedroom/family room with full bath & kitchenette. Three TimberTech decks, James Hardie siding, new roof, Marvin Ultimate windows & sliders, radiant-heated baths and smart entry doors with switchable privacy glass complete this distinctive downtown Greenwich waterfront residence. Concierge property management available for effortless living.
