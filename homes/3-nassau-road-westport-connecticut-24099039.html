About this home

SUMMER FURNISHED RENTAL - Discover Westport's Premier Coastal Gem. Immerse yourself in modern elegance with this stunning new beach house, located in the highly sought-after Saugatuck Shores neighborhood. Constructed in 2022, this five-bedroom, four-and-a-half-bathroom residence spans over 3,700 square feet of finished living space. Tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac, the home offers easy access to the vibrant energy and amenities of Saugatuck. The first floor features an open layout with soaring 9-foot ceilings, a sunlit living room with serene water views, and a sleek gas fireplace. The space effortlessly flows into a gourmet kitchen and formal dining area. A flexible first-floor bedroom, paired with a full bath, is perfect for guests or a home office. The second floor boasts a luxurious primary suite with its own gas fireplace, breathtaking water vistas, and a spa-like bath for the ultimate retreat. Three additional bedrooms, paired with two beautifully designed bathrooms, provide comfort and privacy. On the third floor, a versatile space awaits-ideal for a studio, lounge, or recreational area, with direct access to a rooftop deck overlooking the water. Outside, this expansive 0.25-acre property is a private oasis. Enjoy a heated gunite saltwater pool, a spacious terrace, and a large yard-perfect for outdoor fun, relaxation, and entertaining. This home masterfully blends contemporary design, comfort, and functionality in one of Westport's most desirable locations.
