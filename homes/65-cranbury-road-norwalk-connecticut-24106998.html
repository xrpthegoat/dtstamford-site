About this home

Welcome to 65 Cranbury Road, a beautifully updated Colonial in Norwalk's sought-after Cranbury neighborhood. This inviting 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home is filled with warmth and charm, offering a wonderful balance of comfort and style. The renovated kitchen is designed for gathering, flowing easily into sunlit living spaces perfect for everyday life and entertaining. Upstairs, generous bedrooms and updated baths provide a relaxing retreat. Step outside and you'll fall in love with the setting-the backyard feels like your own private escape with tranquil pond views and natural beauty to enjoy year-round. The landscaped front and backyards are perfect for play, gardening, or simply unwinding at the end of the day. All of this, just minutes from schools, parks, shopping, and everything you need. A special place to call home where nature and neighborhood come together.
