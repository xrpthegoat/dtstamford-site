About this home

Welcome to the desirable Fawn Ridge Condominium Community! This charming rental situated on the Norwalk / Wilton border features 2 spacious bedrooms, each with walk-in closets and 2 full baths, bathed in natural light throughout. This home combines the tranquility of nature with modern comforts.
