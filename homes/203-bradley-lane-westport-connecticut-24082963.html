About this home

Modern, upgraded end-unit in gated Bradley Commons, ideally located in vibrant Saugatuck! Just minutes to the train, Compo Beach, schools, restaurants, and downtown Westport. This 2 bed, 2.5 bath townhome offers 1,756 SF of stylish living with an open floor plan, hardwood floors, high ceilings, and abundant natural light. The chef's kitchen features granite counters, breakfast bar, pantry, and stainless steel appliances. Dining area opens to a private deck. Living room includes a gas fireplace and powder room. Upstairs: spacious primary suite with walk-in closet and double vanity bath, plus en-suite 2nd bedroom and laundry. Finished lower level offers flexible space for office, gym, or guest suite with patio access, storage, and 1-car garage. Professionally landscaped, well-maintained complex with ample guest parking.
