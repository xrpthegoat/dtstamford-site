About this home

Simply charming ranch on Jonathan Road, a most inviting and quiet country cul-de-sac. Enjoy the ease of one-level living in a setting that feels both like a private retreat yet is convenient to downtown New Canaan. Nestled amongst grand estates and farm houses, and with plenty of room to expand, it is easy to bring your own ideas and create the experience you've always wanted home to be. The expansive front yard has beautiful stone walls and the back yard is highlighted by a sparkling pool and a fun little cabana. Just minutes from town and close to lovely Scotts Corner. Enjoy the local cafes, farmers markets, galleries, nurseries, and shops. A rare chance to enjoy all this potential in one special property. House is being offered "As Is'
