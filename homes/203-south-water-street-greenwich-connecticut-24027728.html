About this home

Like new, fully remodeled in 2018, this sophisticated townhome-style condo offers over 2,700 sq. ft. of living space across three levels. With modern updates, low common charges, and a prime location, this home is like new and offers excellent value. Featuring 9 ft. ceilings and sun-filled interiors, the residence has blonde maple hardwood floors throughout. The main level offers a spacious 700 sq. ft. living room, a formal dining area, and an open-concept eat-in kitchen, all perfect for entertaining. Balcony with southern exposure. The expansive primary suite boasts dual walk-in closets, a spa-like bath with a soaking tub, separate shower, double vanities, and a private river-facing balcony. Two additional ensuite bedrooms and a laundry room complete the second level. The versatile lower level, built in garage, flexible space for a playroom, gym, or office, plus ample storage. Two additional parking spaces. Located steps from Mill Street's shops and restaurants, with easy access to Byram Beach, Pool & Park, Shubert Library, top-rated schools, and Port Chester Train Station, this pet-friendly townhome offers convenience and exceptional value. One of the largest condos in Greenwich. Low common charges & pet-friendly. Annual taxes: $6,078 (Note: Third-party price history is incorrect.) An incredible opportunity to own a spacious, updated home at a fantastic price!
