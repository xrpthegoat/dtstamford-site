About this home

Welcome to Stamford! This split level is ready for new owners to enjoy. Situated on a corner lot, step into the foyer to the living room with warm toned hardwood floors. It opens directly to the dining room and eat-in-kitchen with tile flooring and corian counters. The family room has sliders out to the patio and private yard. Upstairs you will find 3 bedrooms and a full bath. A lower level office/den can also be used for play space or a home gym. There is a full bath on this level, laundry area and access to the attached garage. Conveniently located to downtown, Merritt and shops, plus Pepe's and Shake Shack!
