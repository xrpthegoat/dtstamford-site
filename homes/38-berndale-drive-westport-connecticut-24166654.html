About this home

This impeccable, newly renovated colonial is now available for rent. Enjoy the ease of this fabulous, walkable location in the heart of Westport-convenient to shopping trains & schools. This wonderful home is sited on a private 1+ acres with mature landscaping, a fabulous sunroom, & multiple patio areas (including a fire pit). This extra large 6 bedrm home offers ultimate versatility with 3 levels, as well as the coveted open floor plan. The Kitchen has been beautifully renovated & boasts white cabinets, expansive quartz countertops, multiple seating areas, top of the line appliances & opens into a spacious Great Room with vaulted ceilings, a floor to ceiling stone fireplace & access to a light filled Sun Room. In fact this entire home has wonderful, oversized windows & is bursting with sunlight. The first floor has 3 bedrooms; one ensuite and the other two with a Jack & Jill Bathroom. The Foyer is dramatic with a charming curved staircase, oversized windows & an inviting window seat. The 2nd floor boasts an oversized Primary Bedroom Suite with a generous walk in closet/dressing room area & a luxurious Primary Bathroom with jacuzzi, large shower & double sink. An additional bedroom on this level also makes an ideal nursery, office or guest room. The walk out lower level is fully finished with a playroom/family room with a fireplace, exercise room, full bathroom, an additional bedroom & an ample storage space and accesses another patio. Huge front yard & ample parking!
