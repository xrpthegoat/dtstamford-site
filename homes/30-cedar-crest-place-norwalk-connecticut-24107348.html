About this home

Welcome to 30 Cedar Crest Place - a solid and well-loved 2-bedroom (with possible 3rd bedroom), 3-bath Cape-style home offering the perfect opportunity to bring your vision to life. Situated on a quiet street in a convenient South Norwalk location, this home is ready for your personal touch. Inside, you will find a traditional layout with hardwood floors, a sunny living room, a formal dining area, and an eat-in kitchen with original cabinetry and generous space for future updates. With three full bathrooms, one on each level, this home provides a flexible setup for everyday living or guest accommodations. Upstairs, two spacious bedrooms and a full bath offer comfortable living quarters, while the finished lower level offers additional space for a family room or rec/play room, complete with its own full bathroom. Outside, the level yard and deck are ready for outdoor entertaining, gardening, or relaxing. A detached garage and driveway offer off-street parking and additional storage. This is a rare chance to own a home with good bones in a great location, just minutes to SoNo, the train, restaurants, shopping, and the beach. *HIGHEST AND BEST BY 7/15 AT 4PM*
