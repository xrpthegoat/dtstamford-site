About this home

Welcome to your dream home! This charming, raised ranch features 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, perfect for families seeking space and comfort. Situated on a generous 1.14-acre level lot, the property features a beautifully manicured yard, providing a perfect backdrop for outdoor activities and gatherings. Step inside to find a renovated kitchen, complete with new quartz countertops and modern appliances, seamlessly connecting to the open dining and living areas featuring a fireplace-ideal for entertaining and family time. Natural light floods the main living spaces, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. An expansive deck right off of the kitchen allows for easy outdoor dining. Completing the first floor is a spacious primary bedroom suite, 2 generously sized bedrooms and full bathroom. The lower level is a true highlight, featuring a fourth bedroom with an ensuite bathroom, a second office for remote work, and a versatile rec room with a second fireplace-perfect for play or relaxation. With direct access to the backyard, outdoor enjoyment is just steps away. Conveniently located minutes from shopping and restaurants on Hope Street, and just a quick drive to New Canaan and downtown Stamford, this home combines privacy with easy access to local amenities. New hot water heater, new chlorination system, new sliders, just to name a few of the many improvements done. Don't miss your chance to own this spacious home in a prime location.
