About this home

Position your business for success in this well located and versatile office suite at 99 Prospect Street Unit 1B in the heart of downtown Stamford. Large windows throughout bring in natural light, creating a bright and productive work environment while complementing the clean, classic interior finishes. Whether you are expanding, relocating, or investing, this is a rare opportunity to secure a professional office space that blends functionality, location, and long term value in one of Stamford's most dynamic business districts.
