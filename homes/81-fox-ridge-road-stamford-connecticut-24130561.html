About this home

Discover sophisticated single-level living in this stunning 3,300-square-foot ranch, privately set on over an acre with stone walls, heated gunite saltwater pool, and irrigation system. The gourmet kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring an exceptional induction range, Sub-Zero and Thermador appliances, quartz counters, and soft-close cabinetry. The dramatic living room captivates with soaring beamed and vaulted ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows framing nature's beauty, and a wood-burning fireplace perfect for cozy winter evenings. French doors from the dining room create seamless indoor-outdoor flow to the peaceful pool area. Retreat to the main floor, serene primary suite with a huge sitting area, walk-in closet, and spa-inspired bath with heated floors. A walk-out lower level offers flexible space for a gym or playroom, while the bonus room over the two-car garage provides an ideal office, in-law suite, or private retreat. Modern convenience includes a whole-house generator ensuring comfort during any weather. Located in a one of the most desirable North Stamford neighborhoods, with the exclusive bonus of private Foxwood Lake rights for year-round recreation. Extensively renovated and move-in ready, this peaceful sanctuary blends luxury with effortless living.
