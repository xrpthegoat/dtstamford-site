About this home

Privately nestled on 1.76 exceptional acres in a coveted Westover setting, 450 Westover Road is a sophisticated retreat offering remarkable privacy, scale, and timeless appeal. Surrounded by mature landscaping and serene natural beauty, the property's expansive grounds create a rare sense of seclusion while remaining just moments from town, schools, and commuting routes. Sunlit interiors, expansive entertaining spaces, and oversized windows create an airy elegance throughout, while refined architectural details add warmth and character at every turn. The stunning primary suite is a true sanctuary, featuring soaring vaulted ceilings and dramatic arched windows that flood the space with natural light while overlooking the picturesque surroundings. Generously scaled bedrooms, renovated baths, and flexible living areas offer both comfort and versatility for today's lifestyle. Complementing the main residence is a separate accessory structure with rich wood interiors and entertaining space, perfectly suited as a private studio, wellness retreat, home office, or guest escape. An exceptional offering combining privacy, sophistication, and extraordinary land in one of the area's most sought-after locations.
