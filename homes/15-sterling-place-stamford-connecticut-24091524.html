About this home

OFFER DEADLINE: HIGHEST AND BEST Tuesday May 6 at 5PM. Welcome to this wonderful 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial located in the highly convenient Upper Springdale neighborhood. This beautifully renovated home truly checks all the boxes, offering a perfect blend of modern updates and classic charm. Renovation includes kitchen, powder room & 2nd level full bath, main roof, windows, & furnace. Hot water htr (2023) The heart of the home is the stunning kitchen, featuring white cabinetry, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and an island that overlooks the dining area - ideal for everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the spacious family room, highlighted by vaulted ceilings, a cozy fireplace, stylish shiplap accent walls, and custom built-ins and powder room. A sliding glass door leads out to the deck, perfect for relaxing or hosting gatherings. Attached one car garage conveniently located with just a few steps up to the kitchen. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms and a newly renovated full bathroom with double sinks. There are hardwood floors throughout. The finished lower level provides additional living space with a recreation room and a full bath. Set on a lovely, level lot, the outdoor space offers plenty of room for play, gardening, or simply unwinding. Location is a commuter's dream - less than one mile to the Metro-North Springdale Train Station - and close to the library, schools, shopping, and dining.
