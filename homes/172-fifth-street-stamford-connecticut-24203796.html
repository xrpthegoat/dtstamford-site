About this home

Perched on an incline overlooking downtown Stamford sits this wooden clad classical Georgian home on an oversized lot in the heart of town. The home features 3 bedrooms, 2 full and 2 half baths, spacious living room with fireplace, formal dining room with a side windowed porch, eat -in kitchen, and a spacious first floor office that can be turned into a primary bedroom suite. While the home requires updating it features a classical entry way & staircase. The property also has a detached 2 car garage with ample storage. In addition the house has an attic for expansion purposes and is sited on a generous .65 acre lot providing a buyer with not only a spacious front yard but also a private large back yard complete with its own grape arbor. In short this is a rare opportunity near Tully Center and convenient to downtown.
