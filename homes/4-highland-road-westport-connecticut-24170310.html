About this home

Step into this beautifully updated Colonial in the heart of Coleytown, where timeless charm meets modern living. Set on over two landscaped acres in a desirable cul-de-sac, the property offers a spacious deck and expansive yard-perfect for entertaining or everyday enjoyment. Inside, a welcoming entry foyer opens to a warm center hall with hardwood floors and seamless flow throughout. The main level features a bright living room, a versatile dining room or home office, and an oversized family room ideal for relaxing or gathering. The eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home, showcasing stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, a peninsula with seating, and a cozy open-hearth fireplace. A breezeway leads to a flexible bonus space with a private entrance-perfect for a playroom, office, or guest suite. A stylish powder room with Waterworks fixtures and a dedicated laundry room complete the main level. Upstairs, you'll find four bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with a spa-like bath and steam shower. A circular driveway and two-car garage provide ample parking and storage. All just minutes from downtown shopping, dining, and top-rated schools, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort, space, and convenience.
