About this home

Grand Colonial in highly sought-after South Wilton, ideally located minutes to the train station, a 15 minute drive to S. Norwalk train and walking distance to the Wilton Center. This impressive 5-bedroom home offers exceptional space and flexibility to suit a variety of lifestyles, with generously sized bedrooms throughout. Hardwood floors flow seamlessly, complementing the expansive kitchen designed for the serious chef. A private library provides a quiet retreat and a large family room with vaulted ceiling is perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or everyday living. Upstairs boasts an oversized primary bedroom suite with spa-like bath and large walk-in closet. 4 additional bedrooms and another large room currently used as an office complete the second level. A walk-up attic offers abundant storage, while the unfinished walk-out lower level presents incredible potential-complete with a fireplace (one of four in the home) and plumbing already in place for an additional bath. Privately set at the end of a driveway and surrounded by nature, this property offers both tranquility and usability with a level backyard ideal for play or outdoor gatherings. Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living with a deck accessible from both the kitchen and family room. A three-car garage adds convenience and additional storage. A rare opportunity in a premier location-homes like this do not come along often. Don't miss it! "As is"
