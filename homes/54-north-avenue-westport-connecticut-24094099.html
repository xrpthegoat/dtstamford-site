About this home

Modern sophistication in a prime Westport location! This 7,555 sqft home with 5 bedrooms and 5 baths offers 3 floors of luxury living. The entire home has a fresh, clean aesthetic w light wood floors, a crisp palette, Marine Wood built-in features & smooth surfaces throughout. The kitchen is anchored by an oversized U shaped work area with seating & there is a full wall of seamless cabinetry including wall ovens & 3 refrigerators! The kitchen & dining area leads directly to a large family room with tons of windows overlooking the 1 acre tree lined property. A more formal living room as well as a very cool library, powder room and mudroom complete the main level. Upstairs there are 2 bedrooms sharing a Jack & Jill bath, 2 ensuite bedrooms, and a lovely primary suite w double walk-in closets, a sleek, modern bath & spacious bedroom. Over the garage there is a massive great room set for play, but could be used as office/studio! Lower level is an open rec room w/high ceilings, polished concrete floors. The patio space is perfect for lounging, dining while enjoying the level backyard. 2 car garage. This special home is located within steps of the Staples/Bedford/Wakeman Farm campus & so close to all Westport, shopping, dining and town amenities!
