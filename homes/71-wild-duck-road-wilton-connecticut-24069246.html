About this home

Located in the desirable South Wilton neighborhood on a cul-de-sac street, right at the border of New Canaan and walking distance to The Lake Club, this 4-bedroom and 5 bath stately home is available for rent fully furnished. The property's primary floor is replete with amenities such as a step-down great room with a projector to enjoy in-home movies, two wood-burning fireplaces, a large library/office, an 8-seater formal dining room, a separate family room with French doors opening into the patio, a large open kitchen equipped with Viking and Miele appliances and two wine fridges in addition to a large central island. The kitchen functions well for catered functions or private chefs. On the second level, the home boasts a primary suite of over 250 square feet overlooking the property, which includes an in-room wood-burning fireplace, an ensuite rain shower and a separate large soaking tub with double sinks. The additional three bedrooms - two include ensuite bathrooms and one is convertible into a home office or can be kept as an additional bedroom along with an additional bathroom across the hall. Look no further, this home brings flexibility for short-term renters (30-day minimum) to longer-term duration. Tenants will need to bring their own linens and the basement will be kept for the owners' belongings. The house is Wi-Fi enabled and also includes solar panels in addition to being pre-equipped with a full home security system!
