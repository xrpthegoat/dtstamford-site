About this home

Historic Charm Meets Modern Luxury. Discover the perfect blend of historic character and modern comfort in this recently updated 1835 Colonial, set on 3+ private acres. Known as the "Bigelow House", this home holds a rich history as a former retreat for the Bigelow Tea family while offering today's buyers an updated, turn-key experience. Freshly painted thruout, this spacious 4 bedroom, 3.5-bath home exudes warmth with multiple wood stoves, wide-plank floors, and thoughtful renovations that respect its heritage. The modern eat-in kitchen boasts quartz countertops, custom cabinetry, and brand-new stainless steel appliances, blending style with function. The main floor offers a living/dining area, a cozy den/office, and a private first-floor bedroom with an en-suite bath-perfect for guests or multigenerational living. Upstairs, the primary suite features a sitting area and private bath, while add'l bedrooms provide plenty of space for family or work-from-home needs. The finished LL includes a newly added mudroom, entryway, and walkout access to a peaceful wooded backyard w/ steps leading to a charming garden area. Completing this special property is a remodeled detached carriage house, offering endless possibilities as a guest suite, home office, or studio. Enjoy the beauty of expansive decks, perfect for outdoor entertaining. Located minutes from Town Center, schools, trains and trails. Current owners installed a new roof and a 200 ft. privacy fence. A Wilton treasure.
