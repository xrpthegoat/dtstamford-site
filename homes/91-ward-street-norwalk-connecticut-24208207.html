About this home

Beautifully expanded and thoughtfully renovated Cape offering a wonderful blend of classic charm and modern updates. This inviting 4 bedroom, 2 full bath home features a flexible floor plan with comfortable living spaces and plenty of room to grow. A versatile den/office opens through sliders to a large deck overlooking the landscaped fully fenced yard, perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying outdoor living. The outdoor space includes a patio and pergola, creating an ideal setting for gatherings or quiet afternoons. The fully finished attic provides valuable additional living space, while the under-house garage offers convenient parking and storage. Numerous updates throughout make this home truly move-in ready. A terrific opportunity to enjoy an expanded Cape with character, functionality, and exceptional outdoor space in a desirable Norwalk location.
