About this home

Exceptional opportunity to own an income-producing multifamily property in the heart of Stamford's highly sought-after South End. This well-maintained property features two spacious units, each occupying its own level and offering 2 bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, a generously sized living room, and an eat-in kitchen. Both units enjoy access to the welcoming front porch. Additional features include an unfinished lower level and third floor providing ample storage space, off-street parking for up to four vehicles, and a spacious grassy front yard. Conveniently located near downtown Stamford, Metro-North, waterfront amenities, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes. A fantastic investment or owner-occupant opportunity not to be missed.
