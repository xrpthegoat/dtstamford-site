About this home

Beautifully set on a side street rise, this stately center hall Colonial has a traditional 4 bedroom, 3.5 bathroom floor plan, plus a bonus wing to suit your flexible space ideas! The main floor includes a front to back stretching living room with the sweetest screened porch, a dining room, an eat-in kitchen with dining area and breakfast bar, and a spacious family room. Upstairs, the primary bedroom has a full bath, three closets and beautiful views of the private property and pool! There are three additional bedrooms, one with an adorable play nook, and a hall bath. The "wing" connects to the main floor and is made up of a living room, office, 2 bedrooms, a full bath and there is a rec room, half baths, storage and a separate entrance on the lower level. A great space for guests, flexible use, and zoned as a legal two family. The 1.35 acre property is bright and blooming already, and the pool is ready for summer fun! Located so close to schools, town and transit, 52 Meadow View Road is ready to be enjoyed!
