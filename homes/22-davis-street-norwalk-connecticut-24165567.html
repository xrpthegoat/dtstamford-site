About this home

Welcome to 22 Davis Street, a rare two-family colonial in the heart of Norwalk offering both income potential and flexible living options boasting $4100 monthly rent including the garage. Built in 1920, this 1,296 square foot property features two self-contained one-bedroom, one-bath units, each with its own kitchen, full bathroom, and in-unit laundry. Unit 1 is has a thoughtful, open layout from entrance to kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and front-facing living room. It has been fully updated and well kept by the tenant. There is also access to the basement from this unit. Upstairs, unit 2 is in excellent condition, with large living room, kitchen, bathroom, and laundry. The living room might be a second bedroom for a creative buyer. Both units include dedicated laundry closets. Outside, an open front porch, gravel back patio, and detached 1 car garage add functional appeal. This well-maintained 2 family offers an opporunity in a desirable dead-end neighborhood, at a price that doesn't exist in this market. Priced at $625,000, this Davis Street property puts you steps from Norwalk's shops, dining, and transit with the versatility of multi-family ownership.
