About this home

Beautifully updated 4-bedroom ranch on one level acre in a prime location close to train stations. A wraparound driveway leads to a spacious family room with fireplace, play area, and multiple seating areas. The primary suite includes a walk-in closet and en-suite bath, plus two additional bedrooms and a full bath. Dining room with bar and kitchen open to a large Azek deck, ideal for entertaining. Two-car garage and shed. Recent updates include landscaping with 55 Green Giant arborvitae for privacy and screening, new tile entry, fresh interior and exterior paint, upgraded HVAC, duct insulation, hot water heater (2024), and new attic and crawl space insulation for efficiency. A Tom Fazio-designed Swilcan Bridge replica adds a distinctive touch for golf enthusiasts.
