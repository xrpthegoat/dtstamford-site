About this home

Classic CT charm in gorgeous Greens Farms. Enter through a circular drive and step inside "The Bedford-Lloyd House."A a high-ceiling foyer with a curved staircase and original millwork welcomes guests and serves as a hub of the 1912 home.The bright, sun-filled 32 ft living room is anchored by a warm fireplace, has room for multiple conversation areas and leads to a beautiful screened porch w whitewashed walls and shiplap ceiling.The formal dining room boasts another fireplace, charming built-in cabinetry, Dutch door, and plenty of space for hosting holidays.The recently renovated Siematic kitchen with granite island, Wolf induction cooktop, Gaggenau ovens, steamer, SubZero refrigerator, Waterworks faucet, breakfast nook & lounge space also offers access to the patio & outdoor areas. A cozy family room is perfect for gathering around the decorative fireplace for a movie or game night. The primary suite includes a light-filled spa bath, dual closets as well as a sunroom separated by French doors. 2 bedrooms are connected with a Jack/Jill bath. 2 are ensuite, and the 6th bdrm could also be set as a play space, office, or family lounge due to its central location.Away from the main home , is a carriage house, with 2 car garage, private 2 bedroom legal-rental accessory dwelling, currently generating $42,300 net cash flow (delivered vacant). Set on over 2 acres of irrigated professionally landscaped grounds, and in close proximity to Westport beaches, station, schools, and retail!
