About this home

Classic 4BR, 3.5BA Colonial on 1.3 Private Acres in North Stamford - Minutes to Everything! Welcome to 66 Don Bob-a timeless Colonial retreat nestled on a picturesque 1.3 acre lot which backs up to beautiful river/brook in the highly desirable North Stamford. Set back on a quiet, park like property, this charming 4 bedroom, 3.5 bath home seamlessly blends classic design with modern updates, offering comfort, style, and flexibility for today's lifestyle. Main Level has a Gourmet Eat In Kitchen featuring granite countertops, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Viking gas range and premium stainless steel appliances. Inviting Family Room with a cozy wood-burning fireplace, opening directly to the screened in porch and large deck-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. Elegant Living & Dining Areas with an open concept flow ideal for gatherings. Luxurious Laundry Room and updated powder room complete the main level. Upper Level Features an Expansive Primary Suite with a walk in closet and a beautifully appointed marble bath. Three Additional Generously Sized Bedrooms and an updated full hall bath. Lower Level - Fully finished basement w/ a full bathroom offers versatile space for a home office, media room, playroom, or gym-tailored to fit your lifestyle. Shed & playground are included. Just minutes from the Trader Joe's shopping center, Stamford Museum & Nature Center, Bartlett Arboretum, Merritt Parkway with NYC just 50 minutes away-this home is the perfect balance of privacy and convenience.
