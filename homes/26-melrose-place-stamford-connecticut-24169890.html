About this home

This beautifully renovated 2-bedroom home in the heart of Stamford offers a rare combination of modern luxury and suburban ease. Also located a few blocks from the town of Greenwich. Experience a bright, open-concept layout with contemporary finishes, updated flooring, and a neutral palette that maximizes natural light. The large, versatile lower level offers incredible extra space, perfect for a home office, media room, or 3rd bedroom. The home has audio and listening devices.
