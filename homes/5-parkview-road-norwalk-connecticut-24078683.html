About this home

The best location in Rowayton - coveted Bell Island. A true beach house with beautiful water views from every room. Steps from two private LI Sound beaches available only to Bell Island association members, East Beach and South Beach. Directly across the street from private Clarke Park. 4 Bedroom, sleeps 10 (1 king, 2 queens, 4 twin beds). Open concept living-dining room, updated kitchen, dedicated office space, and a magical third-floor retreat [work, read, paint overlooking the water]. Stroll into Rowayton Village to sip coffee at Arden's, indulge in fresh lobster at The Restaurant, or explore local art at The Rowayton Arts Center. This chic coastal enclave, just 40 miles from NYC, offers an instant sense of community with an effortless beach vibe. Kayaks & bicycles included.
