About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST - DEADLINE TUESDAY, 11/5 AT 2 PM. Welcome to 164 Gray Farms Rd, a charming Cape-style home set on 1+ acres on one of Stamford's most desirable streets. Step into the spacious living room that runs from front to back, with large windows and a cozy fireplace, flowing seamlessly to the dining room, kitchen, and the spacious family room. This Core-4 layout allows effortless movement throughout the main level. The huge primary suite upstairs offers soaring ceilings, a walk-in closet, and an ensuite bathroom with dual sinks. The 2 additional bedrooms on this level share a full bathroom which conveniently houses the washer and dryer. The finished walk-out basement presents lots of potential, whether you envision a playroom, an office, or adding a bedroom with egress. The two-car garage and recent updates - including an updated furnace and a newer roof provide extra peace of mind. With a large flat property, this home has room for outdoor entertaining and offers expansion possibilities for future growth. Conveniently located close to the Merritt Parkway, I-95, Springdale and Stamford Train Stations, this home provides the convenience of city living in a setting that feels worlds away.
