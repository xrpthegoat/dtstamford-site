About this home

This is the second of 2 custom homes, beautifully sited at the end of a private lane in the heart of Greens Farms. Combining classic design with modern detailing by an established local builder of premier residences, this masterfully crafted home is sure to delight even the most discerning buyer. The main level showcases expert millwork and warm white oak accents throughout, flowing from the Living and Dining Rooms with paneled walls into the Study with built-in cabinetry. The Chef's Kitchen with Wolf and Cove appliances adjoins the sunny Family Room which opens to a picturesque 1.4 acres with salt water Swimming Pool and Spa, surrounded by a handsome granite patio. An elegant Primary Suite with fireplace, luxurious Bathroom, dressing area and 2 walk-in closets with custom cabinetry, plus 4 additional Bedrooms with 3 additional Baths, a Family Room/Lounge and Laundry Room grace the second level. The third floor has a Playroom and an additional Flex Space/Office. The lower level features a Gym with mirrored walls and rubber floor, a Sauna, a handsome Bar and gathering area with a glass garage door that opens to a patio, a full bath plus an additional room with a private entrance for Media, Office or use as a Bedroom. Available for immediate occupancy.
