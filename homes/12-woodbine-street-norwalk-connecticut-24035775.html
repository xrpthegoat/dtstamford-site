About this home

A commuter's dream in the best beach town in CT! This newer Colonial features a fabulous, bright, and open floor plan, as well as 9 ft+ ceilings. The main floor fetures a gourmet kitchen with large center island, an open Living Room with fireplace, separate study/office with French Doors, mudroom with built ins, and a 2 story foyer. Upstairs you'll find the Primary Suite with vaulted ceilings, 2 walk in closets and a spa like bathroom with dual sinks. There are 3 additional bedrooms, all with ensuite baths and the laundry room. The lower level offers bonus space for a family room/playroom, an entertainment room with dry bar, an office and tons of storage. There is a private, fenced backyard with blue stone patio. All this just minutes from Rowayton's quaint shops and restaurants, the waterfront and the train station.
