About this home

Rare Find in Wilton Crest! This stand-alone 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath Townhouse offers exceptional privacy with no shared walls-a unique feature in this sought-after complex. Highlights include a private attached 1 car garage, a dedicated workshop, and a welcoming foyer that opens to a serene back patio framed by natural rock walls. The spacious living room features hardwood floors, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliders to a balcony, perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The open-concept layout continues into a generous dining area and a bright, functional kitchen with ample cabinet space. Upstairs, enjoy a large primary suite with multiple closets and a full bathroom featuring dual sinks, a walk-in shower, and a jetted tub. A second ensuite bedroom offers comfort for guests or family. Additional features include central air, new heating & A/C systems, and excellent storage with a partial basement area, laundry room, and garage. Wilton Crest residents enjoy a pool, tennis courts, and low-maintenance living-just minutes from Wilton Center's shops, dining, and the train station for easy commuting. This is Wilton living at its finest!
