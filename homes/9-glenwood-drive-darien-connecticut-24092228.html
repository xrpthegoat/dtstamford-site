About this home

A serene water garden below a magnificent dogwood tree is one of the many highlights of this charming residence nestled in a tranquil Darien neighborhood that delivers beauty, comfort and convenience. This delightful home offers three bedrooms and two full bathrooms, spread across a generous 1,838 sq feet of well-designed living space. Step inside to discover a remodeled island kitchen, featuring French doors that open to a newly built deck-perfect for outdoor dining or simply enjoying the serene views of your lush gardens filled with over 50 perennials including tulips, roses, lavender and 18 gorgeous hydrangeas. The main level is home to two cozy bedrooms that share a well-appointed full bath, alongside a spacious living room w/ a fireplace and a formal dining area. Upstairs you'll find the impressive primary bedroom suite, a true retreat with high ceilings, built-ins, and a private balcony that overlooks the picturesque backyard gardens. This suite also offers a sitting area, dressing room, and a luxurious full bath. A dedicated office and a laundry room complete the upper level. Enjoy the ease of electric heating and cooling, with an impressively low average monthly electric bill of just $438 over the past year. Situated on a quiet street with minimal traffic, this home is just moments away from the vibrant Noroton Heights developments, bustling with new shops, restaurants, and close to train and town beaches. Embrace this ideal blend of tranquility and convenience.
