About this home

Welcome to 90 Culloden Road. Capture the essence of old world charm in this 2 bedroom, 1.5 bath colonial in desirable Glenbrook. Upstairs boasts a full bath and 2 nice bedrooms. Entertain outside in the private backyard. Bring your design ideas and make this home your own. House and garage being sold "as-is".
