About this home

Welcome to this beautifully crafted new construction home in the heart of Westport, where timeless design and thoughtful details come together to create a luxurious space. From the moment you step inside, you're welcomed by a bright and open floor plan with high ceilings and wide-plank flooring that runs throughout the main living space. Natural light fills every room, highlighting the custom finishes and high-end craftsmanship that define this exceptional home. The kitchen is a true showpiece, designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Full custom inset cabinetry and professional appliances, sleek quartz countertops and double oversized center islands provide plenty of prep space and seating. On the exterior, the home is clad in classic hardy board, an expansive outdoor kitchen, and elaborate stone detail with plenty of room for a pool, spa, or however you wish to complete your outdoor oasis. At the end of a quiet cup-de-sac, yet situated in a prime Westport location close to schools, beaches, shopping, and commuting options, this new construction offers everything you need in a forever home-modern comfort, classic style, and room to grow.
