About this home

Originally the artist's studio for the famous New Yorker cartoonist Perry Barlow, this charming cottage features a large, bright main window and a newly painted, white open-plan living space with brand new propane heating system and tankless hot water. Separate kitchen with gas range and eating area, plus small bathroom with stall shower. Tranquil, wooded and private setting is peaceful, yet just minutes from MetroNorth (with a shuttle bus available at the end of the driveway), plus all of downtown Westport's shopping and restaurants. The studio is separate and private from the main house, with its own driveway parking, and garage parking available on snowy days. No laundry in the unit, but owner offers laundry services, if desired. Would be a great commuter's home, or even a workspace or artist's studio for someone living locally. ***INCLUDED: Electricity, water, trash, wifi, snow and grounds maintenance. Tenant only pays propane for heat/ hot water. Good credit a must, apply through RentSpree link. Easy to show and immediate availability.
