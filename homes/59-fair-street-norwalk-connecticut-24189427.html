About this home

Welcome home to this charming second-floor apartment in a beautifully maintained 1929 Colonial offering a thoughtfully designed living space. This inviting one bedroom home blends timeless character with modern updates. The rental unit features one spacious bedroom, a dedicated office, updated kitchen and a brand new remodeled bathroom. Natural light fills the home enhancing it's warm and welcoming feel. The living room features a cathedral ceiling, skylights and a ceiling fan to make this space feel light and airy. Step outside and enjoy your own private back deck - an ideal spot for morning coffee or relaxing at the end of the day. This is a well cared for home that offers comfort, charm and pride of ownership in a peaceful residential setting. Off-street parking and conveniently located near Route 7, I-95 and Route 15.
