Reduced for Quick Sale - Incredible Value in Cos Cob! Don't miss this opportunity to own a spacious, versatile home in desirable Cos Cob! Yes, it backs to I-95, but that also means ultra-convenient access for commuters-and the layout, size, and features of this home far outweigh the trade-off. Bring your creativity and take advantage of this rare layout, prime location near shopping, schools, and transit, and amazing value for the square footage.
