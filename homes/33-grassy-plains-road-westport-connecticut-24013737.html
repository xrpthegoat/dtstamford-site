About this home

Explore this custom Nantucket-style home nestled in the private Reserve at Poplar Plains community. Absolutely gorgeous home brimming with sophistication, set at the end of a cul-de-sac of similarly impressive homes, this home boasts gorgeous views of the 33-acre conservation, with easy accessibility to the Merritt, I-95, downtown, train or the Compo beach. The open floor-plan with 9 feet ceilings throughout, features custom hand-crafted millwork, tray ceilings, two gas fireplaces, a spa-like master bathroom with heated floors, and upgraded Carrara marble finishes throughout. The custom chef's kitchen features state-of-the-art Sub-Zero/Wolf/Thermador appliances set in modern design, and makes for a great room perfect to entertain gatherings of any size. A large foyer, flanked by the well appointed formal living room and dining room welcome you into elegance and charm. A wide staircase featuring grand light fixtures leads to an intimate lounge with a skylight, and four large en-suite bedrooms. Two additional, bonus rooms can be used as office, gym or spare bedrooms. The private bluestone patio, showcased by grand French doors, is the perfect place to enjoy the nature. This home is low-maintenance, dual-zoned for energy efficiency and made for comfortable living with modern upgrades and smart-home automations. The enormous lower level can be finished for even more space. This home is ideal for those seeking all that Westport has to offer! Agent/co-owner.
