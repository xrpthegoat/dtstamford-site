About this home

Welcome home to this beautifully updated gem in one of Norwalk's most convenient locations! Bright, inviting spaces and thoughtful updates make this home as comfortable as it is stylish. The white kitchen features granite countertops, abundant cabinetry, gas cooking, and a casual dining area that opens to the welcoming living room with a cozy fireplace. Hardwood floors and oversized windows fill the home with natural light, creating a fresh, modern feel throughout. A spacious formal dining room is perfect for entertaining family and friends. Upstairs, the primary suite offers vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet, and a private bath with double granite vanity and walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms share a full hall bath. Want more space still? The finished lower level adds approximately 900 square feet of flexible living space-ideal for a family room, office, gym, or playroom-and includes abundant storage plus your own private sauna. Outside, enjoy a spacious deck overlooking the level, fully fenced backyard, perfect for entertaining, pets, or play. Additional features include central air, an attached one-car garage, and an EV Charger. Just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping, restaurants, and parks, this move-in-ready home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and lifestyle. Make this house your new home!
