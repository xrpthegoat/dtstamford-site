About this home

Some homes are simply unforgettable. Set high above the street on 1.19 lush acres in coveted Old Hill, this extraordinary new residence blends striking architecture and sophisticated design. A gracious front porch overlooks a courtyard-like driveway. Created by Sam Development LLC.,CMS Architects, and Interior Designer Mary Beth Woods, this distinctive home offers approximately 6,000 SF across four levels, with 5 bedrooms, 5 baths, 2 half baths, 9' ceilings on the first and second floors, 6" white oak flooring, and exceptional details. The spectacular kitchen features high-end appliances, oversized island and walk-in pantry, opening to the family room with gas fireplace and breakfast area. Glass sliders lead to a granite patio and landscaped grounds. An elegant dining room with built-in buffet and bar and a glass doored living room/study complete the main level. The vaulted, beamed primary suite features a spa-like bath and two walk-in closets. Three additional bedrooms, spacious laundry, finished third- floor space with half bath, and a lower level with family room, bedroom,full bath, gym, and storage offer versatility. The detached 1,050 SF ADU is ideal for in-laws, guests, office/studio, or future pool house. Three-car garage with bonus room, spray-foam insulation, approximately 100 recessed lights. 22-KW Generac generator, 400-amp electrical services, and EV outlet. Come experience this exceptional Old Hill property and discover a home that stands apart.
