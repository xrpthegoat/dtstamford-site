About this home

A rare opportunity to own a classic Shore Colonial in one of Old Greenwich's most sought-after neighborhoods. Set on 0.36 acres and significantly expanded in 2004, this spacious 6 BR 4.5 BA residence offers over 4300 SF of thoughtfully designed living space, blending comfort, functionality, and timeless appeal. A welcoming wraparound porch leads to elegant formal living and dining rooms, a cozy family room, and a bright eat-in kitchen with direct access to a bluestone patio and expansive private backyard, perfect for summer entertaining, outdoor dining, and everyday enjoyment. The second floor features a primary BR with luxurious bath, fireplace, walk-in closet, and private sun deck, along with four more bedrooms and an additional full bath. The third floor offers flexible space for family and guests. The walkout lower level includes a recreation room, exercise area, and another full bath. Ideally located midway between the shops, schools, and train in OG Village and the sandy shores of beautiful Tod's Point Beach. Zoned for Old Greenwich and Eastern Middle Schools.
