About this home

Welcome to this desirable Newfield neighborhood offering that's sure not to disappoint. Located at the end of a cul-de-sac, 1 Robinson Drive has been completely updated & expanded with top grade materials & craftsmanship. This 4 bedroom home features freshly painted interiors, hardwood floors, charming fireplace w/custom imported mantel & updated kitchen & marble baths. The first floor has an open floor plan, a renovated eat-in kitchen, living/dining room+an expanded sun room w/paladin windows & vaulted ceilings. A main level bedroom with glass doors can also serve an an office, that's ideal for today's living. On the first floor, there is direct access to a large new Trex deck w/view rail & private stone patio capped with a pergola-the perfect place for alfresco dining & entertaining. Upstairs, there is an oversized primary suite w/trey ceiling, two walk-in custom closets+an expanded & renovated marble bath with large shower & jetted tub. The 2 additional guest bedrooms all feature spacious custom walk-in closets, hardwood floors, & share a renovated guest bath. Not to be overlooked is the family room w/custom built-in entertainment center, 1/2 bath, laundry & office walking out to the stone patio. Recently redone roof, & natural gas utilities allow for energy efficient living. The exterior is a gardener's delight with Belgian block lined perennial gardens, 2 sheds, pathway lighting, fully fenced, & specimen plantings. All located in a convenient sought after neighborhood.
