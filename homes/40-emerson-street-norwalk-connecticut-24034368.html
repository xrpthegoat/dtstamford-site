About this home

NOW OFFERED UNFURNISHED! You will absolutely love living in this charming 3 bedroom cottage/cape in a truly convenient East Norwalk location. Craftsman details throughout including wainscoting, crown molding, built-in storage and hardwood floors. Move in and enjoy the formal living room with raised hearth fireplace and the updated kitchen with Bertazzoni gas range, stainless appliances and abundant storage. The kitchen opens to both the dining area and the family room with access to the sunny deck and private back yard. The carpeted primary bedroom and crisp, white-tiled full bath complete the main level. The upper level offers two additional bedrooms, and the full basement includes a washer & dryer and additional storage. Walking distance to the train station, shopping and East Norwalk restaurants and amenities. A sunny, happy home! Sorry, no pets. Also offered furnished. See MLS#24031543
