About this home

Tucked within a peaceful garden setting, this beautifully renovated end-unit residence offers an effortless blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Enjoy the ease of garage parking within the building before stepping inside a thoughtfully designed condo featuring an open-concept layout, soaring 9-foot ceilings, abundant natural light, and serene views of the beautifully landscaped grounds. The beautifully appointed kitchen combines style and functionality with crisp white cabinetry, granite countertops, a classic subway tile backsplash, and stainless-steel appliances. A convenient in-unit washer and dryer enhance the ease of everyday living. Rich hardwood floors, custom built-ins, crown molding, and refined millwork add warmth and timeless character throughout. The spacious bedroom opens through French doors to a private bluestone patio-an inviting outdoor sanctuary for morning coffee, quiet afternoons, or an evening glass of wine. Ideally located just moments from downtown Westport's acclaimed restaurants, boutique shopping, library, Levitt Pavilion, Westport Country Playhouse, Longshore Club Park, Compo Beach, the marina, and Old Mill Beach. With convenient access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and Metro-North, this exceptional residence offers the perfect balance of coastal charm, vibrant community, and effortless convenience in one of Fairfield County's most desirable coastal communities.
