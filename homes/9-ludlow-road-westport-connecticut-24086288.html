About this home

Wonderful Old Hill Location, walk to elementary school, down town for concerts at the Levitt, The Library, shopping, restaurants and walking trails along riverfront. 2 BR, 2.5 BA condo is a desirable end unit. The condo includes a 2 car tandem under condo garage, guest parking & great storage place.The condo roof is 6 years old and was painted in 2021. Just 0.5 miles to downtown and 3 miles to Longshore and beach. Don't miss great opportunity.
