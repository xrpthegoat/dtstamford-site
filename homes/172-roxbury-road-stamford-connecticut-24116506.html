About this home

This remarkable unique cottage style home on secluded .77 acre in Westover area has unbelievable charm. It is one of the renowned Craftsman built homes. Master bedroom includes walk-in closet. Hardwood floors throughout. Living room with lots of windows and two French doors leading to expansive deck overlooking Gazebo and treed property where nature abounds.
