About this home

Whether you work from home or commute to the office, this attractive Colonial in mint condition offers large rooms, super-sized master en-suite, formal dining room and a private office plus recreation or media room in the lower level. You can walk to Glenbrook train station or have easy access to I-95 and the Merritt. This 3 bedroom 3.5 bath home was renovated with loving care. Interior offers a serene and elegant color palate, large master bedroom, large living room area, large kitchen with generous space for breakfast/informal dining. All baths are updated with tiling, vanities and attractive fixtures. Great closet space throughout; full attic and, shimmering brand new flooring. House is bright with windows in every room. Located in residential street, it is steps from restaurants, shops and CVS. Good credit and landlord references required.
