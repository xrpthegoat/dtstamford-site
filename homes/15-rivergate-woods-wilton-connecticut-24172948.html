About this home

HIGHEST & BEST DUE WEDS 5/13 @ 12PM. Tucked at the end of a private driveway, surrounded by mature trees and a sense of peace, while remaining minutes from everything that makes Wilton one of CT's most sought-after towns to put down roots. A stone front porch welcomes you, setting the tone for a home that balances warmth and elegance with features like skylights, vaulted ceilings, and beams. The foyer opens into a striking living room where an open staircase and a floor-to-ceiling fireplace command attention. The dining room features a built-in cabinet and generous proportions that make it a natural setting for hosting. The kitchen is appointed with wood cabinetry, granite countertops, and dedicated cookbook storage. In the family room, expansive views of the natural landscape bring the outside in and the connection to the rear patio makes it easy to move a dinner party outdoors. Upstairs, two bedrooms share the hall bath, while the primary bedroom has a full bath, Juliet balcony, and walk-in closet. The walk-out LL has a spacious rec room, laundry area, bedroom and full bath, offering flexibility and versatility. Step out to the screened-in porch, another idyllic place to relax. Wilton is a small town with a big heart and love of community and nature, known for top-rated schools and charming town center. Surrounded by well-appointed homes, tranquil ponds & foliage beautiful in every season, 15 Rivergate woods is not just a beautiful place to live, but an exceptional one.
