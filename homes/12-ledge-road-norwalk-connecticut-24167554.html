About this home

Set on a quiet street in the seaside village of Rowayton, 12 Ledge Road captures the essence of coastal living with an easy walk-to-town lifestyle. This 5-bedroom, 4.5-bath home offers over 4,600 square feet of beautifully designed living space, blending comfort and style throughout. An inviting oversized front porch leads into a striking two-story living room, where natural light and an open floor plan create an effortless flow. The dining room and spacious chef's kitchen are ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, while a first-floor flex bedroom or private office adds versatility. Outside, a thoughtfully designed patio features an outdoor kitchen, fireplace, and lounge, perfect for summer evenings and relaxed gatherings, along with an outdoor shower for the ultimate post-beach rinse. Upstairs, the serene primary suite is a true retreat with a fully renovated, spa-like bath and oversized walk-in closet. Three additional bedrooms include one en suite and two connected by a Jack and Jill bath. The finished lower level expands the living space with a full bath, gym area, and wine cellar. Enjoy Rowayton's coveted lifestyle with nearby beaches, parks, and a vibrant, close-knit coastal community just moments from your door.
