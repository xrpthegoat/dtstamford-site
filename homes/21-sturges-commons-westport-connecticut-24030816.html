About this home

Unique opportunity on one of the most coveted streets in the Hunt Club neighborhood. Privacy plus convenience can be enjoyed at this stone residence sited on a park like acre with an inground gunite pool & spa surrounded by walls of greenery, specimen trees and plantings.
