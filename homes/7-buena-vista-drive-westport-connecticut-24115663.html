About this home

Location, Location, Location! 7 Buena Vista Drive, is a rare and exceptional property in one of Westport's most sought-after beach neighborhoods. This prime 0.18-acre property sits at the top of Compo Hill and offers incredible views of the Long Island Sound. It is the perfect canvas to build your dream home just moments from the shoreline, where you can enjoy coastal living at it's finest. Located on a quiet, tree-lined cul du sac surrounded by upscale residences, this coveted address seamlessly combines the tranquility of beachside life with convenient access to Westport's vibrant downtown area, top-rated schools, and commuter routes. Whether you're envisioning a modern coastal retreat or a timeless New England classic, 7 Buena Vista Drive presents a unique chance to create a personalized sanctuary in an unbeatable location. The house is being sold as is.
