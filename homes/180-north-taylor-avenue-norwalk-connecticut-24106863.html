About this home

Centrally located expanded Cape offering 3 levels of spacious living. Charm and character abound with crown molding throughout and gleaming granite and solid hardwood flooring. Lower-level walk-out in-law suite with brand new laminate floors which includes living/dining area with fireplace, full bath, separate bedroom with walk-in closets, and a sun-filled office nook. Main level features a large eat-in kitchen, formal dining room, half bath, and living room with custom floor-to-ceiling fieldstone fireplace. Upper level includes 3 oversized bedrooms and 2 full baths, including a primary suite with ensuite bath. 1 car garage off the kitchen with a loft for recreation or additional storage. Generous backyard backs to protected woodlands for lasting privacy and serenity-Ideal for nature lovers. Step across the street to the Oak Hills walking trails that lead right into the golf course and golf-front restaurant/bar. Perfect for entertaining inside or out and room for extended family or additional income opportunity.
