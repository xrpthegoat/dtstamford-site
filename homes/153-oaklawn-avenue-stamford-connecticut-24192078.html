About this home

Welcome to this modern ranch home in Stamford's desirable Newfield neighborhood, offering the perfect blend of comfort, versatility, and charm. Located at 153 Oaklawn Ave, this 2-bedroom home offers a thoughtfully designed layout with a charming loft, perfect for a home office, reading nook, or additional space. The sun-filled living room features skylights that fill the space with natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The eat-in kitchen is equipped with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, creating the perfect space for everyday living and entertaining. Just off the kitchen, step out onto an oversized deck, ideal for entertaining or relaxing while overlooking a spacious backyard, perfect for gatherings, play, or pets. The fully finished walk-out lower level adds exceptional value, featuring two additional bedrooms, a full bath, and a private entrance, making it ideal for extended family, guests, or additional living accommodations Additional highlights include a newly installed furnace and central air for year-round comfort.
