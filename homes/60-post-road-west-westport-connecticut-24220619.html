About this home

Renovated, modern office building at 60 Post Road West, just minutes from downtown Westport. An ideal opportunity for an owner-occupant or an investor looking to buy and lease. The first floor features a welcoming reception area, kitchen area, private office and conference room, plus 2 bathrooms. The top floor offers a bright, open floor concept that can be built out to suit whatever style your business needs. Highlights include abundant natural light, a flexible floor plan that can accommodate a wide range of office uses, and 10 on-site parking spaces. Excellent Post Road visibility and convenient proximity to downtown Westport's shops, restaurants and amenities.
