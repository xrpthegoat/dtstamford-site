About this home

This beautifully renovated home blends timeless sophistication with modern comfort. Inside, the sun-filled main level features rich hardwood floors and refined details throughout. At its heart, a gourmet kitchen impresses with a 2" quartz island, custom cabinetry, premium Thermador appliances, and seamless access to an expansive deck, ideal for effortless indoor-outdoor living. The main-level primary suite is a true retreat, with vaulted ceilings, an ensuite bath, and a custom walk-in closet. A second bedroom/home office, a living room with fireplace, a cozy den, and a full bath complete the main level. Upstairs, you'll find two spacious bedrooms and a beautifully updated bathroom. The fifth bedroom, which can be used as an office, gym/yoga room, or playroom, completes the second floor. The lower level offers a private entrance, garage access, and flexible use as an in-law suite, au pair quarters, guest space, playroom, or media room. Thoughtfully designed, it features a bedroom, full bath, living area, wet bar, refrigerator, and dedicated washer/dryer. Exterior updates include a new roof, fencing, and fresh paint and trim. Outdoors, enjoy multiple entertaining areas including a landscaped yard, stone patio, and oversized deck, perfect for gatherings or quiet evenings. Minutes from all schools, Waveny Park, Metro North train, Merritt Parkway, and a vibrant town center, this home offers luxury, location, and livability in perfect balance.
