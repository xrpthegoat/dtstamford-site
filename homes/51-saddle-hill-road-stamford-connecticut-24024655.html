About this home

Pristine, chic, and bucolic are only a few words to describe this tastefully updated lakefront gem in North Stamford. This 1.16-acre lot, boasts total privacy, overlooking a crystal-clear lake shaded by beautiful trees. Located at the end of a windy road, this secluded, contemporary Hi-Ranch directly faces the lake with an adjacent patio so that you can enjoy its' beauty and tranquility for hours. Walk through the front door into the entry foyer of the lower level, which is complete with hardwood floors, a generously sized living room with electric fireplace and bedroom with ensuite bath. The lower level, with a direct view of the lake, is great for entertaining. The main level features an updated kitchen, featuring custom cabinetry with millwork details, an induction electric cooktop, porcelain tile floor, all Bosch appliances, an exhaust hood, a wine cooler, and a garbage disposal unit (2020). An ample dining area with French doors to the deck and backyard provides easy outdoor entertaining. The spacious living room with wood-burning fireplace also boasts French doors facing the home's front, providing yet another perfect view of the lake from a main-level deck. The hallway leads to a large primary bedroom with an ensuite bath. The two guest bedrooms are good-sized, with one including an ensuite bath and either can also serve as a home office. Hardwood floors adorn most of the main level.
