About this home

Welcome to Unit F7 at 85 Riverside Drive, a 2-bedroom, 1-bath condominium located on the second floor of a well-maintained riverside complex in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Overlooking the river and landscaped grounds, this home offers a quiet, residential setting just minutes from downtown Stamford's shopping, dining, entertainment, and transportation. The unit features a functional layout with a generously sized living and dining area, ideal for everyday living or entertaining. Sliding doors open to a private deck with peaceful river views and excellent natural light. Two well-proportioned bedrooms, ample closet space, and in-unit laundry add to the home's overall utility. Additional benefits include a reserved off-street parking space and a large, private, lockable storage room on the ground level of the building. Residents enjoy access to an in-ground pool, low common charges, and a professionally managed community. The complex allows rentals, making this an excellent opportunity for both owner-occupants and investors seeking long-term value in a prime Stamford location.
