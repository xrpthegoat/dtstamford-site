About this home

Don't miss this incredible opportunity to make this stunning 4-bedroom Cape on Spring Hill the next chapter of your story. Step into a bright, spacious living room that seamlessly opens to the kitchen and a charming dining area-perfect for entertaining or cozy family dinners. The dining room window overlooks the backyard and also perfectly frames colorful sunsets. Step out of the dining room onto an elevated deck ready for your summer BBQs. Down the steps from the deck, the vinyl fenced-in backyard is perfect for pets, and get-togethers. On a cool evening, pull up a seat, warm your hands, and roast marshmallows over your stone fire pit. Under the deck, you'll find cavernous storage space or room for an outside workshop. Back indoors on the other side of the main floor, you'll find a beautifully renovated bathroom and versatile spaces-two bedrooms or an office to suit your lifestyle. Upstairs are two tranquil bedrooms and a sizeable walk-in closet. The highlight of the home is the enormous finished lower level with a fully-equipped bath-an ideal space for a home theater, gym, office, guest suite, or all of the above. A separate laundry room adds convenience and privacy. Located less than a mile from premium shopping, gyms, local boutiques, and an upscale mall. Also, just minutes from Washington Street's vibrant restaurant scene. Easy access to the interstate means you're perfectly positioned for quick trips to the beach, hiking trails, and Norwalk's picturesque parks.
