About this home

Serene Location! Enjoy all that living in Fairfield County has to offer in this very spacious Ranch home with approximately 2,040 square feet located in the quiet town of Wilton. This lovely home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, 2 fireplaces, & hardwood floors throughout. Brand new, modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a double oven, farm sink, lots of cabinets, and an L-shaped island. Baths are remodeled with double sinks and large vanities, and towel warmers! Central air, as well as ceiling fans throughout. The living room fireplace is near a radius/arched window creating a dramatic focal point in the room. Four panel, wood-framed, glass doors lead to the deck. In addition, there is a great size deck and a separate patio area. Lots of privacy for entertaining or just relaxing after a hectic day! The lower level offers a complete recreation room, T.V. room, a gym area, gaming tables, pinball machine, and so much more. You can create office space here too. Easy access to the Merritt Parkway, Westport, shopping, & restaurants. Half hour or less ride to Stamford & Greenwich too. Enjoy all that Fairfield County has to offer!
