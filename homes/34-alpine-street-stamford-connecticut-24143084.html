About this home

Experience comfortable living in this well-appointed three-bedroom, two-bathroom house featuring 1,876 square feet of thoughtfully designed space. The newly renovated eat-in kitchen serves as the heart of the home, while beautiful hardwood floors flow throughout, creating warmth and continuity. Cozy evenings await by the inviting fireplace, perfect for gathering with family and friends. The primary bedroom offers a peaceful retreat with an adjoining sitting room, ideal for relaxing, reading or late night TV. The heated finished basement provides additional living space year-round, expanding your options for recreational needs. Access the screened porch for bug-free relaxation or the covered patio for outdoor dining and entertaining. The spacious yard offers plenty of room for outdoor activities and gardening. Location convenience enhances daily life with Trader Joe's and Whole Foods nearby for grocery shopping, plus excellent dining and shopping options throughout the area. Nature enthusiasts will appreciate being just one mile from Stamford Museum and Nature Center, while the Bartlett Arboretum and Gardens provides another beautiful outdoor destination. Easy access to Merritt Parkway ensures smooth commuting to surrounding areas. This property combines modern updates with classic charm in a neighborhood that offers both convenience and natural beauty.
