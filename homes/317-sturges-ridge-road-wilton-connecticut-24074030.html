About this home

Privately nestled on two high acres, this updated colonial blends charm with comfort. A wraparound porch, spacious side yard, and stone-walled back patio create the perfect setting. Inside, a grand two-story entry into the home flows into over-sized formal living and dining rooms with gleaming hardwood floors. The family room, featuring a striking stone fireplace, opens to the eat-in kitchen with easy backyard access. A first-floor den/office with built-ins and a wet bar leads to the all-season sunroom, offering a light-drenched space to relax or entertain. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat with a cozy sitting area, vaulted ceilings, a wood-burning fireplace, beautifully renovated bath, and spacious, custom built walk-in closet. Three additional, spacious bedrooms share an updated bathroom with separate laundry space. All spaces feature over-sized closets with closet systems. Versatile additional space above the garage provides endless possibilities-perfect for a playroom, office, or gym. With updated mechanicals (oil tanks, furnace & appliances), this home ensures both comfort and peace of mind. Designed for effortless living and entertaining, this home, with its flexible floor plan, provides the ideal balance of privacy, space, and functionality with close proximity to Wilton schools, town center and local amenities!
