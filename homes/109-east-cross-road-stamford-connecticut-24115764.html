About this home

This updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial in Upper Springdale, bordering New Canaan, offers comfort and functionality. Set on a 1-acre lot with gardens, mature trees, and a large stone patio, the property features a fully fenced-in backyard, ideal for pets or play. The first floor living room features a brick wood-burning fireplace and an adjacent spacious screened porch and open deck. The dining room includes custom built-ins, and the open-concept kitchen offers white cabinetry, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, and a wine cooler, all enhanced by recessed lighting. Upstairs, the primary bedroom features an ensuite bath with designer tile, heated floors, and a heated towel bar. Three additional bedrooms, all with hardwood floors and custom closets, share a modern second bathroom with stylish tile and dual sinks. The walk-out lower level provides space for an office, recreation area, or gym, with built-in cabinetry and a beverage fridge. Upgrades include new windows, smart thermostats, and more-see the MLS for a full list. Conveniently located near the train station, highways, schools, and shopping, this home blends accessibility with a peaceful setting. Multiple offers, highest and best by 9/8, 8PM
