About this home

Live in the highly coveted Compo Beach community! This home is a 5 minute walk to Compo Beach, and a 5-10 minute drive to restaurants, train, etc! Built in 2006, this Nantucket shingle-style home sits on an oversized, uniquely private lot, boasting over $700,000 in additional upgrades including oak custom cabinetry, bar, closets and millwork, exterior mahogany railings and shutters, Italian glass and marble details, limestone and shell/pebble kitchen counters, brand new roof, Control 4 TV/Audio system, central vac, new HVAC system, family room fireplace, garage built-ins, electric and alarm upgrades, additional kitchen cabinetry, irrigation, landscape and much more! An abundance of trees and shrubs define this home's ultimate coastal garden, offering a lush, serene backdrop for indoor/outdoor living. It's designed for ease and privacy in a lively beach community. The open floor plan layout offers both an entertainment space of living room and lounge and a family space with gourmet eat-in kitchen and family room. Both areas have a half bath. Dining area and mudroom/pantry complete the first floor. On the 2nd level, the luxurious master wing includes a vaulted bedroom with fireplace, walk-in custom oak closet, entry area with oak closets/cabinets, bath, private office (or nursery, etc) and small walk-out balcony overlooking the property. Another multi-purpose wing includes 2 bedrooms en suite, and a vaulted den/bedroom with bath access.
