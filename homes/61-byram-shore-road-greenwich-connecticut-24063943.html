About this home

Unparalleled Waterfront Estate in Greenwich. Immerse yourself in year-round resort living at this captivating waterfront property. Witness breathtaking sunrises from your private deep-water dock, perfect for mooring a boat exceeding 30 feet. Embrace an idyllic lifestyle on Byram Shore Road, a prestigious location with effortless access to New York City.
