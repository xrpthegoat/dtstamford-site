About this home

A beautiful 2-bedroom townhouse in the coveted waterfront community of Palmer Landing. Best location in the complex with a first-floor end unit enjoying picturesque views of the harbor from sunrise to sunset. An open floor plan with a first-floor primary bedroom and fire-lit living room leading to a sun-splashed waterfront deck. The kitchen and baths have been tastefully remodeled. The dining area and kitchen enjoy water views, as well. A powder room and large laundry room complete the first level. The second bedroom works well as a home office. Newly re-finished hardwood floors. New wall to wall carpet on second floor. One of the few units with an attached garage along with a second parking spot and ample guest parking. Palmer Landing has gated entry, private pool, boathouse and marina. Heat and water included in common charges. Conveniently located on Shippan Point, only minutes to beaches, downtown shopping & dining, and Metro North train. An easy commute to Greenwich, Westchester or NYC. Ask listing agent about a 50-foot boat slip is available separately. Love where you live! Accepting highest and best offers by 6:00pm Sunday June 1.
