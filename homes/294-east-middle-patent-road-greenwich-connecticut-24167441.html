About this home

A rare single-level luxury compound set on two private acres - Greenwich address, Stamford taxes and schools. Designed for effortless resort-style living, the home centers on an expansive primary wing opening directly to a gunite pool, spa, and sweeping bluestone terraces, complemented by a spa-inspired bath, infrared sauna, and steam shower. A fully self-contained guest residence within the home - with its own entrance, kitchenette, living and dining areas, full bath, independent HVAC, and security - lives like a separate private house for extended family, live-in staff, long-term guests, or flexible work-from-home living. Two additional en-suite bedrooms adapt seamlessly as family suites, private offices, wellness spaces, or a gym. Comprehensively renovated in 2026, the residence features a custom rift-cut white oak kitchen anchored by a dramatic 6' x 12' island, designer baths, new flooring, roof, driveway, and extensive stonework. Soaring cathedral ceilings, walls of glass, and skylights flood the home with natural light throughout. Additional highlights include three fireplaces, wet bar, five-zone integrated sound, whole-house generator, oversized four-car garage, and a 5,400+ sq ft lower level with fireplace and wine storage offering exceptional future build-out potential. Two fully usable acres with no wetlands or conservation restrictions, and privacy preserved in perpetuity.
