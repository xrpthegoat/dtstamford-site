About this home

Fantastic Opportunity for many buyers! Come check out this 2-family house in desirable East Norwalk by the Sea. This home offers a newer roof & newer siding. The first floor features a 1 bedroom, 1 bath apartment with high ceilings, hardwood floors, an eat in kitchen, large sunny rooms, washer/dryer hookup and interior access to a full, partially finished lower level. The 2nd floor apartment also features 2-bedrooms & 1 bath with hardwood flooring, washer/dryer hookups and offers a full, unfinished walk up attic with tremendous potential to be finished. There is an expansive, level backyard and 2 separate entrances. Home interior is in need of work, but well worth the effort! There are separate hot water & electric meters but heat is shared between apartments. This property is subject to Probate Approval and sold "AS IS".
