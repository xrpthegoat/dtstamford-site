About this home

Enjoy the privacy of a standalone Colonial with the ease of low-maintenance living in this exceptional home, one of just seven residences in a beautifully maintained planned community. Tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac overlooking a tranquil pond, this home offers the perfect balance of serenity and convenience. The thoughtfully designed floor plan features an open-concept kitchen, breakfast area and family room with gas fireplace, formal living and dining rooms, plus a versatile first-floor den/office adjacent to a full bath which double as a 5th bedroom. The chef's kitchen is appointed with Thermador stainless steel appliances, including a cooktop, wall oven, microwave, warming drawer, refrigerator and dishwasher, center island, and InSinkErator. Upstairs are four spacious bedrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with fireplace, two walk-in closets, dual-sink vanity, soaking tub and walk-in shower. Hardwood floors, security system, irrigation, attached two-car garage with only two steps into the kitchen, and over 1,000 SF of unfinished lower level and walk-up attic provide exceptional flexibility for future expansion. Enjoy the wraparound porch, including a generous side porch overlooking the pond and private natural setting. Minutes to shopping, dining, beaches, parks, vibrant South Norwalk, and the charming downtowns of nearby New Canaan, Darien and Westport.
