About this home

Exceptional three-bedroom antique Colonial exudes tremendous curb appeal on 1.51 pastoral acres with heated pool, inviting terrace, fenced-in level yard, meadow teeming with native plants surrounding pond in a peaceful country setting across from a horse farm. Enduring charm defines this well maintained, thoughtfully updated 1800 home rich in classic architectural details with wide plank wood floors, French doors and built-in cabinetry. Marble-clad kitchen offers a basketweave tiled backsplash, a nod to the home's history as a basket shop. Living room with fireplace, dining room and sunroom with pellet stove, skylights and outdoor access are great for relaxed living and entertaining. Primary suite with adjacent sitting room, office, nursery or additional bedroom; third bedroom and full bath complete the second floor.
