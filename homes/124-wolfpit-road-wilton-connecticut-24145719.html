About this home

Welcome to this enchanting midcentury cottage in coveted South Wilton, where privacy meets everyday convenience. An open-concept main level invites effortless living and entertaining, featuring a renovated kitchen flowing seamlessly into the living and dining areas, anchored by a wood-burning fireplace and opening to a spacious wrap-around deck. The serene primary bedroom offers double closets and sliders to the deck, while a second bedroom and a spa-like hall bath with steam shower and elegant Rohl fixtures complete the main floor. The lower level adds a bonus Den room with storage, a full bath with radiant heated floors, a dedicated laundry room, and direct access to the one-car attached garage. A walk-up attic provides generous storage and exciting potential for future expansion. Set on over an acre, the magical grounds are dotted with perennial gardens, graceful low stone walls, and winding stone paths, creating a true outdoor retreat. Ideally located within a mile from Wilton town center and just 15 minutes to downtown New Canaan, this special home offers timeless style, comfort, and location in perfect harmony.
