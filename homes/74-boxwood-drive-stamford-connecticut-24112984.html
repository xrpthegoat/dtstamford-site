About this home

Nestled on a quiet cul-de-sac in the desirable Newfield neighborhood, this beautifully updated, freshly painted modern ranch offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and style. With 3 spacious bedrooms plus office or 4th bedroom and 2 full baths , including a private primary en-suite, this home is designed for easy one-level living. Step inside to discover smart home features throughout, enhancing everyday living with seamless technology. The open layout offers a bright and airy feel, while thoughtful updates provide both functionality and modern appeal. Enjoy cooking and entertaining in the well-appointed kitchen, and take advantage of ample storage throughout the home. Step outside into your private outdoor oasis-complete with a fully fenced-in yard, a heated swimming pool, and space for relaxation or gatherings. Eco-conscious renters will appreciate the dedicated wall charger for an electric car. Located within walking distance to Glenbrook train station, local places of worship and Strawberry Hill Magnet School. Just minutes from shopping, schools and parks, this home offers peace and privacy without sacrificing accessibility. Gas powered generator to add to convenience. Don't miss the chance to enjoy all the benefits of one-level living in one of the area's most beloved communities.
