About this home

Tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac south of the village, this beautiful and expanded Old Greenwich home offers the perfect blend of elegance and convenience. Just a short distance to village shops, restaurants, Old Greenwich Elementary, and the NYC train, the location is unbeatable. The 2008 renovation introduced new windows, a gourmet chef's kitchen, and a sunlit family room with a fireplace. Upstairs, a serene primary suite boasts two walk-in closets and a marble bath, complemented by three additional bedrooms and a second-level sitting room. A timeless retreat in an exceptional setting.
