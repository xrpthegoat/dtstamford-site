About this home

This welcoming water view private apartment is attached to a home located in the heart of the Village Creek Water Community. Village Creek is a coastal community nestled along the Long Island Sound. The apartment boasts an open living space with a wall of windows providing views of the water, an updated kitchen, living/ dining room combination, private entrance, private patio, and off-street parking for two cars. Excellent location for running or walking, with access to all the community amenities that include pickleball, tennis and basketball courts, dog park, playing field and the private Village Creek beach. Proximity to Metro North (only 1 hour from NYC), I95 and the Merritt. If you're looking for privacy and a peaceful location, that is steps from the beach, minutes from downtown SoNo's unique shops, movie theater and fabulous restaurants and cafes this is just the place you've been looking for!
