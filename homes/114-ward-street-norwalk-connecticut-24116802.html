About this home

Incredible investment opportunity - Multi-family potential with two separate income streams or the perfect setup for extended family. This distinctive Saltbox-style Colonial offers 3-4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, and a flexible three-level layout in a prime Norwalk location. The main level includes light-filled living spaces with hardwood floors, a full bathroom, and a spacious kitchen and dining area. Upstairs, you'll find two large bedrooms with excellent natural light and generous closet space. The fully finished lower level, with its own private entrance, functions as a self-contained 1-bedroom apartment complete with kitchen, full bath, and laundry hookups-ideal for generating rental income, hosting extended family, or accommodating guests. Outside, there's a private driveway for multiple cars plus ample on-street parking. Recent updates include new vinyl siding, upgraded electrical, a newer furnace, and new gutters with a leaf guard system for low-maintenance living. Located on a one-way street within walking distance to Main Street restaurants, Stew Leonard's, and shopping, and just minutes from I-95, train station, beaches, and downtown Norwalk.
