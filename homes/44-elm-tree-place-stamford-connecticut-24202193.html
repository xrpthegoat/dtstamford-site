About this home

Well-maintained two-family home at 44 Elm Tree Place offering large, flexible units with strong functionality. The first-floor unit features 2 spacious bedrooms, 1 full bath, living room, dining area, and kitchen. The second-floor unit offers 3 generous bedrooms, 1 full bath, living room, dining area, kitchen, plus a finished bonus room in the attic currently used as a bedroom-with additional attic space offering great potential for expansion or storage. Both units include their own laundry. The full unfinished basement provides extra storage, and mechanicals are cleanly separated: gas furnaces for each unit with separate heat, hot water, and electric, plus a third owner's meter. Outside, a detached four-car tandem garage and an oversized 0.24-acre lot deliver a large parking area with ample off-street parking. Conveniently located near shopping, dining, groceries, schools, parks, and major commuter routes, with easy access to all Stamford has to offer.
