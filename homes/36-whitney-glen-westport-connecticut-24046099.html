About this home

Meticulously maintained and beautifully updated combined with a super convenient location makes this large 2 bedroom, 2 1/2 bath townhouse with a finished lower level and one car garage highly desirable. Steps away is the heated saltwater pool with ample lounging space for you and your guests to enjoy all summer long. Please note that one resident must be at least 62.
