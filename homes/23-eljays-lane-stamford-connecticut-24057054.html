About this home

Located in "Wonderful Westover", this contemporary ranch home is situated on a private cul-de-sac, minutes from both the Merritt Parkway and I-95. Westover boasts lush, large parcels available as in North Stamford but a location closer to everyday necessities. The home shares a pond with 2 other homes. The open floorplan of this ranch give beautiful, large windows, high ceilings and the country feel of light, greenery and space. Hardwood floors are throughout most rooms and 2 fireplaces with lovely stone hearths make for cozy winter nights. The step-down living room welcomes you as you enter the foyer and immediately look through to the foliage out back creating an exciting and dramatic entry. the large deck at this level invites you to enjoy the landscape even more. All rooms are nicely sized and the single floor layout makes for very easy living. The charming lower level has an inviting family room and a former Tea Room. Both areas can serve you in numerous ways. Walk out from here to a large brick patio and enjoy the treed yard and pond.
