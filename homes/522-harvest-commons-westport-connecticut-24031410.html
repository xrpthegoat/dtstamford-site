About this home

This beautifully maintained two-bedroom condominium in Westport offers easy living with spacious rooms, vaulted ceilings, and private outdoor spaces perfect for entertaining. A lighted walkway leads you into the unit's private courtyard entrance, where you're welcomed by walls of glass that flood the expansive rooms with natural light and provide serene views of your private garden and patio. The lower level includes an office, a large recreation room, and a full bath for added convenience. The oversized two-car garage adds extra storage and ease. Ideally located within walking distance to Balducci's and just minutes from downtown Westport's vibrant shopping, dining, arts, and culture scene, this community also offers proximity to the train and nearby beaches. Designed for privacy and relaxation, the complex features a heated pool, clubhouse, and two Har-Tru tennis courts-ideal for both leisure and recreation. This sought-after Westport condominium combines comfort, privacy, and prime location in a picturesque natural setting.
