About this home

From the aesthetic of noted architect Judith Larson and the Master craftsmanship of Redcoat Homes comes another modern statement of state-of-the-art technology and luxurious comfort. A two story entry welcomes you to this new construction where quality and high end finishes are showcased by the abundance of sunlight streaming through oversized windows. Formal and informal spaces are enriched with custom millwork including white oak flooring throughout this 6 bedroom 6.5 bath home. The enormous great room features a spectacular kitchen that will fulfill all of your culinary dreams; equipped with custom cabinetry, high end appliances, solid stone counters, and huge island with breakfast seating. The dining and family room with fireplace and beamed ceiling always welcomes family and guests. French doors bring the outdoors in and leads you to the lovely grounds with a salt water pool. Six bedrooms includes a sumptuous primary suite with his and hers dressing rooms and marble bath with walk in shower and free standing soaking tub. The finished lower level completes the picture with a huge recreation room, a gym and an ensuite bedroom. A highly coveted cul de sac in the prime Long Lots area is the location of this perfectly sited, unbeatable Westport masterpiece
