About this home

Very spacious 2-bedroom/ 2-full bathroom, 1324 sqft corner unit at the popular Regency Towers on Strawberry Hill. Southwestern views provide bright light through some-dozen large windows, bathing the wonderfully open floor plan home in sunny, natural light. Enormous living room frames the private balcony, providing outdoor dining/relaxation, city views and spectacular sunsets. Large, formal Dining Room off Kitchen w/ newer appliances. Oversized Primary Bedroom Suite offers a full wall of closet-space & a private bath w/ walk-in shower. Large Second Bedroom features a walk-in closet & is just 2-steps to the second Full Bath w/ tub/shower. Back-in-style parquet floors highlight the home plus an abundance of large closets, 6 in total, provide plenty of storage. Semi-private laundry is just steps down the hall and secure, covered parking (1 deeded spot) is included. Monthly HOA fee includes Heat, Water, Cooking Gas, Hot Water & more! You only pay electric ($45/ mo per owners). Building Amenities: Around-the-clock doorman service, live-in superintendent, modernized security monitoring throughout the building, impressive front lobby, ample visitor parking. Priced to sell, allowing buyer to update as they see fit. Set atop Strawberry Hill and located a couple minute drive to all that Downtown Stamford has to offer (fabulous restaurants, entertainment, shopping, library, parks) plus close to train, hospitals, beaches & I95, the welcoming community of Regency Towers awaits.
