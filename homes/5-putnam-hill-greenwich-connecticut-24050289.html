About this home

Convenient in town living. Greenwich Avenue, train station, public & private schools, YWCA, YMCA, Greenwich Harbor & Bruce Museum-all on your doorstep. Opportunity to own this bright, sunny and stylish 3-bedroom, 2-bath corner unit in a fabulous elevator building. Enjoy the convenience of an in-unit washer/dryer combo and ample closet space, plus a large storage locker in the basement. Relax on your private balcony with spectacular views. HOA includes property taxes, heat, hot water, trash pick up, grounds maintenance & snow removal. Garage parking available on a first come first serve basis
