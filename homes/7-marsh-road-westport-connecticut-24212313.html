About this home

Welcome to Saugatuck Shores, where you're not simply purchasing a home, you're embracing a lifestyle. This exceptional beach house offers an inviting floor plan, luxurious modern finishes, custom millwork, incredible water views, and a spacious private backyard. Enjoy your favorite wine al fresco from the charming screened-in front porch, expansive rear deck, or your private balcony off the primary suite. Step inside to a spacious living room anchored by a beautiful stone fireplace, an elegant formal dining room, and a stunning kitchen featuring custom white cabinetry, professional-grade stainless steel appliances, a large center island, and Carrara marble countertops. Coffered ceilings and custom millwork add character throughout the main level, which is completed by a beautiful full bath with an elegant glass-enclosed shower. Upstairs, the impressive primary suite boasts panoramic water views, a luxurious en-suite bath, walk-in closet, and access to its own private balcony. Two additional generously sized bedrooms feature beautiful wood beams that add warmth and distinctive character, while a full hallway bath is finished with marble tile and a tub/shower combination. Perfectly positioned to enjoy everything this sought-after waterfront community has to offer, while remaining just minutes from exceptional restaurants, shopping, nightlife, major highways, and the train. A rare opportunity to enjoy coastal living in one of Saugatuck's most desirable settings.
