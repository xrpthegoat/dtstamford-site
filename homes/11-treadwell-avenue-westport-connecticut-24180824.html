About this home

Experience the perfect blend of charm, sophistication, and convenience in this beautifully updated Colonial home, nestled in the vibrant Saugatuck Enclave. Just a short stroll away from the Metro North train station, popular restaurants, and the scenic river, this residence offers both tranquility and accessibility. Boasting four spacious bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and a half bath, this home is designed for modern living. Enter through the inviting front door into a gracious foyer, flanked by a home office on the left and an expansive formal living room on the right, featuring a cozy wood-burning fireplace. The heart of the home is the open-concept kitchen and family room, bathed in natural light from numerous windows. A formal dining room seamlessly connects to the living room, creating an ideal space for entertaining. Upstairs, the primary suite occupies the entire rear side of the home, providing a private retreat away from the other three bedrooms. The lower level offers a versatile playroom or movie room, along with convenient laundry and ample storage space. Outside, enjoy informal gatherings on the patio, surrounded by a generous, well-maintained yard. This home is a delightful haven with close proximity to all the amenities that make Saugatuck a sought-after community.
