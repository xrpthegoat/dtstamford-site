About this home

Welcome to your private oasis in prestigious North Stamford - a rare opportunity to purchase a beautifully maintained 10,000+ square foot estate nestled on a flat, landscaped 2-acre parcel. Offering 7 spacious bedrooms, 6 full bathrooms, and 2 half baths, this unique custom built in contemporary ranch impresses with soaring vaulted ceilings, high ceilings throughout, a first-floor en-suite, multiple living areas, and endless flexibility for multigenerational living or extended guests. From the moment you enter, you are greeted with space, elegance, and functionality. This home features two separate areas ideal for in-law suites or au pair quarters, making it perfect for larger families or households seeking privacy and independence under one roof. The thoughtfully designed layout includes generous formal and informal living areas. The home's size and flow make it truly exceptional for hosting - from intimate gatherings to larger events. Step outside to your expansive raised stone patio, surrounded by a beautifully manicured yard offering plenty of room for recreation, play, or relaxation in a peaceful, park-like setting. This is a rare chance to enjoy the scale and serenity of a true estate while remaining minutes from downtown Stamford, premier schools, shops, and commuter routes.
