About this home

Tucked away on one of Wilton's most picturesque streets, this private retreat feels like a getaway every single day. Set back from the road, the property welcomes you with an expansive, level lawn that gently rolls down to a serene private pond with its own dock-ideal for year-round fun. Imagine fishing on a quiet morning, paddling across the water on a sunny afternoon, or lacing up your skates for some winter magic. Inside, sunlight pours into every corner, highlighting spacious rooms that seamlessly connect indoor and outdoor living. The main floor features a bright, inviting eat-in kitchen with a center island and dining area, perfect for both casual meals and festive gatherings. The living room has walls of glass framing stunning views and a cozy double-sided fireplace shared with the dining room. Just steps away, the family room offers the perfect spot to unwind. And when the weather calls, step out onto the wrap-around deck, where there's plenty of room to host lively get-togethers or simply soak in the peace and quiet. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true haven, complete with an updated bath, a fireplace for cozy evenings, and tranquil views. Two additional generously sized bedrooms and a convenient laundry room complete the second floor. For added flexibility, a spacious bedroom and/or office with a brand-new bath sits above the garage, offering a private space for guests, work, or creative pursuits. It's more than a home-it's a sanctuary.
