About this home

Recently renovated and move-in ready, this bright 4-bedroom Colonial sits in the heart of sought-after Glenbrook. Offering multiple spacious levels, including a finished third floor and a high-ceiling lower level, the home blends classic charm with modern updates. The updated kitchen features quartz countertops, newer cabinetry, backsplash, and appliances. A spacious living room with a wood-burning fireplace and a formal dining room make it ideal for entertaining. The primary bedroom includes a private outdoor patio, while a main-level bonus room offers flexibility for a 5th bedroom or home office. Enjoy a flat, fenced yard, long driveway, and detached 2-car garage. 5 year old roof, and 3 year old bathrooms and boiler. Convenient to train, schools, and shops.
