About this home

Elegant Cape-Style Home in the Heart of Cos Cob Nestled in the desirable heart of Cos Cob, this beautifully maintained Cape offers timeless charm and spacious comfort. Featuring four well-appointed bedrooms and two full baths, the home boasts a gracious living and dining area, a sunlit eat-in kitchen that flows seamlessly into a cozy family room, and abundant storage-including a cedar-lined closet. Outdoor living shines with a private terrace and two inviting decks, perfect for entertaining or quiet relaxation. Recently refinished hardwood floors on the main level enhance the home's warmth and elegance. Enjoy the convenience of going to top-rated schools, charming boutiques, the train station-and a variety of acclaimed gourmet dining options just moments away.
