About this home

This fantastic modern colonial is set within the Compo Commons neighborhood of Westport, sitting on a level half-acre lot with a beautiful lawn. Back patio and flat, open backyard with room for a pool. Spacious three-car garage. Beautiful accents and landscaping on the exterior, while maximizing space and leading to light and bright sun-filed rooms inside. The impressive stone front walk leads you to double front doors. Enter into your sizable foyer, adorned with breathtaking high ceilings and an elegant staircase. New kitchen with plenty of counter & cabinet space, with modern appliances. Fireplace on the first level. Upstairs, find an incredible master bedroom suite complete with separate sinks and a spa. On the top floor is a large, sunny bonus room to suit to your liking. This property is perfectly positioned for convenience and ease, with shopping & dining available down the street at the Compo Shopping Center or in downtown Westport (less than 1.5 miles away). Commute to New York City via either I-95 or the Westport Train Station, each located only 5 minutes (1.4 miles) away, without living in earshot of the traffic & trains. Discover your dream home at 8 Sandhopper Trail-a modern colonial home awaits!
