About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST DEADLINE: SUNDAY, 6/7 BY 5PM -- Beautifully renovated and perfectly positioned in one of Westport's most coveted and centrally located neighborhoods, this 4-bedroom home offers the turnkey lifestyle today's buyers are seeking, with modern updates, flexible living spaces, and easy access to everything that makes Westport so special. Just minutes from top-ranked schools, downtown shopping and dining, Compo Beach, and transportation -- the location is simply exceptional. The sun-filled main level features a welcoming living room with vaulted ceiling, fireplace, and seamless flow into the dining room. The renovated kitchen showcases crisp white cabinetry, quartz countertops, marble backsplash, chic lighting, and sweeping views of the private backyard. The primary suite has been thoughtfully transformed with a tray ceiling, large walk-in closet, and luxurious marble bath with double vanity, soaking tub, and shower. Two additional bedrooms and a renovated full bath complete the upper level. The lower level offers outstanding flexibility with a fourth bedroom, renovated full bath, convenient home office, and spacious family room featuring custom built-ins, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliders to the patio and backyard. An additional lower level provides a large recreation room, dedicated laundry room, cedar closet, and abundant storage. Move right in and enjoy a turnkey lifestyle in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods.
