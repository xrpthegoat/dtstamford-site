About this home

Experience the pinnacle of luxury living in this exceptional 4,600 sq ft, 5-bedroom home, ideally located in highly sought-after Old Hill. Perfectly positioned on a pristine 1-acre lot, this stunning home offers a rare "walk-to-everything" lifestyle-just steps from shopping, top-rated restaurants, and Earthplace. Designed for both elegant entertaining and everyday comfort, the home features an open-concept floor plan enhanced by timeless architectural details and modern upgrades. Sun-drenched living spaces on the main level include a gourmet kitchen, formal living room, dining area, family room, office, and a versatile pool cabana or fitness room-all seamlessly opening to an expansive patio for effortless indoor-outdoor living. Two first-floor ensuite bedrooms provide flexible accommodations. Upstairs, the luxurious primary offers a private retreat, complemented by a large playroom, laundry room, and two additional bedrooms with a shared bath-creating a versatile layout. The resort-style outdoor setting is equally impressive, featuring a gunite pool with spa, pergola, fire pit, outdoor grill area, vegetable garden, and a fully integrated lighting and sound system. Mature, century-old trees, curated specimen plantings, and extensive stonework create a private, park-like oasis. For the buyer seeking privacy while being near all the amenities that Westport has to offer, this extraordinary property combines classic elegance, modern amenities, and an unbeatable location.
