About this home

Welcome to this professionally landscaped, energy-efficient custom-built 4,100 sq ft home with 5 beds and 4.1 baths, located across from North Mianus School. Enjoy a bright living room with gas fireplace, sunlit dining room with large glass doors, and a custom chef's kitchen with quartzite counters. The finished lower level adds 1,623 sq ft with 9 ft ceilings, fireplace, and French doors to a second patio. Special rental for a special renter.
