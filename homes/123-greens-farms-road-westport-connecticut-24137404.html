About this home

Classic Greens Farms Colonial - Timeless Elegance Meets Modern Comfort ~ Nestled in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods, this beautifully updated Center Hall Colonial offers the perfect blend of timeless charm and modern luxury. Ideally located just minutes from Sherwood Island State Park-with its miles of golden sand beaches, scenic picnic areas, and sweeping views-this home provides easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and downtown Westport. Set on a picturesque property with a gentle brook at the far end of the level backyard, the home invites peaceful outdoor living with a charming front porch & back porch, expansive deck, and stone patio-ideal for al fresco dining and entertaining. Inside, a gracious two-story foyer with a curved staircase opens to a formal living room with fireplace and a formal dining room complete with butler's pantry and walk-in pantry. The gourmet kitchen features top-of-the-line new Thermador appliances, stone countertops, and a spacious dining area with access to the deck. A family room w/fireplace with Fr doors to an enclosed porch, a pvt office, and a well-designed mudroom with easy access to the two-car garage complete the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat, offering a fireplace, balcony with beautiful property views, walk-in closet, and luxurious bath. Three additional bedrooms share access to full baths, complemented by a large recreation/media room, half bath and convenient second-floor laundry.
