About this home

*Charming Compo Beach Rental. Experience the best of Westport living in this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom home located just steps from Compo Beach and the Longshore Club. Outdoor Living: Enjoy the private backyard deck with a BBQ, string lights, and a cozy seating area. Dogs considered on a case-by-case basis (no cats). Non-smoking home.
