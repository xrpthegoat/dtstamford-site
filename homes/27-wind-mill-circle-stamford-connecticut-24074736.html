About this home

A rare offering of a unique property w/ winding river views, an expansive lawn, an in-ground pool & exquisite landscaping. A resort like atmosphere unmatched anywhere at this price. Conveniently located on a mid-North Stamford cul-de-sac you will find this classic colonial has been maintained very well. Recent updates include a newer furnace installed 2020, a newer hot water heater installed 2021 & newer air conditioners installed 2022. The main level has spacious bright rooms w/ great flow. The 2nd floor offers the primary suite w/ beautiful views, 3 additional bedrooms & hall bath along w/ an office / den. The finished lower level is the perfect recreation room, & there is also the laundry & storage. This is one of those properties that do not come on the market often.
