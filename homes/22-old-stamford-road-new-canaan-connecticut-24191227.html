About this home

Exceptionally crafted new construction by a highly regarded local builder, this home offers 5 bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, and 4 finished levels directly across from Bristow Bird Sanctuary with access to Mead Park, shops, train, and restaurants. Distinctive craftsmanship is found throughout, with custom millwork and cabinetry, detailed mouldings, white oak floors, quartz counters, and designer fixtures. The main level features an open floor plan centered around a beautifully appointed kitchen, expansive dining area, and living room with access to a private fenced patio and landscaped grounds. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a walk-in closet and spa-like bath with double-sink vanity, soaking tub, water closet, and walk-in shower with ceramic walls and Carrera marble flooring. Completing the 2nd level are 2 additional bedrooms, full bath, and spacious laundry room. The finished 3rd level features vaulted ceilings, 4th bedroom, full bath, and flexible playroom. The finished lower level includes a 5th bedroom, full bath, and rec room, offering versatility for guests, work, fitness, or media use. Additional amenities: heated garage on main level with an electric vehicle charging station, irrigation system, town water and sewer, natural gas connected in street, and custom pull-down blinds throughout. Combining quality construction, 4 finished levels, and a premier walk-to-town location, this new home delivers details and functionality that set it apart.
