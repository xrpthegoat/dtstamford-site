About this home

Introducing a truly exceptional home, meticulously renovated to offer the epitome of modern living. This stunning colonial boasts 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, and is nestled on a peaceful street in Brookside. Upon entering, you are greeted by an open concept first floor featuring an impressive staircase w/custom millwork, living room w/ wood fireplace, state-of-the-art kitchen, showcasing a 6-burner Thermador range w/ skillet top & beautiful wet bar w/wine chiller. Step outside to discover the exceptional level fenced-in yard, complete with a blue stone patio & custom built-in grill station, ideal for outdoor entertaining. Complemented by new landscaping and irrigation system, ensuring a lush and vibrant outdoor space. The second floor of this home is equally impressive, w/ 9 ft+ ceilings, creating an airy and spacious feel. The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat, boasting a custom walk-in closet and a lavish 5-piece en suite bath. Convenience is key in this home, with a laundry room located on the second floor and ample storage available in the attic and unfinished basement. Additional features of this home include a main level office & mudroom, 1-car garage, natural gas high-efficiency hot water, 3-zone heating, central air. Located in a prime location, this home is close to shopping, top-rated restaurants, & offers easy access to I-95 and the Darien and SoNo train stations, making commuting a breeze.
