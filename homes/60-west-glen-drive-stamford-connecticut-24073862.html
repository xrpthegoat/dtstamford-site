About this home

Stylish & Functional Family Retreat in desirable Westover area of Stamford. Set on a pristine 1-acre lot bordering Greenwich, this 4-bed, 3.5-bath Colonial blends classic character with modern upgrades for the ultimate family-friendly lifestyle. Designed for seamless entertaining and everyday ease, it features hardwood floors, crown moldings, and sun-drenched spaces throughout. The kitchen is outfitted with custom cabinetry, granite counters and has an abundance of natural light with a spacious eat-in area and breakfast bar open to an expansive flagstone patio, perfect for hosting summer gatherings. The soaring family room stuns with oversized transom windows, a statement stone fireplace, and custom built-ins, while the cozy office with French doors and a fireplace is the ideal work-from-home escape. The custom mudroom with built-in lockers, drawers and closets helps keeps life effortlessly organized. Upstairs, the primary suite boasts a spa-like bath with a tub, steam shower, and skylight.Three additional bedrooms feature hardwood floors and large closets and a stylishly renovated hall bath. The finished lower level is a bonus space for a playroom or home gym, and a guest suite. Extras: full-house generator, central air, security system and sprinkler system. Convenient Stamford location and minutes from Mianus River Park and top Greenwich amenities, this beautifully curated family home is a rare find!
