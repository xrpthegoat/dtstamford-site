About this home

Quintessential and Sophisticated New England four bedroom Colonial on lovely 2.32 acres. This stunning property was completely renovated in 2005. Gracious rooms, arched doorways, beautiful mouldings & extra large windows, bringing in so much natural light, make this home feel grand but still intimate. Beautiful gourmet kitchen with two sinks, top of the line appliances, including two dishwashers & warming drawer, Butler's pantry with beverage refrigerator & large storage pantry is the heart of the home. Adjacent to the kitchen you will find a fabulous family room with stone fireplace, making this the perfect entertaining space. Formal living room and dining room, both with fireplaces, are so charming and add to the allure of this extraordinary home. Completing the first floor is a study/library, mud room & half bath. Upstairs you will find four large bedrooms, including the posh primary suite with serene spa-like bath, large walk-in closet and deck, and two additional full baths. The finished walkout lower lever (1464 sq.ft.) includes two offices, play room & full bath. Enjoy the bucolic outdoors, relaxing & dining on the deck just off the kitchen or playing sports in the private, spacious backyard. So many extras including generator, invisible fence, whole house audio & security system. This gem, in a very desirable location, has been impeccably maintained and is a rare find, awaiting the next lucky owners. Do not miss out on this opportunity to make 123 Ruscoe Rd. yours!
