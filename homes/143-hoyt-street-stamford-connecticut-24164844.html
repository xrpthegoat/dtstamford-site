About this home

Welcome to The Buckingham at 143 Hoyt Street, Unit 1K - a beautifully updated 2-bed, 1-bath condo in the heart of downtown Stamford. Ready for immediate occupancy, this modern, light-filled unit features an open floor plan with stylish finishes throughout. The kitchen offers maple cabinetry with glass-front uppers, granite countertops, dark subway tile backsplash, and stainless steel appliances. A granite breakfast bar opens to the expansive living area, accented by a white brick feature wall and crown molding. The primary bedroom showcases a custom built-in wardrobe with LED-lit shelving, shoe racks, drawer storage, hanging space, and entertainment center, plus a walk-in closet and second closet. The second bedroom features a double closet and great natural light. The bathroom features a vessel sink vanity, LED-lit mirror, designer damask wallpaper, and a walk-in shower with glass enclosure, mosaic tile, and rainfall head. Highlights include in-unit washer/dryer in pantry room with built-in shelving, private balcony overlooking the pool, assigned indoor garage parking, and hardwood-style flooring throughout. Residents at The Buckingham enjoy exceptional amenities: stunning lobby with crystal chandelier, granite floors, and 24/7 doorman, heated pool with sundeck, fitness center, sauna, library, and community room with kitchen, fireplace lounge, and game area. Ideally situated near dining, shopping, Stamford Transportation Center, Metro-North, and I-95.
