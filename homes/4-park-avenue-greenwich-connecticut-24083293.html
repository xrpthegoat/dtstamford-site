About this home

Welcome to this beautifully crafted Shore Colonial that seamlessly blends timeless charm with modern luxury. This stunning home features grand living spaces, exquisite details, and high-quality finishes throughout. Step inside to a bright and spacious main level, where coffered ceilings and elegant design create an inviting atmosphere. The large dining room flows effortlessly into the chef's kitchen, complete with Viking appliances, custom cabinetry, and a connected family room with a cozy fireplace-perfect for entertaining or everyday living. The primary suite is a true retreat, boasting a marble bath with a spa-like double shower and 2 large walk-in closets. Additional bedrooms offer plenty of space, including a third-floor bedroom and full bath, ideal for guests or a private home office. The lower level, approximately 1,000 sq. ft., features two versatile rooms, a full bath, and a laundry area. Outdoor living is just as impressive with a charming front porch, a large patio, and a double-level backyard, providing ample space for play and entertaining. The white picket fence adds classic curb appeal, while the side entrance with a mudroom ensures convenience. A two-car detached garage completes the property. Located in a prime Old Greenwich location, this home is just a short walk to town, schools, and the train, with easy access to the beach by bike. Additional highlights include two fireplaces, a 3-zone Buteras Hydra air system, and impeccable craftsmanship throughout.
