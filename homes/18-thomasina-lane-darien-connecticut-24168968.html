About this home

Thoughtfully built and meticulously maintained, this five-bedroom colonial offers four finished levels of living on a highly desirable in-town cul-de-sac. Ideally located just moments from beaches, train, downtown Darien and Darien Commons the home blends convenience with a true neighborhood feel. The bright open layout features hardwood floors throughout, detailed millwork, and a spacious kitchen with Viking and Miele appliances, custom cabinetry, and honed granite countertops. The heart of the home flows seamlessly into the main living areas, including a living room, dedicated dining room, and family room complete with a wood-burning fireplace. A convenient mudroom and powder room complete the first floor. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a spacious oversized bath and two walk-in closets. Three additional bedrooms include one en-suite bedroom, while the other two are connected by a Jack-and-Jill bathroom. The finished walk-up third floor with an updated full bath provides a private and flexible space for work, recreation, or guests. Plus a full finished basement provides the perfect playroom, home gym, movie watching, and extra space. Detached two-car garage provides ample storage. Set on a flat, usable property with wonderful outdoor space, this is a rare opportunity on one of Darien's most beloved streets in the coveted Hindley Elementary School district.
