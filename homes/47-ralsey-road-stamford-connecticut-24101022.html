About this home

Shippan Point Jewel - Updated, Sun-Filled, and Steps from the Water Welcome to this beautifully expanded Cape-style home in the highly sought-after Shippan Point waterfront community. Blending modern updates with timeless charm, this 4-bedroom retreat offers sunlit spaces in every room and a layout designed for both comfort and function. The upstairs primary and bedrooms are generously sized and uniquely appointed, offering flexibility for family, guests, or work-from-home needs. Enjoy deeded beach rights to Ralsey Road's west-facing beach, where you'll experience gorgeous sunsets throughout the year. Step outside to a private backyard oasis, complete with decking and a flagstone patio ideal for outdoor entertaining. An oversized 2-car garage provides ample storage and convenience. Additional highlights include: Located outside of the flood zone Easy access to biking and running routes throughout Shippan, Just minutes to parks, marinas, shopping and commuting. 47 Ralsey Road offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the best of coastal living with everyday convenience.
