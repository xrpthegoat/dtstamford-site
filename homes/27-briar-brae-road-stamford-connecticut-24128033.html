About this home

Nestled in the picturesque setting of Stamford, Connecticut, 27 Briar Brae Road welcomes you with its enchanting charm and timeless elegance. This delightful Colonial-style home, boasting 1,672 square feet, is perfectly situated on a square 1 acre lot that offers a serene escape with its mature trees and lush landscaping. Step inside to discover a home that seamlessly blends classic allure with modern comforts. The inviting living room is highlighted by a beautiful gas fireplace, ideal for cozy evenings. Adjacent is a spacious dining area, perfect for entertaining or enjoying intimate family meals. The kitchen, a dream for any culinary enthusiast, features ample cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, a gas stovetop and the convenience of an outdoor deck for easy dining or morning coffee. This home offers three generously-sized bedrooms, with a versatile family room on the first floor that easily transforms into a fourth bedroom, accommodating the evolving needs of any household. Two well-appointed bathrooms ensure convenience and privacy for residents and guests alike. Enjoy the tranquility of your private outdoor space, perfect for summer gatherings or quiet reflection. Additional amenities include a 1 car garage for secure parking, and the convenience of a washer and dryer. Experience the blend of elegance and warmth at 27 Briar Brae Road, a charming retreat that promises a comfortable and inviting lifestyle.
