About this home

Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully updated Cape Cod in sought-after Springdale, just minutes from parks, the train station, shopping, and dining. Step inside to a welcoming living room with a cozy fireplace, leading to a sun-filled eat-in kitchen with dining area and seamless access to the back deck and private level yard. The main floor features two spacious bedrooms, a renovated full bath, a well-appointed mudroom, laundry room, and direct entry to the attached one-car garage. Upstairs, the expansive primary bedroom is joined by a fourth bedroom and a stylishly renovated second full bath. Outdoors, the oversized backyard offers lush landscaping and the perfect setting for entertaining or simply relaxing in a tranquil setting.
