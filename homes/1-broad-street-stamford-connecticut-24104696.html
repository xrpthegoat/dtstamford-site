About this home

Experience luxury living at its finest in the heart of Stamford, with breathtaking views of downtown, Mill River Park & the Long Island Sound. This exquisite 3 bedroom, 3 1/2 bath home offers an expansive open floor plan with floor-to-ceiling windows designed to maximize light & showcase the stunning scenery. Thoughtfully designed with attention to every detail, the home features custom electric shades & drapes. Step into a gracious foyer w a large closet & a stylish powder room. The sun-drenched living room, complete with custom bookshelves & access to a private terrace, flows seamlessly into the dining area & a beautifully renovated gourmet kitchen. The kitchen is a chef's dream-equipped with Viking stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops & matching quartz backsplash. The serene primary suite is filled with natural light & gorgeous views. It boasts a spacious walk-in closet & a luxuriously renovated bath featuring radiant heated floors, a large walk-in shower, separate water closet, double vanity & dedicated makeup area. Each additional bedroom includes its own en-suite, renovated bathroom. Park Tower offers a full suite of luxury amenities, including a fitness center, 24/7 concierge service, heated indoor pool, theater room, billiards room, social lounge & a rooftop terrace w 3 gas grills-perfect for entertaining against the skyline backdrop. Complimentary shuttle service to the Stamford train. Enjoy elevated urban living in this exceptional home.
