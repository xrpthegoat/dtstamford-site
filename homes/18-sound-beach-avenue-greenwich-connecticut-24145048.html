About this home

Spectacular home in the heart of sought after Old Greenwich! Beautifully finished four bedroom 3.5 bath Colonial with open floor plan. First floor includes an eat in chef's kitchen with top of the line stainless steel appliances highlighted by a unique serving window, which flows seamlessly into the living room and dining area, French doors leading out to the expansive deck which overlooks a beautifully landscaped back yard. Primary suite with a luxurious ensuite bath, two additional bedrooms and another full bath complete the second floor. Finished lower level with a fourth bedroom, full bath and gym/playroom or guest area with walk out to yard. White oak hardwood floors throughout. Exterior showcases with white James Hardie siding, sleek black windows and clean architectural lines that create striking curb appeal! Minutes to schools, train, beaches, parks, library, shops, restaurants and I-95 for an easy commute/trip into NYC! Enjoy everything that Greenwich has to offer, including the lowest property taxes in the entire area! As per builder original foundation, and first floor joists and decking under the hardwood is original. Additional sq footage in finished lower level is approx. 682. Great investment opportunity!
