About this home

Discover this inviting four-bedroom, four-bath split-level home in one of North Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, just minutes from the New Canaan border. Situated on a beautifully manicured one-acre property, this home seamlessly blends comfort, style, and functionality ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. Inside, you'll find hardwood floors throughout much of the home, a formal dining room, an eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, and a generous living room with a gas fireplace. The family room provides additional gathering space with a charming wood-burning stove, while a versatile bonus room can easily serve as a home office, guest bedroom, fitness room, or den. Step outside to your own private retreat, complete with a sparkling pool surrounded by lush landscaping and expansive outdoor living space an ideal setting for relaxation and summer entertaining.Offering the perfect combination of privacy, convenience, and flexible living space, this well-maintained North Stamford home presents a rare opportunity in a highly desirable location.
