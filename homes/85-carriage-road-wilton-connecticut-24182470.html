About this home

First time on the market in over 45 years, this lovingly maintained Colonial is ready to welcome its next owners. Offering more than 3,700 square feet of living space, plus an additional 1,300-square-foot basement with both interior and exterior access, this home provides exceptional space and endless possibilities. Featuring five bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms, this home is ready for your personal updates and vision. While it does require some TLC, it has solid bones and presents an outstanding opportunity for renovation and customization. Don't miss the very large fully screened patio to enjoy never ending evenings free of bugs! Come discover this charming hidden gem in Wilton and imagine the possibilities. Please note that the attached greenhouse off the kitchen requires a new roof. The property is being sold as is. Considering the high interest on the property the owner is asking for highest and best offers by Wednesday July 1st 6pm.
