About this home

This 4-bedroom, 3-full, 1-half bath Colonial offers 3,430 sq ft of thoughtfully designed living on a 1.01-acre lot in Westport's Old Hill neighborhood. The main-level primary suite features a spa-inspired bathroom, walk-in closet, and abundant natural light. A chef's kitchen with custom cabinetry opens to spacious living and dining areas, creating an open flow for entertaining or daily living. Additional living spaces include a home office, family room, and flex room adaptable for media, gym, or recreation. Outdoor amenities include a private yard, patio, and mature landscaping. Features include a security system, ample storage, premium finishes, and high ceilings throughout. Located 0.5 miles from Longshore Club Park, 0.6 miles from Kings Highway Elementary School, 1.2 miles from Staples High School, and 1.0 miles from Westport Center and Compo Acres Shopping. Nearby restaurants, cafes, and local shops within 0.5-1.0 miles, with easy access to I-95. Includes flexible floor plan, vaulted ceilings,and multiple living spaces. Ideal for varied living arrangements, work-from-home setups, and entertaining.
