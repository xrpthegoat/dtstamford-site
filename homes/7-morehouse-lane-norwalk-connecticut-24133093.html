About this home

YOU'LL FALL IN LOVE WITH THIS HOME THE MOMENT YOU ENTER THE FRONT DOOR! CUL-DE-SAC LOCATION! Set on a beautiful, waterfront, level 1.21-acre lot, this warm & inviting Colonial-style home offers the perfect blend of comfort, space & updates. With 2,766 square feet, 4 bedrooms, and 2.5 baths, there's plenty of room for everyone to spread out and enjoy. The updated kitchen features high-end SS appliances & fixtures such as Sub Zero, Wolf, Asko, KWC etc., and flows nicely into the family room, dining room & living room making it easy to cook, relax and spend time together. A Great Room adds extra flexibility that's perfect for a home office, media room, playroom or workout space. Enjoy a serene connection to nature from the expansive 3-Season screened-in porch with skylight & a new deck overlooking a fenced back yard & fishing pond. It's the ideal spot to unwind, watch the seasons change & enjoy the outdoors without leaving home. Beautiful hardwood floors throughout most of the home. Recent updates include NEW Central Air Condensor & Air Handler 2021, NEW Deck 2025, NEW Fiberglass Front Door & Front Railing 2025, New Washer/Dryer 2024, Landscape Lighting 2019 & more! Features Anderson Renewable Windows, 50-Year Architectural Roof, Underground Utilities, Generator Ready & more! Located in a sought-after West Norwalk neighborhood bordering Darien & New Canaan, this home is close to shopping, restaurants, and commuter routes. It's a wonderful place to be at home!
