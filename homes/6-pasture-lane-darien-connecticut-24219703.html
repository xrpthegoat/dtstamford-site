About this home

Tucked away on a peaceful cul-de-sac and set on just over an acre of beautifully landscaped property, this inviting five-bedroom home offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience in one of Darien's most desirable locations. Thoughtfully updated with a newly renovated kitchen and baths, the home features a wonderfully flexible floor plan designed to accommodate both everyday living and effortless entertaining. Generous gathering spaces and versatile bedroom arrangements provide plenty of room for family and guests alike. Step outside to your own private retreat, where a sparkling swimming pool and expansive grounds create an idyllic setting for summer fun, relaxation, and outdoor entertaining. The location is truly exceptional! Just a short stroll to Weed Beach, enjoy easy access to the beach, tennis and paddle tennis courts, picnic areas, and grills. Adding to the appeal, the property is adjacent to the recently renovated and enhanced Hindley Elementary School. Offering the rare combination of a quiet cul-de-sac setting, generous property, fabulous recreational amenities, and beautifully updated interiors, this is a home to enjoy in every season. A wonderful opportunity to embrace the very best of Darien's coastal lifestyle!
