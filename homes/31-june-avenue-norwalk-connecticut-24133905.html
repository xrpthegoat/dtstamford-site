About this home

31 June Avenue blends a classic layout with modern versatility - an exceptional opportunity to own in one of Norwalk's most desirable enclaves. Built in 2004, this four-bedroom, two and a half bath home offers an inviting floor plan with generous proportions, hardwood floors and abundant natural light throughout. The main level features a welcoming living room, formal dining room, eat-in kitchen, family room with classic fireplace, office, laundry room and powder room. Upstairs, the serene primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with walk-in closets and private full bath. Three additional well-sized bedrooms and a second full bath complete the upper level. An attached two-car garage and private patio add convenience and outdoor enjoyment. The expansive finished lower level presents endless possibilities -a home office, gym, or playroom - with ample space remaining for storage and future customization. Close to schools, Norwalk hospital and shopping!
