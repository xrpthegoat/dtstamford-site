About this home

Beautifully maintained and move-in ready, this exceptional rental is located on a peaceful private lane. Built in 2014, the home offers a versatile floor plan spanning four finished levels, providing abundant space for everyday living, working from home, and entertaining. The bright, open-concept chef's kitchen serves as the centerpiece of the home and features two islands-perfect for casual dining, meal preparation, and gathering with friends and family. Sunlight fills the main living areas, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Upstairs, the second floor includes a generous primary suite along with three additional bedrooms. One bedroom is privately situated in its own wing with a full bath, offering excellent flexibility for guests, an in-law arrangement, or a secondary living space. The third floor features an ideal work-from-home setup with an office (or additional bedroom), a comfortable sitting area, and a full bath. The fully finished lower level offers even more space, including a large playroom or media room plus a dedicated gym area. Offered fully furnished, this home provides an effortless lifestyle-simply unpack and enjoy everything Westport has to offer, from top-rated schools and a vibrant downtown to beautiful beaches and coastal living. Long term tenant preferred.
