About this home

Experience the perfect harmony of sophisticated architecture and bucolic charm in this Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac, centrally located in the Staples neighborhood. The residence situated on 1.07 acres offers a "farm-to-table" lifestyle without sacrificing modern-day luxuries of Westport living. The barn and orchard pay homage to the property's farming past, while maintaining a meticulous landscaped oasis. This home is for those who value both grand-scale entertaining and quiet everyday comforts. It's conveniently located to all town amenities, trendy downtown dining, and transportation hubs, while still maintaining the privacy of country living.
