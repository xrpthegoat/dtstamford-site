About this home

Charming, secluded ranch home on corner of cul-de-sac, bordering Rowayton. Basement and pull-down attic provide ample storage. The yard is landscaped to be a private sanctuary with a patio for entertaining. Two-car garage with new garage door opener, extra guest parking. Walking distance to the Rowayton train station and convenient to local village restaurants, shopping and beach.
