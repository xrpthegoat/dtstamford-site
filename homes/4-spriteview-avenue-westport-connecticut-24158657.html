About this home

Saugatuck Island in Westport, Connecticut, is a unique and desirable waterfront community that offers residents a serene and picturesque living experience. This private island, accessible by a bridge, is nestled at the mouth of the Saugatuck River and Long Island Sound, providing a tranquil retreat with stunning water views and a sense of seclusion while still being close to the amenities of Westport with the convenience trains, trendy dining option and water activities. Enjoy the panoramic views at this tranquil setting with a private dock and water access. Perfect setting for watching amazing sunsets and entertaining on you private patio.
