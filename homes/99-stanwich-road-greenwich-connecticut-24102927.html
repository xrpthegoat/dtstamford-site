About this home

Picturesque Stanwich Colonial with golf course views, located less than 10 minutes from downtown Greenwich in the North Street school district. This beautiful 0.5 acre property is set back from Stanwich Road on a quiet drive with a lush lawn, expansive patio, and professional landscaping. The sophisticated 1925 home was transformed for today's lifestyle with a light-filled open floor plan. The main floor features a dramatic great room with dining and living space, oversized fireplace, and French doors to the patio. Kitchen with marble countertops, a new eat-in banquette, separate bar and pantry area. Upstairs, a spacious master suite with an oversized walk-in closet and marble bathroom plus 3 more bedrooms, 2 baths, and laundry. Finished basement with recently updated playroom. Detached 2 car garage with heating and cooling system.
