About this home

This luxury custom designed home in a private country setting offers maximum privacy and comfortable living. The main level offers single floor living featuring a private primary suite with dressing room, full bath and French doors to a full screened porch with fireplace. The living room under a cathedral ceiling with fireplace and its own terrace access. An eat in kitchen with bay windows, breakfast nook, granite counters, Sub-Zero and Wolf range, powder room. A private office with bay windows. Laundry off a stone floored mudroom with built ins. Upstairs, a loft family room under a vaulted ceiling. Additional 2nd floor space includes a bedroom with full bath, and recreation room with adjoining bedroom and full bath that functions as a self-contained guest suite. The lower level adds a media room with wet bar, built ins and half bath, plus a second recreation room. Each room thoughtfully designed without compromise. Geothermal heating and cooling, whole house generator, noise reduction windows, cedar siding, stone exterior, wood shingle roof, three car attached heated garage and two fireplaces set the stage for effortless living.
