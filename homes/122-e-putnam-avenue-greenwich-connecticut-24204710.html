About this home

Enjoy Cos Cob living in this bright and inviting one-bedroom apartment, ideally situated in a well-maintained elevator building. Centrally located with convenient access to Cos Cob's shops, dining, parks, schools, and Metro-North, this updated apartment offers an easy, low-maintenance lifestyle in the heart of the community.
