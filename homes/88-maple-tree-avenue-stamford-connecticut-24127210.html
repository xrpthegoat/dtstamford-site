About this home

Welcome to The Maples, an exceptional townhome community nestled in a private, wooded enclave on the Darien border. This expansive 3,300 sq. ft. residence offers 3 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and 2 half baths spread across four finished levels. The main floor is designed for entertaining, featuring an open-concept layout that seamlessly connects the bright white kitchen-complete with marble countertops, matching backsplash, abundant cabinetry, and new appliances-to the dining and living areas. Sunlight streams through oversized windows, while a gas fireplace and sliders to a private deck overlooking Noroton River create a perfect setting for both relaxation and hosting. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a true retreat with a spa-like bath featuring radiant heated floors, a generous walk-in closet, & a private deck with peaceful wooded views. A 2nd bedroom, full bath, and a convenient laundry area complete this level. The 3rd floor provides exceptional versatility with a third bedroom, full bath, AND bonus living space-ideal for a guest suite, playroom, office, or in-law/Au pair setup. The finished lower lvl adds even more flexibility, perfect for a large family room, gym, or office, with walk-out access to a patio surrounded by professionally landscaped grounds. Add'l highlights include an attached garage, C/A, & ample storage in the basement and attic. Ideally located Darien Commons/Train Station(1mi) & Glenbrook Train Station (1/2 mi) Cove Island Park/Beach (2mi)
