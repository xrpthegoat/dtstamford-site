About this home

The Cottages at Richards Avenue offers new, modern construction in a luxury, gated community of single family homes (attached at the garage) with full first floor living. At least one occupant must be 60 or over. This 3 Bdrm, 2-1/2 Bath Home features an open floor plan with gourmet kitchen expanding into dining room/living room, and sliders to private screened in porch. 1st floor master suite w/ luxurious bath & spacious walk-in closet, private garage, mudroom/laundry. Maintenance free living w/energy efficient solar panels, keyless entry, and optional concierge services. Second floor contains loft/study and 2 additional bedrooms with an abundance of closet storage, and a storage room. Fenced in area for pet. Community gazebo overlooking the river. Visit the website for full details. Featured on This Old House's 2021 Cottage Community Idea House series.
