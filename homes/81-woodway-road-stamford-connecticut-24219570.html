About this home

Beautifully updated and thoughtfully expanded, this 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers flexible living space inside and out. The renovated kitchen features custom wood cabinetry, granite counters, stainless steel appliances and a large island with seating, opening to the living and dining rooms. Hardwood floors and an oversized bay window brighten the main living areas. Tucked into its own wing, the primary suite has vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet and a bath with double sinks, tiled shower and soaking tub. Two bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level. Downstairs, the family room features a wood-burning stove, built-in wood shelving and cabinetry, plus projector and surround sound wiring. A fourth bedroom, third bath, laundry and two cedar closets add flexibility and storage. Sliding doors lead to a private, tree-lined backyard with a slate patio for grilling and gatherings, a second patio with an outdoor fireplace for evenings by the fire, and a large shed. New vinyl fencing (2025) encloses the yard. Extras include central air, whole-house humidifier, irrigation, generator readiness and oversized garage. Recent mechanical updates include a new water heater and replacement of an A/C unit. Enjoy walks into Springdale, nearby golf courses, and easy access to Talmadge Hill train station, the Merritt Parkway, and Darien and New Canaan shops and restaurants.
