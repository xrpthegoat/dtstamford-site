About this home

Stunning updated Colonial! Life doesn't get sweeter than this beautiful 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath Center Hall Colonial nestled in the desirable Hycliff community. Thoughtfully updated with all the bells and whistles, this home perfect blends classic charm & modern comfort! Step inside to an inviting open floor plan that connects the kitchen & dining areas-ideal for entertaining! The sleek, updated kitchen features modern appliances & flows seamlessly out to a private backyard oasis with a charming patio-perfect for al fresco dining or summer gatherings. The spacious living room features a cozy wood-burning fireplace, while the adjacent sunroom/library offers a quiet retreat for book lovers or a bright home office. A stylish updated main-level powder room adds to the home's elegant feel. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat, with generous space for a king-sized bed, a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a soaking tub, double vanity, & custom shower, plus the convenience of an in-suite washer/dryer. Note bonus 2nd home office/playroom. To the left of the staircase features a 2nd full bath with tub, a spacious guest suite with a full bath with shower, plus 2 additional well-sized bedrooms! Additional features include: Attic & basement for storage, attached two car garage. Access to private Hycliff community amenities: pool, tennis court & playground! Don't miss your chance to own this exceptional home in one of Stamford's most coveted neighborhoods!
