About this home

Welcome to 256 Mill Road, a charming and enchanting home nestled on a picturesque country road. Overlooking the tranquil Silvermine River, this property offers the ideal setting for your own personal retreat. The home features three bedrooms, two and half bathrooms, living room with fireplace, dining room, walkout lower level with a picturesque sitting room/library. The light-filled rooms provides a warm and inviting atmosphere. With easy access to outdoor living areas with stunning gardens, it is perfectly designed for both entertaining guests and enjoying everyday life in comfort and style. Located in the heart of Silvermine, residents can enjoy the convenience of local dining at renowned establishments such as Gray Barns, The Mercantile, and Silvermine Market. The property is just minutes from New Canaan town center and only 60 minutes from New York City.
