About this home

Charming picture-perfect Colonial Cape set on a beautifully landscaped property, this meticulously maintained home is filled with warmth, character, and timeless appeal. Light and bright throughout, the inviting open floor plan is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The welcoming foyer with coat closet and powder room opens to a gracious living room featuring a fireplace and bay window. The beautifully updated kitchen offers quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar, seamlessly flowing into the casual dining area with sliders leading to the expansive deck and private backyard retreat. A cozy sitting room just off the kitchen creates the perfect spot to relax, while the sunroom adds wonderful additional living space filled with natural light. The first-floor primary suite features an updated bath for easy one-level living. Completing the main level is a versatile laundry/mudroom/home office conveniently located off the attached two-car garage. The second floor offers three spacious bedrooms and an updated full bath. Gleaming hardwood floors throughout, newer first-floor A/C splits, and thoughtful updates enhance the comfort and appeal of this exceptional home. A true gem offering charm, functionality, and move-in-ready condition.
