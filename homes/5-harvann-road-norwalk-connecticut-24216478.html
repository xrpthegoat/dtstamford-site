About this home

Move right in and feel at home in this beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath colonial, tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac. Thoughtful details shine throughout, from hardwood floors and custom trim work to an inviting eat-in kitchen featuring custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, and Carrara marble countertops. Sliding doors lead to a three-season room overlooking the private yard-a perfect spot for morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite offers a relaxing retreat with a vaulted ceiling, walk-in closet, and an ensuite bath with a stunning walk-in shower. Two additional generously sized bedrooms, a beautifully appointed hall bath, and a convenient laundry area complete the second floor. Previously updated electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and windows, along with ample closet and storage space, add to this home's comfort and appeal.
