About this home

Set on two level acres surrounded by mature trees, this inviting four bedroom, three and a half bath Colonial offers an ideal combination of space, privacy, and versatility. The updated kitchen opens to a spacious family room featuring a wood-burning fireplace and an oversized picture window that fills the room with natural light while overlooking the expansive backyard. A flexible living area above the garage, complete with a full bathroom and laundry, provides options for extended family, an au pair, guests, or additional entertaining space. The full, unfinished basement offers excellent potential for future living space. Recent improvements, including a newer roof, windows, siding, and upgraded electrical, add to the home's appeal. With its classic Colonial charm, versatile floor plan, and expansive, usable grounds, this home offers the perfect setting for comfortable everyday living.
