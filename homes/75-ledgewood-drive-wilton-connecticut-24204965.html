About this home

75 Ledgewood feels like a hidden retreat, set on two private acres bordering protected Wilton land. Surrounded by mature trees and filled with beautiful light, this south-facing, classic Colonial offers a rare sense of privacy while still keeping you close to everything. Inside, thoughtful updates, warm interiors and an easy connection to the outdoors create a home that feels incredibly inviting. The fully updated kitchen opens directly to the deck, while living and dining rooms offer plenty of space to gather. A sun-filled family room with double-sided brick fireplace opens to the patio, and a separate office space with built-ins invites you to work or unwind. Upstairs are four bedrooms and two full baths, including a primary suite with renovated bath featuring heated floors and a walk-in closet. Hardwood floors and abundant natural light carry throughout the home. Outside, the deck, bluestone patio and expansive yard create multiple spaces to dine, entertain or simply enjoy the private, natural surroundings. Meticulously maintained, 75 Ledgewood combines the privacy of a tucked-away setting with the convenience of desirable South Wilton - minutes to town, top-rated schools and commuter routes to NYC.
