About this home

Completely reimagined and move-in ready, this spacious 6-bedroom, 4 full and 1 half bath home offers nearly 3,000 square feet of versatile living on a peaceful, private 0.33-acre lot in desirable Greenwich. Thoughtfully expanded and extensively updated, it blends modern comfort with timeless appeal with many recent improvements. The main level features four bedrooms, 2.5 baths, a welcoming living room, and a spacious family room with vaulted ceilings and exposed beams. The luxurious primary bath features a jetted soaking tub, heated floors, and a solar-powered skylight. The walkout lower level offers exceptional flexibility with two additional bedrooms, two full baths, a large recreation room, and a mudroom-ideal for an in-law suite, au pair accommodations, home office, or guest space. Enjoy the added benefits of a whole-house generator and smart garage door for seamless everyday living. The outdoor space includes two expansive decks overlooking a private backyard with a playscape, perfect for entertaining. Conveniently located minutes from the Merritt Parkway, I-95, the Boston Post Road, and Metro-North train stations, this exceptional home offers an easy commute to New York City while enjoying the privacy and charm of a sought-after Greenwich neighborhood.
