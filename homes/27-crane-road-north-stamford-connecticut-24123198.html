About this home

This spacious and charming 5 BR, 2 BA Cape is full of thoughtful updates, making it the perfect place to call home. Set on a corner lot in a quiet, walkable neighborhood just off Newfield Avenue, it offers both comfort and convenience for today's busy families. The beautifully landscaped yard, framed by a classic New England rock wall, is ideal for gardening, playtime, or summer barbecues. A one-car garage plus two versatile lower-level rooms provide ample space for storage, hobbies, or a future playroom. Inside, the inviting layout is designed for everyday living. To the right of the entry, a bright, cozy living room is perfect for movie nights, while the den to the left makes a great game room or reading nook. The updated kitchen flows into the dining area and opens to a spacious three-season porch-easily transformed into a year-round retreat. The main floor also features a full bath, a small bedroom (ideal as a nursery or office), and a generously sized primary bedroom. Upstairs, three more bedrooms and another full bath offer plenty of room for kids, guests, or a growing family. A fully owned solar system keeps electric bills as low as $9.73 per month-a huge savings. The location is unbeatable: stroll to Belltown Park, shops, houses of worship, the Tully Center, or hop on public transportation right on Newfield Avenue. Offered As Is but move-in ready, this home is waiting for its next family to make memories. Schedule your showing today!
