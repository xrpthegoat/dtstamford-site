About this home

Welcome to 100 Winfield Street, a charming Cape Cod-style home in the highly coveted East Norwalk area, ideally located near the Westport line and close to top local amenities. This home offers a rare layout for a Cape, featuring three bedrooms on the upper level-a setup that lives more like a Colonial and is ideal for those who prefer all bedrooms together. With two full bathrooms, one upstairs and one on the main level, the home offers comfort and convenience. Fueled by natural gas, the property provides efficient heating for New England winters. The location is a standout. Enjoy a 10-minute walk to the East Norwalk train station for an easy NYC commute. You are just 7 minutes to Calf Pasture Beach and 6 minutes to Taylor Farm Dog Park. Washington Street in SoNo, known for dining and nightlife, is also 6 minutes away. Everything you need is within a 10 minutes. Located in C zoning, the property may offer potential for conversion to a legal two-family, subject to approvals. A compelling opportunity in one of Norwalk's most convenient neighborhoods.
