About this home

Fabulous new construction. Over 7000 square feet. There are 6 bedrooms and 6 baths plus 2 half baths. The lower level is fully finished with a bath a large bedroom and a second family room. There is a first floor guest suite. The Eat in kitchen is open to the great room with a fireplace and french doors leading out to the large level fully landscaped back yard with a patio. There is a study and a formal dining room with a large butler's pantry. The ceilings throughout the home are double height with vaulted ceilings in the double bedrooms upstairs. The primary bedroom suite has a gas fireplace and large walk in closets. There is a bonus room on the second level with a private deck. There are 2 laundry rooms in the home , one on the second floor and another in the lower level. There is ample storage in the many closets and the kitchen pantry. This home was carefully planned and built to the highest standards. Full house generator, a 3 car garage and a pool site complete the package that is ready for you!
