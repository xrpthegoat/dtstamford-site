About this home

Classic charm, endless potential, and a desirable Glenbrook location. This 3-bedroom, 2-bath Colonial offers nearly 2,000 SF on a level lot at the end of a quiet street. A welcoming front porch, mature landscaping, and detached 2-car garage create exceptional curb appeal. Inside, hardwood floors, abundant natural light, and timeless character complement a functional layout featuring a spacious kitchen, living room, dining room, den, mudroom, and first-floor bedroom and full bath. Upstairs are two bright bedrooms and another full bath, while the finished third level offers flexible space for a home office, playroom, or guest area. Enjoy a patio and wraparound yard, all conveniently located near the train, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes.
