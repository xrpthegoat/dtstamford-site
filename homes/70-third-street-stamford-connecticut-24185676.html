About this home

Location! Location! Location! Welcome to 70 3rd Street, a beautifully maintained and inviting Colonial nestled in a prime Stamford location. This charming home offers 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and 1 half bath, blending timeless character with thoughtful modern updates. Imagine starting your mornings with a cup of coffee on the screened-in porch, beautifully renovated in 2023, overlooking the private backyard. Inside, you'll find warm and comfortable living spaces perfect for everyday living and entertaining. The current owners have invested in numerous improvements, including exterior painting and new shutters (2023), a new fence (2023), and a stunning new hearth (2023). Additional updates include a new sump pump (2022), new paved driveway (2022), new dishwasher (2026), interior painting (2021), attic insulation (2020), and crawl space encapsulation (2020). Major mechanical upgrades, including a high-efficiency gas furnace and central air conditioning installed in 2017. The kitchen was completely remodeled in 2017 and features updated cabinetry, countertops, and appliances, creating a functional and stylish space for cooking and gathering. Conveniently located in the heart of Stamford, this move-in ready home offers walking distance to shopping, dining, parks, transportation, and all the amenities the city has to offer. Don't miss the opportunity to make this charming Colonial your next home.
