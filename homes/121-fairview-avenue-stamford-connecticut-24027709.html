About this home

121 Fairview is a stately 6 bed, 5.5 bath brick colonial on a quiet street in the heart of sought after Shippan Point. The first floor boosts a formal dining room, living room , office , and mudroom.. the updated kitchen opens into a sun-drenched family room with French doors leading to a large private fenced in backyard. The second floor has four bedrooms, all with ensuite bathrooms, including two possible primary bedrooms. The third floor has a fifth full bathroom, with two possible bedroom/office spaces, and a possible gym/play area. The expanded back yard has a charming patio with pergola, perfect for entertaning. The basement was recently repainted and offers plenty of storage, as does the detached two car garage. Very close to Chelsea Piers, Metro-North, and downtown Stamford!
