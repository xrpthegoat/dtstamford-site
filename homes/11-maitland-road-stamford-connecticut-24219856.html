About this home

Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully appointed, this charming Cape Cod is ideally situated in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. Offering a flexible 3-4 bedroom layout and four full bathrooms, the home provides exceptional versatility to accommodate a variety of living arrangements and lifestyles. Outside, enjoy a fully fenced yard perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or play. Recent improvements include a roof just one year old along with a brand-new detached two-car garage. Conveniently located just minutes from shopping, dining, everyday amenities, and major highways, this home offers the perfect blend of classic charm, flexible living space, and accessibility. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy all that Glenbrook has to offer!
