About this home

35 Herman is a newly built home by luxury builder Casa Tua. 6 br 4.2 bath, and 5k+sq ft. The first floor features a modern custom-built white oak kitchen with Viking appliances, a 48-inch refrigerator and range oven, a waterfall quartz 10ft island and backsplash. Open concept living offers dining room and family room with slider access to side yard and stone patio in the serene and private backyard. A great room with a stunning 60-inch fireplace and a gorgeous powder room complete the sun-drenched first level. The second floor features the soundproof primary with 18ft cathedral ceilings, custom-built his and hers closet and a spa bath with heated flooring, large steam shower, smart toilet, and stylish soaking tub. 3 additional br, 2 bath and laundry room with plenty of closets completes the second floor. The third floor has an additional 2 br, and a full bath. The lower level has a 2-car garage, (EV ready), a spacious mudroom with built-ins, a play area/gym and a half bath. Wide white oak flooring throughout, 5 zones, Marvin windows and doors, shiplap pvc siding and battens design, and interior ultra-chic finishes that blend effortlessly. Move in to enjoy Darien's award-winning schools, restaurants, shops, stunning beaches and Great Island!
