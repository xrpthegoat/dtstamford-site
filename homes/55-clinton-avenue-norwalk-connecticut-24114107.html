About this home

Charming Colonial with White Picket Fence and Fabulous Backyard Fall in love with this updated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath colonial farmhouse, nestled behind a classic white picket fence in a convenient Norwalk location. A welcoming front porch sets the tone, leading into a warm and inviting interior with hardwood floors throughout. The spacious eat-in kitchen features stainless steel appliances, Corian countertops, and sliders to an oversized deck-ideal for al fresco dining and entertaining. The backyard is a true highlight: fully fenced, level, and beautifully maintained with plenty of space for play, gardening, or simply relaxing in the sunshine. Whether hosting summer barbecues, creating a firepit lounge, or setting up a play area, this outdoor space is full of possibilities. A separate shed provides great storage for tools, bikes, or hobby gear. Additional features include a separate living room, main-floor laundry, and a walk-up attic for future potential. Located just minutes from shopping, dining, I-95, and public transportation, this delightful home offers the perfect blend of charm, comfort, and convenience. A great starter home or condo alternative-don't miss this one!
