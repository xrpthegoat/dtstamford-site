About this home

Set on level, usable, park-like property in a prime North Stamford neighborhood, this beautiful cul-de-sac of upscale residences is a wonderful backdrop for the delightful Modern home that is ready for you to enjoy. Designed for flexibility & comfort, this spacious home offers room for everyone, incl home office spaces or multi-generational/in-law accommodations. A large driveway w/plenty of parking for entertaining leads to the attached 3-car garage. The elegant entrance opens into dramatic, sun-splashed interiors. Soaring ceilings, oversized windows & hardwood floors add to the refined atmosphere in the living rm w/ fireplace, as well as the holiday-sized dining rm w/ doors to a peaceful deck. The gourmet kitchen has granite counters, a center island, abundant cabinetry/pantry space & stainless steel appliances. The oversized family rm has the 2nd fpl, wet bar & doors to the stone patio. Upstairs, the primary suite has a glamorous flair w/lux bth incl glass shower, dbl vanity, spa tub, priv balcony, dressing rm w/cedar clst, king-size BR & priv sitting rm/ofc. Each additional BR has a priv full bth. Bonus spaces in the bsmt incl poss finishable rec area, workshop, storage. Mudroom leads to garage. Many recent updates incl new boiler, hot water, fuel tank, automatic whole-house generator, fresh paint & more. The gorgeous patio leads to a vast, level backyard - it is difficult to find such a wonderfully usable property. The best of Connecticut living, in a prized location.
