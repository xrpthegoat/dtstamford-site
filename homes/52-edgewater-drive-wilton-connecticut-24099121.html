About this home

Highest and Best due Thursday at 5 pm Set on a hill with a beautiful view at the end of one of South Wilton's most sought-after cul de sacs, this home has great curb appeal and a gorgeous, professionally-landscaped yard. Cook your favorite meal in the beautiful white kitchen with high-end appliances and a sun-filled eating area. There is a large living room with fireplace to entertain or read your next book, and a spacious family room perfect for viewing parties or cozy nights in front of the fireplace. Dine al fresco on the patio or sip cocktails in the outdoor living area. In addition, there is a lower level wine room and rec room to enjoy. At night, retire to your primary suite. Upstairs there are 3 additional bedrooms and a large hall bathroom. The yard has beautiful plantings, a fire pit, and plenty of room to play, with room to install a pool. This home has a side entrance, mud room/laundry room and a 2-car garage. It's move-in ready as the kitchen and baths have been updated, house is painted inside and out, floors are refinished, mechanicals are updated, and windows have been replaced. It's in an extremely convenient location that is close to both schools and town, but still offers privacy. You will love living here!
