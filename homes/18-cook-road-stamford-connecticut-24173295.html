About this home

Wow- amazing price point to get into one of Stamford's best neighborhoods! Charming beach area Cape home just over the Old Greenwich line in the Private Davenport Point Waterfront Assoc. with association docks! This home has been updated and modernized with newer kitchen and baths. Bring your boat/kayak/fishing pole and love for the sea- great family neighborhood with private roads and real sense of community. Walk to Southfield park with tennis courts, playground, sandy beach and waterfront boardwalk with water taxi to Harbor Point. Bike to Old Greenwich village or Tod's Point. Commuter's dream with Stamford & OG train stations less than 5 minutes away. HOA only $650 for the year! How do I know so much about this great neighborhood?
