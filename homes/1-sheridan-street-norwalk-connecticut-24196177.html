About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 3 bedroom, 2 full bath Cape Cod at 1 Sheridan Street. Completely remodeled inside and refreshed outside, this move-in ready home blends modern finishes with classic charm. Step inside to an open, light filled layout featuring a stylish new kitchen with white cabinetry, stainless-steel appliances all brand new, subway tile backsplash, and clean modern counters that flows nicely into the living and dining areas. Hardwood flooring runs through the main living spaces, creating a warm, cohesive feel. Both full bathrooms have been fully renovated with contemporary vanities, stylish tile, and updated fixtures. The exterior has been freshly painted and paired with a brand new driveway, giving the home excellent curb appeal. Enjoy a spacious, level backyard ideal for outdoor living, entertaining, or simply relaxing. Located in a convenient Norwalk neighborhood close to Metro-North, I-95, local shops, restaurants, and the vibrant SoNo scene, this home offers easy commuting and everyday lifestyle amenities. Don't miss this turnkey opportunity schedule your private showing today! Potential to convert to a two-family, subject to town approvals. Buyers to perform their own diligence.
