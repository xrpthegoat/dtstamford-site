About this home

This SUMMER rental is for 6 Weeks starting June 15, 2025 and ending July 31, 2025. The house is also being offered for July 2025 for $9,000. Enjoy nature in a quiet location strolling distance to the beach and Long Shore Golf Course, drive to town, and easy access to the train. Many fine restaurants and fantastic shopping Great area for cyclists, runners, and walkers.
