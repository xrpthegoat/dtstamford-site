About this home

Evoking the charm of an English countryside cottage, this 1936 residence is located within the highly sought-after Hycliff Association in Stamford. Designed with an emphasis on light, flow, and comfortable living, the home offers well-proportioned spaces ideal for both everyday life and entertaining. The main level features a spacious living room filled with natural light, an adjacent all-season room, a chef's kitchen, and an inviting dining area. A convenient laundry room adds functionality. The home includes four bedrooms and three-and-a-half bathrooms, providing flexibility for a variety of living arrangements. The primary suite features a panoramic terrace overlooking the landscaped grounds - a private outdoor retreat ideal for morning coffee or evening relaxation. The professionally designed exterior showcases a tiered backyard inspired by an English garden. Stone pathways connect curated plantings and lawn areas, creating distinct outdoor spaces for entertaining and recreation. A wrap-around rear porch extends the living area outdoors and enhances the home's indoor-outdoor flow. Both the interior and landscaping were thoughtfully updated in 2021, preserving original architectural details while incorporating modern finishes and improvements. Residents of the Hycliff Association enjoy access to a private pool, tennis court, playground, and park within a close-knit residential setting. Conveniently located near schools, shopping, and transportation.
