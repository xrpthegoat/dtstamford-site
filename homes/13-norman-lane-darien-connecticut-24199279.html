About this home

Beautiful 2-Bedroom Condo in Prime Darien Location. Welcome home to this beautifully maintained two-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath condo in a quiet, desirable location at the far end of the complex. Enjoy peace and privacy while remaining conveniently close to downtown Darien, shopping, I-95, and all the area has to offer. The bright, open-concept main level features a spacious living and dining area and a modern kitchen with stainless-steel appliances. New heating and AC system provides improved efficiency and year-round comfort. The large, finished lower level offers flexible additional living space, perfect for a game room, home gym, office, media room, or whatever suits your lifestyle. Additional features include: Attached one-car garage, one additional assigned parking space, ample visitor parking, beautiful community swimming pool and pool house, quiet, private location within the complex. This exceptional rental combines comfort, convenience, and plenty of flexible living space in a terrific Darien location.
