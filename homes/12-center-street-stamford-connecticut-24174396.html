About this home

This is the multifamily property investors, owner-occupants, and business owners have been waiting for. Measuring over 4,000 square feet and being delivered without residents, this fully remodeled, move-in ready property offers an extraordinary combination of flexibility, income potential, lifestyle, and functionality rarely found in Glenbrook. Whether you're searching for a true cash-flowing investment property, a house-hacking opportunity, a multigenerational setup, or the ideal live/work environment, the possibilities here are endless. The property features three separate living spaces, an attached oversized heated two-car garage with oversized bay doors and high ceilings, a private office with half bathroom, a carport, and parking for approximately eight vehicles on a level lot. The centerpiece of the property is the stunning duplex owner's unit - a beautifully renovated 3-bedroom, 2-full-bath residence designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Flooded with natural light, the open-concept layout centers around a tremendous great room featuring a wood-burning stove and double sets of French doors leading to your own private deck. A second private balcony further extends the living space outdoors. The chef's kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, a gas cooktop, abundant counter space, and a large center island with seating, all opening seamlessly into the dining area.
