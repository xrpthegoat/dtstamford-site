About this home

1925 Colonial with the charm and character of yesterday . Updated kitchen & bath located on a tree lined street in Glenbrook less than 1 mile from the Glenbrook Train Station. Entry foyer opens to spacious living room with window seat, hardwood floors and sunroom/office for additional family space, formal dining room has built-in corner cabinet. The updated white kitchen has granite counter tops, stainless appliances and adorable breakfast nook. Front or back entry to stairs leading to the second level with ample closets, even hall cedar closet. Hardwood floors throughout except kitchen and baths, wall sconces sprinkled throughout. Walk up attic for storage and possible additional living space. Unfinished basement, two car garage and lofted storage space with level yard. You will LOVE this adorable home in convenient location with close proximity to I-95 or Merritt Parkway. Don't Wait, see it today!
