About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained end-unit townhouse, ideal for anyone who values both peaceful living and easy access to everything. Set against lush, landscaped grounds, this home provides a serene retreat just minutes from all that Westport and Norwalk have to offer. Enjoy an open floor plan featuring a bright white kitchen with a service bar and all-new stainless steel appliances. The open-concept dining and living area is filled with natural light from glass sliders, and highlighted by brand-new hardwood floors and a wood-burning fireplace. Step out to your private patio, perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. Upstairs, you'll find two cozy bedrooms, including one with a walk-in closet and skylights, plus a shared full bath and in-unit laundry for your convenience. This community offers a resort-style lifestyle with a in ground pool, and is just minutes from:Westport train station, Saugatuck Village, Norwalk & Westport beaches, local shops, restaurants, and entertainment. Enjoy Norwalk schools and amenities while being close to everything Westport has to offer - truly the best of both worlds!
