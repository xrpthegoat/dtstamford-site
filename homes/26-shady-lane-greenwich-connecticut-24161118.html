About this home

Set on a private one-acre corner lot, this stately 1900 Georgian Colonial offers over 7,500 sqft of living space, blending timeless architecture with modern updates. Featuring 9 bedrooms, 8.5 baths, and 8 fireplaces, the home showcases exceptional scale and craftsmanship. Inside, you'll find 12-foot ceilings, intricate millwork, leaded glass windows, and custom built-ins that reflect its historic character. A sweeping central staircase anchors the home, with sun-filled formal living and dining areas that open to a full-length veranda overlooking the private grounds and gated pool. The kitchen and bathrooms have been updated, offering modern functionality while preserving the home's original charm. A cozy den with exposed oak beams and a fireplace adds warmth and contrast to the main level. Upstairs, the primary suite includes a fireplace, dressing area, walk-in closet, and balcony with distant views of Long Island Sound. Additional bedrooms and a spacious third-floor living area provide flexibility for guests or extended living. Located in an R-20 zone with possible subdivision potential, this is a rare opportunity to own a distinctive property that combines historic elegance with long-term upside.
