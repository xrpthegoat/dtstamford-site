About this home

Charming end-unit townhouse offering a bright, comfortable layout just minutes from downtown Stamford. Set along a picturesque row of townhomes with adorable facades, this inviting home benefits from its end-unit location, allowing extra natural light to pour in throughout. The main level features an easy-flow floor plan with a living room, dining area, and kitchen, plus direct access to a private outdoor area-perfect for relaxing or enjoying a little space outside. Upstairs, you'll find two well-proportioned bedrooms and two full baths, providing a functional and comfortable layout. Additional features include gas heat, central air, and one assigned parking space located in the rear. A great opportunity to enjoy townhouse living with convenient access to downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, transportation, and more. No pets. Photos shown are of a similar unit.
