About this home

Mid-City neighborhood near Ridgeway Shopping Center, the updated 4BR Cape Cod is located on a Cul-de-sac built on the .29 Park-like grounds overlooking the scenic Rippowam River. Enjoy nature views from the expansive 2-tier rear deck. An inviting living room has a bow window allowing sunlight to flow to the spacious renovated eat-in kitchen with large dining area, new floors and appliances. abundant counterspace. The original breezeway conversion is now the perfect home office/reading area/den which accesses both front lawn & rear lawns. Two paved driveways allow ample guest parking. A detached storage shed is ideal gardening & lawn furniture. The 1 car front load attached garage offers additional storage space. The adorable Cape Cod with main level primary bedroom option & guest bedroom is a retreat for nature lovers and is centrally located at the Bulls Head intersection (High Ridge & Long Ridge Road) plus quick ride to Ridgeway Shopping Center.
