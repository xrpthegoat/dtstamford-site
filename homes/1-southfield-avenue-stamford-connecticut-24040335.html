About this home

Discover the charm of this end-unit one-bedroom, one-bath condo in the Waterside Green Complex. Perched on the third floor, this unit delivers unparalleled privacy with no neighbors above. The interior features beautiful hardwood floors and is bathed in natural light. The living area and kitchen are enhanced by recessed lighting. The kitchen itself boasts granite countertops, a soft-toned backsplash, and sleek stainless steel appliances. The bathroom is meticulously maintained, and for added convenience, a stackable washer and dryer are included. With popular dining spots, shops, and public transit just moments away, this is an opportunity you don't want to miss. This is a Below Market unit with income restrictions of $180,500 for 1-3 people. Please note, this unit cannot be rented.
