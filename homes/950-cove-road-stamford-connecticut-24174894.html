About this home

Experience the best of the Cove lifestyle in this beautifully updated 1-bedroom, 1-bath condo just moments from Cove Island Park, the beach, and scenic waterfront trails. This move-in ready home features a spacious living and dining area with a wood-burning fireplace and sliders leading to a private balcony. The large bedroom offers abundant closet space and its own second private balcony, creating the perfect indoor-outdoor retreat. Additional highlights include an in-unit washer/dryer, private storage, reserved covered parking, access to a residents' club room/gym, and a very low HOA. Perfect as a primary residence or investment opportunity. Current tenant lease is for $2,450 expires 6/30/26. Close to Chelsea Piers, Metro-North, downtown Stamford, and I-95. No escalation clauses.
