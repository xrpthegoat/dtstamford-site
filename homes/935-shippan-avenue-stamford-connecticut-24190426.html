About this home

Experience the best of coastal living in Stamford's sought-after Shippan WATERFRONT community. This beautifully maintained townhouse offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience just moments from beaches, marinas, parks, downtown Stamford, and Metro-North. Designed for today's lifestyle, the spacious main level features an open-concept living and dining area, a well-appointed kitchen with granite countertops and stainless appliances, and a private balcony. Whether you're seeking a full-time residence or a low-maintenance coastal retreat, this home offers an exceptional opportunity in one of Stamford's most desirable waterfront communities.
