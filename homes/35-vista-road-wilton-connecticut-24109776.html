About this home

Custom-built 2006 residence is thoughtfully designed, impeccably maintained, sited on a flat, picture-perfect 2.42-acre lot directly across from protected preserve land. Over 3,800 sq ft of living space in the main home plus a legal one-bedroom apartment -ideal for guests or supplemental income. Enjoy the bright, open-concept main level which is anchored by a double-height living room, wood-burning fireplace and walls of windows that flood the space with natural light. Chef's kitchen offers granite countertops, SS appliances, center island with seating, opening directly to a breakfast nook and outdoor deck. Formal dining room, home office, and full bath provide both function and flexibility for work and entertaining. Main level primary suite bath with double vanity, soaking tub, and walk-in closets. Upstairs, three additional bedrooms, full bathroom, oversized bonus room; perfect as a gym, media room or playroom. Walk-in attic for added storage or potential expansion. Added features: front and back staircases, mudroom, dedicated laundry area w/ pantry, 4-car garage, generator hook-up and perennial gardens. The grounds are perfect for veggie gardening, bird watching, and enjoying seamless indoor-outdoor living. Legal apartment with living room, kitchen, full bath, bedroom, and private deck. Whether entertaining guests or enjoying peaceful mornings on the manicured grounds, this home blends elegance, comfort, and enduring value in one of Fairfield County's most desirable towns.
