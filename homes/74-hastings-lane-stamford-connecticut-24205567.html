About this home

Sophistication, elegance & privacy define this exceptional Colonial, ideally situated on a sought-after private cul-de-sac. Beautifully secluded yet conveniently located, this four-bedroom, 2 full and 2 half bath home offers timeless charm, thoughtful updates & exceptional outdoor living. The main level features a formal living room, dining room & spacious family room with hardwood floors throughout. The open floor plan is ideal for both everyday living & entertaining. The custom gourmet kitchen, renovated in 2016, features stainless-steel appliances, granite countertops & an oversized center island with seating for six. The stunning primary suite offers serene rear-yard views, renovated en-suite bath with soaking tub, separate shower & double vanity, plus walk-in and additional closet. Three additional bedrooms provide flexibility for family, guests or home office. The lower level adds approximately 991 sq. ft. of versatile living space. Enjoy a private resort-style oasis with meticulously landscaped grounds, mature 30-foot arborvitae, sparkling swimming pool with expansive decking, hot tub, gazebo & fenced garden. Multiple secluded areas are perfect for relaxing or entertaining. A rare opportunity to enjoy privacy, elegance & resort-style living-don't miss this truly special home!
