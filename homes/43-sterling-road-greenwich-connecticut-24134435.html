About this home

Opportunity to acquire an exceptional 6.5 acre Greenwich country compound set amongst very high-end homes in a private, gated association. There are stunning vistas & a bucolic setting w/manicured & mature plantings plus expansive lawns for horses. Amenities include pool & spa, pool house, tennis & basketball courts, spaces for vegetable & flower gardens, carriage house or art studio & office. Masterful blend of timeless architecture with well sited windows that seamlessly integrate the natural landscape. This 9200 square foot home offers high ceilings & spacious formal & informal rooms, which accommodate both intimate family gatherings & grand parties. Enter into a double height grand foyer w/curved metal railed staircase & see marble & wood floors, great wall space for displaying art. First floor offers a large living room with floor to ceiling stone fireplace & a greenhouse area for enjoying the view and sun, formal dining room, first floor primary suite with adjacent office, spacious dine-in gourmet kitchen, family room with fireplace & access to different outdoor patios and decks. The second floor offers another luxe primary suite, several bedrooms & a great bonus room with beamed ceiling for office or game space. Bonus: koi pond with waterfall, vegetable garden area, office spaces, 5 garages & generator. Conveniently located & minutes to schools and shops. Easy drive to downtown Greenwich, highways & train.
