About this home

* highest and best 8pm Tuesday 8/5 * Enjoy the privacy of a single-family home with the ease of HOA living at 10 Twin Ledge Rd #10. This spacious Colonial on a quiet cul-de-sac in the desirable Twin Ledge Association offers over 2,700 sq ft with 3 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and a walk-out lower level for added flexibility. The eat-in kitchen opens to a sunny deck-perfect for entertaining and relaxing-overlooking Oak Hills Golf Course and parkland. Inside, the living room features a gas fireplace and connects to a bay-windowed dining room. A half bath completes the main level. Upstairs, find two bedrooms, a full bath, laundry, and a generous primary suite with walk-in closet and a stunning new en-suite bath (2024) with soaking tub, double vanity, and shower. The finished basement includes a second fireplace, full bath, and walk-out access to the patio and backyard-ideal for a rec room, office, or guest suite. Enjoy multiple outdoor entertaining spaces and all-day sun. Recent upgrades include a new roof (2019), AC system (2020), water heater (2024), Samsung fridge (2023), dishwasher (2024), washer (2020), 10 window replacements, and more. A one-car garage, attic storage, and a quarterly HOA fee covering water, lawn care, snow removal, trash, and more complete the package. Located minutes from Route 1, South Norwalk and Darien trains, and all West Norwalk has to offer-discover easy living without compromise.
