About this home

Nestled at the end of a cul-de-sac in one of Darien's most desirable neighborhoods, 9 Wilson Ridge Road East offers a wonderful opportunity to create a home that's distinctly your own. This four bedroom two and a half bath home has a welcoming floor plan for today's lifestyle. The main level has a living room with a fireplace, a formal dining room and a spacious eat in kitchen with a generous family room. A separate Den offers a private space for work or relaxation while the Sunroom provides an inviting spot to relax and enjoy the outdoors. Upstairs the primary suite is joined by 3 additional bedrooms, hardwood floors throughout the home. A large unfinished basement offers exciting potential for additional living space. A two car attached garage adds everyday convenience. Set on a beautiful half acre lot, this home is just minutes from the Noroton Heights train station, Darien Commons, and Heights Crossing, with restaurants, cafes, shops and daily essentials close by. With its inviting setting and exceptional location, this is a rare opportunity!
