About this home

*Highest and Best Tuesday 5pm* Welcome to a rare opportunity in Norwalk's sought-after Bettswood neighborhood--two parcels for the price of one. This charming cape offers not only a beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 1-bath home, but also the bonus of an adjoining lot at 43 Parkhill Ave, giving you space, privacy, or future potential. The main floor features a sun-drenched living room with fireplace, hardwood floors, a large eat-in kitchen with new appliances, and a cozy sunroom overlooking the backyard. Upstairs, you'll find approximately 800 square feet of high-ceilinged space waiting for your vision--home office, additional bedrooms, studio--you decide. Downstairs, a full unfinished basement provides storage or more expansion potential. The flat lot offers a great yard or future addition footprint. Tucked on a cul-de-sac just moments from Stew Leonard's, Wall Street, the Green, and I-95, this location blends quiet neighborhood living with unbeatable convenience. Opportunities like this don't hit the market often-take the time to imagine what's possible.
