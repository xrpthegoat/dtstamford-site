About this home

Charming 4-bedroom, 2 full bathroom home located in Norwalk's desirable Spring Hill neighborhood. Freshly painted throughout, this well-maintained residence offers a warm and inviting layout highlighted by a stunning floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace with a real wood-burning hearth, perfect for cozy evenings at home. The kitchen features updated appliances, while thoughtful modern upgrades include an electric vehicle charger in the basement. Step outside to enjoy the large, fully fenced backyard, offering ample space for entertaining, gardening, pets, or play. Conveniently located just minutes from I-95, the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, and public transportation. Combining comfort, character, and an unbeatable location, 4 Eagle Road is ready to welcome its next owner home.
