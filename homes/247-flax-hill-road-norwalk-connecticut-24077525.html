About this home

Charming & Sun-Drenched 2-Bedroom Apartment recently painted in the Heart of Norwalk - Ideal for Commuters! Welcome to 247 Flax Hill, where charm meets convenience in the vibrant heart of Norwalk! Just steps from the train station, this bright and airy 2-bedroom apartment is perfect for commuters and anyone who appreciates the energy of a walkable, lively neighborhood. Inside, the sun-filled living area features gleaming hardwood floors, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. The eat-in kitchen is perfect for enjoying your morning coffee.
