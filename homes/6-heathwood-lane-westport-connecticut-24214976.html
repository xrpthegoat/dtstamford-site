About this home

Refreshed 4BR, 2.5BA Colonial on 1.01 private acres down a quiet lane in Westport's Old Hill section, with new paint and refinished floors throughout. The kitchen features wide-plank pine floors, exposed beams, a large quartz island with cooktop and prep sink, new stainless appliances, and a built-in hutch with a second sink. The living room offers a fireplace and built-ins. The family room has soaring ceilings, a fireplace and sliders to the deck. A knotty pine paneled bonus room with a beamed ceiling, wall of built-ins and wet bar works well as a den, study or game room. Upstairs are 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths and laundry. The finished lower level adds office space, extra living space, a workshop and storage. Expansive decks and patios overlook the wooded yard. Central air. Multi-year lease preferred; pets case by case.
