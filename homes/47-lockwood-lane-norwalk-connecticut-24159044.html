About this home

Welcome to 47 Lockwood Lane, a timeless stucco charmer nestled in the sought-after Bettswood neighborhood of East Norwalk. Brimming with character and architectural detail, this inviting home blends classic craftsmanship with thoughtful updates and a warm, welcoming layout. Beautiful hardwood floors flow throughout and a dramatic double-height entry leads to the second floor. A stunning curved staircase with decorative metal railing creates a striking focal point and sets the tone for the home's historic charm. The main floor offers a gracious living room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace, plus a charming den with its own fireplace that offers a wonderful secondary living space-ideal as a playroom or home office. Rounding out the main level is a formal dining room, an updated kitchen with sliders that open to a deck overlooking the backyard, plus a powder room. Upstairs, you'll find three well-proportioned bedrooms with ample closet space, a full bathroom, and a terrace that provides a lovely outdoor escape. The basement offers excellent storage space, laundry, garage access, + room for a home office, gym, or recreation area. Outside, the property features a serene, level backyard with plenty of room for entertaining + gardening. A true commuters dream, this home is conveniently located near the East Norwalk Metro-North station, Stew Leonards, Calf Pasture Beach + town Green.
