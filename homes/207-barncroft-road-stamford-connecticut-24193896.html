About this home

Set on 1.5 picturesque acres in Stamford's desirable Westover neighborhood, this sprawling ranch offers comfortable living surrounded by a pond, and mature perennial gardens. The main level features a spacious eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a formal dining room, a private office, and a light-filled living room with hardwood floors, a fireplace with a custom mantel, and a large bay window overlooking the grounds. The primary bedroom suite includes its own full bathroom, while two additional bedrooms and another full bathroom complete the main level. The finished walkout lower level provides additional living space with a kitchenette and full bathroom, offering flexibility for guests, a potential in-law setup, or everyday use. A large deck overlooking the scenic backyard creates an inviting setting for relaxing and entertaining. Additional highlights include central air, a two-car garage, and a convenient location near shopping, parks, downtown Stamford, Metro-North, and major commuter routes.
