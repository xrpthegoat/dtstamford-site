About this home

Don't miss this exceptional owner-occupied or investment opportunity in Norwalk! This well-maintained two-family home with a finished lower-level in-law apartment offers flexibility for a variety of living arrangements. The second-floor unit features 2 bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, a spacious living room, an eat-in kitchen, and a large deck. The third-floor unit offers 3 bedrooms (one smaller bedroom), 1 full bathroom, a living room, a kitchen, and its own deck. The finished lower-level in-law apartment includes 2 bedrooms, 1 full bathroom, a spacious living room, and a separate walk-out entrance. Enjoy a spacious backyard, an oversized 30' x 12' storage shed, and ample off-street parking. The property is located on a street experiencing continued investment and renovations, offering convenient access to shopping, restaurants, public transportation, and major highways. Seller intends to deliver the property without occupants at closing. The property shares a generously sized driveway with the neighboring property through a recorded easement, providing convenient access for both homes.
