About this home

Welcome to this light and bright 4-bedroom gem nestled in the fabulous waterfront community of Saugatuck Island. This home offers a luxurious retreat just steps away from the community beach, with both comfort and functionality in mind, for an easy living experience. The open and airy living room, featuring a soaring ceiling, seamlessly connects to the dining area and expansive wraparound deck through glass sliders. The kitchen, overlooking the deck and beautifully landscaped gardens, is open to the dining area and will be a chef's dream. The primary bedroom offers lovely water views, while two additional bedrooms with ample storage and a full bath complete the main level. The lower level features a spacious family room for TV viewing or relaxing. Enjoy cozy evenings by the fireplace or step into the lush, fully fenced garden w/stone patio for al fresco dining. The fourth bedroom, full bath, and mudroom/laundry room complete the downstairs. The private yard is an ideal setting for outdoor entertainment and relaxation while enjoying the sea air. There is plenty of space for games and play. This exceptional property offers access to a private beach for Island dwellers, along with opportunities for swimming, kayaking, and neighborhood gatherings. Conveniently located close to downtown and the train station, you will love living in this coastal, water community!
