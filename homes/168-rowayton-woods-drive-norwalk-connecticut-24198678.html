About this home

Sophisticated style and renovated interiors welcome you to this updated three bedroom condo in the desirable Rowayton Woods community! One level living after six entry steps highlights the open floor plan with NEW wood floors, NEW kitchen quartz countertop and SS appliances, NEW BR carpets, NEW washer & dryer, two refreshed bathrooms and freshly painted interiors. Imagine sun-filled spaces throughout the day & quiet moments on your deck enjoying sunset views at dusk. Comfortable lifestyle living in Dining & Living Room areas centered by the wood-burning fireplace and built-in bookshelves to display favorite photos, collected books & decorative items. Prepare memorable meals in the light-filled Kitchen with all-new SS appliances and sizeable quartz counter for casual dining. The Primary BR offers two sets of closets - a deep walk-in closet and another with fitted cabinetry - and a bright, refreshed bath. Two additional bedrooms share the renovated hall bath with beautiful new shower with glass door and herringbone tile. Plenty of closet space within the condo and extra space in the unit's dedicated storage area. Add to this a deeded garage and a reserved parking space! Enjoy the Rowayton Woods amenities including the swimming pool, tennis court and a lush community garden for resident's use. The charming Rowayton Village with great dining, shops and waterfront is just a few minutes away and commuting is easy from nearby trains. So much to love when living in Rowayton Woods!
