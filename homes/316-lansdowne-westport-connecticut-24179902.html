About this home

Enjoy a Country Club life-style in this fully renovated almost 2,000 square foot end-unit townhouse offering Primary & 2nd bedrooms, 2 baths both on the main level. The ante room welcomes you into the light-filled expansive living room w/gleaming floors, vaulted ceiling, custom built-ins & wood-burning fireplace. A separate large dining room, plus den/sun room provides ample space for comfortable living & entertaining. The den flows seamlessly off the living room & sliders invite you onto the private back patio. For ease of entertaining & located between the living & separate large dining room, utilize your built-in bar complete with wine cooler.
