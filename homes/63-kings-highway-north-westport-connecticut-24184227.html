About this home

Welcome home to this light-filled historic four bedroom cape within walking distance to the wonderful town of Westport. The front to back living room boasts a wood burning fireplace for cozy evenings and opens to the dining room for entertaining family and guests. The renovated kitchen will delight the cook in the family. There are four charming bedrooms on the second level. Spend time on the back patio overlooking a large open garden area. Enjoy all that Westport has to offer its' residents, to include exceptional public and private schools. Enjoy the Longshore Golf Course, the Longshore Club Park with three pools and tennis courts. Compo Beach for summer fun. The Westport Playhouse. And a walking riverfront downtown featuring gourmet restaurants and boutique shops. All this within one hour to NYC. Owner prefers to rent the home furnished yearly, but could be available unfurnished for $6,000 per year, or ask about for six months.
