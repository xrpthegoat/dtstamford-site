About this home

Featured in multiple publications and set along New Canaan's coveted Smith Ridge Road, this magazine-worthy, extraordinary estate is a masterclass in privacy, beauty, and timeless luxury. Privately nestled on more than five spectacular acres just moments from town and the New Canaan Country Club, the property offers a lifestyle that feels remarkably tucked away and private. Surrounded by sweeping lawns, curated grounds, and an atmosphere of complete tranquility, it is the kind of place that instantly slows time and is rarely available. Sunlight pours through every room, illuminating interiors that have been meticulously maintained and thoughtfully designed with an uncompromising commitment to quality. From the highest-end designer kitchen to the gracious entertaining spaces, every detail reflects exceptional craftsmanship, sophistication, and enduring style. The primary suite is a destination unto itself-a private retreat defined by sumptuous finishes, spa-like comforts, and an effortless sense of luxury. Generous secondary bedrooms, multiple work-from-home spaces, and a versatile private wing provide flexibility without sacrificing elegance. Whether hosting friends or family or enjoying the quiet beauty of the surroundings, every space has been designed to elevate daily living. Expansive patios, open lawns, and exceptional privacy create a setting that feels more akin to a private resort than a residence.
