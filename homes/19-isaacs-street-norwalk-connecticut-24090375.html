About this home

Welcome to the Phoenix on Isaac, perfectly situated in the vibrant heart of SONO! This stylish 1-bedroom, 1.5-bath condo offers 888 square feet of contemporary living space designed with comfort and flexibility in mind. Step into the spacious, open-concept living room flooded with natural light, seamlessly flowing into the kitchen complete with sleek stainless steel appliances and granite countertops. The generously sized bedroom is a private retreat featuring an en-suite bathroom, ensuring convenience and tranquility. Additionally, the bright and versatile den includes balcony access and a half bath, providing the perfect space for a home office, nursery, or cozy flex room tailored to your lifestyle. Enjoy everyday ease with in-unit laundry featuring a brand-new washer and dryer, 1 private parking spot, ample visitor parking, and community amenities including a fitness room and elevator. Unmatched in convenience, Phoenix on Isaac puts you steps away from the bustling Norwalk Music & Arts District, popular restaurants, boutique shopping, Norwalk Hospital, Mathews Park, Stepping Stones Museum, and the Norwalk Library. Easily accessible to I-95, the Route 7 Connector, SONO's dynamic nightlife, South and East Norwalk train stations, and The SONO Collection mall-this location truly offers the best of urban living.
