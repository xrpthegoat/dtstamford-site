About this home

This beautifully maintained classic Connecticut Colonial blends timeless charm with modern updates throughout. Thoughtfully renovated and expanded by the current owners, the home offers a fresh, contemporary feel ideal for today's lifestyle. Upon entering into the lovely foyer, sunlight floods the dining room, living room, and adjacent, brand new screened in porch. The stunning kitchen family room, renovated by True North, features high-end finishes and a layout ideal for cooking and entertaining. The spacious Family Room was enlarged to include soaring vaulted ceilings and a newly added mudroom-perfect for everyday functionality. On the second floor, the newly added master bedroom suite has soaring ceilings, a dressing room with ensuite bathroom, and a balcony overlooking the lovely grounds. Three additional bedrooms, and one recently renovated large bathroom completes the second floor.. This home is designed for entertaining both indoors and out. Step onto the flagstone terrace, or the over sized screened in porch, and take in the peaceful, flat backyard-an ideal setting for gatherings or quiet relaxation. The 2.2-acre professionally landscaped lot, offers year-round beauty and privacy. The lower level has an additional family room, as well as an unfinished area for storage and laundry. Additional features include central air conditioning (second floor only)
