About this home

Two houses are better than one! Charming renovated cottage sporting a fresh Scandinavian chic interior with new floors, new kitchen, new appliances, updated baths, new lighting, freshly painted inside and out and the list goes on! This secluded oasis has a beautiful in ground heated pool as well as a separate accessory dwelling in the red barn complete with two more bedrooms, kitchen and living room for over night guests, extra family or an amazing home office retreat. Main house has SOLAR so electric bills are virtually non existent. Primary bedroom with decorative fireplace and French doors leading to a stone terrace is on the main level and there is an additional sunroom/office/workout area connected to that room. Beautiful walking neighborhood with access to town trails and conveniently located between both Wilton and Ridgefield town centers. Enjoy a cool drink on one of the stone terraces overlooking the pool and gardens,swing on the hammock nestled among the trees, run in the yard or dip in the pool or simply take a stroll down the country lane. A true country retreat!
