About this home

This spacious designer gem tucked away in a fantastic walk-to-beach location was just completely renovated top to bottom. It features a stunning kitchen with modern appliances, gorgeous spa-like bathrooms, hardwood floors, contemporary light fixtures and an airy, light-filled open layout. The first floor boasts a cozy living room with a fireplace and French doors leading to a private patio with stone walls, ideal for entertaining or relaxing. Upstairs, two generously sized bedrooms, each offering ensuite baths and ample closets, plus a new laundry for added convenience. The finished lower level provides versatile space for a recreation room, gym or office, tailored to your lifestyle; plus added unfinished space for expansion. This Nantucket-style condo with two-car garage is move-in ready and a great sanctuary for discerning buyers. Just a five-minute stroll to the beach and the charming Old Mill market, gorgeous walk to Longshore Country Club, quick access to Metro North railroad, dining, and top-rated schools, this stylishly renovated condo places you at the heart of Westport's vibrant lifestyle. Don't miss this chance to make your Westport dreams a reality!
