About this home

Here is the opportunity to live in Westport that you've been waiting for! This timeless Colonial, nestled on a quiet corner lot in a highly sought-after neighborhood, offers the perfect blend of updates, charm, comfort & functionality.You're welcomed by a bright front-to-back living room with a cozy fireplace, leading into the spacious 1st-floor office.The formal dining room,with 2 sets of pocket doors, provides seamless access to the expansive eat-in kitchen which is a chef's dream--complete with a large center island & double wall ovens.French doors bathe the space in natural light & open onto a lovely stone patio, perfect for outdoor entertaining.Adjacent, the family room with a wood stove insert also opens to the patio.An oversized laundry room & custom screened-in porch with an architecturally designed wood ceiling round out the main floor. The primary suite features an open dressing area, double closets, and beautifully updated bath with heated floors. Three additional generously sized bedrooms share a renovated full bath.The large unfinished basement is a blank canvas, ready to become the perfect media room or playspace. The attached two-car garage boasts a 2-story ceiling high enough for car lifts making it ideal for car collectors.A circular driveway, new stone front steps, and lush landscaping enhance the curb appeal, & the location ensures easy access to every Westport amenity!The automatic generator, city water&sewer,& natural gas heat make this a true find!
