About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST - TUESDAY DECEMBER 16TH at 5PM. Welcome to 57 Creeping Hemlock - where thoughtful updates and flexible living space create an inviting home in the lovely Cranbury section of Norwalk. This 3 bedroom, 2 bath home offers 1,691 square feet of comfortable, light filled living on a private third acre lot. The remodeled kitchen features stainless steel appliances with an island for casual dining, while the living room complete with brick fireplace and a slider to a generous screened porch which invites seasonal relaxation and a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. The main level features a spacious family room with a gas stove that provides the option for a first floor primary bedroom. Upstairs, 3 carpeted bedrooms (including a office/flex room) ideal for remote work, guests or hobbies. Outside a rear deck and fully fenced yard provide the perfect backdrop for outdoor dining, weekend entertaining or quiet mornings with coffee in hand. Ideally located with easy access to the Merritt 7 train station (less than 1 mile), Merritt Parkway, Route 7, shopping and dining. This home balances privacy and convenience beautifully.
