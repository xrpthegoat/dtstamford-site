About this home

Please note - Offer deadline Sunday, 4/13 by 6pm. WOW! Idyllic 1-acre property offering privacy and tranquility in the heart of highly coveted Cranbury neighborhood! Enjoy the best of both worlds in this updated, chic 4 BR 2.5 BA Colonial - A serene, nature-inspired setting, yet convenient location - minutes to shopping, beaches, parks, trains, and highways. Over $80K in seller upgrades! Tucked away on a lovely street, bordering Westport, this spacious home has beautiful interiors with terrific entertaining flow and filled with an abundance of natural light and vistas from every window. Gorgeous, refinished hardwood floors throughout, Central Air, and updated features make this a MOVE-IN READY, fun-filled home. Enjoy watching waterfowl from your rear deck or windows, sip wine by a fire pit OR enjoy the therapeutic benefits of your new, custom built, wood-fired cedar hot tub! Inside, you'll find delightful, versatile spaces including a fully updated, sunny Kitchen with quartz countertops and adorable breakfast nook, spacious Living and Dining Rooms, a Powder Room PLUS Office/Den/Play room off the kitchen. Just a few steps up to an enormous 24'x24' Family Room w/new wood burning stove and plenty of room for games, TV viewing and more. All 4 BRs are on the 2nd level, plus a hall Laundry and 2 full Baths (primary Bath is ensuite). The huge walk-out basement is dry and spacious. Two car garage and a nice, one-acre lot offers privacy and plenty of room for sports and outdoor fun.e
