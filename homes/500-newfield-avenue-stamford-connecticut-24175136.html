About this home

Very large, bright, highly desirable top floor 2BR unit in the heart of Newfield area, just a few minutes to downtown Stamford. Enter to a foyer with a powder room and 2 large closets. Foyer leads to a huge living room and dining area with beautiful hardwood floors and a nice private terrace. Kitchen with dishwasher & breakfast bar, tile floors, and pantry. Open floor plan with terrific flow for entertaining. Two nicely sized bedrooms also with hardwood floors. Excellent closet space/ storage. ACs in every room. Elevator building, on-site super, coin-op laundry on every floor. Walk to Shops/ Restaurants/ Park/ Playground. On bus line. One off-street assigned parking space included with rent. Second space $50 additional fee per month (limited availability.) Cat considered with one time, non-refundable $250 pet rent per cat. Excellent credit and proof of income required.
