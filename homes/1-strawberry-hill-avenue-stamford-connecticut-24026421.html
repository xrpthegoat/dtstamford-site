About this home

Fabulous opportunity to live in one of Stamford's most sought-after luxury buildings. This is a FURNISHED OR UNFURNISHED RENTAL at One Strawberry Hill Avenue with a 24-hour doorman to greet you and your guests and hold your packages, a beautifully furnished lobby, library, two exercise rooms, and a fabulous, heated pool (84 degrees today). This apartment has two good sized bedrooms, two full marble baths, dining area off the kitchen and a large living room with sliders to your own western facing balcony. You'll see amazing sunsets. A full-size washer and dryer are in the unit. One garage parking space comes with the apartment and a 2nd space can be rented. There is also ample parking for your visitors. The building is located just steps from the heart of downtown Stamford, offering great restaurants, shopping, library, a seasonal farmers market and the train. A short drive will take you to the Merrit Parkway, I95 and Stamford beaches. The building's recent updates include a new roof, newer paint and carpet on the hallways of all floors, new windows and sliders and new decking around the pool.
