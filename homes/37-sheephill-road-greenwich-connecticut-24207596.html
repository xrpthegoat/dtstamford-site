About this home

Pristine, meticulously maintained townhouse in a small, private, sought-after Riverside community with an inground pool. Backing to serene trees, this very bright home features hardwood floors throughout. The updated kitchen offers granite counters and a cozy banquette breakfast nook, complemented by a formal dining room. The first-floor deck overlooks the trees - perfect for morning coffee or evening relaxation. Three light, spacious bedrooms include a primary with tranquil tree views. A bonus dormered and finished third floor with closets makes an ideal fourth bedroom, home office, or playroom. The finished, heated walk-out lower level is carpeted with a full bath and opens to a private back patio. One garage parking space plus one outdoor space included. Conveniently close to the Riverside train station, schools, shopping, and all that Riverside has to offer. Available immediately in one of Riverside's most desirable locations. Cats are allowed. HOA does not allow dogs or other animals. Tenant pays heat and electric. Garage #2 belongs to unit & has auto remote. Pool is close to unit.
