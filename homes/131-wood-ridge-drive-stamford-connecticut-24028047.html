About this home

Welcome to the mid ridges one of Stamford's best family friendly neighborhood! This lovely home offers a cozy living room with high ceiling, Eat in Kitchen with stainless steel appliances and a large dining room for family gatherings. Four bedrooms, master suite with attached bath and hardwood floors and 2 more bedrooms on the upper level. The 4th bedroom in the lower level can be used as an office or guest bedroom. The large family room leads into the patio and a beautiful private backyard where you can enjoy summer barbeques and family picnics. Close to shopping, restaurants, public transportation and the Merritt Parkway and much, much more.
