About this home

Nestled on the Old Greenwich/Stamford border, this pristine 3 bedroom, 2.1 bath Avon model offers an unmatched blend of luxury, style, and comfort. Every bedroom features spacious walk-in closets, while custom touches elevate this home to exceptional standards. Step inside to find an elegant powder room adorned with wainscoting and rich plantation shutters throughout. The mudroom/laundry room boasts custom cabinetry, including a convenient wine storage area. At the heart of the home is a contemporary gourmet kitchen, complete with a Wolf stove, large leathered granite bar, and a formal dining space-perfect for entertaining. The primary suite is a true retreat with a tray ceiling, dual walk-in closets, and an opulent ensuite bath featuring double sinks, a whirlpool tub, and an oversized shower. Hardwood flooring graces the first two levels, adding warmth and sophistication. The finished lower level offers a sleek, modern aesthetic with new laminate flooring, providing endless possibilities for a family room, play area, home gym, or studio, and is fully plumbed for an additional bathroom-ideal for future guests. Enjoy your private outdoor oasis on the expansive patio, perfect for al fresco dining or quiet relaxation. Complete with a 2-car garage, this home offers convenience and elegance in equal measure. Living at Palmer Hill means access to top-tier amenities, including a clubhouse, pool, hot tub, fire pit, gym with a yoga room, and a business center.
