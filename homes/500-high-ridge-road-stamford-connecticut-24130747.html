About this home

Nicely maintained home in immaculate condition. Lovely grounds, stone work and plantings. Open floor plan with the living room, dining room and kitchen. The kitchen is light and bright with white cabinets, stainless appliances, and a breakfast bar. The door leads to the private backyard with 2 patios. The master bedroom on the first floor and is set up like a den. There is an 2nd first floor bedroom as well. The bathroom has a pedestal sink and white tub . The second floor has a 3rd bedroom, office and full bath. There is a finished rec room in the lower level. A large garage and central air complete this wonderful home in a most convenient location.
