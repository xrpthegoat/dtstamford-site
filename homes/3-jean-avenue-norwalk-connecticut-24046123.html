About this home

Welcome to this charming colonial house featuring 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths, and an attached 2-car garage, all set on a large, private lot with an expansive backyard and spacious deck, ideal for outdoor relaxation. The master suite boasts a generous walk-in closet, while abundant natural light fills every corner of the home, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Enjoy the convenience of easy access from the garage to the house through a breezeway. A working fireplace adds cozy charm, making this home perfect for those looking to add their own personal touches and style. Located close to major highways for easy commuting, yet set in a peaceful and quiet neighborhood, this home offers the perfect blend of serenity and accessibility.
