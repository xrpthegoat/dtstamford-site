About this home

Renovated 3-bedroom midcentury home in a great Westport location! Updated: kitchen, bathrooms, hardwood flooring, AC, water heater, generator and septic system. Open plan kitchen/living space with vaulted ceiling and skylights. Stainless steel appliances with center island is framed by chic blue cabinets. Additional living space includes a bright playroom/family room with dry bar and a home office with picture window, which could also serve as a sleeping area with an updated full bathroom with shower. Upstairs, 3 bedrooms are served by another updated bathroom. Outdoor space includes a screened porch for summer nights, sliders leading to a deck overlooking the 1-acre property and room to walk and bike on a flat, 1/2 mile cul de sac. One car garage, separate laundry room, pull-down attic and whole-house generator complete the home. Convenient location: walk to Wakeman Town Farm for your weekend veggies and then on to the playing fields to kick around the ball or stretch your legs. Grab a latte and a scone on your way home at Gruel Brittania once they open at the Christie's location. Close to the award-winning Staples High School. Landlord covers landscaping. Pets considered. Excellent credit a must, application through RentSpree. Showings start June 9th.
