About this home

Charming Renovated Home with Separate Income Potential - Minutes to Train & Shopping! Don't miss this rare opportunity to own a beautifully renovated home and cottage in the heart of Darien. This move-in ready home features 3 spacious bedrooms, 2 full baths, whole house generator and has been thoughtfully updated throughout creating a turnkey retreat. Renovation list available at the property. Set on two individually taxed plots sold together (.12 and .11 acres), 19 William Street and 19 Henry Street. This property is a serene oasis complete with stunning gardens, professional landscaping and a detached 2-car garage. An added bonus: a fully equipped 808sf 2 bedroom, 1 bath private auxiliary cottage on the property, currently tenant occupied, perfect for guests, extended family, or rental income. Enjoy coastal living with proximity to Darien's beaches, parks, and the vibrant Noroton Heights revitalization - just steps from shops, restaurants, and the train making commuting a breeze.
