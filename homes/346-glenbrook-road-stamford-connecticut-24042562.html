About this home

Nicely maintained colonial with an open floor loaded with charm and character. Great location next to St. Maurice church. Spacious living room with a wood burning stove and hardwood floors and an extra reading nook. Formal dining room with hardwood floors open to the living room and kitchen. Updated kitchen with granite counter tops and breakfast bar with a gas range plus a 5 burner electric cooktop. The cozy family room has a wood burning stove and french doors to the lovely backyard. Large laundry room with cabinets, refrigerator and wine cooler. A powder room completes the first floor. The 2nd floor has a spacious master with a walk in closet and bath. There are two other bedrooms plus an updated full bath. The 3rd floor has the 4th bedroom. A beautiful private fenced in back yard is another big benefit to this fine home located in a most convenient location close to shops, restaurants, I-95 and Glenbrook train station.
