About this home

Welcome to this charming 3/4 bedroom home nestled in the sought-after Riverside Acres neighborhood. The heart of the home, a newly updated kitchen, seamlessly connects to a spacious living room with a cozy fireplace and a formal dining room. The adjacent family room offers additional living space, perfect for relaxation or entertaining. Beautiful hardwood floors span the entirety of the first floor, adding a touch of warmth. The second level features three well-appointed bedrooms and two updated bathrooms.. The lower level boasts an additional 733 sq.ft. as a versatile family room, an extra guest bedroom, a laundry area, and direct access to a spacious two-car garage. An outdoor shed is currently being used as a home office. Fabulous outdoor patio. Steps to the private lake beach, close to school and parks. Turn-key.
