About this home

Envision your next chapter at 19 Woodchuck Court, a beautifully maintained Colonial at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Norwalk's desirable Flax Hill neighborhood. Ideally located just minutes from the Darien and Rowayton borders, this home offers the perfect blend of privacy and accessibility-close to I-95, Metro-North stations, Long Island Sound, and Norwalk's many shops, restaurants, and amenities. Inside, natural light, hardwood floors, and central air create a warm and inviting atmosphere. The living room features a large bay window and cozy fireplace, opening to an adjacent dining area. The eat-in kitchen is ideal for entertaining, complete with white cabinetry, granite countertops and a sliding glass door leading to a spacious patio for easy indoor-outdoor living. The main level includes a vaulted-ceiling family room with fireplace, a half bathroom, three bedrooms, one full bathroom, and a laundry area with direct access to the attached two-car garage. Upstairs, three additional bedrooms and two full bathrooms provide ample space for family or guests. A finished lower level offers versatility for recreation, fitness, or a media room, with plenty of storage in the unfinished area. The private backyard is a true retreat, featuring an in-ground vinyl pool, patio, landscaped gardens, and cabana or shed. This move-in-ready home combines comfort, functionality, and timeless charm.
