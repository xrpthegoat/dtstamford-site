About this home

Welcome to the Mill River House, ideally located in the heart of Downtown Stamford, just steps from restaurants, shopping, entertainment, and all the city has to offer. This exceptional three bedroom residence offers a rare private entrance from the sidewalk via a private staircase, while also providing access through the building's elegant main lobby with doorman service or directly from the gated parking garage. The first level of the building features commercial office space, while this residence is privately situated above. Enter into a spacious open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area highlighted by gleaming hardwood floors, oversized windows, high ceilings, and abundant natural light. Sliding glass doors lead to a large private patio, elevated well above the street, an ideal space for outdoor dining, entertaining, or relaxing. The well-appointed kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a generous center island. Just off the living room, the primary suite offers direct access to the private patio, a walk-in closet, and a full bath. Located in a separate wing for added privacy, two additional spacious bedrooms include one with a walk-in closet, along with a full hall bath and convenient laundry room. Mill River House offers an array of amenities, including a doorman, meeting room, full fitness center located directly across the hall from the unit, and a beautifully landscaped center courtyard with community grills.
