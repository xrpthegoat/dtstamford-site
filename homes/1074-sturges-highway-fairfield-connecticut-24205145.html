About this home

A rare Greenfield Hill find, 1074 Sturges Highway pairs the presence of a classic Colonial with a sleek, thoroughly modern point of view. Thoughtfully reimagined, the home retains its original craftsmanship while introducing clean-lined updates and a minimalist sensibility that feels chic, current and effortless. Inside, the balance between old and new is masterfully struck. Original millwork and architectural details are paired with elegantly edited finishes, creating spaces that feel sophisticated without ever tipping into formal. The layout flows naturally for everyday living, with room to gather, entertain and simply enjoy the house. Set on over 2 acres of impeccably manicured and extremely private grounds, the property offers a rare sense of seclusion. A gorgeous patio with fireplace extends the living space outdoors, setting the scene for summer dinners, cocktails and fireside evenings. A newly constructed garage with loft above adds flexibility for a home office, studio, gym or creative retreat. Where classic architecture meets a fully modern point of view, 1074 Sturges Highway is a masterclass in understated sophistication - Greenfield Hill living, reimagined. Fairfield Schools: Dwight, Ludlow, Ludlow.
