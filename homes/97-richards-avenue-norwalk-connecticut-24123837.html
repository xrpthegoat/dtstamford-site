About this home

Welcome to 97 Richards Avenue #D5! This ranch-style, third-floor unit has it all. Walk into your entryway complete with a huge coat closet and a laundry closet that houses the washer and dryer. The large, open-concept living and dining room make entertaining and everyday-living a breeze and feature wall to wall carpeting and two sets of sliders that lead out to the spacious and secluded balcony - perfect for enjoying those fall nights and admiring the natural landscape. The eat-in kitchen features ample cabinet space, granite counters, subway tile backsplash, and stainless steel appliances - including a brand new dishwasher and garbage disposal! The primary suite offers everything you need with a generously-sized walk-in closet and ensuite bathroom equipped with a full tub and shower. In addition, there is another great-sized bedroom with a large closet and a second full bathroom for guests. This unit has been lovingly maintained and updated and allows you to move right in! This unit comes with two assigned parking spaces (#37 and #137), a private storage space in the complex, and owners get full access of the recently updated in-ground pool throughout the summer! THIS SALE IS CONTINGENT UPON THE SELLERS FINDING SUITABLE HOUSING. Please note that the monthly common charges are $525 and that there are two special assessments currently running- $196 for bridge repairs and $199 for capital improvements - which brings monthly cost to $880. BOTH SPECIAL ASSESSMENTS END IN DECEMBER
