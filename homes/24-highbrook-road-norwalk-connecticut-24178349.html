About this home

Experience the perfect blend of mid-century character and modern comfort in this beautifully restored 1940s custom Ranch. Nestled on a quiet, in-town street , this 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home features striking architectural details like tray ceilings and original brass hardware. The main level offers a bright layout with a master bedroom w bathroom , an inviting living room anchored by a classic fireplace , a formal dining room , and a fully equipped kitchen featuring a refrigerator, stove, and dishwasher. You can also enjoy year-round peace of mind with a newer roof, central air conditioning, and an updated, energy-efficient natural gas furnace. What truly sets this home apart is its exceptional indoor and outdoor spaces. The lower level features an enormous, fully finished recreation room, providing endless possibilities for a home theater, gym, or playroom. Step outside to relax on your huge, private three-season porch or enjoy the outdoors on a secluded back deck overlooking a lovely 0.19-acre lot. Complete with an attached garage , and an in-unit washer and dryer , this home sits in an ideal neighborhood served by Tracy Elementary, West Rocks Middle, and Norwalk High School.
