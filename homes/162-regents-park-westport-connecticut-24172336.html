About this home

Great opportunity if you want to get to know Westport before purchasing your new home. This rental is available for 8 months, mid-September through May 5, 2027. Perhaps you are planning a construction project this fall and need to get out of the house during renovation. This updated, professionally decorated, fully furnished townhouse could be your solution. It's a bright end unit with lots of light and an open living/dining room area. A lovely patio is accessible through both the living room and dining room areas. The eat-in Beverly Ellsley custom kitchen has exquisite built-ins, rich wood counters, new double ovens and new dishwasher. Upstairs both the primary and secondary bedroom are en-suite. The lower level offers a movie viewing room with a full bath and wet bar. Regents Park is a well-known gated townhome community in the Greens Farms area of Westport giving you easy access to downtown shopping, the train to NYC, and the beaches. It offers a security guard, Har tru tennis court with lights for evening play, and pickleball. Give Westport a try, you won't regret it!
