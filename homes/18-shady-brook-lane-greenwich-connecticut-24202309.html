About this home

Beautifully renovated by the current owners, this 5 bedroom, 5 bath home features refinished hardwood floors throughout, a modern open floor plan with eat-in kitchen/dining that opens to a generous new deck overlooking the level yard, and a versatile main-floor bedroom with a full bathroom that adds flexibility for guests, family, or working from home. The 2nd floor offers 3 well-proportioned bedrooms (one en-suite) and a hall bathroom, a few steps up, a primary bedroom/primary bath, luxurious walk-in-closet, and ample storage. The walk-out Lower Level is fully finished with a playroom, rec room, full bath and den/gym, separate laundry room and lots of storage. Located in a quiet Old Greenwich neighborhood no car needed to get to Binney Park, Perrot Library, Metro North and OG Town.
