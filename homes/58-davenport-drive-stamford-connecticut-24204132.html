About this home

Welcome to 58 Davenport Drive, a beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home located in the sought-after Davenport Point Association, a private waterfront neighborhood with private roads and neighborhood docks offering convenient access to Long Island Sound. The updated kitchen features quartz countertops with matching backsplash, a large peninsula with seating, Thermador built-in refrigerator, 6-burner gas range, and Bosch dishwasher. The kitchen flows into the dining area and sunroom overlooking the backyard and pool. The spacious primary suite includes patio access with steps to the pool, a walk-in closet, laundry area, and ensuite bath. Enjoy a private backyard with a heated saltwater gunite pool and spa, plus a new pool patio completed last summer. Walking distance to Boccuzzi Park, the beach, waterfront boardwalk, Prime, Hinckley Yachts, and neighborhood docks, with easy access to Stamford and Old Greenwich train stations.
