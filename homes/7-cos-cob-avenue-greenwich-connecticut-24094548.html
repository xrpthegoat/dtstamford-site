About this home

Just completed total re-construction of a 3-bedroom, 2-bath first floor unit of a two-family home in the heart of Cos Cob . Walking distance to Cos Cob Elementary School, local parks and Cos Cob town center offering a full range of dining, shopping and everyday services, NEW EVERYTHING! Leave your car behind and meet a friend for coffee and croissant. Or meet family or friends for lunch at IlPastaficio known for artisanal pasta, salads and antipasti. Or, meet for dinner at the newly opened LaBistro which is receiving rave reviews. If you are in a rush, grab a great pizza at Arcurio's pizzeria or seafood at Fjord Fish Markwet. The home has an open and airy layout features new floors, wall board and high-end finishes throughout. New kitchen with custom cabinets, quartz countertops. Spacious family room with new, modern fireplace opening to a professionally landscaped yard. Lux bathrooms with heated floors and designer tile and fixtures. Separate laundry room with washer and dryer. Brand new HVAC, plumbing and electrical systems. A perfect blend of comfort, convenience and modern living.
