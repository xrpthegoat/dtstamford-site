About this home

Direct waterfront location in the desirable East Norwalk beach community. Enjoy panoramic water views, captivating sunsets, and gentle summer breezes from your expansive balcony, accessed from the living room/dining room and primary bedroom sliders (new 2021) overlooking L.I. Come live and enjoy all that waterfront living and the beach area of East Norwalk has to offer. It's a wonderful lifestyle!
