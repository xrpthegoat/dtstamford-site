About this home

Fall in love with the former home of Robert M Jones, American graphic designer and printmaker. This easy living 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath colonial awaits its new owners. Welcoming you home, is this beautiful front porch where you can sit and enjoy some quiet time or visit with a neighbor. Once inside, you can cozy up to a roaring fire in the living room while you watch your favorite football team or stream your favorite show. This low traffic street holds its own privacy. Your private, fully fenced in back yard is perfect for enjoying quiet mornings and serene ambiance with the water fountain. Entertaining is a wonderful experience by the fire pit. Ever thought of growing your own veggies? Well, you have plenty of room for that as well! The outdoor building allows for plenty of storage space for bicycles, lawn mower or perhaps even a man cave/she shed. The possibilities are limitless! There is also a wonderful room in the lower level with lots of natural sunlight. It's great for a TV room, exercise room or game room just to name a few. You are minutes away from the downtown area and shopping with easy access to Metro North and I-95. What are you waiting for?
