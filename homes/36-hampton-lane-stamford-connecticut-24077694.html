About this home

Welcome to 36 Hampton Lane, a picture perfect house that is totally updated with an open floor plan at the end of the cul-de-sac. Spacious and bright expansive living room, dining room and brand new kitchen with granite counter tops & stainless steel appliances. The full bath is nicely updated and has easy access for guests right off the kitchen. The bedrooms have great closet space. The master has a walk in closet and a tastefully updated bath. There is a beautiful trex deck with a nice yard. There is gas that goes to the grill which is a nice plus. The lower level has a family room with a fireplace and and engineered hardwood floors. There is a large office that could be a 4th bedroom. Laundry, pantry and newer half bath complete the lower level. Central air, new roof, and siding in 2022 are a few more features of this wonderful home with city water. Sewer lines are currently being put into the street.
