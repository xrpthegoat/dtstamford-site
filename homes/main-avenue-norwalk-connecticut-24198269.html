About this home

Excellent opportunity to acquire a nail salon business in a highly desirable and convenient Norwalk location along Main Avenue, a well-trafficked retail corridor surrounded by established office buildings, retail, restaurants, markets, luxury condominiums, and other professional businesses. The salon is conveniently located within a shopping center with parking available both directly in front of the building and in the rear, providing easy access for customers and employees. The surrounding mix of residential, commercial, and office properties creates a strong customer base and excellent potential for continued business activity. The property offers convenient highway access and is strategically positioned for commuters and customers traveling throughout Fairfield County and beyond. Its proximity to New York City is an additional advantage, with Flushing, Queens approximately 50 minutes away, depending on traffic. A great opportunity for an owner-operator, investor, or beauty professional looking to establish or expand a business in a strong commercial location.
