About this home

Prime end-unit one-bedroom condo in the prestigious Wilton Crest community featuring a bright living room with private balcony overlooking beautifully landscaped grounds. This well-maintained unit offers enhanced privacy and natural light from its desirable end position, plus the convenience of a private garage with additional parking space directly behind. Residents enjoy resort-style amenities including swimming pool and tennis court, while the walkable location provides easy access to downtown Wilton and scenic Schenks Isle. Additional features include secure basement storage unit, in-building laundry facilities, and professional management. Perfect for those seeking low-maintenance living in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after communities with excellent commuter access and charming small-town atmosphere. Garage is C5.
