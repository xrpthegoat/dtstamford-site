About this home

Home Sweet Home in desirable Cranbury! This first-time listed, well-maintained raised ranch is the perfect place to make your own! Open living room/dining area with access to large deck. Eat-In Kitchen has updated stainless steel appliance, pantry and a breakfast nook. Pull-down attic provides plenty of storage. Four large bedrooms and a full bath complete the main level! Lower level can be used as a family room with fireplace and a home office/bedroom, or as an In-Law suite, and includes a half bath. Lower level gives direct access to the 2-car garage and laundry room. Situated at the end of a cul-de-sac, buyers will love this quiet, peaceful neighborhood! Large, portable generator is included in sale of house! Also included is an outdoor outlet for above-ground pool. Don't miss this incredible opportunity!!
