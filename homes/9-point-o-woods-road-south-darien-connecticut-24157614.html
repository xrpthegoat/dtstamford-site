About this home

Located on a quiet cul-de-sac in Darien's sought-after Ox Ridge School district, this inviting 4 bedroom home offers timeless character, privacy, and versatile living. Sun-filled living spaces and a well-designed floor plan enhance everyday comfort while maintaining the home's character. The primary bedroom features a fireplace, creating a warm and inviting retreat, complemented by three additional bedrooms thoughtfully arranged for family or guests. Adding to the home's versatility is a 2-bedroom guest cottage, connected to the main house by a breezeway-ideal for extended family, visiting guests, au pair living, or a private home office. Outside, beautifully landscaped, park-like grounds surround a heated pool and patio, providing the perfect setting for relaxation and entertaining. A rare opportunity in one of Darien's most desirable neighborhoods.
