About this home

Homes with this much character don't come along often. From the moment you step onto the stunning bluestone covered front porch, you'll understand why homes like this are so rarely found and so fiercely loved. Built in 1939, this enchanting 4 Bed/3 Bath carries the kind of craftsmanship, character, and soul that simply cannot be replicated in today's construction. Thoughtfully arranged within a cozy 1,864-square-foot footprint, every inch of this home has been put to work. There's nothing wasted, nothing superfluous - just warm, efficient living spaces that feel intimate. One of the upstairs bedrooms opens directly onto a private upper porch, a rare and romantic feature perfect for morning coffee, evening stargazing, or simply stealing a quiet moment above the world. Out back, a lovely patio awaits, an ideal setting for summer gatherings, container gardens, or a peaceful retreat at the end of the day. And the location? Practically unbeatable. Stroll to the historic Cannondale Village and train station, making this an exceptional find for commuters or weekenders who want the serenity of a classic New England neighborhood without sacrificing connectivity to the city. Yes, this home has history and opportunity. Potential to expand above the garage. The bones are solid, the charm is undeniable, and the potential to love it as is or transform this gem into a showpiece is absolutely there. Come see what makes this place shine.
