About this home

Nestled on a picturesque property with protected open space directly across the street, 31 Vista Road offers privacy, charm, and flexibility in a convenient Wilton location just minutes to both Wilton Center and Ridgefield's vibrant shops and restaurants. Lovingly maintained, this inviting home blends timeless character with everyday comfort, featuring hardwood floors, exposed beams, a welcoming family room with fireplace, and an open dining area ideal for gathering. The eat-in kitchen overlooks the peaceful backyard and showcases its original vintage 1961 countertops alongside stainless steel appliances and abundant cabinetry. The **second-floor addition, completed in 2008,** added two spacious bedrooms, a full bath with a thoughtfully designed separate vanity and shower/WC layout, and a generous den connected by a spiral staircase to a finished 27' x 15' loft with standing headroom-ideal for recreation, hobbies, or storage. Surrounded by mature trees and level lawn, this special property offers the perfect balance of nature, convenience, and classic New England charm.
