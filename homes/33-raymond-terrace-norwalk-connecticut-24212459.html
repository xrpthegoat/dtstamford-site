About this home

Attention Metro North Train Commuters!! 3 Blocks from East Norwalk Train Station in a great neighborhood and a less than a five minute drive to Calf Pasture beach. Beautiful updated cape featuring new blue stone front walkway/ patio. Inside has hardwood floors throughout out. Cozy family room with fireplace, additional sitting room leads into upgraded kitchen with stainless steel appliances. Walk out to a larger Trex composite deck overlooking a huge newly fenced in level back yard with fire pit. additional fenced in garden patch, rose bushes, plated hydrangeas and large ornamental sea grass plants aligning the back of the lawn. Amazing sun lit space for relaxing and entertain. Central air through-out Second floor features expansive master bedroom with half bath, lower level has two additional bedrooms and full bath. Neighborhood is great with annual block parties, movie nights and Holiday events. Make this house the next place to call home. Off street parking. Attached Garage for one car and a drive way which fits two cars.
