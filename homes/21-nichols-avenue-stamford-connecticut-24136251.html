About this home

Wonderful opportunity to own this charming 3-bedroom, 1-bath Craftsman-style home in Stamford's desirable Mid-Ridges neighborhood. This move-in ready, single-level residence offers an open layout with beautiful hardwood floors throughout. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances and direct access to a private stone patio-ideal for entertaining. The spacious living and dining areas provide great flow and natural light. Three well-sized bedrooms with ample closet space and a full bath with tub/shower complete the main level. The large basement, equipped with a 4-year-old boiler for heat and hot water, offers generous storage space. A fully enclosed glass front porch adds additional year-round living area. The landscaped backyard with flagstone patio creates a peaceful setting for relaxing or gatherings. Recent updates include central air conditioning, a newer heating system, and newer windows. Conveniently located near downtown Stamford, parks, I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and public transportation. Please schedule your private viewing before it's gone!
