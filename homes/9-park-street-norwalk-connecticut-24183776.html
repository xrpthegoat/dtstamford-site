About this home

Desirable ranch styled in Park Towers Condominium Association, located on the beautiful historic town green which frequently boasts festivals, and concerts in the summer. This unit, with the largest floorplan (1,056 SF) in the association features a large and spacious Living/Dining area with Tile & Vinyl Flooring throughout, 2 bedrooms & 1 full bath; Bedrooms provide ample closet space. The Kitchen is bright with new Stove and Refrigerator coming soon. In Wall AC Units in each room. This is a well-managed complex with an active HOA and management. Plenty of parking for residents and guests, as well as a parking garage under the building for an additional $55.00 (currently there is a wait list). Building offers an elevator and laundry on site! A great location for commuters, easy access to I95, Merrit Parkway, East Norwalk Train Station and bus routes. Conveniently located and close to SoNo restaurants, nightlife, schools, and excellent shopping at the new SoNo mall. Just a quick walk or short drive to all Norwalk has to offer. Sorry No Pets and No Smoking, Security Deposit and Credit Check (with a credit score of 730 or higher) required.
