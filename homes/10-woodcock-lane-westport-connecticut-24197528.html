About this home

Nestled on a peaceful cul-de-sac in Westport, this inviting ranch-style home offers the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience. Set on over an acre of beautifully landscaped property, this spacious updated and renovated 3-bedroom, 2-bath rental features bright, sun-filled living spaces, generous room sizes, and an easy one-level floor plan ideal for everyday living. The finished lower level provides exceptional flexibility with a large recreation room, laundry area, a separate room that is ideal for a home office or guest space, a full bath, and a wet bar with refrigerator-perfect for extended guests, or in-law suite. Enjoy a tranquil setting just minutes from Westport's vibrant downtown, beaches, shopping, restaurants, parks, and Metro-North. The expansive yard provides plenty of outdoor space to relax or entertain plus a screened in porch to enjoy the natural surroundings.
