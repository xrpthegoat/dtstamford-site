About this home

Welcome to this charming Colonial nestled in the heart of Rowayton! This well-maintained 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home offers timeless character, comfortable living spaces, and an unbeatable location. The inviting floor plan features sun-filled rooms, hardwood floors, and a seamless flow that's perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. Oversized family room with brand new carpeting. The spacious updated kitchen opens to the dining room with built-ins, while the cozy living room with fireplace provides a warm and welcoming place to gather. Upstairs, you'll find three generously sized bedrooms and a full bath, with a convenient half bath located on the main level. Outside, enjoy a private yard with plenty of space for relaxing, gardening, or entertaining. New Metal Roof, city water, city sewer and Rowayton elementary school. Ideally situated just minutes from Rowayton Village, beaches, parks, Metro-North, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes, this home offers the perfect blend of neighborhood charm and everyday convenience.
