About this home

Charming, immaculate cottage located on a bucolic 3.5 acres property with its own lake. Located in North Stamford on a quiet country road, set back from the street. Cottage is privately situated from the Main House which is on the same property, but on a shared driveway. Cottage is fully renovated, has vaulted ceilings, skylights, a front porch, large patio and access to a heated in ground, saltwater pool with a spa. Cottage has 1 1/2 bathrooms and its own laundry. Kitchen rivals the kitchen of any larger home. It is amply sized and has a skylight, gas range, dishwasher and refrigerator. Plenty of cabinet and counter space. The Primary Bedroom has a large walk-in closet, and is ensuite. The Primary Bathroom has a single sink, white vanity, tub/shower combination, vaulted ceilings and a skylight. Landlord to take care of snow removal, lawn maintenance and all exterior maintence. Propane is tenant's responsibility as is wifi/cable. Cottage is unfurnished. Please reach out to the listing agent for more information!
