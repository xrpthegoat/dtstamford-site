About this home

Stunning Updated Colonial on Corner Lot with SEPARATE one bedroom In-law Cottage. This beautifully renovated 5-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial is a must-see! Situated on a flat corner lot, this home offers expansive living space and thoughtful upgrades throughout. A second-level addition completed in 2014 added even more room, while a chef's kitchen (adorned with Sub Zero and Wolf appliances), fully renovated in 2021, is perfect for cooking and entertaining. With foam insulation, underground electrical wiring, and room for a whole-house generator, new septic, this home is as energy-efficient as it is stylish. New windows and doors (except one window and door) and a new roof (installed with the addition) ensure peace of mind for years to come. The upper level boasts all new mechanicals, ensuring modern functionality. The separate, private one-bedroom in-law cottage provides flexibility for extended family, a nanny suite, or a home office. Enjoy outdoor living on the expansive deck, ideal for summer relaxation. With so much attention to detail, this home truly offers a blend of luxury, comfort, and convenience. Don't miss the opportunity to make this exceptional property your new home! All you need to do is unpack. Owner/Agent
