About this home

Set on 1.37 acres in Norwalk's sought-after Silvermine neighborhood, this inviting property combines a natural setting with spacious and versatile living areas. Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac, the grounds feature a scenic brook, established trees, and a tree-lined driveway that creates a private and welcoming approach. The high-ranch home offers four bedrooms, three full bathrooms, with ample room for everyday living and entertaining. Inside, the kitchen flows into a separate formal dining room, ideal for hosting gatherings. The lower level adds even more living space with a comfortable family room featuring a wood-burning fireplace and direct access to the backyard. The lower level can easily be made into the perfect in-law set up. Outdoor amenities include a generous deck, patio, and Jacuzzi, offering plenty of options for relaxation and entertaining throughout the seasons. An enclosed two-car garage and additional off-street parking provide added convenience. Ideally located near the Merritt Parkway, local shopping, and Silvermine Golf Course, this property presents a wonderful opportunity to enjoy space, privacy, and a serene setting while remaining close to everyday amenities.
