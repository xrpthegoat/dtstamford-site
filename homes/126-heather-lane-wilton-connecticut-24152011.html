About this home

Welcome to 126 Heather Lane, Wilton, CT - a beautifully maintained ranch-style home that reflects pride of ownership, tasteful updates and thoughtful care throughout. Offering over 1,800 square feet of living space, this inviting residence features three spacious bedrooms and two and a half baths, with hardwood floors throughout and an easy, open flow ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The primary bedroom includes an en-suite bath, while both the living room and family room feature cozy fireplaces, adding warmth and charm. New windows and sliders throughout the home bring in abundant natural light and enhance energy efficiency. The lower level boasts impressive 9-foot ceilings and offers excellent potential to be finished into a playroom, recreation space, or bonus room. Additional highlights include a new roof, two-car garage, and a large, level yard that is fully fenced - perfect for outdoor enjoyment. An oversized deck provides a wonderful space for relaxing or entertaining. Ideally located just minutes from Route 7, this home offers easy access to shopping, schools, and commuting routes. Truly a special property-this is a gem not to be missed.
