About this home

Located on a quiet mid country lane in the North Street school district, this center hall colonial offers privacy and tranquility with views of the one and a half acre property and pond. The main floor features a generously sized living room with fireplace and french doors to a sunroom with separate heating and cooling, formal dining room, powder room, and adjoining family room with fireplace and French doors to the rear yard as well as a laundry/mudroom with full bath and access to the attached 2 car garage. Second floor has an en suite primary and three additional bedrooms and hall full bath. carpeted lower level suitable as gym-playroom. Agent to Agent Rmrks: Breakfront remains in kitchen. Owner/tenant share snow removal. Tenant occupied, Appointment required. LB accompanied.
