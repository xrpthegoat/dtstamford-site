About this home

Lovingly cared for by the same family for 50 years, this inviting home is ready to welcome its next chapter. Offering 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms, and a warm, welcoming layout, this home blends timeless charm with comfortable living. Hardwood floors throughout the living spaces and a cozy fireplace create an inviting atmosphere. The spacious family room opens to a beautiful stone patio overlooking a private, manicured yard, the perfect setting for outdoor entertaining, summer gatherings, or simply relaxing at home. The finished lower level offers additional flexible living space and laundry room. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, everyday amenities, and recreational opportunities, the home offers easy access to all that Norwalk has to offer. Enjoy nearby shopping at Connecticut Avenue, explore the shops and dining at The SoNo Collection and vibrant South Norwalk, or spend time outdoors at local parks and waterfront destinations. Commuters will appreciate Norwalk's access to major roadways and Metro-North rail service, connecting to surrounding communities and New York City. With generous living areas, a wonderful indoor-outdoor flow, and a setting that feels like home, this property is ready for a new family to settle in and create lasting memories for years to come. Property is being offered and sold in as-is condition
