About this home

Welcome to 208 Flax Hill Road, where comfort meets convenience in this beautifully maintained corner unit condo, just steps away from the vibrant SoNo district. This rare gem is one of the few units in the complex that features a fully fenced-in private patio, perfect for relaxing summer evenings or cozy fall nights under the stars. Step inside to find a spacious living and dining area filled with natural light, perfect for entertaining or simply enjoying your day-to-day. The kitchen features a gas range oven and a charming window to gaze out of while preparing meals. You'll also love the in-unit washer and dryer, a highly sought-after convenience in this community. Upstairs, the generous primary bedroom offers great closet space, spacious second bedroom ideal for family, guests, or a home office. A full bathroom with a tub adds versatility. Situated in a prime Norwalk location, you'll enjoy being just a short walk to the SoNo train station, Maritime Aquarium, SoNo Collection Mall, popular dining spots, entertainment, and nightlife. Whether you're commuting or exploring the best of what Norwalk has to offer, this home puts you right in the center of it all.
