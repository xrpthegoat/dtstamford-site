About this home

Set at the end of a private cul-de-sac in Westport's coveted Coleytown neighborhood, 5 Michele Lane is a statement of craftsmanship, scale, and resort-style living. Surrounded by mature plantings for exceptional privacy and just steps from the Saugatuck River, this custom Colonial blends timeless architecture with elevated design throughout. A classic wood shingle exterior and wood roof set the tone, while the interior showcases 9'+ ceilings, marble tile, extensive millwork, and refined trim details. A dramatic Venetian plaster foyer introduces the home's elegant yet inviting aesthetic. Spanning three expansive levels plus a walk-up attic, the residence offers 6 bedrooms, 7 full baths, and 2 powder rooms-designed to accommodate both grand entertaining and comfortable everyday living. Seven fireplaces add warmth and architectural character across the home's generous living spaces. The outdoor setting is truly exceptional, featuring a resort-caliber gunite pool with integrated spa, expansive bluestone patio, outdoor kitchen, and a beautifully designed pergola-creating a private retreat ideal for entertaining or relaxation. Inside, the home continues to impress with well-proportioned rooms, a luxurious primary suite, and en-suite bedrooms throughout. The finished lower level offers additional space for recreation, wellness, or guests, complemented by a private sauna, wine cellar, additional laundry, guest suite, rec room, gym and lounge.
