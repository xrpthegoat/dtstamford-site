About this home

This charming 2 bedroom cottage is one of 2 small houses behind the main house on King Highway. This rental comes with one bay of a 2 car garage. It is a very quiet setting in a perfect location close to everything. 1 mile to main street with shops ,restaurants, library and much more. High ceilings, large sunny rooms, and move in condition. Take advantage of all Westport has including great schools, beaches, Longshore park with golf, pool and tennis. Lawn care , water and snow plowing are included in rent.
