About this home

Absolutely adorable 3 BR historic Dutch Colonial home with brand new bathroom, new roof, newer kitchen, City water, and huge two-car garage with loft! Located in cozy Georgetown, just a 6-minute walk to popular Caraluzzi's market plaza (grocery, cleaners, liquor store), and a few minutes drive (or longer walk) to vibrant Georgetown center restaurants and shops. Beautiful hardwood floors throughout, high ceilings, granite countertops and large peninsula, and dining area. Beautiful light, and the quintessential and coveted front porch to wile away the hours sipping your drink of choice, reading or just having a grateful moment or two. Extensive masonry and gorgeous tree-protected patio with included fire pit give you lots of options for warm weather entertaining. Use your imagination and create a fabulous work space, play space, or exercise room in the 600 sf loft. Fabulous opportunity to access Wilton's award-winning schools, super convenient shopping and restaurants, in a wonderful, quiet neighborhood, at a coveted price point. Don't wait!
