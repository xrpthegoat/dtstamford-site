About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's Cove neighborhood, this beautifully maintained Colonial offers the perfect balance of charm, comfort, and convenience. Nestled in a seaside community surrounded by mature trees and thoughtfully landscaped grounds, the home enjoys a private setting while remaining just minutes from Cove Island Park, Chelsea Piers, Metro-North, downtown Stamford, shopping, restaurants, and I-95 via Exit 9. Sun-filled living spaces feature hardwood floors, a welcoming wood-burning fireplace, and an easy flow from the living room to the spacious kitchen and dining area, making everyday living and entertaining effortless. A versatile front room offers the flexibility of a home office, playroom, or sunroom, while a convenient mudroom adds everyday functionality. Upstairs, generously sized bedrooms provide comfort and natural light. Step outside to an expansive, fully fenced backyard complete with a stone patio, lush lawn, and mature landscaping that creates an exceptional setting for outdoor dining, entertaining, play, or simply relaxing in privacy. A detached garage and ample off-street parking complete the property. This home offers timeless character, functional living spaces, and an outstanding location just moments from Stamford's waterfront, parks, commuting options, and everyday amenities. Original architectural plans for the home's addition are included in the listing documents.
