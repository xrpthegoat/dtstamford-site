About this home

Lovingly cared for by its owners, this 6-bedroom, 3.1-bath center hall colonial sits on 1.52 beautiful acres along a quiet lane in one of Greenwich's most sought-after locations. Its well-designed floor plan suits both relaxed everyday living and easy, gracious entertaining. The main level is bright and inviting. A spacious family room opens to the kitchen, making it the natural heart of the home. The living room is filled with light from three exposures, and a den/office both that opens directly onto an enclosed porch, a peaceful spot for morning coffee or working from home. The formal dining room flows easily into the open kitchen, and from there it's just steps to an expansive private rear terrace, ready for summer dinners and weekend gatherings. Thoughtful details make daily life easy: front and back staircases, plus a mudroom off the large two-car garage with its own separate entrance leading into the kitchen. Upstairs, the second floor offers a comfortable layout with five generously sized bedrooms and a primary suite with expansive closet space. The unfinished lower level is a true blank canvas, ready for a gym, media room, playroom, or whatever you imagine. Privacy and convenience come together here. The home is tucked away on a peaceful lane, yet only minutes from downtown Greenwich, schools, shopping, dining, and everything Greenwich has to offer. Opportunities like this are rare.
