About this home

BEST AND FINAL Monday 6/8 at 4PM. Spacious 3 Bedroom over sized Cape/Colonial, with all bedrooms on the second level. Located at the end of a quiet walkable street in South Wilton, surrounded by lush woods, great privacy, minutes from town with CITY WATER! Light filled Eat in Kitchen boasts Sile Stone counters and seating at large island, farm sink, tons of cabinetry and storage space. Spacious and airy first floor living includes Family Room with built ins, Dining Room, Living Room with wood fireplace, all with gleaming hardwood. Convenient Den/Office adjacent to the Kitchen. Side door entry right from garage lands you in Mudroom with main floor Laundry. This bright, sun drenched First Floor includes Full Bath. Second Floor surprises with extra large Primary, huge walk-in closet and sitting areas with ceiling fan. Two additional bright bedrooms, both with walk-in closets, and one with sweet ballet corner-think flooring and mirror for yoga, pilates, meditation or creative play. Large sparkling white Full Bath, with two sinks and shower/tub. Fantastic Lower Level, finished for Media/Rec/Playroom. Amazingly beautiful grounds, overlooked from back patio, showcases professional landscaping, huge flat back and side play areas. You would never believe this was .36 acres! Over-sized detached 1-car garage with shed. Minutes from shopping, restaurants, trains, highways, Westport, Norwalk and the NRVT! Award winning Wilton Schools. Beautiful, walkable neighborhood. 4 bedroom septic.
