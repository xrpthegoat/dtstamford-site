About this home

Welcome to Briarpatch, an enchanting 1781 Colonial/Saltbox home that has been lovingly restored and moved to its current location on desirable Maywood Rd. This antique gem which exudes 18th-century Connecticut country charm, offers modern comfort while preserving its rich historical architectural details. With 2.32 acres of pure privacy, yet conveniently close to beaches, restaurants, transportation hubs, and shopping, this home offers the best of both worlds. Step inside to discover wide-planked chestnut floors that seamlessly blend with the home's historical allure. The residence features five bedrooms, a first floor primary bedroom suite, options for an in-law suite, and four full bathrooms, making it ideal for accommodating guests or family. The inviting living spaces boasts three working fireplaces, perfect for cozy gatherings. Basement steel beams help support this sturdy historic home. Relax on the spacious backyard deck/patio overlooking the lush perennial gardens. Recent noteworthy improvements include a new 5 bedroom septic system, CT Basement System installation and 11 Kw solar panel system. See documents section for the complete list. Briarpatch is not only a historical treasure but a great opportunity for those seeking a unique and charming home, *see agent remarks re: tax card and latest info regarding offers.
