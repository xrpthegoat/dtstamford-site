About this home

Welcome to a home where everyday living feels easy & every gathering feels special. This splendid Dutch Colonial situated on a quiet cul-de-sac lane in bucolic North Stamford offers the perfect blend of charm & comfort. The main level has a fabulous floorplan where formal living spaces flow into casual retreats. The expansive living room, featuring a wood-burning fireplace, opens to a spacious dining room made for celebratory dinners. Beautifully updated, the eat-in kitchen is a real pleaser with white cabinets, stainless steel appliances & quartz countertops. It leads to the enclosed three-season porch & multi-level deck-ideal for summer barbecues, morning coffee or dining under the stars. Just off the kitchen, the rustic-style family room with beamed ceilings, a brick fireplace & sliders to the porch creates a cozy retreat. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a peaceful escape with a walk-in closet, spacious bath with a large walk-in shower and a versatile sitting room that could serve as an office, fitness room, nursery or den. Two additional bedrooms include an oversized third bedroom that can easily be converted back to create a fourth. The retro-style hall bath has a tub/shower combination. The walkout lower-level with full-height ceilings has a 3rd fireplace and provides exceptional bonus space with endless possibilities. Beautifully set on a picturesque one-acre corner lot framed by mature trees. Other features include a two-car garage, circular driveway & central A/C.
