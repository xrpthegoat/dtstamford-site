About this home

Experience the elevated ease of modern luxury in this brand new SIGNATURE Home by SIR Development, Westport's most respected ground-up RE developer with 30+ years & 300+ completed homes. Set on a scenic park-like acre w/ gunite pool & circular drive, conveniently located within walking distance of Long Lots School & shops, just 1.7 mi to train. Experience the grandeur of the soaring two story foyer, the generous artisan millwork, decorative accents & remarkable scale of 10 ceilings w/ character grade oak floors on main level. LR has unique modern built-ins & DR has a wall of glass. Grand in scale, the Great Room has a distinctive modern fpl, reclaimed antique wood beams that give organic authenticity, walls of glass & an open flow to breakfast rm into the kitchen, which was designed w/upgrades: full-size Sub Zero refrigerator & freezer, fully integrated Wolf cooktop & wall ovens, stunning waterfall island, cabinetry & pantry. Tucked away, arts & crafts workshop or office has a private entry & built-ins. The mudroom leads to 3-car garage & pool. Grand primary suite w/ incredible closets & lux bath. All en-suite BRs on 2nd flr. Finished 3rd floor w/ 2nd office, bonus rm & bath. The walk-out LL has 6th BR, full bath, gym, media, playroom. Quality construction, cohesive design & functional layouts are hallmarks of SIR SIGNATURE Homes.
