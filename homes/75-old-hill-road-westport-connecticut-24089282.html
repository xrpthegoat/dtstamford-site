About this home

Stunning colonial with contemporary flair set on a private acre in much sought after Old Hill. Convenient to town, train and beach. This is not your cookie cutter colonial with wonderful, flexible, open floor plan and top of the line remodeled kitchen. Current piano room could be formal dining room or office/study. Impressive Primary Bedroom suite with beautiful bath. Large upstairs room with full bath could be family room, in-law/au pair suite or lavish office. Spacious walk out lower level includes family room/playroom, bedroom, full bath and gym. Beautiful lot includes a lovely mahogany deck, mature plantings, stone wall and circular driveway. Furnace, full house generator, Sonos system, dehumidifier, 2 instant hot water heaters, top of the line kitchen appliances, new propane tank, attic fan, alarm system, flooring and BBQ all new in 2019. Truly an exceptional home in an exceptional location.
