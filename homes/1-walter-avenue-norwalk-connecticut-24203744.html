About this home

Lovely ranch-style, main-level, end unit condo, located in Norwalk's desirable Wolfpit neighborhood. This 1 bedroom, 1 bath condo with a comfortable, open Living Room / Kitchen area, is filled with plenty of natural light passing through its six full windows. Low monthly common charges include heat, and hot/cold water. There is a handy laundry room in the lower level of the building. Assigned parking is conveniently located in the lot directly behind the front building. Current tenant's lease ends 6/31/27. Ideal for investors, or future homeowners!
