About this home

Highly desirable, centrally located Springdale townhouse featuring two spacious bedrooms, one of which includes its own en suite bath. The second bedroom has access to the full hall bath with a tub/ shower combination. The renovated kitchen boasts granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. An open-concept living and dining areas flow seamlessly, with gleaming hardwood floors and a slider leading to a private back patio. The lower level offers expansive bonus room, perfect for a home office nook, along with a half bath and laundry area. Completely move in ready as it's been freshly painted throughout and has brand new carpeting in the bedrooms, lower level and hallways. This unit also includes a one-car attached garage and an additional designated parking space. Fantastic commuter location close to Metro-North station as well as the Merritt Parkway.
