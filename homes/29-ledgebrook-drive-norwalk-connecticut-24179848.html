About this home

Beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath townhouse in sought-after Ledgebrook, set in a park-like West Norwalk community surrounding a pool and pond. A welcoming entry foyer opens to a sun-filled living/dining great room anchored by a floor-to-ceiling exposed-brick wood-burning fireplace. Updated wide-plank flooring, crown molding, recessed lighting, and oversized windows fill the home with light and frame green, leafy views. The kitchen offers ample cabinetry double sink under a garden window, pantry, and breakfast bar. Both full baths are renovated one with a marble vanity and marble-tiled shower, the spacious primary bath with a double granite vanity and subway-tiled tub/shower. Three generous bedrooms with bright windows; the oversized primary fits a king plus office. In-unit washer/dryer, central air, detached garage with opener, and a private covered patio backing to mature trees. Residents enjoy the community pool, clubhouse, hot tub, sauna, fitness room, walking trails, and pond. Pets welcome. ~5 min to Metro-North; near I-95, Merritt, SoNo, Stamford & Westport. Move-in ready.
