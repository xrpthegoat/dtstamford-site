About this home

Bring your boat! This is THE waterfront property you have been searching for! A truly impeccable home w. stationary pier and floating dock, beautifully located on Norwalk Harbor's Gregory Point, it offers stunning sunsets and commanding water views from every room. The moment you walk onto the front porch, through the front door and into the light-filled entry foyer you will sense the quality workmanship and tasteful elegance of this completely renovated (2004-05) home. The living room and well-appointed kitchen provide water views from many angles. The formal dining room offers space for entertaining and the family room w. fireplace and wet bar are perfect for casual gatherings. Upstairs, the primary suite will take your breath away w. its size and even more expansive water views. It includes a huge bath w. soaking tub and separate shower, laundry, multiple walk-in closets and even space for a baby grand! There are two additional bedrooms on the second floor, plus hall bath. A home office or possible 4th bedroom is located on the first floor. Outside, Ipe decking leads from the covered front porch to an outside deck, to the open waterside bluestone patio which provides wonderful outdoor living space. Manicured gardens and lawns offer color all season long. A charming detached two-car garage completes the picture of this perfect seaside residence. This quiet/private neighborhood is easily accessible to beaches, boating, golf, tennis and so much more that Norwalk offers.
