About this home

A circular drive welcomes you as you approach this grand Center Hall Colonial, on 2 scenic acres with a fantastic Har-Tru tennis court, pool site & beautiful perennial gardens, sited on a quiet private lane in central Westport. Timeless elegance & quality construction is evident, from the moment you step into the impressive Entry Hall of this masterfully crafted home. Be delighted by the 10' ceilings, fine millwork, tall windows, coffered ceilings, the generous sized rooms, the great light & beautiful views throughout the main level featuring glamorous & beautifully appointed Living & Dining Rooms, a warm and welcoming Family Room with a floor to ceiling fieldstone hearth, the sunny Play Room & Library/Office. The substantial moldings & exceptional detail continues into the heart of the home Eat-In Kitchen, with an enormous island, spanning much of the rear of this 6800 square foot home. Upstairs you will find the gracious Primary Suite with a Sitting Room both with fireplaces, a private Bath, 2 huge walk-in closets & a large deck which overlooks the gorgeous backyard & the expansive terrace off the Kitchen. Four additional spacious en-suite Bedrooms, also accessible by the rear staircase, complete the 2nd level. This home has a spacious walk-up Attic that is plumbed for a Bath plus a full dry Basement & 3 car Garage. Conveniently located near YMCA, town, commuter access & all amenities make this warm, well-maintained custom home truly special
