About this home

Enjoy this charming 3 bed 2 full bath ranch, perfectly situated in a prime, convenient location, offering easy access to all the amenities and I-95 while providing a comfortable living space. Enjoy the natural light coming in through the oversized windows. Ideal floor plan that flows seamlessly from room to room with adequate separation. The well-appointed kitchen boasts high ceiling, granite countertop, ample counter space, stainless steel appliances and plenty of storage. Breeze through the sliding door from the kitchen into the covered patio to enjoy the outdoors. The entire main level has refinished hardwood floors, recessed lighting, central A/C and heat. Energy efficient features including solar panel & programmable thermostat. Basement has full apartment and kitchen with separate entrance, and is NOT legal. Mostly paved yard for plenty of parking with some lawn in the back. The property also comes with underground irrigation system that is programmable.
