About this home

22 Sturges Commons offers the rare combination of timeless elegance, true neighborhood living, and unmatched convenience in one of Westport's most sought-after locations near the Hunt Club. Just one traffic light to the Greens Farms Station train station, this home makes commuting to New York City effortless while allowing you to return home to a peaceful walking neighborhood where charming streets converge, creating the classic community feel that is increasingly rare in Westport, CT real estate. Set on beautifully landscaped property, the home is designed for both relaxed everyday living and exceptional entertaining. The fully fenced backyard features a sparkling pool and spa, expansive outdoor spaces for dining and gatherings, and plenty of room for sports and play. Inside, warmth and sophistication define every space. The gourmet chef's kitchen opens seamlessly to a spacious step-down family room with soaring ceilings and abundant natural light, creating the perfect heart of the home. Formal living and dining rooms provide elegant entertaining spaces, while multiple options for home offices and flexible living make this property ideal for today's lifestyle. Offering 5 ensuite bedrooms, 8 baths, a third-floor office or yoga retreat, and a finished lower level complete with gym, media room, and playroom, this exceptional Westport home blends luxury, comfort, and functionality in one of the best commuter locations in Fairfield County.
