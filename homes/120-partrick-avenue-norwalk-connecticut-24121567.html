About this home

Welcome to 120 Partrick Avenue, a meticulously maintained single-owner home in Norwalk's highly sought-after Cranbury neighborhood, proudly owned and cared for since 1975 and now on the market for the very first time. This rare find offers fresh paint, gleaming hardwood floors, and a bright inviting layout, complemented by modern updates including a high-efficiency natural gas boiler, upgraded 200-amp electrical panel, and an on-demand full-house Kohler generator connected directly to city gas for year-round peace of mind. Comfort is at the forefront with a split unit on the first floor and central air on the second, along with city water and sewer for convenience. The spacious two-car garage with extra storage, a charming covered front porch, and a large, flat backyard provide the perfect setting for everyday living, entertaining, or play. With its thoughtful upgrades, timeless character, and unmatched pride of ownership, this home presents an exceptional opportunity to own a truly special property in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods.
