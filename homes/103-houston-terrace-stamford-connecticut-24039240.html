About this home

Welcome to 103 Houston Terrace, located in the coveted Cove neighborhood of Stamford, CT. Just blocks away from the water, this meticulously maintained home has a fully renovated kitchen. The primary bedroom and bath, as well as 2 additional bedrooms, a hall bath and a huge laundry room are located on the second floor. Additionally, the basement is partially finished with a den and laundry. The pride of ownership shines through the lush landscaping, herb garden and raised-bed gardens to grow your own flowers or vegetables. The private patio with gazebo offers great outdoor entertaining space or the privacy to sit and enjoy the peace of the backyard. 103 Houston Terrace is a turn-key property.
