About this home

This sunny, turn-key 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath Colonial has been thoughtfully updated and lovingly maintained, featuring an updated full bath, a finished walk-up attic, and bright, inviting living spaces throughout. Step inside to an open-concept living room anchored by a fireplace, with plenty of space for two distinct seating areas-perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The space flows seamlessly into a sun- filled dining room, which offers the flexibility to also serve as a home office or den. The updated farmhouse- style kitchen features freshly painted cabinetry, updated flooring, and stainless steel appliances, with direct access to a beautiful patio overlooking the fully fenced backyard. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms filled with natural light and a newly updated full bath. One bedroom provides access to the finished walk-up attic, offering valuable bonus space for a home office, guest room, playroom, or more. Tucked away on a charming cul-de-sac in coveted East Norwalk, this home offers the perfect combination of neighborhood living and convenience, with a sidewalk route to the train station (5 min walk). Spend summer evenings at nearby Calf Pasture Beach just minutes away or head to SoNo for dinner, shopping, and entertainment. Move right in and enjoy all that East Norwalk has to offer.
