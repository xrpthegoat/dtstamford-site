About this home

Welcome to 15 Silwen Lane in the Wolfpit area of Norwalk! Well maintained raised ranch from the inside out & it certainly shows. Wonderfully landscaped .30 acre lot on the corner of Silwen Ln and Mark Dr. Low traffic area perfect to come home and relax to yet still so close to the shops/restaurants of Westport Ave. Exterior walkways Flagstone lined with thick Belgian Block give much curb appeal and comfort to next renters and their guests. Hardwood floors throughout, crown molding, plenty of windows for natural light, and open concept give a comforting feel to the place called home. Kitchen includes recessed lighting, granite countertops and plenty cabinet space. Leads to dining area & expansive composite deck perfect for entertaining. Tiled EIK + bathrooms. Main level also includes three spacious bedrooms and a full tub bathroom. All bedrooms are light/bright + have PLENTY of closet space. Lower level (partial) includes a BONUS multipurpose room, utility room, storage area + full access to larger one car garage. In-law space hasa separate entrance + (Other tenant resides). Solar panels, thermopane windows, zoned heating (oil) keep the utilities LOW. Driveway parking for 3+ cars not including one car attached garage. A MUST see! **See agent remarks**
