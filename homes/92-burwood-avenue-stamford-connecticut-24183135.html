About this home

Welcome to this newly renovated 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath apartment offering approximately 1,800 SF of stylish and comfortable living space in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. This spacious residence features a brand-new kitchen with updated countertops and backsplash, new appliances, and a dedicated wine/beverage refrigerator. Recent renovations also include updated bathrooms, a refreshed entryway, and a separate office space perfect for working from home. Enjoy your own private outdoor deck complete with a built-in BBQ station, ideal for relaxing or entertaining guests. The apartment also includes one garage parking space and the added value of your gas utilities/heat included in the rent! Conveniently located near the waterfront, local parks, downtown Stamford, and downtown Old Greenwich, this home offers the perfect balance of quiet residential living and easy access to shopping, dining, entertainment, and transportation.
