About this home

ALL OFFERS BY MONDAY, MAY 4TH - 5pm. Set in Stamford's coveted north backcountry, this spectacular ranch offers a rare blend of privacy, scale, and serene waterfront living. Overlooking a secluded pond framed by mature specimen trees, the setting is nothing short of breathtaking-an everyday escape defined by tranquility and natural beauty. An airy, open-concept layout with 9-foot ceilings enhances the sense of space and light throughout. With views of the water in every room.The main level is designed for both comfort and effortless entertaining, featuring soaring beamed ceilings in the living and dining rooms, two fireplaces, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow. A gourmet kitchen opens to the family room, with french doors leading to a screened porch and expansive deck-perfect for taking in the magnificent pond views. Step outside to your own private waterfront experience, complete with a dock ideal for kayaking, fishing, or simply enjoying peaceful moments by the water. The home offers 5 bedrooms and 4 full baths, including a primary suite with a beautifully updated bath, dressing room, and private balcony overlooking the pond. A fully finished lower level with separate driveway and entrance adds exceptional flexibility, featuring a third fireplace, full kitchen, bath, wet bar, and potential for additional bedrooms-ideal for in-law or au pair living.
