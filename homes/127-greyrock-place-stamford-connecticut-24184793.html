About this home

Beautiful, newly renovated 15/F 1-bedroom residence w/ stunning sunrise & city views in The Biltmore Luxury Condominium building. Brand-new LVP floors throughout. Building is pet-friendly (size limit) and allows 60-day minimum rentals. Stamford Train Station is less than 1 mile. Attached garage company charges approx. $120/month. Move-in ready. Impeccably updated. Experience luxury downtown living at its best.
