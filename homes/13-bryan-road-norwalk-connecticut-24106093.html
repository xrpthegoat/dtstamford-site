About this home

When thinking of moving to Rowayton, 13 Bryan Road satisfies every desire. It sits high and dry, yet is just a stone's throw to the water. The picturesque cul de sac is lined with classic properties and mature flowering trees & sits at the very center of the seaside village with shops, restaurants and marinas. Plentiful windows show off a sun-drenched interior and the stunning private gardens. The backyard oasis features an outdoor kitchen, the ultimately gathering spot for al fresco dining and summer barbeques. The ample living space on the main floor offers a perfect flow with areas for dining, living and working from home. Guests and family will undoubtedly be drawn to the vaulted-ceiling living room with fireplace that overlooks the professionally landscaped yard. On the upper level, you'll find a private bedroom suite, 2 additional sunny bedrooms & a primary suite with ample closets and sumptuous spa bath (with heated floors!) Don't miss this opportunity to experience such a harmonious blend of coastal living and modern comfort on one of Rowayton's most sought-after streets. Come and see for yourself why this home is the perfect sanctuary for those seeking a serene seaside lifestyle.
