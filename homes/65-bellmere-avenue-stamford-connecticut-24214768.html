About this home

Highest & Best by Sunday (10/25) at 4PM. Set on a cul-de-sac in Stamford's Belltown neighborhood, this spacious home offers hardwood floors throughout, generous living spaces, and a beautifully landscaped backyard. The main level features a large living room, a formal dining room with French doors opening to a light-filled sunroom, and a spacious eat-in kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a dedicated dining area and direct access to the deck overlooking the backyard. Two large bedrooms and a full bathroom complete the main level. Upstairs, the large primary bedroom features a walk-in closet and access to additional attic storage, along with another bedroom and a full bathroom. The walk-out lower level offers an abundance of possibilities, with plenty of room for a home gym, office, workshop, additional living space, and storage. Outside, the beautifully landscaped backyard provides privacy and ample space to relax, entertain, or enjoy the outdoors. Conveniently located near parks, shops, local restaurants, and Stamford's many amenities.
