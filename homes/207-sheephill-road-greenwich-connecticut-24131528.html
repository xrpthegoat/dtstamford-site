About this home

Classic Cape Cod Charmer in Riverside - This beautifully updated four-bedroom, two-bath Cape Cod offers a rare combination of classic charm, modern upgrades, and exceptional value. The home features a remodeled kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a gas range with renovated bathrooms. Central air conditioning and a gas HVAC system ensure year-round efficiency and easy living. A large stone patio provides the perfect setting for entertaining or relaxing outdoors. The attached garage adds convenience and extra storage. The lower level includes finished bonus living space and an additional space dedicated to laundry & storage. Ideally located, this home is just moments to Schools, Metro North, and the Mianus River & Park.
