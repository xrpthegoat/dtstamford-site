About this home

Set atop a native stone lined property in one of New Canaan's most convenient locations, this spacious 5 bedroom, 5 full bath home offers timeless appeal, generous scale, and incredible flexibility for today's lifestyle. Just minutes from Waveny Park, top-rated schools, and only 0.6 miles to Talmadge Hill Train Station, the location is ideal for both everyday living and commuting. An appealing 57 foot front porch creates a warm welcome into the impressive foyer, setting the tone for beautifully proportioned interiors beyond. Elegant formal living room with fireplace and dining room, provide inviting spaces for entertaining, while the expansive renovated kitchen with oversized center island, gas range, pantry, and sunlit breakfast room opens to the patio for effortless indoor/outdoor living. The adjoining family room with fireplace is the heart of the home, offering a warm and comfortable gathering space, and a private first floor office provides an ideal work from home retreat. Upstairs, the luxurious primary suite is a true sanctuary, featuring an expansive bedroom, walk in closet, and a stunning spa inspired bath with vaulted ceilings, dual vanities, a sculptural soaking tub, and a separate shower, creating a serene retreat filled with natural light. Three additional generously sized bedrooms, well appointed baths and a convenient laundry room complete this level. The third floor offers exceptional versatility as a 5th bedroom suite, gym, recreation or media room.
