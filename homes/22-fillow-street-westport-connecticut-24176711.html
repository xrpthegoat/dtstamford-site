About this home

For the first time since it was built in 1956, 22 Fillow Street is ready for its next chapter. Set on a beautiful .40-acre property in Westport's cherished Flower Estates neighborhood, this solid, well-built home offers a rare opportunity to become part of one of the town's most established and beloved in-town communities. Flower Estates is one of those increasingly rare Westport neighborhoods where the setting still feels personal, connected, and timeless - a true neighborhood close to town, schools, the train, and the beach. From here, you are just minutes to downtown Westport, approximately 10 minutes to the Westport train station, about 10 minutes to Compo Beach, and only minutes to Coleytown Elementary School. The home has been lovingly owned by one family since new and features hardwood floors throughout, wonderful natural light, and quality construction that has stood the test of time. Mechanicals recently updated. The .40-acre property is spacious, pretty, and full of possibility. Whether you envision updating the existing home, designing a larger residence, or adding outdoor entertaining spaces, this property offers flexibility in a location that is hard to duplicate. 22 Fillow Street is more than a house - it is a chance to continue a Westport tradition and enjoy a lifestyle defined by neighborhood, convenience, beach, town, train, and community.
