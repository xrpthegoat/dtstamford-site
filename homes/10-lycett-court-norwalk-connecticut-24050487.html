About this home

Welcome to 10 Lycett Court -- a 4 bedroom, 2.5 bath raised ranch style home with over 2200 square feet, a beautiful level .42 acre yard and a prime location at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac. Perfect for children to play safely in the neighborhood and for adults to relax in this peaceful private setting! Some special highlights of the home include the spacious lower level family room with large corner fireplace, an extra bedroom on the lower level off the family room that can also serve as an office, craft room or gym ... and a welcoming screened porch that invites warm weather entertaining and is the perfect place for reading and relaxing.You will love the convenience, too, with easy access to commuting, shopping and the West Norwalk schools.
