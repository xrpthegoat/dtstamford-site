About this home

Step into refined suburban living at 9 Benders Drive, a beautifully appointed residence in the sought-after Glenville neighborhood. Built in 2015 and thoughtfully maintained, this gracious home offers 5 bedrooms and 4.2 baths of well-planned living space, perfect for both everyday life and elevated entertaining. Light-filled interiors feature generous room proportions, rich hardwood floors, and a thoughtful layout that effortlessly balances formal and casual spaces. The heart of the home flows from a welcoming foyer into living and dining areas designed for memorable gatherings, while a comfortable family room with fireplace invites relaxed moments with loved ones. The well-equipped kitchen and adjacent dining area offer both functionality and style, ideal for daily living and hosting. Upstairs, the primary suite provides a private retreat with ample space, complemented by spacious secondary bedrooms and well-appointed baths. The finished lower level adds valuable flexible space for recreation, media, or a home office, enhancing everyday convenience. Set on a generous parcel with mature landscaping, beautifully done outdoor living spaces on a quiet cul-de-sac setting, this residence offers privacy and lifestyle appeal just moments from schools, parks, and Glenville's vibrant downtown amenities. A rare opportunity to enjoy polished, move-in-ready living in one of Greenwich's most desirable communities.
