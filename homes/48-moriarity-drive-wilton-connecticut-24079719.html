About this home

Prepared to be Wowed with this beautiful 4 bedroom colonial located in desirable South Wilton and sited on over 2 lush acres. Filled with character and light, this home offers the space, layout and features you want for how we live today. Well equipped eat-in-kitchen will delight the serious home chef and sliders lead to the oversized deck for all of your outdoor entertaining needs. A large dining room right off the kitchen, oversized living room with fireplace and family room leading to a delightful screened in porch, so you can barbecue and enjoy the serene outdoors without worry of mosquitos. Great for Spring, Summer and Fall. First floor laundry and half bath complete the first floor. Upstairs boasts a sophisticated, sun-filled primary bedroom with 2 closets and a spa-like full bath with double sink vanity, shower, jacuzzi tub. and plenty of storage. Three additional bedrooms and another renovated bath make this home a must see. Need to work from home? Looking for additional space for playroom, gym or media room? The finished lower level can accommodate all those needs. And there's more! An oversized 2 car garage with 50 Amp outlet, wired for EV charger, and professional landscaping add to the splendor. Enjoy all that Wilton has to offer: restaurants, shopping, hiking trails, close to trains and highways, and so much more. The pristine home you thought only existed in your dreams is right here. Move right in and make your home ownership dreams a reality.
