About this home

HIGHEST AND BEST DEADLINE: Monday, Jan. 12 at NOON. New Year, New Home: This lovingly cared for home is ready to pass the torch to the next owner. Sunlight pours into the three-season room - turn this into a four season room and this would make a wonderful reading nook. The three bedrooms are spacious and include large closets.
