About this home

Set on two private acres along the Greenwich border in one of Stamford's most coveted residential settings, 43 Ridgecrest Road blends timeless elegance with thoughtful modern upgrades. An expanded kitchen and family room anchored by Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances opens to an expansive rear patio designed for effortless entertaining, complete with a hot tub for year-round enjoyment. A butler's pantry and custom office built-ins add functionality, while the third floor provides a wonderful playroom or recreation space. The primary suite offers a spa-inspired bath with steam shower, heated floors, soaking tub, and private balcony. Mechanical systems and HVAC have been comprehensively updated. The property's positioning allows for a future pool or tennis court. Many who live nearby say there is simply something special about Ridgecrest Road.
