About this home

Rare opportunity to own recently built 2020 construction. Colonial residence boasting 4 bedrooms, 3 full baths, 2 half baths and an attached 2-car garage. The first floor features soaring 9-foot ceilings, a kitchen adorned with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a gas stove, a spacious dining area, inviting living room, and a convenient powder room. Upstairs, the primary suite dazzles with a large shower and heated floors, along with a walk-in closet. Additionally, there's a separate laundry room and two more bedrooms. The third floor, is perfect for guests with full bath, offers endless possibilities for a fourth bedroom suite or a playroom, complemented by a separate storage area. The lower level features a cozy living area with another half bath and additional room for home gym set up. Oversized windows bathe the interiors in natural light, creating an open and airy ambiance, while hardwood floors grace every room. Enjoy the convenience of natural gas for heating and cooking, as well as a tankless hot water system. This home also boasts energy-efficient zoned HVAC. Ideally located for easy access to shopping, trains, and downtown Stamford.
