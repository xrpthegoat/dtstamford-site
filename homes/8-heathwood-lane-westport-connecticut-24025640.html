About this home

Cute as a button. You will find so much more than expected both inside & out of this wonderful home. Privacy and nature surround you in this 3 bedroom 2.5 bath raised ranch in Old Hill. This home is spotless. The main floor has a living room, dining room and eat-in kitchen. A master bedroom with ensuite bath and 2 additional bedrooms complete the main level. The lower level has a spacious playroom/den with sliding doors, an office nook or studio space and a laundry room, plus an attached 2 Car Garage. An amazing bi-level, 50+/- foot deck overlooks a babbling brook. Convenient to the Westport Family YMCA, Merritt Pkwy, I-95, Metro-North and downtown. This is the rental you have been looking for! The owner prefers a multiyear lease. Road to be paved in the next few weeks. Brand new stainless steel kitchen appliances on order.
