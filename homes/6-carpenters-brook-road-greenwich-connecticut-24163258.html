About this home

Set behind gates and approached by a striking cherry blossom-lined drive, this fully reimagined storybook countryside estate represents a rare convergence of architectural character and modern, custom-built luxury. Rebuilt to the studs in 2022, the residence showcases uncompromising construction quality, bespoke finishes, and a level of design typically reserved for newly constructed estates without sacrificing the charm and scale of a classic French Normandy. Spanning 4.24 acres, the grounds are nothing short of extraordinary formal gardens, stone walls, a natural brook, and mature plantings create a private, estate-like setting with complete seclusion. The interiors are anchored by a statement gourmet kitchen expansive, refined, and outfitted with top-tier appliances and custom detailing seamlessly flowing into beautifully scaled living and entertaining spaces. The property offers 4 bedrooms and 3.5 baths, along with both attached and detached two-car garages, offering versatility for collectors or creative use. A separate cottage enhances the estate's livability, ideal for guests, staff, or extended stays. A turnkey, design-driven estate for the buyer who demands privacy, presence, and precision in every detail.
