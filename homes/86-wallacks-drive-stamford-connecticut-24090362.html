About this home

Prestigious Waterfront Estate at Wallack's Point Experience the pinnacle of coastal luxury with this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to own 1.46 acres of DIRECT Long Island Sound frontage in the gated enclave of Wallack's Point. With sweeping, unobstructed water views and nightly sunsets that feel like art, this premier waterfront property is among the most coveted on the Connecticut shoreline. Tucked into a serene, nature preserve-like setting, this secluded estate offers rare privacy just one hour from New York City. Mature landscaping frames the grounds, enhancing the tranquility and natural beauty of the site. Step onto your private beach, and take in the panoramic views that stretch endlessly across the Sound. The property includes: Main residence with an expansive footprint and a two-story added section featuring two bedrooms-ideal for in-laws or guests. Separate cottage, also two stories, with two bedrooms-perfect as a guesthouse, studio, or additional rental. With endless potential to renovate or reimagine, this is a generational property-an extraordinary canvas for your dream coastal estate. Bring your architect, bring your vision.
