About this home

Stately Silvermine Brick Colonial on Private Cul-de-Sac. This wonderful 2001-built home is ideally situated on a peaceful lane in the desirable Silvermine area, near the New Canaan border. Set on a private, level 1.05-acre lot with public water, this exceptional home offers an abundance of natural light, spacious living areas, and a wonderful blend of elegance and comfort. The inviting main level features a gracious living room, formal dining room, gourmet kitchen, and a spacious family room with fireplace. A dedicated office provides an ideal space for working from home or meeting clients. The impressive great room features French doors leading to a large multi-level patio with a pizza oven - creating the perfect setting for entertaining, dining, and enjoying the beautiful private backyard. The luxurious primary bedroom suite offers a spacious retreat with a large bath featuring a Jacuzzi, a comfortable sitting room/lounge, and two walk-in closets. Additional bedrooms provide plenty of space for family and guests. The fully finished lower level adds approximately 1,762 square feet of versatile living space, including a large recreation room, playroom (possible fifth bedroom) and full bath - ideal for guests, recreation, hobbies, or extended living.
