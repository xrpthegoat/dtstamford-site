About this home

Perched at the point of Saugatuck Island, Seven Sandpiper Road is pure New England perfection. The three story Nantucket style home is meticulously designed by architect Peter Cadoux to soak up the sparkling Long Island Sound views from all angles. The main floor holds the horizon directly at eye level from the open concept living areas - a light and bright gourmet kitchen with island seating, a breakfast nook and butler's pantry leads into the spacious family room with stone fireplace and deck access, but the show stopping sun room wins for best seat in the house where the floor to ceiling windows feel like the bow of a ship. The formal dining room also opens to the deck set for both lounging and grilling. In the primary wing, a breathtaking bedroom looks over the gorgeous grounds and water beyond, while the beautiful his and hers combined bath takes in the south facing Sound view. There are dual walk-in wardrobe closets as well as a second laundry closet. An stunning office rounds out the main floor, while the third floor holds a guest suite/media room with balcony and one of the most spectacular "roof" level decks in all of Westport! On the ground level, there are two bedrooms of generous size, each with their own ensuite bath and custom closets as well as a true library style office.
