About this home

Best and Final Friday 5-8-26 5pm. A very rare opportunity to join a delightful waterfront community on Coopers Pond in the Turn of River neighborhood in Stamford. This property provides a peaceful oasis with picturesque views of the water to enjoy all four seasons. Mature landscaping and beautiful flowers adds to the natural beauty and privacy of the yard. This home features bright open space. There are 4 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms. The Primary suite also features a private balcony overlooking the backyard and the water! The Kitchen was lovingly updated in 2022, which includes an incredible island for entertaining and gorgeous Quartz countertops. A natural gas range and stainless steel appliances complete the space. Being a front to back split level with natural lighting, there is an open concept feel while still providing enough private space that you won't feel like everyone is in your business! This layout adds a huge luxurious feeling to the main gathering space. There are a number of outdoor patios, accessible from the kitchen area as well as the lower level family room. A portion of the yard is fenced for privacy and safety of loved ones and pets. There is a one car garage in addition to a wide driveway that will easily fit a total of 5 cars. This home comes complete with a generator which has just been serviced. There is plenty of storage space in the lowest level of the home. This property is NOT in a flood zone and currently no special flood insurance needed!
