About this home

Get ready to make 19 Vineyard Lane home! This wonderful family home is ideally situated in the heart of Westport, offering 5 bedrooms and 2.5 baths designed for comfortable, everyday living and effortless entertaining alike. Lovingly cared for by its current owner for 50 years, this home features a first-floor office, while the partially finished basement offers space for a gym or playroom. Set on 1.24 flat acres, beautifully maintained with mature trees, an irrigation system, and room for a pool. Additional highlights include a whole-house generator, security system, irrigation and central air conditioning. Location is everything, and this home delivers. It's walkable to Wakeman Town Farm, to the playing fields at Wakeman and to Staples High School, making morning drop-offs a breeze. Right around the corner is recently opened local favorite Gruel Britannia, perfect for a quick coffee, bite, or catch-up with neighbors. This is more than a house - it's a place to put down roots in one of Westport's most desirable neighborhoods. Convenient to schools, beaches, shopping, and minutes to the train for seamless access on Metro North to NYC. Homes like this don't come along often.
