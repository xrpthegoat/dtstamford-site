About this home

This is the One! A beautifully updated four-bedroom Colonial set on a quiet street in coveted North Darien, offering the perfect blend of classic charm and modern living. The home features gracious formal spaces including a sun-filled living room, elegant dining room, and inviting den. At the heart of the home, a stunning updated kitchen opens seamlessly to a spacious family room, creating an ideal layout for everyday living and entertaining. A playroom and dual home offices provide the flexibility today's buyers are looking for. Perfectly positioned on the Wee Burn Country Club, the property enjoys sweeping, picturesque views that set it apart. Outdoor living is just as exceptional, with a beautifully designed stone patio featuring sitting walls and a fireplace-perfect for gathering year-round-plus a large, flat yard ideal for play and recreation. A detached three-car garage is complemented by a fantastic space above-perfect as a media room, private office, or creative studio. A rare opportunity to own in an unbeatable location with both privacy and views.
