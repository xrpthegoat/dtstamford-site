About this home

Enjoy easy one-level living in this inviting 4-bedroom, 2-bath ranch on the Darien/Stamford border. A spacious, open-concept kitchen and living area creates a welcoming setting for everyday living and entertaining. The generous, sun-filled primary bedroom features sliding doors to the oversized rear deck, while a newly renovated bathroom adds a fresh, updated touch. Beautifully landscaped and fully fenced, the property offers mature trees and ample outdoor space to relax, play, and gather. There is also room to explore adding a garage, subject to zoning and necessary approvals. Conveniently located with easy access to I-95 and just minutes from beaches and downtown Stamford's dining, shopping, and entertainment.
