About this home

In-town living at it's finest! This unbeatable colonial is on the market for the first time since the home was built and is sure to please from the moment you walk through the front door! The main level boasts an inviting front foyer, huge sun-filled living room w/ gorgeous wood burning fireplace, & beautiful white kitchen w/ granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, dishwasher, & tons of cabinet space. Also on this floor is a true formal dining room, family room/den, & powder room. As you make your way upstairs, you'll notice the expansive Primary Bedroom complete with lovely en suite, walk-in closet, & office area. There's an additional 2 spacious bedrooms & full hallway bath as well. This amazing home also features an attached 2 car garage, central A/C, hardwood floors, & main level laundry. Finished lower level provides even more living space - perfect use as a rec./playroom, media room, or home gym. Fully functional in-law setup w/ separate entry offers the ultimate space for guests, extended family, or use as an income property. Close proximity to Mead Park, Merritt Parkway, train station, playground, shopping, restaurants, & all that New Canaan has to offer! Don't miss out on this amazing opportunity!!!
