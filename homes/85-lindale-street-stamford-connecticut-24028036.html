About this home

This is a unique one-of-a-kind two-story condo in the historic Lindale Manor, less than a 10 minute walk from Downtown Stamford. The most noticeable feature when you walk into this condo is the view from the large bay windows in the living room. You'll enjoy the layout of the main level which consists of the entire living area of the condo. The roomy eat-in kitchen has plenty of room for a large table and chairs. There is plenty counter space and cabinet storage, including the dry bar area. The main level also features a half bath and laundry room. The upper level is where you'll find the two bedrooms and full bathroom. This level is heated and cooled with a central AC system and the lower level with electric heat and windows AC units. The Lindale Manor community has a total of only 7 condos that share a deck and patio area for all to enjoy. There is one reserved parking space in the driveway and plenty of free parking on the street as well. The HOA fee includes grounds maintenance and snow removal.
