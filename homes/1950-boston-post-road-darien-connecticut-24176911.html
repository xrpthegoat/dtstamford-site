About this home

Beautiful, light-filled apartment in nearly new construction offering refined living with convenient access to Darien beaches and Great Island. This 1-bedroom, 1.5-bath residence spans approximately 1,116 square feet and features a loft located in an elevator building. The open-concept kitchen, dining, and living area is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining, highlighted by cathedral ceilings, abundant natural light, and a gas fireplace. Chef's kitchen features a center island with breakfast bar seating, quartz countertops, premium Bosch appliances, and ample storage. Spacious primary suite includes a walk-in closet and modern full bath with oversized shower. Upstairs loft provides additional flexible living space. Additional amenities include guest half bath, in-unit washer/dryer, basement storage space, and one dedicated parking space with ample guest parking available. Tenant responsible for credit check, liability and personal property insurance, gas heat, electricity, internet/cable, water (separately metered to unit), and hot water. Please inquire regarding pet restrictions. No smoking. Maximum occupancy of three permitted.
