About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath Colonial set on 0.46 acres in sought-after Westover. Freshly painted inside and out, this home features hardwood floors throughout the main level. The first floor offers a spacious living room and an open-concept dining area with French doors leading to the large back deck. The eat-in kitchen is appointed with cherry cabinetry, black granite countertops, and a granite island for casual dining, with an additional French door opening to the deck and level backyard-ideal for entertaining. A conveniently located powder room and attached 2-car garage with a workbench complete the main level. Upstairs features engineered hardwood floors and a large, comfortable family room with a wonderful fireplace. The primary bedroom offers multiple closets, charming window seats, and an en-suite bath with a walk-in shower. Two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath with radiant heat complete the upper level. The unfinished basement provides ample storage space. Outside, the property is accented by a beautifully landscaped front yard with stone walls and mature plantings. This prime location offers city water & sewer, minutes to the city's wonderful golf courses and the Merritt Parkway, a short drive to the train, I-95, Downtown Stamford, and Harbor Point, providing shopping, dining, and entertainment options. Move right in and enjoy all that this special home has to offer!
