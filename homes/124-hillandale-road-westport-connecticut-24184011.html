About this home

An exceptional home in coveted Greens Farms, 124 Hillandale Road captures the essence of classic Connecticut living. Timeless, elegant, and impeccably maintained, this beautiful estate is surrounded by mature landscaping and a peaceful, private setting, while remaining just minutes from everything Westport has to offer. Spanning four finished levels connected by an elevator, the home seamlessly blends classic architecture with modern comfort. Sun-filled living spaces, beautiful millwork, and thoughtfully designed interiors create an atmosphere that is both sophisticated and welcoming. Whether hosting family and friends or enjoying a quiet evening at home, the layout is designed to accommodate every stage of life with ease. Every detail has been carefully considered, from the state-of-the-art movie theater and golf simulator to the fitness and recreation spaces that make everyday living feel a little more special. Outside, beautifully landscaped grounds and picture perfect pool with auto-cover provide the perfect backdrop for relaxing, entertaining, and making lasting memories, with an outdoor fireplace and multiple gathering spaces to enjoy throughout the seasons. Effortlessly elegant, 124 Hillandale Road embodies the very best of Greens Farms living-a home where timeless design and modern amenities come together to create something truly special.
