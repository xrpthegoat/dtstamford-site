About this home

Charming New England colonial on Mayapple Road! This 4-bedroom, 2.2-bath home spans over 4000 sq ft on 1.3 acres. The custom kitchen offers quartz countertops, high-end stainless steel appliances, and a fireplace. Enjoy scenic views of Doughnut Lake from the sliders that open to a screened-in porch and deck. Inside, there's a living room with a fireplace, a formal dining room, and a first-floor office with wainscoting and crown molding. Brand new hardwood floors flow throughout. All bedrooms are on the second floor, including a primary with a full bath and his-and-her closets. Bathrooms are fully remodeled with custom tile and vanities. The finished walkout basement provides a spacious living area with a wet bar, wine cellar, and exercise room. Additional features include a three-car heated garage and a den/second office behind the garage. To complete the package, there's an extra pool room or great room, perfect for entertaining or relaxation. Newer roof and central air units ensure comfort! Don't miss this incredible opportunity!
