About this home

Beautiful, classic South Wilton Colonial with open floorplan in quiet, social, cul de sac neighborhood. Cook your favorite meal in sun-filled, high-end white kitchen with Wolf range, while friends gather at the large island or spread out into the expansive open great room. Step out to the large patio to grill or enjoy cocktails while dining al fresco overlooking the picturesque private level yard. There is an open field to play soccer, lacrosse, football or start an amazing garden. Entertain large groups in the banquet-sized dining room then have dessert by the fireplace in the living room. There is a luxurious first floor primary suite to retire to after a long day with a bath and an extra room for a nursery, office or reading nook. Upstairs there are four additional bedrooms and two full baths. The lower level boasts a separate side entrance with mudroom and large rec room with wet bar. An additional office to work from home, or guest room is also on the lower level with its own entrance. The home has had renovations throughout over the years and has been featured in multiple magazines. The neighborhood is great for running, walking, biking, trick-or-treating and annual block parties. When not feeling social the house and yard are very private. Enjoy all Fairfield County has to offer, as you are minutes to Westport dining and shopping, train to NYC, beaches, trails and award-winning Wilton schools. You will love living here!
