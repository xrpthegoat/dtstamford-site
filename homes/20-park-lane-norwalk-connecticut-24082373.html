About this home

The one you've been waiting for! Charming, vintage Harbor View cottage has just become available after almost 30 years! Fabulous location within this desirable private, waterfront association is just one block from waterfront and Clubhouse with Dock. You enter through a 3-season Enclosed Porch with walls of windows, making a great cozy place to relax. There is a Living Room with a fireplace, Dining Room with many shelves to display your special treasures, and a well-equipped Kitchen leading to the rear Deck. A Utility Room is just off the Deck. Upstairs, you'll find 3 bedrooms and a remodeled full bath. The sunny, spacious backyard with perennials planted along the perimeter, is an ideal spot for summer enjoyment. There is a handy shed at the rear of the property offering extra storage. As a member of the Harbor View Beach Association, you'll enjoy incredible amenities, including a clubhouse with dock, tennis courts, playground, and ball field. Plus, the association handles private road maintenance, community upkeep, snow plowing, and garbage collection. Launch your boat, kayak, or paddleboard, and enjoy the coastal lifestyle. All SONO amenities are close by: restaurants, boutiques, the Maritime Aquarium, and the SONO Collection Mall with Bloomingdale's and Nordstrom. Convenient to Metro North, I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Close proximity to NYC is available at the nearby Metro North SONO railroad station. Schedule your tour today! House being sold "as-is."
