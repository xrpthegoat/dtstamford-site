About this home

Highly Coveted & Desirable 4 Bed/ 2.5 Bath Colonial/Raised Ranch on cul de sac in Woodway Acres neighborhood of Upper Springdale. Beautifully Renovated in 2021 the home features main level open floor plan Kitchen & Great Room w/ Dining Area overlooking private backyard, large patio and in-ground pool plus office, mudroom, powder room & garage. Second floor offers Primary Bedroom Suite plus 3 additional Bedrooms, Full Bathroom & Laundry. Situated in a quiet neighborhood bordering the Noroton River, you're a short walk to school, train, shopping, dining, parks, library, Woodway Country Club and all that Springdale Village has to offer. Just 1-mile to Merritt Pkwy and minutes from Stamford, Darien & New Canaan downtowns. This "hidden gem" will not last, make it yours!
