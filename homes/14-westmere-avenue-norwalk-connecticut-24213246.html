About this home

Set along the tranquil waters of Farm Creek in Rowayton's coveted Bell Island neighborhood, this fully furnished 4-bedroom, 4.5-bath residence offers 4,538 square feet of refined coastal living. Water views, timeless architectural details, and inviting indoor-outdoor spaces define a home designed for both relaxed living and effortless entertaining. The main living spaces overlook the tidal creek, creating a serene backdrop throughout the home. The kitchen is thoughtfully appointed with a professional six-burner range, marble-topped island, white cabinetry, glass-front storage, and a striking tile backsplash. A bay-window breakfast nook captures the water views, while hardwood floors, recessed lighting, and built-in speakers lend a polished finish to the main level. In the game room, French doors open to an expansive deck overlooking the creek-an ideal setting for outdoor dining, sunset gatherings, or quiet mornings by the water. The second floor features an expansive primary suite with a private balcony overlooking the water, creating a serene retreat with a true spa-like feel. The beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom includes a soaking tub, separate walk-in shower, and private water closet. Three additional bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, provide exceptional comfort and privacy for family and guests. A versatile bonus room with an extended window seat and sweeping creek views offers additional flexibility as a private office, fitness room, or media
