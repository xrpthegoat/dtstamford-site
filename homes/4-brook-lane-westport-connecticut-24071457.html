About this home

OH CANCELLED for 3/16 Welcome to this charming 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom colonial nestled on a beautifully landscaped half-acre lot in the coveted Long Lots area of Westport, CT. This spacious residence boasts a MAIN FLOOR PRIMARY SUITE, offering convenience and privacy, with a custom walk in closet and beautiful full spa-like bath with dual sinks, separate tub & shower. The first-floor layout is perfect for both relaxing and entertaining. The home features generous living spaces: including a warm and inviting living room with fireplace, a comfortable family room and a spacious dining area. A cozy eat in kitchen with an adjacent mud room off the garage highlight the first floor. Upstairs boasts three large bedrooms and two full baths which includes one bedroom suite with a full bath which could be used as another primary option. Laundry is also upstairs for convenience. The lower level is a great flex space for a gym, game room or play area with plenty of storage. Situated on half an acre of meticulously manicured grounds, the fully fenced in backyard with expansive deck is ideal for outdoor activities and gatherings. Generator included. Located in a peaceful walkable neighborhood, this property is just minutes away from Westport's vibrant town center, beaches, and shopping. Don't miss the opportunity to make this lovely home yours!
