About this home

Located at the end of a cul-de-sac and perfectly sited on a level .56 property this home is very convenient to downtown Westport, Compo Beach, Longshore and transportation. Features include spacious rooms, renovated baths, family room with access to rear deck, LR w/ FP, DR with sliders to large deck, and kitchen on the main level. The upper level includes the MBR w/ bath and three additional bedrooms with hall bath. The lower level family room has a half bath and a laundry area. Two large decks provide ample space for outdoor entertaining and relaxation, while the flat, spacious backyard adds even more versatility for outdoor activities.
