About this home

If you've been searching for a well-located condo that offers both convenience and privacy, 48 Dean Street is one to see. This 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath unit is part of an intimate 7-unit complex with low common charges, making it an attractive option for both homeowners and investors. The layout is highly functional, featuring a spacious main living area with a half bath on the first floor and seamless access to a private deck through sliding doors-overlooking a tree-lined backdrop that adds a sense of privacy not often found in condo living. The kitchen has been tastefully updated with granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, offering a clean, modern feel that's ready for immediate use. Upstairs, two well-proportioned bedrooms, a full bath, and laundry complete the space. Located in the desirable Cove neighborhood on the Darien border, this property offers exceptional convenience. Enjoy close proximity to Cove Island Park with beach access, and open green space, along with easy access to Chelsea Piers, local restaurants, nightlife, and I-95-making commuting and day-to-day living effortless. Pet-friendly and ideally positioned, this is a strong opportunity to own in a location that continues to see consistent demand.
