About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained townhome offering a stylish and functional layout in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. The main level features an inviting open floor plan with comfortable living space and an updated kitchen with crisp white cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, beautiful countertops, under-cabinet lighting, and ample cabinetry. Step outside to the private deck, a great spot to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind at the end of the day. Upstairs, you'll find a spacious bedroom, full bathroom, convenient laundry closet, and plenty of closet space. Additional highlights include a one car garage beneath the building and a large private storage room. Ideally located close to the Glenbrook Metro-North station, shopping, restaurants, parks, and everyday amenities, this townhouse combines low-maintenance living with convenient access to downtown Stamford and surrounding Fairfield County. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy an updated home with an open layout, private outdoor space, garage parking, low HOA fees and a convenient Glenbrook location.
