About this home

Welcome to 6 Kingsbury Road, an impeccably updated 4BR, 2.5BA split-level residence blending timeless design with modern comfort. Set on a tranquil, tree-lined street near Birchwood Country Club and just minutes from the Westport border, this move-in-ready home features gleaming hardwood floors, vaulted ceilings with skylights, and a chef's kitchen with granite countertops, shaker cabinetry, and stainless-steel appliances. The sunlit living room offers custom-built-ins and charm, while the lower level includes a private suite with a separate entrance, perfect for guests, remote work, or flexible living needs. Freshly painted cabinets, updated lighting, and a versatile lower level make this home ideal for today's lifestyle. Step outside to a wraparound deck overlooking a serene, partially fenced yard, perfect for entertaining or quiet evenings. Additional highlights include a 2-car garage with finished floor, a new radon system, and public utilities. Close to fine dining, golf, shopping, and transit, experience elevated living in a prime Fairfield County location.
