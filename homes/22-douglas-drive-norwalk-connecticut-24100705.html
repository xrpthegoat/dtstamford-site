About this home

Meticulously updated 4 bedroom 2.5 Bath expanded Cape featuring a main floor Primary Suite option, sparkling in-ground pool, over 2,800 Sqft of versatile luxury living. The ideal opportunity awaits, simply move in & enjoy or expand! Perfectly nestled on over an acre of lush grounds in the coveted Flower District of West Norwalk, this home altogether offers the ultimate blend of modern upgrades & convenience! Appreciate a fabulous open layout throughout that lends itself to countless uses of space for each unique lifestyle. The welcoming formal entryway flows into the bright sunny living spaces just perfect for daily enjoyment or entertainment. A brand new eat-in Kitchen w/ gorgeous custom cabinetry equipped w/ granite countertops & stainless steel appliances optimize meal prepping. The charming breakfast nook, highlighted by a beautiful bay window is lovely for casual meals. The oversized Living room and Dining room boasts dual functionality and thoughtful design. The cozy Den/Library features beautiful wooden built ins ideal for reading & relaxing, enjoying as a Game room w/ friends & family, or use as formal Dining room for holiday occasions w/ seamless integration to the additional living spaces. The breathtaking new Family room captivates w/ soaring ceilings, rustic stone fireplace, coffered ceilings, encompassed by stunning glass sliders w/ vistas of the sparkling pool, newly laid slate patio, & manicured fenced grounds for indoor outdoor living at its finest.
