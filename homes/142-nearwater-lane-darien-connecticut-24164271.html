About this home

A rare opportunity to own a new construction home with Deeded Beach Rights on coveted Nearwater Lane in one of Darien's most desirable coastal neighborhoods. A seamless blend of sophisticated design, modern comfort, and coastal living. Moments from Weed Beach, downtown Darien, commuter rail service, and a Blue Ribbon elementary school. Designed for everyday living and entertaining, the home features soaring 10-12-foot ceilings, open-concept floor plan, and abundant natural light. Andersen A-Series windows, glass railings, refined finishes create bright, elegant living spaces. The bluestone patio provides an inviting outdoor retreat.The flexible layout includes four ensuite bedrooms, a luxurious primary suite featuring a spacious walk-in closet, spa-inspired bath, and private balcony with water views. A first-floor bedroom with private access and a finished lower level with a separate entrance offer versatility for guests, extended family, or a home office.At the heart of the home, a chef's kitchen is anchored by an oversized quartz island and appointed with Sub-Zero, Wolf, Cove, and Kohler fixtures. Additional highlights include second-floor laundry, a detached garage, pre-wired audio and security systems, underground utilities, whole-house generator connection, and modern mechanical systems.An exceptional opportunity to enjoy the convenience of new construction in one of Darien's premier locations. Deeded Noroton Bay Beach rights; buyers to verify.
