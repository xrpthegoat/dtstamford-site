About this home

Central Greenwich off Milbank Ave is this Putnam Park first floor, garden corner unit. Beautifully updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances and laundry. Redone bath (with walk- in shower). There are refinished hardwood floors, energy efficient windows & plenty of closets. The primary is spacious & the second bedroom could be den/office. Easy outside access with no steps via lovely terrace. Over the patio is an electric awning. This in-town community has 12+ manicured acres & features convenient parking. Indoor garage space available for $85/month. Bonus: additional storage in basement. Monthly maintenance includes heat, hot water, trash & snow removal, landscaping & outdoor parking. Pet friendly complex. Close to Whole Foods, Greenwich Ave, train, shops, restaurants and schools.
