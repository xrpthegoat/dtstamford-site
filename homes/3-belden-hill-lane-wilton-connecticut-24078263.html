About this home

*Highest and Best Deadline Tuesday, 5pm* Steeped in history and brimming with charm, this pre-Revolutionary 1770 antique home seamlessly blends period details with modern comforts. Known at one point in time as the 'Gregory Homestead', this home is set on a breathtaking 2.39-acre lot of open yard and woodlands. This two-bedroom, one-bathroom gem offers warmth and character at every turn. Large-plank hardwood floors, exposed beams, and multiple wood-burning fireplaces create an inviting atmosphere, while beautiful French doors lead from the living room to a private patio overlooking the serene backyard--your own private nature reserve. A cozy den with its own fireplace, a small yet functional kitchen with granite counters, and an updated bathroom upstairs add to the home's appeal. The original horse barn adds a touch of rustic nostalgia, and a whole-house generator ensures peace of mind. Located just one minute from Wilton's charming downtown and with easy access to major routes, this rare and remarkable home is a timeless retreat in an unbeatable location. Experience the magic of historic living in Wilton!
