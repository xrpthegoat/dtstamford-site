About this home

Nestled at the end of a cul-de-sac in Rowayton, Norwalk's beloved coastal village, this beautifully renovated ranch offers easy, turnkey living with room to spread out. Set on 0.46 ac and upgraded top-to-bottom (interior to exterior) through 12/25, the home delivers 2,264 sq ft of updated style, comfort, and modern convenience. Designed for seamless single-level living, the main floor features a refined open layout with hardwood floors, a sunlit living dining area anchored by a cozy fireplace, an upgraded kitchen, three serene bedrooms, two full baths with heated tile floors, and laundry room. The fully finished LL expands the way you live, with a family/playroom, office, gym/bedroom, full bath, storage, and second laundry room, ideal for guests, hobbies, work-from-home, or multi-generational needs. Enjoy the indoor-outdoor flow to a professionally landscaped yard with views of a neighboring pond, perfect for morning coffee and entertaining. Completing the lifestyle, an updated garage with great storage, a he/she shed, and access to all Rowayton's signature amenities, community events, summer concerts, paddle, tennis, pickleball, beach days and boating, winter polar plunges, saunas, arts, dining, and easy Metro-North/I-95 commuting. A rare opportunity for effortless one-floor living with flexible space below, polished, turnkey, and inviting is ready to welcome you to Rowayton's lifestyle.
