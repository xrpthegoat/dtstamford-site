About this home

Lovingly maintained by the same family for 54 years, this charming 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers the perfect blend of seclusion and convenience. Set back at the end of a cul-de-sac, it is a private, tranquil oasis. Thoughtful updates include a newer roof, gorgeous new kitchen cabinetry, fresh paint, and new lower-level flooring. An expansive yard and two stunning decks invite you to relax and soak in the outdoors, while an abundance of windows bathes every room in warm, natural light. Whether you're hosting, unwinding, or simply enjoying the peace and quiet, this home delivers. Move in as-is or take advantage of incredible expansion possibilities. Conveniently located near major transportation, top-rated schools, DeCicco's, shops, restaurants, and parks. A rare find - ready for its next chapter.
