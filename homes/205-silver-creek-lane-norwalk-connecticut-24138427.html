About this home

Incredible opportunity at this free standing 3 bedroom, 2.5 bath condominium at The Falls at Silvermine. The welcoming 26 home community includes a gorgeous in-ground pool. The home was completely and stylishly renovated in 2023. The foyer leads to a large living room offering a vaulted ceiling, wood burning fireplace, and a 25 foot curved atrium window facing the woods. The spacious kitchen features white cabinets, quartzite counters, stainless steel appliances and stone backsplash. The main level also includes a formal dining room, office/den, powder room and sliders to decks. Upstairs, the primary suite has vaulted ceilings, a walk-in closet and a large bath with double vanity, oversized shower, soaking tub ands stylish fixtures and tile. The 2nd and 3rd bedrooms share another beautifully remodeled bath with large shower and a skylight. The unfinished, walk-out basement brings an opportunity for another 1400 sq/ft of space with a slider and windows already in place. 2 car detached garage. Hardwood floors. Incredible location near shops, dining, golf and easy access to Routes 15 and 7
