About this home

MULTIPLE OFFERS HAVE BEEN RECEIVED - SELLER IS REQUESTING ALL OFFERS BE SUBMITTED BY MONDAY, APRIL 20 AT 8PM. Filled with natural light from large, plentiful windows and featuring two fireplaces, this lovely 3-5 bedroom, 2-bath home blends authentic New England character with thoughtful updates in a peaceful pondside setting close to town and schools. Originally an 1800s home that was carefully deconstructed and rebuilt at its current location in 1952, the property retains beautiful antique details, including historic wood paneling and charming architectural elements. Inviting living spaces include a spacious family room addition and a second-floor sitting area. Flexible bonus rooms offer options for a nursery, home office, guest or au pair space, or potential primary-suite expansion. Main-level laundry, mudroom/office, attached garage, and central A/C provide everyday convenience. Set on a serene 0.80-acre lot, the property features multiple outdoor living areas for entertaining and relaxing, including a welcoming front porch, a firepit gathering space, and seating areas overlooking the picturesque pond and seasonal brook. Spring brings blooming daffodils and irises along the water's edge, enhancing the natural beauty of the setting. Backing to a private lane with a warm neighborhood feel and located close to Wilton's town center, schools, and the Norwalk River walking trail, this home offers a rare combination of historic charm, natural beauty, and convenience.
