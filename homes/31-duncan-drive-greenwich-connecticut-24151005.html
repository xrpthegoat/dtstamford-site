About this home

First time on the market, welcome to the highly desirable Bailiwick Association. This original 1969 Perry Duncan-designed Cape offers timeless architecture and endless possibilities to make it your own. The home features 4 bedrooms and 3.2 bathrooms with an open floor plan, a true main-floor primary suite, generously sized rooms, and a covered rear terrace ideal for indoor-outdoor living. A partially finished lower level adds flexibility, while the property is beautifully sited behind a classic stone wall on 1.07 level acres with expansion potential. Enjoy summer to the fullest with the 2023 custom in-ground swimming pool and the peace of mind provided by a new generator. Set in a vibrant, sought-after neighborhood, this home offers the perfect balance of privacy and convenience-just minutes to the thriving Glenville downtown, Greenwich Avenue, Metro-North, and an easy commute to New York City. A true neighborhood setting in an unbeatable location. There's truly no place else you'll want to be.
