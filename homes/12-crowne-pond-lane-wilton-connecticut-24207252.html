About this home

Easy living and convenient commuting converge at 12 Crowne Pond Lane, a beautifully updated and meticulously maintained home. Tucked away in an exclusive enclave of just three homes, this 4 bedroom, 3 bath single-family sanctuary combines true privacy with the benefits of a maintenance-free planned community. The main level offers a fluid, open-concept floor plan designed for effortless entertaining. At its heart is a bright white kitchen featuring granite countertops and stainless steel appliances, seamlessly connecting to the family room with a cozy wood-burning fireplace and sliders opening to an expanse of back deck. Upstairs, the spacious primary suite serves as a quiet retreat, complete with a generous walk-in closet and a spa-inspired bath highlighted by marble surfaces, double sinks, a soaking tub, and a custom marble waterfall tile shower. Three additional bedrooms, a second full bath, and dedicated upper-level laundry complete the space, while a finished lower level provides flexible room for a gym, office, or media center. Complete with city utilities, a private lawn, and close proximity to Wilton Center, top schools, and train station, this home offers the ultimate suburban lifestyle.
