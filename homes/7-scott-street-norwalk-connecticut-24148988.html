About this home

7 Scott Street sits in a convenient Norwalk location near shops, dining, schools, and major commuting routes. The home offers 3 bedrooms with wall-to-wall carpeting, including a primary bedroom with an en suite tiled bath. Living and dining rooms feature hardwood floors. The kitchen includes a skylight and opens to a deck for easy indoor-outdoor living. A second-floor room adds flexible space and features hardwood floors. Attic space includes a rough-in and offers potential for a 4th bedroom, subject to town approvals and all independent diligence. Minutes from Norwalk amenities and commuter access, this is a solid opportunity for an owner-occupant or investor seeking value and location.
