About this home

Rare opportunity to own a unit in the desirable Fawn Ridge complex on Wilton school system side, conveniently located to highways and shopping! This end-unit townhome offers 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, gorgeous eat-in kitchen with granite countertops, and slider to spacious deck for outdoor dining. Large living room with wood burning fireplace with a window bay perfect for reading a book. Upstairs are two large bedrooms and a large bathroom with whirlpool tub and walk-in closet. Bonus finished room in basement can be an office, gym, play or rec room. Garage is deep and offers space for storage as well as a standard SUV. Hardwood throughout the townhome, sun-lit windows, beautiful paint choices, low HOA fee, nothing to do but move in and enjoy the pool, tennis courts and everything South Wilton offers!
