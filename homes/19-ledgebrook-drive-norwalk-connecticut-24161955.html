About this home

Rarely available and truly exceptional, this sun-drenched corner townhouse at 19 Ledgebrook Drive offers the largest 3-bedroom floorplan in the complex along with a level of style, scale, and updating that is hard to find. Soaring ceilings, dramatic architectural lines, oversized windows, and beautiful natural light create an airy, elevated feel throughout the home. The living spaces are both warm and sophisticated, with rich hardwoods, exposed beams, a striking fireplace, and an effortless flow that makes everyday living and entertaining feel special. The bedrooms are generously sized, including a stunning primary retreat, while the beautifully updated baths and thoughtfully improved interiors reflect pride of ownership at every turn. Recent upgrades include whole-house replacement windows, updated bathrooms, a replaced central air system, new front and balcony doors, hardwood flooring in the bedrooms, refinished existing hardwood floors, custom blinds and Roman shades, reverse osmosis water system, new washing machine, water heater, and more. This is a premium, move-in-ready home for the buyer who values space, light, design, and quality in one of West Norwalk's most desirable townhouse settings.
