About this home

This sophisticated contemporary home offers an airy design filled with natural sunlight and striking architectural details that beautifully capture the surrounding landscape. Vaulted tongue-and-groove ceilings and an open floor plan create a seamless flow, while the dramatic living room is anchored by a floor-to-ceiling brick fireplace and a wall of sliders leading to an expansive deck with scenic, panoramic views. The dining room connects effortlessly to a thoughtfully designed kitchen with a distinctive mid-century modern aesthetic, ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The generous primary bedroom suite serves as a private retreat, featuring serene views and a screened-in porch, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing weekends while taking in breathtaking sunsets. A spacious office with oversized windows makes WFH easy. The lower level offers two additional bedrooms and a spacious family room with patio access, providing excellent indoor-outdoor living and entertaining opportunities. A cozy fireplace creates the perfect setting for games, gatherings, or quiet evenings at home. A full bath and laundry/utility room, complete the lower level. Unique and inviting, this exceptional home seamlessly brings the outdoors inside, offering peace, tranquility, and ever-changing seasonal views from nearly every room. Experience nature, comfort, and contemporary living in one spectacular setting.
