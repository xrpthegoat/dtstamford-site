About this home

Discover this updated gem set majestically on a hilltop, surrounded by mature trees and beautiful landscaping. With almost 7,000 SF of living space, this secluded 4-acre retreat is nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac.Entering the spacious foyer, one is drawn to the beautifully appointed living room, perfect for holidays and entertaining. The light-filled sunroom beyond has patio access and doubles as a private office space. The formal dining room leads to a chef's eat-in-kitchen with a large center island and a window framing the lush perennial gardens and a wisteria-covered pergola. Off the kitchen is a cozy family room with a fireplace, which then opens to a second at-home office. The back staircase connects to a spacious entertainment room with a big screen TV and vaulted ceilings. There are 5 well-proportioned bedrooms on the second floor. The primary suite is a sanctuary with a sitting area, 2 walk-in closets, and a sun-filled bathroom with a steam shower and freestanding tub. There is an open area with glass whiteboards that is suitable for workstations or a relaxing read. The finished lower level is set up for a workout in the gym or private movie watching. Outside, the large patio and heated pool create a quiet oasis for entertaining or dining alfresco day or night. An expansive sports field completes this breathtaking property. Welcome to serenity! Convenient to New Canaan's great shops and restaurants and the delightful hamlet of Scotts Corners.
