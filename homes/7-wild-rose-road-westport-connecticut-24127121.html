About this home

Every plank, beam, and finish in this 3BR, 2.5 bath was selected with intention. A masterclass in design with warmth, quality, and craftsmanship in every detail, and finishes typically reserved for homes at much higher price points. Reimagined by Alex Brandfon, founder of ZIBA Hardwoods, the home reflects a vision of luxury and ease, where reclaimed character and modern precision come together seamlessly. Taken down to the foundation, with only portions of the exterior walls remaining, this home is essentially brand new. At 1,800 SF, it lives larger than its footprint, with a layout that's open, airy, and light-filled. Wide-plank flooring, reclaimed beams, and lofted ceilings feel like a work of art. The kitchen is a showpiece with rift-sawn white oak cabinetry, a painted shiplap ceiling with exposed beams, and a Calacatta marble island that invites gathering and flows to the dining room. The living room centers around a gas fireplace framed in stone creating a cozy yet sophisticated focal point. Fabulous first-floor office, mudroom with custom built-ins, covered porch, landscaped gardens and spacious bluestone patio. Upstairs, retreat to the vaulted primary suite w/ spa-like bath and walk-in closet. Two additional bedrooms, full bath & laundry complete the level. The garage offers EV hookup and is generator-ready. Quiet street within short walking distance to town, this home offers convenience and privacy. More than a renovation, it's a transformation at its finest.
