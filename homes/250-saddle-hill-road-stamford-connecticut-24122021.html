About this home

Nestled in the rolling hills of North Stamford, this celebrity French Country Colonial is a masterpiece of artistry and history. Offering 4 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, and a serene cul-de-sac location, this exceptional home pairs panoramic views from all windows with a level of craftsmanship rarely found today. Step inside and be transported to the French countryside with interiors curated by Howard Kaplan, the renowned founder of French Country design. Every detail tells a story: hand-stenciled walls and ceilings, 19th-century doors and tiles sourced from England and France, and six etched glass doors from a Parisian salon. Even the front door hails from an English manor house in the Lake District. The striking cedar Mansard roof completes the authentic European aesthetic. The grounds are equally enchanting. Spread across 1.5 acres of lush, landscaped privacy, the property features extensive stonework, multiple outdoor seating areas of the neighboring pond, waterfall and landscaping designed for all-year interest. There is an in-ground pool, and a separate storybook guest house. Once a music studio, the guest space boasts an open-concept layout with a stone fireplace, full kitchen, and bath - perfect for creative retreats or hosting guests.
