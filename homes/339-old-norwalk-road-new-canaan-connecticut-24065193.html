About this home

Welcome home! This impeccably renovated New Canaan estate embellishes the modern luxury of today's standards while grasping the classic elegance and architecture of the period in which this home was built. The gorgeous grounds features professionally manicured landscaped with an abundance of stonework and gracious iron fencing. Luxury amenities include a custom heated pool , built in outdoor kitchen, bluestone terrace, two porches one covered the other one is screened in to complete the multitude outdoor living spaces. Enter into the home through this inviting foyer that will certainly make an impression to your most discerning guests. Impressive design and architectural details are embellished through out this home. This 5 bedroom home offers over 4100 square feet of living space on the first and second floor. The primary suite boasts an expansive foot print which includes vaulted ceilings, walls of windows, floor to ceiling paneling and a large sitting room and marble bath. Four more bedrooms are included on this floor with a reading nook to greet you at the top of the stairs. Finished lower level creates additional entertaining space. Nothing has been left unturned. A unique opportunity awaits as this is a one of a kind truly special property.
