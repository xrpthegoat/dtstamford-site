About this home

Nestled on 1 beautifully landscaped acre, this charming 3 bed, 2 bath one level home is a perfect blend of charm and modern design. From the foyer step into the spacious & modern open concept living, dining and kitchen rooms. The light filled living room with inviting fireplace includes an oversized picture window that showcases stunning property views. Adjacent, a warm family room / library offers equally beautiful views and a calming area to read and unwind. The open kitchen includes handsome granite counters, spacious dining area, and deluxe appliances. The primary bedroom is a wonderful retreat with beautiful views, large closets, and en suite bath with shower, tub, and vanity. The second and third bedrooms are perfect for family, guests or could also double as an additional den/office. Completing the home is the wonderful level lot with specimen trees, gardens, and handsome stone walls. Ideally located just minutes from the most charming New England village, New Canaan, known for its shopping, library, theater, outstanding restaurants, train and parks.
