About this home

Cute as a button this Nicely updated 2 bedroom unit on the first floor is at the end of the cul-de-sac. Open Living room, dining room & kitchen. Mud room at front entrance. Updated kit & bath. Washer & dryer in basement to use and share with other tenants. There is also storage in the basement. This adorable unit is just down the street with a 5 minute walk to Cove Island Beach/park as well as Terry Conners skating rink. Very close to Chelsea Piers. Great location with a few minutes to I-95, and 5 minute ride to downtown Stamford and the train station. Credit score of 700 or better required. Parking in the driveway or front of the house.
