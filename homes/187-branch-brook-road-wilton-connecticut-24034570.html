About this home

Welcome to 187 Branch Brook Road, a charming 4-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom Cape Colonial nestled in the heart of Wilton, CT. This freshly painted home, lovingly maintained by the same owner for 45 years, exudes timeless elegance and potential. Step inside to find hardwood floors that flow throughout the home, offering a warm and inviting atmosphere. The solid bones of this house present a fantastic opportunity to personalize and create your dream home. The freshly painted interior and exterior give this home a clean slate ready for your creative touch. The spacious living areas and two cozy fireplaces are perfect for both relaxing and entertaining, with ample natural light highlighting the home's classic charm. Surrounded by nature and gorgeous gardens this home is located directly across the street from the entrance to the Wilton Town Forest with its abundance of hiking trails, offering the perfect blend of convenience and outdoor adventure. Imagine stepping out your front door and onto miles of scenic trails, ideal for hiking, biking, and nature walks. Electrical has been upgraded to 200 Amp, there is a whole home GENERATOR, driveway was recently repaved and the roof was replaced just 8 years ago. This cheerful home has been well loved and maintained. Don't miss this incredible opportunity to envision the endless possibilities and create lasting memories in this beloved home.
