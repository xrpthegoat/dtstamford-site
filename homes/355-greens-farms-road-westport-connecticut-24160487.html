About this home

An exceptional home in Westport's Greens Farms neighborhood, 355 Greens Farms Rd showcases refined scale, flexibility, and location. Four beaches are clustered within roughly a 2.5-3 mile radius, making the property 5-10 minutes to multiple beaches. This impressive residence delivers expansive living with thoughtfully designed spaces suited for modern lifestyles. Offered as a fully furnished summer rental, the property presents a truly turnkey experience. High-quality furnishings enhance the home's scale and character, allowing immediate enjoyment without the need for additional setup. The home features five to six generously sized bedrooms, allowing the layout to adapt effortlessly to a variety of needs. Two dedicated home offices provide polished, private environments ideal for focused work or creative use. Throughout the residence, abundant natural light and well-balanced proportions elevate the sense of comfort and openness. Living areas are designed for both grand entertaining and everyday ease, with multiple spaces that flow seamlessly while maintaining definition and purpose. The home's size allows for elegant furnishing, ample storage, and the ability to enjoy each room without compromise. Outdoor dining on the expansive deck overlooking the pool & spa provides relaxing summer evenings. Ideally positioned, the residence is close to the Greens Farms train station and major highways.
