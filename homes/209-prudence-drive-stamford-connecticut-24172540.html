About this home

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac on a level half-acre, this lovingly maintained home has been cherished by the same family for the past 30 years. Set in a peaceful, private setting, it's hard to believe you're just moments from everything Stamford has to offer! The main level features three generously sized bedrooms, hardwood floors throughout, and a spacious living room with cathedral ceilings. The dining room opens through sliders to an expansive deck which is perfect for entertaining on warm summer evenings. The lower level offers exceptional flexibility with a large great room featuring a fireplace and built-in cabinetry, sliders to the level backyard, an additional bedroom, full bath, and a small kitchenette ideal for guests, extended family, or entertaining. Additional highlights include natural gas, city water, and city sewer. Quiet, convenient, and ideally located, this is truly one of the best values on the market. Property is being sold as-is, inspections are for informational purposes only.
