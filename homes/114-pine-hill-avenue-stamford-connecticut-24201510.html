About this home

Exceptional opportunity to own a spacious two-family offering impressive layouts, outdoor living and tremendous future potential. The 1st-floor unit spans approximately 1,365 sq. ft. with 3 bedrooms, 1 full bath, hardwood floors, a large living room with fireplace, formal dining room and well-sized kitchen. A huge screened-in porch and adjoining deck extend the living space and overlook the yard. Upstairs, the expansive 2-bedroom, 1-bath unit features hardwood floors, an oversized eat-in kitchen with pantry, remarkably large bedrooms, excellent closet space and a flexible living room offering potential as a 3rd bedroom. Adding significant value is the 1,300+ sq. ft. walkout lower level with multiple rooms, storage, laundry for both units and direct exterior access-ideal for future finished living space or a potential in-law setup. Separate gas and electric utilities and ample off-street parking add convenience for owners and tenants alike. Ideally located near the Glenbrook train station and the restaurants, cafes and neighborhood conveniences along Hope Street. A rare combination of space, versatility, location and untapped potential!
