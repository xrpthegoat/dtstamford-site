About this home

BEST&FINAL by 1 pm Monday 10/7/24. See more at www.139TurnerRdStamfordCT.com. Beautiful, fully remodeled and renovated, home provides a treasure trove of style with the finest materials and exquisite finishes. Balance of high-end modernization while preserving the charm of a Cape Cod gem. This home feels like walking into a magazine layout. Solid hickory hard wood floors in foyer, living room (with whimsical color pops, built-ins and a beautiful stone fireplace) and the large, stylish dining room. Huge kitchen with high-level stainless appliances, custom cabinetry, glass tiled backsplash, quartz countertop and gorgeous porcelain floors. Beverage center and new, stacked, full-size washer and dryer. Attached 2 car garage (with a pull-down attic) is finished to allow use as a party barn with access to the park-like, ample, fenced yard. Magnificent sunroom boasting custom automated shades, porcelain flooring and French doors leading to the large, enclosed deck. A main level suite (used as 4th bedroom) with a brand-new full bath, wide plank wood floors and office/sitting space. Lower level with rec room, second washer and dryer and ample storage. Upper level, with hardwood floors throughout (including under the new carpeting). Large primary suite has its own spa-like bathroom and custom walk-in closet. Two more bedrooms, with great closets. Luxurious third bathroom with a whirlpool tub. Sited on a lovely street in the sought after Newfield/Pepper Ridge area, 3 miles to downtown.
