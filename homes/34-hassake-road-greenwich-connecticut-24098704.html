About this home

Totally gut renovated short term summer rental in wonderful Old Greenwich neighborhood. This 4 Br/ 3 1/2 bath house is meticulously maintained and decorated with beautiful furnishings. Short drive to beach, train, Binney park and village, restaurants. Whole house renovation/addition recently complete. Chefs kitchen with 48 inch range and walk in pantry which opens up into the living room and cozy reading nook. A wet bar sits next to chic formal dining room. Primary bedroom suite with walk in shower, soaker tub, heated floors and two additional bedrooms, bathroom on second floor. First floor has an office/ bedroom and full bathroom. Outdoor area features a deck, fire pit on lower patio and an outdoor shower for those beach days, trampoline. Finished Walkout basement/ tv room.
