About this home

Perched up high amongst natural stone and foliage, privately situated amongst Connecticut's most stunning and historic towns, this beautiful home was meticulously modernized from top to bottom! Boasting items like a completely new septic system built for a 4 bedroom house for expansion possibilities (40K), a new well pump (20K) a state of the art kitchen (100K) featuring a hide away charging port directly in the island, and an ergonomic pull out Lazy Susan! Enjoy your breakfast coffee or dine Al Fresco right off the kitchen on the just finished expansive outdoor stone patio! A complete overhaul was also done to the exterior underground drainage for proper water flow keeping heavy rains away from the home. On winter nights, enjoy the luxurious warmth of radiant floor heating below your feet and an electric heat producing ambient multi colored fireplace. All this modernization and open floorplan for ease of living, yet the home still feels classic, quaint, and cozy! Indulge in the quiet privacy that 1.14 acres of land provides!
