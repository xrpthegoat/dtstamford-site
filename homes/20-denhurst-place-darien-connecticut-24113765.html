About this home

Welcome to 20 Denhurst Place - a lovingly maintained Colonial proudly owned by the same family since it was built. Perfectly situated on a quiet private street in highly sought-after Darien, this inviting home combines comfort, convenience, and timeless charm. Enjoy a premier location just a short walk to Holmes Elementary School and the Glenbrook Train Station, with easy access to I-95, the Merritt Parkway, and the brand-new Darien Commons shopping and dining center and Noroton Heights Train Station (Darien). Sunlight fills the home's well-designed spaces, showcasing beautiful hardwood floors throughout. The spacious family room features a cozy wood-burning fireplace and opens directly to the screened porch - perfect for morning coffee, reading, or summer entertaining. The updated kitchen boasts granite counters and newer stainless-steel appliances. Upstairs, the serene primary suite offers a walk-in closet and beautiful treetop views. The level lot provides a private, usable backyard with room to play or garden. PLUS, attached two-car garage, CENTRAL AIR, GAS heat and stove. A full unfinished basement with high ceilings provides endless potential for a playroom, gym, workshop, or creative retreat. Laundry and a powder room conveniently adjoin the kitchen area. A rare opportunity to own a cherished home in a coveted neighborhood with award-winning schools and unbeatable access to everything Darien has to offer. Don't miss out - it won't last long.
