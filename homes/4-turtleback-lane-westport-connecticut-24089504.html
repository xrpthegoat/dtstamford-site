About this home

Walk to Winslow Park, Trader Joes, Gold's Deli and many convenient amenities from this welcoming home in the heart of Westport. Located on a quiet cul-de-sac, this home features a 2-story Entry which invites you into an open floor plan with modern accents. The updated Kitchen with breakfast bar and stainless appliances, opens to a fantastic deck overlooking the pretty .50 acre property. A sunny Living Room with stone fireplace and oversized bay window adjoins the Dining Area with its wall of windows. The Primary Bedroom, with a private Bath, plus 2 additional Bedrooms and an updated Hall Bath complete this floor. You will find the 4th Bedroom on the Garden Level, as well as the Family Room with fireplace, which accesses the backyard. Two car garage, natural gas heat and city water add to the appeal of the lovely home.
