About this home

Beautifully renovated and move-in ready, this charming 3 bedroom, 2.5-bath home is tucked away at the end of a quiet street in the heart of vibrant South Norwalk, offering added privacy and minimal through traffic. Updated in 2024, the home combines modern comfort with timeless charm and features hardwood floors, central air, an updated kitchen with newer appliances, and a bright, welcoming layout throughout. The main level offers a spacious primary bedroom with a full en-suite bath, along with a convenient half bath for guests. Upstairs, you'll find two additional bedrooms and a full bath. The finished lower level adds valuable space perfect for a home office, gym, playroom, media room, or cozy recreation area, plus laundry hookups and additional storage. While the lower level features lower ceiling height, it offers great versatility and functionality for everyday use and includes a separate walk-out entrance providing direct access to the backyard. Enjoy being just minutes from South Norwalk's restaurants, shopping, waterfront, Metro-North train station, and I-95, making commuting and daily living easy and convenient. Additional highlights include an in-ground sprinkler system and invisible electric pet fence. The corner lot may offer potential for a future garage with access from either Avenue A or Couch Street, subject to verification and municipal approvals.
