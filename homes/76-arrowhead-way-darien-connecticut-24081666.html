About this home

Nestled within the highly coveted Tokeneke Association, this custom-designed English country manor embodies understated elegance and timeless craftsmanship. Ideally located near Scott's Cove and featuring a private dock, the five-bedroom residence was created with meticulous attention to detail throughout. An impressive foyer sets the tone, revealing the dining room's classic period charm and a richly paneled hallway that leads to an exquisite private office. The expansive family room, highlighted by soaring ceilings, flows seamlessly into a finely crafted gourmet kitchen, a newly designed mudroom, and a well-appointed guest suite. The second floor is anchored by an oversized primary suite with a custom walk-in closet and spa like bath, complemented by three additional on suite bedrooms and an oversized great room. Impeccably appointed throughout, this exceptional home offers a refined balance of sophistication and comfort, perfectly suited for both elegant entertaining and everyday living.
