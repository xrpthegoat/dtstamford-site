About this home

Welcome to modern luxury and easy living in downtown Stamford. Flooded with light throughout, this beautiful, move-in-ready condo features one of the building's most expansive floor plans, uniquely offering a large living room which boasts dramatic sixteen-plus-foot ceilings. On the main level, you will find an open-concept kitchen and dining area, providing access to your own private patio. Two large bedrooms, in-unit laundry, as well as two nicely updated bathrooms complete this level. Upstairs, a flexible loft features two distinct rooms that are perfect for a home office, guest bedroom, or a secondary lounge. Situated in a premier, pet-friendly building, residents enjoy first-class amenities including an on-site concierge, state-of-the-art fitness center, courtyard with grills and picnic tables, and secure garage parking with direct elevator access. Positioned in the heart of it all, you are just a few steps from Mill River Park and 5 minutes from I-95, MetroNorth, and the vibrant dining and nightlife of Bedford Street and Landmark Square, making this home perfect for commuters and anyone looking for convenient, urban living at its best.
