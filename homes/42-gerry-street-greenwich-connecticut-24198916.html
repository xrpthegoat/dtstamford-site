About this home

Welcome to 42 Gerry Street, a thoughtfully updated two-family property where the big-ticket improvements are already checked off the list, so you can spend more time enjoying the investment and less time making repair calls. Both units were renovated within the last two years, with the upper-level unit receiving the most recent refresh, including a brand-new dishwasher, newer appliances, and washer/dryer. The ground-level unit offers 3 bedrooms and 1 bath, while the upper unit provides an updated space ideal for extended living or an owner-occupant setup. A new roof, installed in winter 2025 and backed by a 50-year transferable warranty, joins a new boiler and water heater. In other words, the unglamorous but very important work has already been done. Conveniently located near I-95, the Merritt Parkway, NYC trains, and public transportation, 42 Gerry Street makes commuting simple and ownership refreshingly straightforward. A smart property with the updates, location, and flexibility to match.
