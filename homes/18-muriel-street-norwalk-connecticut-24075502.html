About this home

Tastefully renovated colonial in the heart of Wolfpit. Newer kitchen with granite counter tops, refinished hardwood floors throughout, new windows, and freshly painted interior. Open floor plan, ideal for entertaining friends and family. The exterior offers a spacious deck for backyard BBQ's and overlooks a fabulous yard - perfect for play and relaxation. This home has solar panels which are a plus and help save money as well. Landlord will be responsible for the monthly solar panel rental cost. Tenant is responsible to maintain an electrical delivery account and be responsible for any usage in excess of what the solar panels generate. It can be with Eversource or another carrier. Great location for commuters with quick access to I-95 and East Norwalk train station. This storybook home is move in ready and just waiting for you!
