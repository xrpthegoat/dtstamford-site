About this home

Luxury Living, Exceptional Convenience & Effortless Style! Welcome to this beautifully designed and thoughtfully finished 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home in the desirable Springdale neighborhood. Ideally located within walking distance of the train, shops, restaurants, and neighborhood amenities, this exceptional home offers a lifestyle of comfort and convenience. The stunning chef's kitchen flows seamlessly into the formal dining room and out to the deck, creating the perfect setting for both intimate dinners and effortless entertaining. The inviting living room, complete with a gas fireplace, is ideal for relaxing, enjoying a movie, or gathering for the big game. Retreat to the luxurious primary suite, featuring a spa-inspired shower and warm tiled floors-a peaceful escape at the end of the day. Three additional bedrooms offer generous space and natural light, while the conveniently located bedroom-level laundry makes everyday living effortless. The finished lower level provides flexible space for recreation, a home office, gym, or media room. Outside, enjoy a level, private backyard perfect for relaxing and entertaining. An attached one-car garage and numerous thoughtful amenities complete this exceptional home. Need furniture? The home is also available for rent fully furnished. Come experience stylish, turnkey living in one of Springdale's most convenient locations!
