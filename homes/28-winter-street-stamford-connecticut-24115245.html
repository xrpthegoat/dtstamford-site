About this home

Charming Cape Cod home at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Stamford's desirable Mid-Ridges. Offering 4 bedrooms and 2 full baths, this home features an open kitchen and living area that flows into a spacious dining room, perfect for everyday living and entertaining. A large patio and deck with built-in seating overlook the fenced-in backyard, creating an ideal outdoor space. Additional highlights include a one-car attached garage, ample basement storage, and a brand-new roof. Conveniently located just minutes from the Merritt Parkway, shopping, dining, and with easy access to downtown Stamford, this home combines comfort, charm, and convenience.
