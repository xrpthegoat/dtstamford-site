About this home

Completely renovated Nantucket-inspired cottage with a cobblestone apron and crushed oyster shell driveway available for Yearly or Academic rental. 90 steps from Compo Beach! Easy flowing Living/dining room with exposed distressed beams and oversized windows maximizing water views from almost every room. Cook's kitchen w Thermador appliances, a coffee bar and all the accessories you could wish for. Large deck off the kitchen with a fire pit table & a Weber grill for BBQs whenever you like. Deep manicured lawn w sprinkler system, outdoor shower and bonus studio w Wifi possible office or gym. Second floor includes a Master bedroom with private deck with sectional & fire pit to enjoy spectacular sunsets and another bedroom with two twin beds. Lawn service and housekeeper maintained by owner. Outdoor shower. Westport also has a municipal country club called Longshore, which offers a newly renovated 18-hole golf course, tennis and paddleboard courts, two swimming pools, and a sailing school (with boat, kayak, and canoe rentals). Enjoy water community living at its best! 10 minutes to Metro North (commuter bus available) and I95. Available Sept 1 2026
