About this home

Ideally located in the heart of Westport and close to all schools, this exceptional Contemporary Ranch has been thoughtfully renovated, offering one-level living with effortless sophistication. A welcoming foyer leads to a stunning kitchen, appointed with a generous center island, heated floors, premium appliances and an elegant coffee station with dedicated water supply. The kitchen opens seamlessly into an expansive great room-the architectural centerpiece of the home-featuring a dining area, gas fireplace, cathedral ceilings, and dramatic walls of glass that flood the space with natural light. Designed for year-round entertaining private patio with a built-in heated seating area, overlooking a picturesque aerated pond and tranquil natural surroundings-blurring the line between indoor and outdoor living. Bamboo root barrier is installed. A separate family room with a wood-burning fireplace offers an inviting retreat and flexible space for work, relaxation, or quiet gatherings. The luxurious primary suite includes sliders to the backyard and a sun-filled spa-inspired bath with dual vanities, a deep soaking tub, oversized shower, and serene finishes. Steam room adds a resort-level touch. Three additional bedrooms are generously proportioned and feature custom built-in closets. Private office provides an ideal work-from-home environment. This "google smart home" delivers effortless living with natural gas, city water and sewer, two-car garage with extra storage,3 cars carport.
