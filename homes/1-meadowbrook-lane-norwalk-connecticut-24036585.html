About this home

ALL OFFERS DUE BY 8/11 at 5pm. Located in the Silvermine area of Norwalk, this five-bedroom, three-bathroom home, offers over 2,600 square feet of living space above ground with an additional 1000 square feet in the lower level just waiting for your vision. The property features a level yard and large rooms, providing ample space and versatility. The first floor includes spacious living areas with hardwood floors, large windows , built-ins, and a fireplace. There is a first floor bedroom and full bath. Upstairs are four bedrooms including the ensuite primary bedroom. The 1.43 acre level yard provides a blank canvas for outdoor enjoyment, allowing residents to create their desired outdoor living space, whether for relaxation, entertainment, or recreation. Close to shopping, restaurants, train stations, recreation, and a short ride to the beaches in Norwalk.
