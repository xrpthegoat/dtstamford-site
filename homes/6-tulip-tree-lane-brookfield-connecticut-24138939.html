About this home

Welcome to 6 Tulip Tree Circle in Brookfield, Connecticut, a bright and spacious single-level condominium in the desirable Stony Hill Village community. Surrounded by mature trees and open green space, this home offers a peaceful, park-like setting just minutes from Route 7, I-84, shopping, and dining. The open layout features a comfortable living room and dining area that flow to a private deck overlooking the landscaped grounds, creating an easy space to relax or entertain. The kitchen is functional and ready for your personal touches. Two well-sized bedrooms and a full bath complete the floor plan, along with in-unit laundry, ample storage, and a one-car garage. Enjoy the convenience of low-maintenance living with beautifully maintained common areas, walking paths, and a location that puts you close to everything Brookfield has to offer, including Candlewood Lake and nearby town amenities. A great opportunity to own in one of Fairfield County's most desirable communities.
