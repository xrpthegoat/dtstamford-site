About this home

Welcome to this sun-filled and beautifully updated Ranch, offering the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and modern updates. There are two bedrooms upstairs, and another two downstairs on the walk-out basement level. Nestled on a peaceful cul-de-sac, this move-in ready home features a flexible floor plan with generous living space across two levels. The thoughtfully renovated kitchen boasts rich custom cabinetry, sleek Corian countertops, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar for casual dining, and even a wine cooler for effortless hosting. Just off the kitchen, a bright sunroom opens to a large deck - your own private retreat for morning coffee or evening gatherings. Adjacent to the kitchen, the cozy family room features a Swiss-made wood-burning fireplace and additional space to relax or unwind. The main level includes a serene primary suite with a renovated en-suite bath, along with a second bedroom and updated full bath. The walk-out lower level expands your living options with two additional bedrooms, two dedicated home office spaces, a shared "Jack and Jill" bathroom, and an additional full bath- perfect for guests, remote work, or multi-generational living. Additional highlights include updated bathrooms throughout, mostly new windows, a large, stand-up attic for storage, and a well-sized shed.
