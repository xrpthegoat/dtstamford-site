About this home

Set well back from the road, this charming 1930's vintage cape offers flexible living and is comfortably perched with glorious east/west exposures that provide naturally bright interiors throughout the day. Fresh paint throughout all interiors, hardwood floors, recessed lighting, multiple bay windows and an oversized kitchen with expansive granite counters and maple cabinets. The two car attached garage leads to a fabulous mud room with tile floor for easy clean up, built in cabinetry, bench seating and rear yard access. The window walled sunroom has sliders out to the maintenance free deck overlooking the private, level rear yard. Nestled in Cranbury on the Westport/Wilton border minutes to Route 15, the 227 acre Cranbury Park and the quaint Cranbury Shopping Center. Flexible living in a most convenient location, not to be missed.
