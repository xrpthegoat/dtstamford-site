About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 2-bedroom, 3-bath condominium in the highly sought-after Harvest Commons community of Westport, CT. This home provides the perfect blend of privacy, comfort, and convenience. The bright, open floor plan features soaring ceilings and oversized windows, filling every room with natural light. The spacious living room boasts a gas fireplace and sliders that lead to a private patio, perfect for entertaining or relaxation. The stylish eat-in kitchen includes granite countertops and stainless steel appliances. There is a light, bright dining room with direct access to a private courtyard for al fresco dining - perfect for entertaining. The main level primary suite with vaulted ceilings, double California closets, & a spa-like ensuite bath is truly a sanctuary. The first floor also features even more flexibility with a second bedroom with built-in Murphy Bed, second full bath, and ample storage. Additional conveniences include a separate laundry area, and attached, direct entry two-car garage. The partially finished lower level features a family room, third full bath, and additional, versatile living space perfect as an office or den. A rare, whole house generator offers added security. Harvest Commons features a heated pool, two tennis courts, & a clubhouse with lush, professionally landscaped grounds. Ideally located just minutes from downtown, beaches, and train station, this exceptional property offers tranquil living in the heart of Westport.
