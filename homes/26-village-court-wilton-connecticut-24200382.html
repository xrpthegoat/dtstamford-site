About this home

Welcome to Grumman Hill Village, a coveted South Wilton community that blends privacy, convenience, and true neighborhood charm. This 1,984 SF, 3-bedroom home stands out for its main-level primary suite and attached two-car garage. Lovingly maintained and freshly painted, the home welcomes you with a bright, open main level where the eat-in kitchen, dining area, and living room flow together seamlessly. A separate den offers a cozy retreat for reading, relaxing, or working from home. The main-level laundry room adds everyday convenience. Step outside to a private back patio, an ideal spot for morning coffee, al fresco dinners, or simply unwinding. Upstairs, two additional bedrooms share a full bath, offering comfortable space for family or guests. Grumman Hill Village is known for its close-knit community spirit, highlighted by a beloved Halloween tradition that brings neighbors together for one of the area's most festive trick-or-treating nights. Natural gas, city water, and city sewer, add everyday ease, while the South Wilton location puts Wilton Center, dining, top schools, train stations, and major commuting routes all within easy reach. This is a rare opportunity to enjoy space, privacy, convenience, and community in one of Wilton's most established and sought-after residential settings.
