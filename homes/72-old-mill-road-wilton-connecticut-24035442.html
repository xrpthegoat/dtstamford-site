About this home

Discover charm and modern comfort in this beautifully updated 1880s antique home. With 3 bedrooms, it features exposed wood beams, farm doors and beadboard paneling, completed by modern amenities such as central cooling and heating, hardwood floors and updated appliances. The inviting first floor includes an open kitchen and dining area adjacent to a grand stone fireplace and cozy seating nook. This space flows seamlessly to a large deck and covered porch, ideal for morning coffee or entertaining guests. A versatile "flex" room offers options for a home office, playroom or yoga studio, while a separate family room provides a perfect setting for movie nights or enjoying the view from the window seat. Upstairs, you will find three bright bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite with vaulted ceilings, ample closet space, and a walk-up attic. The property boasts over an acre of landscaped gardens, featuring peonies, lilacs, stone walls, a firepit area and a lush lawn. Recent upgrades include a new water heater, oil tank, refinished garage with automatic door, freshly paved and gravel driveway, recessed lighting and stylish fixtures. Additional modern conveniences include first-floor laundry and upgraded electrical. Located just minutes from Georgetown center, shopping and Route 7, this home offers a private and charming sanctuary for the discerning buyer.
