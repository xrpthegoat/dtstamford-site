About this home

A charming Rowayton home, in a prime location, expertly styled by Lynn Morgan Designs. Situated in the vibrant heart of Rowayton, this residence is just steps away from 101, Rowayton Seafood, quaint downtown Rowayton, and Bayley Beach. Escape to your serene .32-acre retreat with a pool, privately tucked away at the end of the street. The impeccably landscaped property offers outdoor spaces perfect for entertaining, along with a private pool for relaxation. The stylish kitchen with a large island flows seamlessly into the updated dining room and spacious living room featuring a cozy fireplace, creating an ideal setting for gatherings. The generous primary suite includes a vaulted cathedral ceiling, a luxurious bathroom, and an office nook for remote work. Two additional bedrooms and a bathroom complete the main floor. The total square footage of 3,167 includes the lower-level, offering a family room, exercise area, bedroom, and full bathroom. The attached two-car garage and driveway offer ample parking for extra convenience in this centrally located Rowayton residence. Viewings by appointment only.
