About this home

A Modern Rowayton Sanctuary, updated with Wilson Cove views, privacy & in-ground pool. Welcome to 5 Dancing Bear Road, a rare private retreat in the coastal community of Rowayton. This 3,000+SF updated modern home offers the perfect balance of sophistication, serenity, & coastal living. Set on 3+professionally landscaped acres with in-ground pool, petite pool house & shed. The property is a hidden gem, surrounded by lush greenery, expansive stone patio with Wilson Cove views-designed for both intimate living & casual entertaining. Inside, discover a bright & open living/dining room w/vaulted ceilings, hardwood floors & walls of glass overlooking nature+a cozy library nook w/ a fireplace & built-ins. The newly renovated modern chef's kitchen features top grade Cafe appliances, custom cabinetry and quartz countertops. A screened-in porch off the main living area is the perfect space to enjoy warm weather. Upstairs, features a renovated & expanded primary suite with a large custom walk in closet & spa like bath. Two additional bedrooms also feature custom closets & share an updated hall bath. Not to be overlooked is the cozy family room w/fireplace & abundant windows with distant water views. The finished lower level offers flex space+ample storage. Whether as a year-round residence or weekend retreat, 5 Dancing Bear offers a rare opportunity to own a modern coastal home-just minutes from the vibrant seaside village of Rowayton-CT's most desirable coastal community.
