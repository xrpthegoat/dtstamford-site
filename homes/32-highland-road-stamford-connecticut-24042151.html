About this home

Enjoy the many updates on this stand-alone three bed, 2.5 bath townhouse minutes from downtown. Open design with newer kitchen and appliances. Built-in glass paneled cabinets in eat-in kitche with sliding glass doors to secluded deck. Large living/dining room with fireplace and sliding doors to second deck overlooking well maintained grounds with ample guest parking. Hardwood floors throughout. Upper level with three bedrooms all with built in storage. Master and additional baths have been recently updated. Garage floor has been treated with pennteck concrete coating which resists water and tire damage. Exercise room (or office) on garage level with new vinyl floor, electric fireplace, storage cabinets, refrigerator. Solar system which is very economical for electric usage. Owner will consider six month rental.
