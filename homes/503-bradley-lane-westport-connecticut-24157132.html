About this home

Welcome to Bradley Commons - a private, gated enclave in the heart of Westport's coveted Saugatuck neighborhood. Originally presented as the model home for the community, this distinguished end-unit residence showcases elevated finishes, thoughtful architectural detail, and a premier setting within the complex. Just a five-minute walk to the Metro-North station, the home offers effortless access to Manhattan while embracing Westport's coastal lifestyle - ideal as a full-time residence or refined weekend retreat. A gracious, light-filled entry framed by windows on two sides introduces a grand, beautifully designed staircase that serves as an elegant focal point. The main level features 9+ foot ceilings, rich hardwood floors, and an open-concept layout designed for modern living. The family room with gas fireplace flows seamlessly to the dining area and private deck - perfect for entertaining or quiet evenings at home. The chef's kitchen is both refined and functional, offering a generous peninsula with seating, stainless steel appliances, custom cabinetry, and a separate pantry - all overlooking a private, manicured green backyard that provides a serene natural backdrop.
