About this home

Welcome to 25 Riverbank Drive, a tranquil retreat in one of North Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods, offering stunning views and a true vacation-at-home lifestyle. This light, bright and airy 4-bedroom home features a dramatic wall of windows with breathtaking sunset views from the living room, truly the heart of the home. The large eat-in-kitchen and formal dining room with direct access to the deck is perfect for seamless entertaining and everyday living. The primary bedroom offers soaring cathedral ceilings, access to a wraparound deck and an ensuite bath making it your own personal sanctuary. Two additional bedrooms exude the same special feeling as the primary bedroom with the same attention to detail. The lower level has a generously sized and versatile family room, screened-in porch, a 4th bedroom and a spa-like bathroom highlighting a huge oversized shower with room for 2! Hardwood floors throughout main level, 2-car attached garage and central air complete the package. Outside, the 2-tiered deck creates multiple spaces to unwind, along with a relaxing hot tub, fenced in area and peaceful natural scenery everywhere you look. Excellent location close to the Greenwich border and a short drive to the Merritt Parkway plus easy access to downtown Stamford, shopping, dining, and the train station. Start your next chapter here!
