About this home

RARE OPPORTUNITY TO OWN WATERFRONT PROPERTY IN EAST NORWALK!! URBAN LIVING AT IT'S BEST This location is too good to be true. Great walkable neighborhood, with restaurants, grocery, SONO, VET's Park and 2 trains stations. This completely renovated home has been designed to take full advantage of the beautiful Mill Pond at the mouth of the East Norwalk Harbor. Recently built additions were added to bring this home into today's modern lifestyle. The entrance greets you with a wall of windows boasting a spectacular view of Mill Pond. The great room features coffer ceiling and plenty of room to entertain and dine. A chef's kitchen offers everything today's buyers desire. Luxury appliances, large quartz island, gorgeous cabinets, wet bar and cathedral ceiling assures this room is the place to be. The oversized deck offers plenty of entertaining space with access to a lower patio. A stunning staircase invites you upstairs to a gracious hall leading you to two bedrooms, shared bathroom and laundry area. This split bedroom design offers separate primary suite with luxurious en-suite bath with custom shower, double sinks, large soaking tub and plenty of room to lounge. The large bedroom offers a balcony and high ceiling making the perfect relaxing place. The lower level has walk out to a patio overlooking pond and family room with bar. There's a room that may be a bedroom or office with a full bath. Beautiful side yard and oversized 2 car garage w/endless possibilities and storage.
