About this home

Fully remodeled one bedroom, one bathroom end-unit condominium on the second floor of Courtland Green in Stamford's Glenbrook neighborhood. This 575-square-foot residence was completely updated approximately one year ago and features hardwood floors throughout the living areas, a well-appointed kitchen with oven/range and refrigerator, and abundant natural light from its end-unit position. The unit offers convenient front and rear access. Heat and hot water are included. The well-maintained community provides landscaped grounds and professional property management. Located near public transportation, shopping, and dining. Pets permitted subject to association rules.
