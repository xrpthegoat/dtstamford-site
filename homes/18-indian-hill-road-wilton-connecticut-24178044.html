About this home

This captivating home offers a unique blend of architectural character, warmth, and modern functionality. From the moment you step inside, the soaring cathedral ceiling and open living spaces create an airy yet inviting atmosphere filled with light and charm. The great room serves as a dramatic focal point of the home, anchored by a contemporary freestanding wood-burning fireplace. The open-concept layout flows seamlessly into the dining area and Euro-style kitchen. A den, convenient powder room with laundry, and flexible living space complete the main level. An open staircase leads to two loft-style bedrooms with hardwood floors and a full bath with shower/tub combination, enhancing the home's distinctive design aesthetic. In addition, the seller has an approved 4-bedroom septic plan on file with the town, offering valuable future expansion potential. Outdoor living is equally appealing with a recently updated deck spanning the side of the home creating the perfect setting for entertaining or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings. Set on nearly two acres, the property overlooks a distant pond and charming wishing well, offering a serene escape. Ideally located with convenient access to both Wilton and Ridgefield amenities including shopping, dining, entertainment, schools, and both the Branchville and Wilton train stations for commuting to NYC. Just over a mile from Weir Farm, this special property offers a rare opportunity for a full-time residence or weekend retreat.
