About this home

Set on a truly parklike level acre in North Stamford on the border of New Canaan, this classic center hall colonial is ready for its next owner. Spacious entry foyer, excellent flow and large scale formal rooms on the main level lead out to a relaxing screened porch overlooking a private rear yard. Bonus sized master bedroom, three comfortably sized additional bedrooms and a walk in laundry room with shelving and tub sink populate the second floor. The finished lower level makes for great rec space and includes two oversized storage areas. Routine maintenance on all systems and important improvements by the previous owner will make this a wise investment. A grand opportunity awaits.
