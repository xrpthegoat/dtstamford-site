About this home

Escape to a world of elegance and serenity at this custom waterfront estate that captures the enchanting essence of New England's coastal charm. Gold Coast Luxury in exclusive Tokeneke Association. Set on a sprawling perched lot, this residence offers an unparalleled living experience with 6 generously sized bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms. Every room in this meticulously crafted home provides breathtaking water views, ensuring a magical ambience throughout. Designed by a local coveted firm, Brooks & Falotico. The chef's kitchen is perfect for culinary enthusiasts, while the formal dining room and expansive living areas offer exquisite spaces for entertaining all with fireplaces and views to last into the evening. Enjoy the privilege of your own private dock and mooring, perfect for fishing, bird watching and a day on the water. Guests will want to extend their stay! Retreat to the charming cottage, a delightful addition for visitors seeking comfort and privacy. Leisurely afternoons await! The estate also features a gym, mudroom, boat garage, library with speak-easy and a leveled lawn ensuring all your lifestyle needs are met within this exclusive escape. Room to build and even design a pool on one of three locations on the property. Experience the ultimate blend of luxury and coastal beauty, just a stone's throw from the captivating waters of Long Island Sound. Indulge in a lifestyle where elegance meets nature, offering a truly dreamy retreat. Stop daydreaming, start living.
