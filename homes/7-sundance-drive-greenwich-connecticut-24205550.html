About this home

A rare opportunity to own an authentic 1965 Mid-Century Modern home set on 2 picturesque, private acres in a quiet sought-after neighborhood of Cos Cob. One-level living showcases the timeless appeal with oak floors throughout, featuring soaring ceilings, expansive windows, and sun-drenched living spaces that seamlessly connect with the surrounding landscape. Nestled in complete privacy, this architectural gem is designed to bring the outdoors in. The dramatic living room is anchored by a wood-burning fireplace and opens to an expansive deck overlooking the tranquil backyard perfect for a pool! A truly special setting offering privacy.
