About this home

Set within one of Greenwich's storied residential buildings, this exceptional two-bedroom, two-bath residence has been completely reimagined following an extensive eight-month gut renovation where no surface was left untouched. Unobstructed views of Long Island Sound and abundant natural light create a remarkable backdrop throughout. The expanded kitchen features exceptional custom cabinetry, a Wolf, Thermador and Cove appliances. Both baths were completely renovated with new fixtures and finishes, while a new laundry area with Miele washer and dryer adds modern convenience. Soaring 9-foot ceilings enhance the sense of scale and grandeur, completing a sophisticated, turnkey residence where timeless elegance meets modern luxury.
