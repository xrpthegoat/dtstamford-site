About this home

Private Oasis Meets Modern Convenience. Nestled at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, this home offers a rare blend of elegance, privacy, and numerous upgrades- no home projects needed. The 2025 interior paint refreshes every room with a modern touch. Renovated in 2021, the laundry room features new floors, streamlined storage, and improved lighting. All three bathrooms have upgraded lighting, and the kitchen boasts a Bosch 36" three-door fridge (2022) and a hot/cold filtered water dispenser (2022) because hydration should be easy. The 2021 gas fireplace inserts come with a remote for easy control. The garage underwent a full renovation, including painted floors, drywall, shelving, better lighting, and additional electrical outlets. A MyQ garage door opener (2021) allows remote operation from anywhere. Outside, a new hose and sprinkler system with a rain monitor (2024, $2K investment) conserves water while keeping the landscape lush. Designed by a Master Gardener, the English Garden features stunning blooms and one of the neighborhood's largest, most private yards, with Copt's Brook flowing through the backyard for added charm. Turn-key and full of thoughtful upgrades, this home is ready for you. Schedule a showing before it's gone!
