About this home

Welcome to this charming and well maintained 3 bedroom, 2 bath Colonial located in a desirable Norwalk neighborhood. The bright and airy main level features an inviting open floor plan with hardwood floors, perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. The updated bathrooms include heated floors, primary bedroom offers walk in custom California closet. Enjoy your morning coffee or unwind in the evening on the large deck overlooking a private, fully fenced yard with a fire pit and built-in swing set. Additional features include central air, a detached garage, paved driveway, and convenient main level laundry. Ideally located close to Stew Leonard's, shopping, dining, major highways, and I-95, with easy access to everything Norwalk has to offer.
