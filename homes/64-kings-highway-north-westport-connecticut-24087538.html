About this home

Enjoy Westport summer life to the fullest in this gorgeous newly renovated home with brand new pool. Bright open floor plan offers 4 Bedrooms, 5 Baths, Home Office, Gym and more. Seamless indoor-outdoor living with large flat backyard, refreshing pool, two bluestone terraces that are perfect for relaxing with your morning coffee and dining alfresco, as well as a wonderful outdoor fireplace to gather around to warm your cool seaside nights. Sought-after walk to town location. Leave the car in the driveway because it's a super easy walk to the heart of downtown Westport, filled with fabulous waterfront restaurants, coffee shops, premier shopping, acclaimed 5-star Library, music-rocking Levitt Pavilion and so much more! Only five minute drive to Westport train station and ten minute drive to Longshore Park and Compo Beach where you can escape for a sunrise jog along the water's edge, enjoy a round of golf and tennis, spend leisure time by the pool or beach, set sail for the day with soothing sea breezes, and witness gorgeous never to be taken for granted sunsets. Come enjoy the spirit and amenities of Westport and create summer memories for a lifetime! Available July 6th through August 24th.
