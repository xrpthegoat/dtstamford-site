About this home

This Stamford neighborhood is a real gem, conveniently located to both Stamford and Greenwich. Westwood Road is lushly landscaped with most homes sited on one acre. This 1950's home is built of solid construction. You enter the home into a large LR with a fireplace and cathedral ceiling, which flows smoothly into the eat-in kitchen. The upstairs consists of three large bedrooms, the master having a full bath and walk-in closet. The lower level, just a few steps from the LR /kitchen area, offers a family room as well as an additional BR/den and full bath. The easy ground level walk-out completes the overall convenient floorplan.
