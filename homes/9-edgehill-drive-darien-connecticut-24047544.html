About this home

TOKENEKE This charming and inviting home is in the Tokeneke neighborhood, a short walk to Rowayton Village and minutes from Darien's downtown, restaurants, shopping, train station and park. The main floor is filled with sunlight throughout the day and includes a large living room with fireplace and French doors leading to a blue stone patio, dining room, kitchen with breakfast bar, and full bath. The blue stone patio overlooks a private and serene backyard, which is perfect for gatherings, barbecues or just relaxing around the firepit. The second floor features a spacious primary bedroom with multiple closets, two additional bedrooms that share a half bath, and a full bath. If you are looking for a place to unwind, look no further - this is the home for you.
