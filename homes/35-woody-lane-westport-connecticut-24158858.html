About this home

Enjoy the resort lifestyle in this beautiful home! Discover unparalleled luxury and privacy at 35 Woody Lane, a meticulously renovated 5-bedroom, 3.5-bath sanctuary on 2.18 acres. Nestled at the end of a serene cul-de-sac in the acclaimed Long Lots neighborhood, this turnkey residence blends timeless elegance with modern sophistication. Step into a chef's dream kitchen featuring a massive island, custom cabinetry, high-end stainless appliances, vaulted ceilings with skylights, and seamless flow to the living areas, ideal for effortless entertaining. The exquisite primary suite offers a spa-like bath, custom built-ins, and peaceful treetop views. Generous secondary bedrooms and baths provide comfort for family and guests. The crown jewel: a resort-style backyard oasis with a sparkling gunite pool, full tennis court, hot tub, fire pit, expansive deck, and lush, private landscaping, your own secluded escape for year-round enjoyment, al fresco dining, and play. Experience the perfect fusion of luxury living and Westport lifestyle, minutes to top schools, Compo Beach, and commuter rail.
