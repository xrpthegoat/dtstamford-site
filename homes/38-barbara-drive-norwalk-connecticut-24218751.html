About this home

Beautiful, Comfortable Living in a Convenient Norwalk Location, Discover the perfect blend of comfort, functionality and convenience in this beautiful home. Thoughtfully designed for easy one-level living. The inviting open-concept layout seamlessly connects the kitchen, dinning room and living room, creating a welcoming space for everyday living and entertaining. The home features two bedrooms and two full bathrooms along with a convenient mudroom and laundry area, all on one floor. The spacious primary bedroom offers a walk-in closet and a private en-suite bathroom. Adding even more versatility, the home features a walk-up attic with wonderful possibilities for a home office, along with an additional flexible room that can easily become your personal yoga studio, exercise room, reading nook or creative space. The possibilities are yours! Enjoy the outdoors on your own .17 acre property while appreciating the peaceful and private neighborhood setting. Ideally located near Stew Leonard's, The SoNo Collection, shopping restaurants, parks, golf courses and commuting routes. Residents also enjoy beach and park privilege, adding even more value to this wonderful location, making it easy to embrace everything Norwalk has to offer. With convenient access to NYC (46 miles away) this home offers the perfect balance of suburban tranquility and everyday accessibility. A Wonderful opportunity to enjoy comfortable living, flexible spaces and exceptional location
