About this home

Welcome to 290 Red Fox Road, a well-maintained ranch on over an acre in sought-after North Stamford, offering the perfect blend of privacy, space, and convenience. With just over 2,300 square feet of living space, this 3-bedroom, 2-bath home is filled with natural light, highlighted by expansive windows and an easy, open flow designed for today's lifestyle. The family room, complete with a fireplace and wood-burning stove, creates a warm and versatile gathering space. The main-level primary suite features an ensuite bath, while two additional bedrooms provide flexibility for guests, work-from-home needs, or everyday living. Freshly painted and recently refreshed, the home is clean, well cared for, and move-in ready. Step outside to a spacious patio overlooking a private, wooded setting-ideal for entertaining or simply enjoying the quiet surroundings. A full walkout basement offers endless potential for expansion, recreation, or customization, plus an existing workshop for added functionality. Minutes to the Merritt Parkway and I-95, this location offers easy commuting without sacrificing a peaceful residential feel. A standout opportunity in one of Stamford's most desirable areas.
