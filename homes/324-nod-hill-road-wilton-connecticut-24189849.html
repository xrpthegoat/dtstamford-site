About this home

Nestled on one of Wilton's most coveted roads, this exceptional Colonial offers timeless architecture, generous proportions, and an ideal floor plan for today's lifestyle. Step through the impressive front entrance into a gracious foyer that opens to a handsome office with custom built-ins, an elegant formal living room with fireplace, a spacious dining room designed for entertaining, and a warm, inviting sunroom. The heart of the home is the family room, anchored by a stone fireplace, soaring ceiling and surrounded by walls of windows and French doors leading to an expansive deck overlooking the beautiful level property. The well-designed kitchen features quality custom cabinetry, updated appliances, abundant workspace, and a sunny breakfast area wrapped in windows that bring the outdoors in. Upstairs, every bedroom is ensuite, including a spacious primary suite with an updated bath. An oversized add'l family room offers exceptional flexibility as a recreation room, media room, fitness space, or additional bedroom. High ceilings, oversized rooms, and quality craftsmanship throughout create a home that is both elegant and comfortable. Situated on a picturesque private lot with a potential pool site, this property offers exceptional privacy while enjoying the prestige of Nod Hill Road. Convenient to Wilton's award-winning schools, town center, and commuting routes, this is a rare opportunity to own in one of Fairfield County's most sought-after locations.
