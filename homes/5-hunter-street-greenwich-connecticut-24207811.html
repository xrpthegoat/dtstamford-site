About this home

Introducing a newly constructed Contemporary Colonial, by Alfieri Enterprises, that captures the essence of Old Greenwich living offering a seamless blend of classic architecture and modern design on a quiet cul-de-sac near schools, shops and the train. Defined by extraordinary craftsmanship, custom details and curated finishes in a stunning 6,000 sq. ft. home. Great for entertaining in open plan chef's kitchen, breakfast room with doors to expansive deck, family room and main level en-suite bedroom/office. Upstairs boasts an enviable primary suite retreat with spa bath, custom walk-in closets, an additional en-suite bedroom and two bedrooms connected by Jack/Jill bath. Top floor media/rec room and a guest en-suite bedroom, and the lower level offers a flex space for gym/gaming, half bath and storage complete this offering. With possible pool site.
