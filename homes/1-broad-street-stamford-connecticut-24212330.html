About this home

Step into Stamford, Connecticut's most luxurious condo-owned building in the heart of Downtown Stamford. Offered in an extensive full renovation state as a corner A-unit-the most desirable layout featuring 10-foot ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows showcasing Long Island Sound and NYC skyline views-Penthouse 33A at Park Tower has since been transformed with renowned designer. Today, this elevated 3 bedroom, 3.5 bath residence seamlessly blends contemporary luxury and privacy. The updated interiors feature high-end finishes including quartz waterfall countertops, custom cabinetry, wide California oak floors, heated bathroom floors, Fully automated shades, Nest AI thermostat controls, with top of line Viking appliances, a brand new Bosch dishwasher, and a custom dry bar. Beyond the residence, Park Tower delivers world-class amenities including a 24-hour concierge and security, a year-round indoor pool, a state-of-the-art fitness center, resident lounge and entertainment spaces, a party room, a rooftop terrace, and complimentary shuttle service to the Stamford Transportation Center-placing Mill River Park, top dining, and Metro-North trains just steps away. Unit comes with a deeded storage unit in the building basement.
