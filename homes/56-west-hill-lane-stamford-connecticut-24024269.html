About this home

On a peaceful cul-de-sac within Westover's most sought-after area, this unique mid-century-modern home, originally designed by Ward Bennet and featured in The New York Times, is nestled on a private, acre-plus, park-like setting that is surrounded by trees and adorned with a tranquil pond. This backyard ia a peaceful haven, ideal for relaxation and outdoor activities. As you step inside, natural light floods the space from every angle, creating a welcoming and warm ambiance. The entryway leads to an office boasting magnificent views of the backyards. Descending the stairs, you'll discover an informal living area, kitchen, dining space, and a bedroom connected to a jack-and-jill bathroom, complemented by a room perfect for a den or workspace. Additionally, the lower level features a screened-in porch, providing an idyllic spot to unwind while appreciating the backyard's beauty. Ascending the stairs, you'll find the formal living area, another versatile space suitable for an office, and the primary suite with a vaulted ceiling and strategically placed windows that have breathtaking views. Discover all the splendor this remarkable property has. Including the convenience of an oversized garage and workshop just steps away from the house. Whether you're seeking a perfect full-time residence or a relaxing weekend getaway, this home has it all!
