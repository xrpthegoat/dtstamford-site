About this home

Looking for a yearly furnished rental in a prime location? Look no further! Nestled in the sought-after Saugatuck Shores, this charming home on a secluded cul-de-sac provides the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. With 3 bedrooms, 2 baths, and a dedicated office, its airy design and expansive windows fill the space with natural light, creating a serene atmosphere year-round. Enjoy stunning views of Long Island Sound and Bermuda Lagoon from the balcony, perfect for sipping your morning coffee and watching the sunrise. Just steps away from the harbor, you'll have easy access to kayaking, paddleboarding, or a refreshing swim in calm waters. Take leisurely walks to the Cedar Point Yacht Club and immerse yourself in the charm of this beachside community. Plus, you'll be close to Westport's downtown, with upscale shops and dining, as well as Longshore's golf and tennis amenities, and Compo Beach. Best of all, all utilities and outdoor maintenance are included, making this an effortless and enjoyable living experience.
