About this home

Attention buyers! Incredible opportunity to acquire a first-floor studio condo in the well-run, pet-friendly Woodside Green community. Sold strictly AS-IS, this unit is the ultimate blank canvas to renovate, add modern finishes. Bring your vision to transform this efficient 368-square-foot floor plan, featuring hardwood floors throughout, a dedicated eating alcove, a walk-in closet. Enjoy easy first-floor access and low-cost costs, as monthly common charges include hot water and cooking gas. The convenient on-site laundry room features brand new machines, and plenty of parking adds to the ease of living here. Enjoy all that Woodside Green has to offer, including a beautiful center green, gazebo, dog area, and etc. Ideally located across the street from grocery shopping, a gym, coffee shops, restaurants, and more, plus steps from Scalzi Park, UConn, and downtown Stamford.
