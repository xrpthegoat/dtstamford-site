About this home

Welcome to this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath condominium in the highly sought-after Heywood Heights community in Stamford's desirable Glenbrook neighborhood. This unit combines comfort, style, and functionality with a floor plan designed for today's lifestyle. Freshly painted throughout and featuring brand-new hardwood floors. This inviting residence offers a spacious living room with sliding doors leading to a private patio overlooking the beautifully maintained courtyard. The well-appointed kitchen opens seamlessly to the dining area, creating an ideal space for both everyday living and entertaining. The generous primary suite features a private full bath and a walk-in closet, while two additional bedrooms offer ample space for family, guests, or a home office. Residents can enjoy exceptional community amenities, including an in-ground swimming pool, abundant parking, assigned storage, and professionally maintained grounds. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, and major commuting routes, this is an outstanding rental opportunity in one of Stamford's most desirable communities.
