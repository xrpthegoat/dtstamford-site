About this home

Charming Dutch colonial 4-5 bedroom 3.5 bath set on a private cul-de-sac, featuring wide plank pine floors throughout. The spacious farmstyle custom kitchen includes a center island with seating, two sinks, a pantry, fireplace, Miele dishwasher, Viking refrigerator and a double oven. A private main level office accessible from the front door can also serve as guest room. The large living/dining great room offers another fireplace and pine paneling for warmth and character. Many outstanding features exist such as antique bullseye glass over the front double doors. Not included in the square footage are several valuable spaces: a newly carpeted in-law apartment on the ground level (5th with a full bath, cedar closet and private driveway entrance, as well as a finished, newly carpeted game/family room. A four season sunporch off the kitchen and an adjoining covered deck adds to living space. This hidden gem on a 1.6 acre wooded lot showcases professional landscaping and offers privacy, yet easy access to beaches, I-95, Rowayton train station, nearby shopping and walk to schools. Outdoor features include a large covered deck, outdoor shower, a stone patio and a fire pit for great outdoor entertaining. A large barn and shed provide ample storage. There is also parking for multiple vehicles and a garage.
