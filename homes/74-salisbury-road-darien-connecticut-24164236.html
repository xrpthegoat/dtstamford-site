About this home

Set on nearly 2 private acres in coveted northern Darien, this exceptional custom Colonial offers refined living with timeless design and superior craftsmanship throughout. Detailed with exquisite custom moldings, the home exudes elegance in every space. A dramatic step-down living room with fireplace creates a warm and inviting atmosphere, while the spacious formal dining room is perfectly complemented by an adjacent butler's pantry-ideal for effortless entertaining. A richly appointed library with its own fireplace provides a quiet retreat. The gourmet kitchen opens seamlessly to a generous family room with fireplace, forming the heart of the home, and is supported by two conveniently located powder rooms. Upstairs, a second family room offers additional flexible living space, an office and five well-proportioned bedrooms and four full baths, including a luxurious primary suite. The lower level is designed for both recreation and lifestyle, featuring a walk-in wine cellar, a large level playroom/craft room with double sinks, and a dedicated workout room. Outdoors, the professionally landscaped grounds provide a private oasis with a beautiful pool and an outdoor fireplace-perfect for relaxing and entertaining year-round. Complete with a three-car garage, this remarkable property combines elegance, comfort and functionality in one of Darien's most desirable settings.
