About this home

Presenting the latest SIGNATURE Home by SIR Development - an incredible showcase of luxury, with a natural organic vibe, set on irresistible park-like property, with-in walking distance to schools. Very private, desirable location. As you approach via the long driveway, the home's striking architecture comes into view. Dramatic glass-lined foyer introduces the sun-splashed interiors of this chic, architect-designed home. The DR has an illuminated stepped ceiling & butlers pantry leading to the incredible gourmet kitchen. Chic, oak-accented space has an exquisite honed Quartz island w/waterfall edges, Wolf & Sub-Zero appliances & a unique ceiling which highlights the designer lighting that is found throughout. Open flow progresses w/the breakfast rm & through to the fam rm w/a fresh take on the classic coffered ceiling & a Venetian plaster, 60" linear gas fpl. Eye-catching steel & glass doors reveal a living room with oak accents & LED lighting. 1st fl office w/built-ins, 2 half baths & a great mudroom that leads to the EV ready 3 car gar complete this level. Upstairs, a dreamy primary suite w/vaulted ceiling, gas linear fpl & spa-inspired bath w/ radiant heated flrs. The sun drenched 3rd flr has a bonus rm, 2nd office & half bth. In the walk-out LL enjoy media, rec rms, gym & 6th BR w/full bth. Private, flat yard is a true retreat, a pergola w/motorized roof, salt water pool w/auto cover, outdoor shower, 3 bluestone patios & built-in grill,PLUS rm for pickleball crt or ADU.
