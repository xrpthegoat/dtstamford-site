About this home

Discover the incredible space and comfort of this surprisingly spacious Raised Ranch! From the moment you arrive, you will appreciate the convenience of an expansive driveway accommodating up to 4 vehicles, along with a handy attached garage. Inside, the main layout offers an abundance of living space just waiting for your personal touch. The lower level provides fantastic flexibility and a excellent possibility for an in-law apartment. Step outside to a large, beautiful flat level lot featuring a wonderful covered porch that is absolutely perfect for outdoor entertaining. Equipped with active solar panels for excellent energy efficiency, this home is situated in a highly convenient Stamford location near shopping, restaurants, parks, etc. Bring your favorite paint colors and refreshing ideas to customize this wonderful property and make it your own!
