About this home

Located in the sought after Glenville area, enjoy the Greenwich amenities and lifestyle while finding the peaceful atmosphere of this quaint neighborhood. Enter into the family room to be welcomed by a wood-burning fireplace with built-ins on both sides. The floor plan flows into the dining room, great for entertaining, and then to the kitchen providing convenience and functionality. Upper level provides two bedrooms and a full bath. A few more steps up leads to the more recent addition of a very large primary suite with plenty of closet space, plus a beautifully designed primary bath. Lower level provides a 4th bedroom which can also function as an office/playroom, and a partially finished basement with use as a family room, exercise or play area. Enjoy the lower level walk-out to the serene level backyard. Additionally find a partially-covered patio constructed with thermal bluestone pavers for entertaining, shed for storage, and plenty of privacy and space!
