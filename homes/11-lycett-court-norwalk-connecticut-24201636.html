About this home

Enjoy comfortable one-level living in this bright and spacious 3-bedroom rental in a 2 family home located on a quiet cul-de-sac. The freshly refinished hardwood floors give the home a clean, updated feel throughout. The main living area offers a generous living room, dining room, and eat-in kitchen with direct access to a large deck overlooking the yard. The primary bedroom features a private bathroom, with two additional bedrooms and a full hall bath. Central air, one garage parking spot, and additional off-street parking add to the convenience. Ideally located close to Oak Hills Park, shopping, restaurants, SoNo, Metro-North, the Merritt Parkway, and I-95. A great opportunity to enjoy a spacious home in a convenient Norwalk location.
