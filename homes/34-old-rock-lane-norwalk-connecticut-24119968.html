About this home

Meticulously maintained with a property steeped in history, this stone front beauty has all the charm of an English Country cottage, sits on 1.29 acres of breathtaking grounds, and is surrounded by mature trees and flowering gardens that create a private, retreat-like setting away from the bustle of everyday life. Designed for both quiet living and vibrant entertaining, the property offers endless options indoors and out: a sparkling in-ground pool with a spacious dining area, a three-season screened porch, and a sunlit back patio perfect for summer gatherings. Inside, a warm foyer opens into an expansive living room anchored by a wood-burning fireplace ideal for cozy holidays and winter evenings. The spacious dining room flows into a bright breakfast nook with a full wet bar, surrounded by floor to ceiling multi-paned windows and bathed in light. The recently renovated custom kitchen features ample cabinetry, granite countertops, and modern appliances, while a private home office/den and spacious mudroom/laundry complete the main level. Upstairs, three oversized bedrooms with gleaming hardwood floors and generous closets are complemented by two beautifully updated baths. The primary suite is a true retreat with a spa-like soaking tub, skylight, and steam shower. The finished lower level adds even more flexibility with a welcoming family room and fireplace, full-service wet bar, renovated bath, and potential in-law or guest suite.
