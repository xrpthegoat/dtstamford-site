About this home

Located in Stamford's highly desirable Glenbrook neighborhood, this rare 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath end-unit townhouse offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and potential. Enjoy easy access to downtown, shopping, dining, and major commuting routes, while coming home to a peaceful, well-maintained community. A welcoming front porch sets the stage for this inviting home. Inside, you'll find a spacious living room that flows seamlessly into the dining area, where sliding glass doors lead to a private back deck-ideal for outdoor dining, entertaining, or relaxing. The bright kitchen and a conveniently located half bath complete the main level. Upstairs, three generously sized bedrooms share a full hallway bath. The finished lower level provides versatile living space that can easily serve as a family room, home office, playroom, or home gym, and also includes a dedicated laundry area for added convenience. Two parking spaces are included! With a few personal updates, this home presents an excellent opportunity to customize the space to your style while enjoying all the benefits of living in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods.
