About this home

Welcome to 15 Forbell Drive, a completely renovated home in desirable West Norwalk where every detail has been thoughtfully updated for modern living. Situated on a sunny corner lot, this move-in-ready residence is flooded with natural light thanks to an abundance of brand new Harvey windows. Enjoy total peace of mind with all major improvements already completed, including a brand-new roof, siding, windows, HVAC system with central air, and efficient propane heat. Inside, you'll find stunning new hardwood floors throughout, beautifully renovated bathrooms, and a designer-inspired kitchen featuring stunning quartz countertops, brand new stainless steel appliances and modern fixtures. The seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle continues with a newly installed patio overlooking the professionally landscaped yard-perfect for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying the changing seasons. Professional lawn treatment is included for one full year, ensuring the property remains pristine. Combining timeless style, quality craftsmanship, and extensive upgrades, 15 Forbell Drive offers the rare opportunity to own a truly turnkey home in a convenient West Norwalk location minutes from major highways, shopping, and restaurants.
