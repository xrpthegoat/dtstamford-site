About this home

Just in time for the 2025/2026 school year! Great opportunity to move right in to this freshly painted and new hardwood floors to this rare stand-alone 2-bedroom, 2.5-bath townhouse. It offers exceptional privacy with no shared walls in the highly desirable Wilton Crest complex. Featuring a private attached 1-car garage and a welcoming foyer that opens to a serene back patio framed by natural rock walls, this home is ideal for peaceful living. The spacious living room includes hardwood floors, a wood-burning fireplace, and sliders to a balcony-perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The open-concept layout continues into a generous dining area and a bright kitchen with ample cabinet space. Upstairs, the large primary suite boasts multiple closets and a full bath with dual sinks, a walk-in shower, and a jetted tub. A second en-suite bedroom offers comfort for guests or family. Additional highlights include central air, new heating and A/C systems, and great storage with a partial basement, laundry room, and garage. Residents enjoy access to a pool and tennis courts, with low-maintenance living just minutes from Wilton Center's shops, restaurants, and train station. Available unfurnished for a 1-2 year lease.
