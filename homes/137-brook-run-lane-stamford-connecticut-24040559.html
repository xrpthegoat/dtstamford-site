About this home

Discover the charm of this delightful Ranch-style home nestled in the highly coveted Mid-Ridges neighborhood. Step inside to find newly revealed hardwood floors. The expansive living room invites you to relax by the cozy fireplace, perfect for those crisp New England evenings. Your kitchen offers ample cabinetry and counter space for all your culinary creations. The adjoining dining room is ideal for hosting memorable meals with ease and style. Convenience abounds with a main-level laundry room, three spacious bedrooms, and two full baths. Need more space? The lower level offers endless possibilities with its above-ground windows that flood the area with natural light, just waiting for your personal touch. While the deck may need a little TLC, imagine the countless hours of outdoor enjoyment it will provide once revitalized. The beautifully manicured, level yard is perfect for family fun, whether it's setting up a playset or enjoying a game of catch. Additional features include an attached one-car garage, efficient natural gas heating, and public water and sewer services. Located just moments from shopping, top-rated schools, houses of worship, and easy access to major parkways, this home offers both convenience and tranquility.
