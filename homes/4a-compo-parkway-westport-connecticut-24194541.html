About this home

Build your coastal dream home on a rare, full acre* property along one of Compo Beach's most coveted cul-de-sacs, where opportunities of this caliber seldom become available. Ideally positioned just across from Longshore and moments from the shoreline, this extraordinary setting places Westport's most treasured amenities at your fingertips: golf, tennis, swimming, boating, beaches, playgrounds, fine dining, and more. To be custom built by Coastal Luxury Homes, Fairfield County's foremost luxury builder, this distinguished residence will offer three beautifully finished levels defined by unrivaled quality and meticulous craftsmanship. Expansive walls of glass will bathe the interiors in natural light, showcase the magnificent grounds, and create a seamless connection to the outdoors. Exquisite millwork, natural stone, refined finishes, a curated selection of top-tier appliances, and generous allowances will elevate every space, while the sumptuous primary suite will serve as a private, resort-inspired retreat. Thoughtfully tailored to your every wish with room for a pool, this remarkable home will reflect your personal style and vision for sophisticated coastal living.
