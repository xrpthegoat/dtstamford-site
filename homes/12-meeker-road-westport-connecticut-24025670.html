About this home

Your journey home begins with a Belgium-block lined drive through a peaceful park-like setting where you will discover this newly transformed stunning property, nestled on over an acre with room for a pool. A welcoming veranda invites you to explore further. Inside, the 6700 sf home is perfect for both grand-scale celebrations and intimate living. Meticulously renovated with a new roof, driveway and mechanicals for luxury and peace of mind. The main level features a gracious entry leading to a Living Room with a fireplace and a sunlit Sitting Room/Den. French doors open to an elegant Dining Room with a Butler's Pantry and fireplace. The newly refreshed kitchen and breakfast room overlook the grounds, connecting the indoor and out. A striking Family Room with a fireplace offers access to the serene backyard. Upstairs, the new Primary suite features a balcony, vaulted ceilings, a fireplace, and spa-like bathroom. Four additional bedrooms, three full baths and a vaulted Bonus Room offer ample space on the second floor. An office and Exercise Room complete the Lower Level. Offering the perfect setting for large scale entertaining or as a private sanctuary, the magnificent grounds present a spectacular backdrop for this classic home including an extensive bluestone patio, masonry walls, specimen trees, plants which bloom all season long and manicured lawns. Conveniently located with easy access to schools and amenities, this home offers a perfect blend of convenience and serenity.
