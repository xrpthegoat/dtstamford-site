About this home

This apartment features hardwood floors, a renovated kitchen and bath, new windows, and fresh paint (renovated 2023, one tenant since). Enter through the side door into a spacious mudroom, hang your bags/coats, slide your shoes into the built-ins then head upstairs to your spacious hideaway moments away from downtown Greenwich. Upon entering the upstairs you are greeted by an open living room, full bath with LG Washer/Dryer. Off the living room, you have access to your bedroom with a large closet that has well-portioned built-ins. Down the open hallway you will enter the tastefully renovated kitchen - granite countertops, new LG refrigerator, oven and dishwasher. Off the kitchen, you have a spacious outdoor deck to enjoy the direct sunlight and tranquility of the property. Easy walk to Metro North and Greenwich Avenue.
