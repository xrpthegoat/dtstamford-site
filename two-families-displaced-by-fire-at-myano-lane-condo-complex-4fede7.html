Two Families Displaced by Fire at Myano Lane Condo Complex

What’s Happening The fire took place at a condominium complex, located at Myano Lane in Stamford, Connecticut.

Uploaded · August 5, 2026 at 7:39 AM ET

What’s Happening The fire took place at a condominium complex, located at Myano Lane in Stamford, Connecticut.

What’s Happening

The fire took place at a condominium complex, located at Myano Lane in Stamford, Connecticut. The building is described as a seven-unit residential complex. According to the report, the fire originated in a middle unit and spread into the attic, prompting the declaration of a third alarm. Despite the intensity, firefighters managed to contain the fire to the unit of origin, preventing it from spreading to neighboring units [1]. One of the adjacent units was unoccupied at the time of the fire. The damage to the affected unit was described as extensive, while the adjacent units suffered significant smoke and water damage. The fire was brought under control by just before 9 p.m. on July 19, 2026 [1]. The cause of the fire remains under investigation, and no specific timeline for the investigation’s completion has been released.
