About this home

Do you desire a fabulously updated condo in the bustling South Norwalk neighborhood? Look no further! Ideally nestled just moments from the waterfront, this 3BR/1.5BA property delights with both a premium location & incredible upgrades. Fashioned for contemporary needs, the residence features beautiful wood flooring, recessed lighting, an openly flowing layout, & a cozy living room. Impressively updated for culinary enjoyment, the stylish kitchen includes stainless-steel appliances, granite countertops, white shaker cabinetry, an undermount stainless-steel sink, trendy open shelving, breakfast bar, and a passthrough window to the adjacent dining area. Bask in the sizable primary bedroom, which has double closets and plenty of room for easy furniture arrangement. Both guest bedrooms are generously sized with dedicated closets and may also be utilized as home offices, gyms, or art studios. Designed for blissful self-care routines, the full bathroom boasts a shower/tub combo, sleek storage vanity, and gorgeous tile flooring. Additional considerations include dedicated and guestparking, in-unit laundry, close to I-95, train station, & public transit, and much more! Explore to discover the exciting local landscape with award-winning cuisine, multiple boutiques, creative art galleries, thrilling nightlife spots & SoNo Collection Mall.
