About this home

Tucked away on a quiet Street in the heart of Cos Cob, this charming 4-bedroom, 3.1-bathroom home offers comfort, convenience, and classic Greenwich living. With its sun-filled interior, flexible floor plan, and private backyard oasis, this home is ideal for anyone looking for a peaceful retreat close to town. The main level features a bright living room with a wood-burning fireplace, an open-concept dining area, and a spacious kitchen with direct access to the patio-perfect for entertaining or relaxing in the fully fenced backyard. Upstairs, the primary bedroom offers ample closet space, en-suite potential. Additional bedrooms are generously sized and share a full hall bath. A finished lower level with full bath, provides versatile space for a playroom, home office, or guest suite, complete with laundry and access to the attached garage. Lovingly maintained with updates throughout, this move-in-ready home sits on a quiet street just minutes from Cos Cob village, train, parks.
