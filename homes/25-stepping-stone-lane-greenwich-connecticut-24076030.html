About this home

This elegant mid-country Greenwich home offers a blend of classic charm and modern comfort. Set on a spacious lot, the home has a welcoming front entrance with balcony. Inside, the two-story foyer greets you with a staircase, hardwood floors, and abundant light. The open layout connects the living spaces, which is ideal for living and entertaining. The home includes a primary suite and a guest suite on the first floor, while the second level features two bedrooms connected by a bathroom. The finished basement adds a family room, a full bedroom, and a bath. Outside, the oversized deck has a pool. Surrounded by mature trees, this property provides a private retreat close to schools, shopping, and dining. With some updates, this home can be transformed into a spectacular residence.
