About this home

Welcome to this Cape-style home located in the desirable Wolfpit neighborhood of Norwalk. The main level features an open living and dining area that flows into the kitchen, creating a flexible and functional floor plan. There is a bedroom and a full bathroom on the first floor, with two additional bedrooms and a half bath upstairs. Outside, you'll find a private backyard-perfect for outdoor dining, play, or relaxing. The property includes a detached garage, a new driveway, and a generator hook-up for added convenience. Ideally situated close to downtown Norwalk, Stew Leonard's, Westport, I-95, and the Merritt Parkway, this home offers both suburban tranquility and easy access to everyday amenities. Sold "as-is."
