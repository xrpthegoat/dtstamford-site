About this home

Exceptional Modern Living on a Pristine 1-Acre Property Experience the pinnacle of modern living at 39 Wedgemere Road-an extraordinary contemporary residence in one of Stamford's most sought-after neighborhoods. Extensively renovated and thoughtfully reimagined over the past four years, this 4-bedroom, 3.1-bath home blends high-end finishes with a refined sense of luxury. Set on a meticulously manicured 1-acre lot with professional landscaping, the grounds are designed for effortless entertaining and everyday enjoyment. Highlights include a gunite pool, expansive outdoor patio with outdoor kitchen, a firepit, greenhouse and lush, private surroundings that create a true sanctuary. Inside, the home features clean architectural lines and a light-filled layout tailored to today's lifestyle. The primary suite is a standout, offering a show stopping walk-in closet and a private deck- perfect for morning coffee or quiet retreat. The flexible layout includes incredible guest or au pair quarters just off the kitchen, complete with a bedroom, full bath, and sitting room-ideal for privacy and comfort. And for those who love to entertain, the renovated lower level provides an impressive space for recreation, media, or gatherings. This is a rare opportunity to live in a turnkey ready home in a premier Stamford location-where every detail has been carefully curated for luxury and livability.
