About this home

Highest and best by 5:00 on Monday 1/19. Tucked behind mature trees on two private acres, this beautifully updated two-story Colonial feels like a world of its own. Set on one of Wilton's most sought-after streets, it offers the rare pairing of refined design and everyday serenity. Inside, the home is pristine and timeless, where custom moldings and newly refinished hardwood floors add warmth and character throughout. The gourmet kitchen anchors the home with custom cabinetry, brand-new appliances, and an easy flow into the sun-filled family room and cheerful breakfast nook. A dramatic wall of glass sliders opens to a blue stone patio and terraced gardens, turning every gathering into a seamless indoor-outdoor experience. Upstairs, the primary suite feels like a private retreat, with a spa-like bath and tranquil wooded views. Three additional bright and generous bedrooms share a well-appointed full bath, while a dedicated laundry room adds everyday ease. Above the two-car garage, a vaulted bonus room offers endless flexibility, whether imagined as a peaceful home office, playroom, or fitness studio. The finished lower level features new flooring, abundant storage, and a workshop ready for creative pursuits. All of this sits just five minutes from Wilton's town center, with easy access to shops, restaurants, several Metro-North stations, the Merritt Parkway, and Route 95. This is a home where privacy, beauty, and modern comfort live in perfect balance.
