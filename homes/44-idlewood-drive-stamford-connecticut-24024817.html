About this home

ONE LEVEL LIVING awaits in this beautifully renovated ranch styled for today's casual entertaining, comfort & ease! Inside you'll enjoy an airy, open flow of sun-filled rooms featuring quality finishes and detailing. Cathedral ceilings, rich hardwood flooring, French doors, 2-fireplaces, built-in shelving & updated baths perfectly complement the fresh space. A sparkling & entertainer's kitchen opens to the window-lined great room with cathedral ceilings, fireplace & custom built-in shelving. A newer primary bedroom features an inviting marble bath and sliders to patio enclave outdoors. Two additional bedrooms, a full bath + powder room complete the main floor. Outside, you'll enjoy a level half-acre of private, manicured property with separate play space, refreshing inground pool and exceptional stonework. An oversized 1-car garage w/storage leads to the expansive lower level with high ceilings - this large, ready-to-finish space is filled with possibility, including an in-law apartment (plans to finish available)! Completely renovated from top-to-bottom, buyers will further enjoy the benefits of newer mechanicals: plumbing, electric, windows, roof, sprinkler system, cedar impressions siding and underground utilities. Located in the heart of convenient & sought-after Newfield, walking distance to houses of worship & popular Newfield Elementary school, Rippowam Middle School, shopping center & very close to the public golf course.
