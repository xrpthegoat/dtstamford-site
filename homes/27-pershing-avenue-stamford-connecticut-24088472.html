About this home

Welcome to 27 Pershing Avenue, a charming single-family ranch nestled in Stamford's sought-after Newfield neighborhood. This home offers 3 bedrooms and 1.5 bathrooms. Step inside to find a bright and inviting living room, a functional kitchen equipped with an electric range and refrigerator, and a convenient lower-level laundry area with washer and dryer included. Outside, enjoy a level 0.22-acre lot with a deck and gutters, perfect for outdoor relaxation and entertaining. The property includes a paved driveway with off-street parking for two vehicles. Located just behind the Grade A shopping center off Newfield Avenue, this home offers easy access to local amenities, schools, and transportation routes. 27 Pershing Avenue presents a wonderful option in a prime Stamford location.
