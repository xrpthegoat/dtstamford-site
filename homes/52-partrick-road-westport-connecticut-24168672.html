About this home

Discover the perfect balance of comfort and convenience with this unique split-level residence that sits on 1.70 private acres, gracefully set back from the road to offer outstanding privacy and tranquility. Step inside to experience spacious, light-filled interiors with an open floor plan, expansive picture windows, and beautiful hardwood floors that seamlessly connect the living spaces-ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The home boasts a brand new roof with a transferable warranty and a full house generator for uninterrupted peace of mind. Entertain effortlessly with spacious front and back decks, a built-in bar, and a unique theater room featuring stadium seating for unforgettable movie nights. The home includes an updated main floor powder room and a dedicated private home office with custom built-in cabinetry and shelving, making working from home a breeze. Enjoy the comfort of 5 bedrooms and 4 full & 2 half bathrooms, including three en suite bedrooms. One suite offers a private entrance, perfect for an au pair/multigenerational living. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite provides a serene retreat with a charming bay window and built-in bench. Additional living space offers versatility for a variety of lifestyle needs. Outdoor amenities are designed for family fun and relaxation, featuring a hot tub, zip line, trampoline, and two tree swings. The decks overlook the serene, private setting, providing the ideal backdrop for gatherings large and small
