About this home

This impeccably maintained 4 Bedroom, 2 1/2 bath Cornell Colonial located in one the most desirable neighborhoods in Wilton is a rare find! Step inside and you immediately feel the warmth & pride of ownership that this home exudes. Gracious living room w/ fireplace is flooded with natural light from the large windows. Cozy den w/ wood burning stove/fireplace and book-cases leads to lovely screened-in porch. The spacious chef's eat-in kitchen, complete w/ top of the line appliances, custom cabinetry, granite countertops & French Doors, steps away from the terrace and pool, is an entertainer's dream. Formal dining room, laundry room & half bath complete the first floor. Bonus room, conveniently located off the kitchen w/ built-ins & vaulted ceilings, ideal for playroom/office/family room. The second floor has 4 large bedrooms & two baths, including the primary suite w/ walk-in closet & ensuite bathroom w/ heated marble floor & walk-in shower. And if you are looking for more places to hang out, head to the lower level with over 1000 sq ft of finished space, perfect for office, exercise & recreation. Relax by the pool & terrace and marvel at nature's paradise. So many features make this property extra special such as the private setting abutting the Town Preserve, multifunctional living spaces, gorgeous curb appeal on beloved cul-de-sac, ideal for walking/biking. The vibrant Bald Hill community hosts many events & has close proximity to Wilton & Ridgefield Town Centers.
