About this home

Located in the quiet Riverhouse community, this spacious 2-bedroom, 2-bath condo offers comfortable and convenient living. Sunlight fills the living and dining area, creating a warm space centered around the fireplace. Sliders lead to a large deck overlooking the Rippowam River, perfect for enjoying the outdoors and peaceful views. The unit includes one assigned covered parking space, then a first-come first-serve basis parking spot, and a large basement storage unit. Ideally situated near Ridgeway Shopping Center, Scalzi Park, and downtown Stamford, the community also features a beautiful in-ground pool and scenic riverfront setting. Enjoy easy living in a prime location. New HVAC as of June 30.
