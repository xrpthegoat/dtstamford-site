About this home

Location, convenience and easy living come together in this bright and inviting 2-bedroom, 1.5-bath townhouse-style condo. Ideally located moments from the Stamford train station-perfect for commuters-and adjacent to sought-after Harbor Point with its vibrant waterfront, restaurants and amenities, the condo is also less than a mile from downtown Stamford. The main level features an open layout with hardwood floors and abundant natural light. Sliders from the living room lead to a private deck, perfect for morning coffee or relaxing outdoors. The kitchen features granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, a breakfast bar and large pantry, with a convenient half bath also on this level. Upstairs, the primary bedroom offers a walk-in closet, while the second bedroom features generous closet space, and a full bath completes this level. Additional highlights include an in-unit washer and dryer, two assigned parking spaces, guest parking and a convenient lower-level storage cage. Freshly painted and move-in ready, this pet-friendly community also has no rental restrictions, offering added flexibility for owners.
