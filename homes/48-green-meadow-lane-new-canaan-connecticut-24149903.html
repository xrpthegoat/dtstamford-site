About this home

Experience the pinnacle of New Canaan modernism at 48 Green Meadow, a 4,400+ sq ft architectural masterpiece designed by the acclaimed Austin Patterson Disston Architects. Tucked away at the end of a scenic, ultra-private cul-de-sac, this estate seamlessly fuses eco-conscious Geo-thermal technology with high-end luxury. The open-concept interior is a light-drenched sanctuary featuring five bedrooms, including a "Zen" primary suite with cathedral ceiling and glass sliders to a private deck overlooking the vast property. 4 additional bedrooms and an upstairs lounge area. A spectacular lower level complete with a bar, fireplace and floor-to-ceiling glass sliders for ultimate entertaining. The lifestyle continues outdoors with a shower and alfresco fireplace creating an unrivaled oasis for relaxation or social soirees-all finished with a professional-grade 4-car garage for the serious collector.
