About this home

Steps from the iconic Shorefront Park pillars, this charming waterfront community Colonial offers a lifestyle defined by ocean breezes, effortless access to SONO, and coastal walks to restaurants, shops and the Metro-North train. Set in one of Norwalk's most loved neighborhoods, this three-bedroom, two-and-a-half-bath Craftsman blends classic character with flexible living. A separate entrance with an efficiency kitchen creates an ideal opportunity for a possible apartment, in-law, guest suite, or ADU. Architectural details shine throughout, including beautiful hardwood floors, distinctive period woodwork, stained glass windows and an updated kitchen that balances charm and function. Four levels of living space provide exceptional versatility, including a walk-up third floor perfect for a home office, playroom, or creative space with peaceful ocean views. A full basement offers plenty of storage, with laundry conveniently relocated from the second floor (Bathroom Closet is plumbed on second floor). Out back, a sweet screened-in porch creates a private retreat overlooking gardening space and play area. A large shed with work bench, tool storage and electricity adds even more flexibility and storage. Walk to train to NYC, Kayak the Norwalk Harbor and bike to Calf Pasture Beach. This is coastal living with heart, history, and room to grow. Don't miss the video attached.
