About this home

Set back from the street in a quiet, beautifully landscaped enclave, this fully updated 3 level - 3BR 3.5BA townhome delivers comfort, style, and space. Ideally located just a short walk from the Springdale and Glenbrook train stations, as well as popular restaurants and shopping, this is an ideal setting for both commuters and those seeking a serene, low maintenance lifestyle. The main level welcomes you with a bright and inviting foyer that leads into an open concept living and dining area. Here, a cozy fireplace and bay window create a warm and inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. The spacious kitchen shines with granite countertops, ample cabinetry, a built in pantry, and sliding doors that open to a private deck ideal for morning coffee or summer cookouts on the grill. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a tranquil retreat with its expansive layout, dual closets, and a spa inspired bathroom featuring a stand up shower, jetted tub, and dual vanity sinks. An additional generously sized bedroom and a second full bathroom complete the upper level. The walk out lower level provides even more versatility, with a family room ideal for entertainment, a bedroom & full bath and access to a private covered patio - perfect for guest room or an in law/au pair suite. Additional features include detached garage parking and ample storage. Privacy and convenience in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. Don't miss this rare opportunity!
