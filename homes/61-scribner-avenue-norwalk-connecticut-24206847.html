About this home

When one chapter ends, another begins...this meticulously maintained and thoughtfully updated house has raised a family and built traditions and memories for over 50 years. Now, it's your turn. There is a cohesive flow to this house which includes living room, eat-in kitchen, 3 bedrooms, 1 bathroom, sunporch, finished lower level and beautiful curb appeal. To add to its value, this home has hardwood floors throughout, a new roof, new Central A/C, new furnace, newly paved driveway and a brand new hot water heater. The kitchen and bathroom have both been beautifully updated! The lower level is the perfect space to gather friends and family for Sunday dinners or weeknight card games with a built-in bar, entertainment area and dining area! The location is great for commuters with easy access to I-95. Minutes to Rowayton, shops, restaurants and marina. This is a rare opportunity to buy a turn-key house at this price! Come, take a tour and fall in love!
