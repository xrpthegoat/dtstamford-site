About this home

Direct Waterfront with one of the most amazing views of the Norwalk Harbor and Long Island sound. Relax in the serenity of the glistening water, watch the boats in the harbor pass by, enjoy the twinkling evening lights of East Norwalk across the water, wake-up to morning sunrises on the horizon, and let's not forget the 4th of July Fireworks! Unbelievable unobstructed views from almost every room of the home let along the deck. Privately yours to enjoy! This home also features a walk up attic--just waiting to be expanded. Right now, it is all one level living. If living on the water is your dream, than this is your dream home.
