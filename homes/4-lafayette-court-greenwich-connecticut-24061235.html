About this home

Nestled near Greenwich Avenue, this bright 1-bedroom gem exudes a charming Parisian ambiance. Located on the 4th floor of a well-maintained building with an elevator and reserved parking, this inviting unit blends classic charm with modern upgrades. Features include an updated kitchen, high ceilings and a sleek renovated bathroom. The spacious eat-in kitchen and versatile home office creates an ideal work-from-home setup, while wraparound windows fill the space with natural light. Enjoy the rooftop terrace with breathtaking views of the town and Long Island Sound. This building features a live in super and additional storage in the basement. Just blocks from necessities, this apartment perfectly balances comfort and convenience, offering a taste of Greenwich living with European flair.
