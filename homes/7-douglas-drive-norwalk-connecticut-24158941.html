About this home

7 Douglas Drive, Norwalk, CT is being offered for sale for the first time in over 50 years. Lovingly maintained by the same owners, this well cared for home sits on a private wooded interior lot in desirable West Norwalk, offering a rare combination of neighborhood convenience and peaceful seclusion while remaining close to major highways, shopping, and multiple Metro North train stations for an easy commute. Inside, the home features a bright layout with a spacious living room and hardwood floors throughout, including beneath the carpets. The expanded dining room was designed as a sun filled sunroom with vaulted ceiling and paddle fan, while the kitchen features raised ceilings with skylights and opens to an expanded deck overlooking the peaceful backyard. Upstairs offers comfortable bedrooms and a renovated primary bathroom with skylights. A flexible office or 4th bedroom addition with adjoining one car garage provides extra space for whatever lifestyle calls for. A new architectural roof and exterior paint were completed within the past two years. Additional improvements over time include gutters with gutter guards, central air on the upper level, a wall unit AC on the main level. This home is also connected to city water and sewer. With approximately 2,400 square feet including a spacious basement, beautiful natural surroundings, and a sought after West Norwalk location, this well cared for home offers a wonderful opportunity to move right in and make it your own.
