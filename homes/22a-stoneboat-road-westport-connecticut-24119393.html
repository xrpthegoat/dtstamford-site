About this home

Private, peaceful, modernized 1 bedroom cottage nestled within a quiet, idyllic Westport cul-de-sac neighborhood. Inside, a modern, loft-style city vibe with soaring ceilings and gorgeous architectural windowed walls meets quintessential New England comfort and charm with exposed beams, cherry wood-like floors and warm accent lighting. Open and airy living room with large sliders out to deck opens to the sparkling eat-in kitchen featuring vaulted ceilings, classic slate-like tile flooring, quartz countertops, stainless steel refrigerator, stainless steel glass-topped range and stunning tree-topped view from a double-elevated picture window as the focal point, like a beautiful piece of artwork. The bedroom also offers high ceilings and recessed lighting. Outside, relax, reflect and rejuvenate on the expansive deck. Immersed in nature and fresh air, it is the perfect place to retreat. Central air and heat and a brand new, full-size, Electrolux (best in quality brand) washer and dryer makes it complete. Water usage and landscaping included. Parking for 1 car only, no exceptions. 1/2 mile to Merritt Parkway and 1-5 miles to everything else you would want or need in your day: downtown Westport (and Wilton) retail, restaurants (Hudson Malone, Nomade, Spotted Horse, Pink Sumo & Starbucks to name just a few), parks, fitness, tennis, pickleball, paddle, sailing, kayaking, rowing, Longshore (golf/pool/tennis), Compo Beach, RR and I-95! Welcome to Connecticut - come hang your hat today!
