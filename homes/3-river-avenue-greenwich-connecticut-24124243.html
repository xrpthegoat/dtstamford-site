About this home

Corner-unit townhome in desirable Byram Shore blends coastal living with commuter convenience. Just seconds to the water and minutes to Metro-North, this location connects you to both vibrant Port Chester and emerging downtown Byram. Inside, hardwood floors, bay window, fireplace, and sliding doors to a private deck offer a great foundation. The open kitchen/dining flows to the living room, with a powder room on the main level. Upstairs: two bedrooms, large bath; top floor: vaulted loft with skylights, perfect as 3rd bedroom, office, or family room. Lower level includes garage, laundry, and storage. With a prime layout and location, just add your finishing touches.
