About this home

Experience the epitome of modern living at the Lounsbury Carriage House found in New Canaan's most sought-after condominium development at Jelliff Mill Falls. This exceptional loft-style residence boasts remarkable architectural details, gorgeous high ceilings and luxurious finishes throughout. With an open floor plan, two fireplaces (one gas, the other wood burning), a lovely private terrace and an exceptionally convenient location, 41 Jelliff Mill Falls represents a chic, carefree lifestyle where superior quality construction meets elegant living. The charming complex is beautifully nestled along the banks of the Noroton River offering breathtaking water views, exquisite landscape design and expansive green spaces.
