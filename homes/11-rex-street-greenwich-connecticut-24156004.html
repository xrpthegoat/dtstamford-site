About this home

A wonderful opportunity to own in the Glenville School district for under $1M! Set on a quiet dead-end street along the Byram River and a stone's throw from Pemberwick Park, this 3-bedroom Mediterranean-style bungalow offers charm and easy living. The inviting, vaulted living room with wood-burning fireplace flows easily into the dining area and kitchen. Hardwood floors throughout. The walk-out lower level offers new flooring, extensive storage and a second washer/dryer. 11 Rex presents an exceptional entry point into Greenwich with immediate livability and future potential. The residence has been formally removed from the Special Flood Hazard Area by FEMA; flood insurance is not required.
