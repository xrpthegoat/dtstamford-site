About this home

Nestled in a highly desirable North Stamford neighborhood, this exquisite 4-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom colonial is perfectly situated on a quiet cul-de-sac. Surrounded by mature trees and offering stunning views of nature and wildlife from every window, this home effortlessly combines privacy, functionality, and modern elegance. The heart of the home is the luxury chef's kitchen, complete with granite countertops, a breakfast bar and island, high-end appliances, and ample storage. The open-concept design flows seamlessly into a sun-drenched family room with vaulted ceilings and skylights, creating a warm and inviting space to gather. The main level also boasts a cozy living room with a fireplace and an adjoining formal dining room-ideal for hosting memorable gatherings. For more intimate moments, retreat to the den, where a second fireplace provides the perfect ambiance for winter evenings spent with a good book or favorite movie. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a tranquil escape, featuring a private luxury bathroom with a large shower stall and a spacious walk-in closet. Three additional generously sized bedrooms, a fully remodeled bathroom and laundry closet complete the upper level. Additional highlights include a large unfinished basement for storage, a two-car attached garage, and a level backyard that's perfect for outdoor activities. This home is truly a must-see for those seeking comfort, style, and an unbeatable location.
