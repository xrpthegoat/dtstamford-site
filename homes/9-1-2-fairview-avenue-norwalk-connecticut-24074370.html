About this home

The Spring Hill Area of Norwalk, this rental has 3 spacious bedrooms 1.5 baths, a comfortable living experience with everything included except for gas & electric. Additional amenities include an attached one-car garage with plenty of storage capability, all situated close to Rte. 7, and bus lines for easy commuting. Spacious rear yard fenced in.
