About this home

*Discover this beautiful 1996 Colonial, located in a quiet South Wilton cul-de-sac on over 2 private acres. The meticulously maintained home offers a welcoming double-height entryway, with 9ft ceilings throughout. A combined dining/living room feature a stately fireplace. The open-plan kitchen & family room offer an easy flow. The kitchen boasts a large center island with stools, a spacious pantry & ample storage. A bay window in the eating area provides a serene spot to watch the birds by the brook. The family room has a 2nd fireplace & French door to the large screened porch, ideal for relaxing or hosting friends. Outdoors, you'll find an expansive stone patio with a stunning fireplace, perfect for crisp fall evenings. Upstairs, the primary suite showcases two walk-in closets & a luxurious updated marble bathroom with a soaking tub & double sinks. Three additional bedrooms all feature double closets & hardwood floors. The updated hall bathroom includes double sinks & a separate tub/shower/toilet area for convenience. Entries from the driveway & garage lead to the mudroom & laundry room, while a home office offers privacy. There's also a bonus room with vaulted ceiling, built-ins & play loft. The owners have made smart upgrades incl. a new roof, gutters, sump pumps & EV charger plus Cat 6 cabling and a new home network. This home truly combines comfort, functionality, and style in a prime location, just steps to Ambler Farm and the Norwalk River Valley Trail... don't miss it
