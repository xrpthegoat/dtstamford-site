About this home

Drive up the circular driveway and discover this spacious, desirable contemporary home bursting with curb appeal, beautifully set on a level, private acre. Light pours through skylights and walls of glass, creating a bright and airy atmosphere throughout. A welcoming entry opens to a stunning two-story living room highlighted by a dramatic stone fireplace and wet bar - perfect for entertaining. The open, flowing layout continues into the inviting family room, featuring a second stone fireplace, and an updated kitchen that connects seamlessly for easy gatherings and everyday living. A generous dining room is perfect for hosting memorable celebrations. Upstairs, you'll find four spacious bedrooms, including a serene primary suite with sliders to a private deck - your own peaceful retreat. The laundry room is conveniently located upstairs. The finished lower level expands the living space with a fantastic recreation room with a screen projector and speakers for movie night, a full bath, and a versatile bonus room ideal for guests, a home office, or workout space. An oversized two-car garage completes this exceptional home that blends comfort, style, abundant natural light, and exceptional entertaining spaces.
