About this home

PLEASE NOTE: BEST AND FINAL OFFERS DUE BY TUESDAY, APRIL 21ST AT NOON. Set within the highly desirable, gated Lansdowne gated community, this inviting, light-filled residence offers comfortable, easy living surrounded by 33 acres of beautifully landscaped, park-like grounds. Spanning over 2,000 square feet, the home features a flexible layout with two spacious primary bedroom suites offering the option of a main-level or second-floor retreat to suit your lifestyle. The inviting eat-in kitchen is anchored by a cozy fireplace and opens to a private patio, perfect for morning coffee or relaxed outdoor dining. The dramatic living room impresses with a vaulted 2-story ceiling and a fireplace, creating a warm yet airy gathering space. Just beyond, a generous private terrace overlooks the serene rear setting, providing an ideal backdrop for entertaining or quiet evenings at home. Additional highlights include a sunroom/office with custom built-ins, a 2nd-floor loft suitable for a den or office, abundant storage, new wall-to-wall carpeting and a two-car garage. Residents of Lansdowne enjoy club-style amenities including a pool, tennis courts and beautifully maintained grounds that offer both privacy and a true sense of community. Thoughtfully designed and ideally located, this home combines comfort, versatility, and low-maintenance living in one of the area's most desirable condominium communities. See Agent Remarks
