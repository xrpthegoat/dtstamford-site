About this home

Just moments from the shore, this beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath colonial offers the perfect blend of classic architecture and modern upgrades in an unbeatable coastal location. With over 2,000 square feet of stylish living space, every corner of this home has been thoughtfully enhanced to provide comfort, light, and a seamless connection to the outdoors. Step inside and you'll be welcomed by sun-splashed rooms with windows that frame views of the surrounding greenery. The sleek, updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances, plentiful cabinetry, and a layout designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The dining and living areas are open and airy-ideal for gathering with friends or enjoying quiet evenings at home. Just outside, a spacious deck expands your living space, offering the perfect perch for morning coffee or sunset cocktails. The oversized backyard is a rare find-lush, level, and fully ready for weekend games, garden beds, or simply soaking up the peaceful, beachy atmosphere. With the beach, parks, and neighborhood eateries all within walking distance, you'll find that everything you love about coastal living is right at your fingertips. Whether you're in the mood for a barefoot walk on the sand or a bite to eat at a local cafe, it's all just around the corner.
