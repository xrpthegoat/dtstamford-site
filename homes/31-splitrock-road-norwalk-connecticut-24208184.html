About this home

Absolutely amazing mid century modern waterfront home. Located in the historic Village Creek community, this renovated home offers lovely and serene water views from every room. Perched higher in the neighborhood, there is no flood insurance required. So many special features highlight this home including a dramatic sun drenched open floor plan encased in floor to ceiling windows and stone fireplace; wrap around upper level balcony/deck off the huge master bedroom suite; his and hers offices; legal in-law apartment with separate entrance; patio and deck with sunken hot tub. Many recent improvements including brand new carpet on upper level, roof (5 years old), new water line from street (2025). Village Creek offers residents exclusive access to a private beach, tennis courts, deep water marina, paddle craft racks, dog park, playground, athletic field and more. This home offers the perfect feel of vacation and everyday living. Only 1 hour from NYC.
