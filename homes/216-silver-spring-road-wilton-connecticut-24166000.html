About this home

Classic modern architecture set on a bucolic, level, private acre along scenic Silver Spring Road. Spanning over 2,800 sq ft, this turnkey residence offers beautifully scaled rooms designed for effortless living. An abundance of natural light fills the home, while high ceilings, handsome millwork and open spaces create a sense of calm and understated style across three finished levels. The main level is a study in simplicity and function, highlighted by a stunning Great Room with fireplace and walls of windows opening to an expansive wraparound deck. The efficient, well-appointed Kitchen blends seamlessly with a spacious Dining Area, also bathed in natural light. An open staircase adds a striking architectural element to the home. Upstairs, the comfortable Primary Suite features an updated bath and generous walk-in closet. Three secondary bedrooms are well-proportioned and share a remodeled hall bath. In addition, there is a wonderful flex space on this level - perfect for a home office or a casual Family Room. The walk-out lower level includes a fireplace and versatile space ideal for casual entertaining, recreation or relaxation. Perfectly located just minutes to downtown Ridgefield and Wilton Center, with convenient access to Metro-North service from Wilton, New Canaan, and Katonah for an easy NYC commute. Enjoy the many features of living in Wilton CT - Top Rated Schools, Beautiful Landscapes, all forms of shopping & dining. Convenient to Down County destinations.
