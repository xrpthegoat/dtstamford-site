About this home

This conveniently located condo is absolutely pristine and move-in ready. The spacious living room opens to the dining area with a built-in bar with a "pass-thru" to the kitchen. The dining room has doors so it has the option to be used as an extra bedroom.The kitchen, with lots of storage space, has access to the private deck. The unit also includes two generously sized bedrooms, a full bath and laundry. There are two assigned parking spaces, and additional storage in a shed located at the end of the parking lot. You will see this unit was made for comfortable living. Located not too far from SoNo, with plenty of restaurants and the train station for an easy commute and shopping right around the corner, this is the place to be!
