About this home

Trade city congestion for space, privacy and the tranquility of West Norwalk at 8 Rising Road. Set on .63 acres with two separate driveways accessing two quiet cul-de-sacs, this 6-bedroom, 4-bath residence offers nearly 3,000 SF above grade plus approximately 1,000 SF in the walkout lower level. A substantial 2021 expansion added an entirely new second floor with 3 spacious bedrooms, including a sun-filled primary suite with custom California Closets and a beautifully appointed bath featuring a quartz double vanity, freestanding soaking tub and Carrara marble-tiled, glass-enclosed shower. Two additional bedrooms feature custom closets and share a Jack-and-Jill bath. The main level offers 3 more bedrooms, 2 full baths, laundry and a bright, comfortable layout. The generously sized kitchen features solid-wood cabinetry, stainless-steel appliances, abundant storage and natural light overlooking the surrounding greenery. The walkout lower level offers versatile space for a home office, gym, recreation, guests or extended-family living, while a 2-car garage adds year-round convenience. Enjoy the best of suburban living just minutes from the dining, shopping and entertainment of New Canaan and Darien, with nearby highways and Metro-North providing convenient access to NYC and the region's major airports.
