About this home

Rare opportunity to own a beautifully maintained home in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods! Located at 278 W Cedar St, this gorgeous 4-bedroom, 2 full bathroom home perfectly blends comfort, space, and charm. Filled with natural light and thoughtfully designed throughout, the home offers spacious living areas ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The inviting layout features generously sized bedrooms, updated bathrooms, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere from the moment you enter. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the main living spaces, creating the perfect setting for gatherings with family and friends. Large windows throughout the home bring in abundant sunlight and highlight the home's bright and airy feel. Outside, enjoy a private yard with plenty of space to relax, entertain, or garden. Conveniently located near parks, shopping, restaurants, schools, and major highways, this home offers the perfect combination of peaceful neighborhood living and easy access to everything Norwalk has to offer. A wonderful opportunity to own a truly special home in an amazing location!
