About this home

8 Rolling Lane offers modern updates in a quintessential New England neighborhood. Fully repainted and thoughtfully remodeled, this split-level home combines comfort, style, and functionality. The updated kitchen boasts a spacious island, stainless steel appliances including a built-in oven, granite countertops, and custom cabinetry, opening seamlessly to the dining area. An oversized living room with a state-of-the-art electric fireplace sets the stage for gatherings, while French doors lead to a cozy family room. The main level also features a renewed full bathroom and a flexible in-home office or guest room with garden views. Upstairs, find three spacious bedrooms and a sophisticated remodeled bathroom with natural stone tile and a glass shower door. Hardwood floors run throughout, adding warmth and character. Additional highlights include a blue stone entryway, a new back patio, and a lower level offering storage and expansion potential. Outdoors, enjoy meticulously kept landscaping, an oversized deck, a designated pool space, and a secure chicken coop with winter heater and automatic door. Located in a peaceful, bike-friendly neighborhood, the property is a short distance to all levels of schools, with quick access to the train station, Whole Foods, Stew Leonard's, shopping, and dining. A true private oasis in the heart of Norwalk.
