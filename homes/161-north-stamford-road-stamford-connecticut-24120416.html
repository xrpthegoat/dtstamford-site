About this home

Vermont in North Stamford, Connecticut! This quintessential country house is located minutes from the shops, restaurants, theatre, and a short drive from Stamford train station with NYC 45 minutes away. The 4-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom home beautifully blends traditional charm and elegance with modern updates throughout. Follow the stone footsteps into the gracious living room, cozy library, and exquisite formal dining room with doors to an expansive terrace. Bask in the warmth of a spacious family room with a fireplace, comfortable center-island chef's kitchen, and sunny breakfast nook with serving bar and wine chiller for entertaining. A newer guest bath and mudroom round out these public rooms on the first floor. The primary suite features a lovely tray ceiling, multiple windows, walk-in closet, and private spa-like bathroom. Three light-filled bedrooms and a full bathroom share the upstairs with inviting media/game room, and a charming third-floor loft completes the home's upper level. A heated driveway provides year-round ease, while perennial gardens and gently rolling lawns frame the backyard with many miles of trails to explore nearby. This idyllic corner of town awaits.
