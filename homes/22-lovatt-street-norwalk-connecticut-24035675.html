About this home

Location, Location, Location! This perfectly situated home in the desirable Wolfpit neighborhood is cute as a button and ready for its new owner. 4 Bedrooms (two on on the first floor, two on the second) offer endless versatility. So many floorplan options to consider...remove the wall between the Kitchen/Living Room to create more openness, and/or open the wall to one of the first floor bedrooms and use as additional living space. Dormer the second floor and add a second bath to create even more functionality. Or enjoy it exactly as it is! The Walk-Out Lower Level can be finished, and the under-house Garage means you can pull right in for maximum convenience. Newer furnace. New hot water heater in 2019. Gas heat, city water and city sewer. Beautiful landscaping enhances the inviting curb appeal of this quintessential cape. A charming stone wall, babbling brook and mature trees create a peaceful backyard oasis. Minutes to the famous Stew Leonard's, Westport shopping and dining, the vibrant SoNo area, beaches, major highways and 1/2 mile to train to NYC.
