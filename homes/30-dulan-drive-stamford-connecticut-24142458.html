About this home

*Please submit all final offers by 5:30pm on Mon, Dec 7th. Seller will review and make a decision no later than Tues, Dec 8th. Please be sure to include all appropriate documentation* Experience refined living in this beautifully appointed 5-bedroom, 3-bath ranch, privately nestled behind lush, mature landscaping on nearly 1.5 acres in coveted North Stamford. A newly paved circular driveway welcomes you to this stylish yet effortlessly livable residence, thoughtfully designed for both intimate daily comfort and sophisticated entertaining. Step inside to the inviting open-concept layout where the kitchen, dining, and living spaces flow seamlessly together, anchored by a charming wood-burning fireplace. The centerpiece of the home is the breathtaking 3 season great room-an architecturally striking space featuring soaring ceilings, and serene views across the pristine, level grounds and picturesque pond. Five generously sized bedrooms offer remarkable versatility, accommodating guests, dedicated work-from-home space, a nanny suite, or multi-generational living with ease. The home's layout masterfully balances openness with privacy, creating an atmosphere of relaxed luxury throughout. Perfectly positioned on a quiet cul-de-sac just moments from the Merritt Parkway, 8.5 miles from the Stamford train station, and minutes from shopping, dining, and everyday conveniences, this residence offers a rare harmony of tranquility and accessibility.
