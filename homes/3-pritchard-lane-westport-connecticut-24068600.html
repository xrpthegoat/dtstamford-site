About this home

Luxury living awaits! This stunning, newly renovated home sits on just under two acres of flat land in one of Westport's most beloved cul-de-sacs. An entertainer's dream, this 5600 sq ft charmer features a newly renovated gourmet kitchen with chef-grade appliances and radiant heated floors. French doors connect the kitchen with an expansive outdoor entertaining area. The beautifully landscaped, private backyard is the perfect place to enjoy every day or host guests. It features a large blue stone patio, a covered seating area, and a stunning gunite pool and spa. Off the kitchen, you'll find the perfect flex space great for a home office or small den that connects to the 2-car garage. The spacious living room offers lots of natural light with a wood-burning fireplace for those cold winter nights. The dining room is perfect for hosting dinner parties, large or small. On the other side of the first floor, you'll find two large bedrooms with a shared full bath. The fully renovated primary suite is a calming oasis with a walk-in closet, a beautiful ensuite with a double rain shower, and French doors that open to a lovely deck, perfect for enjoying a morning coffee. The second floor also includes two large bedrooms and an additional full bath. The 3rd floor is a perfect getaway space, with a bedroom and full bathroom. The finished lower level has a rec room w/fireplace, full bath & laundry room. This home truly has it all!
