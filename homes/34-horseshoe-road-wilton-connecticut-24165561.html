About this home

Welcome to this exceptional luxury Colonial in the heart of South Wilton, where convenience, comfort, and timeless style come together. Enjoy the rare ability to walk to the town center, train, library, grocery store, restaurants, and scenic walking trails, making everyday living both easy and connected. This home offers a flexible lifestyle for commuters, weekend escapes, and year-round enjoyment. Meticulously maintained and thoughtfully renovated, this move-in ready home features hardwood floors throughout the first level and a warm, inviting layout that works as well for quiet nights as it does for entertaining. Relax in the expansive family room with a cozy gas fireplace, or host memorable dinners in the dining room with its classic wood-burning fireplace and doors opening to an expansive back deck. The gourmet eat-in kitchen is the true heart of the home, outfitted with top-of-the-line appliances, granite countertops, a double oven, gas cooktop, and generous space for gathering, cooking, and casual meals. Upstairs, the large primary suite offers a peaceful retreat with a spa-like bathroom featuring a jetted soaking tub, separate shower, and dual sinks. Updated bathrooms throughout add a clean, modern feel. A huge, light-filled playroom with a large loft provides flexible space for recreation, work, or creative use.
