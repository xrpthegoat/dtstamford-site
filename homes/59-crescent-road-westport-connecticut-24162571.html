About this home

This beautifully maintained home in the heart of Westport offers the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience. Sun-filled interiors feature an open and inviting layout with spacious living areas, hardwood floors, and seamless flow for both everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen features quality cabinetry, top-of-the-line appliances, and ample counter space, opening to the family room. The spacious dining room is ideal for gatherings, while the living room boasts a cozy gas fireplace perfect for entertaining or just relaxing. Upstairs, generous bedrooms provide flexibility for family, guests, or home office needs, while the primary suite features custom cabinetry, a spa-like bath, and a walk-in closet with built-ins. The property is complemented by a beautifully landscaped backyard with fruit trees, specimen plantings, and a deck overlooking the yard-perfect for outdoor gatherings, play, or relaxation. Enjoy a truly convenient lifestyle with the ability to walk to Le Pain Quotidien for your morning coffee, as well as nearby fitness options, grocery stores, and more. Located just minutes from Westport's vibrant downtown, beaches, train, and top-rated schools, this home delivers easy access to everything you need. Move-in ready and full of charm, this is an exceptional opportunity not to be missed.
