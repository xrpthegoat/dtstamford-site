About this home

Rare opportunity to experience refined living in this unique end-unit townhouse within Wilton Crest. Flooded with natural light, the home boasts airy, well-appointed interiors. A vaulted living room, complete with hardwood floors and a fireplace, seamlessly extends to a private deck offering serene pond views - ideal for relaxation or entertaining. This low-maintenance residence combines comfort and convenience in a tranquil setting, a truly rare find.
