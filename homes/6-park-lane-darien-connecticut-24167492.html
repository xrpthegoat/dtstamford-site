About this home

Picturesque 1928 farmhouse set on a quiet, private neighborhood street, offering a seamless blend of timeless character and thoughtfully renovated modern style. Featuring 9-foot ceilings, pine hardwood floors and original oversized pocket doors, this home is rich in classic details while providing a well-designed layout ideal for both entertaining and everyday living. Gracious dining and living rooms flow effortlessly to a charming screened porch and into a spacious eat-in kitchen, creating a warm and cohesive living space. The second floor features three bedrooms and an updated hall bath highlighted by a beautiful clawfoot tub, along with a dedicated office space perfect for working from home or study. The finished third floor offers exceptional flexibility as a primary suite or playroom, complete with a cozy sitting room and a beautifully renovated full bath featuring mosaic marble floors and Waterworks fixtures. A full, unfinished basement provides generous storage and opportunity for future expansion. Outside, the beautifully landscaped grounds showcase a welcoming patio ideal for outdoor entertaining, along with a lush, level lawn offering ample space for recreation and relaxation. A thoughtfully constructed stone retaining wall enhances both the property's architectural appeal and functionality. Conveniently located close to everything Darien has to offer, this special home combines charm, comfort, and everyday convenience in a truly inviting setting.
