About this home

Updated Home in Prime Norwalk Location! This beautifully maintained 2-bedroom, 1-bath residence offers an excellent alternative to condo living, featuring thoughtfully designed living space throughout. The main level boasts an eat-in kitchen with gas range and direct access to an expansive deck-ideal for relaxing or entertaining with added privacy. A bright and sun-filled living room with hardwood floors, two generously sized bedrooms, and a full bath complete this level. The upper level includes a spacious 792 sq ft unfinished attic, perfect for storage or potential future expansion. The lower level offers a laundry area, mechanicals, and a one-car garage. Recent upgrades include central air conditioning and a gas furnace, making this home truly move-in ready. Ideally situated near shopping, dining, major highways, and train stations, this property provides convenient access to all that Norwalk has to offer. A fantastic opportunity for first-time buyers or those seeking low-maintenance living in a highly desirable location. Please schedule your private viewing before it's gone!
