About this home

Charming and freshly painted 4-bedroom home ideally located in one of Norwalk's most desirable neighborhoods. Offering a perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience, this property is a must-see. Inside, you'll find fresh paint throughout and brand-new flooring in the kitchen, two of the bedrooms, and the enclosed back porch-creating a bright, modern feel. The spacious living room features a cozy fireplace, perfect for relaxing nights at home or gathering with guests. Step outside to the freshly stained deck, ideal for morning coffee, summer BBQs, or quiet evenings overlooking the yard. The home also includes a detached garage, providing convenient parking and extra storage space. Oil hot air and central air installed. Located just minutes from top Norwalk amenities, you'll enjoy easy access to Calf Pasture Beach, Norwalk Green, SoNo's dining and entertainment, and the East Norwalk Metro-North station-perfect for commuters. Nearby parks, schools, shopping, and I-95 add to the everyday convenience.
