About this home

Prime location! Privacy! You can truly have it all with this light-filled Cape-style house in coveted South Wilton. The tranquil setting is one mile from the popular, lively town center and top schools. Easy access to NYC, just over an hour away via the Merritt, I-95, or the Metro RR at South Norwalk. Perfectly situated near the arts district and popular restaurants in Norwalk, Westport, and New Canaan, and 20 minutes to the beaches. This home has a classic exterior with original stone features with brand new Hardie Board siding. The interior boasts a spacious, open living plan with elegant, inspired upgrades and additions designed by a top-notch architect. This effortless flow creates a welcoming atmosphere throughout. The new "Zen" room with a peaked ceiling features walls of glass blurring the line between indoors and out, creating a retreat-like setting year-round. It leads onto an oversized deck perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining, surrounded by a bucolic landscape. The bright kitchen with sleek island, updated appliances, and smart upgrades elevate everyday living. Bathrooms have been beautifully refreshed, including a newly added charming ensuite. Major improvements include a 5-zone mini-split HVAC, upgraded electrical and lighting, new water heater, new filtration system, and more! Recent exterior upgrades also include a striking flagstone path and vibrant landscape. This much-loved home is ready to welcome you.
