About this home

Welcome to 1 Linden Street #A6, a bright and spacious 2-bedroom, 1-bath condo in the West Rocks neighborhood of Norwalk. This quiet top-floor end unit at Linden Terrace spans 1,144 square feet and has been thoughtfully upgraded with crown molding, solid 6-panel doors, wood floors, recessed lighting, granite countertops, and wainscoting in the hallway. The freshly painted kitchen features granite counters, a new stove, dishwasher, refrigerator, microwave, and garbage disposal. The bathroom includes a bathtub liner installed in 2022. Large closets with shelving provide generous storage throughout, and the in-unit washer and dryer add convenience. Sliders open to a private deck, and a separate storage room is located in Building B. The $354 monthly HOA fee covers grounds maintenance, trash pickup, snow removal, water, sewer, property management, pest control, pool service, and flood insurance. Enjoy access to the community pool and picnic area, plus your own parking spot with visitor spaces available. Built in 1972, this home offers comfortable condo living with low-maintenance appeal in a well-managed 41-unit complex. Electric baseboard heating keeps things simple, and the top-floor location ensures peace and natural light. Conveniently located near I-95, Merritt Parkway, train, and shopping--everything you need is around the corner. Come take a look at one of Norwalk's most well-priced 2-bedroom on the market!
