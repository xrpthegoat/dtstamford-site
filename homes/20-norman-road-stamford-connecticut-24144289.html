About this home

This rare opportunity in a coveted location provides the perfect balance of quiet living and conveniences. Minutes to Train Station, Houses of Worship, Restaurants, Shopping and Playground. Strawberry Hill Area. From the moment you enter this home you are embraced by the blend of beauty and comfort-from the impressive foyer with soaring ceiling to the bedrooms filled with stunning contemporary design. The main level includes a step-down formal living room with woodburning fireplace, formal dining room, great eat-in kitchen with a center island-cooktop and counter, abundance of cabinetry, built-in eating nook and pantry. Steps away is a wonderful and spacious heated sunroom surrounded by four sliding doors and skylights, a perfect place to enjoy morning coffee, snacking or to relax and unwind. A large laundry room and a full bath is conveniently located on this level. On the upper level you'll find 2 spacious bedrooms with walk-in closets designed with contemporary finishes. A shared large bath features double sinks, separate shower and a jet tub. Additional two heated rooms above the garage used as a nursery and a walk-in closet. Beautiful finished lower level and a large storage utility room. This bright home is filled with natural light coming through the many windows, bay windows, sliding doors and skylights. Many upgrades from new heating and cooling systems to a special water filtration system throughout the home with separate shut off valves.
