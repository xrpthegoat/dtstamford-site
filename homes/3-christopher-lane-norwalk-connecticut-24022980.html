About this home

Welcome to 3 Christopher Lane, a gorgeous colonial home set on a professionally landscaped half-acre lot in sought-after Christopher Estates. This exquisite property features mature perennial gardens and trees, stone walls and a wonderful floor-plan designed for today's modern lifestyle. Step into the grandeur of a two-story foyer that sets the tone for this stunning home. The spacious eat-in kitchen featuring a granite island, double sink, and stainless steel appliances, flows effortlessly into a fabulous four-season sunroom and patio, perfect for year-round entertaining. The formal living room with fireplace offers an elegant setting for gatherings. The main level also includes a formal dining room and a versatile library or home office, with protective sun film on the windows to safeguard your art and books. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a luxurious retreat with a large walk-in closet and a spa-like, five fixture bath. Two additional bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bath, while a third ensuite guest room provides extra comfort and privacy. Convenient location near town and train, Cranbury park and downtown Westport. Recently painted, new driveway, Tesla charging station, amazing storage space and countless upgrades, too many to list! You won't want to miss this.
