About this home

Welcome to Crowne Pond Lane A beautiful South Wilton community of just 26 homes, ideally located in the heart of Wilton Center. This beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath colonial, built in 2001, offers nearly 3,000 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space. With 9-foot ceilings, hardwood floors on the main level, and luxury vinyl tile on the second floor, this home blends timeless elegance with everyday functionality. Location, Location, Lifestyle Top-Rated Wilton Schools - All three schools (Elementary, Middle, and High) are located within a mile, offering unmatched convenience for busy families. Minutes to Everything - Walk to the Wilton YMCA, Library, Town Green, restaurants, cafes, and boutique shops. Enjoy the perks of downtown living with the peace of a private, tucked-away community.
