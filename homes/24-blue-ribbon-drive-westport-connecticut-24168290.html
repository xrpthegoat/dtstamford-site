About this home

**PLEASE SUBMIT ALL OFFERS BY TUESDAY MAY 5TH AT NOON** Classic charm meets modern comfort in this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 3 full and 1 half bath home, set in one of Westport's most loved walking neighborhoods-just minutes to top schools, vibrant downtown, and an easy hour to NYC. A bright, spacious eat-in kitchen is the heart of the home, opening to a back deck for effortless indoor-outdoor living and flowing into a sun-filled family room with vaulted ceilings and custom built-ins-perfect for gathering or unwinding. Upstairs, the primary suite is a true retreat, featuring a luxe bath with soaking tub, heated floors, heated towel bars, and generous walk-in closets. All baths are beautifully updated, and the home offers incredible flexibility with a first-floor office, elegant living room, and a bonus sunroom ideal as a playroom, lounge, or creative space. Step outside and you'll find your own private resort-a gorgeous heated pool with a chic cabana, made for summer entertaining, long weekends, and effortless staycations. A finished lower level adds even more space to spread out, making this home as functional as it is inviting.
