About this home

Welcome to 54 Linden Street - A Home That Truly Wows Get ready to fall in love! This isn't just a house, it's a complete transformation. Originally built in 1953, the property was fully rebuilt from the ground up in 2007 and refreshed again in August 2025 with brand-new interior and exterior paint. Move-in ready, it combines modern style, comfort, and plenty of surprises. Step inside to a stunning staircase and custom chandelier that set the tone. The main floor offers flexibility: a formal living room (currently used as a playroom), a stylish dining area, and an open kitchen that flows into a cozy living room with a fireplace perfect for both entertaining and quiet nights in. A first floor bedroom and full bath make an ideal guest suite or home office. Upstairs, you'll find three spacious bedrooms, each with its own full bath. The primary suite is a true retreat with two walk-in closets, a spa-like bathroom featuring a jacuzzi tub, separate shower, dual sinks, and even a fireplace. For convenience, the laundry room is located right on the second level. A dedicated Movie Room provides the ultimate spot for epic movie nights or easily converts into a sixth bedroom or lounge space. On the third floor, a massive open space runs the full length of the home, perfect for a gym, game room, in-law suite, or anything your lifestyle calls for.
