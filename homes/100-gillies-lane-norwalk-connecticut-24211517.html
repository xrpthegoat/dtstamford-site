About this home

Welcome to this impeccably maintained 1,800 sq ft condo in desirable West Norwalk - the ultimate spacious house alternative with a seamless, thoughtful floor plan, and the largest 3 bedroom condo on the market. Expansive living room featuring wood burning fireplace and outdoor deck with retractable sun shades, perfect for relaxing or entertaining, flows easily into a dedicated dining room, all with vauletd ceilings. The fully updated chef's kitchen boasts sleek stainless steel appliances, including premium Wolf electric cooktop, double ovens and ample cabnets. Retreat to the primary suite featuring a newly updated bathroom and an abundance of closet space. Two additional generous bedrooms and refreshed hall bath offer plenty of room for family, guests, or a home office and more oversized closets and storage. A new in-unit washer and dryer, plus a 1-car parking space in the garage under the unit with added storage complete the package. Set within the beautifully maintained Ledgebrook community, enjoy top-tier amenities including community pool, exercise room, guest parking, and peaceful walking trails surrounded by mature trees and a pretty pond. Ideally located near Norwalk's transportation, shopping, SONO restaurants, and the shores of Long Island Sound. 60 minutes to NYC. Move-in ready. Welcome home!
