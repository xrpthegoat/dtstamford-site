About this home

Springdale home owned and cared for by the same family since 1941 offers easy living and convenience to new owners who are ready to simply refresh or tap the tremendous potential to make it their own. Originally configured as a 3-bedroom home, a wall can be added back to the large primary bedroom to restore the space. Finishing the enclosed front and back porches as well as the walk-out basement can potentially add ~850 sqft of living space. The basement has potential as a family room or even in-law suite and the large front porch could hold an office, sunroom or mudroom. City water & sewer and gas service are in place. Walk to Metro North Springdale station is 6 mins and ride into Manhattan 69 mins. Rare 2 car garage. Original hardwood floors on 1st and 2nd levels. Updated electrical wiring throughout plus new 200 amp electric service. Eat-in-kitchen is outfitted with gas range, dishwasher, refrigerator, ample storage and adjacent dining room. Well-sized main floor powder room with storage and updated upstairs bathroom with tub, classic subway tile and shelving. Basement holds spacious laundry, new 50-gal gas-powered water tank, and ducted heating system. Beautiful views of landscaped yard and mature trees. Tranquil outside space. Close to all that Stamford has to offer. Sold As Is.
