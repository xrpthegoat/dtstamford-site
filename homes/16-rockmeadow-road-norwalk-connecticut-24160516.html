About this home

Back on market, Buyer couldn't get financing. Tucked away in a quiet residential area of desirable West Norwalk, you'll find the Rock Meadow complex. This bright & roomy top-floor unit offers a Kitchen w/stainless steel appliances, large Living/Dining Room with balcony, spacious Bedroom with closet system and slider to a second balcony. In-unit washer/dryer, central air & central vacuum, new water heater 2/2026. Plenty of storage space, including huge private attic (accessed via pull down stairs) and oversized storage closet on the lower level next to garage. Both balconies overlook beautiful mature landscaped grounds. One assigned parking spot (Q) and ample visitor parking to right of building. Lovely picnic/recreation area & pet friendly complex (2 pets permitted). Conveniently located to restaurants, stores, highways, SoNo Collection Mall and train station.
