About this home

Welcome to this fully renovated 4 bedroom, 2 bath Colonial ideally located in the heart of Springdale. A charming front porch with a sleek standing seam metal roof creates a striking first impression and leads into a bright, oversized living room filled with natural light. An oversized sliding glass door opens to a brand new Trex style deck overlooking the backyard, creating a seamless flow for indoor and outdoor living. The stunning modern kitchen features shaker cabinetry w/ soft close drawers, quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances - a clean, timeless design. Adjacent to the kitchen is a dedicated dining area, along w/ a versatile 1st floor bedroom and/or home office w/ a beautifully renovated full bath featuring a stylish walk in shower. Upstairs, a luxurious primary suite offers vaulted ceilings & a spacious walk in closet. Two additional bedrooms and a brand new hall bath complete w/ a dual vanity, soaking tub, & sophisticated finishes. The lower level provides excellent flex space perfect for a den or playroom, with direct walk out access to the backyard. A well appointed laundry area with folding station, abundant basement storage, and additional attic storage enhance the home's functionality. Natural gas, city water, and city sewer, perfectly positioned on a quiet street just minutes from the Springdale train station, local shops, restaurants, downtown Stamford, and convenient access to both I95 and the Merritt Parkway.
