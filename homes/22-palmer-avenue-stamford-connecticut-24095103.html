About this home

Welcome to 22 Palmer Avenue! Ideally situated in Stamford's desirable Cove neighborhood, this duplex townhouse-style home offers the perfect combination of comfort, space, and investment potential. This side-by-side multifamily property features two well-appointed units-one with 2 bedrooms and the other with 3 bedrooms-each offering 1.5 bathrooms. Both units include updated full bathrooms, hardwood floors, large closets, and basements with spacious, functional layouts. Additional highlights include new fencing, in-unit laundry, solar panels, and plenty of off-street parking for residents and guests. Enjoy the convenience of being just minutes from Cove Beach, Downtown Stamford, Chelsea Piers, the Metro-North train station, and a variety of shops, restaurants, parks, and major highways. Whether you're looking to live in one unit and rent the other or expand your investment portfolio, this is a rare opportunity in a prime location. Both units will be delivered unoccupied at closing. The new owner will assume the existing solar panels and is responsible for completing the transfer process with the solar company.
