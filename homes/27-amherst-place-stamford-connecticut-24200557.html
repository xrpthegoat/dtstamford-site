About this home

Nestled on a coveted cul-de-sac in Stamford's highly sought-after Mid-Ridges neighborhood, this beautifully updated raised ranch offers a rare opportunity to enjoy the serenity of waterfront living without sacrificing convenience. Ideally located between the Merritt Parkway, I-95, and Metro-North, you're just minutes from Stamford's finest restaurants, shopping, and entertainment. At the heart of the home is a stunning kitchen with Quartz countertops and stainless steel appliances that flows effortlessly into the dining room and spacious living room, creating the perfect space for entertaining or everyday living. Step outside to the expansive deck and let the peaceful Rippowam River steal the show. Sip your morning coffee, host unforgettable gatherings, or unwind in Adirondack chairs along the riverbank as the water gently flows by and the seasons transform the landscape. The main level offers three spacious bedrooms and an updated full bath. The walkout lower level features a generous family room, fourth bedroom, full bath, and sliders to the backyard-ideal for guests, a home office, or multi-generational living and equipped with a convenient kitchenette. A large storage shed provides abundant space for tools and outdoor equipment. Meticulously maintained and truly move-in ready, this is more than a home-it's a private retreat in one of Stamford's most desirable neighborhoods. click on movie icon for virtual tour.
