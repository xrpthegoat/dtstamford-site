About this home

This updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath duplex offers exceptional value in a highly convenient Greenwich location, just minutes from the Port Chester train station. The home features an updated kitchen with stainless steel appliances that opens seamlessly to a spacious layout. There is a first floor primary bedroom. Upstairs you'll find two bedrooms, a full bath and in-unit washer/dryer provide everyday comfort and practicality. Enjoy outdoor living on the expansive deck, ideal for dining and entertaining. Additional highlights include parking for two cars in a shared driveway and an option for a garage space as well. A wonderful opportunity to live close to shops, restaurants, and transportation while enjoying the comfort of a thoughtfully updated home.
