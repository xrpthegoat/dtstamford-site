About this home

Welcome to this beautifully maintained and exceptionally spacious home located on a quiet cul-de-sac in one of Norwalk's desirable neighborhoods. Offering exceptional space, comfort, and versatility, this home features two spacious living rooms, a beautifully renovated full-size kitchen, a cozy fireplace, central air conditioning and heating, and fresh interior and exterior paint throughout. A unique highlight of the property is the beautifully finished detached guest house, offering flexible space ideal for a home office, fitness studio, creative workspace, guest accommodations, or recreation room. Enjoy a fully fenced private backyard, perfect for entertaining, relaxing, gardening, or outdoor activities. The home also includes an attached two-car garage and an oversized driveway with parking for up to nine vehicles-an exceptional feature rarely found in rental homes. Conveniently located near shopping, restaurants, parks, schools, Metro-North, I-95, Route 7, and the Merritt Parkway, this home offers both privacy and easy access to everything Fairfield County has to offer.
