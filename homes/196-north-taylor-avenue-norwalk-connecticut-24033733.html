About this home

Available for quick close! Wonderfully spacious 5 bedroom home, with added bonus of main level bedroom and full bath - perfect for guests or family. This house checks all the boxes for style, comfort and function. As you step through the airy two story entry way you will find yourself in the open concept living and dining room, creating delightful entertaining space. From here, head into the super, light-filled kitchen, equipped with a large island, granite countertops, stainless steel appliances and a charming eat-in dining area - with bay window overlooking all the greenery of the private back yard. The kitchen also flows seamlessly into the generously-sized family room with wood-burning fireplace and access out to the patio. In addition to the bedroom and full bath on the main level you have the benefit of an office for work from home options. Primary bedroom with en suite bath and 3 further bedrooms with family bathroom are located upstairs, along with convenient laundry room.The finished lower level offers a versatile space for a playroom, gym, or entertaining area with a custom-built in bar, beer tap, and fridge! Located close to Oak Hills Park, you will enjoy easy access to a beautiful 18-hole golf course, restaurant, tennis center, and nature trails - perfect for outdoor activities and dog walking. Natural Gas/Public Water/Sewer. Convenient to shopping/schools and a quick drive to South Norwalk train station!
