About this home

Fully renovated mid-city Colonial offering nearly 2,500 square feet of well-designed, move-in-ready living space. This 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath home features a new kitchen and baths, new windows, and new HVAC. A foyer opens to a living room with fireplace, dining room, and flexible office/den. The bright kitchen includes quartz countertops, new cabinetry and appliances, and a breakfast nook. The second floor offers a spacious primary suite with walk-in closet and bath, plus two additional bedrooms. The upper level provides additional space with a full bath, ideal for a bedroom, guest suite, or playroom. A dry, full basement with epoxy flooring adds clean storage and utility space. Outside, enjoy a level yard, expansive deck, detached garage, and ample off-street parking. Walk to Metro-North, downtown Stamford, dining, shopping and Stamford Hospital.
