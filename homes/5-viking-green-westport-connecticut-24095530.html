About this home

Welcome to your riverside staycation! Poised on a private cul-de-sac in the Red Coat area of Westport, this beautiful home offers a tranquil, private setting minutes from town. Exquisite living spaces include a great room with a cathedral ceiling and stone fireplace and a dining room with river views. The kitchen forms the heart of the home with a spacious island, professional appliances and a wet bar made for your summertime soirees. Relax in the lovely den or bask in the sunshine on the large deck overlooking the river. Completing the first level is a home office, half bath, and a primary bedroom suite with a spa-like bath. The upper level offers three additional bedrooms, two full baths & laundry area. The lower level is home to a gym with ping pong table, family room with a fireplace, a second home office with river views, playroom, music room and full bath. (Available 6/28/25-8/10/25 or 6/28/25-8/17/25)
