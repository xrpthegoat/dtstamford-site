About this home

Heat, Hot Water & Electricity included. Lovely, recently updated apartment with plenty of natural light! This spacious 2nd floor 1 BR apt w/Mill Pond water views from your private balcony in East Norwalk offers open floor plan Eat-in-Kitchen w/granite countertops, plenty of cabinets and stainless appliances, a double sided breakfast bar which opens to the Living Room. Down the hallway you'll find a very large double closet, a full Bath w/Tub and a large Bedroom. Recessed lighting & beautiful hardwood flooring throughout. Stackable Washer & Dryer in apartment. Central Air, Electricity, clean & efficient gas heat all paid by Landlord. Enjoy morning coffee or evenings relaxing on your covered deck with Mill Pond water views. Located in a walk to everywhere 06855 neighborhood. Commuters delight...walk to East Norwalk Train Station or minutes to Beaches, SoNo Train station, I-95 & Merritt Pwy., SoNo shops, restaurants, nightclubs & the Maritime Aquarium. Credit, background reports required and paid by Tenant w/application. Must have excellent credit and references. 2 Months Security plus 1st months rent due at lease signing. $100,000 Liability Insurance must be carried by Tenant throughout the term of the lease. Absolutely no Pets or Smoking. Additional storage space in Basement. Reserved parking for 2 cars conveniently located at the base of the staircase along along fence. Apt will be cleaned & painted prior to move-in
