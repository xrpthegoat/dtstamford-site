About this home

Pool & Prime Location!Single Family rental in the heart of East Norwalk, a sought-after water community. The spacious home offers an unbeatable lifestyle with walking distance to Calf Pasture & Shady Beach,Taylor Farms, Ludlow Park, Norwalk Cove Marina, Shorehaven Golf Club as well as The Shore & Country Club. Perfect for those seeking coastal living with modern amenities! Commuters dream; close to both South & East Norwalk Train Stations as well as 1-95 and the Merritt Parkway... Features a fenced-in backyard. Enjoy your mornings in the front enclosed three-season front porch. The eat-in kitchen is a chef's dream with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and sliding glass doors that open to backyard. The main floor includes a large bedroom and renovated bathroom. Upstairs, the primary bedroom boasts a skylight and walk-in closets, plus two additional large bedrooms with their own walk-in closets. The luxuriously renovated bathroom offers a jacuzzi tub, and skylight for spa-like experience. Finished lower level with Recreation Room, wet bar, half bath, and laundry area. Plenty of storage available in the attic. Minutes away from SONO and Downtown Westport
