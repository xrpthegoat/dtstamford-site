About this home

This beautifully renovated two-family home in a highly desirable Stamford location is the one you've been waiting for. Conveniently situated near shopping, dining, entertainment, and transportation, it's just minutes from downtown Stamford and the train station. The first-floor unit features three spacious bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a bright living room, a dining area, and an eat-in kitchen. The lower level offers flexible space ideal for a home office, family room, or playroom. Additional highlights include a sunroom, a new porch, and an attached garage. The second-floor unit includes three to four bedrooms, one full bathroom, an eat-in kitchen, and a comfortable living room. Completing the property is a beautiful private backyard-perfect for entertaining and enjoying summer barbecues.
