About this home

Welcome to this light-filled, contemporary 2 bedroom, 2.5 bath townhouse, ideally situated in one of South Wilton's most convenient locations! Soaring ceilings, skylights, and an abundance of natural light create a bright, airy atmosphere throughout. The spacious kitchen features new stainless steel appliances and opens to a private, enclosed deck, also accessible from the primary suite. The inviting living room offers multiple exposures, a cozy fireplace, and a private balcony all perfect for relaxing or entertaining. The generously sized first-floor primary suite boasts cathedral ceilings and a beautifully updated, spa-inspired bathroom featuring a luxurious soaking tub and separate shower. Upstairs, a second bedroom and full bath provide a comfortable and private retreat for guests or family. The expansive lower level ( the largest in the complex!) includes a finished office/playroom, ample room for a home gym, exceptional storage, and direct access to the attached one-car garage. Additional highlights include central air, second floating parking space, and plenty of guest parking. Enjoy resort-style amenities, including a pool and tennis courts, all just moments from South Wilton's shops, restaurants, and Metro-North. An exceptional combination of space, natural light, comfort, and convenience. A wonderful opportunity not to be missed!
