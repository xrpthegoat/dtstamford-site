About this home

Your Private Oasis Awaits: Space & Versatility at 32 Styles Lane! Discover the perfect blend of classic charm and modern scale in this expansive 3/4-bedroom, 3-bathroom residence. Tucked away in a quiet Broad River enclave, this home is designed for those who refuse to compromise on space or style. The Heart of the Home The main level invites you in with hardwood floors and a seamless flow. Gather by the wood-burning fireplace in the living room or host dinners in the sun-drenched dining area. The showstopper is the massive family room addition, boasting soaring vaulted ceilings, a cozy wood-burning stove, and a wall of windows that overlooks your private retreat. Step out onto the oversized deck and flat, level backyard-a rare find perfect to add a pool, play, or grand-scale entertaining. Flexibility for Modern Living Whether you need a quiet home office, a gym, or an in-law setup, the layout delivers. The lower levels feature a guest suite/den, laundry, and a full bath, plus an additional finished level for recreation or storage. The rare 3-car garage offers incredible ADU or professional studio potential with its own private entry. The Primary Retreat The upper level features three bright bedrooms and a full bath, leading to the stunning primary suite addition.
