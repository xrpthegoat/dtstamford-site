About this home

Prepare to be wowed by this renovated home that is walking distance to the beach. The floorplan has a wonderful flow for easy living and entertaining. Filled with character and light, this home offers the space, layout and features the savvy buyer is looking for today. Kitchen boasts stainless steel appliances, quartz counters, breakfast bar and stylish tile backsplash. Host holiday parties with the open concept living room and dining room, and delight in the meticulously manicured gardens in the private level backyard. Fabulous renovated first floor bath with gorgeous basketweave tile floor and contemporary vanity and shower. 3 bedrooms complete the first floor. Upstairs boasts stunning primary bedroom suite with spa-like full bath, 2 walk-in closets, vaulted ceilings and stunning views overlooking spectacular garden. Additional storage and cedar closet complete this sophisticated space. Looking for a workshop space? Basement provides the perfect area. Underground sprinkler, 1 car garage, new roof 2023 and so much more! Soundview Manor private beach association with beach rights-enjoy the Long Island Sound just footsteps away. Only minutes away to Cove Island Park, Cummings Beach, tennis courts, Chelsea Piers, playground, Metro North and I-95, this pristine home is right here and ready for you.
