About this home

Quietly tucked away on ~2.5 private acres in the highly coveted Cranbury neighborhood, this stunning colonial estate offers an unparalleled lifestyle. Located just minutes from the heart of Norwalk, Westport, New Canaan and Wilton this one-of-a-kind home is a rare gem. Completely transformed inside and out by a visionary designer and architect duo, this home has been featured in countless magazines - and it shows. Every inch has been reimagined with intention, craftsmanship, and elevated style. This meticulously updated residence boasts 4 spacious bedrooms, with 3.5 stunning bathrooms, providing luxury and privacy. Every detail has been considered - from the beautifully appointed baths, to the luxurious chefs kitchen (with the most incredible Imperial Danby Marble), to the whole new house systems, all the way to the brand new roofs on home & structures + the finest lighting fixtures... just to name a few! Step outside to your own private oasis. Manicured grounds, mature plantings, and lush flower beds create a storybook setting. The fully updated sparkling pool, brand new bluestone patio and classic cedar split-rail fencing complete this idyllic retreat. Multiple outbuildings provide exceptional flexibility - ideal home office, artist studio, gym, hangout space, or potential pool house. The possibilities are endless. This home is the perfect combination of luxury, comfort, & convenience, and it's ready for you to move in & enjoy!
