About this home

Welcome to 20 Walnut Ridge Lane where you can enjoy privacy within a quiet enclave of newer homes, yet just minutes to Stamford's vibrant downtown. Built in 2005 and recently renovated, this 3894 sq. ft residence offers 4 bedrooms and 4.5 baths on a 1/4 acre lot. A dramatic double-height entry sets the tone for the open-concept layout featuring an enormous kitchen with professional appliances that opens to the family room with French doors to the patio for effortless entertaining. Newly refinished hardwood floors, fresh paint, and walls of windows create a bright, airy atmosphere throughout. Upstairs, the primary suite includes dual walk-in closets and a full en-suite bath, while two bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bath and another has its own en-suite. The finished lower level spans over 1,000 sq ft and offers endless possibilities with a full bath, French doors, and walkout access to the backyard. It can serve as a guest suite, office, or in-law setup, along with open space ideal for a media room or gym. The garage features a brand-new epoxy floor and EV charger, and the home includes fully paid solar panels, offering significant savings year after year. Set in a quiet residential area with nearby parks, schools, and shopping, this home combines modern updates, energy efficiency, and a convenient location close to Metro-North and I-95, truly checking every box.
