About this home

Location meets Possibility in this immaculately presented Springdale ranch with full, heated lower level with separate entrance + attached 2 car garage! Lovingly maintained by the original owner, this home is move-in ready offering fresh paint, a newer roof, newer windows/sliding door + newer furnace/hot water system. Freshly painted inside + out, you'll enjoy a solidly constructed, clean home perfect to enjoy right now, and create additional space at your leisure. The main floor features a sunny living room with large bay window, fireplace + built-in shelving, the eat-in-kitchen leads to the entertainer's deck where you'll enjoy postcard quality vista views and breathtaking sunrises. Add more living space in the large, heated, above-grade lower level featuring windows, large slider, garage access + separate entry. The private backyard features firepit area, garden plots, storage shed, additional parking spaces and a 2-car attached garage with workspace. Idyllic, sought-after Springdale location is walking distance to popular Toquam Magnet Elementary School, Springdale Train Station, neighborhood restaurants + shops.
