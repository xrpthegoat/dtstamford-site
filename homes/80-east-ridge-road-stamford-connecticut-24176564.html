About this home

80 East Ridge Road - Deerfield Estate, Stamford, CT. Nestled in a prestigious North Stamford enclave, this residence sits on 1.16 landscaped acres, offering an extraordinary blend of architectural grandeur, absolute privacy, and effortless access to NYC. From the moment you arrive, the transformation is striking. 20 newly planted trees frame a re-landscaped garden - a lush sanctuary crossed by a serene private stream visible from inside the home. Step outside to 2 generous decks, a jacuzzi, a flower and vegetable garden, and an outdoor fireplace for gatherings. A four-season solarium completes this outdoor paradise. Inside, soaring ceilings, skylights, and oversized new windows flood every room with extraordinary natural light. The spectacular see-through fireplace anchors both the living room and family room, creating a warm, convivial heart to the home. The chef's kitchen features premium granite countertops, top appliances, and floor-to-ceiling sliders opening directly onto the deck. The primary suite is a true spa retreat: radiant heated flooring, an oversized walk-in shower, and double sinks. Three additional bedrooms and a fully finished basement - Murphy bed included, ideal as guest room or home office - complete the picture. 2-car garage with an EV outlet for your electric car, ample parking, full furniture package available. 5 minutes to Whole Foods and restaurants. 50 minutes to Manhattan by train or Merritt Prkwy. Turnkey. Luminous. Exceptional.
