About this home

Set on one of Darien's most coveted cul-de-sacs, this classic 5-bedroom colonial offers privacy in a wonderful neighborhood. The property sits on a flat, park-like 1.2 acres with mature plantings and well-established gardens, creating a peaceful, serene setting. Inside, a flexible floor plan features generously sized rooms, hardwood floors, and large windows that bring in plenty of natural light - the home is bright and open. There is plenty of space for entertaining and family gatherings. A large terrace off the back overlooks the expansive lawn and property, a lovely spot for outdoor living. Move right in and enjoy it as is, or take the opportunity to renovate and make it your own. A rare offering on one of Darien's most desirable streets.
