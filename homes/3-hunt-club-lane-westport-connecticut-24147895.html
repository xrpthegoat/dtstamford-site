About this home

Tucked away on a quiet cul-de-sac on over an acre in the highly desirable Hunt Club neighborhood of Westport, this exceptional ranch-style home offers a rare opportunity to own a custom-built, mid-century residence never before offered on the MLS. Adjacent to the Fairfield County Hunt Club and lovingly maintained by its original family owner, the home blends timeless design with pride of ownership throughout. The classic brick exterior opens to a thoughtfully designed interior featuring a primary bedroom with full bath, two additional bedrooms, and a second full bath. Sun-filled living and dining rooms flow together, highlighted by a striking Vermont granite fireplace and oak floors throughout. A warm, paneled den with a brick fireplace opens to a spacious family room with skylights, creating a perfect retreat. The original custom kitchen retains its character while offering modern convenience, with stainless steel appliances, double ovens, a Sub-Zero refrigerator and dining area. The full basement includes laundry area and presents potential to be finished. Recent improvements include fresh interior paint, many updated electrical and lighting fixtures, roof, boiler, and a full basement de-watering system, providing peace of mind for the next owner. A truly special home in a coveted location, offering classic mid-century style, solid construction, and the chance to create something extraordinary in one of Westport's most sought-after neighborhoods.
