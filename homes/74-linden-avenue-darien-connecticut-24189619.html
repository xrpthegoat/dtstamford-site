About this home

Beautifully updated apartment offering comfort, convenience, and exceptional value in a prime Darien location. The entire unit has been freshly painted and features gleaming hardwood floors, a bright, newly remodeled bathroom, and an updated eat-in kitchen complete with a dishwasher, microwave, new electric range, and refrigerator with freezer. Abundant windows fill the home with natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Additional amenities include shared laundry facilities and private storage within the building. The unit also comes with two off-street parking spaces. Ideally located just minutes from the train station for an easy commute and within the Darien Public Schools district, which has received multiple educational recognitions. Residents also enjoy access to the town beach. The landlord provides heat, hot water, trash removal, snow removal, and all exterior grounds maintenance. The tenant is responsible for electricity and internet service. A charming, beautifully updated apartment in an outstanding location offering convenience, quality, and easy living.
