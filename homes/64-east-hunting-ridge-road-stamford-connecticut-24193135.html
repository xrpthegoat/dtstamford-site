About this home

Welcome to this sun-filled ranch offering breathtaking, year-round views from nearly every window in the house. Set on 1.5 beautifully maintained acres - a mix of open, cleared grounds and natural woods - this 5-bedroom, 3-bath home is a rare find for anyone seeking space, light, and privacy in one package. Inside, 3,800 square feet of living space unfolds across thoughtfully designed rooms with high ceilings throughout. The kitchen is a true centerpiece - a cook and entertainer's dream, outfitted with high-end appliances, stunning quartzite countertops featuring a porcelain farmhouse sink and large center island. A convenient laundry room sits just off the kitchen, and closet space throughout the home is exceptional. The family room is a showstopper: vaulted ceilings, floor-to-ceiling windows on two full walls, and a wood-burning stove create a warm, light-filled gathering space for every season. The primary suite offers a private ensuite bath, while two additional full baths serve the rest of the home with ease. Downstairs, the finished walkout basement adds remarkable flexibility to entertain family and guests of all ages, a home office setup, or a true in-law suite. Step outside to an 800-square-foot bluestone patio, framed by elegant mahogany railings, the perfect spot to take in the property's year-round scenery.
