About this home

Welcome to 42 Givens Ave. Located in one of Stamford's most convenient and desirable areas, this residence combines upscale finishes, spacious living, and unbeatable proximity to Cove Island Beach, Chelsea Piers, schools, shopping, I95 and Darien. The main level welcomes you with gleaming hardwood floors, tons of natural light and an open-concept living and dining area with a deck just off the living space, making it perfect for entertaining. The updated gourmet eat-in kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances - ideal for everyday living or hosting. The main floor includes two bedrooms with spacious closets and full bath with washer/dryer. Upstairs, the primary suite is a luxurious retreat with en-suite bathroom and vaulted ceilings. Enjoy the ease of two reserved off-street parking spaces. Efficient natural gas heating and central air cooling. Tenant responsible for all utilities (natural gas and electric), cable/wifi, credit check, tenant insurance and two months security (one month if over 62). Landlord covers water, landscaping, snowplowing, sewer and trash/recycling. Pets are welcome! Available 11/15/25, Minimum one year lease. Come experience this beautiful rental and feel right at home.
