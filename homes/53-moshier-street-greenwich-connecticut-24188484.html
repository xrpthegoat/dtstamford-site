About this home

Spacious one-bedroom apartment on the third floor of a brick 3 family house. Totally renovated kitchen with golden harvest granite counters, stainless sink, dishwasher and washer/dryer, plus all-marble luxury bath with jacuzzi tub, shower and skylight. This apartment is in a quiet residential area one-half block from Pemberwick Park and nature trails. Easy commute to NYC via Metro North and to I-95 and the Merritt Parkway. Heat and hot water, grounds maintenance, trash and snow removal are all included in the rent; electric and cooking gas utilities are not. Dogs not allowed. Approx. 615 sq. ft. Off-street parking for up to two cars.
