About this home

Lovely and Level - Welcome to 105 Cherry Lane- Fall in love the moment you arrive at this beautifully maintained ranch-style home in the heart of South Wilton. Perfectly positioned close to town, schools, and transportation, this move-in-ready gem offers both comfort and convenience. Extensively remodeled in 2013, the home features a new roof, windows, and siding, along with stylish interior updates to the kitchen, primary bath, and flooring. Striking front windows flood the home with natural light, creating an airy, open feel in the gorgeous living room, which flows seamlessly into the dining area and updated eat-in kitchen.Flexible living spaces provide room for everyone, including an additional family area and a dedicated office nook. The main-floor primary suite is bright and expansive, complete with an updated bathroom and walk-in closet. A second ensuite bedroom with its own walk-in closet, plus two additional generously sized bedrooms and another full bath, ensure space for all. Set on a level lot, the property is a private retreat with a walk-out deck, lush perennial gardens, a full fence to keep deer away, a new garden shed, and an oversized two-car garage with extra storage. Recent upgrades include a new front walkway, LVP flooring in the kitchen, living, and dining rooms, a new driveway, irrigation system, and a full-house generator for peace of mind.105 Cherry Lane truly is the home you've been waiting for-stylish, spacious, and ready to welcome you.
