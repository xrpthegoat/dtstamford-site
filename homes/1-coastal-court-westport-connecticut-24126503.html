About this home

Introducing 1 Coastal Court, a rare opportunity to own in Westport's only gated community, an exclusive four-lot enclave envisioned and built by Joe Feinleib, founder of Coastal Luxury Homes and one of Fairfield County's most respected builders.This private neighborhood blends timeless architecture with modern luxury. Each custom residence, ranging from 5,000 to 7,500 square feet, plus generous garages, balconies, patios and covered porches, will be designed in collaboration with the homeowner - ensuring every detail reflects your vision. Expansive layouts, pool sites, and inviting outdoor living spaces create the perfect setting for today's lifestyle, balancing sophistication with everyday comforts. Working with Coastal Luxury Homes is a dynamic, collaborative and easeful process with every detail curated to suit the homeowners' vision of the perfect home. Whether you dream of having your own golf simulator, outdoor chef's kitchen, sauna or elevator...everything is possible. Ideally located just moments from Westport's award-winning schools, vibrant downtown, the train, and Compo Beach, this unique neighborhood offers a rare combination of privacy and convenience. Mature landscaping and thoughtful design create a serene retreat, while the best of dining, shopping, and recreation are just minutes away. This is more than a new home - it's the chance to create your dream residence with an award-winning builder in a community unlike any other in Westport.
