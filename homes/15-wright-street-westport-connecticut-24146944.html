About this home

Another opportunity on one of Westport's most desirable streets! This Antique gem has been lovingly cared for by the same family for 31 years. It underwent massive renovations in 2017. Some walls were removed to create a beautiful open gourmet kitchen which opens to a charming Family Room with fireplace. There is also a front living room with pretty builtins. All three full baths have been updated. The hall bath on the second floor features radiant heat in the floor. This gem also offers Central Air throughout the home. Outside there is a large stone patio-perfect for entertaining al fresco surrounded by beautiful gardens and specimen trees. And if all of this is not enough there is a one car garage and a driveway that accommodates two more cars. Walk to everything that downtown Westport offers! This is a wonderful opportunity to enjoy the charm of yesteryear melded with today's lifestyle and conveniences! NOTE: BEST AND FINAL TODAY 1/12 AT 5 PM
