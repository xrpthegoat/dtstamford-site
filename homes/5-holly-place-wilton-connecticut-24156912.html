About this home

Summer furnished rental! Come out to the country and enjoy this beautifully updated, spotless and spacious colonial style home, fully furnished for all your needs. Plenty of room to work remotely (upstairs or on the first floor) and a large level backyard to enjoy the outdoors. Enclosed porch and large patio overlooking gorgeous and private back yard. Convenient to Wilton center, take a train to NYC, enjoy Merwin meadows lake and playground with the little ones, 15 minutes to Westport beaches or walk the Norwalk valley river trail. Room for four people to relax and enjoy the summer months. First floor has living room with fireplace, office with beautiful windows to view the outdoors, stunning new eat in chefs kitchen as well as formal dining room. Large family room is bright with cathedral ceiling and two desks for remote learning. Finished lower level recreation/play room has ping pong table and play area. Term is flexible!
