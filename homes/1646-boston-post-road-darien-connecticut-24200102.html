About this home

Offered by its original owners for the first time, this well maintained raised ranch sits on a .23-acre lot with a fenced, private backyard with deck and patio in the sought after Norton section of Darien. Built by it's owners and well cared for ever since, the home offers over 3,000 square feet of living space across three functional levels - 4 bedrooms, 2 full baths, large living and family rooms, eat-in kitchen, separate dining room, ample storage areas and oversized 1-car garage. The versatile floor plan flows easily between entertaining spaces and everyday living. Centrally located near Darien's top-rated schools, Long Island Sound, beach access, town parks, award-winning Darien Library, shopping, RR stations, YMCA and numerous restaurants choices. A great opportunity to make this great space your own place to call home.
