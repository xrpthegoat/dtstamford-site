About this home

Come see this beautifully maintained end-unit 2 bedroom, 1 bathroom townhouse ideally situated just a 5 minute walk from the Glenbrook Metro-North Station, offering direct trains to NYC. The home features a newly renovated bathroom and an updated kitchen complete with quartz countertops, a limestone backsplash, a 30-inch stainless steel sink, and stylish modern lighting. Throughout the home, you'll find hardwood floors, spacious updated closets, and plenty of storage space in both the attic and outdoor shed. The generous outdoor area is perfect for grilling and gatherings, while the dog-friendly park across the street adds to the appeal for pet owners. With its prime location, you'll enjoy easy access to walkable restaurants and cafes, short drive to Cove Island Park Beach, and convenient commuting options via I-95 or the Merritt Parkway. This townhouse is both investor-friendly and move-in ready, offering a perfect blend of comfort, style, and location in the heart of Glenbrook.
