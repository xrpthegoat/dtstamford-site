About this home

Centrally located on a quiet triple cul de sac, this inviting home offers an open floor plan, abundant natural light, and thoughtful updates throughout. It features 3 bedrooms and 3 full bathrooms, with hardwood floors running throughout the home. The living room with fireplace flows into the dining room, where sliders open to a spacious deck with an awning. The updated kitchen features a skylight, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances. The primary suite is a true retreat, complete with an oversized walk in closet outfitted with California Closets cabinetry and shelving, plus a private spa like bath with double sinks, radiant heated floors, a shower stall, jacuzzi tub, and sauna. The lower level family room offers flexible space and could serve as a 4th bedroom, with a full bath and an alcove ideal for a home office. The home underwent a major renovation and addition in 2005, including updated electrical, plumbing, windows, and roof, along with an 80 gallon water heater replaced in 2021, a furnace updated in 2013, and the roof was replaced in 2020.
