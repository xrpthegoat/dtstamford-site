About this home

Wonderful Rowayton home with great flow for entertaining, relaxing or working from home. Kitchen features marble counters, stainless appliances, wine fridge, custom cabinets, wide plank floors. Living room w fireplace and beamed hearth flows to dining room, sun filled family room w gas fireplace and view to patio. Primary suite seamlessly combines 9' ceiling bedroom, huge walk in closet and bath w soaking tub & shower. There are 4 additional bedrooms, 2 full baths and an oversized laundry room on 2nd fl. Beautiful yard and patio. 2 car garage, pull down attic and unfinished basement. Walking distance to Rowayton village, 5 Mile River, library, dog park, nature preserve, paddle courts, tennis, train station and schools. Enjoy beach rights for Bayley Beach this summer. Yearly lease, no smoking, must ask about pets. Tenant pays all utilities, credit check and references requested. Aug 1 start date.
