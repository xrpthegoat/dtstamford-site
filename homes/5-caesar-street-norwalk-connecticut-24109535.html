About this home

This beautifully updated and move-in ready Cape offers modern comfort with thoughtful touches throughout. The home has been meticulously maintained and features stylish finishes, a spacious layout, and impressive upgrades. You'll love the open gourmet kitchen with quality cabinetry and newer appliances, updated sleek bathrooms, and hardwood flooring throughout the main living areas. Spacious bedroom on the main level w/ full bath and two additional generous size bedrooms one w/a walk in closet as well as a jack-and-jill bath upstairs. The finished lower level adds valuable living space - w/ space for a playroom and a possible guest room area. A dedicated mudroom and laundry area enhance everyday convenience. Located on a peaceful cul-de-sac, the fully fenced in backyard has space to play, a maintenance free deck leads to a spacious patio area perfect for entertaining. Newer roof, siding, windows, gas furnace, central air. This home is just minutes from shopping, Stew Leonards, restaurants, schools, Calf Pasture beach, downtown SoNo, train station, and public transportation.
