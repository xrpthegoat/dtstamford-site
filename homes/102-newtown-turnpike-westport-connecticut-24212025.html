About this home

Vintage charm reimagined in this beautifully renovated, light-filled home. Transformed from its original space, it is now an exceptional 5 BR, 5 BA residence offering the perfect balance of character, space and contemporary style. Sited on a sunny half-acre, the home features an open floor plan, wide board floors and immaculately updated interiors throughout. The all-new kitchen showcases custom cabinetry, quartz countertops, high-end appliances while five newly finished baths bring a fresh, sophisticated feel. a spacious family room opens to a large deck overlooking a level, sun-filled yard-idea for entertaining and recreation. Upstairs, the primary suite features a large bath with separate shower, tub and water closet. three additional en suite bedrooms complete this floor. Just minutes from town, shopping and transportation the convenient Country Store.
