About this home

Incredibly located moments from town and convenient to schools, shopping, commuting options and more, this sparkling home showcases pride of ownership. A new 2026 driveway leads to the landscaped setting, where this gracious home with an in-ground Wagner pool is set amid .74+/- acre. The two-story foyer introduces crisp, timeless spaces, and a home that has been impeccably maintained. The living rm w/ the first of 3 fireplaces and a beautiful dining rm w/lit tray ceiling are ready for entertaining. The large gourmet kitchen w/ marble counters, La Cornue range plus wall ovens, Sub Zero, island & pantry has a sun-filled breakfast rm that leads to the lrg fam rm w/ custom built-ins, stone fpl w/ gas log set & two sets of doors to the newer deck. The mudrm has a casual entrance, door to pool, half bath & access to the 3-car garage. Upstairs, a hall w/ window seats leads to the primary suite w/ fpl, dual walk-in clsts & luxe bth. Each remaining BR is spacious & well-appointed. A flexible area w/ the 5th BR & study/play rm could become an in-law suite. The finished 3rd flr is a wonderful space for a play rm or hobbies. The vast lower level includes a great rm, a den/lib w/ custom built-ins & a full bath. The usable backyard leads to a fenced-in Wagner gunite pool w/ a "pool house." This entire home and property is in outstanding, mint condition. There is nothing for you to do but move in and enjoy this fine luxury residence.
