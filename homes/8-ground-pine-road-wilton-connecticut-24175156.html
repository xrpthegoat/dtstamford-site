About this home

Turn-key, 3-bedroom, 2-full bath split-level home set on a level 2+ acre property in a sought-after neighborhood, perfect for walking and bike riding. Enjoy direct access to Sackett State Park, offering miles of scenic trails for hiking and walking your dogs. The updated kitchen features quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar that opens seamlessly to the dining area and spacious living room, creating an ideal layout for everyday living and entertaining. Two bedrooms share a full bath, while the primary suite offers a walk-in closet, raised ceiling with skylight, and a private full bath. The finished lower level includes a welcoming family room with a fireplace, built-in shelving, office, full bath, and walkout access to the backyard and deck. Hardwood floors throughout, a gracious entry foyer, and fresh interior and exterior paint add to the home's appeal. Solar panels help keep electric costs low. Conveniently located close to town, schools, and New Canaan, this move-in ready home offers the perfect combination of comfort, convenience, and outdoor recreation. Don't miss this exceptional opportunity!
