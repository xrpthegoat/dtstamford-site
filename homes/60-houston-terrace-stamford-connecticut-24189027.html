About this home

3 bedroom house near the beach and close to Darien border. The feel of single-family home living without the maintenance. Close to shopping and all public transportation. Main floor has a luxury Kitchen with breakfast bar, Large living room, dining room, and half bath for guests. The gourmet eat-in kitchen with granite counter tops and Stainless Steel appliances is the perfect spot for entertaining. Beautiful hardwood floors run through the main and second levels. Second floor features three bedrooms and a full bath. Primary bedroom holds a king, second bedroom holds a queen, and the third bedroom is great for a nursey or home office with large closet. Pull down stairs to unfinished attic for extra storage. Need more storage? Check out the unfinished, walkout basement with your own washer and dryer. Unit has been freshly painted and is available for immediate occupancy. Lovely, private side patio for alfresco dining as well as large front porch. Excellent credit, proof of income and references required.
