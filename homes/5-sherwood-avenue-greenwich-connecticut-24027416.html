About this home

Meticulously restored and renovated by Fletcher Development in 2023, this classic Tudor home is set behind secure gates on 28 parklike acres. Inside, you'll find a spacious and well-designed layout featuring a double height formal living room, sunroom, office, dining room, and gourmet kitchen fully equipped with Wolf, Sub Zero and Miele appliances. There are four generously sized bedrooms, each with its own accompanying bathroom. Incredible attention to detail and craftsmanship is evident throughout, creating a seamless blend of historical charm and modern luxury. The property offers a retreat-like atmosphere for those seeking a refined and comfortable living experience in a picturesque setting.
