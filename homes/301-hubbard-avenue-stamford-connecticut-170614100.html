About this home

Welcome to this move in ready, charming Colonial in Stamford's convenient Mid-City neighborhood. Entertaining is effortless in the warm and inviting living room, centered around a wood-burning fireplace and adjacent family room. The sunny kitchen features new refrigerator, dishwasher and gas stove. A formal dining room sets the stage for intimate dinner parties, and a powder room completes the main floor. Upstairs, a luxurious primary suite offers a walk in closet and full bath. Down the hall are three additional bedrooms with generous closet space and an updated full bath. Enjoy Summer evenings grilling on the patio in the private, flat backyard. Recent upgrades include a new gas boiler (2019), new washer/dryer (2021), 3 mini splits (2020) and new garage doors (2020). All of this within walking distance to vibrant Downtown Stamford and minutes from the Metro-North station for an easy NYC commute. Don't miss the chance to make this house your home!
