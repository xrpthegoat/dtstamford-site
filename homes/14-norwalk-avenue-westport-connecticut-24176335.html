About this home

Classic Nantucket-style beach house just five homes from the sand at Compo Beach, offering the ultimate coastal lifestyle. Move right into this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath home filled with warmth, charm and natural light. The inviting family room features a wood-burning fireplace, while the renovated kitchen opens effortlessly for easy everyday living and entertaining. The sunlit primary suite includes a private balcony with views of Long Island Sound. Main floor laundry adds convenience. Outside, glass sliders lead to a beautifully landscaped patio and serene backyard oasis - the perfect place to relax, entertain and enjoy the salt air. Detached shed plus crawl-space storage complete this special beachside retreat.
