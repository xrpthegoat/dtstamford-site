About this home

Discover this beautifully updated brick 4-family building, situated in the southend of Stamford. Recently enhanced with a new roof and thermopane windows, this property marries classic charm with modern upgrades to deliver an exceptional rental opportunity. Each of the four units features a thoughtful layout of 5 rooms, including two bedrooms, a fully updated kitchen, a dedicated dining area, and a spacious living room. The kitchens and bathrooms have been tastefully remodeled with contemporary fixtures and finishes, complemented by new luxury vinyl plank flooring throughout. Five new electric panels and a state-of-the-art fire alarm system ensure safety and efficiency. New boilers and water heaters provide reliable comfort for all units. Ample storage in the walk-up attic, and benefit from washer/dryer hookups in the basement. Ideally located within walking distance to the marina, restaurants, and bus lines, this property offers residents both convenience and a vibrant community atmosphere. Ready to be rented, this building is an outstanding investment.
