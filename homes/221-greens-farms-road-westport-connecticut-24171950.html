About this home

The Bedford House: Architecturally and historically significant estate in Greens Farms. This exceptional property offers a rare opportunity to own one of Westport's true gems with a remarkable provenance. Set on 2.33 acres with a gunite pool & spa, court and a 2400 SF barn w/ 3 car garage, loft and more, this estate blends timeless elegance with modern comfort. Originally built in 1904 and designed by noted architects Visscher and Burley, this Colonial Revival style home has undergone thoughtful renovations over the years to preserve and enhance its original character. High ceilings, oversized windows, detailed millwork and sun-filled rooms create a sense of warmth and sophistication. Recent renovations include a new cedar roof on both house and barn, a like-new pool, spa & mechanicals, refinished hardwood floors, updated HVAC and new appliances. A unique highlight of this estate is its location across from Nyala Farms, offering scenic views of 50 acres of open meadows and fields with a deed restriction preventing any further development. Enjoy the ultimate lifestyle, minutes to the train, beaches, schools, shops and fine dining.
