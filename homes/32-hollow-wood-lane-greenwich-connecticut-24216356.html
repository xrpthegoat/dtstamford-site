About this home

An exceptional opportunity to enjoy Greenwich at an attractive price point! This inviting Pemberwick home offers 4 bedrooms and 3 full baths, with a versatile layout featuring one main-level bedroom and three upstairs, including a flexible two-bedroom suite. Generous storage, main-level laundry, and new appliances add convenience. Conveniently located near Pemberwick Park and playground, with easy access to downtown Greenwich, Metro-North, shopping, dining, beaches, parks, marina, ferry, and town pool. Enjoy more space, flexibility, and the Greenwich lifestyle with convenient access to New York City.
