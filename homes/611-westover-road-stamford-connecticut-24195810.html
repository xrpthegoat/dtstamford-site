About this home

Ultimate Private Retreat: Expansive 5 BR, 4 BA classic single-family home situated on over 5 acres of serene, ultra-private land in the highly desirable Westover area. This rare rental seamlessly combines timeless charm with modern convenience, featuring a brand-new high-efficiency HVAC system and a portable backup generator for ultimate peace of mind. Turn-key and move-in ready with beautifully refinished hardwood floors and newly painted interiors throughout. The sprawling floor plan boasts a large living room, a formal large dining room, two cozy fireplaces, two spectacular screened porches perfect for peaceful outdoor living and a lower-level workshop also includes an attached 2-car garage. Commuter's dream with easy access to the Stamford Train Station just 3 miles away. Pet-friendly with approval.
