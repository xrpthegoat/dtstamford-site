About this home

This wonderful chalet-style home is nestled in the woods and is deeply connected to its natural surroundings, making it a unique retreat. Exposed timber beams, multiple wood balconies and other charming features enhance the handcrafted Alpine feel of this home. Large windows in main rooms offer views reflecting trees and sky. The beautiful stone fireplace is the centerpiece of this home, anchoring the open, but cozy main living space. Much of the property is surrounded by Greenwich Land Trust and is across from Mianus River Park.
