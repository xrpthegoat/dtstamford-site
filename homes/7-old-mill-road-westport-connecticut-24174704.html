About this home

Stunning Waterfront Retreat with Four Decks, Private Yard & Breathtaking Views! Escape to this beautifully appointed 2-bedroom, 2-bath furnished rental set directly on picturesque Old Mill Pond in the highly sought-after Compo Beach neighborhood. Designed for relaxation and coastal living, this light-filled home offers spectacular water views from every room and the perfect blend of comfort, charm, and convenience. Enjoy morning coffee at sunrise and evening cocktails at sunset from any of the four spacious decks overlooking Long Island Sound. Launch your kayak or canoe right from the backyard or stroll across to the beach for a swim and a day in the sun. Inside, the home is bright, airy, and equipped with central air for effortless summer comfort. Just minutes from the train, golf, tennis, shopping, and fine dining, this is the ideal seasonal getaway for those looking to enjoy the best of waterfront living. A rare opportunity to experience a true coastal escape. Credit check and references required.
