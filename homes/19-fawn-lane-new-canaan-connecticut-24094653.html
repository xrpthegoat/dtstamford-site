About this home

Magnificent Stone & Shingle home on a quiet cul-de-sac. Perched atop a hill on two flat, meticulously landscaped acres, this exceptional stone and shingle "Arts & Crafts" style home offers rare views of Long Island Sound. A dramatic foyer opens into a stunning great room featuring 10' foot coffered ceilings, carved marble fireplace, and French doors leading out to a limestone terrace, beautiful pool, expansive lawn, and lush garden. Designed for grand entertaining, the oversized dining room easily accommodates large gatherings. The chef's kitchen boasts exquisite granite countertops, high-end appliances, and a spacious eat-in area perfect for casual dining. A mahogany paneled library, complete with fireplace, custom built-ins, and French door access to the backyard, provides a refined retreat. The luxurious primary suite features a large walk-in closet and a spa-inspired bath. Three additional ensuite bedrooms offer comfort and privacy for family or guests. The lower level enhances the home's appeal with a guest bedroom and sitting room which has a separate private entrance, a home theater with ample seating, a large fully equipped gym, and a sauna. This meticulously maintained, like-new residence combines timeless elegance with modern amenities, offering breathtaking views and ultimate privacy.
