About this home

Charming 1920's colonial-style gem with enclosed front porch and an open floor plan, accented by handcrafted built-ins and oversized windows, creating a bright, airy space that's perfect for hosting lively gatherings or simply enjoying the comforts of everyday life. The updated kitchen features stainless steel appliances and granite countertops, and has beautiful views of the private rear yard. Step outside through the mudroom adjoining the kitchen, and you'll find an enchanting outdoor retreat. Relax on the deck, surrounded by beautifully landscaped grounds, stone walls, and vibrant blooms, all offering a sense of privacy and tranquility. A large, detached two-car garage with overhead storage provides additional convenience. Upstairs, there are three spacious bedrooms with hardwood flooring. The primary bedroom features a spacius walk in closet with built-ins. Both bathrooms feature tubs with showers. The lower level has a separate entrance and features a half bath and storage utility room. Nestled on a quiet side street, this home is a commuter's dream -- just a short walk to the Noroton Heights train station for easy access to NYC. Also walking distance is Middlesex Middle School, as well as local shops, markets, Equinox gym, and lovely restaurants. Move right in and enjoy all Darien has to offer, including top-rated public schools that are consistently ranked among the best in the state. Highest and best bids due Sunday at 6pm, please.
