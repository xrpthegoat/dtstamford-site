About this home

Nestled on one of the largest lots in the coveted waterfront community of Shorefront Park, this exceptional and updated four-bedroom home has views of Long Island Sound from every floor. Fully renovated in 2010 and elevated in 2014 it blends the timeless beauty of its 1920s origins with modern amenities for comfort and style. An oversized IPE back deck is perfect for outdoor entertaining, and is complimented by a hot tub, outdoor shower, and plenty of space for al fresco dining and relaxing. With front porches on every level and a fully fenced-in backyard, this property offers many outdoor living options. Inside, the first floor features an open-concept layout, highlighted by a kitchen with high-end finishes, a mudroom with built-in cubbies, and hardwood floors throughout. Numerous windows flood the generous living area with natural light, while a cozy fireplace adds warmth and ambiance. Upstairs, the second floor offers three spacious bedrooms, a full bath, and a walk-in laundry room. The third floor is a luxurious primary suite with vaulted ceilings, his and hers walk-in closets, a plumbed area for a wet bar, and a Juliet balcony with French doors. The spa-inspired master bath features a private water closet, a double shower with 10 showerheads, an oversized jetted tub, and double vanity sinks. This property also includes a detached three-car garage with 50+ amp service and the potential for additional living space.
