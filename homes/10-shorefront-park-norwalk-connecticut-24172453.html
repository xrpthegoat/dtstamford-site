About this home

A very special offering, this fully-renovated gem is a rare opportunity to live in the coveted Shorefront Park waterfront community! A quiet neighborhood of 80 homes where seasonal gatherings are held at the two private waterfront parks owned by the community association. Stroll to the "beach lot" and enjoy a spectacular sunrise over the water every morning. Store and launch your kayaks at the "boat lot", just a short walk from your home. The South Norwalk Boat Club and marinas along Water Street are just outside the neighborhood. When you're not enjoying the water, there is so much to explore in South Norwalk! Power walk to the new Woodward Park pickleball courts. Head into SoNo for brunch or dinner or to the South Norwalk Metro North station for a trip into NYC. Wander to the soon-to-be-developed Manresa Island Park which will offer walking trails and 1.75 miles of public waterfront access. A year-round vacation lifestyle can be yours! Just bring your furniture, move in and enjoy. This is one of the largest lots in Shorefront Park so there is room to expand, if desired.
