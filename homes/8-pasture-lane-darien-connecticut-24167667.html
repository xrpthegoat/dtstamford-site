About this home

Welcome to 8 Pasture Lane, a property that redefines FUN in luxury living with resort-style elegance in one of Darien's most desirable locations. This stunning 6-bedroom, 5,500 sq. ft. home offers impeccable design, thoughtful architecture & an extraordinary flow that's as unique and functional as it is beautiful. From the moment you step inside, the first floor sets the tone for a home that's both inviting & impressive. Its seamless layout creates an open and effortless connection between spaces, with true showstoppers including the awesome kitchen-family room & vibey primary suite. And let's not forget the unbeatable location-just minutes from beaches, shops, top-notch restaurants, & the train, 8 Pasture Lane seamlessly combines convenience with tranquility. A lifestyle designed for making memories & savoring every moment.
