About this home

YOUR NEXT CHAPER SHOULD START RiGHT HERE IN WILTON! Welcome to Glen River, a premier townhouse community offering an exceptional combination of location, privacy, lifestyle, & value. Aggressively priced, this beautifully maintained 1bed+Den townhouse lives like a 2bed & is ideally positioned in one of the most private settings in the complex, overlooking a peaceful tree-lined backdrop. Walk to everything Wilton Village has to offer, including charming boutiques, restaurants, coffee shops, ice cream, grocery store, library, parks, Metro-North train, & Norwalk River Valley Trail. Inside an open kitchen flows into living/dining room where dramatic floor-to-ceiling stacked stone wood-burning fireplace creates a beautiful focal point. Sliders open to a private patio & bring abundant natural light into the home. Upper-level features spacious primary bedroom w/ vaulted ceilings & large walk-in closet, versatile den, full bath, laundry, and generous storage. The den provides excellent flexibility as a 2nd bedroom, office, guest room, or additional living space. a 1-car garage w/ additional storage adds additional convenience. Enjoy Glen River's beautifully maintained grounds and resort-style in-ground pool w/ lounge chairs, tables, umbrellas and changing area. Located in one of CT's most highly regarded school districts, this is an incredible opportunity to enjoy Wilton living, walkability, and top-rated schools at a very attractive price.
