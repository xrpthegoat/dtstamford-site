About this home

262 Atlantic Street presents a rare opportunity to own or occupy a free-standing commercial building in the heart of downtown Stamford. Offering over 13,000 square feet across three levels, this property is ready for immediate build-out, making it an ideal blank canvas for retail, creative office, medical, or a variety of commercial uses. The building features a flexible open layout, three bathrooms, and excellent street frontage along highly trafficked Atlantic Street, surrounded by restaurants, shops, and new development. Located just steps from the Stamford Metro-North and Amtrak station, and minutes from I-95, this location offers outstanding accessibility and visibility. With a full-height basement, expansive first floor, and a second level with open mezzanine views, the space invites customization to meet the vision of any investor or business owner. Whether you're expanding a growing brand, launching a new concept, or seeking a long-term asset in one of Fairfield County's most vibrant downtown corridors, 262 Atlantic Street offers unmatched potential and prime positioning in a high-demand market.
