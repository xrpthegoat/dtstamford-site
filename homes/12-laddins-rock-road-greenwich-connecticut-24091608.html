About this home

Welcome to this meticulously maintained 5-bedroom, 4-bathroom home ideally situated just steps from the Library and beautiful Binney Park. The main floor features an open layout with sun-filled living areas that flow seamlessly, featuring hardwood floors and coffered ceilings. The heart of the home is the generous kitchen, dining and family room, opening to a private backyard-ideal for hosting gatherings, kicking a soccer ball or enjoying quiet afternoons outdoors. The expansive primary suite is a true retreat, complete with a large walk-in closet, and a spa-like en-suite bathroom. 4 additional bedrooms accommodate family, guests or a WFH office. Lower Level boasts laundry & gym/flex space. A short stroll to Old Greenwich shops, dining, beach and Metro-North.
