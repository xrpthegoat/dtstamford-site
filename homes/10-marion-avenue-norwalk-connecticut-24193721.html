About this home

Welcome to 10 Marion Avenue, where timeless Marvin Beach charm meets thoughtful modern updates in one of Norwalk's most coveted waterfront neighborhoods. This home embodies the timeless character and coastal charm that define Marvin Beach. Warm, inviting, impeccably maintained, and full of character, it reflects the pride of ownership that is evident throughout. One of the home's most special features is the welcoming front porch, where you can enjoy peaceful water views and the coastal lifestyle that makes this neighborhood so desirable. Inside, classic architectural details blend seamlessly with tasteful updates, creating a home that is both charming and move-in ready. The spacious primary suite is a true retreat. Bright, comfortable living spaces and a thoughtfully designed layout make everyday living and entertaining effortless. Whether you're relaxing on the front porch, taking a short stroll to the water, or simply enjoying all that this exceptional neighborhood has to offer, 10 Marion Avenue captures the very best of Marvin Beach living. A rare opportunity to own a beautifully cared-for home in one of East Norwalk's most beloved waterfront communities.
