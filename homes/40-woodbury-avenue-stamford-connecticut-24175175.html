About this home

Welcome to 40 Woodbury Avenue, a beautifully maintained, move-in ready home in Stamford's highly desirable Springdale neighborhood. Freshly painted throughout, this pristine 3-bedroom, 1.5-bath home features hardwood floors throughout and blends charm, comfort, and convenience with tasteful updates inside and out. In addition to the upper living levels, the lower level offers a laundry area, additional storage, and flexible bonus space ideal for a home office, playroom, or gym, with walkout access to the backyard and patio. Set on a rare half-acre lot, the property offers exceptional potential for future expansion while providing the perfect setting for outdoor enjoyment, with beautifully landscaped gardens featuring pollinator-friendly plantings, a greenhouse and enclosed garden area, automatic drip irrigation, a white picket fence, and pergola. A barn with electricity adds exceptional flexibility for hobbies, workshop space, or additional storage. Other highlights include ample parking, and a generator hookup with interlock system for added peace of mind. Conveniently located just minutes from the train station, shopping, restaurants, grocery stores, and major highways, yet tucked away in a quiet neighborhood, this turnkey home offers the perfect balance of convenience and country-like charm.
